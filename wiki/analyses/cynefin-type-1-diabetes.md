# Cynefin Application to Type 1 Diabetes

## Summary

Riva Greenberg and Boudewijn Bertsch demonstrate that managing type 1 diabetes is primarily a complex problem rather than a complicated one. Blood sugar levels emerge from countless interacting variables and cannot be controlled through cause-effect analysis. The Cynefin Framework provides a more realistic paradigm that reduces patient guilt while enabling better management strategies.

## Key facts

- Blood sugar levels are not manufactured by calculations and treatment decisions but are emergent properties of many unpredictable and unknown processes in the body and environment. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p192]
- The Cynefin Framework shows that extremely high or low blood sugar requires Best Practices (Clear domain), while daily management is primarily Complex. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p194]
- Mastery in diabetes means knowing how to recognize patterns and influence blood sugar, and respond to any number to nudge it in the right direction—not never making mistakes. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p202]
- The 'control paradigm' in healthcare inadvertently contributes to patient disengagement by setting impossible goals. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p199]

## Inferences

- Inference: The application of Cynefin to healthcare suggests its utility extends beyond organizational management to personal health and medical practice.
