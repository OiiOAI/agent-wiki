# Munger Investment Principles

## Summary

The Munger Investment Principles encompass ten key areas of investment decision-making: Risk, Independence, Preparation, Intellectual Humility, Analytic Rigor, Allocation, Patience, Decisiveness, Change, and Focus. These principles form a comprehensive checklist for evaluating investments, reflecting Munger's multidisciplinary approach and his emphasis on preparation, discipline, patience, and decisiveness working together as a unified system.

## Key facts

- The ten principles are: (1) Risk—measure especially reputational risk, incorporate margin of safety, avoid people of questionable character; (2) Independence—objectivity requires independence of thought; (3) Preparation—develop through voracious reading, cultivate curiosity, develop fluency in mental models; (4) Intellectual humility—stay within circle of competence, identify disconfirming evidence; (5) Analytic rigor—use scientific method and checklists; (6) Allocation—proper allocation is the investor's number one job; (7) Patience—resist human bias to act, compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world; (8) Decisiveness—when proper circumstances present, act with conviction; (9) Change—live with complexity, adapt to reality; (10) Focus—keep things simple, guard against hubris and boredom. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p72]
- Munger applies a 'prior to pulling the trigger' checklist including: current price and volume, disclosure timing, contingent exit strategies, better capital uses, liquid capital availability, and opportunity cost. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p68]
- Munger's evaluation considers both rational factors and 'subconscious influences where the brain at subconscious level is automatically forming conclusions which are useful but which open malfunction.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p63]
- Munger's famous saying: 'A great business at a fair price is superior to a fair business at a great price.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p67]
- Munger believes a successful investment career boils down to only a handful of decisions, so when he likes a business, he makes a very large bet and holds for a long period. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p60]

## Inferences

- Inference: Munger's ten principles work as an integrated system rather than as independent rules, reflecting his ecosystem approach to investment analysis.
