# Modern Feminist Theory

## Summary

Modern Feminist Theory encompasses a range of theoretical approaches addressing gender inequality, oppression, and difference. The chapter structure reveals a progression from micro-social to macro-social analyses, covering cultural feminism, existential-phenomenological analyses, sociological theories, liberal feminism, psychoanalytic feminism, radical feminism, socialist feminism, intersectionality theory, and engagements with postmodernism.
