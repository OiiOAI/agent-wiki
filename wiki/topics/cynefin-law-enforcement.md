# Cynefin and Law Enforcement

## Summary

Lou Hayes Jr. applies the Cynefin Framework to U.S. policing, using it to describe phenomena, environments, philosophies, mindsets, and strategies. The framework influenced training design, investigative approaches, and intelligence functions in suburban Chicago police departments.

## Key facts

- Cynefin helped transform how Lou Hayes' tactical team approached operational strategy by showing the 'why' behind decisions rather than just the 'how'. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p207]
- The framework helped detectives understand differences between investigating financial cyber-fraud schemes versus disrupting emergent street gang patterns. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p208]
- Intelligence analysts were previously conditioned to rely on raw unadulterated data through a Complicated lens; Cynefin helped them develop competing Complex theories requiring judgment and bias. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p208]
- Cynefin helped distinguish when to use Command & Control versus Mission Command approaches based on situation type. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p209]
