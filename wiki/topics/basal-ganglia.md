# Basal ganglia

## Summary

The basal ganglia comprise four principal structures: the striatum (caudate nucleus and putamen), globus pallidus (external and internal segments), substantia nigra (pars compacta and pars reticulata), and subthalamic nucleus. These interconnected nuclei form parallel cortico-basal ganglia-thalamocortical circuits involved in skeletal motor, oculomotor, associative, and limbic functions. The basal ganglia play roles in movement initiation, motor learning, habit formation, and reward processing.

- The basal ganglia output nuclei project to specific thalamic nuclei and brain stem areas, including the pedunculopontine nucleus and superior colliculus.
