# Team Topology Design Principles (Chapter 4)

## Summary

Chapter 4 of Team Topologies outlines critical principles for designing effective software delivery teams, emphasizing that team structure significantly impacts delivery speed and that no single topology fits all organizations.

## Key facts

- Ad hoc or constantly changing team design slows down software delivery. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1255]
- There is no single definitive team topology but several inadequate topologies for any one organization. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1256]
- Technical and cultural maturity, org scale, and engineering discipline are critical aspects when considering which topology to adopt. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1257-L1258]
- The feature-team/product-team pattern is powerful but only works with a supportive surrounding environment. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1259-L1260]
- Splitting a team's responsibilities can break down silos and empower other teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1261]
