# Life and Mind

## Summary

Life and Mind is the theme of Part Two of 'Mind in Life,' examining the deep continuity between biological life and conscious experience. Thompson uses the theory of autopoiesis to build a bridge between the philosophy of the organism and the philosophy of mind. The central thesis is that life and mind share basic organizational properties, with mental properties being an enriched version of those fundamental to life. Key topics include autopoiesis and its relation to Kant's natural purposes, the debate over whether autopoiesis entails teleology, Jonas's philosophy of the organism, and the concepts of sense-making, immanent purposiveness, and the self-transcendence of living beings.
