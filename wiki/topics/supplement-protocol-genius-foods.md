# Supplement Protocol (Genius Foods)

## Summary

A comprehensive supplement protocol recommended in Genius Foods for supporting brain health, cognitive function, and overall wellness. The protocol includes fish oil (EPA/DHA), vitamin D3, B vitamins (folate, B12, B6), vitamin K2, turmeric, astaxanthin, and probiotics, with specific dosage recommendations and rationale for each.

## Key facts

- Fish oil supplementation should provide approximately 500 mg DHA and 1000 mg EPA daily from fish oil or fatty fish, and should be refrigerated to maintain freshness. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p427]
- Krill oil provides omega-3s in phospholipid form, potentially more bioavailable than the triglyceride form in fish oil, and also contains choline and astaxanthin. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p428]
- Vitamin D3 supplementation of 2000 to 5000 IU per day is recommended, with blood levels checked every six months to maintain 40 to 60 ng/ml. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p430]
- Low vitamin D was found to be the strongest environmental risk factor for developing dementia according to a meta-analysis. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p429]
- B vitamin supplementation (400 mcg folate as methylfolate, 500 mcg B12 as methylcobalamin, 20 mg B6) is recommended when B levels are low or homocysteine is elevated (ideally below 9 umol/l). [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p433]
- Elevated homocysteine is linked to a twofold risk for developing dementia, heart attack, and stroke, with brain shrinkage odds up to ten times higher. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p432]
- Vitamin K2 supplementation of 50 to 100 mcg K2 MK-7 daily is recommended for calcium homeostasis and arterial health. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p433]
- Turmeric supplementation of 500 to 1000 mg is recommended, with formulations containing piperine or phytosome for enhanced bioavailability. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p434]
- Astaxanthin at 12 mg daily with a fat-containing meal is recommended for cognitive function protection and antioxidant effects. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p435]
- Probiotic supplementation of 5 to 10 billion CFUs with multiple different strains is recommended, ideally taken with prebiotic fiber. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p436]

## Inferences

- Inference: The supplement protocol is designed to address common nutritional deficiencies (vitamin D, B vitamins) while providing targeted support for brain health through omega-3s, antioxidants, and anti-inflammatory compounds.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The research on probiotics is described as 'new and evolving,' indicating ongoing scientific investigation.
- Uncertain: There is not a complete scientific consensus on ideal vitamin D levels, though 40 to 60 ng/ml appears to correlate with lowest mortality.
