# Building Wealth

## Summary

Building wealth is about understanding how wealth is created through specific knowledge, leverage, and accountability. The key principles include seeking wealth (not money or status), owning equity in a business, giving society what it wants but doesn't know how to get, playing long-term games with long-term people, and learning to sell and build.

## Key facts

- Getting rich is about knowing what to do, who to do it with, and when to do it; it is much more about understanding than purely hard work. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p25]
- You will not get rich renting out your time; you must own equity—a piece of a business—to gain financial freedom. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p26]
- You will get rich by giving society what it wants but does not yet know how to get, at scale. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p26]
- Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p27]
- Learn to sell and learn to build; if you can do both, you will be unstoppable. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p27]
- The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers; most people haven't figured this out yet. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p26]

## Inferences

- Inference: Naval's wealth principles emphasize that wealth comes from creating value for society through unique abilities and leverage, not from exchanging time for money.
