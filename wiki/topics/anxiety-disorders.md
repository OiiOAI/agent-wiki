# Anxiety Disorders

## Summary

Anxiety disorders stem from abnormal regulation of fear and include panic disorder, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), generalized anxiety disorder, social anxiety disorder, simple phobias, and obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD). These disorders involve dysfunction in amygdala-based fear circuits and are distinguished by the nature, intensity, and time course of symptoms. Treatment includes cognitive-behavioral therapy and medications such as selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors and benzodiazepines.

## Key facts

- In the United States 28.5% of the population suffers from one or more anxiety disorders over the course of their lifetimes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1469]
- Panic disorder is characterized by unexpected panic attacks with symptoms including palpitations, shortness of breath, sweating, and fear of losing control or dying. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1470]
- Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) follows severe danger or injury and is characterized by emotional numbness, reliving painful traumatic episodes, disturbed sleep, nightmares, and hyperarousal including exaggerated startle response. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1470]
- Animal models of fear have elucidated amygdala-based fear circuitry that mediates defensive behaviors and appropriate physiological responses to danger. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1471]
- Functional neuroimaging has revealed heightened activity in the amygdala in social anxiety disorder and PTSD. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1472]
