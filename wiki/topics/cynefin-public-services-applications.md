# Cynefin Applications in Public Services

## Summary

The Cynefin Framework has been applied extensively in Welsh public services since before 2008, influencing organizational change, health services, and government policy. Key applications include the Valleys Project, SenseMaker projects in the NHS, and alignment with the Wellbeing of Future Generations Act.

## Key facts

- Dave Snowden's ideas spread in Wales through Summer Schools with favorites like the 'Gorilla playing basketball eye test' and 'How to organize a party for teenagers' story. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p126]
- Within Welsh NHS, enthusiasm from Matt Wyatt and Sion Charles led to cohorts trained and multiple SenseMaker projects. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p128]
- The Valleys Project in South Wales engaged communities across the South Wales coalfield using Cynefin approaches. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p130]
- Dave Snowden defined a small country as any group of less than 5 million loosely connected people, including Nova Scotia, New Zealand, Singapore, Nordic Countries, and Wales. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p127]
