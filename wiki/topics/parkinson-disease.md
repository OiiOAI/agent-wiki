# Parkinson disease

## Summary

Parkinson disease is a common degenerative disorder affecting 1-2% of the population over 65 years of age. It is characterized by resting tremor, bradykinesia, rigidity, and impairment in movement initiation and sustainment. The pathological hallmark is progressive loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra pars compacta. Both sporadic and hereditary forms exist, with several genetic loci (PARK1-PARK11) identified. The pathophysiology involves abnormal neuronal firing patterns and synchronized oscillatory activity throughout basal ganglia-thalamocortical circuits.
