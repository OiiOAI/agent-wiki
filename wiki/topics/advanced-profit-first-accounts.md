# Advanced Profit First Accounts

## Summary

Advanced Profit First accounts extend beyond the five foundational accounts to address specific business needs. These include the Vault for emergency reserves, Stocking for inventory purchases, Drip for prepaid projects, Pass-Through for reimbursements, Equipment for future purchases, and others that provide financial clarity for unique business situations.

## Key facts

- The Vault is an ultra-low-risk, interest-bearing account for short-term emergencies providing a three-month operating reserve. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p167]
- The Stocking Account accumulates funds for large inventory purchases, allocating 1/20th of the needed amount each allocation period. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p168]
- The Drip Account handles retainers and advance payments, spreading large upfront payments into monthly income as work is completed. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p170]
- The Pass-Through Account receives reimbursement income that is not allocated for Profit or Owner Comp, going directly to pay corresponding vendors. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p168]
- Mo's Rule: 'When in doubt add an account.' Additional accounts customize Profit First for unique business requirements. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p165]
- Sales Tax collected is immediately allocated to a separate Sales Tax account and is never treated as income. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p171]

## Inferences

- Inference: Advanced accounts provide granular financial visibility that enables better decision-making for specific business needs.
