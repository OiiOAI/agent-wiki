# Spinocerebellum

## Summary

The spinocerebellum comprises the vermis and intermediate parts of the cerebellar hemispheres. It receives extensive sensory input from the spinal cord conveying touch, pressure, and limb position information through direct and indirect mossy fiber pathways. The spinocerebellum modulates descending motor systems, projecting to fastigial and interposed nuclei that influence brain stem reticulospinal and vestibulospinal tracts and the corticospinal and rubrospinal systems.

- The ventral spinocerebellar tract is active only during active movements and transmits an efference copy or corollary discharge of spinal motor neuron activity. - The spinocerebellum modulates and initiates, rather than controls, descending commands to the spinal cord. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1023]
