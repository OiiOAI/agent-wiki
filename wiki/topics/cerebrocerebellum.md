# Cerebrocerebellum

## Summary

The cerebrocerebellum consists of the lateral hemispheres of the cerebellum and is involved in planning movement. Unlike other cerebellar regions that receive sensory information directly from the spinal cord, the lateral hemispheres receive input exclusively from the cerebral cortex through the pontine nuclei and middle cerebellar peduncle. The cerebrocerebellum may also have cognitive functions including verbal skills and problem-solving, and may participate in mental rehearsal of movements and motor learning.

## Key facts

- The lateral hemispheres receive input exclusively from the cerebral cortex, transmitted through the pontine nuclei and through the middle cerebellar peduncle to the contralateral dentate nucleus and lateral hemisphere. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1025]
- Some neurons in the dentate nucleus fire some 100 ms before a movement begins and even before the discharge of neurons in either the primary motor cortex or interposed nuclei. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1027]
- Clinical observations suggest that the cerebrocerebellum has a role in the planning and programming of hand movements. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1027]
- The lateral hemispheres in humans are much larger than in monkeys, just as the frontal regions of the cerebral cortex are greatly expanded. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1025]

## Inferences

- Inference: The enlarged lateral hemispheres in humans compared to monkeys suggest expanded cognitive roles in humans paralleling the expansion of prefrontal cortex.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact contribution of the cerebrocerebellum to purely cognitive tasks remains to be fully determined.
