# Differentiation and Survival of Nerve Cells

## Summary

Differentiation and Survival of Nerve Cells covers how neural progenitor cells generate diverse neuronal and glial cell types, how neurons migrate to their final positions, how neurotransmitter phenotype is specified, and how neurotrophic factors regulate neuronal survival through suppression of programmed cell death. Neural progenitors use both symmetric and asymmetric cell division. Radial glial cells serve as both stem-like progenitors and migration scaffolds. Delta-Notch signaling and bHLH transcription factors regulate cell fate. Neuronal migration occurs along radial glia (cortex) or axonal tracts. Target-derived neurotrophic factors (NGF, BDNF, NT-3) promote survival by suppressing a latent caspase-dependent apoptotic program.

## Key facts

- Neural progenitor cells undergo both symmetric division (producing two stem cells for amplification) and asymmetric division (producing stem cell and differentiated neuron). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1239]
- Radial glial cells are neural stem cells that generate both neurons and astrocytes, and serve as scaffolds for neuronal migration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1239]
- Delta-Notch signaling creates lateral inhibition: cells with higher Notch activity become glia, while cells with lower Notch become neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1241]
- Neuronal migration follows an 'inside-first, outside-last' pattern in the cerebral cortex, with early-born neurons settling in deep layers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1244]
- Reelin signaling from Cajal-Retzius cells is required for neurons to detach from radial glial scaffolds and complete migration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1245]
- Sympathetic neuron transmitter phenotype is plastic: neurons initially use norepinephrine but switch to acetylcholine upon innervating sweat glands due to target-derived cytokines. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1250]
- Approximately half of all neurons generated during development die through programmed cell death, with extent regulated by target-derived neurotrophic factors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1253]
- Neurotrophic factors suppress a latent death program by activating PI3K-Akt signaling, which inhibits pro-apoptotic Bcl-2 family proteins. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1256]
