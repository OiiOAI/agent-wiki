# Mastery in Relationships

## Summary

Mastery principles apply directly to intimate relationships, requiring the same five keys as mastery in any skill: instruction (seeking guidance), practice (dedicated time together), surrender (letting go of ego), intentionality (cultivating positive attitude), and the Edge (staying open to adventure and intimacy). The most important learning and development occurs during the long periods on the plateau rather than during dramatic ups or downs.

## Key facts

- If you have to work at a sport to achieve mastery, you also have to work, and generally work even more diligently, to achieve mastery in relationships. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p98]
- In relationships, as in other areas of mastery, the most important learning and development takes place during time on the plateau. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p98]
- Instruction in relationships: seeking the best guidance available—whether counselor, book, or sympathetic friend—is essential on the path of mastery. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p99]
- Practice in relationships: couples on the path of mastery set aside specific times just for the relationship, apart from children, friends, work, and usual entertainments. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p99]
- Surrender in relationships: the ability to surrender to your art is a mark of the master, whether the art is martial or marital. The trick is learning to lose your ego without losing your balance. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p99]
- Intentionality in relationships: cultivating a positive attitude combined with mental toughness and openness can be applied to relationships as well as to sports. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p99]
- The Edge in relationships: a couple on the path stays open to experience and willing to play new games together. The greatest adventure is intimacy—the willingness to strip away one layer of reticence after another. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p99]
