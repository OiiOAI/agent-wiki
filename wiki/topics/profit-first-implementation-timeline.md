# Profit First Implementation Timeline

## Summary

The Profit First implementation follows a structured timeline starting with Day One setup, progressing through weekly and twice-monthly rhythms, quarterly distributions, and ultimately becoming a permanent way of life. Each phase builds upon the previous, creating sustainable financial habits through incremental changes.

## Key facts

- Day One requires: 1) Telling your people (accountant/bookkeeper), 2) Setting up accounts with CAPs and TAPs, 3) Making first distributions, 4) Celebrating. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p108]
- Week One focuses on cutting expenses by at least 10 percent of total expenses. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p118]
- Twice monthly (10th and 25th): Deposit all revenue, allocate to accounts based on CAPs, transfer PROFIT and TAX to no-temptation bank, pay owner salary, pay bills. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p121]
- Each quarter: Take 50 percent of PROFIT account as distribution, leave 50 percent as reserve, pay quarterly tax estimates, adjust CAPs closer to TAPs by approximately 3 percentage points total. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p127]
- Year One: Finalize taxes, determine if TAX account is sufficient, adjust percentages as needed, maintain three-month reserve in PROFIT account. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p128]

## Inferences

- Inference: The implementation is designed to be gradual to prevent overwhelming the business owner and to build sustainable habits.
