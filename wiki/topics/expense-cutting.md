# Expense Cutting Strategies

## Summary

Expense cutting in Profit First involves systematically reducing operating costs without compromising business operations. Key strategies include labeling expenses as Profit-generating (P), Replaceable (R), or Unnecessary (U), negotiating vendor contracts, and implementing the 'just one more day' technique to test whether purchases are truly needed.

## Key facts

- Label each expense: P for profit-generating, R for replaceable with cheaper alternatives, U for unnecessary. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p138]
- Cancel whatever you don't need to help your business run efficiently and keep your customers happy. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p119]
- Negotiate every remaining expense, except payroll. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p119]
- The 'just one more day' technique: When you need to purchase something, challenge yourself to go just one more day without it to test true necessity. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p143]
- Most businesses can easily cut 10 to 20 percent of expenses overnight by eliminating frivolous costs. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p118]

## Inferences

- Inference: Cutting expenses creates immediate cash flow improvement without requiring revenue growth.
