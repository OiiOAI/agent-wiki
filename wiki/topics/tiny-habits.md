# Tiny Habits

## Summary

Tiny Habits is a behavior change method developed by BJ Fogg at Stanford University. The method focuses on making new habits extremely small (tiny) rather than relying on motivation or willpower. The core framework involves three elements: Motivation, Ability, and Prompts (B=MAP), plus the essential technique of Celebration to wire habits into the brain. The approach emphasizes starting tiny, finding effective Anchors, and celebrating successes to create lasting change.

## Key facts

- The Tiny Habits method is based on the Fogg Behavior Model: B = M × A × P (Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and Prompt converge). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p117]
- The method has six steps: Clarify the Aspiration, Explore Behavior Options, Match with Specific Behaviors, Start Tiny, Find a Good Prompt, and Celebrate Successes. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p118]
- The central philosophy is that simplicity changes behavior—not motivation manipulation. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p112]
- Habits can form very quickly (often in just a few days) when people have a strong positive emotion connected to the behavior, not through repetition alone. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p160]
- The book states: 'People change best by feeling good, not by feeling bad.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p181]
- The book provides 300 sample recipes for Tiny Habits in the appendix. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p130]
