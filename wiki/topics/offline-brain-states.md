# Offline Brain States

## Summary

Offline brain states refer to periods when the brain is disengaged from external input, particularly during sleep (non-REM) and quiet waking. These states feature internally generated activity patterns, including sharp wave ripples and replay events, that serve memory consolidation and prospective planning functions.

## Key facts

- The two major neocortical events during non-REM sleep—slow oscillations and sleep spindles—have a nuanced relationship with hippocampal sharp wave ripples. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p211]
- Replay during sharp wave ripples allows previously acquired memories to be flexibly manipulated internally to evaluate alternative future paths and plan optimal behavioral trajectories. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p214]
- Stitching together neuronal trajectories from sharp wave ripples can be conceived as a mechanism for preconscious contemplation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p215]
