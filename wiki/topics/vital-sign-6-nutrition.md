# Vital Sign 6: Nutrition

## Summary

A section of the book focusing on nutrition as the sixth vital sign for physical durability. The authors emphasize protein and micronutrients as the two key elements of diet, explaining how proper nutrition supports muscles, tendons, ligaments, tissues, cartilage, bones, and influences inflammation levels.

## Key facts

- The authors focus on two key nutritional elements: protein and micronutrients, believing everything else falls into place when these are prioritized. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1493-L1496]
- Daily nutrient intake affects all components that allow movement including muscles, tendons, ligaments, cartilage, and bones. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1464-L1466]
- Protein needs increase with age as the body gradually loses muscle mass starting in one's thirties at about 3 to 5 percent per decade. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1528-L1531]
- Micronutrients (vitamins and minerals) assist in cell growth, immune function, energy production, nerve conduction, and muscle contraction. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1539-L1542]
- The authors recommend an 800-gram daily intake of micronutrient-rich foods as a target. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1554-L1557]

## Inferences

- Inference: Focusing on protein and micronutrients provides a simpler framework for healthy eating than following specific diet plans like Paleo, Keto, or Mediterranean.
