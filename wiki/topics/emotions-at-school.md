# Emotions at School

## Summary

Schools face significant emotional challenges: 77 percent of high school students report negative daily feelings (tired, stressed, bored), 40 percent of teachers leave within five years, and bullying remains prevalent. Effective social and emotional learning (SEL) requires systemic approaches, adult role modeling, and integration across all grade levels from preschool through college.

## Key facts

- When asked how they feel each day in school, 77 percent of 22,000 surveyed high school students responded with negative feelings; the three most frequent were tired, stressed, and bored. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p170]
- Forty percent of teachers leave the profession within five years, and in some communities the rate is as high as 30 percent annually. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p169]
- One in five American children experiences a mental health issue such as depression or anxiety, and over half of all seventeen-year-olds report having either experienced or witnessed trauma. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p171]
- In a study of two thousand teachers, only about 50 percent said they have strong individual relationships with their students, and only 34 percent of students reported having such relationships. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p172]
- Classroom corporal punishment is still allowed in nineteen states, and suspensions and expulsions disproportionately applied to Black students have continued to rise. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p172]
- RULER has been integrated into about a quarter of New York City's 1,700 public schools. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p192]
