# Curiosity in Education

## Summary

The book explores how educators can foster purposeful curiosity in students through several approaches: turning students from passive recipients into curious owners of their learning journey; creating safe atmospheres where students feel comfortable asking questions without fear of judgment; encouraging project-based work aligned with student interests; and providing hands-on experiences that allow students to directly engage with their surroundings and embrace uncertainty. The author argues that academics are paid to be purposefully curious and should actively protect the spirit of inquiry in their teaching.
