# Multinutrient Supplements for Mental Health

## Summary

Broad-spectrum multinutrient supplements containing minerals and vitamins in balance have been studied for treating mental health conditions including ADHD, mood disorders, aggression, and stress. Key products include EMPowerplus, Daily Essential Nutrients (DEN), and others, with research showing approximately 80-85 percent of participants achieving long-term remission.

## Key facts

- EMP and DEN have over 50 peer-reviewed studies demonstrating mental health benefits. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p259]
- Long-term follow-up showed 80-85 percent of participants remained in remission from mental health symptoms with ongoing multinutrient treatment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p270]
- No deaths have been attributed to vitamin supplements since Canada began keeping records in 1967. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p267]
- In the US, no one has ever died from a vitamin pill according to the 2017 American Association of Poison Control Center report. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p267]
- The dropout rate due to side effects across multinutrient research was about 5 percent, with no difference between active and placebo groups. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p269]
- Over 200 research participants showed long-term safety with no recurring pattern of negative blood changes. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p270]
- Most over-the-counter supplements have never been tested for health benefits. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p252]

## Inferences

- Inference: The breadth of nutrients in balanced formulas is essential; single-nutrient supplementation can be harmful because nutrients work synergistically.
