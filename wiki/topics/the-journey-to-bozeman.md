# The Journey to Bozeman

## Summary

The narrative journey from pages 68-129 takes the narrator, Chris, John, and Sylvia from Bowman through extreme heat across Montana's plains, over the Beartooth Highway (one of the highest paved roads in the world), through Red Lodge and above timberline into alpine meadows, through Yellowstone Park, and finally toward Bozeman, Montana—the destination tied to Phædrus's past. The physical journey parallels the intellectual journey from the plains of ordinary thought into the high country of abstract philosophy.
