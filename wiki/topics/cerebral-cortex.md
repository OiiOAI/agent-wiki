# Cerebral Cortex

## Summary

The folded gray matter covering the two cerebral hemispheres, the site of higher cognitive functions including perception, movement, language, thought, and memory. The cortex is divided into frontal, parietal, occipital, and temporal lobes, each with specialized functions. It contains about 52 cytoarchitectonically distinct areas (Brodmann's areas) and operates through both serial and parallel processing in interconnected regions.

- Operations responsible for cognitive abilities occur primarily in the cerebral cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p61]
- The cortex is divided into four lobes: frontal (short-term memory, planning, movement control), parietal (somatic sensation, body image), occipital (vision), and temporal (hearing, learning, memory, emotion).
