# The Keys (Monomyth Summary)

## Summary

Campbell's diagrammatic summary of the mythological hero's journey: departing from common-day hut or castle, the hero is lured or proceeds to the threshold; encounters a shadow presence; defeats or descends into death; journeys through unfamiliar forces with tests and helpers; arrives at the nadir gaining supreme ordeal and reward; undergoes the return crossing; and finally brings the boon back to restore the world. The changes rung on this simple scale defy description.

## Key facts

- The mythological hero sets forth from his common-day hut or castle, is lured or carries himself to the threshold of adventure. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p191]
- Beyond the threshold the hero journeys through a world of unfamiliar yet intimate forces, some threatening him (tests), some giving magical aid (helpers). [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p191]
- The triumph may be represented as the hero's sexual union with the goddess-mother of the world (sacred marriage), recognition by the father creator (father atonement), his own divinization (apotheosis), or the theft of the boon (bride-theft, fire-theft).
