# Emotions at Work

## Summary

The workplace is a major arena for emotional intelligence, with half of workers reporting stressed, frustrated, or overwhelmed feelings. Supervisors' EI skills significantly impact employee well-being, engagement, and organizational outcomes. Creating emotionally intelligent workplaces requires addressing both individual skills and organizational culture and structure.

## Key facts

- About 50 percent of workers use stressed, frustrated, or overwhelmed to describe their feelings at work, and about a third report feeling happy or proud less than 50 percent of the time. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p200]
- Forty-five percent of physicians report feeling burned out, with 70 percent of medical residents describing their typical day with negative terms such as meh, bored, polarized, overwhelmed, stressed, and pensive. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p201-202]
- Thirty-five percent of employees miss three to five workdays monthly due to job stress. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p201]
- Employees want to feel happy (first choice), excited, joyful, appreciated, supported, fulfilled, respected, inspired, accomplished, and valued at work. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p200-201]
- Companies with 'a culture of companionate love'—characterized by feelings of affection, compassion, caring, and tenderness—show benefits including lower employee exhaustion and fewer sick days. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p208-209]
- Companies such as Southwest Airlines, Pepsi, Whole Foods, and Zappos explicitly include love and caring in their stated management principles. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p209]

## Inferences

- Inference: The contrast between high-power individuals being less responsive to others' emotions and the documented benefits of emotionally intelligent leadership suggests a skills gap that organizational culture can either enable or suppress.
