# Vestibulocerebellum

## Summary

The vestibulocerebellum (flocculonodular lobe) is the phylogenetically oldest part of the cerebellum. It receives information from the semicircular canals and otolith organs sensing head motion and position relative to gravity. Unique among cerebellar divisions, its output bypasses the deep cerebellar nuclei and proceeds directly to the vestibular nuclei. The vestibulocerebellum regulates balance and eye movements, and has been co-opted by visual guidance of eye movements in more recent phylogeny.

- The vestibulocerebellum receives information from the semicircular canals and otolith organs, which sense the head's motion and position relative to gravity. - Purkinje cells in the midline parts of the vestibulocerebellum project to the lateral vestibular nucleus to modulate the vestibulospinal tracts controlling axial muscles and limb extensors for balance.
