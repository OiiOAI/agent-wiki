# Techniques for Behavior Change

## Summary

External techniques for motivating or managing oneself are ineffective without underlying autonomous motivation; genuine change requires self-acceptance, interest in one's inner world, and personal readiness to change.

## Key facts

- There are no techniques that will motivate people or make them autonomous; motivation must come from within, not from techniques. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p194]
- When people are really ready to change for their own personal reasons and willing to face the feelings underlying their maladaptive behaviors, then they will have the motivation for change. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p194]
- Without a true resolve, without reasons for change that are personally important, techniques will not help. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p194]
- Using techniques is likely to be helpful only if it feels right for a person and only if the person has made a true choice to change. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p195]
- The starting place for change is accepting oneself and taking interest in one's inner world. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p195]
- Discovering the reason for a behavior must not be an occasion for blame; the process of change is facilitated by awareness but hindered by blaming oneself or others. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p195]
- Pressuring does not help; indeed, it is likely to hurt just as blaming oneself is likely to hurt; when people feel pressured, compliance or defiance results, neither of which produces meaningful change. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p196]
