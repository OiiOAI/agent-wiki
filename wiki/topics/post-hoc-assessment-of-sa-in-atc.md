# Post-Hoc Assessment of Situation Awareness in Air Traffic Control

## Summary

Post hoc assessment of situation awareness (SA) in air traffic control (ATC) contexts involves evaluating operator cognitive states after incidents or accidents have occurred. Since measuring SA in real-time is often impractical or unsafe, researchers must rely on retrospective data sources including Operational Error (OE) reports, accident investigation databases, voice recordings, and radar replay systems. The FAA documents ATC incidents involving loss of separation in Operational Error (OE) final reports using form FAA 7210-3, while the NTSB investigates major aircraft accidents through comprehensive subgroup analysis. Key findings indicate that controller awareness of developing errors is associated with less severe errors, and that factors related to SA (such as radar display issues and communication errors) are significant contributors to operational errors.

## Key facts

- Post hoc SA assessment is challenging due to the lack of direct information about cognitive processes of relevant personnel. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p89]
- The FAA documents ATC incidents involving loss of separation in Operational Error (OE) final reports (FAA 7210-3). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p90]
- The NTSB investigates major aircraft accidents through compilation and analysis of subgroup reports into a single final report. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p90]
- Endsley (1995) defined SA as 'the perception of the elements in the environment within a volume of time and space, the comprehension of their meaning, and the projection of their status in the near future'. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p91]
- SA forms the critical inputs to, but is separate from, decision making that is the basis for all operator actions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p91]
- Controller awareness of error development is associated with less severe errors (Rodgers and Nye, 1993; Durso et al., 1998). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p92]
- Major errors account for only 15 out of 1,053 operational errors in the Rodgers and Nye (1993) study sample. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p95]
