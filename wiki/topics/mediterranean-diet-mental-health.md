# Mediterranean diet for mental health

## Summary

The Mediterranean diet is recommended as the optimal dietary pattern for mental health. It focuses on whole foods, real food, and includes fresh foods high in vegetables and fruits, nuts, healthy fats, dairy products, lean meat, fish, and whole grains while being low in processed packaged foods. The diet provides minerals, vitamins, essential fatty acids, and phytonutrients that support brain function.

## Key facts

- The Mediterranean diet is simple, old-fashioned, and closest to what your grandparents probably ate, including fresh foods, high in vegetables and fruits, nuts, healthy fats, dairy products, lean meat, fish, and whole grains. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p182]
- The Mediterranean diet is low in processed packaged foods. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p182]
- Phytonutrients can only be found in whole, real foods. Scientists have already identified several hundred of them, but most don't have names yet and very few have defined functions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p191]
- The Mediterranean-style diet should include a variety of fruits and veggies every day, lots of beans and legumes, at least two servings per week of fish, whole grains, and healthy oils. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p199]
