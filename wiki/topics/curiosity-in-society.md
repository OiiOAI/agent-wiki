# Curiosity in Society

## Summary

The book argues that society should embrace and protect purposeful curiosity as a marker of civilization. Key recommendations include bringing back the spirit of exploration, embracing elements of surprise, focusing curiosity on things that capture attention, questioning everything, and moving from individual to collective orientation. The author contends that curiosity journeys can benefit society by making the world safer, healthier, happier, more ingenious, and more empathetic. Society should resist becoming fixed in ways as people age and remain interested in everything and everyone.
