# Meditation Practice

## Summary

The practical application of mindfulness meditation techniques covered in 'The Mindful Geek.' Encompasses formal sitting meditation, meditation in daily life, specific techniques like Focus on Relaxation and Focus on Body Sensation, and practical advice for establishing and maintaining a regular practice.

## Key facts

- The book recommends at least 10 minutes of meditation each day as an integral part of reading and practicing. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p88]
- Taft compares meditation practice to brushing teeth or taking a shower rather than training for a marathon - it should be a daily routine. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p89]
- Seven methods for establishing a regular practice include: making a contract with yourself, making a calendar, using social pressure, sitting with a group, making it hard to fail, working with a meditation coach, and consciously listing benefits weekly. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p89-94]
- Meditation in daily life can transform waiting time at the DMV, in traffic, or at airports into valuable practice. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p107]
