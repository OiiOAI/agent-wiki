# Military Reform Movement

## Summary

The Military Reform Movement was a guerrilla movement within the Pentagon founded by John Boyd after he retired from the Air Force in 1975. The movement affected the monolithic and seemingly omnipotent Pentagon as few things in history have done. For a few years, Boyd was one of the most powerful men in Washington as he and his followers challenged military procurement and doctrine.

## Key facts

- Boyd founded the Military Reform Movement after retiring from the Air Force in 1975. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p12]
- The movement was described as a guerrilla movement that affected the Pentagon as few things in history have done. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p12]
- The movement challenged military procurement practices and existing doctrine. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p12]
- For a few years Boyd was one of the most powerful men in Washington. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p12]
