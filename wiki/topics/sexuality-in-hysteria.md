# Sexuality in Hysteria

## Summary

Both Breuer and Freud emphasized the central role of sexuality in the aetiology of hysteria, despite initial resistance to this view. Breuer observed that the great majority of severe neuroses in women have their origin in the marriage bed, and sexual traumas occur both at puberty and in marriage. Young women of refined moral organization feel that sexual content is incompatible with their ethical standards and repress it from consciousness. The sexual needs of hysterical patients are not stronger than in healthy people, but they fall ill from them—precisely owing to struggling against them, owing to their defence against sexuality. Freud later made the sexual aetiology of the neuroses a cornerstone of psychoanalytic theory.
