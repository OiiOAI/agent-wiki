# Autism Spectrum Disorder

## Summary

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by impaired social interaction, impaired verbal and nonverbal communication, and restricted or circumscribed interests with stereotyped behaviors. It affects approximately 1% of the population with a 4:1 male-to-female ratio. The disorder has a strong genetic component with up to 10% of children with autism carrying de novo copy number variations. Neurological abnormalities include altered cortical development and amygdala dysfunction.

- Monozygotic twin concordance for autism ranges from 60% to 91%. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1478]
- The brains of children with autism often show precocious growth during the first few years of life, particularly of the frontal lobe. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1479]
