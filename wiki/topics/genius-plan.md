# The Genius Plan

## Summary

The Genius Plan is the dietary framework presented in 'Genius Foods,' designed to optimize brain health through nutrition. The plan involves eliminating inflammatory foods (processed carbohydrates, wheat/gluten, industrial oils, refined sugars), consuming nutrient-dense 'Genius Foods,' and implementing intermittent fasting with strategic carb refeeds. The initial two-week phase involves ultra-low-carb eating to achieve metabolic flexibility, followed by strategic carbohydrate reintegration.

## Key facts

- The Genius Plan involves consuming a diet high in nutrient-dense foods and devoid of foods that cause hormonal dysregulation, oxidative stress, and inflammation. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p381]
- Foods to eliminate include refined carbohydrates, wheat/gluten, industrial-grade emulsifiers, processed meats and cheeses, concentrated sweeteners, commercial cooking oils, non-organic soy, and synthetic sweeteners. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p383-384]
- The initial two-week phase involves ultra-low-carb eating (20-40 grams net carbohydrates per day) to achieve metabolic flexibility and fat adaptation. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p401]
- After two weeks, higher-carb, lower-fat 'refeed' meals can be introduced a few days per week to refill muscle glycogen and upregulate hormones like leptin. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p402]
- The plan emphasizes eating one huge 'fatty salad' daily with dark leafy greens and extra-virgin olive oil. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p394]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Genius Plan combines multiple evidence-based dietary strategies—low-carb eating, time-restricted feeding, and nutrient density—into a cohesive approach for brain optimization.
