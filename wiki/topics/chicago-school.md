# The Chicago School

## Summary

The Chicago School was the first major school of American sociology, founded in 1892 at the University of Chicago by Albion Small. It was characterized by its concern for the city and urban problems, its use of Chicago as a laboratory for studying urbanization, and its development of symbolic interactionism. Key figures included Robert Park, W.I. Thomas, and later George Herbert Mead.

## Key facts

- The department of sociology at the University of Chicago was founded in 1892 by Albion Small. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p88]
- Small founded the American Journal of Sociology in 1895 and cofounded the American Sociological Society in 1905. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p88]
- The early Chicago department had a strong connection with religion and believed sociology must be interested in social reform. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p88]
- The Chicago school used the city of Chicago as a laboratory to study urbanization and its problems. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p35]
- Park had studied with Simmel, and Simmel's ideas on action and interaction were instrumental in developing the Chicago school's theoretical orientation. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p61]
- The Chicago School dominated American sociology from its founding in 1892 through the 1930s. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p91]
- The Chicago school was characterized by urban ethnology using participant observation techniques. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p91]
- The school began to decline in the 1930s due to the discipline's increasing preoccupation with being scientific using sophisticated methods and statistical analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p94]
- By 1935, the revolt against Chicago led to a non-Chicago secretary of the American Sociological Association and establishment of the American Sociological Review. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p94]
- According to Wiley, "the Chicago school had fallen like a mighty oak" (1979:63). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p94]
