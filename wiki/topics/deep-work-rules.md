# Deep Work Rules

## Summary

Four rules for developing a deep work habit: work deeply, embrace boredom, quit social media, and drain the shallows. These strategies address scheduling, training, tool selection, and time management.

## Key facts

- Rule #1 (Work Deeply) covers how to integrate deep work into your schedule with rituals, grand gestures, collaboration, 4DX framework, and shutdown habits. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- Rule #2 (Embrace Boredom) improves the limit of concentration through Roosevelt dashes, productive meditation, and memory training. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p94]
- Rule #3 (Quit Social Media) addresses tool selection through the craftsman approach, the 30-day abstinence test, and deliberate leisure planning. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p108]
- Rule #4 (Drain the Shallows) focuses on scheduling every minute, quantifying depth of activities, and reducing shallow work. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p127]
