# Brain Basics

## Summary

Brain basics covers fundamental neuroscience concepts including the structure and function of neurons, the major divisions of the brain (cortex, subcortical regions, cerebellum), the lobes of the cortex, brain orientation terminology, and the autonomic nervous system. Neurons communicate via synapses using neurotransmitters. The cortex is a layered sheet of neurons arranged in columns, while subcortical regions are organized as clumps. The autonomic nervous system regulates the body's internal environment through fight-or-flight and rest-and-digest responses.

## Key facts

- The brain is a massive network composed of billions of brain cells wired together to pass information back and forth. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p327]
- A neuron consists of a cell body, dendrites (branch-like structures), and an axon (root-like structure with axon terminals). [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p327]
- A neuron fires by sending an electrical signal down its axon to axon terminals, which release neurotransmitters into synapses that excite or inhibit other neurons. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p327]
- Through synaptic communication, one neuron influences thousands of others and thousands of neurons can influence one neuron simultaneously. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p327]
- The cortex is a sheet of neurons arranged in layers (four to six) wired into circuits and networks, organized into columns. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p328]
- Subcortical regions are organized as clumps of neurons, in contrast to the layered cortex. The amygdala is an example. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p328]
- The cerebellum is toward the bottom and back of the brain and is important for coordinating physical movements. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p328]
- Scientists use terms like 'dorsal anterior' (upper front) and 'medial' (inner wall) instead of compass directions to locate brain regions. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p329]
- The cortex is divided into discrete areas called lobes, which are major divisions of the brain. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p329]
- The autonomic nervous system carries brain commands to internal organs and sends sensations from organs back to the brain, controlling heart rate, breathing, digestion, hunger, and other bodily functions. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p331]
- The autonomic nervous system controls 'fight or flight' responses that spend energy resources and 'rest and digest' responses that replenish them. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p331]
