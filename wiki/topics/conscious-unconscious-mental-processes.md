# Disorders of Conscious and Unconscious Mental Processes

## Summary

This chapter explores the distinction between conscious and unconscious cognitive processes, drawing evidence from normal perception, brain damage cases, and neuroimaging studies. Key phenomena include blindsight, unilateral neglect, change blindness, and disorders of agency and memory. The chapter emphasizes that most cognitive processing never enters consciousness.

## Key facts

- Conscious and unconscious cognitive processes have distinct neural correlates. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1521]
- The study of blindsight and unilateral neglect provides striking examples of unconscious processing affecting behavior without conscious awareness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1522]
