# Situation Awareness and Aging

## Summary

This chapter examines how normative age-related cognitive changes affect the acquisition of situation awareness (SA) across three stages: perception, comprehension, and projection. The analysis integrates cognitive aging research with SA theory, identifying both vulnerabilities (slowing, reduced processing resources, disinhibition) and potential compensations (expertise, environmental supports, automatization) that determine SA outcomes in older adults.

## Key facts

- Aging-related changes in cognitive abilities that affect attention and memory may result in older individuals experiencing difficulties in performing essential tasks in work-related settings. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p289]
- SA formation may be critical to continued well-being and adaptive functioning of older adults, as demonstrated in scenarios like crossing busy streets. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p289]
- Most theoretical work on SA relies on young adult populations, raising questions about generalizability to middle-aged and older adults. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p290]
- A driving study found middle-aged drivers (40-59 years) reported fewer difficulties than both younger (18-39 years) and older drivers (60-84 years), attributed to expertise and compensatory mechanisms. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p310]

## Inferences

- Inference: The chapter argues that existing SA models and measurement techniques developed primarily from young adult populations may not adequately account for normative age-related cognitive changes.
- Inference: The authors conclude that expertise can substantially mitigate age-related SA decline in domain-specific contexts, though absolute performance standards may still reveal age effects.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Empirical research directly examining SA and aging remains limited, with most conclusions drawn from theoretical integration rather than direct studies.
- Uncertain: The long-term trajectory of SA changes across the adult lifespan requires further investigation.
