# Behavior Design

## Summary

Behavior Design is Fogg's comprehensive system for thinking clearly about human behavior and designing ways to transform life. The system combines models (how to think about behavior) with methods (how to design for behavior). The Behavior Design process involves four steps: clarify the aspiration, explore behavior options, match with specific behaviors, and start tiny. Fogg developed this approach through research at Stanford and applied it to product design (including Instagram), then expanded it for personal behavior change.

## Key facts

- Behavior Design consists of Models (how to think clearly about behavior) and Methods (how to design for behavior). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p29]
- The four steps in Behavior Design are: Step 1 Clarify the Aspiration, Step 2 Explore Behavior Options, Step 3 Match with Specific Behaviors, Step 4 Start Tiny. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p79]
- Fogg Maxim #1 states: Help people do what they already want to do. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p74]
- Behavior Design encompasses three categories: Models (how to think clearly about behavior), Methods (how to design for behavior change), and Maxims (guiding principles). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p314]
- Fogg's vision is that Behavior Design could become common knowledge, enabling healthcare workers, policy makers, and families to create positive change at scale. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p302]
- Fogg states that 'breakthrough discoveries' like Behavior Design 'have the potential to be used for good or evil,' comparing it to chemistry principles that can create either fertilizer or chemical weapons. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p305]
- Fogg believes his work at Stanford and with industry leaders is important, but 'that's not what's going to change the world'—the readers are. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p301]
