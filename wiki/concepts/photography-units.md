# Photography exposure measurement

## Summary

Units and parameters used in photography to control exposure and image quality, including ASA/ISO speed (film sensitivity), shutter speed, f-number (aperture), focal length, and megapixels.

## Key facts

- ASA/ISO speed is an arithmetic system where 200 ASA is twice as fast as 100, and 400 is twice as fast as 200. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- The f-number is the ratio of lens focal length to aperture diameter; lower f-numbers allow more light. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- A 35mm camera uses film 35mm wide, which is considered adequate for good-quality photographs. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- Color temperature is measured in kelvins, ranging from about 1500 K (candlelight) to 12,000 K (blue sky). [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
