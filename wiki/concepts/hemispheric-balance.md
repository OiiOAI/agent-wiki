# Hemispheric Balance

## Summary

McGilchrist argues that the Western world has become dangerously skewed towards left hemisphere dominance, with the left hemisphere (the 'emissary') usurping the role of the right hemisphere (the 'master'). The challenge is to restore proper balance, allowing each hemisphere to fulfill its proper role.

## Key facts

- The left hemisphere is 'a wonderful servant, but a very poor master'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p598]
- The right hemisphere, though not dependent on the left hemisphere in the same way the left is on the right, nonetheless needs it in order to achieve its full potential. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p598]
- The left hemisphere is dependent on the right hemisphere both to ground its world at the 'bottom' end, and to lead it back to life at the 'top', but appears to be in denial about this. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p598]

## Inferences

- Inference: The shift away from right hemisphere dominance is not about evolution or emotion versus cognition; it is about two modes of being, each with its cognitive and emotional aspects.
- Inference: The right hemisphere, despite being more closely in touch with emotion and the body, also has the most sophisticated and extensive, and quite possibly most lately evolved, representation in the prefrontal cortex.
