# Assortment Effect

## Summary

The psychological term for the observed tendency for men and women of similar beliefs and self-perceptions to attract one another.

## Key facts

- Assortment Effect is defined as the psychological term for the observed tendency for men and women of similar beliefs and self-perceptions to attract one another. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- For instance, a man with low self-esteem will attract women with low self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- A man with a positive attitude towards sex will attract women with positive attitudes towards sex. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
