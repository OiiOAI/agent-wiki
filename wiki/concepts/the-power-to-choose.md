# The Power to Choose

## Summary

The power to choose is linked to consciousness. Without consciousness, there is no true choice. Choice begins when you disidentify from the mind and its conditioned patterns, when you become present. Until that point, the conditioned mind runs your life and you are compelled to think, feel, and act in certain ways according to mental conditioning. Jesus' statement 'Forgive them, for they know not what they do' reflects this understanding.

## Key facts

- Choice implies consciousness — a high degree of consciousness. Without it, you have no choice. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- Choice begins the moment you disidentify from the mind and its conditioned patterns, the moment you become present. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- Until you reach presence, you are unconscious spiritually and compelled to think, feel, and act according to the conditioning of your mind. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- Jesus said: 'Forgive them, for they know not what they do,' which reflects that unconscious people have no choice. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- The mind, conditioned as it is by the past, always seeks to re-create what it knows and is familiar with, even if it is painful. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p200]
- The mind always adheres to the known. The unknown is dangerous because it has no control over it. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p200]
- Nobody chooses dysfunction, conflict, pain, or insanity. They happen because there is not enough presence to dissolve the past. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p201]
