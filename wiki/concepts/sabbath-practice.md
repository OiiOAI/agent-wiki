# Sabbath Practice

## Summary

The Sabbath is a concept traced to Biblical Hebrews meaning 'day of rest.' The practice involves designating one day each week to disconnect from work and digital life for self-renewal.

## Key facts

- The word Sabbath is Hebrew for 'day of rest.'. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p123]
- Stephen Covey, in his best-selling book, refers to this principle as self-renewal. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p123]
- The book recommends designating one day each week to disconnect from the web and all things tech-related. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p123]
- Suggested Sabbath activities include getting a massage, cooking a new recipe, sleeping, cleaning the yard, doing volunteer work, or meditating. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p123]
