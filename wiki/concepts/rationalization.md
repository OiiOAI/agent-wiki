# Rationalization

## Summary

Rationalization is the process by which institutions in the Western world have grown progressively more rational. Weber identified formal rationality as involving concern for individual choices of means and ends made in reference to universally applied rules and regulations. The bureaucracy is the classic example of rationalization. This process affects not only capitalist but also socialist societies.
