# Anxious Attachment Style

## Summary

The anxious attachment style affects approximately 20 percent of the population. People with this style crave intimacy, are often preoccupied with their relationships, and worry about their partner's ability to love them back. They have a supersensitive attachment system that detects even slight threats to the relationship. Their needs include reassurance, consistency, and emotional availability from partners.

## Key facts

- Anxious people crave intimacy, are often preoccupied with their relationships, and tend to worry about their partner's ability to love them back. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p16]
- People with anxious attachment have a supersensitive attachment system that can detect even slight hints that something may be wrong in the relationship. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p69]
- Once the attachment system of an anxious person is activated, they cannot calm down until they get clear indication from their partner that the relationship is safe. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p69]
- Anxious people tend to interpret a calm attachment system as boredom rather than as security and healthy love. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p82]
- Anxious individuals are more vigilant to changes in others' emotional expressions and can perceive onset and offset of emotions earlier than others. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p70]
