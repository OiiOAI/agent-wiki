# Black Swan

## Summary

Black Swans are rare, high-impact events that are predictable in hindsight but not in foresight. The book examines errors related to Black Swans in risk management, including nonpredictive approaches (building systems immune to perturbations), the distinction between Thalesian and Aristotelian thinking, and the conflation of event and exposure.

## Key facts

- The nonpredictive approach to Black Swans involves building stuff immune to perturbations—hence robust to changes in future outcomes. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p528]
- The Thalesian approach focuses on exposure and payoff from decision, contrasted with the Aristotelian focus on logic and True-False distinction. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p528]
- Conflation of event and exposure is the error of mistaking a function of a variable for the variable itself. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p528]
