# Work/Life Integration

## Summary

Work/life integration represents a Level 3 approach to balancing professional and personal life, contrasting with the Level 2 concept of work/life balance. Rather than treating work and life as separate spheres requiring equilibrium, integration recognizes that much of what 'life' provides—meaning, connection, engagement—can be obtained through work itself, even in seemingly menial jobs.

## Key facts

- At Level 1, people work and ignore the rest of living, assuming they'll enjoy themselves eventually. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p96]
- At Level 2, people realize the importance of life outside work and struggle to balance both spheres. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p96]
- At Level 3, people integrate work and life, realizing that much of what 'life' provides can be gotten at work—even in menial work like being an elevator operator. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p96]
