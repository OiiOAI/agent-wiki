# Concept Cells

## Summary

Individual neurons in the human brain that fire selectively to specific concepts or individuals, representing the neural basis of how the brain stores and retrieves abstract semantic knowledge.

## Key facts

- Research with epilepsy patients implanted with intracranial electrodes has revealed individual neurons that respond selectively to specific concepts like 'Jennifer Aniston.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p119]
- These neurons fire not just to photographs of the person but also to their name written textually, demonstrating abstract concept representation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p119]
- The research was conducted by Rodrigo Quian Quiroga in collaboration with Christof Koch and Itzhak Fried using UCLA technology for recording individual human neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p119]
- Concept cells represent information in a sparse code—only a small fraction of neurons fire for any given concept—allowing many different concepts to be stored without interference. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p119]
- Concept cells respond to the explicit encoding of multimodal percepts by single neurons in the human brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p183]
- Concept cells encode meaningful associations and can modify their response patterns to encode new associations on the fly. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p183]
- There are more neurons encoding familiar concepts than novel ones, preferentially responding to personally relevant images. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p183]
- Concept cells represent the building blocks of declarative memory functions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p183]

## Inferences

- Inference: Concept cells provide the neural mechanism for Aristotle and Aquinas's philosophical notion that mental images give rise to abstract concepts.
- Inference: The Jennifer Aniston neuron example demonstrates that concept cells encode abstract representations linked by semantic associations (both actresses appeared in the same sitcom).
- Inference: The rapid encoding capability of concept cells for new memories suggests these neurons are fundamental to learning and memory consolidation.
