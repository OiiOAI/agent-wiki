# Distill (CODE Step 3)

## Summary

Distill is the third step of CODE, involving finding the essence of notes so main points are easy to identify quickly. The author emphasizes that every idea has an essence — the heart and soul of what it communicates — and distilling notes to their core allows rapid recall without rereading entire sources. The practice of annotation during capture helps identify the most important passages.

## Key facts

- The author uses Einstein's E=mc2 as an example of distilling complex thinking into a simple, elegant summary. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p44]
- Distillation is important because during a busy workday, there is no time to review long notes — only quick access to main takeaways is feasible. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p44]
- The author recommends asking 'How can I make this as useful as possible for my future self?' when taking notes. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p44-45]
- Chapter titles, headings, and bullet-point lists represent distillation already performed by the author and are particularly worth capturing. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p64]
