# Autocatalysis (Business)

## Summary

A phenomenon where success breeds further success through self-reinforcing dynamics. Disney exemplifies this: classic movies generate video sales, which fund new movies, which generate more sales. Businesses with strong brands and distribution achieve autocatalytic growth.

## Key facts

- Disney is an amazing example of autocatalysis—all those movies in the can with owned copyright; when the video cassette was invented, Disney didn't have to invent anything, just release catalog titles. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p248]
- Every parent and grandparent wanted descendants to watch Disney videos at home—a billion-dollar tailwind requiring no new invention. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p248]
- Coca-Cola's strategy aimed to start an autocatalytic reaction in chemistry—a multifactor-triggered lollapalooza effect combining Pavlovian conditioning, social proof, and operant conditioning. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p296]
