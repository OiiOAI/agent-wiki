# Fusiform face area

## Summary

A region in the fusiform gyrus of the temporal lobe specialized for face perception. In this study, dreams containing faces were associated with increased high-frequency EEG activity in a temporo-occipital region corresponding to the fusiform face area, consistent with its known role in waking face perception.

## Key facts

- Dreams containing faces were associated with increased high-frequency activity (25-50 Hz) in a region closely matching the fusiform face area. [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p5]
- Region of interest analysis confirmed significantly higher high-frequency activity in the right fusiform face area for dreams containing faces compared to those without (p=0.023). [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p5]
