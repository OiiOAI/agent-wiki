# Bank Balance Accounting

## Summary

Bank balance accounting is the default cash-management approach used by most entrepreneurs, where they check their bank account balance regularly and make decisions based on what they see. When balances are high, they invest in equipment and expansion; when low, they make collection calls and sell harder. This approach differs from traditional GAAP accounting but aligns with how humans naturally think about money.

## Key facts

- Most entrepreneurs check their bank accounts daily or multiple times daily rather than studying formal accounting reports. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p35]
- Bank balance accounting leads to reactive decision-making: high balances trigger spending, low balances trigger panic sales. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p34-35]
- Profit First accommodates bank balance accounting by organizing multiple accounts so entrepreneurs can see their cash position at a glance. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p66]
