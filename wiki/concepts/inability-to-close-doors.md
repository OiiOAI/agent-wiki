# Inability to Close Doors

## Summary

Inability to close doors is the obsession with keeping options open. Dan Ariely and Jiwoong Shin's computer game showed players scored 15% fewer points when doors disappeared after 12 moves, yet they continued frittering points trying to keep all options open even when they knew the point values.

## Key facts

- Dan Ariely and Jiwoong Shin's computer game showed players scored 15% fewer points when forced to close doors, yet continued trying to keep all options open even knowing the point values. - General Xiang Yu in 3rd century B.C. and Cortés in the 16th century burned their ships to remove retreat options and focus their troops on the only path to victory. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p153]
