# Celebration (Tiny Habits)

## Summary

Celebration is a core technique in the Tiny Habits method where you create a positive feeling immediately after performing a new behavior to wire it into your brain. It is the most important skill for creating habits quickly. When you celebrate effectively, you tap into your brain's reward circuitry, causing dopamine release that encodes the behavior sequence. Celebration teaches people to be nice to themselves—a skill with far-reaching benefits.

## Key facts

- Celebration is the best way to create a positive feeling that wires in new habits—it's free, fast, and available to everyone. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p162]
- You must celebrate immediately after a behavior—immediacy is critical because dopamine is released and processed very quickly. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p163]
- Incentives like a monthly massage don't wire in habits because they happen too long after the behavior for dopamine to build a neural bridge. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p163]
- Celebration options include saying 'Yes!' or 'Yay!', doing a fist pump, smiling big, nodding your head, giving yourself a thumbs-up, or thinking 'Good job.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p169]
- A Power Celebration is an extremely potent celebration reserved for habits that need to wire in very quickly. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p170]
- To wire in a habit fast or help yourself remember, rehearse the behavior sequence (Anchor then habit) and immediately celebrate, repeating seven to ten times. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p175]
- You can celebrate at three moments: when you remember to do the habit, while doing the habit, and immediately after completing the habit—each with different effects. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p176]

## Inferences

- Inference: Once a habit becomes automatic, celebration is no longer needed—the habit will persist without reinforcement.
