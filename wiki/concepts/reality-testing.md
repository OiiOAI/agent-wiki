# Reality Testing

## Summary

Reality testing is the mental function that distinguishes perceptions from ideas and determines what is real versus what is wishful or imagined. Freud traces its development to early infancy, when the organism learned to distinguish internal from external perceptions based on whether muscular action could make the perception disappear. In sleep, withdrawal of cathexis from the conscious system abandons reality testing, allowing hallucinatory wish fulfillment to be accepted as real.

## Key facts

- Reality testing is the function of orientating the individual in the world by discrimination between what is internal and what is external, ascribed to the system Cs. (Pcpt.) alone. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4600]
- Hallucination brings belief in reality with it when a thought has followed the path to regression as far back as to unconscious memory traces of objects and hence to perception. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4598]
- In sleep, withdrawal of cathexis from the system Cs. abandons reality testing, and excitations on the path of regression find that path clear as far as the system Cs. where they count as undisputed reality. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4601]
- In amentia, the ego breaks off its relation to reality by withdrawing cathexis from the system of perceptions, and reality testing is got rid of. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4601]

## Inferences

- Inference: Reality testing emerged evolutionarily as a mechanism to avoid the frustrating repetition of hallucinatory wish fulfillment that characterized early mental life.
- Inference: The capacity to distinguish perception from idea is fundamental to all subsequent ego functions and healthy adaptation to external reality.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise nature and mode of operation of the system Cs. remains incompletely understood.
- Uncertain: How the ego maintains reality testing during waking life while also managing unconscious processes is not fully explained.
