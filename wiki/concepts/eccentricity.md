# Eccentricity

## Summary

A measure of how much an orbital path deviates from a perfect circle. For closed orbits, eccentricity ranges from zero (perfect circle) to one.

## Key facts

- Eccentricity measures the extent to which a curved path differs from a perfect circle, calculated as the difference between long and short axes divided by their sum. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L185]
- An object with highly eccentric orbit comes very close to the sun at one end while the other end is much farther away. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L190]
- For a closed orbit, eccentricity always has a value between zero (perfect circle) and one. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L195]
