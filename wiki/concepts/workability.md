# Workability

## Summary

Workability is a central ACT principle stating that any behavior is classified as a toward move or away move depending on whether it helps a person move closer to or farther from the life they want. An activity can be a toward move or away move depending on the situation. Only the individual can decide whether something is workable for them.

## Key facts

- Any activity can be a toward move or an away move depending on the situation and context. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p22]
- If what a person is doing in a particular situation helps them move closer to the life they want to build, it is termed 'workable' (a toward move). [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p23]
- Only the individual can ever decide whether something is workable for them. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p23]
- The principle of workability underpins everything in The Happiness Trap book. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p23]
