# Neurons That Fire Apart Wire Apart

## Summary

'Neurons that fire apart wire apart' is a key law of neuroplasticity describing how neural connections weaken when neurons are not activated together. Applied to OCD treatment, by not acting on compulsions, patients weaken the link between the compulsion and the idea that it will ease their anxiety, which is crucial because while acting on a compulsion eases anxiety short-term, it worsens OCD long-term.
