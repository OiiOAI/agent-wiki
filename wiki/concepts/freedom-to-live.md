# Freedom to Live

## Summary

The result of the miraculous passage and return. The goal of myth is to dispel life ignorance by effecting a reconciliation of individual consciousness with the universal will through realization of the true relationship of passing phenomena of time to imperishable life. The hero, powerful in insight and calm in action, becomes the conscious vehicle of the wonderful Law.
