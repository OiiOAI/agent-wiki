# Manifest and Latent Functions

## Summary

Manifest and latent functions are concepts developed by Robert Merton to clarify functional analysis. Manifest functions are those that are intended and recognized by actors, while latent functions are unintended consequences that are functional for a designated system. Merton distinguished these from dysfunctions (negative consequences) and nonfunctions (irrelevant consequences). These concepts helped move structural functionalism toward empirical analysis and away from purely theoretical assertions about universal functionality.
