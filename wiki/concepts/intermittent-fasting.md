# Intermittent Fasting

## Summary

Intermittent fasting is a dietary approach involving periods of not eating, which triggers cellular repair processes and metabolic benefits. The 16:8 protocol (fasting for 16 hours, eating during 8) is the most popular method. Benefits include improved insulin sensitivity, enhanced fat loss, activation of autophagy, increased BDNF, and reduced inflammation.

## Key facts

- The most popular intermittent fasting protocol is the 16:8 fast protocol, involving fasting for 16 hours while eating during an 8-hour window. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p368]
- Fasting for 14 to 16 hours onward for females and 16 to 18 hours onward for males causes growth hormone to begin increasing. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p328]
- After 24 hours of fasting, growth hormone has been reported to shoot up as high as 2,000 percent. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p328]
- Intermittent fasting activates autophagy, the body's waste disposal system whereby cellular junk gets cleaned up, associated with dramatically longer lifespans in animals. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p367]
- Benefits of intermittent fasting include improved decision making, improved insulin sensitivity, enhanced fat loss, activation of survival genes involved in antioxidant protection, increased BDNF, reduced inflammation, and enhanced synaptic protection. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p366-368]

## Inferences

- Inference: termittent fasting may be particularly beneficial for brain health due to its effects on BDNF, autophagy, and growth hormone.
