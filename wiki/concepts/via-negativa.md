# Via Negativa

## Summary

A philosophical and practical approach emphasizing subtraction over addition—removing harm, weaknesses, and errors rather than adding benefits. Part of Book VI of Antifragile, via negativa represents the wisdom that in many domains, what we do not do matters more than what we do, and that removing harmful factors is more effective than adding beneficial ones.

## Key facts

- Via negativa shows the wisdom and effectiveness of subtraction over addition—acts of omission over acts of commission—demonstrating that removing harmful factors is more effective than adding beneficial ones. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p37]
- In health applications, removing medication, gluten, fructose, tranquilizers, or other unnatural stressors by trial and error is more robust than adding medication with unknown side effects. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p40]
- The principle 'Less is more and usually more effective' underlies the via negativa approach across domains. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p28]
- Via negativa is the method of defining or achieving something by what it is not, removing what is wrong rather than adding what is right. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p379]
- Negative knowledge (what is wrong, what does not work) is more robust to error than positive knowledge (what is right, what works). [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p381]
- A single small observation can disprove a statement while millions of observations can hardly confirm it — disconfirmation is more rigorous than confirmation. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p381]
- Charlatans are recognizable by giving only positive advice, exploiting sucker-proneness for recipes that seem obvious but evaporate. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p380]
- The barbell strategy is a form of via negativa — it is necessary to first remove fragilities. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p384]
- 'Subtraction Adds to Your Life': cutting medical expenditures for elective surgeries and treatments would extend people's lives in most rich countries through simple convexity analysis of conditional iatrogenics. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p451]
- Druin Burch in Taking the Medicine writes: 'The harmful effects of smoking are roughly equivalent to the combined good ones of every medical intervention developed since the war.'. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p452]
- Ennius wrote: 'The good is mostly in the absence of bad' (Nimium boni est, cui nihil est mali). [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p452]
- The Salerno School of Medicine regimen: 'joyful mood, rest, and scant nourishment' (Si tibi deficiant medici, medici tibi fiant haec tria: mens laeta, requies, moderata diaeta). [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p452]
- Many people benefit from removing products that did not exist in their ancestral habitat: sugars, processed carbohydrates, wheat products, milk for non-Northern-European populations, sodas, vitamin pills, and food supplements. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p453]
- The Greek Orthodox Church has approximately 200 fasting days per year—'harrowing fasts' involving no animal products, no sweets, and for some, no olive oil. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p458]
- Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr. stated: 'If all the medications were dumped in the sea, it would be better for mankind but worse for the fishes.'. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p453]
- Valter Longo found that mice in initial phases of starvation could withstand high doses of chemotherapy without visible side effects; autophagy (cells eating themselves) may be the mechanism for longevity benefits. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p461]
- Via negativa in theology and philosophy focuses on what something is not—an indirect definition rather than direct characterization. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p532]
- In action, via negativa is a recipe for what to avoid, what not to do—subtraction, not addition, particularly in medicine. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p532]

## Inferences

- Inference: Via negativa provides a practical decision-making heuristic for complex systems where understanding causal mechanisms is limited—by removing known harms, one can improve outcomes without requiring complete knowledge of how the system works.
- Inference: Via negativa applied to prophecy means forecasting what should break (the fragile) rather than what new will emerge. This is the only valid method of prophecy because predicting new innovations is essentially impossible while identifying fragility is tractable.
- Inference: The 'Cretan diet' research mistakenly attributed Cretan longevity to specific foods, missing the second-order effect of approximately 200 annual fasting days that introduced beneficial convexity effects through irregular consumption.
- Inference: Religion's protective function against iatrogenics includes limiting intervention bias for marginal diseases—church attendance for mild cases allows nature to work while avoiding medical harm.
- Inference: Via negativa is the primary method for building antifragility: remove what causes fragility rather than adding complex defenses.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The scope of via negativa—while effective in many domains, there may be cases where addition is necessary or more efficient than subtraction.
- Uncertain: The claim that 'cutting medical expenditures by a certain amount...would extend people's lives' is presented as a speculation developed with statistician Spyros Makridakis and may require independent verification.
