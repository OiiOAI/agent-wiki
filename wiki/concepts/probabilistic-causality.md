## Summary

Probabilistic causality is an approach to causation that incorporates probability theory into causal reasoning. The chunk references a comprehensive summary in Hitchcock (2016) and foundational works by Reichenbach (1956), Suppes (1970), Cartwright (1983), and Spohn (2012).
