# The Hacker

## Summary

The Hacker is willing to stay on the plateau indefinitely without progressing, content to develop a basic level and make do. They skip essential developmental stages and do only enough to get by. In relationships, they view partnership as a comfortable refuge rather than an opportunity for growth.

## Key facts

- The Hacker is willing to stay on the plateau indefinitely after getting the hang of something, skipping stages essential to mastery. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p25]
- The Hacker develops a solid forehand and figures they can make do with a ragged backhand in tennis, or at work does only enough to get by. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p25]
- The Hacker looks at marriage not as an opportunity for learning but as a comfortable refuge from uncertainties. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p25]
- Leonard notes the categories are not neat; one can be a Dabbler in love and a master in art, or a Hacker on the golf course while mastering their job. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p26]
- At lectures, audiences typically split into nearly even thirds when identifying with the Dabbler, Obsessive, and Hacker types. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p26]
