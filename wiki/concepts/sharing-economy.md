# Sharing Economy

## Summary

The sharing economy is identified as a major development where trust serves as the essential engine enabling person-to-person commerce. This economy relies on technology platforms that facilitate transactions between strangers based on assumptions or creation of mutual trust.

## Key facts

- Trust is described as the engine of the sharing economy. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p387]
- The sharing economy is being driven by average, everyday people, disrupting industries once led by specialists. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p387]
- Examples include ride-sharing, person-to-person home and vacation sharing, peer-to-peer lending, crowdfunding, and open source technology. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p387]
- Technology creates platforms where relevance and quality are decided by transparent ratings and reviews from everyday people. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p387]
- In the sharing economy, there is a natural and necessary convergence of doing the right thing and doing the economic thing. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p387]
