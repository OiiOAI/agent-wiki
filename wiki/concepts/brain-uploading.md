# Brain Uploading

## Summary

Brain uploading is the proposal to record all details of a brain and simulate it on a computer, theoretically allowing immortality by separating mental identity from the biological body. Hawkins expresses skepticism, arguing that even if technically feasible, uploading creates copies rather than extending the original self's existence.

## Key facts

- Uploading would require mapping every neuron and synapse and recreating this structure in software to simulate the brain identically. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p190]
- The human brain has about 100 billion neurons and several hundred trillion synapses. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p190]
- If the neocortex is uploaded without the body, severe pain might result everywhere because the brain has representations of body parts that no longer exist, similar to phantom limb pain. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p190]
- Uploading a brain creates two separate individuals that immediately begin having different experiences and drift apart. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p192]
- Hawkins concludes that copying yourself is 'a fork in the road, not an extension of it' and does not achieve immortality. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p193]

## Inferences

- Inference: Brain uploading is compared to having children, where both create new separate beings rather than extending one's own existence.
