# Cheater Detection

## Summary

Cheater detection is a hypothesized specialized cognitive module that evolved to identify individuals who violate social contracts—accepting benefits without fulfilling reciprocal obligations. Cosmides demonstrated this module operates faster and more accurately than general logical reasoning.

## Key facts

- Cosmides found 65-80% of people correctly solve cheater detection problems on the Wason Test, compared to only 5-30% solving equivalent abstract logic problems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p108]
- Cheater detection develops early, operates regardless of experience and familiarity, and specifically detects intentional cheating but not accidental violations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p109]
- Patient R.M. with focal brain damage showed impaired cheater detection but normal reasoning on similar non-social tasks, suggesting specialized neural circuitry. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p109]
- Dan Chiappette found that people rate cheaters as more important to remember, look at them longer, remember their faces better, and recall social contract information about them. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p110]
