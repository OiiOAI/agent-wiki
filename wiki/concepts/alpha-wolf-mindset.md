# Alpha Wolf Mindset

## Summary

The Alpha Wolf Mindset is a dominant pattern where a person believes the world belongs to them and others are in their world. It is more common in men than women. Features include giving people value based on how they can be used, dominating by showing zero tolerance, seeing compassion as weakness, and meeting challenges with aggression. This Mindset is often found in business at high organisational levels where Alpha Wolves 'chew up' staff using aggression. The probability of changing this Mindset is not good unless the person recognises they have a problem. Dealing with them requires pragmatism: accepting their ways without rolling over, being assertive (which they usually respect), avoiding confrontation, staying at peace within yourself, and recognising when to move on.
