# Focus on Body Sensation

## Summary

A meditation technique that involves systematically directing attention through different regions of the body, noticing and investigating the physical sensations in each area with curiosity and acceptance.

## Key facts

- Meditating on the body means meditating on body sensation, not on mental images of the body. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p97]
- Body sensation is described as the 'native language' of the body that must be learned to achieve deep mindfulness. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p98]
- When exploring body sensation, one should notice location, size, shape, texture, and quality of sensations. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p100]
- The book compares exploring body sensation to a blind person reading a Braille description of a cathedral. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p97]
