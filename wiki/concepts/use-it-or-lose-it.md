# Use-It-or-Lose-It Principle

## Summary

The use-it-or-lose-it principle is a fundamental law of neuroplasticity stating that brain circuits that are not used will weaken and eventually disappear, while those that are actively used will be strengthened and maintained. Applied to OCD treatment, this principle suggests that each moment spent reinforcing obsessive thoughts deepens the obsessive circuit, while bypassing the obsession by not acting on compulsions weakens the pathological network.

## Key facts

- The use-it-or-lose-it principle states that brain circuits not used will weaken and disappear, while those used will be maintained. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p177]
- Each moment spent thinking of the OCD symptom—believing that germs are threatening—deepens the obsessive circuit. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p180]
- By bypassing the obsessive circuit through not acting on compulsions, patients are on the road to losing it. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p180]
- With obsessions and compulsions, the more you do it, the more you want to do it; the less you do it, the less you want to do it. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p180]
