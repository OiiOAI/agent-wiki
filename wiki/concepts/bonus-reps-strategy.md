# Bonus Reps Strategy

## Summary

A mini habits principle where any extra energy or ambition should be directed toward performing additional repetitions beyond the mini requirement, rather than toward increasing the minimum requirement itself. This keeps the bar low enough to never fail while allowing for impressive results.

## Key facts

- The principle advises: 'Be the person with embarrassing goals and impressive results instead of one of the many people with impressive goals and embarrassing results.'. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p127]
- Guise warns that bigger requirements 'look good on paper, but only action counts.'. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p127]
- The author documented averaging 1,000 words in week one, 1,500 in week two, and 2,000 in week three of writing with a 50-word minimum requirement. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p124]

## Inferences

- Inference: The natural overflow of effort (thousands of words, dozens of push-ups) from tiny requirements demonstrates that intrinsic motivation often exceeds what is required when friction is minimized.
