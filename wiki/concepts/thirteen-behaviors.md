# The Thirteen Behaviors of High-Trust Leaders

## Summary

The Thirteen Behaviors are common to high-trust leaders and people worldwide. These behaviors are powerful because they are based on principles governing trusting relationships, grow out of the Four Cores, are actionable, and are universal. The first five behaviors flow primarily from character, the second five from competence, and the last three from an equal mix. The book details Behavior #1 (Talk Straight) and Behavior #2 (Demonstrate Respect) in this section.

## Key facts

- The 13 Behaviors are based on principles rather than fads or techniques. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p161]
- The first five behaviors flow initially from character, the second five from competence, the last three from both. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p169]
- Any strength pushed to extreme becomes a weakness. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p169]
- The behaviors work together to create balance—Talk Straight must be balanced by Demonstrate Respect. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p169]
