# Tetris Effect

## Summary

The Tetris Effect refers to the phenomenon where people who spend significant time engaged in a task experience related imagery in their dreams afterward. Stickgold's research demonstrated that people learning Tetris reported seeing Tetris shapes in their dreams, and notably, even amnesic patients with hippocampal damage who couldn't remember playing the game still experienced Tetris-related dream imagery.

## Key facts

- People learning the computer game Tetris reported dreaming about it at sleep onset, describing images like 'Tetris shapes floating around in my head like they would in the game'. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p102]
- Three of five amnesic patients with hippocampal damage saw Tetris images in their dreams despite having no memories of playing the game. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p102]
- The Tetris Effect has gained considerable notoriety, spawning its own Wikipedia page, a signature lunchbox, and a PlayStation virtual reality game bearing the same name. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p102]
