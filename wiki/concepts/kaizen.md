# Kaizen

## Summary

Kaizen is a Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement that became a foundation for quality improvement in Japan, using the PDCA cycle and QC story format together with seven basic quality tools.

## Key facts

- The seven basic tools (check sheet, histograms, Pareto chart, fishbone diagram, graphs, scatter diagrams, and stratification) together with the PDCA cycle and the QC story format became the foundation for kaizen in Japan and are still being used today. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p5]
- The concept behind the term control (kanri) is deployed throughout Japanese organizations according to Ishikawa's 1985 redefinition of PDCA. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p5]
