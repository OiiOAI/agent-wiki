# Learned Helplessness

## Summary

Learned helplessness is a psychological phenomenon where people who believe their efforts don't matter stop trying. This connects to Essentialism because it explains why people forfeit their power to choose and become trapped in Nonessentialist patterns of accepting everything without discernment.

## Key facts

- Martin Seligman and Steve Maier's experiments with dogs showed that those who had learned helplessness in earlier situations did not try to escape electric shocks later, demonstrating learned helplessness. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p39]
- The ability to choose cannot be taken away or even given away—it can only be forgotten. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p38]

## Inferences

- Inference: When people believe they don't have a choice, they either check out and stop trying, or become hyperactive and accept every opportunity—leading to Nonessentialist behavior patterns.
