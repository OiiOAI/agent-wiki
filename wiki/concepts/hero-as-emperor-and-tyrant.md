# Hero as Emperor and Tyrant

## Summary

The hero blessed by the father returns to represent the father among men as teacher or emperor. However, a deterioration may take place: no longer referring boons to their transcendent source, the emperor breaks the stereoscopic vision, becomes the tyrant ogre (Herod-Nimrod), and the world must be saved from him. The hero of yesterday becomes the tyrant of tomorrow unless he crucifies himself today.

## Key facts

- The hero blessed by the father returns to represent the father among men as teacher (Moses) or emperor (Huang Ti); his word is law, and since he is centered in the source, he makes visible the repose and harmony of the central place. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p272]
- The Emperor Jemshid declared 'I am unequalled, for to me the earth owes all its science, never did exist a sovereignty like mine'—and when he thus forgot the worship of the great Creator, his earthly grandeur faded and the day of Jemshid passed into gloom. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p272]
- When the emperor stops referring the boons of his reign to their transcendent source, man's perspective flattens to include only the human term; the experience of supernal power immediately fails, the upholding idea of the community is lost, force is all that binds it, and the emperor becomes the tyrant ogre. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p273]
- The work of the hero is to slay the tenacious aspect of the father (dragon, tester, ogre king) and release from its ban the vital energies that will feed the universe. Slayer and Dragon, sacrificer and victim, are of one mind behind the scenes where there is no polarity of contraries. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p276]

## Inferences

- Inference: The emperor-tyrant represents the principle of conservation or stagnation—holding fast to what has been achieved rather than allowing the transformative flow of life to continue, thus requiring the hero to destroy what was previously created.
