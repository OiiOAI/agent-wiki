# Depression as Body Budget Mismanagement

## Summary

Depression is proposed to be a disorder of misbudgeting and prediction rather than simply a mental illness. The theory suggests that a depressed brain is locked into a cycle of budgeting imbalances, unbroken by prediction error because it is ignored, tuned down, or doesn't reach the brain. In effect, you're locked into an uncorrected prediction, trapped in an adverse past when metabolic needs were high. The traditional view is that negative thoughts cause negative feelings, but Barrett suggests it's the other way around—feelings drive next thoughts and perceptions as predictions. Depression is also a neurological, metabolic, and immunologic disease caused by an imbalance of many entwined parts of the nervous system.
