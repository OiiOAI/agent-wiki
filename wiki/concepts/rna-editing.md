# RNA Editing

## Summary

RNA editing is a post-transcriptional modification where enzymatic processes alter the nucleotide sequence of mRNA, exemplified by the Q/R editing of GluA2 that determines Ca2+ permeability of AMPA receptors.

## Key facts

- The GluA2 gene encodes glutamine in its M2 pore loop, but post-transcriptional RNA editing replaces this codon with one for arginine. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p281]
- Inclusion of the edited arginine residue prevents AMPA receptor Ca2+ permeability through electrostatic repulsion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p281]
- Mice engineered to prevent GluA2 RNA editing develop seizures and die within weeks due to excessive Ca2+ influx through AMPA receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p281]
