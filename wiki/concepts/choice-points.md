# Choice Points

## Summary

Choice points are key decision moments in ACT where individuals can consciously choose between unhelpful patterns (like OBEY or STRUGGLE modes) and more effective responses. The book presents several specific choice points to help readers navigate difficult situations.

## Key facts

- Choice points are presented as opportunities to consciously choose how to respond to difficult thoughts and feelings. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p305]
- The choice point 'Stay Stuck or Get Unstuck?' addresses HARD barriers: Hooked, Avoiding discomfort, Remoteness from values, and Doubtful goals. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p305]
- The choice point 'OBEY, STRUGGLE or Unhook?' addresses the three default responses to difficult thoughts and feelings. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p306]
- The choice point 'Get Swept Away or Drop Anchor?' highlights the importance of dropping anchor as a fundamental unhooking skill. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p306]
- When facing challenges, the options are: leave, stay and live by values, or stay but make things worse. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p307]
