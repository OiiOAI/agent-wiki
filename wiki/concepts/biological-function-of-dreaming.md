# Biological Function of Dreaming

## Summary

The biological function of dreaming, as proposed by Revonsuo's Threat Simulation Theory, refers to the adaptive role that dreaming plays in enhancing reproductive success. According to evolutionary biology, a system is functional in the evolutionary sense if it promotes the organism's inclusive fitness. The theory argues that dreaming serves to simulate threatening events encountered in waking life, rehearsing threat perception and threat avoidance skills in a perceptually and behaviorally realistic virtual environment, thereby enhancing survival and reproductive success in ancestral environments.

## Key facts

- According to Tooby and Cosmides, the biological standard is the only relevant standard for analyzing why brain and cognition are organized as they are; a system promotes inclusive fitness if it increases the probability that the organism or its kin will produce offspring. [raw/books/neuroscience/The reinterpretation of dreams - An evolutionary hypothesis of the function of dreaming.pdf#p3]
- The biological function of dreaming is hypothesized to be simulating threatening events to rehearse threat perception and threat avoidance skills, which would have enhanced survival and reproductive success in the ancestral environment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The reinterpretation of dreams - An evolutionary hypothesis of the function of dreaming.pdf#p6]
- The natural functions of dreaming cannot have been dependent on conscious recollection or verbal reporting of dream content, since such abilities were unlikely to occur in ancestral conditions and species where dreaming evolved. [raw/books/neuroscience/The reinterpretation of dreams - An evolutionary hypothesis of the function of dreaming.pdf#p3]
- The theory distinguishes between natural (biological, adaptive) functions and invented (derivative psychological or cultural) functions of dreaming, arguing that no matter how meaningful personal uses of recalled dreams may be, they were invented by humans, not by natural selection. [raw/books/neuroscience/The reinterpretation of dreams - An evolutionary hypothesis of the function of dreaming.pdf#p3]

## Inferences

- Inference: dreaming has an adaptive function, it must have been effective in the evolutionary context where humans lived as hunter-gatherers for hundreds of thousands of years.
