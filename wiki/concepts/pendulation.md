# Pendulation

## Summary

Pendulation is a technique for working with overwhelming emotions by alternating attention between emotionally neutral body locations (hands, feet) and emotional hotspots (chest, belly, face). The practice involves spending time at a neutral spot until stabilized, then briefly contacting the edge of the overwhelming emotion, then returning to neutral. This back-and-forth 'pendulum' motion allows emotional material to be processed without triggering overwhelm or dissociation. The book notes this technique is used in PTSD treatments by practitioners like Bessel van der Kolk and Peter Levine. It is presented as 'tremendously integrating' because it allows different body regions to 'talk to each other' and may gradually calm intense emotional hotspots.
