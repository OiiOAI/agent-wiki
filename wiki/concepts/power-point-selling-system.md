# Power Point Selling System

## Summary

The Power Point Selling System is a fully orchestrated selling methodology developed by E-Myth Worldwide. It follows six primary steps and three main Benchmarks: Appointment Presentation, Needs Analysis Presentation, and Solutions Presentation. The system combines Structure (predetermined elements: words, materials, dress) with Substance (how the salesperson delivers the message). Case studies show revenue increases of 100% to 500% across different industries.

## Key facts

- The Power Point Selling System consists of six primary steps: identifying Benchmarks, scripting words, creating materials, memorizing scripts, identical delivery, and effective communication. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p245]
- The three Benchmarks are: Appointment Presentation, Needs Analysis Presentation, and Solutions Presentation. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p246]
- A career development company using the system with inexperienced people saw revenues increase 300% in one year. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p245]
- An advertising agency using the system with inexperienced people saw revenues increase 500% in two years. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p245]
- A health spa saw revenues increase 40% in two months using the system with inexperienced people. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p245]
- Gerber states that 'Selling is opening,' not closing. The Needs Analysis Presentation opens up the prospective customer. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p252]
- The Information System tracks 13 data points across the selling Benchmarks from initial calls through final sales. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p254]
