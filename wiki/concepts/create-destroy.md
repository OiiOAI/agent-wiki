# Create-Destroy

## Summary

Create-destroy is a disciplined thinking technique described by Richard Rumelt where one actively tries to destroy or invalidate existing strategic alternatives by exposing their fault lines and internal contradictions. This discipline is essential for generating higher-quality alternatives after initial insights, as it forces rigorous examination of weaknesses rather than accepting shallow or tweaked versions of existing ideas.

## Key facts

- Create-destroy is the discipline of trying to destroy one's own existing alternatives to expose their weaknesses and internal contradictions. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]
- Creating new higher-quality alternatives requires trying hard to 'destroy' any existing alternatives. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]
- Trying to destroy one's own ideas is not easy or pleasant and takes mental toughness. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]

## Inferences

- Inference: The create-destroy discipline helps overcome the tendency to accept initial insights without sufficient scrutiny.
