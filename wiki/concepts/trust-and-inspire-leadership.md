# Trust and Inspire Leadership

## Summary

Trust and Inspire is a leadership style that contrasts with the traditional 'Command and Control' approach. This modern leadership philosophy emphasizes trusting the people you work with and inspiring them to make a difference, recognizing that to be trusted is the most inspiring form of human motivation.

## Key facts

- The old Command and Control style of leadership is rooted in the Industrial Age and is insufficient for today's leadership needs. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p392]
- Trying to operate in today's world with Command and Control is described as like trying to play tennis with a golf club—the tool is completely ill-suited. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p392]
- You cannot Command and Control a person's best contribution; they have to choose to give it. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p392]
- The Trust and Inspire style trusts the people you work with and inspires them to make a difference. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p392]
- To be trusted is described as the most inspiring form of human motivation that brings out the best in people. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p392]

## Inferences

- Inference: The shift from Command and Control to Trust and Inspire reflects broader changes in the nature of work, particularly the rise of knowledge work where employee engagement and creativity are essential.
