# Seven Low-Trust Organizational Taxes

## Summary

The Seven Low-Trust Organizational Taxes are costs incurred by organizations operating in low-trust environments: redundancy, bureaucracy, politics, disengagement, turnover, churn, and fraud. These taxes reduce speed and increase cost across all organizational operations.

## Key facts

- Redundancy tax refers to unnecessary duplication manifested in excessive hierarchy, layers of management, and overlapping structures designed to ensure control, rooted in the paradigm that people cannot be trusted without supervision. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p299]
- Bureaucracy includes complex rules, regulations, procedures, and processes reflected in excessive paperwork, red tape, controls, and multiple approval layers. In 2018, compliance with federal rules in the U.S. cost an estimated $1.9 trillion (about 10% of GDP). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p300]
- Politics tax involves the use of tactics and strategy to gain power, creating conflict with the 'enemy within' and generating behaviors such as withholding information, infighting, hidden agendas, and meetings after meetings. Indirect costs are estimated at $100 billion per year. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p300]
- Disengagement occurs when people continue working but have effectively quit, giving only required effort. Gallup's 2017 study estimated the cost at approximately $7 trillion in lost productivity worldwide, with 85% of employees globally not engaged or actively disengaged. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p301]
- Turnover represents a huge cost in low-trust cultures, with replacement costs ranging from 25% to 250% of pay. Fortune 100 Best Companies to Work For have voluntary turnover rates approximately half that of industry peers. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p302]
- Churn is the turnover of external stakeholders (customers, suppliers, distributors, investors) when trust is low internally or externally. Acquiring a new customer costs up to 500% more than keeping an existing one. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p303]
- Fraud tax (flat-out dishonesty, sabotage, deception) causes organizations to lose an estimated 5% of annual revenue according to a 2018 Association of Certified Fraud Examiners study. Most other organizational taxes are responses to the fraud tax. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p303]
