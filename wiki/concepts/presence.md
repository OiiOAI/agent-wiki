# Presence

## Summary

Presence is the state of conscious awareness free from identification with thought. It is consciousness recognizing itself—Being becoming aware of Being. In this state, one experiences stillness, peace, joy, and connection with life itself. Presence arises when the mind's compulsive thinking ceases, creating 'gaps' in the mental stream. It is the antidote to psychological time and the key to spiritual enlightenment. The practice involves 'watching the thinker'—observing the mind without judgment while feeling one's own sense of 'I am' existence. Presence is accessible at any moment and grows stronger with practice.

## Key facts

- Presence means consciousness becoming conscious of itself, or life attaining self-consciousness. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p97]
- Presence is the watcher or witness that arises when one observes the mind. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p31]
- The practice of watching the thinker creates gaps in the mental stream that expand with practice. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p31]
- Presence generates an energy field of high vibrational frequency that cannot be invaded by unconsciousness or negativity. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p78]
- Presence can be accessed by directing attention into the Now, becoming intensely conscious of the present moment. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p32]
