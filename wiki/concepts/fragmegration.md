# Fragmegration

## Summary

Fragmegration is James Rosenau's concept describing the simultaneous processes of fragmentation and integration occurring in global politics. It captures how globalizing processes create both disintegrative and integrative effects across multiple domains including economics, culture, and politics. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p500]
