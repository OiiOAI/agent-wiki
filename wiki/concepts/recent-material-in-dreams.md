# Recent Material in Dreams

## Summary

Recent material (Tagesreste, 'day's residues') refers to the tendency of dreams to incorporate impressions from the day immediately preceding the dream. Freud establishes that while any period of life can provide dream material, the most recent impressions are particularly significant. This preference for recent material is connected to both the fresh psychical charge of such impressions and their role as points of contact for unconscious material.

## Key facts

- Freud states that 'in every dream it is possible to find a point of contact with the experiences of the previous day.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1433]
- The 'dream day' (day immediately preceding the dream) is the exclusive source of recent impressions; apparent exceptions from earlier days have been recalled on the previous day. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1433]
- Indifferent impressions that are capable of entering dreams while recent lose that capacity as they age, suggesting fresh impressions have special psychical value. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1446]
- The dream of the young medical colleague dreaming of himself already in the hospital represents a 'dream of convenience' or 'dream of impatience.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1398]

## Inferences

- Inference: The preference for recent material may relate to how recent impressions serve as 'attachment points' for unconscious material seeking to reach consciousness through the dream.
