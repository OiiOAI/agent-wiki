# Incubation

## Summary

A latent period between a traumatic experience and the emergence of hysterical symptoms during which psychical processing of the trauma occurs. Charcot described this interval as the 'period of psychical working out' (élaboration). In Katharina's case, after the scene of discovery there followed a short period of working out after which the symptoms of conversion set in, with vomiting as a substitute for moral and physical disgust.
