# Late-Night FM DJ Voice

## Summary

The Late-Night FM DJ Voice is one of three voice tones available to negotiators. It is characterized by being deep, soft, slow, and reassuring, with downward inflection that conveys calm authority. It is used to create safety, ease confrontation, and establish trust without triggering defensiveness.

## Key facts

- The Late-Night FM DJ Voice is described as 'the voice of calm and reason' and is used selectively to make a point with downward voice inflection. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p32]
- When you inflect your voice downward, you convey that you have it covered; upward inflection invites a response and uncertainty. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p34]
- The three voice tones available to negotiators are: late-night FM DJ voice, positive/playful voice, and direct/assertive voice (which should be used rarely). [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p33]
- The positive/playful voice should be the default voice—easygoing and encouraging—with a relaxed attitude and smile while talking. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p33]
- When people are in a positive frame of mind, they think more quickly and are more likely to collaborate and problem-solve. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p33]
