# Tiny Habits Recipe

## Summary

The Tiny Habits Recipe is the core format for designing new habits in the Tiny Habits method. It follows the structure: 'After I [ANCHOR], I will [NEW HABIT].' Each recipe has three components: the Anchor Moment (an existing routine), the Tiny Behavior (the new habit scaled back to be super easy), and the Celebration (something you do to create a positive feeling called Shine).

## Key facts

- The recipe format is: 'After I (ANCHOR), I will (NEW HABIT).'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p130]
- Sample recipes include: 'After I flush the toilet, I will do two push-ups,' 'After I pull the car over, I will write down the most important task of the day,' and 'After I brush my teeth, I will floss one tooth.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p130]
- The recipe has three components: Anchor Moment (existing routine), Tiny Behavior (scaled-back habit), and Celebration (creating Shine). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p134]
- Fuzzy Anchors ('after dinner' or 'whenever I feel stress') don't work; Anchors must be precise events. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p133]
- You can start with a habit you want and find an Anchor, or start with your reliable routines and find new habits to plug in (the Power Move). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p137]
