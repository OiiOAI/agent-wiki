# Competence

## Summary

Competence refers to the psychological need to feel effective or capable in dealing with one's environment. Drawing on Robert White's work on the 'Concept of Competence,' Deci argues that feeling competent is essential for both intrinsic and extrinsic motivation. People must feel sufficiently capable at instrumental activities to achieve desired outcomes. The feeling of competence results when a person takes on and meets optimal challenges. Competence alone is not enough for well-being; it must be accompanied by autonomy for optimal results. Deci found that positive feedback affirming competence could enhance intrinsic motivation, but only when delivered in a noncontrolling manner.

## Key facts

- Robert White argued that competence—the desire to feel effective in dealing with the environment—could be thought of as a fundamental human need. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p65]
- Feeling competent is important for both extrinsic motivation (achieving bonuses and promotions) and intrinsic motivation (enjoyment and personal accomplishment). [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p64]
- The feeling of competence results when a person takes on and meets optimal challenges—challenges that are not trivially easy but realistically within reach. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p66]
- An experiment showed that subjects who received evidence of their own competence were subsequently more intrinsically motivated than those who saw evidence of relative incompetence. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p66]
- Perceived competence without perceived autonomy is insufficient for well-being; being a competent 'puppet' does not nourish humanness. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p70]
