# Paraphrasing in NVC

## Summary

Paraphrasing in NVC involves reflecting back what we have understood from another person. Questions may focus on what others are observing, how they are feeling and the needs generating those feelings, or what they are requesting. Paraphrasing saves time in conflict resolution.

## Key facts

- Paraphrasing questions reveal our understanding while eliciting corrections from the speaker. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p139]
- Questions that ask for information without first sensing the speaker's reality may give speakers the impression that we're examining them or working on a case. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p140]
- Studies in labor-management negotiations demonstrate that time required to reach conflict resolution is cut in half when each negotiator agrees to accurately repeat what the previous speaker had said. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p144]

## Inferences

- Inference: The tone of voice used in paraphrasing is highly important; people are sensitive to hints of criticism, sarcasm, or a declarative tone that implies telling them what is going on inside them.
