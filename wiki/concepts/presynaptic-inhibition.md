# Presynaptic Inhibition

## Summary

Presynaptic inhibition is a mechanism by which GABAergic interneurons in the spinal cord reduce neurotransmitter release from primary sensory afferents. This is achieved through depolarization of sensory terminals via inward Cl- current, which inactivates Na+ channels and reduces action potential size at the terminal. Presynaptic inhibition allows selective modulation of sensory input to the spinal cord and is regulated by descending pathways during movement.

## Key facts

- Presynaptic inhibition is mediated by GABAergic interneurons that depolarize primary sensory terminals through activation of GABAergic receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p825]
- Terminal depolarization inactivates Na+ channels, reducing action potential size and diminishing neurotransmitter release from sensory afferents. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p825]
- Presynaptic inhibition of Ia axons on agonist motor neurons is reduced at movement onset, while inhibition on antagonist motor neurons is increased. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p825]
- Presynaptic inhibition helps stabilize movement execution by preventing excessive sensory feedback and self-reinforcing oscillatory activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p825]
- Presynaptic inhibition occurs when a neuron makes synapses onto the axon terminal of another cell, reducing the amount of transmitter that will be released by the postsynaptic cell onto a third cell. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p337]
- Presynaptic modulation usually occurs early in sensory pathways for reasons that are not well understood. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p337]
- Three mechanisms for presynaptic inhibition have been identified: activation of ionotropic GABA receptor-channels increasing Cl- conductance; G protein-dependent modulation closing Ca2+ channels; and G protein βγ-subunit action directly decreasing Ca2+ sensitivity of release machinery. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p337]
