# Evolution of the Eye

## Summary

The evolution of the eye demonstrates how evolution, through trial and error without a priori planning, has produced diverse optical solutions. Starting from simple photosensitive skin patches, eyes evolved through cup, pinhole, and lens stages. Different organisms developed unique solutions including mirror eyes (scallops), parabolic mirror eyes (Gigantocypris), and scanning eyes (Copilia).

## Key facts

- The eye, and particularly the retina, is a true extension of the central nervous system with neurons forming an extraordinarily compact circuit. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p102]
- Eyes evolved from organisms taking advantage of solar energy, with plants converting light into food through photosynthesis while animals convert light energy into neuronal signaling for seeing. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p102]
- Teleception (remote sensing) arose to extend predictive capacity, buying more time for the brain to respond to threats before they arrive. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p103]
- The eye evolved through stages: pit eye, pinhole eye, lens eye, with various intermediate solutions implemented across species. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p106]
- Scallops have two back-to-back retinas with a mirrored surface that focuses light like a Newtonian telescope, a unique mirror eye arrangement. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p107]
- Copilia has a transparent immobile lens, a second internal lens, and photoreceptors that scan the horizon five times per second. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p108]
- All visual solutions share exploitation of light properties: that it goes straight and generates images through reflection or refraction. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p111]

## Inferences

- Inference: The diversity of eye designs across species demonstrates that evolution tries everything feasible with functional advantage, rather than following a predetermined plan.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether evolution has truly tried every possible solution in eye design, as the text acknowledges this cannot be definitively known.
