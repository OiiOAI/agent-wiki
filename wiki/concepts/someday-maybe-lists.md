# Someday/Maybe Lists

## Summary

Someday/Maybe lists serve as a parking lot for projects one might want to do someday but not currently. These are non-actionable items kept for future reassessment, including goals like learning a language, taking a trip, or pursuing a hobby. Reviewing this list periodically—typically during the Weekly Review—allows one to move items into active projects when the time is right.

## Key facts

- Someday/Maybe lists hold projects that would be impossible to move on at present but that one doesn't want to forget. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p84]
- Someday/Maybe items should be reviewed periodically, suggested to be included in the Weekly Review. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p84]
- Similar lists can include books to read, wines to taste, recipes to try, movies to rent, and weekend trips to take. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p84]
