# Proto-Self

## Summary

The proto-self is the nonconscious forerunner for the levels of self which appear in consciousness as the core self and autobiographical self. It is a collection of brain devices whose main job is the automated management of the organism's life, continuously and nonconsciously maintaining the body state within the narrow range required for survival.

## Key facts

- The proto-self is the nonconscious forerunner for the levels of self which appear in our minds as the conscious protagonists of consciousness: core self and autobiographical self. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p23]
- The proto-self consists of brain devices whose main job is the automated management of the organism's life. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p23]
- The deep roots for the self are found in the ensemble of brain devices which continuously and nonconsciously maintain the body state within the narrow range required for survival. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p23]
- The proto-self is not a homunculus—it does not perceive or know anything; it is a collection of brain devices for life regulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p23]
- The sense of self in the act of knowing belongs to the organism as represented by the proto-self. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p26]
- The organism as represented inside its own brain is a likely biological forerunner for the sense of self. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p23]
- The proto-self is located in brainstem nuclei, hypothalamus, basal forebrain, insular cortex, S2 cortices, and medial parietal cortices—not in early sensory cortices or hippocampus. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p155]
- The proto-self is not conscious, has no powers of perception, holds no knowledge, and is not a rigid homunculus—it emerges dynamically from multifarious interacting signals. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p153]
- The proto-self requires intact somatosensory regions (particularly interoceptive and visceral divisions) but does not require visual, auditory, or fine-touch cortices. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p157]
- Core consciousness emerges when the mechanism generating consciousness operates on the nonconscious proto-self. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p153]
- The proto-self is a nonconscious collection of representations of the multiple dimensions of current organism state. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p199]
- The proto-self is implemented by brainstem nuclei, hypothalamus, basal forebrain, and somatosensory cortices. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p192]
- Bilateral damage to proto-self structures at the level of upper brain stem and hypothalamus causes maximal disruption of consciousness, less severe at higher cortical levels. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p234]
- The proto-self is a reference rather than a storehouse of knowledge or an intelligent perceiver; it participates in the process of knowing, waiting for the brain to explain what is happening. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p191]
- The proto-self is a coherent collection of neural patterns representing the state of the organism's body in its moment-to-moment existence. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p252]
- Proto-self precedes basic feeling and both precede the feeling of knowing that constitutes core consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p281]
- Proto-self is supported by brainstem nuclei, hypothalamus, basal forebrain, and somatosensory cortices. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p267]

## Inferences

- Inference: The proto-self concept posits that consciousness evolved from ancient brain systems that regulated homeostasis, making self-awareness a biological adaptation rather than an metaphysical emergence.
- Inference: The proto-self provides the foundation for the second-order mapping that creates core consciousness by representing the baseline state against which changes are measured.
- Inference: The proto-self likely evolved from ancient brain systems involved in homeostasis, suggesting that consciousness is fundamentally rooted in the body's internal regulatory needs.
