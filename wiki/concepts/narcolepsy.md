# Narcolepsy

## Summary

Narcolepsy is a sleep disorder caused by the selective loss of orexinergic (hypocretin) neurons in the lateral hypothalamus. Patients experience chronic daytime sleepiness, cataplexy (emotionally triggered muscle weakness), hypnagogic hallucinations, and sleep paralysis. The orexin neuropeptides normally promote wakefulness and suppress REM sleep; their absence destabilizes both the wake-sleep and REM-non-REM switches, allowing inappropriate transitions into REM sleep during wakefulness.

## Key facts

- Narcolepsy is associated with the highly selective loss of more than 90% of orexin neurons in the posterior lateral hypothalamus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1141]
- Orexinergic neurons promote wakefulness by activating monoaminergic neurons in the locus ceruleus and dorsal raphe, as well as REM-off GABAergic neurons in the periaqueductal gray matter. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1142]
- Cataplexy is triggered by pleasant experiences that activate prefrontal cortex and amygdala neurons, which can trigger REM sleep paralysis pathways in the absence of orexin inhibition. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1142]
- Narcolepsy is linked to genes affecting immune function and has been triggered by influenza epidemics and certain influenza vaccines, suggesting an autoimmune etiology. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1141]
- Treatment includes wake-promoting medications (amphetamine, modafinil), strategically timed naps, antidepressants for cataplexy, and sodium oxybate to enhance deep sleep. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1142]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery that orexin loss causes narcolepsy provided the first clear evidence that a specific neurotransmitter deficiency could cause a human sleep disorder.
