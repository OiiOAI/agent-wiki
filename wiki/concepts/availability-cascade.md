# Availability Cascade

## Summary

An availability cascade is a self-reinforcing cycle of public concern in which media attention to a risk leads to increased public fear, which generates more media coverage, leading to political action even when the underlying risk is trivial. The phenomenon involves 'availability entrepreneurs' who deliberately maintain the flow of alarming news. Kuran and Sunstein illustrated it with Love Canal and Alar, where minor hazards spiraled into major policy actions driven by emotional reactions rather than actual risk magnitudes. The cascade occurs because availability in memory drives both risk perception and issue salience for policymakers.

## Key facts

- Kuran and Sunstein defined the availability cascade as a self-reinforcing cycle of public alarm that can lead to policy action on risks that are statistically negligible. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p186]
- Love Canal received intense media coverage after 1979, leading to mass relocation, Superfund legislation, and billions in cleanup costs, yet subsequent research found no clear evidence of health damage. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p187]
- The Alar incident in 1989 involved media hysteria over a chemical that caused tumors in mice only at enormous doses; industry suffered severe losses, the product was banned, and later research suggested the risk was very small. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p188]
- Scientists who attempted to calm public fear were ignored or accused of conspiracy, demonstrating that availability cascades resist factual correction. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p187]
- Kuran and Sunstein coined the term 'availability entrepreneurs' for individuals or organizations that deliberately sustain alarming news flows to maintain public and political attention. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p187]

## Inferences

- Inference: Availability cascades represent the intersection of cognitive biases (availability heuristic) with social and political dynamics, showing how individual psychological tendencies can aggregate into collective policy errors with real economic and human costs.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The causal responsibility of Alar and Love Canal for health effects remains disputed; the chunk presents the Kahneman/Sunstein/Slovic perspective that risks were magnified, while acknowledging ongoing controversy.
