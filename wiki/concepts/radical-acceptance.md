# Radical Acceptance

## Summary

Radical acceptance is a DBT distress tolerance skill that involves fully accepting reality as it is—including past and present circumstances, other people as they are, and the situation as it is—without fighting, judging, or resisting. It means acknowledging pain while recognizing that you can survive it and that there are alternatives to the situation.

## Key facts

- Radical acceptance involves accepting reality as it is, including past and present circumstances, other people as they are, and the situation as it is, without judging or resisting. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p342]
- Factors that interfere with radical acceptance include believing that life should be fair, believing that pain must be resolved before one can be happy, and believing that experiencing pain is intolerable. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p343]
- Radical acceptance is part of the Reality Acceptance Skills set, which help reduce suffering by accepting and entering fully into life even when it is not the life you want. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p26]
- Radical means all the way, complete and total acceptance in mind, heart, and body. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p366]
- What must be accepted: reality as it is (past and present facts), realistic limitations on the future, and that everything has causes. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p366]
- The path out of hell is through misery; refusing to accept the misery part of climbing out keeps one stuck in hell. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p366]
- Factors that interfere with acceptance include lacking skills, believing acceptance means approval, and being overwhelmed by emotions like anger or shame. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p367]
- Practicing radical acceptance step-by-step: observe resistance, remind yourself facts cannot be changed, acknowledge causes, practice with whole self, act opposite, cope ahead, attend to body sensations, allow grief. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p368]

## Inferences

- Inference: Radical acceptance differs from approval, compassion, love, or passivity—it does not mean agreeing that situations are good or right.
