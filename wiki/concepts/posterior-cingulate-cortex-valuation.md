# Posterior cingulate cortex valuation signals

## Summary

Neurons in posterior cingulate cortex (CGp) encode an ongoing estimate of the subjective value or salience of extra-personal events and actions, potentially updating value-related information in brain areas like parietal cortex.

## Key facts

- CGp neurons encode an ongoing estimate of the subjective value or salience of extra-personal events and actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p454]
- CGp neuronal activity correlates with risk preferences rather than representing the objective value of each target. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p454]
- CGp responses to task-related events are modulated by their associated value. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p454]
