# Directed Creativity

## Summary

Directed Creativity is a methodology proposed by Gary Klein as an alternative to traditional brainstorming. The central premise is that decision makers must discover what they need at the same time they search for a solution, meaning goal definition and solution-finding proceed simultaneously rather than sequentially. The approach has three components: goals (what needs to be achieved), leverage points (opportunities or resources available), and connections (recognizing how leverage points can satisfy goals). This reverses the typical creativity session dynamic by making goal exploration part of the creative process rather than a fixed starting point.

## Key facts

- Traditional brainstorming fails because individuals outperform groups both in number and quality of ideas generated. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p191]
- Research concluded that 'productivity loss in brainstorming groups is highly significant' and 'the long-lived popularity of brainstorming techniques is unequivocally and substantially misguided'. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p191]
- Directed creativity is based on three components: goals, leverage points, and the connection between them. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p193]
- Creativity can proceed from either direction—sometimes goals initiate the search for solutions, sometimes new opportunities inspire goal discovery. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p193]
- The directed creativity process has seven tactics: present the dilemma, send team members off to work alone, present ideas, critique the ideas, integrate ideas, conduct additional rounds, and converge on a solution. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p198-199]

## Inferences

- Inference: The critique of brainstorming is based on controlled experimental comparisons, not anecdotal evidence

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The directed creativity approach has not been tested using the same rigorous controlled standards that were applied to criticize brainstorming
