# Shine (Feeling Successful)

## Summary

Shine is Fogg's term for the positive feeling that wires habits into the brain. Celebrating immediately after a behavior creates an emotional anchor that makes the habit stick more effectively than repetition alone. Fogg describes it as firing off positive emotions on demand, and emphasizes that people change best by feeling good, not by feeling bad.

## Key facts

- Shine is created by celebrating immediately after doing a new habit. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p232]
- The effects of feeling successful ripple out to other areas of life. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p276]
- Fogg identifies 32 different types of messages that affirm success. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p277]
- The most emotionally powerful feedback combines two elements: it relates to a domain you care about AND it's in an area where you feel uncertain (the 'Power Zone'). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p278]
- When pushing yourself too much to expand a habit creates pain or frustration, Fogg recommends celebrating extra hard to offset negative feelings. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p206]
- Fogg's personal theme is 'strengthen others in all my interactions'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p279]
