# Organismic Integration

## Summary

Organismic integration is the central principle in Deci and Ryan's theory of human development. It refers to the basic tendency within people to move toward greater coherence and integrity in the organization of their inner world. This process encompasses both intrinsic motivation (the proactive engagement with the environment) and the natural movement toward a more complex yet organized sense of self. Only when the processes that initiate and regulate action are integrated aspects of one's self can behavior be autonomous and the person authentic. The development of integration allows authenticity—being true to one's self.
