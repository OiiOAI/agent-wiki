# Dispositional Fixation in Obsessional Neurosis

## Summary

The specific disposition to obsessional neurosis lies in the pregenital sadistic-anal stage of sexual organization. The extraordinary part played by hatred and anal erotism in obsessional neurosis follows from this hypothesis.

- Impulses of hatred and anal erotism play an extraordinary part in the symptomatology of obsessional neurosis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4037]
