# External Shoulder Rotation

## Summary

A shoulder movement where the arm rotates outward in its socket, described as a challenging but important movement for most people. The authors recommend practicing this through mobilizations and daily reminders, noting it fosters advantageous upper body organization and greater arm power.

## Key facts

- Most people's arms rotate forward well, but rotating back (external rotation) is another story. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1431-L1432]
- External rotation is a good resting position that fosters advantageous organization of the upper body and gives more power in arms. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1432-L1434]
- Working with an Olympic gold medal bobsledder on external shoulder rotation helped her connect more forcefully with the sled, increasing her speed. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1435-L1437]
- To practice external rotation, think of trying to 'snap' a carried object in half or roll arms back so pectoral muscles lie flat and palms face forward. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1440-L1443]
