# Boldness

## Summary

Bold behavior involves acting against social norms to express interest, requiring courage and vulnerability. Greater boldness leads to greater polarization of responses and stronger attraction.

## Key facts

- Bold behavior requires a lot of courage to disrupt social norms and requires quite a bit of vulnerability. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p182]
- The bolder your action, the greater attraction you're going to create; the bolder the action, the more vulnerability you show, and the more you polarize responses. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p183]
- The author's mantra is 'Always err on the side of assertiveness'—choosing the bolder action rather than waiting for safer opportunities. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p183]
