# Habitus

## Summary

Habitus is a concept developed by Pierre Bourdieu referring to internalized cognitive structures through which people deal with the social world. These are schemes of perception, thought, and action that people acquire through long-term occupation of a position within the social world. Habitus both structures the social world (as a structuring structure) and is structured by it (as a structured structure). It is durable, transposable across fields, and operates below the level of consciousness while guiding practical activities. Bourdieu uses habitus to escape both subjectivism and objectivism in social theory.

## Key facts

- Habitus are 'the mental, or cognitive structures' through which people deal with the social world. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p433]
- Habitus is 'the product of the internalization of the structures' of the social world. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p433]
- Habitus is both a 'structuring structure' that structures the social world and a 'structured structure' that is structured by the social world. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p433]
- Habitus is acquired as a result of long-term occupation of a position within the social world, and those occupying the same position tend to have similar habitus. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p433]
- Habitus functions 'below the level of consciousness and language, beyond the reach of introspective scrutiny and control by the will.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p434]
- Habitus allows people to make sense out of the social world but does not determine thought and choice—it merely 'suggests' what people should think and choose. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p434]

## Inferences

- Inference: The concept of habitus allows Bourdieu to explain both the regularities in social behavior and the capacity for innovation and improvisation.
