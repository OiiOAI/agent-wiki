# Status Epilepticus

## Summary

Status epilepticus is a life-threatening medical emergency defined as repeated convulsive seizures without return to full consciousness between episodes. It requires aggressive seizure management and medical support because 30 or more minutes of continuous convulsive seizures leads to brain injury or death. Systemic complications include cardiac arrhythmias, pulmonary edema, hyperthermia, and muscle breakdown.
