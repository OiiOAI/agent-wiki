# Virtual Panel of Experts

## Summary

The virtual panel of experts is a personal thinking technique where Richard Rumelt imagines a group of respected experts whose judgment he values, using internal mental dialogue to both critique his own ideas and stimulate new ones. The technique works because humans are adept at recognizing and comprehending well-integrated personalities, making it richer than abstract theories.

## Key facts

- Rumelt invokes a virtual panel of experts that he carries around in his mind to critique his own ideas and stimulate new ones. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]
- The panel contains respected executives, educators, colleagues, and people whose work he knows from their writings or biographies. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]
- The panel includes Professor Bruce Scott, Professor Alfred D. Chandler Jr., David Teece for technology strategy, and Steve Jobs for product and design insight. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p97]
- The technique works because humans have built-in software for understanding other humans that is more expert at recognizing personalities than almost anything else. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p98]

## Inferences

- Inference: The virtual panel technique leverages personality recognition abilities rather than abstract reasoning to provide richer criticism and advice.
