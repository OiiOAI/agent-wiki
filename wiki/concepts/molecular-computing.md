# Molecular Computing

## Summary

Molecular computing refers to the use of biological macromolecular systems as computing elements, an area actively explored by molecular biology laboratories. This approach leverages chemical reactions that can occur at thresholds of concentration, heat, or time to produce essentially digital responses to initial conditions, analogous to operations in conventional computers.
