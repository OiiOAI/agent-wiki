# 24-Hour Fast

## Summary

The 24-Hour Fast is a self-assessment designed to test metabolic flexibility and evaluate one's psychological relationship with food. By not eating for 24 hours (while consuming noncaloric beverages), one can observe how the body responds to food absence—crashing energy and intense cravings indicate metabolic inflexibility, while mild discomfort without major swings suggests a more adaptable system.

## Key facts

- The 24-Hour Fast is designed to test whether someone possesses metabolic flexibility and can psychologically do without food for a day. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1629-L1631]
- Drinking noncaloric beverages is permitted during the fast. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1631-L1632]
- The 24-Hour Fast is not designed to be a test of willpower but rather a test of metabolic flexibility. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1642-L1643]
- One benefit of going for blocks of time without eating is that the body learns to tap into stored fat. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1651-L1653]
- If in the absence of food you develop crazy cravings and your energy level crashes, it is a sign of metabolic inflexibility. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1668-L1670]
- If you felt slightly uncomfortable, maybe a little spacey, but did not suffer any big swings during the fast, you have a more adaptable system. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1670-L1672]
- The preparation involves having a normal meal the night before and planning the next meal at the same time the following day. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1673-L1675]
- Morning coffee without milk or sugar is permitted during the fast. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1675-L1676]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book suggests that many people have lost the ability to go without food due to constant eating patterns in modern culture, and this represents a form of metabolic inflexibility.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The book acknowledges that everyone is different and some people may struggle with a one-day fast even while doing everything right.
