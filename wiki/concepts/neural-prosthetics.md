# Neural Prosthetics

## Summary

Neural prosthetics are mechanical devices driven by decoded neural signals, representing a new approach to treating neurological conditions. Motor systems have become a target for neural prosthetics applications, particularly to aid patients with paralysis caused by spinal cord injury and stroke.

## Key facts

- Motor systems have become a target for the application of neural prosthetics. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p791]
- Neural prosthetics work by decoding neural signals and using them to drive mechanical devices. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p791]
- Neural prosthetics are being developed to aid patients with paralysis caused by spinal cord injury and stroke. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p791]
