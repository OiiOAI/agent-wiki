# Crew Resource Management

## Summary

Crew Resource Management (CRM) is a training program introduced in the aviation industry to reduce human error and improve crew performance. Originally focused on individual attitudes and interaction styles, CRM evolved to emphasize team process skills. The training addresses situational, socio-psychological, and other factors that influence aircrew performance. Modern CRM training includes skills such as communication, leadership, preparation and planning, and adaptability, delivered through practice and feedback rather than lecture alone.

## Key facts

- CRM training was introduced to achieve the goal of reducing human error in aviation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p337]
- CRM addresses 'situational, socio-psychological, and other factors that influence aircrew performance' (Caro, 1988). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p340]
- Early CRM programs were designed to impact the attitudes and styles of individual crew members based on the assumption that changed attitudes would lead to improved interactions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p340]
- In the late 1980s, CRM training shifted from awareness training to training team-process skills. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p345]
- Research showed that training including practice and feedback was superior for transfer over other methods like lecture, discussion, and modeling. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p345]
- CRM research identified team process skill areas including leadership, communications, preparation and planning, and adaptability. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p349]
- Research demonstrated increases in the use of CRM skills as a consequence of training (Salas, Fowlkes, Stout, Milanovich, & Prince, 1999). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p346]
