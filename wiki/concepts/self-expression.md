# Self-Expression

## Summary

Self-expression is presented as a fundamental human need as vital to survival as food or shelter. The book describes how the author's medical condition and inability to speak led to discovering writing as a refuge, ultimately leading to the realization that self-expression allows sharing the stories of our lives. Finding your voice and speaking your truth is described as a radical act of self-worth.

## Key facts

- Self-expression is a fundamental human need as vital to our survival as food or shelter. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p203]
- We must be able to share the stories of our lives—from the small moments of what happened today at school to our grandest theories of what life is about. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p203]
- Finding your voice and speaking your truth is a radical act of self-worth: Who are you to speak up? Who says you have anything to offer? Who are you to demand people's attention?. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p204]
