# Miniature End-Plate Potential

## Summary

Miniature end-plate potentials are small, spontaneous depolarizations at the neuromuscular junction resulting from the release of neurotransmitter from single synaptic vesicles, providing evidence for the quantum hypothesis of transmitter release.

## Key facts

- Miniature end-plate potentials (mEPPs) are spontaneous depolarizations of 0.5-1.0 mV occurring at approximately 1/s at the neuromuscular junction. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p260]
- Del Castillo and Katz concluded that each mEPP represents the action of a multimolecular packet or quantum of transmitter, with the large evoked EPSP being an integral multiple of quanta. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p260]
- Statistical analysis of baseline noise during very low ACh application revealed that the elementary postsynaptic response to a single channel is approximately 0.3 μV in amplitude and 1.0 ms in duration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p260]
