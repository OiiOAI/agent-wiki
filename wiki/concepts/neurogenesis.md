# Neurogenesis

## Summary

The process of stem cells dividing and developing into functional new brain cells (neurons) in the adult brain, firmly established in 1998 and believed to be confined to part of the hippocampus and the subventricular zone associated with smell.

## Key facts

- Neurogenesis is the process of stem cells dividing and developing into functional new brain cells, or neurons, in the brain. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p224]
- That this happens in the adult human was firmly established in 1998. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p224]
- Neurogenesis is believed to be confined to part of the hippocampus and another brain area called the subventricular zone, which is associated with the sense of smell. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p224]
- Neurogenesis occurs in certain regions of the adult mammalian brain, contrary to the previous dogma that new neurons cannot form in adulthood. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1286]
- Evidence for adult neurogenesis has accelerated research on stimulating neurogenesis and replacing neurons following injury. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1286]
- Therapeutic interventions may retain or replace injured central neurons through stimulation of neurogenesis in regions of injury. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1286]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery that neurogenesis occurs in the adult brain suggests that endogenous repair mechanisms could potentially be harnessed for therapeutic purposes.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The functional significance and therapeutic potential of adult neurogenesis in humans requires further investigation.
