# Anterior Cingulate Cortex in Monitoring and Decision

## Summary

The anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) is critically situated at the interface between limbic structures and lateral frontal cortex, serving to integrate feed-forward loops that receive inputs to decision-making with feedback loops that collect neural influences from decision outcomes. The ACC has been consistently shown to be a decision outcome predictor, whether that outcome is favorable or unfavorable, and a predictor of possible conflict. It also shows magnitude-based salience signals in response to stimuli predicting both rewards and punishments.
