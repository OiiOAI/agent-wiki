# Planck Knowledge vs. Chauffeur Knowledge

## Summary

Munger distinguishes between 'Planck knowledge' (those who really know, who've paid the dues and have the aptitude) and 'chauffeur knowledge' (those who have learned to prattle the talk, often with fine voices and big heads of hair, making a big impression but lacking real expertise). He notes this distinction applies to practically every politician.

## Key facts

- Munger tells the story of Max Planck's chauffeur who memorized a lecture on quantum mechanics and delivered it in Munich, then answered a physics professor's question by saying he would ask his chauffeur to reply. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p445]
- Munger states: 'We have two kinds of knowledge: Planck knowledge, that of the people who really know. They've paid the dues, they have the aptitude. And then we've got chauffeur knowledge. They have learned to prattle the talk.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p446]
- Munger says: 'I think I've just described practically every politician in the United States.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p446]

## Inferences

- Inference: The challenge of life is getting as much responsibility as possible into the hands of people with Planck knowledge while keeping it away from those with only chauffeur knowledge, against which there are 'huge forces working.'
