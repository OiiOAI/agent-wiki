# Suprachiasmatic Nucleus

## Summary

The suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) is a small region of approximately 20,000 neurons located in the hypothalamus just above the optic chiasm. It serves as the master circadian clock, generating endogenous approximately 24-hour rhythms that regulate sleep-wake cycles and other physiological processes. Lesioning the SCN causes complete loss of circadian rhythmicity while transplantation can restore circadian sleep rhythms.

## Key facts

- The suprachiasmatic nucleus is so named because of its location just above the optic chiasm, where the optic nerves cross underneath the hypothalamus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1198]
- The firing frequency of neurons in the suprachiasmatic nucleus follows an endogenous circadian rhythm, demonstrating that these ~20,000 neurons constitute the master circadian clock. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1198]
- Lesioning the suprachiasmatic nucleus in animals causes complete loss of circadian rhythmicity of sleep-wake cycles, though total daily sleep amount remains normal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1198]
- Transplantation of the suprachiasmatic nucleus from another animal can restore a circadian sleep rhythm, with the host adopting the donor's sleep-wake cycle. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1198]
- The suprachiasmatic nucleus maintains circadian rhythm in vitro, as shown by rhythmic vasopressin release from isolated SCN neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1201]
