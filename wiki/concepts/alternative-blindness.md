# Alternative Blindness

## Summary

Alternative blindness is the tendency to forget to compare an existing offer with the next-best alternative. MBA brochures hide that the real cost is $200,000 (tuition plus lost earnings) and that other programs might achieve similar results for less.

## Key facts

- An MBA brochure showing $100,000 tuition offset by $400,000 extra lifetime earnings ignores lost earnings during the two-year program, making the real cost $200,000. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p159]
- Warren Buffett measures each deal against the second-best deal available: 'even if it means doing more of what we are already doing.'. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p159]
