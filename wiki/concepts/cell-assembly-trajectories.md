# Self-Organized Cell Assembly Trajectories

## Summary

Self-generated, sequentially evolving neuronal activity is the default state in most neuronal circuits. Self-organized cell assembly trajectories are generated by the interaction of competitive processes (excitation and inhibition) combined with an adaptation component. Large recurrent networks can generate enormous numbers of unique trajectories, with each trajectory determined by the initial conditions. The brain does not simply wait to be stimulated; even cultured brain tissue generates spontaneous activity. These trajectories can be matched by experience to become meaningful patterns for downstream reader mechanisms.

## Key facts

- Self-generated, sequentially evolving activity is the default state of affairs in most neuronal circuits when adaptation mechanisms are present. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p185]
- Neuronal trajectories can be generated by two fundamentally different mechanisms: internally by the brain's own dynamics, or externally by environmental and body-derived cues. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p172]
- During theta oscillations, the spike-timing sequence of neuronal assemblies predicts the sequence of passed and upcoming locations in the rat's path, with larger time lags representing larger distances. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p173]
- Theta oscillation is essential for internally organized hippocampal neuronal trajectories as well as for memory-guided spatial behaviors. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p180]
- Self-organized neuronal sequences are not limited to the hippocampus; similar evolving neuronal trajectories have been detected in the medial prefrontal cortex, posterior parietal cortex, entorhinal cortex, and motor cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p184]
- Birdsong and grooming are examples of neuronal trajectories that can be interpreted by relatively simple reader mechanisms due to their limited variability. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p197]

## Inferences

- Inference: Learning may be an inside-out matching process where spontaneously occurring neuronal trajectories are matched to useful actions rather than building new trajectories from experience.
- Inference: The richness of information depends not on the sender's sequence patterns but on the ability of receiving structures to link patterns to behaviorally meaningful events.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact physiological mechanisms that generate self-organized sequential activity are not perfectly understood.
