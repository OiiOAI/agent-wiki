# Derivatives Criticism

## Summary

Charlie Munger was notably critical of derivatives, viewing them as a dangerous and potentially catastrophic system. He described the accounting for derivatives under GAAP as a 'sewer' and predicted significant blowups in major financial institutions' derivative books.

## Key facts

- Munger states: 'I hate with a passion GAAP as applied to derivatives and swaps. JP Morgan sold out to this type of accounting to front-and revenues. I think it's a disgrace.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p123]
- He says: 'To say accounting for derivatives in America is a sewer is an insult to sewage.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p124]
- Munger predicts: 'I'll be amazed if we don't have some kind of significant blow up in the next five to ten years.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p124]
- Berkshire Hathaway was running off its derivative book, which Munger describes as 'a crazy idea for people who are already rich.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p124]
- He describes the derivatives system as 'almost insanely irresponsible' with complexity that makes accounting nearly impossible. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p121]
- EBITDA should be substituted with 'bullshit earnings' whenever the word is seen. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p117]
