# Embeddedness

## Summary

Embeddedness refers to the concept that economic action is embedded in social structures and relationships. Mark Granovetter's influential paper argued that economic behavior cannot be understood apart from the social context in which it occurs.

## Key facts

- Mark Granovetter argued that economic action and social structure are interrelated, with the problem of embeddedness being central to understanding economic behavior. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p366]

## Inferences

- Inference: The embeddedness concept challenges neoclassical economics' assumption of atomized, utility-maximizing individuals by emphasizing that economic actors operate within social networks and relationships.
