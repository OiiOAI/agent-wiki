# Radical Feminism

## Summary

Radical feminism is based on the belief that women are of absolute positive value as women and are everywhere oppressed—violently oppressed—by the system of patriarchy. It sees patriarchy as a primary power arrangement, not an unintended consequence of biology, socialization, or class. Central to radical feminism is the image of patriarchy as violence practiced by men against women, ranging from overt physical cruelty to hidden practices of exploitation and control. Strategies for change include consciousness-raising, sisterhood, critical confrontation, and separatism.

## Key facts

- Radical feminism is based on two central beliefs: (1) women are of absolute positive value as women, asserted against the universal devaluing of women; and (2) women are everywhere oppressed—violently oppressed—by the system of patriarchy. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p372]
- Radical feminists see patriarchy not as an unintended secondary consequence of biology, socialization, sex roles, or class, but as a primary power arrangement sustained by strong and deliberate intention. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p369]
- Patriarchy exists as a near-universal social form because men can muster the most basic power resource, physical force, to establish control. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p373]
- Radical feminists argue that violence exists whenever one group controls in its own interests the life chances, environments, actions, and perceptions of another group, as men do to women. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p373]
- Strategies for defeating patriarchy include a basic reworking of women's consciousness, recognition of women's own value and strength, the establishment of a broad-based sisterhood, critical confrontation with patriarchal domination, and degree of separatism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p373]
- Lesbian feminism, as a major strand in radical feminism, is the practice and belief that 'erotic and/or emotional commitment to women is part of resistance to patriarchal domination.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p373]

## Inferences

- Inference: Radical feminists may be faulted for their exclusive focus on patriarchy, which some critics argue simplifies the realities of social organization and social inequality.
