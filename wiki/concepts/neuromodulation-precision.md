# Neuromodulation and Precision

## Summary

Neuromodulatory systems modulate synaptic efficacy and are associated with different precision parameters in Active Inference. Acetylcholine (from basal nucleus of Meynert) signals precision of likelihood distributions. Noradrenaline (from locus coeruleus) signals precision of transitions. Dopamine (from substantia nigra and VTA) signals confidence in policies. Precision acts as a multiplicative weight on prediction errors and is essential for attention and multisensory integration.

## Key facts

- Precision acts as a multiplicative weight on prediction errors and is a measure of confidence in a probability distribution. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p103]
- If we have very precise beliefs about how data are generated, our beliefs about hidden states can be updated by observing data more than if we are not confident in those beliefs. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p103]
- Dopamine signals can be explained in Active Inference as enhancing confidence that one is pursuing a policy that minimizes expected free energy. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p106]
- Pupillary size is related to noradrenaline and can be used to probe someone's implicit generative model through peripheral measurements. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p107]
