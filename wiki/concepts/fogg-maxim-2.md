# Fogg Maxim #2

## Summary

Fogg Maxim #2 states: 'Help people feel successful.' This principle complements Fogg Maxim #1 ('Help people do what they already want to do') by focusing on the emotional component of behavior change. The maxim specifically says 'feel successful' rather than 'be successful' because the feeling of success—created through celebration—is what wires habits into the brain.

## Key facts

- Fogg Maxim #2: 'Help people feel successful' (not 'be successful'). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p164]
- Every product or service that is growing and thriving today helps users feel successful. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p164]
- If you try a product and it makes you feel clumsy, stupid, or unsuccessful, you will likely abandon it. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p165]
- Helping yourself feel successful is what Tiny Habits is all about. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p165]
