# Stress-Induced Dendritic Remodeling

## Summary

Chronic stress produces contrasting patterns of dendritic remodeling in different brain regions. Stress causes atrophy of apical dendrites in hippocampal CA3 neurons and prefrontal cortex neurons, while simultaneously causing hypertrophy of amygdala neurons. These structural changes mediate anxiety behaviors and affect decision-making through frontostriatal reorganization.

## Key facts

- Stress-induced atrophy of apical dendrites of hippocampal CA3c neurons involves glucocorticoid secretion and excitatory amino acid receptors. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Chronic psychosocial stress causes apical dendritic atrophy of hippocampal CA3 pyramidal neurons in subordinate tree shrews. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Acute stress causes correlated memory defects and hippocampal dendritic spine loss through corticotropin-releasing hormone signaling. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Repeated stress induces dendritic spine loss in the rat medial prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Brief uncontrollable stress causes dendritic retraction in infralimbic cortex and resistance to fear extinction in mice. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Chronic stress causes frontostriatal reorganization and affects decision-making. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Chronic stress induces contrasting patterns of dendritic remodeling—atrophy in hippocampal and prefrontal neurons, hypertrophy in amygdaloid neurons. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Acute corticosterone treatment is sufficient to induce anxiety and amygdaloid dendritic hypertrophy. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Stress and excitatory synapses interact bidirectionally in health and disease. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p762]
- Reversibility of apical dendritic retraction in medial prefrontal cortex following repeated stress is possible. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p764]

## Inferences

- Inference: The opposing effects of stress on different brain regions may explain why chronic stress can impair some cognitive functions while enhancing emotional memory formation
