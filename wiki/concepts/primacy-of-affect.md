# Primacy of Affect

## Summary

McGilchrist presents evidence that affective judgment occurs before cognitive assessment, supporting the view that affect—not cognition—is primary. Research shows we make intuitive assessments of wholes before any cognitive processes come into play, and pieces of information are judged in light of the whole rather than the reverse. Panksepp suggests that at a deep affective level, the right hemisphere is more in touch with true inner feelings. Nietzsche wrote that thoughts are shadows of feelings—always darker, emptier, simpler. The essential core of being is subcortical, with perceptual-cognitive awareness developing on the back of affective awareness.

## Key facts

- Affective judgment is not dependent on cognitive processes; we make intuitive assessments of wholes before cognitive assessment of parts. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p262]
- Panksepp suggests that at a deep affective level, the right hemisphere is more in touch with true inner feelings. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p262]
- Max Scheler's concept of Wertnehmung refers to pre-cognitive apprehension of the value of something. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p262]
- Nietzsche wrote that thoughts are shadows of feelings—always darker, emptier, simpler. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p263]
- Panksepp suggests Descartes' 'I think, therefore I am' may be superseded by 'I feel, therefore I am'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p263]
- Damasio notes that the apparatus of rationality does not work without biological regulation, suggesting rationality is built from affect. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p263]

## Inferences

- Inference: The left hemisphere's view that affect is secondary to cognition is itself a retrospective cognitive assessment that misses the primary role of feeling in experience

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Damasio interprets affect as useful for cognition rather than seeing thinking as existing to serve feeling
