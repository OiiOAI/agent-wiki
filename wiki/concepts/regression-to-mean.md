# Regression to Mean

## Summary

Regression to Mean describes the statistical phenomenon where extreme performances are interspersed with less extreme ones. Extreme results (good or bad) tend to be followed by results closer to the average, which people often mistakenly attribute to interventions rather than natural variation.

## Key facts

- The most successful stock picks from the past three years are hardly going to be the most successful stocks in the coming three years due to regression to mean. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p53]
- Ignoring regression to mean can have destructive consequences, such as teachers concluding that the stick is better than the carrot after praising top performers who then regress. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p54]
- When a CEO hires a consultant and sales improve afterward, the improvement may be natural regression rather than the consultant's effectiveness. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p54]
