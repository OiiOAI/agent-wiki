# Ego

## Summary

The ego is the false, mind-made self created through unconscious identification with the mind. It consists of thought activity and can only exist through constant thinking. The ego lives in fear, feels vulnerable and threatened, and is always concerned with keeping the past alive (to maintain identity) and projecting into the future (for salvation). The ego cannot afford to be wrong, as being wrong threatens its existence. The ego also seeks to find wholeness externally through possessions, success, relationships, and other identifications. True transformation occurs when consciousness withdraws from identification with the ego, allowing Being to become the foundation of identity.

## Key facts

- The ego is defined as 'a false self, created by unconscious identification with the mind'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p34]
- The ego consists of mind activity and can only be kept going through constant thinking. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p34]
- The ego sees itself as constantly under threat, and its continuous message to the body is 'Danger, I am under threat'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p52]
- The ego's ultimate fear is death; 'to the ego, death is always just around the corner'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p52]
- The ego cannot afford to be wrong because being wrong threatens its sense of self with annihilation. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p52]
