# Nose-Breathing Walk

## Summary

The Nose-Breathing Walk is a walking technique that allows practitioners to work on CO2 tolerance while accumulating daily steps. It involves breathing exclusively through the nose with a 10-second inhale, breath hold, and slow exhale, repeated every 1-2 minutes throughout the walk.

## Key facts

- The Nose-Breathing Walk works on CO2 tolerance while walking. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1278-L1280]
- The technique involves taking a 10-second inhale through the nose only. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1282-L1285]
- After inhaling, hold the breath as long as possible while continuing to walk. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1282-L1285]
- Exhale slowly through the nose and repeat every 1-2 minutes depending on tolerance. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1285-L1287]
