# Déformation Professionnelle

## Summary

Déformation professionnelle (or "man with the hammer") is the tendency to view all problems through the lens of one's own expertise. Surgeons see surgical solutions, armies see military solutions, Excel is used for everything. This becomes dangerous when specialized tools are applied outside their domain.

## Key facts

- "If your only tool is a hammer, all your problems will be nails," said Mark Twain, summing up déformation professionnelle. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p201]
- Charlie Munger stated: "You've got to have multiple models from multiple disciplines because all the wisdom of the world is not to be found in one little academic department.". [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p201]
- Excel spreadsheets may be one of the most dangerous recent inventions because they are used even when inappropriate. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p202]
