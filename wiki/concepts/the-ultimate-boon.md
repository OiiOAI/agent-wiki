# The Ultimate Boon

## Summary

The life-transmuting trophy the hero brings back from the underworld journey. The boon may be the elixir of immortality, fire, food, grace, or indestructible life. The gods and goddesses are guardians, embodiments, or bestowers of this power—not ends in themselves. The real prize is the power of their sustaining substance, the Imperishable, not the deities themselves.

## Key facts

- The gods and goddesses are to be understood as embodiments and custodians of the elixir of Imperishable Being but not themselves the Ultimate in its primary state. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p141]
- The miraculous energy of Zeus's thunderbolts, the fertility of Viracocha's rain, the virtue of the consecration bell, and the light of ultimate illumination are all the same Imperishable. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p141]
- Physical immortality searches represent misunderstanding of the traditional teaching; the actual problem is enlarging the pupil of the eye so the body no longer obstructs the view. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p146]
