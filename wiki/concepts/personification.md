# Personification

## Summary

Personification is the tendency to respond to individual human stories rather than abstract statistics. It exploits our evolutionary "theory of mind"—our ability to empathize with others. This explains why single victim photos elicit donations more effectively than data about millions affected.

## Key facts

- The "theory of mind" developed over 100,000 years of group survival, enabling us to understand others' thoughts and feelings. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p191]
- In ultimatum games, subjects offer 30-50% of money to strangers; below 30% is considered unfair—purely because they can imagine the other person's feelings. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p191]
- Slovic's experiment showed 50% less donation when people saw famine statistics ($1.38 average) versus a photo of a single child ($2.83 average). [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p192]
