# Labeling

## Summary

A meditation technique where you give a mental name to sensory experiences as you notice them. Labeling helps focus attention, fills the mental talk channel with calm neutral words, and research suggests it increases the potency of mindfulness's beneficial effects.

## Key facts

- Labeling means giving a name to sensory experience that you have noticed. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p41]
- The basic labeling instructions are: notice something, label it, go to step 1. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p41]
- Labels should be spoken very softly in your mind, like whispering sweet nothings to a baby. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p41]
- Labeling is not the same as a mantra - you create a label each time you notice the thing you are meditating on, naming a specific object. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p42]
- Research evidence suggests that the process of labeling in meditation (particularly labeling affect) increases the potency of mindfulness's beneficial effects. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p41]
- Labeling is described as 'a way of validating someone's emotion by acknowledging it' and 'a shortcut to intimacy, a time-saving emotional hack'. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p51]
- Labels almost always begin with 'It seems like,' 'It sounds like,' or 'It looks like,' avoiding the word 'I' which raises guard. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p53]
- A UCLA brain imaging study found that labeling an emotion disrupts its raw intensity by moving brain activity from the amygdala to areas governing rational thinking. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p52]
- The rule of labeling is to be quiet and listen after throwing out a label, allowing the other person to reveal themselves. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p53]
- Labeling negatives diffuses them while labeling positives reinforces them. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p54]
