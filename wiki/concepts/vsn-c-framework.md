# VSN-C Framework

## Summary

The Vision–Steps–News…Contributions framework is a foundational structure for organizing spoken and written communication. It ensures audiences and readers are engaged by immediately showing the speaker's vision, demonstrating steps taken, announcing recent news, and concluding with explicit contributions.

## Key facts

- VSN-C stands for Vision, Steps, News, and Contributions. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p35]
- You must show your hand immediately—typically within five minutes—to convince audiences to listen. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p34]
- Vision combines a problem people care about and an approach to solving that problem. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p35]
- The Step just completed constitutes News because it has just been accomplished. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p37]
- A slide titled Contributions should conclude slide-based talks, not Thank you or Conclusions. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p38]
- Contributions synonyms include Recommendations, Business messages, Assured benefits, Projected earnings, and What your gift will do, but never Conclusions. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p39]
- VSN-C stands for Vision, Steps, News, and Contributions—a framework for structuring communications. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p339]
- The framework can be adapted with artistic license regarding which elements to include and their placement. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p339]
- VSN-C is used for reviews of papers and books, including a summary with details plus evaluation and opinion about who should read the work. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p339]
- When writing recommendations, VSN-C helps establish that the candidate has a Vision and characteristics needed to pursue that Vision. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p343]
- For briefings, a Business Messages slide functions as a special kind of Contributions slide. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p352]
