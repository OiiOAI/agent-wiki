# Snow White Mindset

## Summary

The Snow White Mindset is a pattern where a person sees themselves as an innocent, passive victim at the hands of others and circumstance, devoid of responsibility or accountability with no power to change things. It is more common in women than men and involves Gremlins such as 'I am not responsible for my own decisions', 'I am not responsible for my own happiness', 'Life is really tough', 'I am a victim of circumstance', and 'There is nothing I can do to change things'. Recovery requires seeing how they present themselves to others, being proactive, making life work, seeking and accepting help, and recognising that happiness is a choice. Key reframes include: nobody likes a victim, nobody owes you anything, and assertiveness is normal adult behaviour.

- The Snow White Mindset is more common in women than in men and involves seeing oneself as an innocent, passive victim at the hands of others. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p94]
