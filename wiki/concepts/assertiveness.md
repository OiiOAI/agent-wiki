# Assertiveness

## Summary

Assertiveness means explaining to someone what is acceptable in your world and what isn't, in a calm manner without expressing emotion. By contrast, being aggressive is expressing emotion and attacking behaviours. An assertive approach prevents waking up the other person's Chimp, making them more likely to hear the message and respond appropriately. The three parts to assertiveness are: say what you don't want using 'I', say how it makes you feel, and say what you do want using 'I'. Lack of assertiveness is usually based in Gremlins such as 'I am not as good as others', 'I can't be as confident as others', or 'my feelings don't matter'.
