# Dream Symbolism

## Summary

Dream symbolism is a constant relation between dream elements and their translations, where a dream element serves as a symbol of an unconscious dream thought. Freud traces its modern discovery to K.A. Scherner (1861) and notes that the same symbolism appears in mythology, folklore, language, and poetry. The majority of dream symbols are sexual symbols.

## Key facts

- Dream symbolism involves a constant relation between a dream element and its translation that Freud describes as a symbolic one, the fourth type of relation between manifest and latent elements. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4924-4925]
- K.A. Scherner (1861) is the discoverer of dream symbolism in modern times, though psychoanalysis has made material modifications to his findings. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4925]
- The same symbolism found in dreams also appears in myths, fairy tales, folklore, colloquial and poetic linguistic usage. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4924]
- The most striking fact about dream symbolism is that the great majority of symbols are sexual symbols. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4927]
- Male genitals are symbolically represented by objects resembling them in shape (sticks, umbrellas, posts), sharp weapons (knives, daggers, firearms), and other elongated objects. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4928]
- Female genitals are symbolically represented by objects enclosing a hollow space (pits, vessels, bottles, boxes, pockets) and by rooms, ships, and cupboards. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4929]
- The number 3 is of symbolic significance for male genitals. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4927]
- Some symbols signify genitals in general, irrespective of sex (small child, small son, small daughter), while some predominantly male symbols may be used for female genitals or vice versa. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4930]
- The human body as a whole is represented by houses; parents appear as imperial or royal couples; children and siblings as small animals or vermin. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4926]
- Birth is represented by falling into or climbing out of water; death by departure or train journeys. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4926]
- Sexual intercourse is symbolized by rhythmic activities such as dancing, riding, climbing, and threatening with weapons. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4930]
- Freud speculates that symbolisms represent a primal language whose roots derived from sexual needs, later detached from sexual meaning and attached to work activities. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4938]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud suggests the symbolic relation may be a residue of an ancient verbal identity, where things once called by the same name as the genitals now serve as symbols for them.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether the number 3 owes its sacred character to its symbolic connection with male genitals remains undecided.
