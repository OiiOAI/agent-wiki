# Moving On

## Summary

Moving On is the fourth movement in the emotional agility framework, encompassing two principles: the Tiny Tweaks Principle and the Teeter-Totter Principle. The Tiny Tweaks Principle suggests that small, deliberate changes infused with values can make a huge difference, especially when applied to routine and habitual behaviors. The Teeter-Totter Principle describes the need to find balance between challenge and competence—neither complacent nor overwhelmed but excited and invigorated by challenges.

## Key facts

- The Tiny Tweaks Principle states that small, deliberate tweaks infused with values can make a huge difference. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p19]
- Tweaking routine and habitual parts of life affords tremendous leverage for change through daily repetition. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p19]
- The Teeter-Totter Principle involves finding balance between challenge and competence so one is neither complacent nor overwhelmed. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p19]
- The ultimate goal is to keep a sense of challenge and growth alive throughout life. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p20]
