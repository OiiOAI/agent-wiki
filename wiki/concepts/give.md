# GIVE

## Summary

GIVE is a DBT interpersonal effectiveness skill for maintaining relationships while being skillful. It is an acronym for (Be) Gentle, (Act) Interested, Validate, and (Use an) Easy manner. This skill helps individuals act in ways that maintain positive relationships and help others feel good about themselves.

## Key facts

- GIVE stands for (Be) Gentle, (Act) Interested, Validate, and (Use an) Easy manner. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
- Gentle means being nice and respectful with no verbal or physical attacks, no threats, no judging, and no sneering. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
- (Act) Interested means listening and appearing interested in the other person with eye contact, leaning toward them, and being patient. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
- Validate means showing with words and actions that you understand the other person's feelings and thoughts, seeing the world from their point of view. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
- (Use an) Easy manner means using a little humor, smiling, easing the person along, being light-hearted, and using a 'soft sell' over a 'hard sell'. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
