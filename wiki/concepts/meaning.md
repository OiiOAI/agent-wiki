# Meaning

## Summary

Meaning in the context of agency refers to the semantic content of internal representations that guide behavior. Unlike information, which can be quantified, meaning is inherently historical, relational, contextual, qualitative, and even subjective. The meaning of internal representations in the brain drives the unfolding of neural activity—the low-level physical details are often incidental to understanding causation. Meaning has causal power: when someone asks you to raise your hand, it is the meaning of those words that causally influences your action, not physical forces. Meaning arises in living systems because they have goals and because evolution has shaped them to represent the world in terms of value and relevance to their persistence.
