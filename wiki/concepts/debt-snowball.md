# Debt Snowball

## Summary

The Debt Snowball is a debt repayment strategy (borrowed from Dave Ramsey's Total Money Makeover) that prioritizes paying off the smallest debt first regardless of interest rates. Once a debt is eliminated, the payment amount is added to the next smallest debt, creating a 'snowball effect' that builds emotional momentum through repeated small wins.

## Key facts

- Pay only the minimum on all debts except the smallest one, then put all financial power into crushing that first debt. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p148]
- Once the first debt is wiped out, tackle the next one by adding the freed-up payments from the first debt to the minimum payment of the second. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p148]
- The Debt Snowball is contrary to logic but plays into the psyche of human beings by providing emotional momentum through tearing up paid-off statements. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p148]
- The trick is that you cannot add new debt as you pay off old debt. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p149]

## Inferences

- Inference: The psychological wins from eliminating small debts fuel continued motivation to eliminate larger ones.
