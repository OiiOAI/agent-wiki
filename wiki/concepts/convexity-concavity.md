# Convexity and Concavity

## Summary

Convexity and concavity describe the nonlinear response of systems to stressors or variations. Convex responses accelerate (benefit more from additional input), while concave responses decelerate (harm increases disproportionately). These properties are central to understanding fragility: concave exposure dislikes volatility (fragility), convex exposure likes it (antifragility).

## Key facts

- Convex exposure means liking volatility; concave exposure means disliking volatility. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p537]
- Everything nonlinear is either convex, concave, or mixed, depending on the intensity of the stressor. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p522]
- When f(x) is convex monotonically, f(x) is right skewed; when concave, it is left skewed. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p550]
