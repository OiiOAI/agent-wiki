# Lollapalooza Effect

## Summary

The Lollapalooza Effect is Munger's term for the phenomenon when two, three, or four forces from his hundred mental models combine and reinforce each other in the same direction, producing results that exceed simple linear addition. Like achieving critical mass in physics, these combined forces can trigger exponential outcomes, though falling short of the threshold yields nothing significant.

- Munger coined the term 'Lollapalooza Effect' to describe factors that reinforce and greatly amplify each other. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p58]
- When several models combine, forces often don't add simply but combine like critical mass in physics, creating a nuclear explosion at a certain threshold. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p58]
