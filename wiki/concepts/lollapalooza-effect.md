# Lollapalooza Effect

## Summary

The Lollapalooza Effect is Munger's term for the phenomenon when two, three, or four forces from his hundred mental models combine and reinforce each other in the same direction, producing results that exceed simple linear addition. Like achieving critical mass in physics, these combined forces can trigger exponential outcomes, though falling short of the threshold yields nothing significant.

## Key facts

- Munger coined the term 'Lollapalooza Effect' to describe factors that reinforce and greatly amplify each other. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p58]
- When several models combine, forces often don't add simply but combine like critical mass in physics, creating a nuclear explosion at a certain threshold. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p58]
- Forces from the mental models sometimes conflict, creating 'huge, miserable trade offs,' and the inability to think in terms of tradeoffs makes one 'a horse's patoot.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p58]
- Munger's core attributes (preparation, discipline, patience, decisiveness) form 'dynamic critical mass' for cascading positive Lollapalooza effects. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p79]
- The Milgram experiment on authority harnesses at least six psychological principles, not three, all operating in the same direction to achieve dramatic results. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p231]
- Tuberculosis was tamed only by routine combined use of three different drugs, illustrating that major effects often require multiple factors working together. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p288]
- Airplane flight follows a lollapalooza pattern requiring multiple factors working in concert. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p288]
- When four or five forces from psychological models come together operating in the same direction, lollapalooza effects can make you rich or kill you—making awareness of these effects essential. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p233]
