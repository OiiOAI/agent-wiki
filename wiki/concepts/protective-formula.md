# Protective Formula

## Summary

A protective formula is a verbal ritual used by hysterical patients to ward off distressing thoughts or hallucinations. Frau Emmy von N. used the phrase 'Keep still! Don't say anything! Don't touch me!' which she had developed from experiences where animals in her hallucinations attacked when movements occurred near her. Similar formulas were observed in melancholic patients attempting to control tormenting thoughts.

## Key facts

- Emmy von N.'s formula 'Keep still! Don't say anything! Don't touch me!' was designed to prevent frightening thoughts from being interrupted in their course. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p591]
- The 'Keep still!' component related to animal shapes that attacked if anyone made a movement in her presence. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p591]
- The 'Don't touch me!' was derived from experiences where her brother, an acquaintance who went mad, and her delirious daughter had seized hold of her. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p591]
