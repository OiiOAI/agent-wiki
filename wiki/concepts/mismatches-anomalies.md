# Mismatches and Anomalies

## Summary

Boyd uses mismatches and anomalies as strategic tools, creating gaps between what an opponent believes threatens their survival and what actually does. These mismatches feed back into the OODA loop system, creating increasing mismatch in subsequent cycles until the adversary becomes isolated.

## Key facts

- Multiple events compressed in time, manipulation of friction, and creation of uncertainty and ambiguity leads to the emergence of mismatches or anomalies. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Mismatches occur between those actions the opponent believes threaten their survival and those which actually do. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Going into the OODA loop, mismatches feed back into the system to create an increase in mismatch in the next OODA cycle. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Repeated and reinforced mismatches lead to a yawning gap between perception and reality of the strategic environment. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]

## Inferences

- Inference: The self-reinforcing nature of mismatches explains why Boyd emphasizes speed and adaptation—once an adversary begins experiencing mismatches, they tend to compound unless they can break the cycle through successful adaptation.
