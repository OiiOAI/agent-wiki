# Sit-on-Your-Ass Investing

## Summary

Sit-on-Your-Ass Investing is Munger's humorous term for the patience-based strategy of making few investments and holding them for very long periods. The approach minimizes transaction costs, taxes, and the cognitive burden of constant decision-making while allowing compound interest to work over extended time horizons.

## Key facts

- Munger calls his approach 'sit-on-your-ass investing' and cites its benefits: lower brokerage costs, less nonsense listened to, and tax advantages of 1-3 percentage points per annum. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p66]
- Munger states: 'If you buy something because it's undervalued, then you have to think about selling it when it approaches your calculation of intrinsic value—that's hard. But if you buy a few great companies, then you can sit on your ass—that's a good thing'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p86]
- Einstein called compound interest 'the eighth wonder of the world'—Munger advises never to interrupt it unnecessarily. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p83]
- Munger says a successful investment career boils down to only a handful of decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p66]
