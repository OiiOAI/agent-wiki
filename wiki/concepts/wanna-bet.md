# Wanna Bet?

## Summary

The phrase 'Wanna bet?' is presented as a powerful tool for belief scrutiny. When someone challenges us to bet on a belief, it triggers us to vet that belief—to inventory the evidence, question sources, and consider alternatives. This engages the 'third step' of belief formation (vetting) that we normally skip. The challenge makes explicit that we are betting on our beliefs with our happiness, attention, health, money, or time.

## Key facts

- When challenged with 'Wanna bet?', we are forced to ask: How do I know this? Who did I get it from? What is the quality of my sources? What other plausible alternatives exist?. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L770-L780]
- Being asked if willing to bet money makes it more likely we will examine information in a less biased way and be more open to updating beliefs. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L770-L775]
- The person who wins bets over the long run is the one with the more accurate beliefs. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L768-L770]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book argues that even without actually placing bets, training ourselves to think 'Wanna bet?' about our beliefs brings out risk explicitly and makes us more comfortable with uncertainty.
