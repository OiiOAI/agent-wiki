# Ventral and Dorsal Visual Pathways

## Summary

The visual cortex contains two major processing streams: the ventral pathway extending from primary visual cortex to the temporal lobe for object recognition, and the dorsal pathway projecting to the parietal lobe for visually guided movement. These pathways were described by Leslie Ungerleider and Mortimer Mishkin and are sometimes called the 'what' and 'where' pathways.

## Key facts

- The ventral or object-recognition pathway extends from primary visual cortex to the temporal lobe. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p614]
- The dorsal or movement-guidance pathway connects primary visual cortex with the parietal lobe and then the frontal lobes for visuomotor integration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p615]
- Macaque monkeys have more than 30 functionally discrete visual areas; humans likely have at least as many, with almost half of the cerebral cortex involved with vision. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p614]
- The pathways are interconnected so that movement information from the dorsal pathway can contribute to object recognition through kinematic cues in the ventral pathway. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p615]
- Feedback connections provide information about cognitive functions including spatial attention, stimulus expectation, and emotional content to earlier levels of visual processing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p615]

## Inferences

- Inference: The separation of 'what' and 'where' pathways is functional rather than absolute, with significant cross-talk between streams.
