# Focus on Now

## Summary

Focus on Now is a meditation technique where practitioners allow their attention to go wherever it wants and simply notice whatever arises. Unlike focused attention meditation, this practice involves no effort to control where awareness rests. It is designed to reduce Default Mode Network activity and induce a flow state through effortless presence in the present moment.

## Key facts

- In Focus on Now, practitioners allow attention to go wherever it wants and just notice whatever comes up without trying to direct attention. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p209]
- The key difference from ordinary mind wandering is that you pay close attention to whatever object awareness is resting on at the moment. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p208]
- Because it doesn't require keeping attention on a specific object, Focus on Now feels effortless. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p208]
- The less effort expended in meditation, the more powerfully Focus on Now switches off the DMN and induces flow states. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p207]
- Even letting go of one percent of effort is a good start; the goal is to let go of as much effort as possible. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p210]
- Focus on Now can be framed as a strong acceptance meditation because sitting and allowing almost anything to unfold is a deeply accepting stance. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p210]

## Inferences

- Inference: Focus on Now may be more effective than focused attention meditation for inducing flow states because it bypasses the effortful control that can prevent effortless states.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The optimal balance between attention and effortlessness varies between individuals.
