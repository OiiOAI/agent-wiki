# Proto-Intentionality

## Summary

Proto-intentionality refers to the goal-directed behaviour that emerges in self-organising systems equipped with subjective values. Such systems 'do whatever is necessary' to maintain valuable states (those that minimise free energy), exhibiting a primitive form of intentionality or 'aboutness' before consciousness arises.

## Key facts

- Stage 1 systems will have subjective values: their valuable states from their own point of view will be states that minimise free energy, and they will do whatever is necessary to maintain those states. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p258]
- The proto-intentionality of Stage 1 systems will be relatively easy to verify as it can be inferred from observable behaviour. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p258]
- With increasing complexity, the system increasingly has to prioritise intentions flexibly and on a contextual basis, holding one intention 'in mind' to guide unfolding choices. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p259]

## Inferences

- Inference: Proto-intentionality is a necessary precondition for consciousness; the step to consciousness occurs when proto-intentional states become felt rather than merely processed.
