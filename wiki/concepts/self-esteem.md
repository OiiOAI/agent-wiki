# Self-Esteem

## Summary

One's unconscious perception of their own value or self-worth. Confidence in sexual interactions is one component of having high self-esteem.

## Key facts

- Self-Esteem is defined as one's unconscious perception of their own value or self-worth. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
- Confidence in sexual interactions is one component of having high self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
- For the sake of clarity, this book uses the term 'non-neediness' in place of self-esteem in most cases to express the relational component of self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
