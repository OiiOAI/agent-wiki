# Hero as Lover

## Summary

The hero as lover is the archetype wherein the hegemony wrested from the enemy, freedom won from malice of the monster, and life energy released from the tyrant is symbolized as a woman—the maiden of the dragon slayings, the bride abducted from the jealous father, the virgin rescued from the unholy lover. The difficult task as prerequisite to the bridal bed has spun the hero-deeds of all time.

## Key facts

- The maiden is the 'other portion' of the hero himself—for 'each is both': if his stature is that of world monarch she is the world, and if he is a warrior she is fame. She is the image of his destiny which he is to release from the prison of enveloping circumstance. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p267]
- The motif of the difficult task as prerequisite to the bridal bed has spun the hero-deeds of all time; in stories of this pattern the parent is in the role of Holdfast, and the hero's solution of the task amounts to a slaying of the dragon. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p269]
- Barriers, fetters, chasms, fronts of every kind dissolve before the authoritative presence of the hero. The eye of the ordained victor immediately perceives the chink in every fortress of circumstance, and his blow can cleave it wide. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p269]
- The unique invisible path opened to the hero with the rolling of the wheel and the apple symbolizes the miracle of destiny: to a man courageously responding to the dynamics of his own nature—'a wheel rolling of itself'—difficulties melt and the unpredictable highway opens as he goes. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p270]

## Inferences

- Inference: The hero as lover represents the psychological integration of opposites—the hero must find and unite with the complementary aspect of himself that he has been separated from, whether by circumstance, by the malevolence of others, or by his own ignorance.
