# Target Allocation Percentages (TAPs)

## Summary

Target Allocation Percentages (TAPs) are the recommended percentages of Real Revenue that businesses should allocate to Profit, Owner's Compensation, Taxes, and Operating Expenses. The percentages vary based on company revenue tiers, with six tiers ranging from under $250,000 to $10-50 million, reflecting the different cost structures and ownership structures at each stage.

## Key facts

- TAPs are organized into six revenue tiers ranging from under $250K to $10M-$50M. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p83-86]
- Companies under $250,000 in revenue typically have one main employee (the owner) and can allocate higher percentages to profit. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p84]
- Companies between $1M-$5M require mandatory systems investment as the founder can no longer manage everything mentally. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p85]
- At $10M-$50M, a business stabilizes and achieves predictable growth with the founder's income almost entirely from profit distributions. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p86]
- TAPs are derived from surveys and evaluations of approximately one thousand of the most fiscally elite companies, across all industries and of all sizes, as well as an analysis of thousands of companies who have implemented Profit First. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p93]
- TAPs are targets you are moving toward, not your starting point. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p93]
- Henry Ford said: 'If you think you can or think you can't, you're right.' Entrepreneurs should be optimistic when assuming profit capabilities. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p93]
- Three methods to determine specific Profit TAPs: 1) Research public companies' income statements, 2) Review tax returns for most profitable year by percentage, 3) Pick percentage based on projected revenue. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p95]

## Inferences

- Inference: TAPs serve as the 'X on the map' - the destination that guides business financial decisions.
