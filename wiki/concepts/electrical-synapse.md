# Electrical Synapse

## Summary

Electrical synapses are specialized neuronal connections that enable direct cytoplasmic communication between neurons through gap junction channels, providing rapid, near-instantaneous signal transmission without synaptic delay.

## Key facts

- Electrical synapses are formed at gap junctions where the separation between neurons is approximately 4 nm, much less than the normal 20 nm nonsynaptic space. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- A gap junction channel consists of a pair of hemichannels (connexons), one from each apposed cell, forming a continuous bridge between the two cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- Each connexon is composed of six identical subunits called connexins, with connexin 36 being the most common in mammalian neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- The pore of gap junction channels has a diameter of approximately 1.5 nm, allowing inorganic ions and small organic molecules up to 1 kDa to pass between cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- Electrical transmission is graded and occurs even when presynaptic current is below the threshold for an action potential, unlike chemical transmission which requires threshold to be reached. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- Most electrical synapses can transmit both depolarizing and hyperpolarizing currents bidirectionally, though some specialized junctions called rectifying synapses permit current flow in only one direction. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p245]

## Inferences

- Inference: The large pore diameter of gap junctions allows not only ions but also second messengers like IP3, cAMP, and even small peptides to pass between cells, enabling metabolic signaling between coupled cells.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise molecular mechanisms by which neurotransmitters modulate gap junction opening through intracellular metabolic reactions are not fully detailed in this section.
