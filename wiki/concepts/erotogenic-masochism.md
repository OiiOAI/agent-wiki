# Erotogenic Masochism

## Summary

The primary, physiological form of masochism in which pleasure arises from pain. It provides the foundation for feminine and moral masochism and is understood as a remainder of the phase when the death drive became fused with Eros.

## Key facts

- Erotogenic masochism lies at the bottom of both feminine and moral masochism. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6045]
- Pain and unpleasure generate sexual excitation as a concomitant effect when their intensity passes certain quantitative limits. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6047]
- In organisms, the libido tames the death drive by diverting it outwards toward objects; the portion that remains inside becomes erotogenic masochism. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6047]
- Erotogenic masochism is evidence of the coalescence between the death drive and Eros that is so important for life. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6048]
- Secondary masochism is produced when sadism directed outward is introjected back inward. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6048]

## Inferences

- Inference: Erotogenic masochism represents the internalized portion of primal sadism that could not be tamed by admixtures of libido.
