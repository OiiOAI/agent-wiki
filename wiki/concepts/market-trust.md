# Market Trust: The Principle of Reputation

## Summary

Market Trust is the level of trust established with external stakeholders (customers, suppliers, distributors, investors) through brand and reputation. A brand can be understood as 'trust monetized'—the direct connection between trust, speed, and cost in the marketplace.

## Key facts

- A brand is defined as 'trust with the customer' or 'trust monetized,' representing the economic value of trust in marketplace relationships. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p316]
- According to a Golin/Harris poll, 39% of individuals would start or increase business with a company because of trust, 53% would stop or switch business due to trust concerns, and 83% would give trusted companies the benefit of the doubt. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p319]
- The Edelman Trust Barometer shows at least 64% of opinion leaders had refused to buy from companies they did not trust, criticizing them to others, refusing business, and refusing investment. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p320]
- Country of origin affects brand trust; companies from Mexico, India, Brazil, and China sometimes pay a trust tax while those from Canada, Switzerland, Sweden, and Australia often receive a dividend. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p320]
- Financial services and media industries generally receive a trust tax while technology and education sectors typically receive dividends in most countries. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p321]
- Warren Buffett stated: 'It takes twenty years to build a reputation and five minutes to ruin it,' illustrating how quickly brand trust can be destroyed despite slow accumulation. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p322]
- Domino's Pizza demonstrated Market Trust behaviors: confronting reality about poor product quality, talking straight by acknowledging problems on national TV, and delivering results by redesigning product and process, resulting in stock price rising from $2.61 to nearly $300. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p323]
