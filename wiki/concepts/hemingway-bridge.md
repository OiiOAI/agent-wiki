# Hemingway Bridge

## Summary

The Hemingway Bridge is a creative strategy where you end a work session only when you know what comes next, creating a bridge to the next session. This preserves momentum and makes resuming work easier. The technique involves writing down next steps, current status, potential forgotten details, and intentions for the next session.

## Key facts

- Ernest Hemingway would always end a writing session only when he knew what came next in the story. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p162]
- The Hemingway Bridge involves writing down ideas for next steps at the end of a work session. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p163]
- The technique preserves today's energy and momentum to fuel tomorrow's work. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p163]
