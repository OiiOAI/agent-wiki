# Strategy Foundry

## Summary

The Strategy Foundry is a methodology developed by Richard Rumelt for small groups of senior executives to do challenge-based strategy work, discover the crux of organizational challenges, and create coherent actions to address them. It is distinct from traditional strategic planning or budget-setting workshops.

## Key facts

- The Strategy Foundry is challenge-based, centered on identifying the key challenge facing the organization rather than setting performance goals or choosing among predetermined alternatives. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p298]
- A Strategy Foundry should consist of fewer than ten senior executives, preferably less than eight, and must include the leader of the company or business division. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p270]
- The foundry works best off-site and typically takes two to five consecutive days. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p270]
- The foundry should be held separately from any budgeting process to avoid conflating strategy with financial forecasting. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p283]
- An outside facilitator can help people speak more frankly and treat the boss as just another participant. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p284]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Strategy Foundry represents a fundamental shift from goal-based to challenge-based strategic thinking, emphasizing diagnosis and action over financial projections.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact optimal size and composition beyond the recommendation of fewer than ten senior executives.
