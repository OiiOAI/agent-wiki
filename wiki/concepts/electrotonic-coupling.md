# Electrotonic Coupling

## Summary

Electrotonic coupling is a form of neuronal communication where neurons form direct electrical bridges called gap junctions, allowing direct ionic current flow between cells. This contrasts with chemical synapses where transmission occurs via neurotransmitter diffusion across a 20-nanometer synaptic cleft. Electrotonic coupling is the oldest form of communication among neurons, allowing groups of neurons to oscillate in phase as a singular ensemble. During development, electrotonic coupling enables coordinated activity before chemical synapses mature.

## Key facts

- Electrotonic coupling forms gap junction channels that create electrically conductive bridges between neurons, in contrast to the 20-nanometer synaptic cleft of chemical synapses. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p93]
- Electrical resonance supported by direct electrical connectivity among cells is perhaps the oldest form of communication among neurons, later enhanced by chemical synaptic transmission. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p18]
- During development, motor neurons are electrotonically coupled until upper parts of the nervous system begin making synaptic connections, after which motor neurons become electrotonically decoupled. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p66]
- Gap junction channels allow conduction of ions directly between cells, making them electrically coupled without neurotransmitter involvement. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p96]
- Electrotonic coupling produces very little delay compared to chemical transmission, which takes 1-5 milliseconds due to transmitter release, diffusion, binding, and channel activation. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p96]
- Many gap junctions regulate current flow in a bidirectional manner, allowing cells of the inferior olive to function almost as one big cell electrically. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p97]
- Gap junction channels are roughly 1.5 nm in diameter, allowing ions, small peptides, and molecules like cyclic AMP to pass from cell to cell. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p98]
- Electrotonic coupling plays a significant role in developmental processes, allowing larger regulatory molecules to flow between developing neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p98]

## Inferences

- Inference: Electrotonic coupling represents a primitive but evolutionarily important form of neuronal communication that predates chemical synapses and enables rapid, synchronous signaling essential for certain developmental and functional processes.
