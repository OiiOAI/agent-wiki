# Intermediate Packets

## Summary

Intermediate Packets (IPs) are the concrete, individual building blocks that make up creative work. They include distilled notes, outtakes, work-in-process, final deliverables, and documents created by others. Treating work as reusable Intermediate Packets rather than one-off tasks enables knowledge workers to build compounding assets over time.

## Key facts

- Intermediate Packets are abbreviated as IPs, a term that represents Intellectual Property. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p151]
- The five kinds of Intermediate Packets are: distilled notes, outtakes, work-in-process, final deliverables, and documents created by others. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p135]
- Intermediate Packets make users interruption-proof by focusing on one small packet at a time. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p136]
- Intermediate Packets increase the quality of work by allowing collection of feedback more often. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p136]
