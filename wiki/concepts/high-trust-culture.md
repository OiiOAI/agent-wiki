# High-Trust Culture

## Summary

High-trust culture refers to organizational environments where trust is the foundational element that enables all other positive cultural attributes. Organizations with high-trust cultures differentiate themselves, compete effectively, and attract talented people whose values align with the organization.

## Key facts

- Culture has reemerged as an imperative for organizational success, with leaders rallying around intentional focus on building their culture. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p391]
- If organizations don't have the foundation of a high-trust culture, all other efforts are insufficient. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p391]
- A high-trust culture is the starting point for any other sustainable cultural modifier. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p391]
- Organizations that create a culture of trust are winning in the marketplace and are organizations and teams that talented people compete to be part of. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p391]
- The Great Place to Work Institute's research shows that trust between managers and employees is the primary defining characteristic of the very best workplaces. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p381]
