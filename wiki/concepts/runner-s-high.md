# Runner's High

## Summary

Runner's high is a euphoric state experienced after exercise that shares similarities with drug or trance states, including distorted perception, atypical thought patterns, diminished awareness of surroundings, and intensified introspective understanding. The phenomenon involves endocannabinoids (anandamide and 2-AG) which pass easily across the blood-brain barrier and produce effects similar to marijuana. Studies show that anandamide levels nearly double after 50 minutes of exercise at 70-80% of maximum heart rate.

- Endocannabinoids pass easily across the blood-brain barrier, making them a more plausible explanation for runner's high than endorphins. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p150]
- A 2003 study showed anandamide nearly doubled in fit male college students after 50 minutes of running or cycling at 70-80% of maximum heart rate. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p150]
- Endocannabinoids and endorphins act as the body's extra-strength aspirin, blocking pain signals in the spinal cord. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p150]
