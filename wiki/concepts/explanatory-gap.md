# Explanatory Gap

## Summary

The explanatory gap is the philosophical and scientific problem of how consciousness and subjective experience are related to the brain and body. While we can establish correlations between consciousness and brain activity, we lack an account explaining how biological processes generate and realize consciousness. Cognitivism perpetuated this gap in materialist form by creating a new 'mind-mind problem' between computational cognition and phenomenological mind. The enactive approach aims to make progress by going back to the roots of mind in life—understanding subjectivity through biological autonomy.

## Key facts

- The explanatory gap is the problem of exactly how consciousness and subjective experience are related to the brain and body. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p8]
- It is one thing to establish correlations between consciousness and brain activity; it is another to have an account explaining how biological processes generate consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p8]
- Cognitivism perpetuated the explanatory gap in materialist form by opening a new gap between subpersonal computational cognition and subjective mental phenomena. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p17]
- The mind-mind problem is the problem of the relation between the computational mind and the phenomenological mind. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p18]
- The mind-mind problem is a version of what is now known as the 'hard problem of consciousness.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p18]

## Inferences

- Inference: The enactive approach aims to address the explanatory gap by enlarging and enriching philosophical and scientific resources rather than closing it reductively.
