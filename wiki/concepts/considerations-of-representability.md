# Considerations of Representability

## Summary

Considerations of representability is a factor in dream formation whereby abstract thoughts are transformed into pictorial images that can be visually represented in dreams. Freud identified that the dream-work recasts unadaptable thoughts into new verbal forms to facilitate representation.

## Key facts

- Considerations of representability favor those subsidiary thoughts that admit of visual representation, and the dream-work recasts unadaptable thoughts into new verbal forms. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p344]
- The transformation of abstract thought into pictures serves condensation and censorship, creating connections through concrete terms which have richer associations than conceptual ones. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p340]
- Words, being nodal points of numerous ideas and capable of ambiguity, are put at the disposal of the dream-work for purposes of condensation and disguise. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p341]
- Silberer demonstrated that when fatigued, thoughts escaping can be replaced by pictures serving as 'autosymbolic' substitutes. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p345]

## Inferences

- Inference: The transformation from abstract to pictorial may also serve condensation by finding verbal forms that are ambiguous and express multiple dream thoughts.
