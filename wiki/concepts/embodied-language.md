# Embodied Language

## Summary

McGilchrist contends that language is not a theoretical system made up of bits strung together by rules, but an embodied skill originating in the empathic communication medium of music and the right hemisphere. Every word eventually leads out of the web of language to the lived world, ultimately to something relating to our embodied existence. The deep structure of syntax is founded on the fixed sequences of limb movement in running creatures.

## Key facts

- Language is not a theoretical system or set of procedures made up of bits strung together by rules, but an embodied skill. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p174]
- The origins of language lie in the empathic communication medium of music and the right hemisphere, where it is deeply connected with the body. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p174]
- Even words such as 'virtual' or 'immaterial' take us back in their Latin derivation to the earthy realities of a man's strength (vir-tus), or the feel of a piece of wood (materia). [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p171]
- The 'kiki/bouba' effect demonstrates that the sounds of words are not arbitrary but evocative in a synaesthetic way of the experience of things they refer to. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p175]
- The deep structure of syntax is founded on the fixed sequences of limb movement in running creatures. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p175]
- The very structure of reason comes from the details of our embodiment - abstract reason builds on forms of perceptual and motor inference present in 'lower' animals. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p175]

## Inferences

- Inference: Language acquisition by children occurs through imitation, a form of empathic identification with those from whom they learn, not through learning explicit rules.
