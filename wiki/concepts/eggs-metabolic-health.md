# Eggs and Metabolic Health

## Summary

Eggs, particularly whole eggs, improve lipoprotein profiles and insulin sensitivity more effectively than yolk-free egg substitutes in individuals with metabolic syndrome. Egg yolks contain lutein and zeaxanthin, carotenoids important for eye and brain health, and provide essential nutrients that support cardiovascular and metabolic health.

## Key facts

- Whole egg consumption improves lipoprotein profiles and insulin sensitivity to a greater extent than yolk-free egg substitute in individuals with metabolic syndrome. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p464]
- Egg yolk consumption increases plasma lutein and zeaxanthin concentrations. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p464]
- Eggs are designated as 'Genius Food #5' in the book for their comprehensive health benefits. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p464]

## Inferences

- Inference: The beneficial effects of whole eggs compared to yolk-free substitutes suggest that nutrients in the yolk, including carotenoids and fats, provide important health benefits beyond protein.
