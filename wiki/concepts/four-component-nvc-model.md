# Four-Component NVC Model

## Summary

The Four-Component NVC Model structures compassionate communication through four sequential elements: (1) observations of what is actually happening without evaluation, (2) feelings experienced in relation to those observations, (3) needs or values connected to those feelings, and (4) specific requests for actions that would enrich life. The model guides users to express themselves honestly while simultaneously paying respectful and empathic attention to others, creating a flow of communication until compassion manifests naturally.

## Key facts

- The first component is observing what is actually happening—articulating what others are doing that is either enriching or not enriching our life—without introducing judgment or evaluation. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p31]
- The second component is stating how we feel when we observe this action—whether hurt, scared, joyful, amused, or irritated. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p31]
- The third component is articulating what needs of ours are connected to the feelings we have identified. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p31]
- The fourth component is making a very specific request that addresses what we are wanting from the other person that would enrich our lives. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p31-32]
- Example: 'Felix, when I see two balls of soiled socks under the coffee table and another three next to the TV, I feel irritated because I am needing more order in the rooms that we share in common. Would you be willing to put your socks in your room or in the washing machine?'. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p31]
- NVC consists of two parts: expressing honestly through the four components and receiving empathically through the four components. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p32]
- The NVC process can be experienced without uttering a word; the essence is in the consciousness of the four components, not in the actual words exchanged. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p32]
