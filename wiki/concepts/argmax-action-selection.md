# Argmax action selection

## Summary

Argmax is the most common deterministic action selection mechanism used in economics, predicting that a subject will always choose the option with the greatest subjective desirability. Formally: chosen = arg max_i(D_i). It offers the advantages of simplicity and clear interpretability. However, subjects rarely choose the 'best' alternative in a deterministic manner, which motivates the use of probabilistic selection criteria. Many models incorporate argmax as an implicit action selection stage for the sake of simplicity, though there is no neurobiological requirement for it — probabilistic mechanisms like softmax can subsume argmax-like behavior at high steepness.
