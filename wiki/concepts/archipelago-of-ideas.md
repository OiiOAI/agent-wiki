# Archipelago of Ideas

## Summary

The Archipelago of Ideas is a technique for planning creative work by gathering a group of ideas, sources, or points that form the backbone of a project, then linking them together in a logical order. Named after Steven Johnson's quote comparing the technique to building bridges between islands, it separates the activities of selecting ideas (divergence) and arranging them (convergence).

## Key facts

- The technique is named after a quote by Steven Johnson about starting chapters as an archipelago of inspiring quotes. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p159]
- An archipelago separates selection (divergent) from sequencing (convergent). [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p162]
