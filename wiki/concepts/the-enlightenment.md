# The Enlightenment

## Summary

The Enlightenment was a period of remarkable intellectual development in the 17th and 18th centuries characterized by emphasis on reason, empirical research, and the belief that people could comprehend and control the universe. Key thinkers included Montesquieu and Rousseau. Early sociology developed largely as a reaction to the Enlightenment.

## Key facts

- The Enlightenment was characterized by the belief that people could comprehend and control the universe by means of reason and empirical research. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p36]
- Enlightenment thinkers wanted to combine empirical research with reason, using science as their model. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p36]
- Early sociology developed as a reaction to the Enlightenment, not directly from it. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p36]
