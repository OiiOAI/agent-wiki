# Trust Restoration

## Summary

Trust restoration is possible when trust has been lost, though it requires increasing personal credibility and behaving in ways that inspire trust. Violations of character (Integrity or Intent) are more difficult to restore than violations of competence (Capabilities or Results). The process applies to all five waves of trust.

## Key facts

- The claim that trust cannot be restored once lost is identified as a myth; though difficult, lost trust can usually be restored and often enhanced through the challenge of restoration. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p361]
- Violations of character (Integrity or Intent) are far more difficult to restore than violations of competence (Capabilities or Results); violations of Integrity are most difficult of all in all relationships. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p363]
- Nike restored Market Trust by Phil Knight acknowledging problems, demonstrating tangible results, and committing to elevate industry conditions, resulting in annual placement on the '100 Best Corporate Citizens' list since 2005. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p366]
- Nelson Mandela demonstrated forgiveness by inviting his jailers to the front row at his inaugural address as an illustration of forgiveness needed to heal his nation. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p374]
- Gandhi stated 'The weak can never forgive. Forgiveness is the attribute of the strong,' emphasizing that forgiveness enables clear judgment and emotional freedom for Smart Trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p374]
- Forgiveness and trust are different: forgiveness cleanses feelings of anger and vindictiveness while trust decisions remain separate, requiring Smart Trust analysis. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p374]
