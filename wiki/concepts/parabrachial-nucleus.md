# Parabrachial Nucleus

## Summary

A nucleus located in the pons that serves as a major relay for visceral and somatic information and has extensive connections with forebrain structures. It plays important roles in autonomic regulation and may be a key structure in the neural correlates of consciousness.

## Key facts

- Severe pathological changes occur in the parabrachial nucleus in Alzheimer's disease. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p361]
- Alzheimer pathology in the parabrachial nucleus may be related to autonomic dysfunction in these patients and possibly to their disproportionate incidence of respiratory and gastroenteric disease. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p361]
