# Jerry Seinfeld's Chain Method

## Summary

A productivity technique using a wall calendar showing the whole year. Break work into daily chunks, and each day you complete work, mark an X. The goal is to not break the chain—the visual chain of X's becomes motivating.

## Key facts

- Jerry Seinfeld's method involves getting a wall calendar showing the whole year and marking a big X each day you finish your work. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p89]
- Seinfeld says, 'After a few days you'll have a chain. Just keep at it and the chain will grow longer every day. Your only job next is to not break the chain.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p89]
