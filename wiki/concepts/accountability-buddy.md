# Accountability Buddy

## Summary

An accountability buddy or group is a support system for implementing Profit First consistently. Michalowicz emphasizes that 'going it alone' is the biggest mistake entrepreneurs make. Research from Camp Widjiwagan demonstrates that peer accountability dramatically improves goal achievement.

## Key facts

- Michalowicz states: 'Going it alone is the biggest mistake (Mistake #1) entrepreneurs make when implementing Profit First.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p196]
- The author cites four benefits of accountability groups: skyrocketing sticktoitiveness, diminished pain through shared experience, enforced action, and rhythm formation. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p198]
- The anecdote from Camp Widjiwagan shows campers achieved zero food waste through peer accountability, dropping from several pounds per meal to zero. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p198]
