# Silberer's Functional Phenomenon

## Summary

Herbert Silberer described 'functional phenomena' observed during experiments with drowsiness—cases where an image represented the fatigue itself, the difficulty of the intellectual work, or the process of falling asleep or waking, rather than the object of the thoughts. These represent a second contribution of waking thought to dream construction. Silberer noted that threshold symbolism (crossing thresholds, leaving rooms, departure, homecoming) often represents the intention to wake or the process of waking.

## Key facts

- Silberer's functional phenomenon represents a state instead of an object—the subjective mode of functioning rather than the object of efforts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1747]
- Silberer observed that in many dreams the last pieces of manifest content, immediately followed by waking, represent nothing more than an intention to wake or the process of waking. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1749]
- Threshold symbolism—crossing a threshold, leaving one room and entering another, departure, homecoming, parting with a companion, diving into water—often represents the intention to wake or the process of waking. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1749]
- Silberer's functional phenomenon constitutes a second contribution of waking thought to the construction of dreams, distinct from secondary revision. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1750]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud notes that he has come across threshold symbolism far less frequently than Silberer's communications would have led one to expect, and that convincing instances of functional phenomena in the middle of dream texture have not been produced.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The full validity and frequency of Silberer's functional phenomenon in ordinary dreams remains uncertain; Freud found fewer instances in his own and his patients' dreams than expected.
