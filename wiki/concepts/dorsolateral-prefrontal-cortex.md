# Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex

## Summary

The dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) is a brain region implicated in cognitive control and executive functions. In the context of social preferences, research using TMS and tDCS has shown that disruption of right DLPFC activity reduces the ability to implement fairness motives, increasing acceptance of unfair offers in ultimatum games while not affecting fairness judgments themselves. This suggests DLPFC is crucial for the behavioral implementation of fairness rather than the evaluation of fairness.

## Key facts

- Low-frequency TMS of right DLPFC increases acceptance rates of unfair offers from 9% to 44% relative to sham stimulation, while left DLPFC TMS leaves acceptance rates unaffected. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p213]
- TMS to right DLPFC fails to affect fairness judgments: subjects continue to view low offers as very unfair but accept them at a much higher rate. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p214]
- Response time data show that TMS to right DLPFC eliminates the conflict between self-interest and fairness: subjects accept unfair offers as quickly as fair offers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p214]
- The effect of right DLPFC TMS is strongly mitigated when computer-generated low offers are used instead of human-proposed offers, suggesting DLPFC is specifically recruited for implementing fairness motives with human partners. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p213]

## Inferences

- Inference: Right DLPFC appears to be specifically involved in the cognitive control of impulses to accept unfair offers, not in the evaluation of fairness itself
- Inference: The cognitive control role of DLPFC in fairness decisions may be one component of a broader executive function system involved in overriding selfish impulses for norm-compliant behavior
