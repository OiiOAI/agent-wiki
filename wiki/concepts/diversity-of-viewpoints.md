# Diversity of Viewpoints

## Summary

Diversity of viewpoints refers to the inclusion of people with different perspectives, ideologies, and experiences in decision-making groups. It is essential for productive group reasoning because it exposes blind spots, challenges biased thinking, and helps groups move toward accuracy. Without diversity, groups drift toward homogeneity and echo chambers.

## Key facts

- If you put individuals together in the right way, such that some individuals can use their reasoning powers to disconfirm the claims of others, you can create a group that ends up producing good reasoning as an emergent property of the social system. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1235-L1245]
- Nobody has found a way to eradicate confirmation bias in individuals, but we can diversify the field to the point where individual viewpoint biases begin to cancel out each other. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1440-L1445]
- Peer review offers much less protection against error when the community of peers is politically homogeneous. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1430-L1435]
- The diversity of viewpoints provided by a well-formed decision pod is nearly impossible to replicate on one's own. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1340-L1345]
