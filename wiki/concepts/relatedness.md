# Relatedness

## Summary

Relatedness is identified by Deci and Ryan as a third, innate psychological need beyond autonomy and competence. It refers to the psychological desire to feel connected with others—to love and be loved, to care and be cared for. The authors argue against the common misconception that autonomy and relatedness are contradictory, noting that when people become more authentic and develop greater capacity for autonomous self-regulation, they also become capable of deeper relatedness to others. This need is essential for understanding human motivation as part of a broader picture of psychological well-being.
