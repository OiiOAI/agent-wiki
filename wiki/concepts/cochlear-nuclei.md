# Cochlear Nuclei

## Summary

The cochlear nuclei are the first relay station in the central auditory pathway, located in the brain stem. They consist of the ventral cochlear nucleus (divided into anteroventral and posteroventral parts) and the dorsal cochlear nucleus. Different cell types within these nuclei—including bushy cells, stellate cells, octopus cells, and fusiform cells—extract specific features of acoustic information including temporal structure, spectral content, and sound localization cues.

## Key facts

- The cochlear nuclei consist of an unlayered ventral cochlear nucleus and a layered dorsal cochlear nucleus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p738]
- Cochlear nerve fibers terminate in the cochlear nuclei in a tonotopic organization: fibers encoding low frequencies terminate ventrally, those encoding higher frequencies terminate dorsally. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p738]
- Bushy cells convey sharply tuned but less temporally precise information and project to the superior olivary complex for sound localization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p740]
- Octopus cells detect synchronous firing in cochlear nerve fibers and require summation of the rising phases of numerous synaptic inputs. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p740]
- Stellate cells provide a continuous representation of the spectrum of sound energy and track modulations in intensity on a timescale important for understanding speech. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p741]
