# Emotion Skills

## Summary

Emotion skills are the abilities to recognize, understand, label, express, and regulate emotions effectively. They are distinct from personality traits, emotional stability, grit, resilience, confidence, or warmth—though they can enhance all of these. Emotion skills must be acquired; no one is born with them fully developed. Research shows they predict academic success, relationship quality, health outcomes, and leadership ability, and can be learned through brief interventions.

## Key facts

- Emotion skills are distinct from personality traits such as confidence or charisma, and from emotional stability or neuroticism. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p50-51]
- Emotion skills are not grit, which is perseverance for long-term goals, though the two work together to support goal achievement. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p50]
- Emotion skills are likely the antecedent to building resilience, according to research by Tom Boyce and colleagues. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p51]
- Young school-age children with more developed emotion skills have fewer conduct problems, are better adjusted, and perform better academically. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p60]
- Among adolescents, higher emotional intelligence is associated with less depression and anxiety and may be a protective factor against suicidal behavior. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p60]
- Studies found that athletes who participated in ten three-hour workshops had significantly higher emotional intelligence scores post-test than peers in control groups. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p61]
