# Relationship Misconceptions

## Summary

The book's epilogue discusses three common misconceptions about romantic relationships that can lead people to stay in unsatisfying partnerships or have unrealistic expectations.

## Key facts

- The first misconception is that everyone has the same capacity for intimacy, when in fact people have very different capacities for intimacy. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p205]
- The second misconception is that marriage is the be-all and end-all, with the belief that when someone gets married it proves the power of love to transform them. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p205]
- The third misconception is that we alone are responsible for our emotional needs and that they are not our partner's responsibility. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p206]
- The authors note that mismatched attachment styles can lead to a great deal of unhappiness in marriage, even for people who love each other greatly. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p206]
