# Anhedonia in Schizophrenia

## Summary

Anhedonia in schizophrenia—affecting an estimated 33–50% of patients—appears to reflect a deficit in anticipatory pleasure and motivation rather than impaired consummatory pleasure. This aligns with reinforcement learning models in which dysfunction in the mesolimbic dopamine system impairs the ability to represent and act toward future rewards.

## Key facts

- Anhedonia affects an estimated 33–50% of people with schizophrenia. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p260]
- Gard et al. found that schizophrenia patients showed comparable consummatory pleasure but reduced anticipatory pleasure compared to controls, using experience-sampling and the Temporal Experience of Pleasure Scale (TEPS). [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p261]
- Anhedonia in schizophrenia may be a failure to anticipate rather than experience pleasure, consistent with a deficit in reward prediction rather than consummatory reward processing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p261]
- Schizophrenia patients may fail to generate or use positive-prediction errors for positive outcomes, producing a 'reinforcement learning recipe' for anhedonia and avolition. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p261]

## Inferences

- Inference: onsummatory pleasure is intact in schizophrenia but anticipatory pleasure is impaired, motivational deficits may be secondary to deficits in generating and updating mental representations of value, rather than a primary hedonic deficit.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Results using the TEPS have not been consistent across studies; some studies found deficits in both anticipatory and consummatory pleasure.
- Uncertain: The extent to which anhedonia arises from antipsychotic medication (which has antidopaminergic action) as opposed to the underlying condition is unclear.
