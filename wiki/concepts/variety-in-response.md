# Variety in Response

## Summary

Variety is one of Boyd's four key qualities for successful operations. Having a repertoire of orientation patterns and responses prevents predictability and enables adaptation to varied challenges. Boyd emphasizes the importance of involving people with varying backgrounds to build variety in response.

## Key facts

- Boyd identifies initiative, harmony, variety, and rapidity as the four key qualities of successful operations. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p285]
- Having one common orientation, thought pattern, belief system or military doctrine to solve all operational contingencies will not suffice. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- To maintain variety in response, one should have variety built into the making of orientation patterns by involving people with varying backgrounds. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- The way to maintain variety is to confront people with varying situations to build trust and variety in response and communication. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Multiple and various actions simultaneously or during non-standard intervals prevent becoming predictable. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]

## Inferences

- Inference: Variety serves both defensive purposes (maintaining one's own adaptability) and offensive purposes (preventing the opponent from predicting one's behavior).
