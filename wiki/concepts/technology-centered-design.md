# Technology-Centered Design

## Summary

Technology-Centered Design is presented in the book's table of contents as a contrasting approach to User-Centered Design. It is introduced as a design philosophy that prioritizes technological capabilities over user needs.

## Key facts

- Technology-Centered Design is identified as a contrasting approach to User-Centered Design. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#L84-L86]
- Technology-centered design involves developing displays for each system based on the technologies that produce them. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p25]
- The number of displays in aircraft cockpits increased from none for the Wright Flyer to hundreds by the 1970s. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p25]
- In technology-centered design, information is often scattered and not ideally suited to support human tasks. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p25]
- Over 44,000 deaths in the US annually are attributed to human error in the medical field, with much being 'design-induced error' rather than faulty humans. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p26]
