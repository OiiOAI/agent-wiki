# Automaticity

## Summary

Automaticity occurs when behavior and reactions become somewhat automatic through experience—the pattern-recognition/action-selection sequence becomes routinized. While automaticity frees mental effort for more demanding tasks, it can be a significant problem for SA when automaticity of mental tasks is involved. Information outside the routinized sequence might not be attended to. Checklists are emphasized in aviation to protect against shortcomings of processes that become too automatic.

## Key facts

- Automaticity occurs when pattern-recognition/action-selection sequences become routinized to the point of being automatic. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p48]
- Automaticity is advantageous to SA because it requires very little conscious attention, freeing mental resources for other tasks. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p48]
- Automaticity of mental tasks can be a problem for SA—information outside the routinized sequence might not be attended to. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p48]
- Drivers often drive right past new stop signs on well-traveled routes due to automaticity. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p48]
- Checklists in aviation protect against errors from processes that become too automatic. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p48]
