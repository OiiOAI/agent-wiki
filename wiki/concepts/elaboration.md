# Elaboration

## Summary

Elaboration is the best-researched and most successful learning method, involving thinking deeply about the meaning of what is read, how it relates to other topics, and how it connects with existing knowledge. It is essentially what happens when taking smart notes and combining them with others—the opposite of mere reviewing.

## Key facts

- Elaboration means thinking about the meaning of what we read, how it could inform different questions and topics, and how it could be combined with other knowledge. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p85]
- Writing for Learning is the name of an elaboration method. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p85]
- Elaboration is verifiably effective for deep understanding, though not necessarily the best choice for isolated encyclopaedic facts. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p85]
- Retention is facilitated by acquisition conditions that prompt people to elaborate information in a way that increases the distinctiveness of memory representations. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p98]
