# Daily Dispatch

## Summary

The practice of sharing one small piece of your creative process every day. The form can vary—blog post, email, tweet, YouTube video—but the consistency builds an ongoing narrative that shows audiences what you're working on right now, creating a living portfolio优于 traditional static resumes or portfolios.

## Key facts

- Kleon advocates focusing on days rather than decades, months, or years: 'Seasons change, weeks are completely human-made, but the day has a rhythm.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p49]
- The practice is to share one little piece of your process daily: early stages share influences, middle stages share works-in-progress, completed projects share final products or lessons learned. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p50]
- Artist Ze Frank is cited complaining that job candidates show things from school or another job, but 'I'm more interested in what they did last weekend.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p52]
- A daily dispatch is described as showing what you're working on right now, like DVD extras before a movie comes out—deleted scenes and director's commentary while the movie is being made. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p52]
- Platform recommendations: Filmmakers on YouTube/Vimeo, businesspeople on LinkedIn, writers on Twitter, visual artists on Tumblr/Instagram/Facebook. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p54]
