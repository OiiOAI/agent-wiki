# Get Better

## Summary

Behavior #7 in Covey's framework. Getting better means continuously improving, increasing capabilities, being a constant learner, developing feedback systems, and acting on feedback. It is the Japanese concept of kaizen and builds enormous trust.

## Key facts

- LEGO had a near-death experience in 2003 but chose to reinvent, focusing on building a culture of innovation; profits grew from 900M euros in 2003 to nearly 5B euros in 2017. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p223]
- Karl Malone improved from 48% free throw shooting as a rookie to 75% for his career by working hard on his weakness. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p222]
- Alvin Toffler stated 'The illiterate of the 21st century will not be those who cannot read and write but those who cannot learn, unlearn, and relearn'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p220]
