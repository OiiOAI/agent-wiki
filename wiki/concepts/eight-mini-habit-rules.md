# Eight Mini Habit Rules

## Summary

The Eight Mini Habit Rules are principles designed to ensure success with the Mini Habits system. They address common pitfalls such as cheating by secretly requiring more than the mini target, failing to celebrate small progress, and pushing through resistance instead of backing off.

## Key facts

- Rule 1: Never, Ever Cheat—do not secretly require more than the stated mini habit, as extra requirements increase willpower cost and jeopardize guaranteed success. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p116]
- Rule 2: Be Happy With All Progress—celebrate all progress using Bruce Lee's philosophy of being happy but never satisfied. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p117]
- Rule 3: Reward Yourself Often, Especially After A Mini Habit—to create positive feedback loops and 'addiction' to living a great life. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p118]
- Rule 4: Stay Level-headed—aim for a calm mindset rather than being 'pumped up,' as steady calmness is best for habit building. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p119]
- Rule 5: If You Feel Strong Resistance, Back Off & Go Smaller—use progressively smaller tasks until resistance is minimal, rather than pushing through willpower. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p120]
- Rule 6: Remind Yourself How Easy This Is—frequently remind yourself of the absurdity of not being able to meet your mini habit requirement. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p10]
- Rule 7: Never Think A Step Is Too Small—there is no step too small in the Mini Habits system. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p10]
- Rule 8: Put Extra Energy and Ambition Toward Bonus Reps, Not A Bigger Requirement—always be willing to meet your tiny requirement and go back inside. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p10]

## Inferences

- Inference: These rules collectively prevent the common failure modes of traditional habit formation: overambitious targets, lack of celebration, and willpower exhaustion.
