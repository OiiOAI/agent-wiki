# Positivism

## Summary

Positivism is a philosophical approach developed by Auguste Comte that emphasizes the application of scientific methods to the study of society. It holds that the social universe is amenable to the development of abstract laws that can be tested through careful data collection. Comte believed sociology would become the dominant science.

## Key facts

- Comte developed positivism as a philosophical system emphasizing that sociology should employ the same scientific techniques used in the natural sciences. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p43]
- Comte's positivism emphasized that the social universe is amenable to the development of abstract laws that can be tested through the careful collection of data. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p46]
