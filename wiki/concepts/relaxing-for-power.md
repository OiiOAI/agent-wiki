# Relaxing for Power

## Summary

Relaxing for power is a LET exercise demonstrating that true power comes from relaxation rather than tension. When comparing a 'resistance arm' (rigid and tense) with an 'energy arm' (relaxed but alive with visualized ki), the energy arm proves far more resilient. This has implications for physical performance and serves as a metaphor for power in all life domains.

## Key facts

- An overwhelming majority of people find the 'energy arm' far more powerful and resilient than the 'resistance arm.'. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L985-L987]
- Electromyographic measurements confirm that the energy arm is less likely to collapse than the resistance arm. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L988-L990]
