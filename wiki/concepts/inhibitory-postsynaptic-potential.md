# Inhibitory Postsynaptic Potential

## Summary

An inhibitory postsynaptic potential (IPSP) is a hyperpolarizing or shunting change in membrane potential caused by opening of ligand-gated chloride or potassium channels, moving the neuron away from action potential threshold.

## Key facts

- IPSPs in spinal motor neurons are generated by GABAergic interneurons acting through GABAA receptors that increase Cl- conductance. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p274]
- IPSPs can counteract EPSPs and prevent the membrane potential from reaching threshold for action potential generation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p275]
- IPSPs in spinal motor neurons result from opening of Cl− channels activated by GABA or glycine, with a reversal potential near −70 mV. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p273]
- When the membrane potential is at ECl (−70 mV), the IPSP is nullified; more negative potentials produce depolarizing (but still inhibitory) responses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p273]
- IPSPs can counteract EPSPs through two mechanisms: hyperpolarization (subtraction) or shunting (division) of excitatory current. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p276]
- Opening Cl− channels can prevent an EPSP from reaching threshold by increasing total membrane conductance (shunting effect). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p276]
- GABAA receptors are ionotropic Cl− channels, while GABAB receptors are metabotropic receptors that activate K+ channels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p273]
- Under some conditions, such as intense stimulation, GABAA receptors can produce depolarizing responses if intracellular Cl− accumulates sufficiently. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p276]

## Inferences

- Inference: The shunting effect represents a particularly effective form of inhibition because it reduces the effectiveness of excitatory inputs without changing membrane potential.
- Inference: hibitory inputs allow the nervous system to perform computational operations beyond simple excitation.
