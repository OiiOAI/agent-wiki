# Fore-pleasure

## Summary

Fore-pleasure is pleasure derived from the excitation of erotogenic zones during preparatory sexual activities. It differs from end-pleasure, which arises from the discharge of sexual substances and extinguishes libidinal tension. Fore-pleasure was already produced on a smaller scale during infantile sexual life. The formula for the new function of erotogenic zones at puberty is: they are used to make possible, through fore-pleasure, the production of the greater pleasure of satisfaction.

## Key facts

- Fore-pleasure is pleasure derived from the excitation of erotogenic zones, while end-pleasure is derived from the discharge of sexual substances and extinguishes libidinal tension. - Fore-pleasure is the same pleasure that has already been produced, although on a smaller scale, by the infantile sexual drive. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2479]
- Fore-pleasure provides the mechanism by which erotogenic zones make possible the production of the greater pleasure of satisfaction. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2479]
- If at any point in preparatory sexual processes the fore-pleasure turns out to be too great and the element of tension too small, the motive for proceeding further disappears and the preparatory act takes the place of the normal sexual aim. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2481]
- The precondition for pathogenic fore-pleasure is that the erotogenic zone or corresponding component drive has during childhood contributed an unusual amount of pleasure.
