# Genetic Enhancement

## Summary

Genetic enhancement refers to the modification of the human genome for purposes beyond disease prevention, including the alteration of traits such as intelligence, temperament, and physical characteristics. The text discusses how advances in understanding the genetic basis of brain activity, mental illness, and personality may lead to technologies capable of selecting or modifying embryonic traits, raising ethical questions about where disease prevention ends and enhancement begins.
