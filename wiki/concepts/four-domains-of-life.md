# The Four Domains of Life

## Summary

Life can be divided into four overlapping domains: Work (paid work, volunteer work, domestic work, training, education), Love (relationships with people important to you), Play (rest, recreation, sports, hobbies, creative outlets), and Health (physical, psychological, and spiritual well-being). The aim is to work on just one domain at a time to avoid overwhelm, with small changes in one area having positive domino effects on others.

## Key facts

- Work is an umbrella term for paid work, volunteer work, domestic/caregiver work, and training or education. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p246]
- Love encompasses relationships with partners, parents, friends, relatives, or work colleagues. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p246]
- Play includes sports, hobbies, creative outlets, and things done for fun, relaxation, or exploration. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p247]
- Health refers to physical well-being, psychological well-being, and spiritual/religious well-being. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p247]
