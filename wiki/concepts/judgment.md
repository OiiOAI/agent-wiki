# Judgment

## Summary

Judgment is underrated in comparison to hard work. Wisdom is knowing the long-term consequences of your actions, and judgment is wisdom applied to external problems. In an age of leverage, one correct decision can win everything. Hard work is necessary but judgment is more important—the direction you head matters more than how fast you move.

## Key facts

- Judgment is underrated; hard work is overrated. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p78]
- Wisdom is knowing the long-term consequences of your actions; wisdom applied to external problems is judgment. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p78]
- In an age of leverage, one correct decision can win everything. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p78]
- The direction you head in matters more than how fast you move, especially with leverage. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p78]
- Clear thinker is a better compliment than smart. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p79]
