# Focal Seizures

## Summary

Focal seizures begin in a localized region of the cortex (the seizure focus) and can spread to other areas. The spatial and temporal organization of a seizure focus depends on the interplay between excitation and inhibition. A critical feature is the 'inhibitory surround' created by GABAergic interneurons that normally contains seizure activity. When extrinsic or intrinsic factors alter the balance of excitation and inhibition, the inhibitory surround breaks down and seizure activity spreads.

## Key facts

- Focal seizures can spread locally from a focus via intrahemispheric fibers and more remotely to homotopic contralateral cortex and subcortical centers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1181]
- The inhibitory surround created by GABAergic interneurons normally prevents the firing of cells outside the seizure focus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1179]
- When extrinsic or intrinsic factors alter the balance of excitation and inhibition, the inhibitory surround begins to break down and seizure activity spreads. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1179]
- Secondary generalization of focal seizure activity spreads to subcortical centers via projections to the thalamus, leading to widespread thalamocortical activation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1181]
- Rapid secondary generalization is more likely if the seizure begins in the neocortex than if it begins in the limbic system. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1180]
