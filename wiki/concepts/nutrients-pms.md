# Nutrients for PMS

## Summary

Research on multinutrient supplementation for premenstrual syndrome (PMS) has shown remarkable results. A study comparing vitamin B6 to broad-spectrum multinutrients found that 72 percent of the multinutrient group and 60 percent of the vitamin B6 group were in remission for PMS symptoms after three cycles. PMS affects 20 to 30 percent of all women, with 1.2 to 6.4 percent experiencing the more severe PMDD.

## Key facts

- PMS regularly affects 20 to 30 percent of all women, and 1.2 to 6.4 percent will experience the more severely symptomatic form known as premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p152]
- In a study comparing vitamin B6 to multinutrients, 72 percent of the multinutrient group and 60 percent of the vitamin B6 group were in remission for PMS symptoms after three cycles. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p153]
- The women who took multinutrients during this study showed greater improvement than the B6 group in health-related quality of life. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p153]
