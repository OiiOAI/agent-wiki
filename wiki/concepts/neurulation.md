# Neurulation

## Summary

Neurulation is the embryological process by which the neural plate folds inward to form the neural tube, which ultimately develops into the central nervous system. This process is induced by signals from mesoderm cells.

## Key facts

- Neurulation is the process by which the outer cell layer (ectoderm) is induced to fold inward to form a tube. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p513]
- The folding of ectoderm is triggered by signals produced from contact with inducer cells from the middle layer (mesoderm). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p513]
- The neural tube formed during neurulation develops into the brain and spinal cord of the central nervous system. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p513]
