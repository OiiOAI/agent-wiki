# Sublimation

## Summary

Sublimation is one of three possible outcomes of abnormal constitutional disposition, alongside perversion and neurosis. It enables excessively strong excitations from particular sources of sexuality to find an outlet and use in other fields, resulting in increased psychical efficiency from a disposition that is in itself perilous. Sublimation is considered the origin of artistic activity. A character analysis of a highly gifted individual may reveal a mixture of efficiency, perversion, and neurosis depending on the completeness of sublimation.

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud infers that 'a drive like this of excessive strength was already active in the subject's earliest childhood, and that its supremacy was established by impressions in the child's life,' reinforcing the connection between childhood experiences and adult achievement.
- Inference: Sublimation is the most adaptive mechanism for resolving conflicts, as it channels drive energy into socially valuable activities rather than suppressing or distorting it.
