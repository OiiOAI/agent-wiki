# Focus on Emotion

## Summary

Focus on Emotion is a meditation technique for contacting emotional sensations in the body with precision and acceptance. The practice involves scanning body regions (face, throat, chest, belly) for emotional sensations and applying the meditation algorithm (notice, label, contact deeply, examine qualities, accept completely). Emotional body sensations are often subtle—not dramatic waves but small muscular and visceral signals. The technique builds 'skill at feeling' which improves decision-making (through better contact with somatic markers), enhances empathy (by making microexpressions perceptible), and creates behavioral flexibility by building space between stimulus and response. A guided practice is provided with regions and labels.

## Key facts

- Focus on Emotion involves scanning body regions for emotional sensations and applying the meditation algorithm to them. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p140]
- Emotional body sensations are described as typically subtle, not dramatic—like the small pleasant sensation of a smile. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p137]
- The technique builds 'skill at feeling' which improves both self-understanding and ability to read others' emotions. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p134]
- Experiments showed that forced smiling (holding a pen in teeth) increased ratings of cartoon funniness, demonstrating embodied emotion. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p138]
- Focus on Emotion creates a 'gap' between stimulus and response, allowing choice rather than automatic reaction. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p135]
