# Decision Swear Jar

## Summary

A decision swear jar is a precommitment contract that identifies language and thinking patterns signaling departure from truthseeking and rational decision-making. When practitioners catch themselves using flagged terms or falling into flagged thinking patterns, they create a stop-and-think moment to interrupt irrational momentum. The technique implements accountability to one's own stated goals.

## Key facts

- The decision swear jar identifies warning signs including: illusions of certainty ('I know,' 'I'm sure,' '100%'), irrational outcome fielding ('I can't believe how unlucky'), and signals of motivated reasoning. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1759-L1782]
- Warning signs include: pejorative characterizations dismissing others ('idiot,' 'donkey'), signals of zooming in on moments out of proportion ('worst day ever'), and expressions that discourage engagement ('yes, but...'). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1774-L1782]
- The word 'wrong' deserves its own swear jar because it is a conclusion, not a rationale, and implies 100% certainty that rarely exists. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1783-L1786]
- Catching oneself using flagged language triggers a contract-breaching alert that prompts reflection and interrupts the moment of potential irrationality. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1790-L1792]

## Inferences

- Inference: The decision swear jar is a practical implementation of Ulysses contract principles applied to internal thought patterns rather than external behaviors.
