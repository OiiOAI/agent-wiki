# Autobiographical Self

## Summary

The autobiographical self is the elaborate sense of self based on autobiographical memory, consisting of implicit memories of past experiences and anticipated future. It is constructed from the autobiographical memory and, when activated, generates its own pulse of core consciousness. Unlike the transient core self, the autobiographical self has stable content based on invariant biographical facts.

## Key facts

- The autobiographical self is based on dispositional records of core-self experiences that can be activated as neural patterns and turned into explicit images. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p173]
- The autobiographical self requires both core consciousness for its development and ongoing core consciousness so its contents can be known. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p175]
- The autobiographical self is architecturally connected to the proto-self and core self, forming a bridge between transient core consciousness and stable memories. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p173]
- In development, early stages contain little more than reiterated states of core self; autobiographical memory grows with experience, allowing autobiographical self deployment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p175]
- The autobiographical self hinges on the consistent reactivation and display of selected sets of autobiographical memories that substantiate identity and personhood moment by moment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p196]
- Autobiographical memories are treated as objects by the brain, each generating a pulse of core consciousness and a sense of self knowing. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p197]
- The autobiographical self arises from a continuously reactivated multisite network based on convergence zones located in temporal and frontal higher-order cortices and subcortical nuclei, paced by thalamic nuclei. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p221]
- Identity records are displayed in sensory cortices and a consistent set is activated at any moment of waking life, forming a backdrop that can be moved to the foreground as needed. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p223]
- Core consciousness and autobiographical self together enable extended consciousness; neither can substitute for the other. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p219]

## Inferences

- Inference: The autobiographical self requires language only at the highest levels of personhood, not for its basic existence, since apes with substantial memory capacity may possess an autobiographical self.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise mechanisms by which the brain coordinates reactivation across distributed convergence zones are not specified.
