# Communicative Action

## Summary

Communicative action is Jürgen Habermas's central concept, referring to a form of social interaction in which participants coordinate their actions not through egocentric calculations of success but through acts of reaching understanding based on common situation definitions. It represents the foundation of all sociocultural life according to Habermas.

## Key facts

- Habermas distinguished between purposive-rational action (work) and communicative action (interaction), arguing that communicative action is the most distinctive and pervasive human phenomenon. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p181]
- In communicative action, 'participants are not primarily oriented to their own successes; they pursue their individual goals under the condition that they can harmonize their plans of action on the basis of common situation definitions.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p181]
- The objective of communicative action is to achieve communicative understanding, not instrumental mastery as in purposive-rational action. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p181]
- Habermas's baseline is undistorted communication, communication without compulsion, which allows critical analysis of distorted communication. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p182]
- Habermas developed discourse as 'that form of communication that is removed from contexts of experience and action' where only the better argument prevails. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p184]
- The 'ideal speech situation' is where force or power does not determine which arguments win out; instead the better argument emerges victorious. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p184]
