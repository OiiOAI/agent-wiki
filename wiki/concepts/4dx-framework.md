# 4DX Framework

## Summary

The 4 Disciplines of Execution adapted for personal deep work habits, consisting of: focus on the wildly important, act on lead measures, keep a compelling scoreboard, and create a cadence of accountability.

## Key facts

- Discipline #1: Focus on the Wildly Important Goals—identifying a small number of ambitious outcomes to pursue with deep work hours. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p81]
- Discipline #2: Act on the Lead Measures—measuring time spent in deep work dedicated toward the wildly important goal. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p82]
- Discipline #3: Keep a Compelling Scoreboard—maintaining a physical artifact that displays current deep work hour count. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p82]
- Discipline #4: Create a Cadence of Accountability—regular weekly reviews to review the scoreboard and plan improvements. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p83]
- Cal Newport used this framework and published nine papers in one year, more than doubling his previous record. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p84]
