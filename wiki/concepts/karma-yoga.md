# Karma Yoga

## Summary

The yogic path of selfless action described by Singer as his approach to both spiritual practice and business life. Singer understood karma yoga as dedicating one's work to the Universal Flow rather than to personal gain or loss, describing his business activities as 'Karma yoga at its highest.'

## Key facts

- Singer described his approach to work as 'Karma yoga at its highest' where he had given his life to the Universal Flow. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p175]
- Singer stated he was not living a life based on what he wanted or didn't want, as those types of thoughts had ceased passing through his mind long ago. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p175]
