# The Moon of Security

## Summary

The Moon of Security addresses the need to feel safe in surroundings and troop. The Chimp looks externally for security, trying to keep everything constant and familiar, while the Human looks internally to beliefs and accepts that vulnerability and risk are part of everyday life. The chapter covers truths about risk and security, practical ways to help the Chimp feel secure including accepting truth, relying on the troop, seeking reassurance, and establishing routines. Basing your security on one other person is identified as unwise.

## Key facts

- The Chimp will always look to external surroundings for its security and try to remove any form of risk or vulnerability, believing security is possible; the Human looks internally to beliefs for security and accepts that vulnerability and risk are part of everyday life. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p217]
- Security truths include: security is relative; we are never fully secure because life is transient; we live in a world in constant change; there is a risk attached to everything we do; we cannot control all risk; we can control some risk; and vulnerability and fears should be faced. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p218]
- For most Chimps the troop is the biggest factor to bring a sense of security; sharing concerns or worries with others and going through experiences together makes it surprising how much can be managed. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p219]
- Basing your security on one other person is usually unwise; when we are dependent on someone else for our well-being it tends to put restrictions on what we can and cannot do; try to gain security from within by basing peace of mind on beliefs. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p219]
- When in love your brain releases chemicals that diminish your ability to think straight; judgement of the other person can be very impaired, so listen to your best friends because they are not so blind. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p220]
- Feeling insecure from time to time is normal for a Chimp, so expect it, accept it, and then take care of it; if you have an insecure Chimp, establishing a routine that is familiar can do wonders to settle it. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p221]
