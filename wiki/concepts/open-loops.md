# Open Loops

## Summary

Open Loops (also called 'incompletes') are defined as anything pulling at your attention that doesn't belong where it is, the way it is. They range from major life goals to minor tasks. Open loops generate unconscious mental pressure until they are captured, clarified with a defined outcome and next action, and organized into a trusted external system. This concept is central to GTD's understanding of why things remain on our minds.

## Key facts

- An open loop is defined as anything that does not belong where it is, the way it is. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p44]
- Most stress comes from inappropriately managed commitments, which manifest as open loops. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p44]
- Anything on your mind means your mind isn't clear—the open loop must be captured and processed. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p45]
- Open loops remain on your mind because you haven't clarified the intended outcome, decided on the next action, or put reminders in a trusted system. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p48]
- The mind cannot give up tracking open loops until reminders are parked in a place you will look without fail. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p48]
