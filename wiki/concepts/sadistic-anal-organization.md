# Sadistic-Anal Organization

## Summary

The sadistic-anal organization is a pregenital stage of psychosexual development characterized by sadistic impulses directed toward objects and the emergence of anal erotism with its receptive passive function. This phase follows the oral phase and precedes the genital organization. Freud describes how the patient's seduction drove him into passivity and how his genital organization broke down under the threat of castration, regressing to this earlier stage. The characteristic features include violent muscular activity (preparatory to eating) and the attachment of passive function from the oral to the anal zone.

## Key facts

- The sadistic-anal organization can be regarded as a continuation and development of the oral one, where violent muscular activity directed toward the object is preparatory to eating. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5359]
- The essential novelty of this stage, compared to the previous one, is that the receptive passive function becomes disengaged from the oral zone and attached to the anal zone. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5359]
- Under the influence of his seduction, the patient's sadism was transformed to a great extent into masochism, its passive counterpart. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5360]
- The patient's seduction offered him a passive sexual aim which was ultimately incompatible with the action of his male genital organ, and at the first external obstacle (the threat of castration) his genital organization broke down and regressed to the sadistic-anal stage. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5359]

## Inferences

- Inference: The building up of the drive to research out of its various components is a characteristic feature of the sadistic-anal stage, as seen in the patient's sexual researches concerning the origin of children and the possibility of losing the genitals.
