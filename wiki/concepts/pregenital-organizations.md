# Pregenital Organizations

## Summary

Pregenital organizations are phases of sexual organization in which the genital zones have not yet taken their predominant part. Two such organizations have been identified: the first is the oral or cannibalistic phase where sexual activity has not been separated from ingestion of food; the second is the sadistic-anal organization characterized by the predominance of sadism and anal erotism with active-passive polarities. These phases are normally passed through smoothly and only become recognizable in pathological cases.
