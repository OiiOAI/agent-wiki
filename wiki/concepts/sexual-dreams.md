# Sexual Dreams

## Summary

Sexual dreams are reported with similar frequency in men's and women's everyday dreams (about 6-7%), though more men than women report ever having had a sexual dream. Women more often dream with known partners while men more often dream with strangers.

## Key facts

- In diary studies of over 10,000 dream reports, there is no significant difference between the prevalence of sexual dreams in men (7%) and women (6%). [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p186]
- While current or past sex partners figure in up to 30% of women's sexual dreams, they appear in only 10-15% of men's sexual dreams. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p187]
- Orgasms are reported in fewer than 4 percent of both men's and women's sexual dreams. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p187]
- Familiar characters appear twice as often in women's sexual dreams while strangers (including multiple partners) figure in about twice as many of men's sexual dreams. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p187]
- Sexual dream frequency correlates not with how often people engage in sexual activities but with how much time they spend thinking about sex. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p186]
