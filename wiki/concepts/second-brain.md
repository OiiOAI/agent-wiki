# Second Brain

## Summary

A Second Brain is a digital archive of memories, ideas, and knowledge stored outside the biological brain to help manage information overload. It enables users to find past information within seconds, organize knowledge to move projects forward, save best thinking for reuse, connect ideas across different areas of life, and spend less time searching and more time creating. The concept draws on PKM (personal knowledge management) and is inspired by historical practices like commonplace books, Zettelkasten, and Memex.

- A report from the International Data Corporation found that 26 percent of a typical knowledge worker's day is spent looking for and consolidating information spread across a variety of systems, and only 56 percent of the time they succeed in finding the required information.
