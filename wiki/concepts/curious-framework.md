# CURIOUS Framework

## Summary

The CURIOUS framework provides seven characteristics to look for when assembling a curiosity-driven team: Collaborative, Unabashedly passionate, Resilient, Iconoclastic, Open to outside interests, Urgent, and Surprise seeking. These qualities ensure team members can dive into challenging curiosity projects with complementary skills and shared purpose.

## Key facts

- Collaborative team members can play well with others, share sense of purpose, take responsibility for mistakes, and actively listen to team members. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p109]
- Unabashedly passionate people show healthy self-confidence, perseverance, enthusiasm, positivity, and focus. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p109]
- Resilient team members recover quickly from setbacks and are adaptable when things do not go as planned. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p110]
- Iconoclastic team members are comfortable challenging the status quo and asking difficult questions that challenge assumptions. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p110]
- People open to outside interests have hobbies and genuine inquisitiveness about life, drawing connections between activities and work. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p110]
- Urgent team members have demonstrated success in executing necessary tasks and know how to compromise for successful collaboration. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p110]
- Surprise-seeking collaborators embrace the unknown, enjoy trying new things, and do not have fixed ideas about topics. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p110]
