# HOMA-IR

## Summary

Homeostatic Model Assessment for Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR) is a simple calculation using fasting blood sugar and fasting insulin levels to determine insulin sensitivity. The formula is (Fasting Glucose × Fasting Insulin) / 405. Values under 2 are considered normal, with optimal being under 1, while values over 2.75 indicate insulin resistance. Higher HOMA-IR values are associated with worse cognitive performance.

## Key facts

- HOMA-IR stands for homeostatic model assessment for insulin resistance and answers the question of how much insulin the pancreas needs to pump out to keep fasting blood sugar at its current level. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p135]
- The formula for HOMA-IR is: Fasting Glucose (mg/dL) × Fasting Insulin / 405. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p135]
- Reference values state that any figure under 2 is normal, with optimal being under 1, while anything over 2.75 is considered insulin resistant. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p135]
- Higher HOMA-IR values are associated with worse cognitive performance in the present as well as in the future. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p135]
