# Psychic Determinism

## Summary

Psychic determinism is the principle that all mental processes, including those that appear accidental, arbitrary, or unintentional, are causally determined by other mental processes. Freud argues that even 'arbitrary' choices of numbers, names, or verbal associations have determinable unconscious causes. The principle extends to everyday phenomena like parapraxes, which reveal hidden motivations. Freud distinguishes between external (real) chance and internal (psychical) accidental events—believing in the former but not the latter.

## Key facts

- Freud demonstrates psychic determinism through analysis of the 'arbitrary' number 2467 that appeared in a letter to Fliess, tracing its components to his age, his father's age, and his feelings about Colonel M.'s career. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2211]
- Freud reports that 'Dora' was the only name he could think of for a case history, eventually tracing this to an association with a nursemaid who had to give up her name when employed in someone else's house—connecting to the case material about a governess. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2209]
- A patient who spontaneously thought of the number 426718 was found to have unconsciously expressed wishes for his siblings' deaths and for a younger sibling to replace him. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2215]
- Freud proposes that the feeling of 'free will' applies only to unimportant decisions, while important decisions feel like compulsion; unconscious motivation fills the gap left by absent conscious motivation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2220]

## Inferences

- Inference: The principle of psychic determinism extends to all mental phenomena, suggesting that even what we consider 'chance' thoughts are actually determined by unconscious processes.
