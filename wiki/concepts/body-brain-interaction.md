# Body-brain interaction

## Summary

Body-brain interaction refers to the indissociable integration of the brain and body through mutually targeted biochemical and neural circuits. Two principal routes connect brain and body: (1) sensory and motor peripheral nerves carrying signals between body and brain, and (2) the bloodstream carrying chemical signals such as hormones, neurotransmitters, and modulators. This bidirectional communication underlies all aspects of mind, including perception, emotion, feeling, and cognition. The author argues that mind is embodied—not just embrained—and that the separation between mind and body is as fictional as the separation between mind and brain.

## Key facts

- Brain and body are indissociably integrated by mutually targeted biochemical and neural circuits via two principal routes: peripheral nerves and the bloodstream. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p107]
- Nearly every part of the body sends signals to the brain via peripheral nerves, entering at spinal cord or brain stem level and eventually reaching somatosensory cortices in parietal lobe and insular regions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p108]
- Chemical substances from body activity reach the brain via bloodstream and influence operation directly or by activating special brain sites such as the subfornical organ. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p108]
- The brain acts on the body through autonomic (visceral) and musculoskeletal (voluntary) nervous systems, and by manufacturing chemical substances released into the bloodstream. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p108]
- Autonomic nervous system signals arise in evolutionarily older regions (amygdala, cingulate, hypothalamus, brain stem) while musculoskeletal signals arise in motor cortices and subcortical motor nuclei of different evolutionary ages. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p108]
- The organism constituted by brain-body partnership interacts with the environment as an ensemble, the interaction being of neither the body nor the brain alone. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p108]

## Inferences

- Inference: This integrated view of body-brain interaction provides the foundation for understanding how emotions (which involve body changes) can influence cognition and decision-making.
