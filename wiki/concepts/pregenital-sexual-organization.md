# Pregenital Sexual Organization

## Summary

A stage in libidinal development where component drives have come together for object choice and the object is extraneous to the self, but the primacy of the genital zones has not yet been established. The dominant component drives are anal-erotic and sadistic.

## Key facts

- Between auto-erotism and the genital phase, there exists a stage in which the component drives have already come together for object choice but the primacy of the genital zones has not been established. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4036]
- In this pregenital organization, the component drives which dominate are anal-erotic and sadistic ones. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4036]
- Activity is supplied by the drive to mastery (sadism), while the passive trend is fed by anal erotism. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4038]
- A stressing of anal erotism in the pregenital stage leaves behind a predisposition to homosexuality in men. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4038]
