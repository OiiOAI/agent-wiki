# Social Connectedness and Physical Health

## Summary

Social integration and connectedness have profound effects on physical health, with protective effects rivaling the detrimental effects of smoking, obesity, high blood pressure, and physical inactivity. The Roseto effect demonstrates that close-knit community structures protect against heart disease.

## Key facts

- Social connectedness predicts lower rates of colds, heart attacks, strokes, cancer, depression, and premature death of all sorts. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p597]
- The positive effects of social integration rival the detrimental effects of smoking, obesity, high blood pressure and physical inactivity. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p597]
- Robert Putnam states that evidence for health consequences of social connectedness is as strong today as was the evidence for health consequences of smoking at the time of the first surgeon general's report. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p597]
- The community of Roseto, Pennsylvania had a heart attack rate less than half the national average despite higher than average risk factors, due to its close-knit social structure; when this structure broke down, the rate rose to match the national average. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p597]
