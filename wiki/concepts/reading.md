# Reading

## Summary

Reading science, math, and philosophy one hour per day will likely put a person at the upper echelon of human success within seven years. The genuine love for reading itself is a superpower. Reading was Naval's first love. The best way to have a high-quality foundation is to stick to science and the basics. Naval treats books as throwaway blog posts, feeling no obligation to finish any book.
