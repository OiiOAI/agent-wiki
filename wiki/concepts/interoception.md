# Interoception

## Summary

Interoception is the perception of the body's internal physiological state, including heartbeats, blood pressure, and organ status. It is the sensory system underlying emotions and the sense of being alive, and plays a crucial role in the beast machine theory of consciousness.

## Key facts

- Interoception is the sense of the internal physiological condition of the body. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p170]
- Interoceptive signals report heartbeats, blood pressure, blood chemistry, gastric tension, breathing status, and other internal organ states. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p170]
- These signals travel through nerves and deep brain regions (brainstem and thalamus) before arriving at the insular cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p170]
- The key property of interoceptive signals is that they reflect how well physiological regulation of the body is going. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p170]
