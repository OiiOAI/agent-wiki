# Retirement

## Summary

Naval defines retirement as when you stop sacrificing today for an imaginary tomorrow. There are three ways to retire: having enough passive income to cover expenses, driving your burn rate to zero (becoming a monk), or doing something you love so much it's not about the money.

## Key facts

- Retirement is when you stop sacrificing today for an imaginary tomorrow. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p66]
- The first way to retire is having passive income that covers your burn rate without you lifting a finger. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p66]
- The second way to retire is driving your burn rate down to zero, becoming a monk. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p66]
- The third way to retire is doing something you love so much it's not about the money. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p66]
