# Preformed Brain Dynamics

## Summary

Preformed brain dynamics refers to the concept that neural networks and their activity patterns are preconfigured by genetic programming and development, rather than being constructed from sensory experience. The brain comes with preexisting 'ideal forms' and a dictionary of neuronal words that acquire meaning only through action-based experience, providing stability against catastrophic interference.

## Key facts

- Rich brain dynamics emerge from genetically programmed wiring circuits and biophysical properties of neurons during development even without any sensory input or experience. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p344]
- The preconfigured brain is an already existing dictionary, although its numerous words and sentences are initially meaningless until experience attaches utility to them. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p343]
- Preexisting neuronal patterns provide the necessary balance to keep the brain's dynamical landscape stable and robust against competing needs like wide dynamic range, sensitivity, and plasticity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p343]
- Sharp wave bursts are present at birth before any exploratory experience, demonstrating that some neuronal dynamics predate experience. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p348]

## Inferences

- Inference: This view contrasts with tabula rasa and connectionist models, explaining why the brain does not suffer from catastrophic interference during learning.
- Inference: The preconfigured brain contains both rigid and plastic components, allowing it to both generalize (via the rigid backbone) and specialize (via plastic additions).
