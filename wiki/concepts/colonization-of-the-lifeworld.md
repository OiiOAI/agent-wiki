# Colonization of the Life-World

## Summary

The colonization of the life-world is Jürgen Habermas's concept describing how social systems (especially the economy and administrative apparatus) come to dominate and distort communicative action within the life-world. This process involves 'de-linguistified' media like money and power replacing everyday language as coordinators of action.

## Key facts

- Habermas sees society as being composed of both life-world and system, which have become increasingly 'decoupled' in modern society. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p443]
- While both life-world and system have undergone rationalization, that process has taken different forms in the two settings. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p443]
- Habermas is concerned with the way system in the modern world has come to control the life-world and the breakdown of the dialectic between them. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p443]
- The rationalization of the life-world involves growth in communicative action increasingly freed from normative constraint and relying on everyday language. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p444]
- The demands on language grow and come to overwhelm its capacities, leading to delinguistified media (money and power) filling the void. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p444]
- Habermas writes of the 'violence' exercised over the life-world by the system through the ways in which it restricts communication. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p444]
- This violence produces 'pathologies' within the life-world including communication that becomes increasingly rigidified, impoverished, and fragmented. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p445]

## Inferences

- Inference: The concept integrates Marxian concerns with system distortion and Weberian concerns with rationalization into a communicative framework.
- Inference: Habermas views social movements oriented to equality, self-realization, environmental preservation, and peace as reactions to system assaults on the life-world.
