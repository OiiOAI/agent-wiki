# Emphatic Traits

## Summary

Emphatic traits are exaggerated, one-sided personality characteristics that serve to distract from and conceal the opposite trait within a person. Greene identifies seven common emphatic trait types: The Tough Guy (masculine facade concealing emotional vulnerability), The Saint (saintly exterior disguising hunger for power or attention), The Passive-Aggressive Charmer (excessive niceness covering aggressive tendencies), The Fanatic (intense conviction masking deep insecurities), The Rigid Rationalist (extreme rationality repressing primitive thinking), The Snob (assertions of superiority hiding insecurity about origins), and The Extreme Entrepreneur (outward self-reliance disguising desire for dependency).

## Key facts

- People with emphatic traits unconsciously develop the opposite trait over many years, honing and perfecting this public image. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p307]
- The seven most common emphatic trait types are: The Tough Guy, The Saint, The Passive-Aggressive Charmer, The Fanatic, The Rigid Rationalist, The Snob, and The Extreme Entrepreneur. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p307]
- People displaying emphatic traits will reveal their opposite nature occasionally through moods, tantrums, or unexpected compassion before quickly covering it up. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p308]
