# Seven Emotional Systems

## Summary

Panksepp proposes seven innate emotional operating systems embedded in the mammalian brain: SEEKING (appetitive exploration), RAGE (anger/defense), FEAR (anxiety/avoidance), PANIC (separation distress), LUST (sexual motivation), CARE (maternal nurturance), and PLAY (rough-and-tumble social interaction). Each system has distinct neuroanatomical substrates and can be activated by electrical/chemical brain stimulation to produce coordinated behavioral responses.

## Key facts

- The SEEKING system promotes energetic search and goal-directed behaviors, making animals intensely interested in exploring their world; it is associated with mesolimbic and mesocortical dopamine circuits emanating from the ventral tegmental area. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]
- The RAGE system mediates anger and is aroused by frustration and attempts to curtail an animal's freedom of action; it parallels the trajectory of the FEAR system. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]
- The FEAR system helps animals reduce pain and possibility of destruction; weak stimulation produces freezing, while intense stimulation leads to flight. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]
- The PANIC system provides neural substrate for understanding social emotional processes related to attachment; it is especially important in elaborating separation distress vocalizations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]
- Sexual LUST, maternal CARE, and rough-and-tumble PLAY represent more sophisticated socioemotional systems engaged at appropriate times in mammalian lives. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]
- Localized electrical and chemical brain stimulation can evoke discrete emotional behaviors and states in specific subcortical areas in all mammals including humans. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p35]

## Inferences

- Inference: Since these seven emotional systems can be similarly evoked across mammalian species, they likely represent homologous brain mechanisms that evolved to solve specific survival challenges.
- Inference: The SEEKING system corresponds to the major self-stimulation system, suggesting that drug addiction may involve overarousal of this natural motivational system.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise neuroanatomical boundaries and complete neurochemical profiles of each system remain to be fully characterized.
- Uncertain: Whether all seven systems have distinct genetic and neural substrates or some may share components is not definitively established.
