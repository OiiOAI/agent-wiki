# The Shadow of the Third

## Summary

A concept describing how the presence of a potential third person exists at the boundary of every couple, representing the forbidden and the allure of what lies outside the committed relationship. The third can be real or imagined, embodied or not, and serves as the fulcrum on which a couple balances their dynamic.

## Key facts

- At the boundary of every couple lives the third: high school sweethearts, strangers on the subway, porn stars, or fantasy figures. Real or imagined, embodied or not, the third is the fulcrum on which a couple balances. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p165]
- Adam Phillips writes: 'The couple is a resistance to the intrusion of the third, but in order for it to last it is indispensable to have enemies. That is why the monogamous can't live without them. When we are two, we are together. In order to form a couple, we need to be three.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p165]
- The third points to other possibilities, choices we did not make, and is bound up with our freedom—the freedom not to love, to stop loving, or to love someone else. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p166]
- Excessive monitoring of a partner can set the stage for 'acts of exuberant defiance.' When the third is exiled, some people seek it in affairs, online encounters, strip clubs, or sex on business trips. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p167]
- When couples acknowledge the possibility of the third and validate one another's freedom within the relationship, they are less inclined to search for it elsewhere. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p170]
- Couples who invite the third into their relationship through shared fantasies, flirting with rules, or open marriages redefine fidelity not by sexual exclusivity but by the strength of emotional commitment. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p172]
