# Psychical Impotence

## Summary

A disturbance affecting libidinous men where the sexual organs refuse to carry out intercourse despite intact anatomy and strong psychical desire, caused by inhibitory psychical complexes, particularly incestuous fixations on mother or sister.

## Key facts

- Psychical impotence manifests as failure of the sexual act with certain individuals while succeeding with others, caused by inhibitory complexes. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3612]
- The condition results from failure of two psychical currents—affectionate and sensual—to combine properly in love. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3613]
- The affectionate current originates in early childhood through family attachments; the sensual current emerges at puberty following earlier paths but encounters incest barriers.
