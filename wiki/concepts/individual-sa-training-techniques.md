# Individual Situation Awareness Training

## Summary

Individual pilot SA training approaches include higher-order cognitive skills training, intensive pre-flight briefings, structured feedback using SAGAT, and SA-oriented training programs. These methods target the underlying skills and knowledge bases critical for achieving good SA in flight, including attention-sharing, task management, contingency planning, information seeking, and self-checking behaviors.

## Key facts

- Higher-order cognitive skills training targets attention-sharing, task management, contingency planning, information seeking and filtering, and self-checking skills. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p365]
- Intensive pre-flight briefings use multimedia tools to help pilots develop a clear mental picture of their flight including hazard areas, weather, and airport approach patterns. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p365]
- SAGAT (Situation Awareness Global Assessment Technique) can be adapted for training by providing feedback comparing pilot responses to the actual situation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p366]
- SA-oriented training programs should focus on developing the schemata and mental models that allow pilots to better understand importance, consequences, timing, risk levels, and capabilities. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p366]
- Attention-sharing has shown some indication that it can be improved through training according to Damos and Wickens (1980). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p353]

## Inferences

- Inference: Training techniques should be carefully tested with control groups to determine whether they result in significant changes in pilots' ability to acquire and maintain SA
- Inference: xperienced pilots may be deficient in their ability to properly assess risk and capabilities (Level 2 SA) from situational cues

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Most major airlines have introduced short SA training courses, but no validation work has been done on their effectiveness
