# The Psychological Mind

## Summary

The Psychological Mind is a simplified model of the brain consisting of three components: the Human (frontal lobe), the Chimp (limbic system), and the Computer (distributed storage system). The Human and Chimp are two independent thinking machines that frequently conflict, with the Chimp often winning control due to its greater emotional power.

## Key facts

- The Psychological Mind comprises three separate brains: the Human (frontal lobe), the Chimp (limbic system), and the Computer. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p15]
- The Human is the rational self that lives in the frontal lobe and works with facts and truth. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p14]
- The Chimp is an emotional machine that lives in the limbic system, given at birth, and thinks independently with its own personality. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p13]
- The Computer is a storage area for thoughts and behaviours that runs automatic programmes for both Human and Chimp. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p14]
- The Chimp is five times stronger than the Human, making willpower alone ineffective for controlling emotional responses. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p38]
- The case of Phineas Gage demonstrated that damage to the frontal lobe removes the Human, leaving only the Chimp personality, resulting in disinhibition and impulsive behaviour. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p12]

## Inferences

- Inference: The three-component model is a simplification for practical use, not a scientifically accurate description of brain function.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact boundaries between which brain regions constitute each component are acknowledged to be simplified.
