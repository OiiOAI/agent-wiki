# Schwerpunkt and Commander's Intent

## Summary

Schwerpunkt (point of main effort) is the German military concept that identifies where to concentrate forces for maximum effect. Commander's intent complements Schwerpunkt but operates on a longer time horizon—while the Schwerpunkt may shift several times to accomplish the mission, the commander's intent provides continuity. German doctrine did not give a name to the non-Schwerpunkt elements; Boyd borrowed the term Nebenpunkt (secondary point) to describe supporting efforts.

- Commander's intent complements Schwerpunkt but generally has a longer time focus. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p145]
- The Schwerpunkt may shift several times to accomplish the commander's intent. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p148]
- German doctrine did not give a name to the parts of the operation that were not the Schwerpunkt; Boyd used Nebenpunkt (secondary point). [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p148]
