# Numbers in Dreams

## Summary

Numbers in dreams appear as a specific mode of representation where the dream-work treats numbers as a medium for expression, similar to how it treats any other idea. The dream-work does not carry out calculations (whether correct or incorrect); instead it throws numbers present in dream thoughts into calculation form to serve as allusions to matter that cannot be represented otherwise. Numbers may represent ages, time periods, or abstract numerical concepts.

## Key facts

- The dream-work does not in fact carry out any calculations at all, whether correctly or incorrectly; it merely throws numbers which are present in dream thoughts into the form of a calculation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p374]
- Numbers in dreams represent the age of the dreamer at particular periods in childhood; 'a quarter-past five in the morning' meant the age of five years and three months. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p366]
- Numbers may represent time periods related to significant life events; 365 days equals one year, and the dreamer calculated 21 days remained of her treatment. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p371]
- The dream-work treats numbers as a medium for expression in the same way as it treats any other idea including proper names and speeches. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p374]
