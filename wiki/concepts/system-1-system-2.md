# System 1 and System 2 Thinking

## Summary

System 1 (the reflexive or fast mind) operates automatically with little conscious awareness, while System 2 (the deliberative or slow mind) requires concentration and mental effort. Duke explains that our brains evolved for automatic processing and that our thin prefrontal cortex cannot handle all decisions deliberatively, making shortcuts and habits necessary for survival.

## Key facts

- Daniel Kahneman characterized System 1 as 'fast thinking' encompassing reflex, instinct, intuition, impulse, and automatic processing, while System 2 is 'slow thinking' for choosing and concentrating. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L335-L339]
- Gary Marcus uses the labels 'reflexive mind' (fast, automatic, unconscious) and 'deliberative mind' (slow, deliberate, judicious). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L339-L342]
- Colin Camerer explained that automatic processing originates in evolutionarily older parts of the brain including the cerebellum, basal ganglia, and amygdala, while the deliberative mind operates from the prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L349-L353]
- Camerer stated that the prefrontal cortex is 'already overtaxed' and getting it to handle more decisions is unrealistic. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L353-L356]
- The goal is to get the reflexive mind to execute on the deliberative mind's best intentions, which poker players must master due to time pressure and immediate financial consequences. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L379-L384]

## Inferences

- Inference: The brain's automatic processing system evolved for survival on the savanna, where quick reactions to potential threats were necessary, which explains why we struggle with deliberative decision-making in modern contexts.
