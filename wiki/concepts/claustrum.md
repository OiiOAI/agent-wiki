# Claustrum

## Summary

The claustrum is a thin sheet of neurons lying next to the lower cortical layers near the insula. It appears to be a satellite of the cortex, receiving input mainly from the cortex and sending output back to the cortex, with connections to many cortical areas. Its function is unknown, but Crick suggests it may have some kind of global function, possibly as part of a hierarchy of processing units that exert global control.

## Key facts

- The claustrum is a thin sheet of neurons lying next to the lower cortical layers near a part of the cortex called the 'insula.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p265]
- Its input comes mainly from the cortex and most of its output goes back to the cortex, receiving input from many cortical areas and possibly sending connections back to all of them. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p265]
- Some but not all visual areas of the cortex project to one part of the claustrum and form a single retinotopic map there in the cat. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p265]
- The claustrum's function is unknown; it is a rather small region of the brain but should not be totally overlooked. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p265]

## Inferences

- Inference: The claustrum's widespread cortical connections suggest it may have a global function, possibly coordinating activity across different cortical areas.
- Inference: Sets of neurons that project very widely over the cortex, such as the claustrum and intralaminar nuclei, could play a role in a hierarchy of processing units exerting global control.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact function of the claustrum is unknown and requires further study.
- Uncertain: Some statements about the claustrum may be somewhat inaccurate as not much work has been done on the monkey claustrum in recent years.
