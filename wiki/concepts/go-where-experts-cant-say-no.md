# Go Where Experts Can't Say No

## Summary

Go Where Experts Can't Say No is a decision-making technique where when you hit uncharted turbulence, you present your response plan to an expert and ask one question: "Can you prove that my plan will fail?" If the expert can prove failure, you need a different plan. If they cannot prove failure, it can work — now. This protects from acting dumb while allowing appropriate risk-taking.

## Key facts

- Present your response plan to an expert and ask: "Can you prove that my plan will fail?" If yes, invent a different one. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p149]
- Ignore the expert's other advice — if the expert says they have a plan that will work better, it will work better yesterday; if they say there's no evidence your plan will work, it can work now. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p149]
- Special Operators call this Go Where Experts Can't Say No — it protects you from acting dumb while allowing appropriate risk. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p149]
