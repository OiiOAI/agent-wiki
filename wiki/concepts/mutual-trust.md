# Mutual Trust (Einheit)

## Summary

Mutual trust, or Einheit (German for unity/oneness), is the foundation of Boyd's organizational climate. It is the internal harmony that converts a mob into a team and underlies all other components of competitive organizational climate. Mutual trust permits implicit communication among team members where very little needs to be written down—it is much quicker not to say something than to spell it out in detail. Unlike enforced harmony, trust encourages individual initiative. People are much more likely to seek initiative in a climate of trust than in a climate of fear.

## Key facts

- Shigeo Shingo wrote that 'the most important factor is maintaining a relationship of trust between labor and management,' and the entire foundation of the Japanese system rests on this belief. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p83]
- Mutual trust enables implicit communication—a raised eyebrow, subtle change in inflection, barely-noticed hesitation mean something if you've worked together long enough. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p84]
- Charles Handy concluded that 'trust gives people a sense of belonging. When people feel they're active members of their work community, they become interested in the company's future and willingly dedicate their time and talent.'. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p85]
- Galford and Drapeau identified five ways to destroy trust: inconsistent messages, inconsistent standards, misplaced benevolence, ignoring office politics, and restricting information flow. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p87]
- Boyd assigned much of the blame for WW I bloodbaths to an 'obsession for control.'. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p86]
