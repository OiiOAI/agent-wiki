# Cognitive Map

## Summary

A cognitive map is an internal representation of the external world that the brain develops and uses to generate meaningful images and navigate space. Unlike sensory maps for touch and vision based on egocentric coordinates, the hippocampal spatial map uses allocentric coordinates centered on the world. The concept emerged from cognitive psychology and was biologically confirmed by O'Keefe's discovery of place cells.

## Key facts

- Cognitive psychologists proposed that the brain achieves analytic triumphs by developing an internal representation of the external world—a cognitive map. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p308]
- Unlike neurons in a sensory system, place cells are not switched on by sensory stimulation; their collective activity represents the location where the animal thinks it is. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p319]
- The spatial map presents a new type of representation based on a combination of a priori knowledge and learning; the general capability for forming spatial maps is built into mind, but the particular map is not. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p320]
- Protein kinase A and protein synthesis are not needed for the initial formation of a spatial map but are essential for the map to become fixed over the long term. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p320]
- Tolman proposed that rats formed internal cognitive maps rather than merely learning stimulus-response associations. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1207]
- The hippocampus contains place cells that fire when an animal is in specific locations, providing a neural substrate for spatial cognition. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1207]
