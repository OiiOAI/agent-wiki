# Man-with-a-Hammer Syndrome

## Summary

Man-with-a-hammer syndrome is Munger's term for the cognitive bias where people with limited expertise overapply their single tool or method to all problems, ignoring alternatives. The folk saying is: 'To the man with only a hammer, every problem looks pretty much like a nail.'

## Key facts

- Man-with-a-hammer syndrome creates a situation where to the man with only a hammer, every problem looks pretty much like a nail. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p389]
- The antidote for man-with-a-hammer syndrome is having a full kit of tools and using them checklist-style. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p389]
