# Displacement (Verschiebung) in Jokes

## Summary

Displacement is a joke technique involving the diversion of the train of thought and the displacement of psychical emphasis onto a topic other than the opening one. Unlike double meaning, displacement is part of the joke-work itself, not merely part of understanding the joke.

## Key facts

- In displacement jokes, the joke itself contains a train of thought in which a displacement has been accomplished, and this displacement is part of the work which has created the joke. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2700]
- The technique of displacement is largely independent of verbal expression; it depends not on words but on the train of thought. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2699]
- Freud distinguishes displacement from double meaning: in the latter, the transition from one meaning to another is part of understanding the joke, whereas in displacement jokes, the displacement is part of the joke-work itself. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2700]
