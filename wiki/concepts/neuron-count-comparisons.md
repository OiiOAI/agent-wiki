# Neuron count comparisons across species

## Summary

This concept uses a sand-grains-in-truck analogy to illustrate neuron counts across species, showing that while humans have roughly 120 billion neurons fitting in one truck, elephants have three trucks worth and whales have five trucks worth. However, intelligence depends more on neural connectivity than raw neuron numbers.
