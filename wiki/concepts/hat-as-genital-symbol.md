# Hat as Genital Symbol

## Summary

Freud proposed that the symbolic meaning of the hat may be derived from the head, treating the hat as a prolonged, though detachable, head. This connects to observations about obsessional neurotics who are excessively sensitive about greeting ceremonies—whether someone removes his hat first or waits for their salutation. The persistence of this symptom despite intellectual understanding suggests an unconscious relation to the castration complex.

## Key facts

- The hat as a symbol may be derived from the head, insofar as a hat can be regarded as a prolonged, though detachable, head. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4737]
- Obsessional neurotics are often troubled by whether acquaintances will greet them first by taking off their hat, or whether they seem to wait for their salutation, and give up acquaintances who no longer greet them properly. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4737]
- The resistance of this sensitivity to rational explanation suggests that a motive less familiar to consciousness is at work, possibly related to the castration complex.
