# Volunteer's Folly

## Summary

Volunteer's folly occurs when people with valuable time volunteer for tasks they could better accomplish by working longer and hiring professionals. A photographer earning $500/hour building birdhouses should instead work an extra hour and donate $200 to hire a carpenter.

## Key facts

- A $500/hour photographer volunteering to build birdhouses is volunteer folly: working an extra hour and donating $200 to hire a professional carpenter achieves more. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p147]
- Celebrities are exempt from volunteer's folly: if Bono or Kate Winslet build birdhouses, they lend priceless publicity to the cause. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p148]
