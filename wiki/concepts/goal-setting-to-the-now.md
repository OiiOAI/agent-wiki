# Goal Setting to the Now

## Summary

Goal Setting to the Now is a methodology that connects future goals to present priorities by working backward through time periods—from a someday goal to five-year goals to annual goals to monthly, weekly, and daily priorities, ultimately identifying the ONE Thing to do right now. The approach emphasizes that success comes from stringing together powerful moments, and that connecting today's actions to future outcomes is essential for achieving extraordinary results.

## Key facts

- Goal Setting to the Now connects future purpose to present priority through a cascading series of questions. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p120]
- The methodology uses a chain: someday goal to five-year goal to annual goal to monthly goal to weekly goal to daily goal to now. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p122]
- The approach requires reading the complete Goal Setting to the Now dialogue to train the mind to connect goals over time. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p122]
- Skipping intermediate steps and asking only what to do right now fails because the moment is too far from the future to see the key priority clearly. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p123]
- Psychologists observed 262 students in three studies comparing outcome visualization versus process visualization, finding process visualization produced better results. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p124]
- This approach engages strategic thinking needed to plan for and achieve extraordinary results. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p124]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Goal Setting to the Now methodology is designed to counteract the planning fallacy by requiring people to think through all the steps needed to achieve a goal.
