# Four Fundamentals of Advantage

## Summary

Competitive advantage comes from four basic sources: information (knowing something others don't), know-how (possessing skills or patents others lack), position (having reputation, brand, or distribution systems others cannot imitate), and efficiency (based on scale, technology, or experience others cannot easily attain). Without such asymmetries, competitive profitability cannot be expected.

## Key facts

- Advantage comes from information, know-how, position, and efficiency. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p174]
- To expect to make a business work under competition, you need to have an advantage in knowledge or resources. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p174]
- Karl Popper's principle suggests it is better to let 'our theories die in our stead' rather than running every idea to the marketplace test. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p174]
