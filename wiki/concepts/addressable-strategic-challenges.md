# Addressable Strategic Challenges

## Summary

Addressable Strategic Challenges (ASCs) are challenges that are both very important and can be overcome. The crux of a strategic situation normally resides among these challenges. One powerful foundry tool is boiling the situation down to these few critical challenges that can be addressed.

## Key facts

- One of the most powerful foundry tools is boiling the situation down to a few addressable strategic challenges, or ASCs, where the crux normally resides. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p292]
- Searching for the few limited challenges that are both very important and can be overcome is the core of the Strategy Foundry. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p292]
- The question 'Why is this hard?' helps break challenges into pieces small enough to think about by identifying obstacles to dealing with the challenge. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p292]
- An obstacle can be overcome if there is focused effort on doing so; management has often been encouraging a direct assault on metrics rather than obstacles. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p292]
