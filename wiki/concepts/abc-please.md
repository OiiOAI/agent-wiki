# ABC PLEASE

## Summary

ABC PLEASE is a DBT acronym for reducing vulnerability to emotion mind by building a life worth living. A stands for Accumulate positive emotions, B for Build mastery, C for Cope ahead of time with emotional situations, and PLEASE covers treating Physical illness, balancing Eating, avoiding mood-Altering substances, balancing Sleep, and getting Exercise.

## Key facts

- A in ABC PLEASE stands for Accumulate positive emotions—building positive experiences now and in the future. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p271]
- B stands for Build mastery—doing things that make you feel competent and effective to combat helplessness and hopelessness. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p271]
- C stands for Cope ahead of time with emotional situations—rehearsing a plan ahead of time to be prepared to cope skillfully. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p271]
