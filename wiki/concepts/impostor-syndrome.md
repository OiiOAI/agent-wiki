# Impostor Syndrome

## Summary

A psychological phenomenon common among educated and creative people where they are unable to internalize their accomplishments, feeling like a phony who doesn't know what they're doing. Kleon notes that none of us know where the good stuff comes from—we just show up to do our thing.

## Key facts

- Impostor syndrome is defined as a psychological phenomenon in which people are unable to internalize their accomplishments. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p27]
- Kleon states that truly creative people don't know where the good stuff comes from—they just show up to do their thing every day. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p27]
