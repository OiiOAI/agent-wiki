# Impression Management

## Summary

Impression management involves techniques actors use to maintain certain impressions in the face of problems they are likely to encounter. It is oriented to guarding against unexpected actions such as unintended gestures, inopportune intrusions, and faux pas. Goffman identified three main methods: dramaturgical loyalty (fostering in-group loyalty, preventing identification with audience), dramaturgical discipline (avoiding slips, maintaining self-control), and dramaturgical circumspection (planning performances, selecting loyal teammates, preventing audience access to private information).

- First method of impression management is dramaturgical loyalty, including fostering high in-group loyalty, preventing team members from identifying with the audience, and changing audiences periodically. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p269]
- Second method is dramaturgical discipline, such as having presence of mind to avoid slips, maintaining self-control, and managing facial expressions and verbal tone. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p269]
- Third method is dramaturgical circumspection, including determining in advance how a performance should go, planning for emergencies, selecting loyal teammates and good audiences. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p269]
- The audience also has a stake in successful impression management and often acts to save the show through giving attention, avoiding emotional outbursts, and giving consideration to neophyte performers. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p270]
