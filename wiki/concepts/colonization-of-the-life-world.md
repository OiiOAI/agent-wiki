# Colonization of the Life-World

## Summary

Colonization of the life-world is Jürgen Habermas's concept describing how the rationalized structures of the social system (economy and state) increasingly intrude upon and dominate the life-world (the domain of communicative action and mutual understanding). This process involves the extension of formal rationality (instrumental action) into spheres previously governed by substantive rationality (communicative action), threatening free communication and mutual understanding that are essential to social integration.

## Key facts

- Habermas engages in 'paradigm combination,' creating his agency-structure perspective by integrating ideas from action theory and systems theory. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p439]
- The life-world represents an internal perspective—'society conceived from the perspective of the acting subject.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p440]
- The life-world involves a context-forming background of processes of reaching understanding through communicative action. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p440]
- System involves an external perspective viewing society 'from the observer's perspective of someone not involved,' attuned to the interconnection of actions and their functional significance. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p441]
- As system structures evolve, they grow more self-sufficient and exercise more steering capacity over the life-world, limiting communicative processes. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p441]
