# Making Room (TAME)

## Summary

Making Room is an ACT technique for contacting difficult feelings and learning to make room for them instead of obeying or struggling against them. The technique is called TAME, an acronym for four steps: Take note (notice and name what's showing up in the body), Allow (give the feeling permission to be there), Make room (open up to the feeling and let it freely flow through you), and Expand awareness (broaden focus to include the world around you). Every painful feeling tells you something important—it indicates you care and that there's something that matters to you. When there's a gap between the reality you want and the reality you've got, painful feelings arise, and the bigger the reality gap, the greater the pain. The goal is not to eliminate feelings but to acknowledge them and make space for them while continuing to engage with meaningful activities.

## Key facts

- TAME stands for Take note, Allow, Make room, and Expand awareness. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p167]
- Making room for emotions involves deliberately contacting a difficult feeling and learning how to make room for it instead of responding in OBEY or STRUGGLE mode. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p164]
- Every painful feeling tells you that you care and that there's something that really matters to you. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p163]
- The bigger the reality gap between what you want and what you've got, the greater the pain that arises. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p163]
- Making room is not a way to eliminate feelings; it is acknowledging them and allowing them to be present without a struggle. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p150]
