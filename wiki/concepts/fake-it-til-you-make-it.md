# Fake It Till You Make It

## Summary

The concept that creative identity is discovered through action, not contemplation. One should pretend to be making something until one actually makes something. Nobody is born with a style or voice—we learn by copying our heroes.

## Key facts

- Kleon interprets 'fake it till you make it' as either pretending to be something until you are, or pretending to be making something until you actually make something. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p28]
- Patti Smith and Robert Mapplethorpe 'dressed up in all their bohemian gypsy gear' and went to Washington Square Park pretending to be artists until they became real artists. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p29]
- The Beatles started as a cover band, emulating Buddy Holly, Little Richard, Jerry Lee Lewis, and Elvis. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p31]
- Conan O'Brien observed that Johnny Carson tried to be Jack Benny but ended up Johnny Carson, and O'Brien himself tried to be Letterman but ended up Conan O'Brien. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p33]
