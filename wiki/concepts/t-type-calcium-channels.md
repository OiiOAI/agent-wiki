# T-type Calcium Channels

## Summary

T-type (low-threshold) voltage-gated calcium channels are characterized by being inactivated at the resting membrane potential but becoming available for activation when the cell is hyperpolarized. A subsequent depolarization transiently opens these channels, allowing calcium influx that generates low-threshold calcium spikes. These channels are encoded by three related genes (Cav3.1-Cav3.3), with Cav3.1 being predominant in the thalamus. They play critical roles in thalamic oscillations underlying absence seizures and sleep spindles.
