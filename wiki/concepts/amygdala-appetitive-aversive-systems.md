# Amygdala Appetitive and Aversive Systems

## Summary

The amygdala contains distinct appetitive and aversive networks of neurons that mediate emotional behaviors for both positive and negative valences. These networks are anatomically intermingled but form functionally distinct circuits, with separate populations encoding rewarding and aversive unconditioned stimuli and their associated conditioned stimuli.

## Key facts

- Paton et al. established that the amygdala contains separate appetitive and aversive neural systems using trace-conditioning tasks with novel visual conditioned stimuli paired with reward, nothing, or aversive air puff. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p34]
- Cross-correlogram analysis revealed that functional interactions between amygdala neuron pairs at short latencies are significantly more likely when neurons share the same valence preference, indicating valence-encoding neurons form distinct functional circuits. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p35]
- Optogenetic activation of nicotine-responsive (appetitive) or footshock-responsive (aversive) neuronal ensembles in the basolateral amygdala elicits distinct, valence-specific physiological responses including opposite changes in heart and respiratory rates. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p35]
- Photoactivation of footshock-sensitive neurons in BLA can induce freezing when paired with a neutral auditory tone (Pavlovian conditioning) or avoidance of a neutral odor, while nicotine-responsive neuron activation can induce approach behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p35]
- Processing in appetitive and aversive amygdalar networks can both regulate and be regulated by diverse cognitive operations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p5]

## Inferences

- Inference: Despite anatomical intermingling of appetitive and aversive neurons, molecular tools can selectively activate these distinct populations based on their activity patterns, enabling causal investigation of valence-specific behaviors.
- Inference: The US representation in the BLA provides a critical link between sensory CS representations and output pathways that mediate appropriate valence-specific behavior.
- Inference: Valence-specific neural ensembles in the amygdala are sufficient to drive not only innate and learned emotional behaviors but also operant conditioning.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise circuit mechanisms by which expectation and prediction error signals modulate neural processing during learning in the amygdala remain to be fully characterized.
