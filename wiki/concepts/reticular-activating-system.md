# Reticular Activating System

## Summary

The reticular activating system is the net-like core of the brainstem that is necessary for consciousness. Discovered by Moruzzi and Magoun over seventy years ago, it is approximately 525 million years old because it is shared by all vertebrates. Lesions in this core cause coma. The system generates arousal and releases five key neuromodulators: dopamine, noradrenaline, acetylcholine, serotonin, and histamine. It exerts influence upwards into the cortex, unlike the PAG which receives signals from the cortex.

## Key facts

- Moruzzi and Magoun established that consciousness is lost following tiny incisions that disconnect the cortex from the reticulate core of the brainstem. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p114]
- The reticular activating system is approximately 525 million years old because it is shared by all vertebrates. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p114]
- Small lesions in this core cause coma, as demonstrated in brainstem stroke patients where a tiny two cubic-millimetre 'coma-specific' region was identified. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p114]
- The reticular activating system releases five key neuromodulators: dopamine (sourced mainly in the ventral tegmental area), noradrenaline (locus coeruleus complex), acetylcholine (mesopontine tegmentum and basal forebrain), serotonin (raphe nuclei), and histamine (tuberomammillary hypothalamus). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p124]
- The reticular activating system exerts its influence mainly upwards into the cortex, while the cortex transmits signals back down to the PAG. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p127]
- The cortex becomes conscious only to the extent that it is aroused by the brainstem reticular activating system. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p120]
- The reticular activating system and PAG respectively represent the origin and destination of forebrain arousal. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p127]

## Inferences

- Inference: Antidepressants (serotonin boosters), antipsychotics (dopamine blockers), and anti-anxiety drugs (noradrenaline blockers) all act on neurons whose cell bodies are located in the reticular activating system, indicating this region is the source of these neuromodulators and crucial for psychiatric function [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p115-116]
