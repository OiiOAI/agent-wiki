# Reliability and Character

## Summary

Charlie Munger places enormous emphasis on reliability and character as foundations for success. His prescriptions for misery include being unreliable, and he cites Louis Vincent's rule about telling the truth to avoid remembering lies. McDonald's is praised for teaching reliability, and his own success is attributed partly to consistent follow-through.

## Key facts

- Munger states: 'Be reliable. Do not faithfully do what you have engaged to do. If you will only master this one habit, you will more than counterbalance the combined effect of all your virtues.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p147]
- Louis Vincent's rule: 'Tell the truth, and you won't have to remember your lies.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p87]
- Munger praises McDonald's as one of America's most admirable institutions for successfully teaching teenagers to show up reliably for responsible work. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p152]
