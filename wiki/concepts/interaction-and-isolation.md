# Interaction and Isolation

## Summary

Interaction and isolation represent the central strategic theme Boyd developed across his Green Book presentations. While Patterns of Conflict emphasizes isolation (how to sever adversary connections), Organic Design emphasizes interaction (how to maintain internal bonds and environmental engagement). The Strategic Game of ? and ? synthesizes both as a unified strategic principle: one must diminish the adversary's ability to interact with his environment while sustaining and improving one's own interaction.

## Key facts

- Boyd asserted that interaction permits vitality and growth while isolation leads to decay and disintegration. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p261]
- Boyd identified physical isolation as occurring when one fails to gain support in the form of matter-energy-information; mental isolation when one fails to discern or make sense of what's happening; moral isolation when one fails to abide by acceptable codes of conduct. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p261]
- Boyd explained that one can physically isolate adversaries by severing their communications with the outside world and internal communications to one another; mentally by presenting ambiguous, deceptive, or novel situations while operating at tempo they cannot match; morally by exposing their violations of their own stated codes. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p264]
- Boyd stated that physical interaction occurs through exchanging matter-energy-information; mental interaction through generating images matching unfolding events; moral interaction through living by stated codes and upholding expected standards. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p264]
- Interaction and isolation are the key terms Boyd coins for the dynamic at the most abstract level of strategic behavior. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Repeated mismatches between perception and reality lead to isolation where the opponent becomes unable to determine what is being done to them and how they should respond. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- An adversary's initial confusion degenerates into paralyzing panic when they cannot eliminate mismatches between perception and reality. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Isolation occurs when the social environment becomes hostile to the aims and actions of the opponent, negating support they may depend upon. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Closed systems are incapable of adaptation; the operational aim should ensure opponents cannot rid themselves of menacing anomalies. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]

## Inferences

- Inference: The interaction/isolation duality represents Boyd's most abstract formulation of the strategic problem, applicable at all levels of organization and across all time scales of competition.
- Inference: The interaction-isolation dynamic represents Boyd's abstraction of the essence of strategic success and failure, applicable across all levels of conflict.
