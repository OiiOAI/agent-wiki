# Optical Disturbances

## Summary

Optical disturbances are changes in the ambient optic array that specify events in the environment. They are not copies or projections of physical motions but lawful changes in array structure that correspond to events. Ten types are distinguished: progressive deletion/accretion, progressive decrementing/incrementing of gaps, shearing or slippage, perspective transformation, magnification/minification, deformation, emergence of new structure, nullification of texture, substitution of new texture for old, and change of color structure.

## Key facts

- Optical disturbances are not copies of physical motions—an optical motion has little in common with a physical motion and should not even be called a motion. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p132]
- Magnification of a form in the array means the approach of something (looming), and minification means recession; when a visual solid angle approaches a hemisphere, imminent contact is specified. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p127]
- The beginning and end of a disturbance in the array are concurrent with the beginning and end of an event in the environment—there is no latency or delay. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p132]
- Some optical disturbances preserve one-to-one correspondence of units over time (transformations, deformations), while others do not (deletion, accretion, emergence, nullification). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p131]

## Inferences

- Inference: The variety of optical disturbances suggests that the visual system must be sensitive to multiple types of structural change, not merely to motions of points on a surface, challenging traditional approaches to motion perception.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The mathematical analysis of invariants preserved under disturbances with non-persistence of units has scarcely been attempted and requires further development.
