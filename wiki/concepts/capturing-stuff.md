# Capturing Stuff

## Summary

Capturing is the process of gathering all items that have your attention into a collection system called 'in'. This includes physical items, digital inputs, and mental thoughts to create a complete inventory of open loops.

## Key facts

- Capturing is complete when you can easily see the outer edges of the inventory of everything that still has some of your attention. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p165]
- Until you have captured everything that has your attention, some part of you will not trust that you are working with the whole picture. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p147]
- Mental gathering or the mind sweep involves writing out each thought on a separate sheet of paper to capture everything in mental RAM. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p157]
- Four categories of things can stay where they are: supplies, reference material, decoration, and equipment. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p149]
