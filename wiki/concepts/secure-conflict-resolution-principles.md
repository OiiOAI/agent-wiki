# Secure Principles for Conflict Resolution

## Summary

The five secure principles for resolving conflict are: show basic concern for the other person's well-being, maintain focus on the problem at hand, refrain from generalizing the conflict, be willing to engage, and effectively communicate feelings and needs. These principles can be learned and help couples manage both bread-and-butter conflicts (daily practical matters) and intimacy-centered conflicts. Conflict resolution is influenced by attachment style, with insecure individuals struggling to adopt these principles due to their underlying fears about responsiveness and rejection.
