# Escape

## Summary

Escape refers to creating space to think and explore away from the constant demands of work and life. Essentialists recognize that they need space to discern the essential few from the trivial many. This requires designing time and physical space for concentrated thinking.

## Key facts

- We need space to escape in order to discern the essential few from the trivial many, but in our time-starved era we don't get that space by default—only by design. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p60]
- Jeff Weiner, CEO of LinkedIn, schedules up to two hours of blank space on his calendar every day to ensure he is in charge of his own day instead of being at the mercy of it. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p65]
- Bill Gates takes 'Think Week' twice a year to seclude himself for a week and do nothing but read and think about the bigger picture. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p67]
- In order to have focus we need to escape to focus. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p62]
