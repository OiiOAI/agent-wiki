# Value of Consciousness

## Summary

Consciousness is valuable because it extends the mind's reach and improves the organism's ability to achieve homeostasis in complex environments. While much adequate life regulation occurs nonconsciously, consciousness enables organisms to link automatic regulation with imagination, allowing for planning, prediction, and novel adaptive responses. The effectiveness of consciousness comes from its connection to the nonconscious proto-self, creating individual concern that orients all problem-solving toward self-preservation.

## Key facts

- Consciousness is valuable because it introduces a new means of achieving homeostasis, linking the world of automatic regulation with the world of imagination. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p303]
- Consciousness allows organisms to represent the context of situations, stop when appropriate, and engage in reasoning-based decision making that extends beyond nonconscious biases. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p302]

## Inferences

- Inference: The 'Faustian bargain' of consciousness—the price of knowing risk, danger, pain, and pleasure—may be the cost of evolution's investment in a more sophisticated homeostatic regulator.
