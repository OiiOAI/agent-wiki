# Precision weighting

## Summary

Precision weighting is the mechanism by which the brain adjusts the influence of sensory signals on perceptual inference based on their estimated reliability. High precision signals have strong influence; low precision signals have weak influence. Attention is described as increasing estimated precision.

## Key facts

- Precision weighting involves transiently altering the estimated precision of sensory signals to increase or decrease their influence on perceptual inference. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p109]
- Down-weighting estimated precision means sensory signals have less influence on updating best guesses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p109]
- Paying attention is equivalent to increasing the precision weighting on corresponding sensory signals, increasing their estimated reliability. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p110]
- Precision weighting plays an essential role in choreographing the dance between predictions and prediction errors needed to reach a perceptual best guess. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p109]
- Precision weighting is achieved by altering priors on precisions, so-called hyperpriors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p116]

## Inferences

- Inference: Since attention increases precision weighting, distraction and inattentional blindness result from precision weighting being directed elsewhere.
