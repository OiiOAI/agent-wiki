# Ego Ideal

## Summary

The ego ideal is a critical agency within the ego that develops from the original narcissism of the childish ego. Freud ascribes to it the functions of self-observation, moral conscience, censorship of dreams, and the chief influence in repression. In group psychology, the leader occupies the position of the ego ideal for group members, explaining their submission and the group member's loss of critical faculty.

- The ego ideal is the heir to original narcissism in which the childish ego enjoyed self-sufficiency. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5683]
