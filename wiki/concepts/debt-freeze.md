# Debt Freeze

## Summary

A Debt Freeze is a method to immediately stop accumulating debt while continuing the Profit First habit. It guides businesses through a rapid paydown of accumulated debt without requiring a crash diet approach. The goal is to cut costs without compromising the business by distinguishing between expenses that generate profit (P), can be replaced with cheaper alternatives (R), or are unnecessary (U).

## Key facts

- A Debt Freeze stops accumulating new debt and rapidly pays down existing debt while continuing the Profit First habit. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p137]
- Label expenses with P for (P)rofit-generating, R for (R)eplaceable with cheaper alternatives, or U for (U)necessary. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p138]
- Work a plan to cut expenses until operating at 10 percent below the target Operating Expenses TAP. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p139]
- Call each credit card company and ask for a new card with a new number to stop automatic charges. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p143]
- Use 99 percent of profit allocation toward wiping out debt, with remaining 1 percent for celebration. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p149]
- The Debt Freeze means more than just 'no more new debt'—it includes cutting unnecessary expenditures and finding ways to become more profitable. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p217]
- Steps include: print and mark up all debt-related documents, calculate how much needs to be cut, end automatic withdrawals, end recurring expenses, and cancel credit cards. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p121]
- The 'just one more day' technique helps resist temporary solutions that create long-term debt problems. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p126]
- Labor costs should be reduced by cross-training existing employees rather than immediate layoffs. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p123]
- The Debt Snowball method (paying smallest debts first) builds psychological momentum toward eliminating larger debts. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p131]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Debt Freeze prevents the 'crash diet' mentality that leads to temporary fixes rather than permanent change.
