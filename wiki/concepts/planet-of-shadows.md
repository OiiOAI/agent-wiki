# The Planet of Shadows and Asteroid Belt

## Summary

The Planet of Shadows represents physical and mental health, where illness and poor maintenance create shadows. Malfunction (illness requiring medical treatment) is represented by the Asteroid Belt encircling the planet, while dysfunction (the machine working but not being used properly or looked after) is represented by the Planet of Shadows itself. The text covers physical maintenance, mental shape, recuperation, recovery from emotional injury, and the importance of sleep and relaxation for Chimp management.

## Key facts

- Malfunction means you are ill and the machine is not working properly, requiring a doctor for treatment—represented by the Asteroid Belt encircling the Planet of Shadows. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p154]
- Dysfunction is when the machine is working fine but is not being used properly or looked after—it needs maintenance and is represented by the Planet of Shadows. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p154]
- The Chimp's physical maintenance default is indifference and an easy life, preferring immediate gratification and dismissing consequences, while the Human knows what it wants and takes great pleasure in being fit and eating sensibly. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p155]
- Recuperation exists at three levels: relaxing (a few minutes out of the day to wind down), resting (significant periods during the day to completely unwind), and sleeping. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p156]
- If you do not allow yourself relaxation, resting, and sleeping on a regular daily basis, your brain will start sending blood supply and all decision-making to your Chimp, resulting in irritability, silly mistakes, rash decisions, and unstable mood swings. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p156]
- Recovery from emotional injury follows a general time period of three to six months with very specific stages of grief reaction, and talking through the event with friends helps the Chimp come to terms with the injury. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p157]
- During night hours of eleven at night to seven in the morning, you are in Chimp mode with emotional and irrational thinking; any thoughts during night waking are from the Chimp and are likely disturbing, catastrophic, and lacking perspective. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p157]
- Mental illness occurs when neuro-transmitters including serotonin, noradrenaline, dopamine, acetylcholine, and GABA get out of balance in the brain. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p158]
