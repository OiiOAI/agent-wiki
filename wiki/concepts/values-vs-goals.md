# Values vs Goals

## Summary

Values represent the heart's deepest desires for how one wants to treat oneself, others, and the world—personal qualities one wants to bring into play in actions and words, such as being open, honest, loving, or caring. Goals are things aimed for in the future: things to get, have, achieve, or do, falling into three categories: emotional goals (how we want to feel), behavioral goals (how we want to behave), and outcome goals (what we want to get or have). The critical distinction is that values can be lived right now regardless of circumstances, whereas goals may be unachievable due to external constraints. This makes values instantly empowering in ways goals cannot be, as they connect us with what is within our control—our actions and how we choose to treat others.
