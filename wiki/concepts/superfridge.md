# SuperFridge

## Summary

SuperFridge is Fogg's specific technique for redesigning the food environment at home to support healthy eating. It involves preparing healthy food in advance and arranging it visibly in the refrigerator so healthy choices are easy and junk food is eliminated. Fogg and his partner Denny each lost over 15% of their body weight using this method.

## Key facts

- SuperFridge involves filling glass containers with pre-washed, pre-cut healthy foods ready to eat. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p209]
- Fogg and his partner Denny each lost more than 15% of their body weight using SuperFridge and have maintained their ideal weight for years. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p209]
- The investment in SuperFridge includes time shopping and preparing food weekly on Sundays. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p209]
- Nothing is put in SuperFridge that conflicts with the eating game plan—external foods like corn chips are eliminated. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p209]
- SuperFridge resulted in weight loss, better sleep, and better energy without requiring willpower. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p209]
