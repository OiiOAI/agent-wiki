# The Stone of Life

## Summary

The Stone of Life is the ultimate reference point within the Computer, containing a person's Truths of Life (evidence-based beliefs about how the world works), Values (principles and ideals), and Life Force (the essential meaning and purpose of life). These elements stabilize the entire psychological system when properly established.

## Key facts

- The Stone of Life contains three elements: Truths of Life, Values, and Life Force. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p62]
- Truths of Life are evidence-based beliefs about how the world works, such as 'Life is not fair' or 'The goal posts move.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p62]
- Values are personal judgment calls reflecting moral and ethical principles, such as 'lying is wrong' or 'family is more important than work.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p63]
- The Life Force is discovered by asking what advice you would give a great-great-grandchild about how to live, representing the essence of one's existence. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p63]
- The Stone of Life holds the key to stabilising the entire Psychological Universe. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p75]
- The Stone of Life is described as the most powerful stabiliser for the Psychological Mind. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p79]
- The Stone of Life consists of absolute beliefs or truths that a person decides to live by. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p84]
- When the Chimp receives information, it checks the Computer before reacting; well-rehearsed truths in the Stone of Life can stop the Chimp from reacting. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p84]
- The reaction speed of the Chimp checking the Computer is estimated at less than 0.02 of a second. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p84]
- Reinforcing the Stone of Life by making it visible every day is a major settling influence. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p78]

## Inferences
