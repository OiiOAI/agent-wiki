# Absurd Dreams

## Summary

Absurd dreams are dreams whose manifest content appears irrational or nonsensical. Freud argues that absurdity in dreams is not meaningless but serves specific psychological purposes, particularly the representation of criticism, ridicule, or derision present in the latent dream thoughts. The dream-work intentionally creates absurd elements when the dream thoughts contain judgments that something is 'absurd' or foolish. Absurdity functions alongside other methods of representing contradiction, such as reversal of relations or motor inhibition.

## Key facts

- Absurdity in dreams serves as a method of representing contradiction in the dream thoughts, alongside reversal and motor inhibition. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p388]
- Absurdity in a dream is intended to reproduce the mood of the dream thoughts, combining derision or laughter with the contradiction, not simply expressing 'no'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p388]
- Judgements and criticisms appearing in dreams are derived from the dream thoughts rather than representing independent intellectual activity during sleep. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p398]
- The dream-work is capable of intentionally fabricating absurdity for which there was no occasion in the material, when the dream thoughts include criticism or ridicule. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p385]
- Absurdity in dreams about dead relatives indicates extreme repudiation and serves to represent repressed thoughts the dreamer would prefer to regard as unthinkable. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p384]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud's analysis of absurd dreams suggests that apparent nonsense in dream content often masks criticism or mockery of specific persons or ideas present in the unconscious mind.
- Inference: The mechanism of creating absurd dreams parallels the use of humor and parody in waking life, where forbidden or critical thoughts are expressed through seemingly irrational content to bypass censorship.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether all dreams deemed absurd by dreamers necessarily contain critical or derisive material in their latent thoughts, or whether some absurdity serves other purposes not fully specified.
