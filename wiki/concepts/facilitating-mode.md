# Facilitating Mode

## Summary

Facilitating is one of three team interaction modes in Team Topologies. This mode helps teams avoid cross-team challenges and detects problems, supporting smoother inter-team collaboration.

## Key facts

- Facilitating is one of three team interaction modes in Team Topologies. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2192]
- Facilitating helps teams avoid cross-team challenges. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2195]
- Facilitating helps detect problems. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2195]
- Facilitating mode means helping or being helped by another team to clear impediments. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2510-L2520]
- Facilitating is the main operating mode of an enabling team. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2520-L2530]
- The facilitating team does not take part in building main software systems but focuses on quality of interactions between other teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2540-L2550]
- Constraint: A team should expect to use facilitating with a small number of other teams simultaneously. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2560-L2570]
- Typical uses: An enabling team helping a stream-aligned, complicated-subsystem, or platform team; or other teams helping a stream-aligned team. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2570-L2580]

## Inferences

- Inference: Teams experiencing frequent cross-team coordination problems may benefit from adopting facilitating interactions.
