# Disliking/Hating Tendency

## Summary

Disliking/Hating Tendency is the obverse of Liking/Loving Tendency, causing humans to be born to dislike and hate as triggered by normal and abnormal forces in life. As a result, history contains almost continuous war. Disliking/Hating Tendency acts as a conditioning device that causes the disliker to ignore virtues, dislike associated people and products, and distort facts to facilitate hatred. This often makes mediation between opponents locked in hatred difficult or impossible.
