# Melioration

## Summary

Melioration is a behavioral principle stating that choice behavior should be biased toward the option with the higher return. The model selects the better option (in terms of return) and acts according to the melioration principle. However, because decision is not deterministic, the model achieves matching through 'probabilistic' melioration rather than deterministic maximization.
