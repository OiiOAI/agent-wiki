# Will Rogers Phenomenon

## Summary

The Will Rogers phenomenon (stage migration) occurs when moving items between groups raises the average of both groups. Oklahoma comedian Will Rogers reportedly said that Oklahomans who move to California raise both states' average IQ. New medical screening techniques that detect smaller tumors migrate healthier patients into early-stage groups.

## Key facts

- The Will Rogers phenomenon raises averages in both groups by moving a mediocre performer from one group to another. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p133]
- New screening techniques reveal minuscule tumors that migrate patients erroneously diagnosed as healthy into stage-one groups, increasing the group's average life expectancy without genuine medical improvement.
