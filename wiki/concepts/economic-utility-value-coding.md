# Economic Utility and Value Coding

## Summary

Economic utility represents a formal, cardinal measure of subjective value that reflects individual preference structures. Dopamine neurons encode not just objective reward values but formal economic utility, integrating factors such as reward magnitude, probability, delay, and risk into a unified subjective value signal.

## Key facts

- Risk attitudes are assessed by the certainty equivalent (CE) in psychophysical choices against an adjustable safe reward, where the physical amount at choice indifference indicates subjective value. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p26]
- In low reward ranges, rhesus monkeys show CEs higher than expected values (EVs), indicating risk seeking, whereas in higher ranges CEs are lower than EVs, indicating risk avoidance. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p26]
- Dopamine neurons show stronger responses to more preferred liquid rewards of the same amount and increased responses to risky rewards of identical expected value, reflecting the enhancing influence of risk on subjective value. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p26]
- Monkey choices satisfy first-order stochastic dominance (preferring gambles whose lower outcome equals the safe reward) and second-order stochastic dominance (preferring mean-preserving riskier gambles with higher expected utility), demonstrating meaningful risk incorporation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p26]
- Dopamine responses to free reward increase nonlinearly with reward amount following the behavioral utility function, demonstrating a prediction error in utility rather than marginal utility. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p27]

## Inferences

- Inference: The dopamine utility prediction error signal bridges the gap between animal learning theory (prediction error) and economic decision theory (utility), providing a neural substrate for economic choice optimization.
- Inference: Dopamine responses encode chosen value prediction error (experienced value minus predicted value of the chosen option), making it suitable for updating decision variables for subsequent choices.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether dopamine neurons in humans code utility in the same way as demonstrated in nonhuman primates requires further investigation.
