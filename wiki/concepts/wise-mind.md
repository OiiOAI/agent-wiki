# Wise Mind

## Summary

Wise Mind is a core DBT concept representing the integration of reasonable mind (cool, rational, task-focused) and emotion mind (hot, mood-dependent, emotion-focused). It is the inner wisdom within each person that brings together left brain and right brain, seeing the value of both reason and emotion. Wise Mind is accessed through mindfulness practices such as breath awareness, guided imagery, and focused attention.

## Key facts

- Wise Mind integrates reasonable mind and emotion mind, bringing left brain and right brain together. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p50]
- Reasonable mind is characterized as cool, rational, and task-focused, while emotion mind is characterized as hot, mood-dependent, and emotion-focused. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p50]
- Wise Mind practice exercises include attending to breath, imagining being a flake of stone on a lake, walking down an inner spiral staircase, dropping into pauses between breaths, breathing 'wise' in and 'mind' out, and asking Wise Mind questions. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p51]
- Goals of mindfulness practice include reducing suffering, increasing happiness, increasing control of the mind, and experiencing reality as it is. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p45]
