# Pre-commitment

## Summary

Pre-commitment is a psychological technique for overcoming temptation by binding your future self to a course of action before you are tempted. Hari used this technique for his 3-month digital detox, removing all internet-capable devices and tying himself to the 'mast' like Odysseus.

## Key facts

- Pre-commitment comes from Homer's Odyssey, where Ulysses had his crew tie him to the mast so he could hear the sirens' song without being able to dive in. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p26-27]
- Yale psychologist Molly Crockett showed in 2013 that making a clear pledge to resist temptation dramatically increases success in holding out for better rewards. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p27]
- Hari used this technique by going completely offline for 3 months in Provincetown—his phone and laptop left with a friend in Boston, a dumb phone with no internet, no cable TV, and the modem removed from his rental house. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p29-30]
- He ordered the last remaining cellphone in the US that couldn't access the web—the Jitterbug, designed for elderly people, which doubles as a medical emergency device. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p25]
- Hari acknowledged this extreme detox couldn't be a long-term solution for most people, including those who can't afford to leave jobs and responsibilities to live in a beach house. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p22-23]

## Inferences

- Inference: Pre-commitment is a valuable individual strategy but insufficient as a systemic solution to the attention crisis.
