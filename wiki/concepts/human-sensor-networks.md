# Human Sensor Networks

## Summary

Human sensor networks are cognitively, culturally, and experientially diverse groups of people who assess situations independently to detect weak signals that individual decision-makers might miss. The approach creates rich data through micro-narratives and provides real-time situational assessment, option identification, and micro-scenarios planning. Technology can be used to make these networks available in real time.

## Key facts

- The SenseMaker approach to human sensor networks was developed for counter-terrorism in DARPA projects before and after 9/11 to create objective decision-making using abductive logic. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p32]
- Creating culturally, experientially, and educationally diverse networks increases confidence in decision-making and the probability of identifying outliers or weak signals. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p32]
- MassSense is a mass situational assessment method where human agents receive and interpret information creating descriptive micro-narratives of their assessment. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p29]
- Attitudinal mapping is an alternative to traditional surveys that works by asking respondents a question to generate micro-narratives which are self-signified by the respondent. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p29]
- Case studies include the City of Malmö's horizon scanning with 40 young people, and Wales' Future Generations Commissioner's People's Platform. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p31]
