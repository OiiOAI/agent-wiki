# Cathedral Effect

## Summary

The Cathedral Effect describes how physical environments shape thinking — spaces with high ceilings encourage abstract thinking while low ceilings promote concrete thinking. The author applies this concept to digital environments, arguing that knowledge workers should intentionally design their digital workspaces to support the kinds of thinking they want to do.

## Key facts

- Studies show that high-ceiling spaces like classic churches evoke abstract thinking, while low-ceiling workshops promote concrete thinking. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p74]
