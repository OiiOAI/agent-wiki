# Profit Distribution

## Summary

Profit Distribution is the quarterly process of sharing accumulated profit with equity owners. Fifty percent of the PROFIT account balance is distributed to owners while the remaining 50 percent remains as a reserve. Profit is a reward for owning the business, distinct from Owner Compensation which is pay for working in the business.

## Key facts

- On the first day of each new quarter, take 50 percent of what is in the PROFIT account and leave the other 50 percent intact as a reserve. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p125]
- Profit distribution is an award to equity owners for having the courage and risk tolerance to start the business, distinct from Owner Compensation which is pay for working. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p125]
- The PROFIT account serves three purposes: 1) Monetary reward for equity owners, 2) A metric to measure growth, 3) Cash reserve for emergencies. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p125]
- The profit distribution can never go back to the company; plowback of profit means you aren't operating efficiently enough. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p126]
- The money from profit distribution must be used only for personal benefit as a reward for having the guts to invest in your business. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p126]

## Inferences

- Inference: Taking profit distributions regularly reinforces the habit of profitability and creates emotional connection to business success.
