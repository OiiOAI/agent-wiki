# Life-Lie

## Summary

In Adlerian psychology, a 'life-lie' (Lebenslüge) is a fabricated story about one's past or future that serves as a way to avoid living earnestly in the present. The greatest life-lie is to not live in the here and now, instead looking at past and future and believing one has been able to see something.

## Key facts

- The greatest life-lie of all is to not live here and now—looking at the past and future, casting a dim light on one's entire life, and believing one has been able to see something. - One tells a great lie to one's life by turning away from the here and now and shining a light only on invented pasts and futures. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p279]
