# Intuitive Physics

## Summary

Intuitive physics is a domain-specific cognitive system that allows humans to understand basic physical principles of objects in the world, including object permanence, gravity, cohesion, and solidity. Infants demonstrate understanding of these principles early in development.

## Key facts

- By five months of age, babies expect objects to be permanent and not to disappear when put out of sight. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p267]
- Infants expect objects to be cohesive and maintain the same shape if they pass behind a screen and reemerge. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p267]
- Infants expect objects to move along continuous paths and not to travel across gaps in space. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p267]
- Infants expect objects not to move on their own without something touching them, and to be solid and not pass through other objects. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p267]

## Inferences

- Inference: Humans may be unique in reasoning about unobservable causal forces like gravity, unlike other animals which understand observable physical properties.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The extent to which nonhuman animals share intuitive physics is not yet fully known.
