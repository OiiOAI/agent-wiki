# Infinite Scroll

## Summary

A user interface design invented by Aza Raskin that automatically loads more content when users reach the bottom of a webpage, eliminating natural stopping points. This removes the moment of decision that would otherwise prompt users to ask whether they want to continue. It has been adopted across social media platforms and is credited with significantly increasing time spent on these sites.

## Key facts

- Aza Raskin invented infinite scroll, which automatically loads content when users reach the bottom of a page, removing the active choice to click to the next page. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p130]
- Raskin estimates that infinite scroll causes an additional 200,000 human lifetimes worth of time to be spent scrolling every single day. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p131]
- Raskin identified that infinite scroll 'catches your impulses before your brain has a chance to really get involved and make a decision.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p173]

## Inferences

- Inference: inite scroll represents a fundamental design choice that prioritizes engagement metrics over user autonomy, and could theoretically be reversed if business incentives changed.
