# Chronic Stress Management

## Summary

Chronic stress occurs when someone has been stressed for a long period and learns to live with it, potentially causing physical changes including altered hormone and chemical systems, damage to health, lowered immune system, and possible depressive illness or anxiety. Prevention involves using time sensibly, being assertive, having realistic expectations, taking responsibility, addressing problems as they occur, and regularly settling the Chimp. When dealing with chronic stress, solutions should address three areas: personal perceptions and attitudes, circumstances and settings, and other people involved.
