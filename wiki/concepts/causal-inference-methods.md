# Causal Inference Methods in Practice

## Summary

The three main practical tools for causal inference discussed in the book are back-door adjustment, front-door adjustment, and instrumental variables. These methods allow researchers to extract causal relationships from data by identifying patterns in causal diagrams. The book positions these as the 'workhorses of causal inference in practice' that enable immediate answers to causal queries by examining the structure of causal diagrams.

## Key facts

- Back-door adjustment, front-door adjustment, and instrumental variables are identified as the workhorses of causal inference in practice. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L249-L251]
