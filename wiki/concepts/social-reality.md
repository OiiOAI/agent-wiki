# Social Reality

## Summary

Social reality refers to categories and concepts that exist only for those equipped to perceive them, created through collective agreement and naming. Like sounds, colors, flowers, and weeds, emotions are socially real rather than physically real. They exist in human minds conjured by human brains wired through culture, not in nature independent of observers.

## Key facts

- Social reality exists for those equipped to perceive it, created through collective agreement and naming. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p150]
- Emotions are real in the same manner as sounds, colors, flowers, and weeds—all constructed in the brain of a perceiver. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p151]
- Emotion concepts have social reality, existing in human minds conjured by human brains. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p154]
- Money, reputation, laws, government, friendship, and beliefs are socially real and people live and die for them. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p160]
- The distinction between real-in-nature versus illusory is a false dichotomy for emotions. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p154]
- Social reality is the human superpower; only humans can create and share purely mental concepts using words. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p286]
- Every psychological concept is social reality; we debate differences between 'will power,' 'tenacity,' and 'grit' as if they were distinct in nature, rather than constructions shared through collective intentionality. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p311]
- The law is about creating and enforcing social reality; empathic claims about pain are fundamentally claims about whose rights matter and whose humanity matters. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p266]
- Social reality is a Faustian bargain: culture works most smoothly if we believe in our mental creations without realizing our involvement, but this same superpower impedes our understanding of ourselves. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p311]
- Culture wires the brain; ideas get under the skin simply by sticking around long enough, and once an idea is hardwired, people might not be in a position to easily reject it. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p274]

## Inferences

- Inference: Because emotions are social reality rather than physical reality, questions of objective accuracy in emotion perception are unanswerable; consensus replaces measurement.
- Inference: Social reality explains how stereotypes can become self-fulfilling prophecies: stereotypes affect behavior, which creates evidence that confirms the stereotype, reinforcing the social reality.
- Inference: The First Amendment was founded on the notion that free speech produces a war of ideas allowing truth to prevail, but its authors did not know that culture wires the brain.
