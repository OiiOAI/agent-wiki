# Enabling Constraints

## Summary

Enabling constraints are limitations that actually foster emergent system behavior that would not occur without them. Unlike fixed or governing constraints, enabling constraints are on the Unordered side of Cynefin and result in emergent, unpredictable outcomes.

## Key facts

- In Cynefin's Complex domain, enabling constraints allow emergent system behavior that would not be possible without limitations. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p217]
- Alicia Juarrero cited the human knee's inability to bend forward as an enabling constraint that makes walking and running easier. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p217]
- Trent Hone documents how Nimitz's order to establish CICs served as an enabling constraint that fostered distributed innovation. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p230]
- Steve Holt notes that TOC does not use the term 'enabling constraint' but refers to similar concepts as 'Injections' - actions to improve system performance. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p218]

## Inferences

- Inference: Enabling constraints represent a paradoxical management approach where limitations can actually expand possibilities.
