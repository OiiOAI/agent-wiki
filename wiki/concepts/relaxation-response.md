# Relaxation Response

## Summary

The body's natural counterbalance to the stress response, which resets the body to its pre-emergency state after a threat has passed. Meditation activates this response, breaking the cycle of chronic stress.

## Key facts

- The relaxation response is the body's built-in antidote to the stress response. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p81]
- The relaxation response breaks the cycle of chronic stress and brings you back to equilibrium. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p81]
- Meditation triggers the parasympathetic response, which eases the body into a less tense state. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p14]
- Meditation lowers cortisol levels, the main hormone implicated in the body's stress response. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p14]
