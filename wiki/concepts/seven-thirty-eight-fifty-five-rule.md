# 7-38-55 Percent Rule

## Summary

The 7-38-55 rule states that only 7% of meaning in communication comes from words, 38% from tone of voice, and 55% from body language and face. For negotiators, this means body language and tone are the most powerful assessment tools for detecting lies and measuring genuine agreement.

## Key facts

- Only 7% of a message is based on words, 38% on tone of voice, and 55% on body language and face, according to UCLA professor Albert Mehrabian's research. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p155]
- When tone of voice or body language does not align with literal meaning, the speaker may be lying or unconvinced. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p155]
- Labels can be used to discover the source of incongruence between verbal and nonverbal communication. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p155]
