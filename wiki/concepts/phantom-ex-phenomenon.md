# Phantom Ex Phenomenon

## Summary

The phantom ex phenomenon is a consequence of devaluing one's romantic relationship where, after putting time and distance between themselves and a former partner, feelings of love and admiration return. Once at a safe distance, the threat of intimacy is gone and one no longer feels the need to suppress true feelings, leading to idealization of the past partner. This serves as a powerful deactivating strategy, allowing avoidant individuals to block themselves from getting close to someone new by keeping the phantom ex on a pedestal.

## Key facts

- Once avoidant individuals put time and distance between themselves and a partner they've lost interest in, the feelings of love and admiration often return. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p103]
- At safe distance from a past partner, the threat of intimacy is gone and one no longer needs to suppress true feelings, allowing recall of all the ex's great qualities. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p103]
- The phantom ex acts as a deactivating strategy that blocks getting close to someone else; even knowing they're out there makes any new partner seem insignificant by comparison. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p103]
- To stop using phantom ex as a deactivating strategy, one must acknowledge that this person is not and never was a viable option by remembering how critical one was of the relationship and leery of committing. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p106]
