# Sleep Kit and Body Profiles

## Summary

The Sleep Kit encompasses the sleeping surface and environment. The book introduces three body profiles for sleep—Ectomorph (lean), Mesomorph (medium athletic), and Endomorph (larger build)—each requiring different mattress support levels. The recommended sleeping position is the foetal position on the non-dominant side. A practical mattress check-up involves testing alignment in standing foetal position on the surface. Pillows are described as 'inner soles for mattresses that don't fit.' The mattress industry is noted to lack regulation, with spring counts and orthopaedic claims being marketing rather than scientific measures.

## Key facts

- There are three body types: Ectomorph (lean build), Mesomorph (medium athletic), and Endomorph (larger build), each requiring different mattress support. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p83]
- The recommended sleeping position is the foetal position on the non-dominant side—right-handed sleeps on left side and vice versa. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p85]
- The British Snoring & Sleep Apnoea Association states that individuals who sleep on their back are more likely to snore or have increased apnoea than side sleepers. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p85]
- The mattress check-up tests: if there's a gap of 6cm or more between head and mattress with aligned vertebrae, the mattress is too firm; if hip drops and head is raised, it's too soft. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p87]
- A pillow is described as 'an inner sole for a mattress that does not fit'—compensating for incorrect mattress firmness. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p88]
- The bedding industry has little regulation; manufacturers can claim 'orthopaedic' properties without clinical testing. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p81]
- Gary Pallister's back condition improved after changing his mattress, which was aggravating rather than helping his condition. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p82]

## Inferences

- Inference: The emphasis on side sleeping and foetal position explains why so many people struggle with sleep quality despite getting adequate hours.
- Inference: Buying decisions based on price and marketing claims rather than body-profile fit leads to poor sleep outcomes.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Individual variations within body types may require adjustments to these general guidelines.
