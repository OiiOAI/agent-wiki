# Children's Lies as Psychological Phenomena

## Summary

Freud analyzes two cases of children lying under the influence of excessive feelings of love. These lies become momentous when they lead to misunderstanding between the child and the person loved, and they reveal unconscious incestuous wishes.

## Key facts

- Children's lies under the influence of excessive feelings of love should cause those in charge to reflect rather than be angry. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4023]
- A girl who took money from her father without permission represented a physical surrender or erotic relation; her father's punishment was experienced as a rejection of her unconscious tender feelings toward him. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4024]
- The appropriation of money from the father was equivalent to a declaration of love, and the phantasy that her father was her lover was so seductive that it overcame the prohibition. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4024]
- A girl who used compasses to draw a perfect circle at school was identified with her father who was an excellent draughtsman; she wanted to boast about his abilities.
