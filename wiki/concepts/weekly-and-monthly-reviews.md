# Weekly and Monthly Reviews

## Summary

Weekly and Monthly Reviews are regular check-ins for maintaining a Second Brain. The Weekly Review involves clearing inboxes (email, calendar, desktop, notes) and choosing tasks for the week. The Monthly Review is more reflective, involving reviewing and updating goals, projects, areas of responsibility, and reprioritizing tasks.

## Key facts

- The Weekly Review was pioneered by David Allen in his book Getting Things Done. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p184]
- The Weekly Review Checklist includes: clear email inbox, check calendar, clear computer desktop, clear notes inbox, choose tasks for the week. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p185]
- The Monthly Review Checklist includes: review goals, review project list, review areas of responsibility, review someday/maybe tasks, reprioritize tasks. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p187]
