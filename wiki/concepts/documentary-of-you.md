# The Documentary of You

## Summary

The 'documentary of you' (also called 'The Big Story' or self-concept) is a highly biased mental construction of who we are, based on a tiny fraction of our experiences. Like a documentary about Africa is not Africa itself, the documentary of you is not you. This concept helps people unhook from harsh self-judgment by recognizing that thoughts about ourselves are just mental constructions.

## Key facts

- The mind makes an incredibly biased documentary, cutting out over 99.99 percent of everything we have done in our lives. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p217]
- The subtitle of that documentary is typically 'I'm not good enough' and we tend to believe we are that documentary. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p217]
- A self-concept enables us to reflect on ourselves, which is essential for personal growth, but we should hold it lightly rather than let it hook us. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p218]
- We should not try to convince ourselves we are 'a good person' or prove our self-worth; we can see judgments as words and pictures inside our head and let them come and go. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p218]
