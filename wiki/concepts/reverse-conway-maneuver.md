# Reverse Conway Maneuver

## Summary

The Reverse Conway Maneuver is a technique described in Team Topologies that leverages Conway's Law in reverse. Instead of accepting the team's communication structure as determining system design, organizations deliberately design desired team structures to produce the desired system architecture. It helps maintain cohesive structures with clear boundaries and loose coupling.

## Key facts

- The reverse Conway maneuver is described in Team Topologies. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L427]
- The reverse Conway maneuver leverages Conway's Law to help maintain cohesive structures with clear boundaries and loose coupling. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L427-L428]
- The reverse Conway maneuver seeks natural and relatively independent system (sub)structure to align teams to. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L426-L427]
- The reverse Conway maneuver involves reconfiguring team intercommunications before the software is finished to increase chances of building effective software systems optimized for flow. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L768-L772]
- The reverse Conway maneuver gained traction around 2015 with the rise of microservices. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L769-L769]
- Organizations should evolve their team and organizational structure to achieve the desired architecture that supports teams' ability to get work done without requiring high-bandwidth communication between teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L776-L779]
- An example of the reverse Conway maneuver: embedding database skills in application teams instead of using a separate DBA team helps avoid a monolithic shared database. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L793-L796]
- To avoid a single 'fan-in' database person or team, organizations should add data capability within the application-development team. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L797-L799]
- The reverse Conway maneuver means evolving team structure to achieve desired architecture. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2680-L2690]
- The homomorphic pull of Conway's law means the existing software architecture will initially push back against new team structures. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2690-L2700]
- The reverse Conway maneuver should be used with temporary explicit collaboration and high facilitating for stream-aligned and complicated-subsystem teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2700-L2710]
- Problems with new responsibility boundaries can be quickly identified during the collaboration phase, giving opportunity to adjust design early. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2710-L2720]

## Inferences

- Inference: The reverse Conway maneuver is a proactive application of Conway's law rather than a reactive one.
