# Exploratory Thought

## Summary

Exploratory thought involves even-handed consideration of alternative points of view and a tolerance of dissent to combat bias. It encourages an open-minded and objective consideration of alternative hypotheses, helping group members reason toward a more accurate representation of the world.

## Key facts

- Exploratory thought involves even-handed consideration of alternative points of view and a tolerance of dissent to combat bias. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1215-L1220]
- Exploratory thought is most likely to be activated when decision makers learn prior to forming opinions that they will be accountable to an audience interested in accuracy who is reasonably well-informed. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1225-L1235]
- Once exploratory thought becomes habitual, it becomes self-reinforcing as people internalize the group's approval for accuracy-focused behavior. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1300-L1305]
