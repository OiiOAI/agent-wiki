# Polyandry

## Summary

Polyandry is a mating system in which one female mates with multiple males. The chunk notes its rarity in human societies and describes an exception where husbands of the same wife are often close relatives.

## Key facts

- Polyandry is rare in human societies and represents an exception that proves the general rule of polygyny. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p526]
- In the few societies where polyandry is practiced, husbands of the same wife are often close relatives, such as brothers. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p526]

## Inferences

- Inference: The preference for brothers as co-husbands in polyandrous societies may reduce conflicts between husbands and facilitate resource sharing
