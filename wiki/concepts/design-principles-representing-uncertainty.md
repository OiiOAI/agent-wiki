# Design Principles for Representing Uncertainty

## Summary

The book presents six design principles (Principles 9-14) for representing uncertainty to support SA and decision making. Key principles include explicitly identifying missing information (to prevent false worlds), supporting sensor reliability assessment, using data salience to convey certainty, representing information timeliness, supporting assessment of confidence in composite data, and supporting uncertainty management activities.

## Key facts

- Principle 9: Explicitly identify missing information - People will often mistakenly treat missing information as if it would agree with other presented information, potentially leading to dangerous false worlds. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p141]
- The 1996 Boeing 757 crash in Colombia occurred partly because the graphical display did not show terrain information, creating a 'false world' that contributed to the pilots' decision to continue descending. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p141]
- Principle 11: Use data salience in support of certainty - Shading can show positional uncertainty without misdirecting operator attention, making targets about which more is known the most salient. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p143]
- Principle 13: Support assessment of confidence in composite data - Categorical (high, medium, or low) indication of system confidence led to slightly faster decision times compared to numerical percentages, analog bars, or rankings. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p146]
- Low specificity in probability categories (three categories) led to faster decision making compared to higher granularity (5 or 11 categories). [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p147]
