# Keep Commitments

## Summary

Behavior #12 in Covey's framework. Keeping commitments is described as the 'Big Kahuna' of all behaviors—the quickest way to build trust in any relationship. Violating commitments is the quickest way to destroy trust. Based on principles of integrity, performance, courage, and humility.
