# Muscle Synergy

## Summary

A muscle synergy is a set of muscles working in tandem to bring about a given movement—a time series of such collectives that is successful in achieving some purpose. Controlling muscle collectives rather than individual muscles reduces the number of degrees of freedom and simplifies the computational burden for motor control. An over-complete pool of functional synergies allows the motor system to select approximately correct movements, reducing the necessity for precise selection and minimizing costly feedback control.

## Key facts

- A muscle synergy is a set of muscles working in tandem to bring about a given movement; controlling muscle collectives rather than individual muscles reduces degrees of freedom and simplifies motor control computation. - The motor system can select from an over-complete pool of similar functional synergies, any number of which get the job done reasonably well, reducing the burden for precise selection. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p42]
- The magnitude of feedback adjustment of movement is inversely proportional to the precision with which the feedforward contribution can achieve the desired endpoint. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p42]

## Inferences

- Inference: Because complex voluntary movements can be executed successfully in many different ways using different muscle combinations, functional synergies must be fleeting, dissipative constructs that are dynamically reconfigured as movement proceeds.
