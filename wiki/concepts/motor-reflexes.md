# Motor Reflexes in Active Inference

## Summary

In Active Inference, motor commands are understood as predictions. Sensory afferents enter the brain stem or spinal cord and synapse on motor neurons. Descending predictions of sensory input are propagated from the cortex to motor neurons, whose output depends on the difference between cortical and sensory inputs. A reflex arc is one of the simplest possible forms of controller that corrects deviations in predicted and observed proprioceptive signals.

## Key facts

- In Active Inference, action simply fulfills descending predictions about data through minimizing the error between predicted and observed sensory consequences of action. - From a neurobiological perspective, sensory afferents enter the brain stem or spinal cord and synapse on motor neurons, and descending predictions are propagated from cortex to motor neurons.
