# The Triad

## Summary

A classification framework mapping items along three properties: Fragile (harmed by volatility), Robust (unaffected by volatility), and Antifragile (benefits from volatility). The Triad serves as a map for navigating decisions across domains from health to politics, allowing comparison of how different systems respond to disorder and stressors.

## Key facts

- The Triad classifies items into three categories: Fragile, Robust, and Antifragile, based on their response to volatility and disorder. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p40]
- The fragile wants tranquility, the antifragile grows from disorder, and the robust does not care too much. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p40]
- The centralized nation-state is on the far left of the Triad, squarely in the fragile category, and a decentralized system of city-states is on the far right, in the antifragile category. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p40]
- Debt always fragilizes economic systems, placing them on the left of the Triad. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p41]
- The middle column (Robust) is not equivalent to Aristotle's 'golden middle'—antifragility is generally desirable but not always achievable. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p41]
- Fragile and antifragile are relative terms; one item to the right of the Triad is more antifragile than another to the left. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p41]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Triad provides a practical tool for decision-making under uncertainty by allowing categorization of policies, entities, and systems to determine appropriate interventions.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether the Triad can be systematically applied to all domains or if some areas resist clean categorization.
