# Chromatolysis

## Summary

Chromatolysis is the structural change that occurs in the cell body of a neuron following axotomy. The cell body swells and the nucleus moves to an eccentric position.

## Key facts

- The cell body of the damaged neuron undergoes chromatolysis, characterized by swelling and the nucleus moving to an eccentric position. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1285]
- Chromatolysis represents a reactive response of the neuron cell body to the loss of its distal axon. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1285]
