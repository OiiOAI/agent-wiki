# Ambivalence

## Summary

Ambivalence, a term coined by Bleuler, describes the state in which opposing pairs of drives are developed to an approximately equal extent. This is characteristic of the sadistic-anal organization of sexuality, where active and passive currents exist alongside each other. Ambivalence gives the sadistic-anal phase a peculiarly archaic coloring.

## Inferences

- Inference: Ambivalence leads to partial paralysis of the will and incapacity for decision, as love provides motive power that is undermined by coexisting hatred.
- Inference: Sadistic components of love may be exceptionally strongly developed and undergo premature suppression, producing sadism persisting in the unconscious as hatred.
- Inference: Ambivalence explains why conflicts with loved ones are particularly intense and difficult to resolve, as they involve simultaneous opposing emotional forces.
