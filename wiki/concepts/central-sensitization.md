# Central Sensitization

## Summary

Central sensitization is a prolonged increase in the excitability of dorsal horn neurons following repeated C-fiber activation, similar to long-term potentiation in memory circuits. It involves NMDA receptors and results in allodynia (pain from innocuous stimuli) and hyperalgesia (exaggerated pain responses).

## Key facts

- Central sensitization involves activation of NMDA-type glutamate receptors on dorsal horn neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p528]
- Repeated C-fiber firing causes a progressive enhancement of dorsal horn neuron responses termed 'wind-up'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p528]
- Peripheral nerve injury activates microglia, which release mediators that enhance neuronal excitability and reduce GABAergic inhibition. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p530]
- Central sensitization contributes to neuropathic pain and mechanical allodynia following nerve injury. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p530]
- Repeated exposure to noxious stimuli results in wind-up, a gradual enhancement in the excitability of dorsal horn neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p592]
- Wind-up is thought to involve N-methyl-D-aspartate (NMDA)-type glutamate receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p592]
- The sensitization of dorsal horn neurons involves recruitment of second-messenger pathways and activation of protein kinases. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p592]
- Activation of NK1 receptors through second-messenger systems enhances the performance of NMDA-type receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p592]
- Phantom limb pain is the persistent sensation of pain that appears to originate from the region of an amputated limb. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p592]
- Patients with phantom limb pain show expansion of cortical representation of the mouth into regions of the hand and arm in the somatosensory cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p595]

## Inferences

- Inference: Central sensitization represents a form of neural plasticity similar to long-term potentiation in other brain regions, where repeated nociceptive input leads to lasting changes in synaptic strength.
