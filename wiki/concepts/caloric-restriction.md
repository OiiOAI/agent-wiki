# Caloric Restriction

## Summary

Caloric restriction (CR) involves eating less total food without attention to what or when it is eaten. It is the most effective dietary strategy for weight loss and metabolic improvement but requires meticulous tracking and strong adherence. Two landmark monkey studies revealed that diet quality matters as much as quantity.

## Key facts

- The Wisconsin monkey study found that calorically restricted monkeys lived significantly longer and were far less likely to die from age-related diseases than ad libitum-fed controls. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p321]
- The NIH monkey study found no significant lifespan difference between calorically restricted and control monkeys, attributed to the control group's higher-quality diet. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p322]
- The Wisconsin monkey chow was 28.5% sucrose by weight versus 4% sugar in NIH chow; Wisconsin control monkeys developed insulin resistance at 40%+ versus 14% for NIH controls. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p323]
- CR monkeys had 50% lower incidence of cancer than controls in both studies. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p324]
- If you are metabolically healthy and eating a high-quality diet, severe caloric restriction may not be necessary—avoiding a crap diet may be all you need. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p324]

## Inferences

- Inference: The monkey studies suggest that avoiding junk food and diabetes may be more important than severe calorie restriction for longevity.
