# Consciousness (Core vs. Extended)

## Summary

Antonio Damasio's framework distinguishes between core consciousness, which involves being awake and aware of the present moment without concern for past or future, and extended consciousness, which builds on core consciousness to include awareness of self and others over time.

## Key facts

- Core consciousness is present when the 'on-off switch' is activated and an organism is awake and aware of the moment and place. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p287]
- Core consciousness is not aware of self and may not be uniquely human. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p287]
- Extended consciousness involves knowing that one is aware of surroundings and caring about implications. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p287]
- Core consciousness requires connections from the brain stem to the intralaminar nuclei of the thalamus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p289]

## Inferences

- Inference: The cingulate cortex and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex are areas where core and extended consciousness overlap.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact neural mechanisms of how information processing reaches consciousness remain mysterious.
