# Interviewing (Candidate Assessment)

## Summary

A high-stakes managerial task with the purpose of selecting good performers, educating candidates about the company, determining mutual match, and selling the candidate on the job. The interview is extremely difficult because managers must assess likely performance in a new environment based on limited time and reference information.

## Key facts

- The four purposes of an interview are: selecting a good performer, educating the candidate about the company, determining if a mutual match exists, and selling the candidate on the job. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1812-L1817]
- The applicant should do 80 percent of the talking during the interview. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1826-L1827]
- When answers go off track, the interviewer should interrupt and redirect to avoid wasting limited interview time. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1830-L1833]
- Interview questions should steer discussion toward subjects familiar to both interviewer and candidate so that words mean the same thing to both parties. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1836-L1838]
- Interview information falls into four categories: technical skills, what the person did with their knowledge, reasons for discrepancies between knowledge and performance, and operational values. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1862-L1866]
- Reference checking is limited because references tend not to volunteer specific critical remarks, and the interviewer often lacks context about the reference's company. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1821-L1824]
- The last ten minutes of a half-hour reference conversation are much more valuable than the first ten minutes due to rapport developed. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1915-L1917]
- Grove believes interviews should be completely straightforward, showing the candidate the real environment rather than tricks. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1922-L1924]
- Careful interviewing does not guarantee success, it merely increases the odds of getting lucky. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L1934-L1935]
