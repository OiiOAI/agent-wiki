# Psychophysiological Assessment of Situation Awareness

## Summary

Psychophysiological assessment of situation awareness involves using measures of central and peripheral nervous system activity to understand the cognitive processes underlying SA. These measures include EEG, event-related potentials (ERPs), eyeblink analysis, heart rate, and electrodermal activity. Wilson proposes five application approaches: assessing operator functional state, detecting perception of critical cues, evaluating expectancy, timing SA probes, and classifying SA levels.

## Key facts

- Psychophysiology examines both central and peripheral nervous system activity to understand the relationship between cognition and physiology. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- Psychophysiological measures can be unobtrusively obtained without interfering with primary task performance. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- These measures are continuously available, unlike discrete subjective reports. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- Continuous physiological recording allows retrospective assessment of conditions during critical events and examination of antecedent conditions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- It is not currently possible to use psychophysiological data to infer whether information is correctly assessed as it is processed by an operator maintaining SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p177]
- However, psychophysiological activity can determine whether a relevant stimulus was detected and ascertained to be important to the task. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p177]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether psychophysiological measures can directly tap high-level cognitive processes involved in SA remains unclear.
