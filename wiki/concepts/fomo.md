# FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)

## Summary

FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) is a psychological phenomenon where people feel compelled to stay connected online through digital media due to fear of missing out on events, trends, or social interactions. This fear is fed by habitual constant connectivity and drives people to constantly check their phones, send messages, and post online. FOMO makes people feel that their lives are less exciting if they are not connected, and a digital detox can help individuals regain control over when and how often they engage with social media.

## Key facts

- FOMO stands for fear of missing out, described as a fear of missing out on the best part or events of the day. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p68]
- FOMO compels people to get constant connectivity, and without it they experience fear. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p69]
- FOMO makes people check their phones, computers, or other digital devices constantly to avoid missing out. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p69]
- A digital detox empowers people to set limits on the effects of FOMO by regaining control over when they check social media. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p69]
