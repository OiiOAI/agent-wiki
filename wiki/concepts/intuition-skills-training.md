# Intuition Skills Training

## Summary

Intuition Skills Training is a program developed by Gary Klein to help people strengthen their intuitive decision-making abilities. Based on the concept that intuition is a natural outgrowth of experience, the training aims to accelerate the development of expertise through deliberate practice. The program has three basic elements: identifying decision requirements of a job, practicing difficult decisions in context through Decision-Making Exercises, and reviewing decision-making experiences to gain feedback.

## Key facts

- The intuition skills training program is based on a regimen of deliberately practicing the decisions you have to make in your job to accumulate meaningful experiences necessary to build intuition. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p58]
- The training program uses deliberate practice, a term describing focused practice with specific objectives rather than random experience accumulation. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p58]
- The genesis of the program was a course developed for Marine Corps rifle squad leaders and officers in 1996, which was so well received it was institutionalized for squad leader training. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p58]
- The program has since been adapted for commercial and Navy pilots, fire departments including the Los Angeles County Fire Department, and business executives. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p58]
- The intuition skills training program is designed to speed up the learning curve using tools based on a framework emphasizing accumulation of experiences over establishment of procedures. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p333]
- Three main tools of intuition skills training are the Decision Requirements Table, the use of DMXs, and the Decision-Making Critique. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p333]
- Klein emphasizes that intuitive decision making can be treated as any important skill that can be learned: by clarifying training objectives, providing appropriate practice, and ensuring feedback. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p333]
- The genesis of the intuition skills training program came from Marine Corps Warfighting Laboratory commander Tony Wood, who asked Klein to teach intuitive decision-making skills to rifle squad leaders in 1996. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p356]
