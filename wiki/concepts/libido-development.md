# Libido Development

## Summary

Libido develops through a series of successive phases resembling the transformation of a caterpillar into a butterfly. The turning point is the subordination of all component sexual drives under genital primacy and the reproductive function. Before this, sexual life is 'pregenital' with sadistic-anal and oral phases. The oral phase (sensual sucking) is the most primitive, followed by the sadistic-anal organization where erotogenic zones of the anus and drives to mastery predominate.

## Key facts

- Sexual life passes through a series of successive phases: an oral phase, followed by a sadistic-anal phase, leading to the genital phase with primacy of the genitals. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p327]
- The first object of the oral component is the mother's breast; the erotic component makes itself independent with sensual sucking and replaces the outside object with an area of the subject's own body, becoming auto-erotic. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p329]
- From the third year of life, a child's sexual life shows agreement with adult sexual life but lacks a firm organization under the primacy of the genitals. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p326]
- From about the sixth to eighth year, a halt and retrogression in sexual development occurs, called the latency period. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p325]
