# The Conservative Reaction to the Enlightenment

## Summary

The conservative reaction to the Enlightenment emerged as opposition to Enlightenment rationalism. Thinkers like Bonald and Maistre emphasized tradition, emotion, religion, and social order over reason. This reaction provided the intellectual basis for French sociology, leading to emphasis on society over the individual, social order, and nonrational factors in social life.

## Key facts

- The conservative reaction turned away from what they considered the 'naive' rationalism of the Enlightenment. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p37]
- Conservatives recognized irrational aspects of social life and assigned them positive value, including tradition, imagination, emotionalism, and religion. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p37]
- The conservative reaction led to a major sociological interest in society as something more than an aggregate of individuals. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p38]
- These ten propositions from the conservative reaction provided the basis for the development of classical French sociological theory. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p39]
