# The Friend Zone

## Summary

The Friend Zone occurs when a man meets a Receptive or Neutral woman but never makes a move or expresses sexual interest, behaving pleasantly like a friend instead. For women, friend and potential lover categories are almost always mutually exclusive. Once inside, escape is almost impossible because a man who does not act on his sexual desires signals neediness and lacks vulnerability.
