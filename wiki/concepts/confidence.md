# Confidence

## Summary

One's belief in themselves and their ability and competence in a certain situation. Confidence is context-dependent, meaning one can be confident in a boardroom but horribly unconfident in a sexual relationship.

## Key facts

- Confidence is defined as one's belief in themselves and their ability and competence in a certain situation. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Confidence is context-dependent; one can be confident in a boardroom but horribly unconfident in a sexual relationship. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Confidence is often confused with self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Lack of confidence in romantic situations is a reflection of neediness, which is a component of low self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Confident behavior is always attractive. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
