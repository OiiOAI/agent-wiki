# ON and OFF Retinal Ganglion Cells

## Summary

ON and OFF ganglion cells form two parallel output pathways from the retina, with opposite polarity responses to light increments and decrements. This arrangement allows rapid signaling of both brightening and dimming in the visual scene and is established at the level of bipolar cells.

## Key facts

- ON cells fire more rapidly when light intensity increases; OFF cells fire more rapidly when light intensity decreases. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p578]
- The ON/OFF distinction is established at bipolar cells: ON bipolar cells depolarize to light (via metabotropic glutamate receptors closing channels), while OFF bipolar cells depolarize in darkness (via ionotropic glutamate receptors). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p583]
- ON bipolar cell axons terminate in the proximal half of the inner plexiform layer; OFF bipolar cell axons terminate in the distal half. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p583]
- ON and OFF ganglion cells have center-surround receptive fields with opposite polarity: ON cells are excited by bright centers/dark surrounds, OFF cells by dark centers/bright surrounds. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p579]
- The complementary ON and OFF representations allow faster detection of changes than a single-polarity code; an increase in firing from 10 to 200 spikes/s is detectable in ~5 ms. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p578]

## Inferences

- Inference: The ON/OFF parallel pathway architecture may have evolved to maximize temporal resolution, as bidirectional rate changes can signal both light increments and decrements with minimal latency.
