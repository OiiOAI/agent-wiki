# Pain and pleasure as levers for survival

## Summary

In the postscriptum to 'Descartes' Error', Antonio Damasio argues that pain and pleasure are not merely passive sensory experiences but are biological 'levers' that organisms require for instinctual and acquired survival strategies to operate efficiently. Pain and pleasure occur when the brain becomes conscious of body-state profiles that deviate from a base range, and these mechanisms were likely the levers that controlled the development of social decision-making strategies throughout evolution.

## Key facts

- Pain and pleasure are the levers the organism requires for instinctual and acquired strategies to operate efficiently. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
- Pain and pleasure occur when we become conscious of body-state profiles that clearly deviate from the base range. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
- The configurations of stimuli and brain-activity patterns perceived as pain or pleasure are set a priori in brain structure. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
- Pain and pleasure are a prime example of mental phenomena that depend on the activation of innate dispositions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
- More often than not, pain-related signals steer us away from impending trouble, both at the moment and in the anticipated future. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p287]
- There seem to be far more varieties of negative than positive emotions, and it is apparent that the brain handles positive and negative varieties of emotions with different systems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p287]

## Inferences

- Inference: Damasio suggests that suffering (the emotional reaction to pain) serves a protective function by increasing the probability that individuals will heed pain signals and act to avert their source or correct their consequences. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p284]
- Inference: The biological mechanisms behind pain and pleasure were selected during evolution as survival instruments, operating even before individual suffering or reason emerged. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
