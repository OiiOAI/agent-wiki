# Clarifying Outcomes

## Summary

Clarifying Outcomes is the principle that one must clearly define what success looks like before knowing how to achieve it. The core insight is that you won't see how to do something until you see yourself doing it, and that creating a clear mental picture of the desired result activates the brain's natural goal-seeking mechanisms.

## Key facts

- The fundamental principle of clarifying outcomes is: you won't see how to do it until you see yourself doing it. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p110]
- Creating a clear picture of what you want triggers something automatic and extraordinary in the mind that generates solutions and methods. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p110]
- One of the most powerful life skills is creating clear outcomes, which requires constantly defining and redefining what one is trying to accomplish. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p111]
- The automatic creative mechanism is teleological, operating in terms of goals and end results once a definite goal is given. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p110]

## Inferences

- Inference: This principle suggests that visualization and outcome definition precede and enable action planning.
