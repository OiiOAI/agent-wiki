# Reframing

## Summary

Reframing is the technique of placing a situation in a different logical frame or perspective, effectively a form of second-order change. By changing the context or interpretation of a problem, the attempted solutions that were perpetuating it become unnecessary or ineffective. The French officer defusing a threatening crowd by reframing the situation ('honest citizens' versus 'canaille') exemplifies this: he changed the frame from confrontation to compliance without direct coercion, allowing both the original threat and its threatened solution to be safely abandoned.

- Reframing means changing the conceptual and/or emotional viewpoint in relation to which a situation is experienced, placing it in another frame that fits the same concrete facts equally well or better, thereby changing its entire meaning. - In reframing, the concrete situation remains unchanged; what changes is the meaning attributed to it and therefore its consequences—not the facts themselves.
