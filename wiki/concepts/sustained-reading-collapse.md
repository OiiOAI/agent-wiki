# Sustained Reading Collapse

## Summary

The ability and tendency to read books deeply for extended periods is collapsing in modern society. Screen reading has trained people to scan and skim rather than read linearly, and this behavior 'bleeds over' into print reading, creating a negative spiral for both reading capacity and attention.

## Key facts

- Between 2004 and 2017, the proportion of men reading for pleasure fell by 40 percent and women by 29 percent in America. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p87]
- 57 percent of Americans do not read a single book in a typical year. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p87]
- Average Americans spend 17 minutes per day reading books but 5.4 hours on their phones. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p87]
- Less than half of Americans now read literature for pleasure—the first time in modern history. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p87]
- Screen reading trains people to scan and skim, and this behavior 'bleeds over' to affect how they read printed books. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p88]
- 54 studies confirm 'screen inferiority'—people understand and remember less of what they read on screens compared to print. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
- Screen inferiority in elementary school children equals two-thirds of a year's growth in reading comprehension. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
- Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi identified reading books as one of the simplest and most common forms of flow that people experience. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p87]

## Inferences

- Inference: The collapse of sustained reading both causes and is caused by attention decline—a mutually reinforcing negative spiral.
- Inference: Deep reading may become a minority interest like opera if current trends continue.
