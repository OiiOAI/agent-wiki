# Reflection and Review

## Summary

Reflection and Review is a process that allows you to remain conscious of your performance over time. The book argues that while habits enable us to do things without thinking, this benefit becomes a downside when we stop paying attention to little errors. Regular reflection and review helps maintain awareness and prevents habits from locking us into outdated patterns of thinking and acting. The author practices an Annual Review and an Integrity Report as part of this process.

## Key facts

- Reflection and review is described as a process that allows you to remain conscious of your performance over time, counteracting the automatic nature of habits. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p199]
- The downside of habits is that we stop paying attention to little errors when we do things without thinking. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p199]
- The author practices an Annual Review to evaluate how his habits are aligned with his goals. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p245]
- The author also writes an Integrity Report—a periodic reflection on whether his actions match his core values. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p246]
- Examples of reflection practices include: Pat Riley's Career Best Effort (CBE) program with the Los Angeles Lakers, Chris Rock's comedy review process, and Eliud Kipchoge's training log analysis.
