# Smart Trust

## Summary

Smart Trust is the concept of extending trust appropriately—avoiding the extremes of blind trust (gullibility) on one hand and excessive suspicion (distrust) on the other. It involves finding the 'sweet spot' where extending trust creates maximum dividends for all parties while managing risk intelligently.

## Key facts

- Smart Trust involves avoiding gullibility (blind trust) on one extreme and suspicion (distrust) on the other. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p63]
- The ability to know when and how to extend Smart Trust creates incredible leverage for getting things done with greater speed and lower cost. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p64]
- While there is risk in trusting other people, there is far greater risk in not trusting them. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p64]
- Smart Trust requires both a high propensity to trust and high analysis, creating a zone of judgment that maximizes dividends while managing risk. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p348]
- The Smart Trust Matrix has four zones: Zone 1 (Blind Trust - high propensity, low analysis) is gullibility; Zone 2 (Smart Trust - high propensity, high analysis) is optimal judgment; Zone 3 (No Trust - low propensity, low analysis) is indecision; Zone 4 (Distrust - low propensity, high analysis) is suspicion. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p347]
- Zone 4 (Distrust/suspicion) is one of the highest risk zones because suspicious people validate everything to death, decreasing speed and increasing cost while missing opportunities for collaboration and synergy. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p350]
- Smart Trust analysis considers three vital variables: the opportunity/situation, the risk involved (possible outcomes, likelihood, importance/visibility), and the credibility of people involved (character and competence). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p352]
- The general guideline for extending trust is to extend it conditionally to those earning it and abundantly to those who have already demonstrated trustworthiness. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p354]
- Smart Trust does not necessarily mean extending trust to someone; based on circumstances, judgment may indicate limited trust or no trust at all, but the approach itself almost always builds trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p352]
