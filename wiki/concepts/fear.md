# Fear

## Summary

Fear is presented as the most urgent survival signal, described as a powerful internal resource that communicates 'do what I tell you to do.' The book distinguishes between real fear and common fears like startle responses, public speaking anxiety, or fear from movies.

## Key facts

- The author describes real fear as replacing every feeling in the body, like an animal opening to full size and using leg muscles to act. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p12]
- Fear is identified as the intuitive signal of highest order and greatest urgency, always in response to something and always having the person's best interest at heart. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p51]
- The author states that women live in a state of constant wariness regarding safety in ways men do not experience. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p48]
- At core, men are afraid women will laugh at them, while women are afraid men will kill them. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p48]
