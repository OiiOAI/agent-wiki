# Limit-Situation

## Summary

A limit-situation arises when through some disaster, drastic upheaval, deep loss, or suffering, one's whole world is shattered and doesn't make sense anymore. It is an encounter with death, be it physical or psychological. The egoic mind collapses, and out of the ashes, a new world can come into being. These situations have the potential to force surrender and entry into the awakened state.

## Key facts

- A limit-situation arises when through some disaster, drastic upheaval, deep loss, or suffering your whole world is shattered and doesn't make sense anymore. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- A limit-situation is an encounter with death, be it physical or psychological. The egoic mind, the creator of this world, collapses. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- Out of the ashes of the old world, a new world can then come into being through a limit-situation. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- There is no guarantee that even a limit-situation will produce awakening, but the potential is always there. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- Some people's resistance intensifies in limit-situations, becoming a descent into hell, while others may achieve partial surrender. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- Murderers on death row have experienced the egoless state and deep joy and peace when forced into complete acceptance of the unacceptable. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p194]
