# Emotions

## Summary

Emotions are complex phenomena underlying which are changes throughout the body involving the brain, nervous system, heart, blood flow, lungs, abdominal organs, muscles, and hormones. These physical changes prepare us for action. Emotions always comprise three interweaving elements: sensations (physical feelings in the body), cognitions (thoughts, names, meanings, and images associated with the emotion), and urges (impulses to act in particular ways). The action tendency—the likelihood of acting in a particular way when experiencing an emotion—is a tendency, not a compulsion. Emotions serve three main purposes: communication (expressing to others what matters), motivation (preparing and moving us to act in helpful ways), and illumination (alerting us to what is important and what we care about). The better our unhooking skills, the more freedom we have to choose how we behave in the presence of emotions.
