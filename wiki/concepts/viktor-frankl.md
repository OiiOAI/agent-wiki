# Viktor Frankl

## Summary

Viktor Frankl was a psychiatrist who survived Nazi death camps and went on to write Man's Search for Meaning. His work on leading a meaningful life is foundational to the concept of emotional agility. Frankl's insight that 'between stimulus and response there is a space, in that space is our power to choose our response, in our response lies our growth and our freedom' encapsulates the central thesis of emotional agility—the importance of creating psychological space between experience and reaction.
