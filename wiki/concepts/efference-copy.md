# Efference Copy

## Summary

An efference copy is an internal signal representing a copy of the motor command sent to effectors. It is used by the nervous system for state estimation, forward internal models, and predicting the sensory consequences of movement. Efference copies allow the brain to distinguish self-generated sensory signals from externally generated signals and to make predictive corrections during movement.

## Key facts

- State estimation uses forward internal models with efference copies of motor commands combined with sensory feedback to estimate current body and environment state. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p865]
- Premovement activity in parietal cortex neurons may be driven by efference copies of motor commands transmitted via reciprocal cortico-cortical connections.
