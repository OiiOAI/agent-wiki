# Emotion Acculturation

## Summary

Emotion acculturation is the process by which people's emotion concepts transform as they are exposed to and adopt the concepts of a new culture. When living in a new culture, individuals acquire new concepts, which translate into new predictions, enabling them to experience and perceive the emotions of their newly adopted home. This process was discovered by cultural psychologist Batja Mesquita, who found that Turkish immigrants in Belgium developed emotional experiences that came to look more 'Belgian' the longer they lived there. The brain of someone undergoing acculturation is driven more by prediction error than prediction, similar to an infant's brain, and the new emotional patterns don't replace the old ones.
