# Psychological Flexibility

## Summary

Psychological flexibility is the technical term for the ability to be present, open up, and do what matters. It is the central construct in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). Research shows that the higher one's level of psychological flexibility, the better one's quality of life.

## Key facts

- Psychological flexibility is the ability to be present, open up, and do what matters. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p304]
- There are over three thousand published studies on ACT, and they all show that the higher your level of psychological flexibility, the better your quality of life. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p304]
- Psychological flexibility enables people to acknowledge painful reality while making room for painful thoughts and feelings, and then connect with values to choose what to stand for. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p310]
