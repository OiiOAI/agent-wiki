# Fundamental Attribution Error

## Summary

The Fundamental Attribution Error describes the tendency to overestimate individuals' influence and underestimate external, situational factors when explaining behavior. This leads to blaming people for circumstances beyond their control while ignoring environmental pressures.

## Key facts

- The fundamental attribution error is the tendency to overestimate individuals' influence and underestimate external, situational factors. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p88]
- Economic success depends far more on the overall economic climate and industry attractiveness than on brilliant leadership, yet all eyes shift to the CEO's office when companies perform well or poorly.
