# Gap Junction

## Summary

Gap junctions are specialized intercellular channels that directly connect the cytoplasm of adjacent cells, allowing electrical and metabolic communication. They are formed by connexin proteins and play roles in rapid signaling, synchronous firing, and intercellular communication in both neurons and glial cells.

## Key facts

- Gap junctions bridge a narrow gap of 4 nm between neurons, much less than the normal 20 nm nonsynaptic intercellular space. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p232]
- A gap-junction channel consists of a pair of hemichannels (connexons), one in each apposed cell membrane. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p232]
- Each connexon is composed of six identical subunits called connexins, encoded by a large gene family containing more than 20 members. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p232]
- The pore of a gap-junction channel has a diameter of approximately 1.5 nm, permitting ions and small organic molecules up to 1,000 Da to pass. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p232]
- Most gap-junction channels close in response to lowered cytoplasmic pH or elevated cytoplasmic Ca2+, decoupling damaged cells from healthy cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p232]
- Gap junctions also form between glial cells, forming networks that allow Ca2+ waves to propagate at approximately 1 µm/s. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p235]

## Inferences

- Inference: The sensitivity of gap junctions to pH and Ca2+ represents a protective mechanism that isolates damaged cells.
- Inference: Gap junctions in glia may play an active role in brain signaling through intercellular calcium waves.
