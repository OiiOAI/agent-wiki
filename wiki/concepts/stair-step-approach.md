# Stair-Step Approach

## Summary

A method for overcoming anxiety through incremental, consistent exposure rather than extreme 'boot camp' immersion. The approach involves progressively challenging oneself with small, achievable steps.

## Key facts

- The author advocates incremental, consistent exposure to fears rather than single extreme exposure, using the analogy that putting someone in a marathon before they've exercised is painful but won't create lasting habits. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p179]
- The example given is starting by asking women for the time, then the next week adding 'how is your day going,' gradually building up to asking them on dates. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p179]
- The approach can be applied to physical escalation: first hold her hand, then put your arm around her, then kiss her on the cheek, and finally go for the kiss itself. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p179]
