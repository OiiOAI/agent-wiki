# Knowledge Building Blocks

## Summary

A knowledge building block is a discrete unit of information interpreted through a unique perspective and stored outside the head. It can include highlighted passages, annotated images, bullet-point thoughts, quotes, or any content that has been curated, translated into one's own words, or drawn from life experience. Like LEGO blocks, they can be rapidly searched, retrieved, moved, assembled, and reassembled into new forms without inventing from scratch.

## Key facts

- A knowledge building block is a discrete unit of information interpreted through a unique perspective and stored outside the head. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p26]
- Examples include passages from books, annotated images, bullet-point lists of thoughts, quotes from films, or thousands of words from books. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p26]
- MIT economist César Hidalgo describes products as 'crystals of imagination' that allow people to turn knowledge into concrete objects others can access. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p52]
