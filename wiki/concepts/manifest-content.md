# Manifest Content

## Summary

The manifest content of a dream is what the dreamer actually reports remembering—the story, images, and events as experienced during the dream. It is a distorted substitute for the latent dream thoughts and must not be mistaken for the true meaning of the dream. The manifest content often appears confusing, absurd, or unintelligible because it represents only fragments and allusions to unconscious material.

## Key facts

- The manifest dream content is what the dream actually tells us, as opposed to the latent dream thoughts which we hope to reach through interpretation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4896]
- The manifest element may be only a fragment of the latent thoughts, or an allusion to them, functioning like an abbreviation in telegraphic style. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4896]
- We must not concern ourselves with what the dream appears to tell us, whether intelligible or absurd, since it cannot possibly be the unconscious material we seek. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4891]
