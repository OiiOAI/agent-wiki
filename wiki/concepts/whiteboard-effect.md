# Whiteboard Effect

## Summary

A collaborative form of deep work where working side by side with someone on a problem pushes both parties toward deeper levels of concentration and more valuable output.

## Key facts

- The whiteboard effect occurs when working with someone else pushes you deeper than if you were working alone. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p79]
- The presence of another party waiting for your next insight can short-circuit the natural instinct to avoid depth. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p79]
- Walter Brattain and John Bardeen used this collaborative deep work to invent the transistor, working side by side in a small lab for a month. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p79]

## Inferences

- Inference: The whiteboard effect represents a collaborative form of deep work common in academic circles that leverages social accountability to push individuals deeper than solo work would allow.
