# Exponential Growth

## Summary

Exponential Growth describes the human inability to intuitively grasp exponential or percentage growth. The author uses the example of folding paper 50 times to reach the sun's distance and the rice-on-chessboard story to illustrate how our Stone Age intuition fails us with compound growth.

## Key facts

- A sheet of copy paper approximately 0.004 inches thick, when folded 50 times, becomes a little over 60 million miles thick—equal to the distance between Earth and the Sun. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p84]
- The magic number of 70 divided by the growth rate percentage gives doubling time; a 7% annual increase in traffic accidents means accidents double every 10 years. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p84]
- At 5% inflation, a dollar will be worth only half what it is today in 14 years, illustrating that we don't intuitively understand what small percentage growth means over time. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p84]
