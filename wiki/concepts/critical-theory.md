# Critical Theory

## Summary

Critical theory is the product of a group of German neo-Marxists associated with the Frankfurt School (Institut für Sozialforschung), founded in 1923. It focuses on critiquing various aspects of social and intellectual life, including economic determinism, positivism, and modern capitalist society, with the ultimate goal of revealing more accurately the nature of society and contributing to human emancipation.

## Key facts

- The Institute of Social Research was officially founded in Frankfurt, Germany, on February 23, 1923. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p172]
- Critical theorists are most disturbed by economic determinists, whom they see as mechanistic Marxists who should have been concerned with other aspects of social life, especially the cultural realm. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p172]
- Critical theorists oppose positivism for tending to reify the social world and see it as a natural process, losing sight of actors and reducing them to passive entities. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p173]
- The critical school shifted its orientation to the cultural level because it considers that the locus of domination in the modern world shifted from the economy to the cultural realm. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p174]
- Critical theorists adopted Weber's differentiation between formal rationality (concerned with the most effective means for achieving any given purpose) and substantive rationality or reason. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p174]
- Marcuse identified 'one-dimensional society' in which individuals lose the ability to think critically and negatively about society due to technological control in capitalism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p175]
- The great contribution of the critical school has been its effort to reorient Marxian theory in a subjective direction, focusing on culture and consciousness. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p177]
- A dialectical approach means a focus on the social totality, with no partial aspect comprehensible unless related to the historical whole. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p178]
