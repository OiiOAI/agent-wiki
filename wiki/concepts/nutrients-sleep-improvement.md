# Nutrients for sleep improvement

## Summary

Research has shown that broad-spectrum multinutrient supplementation can improve sleep quality. A controlled study found that all measured sleep parameters improved: time to drop off to sleep, number of night awakenings, and sleep quality. Along with improved sleep, there were also observations of improved coping with stress, less anxiety, and better mood.
