# Five Principles of the New Science of Mind

## Summary

Kandel identifies five foundational principles underlying the new science of mind: mind and brain are inseparable; mental functions are carried out by specialized neural circuits; all circuits are made of nerve cells; circuits use specific signaling molecules; and these molecules have been conserved through evolution.

## Key facts

- First principle: Mind and brain are inseparable—the brain constructs sensory experiences, regulates thoughts and emotions, and controls actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p14]
- Second principle: Each mental function is carried out by specialized neural circuits in different brain regions, supporting 'biology of mind' rather than 'biology of the mind.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p14]
- Third principle: All neural circuits are made of the same elementary signaling units—nerve cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p14]
- Fourth principle: Neural circuits use specific molecules to generate signals within and between nerve cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p14]
- Fifth principle: Signaling molecules have been conserved through millions of years of evolution, present in bacteria and yeast as well as in humans. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p14]
- Kandel states that biology of mind will be to the 21st century what biology of the gene was to the 20th century. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p15]
