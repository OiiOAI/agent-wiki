# Lateral Truth

## Summary

Phædrus discovered 'lateral truths' as knowledge that doesn't move forward like an arrow in flight but expands sideways. These truths are seen out of the corner of one's eye, from unexpected directions. Lateral truths point to the falseness of axioms and postulates underlying one's existing system of getting at truth. His pursuit of lateral truth represented a drift away from frontal scientific truths toward unexpected insights.

## Key facts

- Lateral knowledge is knowledge that's from a wholly unexpected direction, from a direction that's not even understood as a direction until the knowledge forces itself upon one. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p101]
- Lateral truths point to the falseness of axioms and postulates underlying one's existing system of getting at truth. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p101]
- Drifting is what one does when looking at lateral truth, unable to follow any known method because these methods are what are screwed up. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p101]
