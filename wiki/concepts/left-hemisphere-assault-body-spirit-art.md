# Left Hemisphere's Assault on Body, Spirit, and Art

## Summary

The left hemisphere has launched determined attacks on three realms that represent escape routes from its self-enclosed world: the body (mechanised and objectified), the spirit (ironised and marginalised), and art (neutralised and commodified). These three realms are vehicles of love and transcendence, all grounded in the right hemisphere.

## Key facts

- The body has become a thing we possess, a mechanism, derived from the nineteenth-century scientific world picture, as Merleau-Ponty feared. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p600]
- The left hemisphere's world is ultimately narcissistic, seeing the world 'out there' as no more than a reflection of itself; the body becomes just the first thing we see 'out there'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p600]
- The schizophrenic view of the body as a machine parallels the 'normal' scientific materialist view of the body. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p601]
- The left hemisphere's attack on religion was well under way by the Reformation, taken further by the Enlightenment, and religion has declined in force in the twentieth century. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p603]
- The left hemisphere has set about neutralising or neutering the power of art, which was not the norm until the advent of modernism. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p605]
- What ultimately unites the three realms of escape from the left hemisphere's world is that they are all vehicles of love. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p609]

## Inferences

- Inference: Through these assaults, we are at risk of becoming trapped in the I-it world, with all the exits through which we might rediscover the I-thou world being progressively blocked off.
