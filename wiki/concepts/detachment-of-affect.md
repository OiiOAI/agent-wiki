# Detachment of Affect

## Summary

Detachment of affect is the process by which affects are separated from the ideational material that generated them and reattached to different elements in the dream. This is the most striking alteration that occurs during dream formation. The dream-work is at liberty to detach an affect from its connections in the dream thoughts and introduce it at any other point it chooses in the manifest dream. This mechanism allows affects to be displaced onto harmless material, evading the dream censorship.
