# Poststructuralism

## Summary

Poststructuralism emerged around 1966 following Derrida's speech announcing a new poststructuralist age. Unlike structuralists who saw people constrained by language structure, Derrida reduced language to 'writing' which does not constrain its subjects. Poststructuralism is characterized by its focus on deconstruction, subversion, and decentering. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p506]
