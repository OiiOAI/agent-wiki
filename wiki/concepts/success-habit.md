# Success Habit

## Summary

The Success Habit is the daily practice of asking the Focusing Question, identified as the foundational habit for achieving extraordinary results. Research from University College London suggests it takes an average of 66 days to establish a new habit, not the commonly cited 21 days. The habit should be applied sequentially—building one habit at a time.

## Key facts

- University College London researchers found it takes an average of 66 days to establish a new habit (range 18 to 254 days). [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p47]
- Australian researchers found that students who acquired one positive habit reported less stress, better diet, and decreased alcohol and tobacco consumption. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p47]
- Michael Phelps was diagnosed with ADHD as a child and told he would 'never be able to focus,' yet used selected discipline to build the habit of swimming daily. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p45]
- Success is described as a short sprint fueled by discipline long enough for habit to kick in. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p44]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book distinguishes between 'being disciplined' (unrealistic for most) and 'using selected discipline' to build specific habits.
