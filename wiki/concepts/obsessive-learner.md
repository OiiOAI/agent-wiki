# The Obsessive

## Summary

The Obsessive is bottom-line oriented, wanting immediate results and refusing to accept plateaus. They redouble effort when progress stalls, pushing themselves mercilessly. In relationships, they try to keep the excitement going through gifts and drama, resulting in a rollercoaster ride with inevitable painful breakup.

## Key facts

- The Obsessive wants to get things right during the very first lesson and stays after class asking for books and tapes to progress faster. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p23]
- When the Obsessive inevitably regresses to a plateau, they redouble their effort, push themselves mercilessly, and refuse counsel of moderation. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p24]
- American corporate managers often exhibit the Obsessive profile, striving to keep profit curves upward at the expense of long-term planning. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p24]
- In relationships, the Obsessive tries to keep the starship going through extravagant gifts and melodramatic rendezvous, not understanding the necessity for plateau periods. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p24]
- The Obsessive manages for a while to keep making brief spurts followed by sharp declines, a jagged ride toward a sure fall. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p24-25]
