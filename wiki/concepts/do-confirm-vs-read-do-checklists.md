# DO-CONFIRM vs READ-DO Checklists

## Summary

Two fundamental types of checklists distinguished by when the task is performed relative to reading the checklist item.

## Key facts

- In a DO-CONFIRM checklist, team members perform their jobs from memory and experience, often separately, then stop to run the checklist and confirm that everything supposed to be done was actually done. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p91]
- In a READ-DO checklist, people carry out tasks as they check them off—a recipe-like format. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p91]
- The WHO Safe Surgery Checklist adopted mainly a DO-CONFIRM format to give people flexibility in performing tasks while having them stop at key points to confirm critical steps have not been overlooked. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p100]

## Inferences

- Inference: The choice between DO-CONFIRM and READ-DO depends on the task complexity and the expertise of users—highly trained professionals may prefer DO-CONFIRM to maintain workflow while still catching errors.
