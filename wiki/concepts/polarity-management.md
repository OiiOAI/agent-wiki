# Polarity Management

## Summary

Polarity management is the practice of acknowledging and holding two opposing forces simultaneously rather than attempting to eliminate one in favor of the other. Barry Johnson, author of Polarity Management: Identifying and Managing Unsolvable Problems, describes polarities as sets of interdependent opposites that belong to the same whole—where the system needs both to survive. In the context of desire, security and adventure are such a polarity: neither can be eliminated without damaging the whole system. Perel applies this framework to the challenge of sustaining erotic desire within committed relationships, arguing that the tension between intimacy and passion is a paradox to manage, not a problem to solve.
