# Fluffy Strategy

## Summary

'Fluffy strategy' is identified by James Roche as one of the common 'strategy sins' that Rumelt critiques in Good Strategy/Bad Strategy, alongside blue-sky objectives and failure to face problems.

## Key facts

- James Roche identifies strategy 'sins' as including fluff, blue-sky objectives, and not facing the problem. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p3]

## Inferences

- Inference: Fluffy strategy represents a category of bad strategy characterized by vague, meaningless strategic statements lacking substance or actionable direction.
