# Gumption Traps

## Summary

Gumption traps are anything that causes one to lose sight of Quality and thus lose enthusiasm for what one is doing. There are two main types: external 'setbacks' caused by external circumstances, and internal traps that operate within oneself, subdivided into value traps, truth traps, and muscle traps. Hundreds or thousands of different gumption traps exist. They are the frustrating, angering, infuriating elements that make motorcycle maintenance interesting.

## Key facts

- A gumption trap can be defined as anything that causes one to lose sight of Quality, and thus lose one's enthusiasm for what one is doing. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p261]
- There are two main types of gumption traps: external 'setbacks' from external circumstances, and internal traps within oneself. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p261]
- Internal gumption traps are divided into three types: value traps (affective), truth traps (cognitive), and muscle traps (psychomotor). [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p265]
- The narrator proposes 'gumptionology' as a new academic field to study gumption traps. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p261]
