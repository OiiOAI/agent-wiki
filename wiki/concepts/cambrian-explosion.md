# Cambrian Explosion

## Summary

The Cambrian Explosion refers to a period approximately 540-480 million years ago when bilaterally symmetric animals rapidly diversified, establishing most major animal phyla that exist today. This dramatic evolutionary event coincided with significant increases in atmospheric oxygen and occurred after the emergence of the last common bilateral ancestor (LCBA).

## Key facts

- The Cambrian Explosion occurred approximately 540-480 million years ago. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p121]
- During this period, bilaterally symmetric animals rapidly diversified into most of the major phyla that exist today. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p121]
- The last common bilateral ancestor (LCBA), believed to have been an acoel flatworm, appeared around 630 million years ago. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p120]
- All Cambrian animals were sea-dwelling creatures; invasion of land occurred later. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p121]
- The dramatic rise in atmospheric oxygen around 800 million years ago coincides with earliest evidence for animal life. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p116]
