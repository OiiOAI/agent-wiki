# Flow State

## Summary

Flow state is a mental state identified by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi in which a person is fully immersed in an activity, energized, and focused. The best moments usually occur when a person's body or mind is stretched to its limits in voluntary effort to accomplish something difficult and worthwhile. Research using experience sampling method found that people are happier at work and less happy relaxing than they suspected.

## Key facts

- Flow state occurs when the best moments happen when a person's body or mind is stretched to its limits in a voluntary effort to accomplish something difficult and worthwhile. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p50]
- Jobs are easier to enjoy than free time because they have built-in goals, feedback rules, and challenges that encourage involvement and concentration. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p51]
- The more flow experiences that occur in a given week, the higher the subject's life satisfaction. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p51]
- Deep work is an activity well suited to generate a flow state. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p51]
- Flow is characterized by complete concentration, a sense of control, clear goals, immediate feedback, merging of action and awareness, loss of self-consciousness, and intrinsic motivation. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p337]
- Csikszentmihalyi and LeFevre found that individuals are engaged in high-skill, high-challenge activities more often at work (54%) than at leisure (18%). [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p338]
- You can only put your conscious attention on one thing at a time—if that's all that has your attention, you're in flow. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p338]
- GTD creates conditions for flow by providing clear goals (project outcomes), feedback (tracking progress), and limiting the stimulus field (complete inventory of commitments). [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p338]
