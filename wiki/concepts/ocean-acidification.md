# Ocean acidification

## Summary

The process by which increased atmospheric CO2 from burning fossil fuels dissolves into the ocean, changing the water's pH balance and affecting the metabolisms of many marine animals, with especially serious effects on organisms that build calcium-based structures.

## Key facts

- As CO2 concentration in the atmosphere rises due to burning fossil fuels, some of the extra CO2 dissolves into the sea, changing the water's pH balance away from its usual state of mild alkalinity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Other Minds_ The Octopus, the Sea, and the - Peter Godfrey - Smith.pdf#p159]
- The metabolisms of a great many sea animals, including cephalopods, are affected by ocean acidification. [raw/books/neuroscience/Other Minds_ The Octopus, the Sea, and the - Peter Godfrey - Smith.pdf#p159]
- There are especially serious effects on corals and other organisms that make hard parts out of calcium, which soften and dissolve in the altered sea. [raw/books/neuroscience/Other Minds_ The Octopus, the Sea, and the - Peter Godfrey - Smith.pdf#p159]
