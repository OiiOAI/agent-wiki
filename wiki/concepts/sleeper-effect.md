# Sleeper Effect

## Summary

The sleeper effect causes propaganda to become more persuasive over time. WWII studies showed soldiers' attitudes toward propaganda movies initially showed no effect, but nine weeks later those who saw the movies expressed more support. The source fades from memory faster than the message.

## Key facts

- WWII studies found propaganda movies initially showed no effect on soldiers' enthusiasm, but nine weeks later, viewers expressed much more support for the war than non-viewers. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p157]
- Carl Hovland explained that the source of an argument fades from memory faster than the argument itself, so knowledge from untrustworthy sources gains credibility over time. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p157]
- Political ads must disclose sponsors, but the sleeper effect still works because voters forget the messenger while remembering the accusations. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p158]
