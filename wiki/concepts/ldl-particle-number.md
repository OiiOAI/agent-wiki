# LDL Particle Number

## Summary

LDL particle number (LDL-p) represents the total number of LDL particles in the blood and is considered a better predictor of cardiovascular risk than standard cholesterol tests. An NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance) lipid profile test measures LDL-p. Lower LDL-p values are better for cardiovascular health.

## Key facts

- LDL particle number (LDL-p) represents the total number of LDL particles and research suggests it is a better predictor of cardiovascular risk. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p165]
- LDL-p can be attained with a test called an NMR (nuclear magnetic resonance) lipid profile. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p165]
- A lower LDL-p number is better, all else being equal. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p165]
