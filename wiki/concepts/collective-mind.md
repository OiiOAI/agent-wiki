# Collective Mind

## Summary

Llinás explores whether the World Wide Web might represent a collective mind—a nervous system composed of minds. While the Web provides near-simultaneous bidirectional communication potentially solving society's 'binding problem,' Llinás argues it currently lacks the modular organization to support true collective consciousness. He warns that homogenization of thought through collective media may decrease variation, the key to survival.

## Key facts

- The Web solves the binding problem for society by providing communicative simultaneity of activation, allowing single messages to reach millions almost instantaneously with bidirectional response possible. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p257]
- Llinás argues the Web is not yet a collective mind because it is noisy, not modular, and lacks sufficient integrative speed to support consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p258]
- The Web most closely resembles a coelenterate nervous system, which even if conscious, would not be capable of supporting high-quality collective cognition. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p258]
- Homogenization of thought through collective media may lead to decreased variation—the key to survival—making society more brittle. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p260]
- The tyranny of the majority may be accelerated by the Web, where ideas gain validity simply through the number of people reporting adherence to them. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p259]

## Inferences

- Inference: Llinás's concern about homogenization echoes McCulloch's insight that reliable systems must be made of unreliable (varied) elements—social uniformity may paradoxically make society more fragile.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether the Web will evolve modular organization capable of true collective consciousness is speculative and depends on technological and social development.
