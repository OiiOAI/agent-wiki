# Divergence and Convergence

## Summary

Divergence and convergence is a fundamental pattern underlying all creative work. Divergence involves opening the space of possibilities and considering many options, while convergence involves discarding possibilities and narrowing toward a solution. The CODE Method maps onto this pattern with Capture and Organize representing divergence, and Distill and Express representing convergence.

## Key facts

- Divergence is the act of opening the space of possibilities and considering as many options as possible. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p156]
- Convergence forces elimination of options, making trade-offs, and deciding what is truly essential. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p156]
- The first two steps of CODE (Capture and Organize) make up divergence. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p157]
- The final two steps of CODE (Distill and Express) are about convergence. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p157]
- The model of divergence and convergence originates from Design Thinking at Stanford Design School and IDEO. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p172]
