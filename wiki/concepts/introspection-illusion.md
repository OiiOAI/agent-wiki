# Introspection Illusion

## Summary

The introspection illusion is the belief that reflection leads to truth or accuracy about one's own mind. Petter Johannson's experiment showed people failed to notice when portrait photos were switched, yet continued justifying their preferences in detail.

## Key facts

- Petter Johannson allowed subjects to choose preferred faces, then switched photos while showing close-ups; most failed to notice and continued justifying why they preferred features of the wrong photo. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p151]
- The introspection illusion creates inaccurate predictions of future mental states and creates an illusion of superiority over others' introspections. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p152]
