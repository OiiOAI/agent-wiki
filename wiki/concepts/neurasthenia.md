# Neurasthenia

## Summary

Neurasthenia is characterized as a sexual neurosis arising from abnormal sexual life, particularly masturbation in males. Freud argued that neurasthenia is always acquired through sexual exhaustion, which either directly provokes the condition or creates a disposition that later manifests with precipitating factors.

## Key facts

- Neurasthenia in males is acquired at puberty and becomes manifest in the patient's twenties, with its source being masturbation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p208]
- Sexual exhaustion can by itself alone provoke neurasthenia; if it fails to achieve this, it affects the disposition so that later factors produce neurasthenia. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p208]
- Coitus interruptus proves to be the main sexual noxa and produces its characteristic effect even in non-disposed subjects. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p209]
- In neurasthenia, the pathologically intensified antithetic idea is combined with the volitional idea into a single act of consciousness, subtracting from the volitional idea and bringing about weakness of will. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p151]
- Simple excess and overwork are not aetiological factors for neurasthenia. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p206]
- Neurasthenia is characterized by symptoms including intracranial pressure, proneness to fatigue, dyspepsia, constipation, and spinal irritation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1161]
- Freud traced neurasthenia to conditions acquired by excessive masturbation or arising from frequent emissions. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1162]
- Sexual factors are never absent in any case of neurasthenia and can produce the neurosis without further assistance. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1164]

## Inferences

- Inference: Sexual exhaustion is the necessary precondition without which neurasthenia cannot come about.
- Inference: Neurasthenia in females is normally derived from neurasthenia in men or produced simultaneously.
- Inference: Freud's view that sexual factors are the specific aetiology of neurasthenia, while other factors like overwork are auxiliary, represents a shift from contemporary medical opinion.
