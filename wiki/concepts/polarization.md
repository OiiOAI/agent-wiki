# Polarization

## Summary

Polarization is introduced as a strategy concept in Part II of the book. It refers to the process of deliberately eliciting both positive and negative reactions from women in order to screen for those most receptive to one's authentic identity as early as possible. Rather than trying to appeal to everyone or avoid rejection, polarization means being genuinely oneself and accepting that some people will不喜欢 you. This approach is more efficient than trying to be universally appealing and allows men to quickly identify compatible partners.

## Key facts

- Part II is an overview of the dating strategy that focuses on polarizing reactions from women to screen for the ones most receptive to your identity as soon as possible. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p18]
- This is a reality-based strategy, not based on fantasies or frivolities of wanting to sleep with every woman or dating a 'perfect 10' by coming up with scripts of pick up lines. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p18]
- Part II is a long-term strategy built to take a man from 'no women in my life' to 'lots of amazing women in my life' as quickly as possible with as little effort as possible. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p18]
- Polarization occurs when you express your truth and make yourself vulnerable; it forces women to decide whether they're Receptive or Unreceptive. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p93]
- The simple act of polarizing demonstrates non-neediness and inspires more Neutral women to become Receptive than other strategies. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p93]
- Being liked by nobody usually means you're not loved by anybody either; avoiding controversy is a horrible attraction strategy. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p94]
- A man cannot be attractive to some women without being a joke or embarrassment to others; you must polarize. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p95]
- Bold, honest approaches earn respect even when rejected; women often introduce the man to available friends after rejecting him. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p95]
- Polarization is defined as behavior that forces a woman to feel strongly about you, whether positive or negative. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
- Polarization is useful for screening out women who are most compatible with you very quickly. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
- Polarization not only invites rejection but uses it as a tool to achieve dating success efficiently. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
