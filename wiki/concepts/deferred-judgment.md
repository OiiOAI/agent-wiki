# Deferred Judgment

## Summary

Deferred judgment is a key Strategy Foundry tool that helps avoid premature convergence on answers. It means deferring judgments about good and bad or important and unimportant until after facts and information accumulate, and holding off on action plans until after critical challenges are identified and diagnosed.

## Key facts

- Deferred judgment helps avoid the problem of too early convergence on an answer, an issue Irving Janis pointed out in his analysis of groupthink. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p285]
- The first meaning is deferring judgments about good and bad or important and unimportant, letting facts and information accumulate without putting them in bins. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p285]
- Deferred judgment also means holding off on action plans until after judgments about which challenges are critical and which are actionable. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p285]
- The facilitator's key role is to quickly develop norms of balanced discussion and avoid early convergence. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p285]
