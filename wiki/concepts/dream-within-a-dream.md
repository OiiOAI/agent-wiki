# Dream-within-a-Dream

## Summary

The dream-within-a-dream is a formal characteristic where part of a dream's content is described in the dream itself as 'dreamt.' Stekel analyzed that this device is intended to detract from the importance of the content, to rob it of its reality. Including something in a dream-within-a-dream is equivalent to wishing it had never happened.

## Key facts

- Including something in a 'dream within a dream' is equivalent to wishing that the thing described as a dream had never happened, representing the most decided confirmation of the reality of the event. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p338]
- What has been 'dreamt' in the dream represents the reality, the true recollection, while the continuation of the dream represents what the dreamer wishes. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p338]
- The dream-work makes use of dreaming as a form of repudiation, confirming the discovery that dreams are wish fulfillments. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p338]

## Inferences

- Inference: The 'dream within a dream' structure reveals how the dream-work can use metaperception (judging something as a dream) to express denial while simultaneously affirming reality.
