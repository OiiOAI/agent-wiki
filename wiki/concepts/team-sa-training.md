# Team Situation Awareness Training

## Summary

Team SA training focuses on enhancing shared mental models, communication, and coordination among team members to improve collective situation awareness. In aviation maintenance, five major areas were identified for improving SA: reducing mental model differences between teams, verbalizing decision rationale, providing feedback across teams, improving shift meetings, and addressing individual SA threats.

## Key facts

- The Team SA Training Course identified significant differences in perceptions and understanding of situations between teams related to differences in mental models held in different teams. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p368]
- Problems were found with not verbalizing the information that went into decisions; only the decision would be communicated between teams, contributing to suboptimal decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p368]
- The Team SA Training Course uses adult inquiry and discovery learning featuring high interaction and participation creating an experiential learning process. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p368]
- After training, participants reported being 15% more likely to keep others up-to-date with their status and 10% more likely to keep up with others' activities. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p371]
- Participants reported 21% improvement in written communication when sending an aircraft with a problem to another station. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p371]

## Inferences

- Inference: Team SA training may be a viable approach for other domains beyond aviation, though research is needed to determine the sources of SA differences in those contexts

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The evaluation represents only an initial prototype implementation; additional validation is needed with succeeding groups of trainees
