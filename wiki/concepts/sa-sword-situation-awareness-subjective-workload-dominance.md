# SA-SWORD

## Summary

SA-SWORD (Situation Awareness Subjective Workload Dominance) is a subjective SA assessment technique adapted from the SWORD workload metric, which itself was derived from the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). The technique uses pairwise comparisons for data collection and geometric means rating calculation. Initial studies showed sensitivity to display type and interrater reliability, though the technique has received limited testing and critics suggest display preference differences may have influenced results rather than true SA differences.

## Key facts

- SA-SWORD was adapted from the SWORD workload technique, which was developed using the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p138]
- SA-SWORD uses pairwise comparisons for data collection and utilizes a geometric means rating calculation algorithm. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p138]
- In the original validation study, SA-SWORD showed a main effect of display with higher SA ratings for the HSF display compared to the FCR display. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p139]
- A limitation of SA-SWORD is that subjects' definitions of SA varied, with approximately half associating SA with information tracking burden and the other half associating it with perceived missing information. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p139]
- Interrater correlation in the SA-SWORD validation study suggested that ratings were reliably related to task conditions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p139]

## Inferences

- Inference: The adaptation required no changes in data collection or analysis procedures, suggesting the pairwise comparison methodology is robust across workload and SA domains.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: This was the first attempt to utilize SA-SWORD, so no conclusions about its effectiveness could be drawn with confidence.
- Uncertain: Critics suggest display preference differences may have caused SA rating differences rather than true SA sensitivity.
