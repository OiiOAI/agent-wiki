# Motorcycle Maintenance

## Summary

The practical skill of maintaining and repairing motorcycles, which serves throughout the book as a metaphor for caring about one's tools, understanding underlying form, and integrating oneself with technology rather than being alienated from it.

## Key facts

- The narrator uses a shop manual and troubleshooting guide for his motorcycle and takes maintenance seriously, having learned from past failures. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p37]
- The narrator's motorcycle (with over 27,000 miles) has developed a unique 'personality' that he cares about. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p39]
- The narrator discovered that incompetent mechanics had sheared a twenty-five-cent pin in the oil-delivery system, causing multiple engine seizures. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p24]
