# The Aporetic Turn

## Summary

The aporetic turn is a phase in crisis management focused on making sense of inputs, building coherence, and spotting opportunities. It involves mapping landscapes from experiences using narratives to identify agents, boundaries, roles, processes, challenges, and opportunities. The approach maintains heterogeneity by engaging diverse participants and avoids premature convergence.

## Key facts

- The aporetic turn focuses on making sense of all inputs, building coherence, spotting opportunities, challenges, and dispositions. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p7]
- Narratives give insights on the disposition of an organisation, identifying agents, drivers of change, explicit and hidden boundaries, informal processes, challenges and opportunities. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p36]
- In crisis we need a map (not a written report) of the current state to inform decision-making with situational assessments. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p36]
- Retrospective coherence is based on faulty, selective memory and creates a false sense of security - we need to beware of it. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p36]
- A landscape is necessary to map current challenges, identify ideas for action, and start pilot actions to amplify beneficial changes. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p37]
