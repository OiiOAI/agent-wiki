# Glutamate Receptor

## Summary

Glutamate receptors are the primary mediators of excitatory synaptic transmission in the central nervous system, divided into ionotropic (AMPA, kainate, NMDA) and metabotropic receptor families.

## Key facts

- Ionotropic glutamate receptors are divided into three pharmacological types: AMPA, kainate, and NMDA, named after selective agonists. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p277]
- AMPA and kainate receptors are blocked by CNQX but not APV, while NMDA receptors are blocked by APV but not CNQX. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p277]
- Glutamate is the major excitatory neurotransmitter in the brain and spinal cord. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p277]
