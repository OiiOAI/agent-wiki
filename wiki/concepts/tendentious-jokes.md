# Tendentious Jokes

## Summary

Tendentious jokes are jokes that serve purposes beyond mere pleasure, specifically exposing (obscene), aggressive (hostile), cynical, or sceptical aims. They overcome obstacles—external or internal (inhibitions, repressions)—that would otherwise prevent satisfaction of suppressed drives. Freud identified a 'fore-pleasure principle' whereby small pleasure from the joke-work itself enables larger suppressed pleasures to emerge.

## Key facts

- There are two main categories of tendentious jokes: hostile jokes (serving aggression, satire, or defence) and obscene or exposing jokes (serving exposure). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2739]
- The obstacle overcome by tendentious jokes may be external (social position of the target) or internal (aesthetic objections, repression). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2744]
- Tendentious jokes bribe the hearer with pleasure yield to take sides with the speaker, achieving 'die Lacher auf seine Seite ziehen' (bringing the laughers over to one's side). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2745]
- Smut develops when sexual aggressiveness is blocked; it pauses at evoking excitement and derives pleasure from signs of embarrassment in the woman. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2740]
- Cynical jokes attack institutions, especially marriage, which civilization tries to suppress most severely in the sexual sphere. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2750]
- Sceptical jokes question what determines truth itself—whether describing things as they are suffices or whether genuine truth must consider the hearer. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2755]
- Jewish jokes created by Jews against Jewish characteristics involve the subject's share in the person found fault with, creating the subjective determinant of the joke-work. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2751]
