# BOLD Functional MRI

## Summary

Blood Oxygen Level Dependent (BOLD) functional MRI is an imaging technique that detects changes in magnetic resonance signal caused by alterations in deoxyhemoglobin concentration. When neurons are active, increased blood flow delivers more oxygen than is consumed, decreasing deoxyhemoglobin and producing a brighter MRI signal. BOLD fMRI enables non-invasive visualization of brain activity during cognitive tasks.

## Key facts

- BOLD fMRI exploits the paramagnetic property of deoxyhemoglobin; when oxyhemoglobin releases oxygen and becomes deoxyhemoglobin, it creates magnetic field inhomogeneities detectable by MRI. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p478]
- In brain regions with increased metabolism, oxygenated blood flow exceeds oxygen consumption, causing relative decrease in deoxyhemoglobin and brighter MRI signal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p480]
- BOLD fMRI reflects energy metabolism, particularly synaptic activity and intracortical processing, rather than direct measurement of neural spiking. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p481]
- The BOLD response is a complex interplay between cerebral blood flow, cerebral blood volume, and oxygen metabolism, making absolute measurements difficult and requiring caution when comparing responses across brain regions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p491]
- BOLD fMRI offers advantages over PET including no injection of foreign substances, finer spatial resolution (sub-millimeter demonstrated) and temporal resolution (approximately 100 ms). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p483]

## Inferences

- Inference: BOLD fMRI measures a vascular correlate of neural activity rather than neural activity directly; the coupling between neural firing and blood flow response can vary across brain states and in disease.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact mechanism by which increased neural activity leads to the hemodynamic response (neurovascular coupling) is not fully understood.
