# Software Development and Cynefin

## Summary

The Cynefin Framework provides a lens for understanding software development as existing across multiple domains. Software code itself is complicated (predictable), but software development becomes complex due to human interaction. The Agile movement, born in 2001, attempted to embrace uncertainty in software development, though its adoption has been uneven.

## Key facts

- Computers are for the most part predictable; programs that start with the same input will generally produce the same output. Software development becomes complex because of human interaction with the computer. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p269]
- In 2001, fourteen leaders of the software world met at Snowbird ski resort in Utah to create what became the 'Agile' movement. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p269]
- The Agile movement has not taken off as hoped; where it has appeared, it is often a token effort surrounded by usual rigor to force plans to progress as written. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p269]
- In 2004, Ludicorp launched Flickr (originally a game tooling feature), and later Slack emerged from an abandoned Glitch game project—both examples of the Complex domain where success is correlated in retrospect but could not have been predicted. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p270]
- The zone of complacency lies at the boundary between Clear and Chaotic domains; software development is as prone to it as any other human endeavor. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p272]
- In 2012, Knight Capital Group lost $440 million in 45 minutes due to a deployment error where new software was released on seven of eight servers, leaving the 8th server running test code. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p271]
