# Compound Interest

## Summary

Compound interest applies to more than just compounding capital—it applies to business relationships, reputation, and knowledge. All the returns in life, whether in wealth, relationships, or knowledge, come from compound interest. When you find the right thing to do, when you find the right people to work with, investing deeply and sticking with it for decades is how you make the big returns.
