# Clear Mind

## Summary

Clear mind is a concept in DBT addiction recovery representing the safest middle ground between two dangerous extremes: addict mind (governed by addiction impulses) and clean mind (believing problems are behind you and being oblivious to relapse risks). Clear mind involves being abstinent while remaining vigilant about potential temptations.

## Key facts

- Addict mind is characterized by impulsivity, one-mindedness, and willingness to do anything for a fix; clean mind involves naivety, risk-taking, and obliviousness to dangers. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p383]
- Both extremes are dangerous; clear mind is the safest place to be, involving abstinence while remembering addict mind and radically accepting that relapse is not impossible. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p383]
- In clear mind, one enjoys success while expecting urges and cues and planning for temptation. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p383]
