# Commitment and the Ups and Downs

## Summary

Commitment in ACT does not mean being perfect or never failing; it means picking yourself up when you stumble and continuing in the direction you want to go. Success is redefined as living by your values, meaning fulfillment is available right now regardless of whether major goals are achieved. When we go off track, we return to the basic formula: Be present, Open up, Do what matters.

## Key facts

- Commitment does not mean being perfect or never giving up; it means picking yourself up, finding your bearings, and carrying on in the direction you want to go. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p295]
- Success in life means living by your values, making fulfillment available right now whether or not major goals are achieved. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p297]
- There is no point in dwelling on the past or crucifying ourselves for being imperfect; we should acknowledge we went off track and hold ourselves kindly. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p298]
- After going off track, consider: What do I want to do now that is important or meaningful? Then take action in line with that value. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p299]
