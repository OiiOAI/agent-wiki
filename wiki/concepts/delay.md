# Delay

## Summary

Delays are ubiquitous in systems and are strong determinants of behavior. A delay in a balancing feedback loop makes a system likely to oscillate. Changing the length of a delay may make a large change in system behavior. Common delays include perception delays, response delays, and delivery delays. System thinkers are always alert to where delays occur and whether they are in information streams or physical processes.
