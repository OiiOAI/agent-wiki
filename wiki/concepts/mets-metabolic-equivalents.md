# METs (Metabolic Equivalents)

## Summary

METs (Metabolic Equivalents) are numbers representing how much energy the body expends at rest versus when working. Being sedentary is defined as activity below 1.5 METs, which should be avoided for more than 30 minutes at a time. Walking can raise METs threefold compared to sitting.

## Key facts

- METs stands for 'metabolic equivalents'. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1049-L1051]
- METs represent how much energy you expend at rest versus when your body is working. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1049-L1051]
- Sedentary behavior is defined as activity below 1.5 METs. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1053-L1056]
- More than 30 minutes without moving should be avoided to stay above 1.5 METs. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1053-L1056]
- Walking can raise METs score threefold. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1056-L1058]
