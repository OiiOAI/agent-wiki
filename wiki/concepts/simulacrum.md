# Simulacrum

## Summary

A simulacrum is a copy of a copy for which no original ever existed. In Baudrillard's analysis, simulacra are reproductions of objects or events that have become predominant in postmodern society, where the distinction between signs and reality has imploded. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p528]
