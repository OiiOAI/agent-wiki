# Advanced Team Decision-Making Model

## Summary

The Advanced Team Decision-Making Model, developed by Klein, Zsambok, and Thordsen, maps four features of child development onto team maturity: team competencies, team identity, team cognition, and team metacognition. This framework provides questions useful in appraising different teams for their degree of development along these four dimensions.

## Key facts

- Team competencies refer to the skills and shared practices that teams develop; mature teams perform basic procedures automatically. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p294]
- Team identity involves members knowing where the team ends and the rest of the world begins, and what the team controls. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p294]
- Inexperienced teams respond to overall goals with 'don't bother me with that, just tell me what to do'; experienced teams want to know as much as possible about overall status. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p295]
- Team cognition includes how teams describe goals, the degree of shared situation understanding, time horizons, and how teams manage uncertainty. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p297]
- Team metacognition is thinking about thinking—the ability to monitor performance and select strategies to avoid weaknesses and capitalize on strengths. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p298]
- The model was applied at the Industrial College of the Armed Forces as part of their strategic decision-making curriculum. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p309]
