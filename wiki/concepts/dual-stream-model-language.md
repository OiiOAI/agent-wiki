# Dual-Stream Model of Language

## Summary

The dual-stream model is a contemporary framework for understanding language processing in the brain. It proposes that auditory speech information diverges into two cortical pathways: a dorsal stream that maps speech sounds to motor articulation programs, and a ventral stream that maps speech sounds to semantic meaning. This model represents a significant advance over the classical Wernicke-Geschwind model.

## Key facts

- The dual-stream model emerged from advances in functional brain imaging (PET, fMRI, EEG, MEG) and structural imaging (DTI, tractography) that revealed a network of connections linking specialized language areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1371]
- The dorsal stream passes above the lateral ventricles and connects the inferior frontal gyrus (Broca's area), premotor cortex, and insula to the posterior superior temporal gyrus (Wernicke's area), with two pathways: dorsal pathway 1 connects posterior superior temporal gyrus to premotor cortex, and dorsal pathway 2 connects to Broca's area. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1379]
- The ventral stream passes below the Sylvian fissure and conveys information for auditory comprehension, transforming auditory signals to mental lexicon representations that link word forms to semantic meaning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1380]
- The dorsal stream is strongly left hemisphere dominant, while the ventral stream is largely bilaterally represented. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1380]
- Broca's aphasia is understood within the dual-stream model as a dorsal stream deficit affecting sensorimotor integration for speech production. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1382]
- Wernicke's aphasia is understood within the dual-stream model as a ventral stream deficit affecting auditory-conceptual processing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1382]

## Inferences

- Inference: The dual-stream model is analogous to the 'what' and 'where' dual-stream model of the visual system, suggesting a common organizational principle in cortical processing architecture.
