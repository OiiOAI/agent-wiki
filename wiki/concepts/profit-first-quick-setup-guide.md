# Profit First Quick Setup Guide

## Summary

The Quick Setup Guide in Appendix 1 provides a step-by-step implementation framework. It details one-time setup of five foundational accounts at two banks, daily practices, biweekly allocations on the 10th and 25th, quarterly profit distributions and tax payments, and annual reviews.

## Key facts

- One-time setup requires five checking accounts at Bank 1 (INCOME, PROFIT, OWNER'S COMP, TAX, OPEX) and two savings accounts at Bank 2 (PROFIT HOLD, TAX HOLD) to remove temptation. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p215]
- Every 10th and 25th: transfer funds from INCOME to other accounts per CAPs, transfer PROFIT and TAX to Bank 2 accounts ($0 balance at Bank 1), disburse owner salaries, and pay bills from OPEX. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p215]
- Every quarter: take 50% of PROFIT HOLD as profit distribution, pay tax liabilities from TAX HOLD, and adjust CAPs with Profit First Professional. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p215]
