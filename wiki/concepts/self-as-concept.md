# The Self as Concept

## Summary

The self is defined as part of social reality—it's not exactly a fiction but neither is it objectively real in nature like a neutron. It depends on other people. The self is a plain, ordinary concept just like 'Tree,' 'Things That Protect You from Stinging Insects,' and 'Fear.' The fiction of the self is that you have some enduring essence that makes you who you are. You do not. Barrett speculates that the self is constructed anew in every moment by the same predictive core systems that construct emotions. Every concept learned includes the state of your body at the time of learning, giving a rudimentary mental basis of self. The default mode network has been called the 'self system.'
