# The Five Waves of Trust

## Summary

The Five Waves of Trust is a framework organizing trust building across different contexts: Wave 1 (Self Trust) establishes personal credibility; Wave 2 (Relationship Trust) applies the 13 Behaviors to interpersonal relationships; Wave 3 (Organizational Trust) creates alignment with internal stakeholders; Wave 4 (Market Trust) builds brand reputation with external stakeholders; Wave 5 (Societal Trust) establishes contribution as a global citizen.

## Key facts

- The five waves are sequential and cumulative: Wave 1 (Self Trust) establishes the credibility that becomes the foundation for Wave 2 (Relationship Trust), which extends into Wave 3 (Organizational Trust), which is visible externally as Wave 4 (Market Trust), which cumulatively affects Wave 5 (Societal Trust). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p283]
- In the Third Wave—Organizational Trust—the focus is on creating alignment that eliminates taxes and increases dividends inside the organization with internal stakeholders. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p283]
- In the Fourth Wave—Market Trust—the focus is on building a reputation or brand that inspires trust in the marketplace with external stakeholders. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p283]
- In the Fifth Wave—Societal Trust—the focus is on building trust within society based on principles of contribution and global citizenship recognized as an economic and social necessity. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p283]
- The 4 Cores and 13 Behaviors are tools used to establish or restore trust in every context—organizations, marketplace, and society—with the main principles being alignment (Organizational), reputation (Market), and contribution (Societal). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p340]
- Trust at the Fifth Wave is a direct result of trustworthiness beginning in the First Wave and flowing outward in relationships, organizations, and marketplace to fill society as a whole, as psychologist Carl Rogers stated: 'That which is most personal is most general.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p340]
