# Collective Intentionality

## Summary

Collective intentionality is the shared knowledge that a concept exists within a group, forming the foundation of social reality. Combined with language, it enables the transmission of purely mental concepts like emotions across individuals and generations, wiring brains for the social world. The human brain's unique combination of collective intentionality and words allows categorization as a cooperative act.
