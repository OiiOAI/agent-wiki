# Brainstorming

## Summary

Brainstorming is the creative process of generating ideas by identifying with mental pictures different from current reality. Once the outcome is clear, the brain automatically starts filling in the gaps to bridge the gap between current and desired states.

## Key facts

- The three basic principles of brainstorming are: don't judge/challenge/evaluate/criticize; go for quantity not quality; put analysis and organization in the background. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p115]
- Once you identify with a picture in your mind different from current reality, you automatically start filling in the gaps through brainstorming. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p112]
- The key is giving yourself permission to capture and express any idea, then later figuring out how it fits and what to do with it. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p113]
- Often you won't know what is a good idea until you have it, and sometimes you only realize it is good later on. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p116]
