# Team-First Thinking

## Summary

Team-first thinking is the philosophy that the team is the most effective means of software delivery, not individuals. This approach treats stable, long-lived teams as the fundamental building blocks of software delivery, with responsibilities matched to maximum team cognitive load. Research by Google found that who is on the team matters less than the team dynamics, and teams matter more than individuals when measuring performance. The approach requires organizations to invest in empowered, skilled teams as the foundation for agility and adaptability.

## Key facts

- The team is the most effective means of software delivery, not individuals. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L534-L535]
- Organizations should restrict team responsibilities to match the maximum team cognitive load. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L537-L538]
- Research by Google found that who is on the team matters less than the team dynamics, and teams matter more than individuals when measuring performance. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L849-L852]
- Google's research found that team dynamics matter more than individual qualifications for team performance. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L844-L847]
- Teams should be the smallest entity of delivery within the organization, and organizations should never assign work to individuals, only to teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L854-L856]
- Organizations need to invest in empowered, skilled teams as the foundation for agility and adaptability. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L565-L567]
- Team ownership helps provide continuity of care that modern systems need to retain their operability and stay fit for purpose. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L896-L898]
