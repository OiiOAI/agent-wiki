# Performance-based measurement of situation awareness

## Summary

Performance-based measurement of situation awareness infers subjects' SA from their observable actions or the effects these actions have on system performance. Unlike knowledge-based or verbalization measures that examine the operator's internal state, performance-based measures focus solely on external outputs—physical actions taken by the operator and their consequences. This approach is particularly valuable for evaluating system adequacy and identifying weak points in the operator's situation awareness.

## Key facts

- Performance-based measurement infers subjects' situation awareness from their observable actions or the effects these actions have on system performance. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p201]
- Performance-based measures examine directly only elements external to the operator; the user's awareness can only be inferred. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p211]
- Performance-based measures are generally suited for demonstrations of procedural knowledge rather than declarative knowledge. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p211]
- Performance-based measures can assess the requirements placed on situation awareness by the decision and control actuation strategies used by the operator. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p212]
- Performance-based measurement can identify the relative impact of situation awareness and other potential blocks to satisfactory performance. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p212]
- A weakness of performance-based measures is that good performance may result despite poor situation awareness through proceduralized responses or luck. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p215]
