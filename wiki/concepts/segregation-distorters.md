# Segregation Distorter

## Summary

Segregation distorters are genes that manipulate meiosis to increase their own transmission probability beyond the expected 50%. They are examples of 'genes that beat the system' (James Crow). While they may harm the organism, they spread because natural selection works at the genic level.

## Key facts

- James Crow called segregation distorters 'genes that beat the system'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p276]
- If the bad effects of a gene are on the body and the good effects are on the gene alone, from the body's point of view the net effect is all bad. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p277]
- Although segregation distorters exist they aren't very common, raising the question of why meiosis is normally fair. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p278]
