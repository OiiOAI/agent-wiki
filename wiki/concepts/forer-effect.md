# Forer Effect

## Summary

The Forer effect (Barnum effect) is the tendency to accept vague, universal descriptions as personally accurate. In 1948, Bertram Forer gave students an identical passage crafted from astrology columns; they rated it 4.3 out of 5 for accuracy. This explains why pseudosciences like astrology and palmistry work.

## Key facts

- Bertram Forer gave students an identical passage from astrology columns, suggesting it was personalized; on average they rated it 4.3 out of 5 for accuracy. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p145]
- The Forer effect explains why pseudosciences work: astrology, astrotherapy, handwriting study, biorhythm analysis, palmistry, tarot readings and séances. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p145]
