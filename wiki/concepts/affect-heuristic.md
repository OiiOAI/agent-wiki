# Affect Heuristic

## Summary

The affect heuristic is a mental shortcut where emotional reactions determine risk/benefit assessments. If you like something, you believe risks are smaller and benefits greater. A Harley owner who reads about dangers will subconsciously rate the experience as 'an even greater sense of freedom.'

## Key facts

- The affect heuristic puts risks and benefits on the same sensory thread: if you like something, you believe risks are smaller and benefits greater. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p149]
- Researchers Hirschleifer and Shumway found a correlation between morning sunshine and stock market performance across 26 exchanges from 1982-1997. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p150]
- University of Michigan research showed subjects preferred Chinese characters that followed a smiling face rather than an angry or neutral one, shown for less than 1/100th of a second.
