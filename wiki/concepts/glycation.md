# Glycation

## Summary

Glycation is a process where glucose molecules bond to proteins or cell surfaces, causing damage. It leads to the formation of advanced glycation end-products (AGEs), which are associated with inflammation, oxidative stress, and accelerated aging of the brain and other organs.

## Key facts

- Glycation occurs when a glucose molecule bonds to a nearby protein or cell surface, causing damage to organs and tissues including the brain. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p91]
- Glycation rate can be expressed as: Glycation = Glucose Exposure x Time. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p92]
- Alzheimer's-affected brains contain three times the amount of AGEs as normal brains. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p94]
- Elevated blood sugar increases risk of dementia even among nondiabetics due to glycation processes. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p95]
- AGEs are strongly associated with aging of the skin, liver, kidneys, heart, and bones. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p97]
