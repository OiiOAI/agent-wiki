# Ohm's Law

## Summary

The fundamental relationship in electrical circuits stating that potential difference equals current multiplied by resistance, forming the basis for understanding electrical circuits.

## Key facts

- Ohm's Law states that the potential difference (in volts) across a conductor equals the current (in amperes) multiplied by the resistance (in ohms). [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1295]
- The ohm is named after German physicist Georg Simon Ohm (1789-1854). [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1300]
