# The Dabbler, The Obsessive, and The Hacker

## Summary

These are three archetypal learner types that fail to stay on the path of mastery. The Dabbler jumps between interests seeking novelty and initial excitement. The Obsessive pushes relentlessly toward goals, refusing to accept plateaus and often burning out. The Hacker settles on a comfortable plateau and refuses to advance further. Leonard notes that in audiences, these categories typically divide into roughly even thirds, with most people able to identify with at least one type.

## Key facts

- The Dabbler approaches each new activity with enormous enthusiasm but abandons it when the first plateau appears. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L178-L181]
- The Dabbler loves the rituals of getting started, new equipment, and the shine of newness rather than sustained practice. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L179-L180]
- The Obsessive is goal-oriented and wants immediate results, refusing to accept plateaus and often redoubling effort when progress stalls. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L189-L192]
- Leonard describes American corporate managers as often fitting the Obsessive profile, sacrificing long-term planning for short-term results. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L194-L196]
- The Hacker stays on the plateau indefinitely after achieving minimal competence, doing only enough to get by. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L203-L206]
- The Hacker looks at marriage or relationships as a comfortable refuge rather than an opportunity for learning and development. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L207-L209]
- Leonard notes that audience polls typically show these three types divide into roughly even thirds. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L215-L216]

## Inferences

- Inference: These three types represent different relationships to the mastery plateau - the Dabbler avoids it, the Obsessive fights it, and the Hacker accepts but never leaves it.
