# Psychical Quantity (Qἠ)

## Summary

Qἠ represents Freud's concept of psychical quantity, distinct from physical quantity (Q), though derived from clinical observation. It appears in two forms: Q in flow (current, passage of excitation) and Q as static cathexis (filling a neuron). The distinction corresponds to kinetic versus potential energy. The binding of Qἠ—converting mobile to bound energy—is central to secondary processes and the ego's inhibitory function. Freud explicitly acknowledged that the ultimate nature of this neuronal motion remained unknown to him.

## Key facts

- Qἠ is measurable in two ways: by the height of the level of cathexis within a neuron and by the amount of flow between cathexes. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p393]
- The bound state of Qἠ combines high cathexis with small current, characterizing processes of thought mechanically. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p369]
- External quantity cannot be represented by psychical quantity; psychical quantity means something quite different which is not represented in reality. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p362]
- Large Qἠ would pass along several associative pathways simultaneously and leave no time for thought cathexis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p368]

## Inferences

- Inference: The concept of Qἠ represents Freud's attempt to quantify psychical processes, later evolving into concepts of psychic energy and cathexis
- Inference: The bound/free distinction anticipated later psychoanalytic concepts of bound vs. free libido and became fundamental to understanding repression as a binding operation

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud acknowledged that the nature of 'neuronal motion' underlying Q was unknown and could not be explained mechanically within the 'Project'
