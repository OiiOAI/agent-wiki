# Role Distance

## Summary

Role distance deals with the degree to which individuals separate themselves from the roles they are in. Goffman was interested in how much an individual embraces a given role. One of Goffman's key insights is that role distance is a function of one's social status—high-status people often manifest role distance for reasons other than people in low-status positions. For example, a high-status surgeon may manifest role distance to relieve tension, while people in low-status positions may manifest defensiveness.
