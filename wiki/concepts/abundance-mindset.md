# Abundance Mindset

## Summary

An abundance mindset is a way of looking at the world as full of valuable and helpful things including ideas, insights, tools, collaborations, and opportunities. It tells us there is an endless amount of powerful knowledge everywhere we look. The shift from scarcity to abundance is presented as one of the biggest transformations that occurs when building a Second Brain, involving letting go of things that no longer serve us.

## Key facts

- An Abundance Mindset tells us that there is an endless amount of incredibly powerful knowledge everywhere we look—in the content we consume, in our social network, in our bodies and intuitions, and in our own minds. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p199]
- An Abundance Mindset tells us we do not need to consume or understand all of it, or even much of it—we only need a few seeds of wisdom, and the seeds we most need tend to continually find us again and again. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p199]
- The shift to a mindset of abundance is about letting go of things we thought we needed to survive but that no longer serve us. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p200]

## Inferences

- Inference: The abundance mindset allows people to engage with information as a curator rather than a collector
