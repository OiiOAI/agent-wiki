# Leptin

## Summary

Leptin is the 'satiety' hormone that helps regulate energy balance by inhibiting hunger. Secreted by fat cells, it controls energy expenditure through the hypothalamus. Leptin plummets with sleep deprivation, and chronic elevation can cause leptin resistance. The hormone also plays important roles in cognitive function and synaptic plasticity.

## Key facts

- Leptin is the 'satiety' hormone that helps regulate energy balance by inhibiting hunger, and it plummets with sleep deprivation. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p324]
- Leptin is secreted by fat cells, so more fat cells means more circulating leptin, which signals the brain to increase calorie burning. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p324]
- Chronically elevated leptin can cause leptin resistance, where the signal of 'satiety' and positive benefits of leptin on metabolism become lost. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p324]
- Very-low-carb diets can offset some metabolic disadvantages of weight loss to the tune of 100 to 300 calories per day by maintaining leptin sensitivity. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p326]
- Leptin is involved in synaptic plasticity in the hippocampus, facilitating long-term potentiation—the creation of strong, long-lasting memories. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p327]
- There is a strong relationship between low levels of leptin and depression and anxiety. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p327]

## Inferences

- Inference: Maintaining leptin sensitivity through sleep optimization, carbohydrate management, and periodic high-carb refeeds may support both metabolic and cognitive health.
