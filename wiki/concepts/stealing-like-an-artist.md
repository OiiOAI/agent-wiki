# Steal Like an Artist

## Summary

The central thesis that all creative work builds on what came before, and that artists should actively collect, study, and learn from influences rather than trying to create something from nothing. Nothing is completely original—every new idea is a mashup or remix.

## Key facts

- When asked where artists get their ideas, the honest answer is 'I steal them.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p13]
- Jonathan Lethem has said that when people call something 'original,' nine out of ten times they just don't know the references or original sources involved. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p14]
- Kleon recommends chewing on one thinker you really love, then finding three people that thinker loved, and repeating this to build a creative genealogy tree. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p19]
- Jim Jarmusch advised to 'steal from anywhere that resonates with inspiration or fuels your imagination' and to select only things that speak directly to your soul. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p18]
