# Rule of Thirds

## Summary

The rule of thirds is a photographic guideline for placing the most important element of an image at one of four intersection points where horizontal and vertical lines divide the image into thirds. This technique creates more visually interesting compositions than centering the subject.

## Key facts

- Professional photographers place the most important element away from the center using the rule of thirds. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p296]
- The rule of thirds divides image space with horizontal and vertical lines, placing important elements at intersections. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p297]
- When images include a horizon, placing the horizon one third or two thirds up from the bottom works well. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p303]
