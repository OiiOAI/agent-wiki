# Lecture Preparation

## Summary

Lecture preparation involves a systematic process including developing learning outcomes, creating an empowerment promise, identifying powerful ideas, preparing for class size, handling difficult situations, learning material, devising examples, creating an outline, and rehearsing. Winston reports spending an average of 20 hours preparing a new lecture, with demonstrations potentially adding another week.

## Key facts

- Each lecture prepared from scratch takes an average of 20 hours to prepare. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p178]
- Demonstrations involving programs written by the lecturer can add a week to preparation time. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p178]
- Reusing a lecture from a friend expert, retold in your own voice, can reduce preparation to 10 hours. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p178]
- Lecture preparation steps include deciding learning outcomes, developing empowerment promises, identifying powerful ideas, ensuring approach fits class size, and rehearsing. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p184]
- In large classes of 70+ students, the lecture becomes theater and the instructor must perform as a personality. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p177]
