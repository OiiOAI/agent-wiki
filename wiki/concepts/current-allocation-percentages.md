# Current Allocation Percentages (CAPs)

## Summary

Current Allocation Percentage (CAP) represents where your business is today financially. This is the number that business owners adjust slowly but consistently over time to get closer and closer to the TAPs. For example, if the Profit TAP is 20 percent but historical profit allocations have been 0 percent, the CAP starts at 0 percent and incrementally increases each quarter.

## Key facts

- CAP is where your business is today and is the number you will adjust slowly but consistently over time to get closer to the TAPs. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p93]
- The key to successful Profit First implementation lies in stringing together a series of many small steps in a repeating pattern. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p94]
- Entrepreneurs typically manage their money in an erratic, noisy rhythm that causes confusion and panic. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p94]
- Always start with CAPs where you are now and increase by 1 percent each quarter. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p100]

## Inferences
