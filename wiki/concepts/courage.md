# Courage

## Summary

The ability to perform an action despite feeling fear and anxiety about doing it. Courage is essential for overcoming social and sexual anxiety.

## Key facts

- Courage is defined as the ability to perform an action despite feeling fear and anxiety about doing it. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- The philosopher states: 'Adlerian psychology is a psychology of courage.'. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p57]
- The book argues: 'Your unhappiness cannot be blamed on your past or your environment. And it isn't that you lack competence. You just lack courage. One might say you are lacking in the courage to be happy.'. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p57]
- Having the courage to change one's lifestyle is described as 'strong medicine' but necessary for happiness. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p60]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book's title 'The Courage to Be Disliked' suggests that true freedom and happiness require the willingness to be disapproved of by others, which is impossible if one seeks recognition.
