# Federal Indictment

## Summary

The federal prosecution of Medical Manager Corporation executives stemming from Bobby Davids' false claims of executive-level accounting fraud. Ten executives were indicted on conspiracy charges carrying potential 15-year sentences, though all charges were ultimately dismissed after Judge Norton found the government's case lacked evidentiary support.

## Key facts

- Ten Medical Manager executives were indicted on December 19, 2005 on conspiracy charges carrying up to 15 years in prison. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p200]
- The indictment listed things Bobby Davids claimed to have done improperly and stated that executives 'caused them to happen'. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p201]
- All indicted Medical Manager executives walked away free after the government's case was dismissed. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p214]

## Inferences

- Inference: The indictment was based largely on Bobby Davids' testimony, which Judge Norton later found noncredible, suggesting the prosecution relied on a single witness without independent corroboration
