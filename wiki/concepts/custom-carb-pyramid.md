# Custom Carb Pyramid

## Summary

A three-tiered carbohydrate intake framework introduced in Genius Foods, designed to help individuals determine their optimal carbohydrate intake for metabolic flexibility. The system progresses from ultra-low carbohydrate intake (ketogenic) for initial metabolic adaptation, to low(er) carb maintenance, and optionally to carb cycling for active individuals.

## Key facts

- The first tier (Days 1-14) recommends consuming only 20 to 40 grams of carbohydrates per day to deplete glycogen and fat-adapt the brain. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p405]
- For long-term ketogenic dieters seeking weight loss, one weekly high-carb refeed of 100 to 150 grams of carbs is recommended. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p405]
- The second tier (after 14 days) recommends consuming 50 to 75 grams of carbohydrates per day for weight maintenance and light physical activity. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p406]
- The optional third tier (carb cycling) allows 75 to 150 grams of carbohydrates per day, consumed after vigorous training. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p406]
- Protein intake should start at 0.5 grams per pound of body weight, increasing to 0.8 grams per pound for weight changes or heavy weight training. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p407]
- Carbohydrates should be concentrated into a single sitting post-workout to avoid prolonged insulin spikes. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p407]
- Eating 2 to 4 meals per day is recommended. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p407]
- For fasting, an 8-hour feeding window is suggested for men and 10 hours for women. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p407]
- Glycogen-depleting workouts consist of 40 to 70 reps per muscle group across 2 to 3 muscle groups, including compound movements. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p406]

## Inferences

- Inference: The three-tier system is designed to progressively transition individuals from fat-adaptation to metabolic flexibility, allowing customization based on metabolic health status and activity level.
- Inference: The weekly high-carb refeed for ketogenic dieters serves to replenish muscle glycogen stores and support hormonal function while maintaining overall low-carb intake.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The science on carb refeeds is described as 'far from settled,' suggesting ongoing research is needed to validate optimal protocols.
- Uncertain: Individual variation in carb tolerance is acknowledged, with experimentation recommended to determine optimal intake.
