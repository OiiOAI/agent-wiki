# Sexual Dimorphism

## Summary

Sexual dimorphism refers to differences in male versus female body size within a species. High degrees of sexual dimorphism are highly correlated with polygyny, being favored by sexual selection where larger size confers advantages in competition for mates.

## Key facts

- Sexual dimorphism refers to difference in male versus female body size. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p526]
- High degrees of sexual dimorphism are highly correlated with degree of polygyny in a species. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p526]
- Sexual dimorphism is favored by sexual selection mediated by threat and fighting behaviors, where being larger confers an advantage for gaining access to mates. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p526]

## Inferences

- Inference: The correlation between body size differences and polygyny suggests that competition among males for access to multiple females has shaped the evolution of physical traits
