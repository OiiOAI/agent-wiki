# Default Mode Network

## Summary

The Default Mode Network (DMN) is a set of brain regions active during quiet rest and mind wandering that includes sub-networks for monitoring the environment, recalling past events, imagining future ones, navigating space, and interpreting others' actions. The DMN is also active during REM sleep and may underlie the neural substrate of dreaming.

## Key facts

- The DMN was discovered when Marcus Raichle realized that the brain regions turning off during mental tasks were the same regardless of the task being performed. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p137]
- The DMN includes sub-networks for monitoring the environment for important changes, recalling past events, imagining future ones, navigating through space, and interpreting words and actions of others. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p138]
- Much of the DMN is also activated during REM sleep, suggesting dreaming may constitute a brain state of enhanced mind wandering. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p139]
- The DMN changes based on prior activities; after learning a motor task, DMN activity patterns during rest predict subsequent performance improvement. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p138]
- Silvana Horovitz found that the DMN is active throughout the hypnagogic period, with dramatic increases in visual processing regions after sleep onset. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p140]
- William Domhoff proposed that the neural substrate of dreaming lies within the DMN. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p139]
- Marcus Raichle identified the default mode network in 2001 as areas that consume the most energy but go 'down' when people focus on tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p65]
- The DMN stores schemas—mental frameworks that allow the brain to process, organize, and interpret information with minimal effort. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p66]
- Different parts of the DMN store schemas for different components: people and things are stored separately from places and situations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p67]
- Amyloid accumulates in the DMN in about 20 percent of older adults long before Alzheimer's symptoms appear. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p69]
- The hippocampus activates and communicates with the DMN at event boundaries to store memories for specific events. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p69]
- Marcus Raichle discovered the default mode network in the 1980s using PET scan technology, observing that the brain remains highly active during mind-wandering. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p103]
- Nathan Spreng noted that the default mode network can be 'suppressed' by constant digital interruption, with the brain in a 'constant stimulus-driven, stimulus-bound environment.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p107]

## Inferences

- Inference: The DMN's role in both mind wandering and dreaming suggests these states share functional similarities in memory processing.
- Inference: The DMN deconstructs experiences into reusable LEGO-like pieces, while the hippocampus provides instructions for how to assemble them into specific memories. This allows us to build new memories using familiar components.
- Inference: The default mode network appears to be essential for creativity, sense-making, and long-term planning — functions that are compromised by smartphone distraction.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Some scientists disagree about whether the default mode network is the primary region involved in mind-wandering — this remains an ongoing debate.
