# Contribution to Others

## Summary

Contribution to others is the third key concept (after self-acceptance and confidence in others) in developing community feeling. It means acting on one's comrades and attempting to contribute. The philosopher clarifies that contribution to others does not connote self-sacrifice; rather, it is something one does in order to be truly aware of the worth of the 'I.' People truly become aware of their worth when they feel 'I am beneficial to the community' or 'I am of use to someone.' The essence of work is presented as contribution to the common good, not merely earning money.
