# Production of Space

## Summary

The production of space is Henri Lefebvre's central concept, arguing that Marxian theory should shift its focus from the means of production to how space itself is produced and reproduced by social forces. Space serves multiple roles in reproducing capitalism and class structures.

## Key facts

- Lefebvre argued for shifting the focus of Marxian theory from the means of production to the production of space. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Lefebvre distinguished spatial practice (production and reproduction of space), representations of space (space as conceived by elites such as urban planners and architects), and representational spaces (lived experiences of people). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Representations of space are dominant over both spatial practices and representational spaces, with elites considering this 'true space' and using it to achieve and maintain dominance. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Lefebvre argued that abstract space is a tool of power, with the ruling class using it to gain control over increasingly large spaces. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
- Space can serve roles as: a force of production, a vast commodity, a politically instrumental entity, a basis for reproducing property relations, a form of superstructure, and a space of positive potential for reappropriation. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
- While abstract space seeks to control and homogenize everyone, differential space would accentuate difference and freedom from control. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
