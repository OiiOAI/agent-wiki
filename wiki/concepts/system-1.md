# System 1

## Summary

System 1 is the fast, automatic, and effortless mode of thinking that operates continuously, generating impressions, intuitions, and intentions. It produces quick judgments based on pattern recognition and associative memory, without conscious awareness or effort. Examples include detecting distance, recognizing emotions in voices, understanding simple sentences, and experiencing the Muller-Lyer illusion. System 1 is responsible for most of our daily decisions and evaluations, operating on the WYSIATI (What You See Is All There Is) principle, and is prone to systematic biases in certain situations.

## Key facts

- System 1 operates automatically and quickly, with little or no sense of voluntary control. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p31]
- System 1 continuously generates impressions, intuitions, impulses, and inline judgments for System 2. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p35]
- System 1 cannot be turned off; if shown a word in your language, you will read it. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p36]
- System 1 is the origin of most of what System 2 thinks and does. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p36]
- System 1 excels at constructing coherent stories from minimal information and maintains a model of what is normal. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p71]
- System 1 detects causal connections automatically and is the source of our sense of cause and effect. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p76]
- System 1 is susceptible to the WYSIATI principle: it has limited information yet constructs a complete story. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p89]

## Inferences

- Inference: System 1 and System 2 are not literal brain systems but useful fictional characters for understanding cognitive processes.
- Inference: System 1 errors are predictable and systematic, not random.
