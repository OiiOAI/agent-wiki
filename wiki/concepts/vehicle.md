# Vehicle

## Summary

Vehicles are entities that work to propagate replicators. DNA molecules gang together into large communal survival machines or vehicles. Individual bodies like our own are vehicles. Vehicles don't replicate themselves; they work to propagate their replicators.

## Key facts

- DNA molecules gang together into large communal survival machines or 'vehicles'. The vehicles that we know best are individual bodies like our own. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p295]
- A body is not a replicator; it is a vehicle. Vehicles don't replicate themselves; they work to propagate their replicators. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p295]
- Gene and individual organism are cast in different, complementary roles—the role of replicator and the role of vehicle. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p296]
- The essential quality that an entity needs to become an effective gene vehicle is an impartial exit channel into the future, for all the genes inside it. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p297]
