# Multinutrients for aggression and explosive rage

## Summary

The benefit of using nutrients to reduce antisocial behaviors and aggression has been documented for decades. Multiple RCTs in children and adults have shown that broad-spectrum multinutrient supplements can significantly reduce aggression, with studies in school settings showing 28-47 percent greater decreases in rule violations and disciplinary incidents compared to placebo.

## Key facts

- The earliest controlled trial over twenty years ago found that giving children a broad array of minerals such as calcium, magnesium, and zinc along with vitamins such as thiamine, riboflavin, and folate resulted in a 28 percent greater decrease in rule violations at school compared with those receiving placebo. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p147]
- An RCT with forty children aged six to twelve showed that those given a multinutrient supplement exhibited 47 percent fewer antisocial behaviors requiring discipline than the forty children who received placebo. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p147]
- Studies showed that multinutrient treatment may decrease aggressive behavior, providing enough evidence for policy changes within prisons. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p148]
