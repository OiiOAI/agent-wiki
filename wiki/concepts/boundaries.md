# Boundaries

## Summary

Boundaries are presented as a key component of non-neediness. A non-needy man maintains clear boundaries — knowing what he will and will not tolerate in his relationships. This includes being willing to express when something is offensive, to tell women what he will not accept, and to walk away when necessary. The book emphasizes that boundaries should be established through honest communication rather than aggression or manipulation. Having strong boundaries demonstrates self-respect and non-neediness, which in turn creates attraction.

## Key facts

- A non-needy man does not seek to impose himself on the boundaries of others — he's merely interested in maintaining his own boundaries while respecting the boundaries of others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p67]
- If a woman says something offensive, a non-needy man will simply make it clear that he found what she said offensive and will not tolerate it again — how she responds is her choice. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p67]
- If a woman needs to leave a date after 30 minutes, a non-needy man will smile and say 'Nice meeting you' and let it go — he will not try to trick her into staying. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p67]
- Your ability and willingness to establish boundaries is inversely proportional to how needy you are. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p68]
- The first step to becoming more vulnerable is often to begin establishing your own boundaries, learning how to say no to people, particularly women. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p68]
- Boundaries are defined as the limit of interaction and communication one finds acceptable. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Boundaries can be strong or weak based upon the person's confidence level. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Strong boundaries are both a cause and effect of True Confidence. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]
- Standing up for one's boundaries often triggers attraction and always generates greater respect. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p265]

## Inferences

- Inference: Boundary-setting requires self-awareness — men who have been needy their entire lives often don't know what they're willing to stand up for and what they're willing to let slide.
