# Complex

## Summary

A complex is an emotionally cathected ideational content present in the mind that influences associations and reactions. Complexes may be conscious or unconscious, and can be triggered by stimulus words in association experiments or by everyday experiences. The concept emerged from the Zürich school (Bleuler, Jung) but Freud had used related terminology earlier.

## Key facts

- The reaction to a stimulus word cannot be a chance one but must be determined by an ideational content present in the mind of the reacting subject. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2984]
- Complexes influence reactions either by the stimulus word touching the complex directly or by the complex succeeding in making a connection through intermediate links.
