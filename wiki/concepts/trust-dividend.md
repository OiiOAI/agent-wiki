# Trust Dividend

## Summary

The Trust Dividend is the positive counterpart to the Trust Tax. When trust is high, organizations and individuals receive a 'performance multiplier' that elevates every dimension of performance. High trust produces increased speed and decreased costs, along with improved collaboration, innovation, engagement, and quality of relationships.

## Key facts

- The formula for high trust impact is: Up Trust leads to Up Speed and Down Cost. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p39]
- Warren Buffett completed the McLane acquisition (a $23 billion company) in less than one month with no due diligence costs because both parties operated with high trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p41]
- High trust organizations have nearly three times higher total return to shareholders than low-trust organizations, according to a study by Watson Wyatt. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p48]
- Schools with high trust had more than three times higher chance of improving test scores than schools with low trust, according to Stanford professor Tony Bryk's research. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p48]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Trust Dividend demonstrates that investing in trust-building yields quantifiable returns beyond mere cost savings.
