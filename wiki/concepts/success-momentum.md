# Success Momentum

## Summary

Success momentum is the phenomenon where feeling successful at something—even something tiny—causes your confidence to grow quickly and motivation to increase, leading to more success. Research from the Tiny Habits method shows that the frequency of successes matters more than their size. This creates a positive feedback loop where tiny wins lead naturally to bigger changes.

## Key facts

- Success leads to success, and the size of the success doesn't matter much—what matters is the frequency of successes. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p194]
- When you feel successful, your motivation level rises, which may move you above the Action Line and enable you to do harder behaviors. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p196]
- The Fogg Behavior Model shows that you can do harder behaviors as your motivation levels rise from feeling successful. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p197]
- Tiny successes can catalyze 'big leap' breakthroughs because when a demotivator (like fear) goes away, you open the door to bigger and harder behaviors. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p197]
