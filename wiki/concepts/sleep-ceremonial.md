# Sleep Ceremonial

## Summary

A sleep ceremonial is a set of rituals performed before sleep that becomes pathological when it involves excessive, rigidly enforced behaviors with underlying symbolic meanings. In obsessional neurosis, these rituals serve as compromises between unconscious wishes and defensive operations.

## Key facts

- A pathological sleep ceremonial involves measures that seem rationally justified but include stipulations that run counter to the stated rational basis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4970]
- Clocks and watches may be symbolically banished as representations of female genitals, related to fear of sexual excitation during sleep. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5073]
- The ceremonial includes checking and repeating stipulations, resulting in hours spent without achieving sleep. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5072]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud interprets fear of clock-ticking as symbolically representing anxiety about nocturnal erections (clitoral excitation), demonstrating the sexual symbolism underlying obsessional rituals.
