# Data Overload

## Summary

Data overload is a significant problem where the rapid rate at which data changes creates a need for information intake that outpaces a person's ability to process it. As people can only take in and process a limited amount of information at a time, significant lapses in SA occur. The human brain becomes the bottleneck. The problem is not just volume but bandwidth—the rate at which data can flow through a person's sensory and information processing mechanisms.

## Key facts

- Data overload occurs when the rate of data changes outpaces the ability of a person's sensory and cognitive system to supply that need. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p55]
- The human brain becomes the bottleneck in data overload situations. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p55]
- Data that is jumbled and disorganized flows through the processing pipeline very slowly. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p56]
- Data presented in streams of text moves through the pipeline more slowly than graphically presented data. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p56]
