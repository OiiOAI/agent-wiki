# Response Vigor

## Summary

Response vigor refers to the speed or intensity with which actions are performed. Niv and colleagues proposed that tonic dopamine levels represent the net rate of rewards, which acts as the opportunity cost of time, determining optimal response vigor. This framework explains how motivational states affect the speed of instrumental responding.

## Key facts

- Every choice of action is accompanied by a choice of the speed or vigor with which that action will be performed, giving rise to response rates in free operant behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p331]
- Niv et al. (2005a) proposed that tonic levels of striatal dopamine represent the net rate of rewards, which serves as the opportunity cost of time and determines optimal responding vigor. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p332]
- When the net rate of rewards is higher, all actions should optimally be performed faster as a result of the elevated opportunity cost of time. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p332]
