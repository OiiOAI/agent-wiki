# DEAR MAN Skills

## Summary

DEAR MAN is an acronym for DBT interpersonal effectiveness skills used when asking for something, saying no, or maintaining one's point of view under pressure. The skills are: Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, (stay) Mindful, Appear confident, and Negotiate.

## Key facts

- DEAR MAN stands for Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, (stay) Mindful, Appear confident, and Negotiate. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p125]
- These skills are used for asking for something, saying no to another's request, and resisting pressure while maintaining one's point of view. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p125]
- Interpersonal effectiveness skills help build new relationships, strengthen current ones, and deal with conflict situations. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p133]
- DEAR MAN stands for Describe, Express, Assert, Reinforce, (Stay) Mindful, Appear Confident, and Negotiate. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p149]
- Describe means describing the current situation by sticking to the facts and telling the person exactly what you are reacting to. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p149]
- Express means expressing your feelings and opinions about the situation without assuming the other person knows how you feel. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p149]
- Assert means asserting yourself by asking for what you want or saying no clearly without assuming others will figure out what you want. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p149]
- Reinforce means reinforcing or rewarding the person ahead of time by explaining positive effects of getting what you want or need. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p149]
- (Stay) Mindful means keeping your focus on your goals, maintaining your position, using 'broken record' to repeat your request, and ignoring attacks or distractions. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p150]
- Appear Confident means using a confident voice tone and physical manner with good eye contact, avoiding stammering, whispering, or retreating. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p150]
- Negotiate means being willing to give to get by offering and asking for other solutions, reducing your request, or turning the problem over to the other person. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p150]
