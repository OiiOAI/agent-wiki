# The Limbic System Is a Fiction

## Summary

The limbic system concept, said to be evolutionarily old and the home of emotion in the brain, is described as a fiction. Experts in brain evolution no longer take the limbic system seriously as a system. Paul Broca named le grand lobe limbique (the limbic lobe) and treated limbic tissue as a single unified entity, laying the first stone toward essentializing it as the home of emotion. Over the next century, Broca's limbic lobe morphed into a unified 'limbic system' for emotion, guided by believers in the classical view. Modern neuroscience has shown that the limbic system is not the home of emotion in the brain, and no single brain area is dedicated to emotion.
