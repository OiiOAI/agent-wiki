# Three Categories of Women

## Summary

Manson divides all women a man encounters into three categories: Receptive (sexually/romantically attracted), Neutral (uncertain and needing to be swayed), and Unreceptive (unavailable or uninterested). The goal is to quickly identify Unreceptive women and move on, polarize Neutral women through authentic expression, and escalate with Receptive women.

## Key facts

- Women who are Unreceptive do not reciprocate signs of interest and show signs of disinterest; they are unavailable or uninterested. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p82]
- Neutral women eventually polarize one way or another; if never approached, they usually become Unreceptive (the Friend Zone). [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p83]
- Women demonstrate Receptivity by initiating with you or enthusiastically reciprocating your advances. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p83]
- Most women, especially beautiful women, expect men to initiate even when attracted. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p84]
- The percentage of women in each category varies widely by demographic context and the man's attributes. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p85]
