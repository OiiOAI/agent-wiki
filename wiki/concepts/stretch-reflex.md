# Stretch Reflex

## Summary

The stretch reflex is a monosynaptic reflex that causes a muscle to contract in response to lengthening of that muscle. Ia sensory axons from muscle spindles make direct excitatory connections with alpha motor neurons innervating the homonymous muscle, while Ia inhibitory interneurons inhibit antagonist motor neurons. This reflex underlies muscle tone and provides automatic compensation for unexpected perturbations.

## Key facts

- The stretch reflex is a monosynaptic reflex pathway in which Ia sensory axons from muscle spindles make direct excitatory connections with alpha motor neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p762]
- Liddell and Sherrington demonstrated that the stretch reflex requires both dorsal roots (sensory input) and ventral roots (motor output). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p762]
- The reflex latency through the spinal cord is approximately 0.5-0.9 ms, consistent with a single central synapse. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p767]
- Ia fibers also excite motor neurons innervating synergist muscles and inhibit antagonists through Ia inhibitory interneurons (reciprocal innervation). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p763]
- In decerebrate preparations, stretch reflexes are dramatically enhanced due to loss of descending inhibition from higher brain centers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p762]
- The stretch reflex acts as a negative feedback servomechanism that tends to maintain muscle at a reference length. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p773]
- The stretch reflex involves Ia afferent fibers from muscle spindles making direct excitatory connections on alpha motor neurons, producing a monosynaptic reflex with a delay of approximately 0.5 to 0.9 ms at a single synapse. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p847]
- Stretch reflexes were shown to require sensory input from muscle to spinal cord and a return path to muscle by Liddell and Sherrington, who demonstrated they could be abolished by cutting either the dorsal or ventral root. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p844]
- The stretch reflex resists lengthening of a muscle, counteracting the stretch and enhancing the spring-like properties of muscles. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p844]
- Decerebrate animals (with brainstem transected) exhibit dramatically increased extensor muscle tone due to unopposed facilitation of stretch reflex circuits by brain stem pathways. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p847]
- The stretch reflex loop acts continuously, with output (change in muscle length) becoming input, tending to keep the muscle close to a desired or reference length. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p857]
- The stretch reflex is a negative feedback system, or servomechanism, because it tends to counteract or reduce deviations from the reference value of the regulated variable. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p857]
- In 1963 Ragnar Granit proposed that the reference value in voluntary movements is set by descending signals that act on both alpha and gamma motor neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p857]
- The monosynaptic stretch reflex declines in strength as humans progress from standing to walking to running because stiffness increases naturally as muscle force increases. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p851]

## Inferences

- Inference: The stretch reflex provides automatic compensation for small perturbations during movement, with larger unexpected loads requiring conscious correction through voluntary motor commands.
