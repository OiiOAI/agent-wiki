# Stream-aligned Teams

## Summary

Stream-aligned teams are the primary team form in the Team Topologies approach. These are teams optimized for flow, with all they need to effect continuous delivery of value and be fully responsive to the associated feedback cycles. System design seeks not just loose coupling but a decomposition that supports flow and lowers dependencies and coordination needs between stream-aligned teams.
