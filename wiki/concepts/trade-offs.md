# Trade-offs

## Summary

Trade-offs are an inherent reality of life. We cannot have it all or do it all. Nonessentialists ask 'How can I do it all?' while Essentialists ask 'Which problem do I want?' Essentialists make trade-offs deliberately and strategically, asking 'What do I want to go big on?' instead of 'What do I have to give up?'

## Key facts

- As economist Thomas Sowell wrote: 'There are no solutions. There are only trade-offs.'. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p54]
- By trying to operate by two incompatible strategies, Continental Airlines started to undermine their ability to be competitive, demonstrating that ignoring the reality of trade-offs is a terrible strategy. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p51]
- We can either make the hard choices for ourselves or allow others—whether our colleagues, our boss, or our customers—to decide for us. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p52]
