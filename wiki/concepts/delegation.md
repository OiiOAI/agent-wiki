# Delegation

## Summary

Delegation is the process of handing off tasks to the appropriate party when you are not the best person to perform the action. Effective delegation requires tracking handoffs and following up appropriately.

## Key facts

- Delegation formats include sending an email, writing a note, leaving a voice mail or text, adding to an agenda list, or direct conversation. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p185]
- Email is usually the fastest delegation method, provides an electronic record, and allows the recipient to deal with it at their convenience. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p185]
- If an action is delegated and you care whether it happens, you must track it in a Waiting For system. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p185]
- Delegation is not always downstream; you may delegate to subordinates, superiors, or peers as appropriate. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p184]
