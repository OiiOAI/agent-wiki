# 800-Gram Challenge

## Summary

The 800-Gram Challenge is a nutrition assessment and eating program developed by EC Synkowski. The challenge involves eating 800 grams (by weight) of fruits and vegetables daily, simplifying nutrition guidelines into one measurable goal.

## Key facts

- The goal is to eat 800 grams of fruits and vegetables per day. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1480-L1482]
- Produce can be fresh, cooked, frozen, or canned in water. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1483-L1484]
- 800 grams of raw produce amounts to approximately 6 cups. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1494-L1495]
- One cup of produce is approximately the size of a fist. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1495]
- Raw leafy greens (spinach, kale, collards, chard) are tallied at reduced rates: 5 cups of leafy greens equals 1 cup of other produce like broccoli. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1496-L1498]
- The approach focuses on adding foods rather than subtracting them. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1488-L1490]
