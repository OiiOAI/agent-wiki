# Practice Theory

## Summary

Practice theory focuses on practice as a routinized way of acting, emphasizing the impact of taken-for-granted assumptions on human conduct. It is derived from Bourdieu, Foucault, Giddens, Garfinkel, Latour, and Butler. Key elements include routinized bodily performances, mental activities, objects, and knowledge. The body is central as the site of the social. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p551]

## Key facts

- Practice theory focuses on routinized ways of acting and taken-for-granted assumptions affecting human conduct. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p551]
- The body is central to practice theory as the site of the social and involves routinized bodily performances. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p552]
