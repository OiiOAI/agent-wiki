# Someday/Maybe

## Summary

Someday/Maybes are things one wants to reassess in the future—ranging from trips to take, books to read, projects to tackle, to skills to develop. They are tracked on a 'back burner' or 'on hold' list and serve as a creative outlet for potential future engagements.

## Key facts

- Someday/Maybes are not throwaway items; they may be some of the most interesting and creative things one will ever get involved with. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p228]
- Activating and maintaining a Someday/Maybe category unleashes the flow of creative thinking by giving permission to imagine cool things without committing to do anything yet. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p229]
- Typical Someday/Maybe categories include things to get or build for home, hobbies to take up, skills to learn, creative expressions to explore, trips to take, and organizations to join. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p229]
- Special categories can include food, children activities, books to read, music, movies, gift ideas, and weekend trips. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p230]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Someday/Maybe list allows the imagination to foster changes in perception and performance, potentially adding wonderful adventures when opportunities arise.
