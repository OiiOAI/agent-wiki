# Viral World Models

## Summary

Viral world models are models of the world that cause the host brain to act in ways that spread the model to other brains. Unlike isolated false beliefs such as phantom limbs, viral models prescribe specific behaviors designed to replicate the model from brain to brain in increasing numbers.

## Key facts

- A viral model prescribes behaviors that spread the model from brain to brain in increasing numbers. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p170]
- A phantom limb is an incorrect model but not viral because it is isolated to one brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p170]
- Flat-Earth belief is also not viral because maintaining it requires trusting only personal experiences, not spreading the belief. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p170]
- The history book example describes memes with three instructions: everything in the book is true and ignore contrary evidence, assist fellow believers, and banish or kill nonbelievers. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p171]

## Inferences

- Inference: Viral world models represent a category distinct from non-viral false beliefs because they include behavioral prescriptions for their own replication.
