# Three Blows to Narcissism

## Summary

Freud's characterization of three major scientific discoveries that progressively undermined human narcissism and self-importance: the cosmological blow (Copernicus showing Earth is not the universe's center), the biological blow (Darwin demonstrating human descent from animals), and the psychological blow (psychoanalysis revealing the ego is not master in its own house). These three blows explain much of the resistance to psychoanalytic theory.

## Key facts

- The cosmological blow to narcissism came through Copernicus's demonstration that Earth is not the stationary center of the universe. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5391]
- The biological blow resulted from Darwin's proof that humans are not superior to animals but share animal descent, having both physical structure and mental dispositions in common with them. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5391]
- The psychological blow consists of two discoveries: that sexual drives cannot be wholly tamed by the ego, and that mental processes are in themselves unconscious and only reach consciousness incompletely. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5395]
- The ego is not master in its own house because it cannot fully know unconscious mental processes and cannot fully control the sexual drives. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5395]
- These two psychological discoveries amount to the statement that the ego is not master in its own house, explaining resistance to psychoanalysis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5395]
