# SA-Oriented Design Future Directions

## Summary

SA-oriented design provides a general process for designing systems to support situation awareness based on supporting the key driver of decision making in dynamic systems. The authors acknowledge that current design principles are not complete and anticipate augmentation as more research is conducted on SA factors across various system domains. Future directions include solid empirical testing, objective SA measurement, and application to ongoing system design efforts in automobiles, military operations, air traffic control, power plants, and space missions.
