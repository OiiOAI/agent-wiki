# Hyperreality

## Summary

Hyperreality is Baudrillard's concept describing a condition where media cease to be mirrors of reality but become that reality, or even more real than reality itself. The real becomes subordinated and ultimately dissolved altogether, making it impossible to distinguish the real from the spectacle.
