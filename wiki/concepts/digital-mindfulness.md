# Digital Mindfulness

## Summary

Digital mindfulness is the application of mindfulness practices in one's digital life, involving the creation of structure and routines for technology use. Mindfulness is a psychological process and state of mind achieved by consciously focusing on the present moment while calmly accepting thoughts, sensations, and feelings without judgment. The practice, rooted in Buddhism and brought to Western medicine by Jon Kabat-Zinn, has been shown to induce the brain to create new gray matter and improve neuroplasticity.
