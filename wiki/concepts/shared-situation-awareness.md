# Shared Situation Awareness

## Summary

Shared situation awareness is the degree to which team members have the same understanding of shared SA requirements. It is a function of the overlap in individual goals between team members—only the subset of information relevant to both team members' goals needs to be shared. High shared SA requires accurate and timely sharing of system information, comprehension, and projections, plus understanding of other team members' task status and how actions impact each other.

## Key facts

- Shared SA is defined as the degree to which team members have the same SA on shared SA requirements. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p197]
- The need for shared SA is a function of the overlap in individual goals between team members; only information relevant to both members' goals needs to be shared. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p216]
- A dangerous state occurs when two team members share incorrect SA—both are wrong in the same way, making it less likely they will discover their picture is false. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p218]
- Shared mental models greatly enhance the ability of teams to develop the same understanding and projections based on lower-level data without requiring extra communications. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p225]
- Cross-training on each other's jobs helps build shared mental models and teams with shared mental models perform significantly better on team tasks. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p226]

## Inferences

- Inference: Shared SA involves not just sharing data but also higher-level comprehensions and projections that can differ between team members as each has access to different underlying information.
