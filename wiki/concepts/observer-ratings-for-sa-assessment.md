# Observer Ratings for Situation Awareness Assessment

## Summary

This chapter by Bell and Lyon describes the development and validation of observer-based methods for assessing situation awareness in operational fighter pilots. The approach uses subject-matter experts (SMEs) and peers to rate pilots on SA-related behaviors, demonstrating that SA can be reliably rated (interrater reliability 0.88-0.97) and that these ratings correlate with actual mission performance in simulated air combat (r = 0.56). The research addresses challenges in measuring SA including confounding with performance and credentials, and proposes that SA be defined in terms of knowledge in working memory about elements of the environment.

## Key facts

- The research was initiated in 1991 by Armstrong Laboratory (now Air Force Research Laboratory) in response to Air Force Chief of Staff questions about SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p145]
- The Air Staff defined SA as 'a pilot's continuous perception of self and aircraft in relation to the dynamic environment of flight, threats, and mission, and the ability to forecast, then execute tasks based on that perception'. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p146]
- The Air Staff definition combines processes, tasks, and linkages between them into a single construct, making it difficult to separate SA from other aspects of skilled performance. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p146]
- Pilots reliably classified their fellow pilots in terms of SA with high internal consistency and interrater reliability. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p148]
- Pearson correlation between SARS composite scores and SME ratings during simulated missions was 0.56 (p < .001, df = 38). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p151]
- There was noticeably less agreement between self-report SARS and peer/supervisory SARS (correlations 0.45-0.57). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p148]
- Pilots ranked lowest in SA by squadron ratings made more decision errors, particularly in tactics selection and flight leadership. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p152]
- High-SA pilots made more radio calls per minute than low-SA pilots for every type of call except acknowledgments. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p154]
- Hours of F-15 experience correlated 0.62 with composite SA rating, and five experience factors together produced a multiple correlation of 0.85 with SA rating. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p156]
- Mission qualification level correlated 0.82 with composite SA score. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p149]

## Inferences

- Inference: The high correlation between experience factors and SA ratings (0.85) suggests that raters may be influenced by pilot credentials, indicating potential confounding between SA ability and overall pilot experience.
- Inference: The finding that high-SA pilots make more information requests suggests they actively maintain SA rather than passively accumulate information.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The distinction between momentary SA and SA ability remains challenging in observer ratings, though rater instructions appeared to induce focus on momentary SA during scenarios.
- Uncertain: Mission success or failure undoubtedly had some influence on SA ratings despite attempts to isolate SA from performance.
