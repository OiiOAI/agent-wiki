# Prediction as Brain Function

## Summary

Prediction is proposed as the ultimate and most common of all global brain functions. The ability to predict is critical for survival in the animal kingdom—predicting the outcome of future events is essential for successful movement. Prediction allows the brain to operate discontinuously in time, conserving energy and computational overhead. The brain predicts by taking advantage of differences in electrical behavior among neurons, implementing a look-ahead function similar to Taylor Series Expansion.

## Key facts

- Prediction is the ultimate and most common of all global brain functions, critical to successful movement and survival in the animal kingdom. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p26]
- The brain must operate discontinuously in time to avoid being computationally overwhelmed; this dt look-ahead function saves time and energy. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p29]
- Neuronal circuits predict by having some neurons respond to the rate of change of stimuli rather than their final amplitude, implementing something similar to mathematical differentiation. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p45]
- The self is proposed as the centralization of prediction—the repository that brings together all predictive functions into a single construct. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p28]
