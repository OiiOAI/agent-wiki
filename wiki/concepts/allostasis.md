# Allostasis

## Summary

Allostasis refers to the process of achieving stability through change, in contrast to homeostasis which simply means a tendency towards equilibrium. In the context of the beast machine theory, interoceptive inference is understood as allostatic regulation of the body's physiological condition. This involves anticipatory responses based on predictions about future bodily states, which can be critical for survival.

## Key facts

- Allostasis means the process of achieving stability through change, as compared to homeostasis which means a tendency towards a state of equilibrium. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p181]
- Interoceptive inference can be understood as allostatic regulation of the physiological condition of the body. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p181]
- Allostatic regulation involves anticipatory responses through predictions about future bodily states and their dependence on different actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p180]
- Anticipatory control can be critical for survival—for example, waiting for blood acidity to go out of bounds before responding could be fatal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p180]
- Running away from a bear is an example of external anticipatory regulation, while the transient increase in blood pressure needed to run effectively is an internal anticipatory response. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p180]
