# Sharp Wave Ripples

## Summary

Sharp wave ripples (SWRs) are the most synchronous population events in the mammalian hippocampus, occurring during both waking and sleep states. They play dual roles in memory: retrospective consolidation of past experience and prospective planning of future actions.

## Key facts

- Sharp wave ripples can play palindrome, replaying place cell sequences in forward order and then backward. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p216]
- Forward sequences of neuronal assemblies during sharp wave ripples can be viewed as internalized vicarious trial-and-error mechanisms for subconscious optimization of future plans. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p217]
- Replay sequences during sharp wave ripples do not necessarily represent temporally compressed veridical episodes; some sequences represent snippets and fragments evaluating alternative paths. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p213]
- Sharp wave ripples during sleep following learning in a maze impair spatial memory performance when selectively abolished. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p212]
- Hippocampal sharp wave ripples couple with neocortical slow oscillations and sleep spindles to facilitate information exchange between brain structures. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p211]
- Sharp wave ripple-associated sequences can better match the rat's future path than its past path in two-dimensional exploration tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p214]
- Sharp wave ripples during post-experience sleep are critical for reinstating default network dynamics after experience-induced perturbation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p375]
- The cycles of a sharp wave ripple event contain sequential series of cell assemblies, each conceptualizable as a letter of a hypothetical neuronal vocabulary, so multiple cycles form a neuronal word. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p374]
- Clusters of sharp wave ripples allow words to be reassembled into novel sentences to match extended sequences of discrete segments of travel paths, situations, or events. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p374]
- Sharp wave ripples allow the flexible generation of a large number of combinations from a finite number of preformed neuronal words through this 'chunking' function. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p374]

## Inferences

- Inference: Sharp wave ripples support both memory consolidation (looking backward) and planning (looking forward), suggesting they are fundamental to the brain's ability to manipulate temporal information.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact mechanisms by which the neocortex reads hippocampal messages during sharp wave ripples remain incompletely understood.
