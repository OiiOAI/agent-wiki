# Emotional Health

## Summary

Emotional health encompasses the ability to regulate emotions and manage interpersonal relationships, distinct from clinical mental illness. It is a critical but often neglected component of healthspan, linked to longevity through suicide, substance abuse, and the physiological effects of chronic stress. The Trauma Tree framework identifies how adverse childhood experiences manifest as adult adaptations including addiction, codependency, and attachment disorders.

## Key facts

- Suicide ranks among the top ten causes of death across all age groups from teens into eighties. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p388]
- More than 100,000 Americans died from drug overdoses between April 2020 and April 2021, according to CDC estimates. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p388]
- Deaths from alcohol and drug abuse account for nearly 40% of all accidental deaths. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p389]
- Middle-aged white men and women are succumbing to drug and alcohol overdoses, liver disease, and suicide at unprecedented rates. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p389]
- Feeling connected and having healthy relationships is as imperative as maintaining efficient glucose metabolism or optimal lipoprotein profile. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p390]
- The Trauma Tree framework identifies five categories of trauma: abuse, neglect, abandonment, enmeshment, and witnessing tragic events. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p393]
- Childhood trauma manifests as four adult adaptations: addiction, codependency, habituated survival strategies (like anger), and attachment disorders. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p395]
- Sleep deprivation and poor emotional health create a feedback loop through the sympathetic nervous system and cortisol. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p365]
- Emotional health requires a set of effective tools and sensors with which to monitor, maintain, and restore emotional equilibrium. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p406]
- These tools and sensors are not innate; for most people, they must be learned, refined, and practiced daily. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p406]
- Clinical psychologist Jim Kochalka calls emotional dysregulation 'the inflammation of the psyche'. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p412]
- Attia's close friend Ric Elias, a survivor of the Hudson River plane landing, noted that 'people get old when they stop thinking about the future'. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p418]

## Inferences

- Inference: Emotional health and physical health are closely intertwined, with mental distress directly affecting physical biomarkers and longevity.
- Inference: Emotional health is connected to longevity because a life worth prolonging requires emotional wellbeing and meaningful relationships
