# Subliminal Cuing and Implicit Bias

## Summary

Sensory stimuli processed below conscious awareness that influence behavior and brain responses. Includes race-based amygdala activation (in 100 milliseconds), implicit racial biases in the fusiform face area, and fear conditioning to other-race faces. The prefrontal cortex can inhibit these automatic responses.

## Key facts

- Faces shown for less than 100 milliseconds (below conscious awareness) can be categorized by race with better-than-chance accuracy. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p88]
- Subliminal viewing of other-race faces activates the amygdala, with more activation in more implicitly racist individuals. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p89]
- The fusiform face area shows less activation for other-race faces, particularly in implicitly racist subjects. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p89]
- Fear conditioning occurs faster for other-race faces—the amygdala is prepared to learn to associate something bad with 'Them.'. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p89]
- When faces are shown long enough for conscious processing, the anterior cingulate and dlPFC activate and inhibit the amygdala. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p89]
- Smelling fear-sweat (from skydiving) activates the amygdala and increases interpreting ambiguous faces as fearful. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p93]

## Inferences

- Inference: Subliminal race bias is not inevitable—subtle manipulations can rapidly change amygdala response to Other faces.
