# The Planet of Happiness

## Summary

The Planet of Happiness addresses how to be happy, stating that happiness is a choice but being happy all the time is unrealistic as there will always be adversity and setbacks. The chapter explores having versus being, clashes between Human and Chimp needs, finding the right partner using the three-column technique (good points, acceptable points, and poison drops), building fulfilling relationships, self-image, self-worth, self-esteem, self-confidence, the happiness approach, the happiness list (immediate and delayed), the virtual twin technique, and happiness promoters including replacing Gremlins with Autopilots.

## Key facts

- Happiness is a choice; being happy all the time is unrealistic—there will always be adversity and setbacks, but it is natural to have ups and downs and you can get back to happiness by working on it. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p197]
- The 'having' aspect includes achievements, possessions, and emotional, physical, and intellectual aspects; the 'being' aspect includes self-image, self-worth, self-esteem, and self-confidence. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p201]
- The Chimp within us thinks in black and white while the Human sees things in shades of grey; success can be seen this way too and it is worth celebrating partial success rather than seeing it as failure. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p194]
- When choosing a partner, write three columns: good points, acceptable points, and poison drops (things you cannot live with); research on many couples indicates that whenever there is even one item in the last column, the relationship is unlikely to last. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p202]
- The handover technique involves imagining handing your partner to another person and being absolutely truthful about them warts and all, to help you see whether the relationship should continue. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p206]
- The virtual twin technique involves inventing a twin who is your best friend and loves you, giving yourself honest advice from that perspective. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p207]
- The Crystal-ball Gremlin looks into the future and sees doom and gloom, then makes a 'what might be' snowball that rolls down becoming a mountain; replace it with an Autopilot of 'cross each bridge as I come to it' and stay in the here and now. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p209]
- Unhappy people are often their own biggest critic; happy people learn to become their own biggest fan and accept themselves as they are and support themselves. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p218]
