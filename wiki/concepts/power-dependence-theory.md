# Power-Dependence Theory

## Summary

Power-dependence theory, developed by Richard Emerson, defines power as 'the level of potential cost which one actor can induce another to accept' and dependence as 'the level of potential cost an actor will accept within a relation.' The central proposition is that 'the power of one party over another in an exchange relation is an inverse function of his or her dependence on the other party.' Molm extended this to include punishment power (negative outcomes) alongside reward power, finding punishment power generally weaker due to the risk of escalating negative reactions.

## Key facts

- Emerson defined power as 'the level of potential cost which one actor can induce another to accept,' and dependence as 'the level of potential cost an actor will accept within a relation.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]
- Yamagishi, Gillmore, and Cook summarize power-dependence theory: 'The power of one party over another in an exchange relation is an inverse function of his or her dependence on the other party.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]
- Emerson stated: 'The dependence of actor A upon actor B is (1) directly proportional to A's motivational investment in goals mediated by B, and (2) inversely proportional to the availability of those goals to A outside of the A-B relation.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]
- Emerson wrote: 'The power of A over B is equal to, and based upon, the dependence of B upon A.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]
- Molm found that punishment power is weaker than reward power, in part because acts of punishment are likely to elicit negative reactions. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]
- Molm argued that 'The actors' dependencies on each other are the major structural determinants of their interaction and of their power over each other.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p327]

## Inferences

- Inference: Power-dependence theory predicts that over time, unequal power and dependency lead to imbalances that move toward a more balanced power-dependence relationship.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Molm (1994) suggested the relative weakness of punishment power may arise because it is not widely used, not because it is inherently less effective than reward power.
