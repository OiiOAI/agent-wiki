# Protomap Hypothesis

## Summary

The protomap hypothesis proposes that the first spatial map in the developing brain is a crude but functional 'sketch'—the brain's best guess about a new situation. With further exploration, additional neurons are recruited to refine this initial map. Hippocampal pyramidal neurons display adult-like place fields on the very first exploratory trip, suggesting preformed neuronal trajectories are matched to the environment.

## Key facts

- Rat pups' first exploratory trip beyond the nest occurs between postnatal days 15-17, and hippocampal pyramidal neurons already display adult-like place fields on this very first trip. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p373]
- The hippocampus possesses many possible neuronal trajectories before exploration has begun, allowing new maps to be retrieved instantly when pups enter novel environments. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p373]
- Emergence of stable entorhinal grid cells is delayed until weaning, while place fields appear earlier, suggesting a sequence of map development. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p373]
- The first spatial map may be only a crude 'protomap' that is refined through plastic neurons from a large reservoir of slow-firing neurons recruited during exploration. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p374]
- Hippocampal neurons fire not because cues at particular parts of the environment make them fire, but because the internally evolving neuronal trajectory reaches the point that it is their turn to become active. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p373]

## Inferences

- Inference: Learning with neurons involves a synthesis of preexisting neuronal events that modestly and transiently affects network dynamics, rather than creating entirely new representations.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The mechanism driving remapping in young pups remains an open question; perhaps nothing 'drives' remapping—it is a matching process between already existing maps and ambulatory exploration.
