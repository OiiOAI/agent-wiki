# Liberal Individualism in American Politics

## Summary

While distrust of 'big government' is frequently associated with the political right in the United States, there exist both right- and left-wing versions of liberal individualism. The right distrusts state intervention in economic affairs and opposes excessive regulation. The left opposes state interference in personal lifestyles and individual liberties, while attacking the 'national security state' and large corporations.

## Key facts

- There are right-wing and left-wing versions of distrust of government in the United States. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- The political right distrusts state intervention in economic affairs and rails against excessive regulation. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- The political left abhors state interference in personal lifestyles and individual liberties, while attacking the 'national security state' and large corporations. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- Both left and right in America have their own versions of liberal individualism. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
