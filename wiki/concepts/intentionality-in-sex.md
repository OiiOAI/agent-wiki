# Intentionality in Sex

## Summary

Intentionality in sex refers to the deliberate cultivation of erotic encounters rather than waiting for spontaneous desire. Perel argues that committed couples must plan and create erotic space, distinguishing between scheduling as obligation versus creating space with openness about what may occur.

## Key facts

- The ideal that really sexy sex should be spontaneous keeps people one step removed from having to will sex, to own their desire, and to express it with intent. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p185]
- Perel urges patients not to be spontaneous about sex: 'Whatever is going to "just happen" already has. Now they have to make it happen. Committed sex is intentional sex.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p186]
- The phrase 'I couldn't resist' has to become 'I don't want to resist.' 'We just fell into each other's arms' has to become 'Let me take you in my arms.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p186]
- Planning for sex helps generate anticipation, which is an important ingredient of desire. Fantasy is the mortar of anticipation—a way of imagining what something is going to be like. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p187]
- Planning is distinguished from scheduling: 'I am not talking about scheduling sex, I am talking about creating an erotic space, and that takes time. What will occur in that space is open-ended, but the space itself is marked by intentionality.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p186]

## Inferences

- Inference: The reluctance to plan sex often stems from an 'infantile wish to be loved just as we are, without any effort whatsoever on our part, because we are so special.'

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The extent to which intentionality might feel transactional or decrease spontaneity for some couples is not addressed in detail.
