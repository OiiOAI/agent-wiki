# Naturalistic Decision Making

## Summary

Naturalistic Decision Making (NDM) is a research approach that investigates how experienced decision makers perform realistic tasks in field settings, as opposed to laboratory studies with novices on artificial tasks. NDM research focuses on understanding how people handle time pressure, high stakes, inadequate information, vague goals, and dynamic conditions using their experience and intuitive abilities.

## Key facts

- Naturalistic decision making studies experienced decision makers performing realistic tasks rather than novices performing artificial tasks in a laboratory. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p17]
- Key features of naturalistic decision-making settings include time pressure, high stakes, experienced decision makers, inadequate information, ill-defined goals, poorly defined procedures, cue learning, context, dynamic conditions, and team coordination. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p22-24]
- Fireground commanders make approximately 80 percent of their decisions in less than one minute. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p22]
- In NDM research settings, the situation was found to change an average of five times per fire incident. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p24]
- NDM research methods include studying critical incidents, conducting field interviews, and observing decision makers in their natural environments. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p18-19]
- The book describes sleeping in fire stations, observing intensive care units, and riding in M-1 tanks, U.S. Navy AEGIS cruisers, Blackhawk helicopters, and AWACS aircraft for field research. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p18]
- NDM research emerged from studying professionals in field settings such as firefighting, military command, and nursing, rather than laboratory experiments. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p202]
- Skilled decision makers under time pressure maintained high-quality performance even with only six seconds per move in chess blitz conditions. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p204]
- Masters kept blunder rates constant at 7-8 percent even under blitz conditions, while class B players saw blunders increase from 11 percent to 25 percent under time pressure. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p205]
- Experienced chess players generated reasonable options as the first ones they considered, confirming that there is little to be gained by generating and evaluating many options. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p208]
