# Neurotic Anxiety

## Summary

Neurotic anxiety is distinguished from realistic anxiety. It is derived from unemployable (frustrated) libido rather than from external dangers. Freud proposes that the prototype of anxiety originates in the act of birth (separation from the mother), and that in neurotic anxiety the ego treats internal libidinal demands as external dangers.
