# Planner Not the Plan

## Summary

Planner Not the Plan is a decision-making principle attributed to Dwight D. Eisenhower: "Plans are worthless, but planning is everything." The method requires developing the planner rather than the plan, training the brain at planning so that when all plans shatter, one can keep moving forward. It creates antifragility by generating positive twists in the future.

## Key facts

- Eisenhower stated: "Plans are worthless, but planning is everything... The very definition of emergency is that it is unexpected, therefore it is not going to happen the way you are planning". [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p136]
- To life, Plan B is just more Plan A — both are based on the same outdated methods and assumptions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p136]
- Instead of developing the plan, you must develop the planner — training your brain at planning to maintain initiative and lead through chaos. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p136]
- Swick training develops this through three components: perspective shifting, old problem new answer, and work-around. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p133]
