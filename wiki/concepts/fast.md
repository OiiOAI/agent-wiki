# FAST

## Summary

FAST is a DBT interpersonal effectiveness skill for maintaining self-respect while being skillful. It is an acronym for (Be) Fair, (No) Apologies, Stick to values, and (Be) Truthful. This skill helps individuals act in ways that keep their self-respect intact during interpersonal interactions.

## Key facts

- FAST stands for (Be) Fair, (No) Apologies, Stick to values, and (Be) Truthful. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p154]
- (Be) Fair means being fair to yourself and to the other person, validating your own feelings and wishes as well as the other person's, and not overapologizing. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p154]
- (No) Apologies means no apologizing for being alive, for making a request, for having an opinion, or for disagreeing, and no looking ashamed. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p154]
- Stick to values means sticking to your own values, not selling out your integrity for unimportant reasons, and being clear on what you believe is the moral way of thinking and acting. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p154]
- (Be) Truthful means not lying, not acting helpless when you are not, and not exaggerating or making up excuses. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p154]
