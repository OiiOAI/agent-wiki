# Sculpting Role of Inhibition

## Summary

The sculpting role of inhibition refers to the function of inhibitory synaptic inputs in shaping the pattern of neuronal firing, particularly in spontaneously active neurons. By counteracting excitatory inputs, synaptic inhibition creates temporal patterning of neural spiking and controls synchronization of neural circuits.

## Key facts

- By counteracting synaptic excitation, synaptic inhibition can exert powerful control over action potential firing in neurons that are spontaneously active due to intrinsic pacemaker channels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
- The sculpting role of inhibition shapes the pattern of firing in spontaneously active neurons and likely happens in all neurons, leading to temporal patterning of neuronal spiking.
