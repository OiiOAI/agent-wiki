# Sexual Motivation

## Summary

Internet pornography harms real-life sexual motivation by creating unrealistic expectations about sex and women. Manson recommends eliminating pornography, limiting masturbation to once weekly (fantasizing only about real women met), and using lotion to prevent desensitization. This 'masturbation and porn diet' restores motivation to pursue real women.

## Key facts

- Pornography creates very unrealistic expectations about sex, women, and sexuality. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p168]
- Porn encourages excessive masturbation which leads to desensitization and sexual dysfunction with real women. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p169]
- Masturbation diet: eliminate all pornography, limit masturbation to once weekly, fantasize only about real unmet women, use lotion and go slowly. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p170]
- Napoleon Hill's concept of Sexual Transfiguration suggests channeling sexual energy into work and accomplishments. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p169]
