# Truthseeking Pod

## Summary

A truthseeking pod is a group of individuals who agree to help each other think more objectively by providing accountability, rewarding accuracy over confirmation, and exposing each other to diverse viewpoints. It represents a 'grown-up version' of the buddy system from school field trips, designed to combat cognitive biases that individuals cannot overcome alone.

## Key facts

- A truthseeking pod requires agreement by all parties to be effective, as demonstrated by David Letterman's awkward exchange with Lauren Conrad when he offered insight without her consent. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1094-L1105]
- A stable and productive truthseeking group can function with as few as three people: two to disagree and one to referee. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1200-L1204]
- Truthseeking pods work by rewarding accuracy and intellectual honesty with social approval, harnessed from the human desire for approval from respected peers. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1260-L1266]
- Once exploratory thought becomes habitual, it becomes self-reinforcing even when away from the group, as people internalize the group's approval. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1300-L1305]

## Inferences

- Inference: The pod transforms individual decision-making habits by reshaping how people process information even in solitude, creating lasting behavioral change.
