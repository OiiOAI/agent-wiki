# Oedipus Dreams

## Summary

Oedipus dreams are dreams in which the dreamer has sexual intercourse or romantic relations with their own mother. Freud distinguishes between straightforward Oedipus dreams and disguised versions, noting that disguised dreams of sexual intercourse with the dreamer’s mother are many times more frequent than undisguised ones. The symbolic interpretation of Oedipus dreams was not unknown to the ancients, as evidenced by Julius Caesar's reported dream and the oracle given to the Tarquins.
