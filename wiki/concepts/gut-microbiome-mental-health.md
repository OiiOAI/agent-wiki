# Gut microbiome and mental health

## Summary

The gut microbiome, composed of microbes inhabiting the gastrointestinal tract, plays a significant role in mental health. Feeding these microbes with prebiotics (nondigestible fiber found in plant-based foods) enables them to function optimally, which is important for overall health. The evidence base for direct mental health effects of gut microbiome interventions is still developing.

## Key facts

- Microbes inhabit many parts of the body, especially the gastrointestinal tract. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
- Feeding your microbes with prebiotics (nondigestible fiber found in many plant-based foods) enables these tiny organisms to function at their best, which is important for our own health. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
- The health and diversity of your unique intestinal microbiome may be affecting your mental health, although the evidence base is not strong so far. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
