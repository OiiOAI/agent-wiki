# Dispositional representations

## Summary

Dispositional representations are dormant neural patterns that constitute the brain's repository of knowledge, including both innate knowledge (in hypothalamus, brain stem, and limbic system) and acquired knowledge (in higher-order cortices). They exist as potential patterns of neuron firing in small ensembles called convergence zones, and when activated, they can fire other dispositional representations, generate topographically organized representations by firing back to early sensory cortices, or generate movements by activating motor cortices.

## Key facts

- Dispositional representations exist as potential patterns of neuron activity in small ensembles of neurons called convergence zones. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p122]
- Dispositional representations do not store pictures per se but rather the means to reconstitute a picture by triggering momentary reconstruction in early sensory cortices. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p123]
- Innate knowledge is based on dispositional representations in hypothalamus, brain stem, and limbic system for biological regulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p125]
- Acquired knowledge is based on dispositional representations in higher-order cortices and gray-matter nuclei, containing records for imageable knowledge and rules and strategies for operating on images. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p125]
- Dispositional representations exist as potential patterns of neuron activity in convergence zones, dormant like the town of Brigadoon until activated. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p122]
- When activated, dispositional representations can fire other dispositional representations, generate topographically organized representations by firing back to early sensory cortices, or generate movement by activating motor structures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p125]
- Innate dispositional representations are located in hypothalamus, brain stem, and limbic system; acquired dispositional representations are in higher-order cortices and subcortical gray-matter nuclei. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p124]
- The firing patterns in dispositional representations result from strengthening or weakening of synapses due to functional changes at microscopic levels within axons and dendrites. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p123]
- Acquired dispositional representations contain records for imageable knowledge and rules/strategies for operating on images. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p124]

## Inferences

- Inference: Dispositional representations are the neural substrate for memory, enabling recall by transiently reconstructing images in early sensory cortices.
