# Local Guidance

## Summary

Local guidance is the process of maintaining precision along the flight path and characterizes routine flight that occupies 95-99% of a pilot's flight time. Information needs for local guidance include depictions of actual and predicted deviations from the flight path—information that is ego-referenced, presenting a forward-looking or three-dimensional view directly ahead of the aircraft within a few thousand feet. The ideal display for local guidance is a forward-looking 3D ego-referenced 'highway (or tunnel) in the sky.' Local guidance during routine operations is predictable and constitutes a skill-based task that easily lends itself to effective automation.
