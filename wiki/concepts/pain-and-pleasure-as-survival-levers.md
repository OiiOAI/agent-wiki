# Pain and Pleasure as Survival Levers

## Summary

Damasio proposes that pain and pleasure function as biological levers essential for deploying survival strategies. Pain signals steer organisms away from trouble in both present and anticipated future, while pleasure reinforces beneficial behaviors. The mechanism involves preorganized brain structures that generate these experiences, though cultural and individual factors can modify thresholds and intensity.

## Key facts

- Pain and pleasure are levers the organism requires for instinctual and acquired strategies to operate efficiently for survival. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p283]
- Pain-related signals more often steer organisms away from impending trouble than pleasure-seeking does, at both present and anticipated future moments. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p288]
- Pain and pleasure configurations are set a priori in brain structure and occur because circuits fire in particular ways instructed genetically. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p283]
- Reactions to pain and pleasure can be modified by education and individual history, changing thresholds, intensity, or providing means to dampen responses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p283]
- Individuals and societies governed more by pleasure-seeking than pain-avoidance may face survival challenges. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p288]
