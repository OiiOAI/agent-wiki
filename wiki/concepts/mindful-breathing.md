# Mindful Breathing

## Summary

Mindful breathing is positioned as the foundational prevention and regulation strategy in RULER. It helps calm the body and mind by decreasing heart rate and resetting the autonomic nervous system through activation of the parasympathetic nervous system. Breathing through the nose is emphasized over mouth breathing, which tends to be faster and shallower and can reactivate the stress response. The practice involves good posture, natural breathing, and counting breaths or repeating calming phrases. Research suggests fifteen minutes of daily practice can positively affect attunement to others, emotional reactivity, attention, memory, immune function, and mental health.

## Key facts

- Mindful breathing helps deactivate the stress response system by decreasing heart rate and resetting the autonomic nervous system through activating the parasympathetic nervous system and inhibiting the sympathetic one.
