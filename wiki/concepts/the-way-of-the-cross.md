# The Way of the Cross

## Summary

The way of the cross refers to the spiritual path of transforming one's worst suffering into enlightenment. It means that the worst thing in life, one's 'cross,' turns into the best thing that happened by forcing surrender into 'death,' forcing one to become as nothing, to become as God. Until recently, this was the primary path to enlightenment, requiring further suffering for the unconscious majority.

## Key facts

- The way of the cross means that the worst thing in your life, your cross, turns into the best thing that ever happened to you by forcing you into surrender. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p198]
- The way of the cross forces you into 'death,' forcing you to become as nothing, to become as God — because God, too, is no-thing. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p198]
- Until recently, the way of the cross was the only way to enlightenment for the unconscious majority. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p198]
- Enlightenment through the way of the cross means being forced into the kingdom of heaven kicking and screaming. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- There is a growing number of people whose consciousness is sufficiently evolved not to need any more suffering before the realization of enlightenment. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
- Enlightenment consciously chosen means relinquishing attachment to past and future and making the Now the main focus of life. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p199]
