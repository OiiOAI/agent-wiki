# Discomfort vs. Suffering

## Summary

The distinction between discomfort and suffering is that discomfort is purely physical while suffering is personal. Affect transforms interoceptive sensation into something about you, with your particular strengths and faults. When you feel wretched, the world seems awful and people are judging you—this is suffering. Discomfort is purely physical, like what a virus causes. A virus is egalitarian toward its victims and brings discomfort but it's nothing personal. With practice, people can learn to deconstruct an affective feeling into its mere physical sensations rather than letting those sensations be a filter through which they view the world, dissolving anxiety into a fast-beating heart.

## Key facts

- Discomfort is purely physical while suffering is personal. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p208]
- Affect transforms interoceptive sensation into something personal, residing inside your affective niche. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p208]
- When you categorize something as 'Not About Me,' it exits your affective niche and has less impact on your body budget. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p214]
