# The Planet of Success

## Summary

The Planet of Success and its Three Moons address the foundations for success. The chapter covers how to define and measure success, distinguishing between Chimp measures (material belongings and achievements) versus Human measures (personal qualities). Partial success, stages of success, and the importance of planning are explored. The three moons—Regal Moon, CORE Moon, and Moon of Carrots—represent stabilisers that enhance the chances of success.

## Key facts

- To define success, Chimps look for material belongings and achievements, whereas Humans look for personal qualities; there is little point chasing after Chimp-defined success only to discover it was not what you really wanted. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p160]
- If you measure success by effort and doing your best, then it is always in your hands to succeed and be proud of yourself, and you can deal with any disappointing level of attainment as an adult Human. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p161]
- Partial successes are often reality checks on what you can realistically achieve; learning to accept that you have made a mistake and rewarding this is far more constructive than criticising yourself for failing to reach total success. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p161]
- Success can happen regardless of plans, but having structured plans improves your chances of success and allows you to know you did everything you could. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p180]
