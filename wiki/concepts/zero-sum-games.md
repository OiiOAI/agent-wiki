# Zero-Sum Games

## Summary

Zero-sum games are competitive situations where one participant's gain necessarily equals another participant's loss. The chunk references this concept as a framework for understanding intergroup competition and conflict.

## Key facts

- Zero-sum games describe scenarios where one side's gain is another side's loss, with no possibility of mutual benefit. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p394]
- Research shows that wearing red boosts athletic performance among closely matched competitors, possibly reflecting evolutionary associations with dominance displays.
