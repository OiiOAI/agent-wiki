# Anchoring Emotions in Negotiation

## Summary

Anchoring emotions involves starting negotiations by acknowledging the counterpart's fears and concerns before making offers. The accusation audit—listing all potential accusations before they are made—lowers expectations and frames the negotiation around avoiding loss rather than gaining concession.

## Key facts

- Anchoring emotions involves an accusation audit that acknowledges all of the counterpart's fears, inflaming loss aversion so they will jump at the chance to avoid a perceived loss. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p114]
- Voss used accusation auditing when reducing contractor rates from $2,000 to $500 per day by listing all the criticisms they would have before making the offer. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p114]
- After anchoring emotions low, the $500 offer became about not losing $500 to another contractor rather than accepting a massive rate cut. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p115]
