# Default Effect

## Summary

The default effect is the tendency to stick with pre-set options. New Jersey made a cheaper insurance option the default and most people took it; Pennsylvania made the expensive option the default and it became the bestseller. Organ donation defaults increased take-up from 40% to over 80%.

## Key facts

- New Jersey advertised the cheaper car insurance as standard and most chose it; Pennsylvania advertised the expensive option as standard and it became the bestseller. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p179]
- Eric Johnson and Dan Goldstein found making organ donation the default increased take-up from 40% to over 80%. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p180]
- Loss aversion plays a role: renegotiating contracts feels like net losses because each concession weighs twice as heavy as any received. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p180]
