# Emotional Connection

## Summary

The book's framework for deep personal bonding with women through sharing vulnerable aspects of one's own life story, motivations, and emotions, which encourages reciprocal sharing and trust.

## Key facts

- Your ability to connect with a woman emotionally is proportional to how self-aware you are of your own emotional processes and motivations. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p196]
- The basic pattern for emotional connection is: become aware of your own emotions, take the lead by sharing those emotions first, which creates trust and encourages her to open up in return. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p197]
- Everybody shares universal emotional realities: ambition, shame, alienation, loneliness, achievement, regret, hardship, friendship, love, heartbreak; the facts change but the feelings are the same. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p198]
- Emotional Connection is defined as a mutual emotional investment between two people. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
- This investment generates a feeling of closeness and greater empathy. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
