# Probe-Sense-Respond

## Summary

Probe-sense-respond is the Cynefin approach to the Complex domain, involving conducting small experiments, sensing what emerges, and responding accordingly. This contrasts with the sense-categorize-respond approach of ordered domains.

## Key facts

- In the Complex domain, the approach is probe, sense, and respond—conducting small experiments in parallel, gaining fast feedback, and enabling rapid exploitation of success. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p315]
- The Cynefin Framework acts as a map of the decision field, orienting users in different territories and understanding liminal spaces between domains. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p295]
