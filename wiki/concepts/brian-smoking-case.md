# Brian (smoking cessation case study)

## Summary

Brian was a 20-year-old man with ADHD, MDD, and panic disorder who used marijuana and cigarettes daily. Within a week of starting multinutrient treatment, substantial changes in mood were noted. By six weeks, he quit smoking cigarettes and reduced marijuana use. When he stopped the nutrients per study protocol, his symptoms returned and he resumed smoking. When restarted on nutrients, his mood and sleep returned to normal within three weeks.

## Key facts

- Brian had been in and out of psychiatric services with diagnoses including ADHD, MDD, and panic disorder. He used both marijuana and cigarettes daily. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p140]
- Substantial changes in Brian's mood were noted within a week of starting multinutrients. By six weeks on the nutrients, Brian's partner reported he was more motivated, less 'on the go,' getting to work on time, less paranoid, less wound up, and more laid back. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p141]
- By six weeks on the nutrients, Brian quit smoking cigarettes (he just didn't crave them anymore) and was no longer using marijuana on a daily basis. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p141]
- When Brian stopped the nutrients per study protocol, within a week he was feeling more stressed out and had resumed smoking and using more marijuana. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p141]
