# Effective Communication in Relationships

## Summary

Effective communication is the ability to express one's needs and expectations to a partner in a direct, non-accusatory manner. It works to achieve two goals: choosing the right partner by quickly determining if they can meet your needs, and ensuring needs are met in existing relationships. The five principles are: wear your heart on your sleeve (be genuine), focus on needs (use verbs like need, feel, want), be specific, don't blame, and be assertive without apology. It is used naturally by people with secure attachment and can be learned by others.

## Key facts

- Effective communication is the quickest, most direct way to determine whether a prospective partner will be able to meet your needs, with their response revealing more in five minutes than months of dating without it. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p171]
- The five principles of effective communication are: wear your heart on your sleeve, focus on your needs using verbs like need/feel/want, be specific, don't blame, and be assertive and non-apologetic. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p180]
- When expressing needs, one should focus on what they are trying to accomplish, not on the partner's shortcomings, and avoid making them feel selfish, incompetent, or inadequate. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p180]
- If a partner responds negatively to effective communication by evading topics, acting defensively, or making one feel foolish or needy, this should be heeded as a serious warning sign. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p172]
- Anxious individuals should use effective communication when they feel they are starting to resort to protest behavior, after thoroughly calming down. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p178]
- Avoidant individuals should use effective communication when they feel an irrepressible need to bolt, explaining their need for space without blaming their partner. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p178]

## Inferences

- Inference: Effective communication provides a role model for partners; once one person demonstrates openness, the other tends to follow suit, setting the tone for the relationship as one where both can be honest.
