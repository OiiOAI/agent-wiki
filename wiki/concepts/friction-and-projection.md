# Friction and Projection

## Summary

Friction and Projection are two main reasons that prevent attracted women from consummating romantic relationships despite finding a man attractive. Friction refers to value differences or external circumstances that prevent a woman from acting on attraction (e.g., religious differences, being married, logistical distance). Projection refers to women who, due to past trauma or trust issues, are afraid of their own sexuality and attack attractive men as a defense mechanism, accusing them of various negative traits that have no basis in reality. Understanding these concepts helps men accept that incompatibility is normal and avoid taking rejection personally.

## Key facts

- Friction is when a woman finds a man attractive but value differences or external circumstances prevent her from acting on that attraction. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p74]
- The most common case of friction is the girlfriend or wife situation — she may be honest about her attraction but values her relationship more. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p74]
- Projection occurs when women who have trust issues or past abuse are confronted with a non-needy man and attack him as a defense mechanism against their own attraction. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p75]
- Projecting women will accuse a non-needy man of being demanding, overbearing, too horny, untrustworthy, or weak — accusations that have little connection to reality. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p76]
- The majority of women will have high degrees of friction and projection when you meet them — incompatibility is a fact of life. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p76]
