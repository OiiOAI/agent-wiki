# Team Topologies Framework

## Summary

The Team Topologies Framework is a dynamic and evolving approach to organizational design based on four team patterns (stream-aligned, complicated-subsystem, platform, and enabling teams) and three interaction modes (collaboration, X-as-a-service, and facilitating). It takes Conway's law into account and focuses on managing cognitive load through teams with clear responsibilities and boundaries. The framework helps organizations succeed more quickly and more continuously than before.

## Key facts

- Team Topologies identifies four team patterns, describing their outcomes, form, and the forces they address and are shaped by. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L429]
- Stream-aligned teams are the primary team form in Team Topologies. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L430]
- Complicated-subsystem and platform teams reduce load for stream-aligned teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
- Enabling teams serve other teams as service providers. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L433]
- Team Topologies focuses on managing cognitive load through teams with clear responsibilities and boundaries. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L425-L426]
- Team Topologies is not a universal formula but a set of patterns for teams and organizations to follow and interpret. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L424-L425]
- Team Topologies describes organizational patterns for team structure and modes of interaction, taking the force that the organization exerts on the system as a driving design concern. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L422]
