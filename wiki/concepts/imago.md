# Imago

## Summary

A psychoanalytic concept referring to the internalized images of significant early figures (especially parents and siblings) that influence later relationships. Freud used this to explain how students transfer their childhood feelings toward their fathers onto their teachers.

## Key facts

- Freud explains that substitute figures for early objects of feeling can be classified according to the imagos of father, mother, brothers and sisters from which they derive. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4351]
- Freud states that all later choices of friendship and love follow upon the basis of memory traces left by these first prototypes. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4351]
