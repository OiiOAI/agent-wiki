# Inside View

## Summary

The inside view, a label coined by Nobel laureate Daniel Kahneman and Dan Lovallo, describes the tendency to ignore related pertinent data and believe that 'this case is different.' This bias leads people to see themselves, their group, their project, their company, or their nation as special and distinct from others. It played a key role in the 2008 financial crisis when policy makers believed the U.S. was different from other nations that had experienced credit booms.
