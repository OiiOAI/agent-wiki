# Sleep Hygiene

## Summary

Sleep hygiene is a set of evidence-based behaviors designed to help the body and brain ease into restorative sleep. The authors recommend ten practices that address sleep timing, physical activity, caffeine consumption, technology use, alcohol intake, temperature control, and bedtime routines.

## Key facts

- The ten sleep hygiene practices are: consistent sleep/wake times, moving throughout the day, careful caffeine management, limiting technology before bed, minimizing alcohol, cooling the bedroom, winding down before bed, creating a dark quiet bedroom, overestimating time needed in bed, and maintaining routines while traveling. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3791-L3801]
- The authors recommend going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, including weekends. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3803-L3815]
- Caffeine takes four to six hours to metabolize half its dose, and consuming 400mg six hours before bed can cost one hour of sleep. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3850-L3865]
- Alcohol disrupts REM sleep and sleep cycles, with even small amounts potentially taking four to five days to fully recover from. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3887-L3915]
- The recommended bedroom temperature for optimal sleep is 65 degrees Fahrenheit. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3922-L3930]

## Inferences

- Inference: The authors' strong emphasis on sleep hygiene suggests that adequate sleep is foundational to recovery and performance, making it the linchpin of all other Vital Signs practices.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The optimal bedroom temperature may vary by individual preference and climate.
