# Seven High-Trust Organizational Dividends

## Summary

The Seven High-Trust Organizational Dividends are benefits gained by organizations operating in high-trust environments: increased value, accelerated growth, enhanced innovation, improved collaboration, stronger partnering, better execution, and heightened loyalty.

## Key facts

- Increased Value dividend operates in two dimensions: shareholder value (Watson Wyatt study showed high-trust organizations outperformed low-trust by 286% in total return to shareholders) and employee/customer value. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p304]
- Accelerated Growth results from customers buying more, buying more frequently, referring more, and staying longer with trusted companies. A 2015 Interaction Associates study shows high-trust companies are 2.5x more likely to be high-performing revenue organizations. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p305]
- Enhanced Innovation thrives in conditions created by high trust: open sharing of ideas, absence of credit concerns, willingness to take risks, safety to make mistakes, and ability to collaborate. The LRN How Report shows people in high-trust cultures are 32x more likely to take risks and 11x more likely to see higher innovation. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p306]
- Improved Collaboration requires trust; coordination requires little trust while creative collaboration requires high trust. Forbes highlighted trust as the 'bedrock' of collaboration in the collaborative economy. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p307]
- Stronger Partnering yields up to 40% dividend of contract value for partnerships based on trust versus those relying only on contract language and SLAs, according to a Warwick Business School study. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p307]
- Better Execution is enhanced by trust; high trust multiplies and accelerates ability to execute strategy while low trust derails even good strategy. FranklinCovey's execution quotient (xQ) consistently shows strong correlation between higher execution and higher trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p308]
- Heightened Loyalty encompasses employee retention, customer loyalty, supplier/distributor partnerships, and investor satisfaction. Dr. Larry Ponemon stated 'Trust is becoming the vital component in customer loyalty and brand strength.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p309]
