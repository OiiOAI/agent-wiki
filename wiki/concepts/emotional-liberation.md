# Emotional Liberation

## Summary

Emotional liberation is the third and final stage in the development toward emotional responsibility. In this stage, people respond to the needs of others out of compassion, never out of fear, guilt, or shame. They accept full responsibility for their own intentions and actions but not for the feelings of others. NVC is designed to support people in relating at this level.

## Key facts

- In emotional liberation, we respond to the needs of others out of compassion, never out of fear, guilt, or shame. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p98]
- In emotional liberation, our actions are fulfilling to us as well as to those who receive our efforts. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p98]
- Emotional liberation involves accepting that we can never meet our own needs at the expense of others. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p98]
- Emotional liberation involves stating clearly what we need in a way that communicates we are equally concerned that the needs of others be fulfilled. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p98]
