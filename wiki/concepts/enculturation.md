# Enculturation

## Summary

Enculturation is a developmental term describing the constitutive power of culture in shaping human mentality. Human cognition emerges from developmental processes of enculturation and is configured by the distributed cognitive web of symbolic culture, rather than being determined by innate factors alone.

## Key facts

- Enculturation describes the constitutive power of culture in human mental development. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p390]
- Human mentality emerges from developmental processes of enculturation and is configured by the distributed cognitive web of symbolic culture. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p390]
- The constitutive role of culture in human cognition implies that it makes no sense to think of culture and nature as separate developmental domains. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p390]
