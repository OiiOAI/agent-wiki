# Leverage

## Summary

Leverage is a force multiplier for judgment. There are three broad classes of leverage: labor (other humans working for you), capital (money), and code/media (products with no marginal cost of replication). The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers by enabling leverage through code and media. Code is the most powerful form of permissionless leverage.

## Key facts

- There are three broad classes of leverage: labor, capital, and code/media. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p49]
- Labor is the oldest form of leverage but is not a great one in the modern world. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p49]
- Capital is a trickier form of leverage that scales very well. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p50]
- Code and media (products with no marginal cost of replication) is the most democratic form of leverage. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p50]
- Code is probably the most powerful form of permissionless leverage. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p50]
- Capital and labor are permissioned leverage; everyone is chasing capital, but someone has to give it to you. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p28]
- Code and media are permissionless leverage; you don't need anyone's permission to use them. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p28]
- Fortunes require leverage; business leverage comes from capital, people, and products with no marginal cost of replication (code and media). [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p28]
- The internet has massively broadened the possible space of careers through leverage. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p26]

## Inferences

- Inference: The new generation's fortunes are made through code or media (as opposed to the previous generation's wealth from capital).
