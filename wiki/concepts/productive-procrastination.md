# Productive Procrastination

## Summary

Having multiple projects to bounce between allows one to move away from stuck projects and return refreshed. Taking time to be bored can lead to good ideas, and side projects often become the work that really takes off.

## Key facts

- Kleon suggests having multiple projects going so you can bounce between them when you get stuck on one. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p49]
- Kleon gets ideas while doing boring tasks like ironing shirts, washing dishes, or taking long walks. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p49]
- Jessica Hische said, 'The work you do while you procrastinate is probably the work you should be doing for the rest of your life.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p48]
- Maira Kalman says, 'Avoiding work is the way to focus my mind.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p50]
