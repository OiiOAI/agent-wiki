# Maui Habit

## Summary

The Maui Habit is a simple morning practice introduced in the book: after putting feet on the floor upon waking, immediately say 'It's going to be a great day' while trying to feel optimistic. This takes about three seconds and serves as an example of the Tiny Habits method while also demonstrating how feeling successful creates habits. Fogg has helped thousands of people adopt this habit, which he first developed after visiting Maui and noticing his own morning mindset affected his entire day.

## Key facts

- The Maui Habit recipe: After I wake up and put my feet on the floor, I will say 'It's going to be a great day'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p11]
- Fogg reports helping thousands of people bring the Maui Habit into their lives with excellent results. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p11]
- The habit works by creating a moment of optimism each morning that opens the door to actually having a good day. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p12]
