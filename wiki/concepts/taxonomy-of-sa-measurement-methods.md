# Taxonomy of SA Measurement Methods

## Summary

Pew presents a taxonomy of SA measurement methods broken down into four categories: (1) Direct system performance measures that assess performance driven solely by SA, including scenario manipulations with nonobtrusive performance measures, disruptions for recovery assessment, and anomalous data detection; (2) Direct experimental techniques including probes/queries and information seeking measures such as eye movements and eye-blink response; (3) Verbal protocols recorded from observers during or after exercises; (4) Subjective measures including self-assessments, expert judgments, peer ratings, and supervisor ratings. Each category is appropriate for particular purposes, and the choice of measurement context and technique should be made with full understanding of the situations to be measured.

## Key facts

- Pew's taxonomy breaks SA measurement methods into four categories: direct system performance measures, direct experimental techniques, verbal protocols, and subjective measures. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p57]
- Direct system performance measures are appropriate only when performance is driven solely or largely by SA, including scenario manipulation with subtle indicators and anomaly detection techniques. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p57]
- Scenario manipulation methods include introducing counterintuitive indicators, disruptions for recovery assessment, and anomalous data without subject knowledge. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p58]
- Direct experimental techniques include probes or queries, eye movements, eye-blink response, and forced information seeking with tracking of frequency and sequence. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p60]
- Verbal protocols are most useful early in evaluation development to solidify SA concepts that need more systematic measurement. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p61]
- Subjective measures include self-assessments, expert judgments, peer ratings, and supervisor or instructor ratings. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p61]
- Think-aloud and disruptive techniques are best suited to preliminary research; scenario manipulation evaluates individual ability; SAGAT examines SA across treatment conditions; subjective approaches are needed when objective data is impractical. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p64]
