# Individual Psychology

## Summary

Individual Psychology is the name Alfred Adler gave to his psychological approach, which the book presents through a dialogue format. Etymologically, 'individual' means 'indivisible'—the smallest unit that cannot be broken down further. Adler opposed dualistic value systems that separate mind from body, reason from emotion, or conscious from unconscious. The approach is characterized by rejecting etiology (cause-based explanation) and trauma, adopting teleology (goal-oriented explanation) instead. It treats all problems as interpersonal relationship problems and emphasizes community feeling, horizontal relationships, and the separation of tasks.
