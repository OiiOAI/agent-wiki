# Silence

## Summary

Silence is presented as an even more potent carrier of presence than words. The book emphasizes that there is always silence underneath and between sounds, and that listening to silence immediately creates stillness within. Only the stillness inside can perceive silence outside. Silence is described as consciousness freed from thought forms—the living realization of presence. The practice involves being aware of the gaps between words and sounds, and recognizing that true communication transcends language.

## Key facts

- Silence is described as 'an even more potent carrier of presence' than words. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p101]
- Listening to silence immediately creates stillness inside; only the stillness in you can perceive the silence outside. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p101]
- Stillness is consciousness freed from thought forms. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p101]
- There is always some silence underneath and in between sounds, even when there is noise. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p101]
- The gaps between words are important carriers of presence, as is awareness of silence wherever one is. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p101]
