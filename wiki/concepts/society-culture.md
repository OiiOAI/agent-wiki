# Society and Culture

## Summary

Society and culture represent two fundamentally different modes of human association. Society is a bounded finite game, encompassing all relations under public constraint, with definite temporal, spatial, and numerical limits. Culture is an unbounded infinite game, consisting of whatever persons do with each other by undirected choice. Society is theatrical, maintaining scripts and punishing deviation; culture is dramatic, perpetually deviating from scripts in ways that continue rather than repeat the past. Culture cannot be contained within the boundaries of society without losing its essential character.

## Key facts

- Society is understood as the sum of those relations that are under some form of public constraint; culture is whatever we do with each other by undirected choice. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p49]
- A society is a single finite game that includes any number of smaller games within its boundaries, with citizenship precisely defined, boundaries inviolable, and the past enshrined. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p60-61]
- Culture has no boundaries; anyone can be a participant in a culture anywhere and at any time. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p62]
- Deviancy is the very essence of culture; whoever merely follows the script, merely repeating the past, is culturally impoverished. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p63]
- Cultural deviation does not return us to the past but continues what was begun and not finished in the past. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p64]
- Where society is defined by its boundaries, culture is defined by its horizon. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p69]
