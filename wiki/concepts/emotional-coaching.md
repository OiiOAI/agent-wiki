# Emotional Coaching

## Summary

Emotional coaching is a parenting approach that combines showing up to emotions with moving through them. It involves encouraging children to brainstorm solutions on their own, focusing on the process rather than pass-or-fail outcomes. The key is taking compassionate pauses to acknowledge and accept a child's distress, helping them step out and mindfully be with their emotions while still doing what needs to be done.

## Key facts

- Moving on is best achieved by encouraging your children to brainstorm—when you support them to find solutions on their own, they develop the autonomy that will help them navigate their world. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p235]
- The key focus of emotional coaching is on the process—being open to experimentation, to giving things a try, and discovering what might be learned—rather than some ideal, pass-or-fail outcome. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p235]
- Taking a small, compassionate pause to acknowledge and accept a child's distress can be enough to help them step out and mindfully and compassionately be with their emotions while still doing what they need to do. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p236]
- The essence of emotional agility may be learning that it's possible to cry and play at the same time. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p236]

## Inferences

- Inference: Emotional coaching bridges the gap between acknowledging difficult emotions and taking constructive action, teaching children that feelings do not need to paralyze them.
