# The Kernel of Strategy

## Summary

The 'kernel' is identified by reviewers as one of the key concepts introduced in Rumelt's Good Strategy/Bad Strategy. It represents the essential core element of a good strategy that sets an organization apart from its competitors and enables leverage.

## Key facts

- Gary Hamel describes the concepts of 'the kernel' and 'the proximate objective' as 'blockbusters' that provide new and powerful ways for leaders to tackle obstacles. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p3]
- Chuck Harrington describes the strategy kernel as 'the battle plan for action that is designed upon a unique set of attributes or conditions' that sets an organization apart from its competitors and results in exceptional and sustainable profits. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p3]
