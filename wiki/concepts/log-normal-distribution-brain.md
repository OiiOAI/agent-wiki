# Log-Normal Distribution in the Brain

## Summary

The brain exhibits log-normal distributions across multiple levels of organization: synaptic weights, firing rates, spine sizes, axon diameters, and population synchrony. This skewed distribution results from multiplicative interactions and supports diverse computational needs including sensitivity to both weak and strong inputs.

## Key facts

- Axon diameters in the brain vary over several orders of magnitude with a strongly skewed distribution; the thickest 0.1% of callosal axons can be 10 micrometers while most are smaller than 0.8 micrometers. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p330]
- Spine sizes on individual layer 5 neocortical neurons and hippocampal pyramidal cells follow a log-normal rule, with giant spines hundreds of times larger than the smallest ones. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p331]
- Connection strengths between brain areas span five orders of magnitude and are best described by a log-normal distribution, with each cortical area connected strongly to a few structures and weakly to many others. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p332]
- Firing rates of cortical pyramidal neurons span four orders of magnitude, with 10-20% of neurons contributing half of all spikes while the rest produce the remaining half. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p333]
- Fast-firing neurons respond more robustly to stimuli proportionally to their baseline rate, illustrating the Weber-Fechner relationship between spontaneous and evoked activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p335]
- Neuronal ensemble size (number of neurons active in a time window) follows a log-normal distribution with rare strongly synchronized events interspersed among medium and small events. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p336]
- Fast-firing neurons form hub 'rich clubs' with more connections and stronger synapses, providing better access to information and sharing it among themselves. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p337]

## Inferences

- Inference: Log-normal organization allows networks to maintain stability against competing needs: wide dynamic range, redundancy, resilience, homeostasis, and plasticity.
- Inference: The skewed distribution implies no definable characteristic ensemble size in cortical networks.
