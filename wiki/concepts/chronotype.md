# Chronotype

## Summary

Chronotype describes an individual's sleeping characteristic—whether they are a morning person (AMer) or evening person (PMer). It is a genetic trait that determines not just sleep and wake times but the timing of all body functions. PMers suffer from 'social jet lag' when forced to conform to early schedules. Caffeine is discussed as both a tool and a crutch, with recommended daily intake of 400mg. Understanding chronotype allows for optimized scheduling and better sleep-work harmony.

## Key facts

- Chronotype describes your sleeping characteristic—whether you're a morning or evening person—and indicates when your body wants to perform various functions. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p27]
- Chronotypes are a genetic trait, usually varying by at most a couple of hours between morning and evening types. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p27]
- As children we tend to be AMers; during adolescence our body clocks switch to run much slower, and they revert to genetic type around age twenty. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p27]
- PMers suffer from 'social jet lag' when forced to operate in a different time zone to their internal body clock. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p28]
- The Food Standards Agency in the UK recommends 400 milligrams as the daily intake of caffeine for the average person. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p29]
- Caffeine has a half-life of up to six hours, meaning it remains in the body much later than many people realize. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p30]
- A Starbucks Grande brewed coffee contains 330 milligrams of caffeine. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p29]
- The University of Munich Chronotype Questionnaire is a validated tool for determining chronotype. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p27]
