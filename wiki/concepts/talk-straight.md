# Talk Straight

## Summary

Behavior #1 of the Thirteen Behaviors, Talk Straight means telling the truth and leaving the right impression. It is based on the principles of integrity, honesty, and straightforwardness. The behavior requires both telling the truth AND communicating so clearly that one cannot be misunderstood. Counterfeits include beating around the bush, withholding information, double-talk, flattery, positioning, posturing, and spinning.

## Key facts

- Only 44% of workers trust that senior management communicates honestly according to Mercer survey. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p175]
- The spin tax creates three meetings instead of one: pre-meetings, meetings, and meetings after the meetings. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p176]
