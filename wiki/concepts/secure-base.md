# Secure Base

## Summary

A secure base is the knowledge that you are backed by someone supportive whom you can rely on and turn to in times of need. Research by Mary Ainsworth found that having an attachment figure present allows children to explore and learn with confidence. For adults, secure partners provide the emotional foundation for taking risks, being creative, and pursuing dreams.

## Key facts

- A secure base is the knowledge that you are backed by someone supportive whom you can rely on with certainty and turn to in times of need. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p33]
- Having an attachment figure present allows a child to go out into a previously unknown environment and explore with confidence. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p33]
- For adults, when partners are dependable and make us feel safe, we can turn our attention to other meaningful aspects of life. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p34]
- The dependency paradox states that the more effectively dependent people are on one another, the more independent and daring they become. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p31]
- Brooke Feeney's research showed that when participants felt their goals were supported by their partner, they reported increased self-esteem and elevated mood. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p34]
