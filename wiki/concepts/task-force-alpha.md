# Task Force Alpha

## Summary

Task Force Alpha was a highly classified operations center at Nakhon Phanom Royal Thai Air Force Base during the Vietnam War. Its purpose was to monitor acoustic, seismic, and other sensors planted along the Ho Chi Minh Trail to detect and target enemy supply movements. The complex included the Infiltration Surveillance Center with extensive computer systems to synthesize sensor data.

## Key facts

- Task Force Alpha monitored sensors along the Ho Chi Minh Trail as part of the 'McNamara Line' electronic battlefield. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p265]
- The $2.5 billion sensor program was a huge windfall for IBM. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p265]
- Boyd developed a grid system for implanting sensors that reduced response time to less than five minutes. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p266]
- The base was officially non-existent for its first several years. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p264]
