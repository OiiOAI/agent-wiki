# Self Trust

## Summary

Self Trust is the first wave in the 5 Waves of Trust model and forms the foundation for all other trust. It encompasses the confidence one has in oneself to set and achieve goals, keep commitments, and act with integrity. Self Trust enables individuals to inspire trust in others and is essential for personal credibility.

## Key facts

- Self Trust deals with the confidence we have in ourselves—in our ability to set and achieve goals, to keep commitments, to walk our talk. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p61]
- A person who doesn't trust themselves can never really trust anyone else (attributed to Cardinal de Retz). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p74]
- Ralph Waldo Emerson stated: 'Self-trust is the first secret of success...the essence of heroism.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p74]
- Research shows that while almost half of Americans set New Year's resolutions, only 8% actually keep them. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p74]

## Inferences

- Inference: Self Trust is developed through consistent behavior—making and keeping commitments, even small ones.
