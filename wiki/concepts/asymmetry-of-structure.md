# Asymmetry of Structure

## Summary

There is an asymmetry inherent in the system-building nature of the left hemisphere, namely the difficulty of escape from a self-enclosed system. The existence of a language-dependent system automatically devalues whatever cannot be expressed in language; the process of reasoning discounts whatever cannot be reached by reasoning. Once the system is set up it operates like a hall of mirrors in which we are reflexively imprisoned.

## Key facts

- The existence of a language-dependent system automatically devalues whatever cannot be expressed in language; the process of reasoning discounts whatever cannot be reached by reasoning. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p323]
- Once the system is set up it operates like a hall of mirrors in which we are reflexively imprisoned. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p323]
- This unwillingness to allow escape is not just passive but appears willed by the left hemisphere, whose behaviour 'looks suspiciously tyrannical'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p323]

## Inferences

- Inference: The left hemisphere's intemperate attacks on nature, art, religion and the body (main routes to something beyond its power) represent the Master becoming a tyrant.
