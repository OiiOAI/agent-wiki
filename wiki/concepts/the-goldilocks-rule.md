# The Goldilocks Rule

## Summary

The Goldilocks Rule states that humans experience peak motivation when working on tasks that are just manageable—challenging enough to require effort but not so difficult as to cause frustration. This concept is based on the Yerkes-Dodson law from psychology research. The book uses the metaphor of flow state, where optimal performance occurs at the boundary between boredom and anxiety. The author describes elite performers like Steve Martin, who spent years mastering their craft through deliberate practice at this optimal difficulty level.
