# Conversion

## Summary

Conversion refers to the process by which psychical excitation is transformed into somatic phenomena in hysteria. When an idea produces lively somatic consequences, the excitation flows off into the paths concerned in these consequences instead of becoming diffused in the brain. Because the idea has physical consequences—because its sums of psychical stimuli have been 'converted' into somatic ones—it loses the clarity which would otherwise have marked it out in consciousness. The affect torn from the repressed idea is used for somatic innervation, producing hysterical symptoms such as paralyses, anaesthesias, or neuralgias that are not directly caused by organic pathology.
