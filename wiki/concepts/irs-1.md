# IRS-1

## Summary

Insulin Receptor Substrate 1 (IRS-1) is a protein involved in insulin signaling believed to be a highly sensitive marker of reduced insulin sensitivity in the brain. Researchers from the National Institute on Aging found that higher levels of the inactive form of IRS-1 predicted Alzheimer's disease development with 100 percent accuracy, with the difference evident ten years prior to symptom emergence.

## Key facts

- IRS-1 (insulin receptor substrate 1) is believed to be a highly sensitive marker of reduced insulin sensitivity in the brain. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p136]
- Alzheimer's patients tend to have higher levels of the inactive form of IRS-1 and lower levels of the active form in their blood. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p136]
- Higher levels of the inactive form of IRS-1 predicted Alzheimer's disease development in patients with 100 percent accuracy. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p136]
- The difference in IRS-1 blood markers was evident ten years prior to the emergence of Alzheimer's symptoms. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p136]
