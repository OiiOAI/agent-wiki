# ELIZA Chatbot

## Summary

ELIZA is a simple chatbot created in the 1960s by Joseph Weizenbaum that simulates a psychotherapist. Solms uses it as an example to critique the Turing test, demonstrating that people can be fooled by a machine exhibiting verbal behaviour into believing they are interacting with a real therapist, even when informed the program is a computer.

## Key facts

- ELIZA was created by Joseph Weizenbaum and simulates a psychotherapist by pattern matching and substituting keywords. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p261]
- Oliver Turnbull interacted with ELIZA via the internet and found many people who interact with her believe she is a real psychotherapist and find her helpful. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p261]
- The ELIZA example demonstrates that the Turing test fails to distinguish intelligent behaviour from consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p261]
