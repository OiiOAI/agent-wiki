# Organizational Inertia

## Summary

Organizational inertia is an organization's unwillingness or inability to adapt to changing circumstances. Rumelt identifies three categories: inertia of routine, cultural inertia, and inertia by proxy. Routine inertia arises from standard procedures and impacted knowledge that limit action to the familiar. Cultural inertia involves deeply embedded work norms and mindsets that resist change. Inertia by proxy occurs when a business chooses not to respond to change because responding would undermine profitable streams that persist due to customer inertia.
