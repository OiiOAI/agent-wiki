# Psychical Conflict

## Summary

Psychical conflict arises when the ego (representing nonsexual drive forces) opposes libidinal trends that have been activated by frustration. The conflict is between the ego drives and the sexual drives. Without such conflict, there would be no neurosis. The conflict leads to symptom formation as a compromise.
