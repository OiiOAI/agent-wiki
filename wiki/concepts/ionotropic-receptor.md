# Ionotropic Receptor

## Summary

Ionotropic receptors are membrane proteins where the ligand-binding site and ion channel are components of the same macromolecule, mediating fast synaptic transmission.

## Key facts

- Ionotropic receptors contain both an extracellular domain that forms the transmitter binding site and a membrane-spanning domain that forms the ion-conducting pore within a single macromolecule. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p250]
- Ionotropic receptors produce fast synaptic actions lasting only milliseconds, commonly found at synapses mediating rapid behaviors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p251]
