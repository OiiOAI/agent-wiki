# Ionotropic Receptor

## Summary

Ionotropic receptors are membrane proteins where the ligand-binding site and ion channel are components of the same macromolecule, mediating fast synaptic transmission.

## Key facts

- Ionotropic receptors contain both an extracellular domain that forms the transmitter binding site and a membrane-spanning domain that forms the ion-conducting pore within a single macromolecule. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p250]
- Ionotropic receptors produce fast synaptic actions lasting only milliseconds, commonly found at synapses mediating rapid behaviors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p251]
- Ionotropic receptors are membrane-spanning proteins where the extracellular domain binds transmitter and the membrane domain forms the ion-conducting pore. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p237]
- Ionotropic receptors produce fast synaptic actions lasting only milliseconds. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p238]
- Ionotropic glutamate receptors (AMPA, NMDA, kainate) are tetramers with three transmembrane helices and one re-entrant loop (M2) forming the pore. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p269]
- Nicotinic ACh receptors, GABAA receptors, and glycine receptors are pentameric, each composed of five subunits surrounding a central pore. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p251]
- The ACh receptor at the neuromuscular junction contains both the binding site for ACh and the ion channel in a single macromolecule. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p242]

## Inferences

- Inference: onotropic receptors evolved as the primary mechanism for fast point-to-point signaling in the nervous system.
- Inference: The structural similarity among ACh, GABA, and glycine receptors indicates a common evolutionary origin.
