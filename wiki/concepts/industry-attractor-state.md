# Industry Attractor State

## Summary

An industry attractor state describes how an industry should evolve given technological forces and demand structure—a direction of efficiency in meeting buyer needs. The concept provides strategic orientation for riding waves of change. Cisco's vision of 'IP everywhere' was an attractor state where all data would move as IP packets regardless of network medium. The critical distinction from corporate visions is that attractor states are based on overall efficiency rather than a single company's desire to capture value.
