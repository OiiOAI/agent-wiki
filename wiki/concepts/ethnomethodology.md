# Ethnomethodology

## Summary

Ethnomethodology is the study of the common-sense knowledge and procedures by which ordinary members of society make sense of and act in their circumstances. Created by Harold Garfinkel (a student of Alfred Schutz and Talcott Parsons), ethnomethodology is closely aligned with phenomenological sociology but focuses more on what people actually do rather than what they think. Ethnomethodologists devote considerable attention to the detailed study of conversations. This contrasts with mainstream sociology's interest in abstractions like bureaucracies, capitalism, and the social system. Although ethnomethodology and phenomenology have important differences, they share a micro orientation to actors and their actions.

## Key facts

- Ethnomethodology is "the study of the body of common-sense knowledge and the range of procedures and considerations by means of which the ordinary members of society make sense of, find their way about in, and act on the circumstances in which they find themselves". - Harold Garfinkel, creator of ethnomethodology, was a student of Alfred Schutz at the New School and previously studied under Talcott Parsons. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p108]
- Ethnomethodologists focus more on what people actually do rather than what they think, devoting attention to detailed study of conversations. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p108]
- Ethnomethodology literally means the methods that people use on a daily basis to accomplish their everyday lives. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p282]
- The world is seen as an ongoing practical accomplishment; people are rational but use practical reasoning, not formal logic. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p282]
- Garfinkel considers social facts to be the fundamental sociological phenomenon, treating their objectivity as the accomplishment of members. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p283]
- Ethnomethodologists refuse to treat actors as judgmental dopes but do not believe people are endlessly reflexive, self-conscious and calculative; action is usually routine and relatively unreflective. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p283]
- Ethnomethodologists are interested not in actors or individuals but in members—membership activities or artful practices that produce large-scale and small-scale structures. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p283]
- Ethnomethods are reflexively accountable—accounts are ways actors explain, describe, criticize, and idealize specific situations. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p283]
- Accounts are reflexive in that they enter into the constitution of the state of affairs they make observable and are intended to deal with. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p285]
