# Being

## Summary

Being is the eternal, ever-present One Life beyond the myriad forms of life subject to birth and death. It is the innermost invisible and indestructible essence present within every form. Being is accessible as one's own deepest self and true nature, but cannot be understood mentally—only felt when the mind is still. The word 'Being' is preferred over 'God' because it is an open concept that does not reduce the infinite to a finite entity. Being is characterized by timelessness, wholeness, and peace. The realization of Being as 'I am' prior to 'I am this' or 'I am that' constitutes enlightenment.

## Key facts

- Being is described as 'the eternal, ever-present One Life beyond the myriad forms of life that are subject to birth and death'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p26]
- Being is the innermost invisible and indestructible essence within every form. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p26]
- Being can be known only when the mind is still and is accessed as the feeling of one's own presence. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p26]
- The realization of Being is described as 'the feeling-realization' of one's own presence, which is enlightenment. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p26]
- Being is contrasted with 'God' because the word God has become a closed concept reduced to a mental idol, whereas Being is an open concept. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p27]
