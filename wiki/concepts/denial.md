# Denial

## Summary

Denial is described as a powerful enemy of successful predictions, functioning as a 'save-now-pay-later' scheme that causes constant low-grade anxiety while preventing protective action.

## Key facts

- The author states that denial keeps people from taking action that could reduce risks and the associated worry. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p14]
- The fall experienced when victimized is far greater for those who denied the possibility than for those who accepted it. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p14]
