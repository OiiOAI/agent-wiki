# Broken-Glass Outline

## Summary

A broken-glass outline is a flexible visual method for organizing communication that radiates spokes from a central title, making it easier to revise than traditional Harvard outlines. It activates visual thinking to help communicators discover ideas and see how concepts fit together.

## Key facts

- A broken-glass outline starts with a title in the center, then draws radiating spokes for major subdivisions. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p57]
- The broken-glass outline was developed to avoid the rigidity and awkward imperatives of formal Harvard outlines. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p63]
- Drawing makes you smarter by actuating the human visual-thinking faculty. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p61]
- For persuasion, broken-glass outlines include four key spokes: Vision, Steps, News, and Contributions. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p63]
- For instruction, broken-glass outlines include three key spokes: empowerment promise, stories, and delivery on promise. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p63]
- Broken-glass outlines can help you learn by arranging and populating standard spokes to tell yourself a story. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p62]
- The broken-glass outline organizes content radially with a central topic and radiating spokes for subtopics. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p29]
- Rehearsal boards are used to practice lectures, with boards photographed for future reference and improvement. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p183]
- The broken-glass outline serves as a foundation for abstract composition by revealing top-level spokes and low-level details. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p238]
- Winston works at a blackboard, photographing successful configurations for future reference. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p258]
