# Procrastination

## Summary

Procrastination is a nearly universal vice affecting 95% of people at least sometimes, with over 20% of surveyed people internationally considering it a defining personal characteristic. Research by Baumeister and Tice found that procrastinators got worse grades but enjoyed better health early in the semester—until the end, when all-nighters caused a sharp rise in illness and stress. Impulsiveness, not perfectionism, is the trait most consistently linked to procrastination. The 'deadline test' showed that procrastinators underestimated how long tasks would take and suffered severe consequences for missing final deadlines.
