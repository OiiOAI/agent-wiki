# The Dabbler

## Summary

The Dabbler approaches new activities with enormous enthusiasm, loves the rituals of starting, but abandons pursuits when the first plateau appears. The Dabbler replays the scenario of starting up repeatedly, never developing deep expertise in any area including careers and relationships.

## Key facts

- The Dabbler approaches each new sport, career opportunity, or relationship with enormous enthusiasm and loves the rituals involved in getting started. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p22]
- When the Dabbler makes first progress and then encounters the inevitable plateau, their enthusiasm quickly wanes. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p22]
- The Dabbler rationalizes abandoning pursuits with statements like it isn't the right sport or too competitive. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p22]
- In love, the Dabbler specializes in honeymoons and when ardor cools, looks for a new partner rather than working through the plateau. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p23]
- Leonard notes the Dabbler might think of themselves as an adventurer but is closer to Carl Jung's puer aeternus, the eternal kid. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p23]
