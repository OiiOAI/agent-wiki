# Quantified Self

## Summary

The Quantified Self (QS) movement involves people tracking their own behaviors using technology—monitoring sleep, exercise, eating, spending, mood, and productivity. Founded by Kevin Kelly and Gary Wolf in 2008, the movement applies self-monitoring techniques historically used by figures like Anthony Trollope and Thomas Jefferson to modern digital tools.

## Key facts

- The Quantified Self movement was created in 2008 by Kevin Kelly and Gary Wolf, catering to users of self-regulation technology who convene to share data and encourage one another. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p111]
- RescueTime founder Tony Wright discovered that nearly a third of his day was spent on 'the long tail of information porn'—visits to unrelated websites—totaling two and a half hours daily. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p110]
- Mint.com data from two billion transactions showed that for 80 percent of users, the upward trajectory of spending was tempered after joining and monitoring their transactions. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p112]
- Anthony Trollope used daily monitoring with a watch and diary to write 2,500 words by breakfast and produce nearly 50 novels, using the record as 'a blister to my eye' when he fell behind. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p109]
