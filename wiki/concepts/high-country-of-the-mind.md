# High Country of the Mind

## Summary

The high country of the mind refers to the uppermost reaches of human knowledge—the most general, most abstract considerations. Few people travel there because there's no profit to be made. It requires adjustment to the 'thinner air of uncertainty' and the enormous magnitude of questions asked. Phædrus wandered through this country following every path, seeing occasionally he made progress but seeing nothing ahead telling him which way to go.

## Key facts

- If all of human knowledge is believed to be an enormous hierarchic structure, then the high country of the mind is found at the uppermost reaches of this structure, in the most general and abstract considerations of all. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p106]
- In the high country of the mind one has to become adjusted to the thinner air of uncertainty, and to the enormous magnitude of questions asked. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p106]
- Questions in this realm include: What is the truth and how do you know it when you have it? How do we really know anything? Is there an 'I,' a 'soul,' which knows? Is reality changing or fixed and permanent?. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p107]
