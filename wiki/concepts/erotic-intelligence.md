# Erotic Intelligence

## Summary

Erotic intelligence refers to the capacity to sustain desire, passion, and erotic imagination within long-term committed relationships. It involves balancing security with adventure, creating erotic space, practicing intentionality, and maintaining a sense of play and mystery despite the familiarity of domestic life.

## Key facts

- Eroticism is absent from the common idea of marriage. While committed couples are expected to have sex and even enjoy it, the lascivious, ardent, needful, frivolous erotic sex of lovers becomes rare after the housewarming party. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p176]
- Octavio Paz likens eroticism to the poetry of the body, the testimony of the senses. Like a poem, it is not linear; it meanders and twists back on itself and reveals another world inside this world. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p189]
- Eroticism, intertwined as it is with imagination, is a form of play—'an alternative reality midway between the actual and the fictitious, a safe space where we experiment, reinvent ourselves, and take chances.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p189]
- Couples with erotic intelligence maintain playfulness with each other, are physically and sensually alive, and see seduction as an end in itself rather than merely a means to sex. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p190]
- For these couples, love is a vessel that contains both security and adventure, and commitment offers one of the great luxuries of life: time. Marriage is not the end of their romance, it's the beginning. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p191]
- Reconciling the domestic and the erotic requires knowing your partner while recognizing their persistent mystery; creating security while remaining open to the unknown; cultivating intimacy that respects privacy. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p191]
- Adam Phillips states: 'If it is the forbidden that is exciting—if desire is fundamentally transgressive—then the monogamous are like the very rich. They have to find their poverty. They have to starve themselves enough.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p183]

## Inferences

- Inference: Erotic intelligence requires active effort and cannot be left to spontaneity in long-term relationships, as what was spontaneous in the beginning has already happened and must now be consciously created.
- Inference: Erotic intelligence involves accepting that desire will wax and wane, that it suffers periodic eclipses and intermittent disappearances, but that with sufficient attention it can be revived.
