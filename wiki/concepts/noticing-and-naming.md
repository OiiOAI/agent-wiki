# Noticing and Naming

## Summary

Noticing and naming is a process central to many ACT unhooking skills, involving noticing thoughts and feelings with curiosity and naming them in a nonjudgmental manner. When noticed and named, difficult thoughts and feelings have reduced effect on behavior because the prefrontal cortex is activated, which moderates other parts of the brain generating emotional storms.

## Key facts

- Noticing and naming involves noticing thoughts and feelings with curiosity and naming them nonjudgmentally, such as 'Here is anxiety' or 'I'm noticing worry.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p66]
- The simple act of noticing and putting feelings into words activates the prefrontal cortex, which moderates other parts of the brain generating emotional storms. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p66]
- The less aware a person is of their thoughts and feelings, the less control they have over their actions. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p66]
- When awareness is brought to thoughts and feelings through noticing and naming, they lose much of their ability to jerk a person around. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p66]
