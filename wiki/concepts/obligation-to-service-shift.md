# Obligation to Service Shift

## Summary

The shift from obligation to service is a transformation that occurs when you begin using your Second Brain not only for remembering, but for connecting and creating. You transition from doing things primarily out of obligation or pressure to doing things from a spirit of service. The book argues that knowledge is meant to be shared and becomes more powerful as it spreads.

## Key facts

- Most people have a natural desire within them to serve others—they want to teach, to mentor, to help, to contribute. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p200]
- Many people put their desire to serve on hold, waiting for a future time when they will have enough time, bandwidth, expertise, or resources. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p200]
- Knowledge is the only resource that gets better and more valuable the more it multiplies. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p201]
- There are problems in the world that you are uniquely equipped to solve. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p201]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book suggests that building a Second Brain naturally surfaces evidence of what you already know, removing the excuse to wait before sharing knowledge
