# Three Fundamentals

## Summary

The Three Fundamentals are the three ways a man can become more vulnerable and less needy: Honest Living (creating an attractive lifestyle based on personal values), Honest Action (overcoming fear and anxiety around women), and Honest Communication (expressing emotions freely and effectively). Each can be worked on independently but they reinforce each other.

## Key facts

- Honest Living correlates directly with the quality of women attracted and the percentage of Receptive women met. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p107]
- Honest Action correlates directly to the quantity of women met and attracted; overcoming fear enables a man to pursue any woman. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p108]
- Honest Communication determines the efficiency of attracting compatible women; poor communication causes 'lost in translation' situations. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p108]
- Men with poor lifestyles but no fear still meet women through pure numbers; men with great communication but fear rarely meet opportunities. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p109]
- Most men have one or two main weaknesses and one natural strength among the three fundamentals. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p110]
