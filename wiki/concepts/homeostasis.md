# Homeostasis

## Summary

Homeostasis is the process by which the body maintains physiological parameters within a narrow range essential for survival. It involves negative feedback loops that detect deviations from normal values and activate corrective responses through behavioral, autonomic, and neuroendocrine systems.

## Key facts

- Claude Bernard first drew attention to the stability of the body's internal environment, stating that 'the internal environment is a necessary condition for a free life.'. - Homeostasis is the active maintenance of a relatively constant internal environment; constancy of the internal environment is the basis of the freedom of action organisms enjoy.
