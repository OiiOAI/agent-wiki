# Unconscious Thought Theory

## Summary

A theory proposing that for complex decisions involving large amounts of information and multiple constraints, the unconscious mind is better suited than the conscious mind due to more neuronal bandwidth.

## Key facts

- UTT proposes that for decisions requiring strict rules, the conscious mind must be involved, but for decisions with large amounts of information and vague constraints, the unconscious mind is better suited. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p86]
- A 2006 study found that subjects distracted after reading car purchase information performed better than those who consciously deliberated. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p86]
- The conscious mind is like a home computer running carefully written programs, while the unconscious mind is like Google's vast data centers sifting through terabytes. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p86]

## Inferences

- Inference: Providing conscious brain time to rest enables the unconscious mind to work on complex professional challenges, diversifying the type of work deployed rather than reducing productive work.
