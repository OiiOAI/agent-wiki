# Essential Variable

## Summary

Essential variables are physiological quantities such as body temperature, sugar levels, and oxygen levels that must be kept within strict limits for an organism to remain alive. Introduced by Ross Ashby, this concept is central to understanding why emotions and moods exist—they serve to regulate the body's essential variables through control-oriented perceptions.

## Key facts

- Essential variables are physiological quantities that must be kept within certain strict limits for an organism to remain alive, including body temperature, sugar levels, and oxygen levels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p177]
- The concept of essential variables was introduced by Ross Ashby, the same cybernetician who developed the Good Regulator Theorem. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p177]
- Human core body temperature must remain between 32°C and 40°C, otherwise death rapidly follows. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p186]
- Emotions and moods are control-oriented perceptions which regulate the body's essential variables—this is what they are for. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p177]
- Actions motivated by essential variable regulation can be both external movements and internal intero-actions such as raising heart rate or dilating blood vessels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p177]

## Inferences

- Inference: By analogy, a desired room temperature would be the 'essential variable' for a central heating system, illustrating the regulatory logic underlying the concept.
