# Imagination

## Summary

Imagination literally means 'to see things that the eyes don't see.' Story is not a product of imagination but its source—story is what powers the default mode network, enabling children to dream the unseen and Operators to act smart in mayhem. Effective imagination combines a single long-term goal (one 'why') with many possible paths (branching 'what ifs'), achieved through defined strategy and unlimited tactics. The technique was used by Beethoven (Fifth Symphony during military occupation), Horatio Nelson (Battle of Trafalgar), and Special Operators who flow through misty woods with agile purpose.

## Key facts

- Imagination's name denotes the digital process in the mind of compiling mental images, but this process is preceded by a script that doesn't come from data—it is invented by the brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p43]
- Story is classified today as a product of imagination, but this is exactly backward—as narratives of screenwriters are the source of movies, story is the source of imagination. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p43]
- Storythinking occurs in the brains of humans, chimpanzees, mice, and crows—biologically very old, since crows and humans' family trees split apart before the T. rex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p45]
- While logic computes what is probable, story creates what is possible—allowing acceleration into the future and initiative in uncertainty without requiring reliable data. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p46]
- Operators storythink about the past (reflecting on events, asking 'why') connected to the future (imagining events, asking 'what if') in a feedback loop where more diverse whys produce more innovative what ifs. - Beethoven wrote his Fifth Symphony during the invasion of his homeland, fusing traditional sonata form with pioneering piccolos and contrabassoons—embodying overarching strategy and infinite tactics. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p53]
