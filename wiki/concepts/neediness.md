# Neediness

## Summary

Being more invested in the opinions and perceptions of others than your opinion and perception of yourself. Needy men defer all decision-making and behaviors to what they believe will win them approval from others.

## Key facts

- Neediness is defined as being more invested in the opinions and perceptions of others than your opinion and perception of yourself. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Needy men end up deferring all decision-making and behaviors to what they believe will win them approval from others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- They will subvert their own identity and desires for the will of others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Neediness is a defense mechanism for social/emotional failures early in life but is a failing strategy for intimacy in adulthood. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Neediness is overcome through investing in oneself, practicing vulnerability, and pursuing Honest Living, Honest Action, and Honest Communication. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
