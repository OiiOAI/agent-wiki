# Reverse Pilot

## Summary

A reverse pilot is a technique for testing whether removing an initiative or activity will have negative consequences. Unlike a traditional pilot that tests adding something, a reverse pilot involves stopping an activity and observing whether anyone notices or objects, helping identify nonessential commitments.

## Key facts

- Daniel Shapero of LinkedIn coined the term 'reverse pilot' for testing whether removing an initiative would have negative consequences. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p144]
- An executive stopped publishing a large weekly report and found that after several weeks nobody had mentioned it, concluding it was not essential. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p144]

## Inferences

- Inference: Many commitments persist simply because no one has questioned them—a reverse pilot provides low-risk evidence that can liberate resources for essential activities.
