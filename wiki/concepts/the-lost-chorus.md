# The Lost Chorus

## Summary

The Lost Chorus refers to a group of scientists from the 1930s-1970s who published over a hundred publications demonstrating that emotion essences do not exist, yet their work was largely overlooked, ignored, or misunderstood after the supposed dark ages of behaviorism ended. These nascent constructionists ran experiments to find physical fingerprints for distinct emotions, failed to do so, concluded that the classical view was unjustified, and speculated about constructionist ideas. The Lost Chorus flourished for half a century and then vanished because they did not offer a fully formed alternative theory of emotion to compete with the compelling classical view. As philosopher Thomas Kuhn wrote, rejecting one paradigm without simultaneously substituting another is to reject science itself.
