# Turning the Mind

## Summary

Turning the mind is a reality acceptance skill involving choosing the acceptance path when at a fork between accepting and rejecting reality. It is distinct from acceptance itself—making the choice puts one on the path, but the choice must be renewed repeatedly. The process involves observing non-acceptance, making an inner commitment, and developing a plan to catch future drift from acceptance.

## Key facts

- Turning the mind is like facing a fork in the road; you must turn your mind toward the acceptance road and away from the road of rejecting reality. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p369]
- The choice to accept does not itself equal acceptance; it just puts you on the path. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p369]
- Step-by-step: observe non-acceptance (look for anger, bitterness, 'Why me?', 'I can't stand this'), make inner commitment to accept, do it again and again each time you come to the fork, develop a plan for catching future drift. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p369]
