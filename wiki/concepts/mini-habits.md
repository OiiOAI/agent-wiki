# Mini Habits

## Summary

A habit-building strategy introduced by Stephen Guise where individuals perform tiny, manageable actions daily—smaller than what they think they can do. The approach leverages willpower strategically and aims to expand one's comfort zone by overcoming two moments of resistance. Unlike traditional habit methods, mini habits emphasize never letting up, increasing self-efficacy and autonomy while destroying fear and doubt.

## Key facts

- Mini habits are defined as tiny behaviors performed daily that are smaller than one's normal capacity. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- The strategy emphasizes using willpower effectively rather than relying solely on motivation. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- Mini habits work by expanding one's comfort zone through consistent practice. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- Two moments of resistance are identified as key barriers in habit formation that mini habits specifically address. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- Mini habits are designed to compete with and replace existing habits. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- The approach increases self-efficacy and provides autonomy to the practitioner. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- Mini habits specifically target and destroy fear, doubt, intimidation, and hesitation. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- Regular practice of mini habits creates bonuses of increased mindfulness and willpower. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p9]
- The book presents Part 6 titled 'Mini Habits – Eight Small Steps To Big Change'. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#L180]
- The eight-step framework includes: Choose Your Mini Habits & Habit Plan, Use The Why Drill On Each Mini Habit, Define Your Habit Cues, Create Your Reward Plan, Write Everything Down, Think Small, Meet Your Schedule & Drop High Expectations, and Watch For Signs Of Habit. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#L180-L189]
- A mini habit is a VERY small positive behavior that you force yourself to do every day. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p22]
- The foundation of the Mini Habits system is in 'stupid small' steps—actions so small they sound ridiculous but are perfect relative to your capabilities. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p21]
- The power of mini habits comes from application, mindset, built-in positive feedback looping, naturally increasing self-efficacy, and leveraging small steps into habits. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p21]
- Examples include: 100 push-ups minified to one push-up, writing 3,000 words minified to 50 words, thinking positively minified to two positive thoughts per day. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p21]
- Mini habits are for good habits only—adding positive behaviors to enrich life—not for quitting smoking or controlling gambling addictions. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p19]
- The recommended maximum is four mini habits at once to maintain focus and avoid divided attention. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p82]

## Inferences

- Inference: The combination of small steps with willpower suggests that mini habits work by making actions so easy that willpower is sufficient to overcome resistance, rather than relying on motivation.
- Inference: The mention of marrying abstract goals with concrete actions indicates mini habits serve as a bridge between desired outcomes and daily behavior.
- Inference: Since mini habits require very little willpower, they preserve the limited willpower resource for other tasks throughout the day.
- Inference: Because the requirement is so small, there is almost no valid excuse to skip it, leading to 100% success rates.
