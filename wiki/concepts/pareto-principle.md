# Pareto Principle

## Summary

The Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule) states that 20 percent of efforts produce 80 percent of results. Joseph Moses Juran expanded this into 'the Law of the Vital Few,' observing that you could massively improve quality by resolving a tiny fraction of problems. Richard Koch applied this to distinguish the vital few from the trivial many in all human endeavors.

## Key facts

- The Pareto Principle observes that 20 percent of our efforts produce 80 percent of results. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p44]
- Some efforts follow a 'power law,' producing exponentially more results than others; Nathan Myhrvold estimates top software developers are 10,000 times more productive than average. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p45]
- Warren Buffett owes 90% of his wealth to just ten investments, exemplifying the concentrated focus on vital few opportunities. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p45]
