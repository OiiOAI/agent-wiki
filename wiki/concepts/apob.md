# Apolipoprotein B (apoB)

## Summary

ApoB is the protein signature on LDL, VLDL, and other atherogenic lipoprotein particles. Every lipoprotein that contributes to atherosclerosis carries the apoB protein. The concentration of apoB particles is more predictive of cardiovascular disease than LDL-C alone. Each standard-deviation increase in apoB raises the risk of myocardial infarction by 38 percent. Atherosclerosis would not occur if LDL concentrations were at neonatal levels (10-20 mg/dL).

## Key facts

- ApoB (apolipoprotein B) is the protein signature found on LDL, VLDL, and other atherogenic lipoprotein particles. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p123]
- Every single lipoprotein that contributes to atherosclerosis carries this apoB protein signature. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p123]
- Each standard-deviation increase in apoB raises the risk of myocardial infarction by 38 percent in patients without a history of cardiac events. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p133]
- ApoB is more predictive of cardiovascular disease than the concentration of cholesterol within LDL particles (LDL-C). [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p133]
- According to Peter Libby and colleagues, atherosclerosis would probably not occur if LDL concentrations were akin to those of neonates (10-20 mg/dL). [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p138]
- The goal of lipid-lowering therapy is to increase the body's ability to get apoB particles out of circulation, primarily by amplifying LDL receptor activity in the liver. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p139]
