# Variational Calculus

## Summary

Variational calculus provides the mathematical foundation for optimizing beliefs and functions, enabling functional derivatives and variational Bayes. The key result is that minimizing free energy under mean-field approximations leads to update rules where each factor of the approximate posterior depends on expectations over other factors.

## Key facts

- Functional derivatives find the function that minimizes a functional expressed as an integral of a function. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p242]
- The Euler-Lagrange equation expresses the condition for a function to minimize a functional when the integrand includes the function's gradient. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p243]
- Variational Bayes leads to update rules where the log of each approximate posterior factor equals the expectation of the log generative model over other factors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p244]
