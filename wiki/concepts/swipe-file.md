# Swipe File

## Summary

A collection of things worth stealing from others—analog or digital—used for inspiration and reference. Journalists call it a 'morgue file.' The swipe file is where you keep the dead things that you'll later reanimate in your work.

## Key facts

- A swipe file is a collection of things you've swiped from others, which can be digital or analog. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p23]
- Newspaper reporters call this a 'morgue file'—a place to keep the dead things that you'll later reanimate in your work. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p24]
- Mark Twain wrote, 'It is better to take what does not belong to you than to let it lie around neglected.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p24]
