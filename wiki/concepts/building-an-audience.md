# Building an Audience

## Summary

Building an audience means creating a group of people who regularly return to hear what you have to say, rather than paying for attention through advertising. The authors argue this is more effective than traditional marketing because audiences return on their own, are more receptive, and don't require ongoing expenditure. They cite their own Signal vs. Noise blog with over 100,000 daily readers as an example, calculating that reaching that many people through traditional advertising would cost hundreds of thousands or millions of dollars.

## Key facts

- The authors distinguish between companies with customers, companies with fans, and companies with audiences, arguing that audiences are the most valuable. [raw/books/business/Rework - Jason Fried.pdf#p125]
- 37signals built an audience of over 100,000 daily readers for its Signal vs. Noise blog over ten years. [raw/books/business/Rework - Jason Fried.pdf#p125]
- The authors estimate reaching 100,000 people daily through traditional advertising would cost hundreds of thousands or millions of dollars. [raw/books/business/Rework - Jason Fried.pdf#p125]
- Building an audience means people give you their attention rather than you having to buy it. [raw/books/business/Rework - Jason Fried.pdf#p125]

## Inferences

- Inference: Content marketing through audience building creates compounding returns over time, unlike paid advertising which stops delivering value when spending stops.
