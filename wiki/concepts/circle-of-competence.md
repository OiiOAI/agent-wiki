# Circle of Competence

## Summary

Circle of Competence is the investment principle that investors should stay within areas where they have genuine understanding and expertise, distinguishing between true mastery (Max Planck) and superficial familiarity (chauffeur who memorized a lecture). Munger applies a basic overlay to limit his investment field to simple, understandable candidates, placing complex or poorly understood investments into 'too tough to understand' baskets.

## Key facts

- Munger tells the story of Max Planck and his chauffeur: the chauffeur memorized a lecture but could not answer a difficult question, representing the difference between true mastery and mere recitation. ## Inferences

- Inference: Recognizing the limits of one's knowledge prevents costly errors that come from overconfident investment decisions outside genuine areas of expertise.
- Inference: The concept implies that investment success comes not from expanding into every opportunity but from ruthlessly identifying and exploiting one's genuine edges.
