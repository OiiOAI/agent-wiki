# Associative Networks

## Summary

Associative networks are neural circuits where neurons with overlapping receptive fields converge on common target neurons. These networks enable capacity for association, metaphor, analogy, and creativity by linking disparate concepts across sensory modalities.

## Key facts

- Neurons at convergent centers receive overlapping projections from specialized neurons, giving them general knowledge from multiple exemplars. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p718]
- Associative networks enable capacity for metaphor, analogy, parable, and symbol—linking disparate things even from different sensory modalities. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p718]
- Atypical widening of associative networks may produce creativity, as broader, more idiosyncratic neural connections allow novel associations. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p719]
