# The Edge

## Summary

The Edge represents the paradox at the heart of mastery: masters are dedicated to fundamentals and practice while also challenging previous limits and taking risks. Playing the edge means walking the fine line between endless goalless practice and intermediate goals, pushing boundaries while staying aware of limits.
