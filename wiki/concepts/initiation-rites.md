# Initiation Rites

## Summary

Primitive initiation ceremonies dramatize the Oedipal aggression of the elder generation, with circumcision functioning as mitigated castration. The rites also provide for the cannibal patricidal impulse of younger males while revealing the benign self-giving aspect of the archetypal father through blood-drinking rituals.

## Key facts

- Australian Aboriginal mythologies teach that the first initiation rites killed all the young men, dramatizing the Oedipal aggression of the elder generation. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p113]
- The Murngin boy's first ordeal involves Great Father Snake calling for his foreskin while women pretend to fight to protect him from being 'eaten'. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p112]
- Australian initiates survive on the fresh-drawn blood of older men during their instruction period, sometimes for a whole moon, as the mythical ancestor Yamminga ordained. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p114]
