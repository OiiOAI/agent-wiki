# Digital Minimalism

## Summary

Digital minimalism is a philosophy and lifestyle approach that helps individuals question what digital communication tools and behaviors add the most value to their lives. It involves choosing reduced dependence on the internet, smartphones, computers, and other devices while regaining control over intentions, focus, and freedom to choose. The philosophy centers on using technology purposefully rather than habitually.

## Key facts

- Cal Newport, a computer science professor at Georgetown University, defines digital minimalism as 'a philosophy that helps you question what digital communication tools (and behaviors surrounding these tools) add the most value to your life.'. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p20]
- Digital minimalism applies to both the digital technology used and the behaviors surrounding that technology. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p20]
- The essential principles of digital minimalism include: people always come before technology, intentional use of technology, and recognizing digital technology as a creative tool rather than a source of cheap relief. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p21]
- Digital minimalism doesn't mean stopping phone use entirely, but rather making purposeful and well-intentioned use of digital devices instead of impulsive usage. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p26]
- The core philosophy of minimalism is that it is a lifestyle choice where one gets rid of the excesses of life to allow focus on aspects contributing to freedom, fulfillment, and true happiness. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p14]
