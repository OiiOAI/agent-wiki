# Freedom from Expectations

## Summary

Ravikant advocates freedom from others' expectations, arguing that other people's expectations are their own problem and that caring for others' opinions wastes valuable time.

## Key facts

- If people have expectations of you, that is their problem, not yours. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p154]
- Courage is not caring what other people think. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p154]
- Your time is all you have; it is more important than money, friends, or anything. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p154]
- You are not responsible for making other people happy. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p155]
