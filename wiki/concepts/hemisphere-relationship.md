# The Relationship Between the Hemispheres

## Summary

McGilchrist examines the working relationship between cerebral hemispheres at three levels. At the millisecond-to-millisecond level (Level One), both hemispheres are necessary for daily functioning and compete as well as cooperate. At the phenomenological level (Level Two), the right hemisphere has primacy in constituting reality while the left hemisphere provides necessary analysis. At the strategic level (Level Three), McGilchrist suggests the left hemisphere has developed an inflated sense of its own importance. The corpus callosum functions primarily as an inhibitory mechanism that maintains separation between hemispheres rather than facilitating integration. Split-brain research reveals that when the divide is compromised, initially there is conflict of will between the hemispheres.

## Key facts

- The corpus callosum acts principally as the agent of hemisphere differentiation rather than integration. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p301]
- In split-brain patients, the initial effect was not things that no longer happened, but things that could not be prevented from happening—things that could not be inhibited. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p299]
- Banich found that interhemispheric interaction has important emergent functions that cannot be derived from the simple sum of its parts. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p306]
- When both hemispheres cooperate, different regions are recruited than when either hemisphere works alone. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p306]
- The Upanishads describe the controller in the heart as 'the bridge that serves as a boundary to keep the different worlds apart'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p301]

## Inferences

- Inference: The necessity for inhibition suggests the hemispheres have conflicting rather than merely complementary interests
- Inference: Compromised corpus callosum function in schizophrenia may explain some features of psychosis through failure of interhemispheric inhibition

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact mechanism of the 'metacontrol switch' in the brainstem that apportions work between hemispheres remains uncertain
