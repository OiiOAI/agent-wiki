# Insecure Conflict Strategies

## Summary

The book identifies six insecure conflict strategies that individuals with insecure attachment styles tend to use during disagreements. These strategies perpetuate negativity and prevent healthy resolution of conflicts.

## Key facts

- The six insecure conflict strategies are: (1) getting sidetracked from the real problem, (2) neglecting to effectively communicate feelings and needs, (3) reverting to personal attacks and destructiveness, (4) reacting 'tit for tat' to a partner's negativity with more negativity, (5) withdrawing, and (6) forgetting to focus on the other's well-being. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p196]
- Expecting the worst in conflict situations—which is typical of people with insecure attachment styles—often acts as a self-fulfilling prophecy. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p196]
- If you assume your partner will act hurtfully or reject you, you automatically respond defensively—thus starting a vicious cycle of negativity. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p196]
