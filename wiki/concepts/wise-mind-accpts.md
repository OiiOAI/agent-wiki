# Wise Mind ACCEPTS

## Summary

Wise Mind ACCEPTS is a distraction skill mnemonic for managing crisis situations by reducing contact with distressing elements. The seven categories are Activities, Contributing, Comparisons, Emotions, Pushing away, Thoughts, and Sensations. These methods work by decreasing emotional arousal during acute crisis.

## Key facts

- ACCEPTS stands for: Activities, Contributing, Comparisons, Emotions, Pushing away, Thoughts, Sensations. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p357]
- Activities distract through tasks like cleaning, exercise, sports, or hobbies. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p357]
- Contributing involves volunteer work, helping others, or doing thoughtful things for people. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p357]
- Different Emotions means engaging with opposite emotional content (scary movies, comedies, religious music) to shift emotional state. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p357]
- Pushing away includes leaving situations mentally, building imaginary walls, and refusing to think about painful situations. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p357]
