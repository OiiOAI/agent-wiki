# Motor Primacy

## Summary

Motor primacy is the thesis that mindness is the internalization of movement. From its very evolutionary inception, the ability to think arose from the internalization of movement strategies. The nervous system evolved to provide an internal plan for movement composed of goal-oriented, mostly short-lived predictions verified by moment-to-moment sensory input. This concept rejects the reflexological view that movement is fundamentally driven by sensory cues.

## Key facts

- Motor primacy holds that from its very evolutionary inception, mindness is the internalization of movement. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p11]
- Active movement is dangerous in the absence of an internal plan subject to sensory modulation; the nervous system evolved to provide a plan composed of goal-oriented, mostly short-lived predictions verified by sensory input. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p24]
- The central generation of movement and the generation of mindness are deeply related—they are different parts of the same process. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p11]

## Inferences

- Inference: mindness arose from internalized movement, then thinking ultimately represents movement—not just of body parts but of perceptions and complex ideas as well.
