# Autopilots

## Summary

Autopilots are constructive, helpful beliefs and automatic behaviours stored in the Computer that support successful and happy functioning. They represent positive programmes that the mind can run automatically without conscious effort from the Human or Chimp.

## Key facts

- Autopilots are positive, constructive beliefs and automatic functioning that help people be successful and happy. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p56]
- Autopilots can be placed into the Computer at any age and include behaviours like riding a bike, staying calm under pressure, or being organized. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p56]
- The Computer acts faster than both the Human and Chimp, running autopilots with complete accuracy before conscious thought can interfere. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p56]
- When the Human or Chimp interprets an experience constructively, an Autopilot gets stored for future reference. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p74]

## Inferences

- Inference: Developing more Autopilots reduces the need for conscious effort in daily functioning and stabilizes emotional responses.
