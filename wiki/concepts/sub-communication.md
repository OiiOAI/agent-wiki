# Sub-communication

## Summary

The communication of intentions and feelings beneath the surface level of words. The book argues that women perceive and respond primarily to sub-communication rather than the literal content of speech.

## Key facts

- Women communicate more in feelings and, more specifically, through intentions—this is sub-communication. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p187]
- Women don't see your features, they see how you present yourself; they don't hear your words, they hear your intentions. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p190]
- What you actually talk about has far less influence on your results than your intentions. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p189]
