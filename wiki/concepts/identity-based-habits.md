# Identity-Based Habits

## Summary

Identity-Based Habits is an approach to habit formation that focuses on who you wish to become rather than what you want to achieve. The book argues that the most effective habits are those that align with and reinforce a desired identity. When habits are tied to identity, repeated behaviors become evidence of the kind of person you are. The two-step process involves deciding the type of person you want to be, and then proving it to yourself with small wins that align with that identity.

## Key facts

- The most effective form of habit change is making it about identity rather than outcomes. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p31]
- Identity-based habits focus on who you wish to become, with each action representing a vote for the type of person you want to be. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p38]
- The two-step process for changing identity involves deciding who you want to be and proving it to yourself with small wins. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p39]
- The book notes that identity can literally mean 'repeated beingness' based on Latin etymology. [raw/books/productivity/Atomic Habits_ The life - changing million copy bestseller - James Clear.pdf#p215]
