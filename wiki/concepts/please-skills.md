# PLEASE Skills

## Summary

PLEASE skills are emotion regulation strategies focusing on physical wellness to reduce emotional vulnerability. The acronym organizes five areas of self-care: treating physical illness, balancing eating, avoiding mood-altering substances, balancing sleep, and getting exercise. These skills address biological factors that contribute to emotional vulnerability.

## Key facts

- PLEASE stands for: Treat PhysicaL Illness, Balance Eating, Avoid mood-Altering substances, Balance Sleep, Get Exercise. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p281]
- Balance Eating means not eating too much or too little, eating regularly and mindfully, and avoiding foods that cause excessive emotional reactivity. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p281]
- Balance Sleep means aiming for 7-9 hours nightly and maintaining a consistent sleep schedule, especially when having difficulty sleeping. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p281]
- Get Exercise requires some form of exercise daily, building up to 20 minutes. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p281]
