# Emotion Labeling

## Summary

Emotion labeling (also called 'affective labeling' in research literature) is the skill of precisely naming the emotions one experiences. The book argues that this skill is foundational to emotional intelligence because 'if you can name it, you can tame it.' Labeling emotions with precision serves four functions: it legitimizes and organizes experiences, helps others meet one's needs, enables one to meet others' needs, and connects people through shared emotional vocabulary. The concept emphasizes moving beyond vague descriptors like 'fine' or 'bad' to specific terms like 'annoyed,' 'frustrated,' 'anxious,' or 'disappointed.'
