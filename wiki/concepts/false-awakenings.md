# False Awakenings

## Summary

False awakenings are dreams in which a person believes they have woken up, often in their habitual sleep environment, when they are actually still asleep. These dreams within dreams can be remarkably detailed, with people appearing to wake up, get out of bed, shower, and prepare breakfast before actually waking.

## Key facts

- In false awakenings, people may 'wake up,' get out of bed, shower, and be about to fix breakfast when they suddenly wake up again for real. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p24]
- People who've experienced false awakenings are often amazed by the dream's exquisitely rendered details that helped fool them into believing they were awake. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p24]
