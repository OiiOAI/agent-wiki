# SA Queries (Situation Awareness Queries)

## Summary

A direct and objective measure of situation awareness discussed in Chapter 7 by Mica Endsley. The technique involves using simulation freezes to query operators about their SA during simulated operations.

## Key facts

- Chapter 7 by Mica Endsley provides a thorough review of simulation freezes and SA queries to obtain direct objective measures of SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]
- The chapter discusses development of queries for different domains and recommendations for administering the technique. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]
