# Instant Stress Management

## Summary

The Moon of Instant Stress addresses sudden or immediate stress using an Autopilot blueprint with seven steps. Stress is described as a healthy reaction meant to be uncomfortable—nature's way of signalling something needs attention. When stressed, the Chimp goes into Fight, Flight, or Freeze mode. The key to managing instant stress is having an Autopilot programme ready in the Computer that operates faster than the Chimp. The seven-step blueprint includes: Recognition and Change, the Pause Button, Escape, Getting Perspective (helicopter technique), Making a Plan, Reflection and Activation, and Smiling.

## Key facts

- Stress is a healthy reaction meant to be uncomfortable; it is nature's way of telling you that something is wrong and you need to act. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p134]
- During stress the body releases adrenalin and cortisol to alert you. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p134]
- Under stress, the Chimp will go into Fight, Flight, or Freeze mode. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p135]
- The Computer is 20 times quicker than the Chimp, so if the Computer has an Autopilot ready, the Chimp will not get a chance to act. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p136]
- The seven steps for dealing with instant stress are: Recognition and change, the pause button, escape, the helicopter and getting perspective, the plan, reflection and activation, and smile. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p136]
- AMP represents Accept, Move on, with a Plan—dealing with something unpleasant by accepting it, deciding to move on, and creating a plan. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p140]
