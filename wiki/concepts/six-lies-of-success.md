# Six Lies of Success

## Summary

The Six Lies Between You and Success are common misconceptions that undermine productivity: (1) Everything Matters Equally, (2) Multitasking, (3) A Disciplined Life, (4) Willpower Is Always on Will-Call, (5) A Balanced Life, and (6) Big Is Bad. Each is presented as a 'lie' that must be rejected to achieve extraordinary results.

## Key facts

- Lie 1: Everything Matters Equally—the truth is that a small amount of causes creates most of the results. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p26]
- Lie 2: Multitasking—Stanford's Clifford Nass found that high multitaskers were outperformed on every measure and 'just lousy at everything'. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p35]
- Lie 3: A Disciplined Life—the truth is we don't need more discipline, just better direction. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p44]
- Lie 4: Willpower Is Always on Will-Call—willpower is a limited resource that must be managed. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p49]
- Lie 5: A Balanced Life—balance is impossible; instead, practice counterbalancing between work and personal life. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p58]
- Lie 6: Big Is Bad—big is not inherently bad; extraordinary results require big thinking. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p69]

## Inferences

- Inference: These 'lies' represent the conventional wisdom that the book argues against, offering 'truths' that replace each misconception.
