# VSN-C Framework

## Summary

VSN-C is a framework for structuring persuasive communications, developed by Patrick Henry Winston. It consists of four elements: Vision (identifying a problem and approach to solving it), Steps (the plan for achieving the goal), News (recent achievement that makes the work current), and Contributions (what the author has accomplished). This framework applies to presentations, written work, posters, and elevator pitches.

## Key facts

- Vision has two parts: a problem someone cares about and your approach to solving that problem. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p222]
- VSN-C openings include if-then, big-questions, interesting-story, mission-blocker, new-opportunity, and imagine-what-it-would-be-like styles. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p223]
- The Contributions section should use active verbs such as argued, identified, suggested, and exhibited. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p229]
- News makes work current by providing a date for a major recent contribution. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p227]
