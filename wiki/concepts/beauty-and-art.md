# Beauty and Art

## Summary

Beauty is a transcendental ideal that cannot be abolished by theory, just as goodness cannot. The left hemisphere has airbrushed beauty out of the story of art, replacing it with 'strength' or 'challenging' in the rhetoric of power. Art theory can pronounce the death of beauty, but beauty persists like King Canute's tide.

## Key facts

- We cannot remake our values at will; societies may dispute what is to be considered good, but they cannot do away with the concept, and the same is true of beauty. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p606]
- Beauty is distinguished from erotic pleasure or any other interest in that it is a 'disinterested love' (Leibniz), a 'disinterested pleasure' (Kant), a form of 'love different from desire' (Burke). [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p609]
- Our relationship with what is beautiful is more like longing or love—a betweenness, a reverberative process between the beautiful and ourselves, which has no ulterior purpose and is non-acquisitive. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p609]
