# Default Mode Network

## Summary

The 'always-on' activity of the brain that occurs when a person is not engaged in any specific task. This is distinct from the debunked claim that humans only use 10% of their brain - here, most brain activity occurs outside conscious awareness.

## Key facts

- The brain is 'on' all the time, and most of the energy consumed by the brain is for activities you are unaware of - even when daydreaming or sleeping. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p18]
- When switching from gazing at scenery to focused reading, there is at most a small percentage change in additional energy required. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p18]
- The 'default mode' is what the brain is doing when you are not busy with anything else. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p18]
- The book distinguishes this from the debunked claim that you 'only use ten percent of your brain.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p18]
