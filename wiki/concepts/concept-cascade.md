# Concept Cascade

## Summary

The concept cascade is the neural process by which efficient multisensory summaries (concepts) unpack into detailed predictions that are checked against sensory input. Cascades begin in the interoceptive network and cascade down through sensory systems. Multiple competing predictions are launched simultaneously, with the control network helping select the winning instance through optimization rather than central authority.

## Key facts

- Cascades begin in the interoceptive network where multisensory summaries are constructed. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p139]
- Cascades end in primary sensory regions where the tiniest details are represented. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p139]
- Multiple competing predictions are launched simultaneously in a storm of probabilities. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p141]
- The control network acts as an optimizer, ramping up firing rates of some neurons while slowing others. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p143]
- The concept cascade explains why emotions feel triggered rather than constructed. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p141]
- The concept cascade is a top-down hierarchy showing how concepts unpack as predictions based on cortex structure. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p336]
- Body-budgeting circuitry (visceromotor limbic circuitry) is at the top of the concept cascade hierarchy and issues but does not receive predictions. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p336]
- Primary sensory regions are at the bottom of the concept cascade hierarchy, receiving predictions but not issuing them to other cortical regions. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p336]
- The concept cascade operates in reverse of the bottom-up hierarchy used for learning concepts. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p336]
- Categorization is a whole-brain activity with predictions flowing from simulated similarities to simulated differences and prediction errors flowing in the opposite direction. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p336]
