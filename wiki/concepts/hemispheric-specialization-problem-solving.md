# Hemispheric Specialization for Problem Solving

## Summary

Research suggests functional specialization between the two hemispheres for different types of problems. The left hemisphere appears specialized for handling well-defined problems through pattern-matching and validity confirmation, while the right PFC is linked to generating courses of action under ill-defined, indeterminate, and ambiguous conditions. The left DLPFC is involved in deductive reasoning and confirmatory activity, while the right ventral lateral PFC is specifically implicated in set shifting and overcoming perceptual fixation.

## Key facts

- The left hemisphere appears specialized for locking on to patterns in the environment for validity confirmation, detecting regularities that the organism can act upon. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p416]
- Well-defined problems are ideal for left hemisphere pattern-matching because they provide sufficient information to detect regularities in the problem space. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p416]
- There is substantial evidence implicating the left DLPFC in deductive reasoning paradigms where subjects judge whether a conclusion follows logically from given information. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p416]
- Recent evidence has linked the right PFC to the generation of courses of action under ill-defined, indeterminate, and ambiguous conditions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p417]
- The same region of right PFC is sensitive to the level of external constraints placed on the task, being activated more when task constraints are reduced. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p420]
- Left DLPFC and right ventral lateral PFC form the neural system engaged in hypothesis generation, but only the right ventral lateral PFC is involved when set shifting occurs for breaking a perceptual set. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p419]

## Inferences

- Inference: The hemispheric specialization suggests a complementary relationship where the left hemisphere provides confirmatory pattern-matching for structured problems while the right hemisphere enables exploratory hypothesis generation for unstructured problems.
