# Moral Systems

## Summary

Moral systems are interlocking sets of values, virtues, norms, practices, identities, institutions, technologies, and evolved psychological mechanisms that work together to suppress or regulate self-interest and make cooperative societies possible. Haidt's definition is functionalist—it defines morality by what it does rather than by specifying what content counts as moral. This definition incorporates both social facts (external to individual minds) and psychological mechanisms (inside-the-mind stuff), drawing from Durkheim's sociological approach and psychological research.
