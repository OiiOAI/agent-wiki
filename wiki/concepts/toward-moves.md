# Toward Moves

## Summary

Toward moves are behaviors that help a person move toward the sort of life they want to build. They are actions that enhance life, making it richer, fuller, and more meaningful. Examples include spending quality time with loved ones, exercising, pursuing hobbies, being kind to others, and engaging in personal growth activities.

## Key facts

- Toward moves are behaviors that help move a person toward the sort of life they want. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p20]
- Toward moves are things said and done that enhance life, making it richer, fuller, and more meaningful. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p20]
- Examples of toward moves include spending quality time with loved ones, keeping fit, pursuing hobbies, having fun, and personal growth activities. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p20]
- Toward moves are defined in contrast to away moves, which take a person away from the life they want. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p20]
