# Time Horizon

## Summary

Time horizon refers to the amount of look-ahead needed for different tasks. As people move up organizational ladders, they need to look further ahead—managers anticipate events weeks ahead, senior managers prepare for events a year or two away, and company presidents see five to ten years into the future. The appropriate time horizon depends on the reaction time of the system at different levels.
