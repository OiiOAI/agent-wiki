# Shine (Tiny Habits)

## Summary

Shine is the word coined by BJ Fogg to describe the internal feeling of success that occurs when you complete a behavior and celebrate. It is the feeling that causes your brain to release dopamine and encode the new habit. Fogg created this new word because English lacks a precise term for this specific positive emotion—'authentic pride' comes closest but is not exact.

## Key facts

- Shine is the feeling of success that wires new habits into your brain through dopamine release. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p167]
- You feel Shine when you ace an exam, give a great presentation and people clap, or smell something delicious you cooked for the first time. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p167]
- Fogg coined the word Shine because no existing English word precisely describes the positive feeling from experiencing success. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p167]
- Celebration is habit fertilizer—each individual celebration strengthens the roots of a specific habit, and the accumulation of celebrations fertilizes the entire habit garden. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p163]
- Shine is described as 'a new word for a powerful emotion' that wires in new habits. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p303]
- Celebrating immediately after doing a new Tiny Behavior creates Shine, which strengthens the habit neural pathway. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p317]
- The book provides 100 different ways to celebrate to help readers find celebrations that work for them. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p325]
- Examples of celebrations include saying 'Yes!' with a fist pump, visualizing fireworks, smiling big, and doing a victory pose. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p325]

## Inferences

- Inference: The extensive list of 100 celebrations suggests that different people respond to different types of positive reinforcement, and finding the 'right' celebration is personal.
