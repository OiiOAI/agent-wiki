# Fear of Regret

## Summary

Fear of regret is the tendency to avoid making decisions that deviate from the crowd, even when objectively better options exist. Active decisions (buying, changing) generate more regret than passive inactions when outcomes are negative, leading people to behave conservatively and miss opportunities.

## Key facts

- When asked who would feel worse, 92% of respondents said the active trader (George) rather than the passive one (Paul), even though both lost the same amount. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p181]
- Active decisions generate more regret than passive inactions when outcomes are negative. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p181]
- "Last chance" offers exploit fear of regret by creating panic, making people act irrationally. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p182]
- Fear of regret causes traders to ofﬂoad exotic stocks on December 31 to conform to the masses. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p182]
