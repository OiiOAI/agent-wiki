# Identity Shift

## Summary

Identity Shift is the process of changing how you see yourself, which drives lasting behavior change. When people adopt new identities ('I'm the kind of person who...'), they naturally align their behaviors accordingly. Identity shifts are powerful because humans have an evolutionary drive to act consistently with their self-concept.

## Key facts

- Humans have a strongly rooted drive to act in ways consistent with their identity; unpredictable individuals were historically shunned from groups. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p211]
- Fogg's phrase 'I'm the kind of person who' is baked into his Tiny Habits evaluation process. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p211]
- Identity shifts help cultivate constellations of behavior, not just isolated habits. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p212]
- Successfully embracing an identity shift in one area often promotes change in other unrelated areas. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p212]
- Strategies for building new identities include: finishing 'I'm the kind of person who' sentences, going to events related to the emerging identity, learning the lingo, wearing identity-signaling clothing, updating social media, teaching others. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p212]
- Adding good habits can create identity shifts that crowd out unwanted behaviors naturally. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p228]
