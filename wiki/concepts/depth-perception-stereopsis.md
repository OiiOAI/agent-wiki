# Depth Perception

## Summary

Depth perception, particularly stereopsis, is computed from the differences between the two eyes' views of the world (binocular disparity). Objects at different distances produce images at noncorresponding retinal positions. Visual cortex neurons are selective for specific ranges of disparity, enabling depth discrimination and surface segmentation.

## Key facts

- Objects lying in the plane of fixation produce images at corresponding points on both retinas; objects in front of or behind the fixation plane produce images at noncorresponding locations (binocular disparity). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p609]
- Individual visual cortex neurons are selective for narrow ranges of disparity: tuned excitatory or inhibitory cells for the fixation plane, near cells for objects in front, and far cells for objects behind. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p609]
- Random-dot stereograms demonstrate that depth assignment in one part of an image can influence calculations in other parts, a phenomenon known as disparity capture. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p609]
- Neurons in area V2 respond to illusory edges formed by binocular disparity cues, even when no contrast boundary exists in the receptive field. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p610]
- Some V2 cells show border ownership, responding only when a figure (not the background) lies on the preferred side of the edge, even with identical local edge information. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p611]

## Inferences

- Inference: Depth computation is inherently global rather than local, requiring integration across the visual scene.
