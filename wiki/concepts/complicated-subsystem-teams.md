# Complicated-subsystem Teams

## Summary

Complicated-subsystem teams are one of the four team patterns in Team Topologies. These teams reduce load for stream-aligned teams by owning and maintaining subsystems that require specialized expertise or have high cognitive demands that would otherwise burden stream-aligned teams.

## Key facts

- Complicated-subsystem teams reduce load for stream-aligned teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
- Stream-aligned teams are internal customers of complicated-subsystem teams' subsystem capabilities. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
- Complicated-subsystem teams support multiple stream teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
