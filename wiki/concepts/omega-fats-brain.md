# Omega-3 and Omega-6 Fatty Acids

## Summary

Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids are essential polyunsaturated fats that profoundly impact brain health. DHA and EPA (omega-3s) support brain structure, reduce inflammation, and boost BDNF, while excessive omega-6 consumption drives inflammation. Modern diets have created a 25:1 omega-6 to omega-3 ratio, far from the ancestral 1:1 balance.

## Key facts

- The human brain is 60% fat, with polyunsaturated fats being critical structural components. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p47]
- Modern Western diets contain omega-6 and omega-3 fats in a ratio of approximately 25:1, compared to a roughly 1:1 ratio in ancestral diets. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p55]
- Omega-3 supplementation with 1320mg EPA and 880mg DHA enhanced executive function by 26% and improved white matter structural integrity. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p61]
- Young men convert approximately 8% of dietary alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) to EPA and 0-4% to DHA. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p64]
- Women are approximately 2.5 times more efficient at converting ALA to DHA compared to men. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p65]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact ancestral omega-6 to omega-3 ratio is estimated rather than precisely documented.
