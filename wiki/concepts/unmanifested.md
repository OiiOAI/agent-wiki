# Unmanifested

## Summary

The Unmanifested is the timeless, formless Source from which all manifested reality derives. It is not separate from the manifested world but pervades it as silence and space. The Unmanifested cannot be an object of knowledge or understanding—it can only be experienced directly through consciousness. Portals into the Unmanifested include: the inner body, the Now (present moment), cessation of thinking, surrender, silence, and space.

## Key facts

- The Unmanifested is the Source from which consciousness emanates, similar to how light emanates from the sun—it is birthless, deathless, and eternally present. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p117]
- Space and silence are two aspects of the same 'no-thing' and represent the Unmanifested externalized—they are the appearance of the Unmanifested in a sense-perceived world. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p128]
- No sound can exist without silence, and nothing can exist without the empty space that enables it to be; the solidity of matter is ultimately an illusion, as even atoms are mostly empty space. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p129]
- The Unmanifested can be accessed through various portals: inner-body awareness, intense presence in the Now, cessation of thinking, surrender, silence, and contemplation of space. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p127]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Tibetan Book of the Dead's description of 'the luminous splendor of the colorless light of Emptiness' at death corresponds to the Unmanifested dimension.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether the Unmanifested is identical to what Eastern traditions call 'God' is presented as a matter of terminology rather than definitive equivalence.
