# Virgin Birth

## Summary

The world-generating spirit passes into earthly experience through a transforming medium—the mother of the world, a personification of the primal element. Campbell traces virgin birth myths across cultures, showing how the goddess-mother fertilizes the void with cosmic power, giving birth to heroes who carry the creative energy forward.
