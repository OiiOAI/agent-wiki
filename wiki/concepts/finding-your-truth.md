# Finding Your Truth

## Summary

A two-part process of 1) removing behaviors which are based on receiving the approval of others rather than your own values and 2) getting in touch with emotions and desires which were previously unconscious.

## Key facts

- Finding Your Truth is a two-part process of removing behaviors based on receiving the approval of others rather than your own values. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
- The second part is getting in touch with emotions and desires which were previously unconscious. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
- Finding one's truth is based on the idea that most of our behaviors and beliefs are actually unconscious habits we picked up for the wrong reasons throughout our lives. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
- Getting in touch with one's real emotions and desires and discarding the unconfident habits and behaviors leads one to become more vulnerable, more confident, and, therefore, more attractive. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
