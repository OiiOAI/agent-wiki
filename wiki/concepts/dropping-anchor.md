# Dropping Anchor

## Summary

Dropping anchor is a three-step technique for disengaging from unhelpful thought streams: (1) Acknowledge thoughts and feelings, (2) Connect with your body through movement or breath, and (3) Engage in what you are doing. This cycle should be repeated at least three or four times to effectively disrupt rumination, worry, and obsession.

## Key facts

- Step 1: Acknowledge your thoughts and feelings by naming them and allowing them to be there. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p205]
- Step 2: Connect with your body by slowly stretching, straightening up, moving, or breathing. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p205]
- Step 3: Engage in what you are doing by refocusing your attention on the activity at hand. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p205]
- The cycle should be repeated at least three or four times for maximum effectiveness. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p205]
