# The Veil (Race Theory)

## Summary

The veil is a concept developed by W. E. B. Du Bois to describe the barrier separating African Americans and whites. Unlike the imagery of a wall, Du Bois conceived the veil as thin, porous material through which each race can see the other, but which nonetheless serves to separate the races. This concept is central to Du Bois's race theory and his understanding of racial dynamics in America.

- Du Bois's concept of the veil creates a clear separation, or barrier, between African Americans and whites. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p96]
