# Drive Fusion and Defusion

## Summary

Drive fusion refers to the combination of the two classes of drives (Eros and Thanatos) into blended formations, while drive defusion refers to their separation. Fusion allows the destructive drives to be bound and channeled through Eros, while defusion releases the death drive in its pure form, leading to pathological outcomes.

## Key facts

- The two classes of drives are fused, blended and alloyed with each other regularly and very extensively. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5926]
- The sadistic component of the sexual drive is a classical example of a serviceable drive fusion. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5926]
- The transformation of object libido into narcissistic libido involves an abandonment of sexual aims, a desexualization, a kind of sublimation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5916]
- When identification takes place, defusion of drives occurs; the erotic component no longer has the power to bind the whole of the destructiveness that was combined with it, and it is released as inclination to aggression. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5939]
- The essence of a regression of libido (e.g. from the genital to the sadistic-anal phase) lies in a defusion of drives. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5927]
