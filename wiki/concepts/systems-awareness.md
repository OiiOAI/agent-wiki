# Systems Awareness

## Summary

Systems awareness is one of three aerospace performance domains for situation awareness, specifically concerning the operator's awareness of internal aircraft systems, particularly automation systems. In routine operations, operators use skill- and rule-based behavior for forward reasoning and convergent thinking. Under failure and fault management conditions, they must use knowledge-based behavior for diagnostic backward reasoning and divergent thinking. Displays designed for routine operations may be poorly suited for fault management. Similarly, automation modes designed for routine circumstances are poorly suited for infrequent automated system failures or improbable circumstances. Even within fault conditions, procedures for simple routine faults may be inappropriate for complex multiple faults—following checklists blindly may be a 'brittle' tactic leading to dangerous paths.
