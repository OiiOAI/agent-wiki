# CODE Method

## Summary

CODE is a four-step method for building a Second Brain: Capture (keep what resonates), Organize (save for actionability), Distill (find the essence), and Express (show your work). These steps represent a map for navigating information streams and are described as flexible and applicable to any profession or notetaking platform. The acronym mirrors 'creative code' hardwired into human imagination.

## Key facts

- CODE stands for Capture, Organize, Distill, and Express — the four steps for building a Second Brain. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p41]
- Capture involves keeping only what resonates — ideas that move you on an intuitive level, creating feelings of pleasure, curiosity, wonder, or excitement. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p42]
- The author recommends organizing for actionability, saving notes according to active projects rather than by subject. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p43-44]
- Distillation involves finding the essence of ideas — the heart and soul of what they communicate — so main points can be identified in just a sentence or two. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p44]
- Express is the ultimate purpose of CODE — sharing ideas, stories, and knowledge with others through creating things that have positive impact. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p45-46]
- The author recommends shifting time and effort from consuming to creating, as knowledge becomes personal and verified only when put to use. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p45]
