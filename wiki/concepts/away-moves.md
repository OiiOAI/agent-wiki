# Away Moves

## Summary

Away moves are behaviors that take a person away from the sort of life they want to build. They keep people stuck or make life worse in the long term. Examples include withdrawing from loved ones, avoiding exercise, losing temper, procrastinating, and cognitive processes like worrying and ruminating.

## Key facts

- Away moves are behaviors that take a person away from the sort of life they want to build. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p21]
- Away moves include withdrawing from or fighting with loved ones, avoiding physical exercise, and procrastinating on important tasks. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p21]
- Away moves can include cognitive processes such as worrying, ruminating, obsessing, and overanalyzing. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p21]
- Away moves are things that negatively impact relationships, impair health and well-being, or inhibit growth in the long term. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p21]
