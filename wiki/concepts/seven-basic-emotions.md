# Seven Basic Emotions

## Summary

Jaak Panksepp's taxonomy identifies seven basic emotional systems that can be reliably reproduced not only across individuals but across all mammals and some other vertebrates. These are LUST, SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, PANIC/GRIEF, CARE, and PLAY. All seven can be evoked by stimulation of the same brain structures and chemicals across species, indicating these emotions are approximately 200 million years old, having evolved before mammals separated from birds.

## Key facts

- Panksepp identified seven basic emotional systems: LUST, SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, PANIC/GRIEF, CARE, and PLAY. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p100]
- The seven basic emotions can be reliably reproduced in all primates and all mammals by stimulation of exactly the same brain structures and chemicals. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p99]
- Mammals separated from birds about 200 million years ago; that is how old these emotions are. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p99]
- The basic emotions provide the fundamental ingredients of the entire human emotional repertoire—all our myriad joys and sorrows appear to be outputs of these seven systems. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p100]
- SEEKING generates exploratory 'foraging' behaviour, accompanied by a conscious feeling of expectancy, interest, curiosity, enthusiasm or optimism. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p102]
- SEEKING is the brain's 'default' emotion—when we are not in the grip of one of the other affects, our consciousness tends towards generalised interest in the world. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p103]
- RAGE is triggered by anything that gets between us and whatever could otherwise meet our current needs. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p103]
- FEAR is mediated by the amygdala, which also mediates RAGE—the contextual factors separating fight from flight are encoded there. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p105]
- Separation anxiety (PANIC/GRIEF) emerges initially in infancy when instinctual attachment to caregivers develops. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p108]
- CARE (maternal instinct) exists in all of us but not to the same degree, mediated by chemicals found at higher levels in females: oestrogen, prolactin, progesterone and oxytocin. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p110]
- PLAY is a biological drive—juvenile mammals engage in vigorous rough-and-tumble play, and if deprived of their quota, they try to make it up the next day. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p111]
- PLAY evolved to promote viable social formations; it is a major vehicle for developing empathy. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p112]

## Inferences

- Inference: The seven basic emotions blend with each other and with higher cognitive processes to produce the full range of human emotional experience [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p100]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: It is uncertain whether LUST should be classified as a 'bodily' or an 'emotional' affect [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p101]
