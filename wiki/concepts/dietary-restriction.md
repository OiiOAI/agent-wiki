# Dietary Restriction

## Summary

Dietary restriction (DR) involves eating less of specific food components rather than reducing total calories. It is the most common strategy for reducing energy intake and includes approaches like low-carbohydrate, low-fat, or eliminating specific food categories.

## Key facts

- Dietary restriction only works if the eliminated food is both plentiful and significant enough that cutting it creates a caloric deficit. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p319]
- A major risk with DR is that one can still become overnourished if not deliberate—for example, cutting carbohydrates but overdoing Wagyu steaks and bacon. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p326]
- Different people respond very differently to the same dietary restriction; some lose tremendous weight on low-carb diets while others gain weight. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p326]
- The art of dietary restriction is finding the best mix of macronutrients for the individual patient in a way they can sustain. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p328]
