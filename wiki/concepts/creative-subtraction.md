# Creative Subtraction

## Summary

Creativity is not just about adding things but choosing what to leave out. Limitations can lead to freedom—constraints force creative solutions. The most interesting work often comes from what artists choose not to show.

## Key facts

- In the age of information overload, those who get ahead will be the ones who figure out what to leave out. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p95]
- Dr. Seuss wrote The Cat in the Hat with 236 words, and then won a bet to write Green Eggs and Ham with only 50 different words, creating a bestselling children's book. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p95]
- Saul Steinberg said, 'What we respond to in any work of art is the artist's struggle against his or her limitations.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p96]
