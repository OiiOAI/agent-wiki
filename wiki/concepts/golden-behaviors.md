# Golden Behaviors

## Summary

Golden Behaviors are the optimal matches identified during Behavior Matching—behaviors that are effective in realizing an aspiration, that a person wants to do, and that a person can do. Fogg identifies three criteria for Golden Behaviors: high impact on the aspiration, existing motivation to do the behavior, and sufficient ability to perform the behavior. These behaviors represent the sweet spot where motivation and ability naturally converge, eliminating the need for artificial motivation boosters.
