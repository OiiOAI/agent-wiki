# Monitoring and Self-Control

## Summary

Monitoring is a crucial step in self-control that includes tracking one's own behavior, weight, food intake, and progress toward goals. Research shows that frequent monitoring consistently improves outcomes: people who weighed themselves daily were more successful at maintaining weight loss than those who weighed weekly. Food diaries doubled weight loss compared to other techniques. Prisoners gain weight because they lack belts and tight-fitting clothes that provide feedback about body changes. The bones left on a plate at sports bars served as monitoring cues that reduced chicken wing consumption.

- People who weighed themselves daily were much more successful at preventing weight regain than those who weighed weekly. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p211]
