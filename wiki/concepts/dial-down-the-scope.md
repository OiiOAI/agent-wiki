# Dial Down the Scope

## Summary

Dial Down the Scope is a technique for overcoming project paralysis by reducing the scope of a project to its smallest viable form. Borrowed from software development, it involves identifying the least important features and dropping or postponing them to enable shipping something small and concrete while protecting the tentative edges of work.

## Key facts

- The technique recognizes that not all parts of a project are equally important. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p165]
- Features most likely to be dialed down are those most difficult or expensive to build, with most uncertainty or risk, or not central to the purpose. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p164]
- Cut content can be saved in a Second Brain as potential material for future projects. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p165]
