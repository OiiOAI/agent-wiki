# Sharing Work Publicly

## Summary

The formula for getting discovered is doing good work and sharing it with people. The Internet has made sharing simple. Opening up your process helps you learn, and sharing without expecting return leads to unexpected rewards.

## Key facts

- The formula Kleon offers is two steps: do good work and share it with people. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p57]
- The secret of the Internet: Step 1, wonder at something; Step 2, invite others to wonder with you. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p58]
- Bob Ross taught people how to paint by giving his secrets away; Martha Stewart teaches people to make their lives awesome by sharing her methods. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p58]
- Howard Aiken said, 'Don't worry about people stealing your ideas. If your ideas are any good, you'll have to ram them down people's throats.'. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p62]
