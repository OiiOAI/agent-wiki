# Inner Body

## Summary

The inner body is an invisible energy field that animates the physical body and serves as the primary gateway into Being (the Unmanifested). Unlike the visible outer body, the inner body is timeless and formless, and can be felt as a subtle energy presence from within. Practicing awareness of the inner body—directing attention into the body and feeling its living energy rather than thinking about it—reclaims consciousness from the mind and deepens spiritual realization.

## Key facts

- The inner body is described as an invisible, formless energy field that gives life to the physical body and serves as the doorway into Being. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p109]
- Practicing inner-body awareness requires feeling the body from within rather than thinking about it, focusing attention on subtle sensations like tingling or aliveness in hands, feet, and other areas. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p109]
- The inner body does not change with time—when one is eighty, the energy field will feel the same as when twenty. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p118]
- Chi is the inner energy field of the body and serves as the bridge between the outer form and the Unmanifested Source. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p124]

## Inferences

- Inference: Tolle implies that regular practice of inner-body awareness can slow physical aging because it reduces psychological burden and increases consciousness in the body.
- Inference: The inner body lies at the threshold between form identity (name and form) and essence identity (true nature), making it a transitional portal for spiritual awakening.
