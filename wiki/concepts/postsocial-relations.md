# Postsocial Relations

## Summary

Postsocial relations describe human ties triangulated with object relations, no longer based on face-to-face interaction. Postsocial systems may arise around Internet-enabled relatedness. Examples include virtual organizations, ATM banking, online shopping, and Internet communities where technology replaces human interaction.
