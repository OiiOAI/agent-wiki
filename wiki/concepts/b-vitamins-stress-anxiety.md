# B vitamins for stress and anxiety

## Summary

There is substantial evidence showing that B vitamins are an effective treatment for stress and anxiety in the general population. Research conducted in multiple countries including South Africa, the United Kingdom, and Australia has demonstrated that B vitamins assist with reduction of general stress, with observable effects within a short period of time. A combined vitamin/mineral approach likely has even larger effects for those with significant anxiety symptoms.

## Key facts

- Over the last two decades, there has been a considerable body of research in many different countries, including South Africa, the United Kingdom, and Australia, that clearly shows the importance of additional B vitamins as a way to improve resilience in the face of stress.
