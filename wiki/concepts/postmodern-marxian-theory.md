# Postmodern Marxian Theory

## Summary

Postmodern Marxian Theory represents a fusion of Marxist ideas with postmodernist perspectives, particularly influenced by structuralism and poststructuralism. Key figures include Laclau and Mouffe (hegemony and radical democracy) and Harvey (time-space compression). These theories tend to subordinate material interests and class analysis to questions of discourse, language, and diverse forms of oppression.

## Key facts

- Laclau and Mouffe's Hegemony and Socialist Strategy detaches ideology from its material base and dissolves the social into ideology or discourse. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p213]
- Laclau and Mouffe displace the proletariat from its privileged position at the center of Marxian theory, replacing the universal discourse of the proletariat with 'a polyphony of voices, each constructing its own irreducible discursive identity.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p214]
- Harvey argues that postmodernism is not discontinuous with modernism—both are reflections of the same underlying capitalist dynamic, with Fordism and post-Fordism coexisting depending on profitability. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p215]
- Harvey's central concept of time-space compression holds that capitalism has always involved 'annihilation of space through time,' with postmodernism representing an intensified phase of this compression. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p215]
- Radical democracy proposed by Laclau and Mouffe seeks to create a new hegemony by articulating diverse democratic struggles—antiracist, antisexist, anticapitalist, and antiexploitation—under a broad umbrella without any one struggle being waged at the expense of others. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p214]

## Inferences

- Inference: By replacing the proletariat with diverse oppressed groups and material interests with discourse, postmodern Marxism fundamentally restructures the political horizon of Marxism from revolutionary class struggle to a coalition of identity-based movements.
