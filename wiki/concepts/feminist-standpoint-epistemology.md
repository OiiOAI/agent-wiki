# Feminist Standpoint Epistemology

## Summary

Feminist standpoint epistemology, as articulated by Patricia Hill Collins and others, holds that all knowledge is created from the standpoint of embodied actors situated in groups that are differentially located in social structure. It posits that knowledge is always partial and interested, never total and objective; it is produced in and varies among groups; and it is always affected by power relations. This understanding has been named 'feminist standpoint epistemology' by Harding (1986).
