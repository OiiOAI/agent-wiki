# Alexithymia

## Summary

Alexithymia is a subclinical phenomenon involving difficulty identifying and describing feelings, distinguishing feelings from bodily sensations of emotional arousal, and is estimated to be present in about 10% of the general population and approximately 50% of high-functioning patients with autism or Asperger syndrome. Neuroimaging studies show that alexithymia predicts reduced activation in anterior insula during interoceptive awareness of emotions.

## Key facts

- Alexithymia is defined as difficulty identifying and describing feelings, and in distinguishing feelings from the bodily sensations of emotional arousal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p251]
- Alexithymia is estimated to be present in about 10% of the general population. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p251]
- Alexithymia was observed in about 50% of high-functioning patients with autism or Asperger syndrome. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p251]
- Silani et al. (2008) found that the degree of severity in alexithymia correlated with less activation in anterior insula when subjects judged how they felt about emotionally arousing pictures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p251]
- Lack of activation in insular cortices during interoceptive awareness of emotions was predicted entirely by the degree of alexithymia, not by AS diagnosis alone. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p251]
- Alexithymia is a difficulty with labeling emotions and relies on vague, black-and-white labels like 'fine' or 'stressed'. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p71]
- Trouble labeling emotions is associated with poor mental health, dissatisfaction in jobs and relationships, and physical symptoms like headaches and backaches. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p72]
- People who cannot clearly express their feelings sometimes express anger instead, sometimes violently. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p72]
- Learning to label emotions with more nuanced vocabulary can be absolutely transformative. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p72]
- Alexithymia refers to difficulty recognizing and expressing emotions, characterized by an extremely limited emotion vocabulary. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p101]
- Studies examining alexithymic individuals' brains found they had less gray matter than non-alexithymic people in areas of the anterior cingulate cortex associated with language processing. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p101]

## Inferences

- Inference: dividual differences in alexithymia correlate negatively with empathic personality disposition, supporting the prediction that deficits in understanding one's own emotions result in empathy deficits.
