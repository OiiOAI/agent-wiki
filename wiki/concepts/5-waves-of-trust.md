# 5 Waves of Trust

## Summary

The 5 Waves of Trust is Covey's model illustrating how trust flows from the inside out through five levels of increasing scope: Self Trust (individual credibility), Relationship Trust (interpersonal behavior), Organizational Trust (structural alignment), Market Trust (brand reputation), and Societal Trust (social contribution). The model emphasizes that trust-building must begin with the individual and expand outward.

## Key facts

- The First Wave—Self Trust—is about personal credibility based on character and competence, establishing the foundation for all other trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p61]
- The Second Wave—Relationship Trust—focuses on consistent behavior and the 13 Behaviors common to high-trust leaders. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p62]
- The Third Wave—Organizational Trust—applies the principle of alignment to create structures, systems, and symbols of organizational trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p62]
- The Fourth Wave—Market Trust—applies the principle of reputation to company and personal brands in the marketplace. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p62]
- The Fifth Wave—Societal Trust—applies the principle of contribution to create value for society at large. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p63]

## Inferences

- Inference: The wave metaphor suggests that trust issues at individual levels ripple outward and become magnified at broader levels.
