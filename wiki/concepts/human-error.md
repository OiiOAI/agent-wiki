# Human error

## Summary

Human error is defined as the failure to perform an intended action that was correct given the circumstances. Research has categorized errors into three types: slips, violations, and mistakes. Slips occur when a person correctly assesses the situation but makes a wrong movement; violations are purposeful nonstandard actions; and mistakes result from problems with the environment or individual factors such as inadequate training, memory lapse, or failure to focus attention. Human error has been recognized as the leading causal factor in aviation accidents for over 20 years.
