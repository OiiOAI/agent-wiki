# Antifragility

## Summary

Antifragility is the power of hearts that get tougher from heartbreak, minds that get wiser from wrong turns, and children who run and fall yet spring back each morning. The Operator without PTSD exemplified antifragility, gaining perspective, confidence, and purpose from combat trauma. The secret involves two components: optimism (discovering positive twists in the past) and "Planner Not the Plan" (branching the future through adaptability).

## Key facts

- Antifragility is "the power of hearts that get tougher from heartbreak, loving harder with experience". [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p121]
- The Operator without PTSD experienced no flashbacks, didn't get anxious in traffic, didn't have nightmares, didn't medicate, and was healthier after deployment than before. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p121]
- The first secret of antifragility is discovering positive plot twists in one's own life story, which creates wonder and integrates the past. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p125]
- The second secret is "Planner Not the Plan," which creates positive twists in the future through adaptability and flexibility. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p136]
- Optimism is defined as "This can succeed" rather than "This will succeed," because can survives failure while will shatters. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p129]
