# Capture (CODE Step 1)

## Summary

Capture is the first step of the CODE method, involving the practice of keeping only what resonates — ideas that move the capturer on an intuitive level. The author emphasizes the importance of acting as a curator, filtering the information stream and selecting only noteworthy ideas and insights for preservation in a trusted digital location.

## Key facts

- The author recommends four capture criteria: Does it inspire me? Is it useful? Is it personal? Is it surprising?. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p60-63]
- Claude Shannon defined 'information' as that which surprises you — ideas that conflict with existing understanding and make the brain pay attention. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p62]
- Neuroscientific research shows that 'emotions organize — rather than disrupt — rational thinking,' and resonance is the intuition telling us something is noteworthy. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p63]
- The author recommends capturing no more than 10 percent of the original source, as any more becomes difficult to wade through in the future. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p64]
- Taylor Swift captures lyrics during any activity — doing dishes, in interviews, whenever inspiration strikes — because waiting risks losing the excitement of the idea. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p51]

## Inferences

- Inference: Capturing what resonates engages the intuitive mind, which learns and responds before the conscious mind can analyze why something is important.
