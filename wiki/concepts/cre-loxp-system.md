# Cre/loxP system for gene knockout

## Summary

The Cre/loxP system is a method for achieving cell type-specific gene knockout using site-specific recombinase technology. A gene of interest is flanked by loxP genetic elements ('floxed'), creating a construct that is not deleted in the absence of Cre recombinase. When crossed with mice expressing Cre recombinase under a cell-type-specific promoter, the floxed gene is deleted only in cells where Cre is expressed.

## Key facts

- In the Cre/loxP system, a gene is flanked by loxP genetic elements ('floxed'), creating a construct that is not deleted in the absence of Cre recombinase. - When floxed mice are crossed with mice expressing Cre recombinase under a cell-type-specific promoter (e.g., CaMKIIa for forebrain neurons), the floxed gene is deleted only in cells where Cre is active. - This system was used to selectively delete the NR1 subunit of the NMDA receptor in CA1 pyramidal neurons, demonstrating that NR1 is required for both LTP and spatial memory.
