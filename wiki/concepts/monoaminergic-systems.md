# Monoaminergic Systems

## Summary

Monoaminergic systems are modulatory neural pathways in the brain stem that use monoamine neurotransmitters (serotonin, norepinephrine, dopamine, histamine) to regulate sensory, motor, autonomic, and behavioral functions throughout the nervous system. They have diffuse projections and widespread influence on arousal, attention, reward, and homeostatic regulation.

## Key facts

- Monoaminergic systems use decarboxylated derivatives of amino acids (tyrosine, tryptophan, histidine) as neurotransmitters. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1046]
- Noradrenergic neurons (A1-A7 groups) are located in the medulla and pons, with the locus ceruleus (A6) providing the major input to the dorsal horn for pain modulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1046]
- Dopaminergic neurons (A8-A17 groups) in the midbrain (substantia nigra, ventral tegmental area) modulate motor systems and reward pathways. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1046]
- Serotonergic neurons (B1-B9 groups) are associated with midline raphe nuclei in the pons and medulla. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1047]
- Monoaminergic neurons fire spontaneously in a highly regular pattern when isolated from synaptic input, with action potentials followed by slow membrane depolarization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1048]
- Dopaminergic neurons provide a reward-prediction error signal, increasing activity when rewards are unexpectedly given or when conditioned stimuli predict rewards. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1052]
- Locus ceruleus neurons exhibit phasic firing during focused attention and tonic firing during search for new behavioral goals. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1052]

## Inferences

- Inference: The monoaminergic systems modulate virtually all higher brain functions because of their diffuse projection patterns and multiplicity of receptor types.
- Inference: Serotonergic neurons in the medulla function as central CO2 chemoreceptors, explaining the link between serotonin defects and sudden infant death syndrome (SIDS).
