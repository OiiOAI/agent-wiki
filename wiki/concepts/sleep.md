# Sleep

## Summary

Sleep is a non-negotiable biological necessity that plays critical roles in metabolic health, cardiovascular function, and cognitive preservation. Adults require approximately 7.5 to 8.5 hours of sleep per night. Poor sleep is associated with insulin resistance, cardiovascular disease, and increased Alzheimer disease risk. Sleep occurs in distinct stages including deep NREM and REM sleep, each serving specific physiological functions.

## Key facts

- Humans require approximately 7.5 to 8.5 hours of sleep per night, a requirement that appears to be hard-wired based on cave studies. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p361]
- Sleep deprivation creates a state functionally equivalent to being legally drunk, with impaired judgment and reaction time. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p358]
- Sleep deprivation causes profound insulin resistance; after just 4.5 hours of sleep per night for four days, healthy young subjects showed approximately 50% reduction in glucose disposal capacity. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p364]
- Multiple studies show sleep deprivation increases insulin resistance by up to one-third. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p364]
- Short sleep (less than 6 hours) is associated with 17% higher hypertension risk, 16% higher cardiovascular disease risk, 26% higher coronary heart disease risk, and 38% higher obesity risk. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p365]
- Sleeping less than 6 hours per night was associated with about 20% higher heart attack risk in Mendelian randomization studies. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p366]
- Deep NREM sleep activates the brain's waste disposal system, flushing out amyloid-beta and tau proteins linked to neurodegeneration. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p369]
- People who sleep less than 7 hours per night over decades have more amyloid-beta and tau buildup in their brains than those who sleep 7+ hours. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p370]
- Treating sleep disturbance may delay the age of onset into mild cognitive impairment by about 11 years. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p370]
- Poor sleep suppresses leptin (satiety hormone) while increasing ghrelin (hunger hormone), leading to approximately 300 extra calories consumed the following day. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p365]
- Our highest priority is to protect our ability to prioritize. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p96]
- Sleep will enhance ability to explore, make connections, and do less but better throughout waking hours. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p96]
- Pulling an all-nighter induces an impairment equivalent to a blood alcohol level of 0.1%. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p92]
- A study showed that twice the number of people who slept for eight hours solved a problem compared to the sleep-deprived group. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p93]
- Jeff Bezos says: 'I'm more alert and I think more clearly. I just feel so much better all day long if I've had eight hours.'. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p94]

## Inferences
