# The Art of Appreciation

## Summary

The art of appreciation involves noticing and savoring pleasant experiences and sensations, cultivating wonder, and making the most of everyday moments. Key practices include fully tasting food, noticing weather and nature, fully engaging in physical contact, looking at loved ones with fresh eyes, and appreciating being alive. The principle 'enjoy but don't cling' means appreciating pleasant feelings when they visit without trying to hold on to them.

## Key facts

- Life gives most to those who make the most of what life gives. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p228]
- The more importance we place on having pleasant feelings, the more we will struggle against uncomfortable ones. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p228]
- Treat pleasant feelings the same as difficult ones: acknowledge them, allow them, and let them freely come and stay and go. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p228]
- As you open your eyes and notice things you have previously taken for granted, you will notice more opportunities, be more stimulated and interested, and find more contentment. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p228]
