# Wedge System

## Summary

The Wedge is a system for gradually upgrading lifestyle as income increases, preventing Parkinson's Law from consuming all gains. Coined by Brian Tracy, it involves saving 50% of any income increase while living on the other 50%, allowing wealth to accumulate faster toward financial freedom.

## Key facts

- Michalowicz credits Brian Tracy with originally coining the term 'Wedge' in entrepreneurial circles. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p193]
- The Wedge system sets aside 50% of every income increase as savings to prevent lifestyle expansion per Parkinson's Law. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p193]
- Example: If take-home increases from $100,000 to $135,000, the Wedge saves $17,500 (50% of $35,000 increase) plus additional profit-first savings on the remaining increase. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p193]
