# Gumption

## Summary

Gumption is the psychic gasoline that drives the motorcycle maintenance process and Quality perception. The term, Scottish in origin, describes the state of being filled with Quality awareness. A person filled with gumption actively engages with reality rather than dissipating energy on stale opinions. In motorcycle maintenance, an adequate supply of gumption is the first and most important tool—without it, no repair is possible. The Greeks called this state 'enthousiasmos,' meaning literally 'filled with theos' (God/Quality).
