# Awe as Self-Deconstruction

## Summary

Awe is described as the feeling of being in the presence of something vastly greater than yourself. It helps create distance from the self, achieving some benefits similar to meditation. Awe can be experienced when hearing ocean waves, gazing at stars, walking under storm clouds, hiking deep into uncharted territory, or taking part in spiritual ceremonies. Barrett describes how a symphony of crickets entering her affective niche helped her feel comforted and sleep better. People who report feeling awe more frequently also have the lowest levels of proinflammatory cytokines that cause inflammation.

## Key facts

- Awe is the feeling of being in the presence of something vastly greater than yourself, helping you get some distance from your self. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p214]

- Barrett developed an awe-inspired concept of being enveloped within nature, which helps change her body budget. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p214]
