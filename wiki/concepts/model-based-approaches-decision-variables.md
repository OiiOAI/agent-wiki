# Model-based approaches to decision variables

## Summary

Model-based approaches construct explicit mathematical models of the decision-making process to estimate decision variables. These approaches progress through stages: choosing modeling style, defining model structure, fitting parameters, validating behavioral predictions, and localizing neural correlates.

## Key facts

- Model-based approaches construct explicit mathematical models of the decision process to estimate decision variables. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p467]
- Mechanistic models explicitly detail how internal variables are updated and used to render choices on each trial. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p468]
