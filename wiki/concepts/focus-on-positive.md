# Focus on Positive

## Summary

Focus on Positive (equivalent to lovingkindness or metta meditation) is a technique involving two components: (1) visualizing a person or being and wishing them well through mental statements like 'I wish you happiness, health, peace,' and (2) experiencing the positive emotional sensations this generates through the body. The book presents scientific evidence that this practice reduces stress, improves immune function, increases vagal tone, and builds personal resources including mindfulness, purpose, and social support. The technique is framed as counteracting negativity bias—the evolutionary tendency to attend to threats rather than benefits. Practical guidance includes starting with non-threatening subjects (babies, puppies, fictional characters) and building up to oneself or difficult people.

## Key facts

- Focus on Positive involves visualizing someone happy and wishing them well with mental statements like 'I wish you be happy'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p188]
- The practice reduces chronic stress by increasing respiratory sinus arrhythmia and decreasing immune stress responses. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p185]
- FOP boosts vagal tone (parasympathetic nervous system function), a key health and wellbeing indicator. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p186]
- Negativity bias is described as the evolutionary tendency to attend to threats more than benefits, making positive practice initially challenging. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p190]
- The book includes a guided FOP practice lasting 3-5 minutes per round with variations for self-wish and conflict persons. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p192-195]
