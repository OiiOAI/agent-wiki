# Acellular Carbohydrates

## Summary

Acellular carbohydrates are sugars and starches that have been removed from the cells they were contained in, such as in fruit juice versus whole fruit, or powdered versus whole grains. Processing food makes it more toxic to the system and research in mice showed that powdered food (versus whole) caused more weight gain even with identical nutritional content.

## Key facts

- Acellular carbohydrate refers to sugar and starch that has been removed from the cells they were contained in, like whole fruit vs. fruit juice, or sprouted bread compared to pulverized and powdered 'whole-wheat' bread. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p140]
- In a study, mice fed the exact same amounts of the exact same food just powdered vs. whole showed that the powdered group gained the most weight. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p140]
- Processing food—carbs, fat, whatever—makes it instantly more toxic to your system. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p140]
