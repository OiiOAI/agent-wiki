# Condensation (Verdichtung) in Jokes

## Summary

Freud identifies condensation as a primary joke technique, occurring with or without substitute formation. Condensation leads to abbreviation and creates substitute formations, notably composite words, sharing identical characteristics with the dream-work of condensation.

## Key facts

- Condensation creates abbreviation while leaving behind in the wording a second trace—the formation of a substitute. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2677]
- Freud uses the term 'reduction' to describe the process of undoing the peculiar process of condensation to discover the original complete meaning. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2673]
- Condensation in jokes shows the greatest similarity to condensation in dream-work, which also leads to abbreviation and creates substitute formations. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2677]
- Condensation with slight modification occurs when the substitute for what is suppressed is not a composite structure but some other alteration of form. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2674]

## Inferences

- Inference: The far-reaching analogy between joke technique and dream-work suggests a common psychical process underlying both, raising the expectation that comparison between jokes and dreams may help illuminate jokes.
