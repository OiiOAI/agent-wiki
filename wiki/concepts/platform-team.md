# Platform team

## Summary

A platform team provides internal services to enable stream-aligned teams to deliver work with substantial autonomy. The stream-aligned team maintains full ownership of building, running, and fixing their application in production. The platform team reduces cognitive load by off-loading lower-level detailed knowledge such as provisioning, monitoring, or deployment. A good platform is built for developers and treats services as a product, following the thinnest viable platform (TVP) principle.

## Key facts

- A platform team enables stream-aligned teams to deliver work with substantial autonomy by providing internal services that reduce cognitive load. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- A digital platform is defined as a foundation of self-service APIs, tools, services, knowledge and support arranged as a compelling internal product. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- Platform teams should treat the services they offer as products that are reliable, usable, and fit for purpose, regardless of whether consumed internally or externally. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- The thinnest viable platform (TVP) principle suggests aiming to keep the platform small while ensuring it helps accelerate and simplify software delivery. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- A platform team should use strong collaboration with stream-aligned teams to understand their needs and rely on fast prototyping techniques. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- Infrastructure teams can be converted to platform teams by adopting product management techniques and treating infrastructure as a self-service product. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]

## Inferences

- Inference: Platform teams themselves may be composed of other fundamental team topologies including stream-aligned, enabling, complicated-subsystem, and lower-level platform teams—creating 'inner topologies' or nested team structures.
