# Memories of the future

## Summary

Damasio introduces the concept of 'memories of the future' to describe the memorized creations of anticipated outcomes, new plans, and new goals that emerged with the development of enhanced memory for past events and anticipation of future ones. These capacities—the ability to memorize categories of objects and events, to manipulate memorized representations and fashion new creations through novel combinations, and to memorize these new creations—enabled the cultural development of social strategies to cope with suffering.

## Key facts

- The most immediately useful variety of creations consisted of imagined scenarios, the anticipation of outcomes of actions, the formulation of future plans, and the design of new goals that can enhance survival. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p282]
- Damasio calls these memorized creations 'memories of the future.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p282]
- David Ingvar also used the term 'memories of the future' with precisely the same meaning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p310]

## Inferences

- Inference: Damasio proposes that enhanced knowledge of the experienced past and the anticipated future was the reason why social strategies had to be created to cope with suffering. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p283]
