# Next Actions List

## Summary

Next Actions lists organize actionable reminders by the context required to complete them, such as Calls, At Computer, Errands, At Office, At Home, Agendas, and Read/Review. This organization enables efficient work based on available time and resources.

## Key facts

- Actions should be organized by the context required: the tool, location, or situation needed to complete them. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p198]
- Common context categories include Calls, At Computer, Errands, At Office, At Home, Anywhere, Agendas, and Read/Review. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p199]
- The Calls list should include phone numbers alongside each item to enable immediate action when a phone is available. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p199]
- Agendas are separate lists for people and meetings where multiple items need to be discussed in real-time interactions. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p202]
