# User-Centered Design

## Summary

User-Centered Design is a design philosophy and process that prioritizes the needs, goals, abilities, and cognitive processes of end users. According to the book's table of contents, Chapter 1 establishes User-Centered Design as the central topic, contrasting it with Technology-Centered Design and identifying Situation Awareness as its key objective.

## Key facts

- User-Centered Design is the focus of Chapter 1 in this book. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#L84]
- User-Centered Design is contrasted with Technology-Centered Design. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#L84-L86]
- User-centered design integrates information in ways that fit the goals, tasks, and needs of users. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p27]
- User-centered design does not mean asking users what they want and giving it to them. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p27]
- User-centered design does not mean presenting users with just the information they need at any given moment through filtering. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p28]
- User-centered design does not mean systems that make decisions for the user. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p28]
- User-centered design does not mean doing things for the user, which can lead to the user being 'out-of-the-loop'. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p29]
- The three principles of user-centered design are: organize technology around user goals/tasks/abilities; organize technology around how users process information; keep user in control and aware. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p29-31]
