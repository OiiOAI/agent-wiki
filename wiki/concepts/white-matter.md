# White Matter

## Summary

White matter consists of myelinated axon fiber tracts connecting different brain regions. Myelin's white appearance gives this tissue its name. These cables enable communication between distant brain areas.

## Key facts

- Myelin wrapping around axons is white, so fiber tract cables in the brain are referred to as white matter. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p722]
- Neurons with related functions have their cell bodies clumped together and send axons in the same direction, merging into fiber tract cables. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p722]
- White matter enables different brain regions to communicate, often connecting distant parts of the brain. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p722]
