# Inspiring Trust

## Summary

Inspiring trust refers to the leadership practice of extending trust to others in ways that bring out their best performance. The concept emphasizes that leaders who genuinely believe in people and extend meaningful trust to them enable those individuals to discover their potential and make significant contributions.

## Key facts

- The first job of a leader, at work or at home, is to inspire trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p382]
- Inspiring trust means bringing out the best in people by entrusting them with meaningful stewardships. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p382]
- When parents tell children 'I love you. I believe in you. I trust you' and give them meaningful jobs to carry out, it creates profound impact. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p382]
- Companies that choose to extend trust to their employees become great places to work. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p381]
- Trust is reciprocal—the more you trust others, the more you yourself are trusted in return. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p383]
