# Relapse Prevention

## Summary

Chapter 7 of the book addresses preventing relapses into digital dependence. It presents four key strategies: filling empty time slots, recognizing behavioral triggers, reaching out to someone, and staying accountable to others.

- The book emphasizes self-forgiveness when mistakes happen, advising readers to pick themselves up and get back on track. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p134]
- The first relapse prevention strategy is filling empty time slots with healthy non-digital habits such as preparing meals, reading, exercising, or reconnecting with friends. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p135]
- The second strategy involves taking note of behavioral triggers including financial insecurity, strained relationships, grief, depression, anxiety, feeling overwhelmed, fear, traumatic memories, isolation, loneliness, chronic pain, and uncertainty.
