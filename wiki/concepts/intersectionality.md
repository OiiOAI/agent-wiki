# Intersectionality

## Summary

Intersectionality is a theoretical framework that traces the consequences of the intersection of multiple systems of oppression—including race, gender, class, sexuality, and global location—for lived experience, group standpoints, and relations among women. Patricia Hill Collins developed this theory to understand how Black women experience oppression differently from Black men or white women. Her 'Fighting Words' (1998) continued her project of redefining social theory from the standpoint of variously situated groups.
