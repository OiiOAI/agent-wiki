# Productive Meditation

## Summary

A deep work training technique that involves focusing attention on a single well-defined professional problem while walking, jogging, or driving, strengthening distraction-resisting muscles and concentration ability.

## Key facts

- Productive meditation requires occupying your body physically while focusing your attention on a single well-defined professional problem. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p101]
- Cal Newport developed this practice during his postdoc at MIT, working out chapter outlines and solving technical problems during walks. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p101]
- The practice strengthens both distraction-resisting muscles and ability to push focus deeper on a single problem. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p102]
- Two key challenges in productive meditation are distractions and 'looping'—repeatedly rehashing what you already know instead of progressing. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p102]
- A structured approach to deep thinking involves reviewing relevant variables, defining a next-step question, and consolidating gains before moving deeper. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p103]

## Inferences

- Inference: Productive meditation takes advantage of commute and exercise time that would otherwise be wasted, turning it into productive deep work training.
