# Lifestyle

## Summary

A blanket term for the quality and types of activities, interests and people one spends the majority of their time with. Your lifestyle is a reflection of your values and self-esteem and also determines your demographics.

## Key facts

- Lifestyle is defined as a blanket term for the quality and types of activities, interests and people one spends the majority of their time with. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p267]
- Your profession, the place you live, hobbies, friends, and weekend trips are all components of your overall lifestyle. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p267]
- Your lifestyle is a reflection of your values and self-esteem and also determines your demographics. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p267]
- A lifestyle can be based upon unconfident behavior (i.e., dressing a certain way or driving a certain car in order to impress women). [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p267]
