# Neurons That Fire Together Wire Together

## Summary

'Neurons that fire together wire together' is a key law of neuroplasticity describing how simultaneous activation of neurons strengthens their connection. In the context of OCD treatment, by performing a pleasurable activity in place of a compulsion, patients form a new circuit that is gradually reinforced instead of the compulsion, leveraging this principle to build healthier neural pathways.

## Key facts

- 'Neurons that fire together wire together' is a key law of plasticity that underlies the Brain Lock treatment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p181]
- By doing something pleasurable in place of the compulsion, patients form a new circuit that is gradually reinforced instead of the compulsion. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p181]
- This new circuit can eventually compete with the older pathological one, and according to use-it-or-lose-it, the pathological networks will weaken. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p177]
