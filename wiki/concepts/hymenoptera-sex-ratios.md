# Hymenoptera Sex Ratios and Worker Control

## Summary

In hymenopteran insects (ants, bees, wasps), full sisters share 75% of their genes rather than the usual 50% because of their haplodiploid system. This genetic peculiarity led to the evolution of worker sterility and a conflict between workers (who favor a 3:1 female-biased sex ratio) and queens (who favor 1:1). Trivers and Hare's studies of ants showed that workers generally win this conflict.

## Key facts

- Hymenopteran full sisters share 75% of their genes because they inherit identical genes from their father. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p212]
- A hymenopteran female is more closely related to her full sisters than she is to her own offspring of either sex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p212]
- Workers prefer a 3:1 female to male ratio of investment; queens prefer 1:1, leading to a conflict of interests. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p213]
- Trivers and Hare studied 20 species of ants and found a close fit to the 3:1 female to male ratio predicted by worker control theory. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p214]
- In slave-making ant species, the sex ratio approaches 1:1 because slaves tend the brood while queens have practical power over sex allocation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p216]

## Inferences

- Inference: True sociality evolved at least 11 times independently in the Hymenoptera and only once in all other animals (termites), likely due to the haplodiploid relatedness peculiarity
- Inference: Worker control over sex ratios represents a conflict of interests 'won' by workers who have practical power in nurseries

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The honey bee seems to do the 'wrong' thing with a large surplus of males, though Hamilton offered a possible explanation involving attendant workers
