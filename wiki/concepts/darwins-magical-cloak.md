# Darwin's Magical Cloak

## Summary

Darwin's magical cloak refers to how Darwin's name functions as a protective cloak that wards off scientific criticism, allowing Floyd Allport and John Dewey to transmute the words of William James and Darwin himself into their opposites and shore up the classical view of emotion. Darwin's essentialist views from 'Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals' were grafted onto James's anti-essentialist ideas, creating what Barrett calls a 'Frankenstein's monster' of a theory (the James-Lange theory). Darwin's name also helped propagate the mistaken idea that the brain evolved as blobs with distinct functions, leading scientists down the fruitless path of searching for emotion blobs.
