# Dynamic Polarization

## Summary

A principle advanced by Santiago Ramón y Cajal stating that electrical signals within a nerve cell flow only in one direction: from the receiving sites of the neuron (usually dendrites and cell body) to the trigger region at the axon initial segment. From there, the action potential propagates along the entire length of the axon to its terminals. This principle is the physiological basis of the model neuron and explains how information flows through neural circuits.
