# Dream condensation

## Summary

Dream condensation (Verdichtung) is one of the two principal factors in the dream-work, referring to the process by which the multiple dream thoughts are compressed into the brief manifest content of the dream. This compression occurs through the construction of collective and composite figures, the creation of intermediate common entities, and the multiple determination of dream elements by numerous dream thoughts.

## Key facts

- Dreams are brief, meagre and laconic in comparison with the range and wealth of the dream thoughts—condensation accounts for this great disproportion between dream content and dream thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1534]
- Condensation operates through: (1) omission of most dream thoughts, (2) construction of collective and composite figures, and (3) creation of intermediate common entities that represent multiple dream thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1546]
- Elements of the dream content are 'overdetermined'—each has been represented in the dream thoughts many times over through multiple associative connections. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1537]
- Condensation also operates on words and names, creating verbal compounds and neologisms that combine elements from different dream thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1549]

## Inferences

- Inference: Condensation serves to disguise the true meaning of dreams from the conscious mind, making them more acceptable by reducing their threatening content.
