# Hysterical Symbolization

## Summary

Hysterical symbolization is the mechanism by which emotionally-coloured ideas find somatic expression through verbal figures of speech taken literally. Phrases such as 'stabbed to the heart,' 'slap in the face,' or 'not being able to take a single step forward' become physical symptoms when the hysterical patient revives the original sensations that justified these expressions.

## Key facts

- The patient had done nothing more nor less than look for a symbolic expression of her painful thoughts and found it in the intensification of her sufferings. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p135-136]
- When a girl of fifteen felt a penetrating pain in her forehead between her eyes, she had felt her grandmother's look so 'piercing' that it had gone right into her brain. - Hysteria may not be taking linguistic usage as its model at all, but both hysteria and linguistic usage alike draw their material from a common source in the expression of emotions.
