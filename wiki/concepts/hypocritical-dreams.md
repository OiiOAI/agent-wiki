# Hypocritical Dreams

## Summary

Hypocritical dreams are a class of dreams that have a particular claim to be described as 'hypocritical' and offer a hard test to the theory of wish fulfillment. They involve content that contradicts the dreamer's true feelings—depicting, for instance, reconciliation with people with whom friendly relations have long ceased. Analysis habitually reveals some occasion which might urge the dreamer to abandon the last remnant of consideration for these former friends and treat them as strangers or enemies. The dream, however, prefers to depict the opposite relationship.

## Inferences

- Inference: The 'hypocritical' aspect of these dreams lies in their depicting the opposite of what the dreamer actually feels or wants toward certain persons—the dream censors hostile impulses by presenting their opposites.
