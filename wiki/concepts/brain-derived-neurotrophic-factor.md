# Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF)

## Summary

A protein produced inside nerve cells when they are active, often described as 'Miracle-Gro' for the brain because it fertilizes brain cells to keep them functioning and growing, and spurs the growth of new neurons.

## Key facts

- BDNF is a protein produced inside nerve cells when they are active. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p222]
- It serves as Miracle-Gro for the brain, fertilizing brain cells to keep them functioning and growing. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p222]
- BDNF spurs the growth of new neurons. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p222]
