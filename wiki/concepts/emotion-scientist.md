# Emotion Scientist

## Summary

An emotion scientist is someone who applies scientific thinking to understand emotions—their own and others'. Rather than judging emotions as good or bad, an emotion scientist seeks to understand them without making value judgments. The concept involves recognizing emotions, understanding their causes and consequences, and using that knowledge to make better decisions. This approach contrasts with being an 'emotion judge,' who evaluates and passes judgment on feelings.

## Key facts

- An emotion scientist seeks to understand emotions without making value judgments or rendering opinions about whether feelings are justified or beneficial. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p58]
- An emotion judge attempts to evaluate feelings and deem them good or bad, useful or harmful, grounded in reality or imaginary. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p59]
- Carol Dweck's research shows that when people believe emotion skills can be taught, they have greater faith in their ability to change outcomes. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p59]
- An emotion scientist has five key skills: accurately recognizing emotions, understanding causes and consequences, having a refined emotion vocabulary, expressing the full range of emotions, and managing emotions.
