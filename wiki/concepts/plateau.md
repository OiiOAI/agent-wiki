# Plateau

## Summary

The plateau is the essential phase of learning where apparent progress stops but actual deep learning continues. Most of the mastery journey is spent on plateaus, making the ability to embrace them crucial for success. The plateau triggers hidden motivations and requires conscious choice to persist through.

## Key facts

- The plateau is a period during which long hours of diligent practice gain no apparent progress. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p17]
- For most people, the plateau triggers disowned emotions and flushes out hidden motivations. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p16]
- To take the master's journey, you must be willing to spend most of your time on a plateau. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p21]
- George Leonard discovered that loving the plateau was a turning point in his aikido practice after about a year and a half. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p35-36]
- Practice exists only in the present while goals exist in the future and past beyond the sensory realm. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p40]
