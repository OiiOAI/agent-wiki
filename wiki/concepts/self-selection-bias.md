# Self-Selection Bias

## Summary

Self-selection bias occurs when we notice events that confirm our existence while ignoring those that don't. If a traffic jam develops 10% of the time, you spend more than 10% of your travel time in it because you only notice when stuck. A phone survey found no household claimed to have no phone.

## Key facts

- In a telephone survey to find average phones per household, the firm was amazed that not a single household claimed to have no phone. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p112]
- If a highway has traffic jams 10% of the time, you spend a disproportionate amount of time in this state because you only notice when stuck. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p111]
- The 'miracle of existence' is a self-selection bias: only someone alive can make such an observation. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p112]
