# Imperial and US Customary measurement systems

## Summary

The historical and still-used measurement systems of Britain and the United States. The British imperial system and US Customary system share common origins but have diverged significantly, particularly in units for volume (gallon, pint, fluid ounce).

## Key facts

- The British imperial gallon equals 4.54609 liters while the US gallon equals 3.7854 liters. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- One UK pint equals 0.5683 liters while 1 US pint equals 0.4732 liters. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- The UK hundredweight equals 112 lb while the US short hundredweight equals 100 lb. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- The avoirdupois system is the primary weight system in both Britain and the US, with 1 pound equal to 16 ounces and 7000 grains. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]

## Inferences

- Inference: The divergence between imperial and US systems occurred gradually after American independence, leading to different definitions for many units.
