# Required Income for Allocation (RIFA)

## Summary

RIFA (Required Income for Allocation) is the amount of money a business owner needs to deposit by the tenth and twenty-fifth of each month to sustain a healthy business, pay their desired salary, and take deserved profits. It replaces the traditional 'monthly nut' concept with a profit-first focus.

## Key facts

- RIFA replaces the 'monthly nut' concept, which Michalowicz describes as 'a horrible distraction' that focuses on expenses rather than profit. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p174]
- The RIFA formula is: (Monthly Required Personal Income / 2) / Owner's Comp Percentage = Required Income for Allocation. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p175]
- For example, if an owner needs $5,000/month and the Owner's Comp allocation is 31%, the RIFA is $16,129 (5000/0.31/2). [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p175]
- To annualize RIFA, multiply the biweekly amount by 24 (the number of allocations per year). [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p175]
- With a RIFA of $16,000, the annual business income requirement would be $384,000. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p175]
