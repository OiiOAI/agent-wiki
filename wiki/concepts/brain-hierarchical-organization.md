# Brain Hierarchical Organization

## Summary

The brain operates hierarchically with lower functions (brain stem, hypothalamus) controlling essential vital processes while higher cortical levels provide flexible control. Lower functions are more reflexive, stereotyped, and closed; higher functions are more open and variable.

## Key facts

- Higher brain functions can operate only on the basis of lower functions, but lower functions can often operate independently of higher ones. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- Brain stem damage is generally more debilitating than cortical damage because lower functions are essential for survival. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- Lower brain functions resemble read-only memory (ROM) operating systems essential for coherent function, while higher functions resemble random-access memory (RAM) space for complex computations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- The hypothalamus and basal ganglia allow animals to generate behavioral spontaneity and complex behaviors that help adjust activity in relation to bodily needs. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
