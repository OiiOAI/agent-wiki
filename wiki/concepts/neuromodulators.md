# Neuromodulators

## Summary

Neuromodulators are brain chemicals that influence how neurons process information without simply increasing or decreasing activity. They fundamentally change how neurons process information and enable long-lasting changes in neural connections during learning.

## Key facts

- Neuromodulators are chemicals that influence the functioning of neurons but do not simply increase or decrease neural activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p96]
- Neuromodulators have complex effects that fundamentally change how neurons process information, and they promote plasticity by enabling significant, long-lasting changes in connections between neurons in cell assemblies activated during learning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p96]
- Some neuromodulators are described as being like hot sauce on tacos—they change the flavor, add heat, and cause us to pay attention. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p96]
