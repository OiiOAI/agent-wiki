# Homosexual Etiology of Paranoia

## Summary

Freud's theory that paranoia (in males) is invariably rooted in a defense against homosexual wishful phantasies. The unconscious homosexual impulse seeks expression, and the ego defends against it through repression, which manifests as paranoia—transforming love into persecution, jealousy, or erotomania.

## Key facts

- Freud, Jung, and Ferenczi investigated numerous paranoid cases and found that a defense against homosexual wish was clearly recognizable at the center of the conflict in all of them. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3769]
- Freud identified four principal forms of paranoia as contradictions of the proposition 'I (a man) love him (a man)': delusions of persecution (contradicting the verb as hate), erotomania (contradicting the object as female), delusions of jealousy (contradicting the subject), and megalomania (rejecting the proposition entirely as self-love). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3773]
- In delusions of persecution, the proposition 'I love him' is transformed by projection into 'He persecutes me, which justifies my hating him.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3773]
- Freud argued that social humiliations prominent in paranoia's causation operate through their homosexual emotional components. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3770]
- Schreber's case showed that even someone with no manifest homosexual signs could harbor powerful unconscious homosexual impulses that expressed themselves in paranoia. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3770]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud's equation of paranoia's mechanism with the transformation of unconscious homosexual love into its opposite (persecution) via projection established a template for subsequent psychoanalytic understanding of paranoid disorders.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether this theory applies to all paranoia or only to certain types, as Freud acknowledged confirmation requires investigation of many instances.
