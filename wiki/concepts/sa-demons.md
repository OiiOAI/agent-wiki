# SA Demons

## Summary

SA demons are factors that work to undermine situation awareness in many systems and environments. The book identifies eight major SA demons: (1) Attentional Tunneling, (2) Requisite Memory Trap, (3) Workload, Anxiety, Fatigue, and Other Stressors (WAFOS), (4) Data Overload, (5) Misplaced Salience, (6) Complexity Creep, (7) Errant Mental Models, and (8) Out-of-the-Loop Syndrome. Understanding these demons provides the foundation for SA-oriented design.

## Key facts

- The book identifies eight major SA demons: attentional tunneling, requisite memory trap, workload/stressors, data overload, misplaced salience, complexity creep, errant mental models, and out-of-the-loop syndrome. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p51]
- The requisite memory trap refers to failures resulting from insufficient space in working memory and natural decay of information. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p53]
- Complexity creep undermines ability to correctly interpret information and project future states (Levels 2 and 3 SA). [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p58]
- Errant mental models can be very insidious; a study found 66% of the time controllers did not properly detect cues that a wrong mental model had been activated. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p60]
- Out-of-the-loop syndrome occurs when automation takes people out of the loop, leading to poor SA on both how the automation is performing and the state of elements it controls. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p60]
