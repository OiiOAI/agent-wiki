# Accusation Audit

## Summary

An Accusation Audit is a preparation technique where you list every terrible accusation your counterpart could level against you before they can say it. By naming and voicing these accusations yourself, you defuse them before they take root and often prompt the other party to claim the opposite is true.

## Key facts

- The Accusation Audit is the first step of going right after negativity, listing every terrible thing the counterpart could say about you. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p60]
- Voss demonstrates the technique on the first day of negotiating class by prefacing the role-play with 'I want to tell you in advance... it's going to be horrible,' labeling audience fears before they manifest. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p60]
- A student named Anna used the Accusation Audit to prepare for a difficult negotiation with ABC Corp, listing accusations like 'You are the typical prime contractor trying to force out the small guy,' which helped her save her company $1 million. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p61]
- The technique works because accusations often sound exaggerated when said aloud, and speaking them encourages the other person to claim the opposite is true. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p67]
