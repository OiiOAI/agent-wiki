# Ki

## Summary

Ki is the fundamental energy of the universe that connects all things and undergirds all creative action. Known as ch'i in Chinese, pneuma in Greek, prana in Sanskrit, and 'the Force' in Star Wars, ki is central to Eastern martial arts. While difficult to measure scientifically and skeptics attribute its powers to suggestion, practitioners find a strong correlation between perception of personal ki and effectiveness of techniques. George Leonard presents ki as a useful concept for gaining a sensation of increased power along with relaxation.
