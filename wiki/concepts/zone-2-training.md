# Zone 2 Training

## Summary

Zone 2 training is a specific intensity level of aerobic exercise characterized by maintaining a pace at which lactate accumulation remains stable. It is defined as the maximum level of effort that can be sustained without accumulating lactate, typically achieved at 70-85% of maximum heart rate. This training modality focuses on building mitochondrial health and metabolic flexibility, enabling the body to efficiently burn both glucose and fatty acids as fuel.

## Key facts

- Zone 2 is defined as the maximum level of effort that can be maintained without accumulating lactate, using primarily fat as fuel. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p248]
- Zone 2 corresponds to approximately 70-85% of maximum heart rate. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p248]
- The primary fuel source during Zone 2 training is fat, which requires healthy mitochondria for metabolism. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p247]
- Professional cyclists spend approximately 80% of their training time (30-35 hours per week) in Zone 2. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p247]
- Zone 2 training stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis, creating new and more efficient mitochondria. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p251]
- Zone 2 activates non-insulin-mediated glucose uptake (NIMGU), helping manage blood glucose independent of insulin signaling. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p251]
- The recommended minimum for most people is about 3-4 hours per week of Zone 2 training (four 45-minute sessions). [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p252]
- Progress in Zone 2 can be tracked by measuring average wattage output divided by body weight (watts/kg). [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p252]

## Inferences

- Inference: Zone 2 training may be more important for nonathletes than for professional athletes because it builds foundational endurance and plays a crucial role in preventing chronic disease by improving mitochondrial health.
- Inference: The lactate threshold that defines Zone 2 can be measured using a handheld lactate monitor, with target levels between 1.7 and 2.0 millimoles.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact Zone 2 heart rate percentage may vary significantly between individuals depending on fitness levels, making the 70-85% range somewhat broad.
