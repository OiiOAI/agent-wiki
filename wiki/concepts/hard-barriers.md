# The HARD Barriers

## Summary

The HARD barriers are the main obstacles to following through on values-based actions: Hooked (getting caught by thoughts from the reason-giving machine), Avoiding discomfort (unwillingness to open up and make room), Remoteness from values (ignoring or neglecting values leads to lack of motivation), and Doubtful goals (goals that are unrealistic, excessive, or lacking necessary resources).

## Key facts

- H: Hooked - the reason-giving machine talks us out of taking action with thoughts like 'I'll fail' or 'I'm too busy'. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p257]
- A: Avoiding discomfort - personal growth necessitates leaving the comfort zone, bringing up difficult thoughts, sensations, emotions, and urges. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p258]
- R: Remoteness from values - if we are ignoring or neglecting our values, we will lack motivation to take action. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p258]
- D: Doubtful goals - if goals are excessive, we will feel overwhelmed and probably give up or try and fail. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p258]
