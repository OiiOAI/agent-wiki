# Integrity

## Summary

Integrity is the first Core of Credibility and represents the root system of trust. It encompasses honesty, congruence (walking one's talk), humility, and courage to act according to values even when costly. Covey emphasizes that integrity is foundational to credibility but insufficient alone—competence is equally necessary.

## Key facts

- Warren Buffett stated: 'I look for three things in hiring people. The first is personal integrity, the second is intelligence, and the third is a high energy level. But, if you don't have the first, the other two will kill you.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p84]
- Oprah Winfrey defined integrity as 'doing the right thing, knowing that nobody's going to know whether you did it or not.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p85]
- Alan Greenspan stated: 'Rules cannot take the place of character.'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p86]
- Three components of integrity beyond basic honesty are congruence (being whole and seamless inside and out), humility (being more concerned about what is right than about being right), and courage (doing the right thing even when it's hard). [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p88]
- The 'Roddick Choice' refers to Andy Roddick calling a point against himself during the Italia Masters when the line judge mistakenly called the ball 'out,' demonstrating integrity even when costly. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p84]
