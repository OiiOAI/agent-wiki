# Stories We Tell Ourselves

## Summary

Men create internal narratives (stories) that justify avoiding action with women. These stories have emotions attached (fear, boredom, anger) and create resistance to change. Examples include 'I'm too good for these girls,' 'I need to get a raise first,' or 'she's stuck up.' Recognizing these stories and acting despite them is the key skill in dating.

## Key facts

- Subconscious resistance to change never fully disappears; it just changes form over time from anxiety to apathy to arrogance. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p156]
- The only important skill in dating is learning to stop buying into your own bullshit and stop believing your own stories. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p158]
- Men tell themselves stories like 'I'll start approaching after a few drinks' or 'I need something amazing to say.'. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p157]
- There's always something missing that would make action possible; this is the story we use to maintain the status quo. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p158]
