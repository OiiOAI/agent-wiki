# Subgame Perfect Equilibrium

## Summary

Subgame Perfect Equilibrium (SPE), introduced by Reinhard Selten (1975), is a refinement of Nash Equilibrium for extensive form games. A strategy profile is an SPE if it represents a Nash Equilibrium in every subgame of the original game, ensuring players have no incentive to deviate at any point during play.

## Key facts

- Kuhn (1953) proved that every finite extensive form game with perfect information has a Subgame Perfect Equilibrium. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p47]
- A strategy profile is an SPE if the relevant parts of the profile are also a Nash Equilibrium for each subgame of the original game. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p47]

## Inferences

- Inference: SPE eliminates non-credible threats by requiring equilibrium behavior in all subgames.
