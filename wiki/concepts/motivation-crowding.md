# Motivation Crowding

## Summary

Motivation crowding occurs when monetary incentives crowd out intrinsic motivation. Bruno Frey's study of Wolfenschiessen found 50.8% supported a nuclear waste repository until a hypothetical $5,000 reward was mentioned, dropping to 24.6%. Childcare late fees increased tardiness.

## Key facts

- Bruno Frey's study found 50.8% of Wolfenschiessen residents supported a nuclear waste repository, but this dropped to 24.6% when a hypothetical $5,000 reward was mentioned. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p129]
- Many nurseries introduced late fees for picking up children after closing time, but studies show tardiness actually increased. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p129]
- Financial incentives work well in industries with generally uninspiring jobs where employees aren't proud of the products. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p130]
