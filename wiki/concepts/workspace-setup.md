# Workspace Setup

## Summary

Workspace setup involves creating a dedicated physical location to serve as the central control center for managing workflow. Essential elements include a writing surface, in-tray, file drawers, and appropriate tools.

## Key facts

- You must have a dedicated, individual, self-contained workspace at home, at work, and even in transit. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p130]
- The basics for a workspace are just a writing surface and room for an in-tray, and probably space for core digital tools. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p129]
- An ideal time frame for full implementation is two whole days, back to back. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p128]
- The workspace should be properly set up and streamlined to reduce unconscious resistance to dealing with input. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p128]
