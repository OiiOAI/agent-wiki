# Movement Planning Under Risk

## Summary

A framework developed by Trommershäuser, Maloney, and Landy that frames rapid pointing movements as economic decisions under risk, where subjects choose movement strategies to maximize expected gain while accounting for their own motor uncertainty.

## Key facts

- The framework treats movement planning as equivalent to choosing among lotteries: the choice of movement strategy fixes the probability of hitting reward and penalty regions, with each resulting in specific gains or losses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p106]
- Subjects in these tasks typically achieved above 90% efficiency (money won relative to maximum expected gain), choosing aim points that maximized expected gain from the first trial with rewards and penalties. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p100]
- In contrast to cognitive decision-making tasks, movement planners under risk successfully compensate for trial-to-trial variation in uncertainty. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p107]
- The sources of uncertainty in motor tasks are endogenous (reflecting the organism's own motor noise), while uncertainty in economic tasks is typically imposed by the experimenter. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p107]

## Inferences

- Inference: Humans are able to learn their own motor uncertainties very well and make use of this knowledge to improve performance, suggesting good estimation of endogenous uncertainty.
- Inference: The contrast between success in movement planning under risk and failure in cognitive decision making under risk may relate to differences in how probabilistic information is acquired and processed.
