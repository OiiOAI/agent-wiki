# The Utopia Syndrome

## Summary

A pathology arising from the belief that ultimate, all-embracing solutions can be found and actualized. The syndrome manifests in three forms: (1) Introjective—personal inadequacy for failing to reach utopian goals, leading to depression or withdrawal; (2) Procrastination—focusing on the journey rather than arrival, avoiding the confrontation with whether goals are attainable; (3) Projective—moral righteousness and missionary responsibility to change the world, often leading to blaming others or society for failures. The core problem is that utopian premises are treated as more real than reality itself.
