# The Fragilista

## Summary

A person who makes others engage in policies and actions where benefits are small and visible while side effects are potentially severe and invisible. The fragilista defaults to thinking that what they do not see does not exist, mistakes the unknown for the nonexistent, and falls for the Soviet-Harvard delusion of overestimating the reach of scientific knowledge. Types include medical, economic, policy, psychiatric, financial, and military fragilistas.

## Key facts

- The fragilista is one who makes you engage in policies and actions where benefits are small and visible and side effects are potentially severe and invisible. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p27]
- The fragilista defaults to thinking that what he does not see is not there, or what he does not understand does not exist—at the core, he mistakes the unknown for the nonexistent. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p26]
- The fragilista falls for the Soviet-Harvard delusion, the unscientific overestimation of the reach of scientific knowledge, becoming a naive rationalist who believes reasons behind things are automatically accessible to him. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p27]
- Types of fragilistas include: medical fragilista (overintervening and denying the body's natural healing), policy fragilista (mistaking economy for a washing machine), psychiatric fragilista (medicating children), financial fragilista (using flawed risk models), and military fragilista (disturbing complex systems). [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p27]
- Political discourse lacks the concept of antifragility; politicians aim at 'resilience' and 'solidity' rather than antifragility, stifling mechanisms of growth and evolution. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p27]

## Inferences

- Inference: The fragilista represents the intellectual and policy failure mode that antifragility theory is designed to counteract—interventions based on incomplete understanding of complex systems.
