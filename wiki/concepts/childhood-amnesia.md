# Childhood Amnesia

## Summary

Childhood amnesia is the phenomenon of very few memories retained from the first years of life. It attracted attention from neuroscientists and psychologists, especially after Sigmund Freud published studies on subconscious repression during childhood.

## Key facts

- The very small number of memories kept from the first years of life is a phenomenon known as childhood amnesia. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p174]
