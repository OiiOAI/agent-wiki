# Needful Freedom

## Summary

Needful freedom is Jonas's term for the dialectical relation of the organism to its material substance. The organism coincides with its material collection at any instant but is not bound to any particular collection; it 'rides their change like the crest of a wave' and is bound only to the form of collection. This creates a paradox: the organism must change constantly (necessity) yet achieves a kind of independence from material constitution (freedom). Without metabolism there is no emancipation of dynamic selfhood from mere material persistence. The organism's condition is thus one of 'needful freedom'—freedom coextensive with life itself.
