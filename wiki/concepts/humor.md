# Humor

## Summary

Freud's theory of humor as a means of obtaining pleasure in spite of distressing affects that interfere with it, acting as a substitute for the generation of these affects. Humor is characterized as the highest of defensive processes, deriving pleasure from an economy in expenditure upon feeling.

## Key facts

- Humor is a means of obtaining pleasure in spite of the distressing affects that interfere with it; it acts as a substitute for the generation of these affects. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2852]
- The pleasure of humor comes about at the cost of a release of affect that does not occur; it arises from an economy in the expenditure of affect. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2852]
- Humor is the most easily satisfied among the species of the comic; it completes its course within a single person. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2853]
- Humor can be regarded as the highest of the defensive processes, scornfully refusing to withdraw the ideational content bearing the distressing affect from conscious attention as repression does, and thus surmounting the automatism of defence. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2857]
- The species of humor are extraordinarily variegated according to the nature of the emotion which is economized in favour of the humor: pity, anger, pain, tenderness, and so on. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2855]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud acknowledges uncertainty about the process by which displacement away from generating of affect takes place in the humorous person himself.
