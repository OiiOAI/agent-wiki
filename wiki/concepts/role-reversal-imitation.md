# Role-Reversal Imitation

## Summary

Role-reversal imitation is a form of imitative and cultural learning central to language acquisition. For the child to engage in role-reversal imitation, she must substitute the adult for herself as the target of the intentional act—this substitution is possible because the child can understand the whole joint attentional scene from an external perspective where she and the adult are represented in the same nonegocentric format. According to Tomasello, role-reversal imitation 'actually creates the intersubjectively understood communicative convention or symbol.'
