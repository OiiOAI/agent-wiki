# Observer Ratings of Situation Awareness

## Summary

Observer ratings involve trained observers assessing an operator's SA, typically based on overt indications such as actions and verbalizations. While observers may have more complete knowledge of reality than operators, they have limited insight into the operator's internal mental model. Observer ratings provide only a partial indicator of SA since operators hold information internally that may not be verbalized. Confederates in studies can elicit more verbalization but may alter operator attention distribution and thus SA itself.

## Key facts

- Observer ratings are appealing because trained observers typically have more information than operators about what is really happening in a given simulation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p131]
- Observers will have only limited knowledge about the operator's concept of the situation and must rely on overt indications to determine SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p131]
- Operator actions and verbalizations most likely do not provide a complete representation of an operator's SA because the operator may have a greater store of internally held information that is not verbalized. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p131]
- A variation employs a confederate acting as an associate who requests certain information from the operator to encourage more verbalization. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p131]
- These confederate elicitation efforts may augment natural verbalizations but may also alter the operator's distribution of attention and thus alter SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p131]

## Inferences

- Inference: The limitation of observer ratings to overt indicators suggests they may better capture behavioral manifestations of SA than cognitive components.
