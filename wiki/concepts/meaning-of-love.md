# Meaning of Love

## Summary

In logotherapy, love is understood as the only way to grasp another human being in the innermost core of his personality. No one can become fully aware of the very essence of another human being unless he loves him. By his love, the loving person enables the beloved person to see essential traits and features and even potentialities not yet actualized. Love is not merely an epiphenomenon of sexual drives; love is as primary a phenomenon as sex. Normally, sex is a mode of expression for love—sex is justified as soon as it is a vehicle of love.

- By his love, the loving person enables the beloved person to actualize potentialities; by making him aware of what he can be and of what he should become, he makes these potentialities come true. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p83]
