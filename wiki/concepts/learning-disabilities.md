# Learning Disabilities

## Summary

Learning disabilities are neurological disorders affecting specific mental functions such as spatial reasoning, symbol relationships, auditory memory, or motor coordination. Barbara Arrowsmith Young demonstrated that these are often milder versions of the thinking deficits seen in patients with brain injuries, and that they can be corrected through targeted brain exercises rather than merely compensated for.

## Key facts

- Barbara had areas of brilliance (auditory and visual memory in the ninety-ninth percentile) coexisting with areas of retardation due to brain asymmetry. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p37]
- She lacked spatial reasoning, needed for mental mapping, organizing space, and movement planning. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p37-p38]
- She had kinesthetic problems, unable to be aware of where her body or limbs were in space, and couldn't tell how far her arms or legs had moved on her left side. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p38]
- She had a visual disability with such a narrow span of vision she could take in only a few letters at a time. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p38]
- She had trouble understanding the relationships between symbols, affecting grammar, math concepts, logic, and cause and effect. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p38]
- She couldn't distinguish left from right, reversed letters (b, d, q, p), read 'was' as 'saw,' and wrote from right to left (mirror writing). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p39]
- She could memorize math procedures but couldn't understand math concepts or why five times five equals twenty-five. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p39]
- Her emotional development suffered because she couldn't pick up inconsistencies when listening to smooth talkers and couldn't have more than one relationship at a time. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p40]
- The term 'learning disabilities' wouldn't be widely used for another two decades after Barbara's diagnosis in the 1950s. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p40]
- Barbara found that learning disorders were often milder versions of the thinking deficits seen in Luria's patients with brain wounds. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p46]
- We all have some weak brain functions, and neuroplasticity-based techniques have great potential to help almost everyone. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p49]
