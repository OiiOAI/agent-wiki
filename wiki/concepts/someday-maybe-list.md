# Someday/Maybe List

## Summary

The Someday/Maybe list holds items with no current action that might be relevant later, such as future goals, potential projects, or deferred ideas. It provides a way to incubate possibilities without cluttering active project lists.

## Key facts

- Someday/Maybe is one of seven primary categories for managing commitments in the GTD system. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p190]
- Items for Someday/Maybe include things like ideas for future projects, vacations to take, or classes to attend when there is currently no action. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p177]
