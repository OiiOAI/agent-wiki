# Dorsal and Ventral Streams

## Summary

The dorsal and ventral streams are two major parallel processing pathways in the primate visual system. The ventral stream ( WHAT pathway) processes object identity, form, color, and texture information and projects to temporal association cortex. The dorsal stream ( WHERE/HOW pathway) processes spatial location, motion, and guidance of actions and projects to parietal association cortex. Both streams originate from primary visual cortex and operate in parallel.

## Key facts

- The ventral visual stream terminates in the inferotemporal lobe and carries information allowing distinction of visual properties of objects such as shape, color, and texture. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p469]
- The dorsal stream terminates in the posterior parietal lobe and carries information for locating objects in space and acting upon them. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p469]
- The dorsal stream has two branches: a dorso-dorsal branch involved in the control of movements (damage produces optic ataxia), and a ventro-dorsal branch involved in visuomotor transformations for object interaction. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p469]
- Patients with ventral visual stream damage can act on objects but cannot consciously recognize or describe them, while patients with dorsal stream damage cannot reach accurately despite knowing object locations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p470]
- The inferior temporal cortex contains neurons selective for individual faces and complex visual shapes, while the posterior parietal cortex contains neurons encoding spatial locations and object-directed actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p469]

## Inferences

- Inference: The separation of 'what' and 'where/how' processing reflects fundamental computational differences in how visual information is used: for recognition versus for action.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact boundary between dorso-dorsal and ventro-dorsal stream functions remains debated in the literature.
