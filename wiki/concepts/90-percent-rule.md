# 90 Percent Rule

## Summary

The 90 Percent Rule is a decision-making tool where you score options between 0 and 100 on your most important criterion. If an option rates below 90 percent, you automatically reject it. This forces you to apply highly selective criteria and avoid settling for 'almost good enough.'

## Key facts

- When evaluating an option, rate it between 0 and 100. If you rate it any lower than 90 percent, automatically change the rating to 0 and reject it. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p99]
- Applying the 90 Percent Rule means that if something is just or almost good enough (a 7 or 8), the answer should be no. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p99]
- Derek Sivers describes saying 'HELL YEAH! Or No'—if the answer isn't a definite yes, it should be a no. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p97]
