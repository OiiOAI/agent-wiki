# Loss of Ipseity

## Summary

Loss of ipseity refers to the erosion of the pre-reflective, grounding sense of self that normally anchors consciousness to lived experience. With this loss, the self must be constructed 'after the fact' from the products of observation, and its very existence comes into doubt. This creates a reflexivity where attention focused on the self and its body makes parts of the self appear alien.

## Key facts

- The self has to be constructed 'after the fact' from the products of observation, and its very existence comes into doubt. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p541]
- There is a loss of the pre-reflective sense of the body as something living and lived, and a loss of immediate physical and emotional experience which grounds us in the world. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p541]
- Emotions lose their normal directedness towards action, towards other beings, arising from a personal past and directed towards a personal future. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p541]
- The condition is associated with a veering between omnipotence and impotence—either there is no self, or all that the observing eye sees is part of the self. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p541]
