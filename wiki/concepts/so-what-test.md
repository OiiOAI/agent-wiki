# The So What? Test

## Summary

A quality control check for deciding what to share online. Before posting, run everything through the 'So What?' test by asking if the content is helpful, entertaining, or something you'd be comfortable with your boss or mother seeing. If unsure, let content sit for 24 hours before deciding.

## Key facts

- Kleon's college professor wrote 'SO WHAT?' on the chalkboard and said to ask that question every time turning in a piece of writing. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p59]
- Kleon advises running everything through The 'So What?' Test: 'Is this helpful? Is it entertaining? Is it something I'd be comfortable with my boss or my mother seeing?'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p61]
- If unsure about sharing something, let it sit for 24 hours, put it in a drawer, and look at it with fresh eyes the next day. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p61]
- Kleon quotes Lauren Cerand: 'Post as though everyone who can read it has the power to fire you.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p59]
- Kevin Kelly is quoted: 'The Internet is a copy machine. Once anything that can be copied is brought into contact with the Internet, it will be copied.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p59]
