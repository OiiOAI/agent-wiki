# Nietzsche Quote on Why

## Summary

Frankl cites Nietzsche's famous statement 'He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how' as a motto holding true for any psychotherapy. In Nazi concentration camps, those who knew a task was waiting for them to fulfill were most apt to survive. The same conclusion was reached by authors writing about Japanese, North Korean and North Vietnamese prisoner-of-war camps. Frankl's own desire to rewrite his confiscated manuscript helped him survive the rigors of the camps—while ill with typhus, he jotted down notes on scraps of paper to reconstruct his work.

## Key facts

- Nietzsche said: 'He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how,' which Frankl sees as a motto holding true for any psychotherapy. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p79]
- In the Nazi concentration camps, those who knew that there was a task waiting for them to fulfill were most apt to survive. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p79]
