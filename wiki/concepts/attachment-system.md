# Attachment System

## Summary

The attachment system is a biological mechanism in the brain responsible for creating and regulating our connection with attachment figures including parents, children, and romantic partners. It consists of emotions and behaviors that ensure we remain safe by staying close to loved ones. The protest behavior triggered by the system (frantic calls, trying to make partner feel jealous) is designed to maintain proximity to attachment figures.

## Key facts

- The attachment system is a biological mechanism specifically responsible for creating and regulating our connection with attachment figures. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p19]
- The attachment system consists of emotions and behaviors that ensure we remain safe and protected by staying close to our loved ones. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p19]
- Protest behavior includes frantic calls, searching behavior, or crying until contact is reestablished with the attachment figure. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p19]
- The attachment system treats proximity to an attachment figure as an absolute necessity for survival. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p19]
- When two people form an intimate relationship, they regulate each other's psychological and emotional wellbeing through the attachment system. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p31]
