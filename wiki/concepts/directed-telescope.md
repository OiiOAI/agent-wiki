# Directed Telescope

## Summary

Martin van Creveld's concept describing how observation changes the thing observed in command and control contexts. This idea from physics (Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle) was applied by Boyd to organizational theory, where it is known as Goodhart's Law: once a measure becomes a goal or target, it ceases to be a valid measure. Boyd recommended van Creveld's directed telescope concept for addressing issues in organic design for command and control.
