# Collaboration Mode

## Summary

Collaboration is one of three team interaction modes defined in Team Topologies. It is characterized as a powerful driver for innovation but carries the trade-off of potentially reducing flow.

## Key facts

- Collaboration is identified as one of three team interaction modes in Team Topologies. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2192]
- Collaboration can be a powerful driver for innovation. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2193]
- Collaboration can also reduce flow. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2193]
- Collaboration mode means explicitly working together on defined areas with shared responsibility. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2350-L2360]
- Collaboration mode is suitable where a high degree of adaptability or discovery is needed, particularly when exploring new technologies or techniques. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2360-L2370]
- Constraint: A team should use collaboration mode with at most one other team at a time. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2410-L2420]
- The cognitive load of ongoing collaboration is much higher than working in a team's natural area. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2390-L2400]
- Collaboration mode tends to blend responsibilities and architecture together due to Conway's law. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2400-L2410]
- Typical uses: Stream-aligned teams working with complicated-subsystem teams; stream-aligned teams working with platform teams; complicated-subsystem teams working with platform teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L2420-L2430]

## Inferences

- Inference: Teams should use collaboration mode when innovation is the primary goal, understanding that flow may be sacrificed.
- Inference: Organizations should use collaboration mode when rapid discovery outweighs the costs of higher cognitive load and boundary blurring.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The optimal duration for collaboration periods is not precisely specified in the text.
