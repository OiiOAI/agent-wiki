# Show Loyalty

## Summary

Behavior #5 in Covey's framework. Showing loyalty means giving credit to others and speaking about people as though they were present. It is based on principles of integrity, loyalty, gratitude, and recognition.

## Key facts

- Southwest Airlines' Colleen Barrett demonstrated loyalty by publicly stating they would defend and support employees when customers were wrong. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p207]
- Jim Collins' 'window and mirror' metaphor: when things go well, look through the window and give credit to others; when things don't go well, look in the mirror and take responsibility. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p208]
