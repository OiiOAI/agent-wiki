# System Ucs

## Summary

System Ucs (Unconscious) is the psychical system in Freud's topographical model that contains unconscious drive representatives. Its characteristics include: exemption from mutual contradiction (conflicting impulses coexist without canceling); primary process with mobile cathexes capable of displacement and condensation; timelessness; and replacement of external with psychical reality. The system Ucs contains the nucleus of drive representatives seeking discharge, which operate according to the pleasure principle. It maintains relations with the preconscious system including cooperation, and its derivatives (including highly organized phantasies) may influence conscious life.

## Key facts

- The system Ucs contains drive representatives seeking to discharge their cathexis, which are coordinate, exist side by side without mutual influence, and are exempt from mutual contradiction. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4558]
- In the system Ucs, displacement allows one idea to surrender its whole quota of cathexis to another, and condensation allows one idea to appropriate the whole cathexis of several others—these are the distinguishing marks of primary process. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4559]
- Processes of the system Ucs pay no regard to reality; they are subject to the pleasure principle alone. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4559]
- The system Ucs is overlaid by the Pcs which has taken over access to consciousness and to motility, yet the Ucs remains alive and capable of development, maintaining cooperative relations with the Pcs. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4562]
