# Level 2 SA: Comprehension

## Summary

Level 2 Situation Awareness encompasses how people combine, interpret, store, and retain information beyond mere perception. It involves the integration of multiple pieces of information and a determination of their relevance to the person's goals. This level is analogous to having high reading comprehension as compared to just reading words. A person with Level 2 SA has derived operationally relevant meaning and significance from Level 1 data. Twenty percent of SA errors involve problems with Level 2 SA. This level sets SA apart from earlier psychological research and places it in the realm of ecological realism by addressing the problem of meaning.
