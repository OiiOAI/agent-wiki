# Integrated Sociological Paradigm

## Summary

The integrated sociological paradigm is George Ritzer's metatheoretical framework for analyzing social reality through four major levels of social analysis: macro-objective, macro-subjective, micro-objective, and micro-subjective. Built upon Gurvitch's insights, the paradigm crosscuts micro-macro and objective-subjective continua. Ritzer argues that sociologists must focus on the dialectical interrelationship among these four levels rather than privileging any single level.

## Key facts

- Ritzer's integrated paradigm begins with the micro-macro continuum ranging from individual thought and action to world-systems. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p406]
- To this continuum is added an objective-subjective continuum ranging from material phenomena to nonmaterial phenomena. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p406]
- The result is four major levels of social analysis, and Ritzer argues sociologists must focus on the dialectical interrelationship among these levels. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p406]
- The macro-objective level involves large-scale material realities such as society, bureaucracy, and technology. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p406]
- Ritzer's thinking on the integrated paradigm was shaped by the work of Georges Gurvitch, who operated with the belief that the social world can be studied in terms of horizontal (micro-macro) and vertical (depth) levels. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p404]
- The key to an integrated paradigm is the notion of levels of social analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p567]
- The four levels of the integrated paradigm are: macro-objective (e.g., bureaucracy), macro-subjective (e.g., values), micro-objective (e.g., patterns of interaction), and micro-subjective (e.g., reality construction). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p569-570]
- The three extant paradigms cut across the levels of social reality horizontally, while an integrated paradigm cuts across vertically. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p570]
- The integrated paradigm does not supersede existing paradigms because while each existing paradigm deals with a given level in great detail, the integrated paradigm deals with all levels but less intensively. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p570]
- The social world is best viewed as an enormous variety of social phenomena involved in continuing interaction and change. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p567]
