# Revealed Preference

## Summary

Revealed preference is an economic concept holding that an individual's preferences can be inferred from their observable purchasing behavior. Amartya Sen critiqued this concept on grounds that behavior alone cannot distinguish between self-interested motives and moral or social compulsions.

## Key facts

- Amartya Sen argued that revealed preference is ambiguous because behavior does not tell an outside observer whether the real motive is selfish interest or moral compulsion. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p366]
- Sen further argued that users of revealed preference make a hidden assumption that preferences are self-interested, whereas people also have a social side and typically act from mixed motives. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p366]
