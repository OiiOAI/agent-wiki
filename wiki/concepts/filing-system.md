# Filing System

## Summary

A general-reference filing system provides organized storage for non-actionable materials kept for future use. Effective filing must be fast, functional, and located immediately at hand.

## Key facts

- Filing must take less than one minute to pick something up, decide it needs no action, and finish storing it in a trusted system. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p140]
- General-reference files should be organized alphabetically in a single system, not by project or area of focus. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p141]
- Physical file drawers should be kept less than three-quarters full to avoid unconsciously resisting filing. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p143]
- Files should be purged at least once a year to keep them from becoming stale and seeming like a black hole. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p144]
