# Steps-Per-Day Inventory

## Summary

The Steps-Per-Day Inventory is an assessment from the book that involves tracking steps over three days and calculating the average daily count. The authors recommend 8,000 to 10,000 steps per day minimum, with 12,000 or more being ideal. The test uses the formula that 1 mile equals approximately 2,000 steps.

## Key facts

- The assessment requires tracking steps for three successive days. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1110-L1113]
- Add three-day totals together and divide by 3 to get average steps per day. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1113-L1115]
- The recommended target is 8,000 to 10,000 steps daily, with 12,000 or more as ideal. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1098-L1099]
- For calculation purposes, 1 mile equals approximately 2,000 steps. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1117-L1119]
- All steps count including both intentional walks and incidental non-exercise activity. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1080-L1083]
- Consistency is just as important as quantity; you cannot bank movement. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1121-L1124]
