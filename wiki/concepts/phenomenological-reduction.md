# Phenomenological Reduction

## Summary

The phenomenological reduction is the philosophical procedure for investigating the correlational structure of subjectivity and world-appearance. It involves 'leading back' (reducere) thought from unreflective immersion in the world to how the world appears to us. The main methodical step is the epoché—suspending or bracketing both the natural positing attitude and theoretical beliefs about objective reality. Practically, the epoché is a trainable skill of suspending inattentive immersion and redirecting attention to how things appear. This enables investigation of experience in an open, nondogmatic manner without being prejudiced by unexamined views.
