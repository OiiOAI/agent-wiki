# Suffering

## Summary

Damasio distinguishes between the sensory perception of pain (the 'pain image') and suffering, which is the feeling arising from the emotional reaction to that perception. He argues that the innocent processing of body change rapidly triggers a wave of additional body-state changes that further deviate the overall body state from the base range, producing an emotion from which the unpleasant feeling of suffering is formed. This distinction is illustrated through observations of patients who underwent prefrontal leucotomy for intractable pain and could still perceive pain but were no longer distressed by it.

## Key facts

- The innocent processing of body change rapidly triggers a wave of additional body-state changes which further deviate the overall body state from the base range, and it is from the subsequent body-state deviations that the unpleasant feeling of suffering will be formed. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p284]
- The organism came to life with a preorganized mechanism to give us the experiences of pain and of pleasure, though culture and individual history may change the threshold at which it begins to be triggered, its intensity, or provide means to dampen it. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p284]
- Suffering offers us the best protection for survival, since it increases the probability that individuals will heed pain signals and act to avert their source or correct their consequences. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p284]
- After prefrontal leucotomy, patients reported that 'the pains are the same' but they 'feel fine now,' indicating the operation abolished the emotional reaction (suffering) while leaving the pain image intact. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p286]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Damasio notes that how to deal with the suffering that arises from personal and social conflicts outside the medical realm is 'a different and entirely unresolved matter.' [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p286]
