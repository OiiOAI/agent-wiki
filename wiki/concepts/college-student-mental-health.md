# College Student Mental Health

## Summary

College students face significant mental health challenges, with over one-third reporting symptoms consistent with mental health disorders. Anxiety and depression are top reasons for seeking counseling. Research supports SEL interventions that reduce stress, increase self-compassion, and improve school engagement.

## Key facts

- Between 2009 and 2015, the number of students visiting campus counseling centers increased by more than 30 percent, with anxiety and depression as top reasons. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p189]
- A 2018 World Health Organization study of nearly 14,000 students from 19 colleges in 8 countries found more than one in three reported symptoms consistent with at least one mental health disorder. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p189]
- The 2018 Healthy Minds Study found 23 percent of college students are taking psychiatric medication, up from 18 percent in 2016. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p189]
- In a Yale study with over 200 undergraduates, students reported less stress and burnout, higher self-compassion and acceptance of emotions, and greater school engagement after a six-week SEL intervention. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p191]
- Students who tracked moods using the Mood Meter app up to six times daily for four weeks showed significant reductions in depression, stress, and anxiety compared to control groups. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p191]
