# Not-Invented-Here Syndrome

## Summary

Not-invented-here (NIH) syndrome causes us to fall in love with our own ideas and rate home-grown solutions as more important than outside ideas, even when objectively inferior. Dan Ariely's blog experiment showed people rated their own responses as more important even when virtually identical to others'.

## Key facts

- Dan Ariely found that people rated their own responses as more important and applicable than others' even when using an identical list of 50 words for answers. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p166]
- The British-designed modern traffic roundabout took 30 years to reach the U.S. and continental Europe despite being an obvious traffic decongestant. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p166]
