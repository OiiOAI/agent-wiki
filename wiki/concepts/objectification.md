# Objectification

## Summary

The decision to view women and social interactions as impersonal processes and objects rather than people and emotional activities. Objectification causes long-term emotional damage and can lead to depression and even lower levels of self-esteem.

## Key facts

- Objectification is defined as the decision to view women and social interactions as impersonal processes and objects rather than people and emotional activities. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Seeing women as numbers, subjects to be studied, games to be won, etc., are all common ways which men objectify their sexual and emotional lives. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Emotions are ignored and discouraged from being expressed. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Objectification causes long-term emotional damage and can lead to depression and even lower levels of self-esteem. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Narcissism and Performance behaviors both encourage objectification in order to achieve short-term success. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
