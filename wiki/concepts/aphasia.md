# Aphasia

## Summary

A language disorder most often occurring when brain tissue is destroyed by stroke—occlusion or rupture of a blood vessel to a portion of a cerebral hemisphere. Important types include Broca's aphasia (expressive, difficulty speaking but understanding intact), Wernicke's aphasia (receptive, difficulty understanding but can form words), and conduction aphasia (difficulty repeating phrases due to disconnection between language areas).
