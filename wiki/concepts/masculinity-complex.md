# masculinity complex

## Summary

The masculinity complex in women refers to a branch that develops from penis envy, characterized by the wish to possess a penis and become like a man. This complex may hinder the normal development toward femininity if not overcome.

## Key facts

- Here what has been named the masculinity complex of women branches off, which may put great difficulties in the way of their regular development towards femininity. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6146]
- The hope of some day obtaining a penis in spite of everything and so of becoming like a man may persist to an incredibly late age. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6146]
- The term 'masculinity complex' seems to have been introduced by Van Ophuijsen (1917). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6146]
