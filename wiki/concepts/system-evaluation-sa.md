# System Evaluation for Situation Awareness

## Summary

System evaluation in SA-oriented design involves both indirect measures (process measures and behavioral/performance measures) and direct measures (subjective and objective metrics). Rapid prototyping and simulations for experimental testing form an essential backbone. SAGAT provides detailed diagnostic information about specific design features and differences in SA effectiveness under varying conditions. The SA requirements analysis from the design process provides the basis for SAGAT query development.

## Key facts

- SA-oriented design principles provide an incomplete recipe, requiring decisions on how to present information to operators. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- Rapid prototyping and simulations for experimentally testing display concepts form an essential backbone for human interface design. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- Testing provides benefits by identifying unexpected interactions among design features and unintended effects on operators early in the design cycle. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- Both indirect (process measures and behavioral/performance measures) and direct (subjective and objective metrics) approaches are used for SA measurement. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- Detailed evaluations should be conducted with representative users and realistic scenarios including both normal operations and failures such as automation failures, missing information, and overload situations. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- While performance measures should almost always be included, significant advantages in sensitivity and diagnosticity are gained by including direct measures of SA. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- SAGAT data provide detailed diagnostic information with respect to specific features of the design. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
- SAGAT can highlight differences in effectiveness of design to support SA under varying conditions such as differing workload levels or scenario events. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p316]
