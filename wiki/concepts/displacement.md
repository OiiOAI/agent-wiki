# Displacement (Dream)

## Summary

Displacement (Verschiebung) is one of the four mechanisms of the dream-work, entirely attributable to the dream censorship. It manifests in two ways: by replacing latent elements with remote allusions rather than components of themselves, and by shifting psychical accent from important elements to unimportant ones, making the dream appear strangely centered.

## Key facts

- Displacement manifests in two ways: replacement of a latent element with something more remote (an allusion), and shifting psychical accent from an important element to an unimportant one. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4944]
- Displacement is entirely the work of the dream censorship. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4944]
- Displacement makes it impossible to find the path back from the allusion to the genuine thing. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4945]
- Displacement of accent is unprecedented as a method of expressing thoughts in normal waking life. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4945]
