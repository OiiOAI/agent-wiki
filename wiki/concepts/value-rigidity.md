# Value Rigidity

## Summary

Value rigidity is the most widespread and pernicious internal gumption trap, defined as an inability to revalue what one sees because of commitment to previous values. In motorcycle maintenance, one must rediscover what one does as one goes, and rigid values make this impossible. When trapped in value rigidity, a person cannot see new facts even when they are staring in the face. The cure involves deliberately slowing down and re-examining previously seen ground, waiting for 'nibbles' from little facts.
