# Drop Anchor

## Summary

Drop anchor is an ACT technique for holding steady during emotional storms. Following the ACE formula (Acknowledge, Connect, Engage), it helps gain control over physical actions and reduce the influence of difficult thoughts and feelings. The technique does not aim to eliminate painful experiences but to prevent them from sweeping one away and disrupting effective action.

## Key facts

- Drop anchor follows the ACE formula: A = Acknowledge thoughts and feelings, C = Connect with body, E = Engage in what you're doing. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p67]
- The technique is designed to hold oneself steady during emotional storms so the storm does not sweep them away. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p65]
- Drop anchor does not aim to get rid of thoughts and feelings, just as a real anchor does not control the weather. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p67]
- The technique aims to gain more control over physical actions so a person can act more effectively when difficult thoughts and feelings are present. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p72]
- Drop anchor interrupts worrying, rumination, obsessing, and away moves. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p73]
