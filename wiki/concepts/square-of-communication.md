# Square of Communication

## Summary

The Square of Communication is a framework for effective communication with four corners (right person, right time, right place, right agenda) surrounding a centre. There are four ways two people can communicate: Human to Human (ideal), Chimp to Chimp (nightmare), Human to Chimp, or Chimp to Human. The framework emphasises the importance of 'packaging' communication through body language, intonation, word choice, and ambience. Key techniques include being assertive rather than aggressive, establishing clear agendas, and managing both your own Chimp and the other person's Chimp during conversations.

- The three parts to being assertive are: say what you don't want using 'I', say how it makes you feel, and say what you do want using 'I'. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p113]
- The four elements of communication packaging are body language, intonation, use of words, and ambience. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p119]

- The word 'should' implies a standard or expectation and is associated with feelings of failure, blame, guilt, threat, and inadequacy; 'could' is associated with opportunity, choice, possibility, and hope.
