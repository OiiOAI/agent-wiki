# Ontological Asymmetry

## Summary

The primacy of the right hemisphere's interaction with whatever exists. The right hemisphere is the primary mediator of experience, from which the conceptualised, re-presented world of the left hemisphere derives and on which it depends. McGilchrist argues that this ontological asymmetry means the right hemisphere should have the balance of power, but three other asymmetries mean the balance is 'dangerously skewed' towards the left.

## Key facts

- The right hemisphere is the primary mediator of experience, from which the conceptualised, re-presented world of the left hemisphere derives and on which it depends. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p320]
- Ontological asymmetry means that while experience is enriched by sending right-hemisphere products to the left for 'unpacking', there is no necessity for that process, whereas returning left-hemisphere products to the right is literally vital.
