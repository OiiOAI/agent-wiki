# Neofunctionalism

## Summary

Neofunctionalism is a self-critical strand of functional theory that emerged in the mid-1980s, seeking to broaden structural functionalism's intellectual scope while retaining its theoretical core. It represents an effort to overcome criticisms of structural functionalism, including its anti-individualism, antagonism to change, conservatism, idealism, and antiempirical bias.

## Key facts

- Jeffrey Alexander and Paul Colomy defined neofunctionalism as 'a self-critical strand of functional theory that seeks to broaden functionalism's intellectual scope while retaining its theoretical core.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p151]
- Neofunctionalism operates with a descriptive model of society that sees society as composed of elements which, in interaction with one another, form a pattern. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Neofunctionalism devotes roughly equal attention to action and order, avoiding the tendency of structural functionalism to focus almost exclusively on macro-level sources of order. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Neofunctionalism retains the structural-functional interest in integration, not as an accomplished fact but rather as a social possibility. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Neofunctionalism focuses on social change in the processes of differentiation within the social, cultural, and personality systems, leading to 'individuation and institutional strains.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p154]
- Alexander and Colomy sought to integrate insights from Marx, Durkheim, exchange theory, symbolic interactionism, pragmatism, and phenomenology into neofunctionalism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p154]
- J. H. Turner and Maryanski (1988) challenged neofunctionalism by arguing that it is not really functional in its orientation since it has abandoned many basic tenets of structural functionalism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p151]
