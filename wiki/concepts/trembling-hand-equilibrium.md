# Trembling Hand Equilibrium

## Summary

Trembling Hand Equilibrium, introduced by Reinhard Selten, requires that a player's strategy be optimal even when other players might make small mistakes in their strategies. It eliminates equilibria that rely on non-credible threats.

## Key facts

- A mixed strategy n-tuply is trembling hand perfect if there is a sequence of mixed strategies that converges to the equilibrium strategy and each player's Nash Equilibrium strategy is the best response to all other players' strategies chosen from any n-tuple in the sequence. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p51]
- In Selten's Horse, (C, C, prop(L) = 1/4) is trembling hand perfect. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p50]

## Inferences

- Inference: Trembling hand perfection formalizes the idea that equilibria should be robust to slight errors in implementation.
