# Chauffeur Knowledge

## Summary

Chauffeur Knowledge refers to superficial knowledge acquired by people who have learned to put on a show—perhaps having a great voice or good presentation skills—without having genuinely mastered the underlying subject. Charlie Munger and Warren Buffett popularized this concept.

## Key facts

- There are two types of knowledge: real knowledge from people who have committed time and effort to understanding a topic, and chauffeur knowledge from those who have learned to put on a show. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p46]
- News anchors are actors; everyone knows it, yet it continues to astound how much respect these script readers enjoy despite moderating panels about topics they barely fathom. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p46]
- True experts recognize the limits of what they know and what they don't know; if they find themselves outside their circle of competence, they keep quiet or say 'I don't know' unapologetically. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p47]
