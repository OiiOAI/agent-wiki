# The Road of Trials

## Summary

Once having traversed the threshold, the hero moves in a dream landscape of fluid, ambiguous forms where he must survive a succession of trials. This phase has produced a world literature of miraculous tests and ordeals. The hero is covertly aided by the advice and amulets of the supernatural helper, or discovers the benign power supporting him throughout his passage.
