# Creativity Through Restrictions

## Summary

Counterintuitively, restrictions and standardization foster creativity rather than limit it. The slip-box uses a single plain-text format and one-idea-per-note rule, which enables notes to be freely recombined. Clear structure allows exploration of internal possibilities; without structure, we cannot differentiate, compare, or experiment with ideas.

## Key facts

- Thinking and creativity can flourish under restricted conditions. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p121]
- Standardization makes it possible for the technical side of note-taking to become automatic, freeing mental resources for actual thinking. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p120]
- The restriction to one idea per note is the precondition to recombine them freely later. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p120]
- Without structure, we cannot differentiate, compare, or experiment with ideas. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p122]
- The scientific revolution started with standardization and controlling experiments, making them comparable and repeatable. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p121]
