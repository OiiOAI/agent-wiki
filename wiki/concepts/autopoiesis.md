# Autopoiesis

## Summary

Autopoiesis (pronounced 'auto-poi-ee-sis') is a concept developed by Chilean biologist Humberto Maturana, referring to systems capable of maintaining and reproducing themselves, including producing the physical components needed for their continued existence. Although autopoiesis is first and foremost a theory of the cell, there are intriguing links between cellular autopoiesis and the free energy principle. Both suggest a strong continuity between 'life' and 'mind', implying that there is more to mind—and to consciousness—than simply what a system 'does'.
