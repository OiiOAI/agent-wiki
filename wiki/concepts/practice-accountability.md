# Practice Accountability

## Summary

Behavior #10 in Covey's framework. Practicing accountability means holding yourself accountable first, then holding others accountable second. Based on principles of accountability, responsibility, stewardship, and ownership. Leaders who generate trust do both in that sequence.

## Key facts

- Steve Young took responsibility for an interception even though the receiver ran the wrong route, increasing trust from coaches and teammates. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p248]
- Commander Scott Waddle took full responsibility for the USS Greeneville submarine crash that killed nine people, traveling to Japan to personally apologize to victims' families. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p249]
- Golin/Harris survey ranked 'assuming personal responsibility and accountability' as the highest factor for CEOs to keep earning trust. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p246]
