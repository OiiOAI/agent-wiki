# Pull Production

## Summary

Pull production is a lean manufacturing technique where each step in the supply chain only produces what is needed by the next step, rather than pushing products based on forecasts. The system signals demand backward through the chain. In startup contexts, pull means responding to experiments that need to be run rather than building features based on customer requests. Both pull and small batches reduce work-in-progress (WIP) inventory.

## Key facts

- In Toyota's just-in-time pull system, when a car comes in for repair and one bumper is used, this creates a 'hole' in dealer inventory that automatically signals the Toyota Parts Distribution Center to send a new bumper. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p186]
- Lean manufacturers discovered that reducing work-in-progress (WIP) inventory is where lean manufacturing gets its name; switching to pull production causes warehouses to shrink dramatically. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p186]
- In the Lean Startup, the product development process responds to pull requests in the form of experiments; it is not the customer but the hypothesis about the customer that pulls work from product development. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p187]
