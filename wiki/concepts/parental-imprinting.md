# Parental Imprinting

## Summary

Parental imprinting is an unusual form of gene regulation where humans express mRNA from only one of the two copies of a gene, either the maternal or paternal copy. This adds complexity to understanding human genetic disorders because the effect of a DNA lesion depends not only on its presence but also on which parent it was inherited from. Studies in mice suggest parental imprinting is more common in the brain than in other body tissues, which could help explain why mapping human behavioral traits is difficult.
