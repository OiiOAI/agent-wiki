# Incest Barrier

## Summary

The incest barrier is a restriction erected during childhood that excludes blood relations from permissible sexual objects. Society must defend itself against the danger that interests needed for establishing higher social units may be swallowed up by the family. The incest barrier is probably among the historical acquisitions of mankind and has become established by organic inheritance in many persons. Psychoanalytic investigation shows how intensely the individual struggles with the temptation to incest during growth and how frequently it is transgressed in phantasies and even in reality.
