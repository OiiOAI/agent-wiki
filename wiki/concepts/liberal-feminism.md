# Liberal Feminism

## Summary

Liberal feminism is the major expression of gender inequality theory, arguing that women may claim equality with men on the basis of an essential human capacity for reasoned moral agency. It holds that gender inequality results from a sexist patterning of the division of labor, and that gender equality can be achieved by transforming key institutions (law, work, family, education, media). Liberal feminism rests on the beliefs that all humans have capacities for reason, moral agency, and self-actualization; that exercise of these capacities can be secured through legal recognition of universal rights; and that gender inequalities are social constructions with no basis in nature.

## Inferences

- Inference: Liberal feminism is consistent with the dominant American ethos: its basic acceptance of democracy and capitalism, its reformist orientation, and its appeal to individualism, choice, responsibility, and equality of opportunity.
