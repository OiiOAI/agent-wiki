# Fixed Action Pattern

## Summary

Fixed Action Patterns (FAPs) are well-defined motor patterns or 'motor tapes' that when switched on produce coordinated, stereotyped movements. They include escape responses, walking, swallowing, and the prewired aspects of bird songs. FAPs reduce the immense degrees of freedom of the motor system and liberate the self from spending time and attention on every aspect of ongoing movement.

## Key facts

- FAPs are sets of well-defined motor patterns, ready-made 'motor tapes' that when switched on produce well-defined and coordinated movements. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p138]
- FAPs are generated centrally by the basal ganglia, which provide the doors that when unlocked may release into action very large functions outside of the basal ganglia. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p142]
- The intrinsic, reciprocal inhibitory activity of the basal ganglia keeps all potential FAPs from expressing themselves until properly released. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p143]
- Tourette's syndrome results from partial destruction of the basal ganglia, producing abnormal continuous liberation of particular FAPs including finger drumming and vocalizations. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p146]
- Parkinson's disease represents the loss of ability to release FAPs due to selective degeneration of the substantia nigra within the basal ganglia. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p148]
- FAPs have two components: strategy (global categorical choice) and tactics (context-dependent implementation of strategy). [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p147]

## Inferences

- Inference: FAPs represent evolutionary optimization of motor control, allowing rapid, stereotyped responses without requiring conscious deliberation for every movement.
