# Minimalist Work Apps

## Summary

The book lists several minimalist apps for work that help minimize dependence on digital technology while maintaining productivity. These include scheduling tools, freelancer management apps, meditation apps, and phone usage trackers.

## Key facts

- Acuity is described as a dynamic cloud-based appointment scheduler that integrates with many services including PayPal and other payment platforms. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p131]
- Bonsai is a freelancer app for managing transactions, integrating with tools for invoice management and timekeeping. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p131]
- Gaia and Simple Habit are meditation apps recommended for two to five minutes of guided mindfulness during busy workdays. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p131]
- Squarespace is described as a tool for email campaigns, analytics, CRM, and content marketing management that integrates multiple functions into one screen. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p131]
- Offtime is an app that blocks distracting apps like games and social media, providing analytics on phone usage. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p131]
- Moment is a phone usage tracking app that can force users to stop using their phone through annoying screen alerts and notifications. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p132]
- Stay on Task is an Android productivity app that reminds users via notifications if they are still doing a task or something else. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p132]
