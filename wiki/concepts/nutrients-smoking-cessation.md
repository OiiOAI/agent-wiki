# Nutrients for smoking cessation

## Summary

Research suggests that multinutrient supplementation may help people quit smoking. A controlled clinical trial found that 42 percent of the multinutrient group achieved full abstinence for twelve weeks versus 23 percent of the placebo group, comparable to or better than other smoking cessation treatments. Participants reported that the nutrients reduced cravings.

## Key facts

- In a study comparing multinutrients plus Quitline support to placebo plus Quitline support, 42 percent of the multinutrient group achieved full abstinence for twelve weeks versus 23 percent of the placebo group. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p142]
- The multinutrient quit rate was higher than observed with Champix (12-week quit rate: 22 percent) as well as Nicotine Replacement Therapy plus Quitline (12-week quit rate: 26 percent). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p142]
- Those who received the multinutrient formula smoked fewer cigarettes during the quit-attempt phase and up to four weeks post-quitting. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p142]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The study had a high dropout rate (58 percent) and the sample size was small relative to drug company-funded studies.
