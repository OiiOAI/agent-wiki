# Types of Onset of Neurosis

## Summary

A 1912 paper by Freud classifying four types of precipitating causes of neurotic illness based on changes in the libido. The key unifying factor is 'frustration' (Versagung), whether external or internal. Type (a) involves external frustration where loss of a love object leads to introversion of libido and symptom formation. Type (b) involves internal frustration where attempts to adapt to reality demands encounter insurmountable internal difficulties. Type (c) involves developmental inhibition where patients never achieved full capacity for achievement and enjoyment. Type (d) involves increases in libido quantity through biological processes like puberty or menopause.

## Key facts

- The most obvious precipitating cause is external frustration: the subject becomes neurotic when a love object is withdrawn without replacement. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3940]
- The second type involves falling ill from internal effort to obtain satisfaction accessible in reality, coming up against insurmountable internal difficulties. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3942]
- Frustration remains the common factor and most inclusive concept linking all types of onset of neurosis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3943]
- The quantitative factor is crucial: all other factors remain ineffective unless they affect a certain amount of libido and bring about damming up of libido of a certain height. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3944]
- Psychoanalysis warns against the unfruitful contrast between external and internal factors, experience and constitution; the cause of neurotic illness lies in a particular psychical situation achievable in variety of ways. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3946]

## Inferences

- Inference: The paper represents a shift from Freud's earlier emphasis on traumatic events toward emphasis on libidinal dynamics and dispositional factors.
- Inference: The concept of 'frustration' (Versagung) covers both external obstacles to satisfaction and internal obstacles arising from ego resistance.
