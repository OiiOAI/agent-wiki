# Pre-Sleep and Post-Sleep Routines

## Summary

Pre- and post-sleep routines are treated as equally important as actual sleep time in the R90 program. Pre-sleep routines (ideally 90 minutes) include technology shutdown, dimming lights, temperature regulation (warm to cool), downloading the day, nose breathing, and security checks. Post-sleep routines (also ideally 90 minutes) include delaying phone use, getting daylight, eating breakfast, hydration, and gentle exercise. These routines directly affect sleep quality and waking-day performance.

## Key facts

- Pre- and post-sleep routines are treated as equally important as the time spent actually asleep in the R90 program. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p47]
- Pre-sleep should ideally be a ninety-minute period for preparation, involving shutting down technology and activities incompatible with sleep. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p47]
- Apple revealed that the average iPhone user unlocks their phone eighty times a day. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p49]
- Body temperature naturally drops in the evening as part of circadian rhythms; a warm rinse followed by cooler sleeping environment approximates this shift. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p50]
- Dimming lights and using warm-colour bulbs (red or amber) avoids suppressing melatonin production that blue light causes. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p51]
- 'Downloading the day' through writing a 'what's on my mind' list helps clear the mind for sleep. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p53]
- Mouth breathing has been proven to significantly increase snoring and obstructive sleep apnoea; nasal strips can help maintain nose breathing during sleep. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p55]
- Cortisol levels are at their highest shortly after waking; immediately checking phones can increase stress. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p57]
- Sleep quality is all about what you do from the point of waking. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p58]
- Eighty-two percent of people in Britain complained of being kept awake by worrying at some point in their lives. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p53]

## Inferences

- Inference: Post-sleep routines are particularly important for PMers who need to compensate for their naturally later body clocks.
- Inference: Keeping phones out of the bedroom overnight and using standard alarms instead of phone alarms is recommended.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The ideal duration of pre- and post-sleep routines may vary by individual needs and schedules.
