# Mu

## Summary

Mu (Japanese) means 'no thing.' Like Quality, it points outside the process of dualistic discrimination. Mu states that the context of the question is such that a yes-or-no answer is in error and should not be given—it says 'unask the question.' Mu becomes appropriate when the context of the question becomes too small for the truth of the answer. In motorcycle maintenance, mu answers given by the machine to diagnostic questions are a major cause of gumption loss but should be recognized as vital answers indicating the need to enlarge the context of the question.
