# Gerschenkron Thesis on Late-Developing Societies

## Summary

Alexander Gerschenkron originally proposed that heavy state involvement in promoting economic development is a general characteristic of late-developing societies. Fukuyama notes that while there is clearly some merit to this argument, there is considerable variance in state behavior—both in extent and competence—between different late-developing societies.

## Key facts

- Alexander Gerschenkron originally proposed that heavy state involvement in promoting economic development is a general characteristic of late-developing societies. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p427]
- There is considerable variance in state behavior regarding both the extent and competence of state involvement between different late-developing societies. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p427]
