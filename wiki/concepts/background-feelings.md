# Background feelings

## Summary

Background feelings are a variety of feeling that originates in background body states rather than in emotional states. They represent the continuous monitoring of the body's overall state between emotions, providing a sense of being and constituting a core part of self-representation. Unlike emotional feelings, background feelings are neither too positive nor too negative and are only subtly perceived.

## Key facts

- Background feelings originate in 'background' body states rather than in emotional states, and are neither too positive nor too negative. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p170]
- Background feelings correspond to the body state prevailing between emotions and are our image of the body landscape when not shaken by emotion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p170]
- Representations of current body states occur in multiple somatosensory cortices in the insula and parietal regions, and also in the limbic system, hypothalamus, and brain stem. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p171]
- Patients with anosognosia lose the ability to sense current body state, suggesting background feelings are essential for an integral sense of self. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p173]
