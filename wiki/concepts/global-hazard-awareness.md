# Global Hazard Awareness

## Summary

Global hazard awareness refers to the pilot's need for far more comprehensive hazard information within a much greater volume of space (e.g., 360 degrees around the aircraft) during unexpected circumstances such as becoming lost, disoriented, or encountering unexpected weather or traffic. Unlike local guidance, global hazard awareness is not a control function and can sacrifice precision in motion depiction to attain greater representation of wider space. Ideal displays for hazard awareness tend to be more two-dimensional (look down), zoomed-out, and world-referenced. The 'tether' display concept provides a compromise between ego-referenced local guidance and world-referenced global awareness.

- Global hazard awareness involves far more comprehensive hazard information within a greater volume of space (e.g., 360 degrees around the aircraft). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p226]
- Global hazard awareness is invoked when a pilot unexpectedly finds themselves lost, disoriented, or if unexpected weather or traffic requires a sudden radical departure from the intended flight path. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p226]
- Ideal displays for hazard awareness tend to be more two-dimensional (look down), zoomed-out, and world-referenced. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p226]
- Because global hazard awareness is less of a control function, displays can sacrifice precision in motion depiction to attain greater representation of wider space. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p226]
- The highly ego-referenced tunnel in the sky is ill-suited for global hazard awareness, providing either a very narrow 'keyhole' view or highly distorted position information when expanded. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p226]
