# Compassion

## Summary

Compassion is the awareness of a deep bond between oneself and all creatures, having two aspects: shared mortality (on the level of form) and shared eternal life (on the level of Being). On the level of form, humans share vulnerability and mortality with all beings, which humbles pride and creates equality. On the level of Being, compassion recognizes the deathless dimension of true nature and the immortality of all creatures. True compassion transcends empathy or sympathy—it merges sadness with joy, the joy of Being beyond form, and has great healing and transformative power.

## Key facts

- Compassion has two sides: on the level of form, humans share vulnerability and mortality with every living being; on the level of Being, all share eternal, radiant life beyond form. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p176]
- True compassion goes beyond empathy—it does not happen until sadness merges with joy, the joy of Being beyond form, the joy of eternal life. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p176]
- In compassion, the seemingly opposite feelings of sadness and joy merge and become transmuted into deep inner peace—this is the peace of God, one of the most noble feelings humans are capable of. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p176]
- Meditating deeply on the mortality of physical forms—including one's own—helps recognize that what is real never dies, only names, forms, and illusions pass away. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p176]

## Inferences

- Inference: Since compassion at the deepest level involves awareness of Being-consciousness, it serves as both a spiritual state and a healing force that affects others through emanation rather than direct action.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The text acknowledges that true compassion as described is 'as yet rare,' suggesting most human experiences labeled compassion fall short of this ideal.
