# Illness and Enlightenment

## Summary

The book addresses how illness can be transformed into a path for enlightenment. Illness is part of the life situation (which exists in time), but underneath lies one's essential Being in the timeless Now. By withdrawing time from the illness, not giving it past or future, and allowing it to force present-moment awareness, one can use it for enlightenment. Surrendering to the illness means accepting the present moment, not accepting a label for one's condition.

## Key facts

- Illness is part of your life situation and has a past and a future, but underneath the conditions exists your Life, your very Being in the timeless Now. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p192]
- As there are no problems in the Now, there is no illness either. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p192]
- The belief in a label someone attaches to their condition empowers it and makes a seemingly solid reality out of a temporary imbalance. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p192]
- By focusing on the instant and refraining from labeling it mentally, illness is reduced to physical pain, weakness, discomfort, or disability. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p192]
- If you are ill or disabled, do not feel you have failed, do not feel guilty, and do not blame life or yourself — all that is resistance. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- If you have a major illness, use it for enlightenment. Anything 'bad' that happens in your life, use it for enlightenment. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- Withdraw time from the illness. Do not give it any past or future. Let it force you into intense present-moment awareness. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
- Become an alchemist. Transmute base metal into gold, suffering into consciousness, disaster into enlightenment. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p193]
