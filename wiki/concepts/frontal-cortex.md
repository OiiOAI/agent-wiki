# Frontal Cortex

## Summary

The most recently evolved brain region, central to executive function, working memory, long-term planning, emotion regulation, and impulse control. The frontal cortex 'makes you do the harder thing when it's the right thing to do.' It includes the dorsolateral PFC (cognitive) and ventromedial PFC (emotional), and is the last brain region to mature (into the mid-twenties).

## Key facts

- The frontal cortex is the last brain region to fully mature, with the most recent subparts the very last—not fully online until the mid-twenties. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p50]
- Von Economo neurons (spindle neurons) are a unique cell type found in the frontal cortex and insula, also present in whales, dolphins, elephants—socially complex species. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p51]
- The dorsolateral PFC (dlPFC) is the most rational, cognitive, utilitarian part—the 'decider of deciders.'. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p58]
- The ventromedial PFC (vmPFC) is the emotional part, connected to the limbic system and essential for gut-feeling decisions. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p59]
- Damage to the vmPFC leaves intelligence intact but produces 'pathological altruism'—people who cannot make social/emotional decisions appropriately. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p60]
- The frontal cortex has extremely high metabolic demands; willpower is a finite resource (cognitive load depletes self-control). [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p53]
- After heavy frontocortical work, people become less prosocial, less charitable, more likely to lie or cheat. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p54]
- Criminal psychopaths show decreased frontal activity and less coupling to other brain regions. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p58]
