# Mandala Meditation

## Summary

Mandala meditation involves drawing or coloring circular pattern designs as a form of meditation. Studies show that coloring mandalas can help practitioners achieve mindfulness and be alone with their minds.

## Key facts

- Mandalas are described as circular pattern designs that tend to be quite mesmerizing. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p125]
- Drawing and coloring mandalas can be used as a form of meditation to be alone with one's mind and practice self-renewal. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p125]
- Studies show that coloring mandalas can lead practitioners to practice mindfulness. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p125]
- The book provides a six-step process for mandala meditation: order/download patterns, schedule time, disconnect from tech, work on mandala with focused attention, store completed work, and take deep breaths before returning to tasks. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p125]
- The recommended schedule is ten-minute sessions daily or one-hour sessions two to three times per week. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p125]
