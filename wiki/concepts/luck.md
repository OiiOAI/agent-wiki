# Luck

## Summary

There are four kinds of luck: blind luck (fortune completely out of your control), luck through persistence and hard work, luck through becoming very good at spotting opportunities, and luck through building a unique character that causes luck to find you. Building your character in a certain way makes your character your destiny.

## Key facts

- The first kind of luck is blind luck where something completely out of your control happens. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p69]
- The second kind of luck comes through persistence, hard work, hustle, and motion. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p69]
- The third way to get lucky is to become very good at spotting luck in your field. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p70]
- The fourth kind of luck is when you build a unique character, brand, or mindset which causes luck to find you. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p70]
- Building your character in a certain way causes your character to become your destiny. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p71]

## Inferences

- Inference: Naval advocates for developing the fourth type of luck through building reputation and character, rather than relying on chance.
