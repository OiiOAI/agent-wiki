# Skills of Change

## Summary

Skills of Change is Fogg's framework for the five categories of abilities needed for successful behavior change: Behavior Crafting (knowing what habits to do), Self-Insight (knowing which habits have meaning), Process (adjusting to change over time), Context (redesigning environment), and Mindset (approach and attitude). These skills are learned through practice, not talent.

## Key facts

- Self-Insight Skill involves finding the tiniest habit that would have the most meaning to you. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p203]
- Process Skill includes knowing when to push beyond tiny and ramp up difficulty, finding your 'comfort edge'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p204]
- Context Skill is the ability to redesign your environment to make habits easier to do. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p207]
- Mindset Skills include: approaching change with openness, flexibility, and curiosity; lowering expectations; celebrating successes; being patient and trusting the process. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p210]
- The skill of embracing a new identity is described as ranking right up with celebration as essential Mindset Skills. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p211]
- Comfort edge is not a straight line but fluctuates; focus on finding your edge in the moment. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p205]
