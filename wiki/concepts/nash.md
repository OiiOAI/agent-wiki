# Nonalcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH)

## Summary

NASH is the more advanced form of fatty liver disease, essentially NAFLD plus inflammation (similar to hepatitis but without viral infection). This inflammation causes scarring in the liver. NASH progresses to cirrhosis in about 11 percent of patients. It was first named in 1980 by a team at the Mayo Clinic as 'hitherto unnamed disease.' By 2025, NASH with cirrhosis is expected to be the leading indication for liver transplantation in the US.

## Key facts

- NASH is NAFLD plus inflammation, similar to hepatitis but without a viral infection. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p97]
- In 1980, a team at the Mayo Clinic dubbed this 'hitherto unnamed disease' nonalcoholic steatohepatitis, or NASH. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p96]
- NASH with inflammation causes scarring in the liver, which may progress to cirrhosis in about 11 percent of patients. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p98]
- By 2025, NASH with cirrhosis is expected to be the leading indication for liver transplantation in the United States. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p98]
- Both NAFLD and NASH are reversible if fat is removed from the liver via weight loss. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p97]
