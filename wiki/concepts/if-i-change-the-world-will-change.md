# If I Change, the World Will Change

## Summary

A central principle of Adlerian psychology expressing that the power to change lies within the individual. If 'I' change, the world will change; no one else will change the world for me. This realization is compared to the experience of a nearsighted person putting on glasses for the first time—previously indistinct outlines become well defined and colors become more vivid.

- The power of one person is great—'my power is immeasurably great.'. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p284]
