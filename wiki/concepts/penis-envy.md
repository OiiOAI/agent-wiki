# penis envy

## Summary

Penis envy is a concept in Freudian psychoanalytic theory referring to girls' realization that they lack a penis and their subsequent psychological response to this discovery. Freud proposed that this envy drives girls to transfer their affection from mother to father and leads to the development of femininity.

## Key facts

- A little girl notices the penis of a brother or playmate, recognizes it as superior to her own organ, and from that time falls victim to envy for the penis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6145]
- The masculinity complex of women branches off from penis envy and may put great difficulties in the way of their regular development towards femininity. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6146]
- After a woman becomes aware of the wound to her narcissism, she develops a sense of inferiority. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6146]
- Even after penis envy has abandoned its true object, it continues to exist in the character trait of jealousy. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6147]

## Inferences

- Inference: Penis envy is part of Freud's broader theory of female psychosexual development, which posits that girls must transition from a masculine (clitoral) sexuality to a feminine (vaginal) one.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The concept of penis envy remains controversial in modern psychology and feminist theory, with many scholars questioning its universality and applicability.
