# Oedipus complex in girls

## Summary

Freud's theory that in girls, the Oedipus complex is a secondary formation, preceded by the castration complex and the discovery of anatomical differences. Unlike boys, whose Oedipus complex is primary and collapses through castration fear, girls' Oedipus complex is made possible by the castration complex.

## Key facts

- In girls the Oedipus complex raises one problem more than in boys: in both cases the mother is the original object, but how does it happen that girls abandon it and take their father as an object?. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6144]
- In girls the Oedipus complex is a secondary formation; the operations of the castration complex precede it and prepare for it. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6149]
- The girl gives up her wish for a penis and puts in its place a wish for a child; with that purpose in view she takes her father as a love object. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6148]
- There is a fundamental contrast between the sexes: whereas in boys the Oedipus complex collapses through the castration complex, in girls it is made possible and led up to by the castration complex. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6149]

## Inferences

- Inference: This represents a significant departure from Freud's earlier view, expressed in The Interpretation of Dreams and Introductory Lectures, that there was 'a complete parallel' between the sexes.
