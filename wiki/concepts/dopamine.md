# Dopamine

## Summary

A neurotransmitter vital to movement, attention, cognition, motivation and pleasure, and addiction.

## Key facts

- Dopamine is a neurotransmitter that is vital to movement, attention, cognition, motivation and pleasure, and addiction. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p222]
- Dopamine systems ascend from the midbrain dividing into the nigrostriatal pathway (substantia nigra to dorsal striatum) and mesolimbic/mesocortical pathways (VTA to ventral striatum and frontal cortex). [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- Excessive dopamine activity (from psychomotor stimulants or paranoid schizophrenia) produces repetitive behavior patterns, persistent thoughts, and delusions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- When dopamine is unavailable (Parkinson's disease or excessive antipsychotic drugs), all behavior is diminished and displeasure sets in. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- DA is synthesized from tyrosine via hydroxylation to L-DOPA, then decarboxylation to dopamine, with possible further hydroxylation to norepinephrine. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p61]
