# Value Hypothesis and Growth Hypothesis

## Summary

The two most important leap-of-faith assumptions in any startup are the value hypothesis and the growth hypothesis. The value hypothesis tests whether a product or service really delivers value to customers once they are using it. The growth hypothesis tests how new customers will discover a product or service. These hypotheses give rise to tuning variables that control a startup's engine of growth. Each iteration of a startup is an attempt to test these assumptions and see if the engine will turn.

## Key facts

- The value hypothesis tests whether a product or service really delivers value to customers once they are using it. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p62]
- The growth hypothesis tests how new customers will discover a product or service. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p62]
- These two hypotheses give rise to tuning variables that control a startup's engine of growth. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p74]
- Facebook validated its value hypothesis through the raw amount of time active users spent on the site, with more than half returning every single day. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p76]
- Facebook validated its growth hypothesis through the staggering rate at which it took over its first few college campuses—almost three-quarters of Harvard's undergraduates within one month. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p76]
