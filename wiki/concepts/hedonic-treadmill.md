# The Hedonic Treadmill

## Summary

The hedonic treadmill (or hedonic adaptation) ensures that humans quickly return to a relatively stable level of happiness despite major positive or negative changes in circumstances. This explains why rising prosperity fails to increase national happiness levels.

## Key facts

- The hedonic treadmill makes sure that modern consumers everywhere are in a 'permanent state of unfulfilled desire'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p596]
- Sam Johnson observed centuries before the research: 'Life is a progress from want to want, not from enjoyment to enjoyment.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p596]
