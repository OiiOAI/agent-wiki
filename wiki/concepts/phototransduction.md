# Phototransduction

## Summary

Phototransduction is the molecular cascade by which photoreceptor cells convert light absorption into neural signals. In rods and cones, light activation of the visual pigment (rhodopsin in rods) triggers a G-protein cascade that ultimately closes ion channels, hyperpolarizing the cell and reducing neurotransmitter release.

## Key facts

- Rhodopsin consists of the protein opsin (348 amino acids, ~40 kDa) with 11-cis retinal covalently attached to lysine 296 in the seventh transmembrane domain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p576]
- Absorption of a photon causes retinal to flip from the 11-cis to the all-trans configuration, the only light-dependent step in vision. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p576]
- Activated rhodopsin (metarhodopsin II) activates the G protein transducin, which activates phosphodiesterase, which hydrolyzes cGMP to GMP. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p577]
- Decreased cGMP closes cGMP-gated Na+/Ca2+ channels in the plasma membrane, causing membrane hyperpolarization and reduced glutamate release. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p577]
- Calcium feedback plays a key role in terminating the response: Ca2+ decrease accelerates guanylyl cyclase activity and increases cGMP-gated channel sensitivity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p577]
- Cones use the same phototransduction cascade as rods but with different kinetics—cone responses peak faster (~40 ms vs rod's slower response—and adapt more rapidly. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p573]

## Inferences

- Inference: The amplification cascade provides extreme sensitivity; one activated rhodopsin can activate hundreds of transducin molecules, and each phosphodiesterase hydrolyzes over 1000 cGMP molecules per second.
