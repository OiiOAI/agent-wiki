# Shallow Work

## Summary

Shallow Work refers to noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks often performed while distracted. These efforts tend not to create much new value in the world and are easy to replicate. Knowledge workers increasingly replace deep work with shallow alternatives in an age of network tools.

## Key facts

- Shallow work is defined as noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p7]
- Shallow work tends not to create much new value in the world and is easy to replicate. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p7]
- Spending enough time in a state of frenetic shallowness can permanently reduce your capacity to perform deep work. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p7]
- Shallow work is defined as noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks often performed while distracted, creating little new value and easy to replicate. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p135]
- For novices, about an hour per day of intense concentration is the limit; for experts, about four hours but rarely more. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p89]
- 37signals found that eliminating one day per week disproportionately eliminated shallow work while leaving deep work intact, proving shallow work is often unexpectedly dispensable. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p127]
- The shallowness of an activity can be quantified by asking: how long would it take to train a smart recent college graduate to complete this task?. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p135]

## Inferences

- Inference: Shallow work doesn't become dangerous until it begins to crowd out bounded deep efforts for the day, but the typical fragmented knowledge workday is more easily consumed by shallowness than many suspect.
