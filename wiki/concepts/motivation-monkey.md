# Motivation Monkey

## Summary

The Motivation Monkey is Fogg's personification of unreliable motivation that tricks people into setting unrealistic goals. The metaphor describes how motivation often helps us reach amazing heights but abandons us when we need it most. The Motivation Monkey represents the psychological trap where people blame themselves for not sustaining motivation-driven change, when in fact the approach itself is flawed. Fogg identifies five key traps: motivation is complex, the Motivation Wave, motivation fluctuation, motivating toward abstractions, and relying on motivation for long-term change.

## Key facts

- The Motivation Monkey 'tricks us into setting unreasonable goals' and 'will often abandon us when we need him most'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p49]
- The five motivation traps identified are: motivation is complex, the Motivation Wave, motivation fluctuation, motivating toward abstractions, and motivation is not the winning ticket for long-term change.
