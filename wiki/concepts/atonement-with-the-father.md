# Atonement with the Father

## Summary

The ordeal of facing the father represents confronting the ogre aspect of the father derived from the victim's own ego—projected from the sensational nursery scene. Atonement (at-one-ment) requires abandonment of the self-generated double monster: the dragon thought to be God (superego) and the dragon thought to be Sin (repressed id). The helpful female figure (Spider Woman, Blessed Mother) protects through the father-shattering initiation.

## Key facts

- The ogre aspect of the father is a reflex of the victim's own ego—derived from the sensational nursery scene that has been left behind but projected before. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p106]
- Atonement consists in the abandonment of the self-generated double monster—the dragon thought to be God and the dragon thought to be Sin, which requires abandonment of attachment to ego itself. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p106]
- In the Book of Job, God makes no attempt to vindicate His work in ethical terms but magnifies His Presence, bidding Job emulate the way of heaven. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p118]
