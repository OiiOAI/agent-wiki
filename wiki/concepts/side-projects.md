# Side Projects and Hobbies

## Summary

Side projects and hobbies are essential for creative vitality. They provide regeneration and often become the most successful work. Having multiple passions rather than choosing one prevents 'phantom limb pain' and fuels cross-pollination of ideas.

## Key facts

- It's the side projects that really take off—they're the 'stuff you thought was just messing around' but that's when the magic happens. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p48]
- Kleon found that playing music again after abandoning it for writing made his writing better—new synapses firing, new connections being made. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p53]
- Steven Tomlinson suggested that if you love different things, keep spending time with them and let them talk to each other. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p52]
