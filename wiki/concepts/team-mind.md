# Team Mind

## Summary

Team Mind is the concept that teams function as intelligent entities with their own cognitive properties—including collective consciousness, preconscious knowledge, working memory, long-term memory, limited attention, perceptual filters, and the ability to learn. This metaphor, while imperfect, helps researchers study team decision making by examining observable team behaviors rather than individual minds.

## Key facts

- Team cognition can be inferred from three sources: the team's behavior, the contents of the team's collective consciousness, and the team's preconscious. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p284]
- The collective consciousness of a team consists of what the team talks about and the gestures members make to each other. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p285]
- The preconscious level of the team mind refers to ideas that team members knew but did not share with others. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p285]
- A team mind has working memory—the ability to hold information briefly before moving to other topics. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p287]
- Teams have long-term memory to store information permanently, though if a team member drops out, the information may be lost. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p287]
- Teams can attend to only one thing at a time and must carefully direct their limited attention. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p287]
- The team mind often functions in an accidental manner—ideas and the flow of attention are unpredictable. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p304]
- Only 8 percent of segment transitions in a five-hour planning session were categorized as natural transitions; over half were unconnected. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p304]
