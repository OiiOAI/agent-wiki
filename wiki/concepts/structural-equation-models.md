# Structural Equation Models

## Summary

Structural Equation Models (SEM) emerged from Wright's path analysis but became a subject of debate regarding their proper interpretation. While originally intended as causal modeling tools, SEM was challenged by Freedman (1987) who questioned how interventions should be modeled, leading many economists and social scientists to retreat to viewing SEM as purely statistical exercises rather than causal inference tools.
