# Unilateral Neglect

## Summary

Unilateral neglect is a disorder caused by lesions in the right parietal lobe in which patients have normal vision but seem unaware of objects on the left side of space. Some patients even ignore the left side of individual objects, demonstrating dissociation between conscious awareness and unconscious processing of visual information.

## Key facts

- Unilateral neglect is caused by lesions in the right parietal lobe. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1522]
- Patients with unilateral neglect have normal vision but seem unaware of objects on the left side of space in front of them. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1522]
- Patients with unilateral neglect can show unconscious awareness of stimuli they claim not to see, as demonstrated by choosing not to live in a house shown burning on the left side.
