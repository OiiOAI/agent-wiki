# Delusions and Reinforcement Learning

## Summary

Kapur's aberrant salience hypothesis proposes that delusions arise when dysregulated dopamine transmission causes irrelevant stimuli to be assigned excessive motivational significance. Reinforcement learning models suggest that abnormal prediction-error signaling causes patients to form inappropriate associations, leading to the characteristic over-valued beliefs of psychosis.

## Key facts

- Kapur proposed that psychosis is a state of aberrant salience, where dysregulated dopamine causes normally irrelevant stimuli to be experienced as highly significant. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p259]
- Patients in the prodromal phase report experiencing new awareness of subtle connections between otherwise unrelated events, consistent with abnormal associative learning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p258]
- Schizophrenia patients tend to 'jump to conclusions' on probabilistic reasoning tasks, suggesting a failure of normal evidence evaluation processes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p251]
- Disruptions in prediction-error signaling could lead to ever more radical changes in inference, producing both bizarre experiences and apparently irrational beliefs. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p259]

## Inferences

- Inference: The observation that patients in early psychosis experience seeing connections in unrelated events, before forming full delusions, suggests a progressive process: abnormal RPE signaling first produces aberrant salience, then normal reasoning processes attempt to explain the unusual experience, resulting in a delusional belief.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether delusional beliefs persist after the physiological disturbance subsides—as other beliefs do—or require ongoing neurochemical dysregulation is uncertain.
