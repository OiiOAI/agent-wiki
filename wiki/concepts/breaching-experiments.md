# Breaching Experiments

## Summary

Breaching experiments are a method in ethnomethodology where social reality is violated to shed light on the methods by which people construct social reality. The assumption is that the methodical production of social life occurs all the time but participants are unaware of it. The objective is to disrupt normal procedures so the process of constructing everyday world can be observed. Garfinkel's famous examples include having students behave like boarders in their own homes.

- In breaching experiments, social reality is violated in order to shed light on the methods by which people construct social reality. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p289]
