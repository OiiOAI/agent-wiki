# Delusion

## Summary

A delusion is a psychopathological formation arising from repression and conflict between suppressed drives and repressing forces. The conviction attached to delusions has a source of truth concealed within them—repressed material that, when it penetrates consciousness in distorted form, carries its conviction with it and protects the distortion from criticism. Displacement of conviction from unconscious truth to conscious error characterizes delusion formation.

## Key facts

- There is a grain of truth concealed in every delusion, something that really deserves belief, and this is the source of the patient's conviction. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2963]
- The conviction is displaced from the unconscious truth on to the conscious error that is linked to it, and remains fixated there precisely as a result of this displacement. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2964]
