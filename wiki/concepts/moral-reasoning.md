# Moral Reasoning

## Summary

Moral reasoning involves cognitive processes for evaluating right and wrong, using concepts like harm, fairness, loyalty, authority, and purity. Brain regions involved include the vmPFC and amygdala. Liberal moral judgments emphasize harm and fairness while conservative moral judgments disproportionately value loyalty, respect, and purity.

## Key facts

- Liberals place more emphasis than conservatives on harm and fairness issues, while conservatives disproportionately value loyalty, respect, and purity. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1182]
- The same brain circuits activate when contemplating whether an act is moral as when contemplating whether a face is beautiful, involving the medial orbitofrontal cortex. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1210]
- For acts that harm people, adults advocate greater punishment for intentional acts; there is far less distinction between intentional and unintentional harm to objects. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p991]
