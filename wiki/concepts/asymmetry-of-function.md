# Asymmetry of Function

## Summary

Asymmetry of function follows from ontological asymmetry: in the functioning together of the two hemispheres, the products of the left hemisphere need to be returned to the realm of the right hemisphere in order to live. While experience is enriched by sending right-hemisphere products to the left for 'unpacking', there is no necessity for that process. One process is literally vital; the other is not.

## Key facts

- The products of the left hemisphere need to be returned to the realm of the right hemisphere in order to live. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p320]
- There is no necessity for the process of sending right-hemisphere products to the left for unpacking, but there is literal vital necessity for returning left-hemisphere products to the right. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p321]
