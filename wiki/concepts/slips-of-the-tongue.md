# Slips of the Tongue

## Summary

Slips of the tongue (Versprechen) are linguistic errors where one word or sound is replaced by another. Freud argued they reveal unconscious thoughts and desires, showing that the intended utterance is interfered with by repressed material.

## Key facts

- Slips of the tongue occur when sounds or words outside the intended utterance interfere with speech production. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2048]
- Contaminations and substitutions in slips of the tongue parallel the dream-work process of condensation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2050]
- Words with opposite meanings are frequently interchanged in slips, revealing internal contradictions in the speaker. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2051]
