# Path Integration

## Summary

Path integration (also called dead reckoning) is a navigation mechanism that calculates position based on self-motion information—integrating distance traveled (speed multiplied by time) and direction of turns without reliance on landmarks. It allows animals to navigate even in complete darkness by keeping track of starting location. The mechanism requires information from local visual and somatosensory cues, proprioceptive feedback, step counting, vestibular input, and corollary discharge from self-motion activity. While intuitive and effective, path integration accumulates errors over time as each estimate is relative to the previous one.

## Key facts

- Path integration calculates position relative to starting location by integrating self-motion (distance = speed × time, direction of turns), allowing animals to find their way without prominent landmarks or in complete darkness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p115]
- Dead reckoning was the main navigation strategy for sailors in the Mediterranean Sea for centuries before Portuguese sailors introduced landmark navigation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p115]
- Gerbil mothers can retrieve pups and return to their nest at the arena border in complete darkness using an internal sense of direction, but when the platform is slowly rotated without their noticing, they carry pups to the original spatial position even though the nest is no longer there. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p116]
- Path integration presents challenges because every change in speed and direction contributes to error accumulation as each position estimate is relative to the previous one. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p116]
- Blind children walked to three landmarks can immediately find routes between them even though they have never experienced those paths, demonstrating that a cognitive map can be constructed solely from dead reckoning navigation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p117]
