# Anal Erotism and Character

## Summary

Freud's theory that people who were anal erotics in childhood typically develop a characteristic triad of adult character traits—orderliness (including bodily cleanliness and trustworthiness), parsimony (which may become avarice), and obstinacy (which may become defiance and revengefulness)—as a result of the sublimation and reaction-formation of anal erotism during the latency period.

## Key facts

- People marked by orderliness, parsimony and obstinacy typically took a comparatively long time to overcome infantile faecal incontinence and enjoyed holding back their stool as infants. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3055]
- Cleanliness, orderliness and trustworthiness function as reaction-formations against interest in what is unclean and disturbing, following the formula that 'dirt is matter in the wrong place.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3057]
- In folk psychology, ancient civilizations, myths, fairy tales, unconscious thinking, dreams and neuroses, money is brought into intimate relationship with dirt, as in the superstition connecting treasure-finding with defaecation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3057]
- The character traits of orderliness, parsimony and obstinacy are the first and most constant results of the sublimation of anal erotism. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3056]
- During the period of sexual latency, reaction-formations such as shame, disgust and morality are created at the expense of excitations from erotogenic zones. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3056]
- Par simony and obstinacy are linked more closely to each other than to orderliness, and are the more constant element of the whole complex. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3054]

## Inferences

- Inference: The connection Freud traces between anal erotism and money (faeces = gold) in folk traditions and neuroses suggests that symbolic equation of precious and waste substances has deep psychological roots.
