# Autopoietic Systems

## Summary

Autopoietic systems, as developed by Niklas Luhmann, are self-producing systems characterized by four defining features: they produce their own basic elements (e.g., money for the economy), they are self-organizing in establishing their own boundaries and internal structures, they are self-referential (using codes to refer to themselves), and they are operationally closed systems with no direct connection to their environment.

## Key facts

- Autopoietic systems produce the basic elements that make up the system; for the economic system, money is the basic element, and both money and the economic system emerged together and depend on each other. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p226]
- Autopoietic systems are closed systems with no direct connection to their environment; they deal only with representations of the environment, such as how the economic system responds to material needs only insofar as they can be represented in monetary terms. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p227]
- The problem of double contingency refers to the fact that every communication must account for how it is received, but how it is received depends on the receiver's estimation of the communicator, creating an interdependent circle that is resolved through social structures. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p229]
- Modern society is characterized by functional differentiation, the most complex form of differentiation, in which every function within a system is ascribed to a particular unit, allowing greater flexibility but increasing vulnerability if any function fails. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p234]
- Functional differentiation causes central problems for modern society because what is necessary for society as a whole may not be dealt with by any functional system, as no single system has a code adequate to represent all problems (e.g., ecological problems cannot be adequately represented in economic terms). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p235]

## Inferences

- Inference: The operational closure of autopoietic systems means that society cannot be steered or controlled from any single center, since each functional system operates according to its own code and cannot fully comprehend or incorporate the codes of other systems.
