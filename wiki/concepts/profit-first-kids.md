# Profit First Kids

## Summary

Profit First Kids is a system for teaching children money management using five labeled envelopes representing different financial priorities. Children earn money through a job/chore list and allocate earnings before spending, building habits that become automatic.

## Key facts

- The five envelopes for children are: Big Dream (up to 25%), Family Support (recurring weekly amount), Impact (5-10% for charity/business), Vault (10% for emergencies/investing), and Mad Money (remaining for purchases). [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p194]
- Michalowicz's daughter Adayla used the system to pay for a six-week Hawaii vacation and a trip to Spain. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p194]
- The goal is to make 'good money management and caring for others...automatic...It requires no extra skills or thought; it's just who she is.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p195]
