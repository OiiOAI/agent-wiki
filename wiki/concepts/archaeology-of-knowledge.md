# Archaeology of Knowledge

## Summary

Archaeology of knowledge is Foucault's methodology involving a search for the set of rules that determine the conditions of possibility for all that can be said within a particular discourse at any given time. It involves analyzing discourse and documents by organizing, dividing, distributing, and establishing series. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p509]
