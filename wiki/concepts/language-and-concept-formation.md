# Language and concept formation

## Summary

Language enables humans to form concepts and solidify abstractions through nouns, adjectives, and verbs. It allows ordering of experience and reflection, giving form and meaning to what is felt and perceived. Language establishes relations between concepts and supports the consolidation of concepts and their relationships, providing the foundation for human intelligence beyond what other animals can access.

## Key facts

- Words are abstractions of reality; saying 'dog' brushes aside all details about specific dogs and refers to whatever defines the animal. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p152]
- Language allows us to order our experience and reflect on it, to give form and meaning to what we feel and perceive, and explain ourselves to ourselves. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p154]
- The degree of abstraction afforded by the use of language may well be what lets humans discard countless details that can then be filled in by inference, which is the quintessence of human intelligence and creativity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p154]

## Inferences
