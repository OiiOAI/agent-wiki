# Mise-en-place (Knowledge Work)

## Summary

Mise-en-place is a culinary philosophy applied to knowledge work. Originally developed in French restaurants in the late 1800s, it involves keeping workspaces clean and organized during the execution of regular work rather than setting aside separate time for organization. For knowledge workers, it means small habits of organizing in the flow of daily work.

## Key facts

- Mise-en-place was developed in France starting in the late 1800s. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p173]
- Chefs learn to keep their workspace clean and organized in the flow of meals they are preparing. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p173]
- For knowledge workers, mise-en-place means maintaining organization during regular work rather than setting aside separate time. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p173]
