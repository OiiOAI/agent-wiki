# Sexual Fantasy

## Summary

Sexual fantasy is a valuable imaginative resource that provides psychological safe space to undo inhibitions and fears. Fantasies combine personal history with collective imagination, allowing individuals to express what they cannot act upon in reality. Michael Bader defines sexual ruthlessness as the quality enabling surrender to one's own rhythms of pleasure without guilt or shame. Fantasy functions to transform inhibiting fear into brazenness, timidity into assertiveness, and helplessness into sovereignty. Sexual fantasies need not be elaborate or scripted; they include any mental activity that generates desire and intensifies enthusiasm, often more feelings than images.
