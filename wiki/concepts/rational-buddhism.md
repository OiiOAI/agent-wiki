# Rational Buddhism

## Summary

Rational Buddhism is Naval Ravikant's personal philosophy that combines evolutionary science with Buddhist teachings. It emphasizes verifying spiritual claims through personal experience and scientific validation while rejecting unfalsifiable or supernatural elements.

## Key facts

- Rational Buddhism requires reconciling Buddhism with science and evolution. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p163]
- Ravikant accepts meditation, clearing the mind, and awareness as verified practices. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p163]
- He rejects claims he cannot verify, such as past life karma. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p163]
- The philosophy maps Buddhist principles into concepts understandable through virtual reality simulation. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p164]
