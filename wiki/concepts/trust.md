# Trust

## Summary

In Fukuyama's framework, trust is defined as the expectation that arises within a community of regular, honest, and cooperative behavior based on shared norms, regardless of the community's members' ability to monitor that behavior. Trust enables spontaneous sociability—the capacity for individuals to form voluntary associations and economic cooperation without explicit contracts or legal enforcement. Fukuyama contends that trust is a crucial cultural characteristic that conditions a nation's economic well-being and competitive ability, and that communities united by trust outperform those requiring formal rules and hierarchies.
