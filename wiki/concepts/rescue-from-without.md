# Rescue from Without

## Summary

The mythological stage where the hero is brought back from his supernatural adventure by assistance from without, rather than by his own effort. Campbell describes this as the world coming to get the hero who is reluctant to leave the bliss of the deep abode. Society or supernatural forces knock at the door to recall the hero to ordinary life.

## Key facts

- The hero may have to be brought back from his supernatural adventure by assistance from without. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p162]
- Bliss of the deep abode is not lightly abandoned in favor of the self-scattering of the wakened state. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p162]
- Society is jealous of those who remain away and will come knocking at the door. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p162]

## Inferences

- Inference: Campbell situates this stage after the hero has achieved the boon but before final reintegration into ordinary society
