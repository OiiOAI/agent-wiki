# In-Group Bias

## Summary

In-group bias refers to the tendency to favor members of one's own group over those of other groups. This favoritism can manifest as preferential treatment, resource allocation, and punishment of out-group members. It operates partly through oxytocin-mediated mechanisms but can be modulated by context.

## Key facts

- Oxytocin makes people more hostile and xenophobic when dealing with out-group members, while promoting prosociality within the in-group. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1198]
- The intergroup bias effect was demonstrable in males but not females and was most pronounced when males looked at pictures of other males. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1122]
- When contemplating corporate morality, people activate Theory of Mind networks, treating corporations as if they were people. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1140]
