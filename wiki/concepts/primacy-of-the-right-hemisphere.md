# Primacy of the Right Hemisphere

## Summary

McGilchrist argues that the right hemisphere has ontological primacy over the left hemisphere in grounding our experience of reality. The right hemisphere brings the world into being initially, while the left hemisphere processes and re-presents what the right hemisphere delivers. This primacy is evident across multiple dimensions: temporal (right hemisphere processes arrive first), logical (the implicit grounds the explicit), and ontological (the right hemisphere engages directly with embodied reality while the left hemisphere operates in a virtual, re-presented realm). The relationship follows a pattern from right hemisphere through left hemisphere processing and back to right hemisphere for reintegration.

## Key facts

- The right hemisphere has primacy because it brings the world into being before the left hemisphere re-presents it. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p254]
- Broad vigilant attention requires the right hemisphere for initial awareness, before focused attention can be directed. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p254]
- The right hemisphere deals with the world before separation, division, and analysis transform it. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p254]
- New experience comes into being first for the right hemisphere before the left hemisphere re-presents it. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p255]
- Referential language has its origins in the body and the right hemisphere. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p255]
- The left hemisphere world is virtual, not present, but a representation. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p272]

## Inferences

- Inference: The pattern of experience progresses from right hemisphere through left hemisphere and back to right hemisphere again, following a roundness rather than rectilinearity
- Inference: The right hemisphere's primacy means the left hemisphere is 'parasitic' on the right, drawing its life from the right hemisphere's engagement with reality

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact neurological mechanisms by which the right hemisphere achieves its grounding function remain to be fully specified
