# Chain Analysis

## Summary

Chain analysis is a DBT technique for understanding problem behaviors by examining the chain of linked events that leads to them. It involves identifying the prompting event, vulnerability factors, all links in the behavioral chain, and consequences. The goal is to identify where skillful behaviors could replace ineffective ones and to develop prevention strategies.

## Key facts

- Chain analysis examines the chain of events that leads to ineffective behaviors, including the consequences that may be making it hard to change them. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p43]
- The eight steps of chain analysis are: describe problem behavior, describe prompting event, describe vulnerability factors, describe chain of events in detail, describe consequences, describe skillful replacement behaviors, develop prevention strategies, and repair consequences. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p44]
- Links in a chain can include actions, body sensations, cognitions/thoughts, events in the environment, and feelings. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p45]
