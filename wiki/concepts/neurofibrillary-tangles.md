# Neurofibrillary Tangles

## Summary

Neurofibrillary tangles are intracellular filamentous inclusions found in neurons affected by Alzheimer disease and other neurodegenerative conditions. They are composed of paired helical filaments and straight filaments made of hyperphosphorylated tau protein, a normally occurring microtubule-associated protein. Tangles do not occur uniformly in Alzheimer disease but preferentially affect the entorhinal cortex, hippocampus, parts of the neocortex, and the nucleus basalis.
