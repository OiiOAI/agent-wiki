# Falsification of History

## Summary

Falsification of history is the brain's tendency to revise memories to match current views. Gregory Markus found people recalled 1973 political opinions as almost identical to their 2003 views despite different original responses. Flashbulb memories (like 9/11) are equally flawed.

## Key facts

- Gregory Markus found people recalled their 1973 political opinions as almost identical to their 2003 views, differing significantly from their actual original responses. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p173]
- Ulrich Neisser found less than 7% of Challenger disaster memories correlated with initial essays three years later; 50% of recollections were incorrect in two-thirds of points. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p174]
