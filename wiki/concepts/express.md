# Express (CODE Step 4)

## Summary

Express is the fourth and ultimate step of CODE, involving showing your work by creating and sharing output. The author emphasizes that knowledge becomes personal and verified only when put to use, and that shifting time from consuming to creating leads to more fulfillment. Expression synonyms include publish, speak, present, perform, produce, write, draw, interpret, critique, and translate.

## Key facts

- The word 'productivity' comes from the Latin 'producere' meaning 'to produce,' implying that productive work must result in output. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p46]
- The author notes that people who love to learn can fall into the habit of consuming more information without applying it. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p45]
- Information is always in flux and a work in progress, so there is no need to wait to get started — simple outputs can be created now and refined over time. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p46]
