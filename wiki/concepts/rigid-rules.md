# Rigid Rules

## Summary

Rigid rules are strict instructions from the mind containing words like 'have to,' 'must,' 'should,' 'always,' 'never.' When they hook us, life constricts. Underneath rigid rules there are always important values that can be lived flexibly without the costs of rigid obedience. The alternative is a freer, more fulfilling life where we put values into play in ways that enhance well-being.

## Key facts

- Karen Horney called this 'the tyranny of the shoulds' and Albert Ellis called it 'musturbation'. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p288]
- When we obey rigid rules, it helps us temporarily avoid anxiety and other uncomfortable feelings but leads to chronic stress, exhaustion, burnout, and lack of fulfillment. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p287]
- Underneath rigid rules there are always important values that can be put into play flexibly. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p288]
- We can learn to act on values flexibly in ways that enhance our well-being without the costs of rigid rules. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p288]
