# Psychical Genesis of Homosexuality

## Summary

Freud's theory of the psychical genesis of homosexuality posits that male homosexual patients had an intense erotic attachment to their mother during early childhood, which later undergoes repression. The child then identifies with the mother and takes his own person as a model for choosing new love objects, finding them along the path of narcissism. This theory is applied to Leonardo, explaining his vulture phantasy as indicating 'It was through this erotic relation with my mother that I became a homosexual.'

## Key facts

- Psychoanalytic investigation found that 'in all our male homosexual cases the subjects had had a very intense erotic attachment to a female person, as a rule their mothers, during the first period of childhood, which is afterwards forgotten.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3534]
- This attachment was 'evoked or encouraged by too much tenderness on the part of the mother herself, and further reinforced by the small part played by the father during their childhood.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3534]
- The transformation mechanism: 'The child represses his love for his mother: he puts himself in her place, identifies himself with her, and takes his own person as a model in whose likeness he chooses the new objects of his love.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3535]
- Freud uses the term 'narcissism' to explain object choice: 'What he has in fact done is to slip back to auto-erotism: for the boys whom he now loves as he grows up are after all only substitutive figures and revivals of himself in childhood – boys whom he loves in the way in which his mother loved him when he was a child.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3535]
- Freud translates Leonardo's vulture phantasy as meaning: 'It was through this erotic relation with my mother that I became a homosexual.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3539]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud infers that 'a man who has become a homosexual in this way remains unconsciously fixated to the mnemic image of his mother' and 'while he seems to pursue boys and to be their lover, he is in reality running away from the other women, who might cause him to be unfaithful.'

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud acknowledges 'we know that they [these explanations] are not sufficiently comprehensive to make a conclusive explanation of the problem possible' and that 'the particular process we have singled out is perhaps only one among many, and is perhaps related to only one type of homosexuality.'
