# Developmental Parcellation

## Summary

Developmental parcellation is the process by which brain regions are partitioned during development through competitive interactions between axonal projections. Terrence Deacon argues that initial connectional nonspecificity and axonal competition introduce global biases in neural computations that result from global patterns of regional parcellation, and that large-scale quantitative relationships determined by this developmental Darwinism play a major role in producing species differences. The sizes of brain structures are not independently determined but are competitively parceled by afferent axons with respect to other brain areas during development.
