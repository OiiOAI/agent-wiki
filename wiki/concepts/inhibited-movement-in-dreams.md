# Inhibited Movement in Dreams

## Summary

The sensation of inhibited movement in dreams (trying to move forward but being glued to the spot, or raising a hand to avenge an insult but finding it powerless) represents a conflict of will and the expression of a contradiction ('no'). Freud argued this sensation is not merely due to motor paralysis during sleep but serves particular representational purposes.

## Key facts

- The 'not being able to do something' in dreams is a way of expressing a contradiction—a 'no'—and in dreams where it appears as a sensation, it gives more forcible expression to a conflict of will with a counter-volition. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p337]
- The sensation of inhibition of movement represents a conflict of will, as an impulse along motor paths is nothing other than a volition, and the certain expectation of feeling that impulse inhibited during sleep makes the process suited for representing volition and 'no'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p338]
- The sensation approximates closely to anxiety because anxiety is a libidinal impulse from the unconscious inhibited by the preconscious; when linked with anxiety in dreams, it represents a sexual impulse. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p338]
- Freud's earlier statement that dreams cannot express a 'no' requires correction based on the 'not being able to do something' phenomenon. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p337]

## Inferences

- Inference: The motor paralysis accompanying sleep is one of the fundamental determinants of the psychical process during dreaming, which is why inhibited movement serves as an excellent representation of suppressed material.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud's footnote from 1930 indicates his statement about anxiety being a libidinal impulse from the unconscious inhibited by the preconscious can no longer stand in light of later knowledge.
