# RULER

## Summary

RULER is an evidence-based approach to emotional intelligence developed at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence. The acronym stands for five skills: Recognize (identifying emotions in oneself and others), Understand (discovering the causes and consequences of feelings), Label (precisely naming emotions), Express (sharing feelings appropriately), and Regulate (managing emotions effectively). The framework positions Labeling as the pivot point connecting Recognition and Understanding with Expression and Regulation.

## Key facts

- RULER was developed at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence under Marc Brackett's direction. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p97]
- Labeling is positioned as the pivot point in RULER, connecting Recognition and Understanding with Expression and Regulation. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p97]
- In RULER, the Feeling Words Curriculum teaches children a minimum of eighty-four terms that describe specific feelings, with the goal that every child will learn this vocabulary by middle school. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p104]
- Brackett notes that people tend to be better at R (Recognition), U (Understanding), and L (Labeling) than at E (Expression) or R (Regulation), which involve putting insights into action. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p147]
- RULER stands for Recognizing emotions, Understanding their causes and consequences, Labeling emotions, Expressing emotions, and Regulating emotions. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p183]
- The Mood Meter is a central RULER tool featuring a four-quadrant framework: yellow (high energy, positive), green (low energy, positive), red (high energy, negative), and blue (low energy, negative). [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p181]
- RULER includes creating an Emotional Intelligence Charter—a collaborative document developed by students and teachers describing how they want to feel and how they'll support each other. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p182]
- Meta-Moment is a RULER strategy where one pauses to imagine how their 'best self' would respond before reacting to a triggering situation. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p166]
- RULER research shows positive impact on students' emotional intelligence skills, social problem-solving ability, work habits, grades, classroom and school climate, and teacher stress and burnout. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p186]
