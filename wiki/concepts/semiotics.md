# Semiotics

## Summary

Semiotics is the study of sign systems, encompassing not only language but also other sign and symbol systems including facial expressions, body language, literary texts, and all forms of communication. Roland Barthes is seen as the true founder of semiotics, extending Saussure's ideas to all areas of social life. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p504]

## Key facts

- Semiotics is broader than structural linguistics, encompassing all sign and symbol systems. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p504]
- Roland Barthes extended Saussure's ideas to all areas of social life, seeing social behaviors as representations or signs. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p504]
