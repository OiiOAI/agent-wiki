# Waiting For List

## Summary

The Waiting For list tracks all commitments delegated to others that require follow-up. It includes final deliverables or projects others are responsible for, with dates of requests and any agreed-upon due dates.

## Key facts

- The Waiting For list tracks deliverables or projects that others are responsible for, triggering follow-up when needed. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p205]
- It is important to include the date of request and any agreed-upon due date for each item on the Waiting For list. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p205]
- The Waiting For list should be kept close at hand with Next Actions reminder lists for integrated review. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p205]
- Having a complete Waiting For list creates relief and improved focus by knowing all commitments from others being tracked. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p205]
