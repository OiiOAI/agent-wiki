# Mood Meter

## Summary

The Mood Meter is a tool for emotion recognition based on James Russell's circumplex model of emotion. It plots emotions along two dimensions—pleasantness (horizontal axis) and energy (vertical axis)—creating four quadrants: Yellow (high pleasantness, high energy), Red (high energy, low pleasantness), Green (high pleasantness, low energy), and Blue (low pleasantness, low energy). It allows users to chart hundreds of emotions and understand their emotional states at a glance.

## Key facts

- The Mood Meter was built on James Russell's circumplex model of emotion, which proposes that emotions have two core properties: energy and pleasantness. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p67]
- The Yellow quadrant represents high pleasantness and high energy—emotions like happy, excited, and optimistic. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p70]
- The Red quadrant represents low pleasantness but high energy—emotions including angry, anxious, frustrated, and scared. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p70]
- The Green quadrant represents high pleasantness but low energy—emotions such as peaceful, contented, and serene. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p70]
- The Blue quadrant represents low pleasantness and low energy—emotions including sadness, apathy, depression, empathy, and concern. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p70-71]
- The Mood Meter was first used in The Emotionally Intelligent Manager (2004) by David Caruso and Peter Salovey. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p68]
