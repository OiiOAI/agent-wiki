# The Meaning of Life

## Summary

Ravikant offers three perspectives on meaning: (1) it is personal and must be found individually, (2) there is no inherent meaning and we must create our own, and (3) from a physics perspective, life locally reverses entropy while globally accelerating the heat death of the Universe.

## Key facts

- The meaning of life is personal; you have to find your own meaning. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p159]
- There is no fundamental, intrinsic purposeful meaning to the Universe. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p160]
- After death, it will be just like before you were born—caring about nothing. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p160]
- Living systems locally reverse entropy while globally accelerating the heat death of the Universe. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p161]
