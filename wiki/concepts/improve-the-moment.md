# IMPROVE the Moment

## Summary

IMPROVE is a distress tolerance mnemonic for strategies to improve the quality of the present moment during crisis, making it easier to survive without making things worse. The seven strategies are Imagery, Meaning, Prayer, Relaxation, One thing in the moment, Vacation, and Encouragement (self-talk/rethinking).

## Key facts

- IMPROVE stands for: Imagery, Meaning, Prayer, Relaxation, One thing in the moment, Vacation, Encouragement/self-Encouragement. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p360]
- Imagery involves imagining relaxing scenes, a secret safe room, or things going well. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p360]
- Meaning involves finding purpose in painful situations and focusing on positive aspects. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p360]
- Vacation means giving yourself a brief break—getting in bed, going to the beach, taking a 1-hour breather. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p360]
