# The Oprah Paradox

## Summary

The Oprah Paradox describes the observation that even people with excellent self-control can struggle to consistently control their weight. Despite having extraordinary willpower in other life domains, personal resources, access to the finest professional advice, and constant monitoring from public exposure, Oprah Winfrey could not maintain long-term weight loss. Research analyzing dozens of studies found that self-disciplined people do only slightly better than average at controlling their weight—a much smaller advantage than they have in other areas of life.

## Key facts

- Despite personal trainers, chefs, nutritionists, counselors, and constant public monitoring, Oprah Winfrey could not maintain long-term weight loss. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p198]
- People with high self-control do only slightly better than average at controlling their weight—much less than their advantages in other life areas. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p199]
- The King Henry VIII and Oprah Winfrey Effect describes how thinness has become a status symbol because it is so difficult for ordinary people to achieve without extensive resources. - Dieting causes the body to assume famine and fight to keep fat cells, with repeated dieting cycles making weight loss progressively more difficult. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p200]
