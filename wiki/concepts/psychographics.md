# Psychographics

## Summary

Psychographics is the science of perceived marketplace reality that explains why customers buy. It examines the unconscious motivations, expectations, perceptions, attitudes, and beliefs that drive purchasing decisions. These psychological factors often override rational considerations in the buying process.

## Key facts

- Psychographics is the science of perceived marketplace reality that tells you why your customer buys. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p155]
- The customer has a Conscious Mind (antenna/sensor gathering information) and an Unconscious Mind (vast sea of expectations that determines yes or no decisions). [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p219]
- 75 percent of buying decisions in print ads are made at the headline alone; in sales presentations, the sale is made or lost in the first three minutes. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p221]
