# Demographics

## Summary

Demographics is the science of marketplace reality that identifies who the customer is through measurable characteristics such as age, sex, income, family status, education, and profession. Every business has a Central Demographic Model representing its most probable customer. This scientific foundation enables understanding of customer behavior.

## Key facts

- Demographics is the science of marketplace reality that tells you who your customer is. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p155]
- Every business has a Central Demographic Model—a most probable customer with a whole set of characteristics including age, sex, income, family status, education, and profession. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p155]
- To determine your Central Demographic Model, you collect addresses from customers via questionnaire, identify them on a map, and draw a Trading Zone around them. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p229]
- Like attracts like; a man attracts women who share his values, interests, education, and success level. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p115]
- When demographics don't match up, friction prevents attraction from turning into intimacy or sex. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p116]
- Social proof only functions within a demographic; once you've narrowed your demographic, cultivate leadership positions within it. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p129]
- Age, money, and looks matter but less than most men think; they are universal demographics where incompatibility causes extra friction. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p126]
- The more money/looks/success you have, the less attractive behavior you need; the less you have, the more attractive behavior you need. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p128]
- Demographics is the idea that you will experience greater success (happiness) and efficiency by pursuing women in areas of your life that you excel at or enjoy. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
- For instance, if you are a musician, then you are more likely to experience dating success by meeting women at concerts and music events. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p266]
