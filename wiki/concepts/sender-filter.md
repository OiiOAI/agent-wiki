# Sender Filter

## Summary

A sender filter is an approach to managing email where you ask correspondents to filter themselves before attempting to contact you. Instead of listing a general-purpose email address, you offer only special-purpose addresses that come with conditions and lowered expectations that you'll respond. This approach resets correspondents' expectations so that the inbox becomes a collection of opportunities rather than obligations.

## Key facts

- Cal Newport's author website lists different individuals for specific purposes rather than a general-purpose email address. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p143]
- Newport's sender filter states: 'I'll only respond to those proposals that are a good match for my schedule and interests.'. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p143]
- Sender filters drastically reduced the number of long, advice-seeking emails Newport received, eliminating messages that would require extensive explanation and writing to respond to. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p143]
- Consultant Clay Herbert uses a FAQ and survey to screen correspondents before allowing communication, then enforces a small fee for serious engagement. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p145]
- Antonio Centeno diverts question-askers to a public location and requires visitors to click checkboxes promising not to ask questions answerable via Google, not to spam, and to do a good deed if responded to within 23 hours. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p145]

## Inferences

- Inference: Sender filters work because they transform the inbox from a collection of obligations into a collection of opportunities that you can engage with on your own terms.
