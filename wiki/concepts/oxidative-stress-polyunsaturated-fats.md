# Oxidative Stress and Polyunsaturated Fats

## Summary

Polyunsaturated fats are highly vulnerable to oxidation, which creates free radicals that cause chain-reaction damage throughout the body. Oxidized polyunsaturated oils in processed foods generate aldehydes and other toxins linked to Alzheimer's disease, mitochondrial damage, and cognitive impairment.

## Key facts

- Radicals are typically 10,000 times more reactive than non-radical molecules and attack nearly all biological molecules. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p50]
- Aldehydes, by-products of fat oxidation, have been found in elevated amounts in Alzheimer's-riddled brains. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p52]
- Consumption of polyunsaturated oil-rich meals causes circulating markers of fat oxidation to increase by about 50% in young people and up to 15-fold in older subjects. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p52]
- Omega-3 fats are more vulnerable to oxidation than omega-6 fats. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p54]
