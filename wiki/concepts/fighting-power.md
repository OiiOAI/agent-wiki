# Fighting Power

## Summary

Martin van Creveld's 1982 work 'Fighting Power' examines military effectiveness through the lens of organizational capability. The book could almost be titled 'Organizational Power' since its focus is on creating organizations capable of generating the high levels of energy and initiative needed to execute Boyd-type strategies.
