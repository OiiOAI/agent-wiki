# Clean Mind and Clear Mind

## Summary

Two contrasting mental states in DBT addiction recovery. Clean Mind refers to behaviors and thoughts that maintain or enable addiction, while Clear Mind refers to the mental state achieved through practicing DBT skills, opposite action, and emotional regulation.

## Key facts

- Clean Mind behaviors include engaging in apparently irrelevant behaviors that lead to problem behaviors, thinking 'I've learned my lesson,' believing 'I can control my addiction,' being in addiction environments, seeing friends who are still addicted, keeping addiction paraphernalia, isolating, and believing 'I can do this alone.'. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p407]
- The worksheet instructs individuals to identify Clean Mind behaviors they engage in and replace them with corresponding Clear Mind behaviors. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p407]
- Clear Mind replacements include stopping or cutting back medication, practicing DBT skills consistently, seeking support from non-addicted friends, engaging in healthy activities, and using opposite action and emotion regulation strategies. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p407]
