# The Now

## Summary

The Now is the only moment that is real—past and future are merely mental constructs. The present moment is described as 'precious' because it is the only point of access into the timeless realm of Being. Time itself is called an illusion, and psychological time (attachment to past and future) is identified as the cause of suffering. The practice involves making the Now the primary focus of life while still using clock time for practical purposes. Acceptance of the present moment, saying 'yes' to what is, is presented as the path to freedom from pain.

## Key facts

- Time is described as an illusion; only the present moment is real. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p56]
- The present moment is described as 'the most precious thing' because it is the only point of access into the timeless realm of Being. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p56]
- Nothing ever happened in the past; it happened in the Now. Nothing will ever happen in the future; it will happen in the Now. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p57]
- Past and future are described as having no reality of their own, like the moon reflecting the sun's light. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p58]
- Meister Eckhart stated: 'Time is what keeps the light from reaching us. There is no greater obstacle to God than time'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p59]
