# Grey Scale Phone Display

## Summary

Grey scale phone display is a life hack that removes the positive psychological reinforcement built into colorful app icons. The book explains that grey tones are the least attractive colors, making phone usage less fun.

## Key facts

- Colorful icons on phones are part of positive psychological reinforcement designed to attract users to tap on them. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p129]
- Grey scale is described as the least attractive color option for smartphone interfaces. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p129]
- Studies show that when icons are in grey scale, the positive reinforcement in the design of those icons is taken away, removing one of the tools that steal attention. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p129]
- Grey scale color tones are preinstalled in most devices through phone settings under color filters. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p129]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book frames grey scale as equivalent to 'turning off one of the pleasure switches' on a device, suggesting that visual stimulation is a key component of phone addiction.
