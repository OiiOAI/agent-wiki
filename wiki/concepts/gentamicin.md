# Gentamicin-Induced Vestibular Damage

## Summary

Gentamicin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic that can poison inner ear structures when used excessively, causing hearing loss, tinnitus, and devastation to the balance system. Despite these risks, it remains prescribed because it is cheap and effective, though usually for brief periods only. Patients who lose vestibular function from gentamicin are colloquially called 'Wobblers' and often experience perpetual falling sensations, psychological breakdown, and high suicide rates.
