# Womb Phantasy and Rebirth Phantasy

## Summary

These two related phantasies express unconscious wishes tied to the Oedipus complex. The womb phantasy expresses a wish to be inside the mother's womb in order to replace her during intercourse with the father—to take her place. The phantasy of rebirth is a softened (euphemistic) substitution for the phantasy of incestuous intercourse with the mother. In the patient, the womb phantasy was expressed as the complaint that the world was hidden from him by a veil, while the veil being torn during evacuation represented rebirth. The necessary condition for his recovery was identified with the primal scene: being sexually satisfied by his father and bearing him a child.

## Key facts

- The patient complained that for him the world was hidden in a veil; the veil was torn only when, after an enema, the contents of the bowel left the intestinal canal, and he then felt well and normal again. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5330]
- The patient remembered having been born with a caul, which was the 'veil' that hid him from the world and the world from him; this complaint was a fulfilled wishful phantasy exhibiting him as back once more in the womb. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5352]
- The necessary condition of his rebirth was that he should have an enema administered by a man, which could only mean he had identified himself with his mother, the man acting as his father, and the enema repeating the act of copulation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5353]
- The womb phantasy is frequently derived from an attachment to the father—there is a wish to be inside the mother's womb in order to replace her during intercourse. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5354]
- The phantasy of rebirth is regularly a softened (euphemistic) substitute for the phantasy of incestuous intercourse with the mother; it is an anagogic abbreviation of it. ## Inferences

- Inference: The two phantasies give expression, according to the subject's attitude being feminine or masculine, to the wish for sexual intercourse with the father or with the mother, and in the patient they were united.
