# Amyloid Plaques in Alzheimer Disease

## Summary

Amyloid plaques are extracellular deposits composed predominantly of aggregated amyloid-beta (Aβ) peptides, particularly the Aβ42 form. These plaques are a hallmark pathology of Alzheimer disease. Aβ peptides are generated by proteolytic cleavage of amyloid precursor protein (APP) by beta and gamma secretases. The Aβ42 peptide nucleates more rapidly into amyloid fibrils than shorter forms, and aggregated forms of Aβ are toxic to neurons in culture.
