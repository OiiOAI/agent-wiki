# Epigenetics

## Summary

Epigenetics is the study of how lifestyle choices affect gene expression through chemical modifications to DNA. While genes cannot be changed, the epigenome can be influenced by diet, exercise, sleep, and other factors, determining which genes are activated or silenced.

## Key facts

- Genetic differences account for less than 1 percent of total genetic variation between individuals. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p30]
- The epigenome is a layer of chemicals sitting atop DNA that tells genes what to do and responds dynamically to environmental inputs including diet. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p31]
- Diet is one of the primary 'maestros' that plays on the genetic keyboard, influencing whether genes are expressed optimally. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p31]
- The 2004 Meaney et al. study showed that offspring of more attentive rat mothers become adults with lower glucocorticoid levels, less anxiety, and better learning, with these changes being epigenetic. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p229]
- Meaney and Francis showed that attentive rat mothers produce offspring who, as adults, are more attentive mothers themselves, passing the trait epigenetically to the next generation. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p229]
- Childhood abuse causes epigenetic changes in hundreds of genes in the human hippocampus. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p229]
- Mothering style in monkeys has epigenetic effects on more than a thousand frontocortical genes. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p229]
- Epigenetic changes can be multigenerational; making male mice diabetic causes them to pass the trait to offspring via epigenetic changes in sperm. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p238]
- Epigenetic changes in gene regulation can be influenced by environmental factors and may be transmitted across generations. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1021]
- This nongenetic but epigenetic transmission of traits multigenerationally resembles ideas proposed by Jean-Baptiste Lamarck. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1021]
- The 5HTT gene variants associated with stress sensitivity differ not in the DNA sequence of the gene but in the promoter sequence affecting gene expression. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1047]

## Inferences

- Inference: The text infers that genes do not determine destiny—they merely predict what the Standard American Diet will do to an individual, implying lifestyle modifications can override genetic predispositions.
- Inference: The discovery of epigenetic inheritance represents vindication of Lamarck's narrow domain of acquired characteristics being passed to offspring.
- Inference: Epigenetics blurs the line between 'environment' and 'heredity' by showing environmental experiences can directly modify gene transmission.
