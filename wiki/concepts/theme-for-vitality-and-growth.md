# Theme for Vitality and Growth

## Summary

The Theme for Vitality and Growth is Boyd's constructive complement to the Theme for Disintegration and Collapse. It addresses how organic wholes (individuals, groups, societies, nations) can improve their fitness to shape and expand influence over the course of events. The theme emphasizes the need for a unifying vision rooted in human nature that attracts the uncommitted, pumps up friendly resolve, and undermines adversary determination.

## Key facts

- The Theme for Vitality and Growth requires a unifying vision: a grand ideal, overarching theme, or noble philosophy representing a coherent paradigm within which individuals and societies can shape and adapt to unfolding circumstances. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p236]
- Ingredients needed to pursue this vision include insight (ability to discern inner nature of things), initiative (internal drive to think and act), adaptability (power to adjust to new circumstances), and harmony (perceiving interaction of disconnected entities). [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p236]
- The aim is to improve fitness as an organic whole to shape and expand influence or power over the course of events in the world. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p236]
- Boyd emphasized that for long-term success under difficult conditions, one needs a unifying vision so noble and attractive that it not only attracts the uncommitted and magnifies spirit and strength of adherents, but also undermines dedication and determination of competitors. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p236]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Theme for Vitality and Growth represents Boyd's recognition that destructive strategies must be balanced with constructive elements for national survival and long-term competitive success.
