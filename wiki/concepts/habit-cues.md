# Habit Cues

## Summary

Habit cues are triggers that initiate habitual behavior. Three types are discussed: time-based cues (specific times), activity-based cues (after specific activities), and freedom-based/general cues (any time before a deadline). Traditional habit theory emphasizes specific cues, but mini habits can work without specific cues because they are too small to fail and naturally develop multiple cues over time.

## Key facts

- The two common habit cues are time-based ('exercise MWF at 3 PM') and activity-based ('exercise 30 minutes after lunch'). [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p89]
- Time-based schedules are rigid with little ambiguity, helping build discipline but lacking flexibility when life throws off schedules. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p89]
- Activity-based cues are more flexible but have ambiguity about when one activity ends and another begins. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p89]
- Freedom-based or general mini habits require doing the small requirement once per day at leisure, with only a 24-hour deadline (before sleep). [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p91]
- Mini habits can develop multiple habit cues over time since they are so small and easy to perform in various situations. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p93]
- The difficulty of a behavior is a primary determinant of how long it takes to make it into a habit, meaning mini habits can become habitual faster than traditional habits. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p93]

## Inferences

- Inference: Traditional habits need single specific cues because they require too much willpower to execute from multiple cues. Mini habits' low willpower cost allows them to naturally grow multiple cues like 'wild' bad habits do.
