# Free Association in Dream Interpretation

## Summary

The technique developed by Freud for interpreting dreams by having the dreamer report all thoughts occurring in connection with each element of the dream, bypassing critical judgment to reveal the dream's hidden meaning.

## Key facts

- The dream interpreter must bring about two changes in the patient: an increase in attention paid to psychical perceptions and the elimination of criticism that normally sifts thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p101]
- The self-observer need only take the trouble to suppress the critical faculty; if successful, innumerable ideas become accessible that would otherwise never be perceived. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p102]
- The method of dream interpretation differs from symbolic interpretation by treating dreams as composite formations requiring interpretation piece by piece. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p104]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud derived this technique from his psychoanalytic work with hysterical patients, where symptom removal coincided with unravelling the mental connections underlying them.
