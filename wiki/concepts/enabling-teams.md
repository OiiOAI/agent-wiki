# Enabling Teams

## Summary

Enabling teams are one of the four team patterns in Team Topologies. They serve other teams as service providers, helping stream-aligned teams learn new techniques, investigate new technologies, and grow in capability, allowing stream-aligned teams to retain focus while growing effectiveness.
