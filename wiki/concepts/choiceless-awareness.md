# Choiceless Awareness

## Summary

Choiceless Awareness (or Nonjudgmental Awareness) is a meditation practice where one accepts the present moment without making judgments or decisions, observing thoughts and sensations as they arise without preference or resistance.

## Key facts

- In Choiceless Awareness, you accept the moment you are in without making judgments. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p140]
- Ravikant reports that 90 percent of his thoughts are fear-based and 10 percent desire-based. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p140]
- After practicing, he ends up in a peaceful, grateful state. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p140]
