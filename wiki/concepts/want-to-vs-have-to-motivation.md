# Want-to vs. Have-to Motivation

## Summary

Want-to motivation reflects genuine interest, identified importance, or integration with core identity. These goals are freely chosen and pursued because of personal enjoyment or inherent importance. Have-to motivation is imposed by nagging, obligation, or internal narratives typically related to avoiding shame. Want-to motivation is associated with lower automatic attraction toward tempting stimuli and fewer perceived obstacles. Have-to motivation can actually undermine self-control and increase vulnerability to unwanted behaviors. Finding the 'want to' hidden in the 'have to' makes it easier to choose things that lead to the life one wants.

- Want-to goals reflect genuine interest, identified importance, or integration with core identity; they are freely chosen. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p123]
