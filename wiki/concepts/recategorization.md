# Recategorization

## Summary

Recategorization is a tool for mastering emotions in the moment by deconstructing affective feelings into their physical sensations and then recategorizing them using a richer set of concepts. With practice, one can learn to deconstruct an affective feeling into physical sensations rather than letting those sensations be a filter through which one views the world. For example, anxiety can be recategorized as excitement, or pounding heart can be recategorized as anticipation rather than nervousness. Research shows that students achieve higher scores on math tests when they recategorize anxiety as a sign that the body is coping. The head of a karate school advises nervous students before black belt tests: 'Make your butterflies fly in formation.'

## Key facts

- Recategorization is a tool of the emotion expert, allowing people to deconstruct an affective feeling into physical sensations and then recategorize them in other ways. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p209]
- Students achieve higher scores when they recategorize anxiety as merely a sign that the body is coping. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p210]
- People who recategorize anxiety as excitement show better performance and fewer classic symptoms of anxiety when speaking in public and singing karaoke. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p210]
- Recategorization can bring tangible benefits including improved exam grades and final course grades for community college math students. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p210]
