# Short-Chain Fatty Acids

## Summary

Short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) are produced when gut bacteria metabolize fiber. They include butyrate, acetate, and propionate, all linked to longevity and health-promoting effects. Butyrate, the most extensively studied, has been shown to raise BDNF levels (promoting neuroplasticity), reduce inflammation, and support regulatory T-cells that help the immune system distinguish between self and foreign substances.

## Key facts

- Short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) include butyrate, acetate, and propionate, all of which have been linked to many longevity- and health-promoting effects. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p237]
- Butyrate has been shown to raise levels of BDNF, which directly promotes neuroplasticity and slows neurodegenerative processes. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p237]
- A high-fiber diet makes the microbiota begin to resemble an inflammation-extinguishing butyrate factory. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p237]
- SCFAs like butyrate increase the production and development of regulatory T-cells (T-regs) in the colon, which help ensure a healthy and appropriate inflammatory response. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p245]
