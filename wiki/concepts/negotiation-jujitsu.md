# Negotiation Jujitsu

## Summary

Negotiation Jujitsu is a strategy for dealing with positional bargainers who refuse to negotiate on the merits. Rather than pushing back against attacks, the practitioner sidesteps and deflects the attack against the problem itself. The technique draws from Oriental martial arts: instead of resisting force directly, one channels it into exploring interests, inventing options, and searching for independent standards.

## Key facts

- When they assert their positions, do not reject them. When they attack your ideas, don't defend them. When they attack you, don't counterattack. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p55]
- Use questions instead of statements. Statements generate resistance, whereas questions generate answers. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p56]
- Silence is one of your best weapons. If they have made an unreasonable proposal or an attack you regard as unjustified, the best thing to do may be to sit there and not say a word. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p56]
- When the other side attacks you personally, recast their attack on you as an attack on the problem. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p56]
- Negotiation jujitsu deals with three basic maneuvers: asserting positions forcefully, attacking your ideas, and attacking you. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p55]
