# Glucose and Willpower

## Summary

Research demonstrates that glucose is the biological fuel for willpower. When people exert self-control, glucose levels in the bloodstream drop. Experiments with lemonade (sugar vs. diet sweetener) show that glucose restores self-control while artificial sweeteners do not. This explains why hypoglycemia correlates with impulsivity and criminal behavior, and why PMS worsens self-control problems as energy is diverted to reproduction.

## Key facts

- The brain uses glucose to produce neurotransmitters, the chemicals brain cells use to send signals. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p47]
- Studies found below-average glucose levels in 90% of juvenile delinquents recently taken into custody. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p48]
- Sugar drinks temporarily restore willpower in ego-depleted subjects, while diet drinks have no effect. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p51]
- Diabetics tend to be more impulsive and have more explosive temperaments than others their age. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p49]
- Children who ate breakfast performed better and misbehaved less in school than those who skipped breakfast. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p50]
