# Accomplishing Gender

## Summary

Accomplishing gender is an ethnomethodological perspective arguing that gender, rather than being biologically determined, is an ongoing social accomplishment. Garfinkel's famous study of Agnes demonstrated how a person assigned male at birth could learn to pass as a woman through acquiring and performing the accepted practices of femininity. This shows that we all learn and routinely use commonplace practices that allow us to pass as men or women.

## Key facts

- Ethnomethodology argues that gender is not simply biological but is an accomplishment achieved through situated practices. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p291]
- Garfinkel studied Agnes, who was defined as male at birth but at age 16 began dressing like a girl and eventually sought sex reassignment surgery. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p292]
- Agnes discovered that dressing like a woman was not enough—she had to learn to act like a woman to be accepted as one. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p292]
- We are not simply born men or women; we all learn and routinely use commonplace practices that allow us to pass as men or women. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p292]
- Even a category like gender, thought to be an ascribed status, can be understood as an accomplishment of situated practices. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p292]
