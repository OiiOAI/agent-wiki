# Sexual Tension

## Summary

Defined as tension arising from the uncertainty of potential sexual possibilities in an interaction. Sexual tension can be generated through teasing (mixed signals) or bold/vulnerable directness.

## Key facts

- Sexual tension builds when the uncertainty of potential sexual possibilities is presented in an interaction. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p193]
- Teasing type behaviors generate sexual tension because they generate uncertainty as to whether or not you're actually interested in a woman—sending 'Yes, I like you' and 'No, I don't' signals simultaneously. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p193]
- Flirting is expressing your sexuality to a woman in a way that makes her feel secure expressing her sexuality back towards you. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p193]
