# Joy of Being

## Summary

Joy of Being is a deep state of well-being that arises when one is free from identification with the mind. Unlike pleasure (which comes from external sources and turns into pain), joy arises from within and has no opposite. It is one of three aspects of inner connectedness with Being (along with love and peace). The book states that glimpses of joy are possible whenever a gap occurs in the stream of thought. True joy cannot be found in achievement, possessions, or relationships—it can only be accessed by being present. When compulsive striving away from the Now ceases, the joy of Being flows into everything one does.

## Key facts

- Joy arises from within, unlike pleasure which is always derived from something outside. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p40]
- Joy, love, and peace are 'deep states of Being' that arise from beyond the mind and have no opposite. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p40]
- Real love and real joy 'don't make you suffer' and cannot 'turn into' their opposites. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p40]
- When compulsive striving away from the Now ceases, 'the joy of Being flows into everything you do'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p73]
- Without psychological time, 'your sense of self is derived from Being, not from your personal past'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p73]
