# Legacy and Mortality

## Summary

Ravikant argues there is no legacy to leave—civilizations, works, and even humanity will eventually become dust. This acknowledgment of futility can bring great happiness and peace by revealing life as a game to be enjoyed.

## Key facts

- There is no legacy; our children will be gone, our works will be dust, our civilizations will be dust. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p127]
- Your life is a firefly blink in a night. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p127]
- Acknowledging the futility of what you are doing can bring great happiness and peace. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p127]
- Keeping death on the forefront and not denying it is very important. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p127]
