# Nonresistance

## Summary

Nonresistance is the practice of not opposing what is. It applies primarily to one's inner life rather than external conduct. The ego believes resistance is strength, but in truth resistance cuts you off from Being, the only place of true power. Nonresistance doesn't mean doing nothing; it means any 'doing' becomes nonreactive.

## Key facts

- Nonresistance only needs to be concerned with the inner aspect, which is primary, and that will transform the conduct of your outer life. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p190]
- Nonresistance doesn't necessarily mean doing nothing; all it means is that any 'doing' becomes nonreactive. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
- The deep wisdom underlying Eastern martial arts is: Don't resist the opponent's force. Yield to overcome. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
- The ego believes that in your resistance lies your strength, whereas in truth resistance cuts you off from Being, the only place of true power. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
- Resistance is weakness and fear masquerading as strength. What the ego sees as weakness is your Being in its purity, innocence, and power. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
- The ego exists in a continuous resistance-mode and plays counterfeit roles to cover up your 'weakness,' which in truth is your power. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
- In surrender, you no longer need ego defenses and false masks. You become very simple, very real. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p191]
