# PreMortem

## Summary

A PreMortem is a technique where team members anticipate potential failures before implementing a plan. Participants write down ways the plan could fail, helping identify blind spots and strengthening the statement of intent. It shifts team members from passive listeners to active anticipators.

## Key facts

- In workshops, participants issue actual intent statements they will use with their work teams, and other participants perform a PreMortem to identify potential confusions. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p256]
- The PreMortem exercise helps participants recognize how vague their intent statements actually are, which they often don't see until receiving feedback. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p256]
- For important tasks, it is worth the extra time to do a PreMortem on the task and intent, giving everyone a few minutes to write down ways they can become confused or mishandle the request. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p256]

- Inference: The PreMortem technique is particularly valuable when combined with the STICC framework, as it addresses the calibration step by proactively identifying misunderstanding risks.
