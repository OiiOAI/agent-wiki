# Liminal Cynefin

## Summary

Liminal Cynefin is an extended version of the original Cynefin Framework that addresses liminal spaces between domains. The concept relates to states of transition where something is neither one thing nor another, neither known nor unknown—a 'gestation' stage of complex becoming, connected to notions of threshold and in-between states.

## Key facts

- Dave Snowden posted a version of liminal Cynefin in October 2017, announcing it as 'More or less the final version of Liminal Cynefin with disorder resolved.'. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p375]
- The liminal spaces in Cynefin are described as places of forming and reforming, where not knowing and unknowing collide—like gestation or pregnancy. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p375]
- The concept relates to the Hebrew term 'tohu va-vohu' (formless and void) from Genesis 1:2, describing a state at the beginning of creation. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p375]
- Dave Snowden commented that liminality is not a one-way process but involves mask-donning and oscillation. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p376]
