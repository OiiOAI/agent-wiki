# Psychiatric Credo

## Summary

Frankl's psychiatric credo states that there is nothing conceivable which would so condition a man as to leave him without the slightest freedom. A residue of freedom, however limited it may be, is left to man in neurotic and even psychotic cases. The innermost core of the patient's personality is not even touched by a psychosis. An incurably psychotic individual may lose his usefulness but yet retains the dignity of a human being. Without this belief, Frankl says, euthanasia would be justified.
