# Prefrontal Cortex

## Summary

The region of the cortex at the front of the brain that evolved most recently and serves as the CEO of most brain functions, overseeing planning, sequencing, rehearsing, evaluating, understanding, and working memory.

## Key facts

- The prefrontal cortex is the region of the cortex located at the very front of the brain. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p225]
- As the last portion of gray matter to have evolved, the prefrontal cortex oversees the qualities that make us most human. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p225]
- It is the CEO of most brain functions, including planning, sequencing, rehearsing, evaluating, and understanding. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p225]
- The prefrontal cortex is the home of working memory, the brain's RAM, which is crucial to decision making. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p225]
- The prefrontal cortex is distinguished from the rest of the frontal cortex by having an additional layer of neurons (internal granular layer IV). [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p28]
- The prefrontal white matter is disproportionately larger in humans than other primates, suggesting higher connectivity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p29]
- Nonprimate mammals have two major regions of prefrontal cortex (orbital and anterior cingulate), while primates have three - the added lateral granular prefrontal cortex is concerned with rational decision-making. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p29]
