# Symptom Formation

## Summary

The construction of a symptom is a substitute for something else that did not happen. When a mental process should normally have developed to consciousness but did not take place, the symptom emerges from the interrupted, disturbed processes that remained unconscious. Neurotic symptoms have a 'sense' (meaning) that relates to the patient's experiences, wishes, and sexual life. Symptoms serve as substitutes for sexual satisfaction that is frustrated in real life.
