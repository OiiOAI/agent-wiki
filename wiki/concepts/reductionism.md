# Reductionism

## Summary

Reductionism is the philosophy that higher-level phenomena can be fully explained by lower-level physical interactions. Mitchell argues against a purely reductionist, mechanistic approach to life, claiming it 'completely misses the point' because basic physical laws dealing only with energy and matter cannot explain what life is or its defining property of agency.

## Key facts

- Reductionism implies biology is just complicated chemistry, chemistry is just complicated physics, and causation only flows up from lowest levels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p11]
- Mitchell states that a purely reductionist approach to life completely misses the point. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p11]
- Living organisms are driven not by energy but by information, and the meaning of that information is embodied in the structure of the system based on its history. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p11]
- Sean Carroll maintains that complete truth resides at the lowest level with fundamental physical interactions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p15]
