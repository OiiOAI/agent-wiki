# Attentional Tunneling

## Summary

Attentional tunneling (or attentional narrowing) occurs when people lock in on certain aspects or features of the environment they are trying to process and drop their systematic scanning behavior. Their SA may be good on the concentrated aspects but quickly becomes outdated on neglected aspects. This is one of the eight SA demons identified in the book. In studying aircraft and air traffic control accidents, 35% of all SA errors fell into this category.

## Key facts

- Attentional tunneling occurs when people lock in on certain aspects of the environment and drop their scanning behavior. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p52]
- 35% of all SA errors in aviation involved attentional tunneling—where all needed information was present but not attended to. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p53]
- The Eastern Airlines crash into the Florida Everglades is a well-known example where crew fixated on an indicator light and neglected to monitor the aircraft's flight path. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p52]
- Cell phone use while driving can quadruple accident risk due to attentional distraction. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p53]
