# Battle of the Sexes

## Summary

The 'battle of the sexes' refers to evolutionary conflicts of interest between males and females over reproductive investment. This chunk revises Dawkins' original position, presenting a more nuanced view where mates engage in cooperative nonzero-sum games as well as conflicts. It also covers the revised explanation for sex differences, land's and Hamilton's solutions to the 'paradox of vanishing variation' in sexual selection, and Dawkins' speculative hypotheses about the human penis and snoring.

## Key facts

- Dawkins now admits his opening sentence about conflict between mates was too cynical; mates are likely engaged in a nonzero-sum game where both can increase winnings by cooperating. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p356]
- The disparity between sperm and egg size is misleading as the basis for sex roles; it is not cheap to make and inject millions of sperms against competition. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p356]
- A self-enhancing initial difference between sexes can grow: As initially favor fighting over parental care while Bs favor parental care over fighting, subsequent generations diverge until As become males and Bs become females. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p357]
- Dawkins' claim that his coy/fast and faithful/philanderer model converges to a stable state was wrong; it actually produces endlessly repeating cycles, as shown by Schuster and Sigmund. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p358]
- R.A. Fisher's runaway sexual selection theory has been mathematically elaborated by Lande; the 'paradox of vanishing variation' problem is resolved by mutation at polygenes and the shifting set of relevant genes. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p358]
- Hamilton's parasite theory solves the paradox by suggesting that females choose males based on hereditary disease-resistance; as parasites evolve, the definition of 'best' male keeps changing, maintaining variation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p360]
- Under Hamilton's theory, the clean tail of a long-tailed bird serves as a 'thermometer' advertising health because diarrhoea would mess it up, and only healthy males can afford the handicap. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p361]
- Humans lost the baculum (penis bone) despite its utility for erection; Dawkins speculates that hydraulic erection failure serves as a diagnostic signal of health, and the loss of the bone forces honest advertisement. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p361]

## Inferences

- Inference: Dawkins acknowledges his remarks about human sexual roles now seem naïvely worded and recommends Martin Daly and Margo Wilson's 'Sex, Evolution, and Behavior' and Donald Symons' 'The Evolution of Human Sexuality' for more thorough treatment.
- Inference: Schuster and Sigmund concluded that 'the behaviour of lovers is oscillating like the moon, and unpredictable as the weather.'

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Dawkins admits his penis and snoring hypotheses are less plausible than pleasing and should not be taken too seriously.
- Uncertain: Whether Hamilton's parasite-based honest advertising applies generally to sexual selection or only in specific cases remains under investigation.
