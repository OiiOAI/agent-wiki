# Vampire Test

## Summary

A method Kleon developed for evaluating relationships and activities. After hanging out with someone, if you feel worn out and depleted, that person is a 'vampire.' If you still feel full of energy, they're not. The test can also apply to jobs, hobbies, and places rather than just people. Vampires cannot be cured and should be removed from your life.

## Key facts

- Kleon tells the story of how Pablo Picasso was notorious for 'sucking all the energy out of the people he met,' squeezing people like tubes of oil paint. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p129]
- Constantin Brancusi refused to have anything to do with Picasso because he 'knew a vampire when he saw one' and wouldn't have his energy juiced. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p129]
- The Vampire Test: If after hanging out with someone you feel worn out and depleted, that person is a vampire. If you still feel full of energy, that person is not a vampire. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p131]
- Vampires cannot be cured. If you find yourself in the presence of a vampire, Kleon advises to 'banish it from your life forever.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p131]
- The Vampire Test works on many things in life, not just people—you can apply it to jobs, hobbies, places, etc. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p131]
