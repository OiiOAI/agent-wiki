# Machine Consciousness

## Summary

Machine consciousness refers to the possibility of artificial systems having genuine conscious experiences. Seth argues that while functionalism might make machine consciousness theoretically possible, consciousness and intelligence are separable and multidimensional. Current AI systems are low on intelligence and lack consciousness. Even if intelligence is achieved, consciousness may not follow. The Garland test—asking whether an entity makes us feel it is conscious—may eventually be passed, with significant ethical implications.

## Key facts

- Functionalism holds that consciousness depends on what a system does, not what it is made of—wetware or hardware—but this substrate independence is unproven. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p235]
- Consciousness and intelligence are separable and multidimensional—intelligence can exist without consciousness and consciousness can exist without high intelligence. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p236]
- Current AI is best described as sophisticated machine-based pattern recognition, possibly with some planning—it does what it does without being conscious of anything. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p237]
- The beast machine theory suggests that the materiality of life will turn out to be important for all manifestations of consciousness because the imperative for regulation goes 'all the way down' to the level of individual cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p240]
- If a future system has conscious status, it also has moral status—we would be obliged to minimize its potential suffering. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p247]
- Thomas Metzinger called in 2019 for a thirty-year moratorium on research aimed at generating synthetic phenomenology due to ethical concerns. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p247]

## Inferences

- Inference: The prospect of machine consciousness may be alluring due to techno-rapture—a desire to transcend biological existence and potentially achieve immortality through mind uploading.
- Inference: The beast machine perspective undercuts the techno-rapture narrative because consciousness arises from biological nature, not despite it.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether artificial consciousness is possible remains genuinely uncertain; the question cannot be definitively answered with current knowledge.
- Uncertain: What kinds of consciousness artificially sentient agents might experience—if they could exist—is unknown.
