# Three States of Mind

## Summary

The chapter identifies three states of mind for both Human and Chimp. The negative state is caused by anxieties, worries, or concerns when the four functional planet systems are not running smoothly—getting the planets spinning correctly removes the negative state. The neutral state occurs when the four planets are spinning well and you are content but not necessarily happy. The positive state is when you add positives and quality to life, achieving the feel-good factor of happiness. Chimp additions for positive state include fun, new experiences, treats, rewards, and socialising; Human additions include laughter, achievements, altruistic behaviours, and future planning.

## Key facts

- The negative state of mind occurs when you do not have the four functional planet systems running smoothly, meaning you are not fulfilling fundamental things the Chimp and Human need to feel relaxed and content. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p197]
- The neutral state is when the first four planets are spinning well and you are content, but this might not mean you are happy—'content' may equal 'happy' for some but for most happiness means something more. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p199]
- The positive state of mind adds positives and quality to life with a feel-good factor where life feels rich, productive, and fulfilling; this quality defines happiness to many people. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p199]
- Chimp additions for positive state include fun, new experiences, pleasurable activities, socialising, treats, rewards, and mental stimulation; Human additions include laughter, satisfying activities, achievements, altruistic behaviours, future planning, and entertainment. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p199]
- Happiness comes at a price because it usually takes effort; the secret is to address the negative state as quickly as possible and accept that it is not normal to remain in this negative state. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p198]
