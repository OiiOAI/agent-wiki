# Multidisciplinary Problem-Solving

## Summary

Munger advocates strongly for multidisciplinary problem-solving as a superior approach to decision-making. He recommends using a checklist of mental models from multiple disciplines and applying them systematically to problems, rather than relying on expertise from a single field.

## Key facts

- Munger recommends using a full list of tools and going through them in the mind checklist-style because this will find answers that won't be found any other way. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p389]
- If you want to succeed like a strong man with two legs, you have to pick up multidisciplinary methods, including doing micro- and macro-economics while knowing psychology. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p409]

## Inferences

- Inference: Munger demonstrates this approach by solving business problems using microeconomic principles (maximization/minimization, adding success factors, catching waves) combined with psychology.
