# Clarify Expectations

## Summary

Behavior #9 in Covey's framework. Clarifying expectations means disclosing, discussing, and validating expectations, and renegotiating them if needed. Violated expectations almost always get translated into trust issues. This is a prevention behavior that avoids problems later.

## Key facts

- AMA/HRI study identifies unrealistic expectations as the number one reason for unethical corporate behavior. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p242]
- Warwick Business School study found outsourcing relationships relying on trust outperformed contracts by up to 40%. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p240]
- Blaine Lee stated 'Almost all conflict is a result of violated expectations'. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p237]
