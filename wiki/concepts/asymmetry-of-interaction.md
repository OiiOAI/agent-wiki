# Asymmetry of Interaction

## Summary

The overall relationship between hemispheres has shifted from stable dynamic equilibrium to inequilibrium. When there are two necessary but mutually opposed entities, an imbalance can be corrected by a swing of the pendulum. But negative feedback can become positive feedback, and in the left hemisphere there is an inbuilt tendency for this to happen. McGilchrist believes we have entered a phase where negative feedback has given way to positive feedback in favour of the left hemisphere.
