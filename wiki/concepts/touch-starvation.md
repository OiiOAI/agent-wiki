# Touch Starvation

## Summary

Touch starvation is a type of withdrawal that people experience when deprived of direct person-to-person contact and friendly touch. Direct person-to-person contact stimulates the release of oxytocin and opioids in the brain, while lack of human touch results in less endogenous opioids and oxytocin. This phenomenon explains why people adopt pets and why digital communication, while connecting us technologically, can leave us emotionally and socially distant.

## Key facts

- Touch starvation is a type of withdrawal that anyone can experience when deprived of direct human contact. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p22]
- Direct person-to-person contact stimulates the release of oxytocin and opioids in the brain, and without human touch, the brain has less endogenous opioids and oxytocin. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p22]
- Dr. Katalin Gothard, a neuroscientist, speculates that touch starvation creates withdrawal symptoms due to lack of brain chemicals. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p22]
- When deprived of human interaction and friendly touch, people become unhappy and may get depressed because the brain is no longer releasing essential hormones like serotonin. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p23]
- Personal contact through human touch lowers heart rate and boosts the immune system. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p22]
