# 10-10-10

## Summary

10-10-10 is a decision-making tool developed by business journalist Suzy Welch that involves asking three questions about any decision: What are the consequences in ten minutes? In ten months? In ten years? This framework triggers mental time travel, engaging deliberative decision-making and accountability to future-self by forcing consideration of consequences across different time horizons.

## Key facts

- The 10-10-10 framework asks: 'What are the consequences of each option in ten minutes? In ten months? In ten years?'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1595-L1597]
- The tool can be enhanced by asking through a past frame: 'How would I feel today if I had made this decision ten minutes ago? Ten months ago? Ten years ago?'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1603-L1605]
- In poker, Annie Duke used a 10-10-10-like strategy when approaching her self-imposed six-to-eight-hour limit, asking how she felt when she exceeded it in the past and whether she was playing her best. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1612-L1617]
- 10-10-10 draws on past experiences including similar decisions we may have regretted, recruiting brain pathways that control executive functioning. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1608-L1610]
