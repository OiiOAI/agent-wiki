# Hall content analysis

## Summary

Hall and Van de Castle's content analysis system is a methodological framework for quantitatively assessing dream content by counting occurrences of different types of characters, settings, objects, social interactions, and activities. This system demonstrated the transparent relationship between dream content and everyday life.

## Key facts

- Calvin Hall pioneered the use of content analysis methods to quantitatively assess dream content, creating profiles of psychiatric patients based solely on blind analysis of dream reports. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p5]
- Hall showed that a large number of dreams reflect daytime activities and preoccupations: skiers dream of skiing, surfers of surfing, teachers of classroom situations. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p5]
- Content analysis methods have reported consistent, statistically significant differences in dream content between groups with divergent waking experience. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p5]
