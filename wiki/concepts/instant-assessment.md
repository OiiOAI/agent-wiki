# Instant Assessment

## Summary

The Instant Assessment is a diagnostic tool in Profit First that quickly evaluates a business's financial health by comparing actual allocation percentages against Target Allocation Percentages (TAPs). It uses real revenue (top-line revenue minus materials and subcontractors) and measures actual profit, owner compensation, taxes paid, and operating expenses to reveal how much needs to change in each category.

## Key facts

- The Instant Assessment uses real revenue rather than top-line revenue to put all companies on equal footing. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p79-80]
- Real Revenue equals Top Line Revenue minus materials and subcontractors costs. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p78-79]
- The assessment calculates a Delta showing the gap between actual allocations and target allocations. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p82]
- Target Allocation Percentages (TAPs) vary by revenue tier from under $250K to $10M-$50M. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p83-86]
