# Degeneracy

## Summary

Degeneracy means 'many to one': many combinations of neurons can produce the same outcome. In emotion research, this means different instances of fear are constructed by different brain patterns at different times and in different people. This principle, common throughout neuroscience, contradicts the classical view's assumption of fixed emotion circuits. Combined with the 'one to many' principle (single neurons participate in many different mental states), degeneracy explains why no neural fingerprints for emotions have been found.

## Key facts

- Degeneracy means 'many to one': many combinations of neurons can produce the same outcome, such as different instances of fear. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p32]
- Core systems exhibit 'one to many': a single brain area contributes to many different mental states, the antithesis of neural fingerprints. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p32]
- Degeneracy brings greater robustness for survival, while one-to-many is metabolically efficient and increases computational power of the brain. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p52]
- Degeneracy means different sets of neurons produce the same outcomes; 'Aggression,' like any other concept, may be implemented differently in the brain each time it's constructed. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p251]
- It is not possible to localize a complex psychological category like 'Aggression' to one set of neurons; even simple actions like hitting or biting have not been localized to a single set of neurons. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p251]
- No two brains are exactly alike; at a fine-grained level in their microcircuitry, they have vast differences; some may translate into behavioral differences, but many do not. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p252]
- The human brain is a high-complexity system because within one physical structure it can reconfigure its billions of neurons to construct a huge repertoire of experiences, perceptions, and behaviors. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p307]
