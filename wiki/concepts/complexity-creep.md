# Complexity Creep

## Summary

Complexity creep refers to the problem of systems becoming too complex for users to form and retain a clear mental model. It results from feature escalation where systems accumulate so many features that users cannot understand how they work. Studies indicate only 20% of people can properly operate their VCR. Complexity primarily works to undermine ability to correctly interpret information and project future states (Levels 2 and 3 SA).
