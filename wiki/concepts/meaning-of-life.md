# Meaning of Life

## Summary

In Man's Search for Meaning, Frankl argues that the primary human motivation is not pleasure (Freud) or power (Adler) but meaning—the will to meaning. He contends that life has meaning under all circumstances, even suffering, and that humans must stop asking what life means and instead recognize that we are the ones being questioned by life, requiring answers through action and conduct.

## Key facts

- Frankl wrote that we should not ask about the meaning of life but should think of ourselves as those who are being questioned by life daily and hourly. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p63]
- Frankl stated that the meaning of life differs from man to man and from moment to moment, making it impossible to define meaning in general terms. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p63]
- Frankl asserted that life embraces the wider cycles of life and death, of suffering and of dying—not merely attaining aims through active creation. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p64]
- Frankl maintained that 'the salvation of man is through love and in love' and that love is the ultimate and highest goal to which man can aspire. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p36]
- Frankl wrote that 'everything can be taken from a man but one thing: the last of the human freedoms—to choose one's own way'. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p55]
- Frankl quoted Dostoevsky: 'There is only one thing that I dread: not to be worthy of my sufferings'. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p56]

## Inferences

- Inference: Frankl's concept of meaning is fundamentally active rather than passive—it requires 'right action and in right conduct' rather than mere contemplation [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p63].
