# Sonic Hedgehog Signaling

## Summary

Sonic hedgehog (Shh) is a secreted morphogen that patterns the ventral neural tube and many other embryonic structures. Synthesized as a precursor that undergoes autoproteolytic cleavage and cholesterol modification, Shh establishes a ventral-to-dorsal concentration gradient in the neural tube. Different Shh concentration thresholds activate distinct transcriptional programs, specifying neuronal identities from floor plate (highest Shh) to dorsal regions (lowest Shh). Shh acts through the patched and smoothened receptors to activate Gli transcription factors, which regulate homeodomain and bHLH transcription factor expression.

## Key facts

- Shh is secreted by the notochord and floor plate, establishing a ventral-to-dorsal concentration gradient that specifies different neuronal identities at different threshold concentrations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1164]
- Shh binds to the patched receptor, relieving patched inhibition of smoothened, which activates intracellular signaling leading to Gli transcription factor activation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1165]
- In the absence of Shh, Gli proteins are proteolytically processed into transcriptional repressors; Shh signaling inhibits this processing, allowing Gli activators to promote target gene expression. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1165]
- Homeodomain transcription factors induced by Shh at different concentration thresholds (Nkx6.1, Nkx2.2) repress each other at progenitor domain boundaries, sharpening the pattern of neuronal specification. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1165]
- Mutations in human Shh pathway genes result in defects in ventral forebrain structures (holoprosencephaly), neurological defects such as spina bifida, and certain cancers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1166]
