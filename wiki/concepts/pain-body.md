# Pain-Body

## Summary

The pain-body is an accumulated negative energy field that occupies the body and mind, created from emotional pain that was not fully processed and absorbed. It has two modes: dormant and active. When dormant, it may be inactive 90% of the time; when active, it takes over the person's consciousness and behavior. The pain-body feeds on pain—it cannot digest joy. It survives by getting its host to unconsciously identify with it. Disidentification is achieved by observing the pain-body with presence, which allows it to be transmuted. The book notes that women's pain-bodies often awaken particularly at times preceding menstrual flow.

## Key facts

- The pain-body is an accumulated negative energy field that occupies the body and mind, created from past pain that has not been dissolved. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p45]
- A pain-body may be dormant 90 percent of the time, while in deeply unhappy people it may be active up to 100 percent of the time. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p46]
- The pain-body cannot feed on joy; it finds joy 'quite indigestible'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p47]
- The pain-body survives by getting the host to unconsciously identify with it, thus becoming 'you'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p47]
- The pain-body is transmuted by observing it with presence—watching it implies accepting it as part of what is. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p48]
- The pain-body is an energy field that has become lodged in a person's inner space, formed from accumulated emotional pain that was not fully felt or released. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p116]
- The pain-body has both a personal aspect (accumulated residue of one's own past emotional pain) and a collective aspect (pain accumulated in the collective human psyche through centuries of suffering). [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p152]
- The collective female pain-body includes accumulated pain suffered by women through male subjugation, slavery, exploitation, rape, childbirth, and child loss over thousands of years, often manifesting during menstruation. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p153]
- Disidentifying from the pain-body—observing it with full attention and acceptance—transmutes it into consciousness, similar to how wood placed near fire is transformed into fire. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p156]
- The pain-body always seeks more pain on which to feed. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p201]
- A person may be recreating a pattern in which intimacy and abuse are inseparably linked, or acting out a pattern learned in early childhood according to which they are unworthy and deserve to be punished. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p201]
- When pain is deep, there is a strong urge to escape from it rather than surrender to it. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p196]
- When you deny emotional pain, everything you do or think as well as your relationships become contaminated with it. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p196]

## Inferences

- Inference: The pain-body cannot survive in one's presence when one is fully conscious, suggesting that conscious awareness is its primary counterforce.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The text does not specify how long it takes to fully transmute a pain-body through conscious attention.
