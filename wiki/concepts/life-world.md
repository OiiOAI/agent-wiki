# Life-World

## Summary

The life-world (Lebenswelt) is the everyday world in which we live, always already pregiven as the horizon of all human activities. It comprises everyday things, living bodies, natural surroundings, and cultural creations. The life-world is subject-relative, in contrast to 'objective nature' as conceived by science through logical and theoretical abstraction. The life-world cannot be given as any kind of intentional object because it is always already there; it is the horizon presupposed by any entity or totality. Generative phenomenology investigates how individual subjectivity is intersubjectively and culturally embodied, embedded, and emergent within the life-world.
