# Kindling

## Summary

Kindling is an experimental procedure where periodic localized electrical stimulation of brain areas (especially the amygdala) induces progressive epileptic seizures that eventually become permanent, producing lasting personality changes.

## Key facts

- Kindling involves applying brief brain stimulation once daily for a week or two, with induced epileptic fits growing larger each day until full motor seizures occur and persist permanently. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p56]
- Kindled cats become temperamental and irritable, and female rats develop persistent estrus (sexual receptivity), suggesting emotional personality changes comparable to humans with limbic seizures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p56]
- Kindling induces permanent changes in functional brain organization without structural changes, and antiseizure medications block its development. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p56]
- Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) may emerge from such permanent brain changes, as antiseizure medications are effective in reducing its severity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p56]
