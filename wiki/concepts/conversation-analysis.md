# Conversation Analysis

## Summary

Conversation analysis is a variety of ethnomethodology focused on the detailed understanding of fundamental structures of conversational interaction. The goal is to understand how conversations are organized through highly detailed data collection including hesitations, silences, laughter, and nonvocal behaviors. Conversations are treated as having stable, orderly properties that are achievements of actors, with sequential organization being the fundamental framework.

## Key facts

- Conversation analysis studies the detailed understanding of fundamental structures of conversational interaction. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p288]
- Conversation is defined as an interactional activity exhibiting stable, orderly properties that are analyzable achievements of conversants. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p288]
- While there are rules and procedures for conversations, they do not determine what is said but are used to accomplish a conversation. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p288]
- Conversation analysis requires collection and analysis of highly detailed data including hesitations, cut-offs, restarts, silences, breathing noises, laughter, and nonverbal behaviors. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p288]
- The fundamental framework of conversation is sequential organization, and the course of conversational interaction is managed on a turn-by-turn or local basis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p289]
- Conversations are context-shaped (what is said is shaped by preceding sequential context) and context-shaping (present turns become part of context for future turns). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p289]
- Conversation analysis has distinctive roots in the work of Harvey Sacks, who was a student of Erving Goffman, not Harold Garfinkel. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p288]
