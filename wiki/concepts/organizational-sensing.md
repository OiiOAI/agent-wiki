# Organizational Sensing

## Summary

Organizational sensing uses teams and their internal and external communication as the senses of the organization, enabling it to detect signals from across the organization and outside it. With well-defined and stable communication pathways between teams, organizations can sense their environment and respond rapidly, functioning like an organism rather than a senseless entity.

## Key facts

- Organizational sensing uses teams and their internal and external communication as the senses of the organization. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3100-L3120]
- Without stable, well-defined neural communication pathways, no living organism can effectively sense anything. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3120-L3130]
- Many organizations with unstable and ill-defined teams are effectively senseless—they cannot sense their environmental situation. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3140-L3150]
- In today's network-connected world, high-fidelity sensing is crucial for organizational survival. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3150-L3160]
- Organizations need to respond rapidly to sensed information to remain competitive. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3160-L3170]
