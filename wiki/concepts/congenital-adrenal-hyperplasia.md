# Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia (CAH)

## Summary

A disorder caused by an enzyme mutation in the adrenal glands, causing them to produce testosterone and androgens instead of glucocorticoids, starting during fetal life. Girls with CAH are born with ambiguous genitals and, when compared with non-CAH girls, show more rough-and-tumble play, fighting, physical aggression, and preference for masculine toys, providing evidence for prenatal testosterone's organizational effects on behavior.

## Key facts

- CAH females do more rough-and-tumble play, fighting, and physical aggression than non-CAH girls, and prefer masculine toys over dolls. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p225]
- As adults, CAH women score lower on measures of tenderness and higher in aggressiveness, self-report more aggression and less interest in infants. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p225]
- CAH women are more likely to be gay or bisexual or have a transgender sexual identity. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p225]
- Drug treatments begun soon after birth normalize androgen levels in CAH girls, so excessive androgen exposure is solely prenatal, allowing study of prenatal effects. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p225]

## Inferences

- Inference: CAH studies suggest prenatal testosterone exposure has organizational effects that increase male-typical behaviors.
- Inference: However, CAH females are not merely prenatally androgenized—they have ambiguous genitalia requiring surgeries, parents know something is different, and they have extensive medical involvement.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: It's impossible to attribute the behavioral profile solely to prenatal androgens given the many confounds in CAH treatment and upbringing.
