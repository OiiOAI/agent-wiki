# Credit Bubble

## Summary

A credit bubble occurs when lending standards become less stringent, easy credit drives up asset prices, and those risen asset values form collateral for further borrowing. The 2008 financial crisis was the bursting of the largest credit bubble in history. Credit bubbles transmit shocks from company to company, sector to sector, and nation to nation, turning individual losses into collective calamity. The process of unwinding or deleveraging when asset prices decline causes debt deflation.
