# Respiratory Pattern Generator

## Summary

The respiratory pattern generator is a neuronal network in the medulla that produces the rhythmic breathing movements necessary for ventilation. The pre-Bötzinger complex in the rostral ventral respiratory group is critical for generating the respiratory rhythm. The system is regulated by chemoreceptors sensing blood oxygen and carbon dioxide levels.

## Key facts

- The brain stem automatically generates breathing movements beginning in utero at 11 to 13 weeks of gestation in humans and continues nonstop from birth until death without requiring conscious effort. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1083]
- The pre-Bötzinger complex in the rostral ventral respiratory group contains neurons that are critical for producing the normal ventilatory motor pattern; lesions of this area disrupt the normal respiratory rhythm. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1083]
- The dorsal respiratory group, located bilaterally in and around the ventrolateral nucleus of the solitary tract, receives respiratory sensory input including afferents from stretch receptors in the lungs and peripheral chemoreceptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1083]
- Cheyne-Stokes breathing, characterized by cycles of gradually increasing then decreasing ventilation alternating with apnea, can occur in patients with congestive heart failure due to increased delay between gas exchange in the lungs and changes at central chemoreceptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1084]
- At altitudes greater than 10,000 feet, even normal individuals experience Cheyne-Stokes breathing, particularly during sleep, due to relative hypoxia sensitizing the CO2 receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1086]

## Inferences

- Inference: The stability of the respiratory pattern generator (generating rhythm from birth to death) contrasts with its flexibility (adjusting to exercise, altitude, sleep states), suggesting multiple parallel control mechanisms.
