# Neuroplasticity

## Summary

Neuroplasticity is the brain's ability to change throughout life, including generating new neurons (neurogenesis). This discovery in the mid-1990s revolutionized understanding of brain health, showing that cognitive decline is not inevitable and that the brain can adapt, learn, and heal at any age.

## Key facts

- Neuroplasticity—the brain's ability to change up until death—was discovered in the mid-1990s, ending the era of 'neurological nihilism'. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p26]
- The creation of new brain cells (neurogenesis) was thought to occur only during development until the mid-1990s discovery that it continues throughout adult life. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p26]
- Inflammation can strongly negate neuroplasticity and even produce feelings of depression. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p37]
