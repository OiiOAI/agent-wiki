# Circumstances of Justice

## Summary

The normal conditions under which human cooperation is both possible and necessary. Rawls divides them into objective circumstances (moderate scarcity, co-existence on a geographical territory, comparable powers so no one can dominate, vulnerability) and subjective circumstances (different plans of life, diversity of ends, limited knowledge and reasoning capacity, pluralism of doctrines). Justice is required when mutually disinterested persons put forward conflicting claims to the division of social advantages under conditions of moderate scarcity.

## Key facts

- The circumstances of justice are the normal conditions under which human cooperation is both possible and necessary. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p124]
- Objective circumstances include: many individuals coexist on the same territory, roughly similar in physical and mental powers, vulnerable to attack, subject to having plans blocked by others, and moderate scarcity. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p124]
- Subjective circumstances include: parties have different plans of life leading to different ends and conflicting claims, they have limited and distorted knowledge and judgment, and there is diversity of philosophical, religious, and political doctrines. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p125]
- Unless the circumstances of justice existed there would be no occasion for the virtue of justice, just as in the absence of threats of injury there would be no occasion for physical courage. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p126]
- Rawls follows Hume's account of the circumstances of justice, emphasizing moderate scarcity and mutual disinterest. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p126]

## Inferences

- Inference: The subjective circumstances (pluralism, conflicting conceptions of the good) make the existence of a shared moral framework problematic and require a procedure like the original position to generate principles that all can accept.
