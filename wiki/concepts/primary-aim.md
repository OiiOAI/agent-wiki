# Primary Aim

## Summary

A Primary Aim is a personal vision of how one wants their life to look, feel, and be. It serves as the foundation for all business development, answering questions about what one values most, what kind of life they want, who they wish to be, and what they would want to say at their own funeral. Without a clear Primary Aim, a business cannot have meaningful direction.

## Key facts

- Your Primary Aim is the answer to: What do I value most? What kind of life do I want? What do I want my life to look like, to feel like? Who do I wish to be?. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p137]
- The Primary Aim provides purpose, energy, and the grist for the day-to-day mill. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p140]
- Great people go to work on their lives, not just in their lives, living out the vision they have of their future in the present. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p139]
- The Primary Aim is the vision necessary to bring your business to life and your life to your business. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p140]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Primary Aim must be articulated clearly before creating a Strategic Objective for the business, ensuring the business serves personal life goals.
- Inference: Great people are distinguished by actively creating their lives rather than being passively created by their circumstances.
