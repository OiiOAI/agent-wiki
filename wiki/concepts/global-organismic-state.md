# Global Organismic State

## Summary

When survival circuits are activated, the resulting whole-body response is a global organismic state. For defensive survival circuits, this is a defensive motivational state that involves heightened arousal, vigilance, and lowered thresholds for defensive responses while suppressing other motivated behaviors. These states are not conscious feelings but rather neural ingredients that, when cognitively interpreted, contribute to conscious emotions.

## Key facts

- Activation of a survival circuit results in a global organismic state involving the whole organism (body as well as brain). [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p75]
- When a defensive survival circuit detects a threat, it triggers widespread release of neuromodulators and hormones, making the organism highly aroused and vigilant. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p75]
- Global organismic states reflect wholesale mobilization of brain and body resources for survival purposes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p75]
- The defensive motivational state is a consequence, not a cause, of the responses that result when a survival circuit is activated. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p99]

## Inferences

- Inference: Global organismic states provide neural ingredients that, along with other factors like perceptions and memories, contribute to conscious feelings of fear when cognitively interpreted.
