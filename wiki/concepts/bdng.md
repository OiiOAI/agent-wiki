# Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF)

## Summary

BDNF is a protein dubbed 'Miracle-Gro for the brain' that promotes neurogenesis, protects existing brain cells, and enhances learning and memory. It is decreased in Alzheimer's and depression, and can be increased through omega-3 consumption, exercise, and ketone production.

## Key facts

- BDNF promotes the creation of new neurons in the memory center of the brain and helps ensure survival of existing brain cells. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p59]
- The Alzheimer's brain may have half the BDNF of a healthy brain. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p59]
- Omega-3 fatty acids, particularly DHA, are among the best dietary means of increasing BDNF. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p59]
- Fish consumption leading to higher blood levels of omega-3 fats is correlated with greater total brain volume over time. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p59]
