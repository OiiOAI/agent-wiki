# Emanations

## Summary

The process by which creation emerges from the void through successive stages of increasingly concrete manifestation. Campbell describes emanations as plantlike mysteries unfolding from the void beyond all voids, progressing through night, space, male-female polarity, to the visible universe. The Hebrew cabala represents this as branches of a cosmic tree rooted in the inscrutable height.

## Key facts

- The first phase of the cosmogonic cycle describes the breaking of formlessness into form. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p210]
- Creation myths are pervaded with a sense of doom continually recalling all created shapes to the imperishable out of which they first emerged. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p210]
- According to Samkhya philosophy, the void condenses into ether or space; from air comes fire, from fire water, from water earth. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p212]
