# The Franchise Prototype

## Summary

The Franchise Prototype is the working model of the entrepreneurial dream—a microcosm where all assumptions are tested before becoming operational. It acts as a buffer between hypothesis and action, where the ultimate question is 'Does it work?' The Prototype is where the system is conceived and perfected, making the business systems-dependent rather than people-dependent.
