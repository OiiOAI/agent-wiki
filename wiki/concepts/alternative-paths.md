# Alternative Paths

## Summary

Alternative paths are all the outcomes that could have happened but did not. Risk is not directly visible, so we systematically fail to consider the invisible paths that could lead to ruin. Success achieved through risky dealings is worth less than the same success achieved through 'boring' methods like laborious work.

## Key facts

- Alternative paths are all the outcomes that could have happened, but did not. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p96]
- Philip Tetlock evaluated 28,361 predictions from 284 self-appointed professionals over ten years and found experts performed only marginally better than a random forecast generator. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p97]
- Ten million dollars earned through huge risk is worth less to a rational mind than the same sum earned through years of drudgery. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p96]
