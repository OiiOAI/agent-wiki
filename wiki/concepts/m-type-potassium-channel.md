# M-Type Potassium Channel

## Summary

The M-type potassium channel (KCNQ family) is a slowly activating voltage-gated K+ channel that contributes to resting membrane potential and controls neuronal excitability. It is closed by activation of muscarinic ACh receptors through a mechanism involving depletion of membrane PIP2, and its closure produces slow depolarization and decreased spike-frequency adaptation.

## Key facts

- The M-type K+ channel is partially activated at the resting potential and carries current that helps determine the cell resting potential and membrane resistance. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p359]
- M-type channels require several hundred milliseconds to fully activate on depolarization, much slower than other delayed-rectifier K+ channels. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p359]
- Closure of M-type channels by muscarinic receptor activation is produced by hydrolysis of membrane PIP2 by activated phospholipase C, not by a diffusible second messenger. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p359]
- Closure of M-type channels decreases resting K+ conductance, depolarizing the cell, increasing membrane resistance, and reducing delayed K+ current, enabling more sustained firing during prolonged depolarization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p359]
- The M-type channel underlies spike-frequency adaptation; closure of these channels during slow EPSP eliminates adaptation, allowing sustained firing during prolonged depolarization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p359]
