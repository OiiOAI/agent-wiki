# Calibration

## Summary

Calibration is the process of adjusting confidence levels in beliefs to more accurately reflect reality. In betting terms, a well-calibrated person would be right about as often as they say they will be. Calibration requires open-minded consideration of diverse viewpoints and alternative hypotheses, and is improved by accountability and truthseeking groups.

## Key facts

- Betting is a form of accountability to accuracy, and in the long run, the more objective person will win against the more biased person. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1248-L1255]
- Calibration requires an open-minded consideration of diverse points of view and alternative hypotheses. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1248-L1255]
- By asking questions like 'Why might my belief not be true?' and 'What other evidence might be out there?', we take a big step toward calibration. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1340-L1350]
