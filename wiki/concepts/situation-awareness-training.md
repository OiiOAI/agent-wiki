# Situation Awareness Training

## Summary

SA-Oriented Training addresses the cognitive skills and knowledge structures underlying high levels of Situation Awareness. Unlike typical training focused on procedures and system operation, SA training targets mental models, schema development, critical cue recognition, attention sharing, task management, and projection skills. Research shows that best pilots have SA scores 10 times better than those with lowest SA, indicating significant individual differences that can be addressed through training. The book presents 12 SA Training Principles and multiple training approaches including ISAT, VESARS, and SAVI.

## Key facts

- SA-Oriented Training focuses on developing mental models, schema, and critical skills rather than just teaching procedures. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p245]
- The best pilots have SA scores 10 times better than those with lowest SA, indicating significant individual differences in SA capability. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p235]
- Mental models provide mechanisms for directing attention, integrating information for comprehension (Level 2 SA), and projecting future states (Level 3 SA). [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p237]
- Schema provide prototypical situation patterns that allow rapid recognition-primed decision making without requiring working memory processing. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p238]
- Key SA skills include task management, comprehension development, projection and contingency planning, information seeking, cognitive skills, and team SA skills. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p243]
- The 12 SA Training Principles include building basic skills, task management training, comprehension/projection training, addressing SA demons, developing multitasking ability, metacognitive skills, team communication training, mental model development, schema building, SA feedback provision, and team SA skill development.
