# Head as Genital Symbol

## Summary

Freud discussed the symbolic equivalence between the head and male genitals, noting that the head frequently appears as a symbol for the male organ in fantasies and symptoms. Patients with obsessional neurosis often express particular abhorrence of punishment by beheading, leading Freud to suggest they treat beheading as a substitute for castration. Dreams by young people concerning castration often mention a round ball interpretable as the father's head.
