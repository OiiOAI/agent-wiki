# Chimp Management

## Summary

Chimp Management is the practice of understanding, nurturing, and managing the emotional Chimp rather than trying to control it through willpower. It involves three primary techniques: exercising the Chimp (allowing emotional expression), boxing the Chimp (using facts and logic to calm it), and feeding bananas (distraction or reward).

## Key facts

- Chimp Management involves three steps: recognizing who is in charge, understanding brain rules, and nurturing/managing the Chimp. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p35]
- The golden rule for recognizing Chimp hijacking is asking 'Do I want these feelings, thoughts or behaviours?' with 'no' indicating Chimp control. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p35]
- Exercising the Chimp means allowing it to express emotions fully before reasoning with it, typically taking less than ten minutes. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p46]
- Boxing the Chimp involves using facts, truth and logic to calm it down and reach agreement after it has exercised. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p47]
- Bananas are distractions or rewards for the Chimp, including the banana of distraction (counting to get out of bed) and the banana of reward (promising praise for task completion). [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p50]
- Nurturing the Chimp must come before managing it; if the Chimp's needs are met, it becomes much easier to manage. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p43]

## Inferences

- Inference: Effective Chimp Management reduces internal conflict and frees the Human to make rational decisions and pursue goals.
