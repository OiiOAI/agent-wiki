# Limiting Step

## Summary

The limiting step (or bottleneck) is the step in a production flow that determines the overall shape and timing of the entire operation. In the book's breakfast factory metaphor, boiling the egg is the limiting step because it takes the longest. The principle is to plan the entire job around this most time-consuming or critical step and offset other tasks backward from it.

## Key facts

- The limiting step is the component that takes the longest to prepare and around which the entire production flow must be planned. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L368-L370]
- Time offsets are calculated by working backward from delivery time, starting with the limiting step and staggering other steps according to their individual throughput times. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L377-L380]
- If toaster capacity becomes limited, it becomes the new limiting step, and the entire production process must be reconceived around it. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L393-L397]
- Equipment capacity, manpower, and inventory can be traded off against each other and balanced against delivery time. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L411-L412]
