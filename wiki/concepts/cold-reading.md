# Cold Reading

## Summary

A technique of making educated guesses about a person instead of asking direct questions. The author presents cold reading as a way to create interesting statements that engage people more than questions.

## Key facts

- Cold reading is making educated guesses about someone instead of asking questions—for instance, 'You look like a California girl' instead of 'Where are you from?'. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p209]
- The author states there is no failing with cold reading: if you're wrong she corrects you or asks what made you think that; if you're right she freaks out at how perceptive you are. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p209]
- Cold reading creates conversational threads through statements rather than questions, which protects against conversations dying and forces sharing of oneself. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p210]
