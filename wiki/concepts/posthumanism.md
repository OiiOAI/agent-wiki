# Posthumanism

## Summary

Posthumanism is defined by opposition to humanism and moving beyond it. It rejects the separability of humanity from the non-human world and the division of knowledge into separate domains. Since humanism underlies much of sociology, posthumanism constitutes a profound challenge to the field. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p550]

## Key facts

- Posthumanism opposes humanism and rejects the separability of humanity from the non-human world. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p550]
