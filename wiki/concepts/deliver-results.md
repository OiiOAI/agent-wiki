# Deliver Results

## Summary

Behavior #6 in Covey's framework. Delivering results means establishing a track record of results, getting the right things done, accomplishing what you're hired to do, being on time and within budget. This is the first half of leadership—getting results in a way that inspires trust.

## Key facts

- Pete Beaudreault stated 'The price of freedom is performance'—when you deliver results, your performance gives you opportunities, alternatives, and choices. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p216]
- British Airways CIO Paul Coby said he doesn't get in the door to talk about new developments unless delivering 24/7 IT operations. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p218]
