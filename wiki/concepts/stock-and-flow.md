# Stock and Flow

## Summary

A framework adapted by writer Robin Sloan from economics describing two types of media content. 'Flow' is the feed—posts, tweets, daily updates that remind people you exist. 'Stock' is the durable content that remains interesting over time, discoverable via search, spreading slowly but building fans. The magic formula is maintaining flow while working on stock in the background.

## Key facts

- Robin Sloan adapted 'stock and flow' from economics into a media metaphor: 'Flow is the feed. It's the posts and the tweets. It's the stream of daily and sub-daily updates that remind people you exist.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p64]
- Stock is described as 'the durable stuff. It's the content you produce that's as interesting in two months (or two years) as it is today. It's what people discover via search.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p64]
- The 'magic formula' is to maintain your flow while working on your stock in the background. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p64]
- Kleon's experience: stock is best made by collecting, organizing, and expanding upon your flow. Many ideas in this book started as tweets, became blog posts, then became book chapters. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p66]
- Kleon advocates owning your own space online (a personal website) rather than relying solely on social networks which 'come and go.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p67]
