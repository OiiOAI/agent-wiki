# Profit First System

## Summary

The Profit First System is a cash management methodology that requires entrepreneurs to allocate percentages of revenue to predetermined bank accounts before paying expenses, rather than treating profit as whatever remains after expenses. The system is built on four core principles derived from behavioral science: using small plates (limiting available cash), serving sequentially (allocating profit first), removing temptation (hiding profit and tax accounts), and enforcing a rhythm (regular allocation schedules).

## Key facts

- The core formula of Profit First is Sales - Profit = Expenses, reversing the traditional Sales - Expenses = Profit. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p59]
- The system requires five foundational accounts: INCOME, PROFIT, OWNER'S COMP, TAX, and OPEX. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p67]
- Profit First recommends allocating funds and paying bills twice monthly on the 10th and 25th. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p60]
- The system is designed to work with human nature rather than requiring entrepreneurs to change their habits. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p44-45]
- Profit and tax accounts should be held at a separate 'no-temptation' bank to prevent easy access. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p67-69]
- The five foundational accounts are: INCOME, PROFIT, OWNER'S COMP, TAX, and OPEX. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p111]
- Two additional accounts are established at a no-temptation bank: PROFIT HOLD and TAX HOLD. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p111]
- The twice-monthly rhythm involves allocating funds on the 10th and 25th of each month. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p120]
- Profit First is not about accounting to the exact penny; it is about handling accounting quickly and easily with numbers that are as close to accurate as possible. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p102]
- The system works with different percentages because TAPs are simply targets; you aren't going to start with them, you are going to build toward them. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p108]

## Inferences

- Inference: The system works by applying Parkinson's Law: when less money is available to operate the business, entrepreneurs naturally become both more frugal and more innovative in how they use resources
- Inference: Profit First creates behavioral change through systematic automation rather than willpower.
