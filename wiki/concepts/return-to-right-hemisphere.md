# Return to the Right Hemisphere

## Summary

In the real, practical, everyday world, what McGilchrist calls the 'return to the right hemisphere' is of ultimate importance. This return involves embracing circularity, social connectedness, embodied understanding, and the capacity for genuine altruism. Music may prove effective in regenerating commonality, and regrounding both art and science in the lived world is essential.

## Key facts

- Happiness and fulfilment are by-products of other things, of a focus elsewhere—not the narrow focus on getting and using, but a broader empathic attention. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p598]
- The Prisoner's Dilemma demonstrates that altruism can be, incidentally, useful and rational—a Gödelian moment for right hemisphere values. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p598]
- The very circularity of things as they really are, rather than as the left hemisphere conceives them, might be a reason for hope. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p610]

## Inferences

- Inference: Regrounding both art and science in the lived world, and moving away from reductive scientific materialism, is essential for human flourishing.
