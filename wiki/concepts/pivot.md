# Pivot

## Summary

A pivot is a structured course correction designed to test a new fundamental hypothesis about the product, strategy, or engine of growth. Upon completing the Build-Measure-Learn loop, entrepreneurs face the decision of whether to pivot (change course with one foot anchored to the ground) or persevere. The Lean Startup method builds capital-efficient companies by allowing startups to recognize it's time to pivot sooner, creating less waste of time and money.

## Key facts

- A pivot is changing course with one foot anchored to the ground—testing a new strategic hypothesis based on what has been learned. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p8]
- The most difficult question any entrepreneur faces is whether to pivot the original strategy or persevere after completing the Build-Measure-Learn loop. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p75]
- The Lean Startup method builds capital-efficient companies because it allows startups to recognize it's time to pivot sooner, creating less waste of time and money. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p75]
- At IMVU, the entire original strategic analysis of the IM add-on market was utterly wrong, discovered empirically through experimentation, leading to a pivot. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p45-49]
- A pivot is a structured course correction designed to test a new fundamental hypothesis about the product, strategy, and engine of growth. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p139]
- The true measure of runway is how many pivots a startup has left: the number of opportunities it has to make a fundamental change to its business strategy. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p153]
- In a zoom-in pivot, what previously was considered a single feature in a product becomes the whole product (Votizen pivoted from a social network to focus on voter contact). [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p165]
- In a customer segment pivot, the company realizes its product solves a real problem but for a different customer than originally anticipated. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p165]
- A platform pivot refers to a change from an application to a platform or vice versa. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p166]
- Votizen's MVP acceleration demonstrated that the first MVP took eight months, the next four months, then three, then one, as the company learned critical things about its customers. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p152]
