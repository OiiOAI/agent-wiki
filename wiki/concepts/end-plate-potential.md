# End-Plate Potential

## Summary

The end-plate potential (EPP) is the depolarizing postsynaptic potential produced at the neuromuscular junction when acetylcholine activates nicotinic receptor channels. It results from simultaneous Na+ influx and K+ efflux through ACh-gated channels and normally exceeds threshold to trigger a muscle action potential.

## Key facts

- The end-plate potential was first studied in detail by Paul Fatt and Bernard Katz in the 1950s using intracellular recordings. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p243]
- The EPP rises rapidly due to sudden ACh release but decays more slowly as ACh is hydrolyzed by acetylcholinesterase and diffuses away. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p244]
- The end-plate current rises and decays more rapidly than the end-plate potential because the latter lags due to membrane capacitance charging. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p244]
- The reversal potential for the end-plate current is 0 mV because ACh-gated channels conduct both Na+ and K+ with nearly equal permeability. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p245]
- Single ACh receptor-channel conductance is approximately 30 pS, with a mean open time of approximately 1 ms. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p247]
- The end-plate potential depends on four factors: total number of channels (N), probability of channel opening (popen), single-channel conductance (γ), and driving force (Vm − EEPSP). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p249]

## Inferences

- Inference: The end-plate potential represents a fundamental model for understanding ligand-gated synaptic potentials throughout the nervous system.
