# Friedrich List vs Adam Smith on Economic Policy

## Summary

Fukuyama argues that Friedrich List's National System of Political Economy simply repeats many mercantilist dicta about the centrality of national power and the subordination of economic means to strategic ends. He contends that Adam Smith would have found nothing in List's arguments that constituted a decisive critique, noting that The Wealth of Nations was itself written as a critique of mercantilist predecessors. Fukuyama considers James Fallows to vastly overstate List's importance to German economic thought and practice.
