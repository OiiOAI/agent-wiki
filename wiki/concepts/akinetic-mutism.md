# Akinetic Mutism

## Summary

Akinetic mutism is a neurological condition in which patients are motionless and speechless but awake, with severely diminished or suspended consciousness. Patients show no emotional expressions and no purpose in their behavior. Damasio's patient L had bilateral frontal lobe damage affecting the cingulate cortex, remaining in this state for six months before gradual recovery.

## Key facts

- Akinetic mutism is caused by dysfunction in the cingulate cortex, basal forebrain, thalamus, and medial peri-cingulate parietal cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p105]
- Patient L showed no response to external inducers (conversations, photographs, songs) or to any apparent internal inducers; emotional neutrality reigned supreme. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p102]
- After recovery, patient L reported having no recall of particular experiences, no fear, no anxiety, and no wish to communicate during the episode. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p103]
- Akinetic mutism involves disruption of core consciousness with preserved wakefulness and preserved minimal attention/behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p105]
