# Direct Realism

## Summary

Direct realism is the philosophical position advocated by James J. Gibson, maintaining that we perceive the world directly as it is, without mental intermediaries. Gibson contrasted this with cognitivist indirect realism, which posits that we perceive only mental representations of the world.

## Key facts

- Gibson advocated direct realism as opposed to cognitivist indirect realism. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p2]
- Is part of Gibson's ecological approach to visual perception. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p2]
