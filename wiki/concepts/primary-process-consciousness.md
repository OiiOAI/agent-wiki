# Primary-Process Consciousness

## Summary

Primary-process consciousness is conceptualized as the ineffable feeling of experiencing oneself as an active agent in perceived events of the world. Rather than simple awareness of external events, it involves affective feeling states arising from the build-up of reverberations in the extending SELF-schema, experienced as a mounting sense of force or pressure to behave in certain ways.

## Key facts

- Primary-process consciousness is defined as the ineffable feeling of experiencing oneself as an active agent in perceived events of the world. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- Primary-process consciousness is probably rooted in fairly low-level brain circuits that first represented the body as an intrinsic and coherent whole. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- Affective states may increasingly provide an internal reference point for more complex abilities as organisms evolve sensory and motor tools. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- Primary-process consciousness is experienced as a mounting sense of force or pressure to behave in a certain way. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The SELF is believed to be the neural template or seed of primary-process consciousness residing deep within medial zones of the brain stem. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]

## Inferences

- Inference: Animals may adjust their behaviors by the way behaviors make them feel, suggesting primary-process consciousness has adaptive value for behavior regulation.
- Inference: Primary-process consciousness is more critically linked to motor than sensory cortices, distinguishing it from secondary or derivative forms of consciousness.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise neural mechanisms by which primary-process consciousness generates subjective feeling states remain unknown.
