# Live and Let Live (WWI)

## Summary

The Live and Let Live phenomenon was an informal system of mutual restraint that emerged repeatedly in World War I trenches, where opposing soldiers evolved stable truces without verbal communication, shared holidays, or officer sanction. Soldiers developed rituals like synchronized shooting patterns to signal and maintain peace.

## Key facts

- Live and Let Live truces emerged passively—guns would go silent during meals or bad weather—and evolved through mutual signaling, such as snipers shooting repeatedly at the same inconsequential target to communicate restraint. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p680]
- These truces were ritualized—shooting repeatedly at the same inconsequential target, renewing the commitment to peace daily at the same time. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p681]
- Officers repeatedly shattered Live and Let Live systems by rotating troops, threatening court-martials, and ordering savage raids requiring hand-to-hand combat. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p682]
