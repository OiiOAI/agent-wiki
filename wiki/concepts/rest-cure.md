# Rest Cure

## Summary

The rest cure, also known as Weir Mitchell's treatment or Playfair's treatment, gained high reputation for treating hysteria in institutions. It consists of a combination of isolation in absolute quiet with systematic application of massage and general faradization. Freud considered it of extraordinary value for hysteria as a happy combination of 'traitement moral' with improvement in the patient's general nutritional state. Freud later recommended combining Weir Mitchell's rest cure with Breuer's cathartic treatment.
