# Collectivist vs. Individualist Cultures

## Summary

The most studied cultural contrast in cross-cultural psychology, comparing collectivist East Asian cultures with individualist America. Collectivist cultures emphasize interdependence, harmony, and group needs; individualist cultures value independence, competition, and individual rights. These differences manifest in parenting practices, moral reasoning, cognitive styles, and neurobiological responses.

## Key facts

- Mothers in individualist cultures view themselves as teachers rather than protectors, abhor a bored child, and value high-energy affect. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p214]
- Mothers in collectivist cultures spend more time soothing their child, maintaining contact, and facilitating contact with other adults. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p214]
- Kids in individualist cultures acquire Theory of Mind later than collectivist-culture kids and activate pertinent circuits more to achieve the same degree of competence. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p214]
- Kids in Japan play more violent video games than American kids yet are less aggressive, partly because Japanese kids rarely have a computer or TV in their bedroom. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p214]
- The 7R dopamine receptor variant occurs in about 23 percent of Europeans but only 1 percent of East Asians, nearly lost through selection during the domestication of rice. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p289]

## Inferences

- Inference: Rice cultivation in East Asia required massive communal labor, creating evolutionary pressure for collectivist values.
- Inference: The genetic selection against novelty-seeking variants in East Asian populations coevolved with collectivist cultural values.
