# Level 1 SA: Perception

## Summary

Level 1 Situation Awareness is the foundational level involving the perception of cues in the environment. It is fundamental to SA formation—without basic perception of important information, the odds of forming an incorrect picture of the situation increase dramatically. Research by Jones and Endsley (1996) found that 76% of SA errors in pilots could be traced to problems in the perception of needed information, due to either failures or shortcomings in the system or problems with cognitive processes. This level represents the first stage in the three-level SA model, where sensory information is acquired through various receptors.

- Cues may be received through visual, aural, tactile, olfactory or taste receptors, with some cues overt (e.g., system alarms) and some so subtle they are registered only subconsciously.
