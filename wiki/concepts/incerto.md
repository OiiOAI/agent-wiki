# INCERTO

## Summary

INCERTO is Taleb's multi-volume investigation of opacity, luck, uncertainty, probability, human error, risk, and decision-making expressed as a personal essay with autobiographical sections, stories, parables, and philosophical, historical, and scientific discussions in nonoverlapping volumes that can be accessed in any order.

## Key facts

- INCERTO is an investigation of opacity, luck, uncertainty, probability, human error, risk, and decision making when we don't understand the world. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p663]
- INCERTO is expressed in the form of a personal essay with autobiographical sections, stories, parables, and philosophical, historical, and scientific discussions. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p663]
- The volumes are nonoverlapping and can be accessed in any order. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p663]
- The INCERTO volumes are: Antifragile (this volume), The Black Swan (2007, 2010), Fooled by Randomness (2001, 2004), and The Bed of Procrustes (2010). [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p663]
- A free technical companion volume exists containing academic papers, miscellaneous notes, and technical remarks. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p664]
