# Wizard of Oz Testing

## Summary

Wizard of Oz testing is a technique where customers believe they are interacting with the actual product, but behind the scenes human beings are doing the work. It allows startups to test whether people would use a product if the tough technical problems were solved, without having to actually solve those problems first.

## Key facts

- In a Wizard of Oz test, customers believe they are interacting with the actual product, but behind the scenes human beings are doing the work. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p100]
- Aardvark used humans to replicate the backend for nine months before automating the system, using this approach to answer whether people would use the product if technical problems were solved. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p99]
- The Wizard of Oz approach allowed Max and Damon to answer: if we can solve the tough technical problems, will people use it? Will their use lead to creation of a product that has real value?. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p100]
