# Geological Time Scale

## Summary

A system of chronological measurement relating stratigraphy to time, used by geologists and other earth scientists to describe the timing and relationships between events that have occurred throughout Earth's history.

## Key facts

- Earth is believed to have formed in the Hadean era 4,500 million years ago as a solid planet with no evidence of life. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L350]
- The Proterozoic era began 2,500 million years ago with earliest multicellular life and mountain ranges beginning to form. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L355]
- The Cenozoic era spans from 66 million years ago to present, divided into Paleogene, Neogene, and Quaternary periods. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L365]
- The oldest known Earth rocks date from around 3.8 to 3.9 billion years ago. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L340]
