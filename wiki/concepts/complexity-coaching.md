# Complexity Coaching

## Summary

Complexity coaching applies Cynefin and complexity thinking to coaching and leadership development practice. Practitioners work with what is, using probing, sensing, and safe-to-fail experiments for adaptive contexts, while engaging with leaders' embeddedness in different systems and relational dynamics.

## Key facts

- Anne Caspari and Johann Entz-von Zerssen developed 'complexity coaching and leadership' drawing on multiple perspectives based on coherent theory and practice. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p157]
- They found that SenseMaker allows engagement with people at their level without requiring them to jump through developmental hoops. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p156]
- Cultivating Leadership looks for colleagues who are disposed to lean into their confusion rather than avoid it. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p179]

## Inferences

- Inference: Complexity coaching represents a fundamental shift from expert-driven to emergent partnership models.
