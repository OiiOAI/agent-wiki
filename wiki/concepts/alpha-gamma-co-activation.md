# Alpha-Gamma Co-activation

## Summary

Alpha-gamma co-activation is the simultaneous activation of alpha motor neurons (which contract extrafusal muscle fibers) and gamma motor neurons (which contract intrafusal muscle fibers within muscle spindles). This mechanism maintains spindle sensitivity during muscle contraction by preventing the spindle from being unloaded.

## Key facts

- When only alpha motor neurons are stimulated, the firing of Ia fibers from muscle spindles pauses during contraction because the muscle shortens and unloads the spindle. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p853]
- If gamma motor neurons are activated at the same time as alpha motor neurons, the pause in Ia fiber discharge is eliminated because intrafusal fiber contraction keeps the spindle under tension. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p853]
- Åke Vallbo showed that during slow finger movements the Ia fibers from spindles in contracting muscles increase their firing rate even when the muscle shortens, due to gamma motor neuron co-activation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p858]
- Activity levels in both static and dynamic gamma motor neurons are set at progressively higher levels as the speed and difficulty of movement increase. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p855]
