# Paramnesia

## Summary

Paramnesia (false recollection) refers to the phenomenon where forgotten or repressed material manifests as incorrect substitute memories. Freud used this concept to describe cases where names are forgotten but incorrect substitute names emerge, as in the Signorelli example.

## Key facts

- Paramnesia occurs when a name is forgotten but substitute names present themselves, as demonstrated in the Signorelli example. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2006]
- The substitute names are connected to both what the person wanted to forget and what they wanted to remember, representing a compromise. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2004]
- Paramnesia is described as faulty recollection in which the error is not recognized but finds credence, distinguishing it from forgetting accompanied by paramnesia where the error is recognized. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2131]
- Paramnesia in pathological cases, particularly in paranoia, plays a part as a constituent factor in the formation of delusion. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2131]
- Freud reports his own paramnesia of confusing character names in Daudet's Le Nabab, where 'Joyeuse' (the feminine form) was a translation of his own name 'Freud'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2132]
- A patient claimed to have seen an announcement of Otto Rank's work 'Der Künstler' a year before publication; investigation showed no such announcement existed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2133]

## Inferences

- Inference: Paramnesia serves as a means for unconscious material to find expression while maintaining the person's self-deception about the source of the memory.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud notes that extensive literature on paramnesia failed to provide hints as to its motivation, suggesting this aspect remained poorly understood.
