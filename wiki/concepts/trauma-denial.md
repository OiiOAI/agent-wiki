# Trauma Denial

## Summary

Trauma denial is Adler's position that traumatic experiences do not determine present unhappiness. Rather than being shaped by past wounds, individuals give meaning to experiences and choose their responses. This rejects the Freudian view that psychic wounds from the past cause current problems, instead placing responsibility on how people interpret and use their experiences.

- Adler definitively denied trauma as a determinant of behavior in his psychological framework. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p35]
- Adler stated: 'No experience is in itself a cause of our success or failure.
