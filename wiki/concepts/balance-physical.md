# Balance (Physical)

## Summary

Balance is described as the 'unsung hero' of physical competencies—an attribute affecting nearly every aspect of mobility that most people don't actively think about until later in life. The book emphasizes that balance can be retained and regained through practice, and that improving balance does not require formal training but can be integrated into daily activities.
