# Mastery

## Summary

Mastery is a lifelong journey of dedicated practice during which skills become progressively easier through sustained effort. It follows an unmistakable pattern of brief progress spurts followed by plateaus. Unlike quick-fix approaches, mastery rewards those who embrace the journey itself rather than focusing solely on goals and destinations.

## Key facts

- Mastery is defined as the mysterious process during which what is at first difficult becomes progressively easier and more pleasurable through practice. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p9]
- Mastery is available to anyone willing to get on the path and stay on it regardless of age, sex, or previous experience. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p14]
- The mastery curve shows learning occurs in spurts followed by plateaus, not in straight lines upward. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p19-21]
- According to Karl Pribram's research, learning involves programming a habitual behavior system, with cognitive and effort systems withdrawing once a skill is learned. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p20-21]
- The journey of mastery is both arduous and exhilarating, bringing unexpected heartaches and rewards, with no final destination. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p19]
- All humans are born with enough raw ability across multiple intelligences to achieve mastery in some field. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p18-19]

## Inferences

- Inference: Mastery requires loving the plateau as much as the spurts, since most of life on the journey is spent on plateaus
