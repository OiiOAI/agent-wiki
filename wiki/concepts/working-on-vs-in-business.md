# Working ON Your Business, Not IN It

## Summary

Gerber's central prescription: business owners must work ON their business rather than IN it. This means conceiving of the business as a product itself—a system that can be replicated 5,000 times. The business should work without the owner constantly present. This perspective shift is what separates extraordinary businesses from struggling ones.

## Key facts

- The core principle is that your business is not your life—it is something apart from you with its own rules and purposes. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p103]
- The primary purpose of your business is to serve your life, not for your life to serve your business. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p103]
- The rule for building the model is: pretend the business is the prototype for 5,000 more just like it. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p104]
- The key questions are: How can I get my business to work without me? How can I get my people to work without my constant interference?. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p115]

## Inferences

- Inference: Until business owners make this perspective shift, their business will control their life rather than enhance it
