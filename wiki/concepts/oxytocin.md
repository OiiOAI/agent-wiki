# Oxytocin

## Summary

Oxytocin is a neuropeptide hormone known for its roles in social bonding, maternal behavior, and pair-bonding. Research in neuroeconomics has shown that intranasal oxytocin administration increases trusting behavior in the trust game without affecting beliefs about repayment or risk-taking in non-social contexts. Oxytocin appears to dampen amygdala activity and reduce fear of social betrayal, and affects caudate nucleus activity during trust decisions.

## Key facts

- Oxytocin increases trust in humans: oxytocin-infused players are more trusting than placebo controls in the trust game, although beliefs about repayment chances are not higher. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p218]
- Oxytocin does not affect risk-taking in a pure risk condition, suggesting its effect is specific to social trust rather than general risk preference. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p218]
- Oxytocin dampens amygdala activity and its connections to the brainstem when subjects view emotionally arousing pictures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p218]
- Oxytocin causes specific activity reduction in caudate nucleus, which may modulate reward learning circuitry involved in updating trust based on trustee behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p229]
- Oxytocin effects are highly context-dependent; while it promotes within-group bonding, it can increase hostility toward out-groups. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p1198]
- The gene coding for CD38 facilitates oxytocin secretion from neurons. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p940]
- Males who had the most activation of reward-related ventral tegmentum when seeing pictures of their children were rated as better parents by their partners. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p933]
- Oxytocin is released during cuddling and increases trust, empathy, generosity, and social bonding. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Mice genetically engineered to lack oxytocin treated mice they had met before like strangers. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Humans who received oxytocin spray showed increased memory for social information and faces of others. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Oxytocin is released during sexual intercourse and orgasm and helps couples bond. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Oxytocin functions to keep males faithful in monogamous relationships. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Oxytocin down-regulates the HPA axis, inhibits the amygdala, suppresses cortisol, and increases feelings of calm and contentment. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]
- Oxytocin may reduce inflammation factors in the blood which promotes wound healing. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p201]

## Inferences

- Inference: Oxytocin may facilitate trust by reducing the fear and anxiety associated with social risk, effectively dampening defensive responses that would otherwise inhibit trusting behavior
- Inference: The selective effect of oxytocin on social but not non-social risk suggests distinct neural pathways for social trust versus abstract monetary risk

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: There is no direct evidence that meditation practice increases oxytocin. However, watching emotionally stimulating movies can raise oxytocin by 47%, suggesting the brain may respond similarly to imagined social connection.
- Uncertain: Whether the Focus on Positive meditation technique actually increases oxytocin has not been directly tested.
