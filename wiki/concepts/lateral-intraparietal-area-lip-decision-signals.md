# Lateral intraparietal area (LIP) decision signals

## Summary

LIP neurons encode decision-related signals that resemble the accumulator variable predicted by random-walk models. Activity in LIP is modulated by the relative value of options and is sensitive to both expected reward magnitude and probability.

## Key facts

- Activity in LIP resembles the accumulation of sensory signals predicted by random-walk models. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p415]
- LIP neurons encode a form of the accumulator variable used in making perceptual choices. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p415]
- Activity in LIP is modulated by prior beliefs and expected rewards, changing the equation for goal values. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p417]
- LIP neurons are sensitive to the value of a particular location in space or a particular action in a relative manner. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p451]
