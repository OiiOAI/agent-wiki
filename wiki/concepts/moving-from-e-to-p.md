# Moving from E to P

## Summary

Moving from Entrepreneurial (E) to Purposeful (P) is one of the Three Commitments, representing a shift from relying on natural abilities and enthusiasm to actively seeking better models and systems. While E approach feels natural and comfortable, it has a ceiling of achievement that cannot be exceeded. The P approach means doing the best something can be done, not just the best one can do. When hitting a ceiling, the Purposeful person looks for new models, systems, new thinking, new skills, and new relationships to push through.

## Key facts

- Entrepreneurial (E) is seeing something to do and racing off with enthusiasm, energy, and natural abilities. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p145]
- All natural ability has a ceiling of achievement that eventually tops out. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p145]
- Purposeful (P) approach means doing the best something can be done, not just the best you can do. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p145]
- Joshua Foer identified the OK Plateau where people stop improving once they reach acceptable performance. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p182]
- The Purposeful person follows the rule that a different result requires doing something different. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p146]
- When E hits a ceiling, people bounce against it repeatedly until resignation sets in, then seek greener pastures. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p147]
- When in P mode, implementing new models and systems may require new thinking, new skills, and new relationships. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p147]
