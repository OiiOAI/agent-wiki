# Nonalcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD)

## Summary

NAFLD is the first stage of fatty liver disease caused by more fat entering the liver or being produced there than exiting it. It is highly correlated with obesity and hyperlipidemia but often flies under the radar with no obvious symptoms. More than one in four people globally have some degree of NAFLD or its more advanced form NASH.

## Key facts

- NAFLD is the first stage of fatty liver disease, where more fat enters the liver or is produced there than exits it. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p97]
- More than one in four people globally have some degree of NAFLD or NASH. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p96]
- NAFLD is highly correlated with both obesity and hyperlipidemia but often has no obvious symptoms. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p96]
- Rising levels of ALT (alanine aminotransferase) are often the first clue that something is wrong with the liver. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p96]
- NAFLD is reversible if fat is removed from the liver, typically via weight loss. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p97]
- NAFLD represents the tip of the iceberg of a global epidemic of metabolic disorders. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p98]
