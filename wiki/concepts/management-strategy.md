# Management Strategy

## Summary

A Management Strategy is not about finding highly competent professional managers but about creating a Management System that produces consistent results. The system transforms people problems into opportunities by orchestrating the decision-making process and eliminating unnecessary decisions. Management Development is described as a marketing tool, not merely a management tool, designed to produce marketing results.

## Key facts

- The successful implementation of a management strategy is not dependent on finding amazingly competent managers with sophisticated techniques. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p187]
- What you need instead is a Management System—the System will become your management strategy and transform your people problems into an opportunity. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p187]
- A Management System is a system designed into your Prototype to produce a marketing result. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p188]
- Management Development is not just a management tool but a marketing tool whose purpose is to create an effective Prototype that finds and keeps customers profitably. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p188]

## Inferences

- Inference: The management system example from the hotel shows that even inexperienced people can produce exceptional results when following well-designed systems.
- Inference: The hierarchy of systems consists of: How We Do It Here, How We Recruit/Hire/Train, How We Manage It Here, and How We Change It Here.
