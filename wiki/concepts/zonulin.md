# Zonulin

## Summary

Zonulin is a protein produced in the gut whenever gluten is present, acting as a cellular gatekeeper that regulates the integrity of tight junctions between intestinal epithelial cells. Where there is zonulin, there is permeability. Zonulin is also capable of altering tight junctions at the blood-brain barrier, which has implications for Alzheimer's disease development.

## Key facts

- Zonulin is produced in the gut whenever gluten is present and acts as a cellular gatekeeper of sorts, regulating the integrity of tight junctions in between epithelial cells. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p247]
- Where there is zonulin, there is permeability. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p247]
- Zonulin is capable of altering the tight junctions at the blood-brain barrier, which has been implicated early on in the development of Alzheimer's disease. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
- Dr. Alessio Fasano, founder of Mass General's Center for Celiac Research, is credited with discovering zonulin as an important mediator of gut permeability. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p247]
