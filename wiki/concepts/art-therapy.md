# Art Therapy

## Summary

Art therapy is an experiential mode of treatment where a person addresses their needs through creative expression. The book identifies it as a recommended approach for dealing with digital addiction.

## Key facts

- Art therapy is described as an experiential mode of treatment where a person addresses their needs through creative expression. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p124]
- The book identifies seven types of art therapy/expression: poetry, music, acting, drawing, dancing, sculpting, and painting. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p124]
- The book recommends signing up for actual classes in chosen art forms rather than using art apps on phones. [raw/books/productivity/Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life - Williams, James W.pdf#p124]
