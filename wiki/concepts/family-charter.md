# Family Charter

## Summary

The Family Charter is a RULER tool that establishes shared emotional guidelines for family members. It involves creating a document describing desired feelings and strategies for supporting each other's emotional well-being, functioning as a proactive guide rather than a reactive set of rules.

## Key facts

- The Family Charter process involves four steps: Set yourself up for success (build the charter); Explore (be an emotion scientist, listen to understand); Strategize (support with short-term and long-term strategies); Follow up (provide regular check-ins and continuous support). [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p166]
- Adults must take the Meta-Moment and be their best self before they can help a child regulate—parents and caregivers are role models whose facial expressions, vocal tones, and body language matter. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p166]
- Ellyn (Esme's mother) didn't have a charter in place when Esme was bullied, but she applied the emotion scientist approach by asking the right questions, listening, and helping strategize future responses. [raw/books/psychology/Permission to feel _ unlocking the power of emotions to help - Marc Ph D Brackett; OverDrive, Inc.pdf#p168]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Family Charter represents a shift from reactive discipline to proactive emotional support, requiring adults to examine and regulate their own emotions first.
