# Chronic Stress

## Summary

A state of unrelenting activation of the fight-or-flight mechanism caused by modern life's persistent psychological threats. Unlike acute stress which evolved to handle immediate dangers, chronic stress causes ongoing physiological activation that damages physical and mental health.

## Key facts

- Human beings evolved to handle acute stressors such as animal attacks, not the constant psychological stress of modern life. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p79]
- Humans can be worried about things that are not currently happening and may never happen at all due to our ability to imagine future scenarios. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p79]
- Chronic stress disrupts serotonin levels and is a major factor in the development of depression and anxiety. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p80]
- Physical problems caused or worsened by stress include heart disease, stroke, immune deficiency, cancer, serious gastrointestinal disorders, and sleep disturbances. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p80]
- Robert Sapolsky states that most Westerners will 'drop dead someday of a stress-related disease.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p80]
