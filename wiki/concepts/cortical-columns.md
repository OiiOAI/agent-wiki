# Cortical Columns

## Summary

Cortical columns are the fundamental functional units of the neocortex, proposed by Vernon Mountcastle as implementing a universal algorithm for all forms of intelligence. Each column occupies about one square millimeter and extends through the neocortex's 2.5mm thickness. Columns are further divided into minicolumns containing about 100 neurons each. The theory proposes that every column creates reference frames to model whatever objects or concepts its inputs represent.

## Key facts

- A cortical column occupies about one square millimeter of the neocortex surface and extends through the full 2.5mm thickness. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p36]
- There are approximately 150,000 cortical columns in a human neocortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p36]
- Each cortical column is further divided into several hundred minicolumns, each containing a little over 100 neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p36]
- Vernon Mountcastle proposed that all cortical columns perform the same basic function, differing only in what they are connected to. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p35]
- Cortical columns cannot be seen under a microscope but are identified by how their neurons respond to the same part of the retina or patch of skin. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p36]
- Every part of the neocortex generates movement; there are no pure motor or pure sensory regions. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p31]
- The theory proposes that each cortical column has cells equivalent to grid cells, place cells, and head direction cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p72]

## Inferences

- Inference: Since every cortical column performs the same basic algorithm, language and vision are fundamentally the same process operating on different inputs.
