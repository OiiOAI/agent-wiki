# Genetic Linkage Analysis

## Summary

Genetic linkage analysis is a strategy for finding genes that affect humans by taking advantage of DNA polymorphisms that differ between individuals. It detects coinheritance of a mutated gene responsible for a disease and a nearby polymorphic DNA marker. The method correlates segregation of DNA polymorphisms with segregation of a genetic trait of interest in families. A statistically rigorous linkage value called a lod score (logarithm of odds) equal to or greater than 3 indicates that a DNA marker is significantly linked to a genetic trait. Classical linkage analysis failed to reveal genes for schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, inspiring alternative strategies.

- Linkage analysis takes advantage of DNA polymorphisms that differ between random individuals. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p111]
