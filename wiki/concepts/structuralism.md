# Structuralism

## Summary

Structuralism emerged in 1960s French thought as a reaction against French humanism and existentialism. It shifted focus from social structures to linguistic structures, marking what is known as the 'linguistic turn.' The central figure in its development was Swiss linguist Ferdinand de Saussure, whose differentiation between langue (formal grammatical system) and parole (actual speech) became foundational.
