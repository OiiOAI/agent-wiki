# Dialectical Materialism

## Summary

Dialectical materialism is the philosophical approach developed by Marx through fusing Hegel's dialectic with Feuerbach's materialism. It focuses on dialectical relationships within the material world, emphasizing that material conditions shape social structures and consciousness. This approach contrasts with Hegel's idealism which focused on consciousness.

## Key facts

- Marx extracted Hegel's dialectic and Feuerbach's materialism and fused them into dialectical materialism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p55]
- The term dialectical materialism was first used by Joseph Dietzgen in 1887 and made central by Georgi Plekhanov in 1891. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p56]
- Marx argued that the problems of modern life can be traced to real material sources and that solutions lie in overturning those structures by collective action. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p55]
- While Hegel applied the dialectic only to ideas, Marx felt it applied to material aspects of life including the economy. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p54]
