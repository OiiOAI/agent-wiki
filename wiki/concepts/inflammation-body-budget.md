# Inflammation and Body Budget

## Summary

Inflammation is a key mechanism linking mental and physical illness through the body budget. Proinflammatory cytokines can cross from the body into the brain, and the brain has its own inflammatory system. When the body budget is chronically unbalanced, chronic inflammation sets in, which affects predictions, particularly those managing the body budget. Inflammation makes body-budgeting regions insensitive to corrections from the body. Chronic inflammation can cause changes in brain structure, interferes with neural connections, and even kills neurons. The immune system is an ingredient in far more illnesses than expected, including diabetes, obesity, heart disease, depression, insomnia, reduced memory, and dementia.
