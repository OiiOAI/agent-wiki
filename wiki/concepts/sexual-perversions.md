# Sexual Perversions

## Summary

Sexual perversions involve either a change in the sexual object (homosexuality, fetishism) or in the sexual aim (sadism, masochism, voyeurism, exhibitionism). They demonstrate that sexuality and reproduction do not coincide. The essence of perversions lies in the exclusiveness with which deviations are carried out, putting aside the sexual act serving reproduction. Freud argues that perverse traits are rarely absent from normal sexual life, and normal sexuality has emerged from earlier sexuality by collecting serviceable parts and subordinating them to reproduction.
