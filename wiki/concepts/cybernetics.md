# Cybernetics

## Summary

Cybernetics, from the Greek kybernetes meaning 'steersman' or 'governor', is the scientific study of control and communication in the animal and the machine, as defined by Norbert Wiener. Emerging in the 1950s alongside AI, cybernetics emphasized control and closed-loop feedback, and proposed that machines could have 'purposes' or 'goals.' Though overshadowed by other AI approaches, cybernetics delivered insights including the Good Regulator Theorem and the concept of essential variables that remain significant for consciousness research.

## Key facts

- Cybernetics was described by Norbert Wiener as 'the scientific study of control and communication in the animal and the machine.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p174]
- The term 'cybernetics' comes from the Greek kybernetes, meaning 'steersman' or 'governor.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p174]
- Cybernetics emphasized control and its primary applications were in systems involving closed-loop feedback from output to input, such as guided missiles. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p174]
- Cybernetics proposed that machines could appear to have 'purposes' or 'goals,' providing a new bridge from the non-living to the living. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p175]
- Cybernetics split away from other AI approaches which emphasized offline, disembodied, abstract reasoning exemplified by chess-playing computers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p175]
- While cybernetics was increasingly relegated to the sidelines, it delivered many valuable insights whose significance is only now becoming recognised. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p175]
- The history of cybernetics reveals how academic disciplines now considered distinct were once part of a common approach. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p185]
- Norbert Wiener published 'Cybernetics' in 1948, defining it as 'the science of communication and control in animal and machine.'. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p113]
- Cybernetics proposed that the same general principles controlling a thermostat may also be seen in economic systems, market regulation, and political decision-making systems. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p113]
- Cybernetics distinguished between 'negative' or 'balancing' feedback, which dampens effects of change and leads to less of the action creating it, and 'positive' or 'reinforcing' feedback, which amplifies change. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p114]
- Feedback is defined as 'a method of controlling a system by reinserting into it the results of past performance,' enabling self-regulation based on actual rather than expected performance. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p114]
- Cybernetics recognized similarity between feedback, the interplay of thesis and antithesis in Hegelian/Marxist dialectics, and Adam Smith's theory of market self-regulation—all implying circular causality. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p114]
- Wiener, von Neumann, and Turing applied cybernetics as a model for the brain, developing the 'computational theory of mind' that fueled the cognitive revolution in psychology. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p117]
