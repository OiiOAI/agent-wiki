# Wealth vs. Money

## Summary

Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep. Money is how we transfer wealth—it's social credits, the ability to have credits and debits of other people's time. Wealth is the factory, robots, computer programs running at night, even money in the bank being reinvested.
