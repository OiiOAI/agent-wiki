# Phantom Vibration Syndrome

## Summary

Phantom vibration syndrome is a common phenomenon where people feel their phone vibrating in their pocket when it is not actually vibrating. A 2012 study found that 89 percent of college undergraduates reported feeling phantom phone vibrations, and it is particularly prevalent among medical interns where fake buzzing is strongly associated with stress. The term was rated 'word of the year' by Australia's Macquarie Dictionary in 2013.
