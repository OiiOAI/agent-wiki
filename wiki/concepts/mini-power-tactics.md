# Mini Power Tactics

## Summary

Mini Power Tactics are advanced Profit First strategies requiring minimal time but providing significant effectiveness. They include renaming the TAX account to 'The Government's Money,' hiding accounts from view, automating outside income transfers, setting up account balance notifications, and using bank checks for immediate payment processing.

## Key facts

- Renaming the TAX account to 'THE GOVERNMENT'S MONEY' makes owners 'way more reluctant to steal money from the government than from the TAX account.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p179]
- Hiding accounts from view (except OPEX) prevents considering those funds when making spending decisions. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p160]
- Bank checks (bank pay processing) pull money immediately upon processing, eliminating float but providing accurate real-time balance awareness. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p180]
