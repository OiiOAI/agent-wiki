# Pain Period

## Summary

The period of time when one begins to open themselves up emotionally and make themselves more vulnerable to others. This temporarily causes one to behave in a less attractive manner as they sort through years of emotional baggage and trauma.

## Key facts

- Pain Period is defined as the period of time when one begins to open themselves up emotionally and make themselves more vulnerable to others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
- This temporarily causes one to behave in a less attractive manner as they sort through years of emotional baggage and trauma. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
- It's usually a period accompanied by a lot of emotional stress and pain. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
- The pain period is necessary to go from a low self-esteem and unconfident person to a high self-esteem and True Confidence person. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p269]
