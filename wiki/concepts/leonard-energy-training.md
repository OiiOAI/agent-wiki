# Leonard Energy Training

## Summary

Leonard Energy Training (LET) is a discipline developed by George Leonard, inspired by his practice of aikido. Introduced since 1973, it uses the body as both a metaphor for daily life problems and a learning facility for changing how one handles them. LET includes exercises for balancing and centering, returning to center, gaining energy from unexpected blows, and cultivating ki.

## Key facts

- LET uses the body as a metaphor for the way you deal with the problems of daily life and as a learning facility for changing how you deal with those problems. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L785-L787]
- LET has been introduced to around 50,000 people since 1973, ranging from athletes to corporate executives to couples. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L782-L784]
- LET exercises include balancing and centering, returning to center, gaining energy from unexpected blows, and exercises involving ki visualization. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L782-L960]
