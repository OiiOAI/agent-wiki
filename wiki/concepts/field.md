# Field

## Summary

Field is a concept developed by Pierre Bourdieu referring to a network of objective positions within a social arena, characterized by struggles over specific forms of capital. Each field has its own specific logic and generates among actors a belief about what is at stake. Fields are arenas of battle where occupants employ various kinds of capital (economic, cultural, social, symbolic) to safeguard or improve their position. The field of power (politics) structures all other fields, and the state is the site of struggle over the monopoly of symbolic violence.

## Key facts

- The field is 'a network of relations among the objective positions within it,' existing apart from individual consciousness and will. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]
- 'The field is also a field of struggles' where occupants seek to safeguard or improve their position and impose principles of hierarchization favorable to their products. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]
- The positions of various agents in the field are determined by the amount and relative weight of the capital they possess. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]
- Bourdieu discussed four types of capital: economic, cultural, social, and symbolic. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]
- The field of power (politics) is of utmost importance, as its hierarchy of power relationships serves to structure all other fields. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]
- Bourdieu laid out a three-step process for analyzing a field: trace its relationship to the political field, map the objective structure of relations among positions, and determine the habitus of agents occupying various positions. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p435]

## Inferences

- Inference: The concept of field allows Bourdieu to analyze how different social arenas (art, education, politics) operate according to their own logics while being connected through the struggles over capital.
