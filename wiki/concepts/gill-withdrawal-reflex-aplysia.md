# Gill-Withdrawal Reflex in Aplysia

## Summary

The gill-withdrawal reflex in Aplysia is a simple defensive behavior that has become a model system for studying the cellular and molecular basis of learning and memory. Touching the siphon produces a brisk withdrawal of both the siphon and gill, and this reflex can be modified by habituation, sensitization, and classical conditioning, providing insights into both short-term and long-term memory mechanisms.

## Key facts

- The gill is an external organ that Aplysia uses to breathe, lying in the mantle cavity and covered by the mantle shelf ending in the siphon. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p201]
- The abdominal ganglion, containing only 2000 nerve cells, controls heart rate, respiration, egg laying, inking, mucus release, and withdrawal of the gill and siphon. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p201]
- Kandel and Kupfermann settled on the gill-withdrawal reflex in 1968 as the simplest behavior for studying learning. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p201]
- Repeated light touches produce habituation: the reflex progressively weakens as the animal learns the stimulus is trivial. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p202]
- Sensitization is produced by applying a strong shock to the head or tail, causing the animal to produce an exaggerated gill-withdrawal reflex. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p202]
- The neural architecture of the gill-withdrawal reflex is invariant: the same 6 sensory neurons relay touch sensation to the same 6 motor neurons in every snail. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p208]
- Learning leads to changes in the strength of synaptic connections between sensory and motor neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p213]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery that the neural circuit for the gill-withdrawal reflex is invariant and precisely wired made it possible to study how learning modifies specific synaptic connections.
