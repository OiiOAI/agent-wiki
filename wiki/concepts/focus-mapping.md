# Focus Mapping

## Summary

Focus Mapping is Fogg's systematic method for matching oneself with Golden Behaviors. The process involves creating a 'Swarm of Behaviors' from brainstorming, then using a two-round sorting process: Round One plots behaviors on an impact dimension (high to low effectiveness for the aspiration), and Round Two adjusts along a feasibility dimension (considering motivation and ability). Behaviors that land in the upper-right corner are Golden Behaviors. The method takes about twenty minutes and eliminates guesswork from behavior selection.

## Key facts

- Focus Mapping uses the Swarm of Behaviors method where people generate many behavior options using Magic Wanding (imagining they could get themselves to do anything). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p66]
- In Round One, behaviors are sorted by impact without considering feasibility. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p73]
- In Round Two, the question 'Can I get myself to do this?' combines motivation and ability considerations. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p73]
