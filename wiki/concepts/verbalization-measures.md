# Verbalization measures of situation awareness

## Summary

Verbalization measures attempt to gather insight into both the operator's knowledge and strategies at each subtask throughout experiment runs by asking the operator to describe (verbalize) their current thought process. These measures provide insight into both situation awareness and thought processes, as well as the perceived importance of information. However, they are limited by the operator's ability to communicate and may not capture all knowledge or strategies used.

## Key facts

- Verbalization methods attempt to gather insight into both the operator's knowledge and the operator's strategies at each subtask throughout the experiment runs. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p209]
- Verbalization measures provide insight into both situation awareness and thought processes. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p210]
- Verbalization measures provide insight into the perceived importance of information. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p210]
- Verbalization measures may be better suited to declarative knowledge that can be easily expressed by the operator compared to procedural knowledge. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p211]
- Subjects may not describe all of their knowledge or strategies during verbalization, making complete identification of their knowledge difficult. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p211]
- Due to the disconnect between situation awareness and decision making, the predictive ability of verbalization measures for estimating final performance is limited. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p211]
