# Emotion Regulation

## Summary

Emotion regulation, defined by James Gross as the process by which individuals influence which emotions they have, when they have them, and how they experience and express them, is positioned as the most complex RULER skill. The book identifies five broad categories of regulation strategies: mindful breathing, forward-looking strategies (anticipation and avoidance), attention-shifting strategies (distraction), cognitive-reframing strategies (reappraisal), and the Meta-Moment. Emotion regulation depends on foundational factors including diet, exercise, sleep, and pleasurable activities. The book emphasizes that regulation is not about suppressing feelings but about owning them and choosing how to respond.
