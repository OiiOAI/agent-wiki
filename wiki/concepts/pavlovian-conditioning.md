# Pavlovian Conditioning

## Summary

Classical conditioning where responses become associated with stimuli through repeated pairing. In business, this means creating brand associations so powerful that mere exposure to the brand triggers desired emotional and behavioral responses.

## Key facts

- Pavlovian conditioning: powerful effects come from mere association—the neural system causes salivation at a bell even without food; human brains yearn for beverages held by attractive people. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p294]
- Coca-Cola strategy: the trade name and trade dress act as stimuli; purchase and ingestion are the desired responses—all powered by Pavlovian association with things consumers like or admire. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p292]
- To enhance Pavlovian effects, choose exotic names (Coca-Cola vs. Glotz's Sugared Water), appearance like wine rather than sugared water, carbonation for champagne association, and unique flavor. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p295]
- Pavlovian effects come from mere association: when people get bad news, they hate the messenger—this makes delivering unwelcome truths psychologically difficult. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p239]
- In Pavlovian conditioning, powerful effects come from mere association; the neural system of Pavlov's dog causes it to salivate at the bell it can't eat. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p308]
- The 'Coca-Cola' trade name and trade dress will act as the stimuli, and the purchase and ingestion of our beverage will be the desired responses. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p306]
- To create lollapalooza results, Coca-Cola must use both operant conditioning and Pavlovian conditioning, along with social proof—all factors working together. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p306]
- For Pavlovian effects, the exotic and expensive-sounding name 'Coca-Cola' was wisely chosen over a pedestrian name, and the beverage was colored to look like wine. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p308]
