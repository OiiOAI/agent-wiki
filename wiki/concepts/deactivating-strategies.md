# Deactivating Strategies

## Summary

Deactivating strategies are behaviors and thoughts used by people with avoidant attachment to suppress intimacy and keep partners at arm's length. They include not committing despite staying together, focusing on partner's imperfections, pining for exes, flirting with others, avoiding saying 'I love you', pulling away when things go well, keeping secrets, and avoiding physical closeness.

## Key facts

- Deactivating strategies are any behaviors or thoughts used to squelch intimacy and suppress the attachment system. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p97]
- Common deactivating strategies include saying 'I'm not ready to commit' while staying together for years. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p97]
- Avoidants focus on small imperfections in their partner and allow these to interfere with romantic feelings. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p97]
- Researchers found that avoidants are slower to recognize words related to their own attachment worries like 'separation', 'fight', and 'loss'. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p95]
- When distracted, avoidants' defenses lessen and their true attachment feelings surface, showing the attachment machinery is still present. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p95]
