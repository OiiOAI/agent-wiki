# Innovation (Business Development)

## Summary

Innovation in the context of business development is the process of continuously asking 'What is standing in the way of my customer getting what he wants from my business?' It involves finding simpler, more effective ways to serve customers and operates through a cycle of Innovation, Quantification, and Orchestration.

## Key facts

- Innovation continually poses the question: What is standing in the way of my customer getting what he wants from my business?. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p127]
- For Innovation to be meaningful, it must always take the customer's point of view. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p127]
- Innovation simplifies your business to its critical essentials and should make things easier for you and your people. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p127]
- Innovation is the mechanism through which your business identifies itself in the mind of your customer and establishes its individuality. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p127]
- Innovation is described as the 'Best Way' skill, producing high energy in companies where it is nurtured. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p127]
