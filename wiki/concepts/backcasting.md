# Backcasting

## Summary

Backcasting is a planning technique that involves working backward from a desired future outcome to determine the steps needed to reach it. Unlike forecasting (which projects forward from the present), backcasting starts with a clear goal and traces the path that would lead to its achievement, revealing necessary actions and potential obstacles that forward-looking planning might miss.

## Key facts

- Research by Deborah Mitchell, J. Edward Russo, and Nancy Pennington found that prospective hindsight—imagining an event has already occurred—increases the ability to correctly identify reasons for future outcomes by 30%. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1869-L1872]
- Backcasting is illustrated by Frederick Law Olmsted's design of Central Park, which he envisioned in its mature state and worked backward to plan features that would develop over decades. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1880-L1884]
- A practical backcasting exercise involves imagining a newspaper headline announcing goal achievement, then identifying the decisions, events, and actions that led to that outcome. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1887-L1891]
- Backcasting reveals the 'positive space' of future outcomes but overrepresents it due to our natural optimism, requiring premortems to balance the picture. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1911-L1913]
- Backcasting starts by defining a positive future outcome and then determines the sequence of actions needed to reach it from the present. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2088-L2095]
- Frederick Law Olmsted used backcasting in designing Central Park, working backward from his vision of the completed park. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2088-L2095]
- Backcasting differs from forecasting by designing desirable futures rather than predicting probable ones. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2088-L2095]
- The technique makes abstract goals concrete by identifying the path from future success to present circumstances. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2088-L2095]

## Inferences

- Inference: Backcasting can be combined with premortems (working backward from negative futures) for comprehensive scenario planning.
