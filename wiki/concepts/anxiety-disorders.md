# Anxiety Disorders

## Summary

Psychological conditions characterized by excessive, irrational fear and worry that impair normal functioning. Includes generalized anxiety disorder, panic disorder, specific phobias, and social anxiety disorder. All share physical symptoms of the severe stress response and cognitive misinterpretation of situations. They often feed into each other and can seed depression. Exercise has been shown to be as effective as medication for treating anxiety.

## Key facts

- Anxiety disorders affect approximately 40 million Americans, or 18 percent of the population, in any given year. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p76]
- Social anxiety disorder afflicts 15 million Americans and involves consuming fear of any social situation involving meeting, talking to, or being seen by others. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p76]
- Anxiety sensitivity means that awareness of physical arousal alone (heart rate or breathing) can trigger anxiety or panic. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p76]
- Exercise has been shown to work like Prozac and other antidepressants and antianxiety drugs by elevating neurotransmitters including serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p74]
- The book presents a patient case (Amy) where regular 30-minute morning elliptical training plus yoga reduced anxiety symptoms and anxiety sensitivity. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p78]
- Exercise serves as 'social lubricant' that reduces anxiousness, allowing socially anxious individuals to practice social skills in a nontoxic setting. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p30]
