# Prometheus and Epimetheus

## Summary

In Greek mythology, Prometheus ('fore-thinker') represents progress through opportunism and optionality, while Epimetheus ('after-thinker') represents backward thinking trapped in retrospective narratives. Taleb uses this framework to contrast antifragile forward-looking action with fragile narrative-dependent thinking.

## Key facts

- Prometheus means 'fore-thinker' and represents progress of civilization through the ability to look ahead and exploit opportunities. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p270]
- Epimetheus means 'after-thinker,' equivalent to someone who falls for retrospective distortion, fitting theories to past events ex post. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p270]
- Optionality is Promethean; narratives are Epimethean—one has reversible and benign mistakes, the other symbolizes irreversibility of opening Pandora's box. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p270]
- You make forays into the future by opportunism and optionality, while reliance on narratives is the opposite—you are domesticated by uncertainty and set back. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p270]
