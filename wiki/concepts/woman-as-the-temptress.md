# Woman as the Temptress

## Summary

When the Oedipus-Hamlet revulsion besets the soul, the world, body, and woman become symbols not of victory but of defeat. A monastic-puritanical, world-negating ethical system then transfigures all mythological images—the queen goddess of flesh becomes the queen of sin. This represents a failure to cope with life through restriction of consciousness.

## Key facts

- When revulsion from the flesh remains to beset the soul, the world, body, and woman above all become symbols no longer of victory but of defeat. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p99]
- Saint Bernard of Clairvaux drove away a young woman who came to soothe his headaches, and God rewarded his chastity with immediate cure. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p101]
