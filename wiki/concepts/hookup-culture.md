# Hookup Culture

## Summary

Hookup culture describes the casual sexual encounters prevalent on American college campuses, characterized by drinking, spontaneous pairing off, and emotional detachment. The 'friends-with-benefits' arrangement represents the ideal college relationship—maintaining friendship while engaging in sex without emotional entanglement. A deliberate attempt exists to keep emotions out of sex for both genders. Ratu, a 22-year-old Ivy League student, describes how commitment is seen as a 'life sentence' and relationships are perceived as a loss of independence. This culture reflects and reinforces generational anxiety about vulnerability and dependency in intimate relationships.
