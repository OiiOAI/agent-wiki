# Pearl Habit

## Summary

A Pearl Habit is a Tiny Habit that transforms an irritant or negative prompt into something positive. The name comes from how oysters create pearls—around an irritant (a grain of sand). Similarly, Pearl Habits use negative events or annoyances as Anchors for beneficial behaviors, turning something irritating into something beautiful.

## Key facts

- Pearl Habits use prompts that start out as irritants then turn into something beautiful through the habit. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p141]
- Example: Fogg used the clicking of his air-conditioning wall control (which woke him up) as an Anchor to relax his face and neck, improving his sleep. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p140]
- Amy created a Pearl Habit: 'After I feel insulted by my ex-husband, I will think of something nice to do for myself,' which eventually transformed their hostile relationship into a civil coparenting arrangement. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p142]
- Amy eventually saw her ex-husband's insults as inadvertent gifts because they prompted her to take better care of herself. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p143]

## Inferences

- Inference: Pearl Habits demonstrate that you cannot always control external events but can control your response, turning negatives into positives.
