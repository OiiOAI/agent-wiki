# Postmodern Social Theory

## Summary

Postmodern social theory emerged as a distinct way of thinking different from modern social theory. It rejects foundationalism, grand narratives (metanarratives), and universal rational foundations. Key figures include Jean-Francois Lyotard, Fredric Jameson, and Jean Baudrillard. Postmodern theory questions the possibility of rational solutions to society's problems.
