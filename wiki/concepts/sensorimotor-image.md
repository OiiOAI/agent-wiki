# Sensorimotor Image

## Summary

A sensorimotor image is the conjunction or binding of all relevant sensory input to produce a discreet functional state that ultimately may result in action. It is generated within the context of what the animal is presently doing—context is as important as content. Sensorimotor images contain a sense of future, a pulling toward action to be performed, representing the oldest part of mindness that brought the mind into existence.
