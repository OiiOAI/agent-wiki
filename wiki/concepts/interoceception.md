# Interoception

## Summary

Interoception is the brain's representation of all sensations from internal organs and tissues, hormones in the blood, and the immune system. It produces the basic spectrum of feeling from pleasant to unpleasant, from calm to jittery. Barrett proposes that interoception is a core ingredient of emotion, analogous to how flour and water are core ingredients of bread. The brain's interoceptive network includes body-budgeting regions that send predictions to control the body's internal environment and a primary interoceptive cortex that represents bodily sensations.

## Key facts

- Interoception is the brain's representation of all sensations from your internal organs and tissues, the hormones in your blood, and your immune system. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p71]
- Simple pleasant and unpleasant feelings come from interoception and are universal, flowing through every waking moment of life. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p71]
- The brain's interoceptive network issues predictions about the body, tests simulations against sensory input, and updates the brain's model of the body in the world. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p85]
- Body-budgeting regions control the body's internal environment by predicting motor changes like heart rate, breathing, and cortisol release. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p86]
