# Nucleus Accumbens in Impulsivity

## Summary

The nucleus accumbens is critically involved in multiple forms of impulsivity, with distinct roles for the core and shell subregions. Premature responding on the 5CSRTT depends on dopamine-dependent mechanisms in the nucleus accumbens, with evidence of opponency between core and shell in the control of responding. High-impulsive rats show reduced D2/3 receptor binding in the shell region, and GAD activity is reduced in the left core subregion. The core and shell subregions interact to produce waiting impulsivity.

## Key facts

- Premature responding on the 5CSRTT depends on dopamine-dependent mechanisms in the nucleus accumbens. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p87]
- Core lesions of the nucleus accumbens exacerbate the effects of systemic D-amphetamine to elevate premature responding, whereas shell lesions reduce such responding following D-amphetamine, demonstrating opponency between core and shell. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p87]
- High-impulsive rats show reduced D2/3 receptor binding in the ventral (nucleus accumbens) but not dorsal striatum before exposure to cocaine. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p88]
- Magnetic resonance imaging showed reduced gray matter density in the left nucleus accumbens core of high-impulsive rats, accompanied by reduced activity of GABA decarboxylase. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p89]
- Elevated DA in the shell region is associated with high impulsivity, while reduced GAD activity in the core reflects reductions in medium spiny output neurons or dendritic spine density. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p89]

## Inferences

- Inference: The opponency between shell and core subregions suggests a push-pull mechanism regulating impulsivity, where elevated shell output is normally counteracted by core output.
