# Five Strategies for Life Through Death

## Summary

This is a practical philosophy for transforming consciousness of mortality into something productive and life-enhancing. The five strategies are: (1) Make the awareness visceral—convert death from an abstraction into felt, physical reality; (2) Awaken to the shortness of life—treat mortality as a continual deadline that clarifies priorities; (3) See the mortality in everyone—recognize shared vulnerability to create empathy and connection; (4) Embrace all pain and adversity—accept life's unavoidable suffering and use it for growth; (5) Open the mind to the Sublime—confront the unknowable to gain perspective and release from banality.

## Key facts

- The five strategies are designed to forge a practical philosophy for transforming consciousness of mortality into something productive and life enhancing. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p578]
- Strategy 1 (Make the awareness visceral) involves meditating on death as physical and present in the body from birth. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p702]
- Japanese samurai warriors meditated on the gut as the center of connection to death, creating physical death awareness. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p704]
- Strategy 2 (Awaken to shortness of life) treats mortality as a continual deadline, giving focus and creative energy to all actions. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p580]
- Strategy 3 (See mortality in everyone) leads to handling human nature with tolerance and grace, even cutting difficult people down to size. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p583]
- Schopenhauer wrote that the deep pain at every death arises from something inexpressible and peculiar to each individual, absolutely lost. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p582]
- Strategy 4 (Embrace pain and adversity) involves seeing events as fateful and using adversity to educate oneself about human nature. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p584]
- Strategy 5 (Open mind to Sublime) trains the mind to experience the unknowable through contemplating vastness, origins, or dwarfing landscapes. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p585]
- William Hazlitt observed: 'Our repugnance to death increases in proportion to our consciousness of having lived in vain.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p581]
- Michel de Montaigne wrote that premeditation of death is premeditation of freedom, and knowing how to die frees us from subjection. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p586]
