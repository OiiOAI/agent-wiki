# Culture of Honor

## Summary

Cultures emphasizing rules of civility, courtesy, and hospitality where taking retribution is expected for affronts to honor of self, family, or clan. Failure to retaliate is shameful. Found worldwide including the American South, pastoralist societies, and regions with minimal rule of law. Parenting in honor cultures tends to be authoritarian, and children are aggressive following honor violations.

## Key facts

- In Nisbett and Cohen's study, Southern male undergraduates showed massive increases in testosterone and glucocorticoids after being deliberately insulted ('asshole') in a hallway. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p296]
- Southern violence is disproportionately rural, among people who know each other, and concerns slights to honor rather than material gain. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p294]
- Southern honor culture may derive from Scottish, Irish, and northern English herders who disproportionately settled the Southern U.S. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p293]
- Honor killings are typically committed by male blood relatives with family approval, often planned rather than spontaneous, and rationalized on religious grounds. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p298]
- The American South traditionally emphasizes legacy, long cultural memory, and continuity of family; in rural Kentucky in the 1940s, 70 percent of men had the same first name as their father. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p293]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Southern culture of honor represents a case where historical pastoralist roots shaped regional culture despite environmental differences.
- Inference: Honor killings represent an extreme form where group reputation takes precedence over individual life.
