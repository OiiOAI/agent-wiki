# Precommitment

## Summary

Precommitment is a strategy for conserving willpower by locking oneself into a virtuous path in advance, making it impossible or unthinkable to stray. Henry Morton Stanley used oaths and public declarations to precommit himself, while modern tools like stickK.com and Covenant Eyes software allow people to set up financial or social penalties for lapses.

## Key facts

- Precommitment was exemplified by Odysseus, who had himself lashed to the mast so he could hear the Sirens' songs without being able to act on the temptation to swim to them. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p140]
- Henry Morton Stanley used precommitment by writing oaths to himself by candlelight and creating a public persona as 'Bula Matari' (Breaker of Rocks) that forced him to live up to his image. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p140]
- stickK.com users who set financial penalties and appointed referees succeeded nearly 80 percent of the time, while those without financial stakes or referees succeeded only 35 percent of the time. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p141]
- In a study of Philippine smokers, those offered commitment contracts were nearly 40 percent more likely to be nicotine-free after one year compared to a control group, demonstrating lasting effects of precommitment. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p142]
