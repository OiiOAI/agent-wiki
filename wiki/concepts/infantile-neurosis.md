# Infantile Neurosis

## Summary

A neurotic disorder occurring in early childhood that Freud considered crucial for understanding adult neuroses. The Wolf Man's infantile neurosis comprised three phases: an initial period of seduction by his sister at age 3.25, followed by an anxiety phase centered on wolf phobia after the dream at age 4, and finally an obsessional neurosis with religious content beginning at age 4.5. Freud argued that such childhood neuroses prove the etiological significance of infantile experiences, independent of later real-life conflicts.
