# Dream lag effect

## Summary

The dream lag effect refers to the observation that waking experiences tend to be incorporated into dream mentation either immediately after the experience or approximately one week later, suggesting a temporal structure to the incorporation of memory into dreaming.

## Key facts

- Waking experiences tend to be incorporated into dream mentation either immediately after the experience, or else about a week later (the dream lag effect). [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p6]
