# Hemispherotomy

## Summary

A hemispherotomy is a neurosurgical procedure that involves complete neural disconnection of one brain hemisphere. The surgeon removes the temporal lobe and cuts through all white-matter tracts connecting the affected hemisphere with the rest of the brain and body. The isolated hemisphere remains inside the skull with its blood supply intact, forming a 'living but isolated island of cortex'. This extreme version of split-brain surgery prevents electrical storms originating in a damaged hemisphere from spreading. Seth observed this procedure performed on a child with severe epilepsy, noting how it raised questions about the material basis of the self.

## Key facts

- A hemispherotomy involves complete neural disconnection of the brain's dysfunctional hemisphere. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p254]
- The surgeon removes the temporal lobe and cuts through all white-matter tracts connecting the affected hemisphere with the rest of the brain and body. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p254]
- The isolated hemisphere remains inside the skull, still connected to its blood supply—it is a living but isolated island of cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p254]
- Anil Seth observed a hemispherotomy performed on a child of just over six years old who had suffered severe epilepsy since birth. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p253]
- Since the disconnected hemisphere is still connected to a blood supply and 'alive', it might potentially sustain its own isolated consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p259]
