# Reason-Respecting Tendency

## Summary

Reason-Respecting Tendency is a psychological tendency where humans have a natural love of accurate cognition and joy in its exercise. This tendency makes people especially prone to learn well when a teacher gives correct reasons for what is taught. It has an important implication: ideas get through best when reasons for them are meticulously laid out. Unfortunately, the tendency is so strong that even meaningless or incorrect reasons will increase compliance with orders and requests.

## Key facts

- Reason-Respecting Tendency is characterized by a natural love of accurate cognition and a joy in its exercise. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p594]
- This tendency accounts for the widespread popularity of crossword puzzles, other puzzles, and bridge and chess columns. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p594]
- The tendency makes man especially prone to learn well when given correct reasons for what is taught. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p594]
- Few practices are wiser than thinking through reasons before giving orders and communicating these reasons to the recipient. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p594]
- Even a person's giving of meaningless or incorrect reasons will increase compliance with orders and requests. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p596]
- This tendency was demonstrated in psychology experiments where 'compliance practitioners' successfully jumped to the head of lines in front of copying machines by explaining their reason: 'I have to make some copies.'.
