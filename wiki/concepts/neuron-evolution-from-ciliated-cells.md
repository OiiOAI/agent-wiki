# Neuron Evolution from Ciliated Cells

## Summary

The hypothesis that neurons first arose to improve sensory-motor integration efficiency during the transition from sponges to Cnidaria, with ciliated cells in sponge larvae serving as the evolutionary precursor to true neurons.

## Key facts

- Gaspar Jekely proposed that neurons first arose to improve the efficiency of sensory-motor integration during the transition from sponges to Cnidaria. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p136]
- Sponge larvae have swimming cells with short cilia for constant random motion and steering cells with long cilia concentrated at one end that are sensitive to light. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p136]
- The hypothesized sequence of neuron evolution: (1) clustering of sensory and motor cells in proximity, (2) growth of sensory cell extensions, (3) electrical communication within extensions (axons), (4) chemical communication across the synaptic cleft. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p137]
- Seth Grant discovered that sponges possess protosynaptic building blocks—genes responsible for presynaptic and postsynaptic sites found in later animals. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p138]
- Sponges lack the molecular signals that turn on synaptic gene expression in a coordinated fashion during development, explaining why they do not form synapses despite having the building blocks. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p139]

## Inferences

- Inference: The inability of sponges to form synapses due to missing developmental signals suggests that synapse formation required both the molecular components and the genetic program to express them coordinately.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact molecular signals that trigger coordinated synapse development remain uncertain based on this chunk.
