# Double Consciousness

## Summary

Double consciousness is a concept developed by W. E. B. Du Bois to describe the sense of "two-ness" experienced by African Americans—feeling of seeing and measuring oneself through others' eyes. This concept captures the psychological experience of being forced to view oneself through the perspective of a dominant white society while maintaining one's own identity. Du Bois developed this concept most prominently in "The Souls of Black Folk" (1903) as part of his broader theoretical work on race.
