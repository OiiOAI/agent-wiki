# Hippocampus as Sequence Generator

## Summary

The hippocampus functions as a general-purpose sequence generator that creates ordered representations of events without necessarily encoding the spatial or temporal dimensions per se. This sequence generation capacity underlies both memory for the past and planning for the future.

## Key facts

- The hippocampus is described as a neuronal sequence generator that can create a nominal order of sequential events and reproduce it upon recall. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p273]
- Hippocampal patients recalled spatial and perceptual details but the order in which they recalled events was unrelated to the order in which events occurred. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p273]
- Sequential neuronal activity can track the succession of events, which can be placed on a timeline when compared to units of a clock, but this does not show that neuronal activity computes time. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p262]
- The hippocampus may be responsible for constructing sequences of information chunks while chunk content is encoded in and retrieved from the neocortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p275]
- Neuronal mechanisms of postdiction and prediction are not that different; planning is constructive memory and 'memory of the future'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p275]

## Inferences

- Inference: The hippocampus serves as a 'librarian' pointing to cortical modules (the library), creating multiplexed pointers that concatenate neocortical information chunks into sequences for both encoding and retrieval.
