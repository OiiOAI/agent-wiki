# Master of the Two Worlds

## Summary

Freedom to pass back and forth across the world division, from the perspective of the apparitions of time to that of the causal deep and back—without contaminating the principles of the one with those of the other, yet permitting the mind to know the one by virtue of the other—is the talent of the master. Nietzsche's Cosmic Dancer exemplifies this talent, turning gaily and lightly from one position to another.
