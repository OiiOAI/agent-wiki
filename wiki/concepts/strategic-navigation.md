# Strategic Navigation

## Summary

Strategic navigation is the process of making key assumptions underlying a strategy explicit and then checking their accuracy as events unfold. It is vital for adapting strategy when assumptions prove wrong.

## Key facts

- Strategic navigation is the process of making assumptions explicit and then checking them as events unfold. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p295]
- Executives should write down the key assumptions underlying their strategy to facilitate adaptation. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p295]
- The heart of moving from difficulty to action is making assumptions, some of which may be wrong; without making them explicit and checking accuracy, adaptation becomes very hard. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p295]
