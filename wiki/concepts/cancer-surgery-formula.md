# Cancer Surgery Formula

## Summary

Munger's model for turnaround situations: diagnose the business to find anything sound that can survive on its own, cut away everything else. If that fails, liquidate. This approach is demonstrated in GEICO's turnaround.

## Key facts

- Fixing sick businesses: look at the mess, figure out if there's anything sound left that can live on its own by cutting away everything else, and if you find sound, just cut away everything else. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p205]
- GEICO had a perfectly magnificent business in a mess but still working—misled by success, they did foolish things, but the wonderful business was still there once folly was cut out. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p206]
- This model repeats over and over—one may find one or two or three in a lifetime that are very good, and twenty or thirty that are good enough to be quite useful. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p206]
