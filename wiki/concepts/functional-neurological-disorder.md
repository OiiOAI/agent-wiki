# Functional Neurological Disorder

## Summary

Functional neurological disorders are conditions where symptoms such as motor problems, paralysis, or blindness are present but no standard physiological cause can be identified. They are the second most common reason for new outpatient neurological referrals, accounting for around 16 percent of cases. Predictive processing offers a framework for understanding them as resulting from misallocated precision-weightings where unwilled patterns of expectation and attention cause genuine symptoms to fall into line with hidden expectations.

## Key facts

- Functional neurological disorders are cases where symptoms such as motor problems, paralysis, or even blindness are present, but no standard physiological cause can be identified. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p51]
- Functional disorders are the second most common reason for new outpatient neurological referrals, accounting for around 16 percent of all such cases. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p52]
- The term 'functional' reflects that some aspect of normal functionality is altered or lost despite the absence of any structural or recognized neurological cause—in other words, there is impairment without evidence of systemic damage or disease. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p51]
- Hoover's sign demonstrates functional weakness—when patients with unexplained leg weakness are distracted, they can exert normal pressure with the afflicted leg, revealing preserved biomechanical ability. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p59]
- Functional disorders may result from unwilled misallocations of precision that act as self-fulfilling prophecies, where predictions of pain or impairment become highly overweighted. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p57]

## Inferences

- Inference: The existence of tubular visual field defects—where the blind zone is the same diameter regardless of viewing distance—provides evidence that functional disorders follow intuitive notions of anatomy rather than optically possible patterns, suggesting predictions are sculpting experience.

## Uncertainties
