# Retrieval Methods

## Summary

The four retrieval methods for finding and reusing notes in a Second Brain are Search, Browsing, Tags, and Serendipity. Search is most useful when you know what you're looking for. Browsing uses folders and manual navigation. Tags create cross-disciplinary connections. Serendipity involves keeping focus broad and leveraging visual patterns and sharing with others.

- Tags can overcome the limitation of folders siloing ideas from each other. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p143]
- Serendipity is amplified by visual patterns and by sharing ideas with others. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p144]
