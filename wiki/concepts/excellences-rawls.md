# Excellences

## Summary

Excellences are characteristics and abilities of a person that it is rational for everyone (including oneself) to want. They differ from exclusive goods (like property) because they are enjoyed by associates as well as the person himself when properly displayed. Excellences form the human means for complementary activities and are conditions of human flourishing.

## Key facts

- Excellences are characteristics and abilities of the person that it is rational for everyone to want us to have. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p394]
- Excellences differ from exclusive goods: imagination, wit, beauty, and natural abilities are goods for others too, enjoyed by associates when properly displayed. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p394]
- Excellences are conditions of human flourishing; they are goods from everyone's point of view. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p395]
- Excellences enable one to carry out a more satisfying plan of life enhancing one's sense of mastery, while being appreciated by those with whom one associates. [raw/books/philosophy/A Theory of Justice_ Original Edition - Rawls, John.pdf#p395]
