# Sixth Amendment Rights

## Summary

Constitutional protections that Michael Singer credits with saving him during the federal prosecution. The Sixth Amendment guarantees the right to be informed of the nature and cause of accusations, which allowed Singer's defense team to force the government to specify exactly which dealer acquisitions and accounting entries they were presenting at trial.

## Key facts

- The Sixth Amendment states 'The accused shall enjoy the right...to be informed of the nature and cause of the accusations'. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p206]
- Singer invoked his Sixth Amendment right to have the assistance of counsel of his choice when requesting a trial date extension for Randy Turk's cancer treatment. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p208]
- Singer credited the Constitution, including the Sixth Amendment, with providing the protections that allowed him to defend himself against the government. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p206]
