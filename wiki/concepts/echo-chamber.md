# Echo Chamber

## Summary

An echo chamber is the result of natural drift toward confirmatory thought, where people only hear ideas that confirm their existing beliefs. The expression conjures up the image of what results from our natural tendency toward confirmation. This occurs even among those most dedicated to truthseeking, like judges and scientists, and represents a major obstacle to accurate decision-making.

## Key facts

- Without an explicit charter for exploratory thought and accountability to that charter, our tendency when we interact with others follows our individual tendency, which is toward confirmation, resulting in echo chambers. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1220-L1225]
- Echo chambers were observed among some groups of poker players during breaks, where one player would complain about bad luck and another would nod in assent as a prelude to telling their own hard-luck story. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1220-L1225]
- In political discourse, virtually everyone asserts they are in the rational group exchanging ideas, while people on the other side are in an echo chamber. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1375-L1380]
- We often don't even realize when we are in the echo chamber ourselves because we're so in love with our own ideas that it all just sounds sensible and right. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1375-L1380]
