# Cognitive Load

## Summary

Cognitive load refers to the total amount of mental effort being used in working memory, both individually and at the team level. Psychologist John Sweller defined three types: intrinsic (fundamental to the problem), extraneous (from the environment, often can be eliminated), and germane (for learning and high performance). Teams suffer when given responsibilities beyond their cognitive capacity, leading to context switching, decreased motivation, and becoming delivery bottlenecks. Organizations should match team responsibilities to cognitive load and reduce extraneous load through automation and better tools.

## Key facts

- Cognitive load is the total amount of mental effort being used in the working memory, applicable to both individuals and teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L922-L922]
- Intrinsic cognitive load relates to aspects of the task fundamental to the problem space. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L928-L929]
- Extraneous cognitive load relates to the environment in which the task is being done and can often be eliminated through automation. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L930-L932]
- Germane cognitive load relates to aspects of the task that need special attention for learning or high performance. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L933-L934]
- When cognitive load exceeds team capacity, teams cease to act like high-performing units and behave like loosely associated individuals. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L942-L945]
- Organizations should minimize intrinsic cognitive load through training and good technology choices, eliminate extraneous cognitive load through automation, leaving more space for germane cognitive load. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L934-L938]
- A team responsible for a complex domain should not have any more domains assigned, not even simple ones, due to disruption of flow. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L969-L971]
- A single team should avoid being responsible for two complicated domains as the team will behave as two subteams while everyone is expected to know about both. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L973-L976]
- The OutSystems Engineering Productivity team split into microteams each responsible for a single domain, resulting in increased motivation and improved flow and quality. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L956-L961]

## Inferences

- Inference: Organizations can increase the cognitive capacity of teams by reducing extraneous load through better tools, automation, and team-first working environments.
