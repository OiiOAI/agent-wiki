# Focus on Relaxation

## Summary

A meditation technique that involves relaxing the body while simultaneously meditating on the bodily sensations of that relaxation. The technique aims to make unconscious relaxation processes conscious, deepening enjoyment and developing the ability to induce relaxation at will.

## Key facts

- Focus on Relaxation involves relaxing muscles and meditating on the sensations of them relaxing. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p83]
- The technique can induce a positive feedback loop: becoming more aware of pleasant relaxation sensations makes them more pleasant, inducing more relaxation. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p82]
- This is different from a nap because you stay conscious and aware of the relaxation sensations. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p83]
- The technique develops all three elements: concentration (focusing on relaxation), sensory clarity (detecting fine details), and acceptance (allowing relaxation to happen). [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p83]
