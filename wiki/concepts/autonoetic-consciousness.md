# Autonoetic Consciousness

## Summary

Autonoetic consciousness, as defined by Endel Tulving, is the awareness of oneself as the subject of experience — a 'feeling of ownership' over memories and experiences. It enables mental time travel, allowing one to recollect one's personal past and imagine one's personal future. LeDoux argues autonoetic consciousness is unique to humans and is essential for emotional experience: no self, no emotion.

## Key facts

- Autonoetic consciousness involves awareness of oneself as the experiencing person, part of the experience, with a 'feeling of ownership'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p292]
- Mental time travel — recollecting the past and imagining the personal future — is a key feature of autonoetic consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p293]
- LeDoux argues autonoetic consciousness is unique to humans and likely depends on the frontal pole. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p305]
- For LeDoux, emotions are autonoetic conscious experiences — 'no self, no emotion'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p344]
- Episodic memory depends on autonoetic consciousness and requires 'what-where-when' representations plus the experiencer's self. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p292]
- Autonoetic consciousness is the ability to mentally model oneself in relation to time, including awareness of personal past and imagined future. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p364]
- Autonoetic consciousness is unique to humans and arose sometime between 50,000 and 200,000 years ago. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p365]
- Autonoetic consciousness depends on unique features of the human prefrontal cortex: the frontal pole region with novel components, enriched connections between higher-order prefrontal networks and lower-order processors, and novel cell types and molecular mechanisms. - An emotion is the experience that something of value is happening to you, and autonoetic consciousness makes emotions possible by allowing personalization of risk through questions like 'How dangerous is it to me?'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p366]

- The autonoetically conscious human mind can generate narratives in which danger is counterfactually minimized so that selfish desires can be satisfied free of guilt or anxiety. ## Inferences

- Inference: Autonoetic consciousness requires language capacities and the ability to form higher-order representations about one's own mental states — capacities LeDoux attributes uniquely to humans.
- Inference: Autonoetic consciousness is a double-edged sword: it enables humanity's greatest achievements (art, music, science) as well as its darkest behaviors (distrust, hate, greed, selfishness).
- Inference: The evolution of autonoetic consciousness posed a threat to organismic unity because it allows conscious decisions that can harm the organism's survival, yet it must have added sufficient survival value to persist.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Some researchers (Menant, Beran) suggest nonverbal autonoetic consciousness may exist in apes, but LeDoux remains skeptical.
- Uncertain: The exact timeline of when autonoetic consciousness emerged (estimates range between 50,000 and 200,000 years ago) is uncertain.
