# Build-Measure-Learn Feedback Loop

## Summary

The Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop is the fundamental activity of a startup, where ideas are turned into products, customers' responses are measured, and learning occurs about whether to pivot or persevere. The goal is to minimize the total time through this feedback loop. The loop is named in order of activity but planned in reverse: figure out what needs to be learned, determine what needs to be measured, then build the product to run the experiment and get that measurement.

## Key facts

- The fundamental activity of a startup is to turn ideas into products, measure how customers respond, and learn whether to pivot or persevere. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p18]
- All successful startup processes should be geared to accelerate the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p18]
- The key insight is that none of the activities (building, measuring, learning) by themselves is paramount; what matters is minimizing total time through the feedback loop. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p73]
- Planning works in reverse order: determine what needs to be learned, figure out what needs to be measured, then build what is needed to run that experiment. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p75]
