# Weekly Review

## Summary

The Weekly Review is a critical maintenance practice in GTD where one reviews all Projects, active project plans, Next Actions, Agendas, Waiting For, and Someday/Maybe lists to ensure the system remains current and trustworthy. It provides an opportunity to gather and process all stuff, update lists, and achieve a state of being clean, clear, current, and complete. Without it, the mind cannot fully trust the system and will resume holding reminders itself.

## Key facts

- The Weekly Review is identified as a master key to maintaining a functional GTD system. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p88]
- The Weekly Review is the time to gather and process all your stuff, review your system, update your lists, and get clean, clear, current, and complete. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p89]
- Without weekly review, people don't look ahead at their calendars consistently enough to stay current about upcoming events and deadlines. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p60]
- All outstanding projects and open loops should be reviewed weekly to keep the mind from taking back the job of remembering and reminding. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p88]
- The Weekly Review is whatever is needed to get head empty again and get oriented for the next couple of weeks. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p248]
- Get Clear involves collecting loose papers, getting 'in' to empty (processing notes, e-mails, texts), and emptying one's head of new items. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p248]
- Get Current involves reviewing Next Actions lists, previous calendar data, upcoming calendar, Waiting For list, Projects list, and relevant checklists. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p249]
- Get Creative involves reviewing the Someday/Maybe list and capturing new, creative ideas. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p251]
- The recommended time for Weekly Review is two hours early in the afternoon of the last workday. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p252]
