# Iemoto

## Summary

The iemoto is a Japanese organizational system characterized by a master-disciple relationship structure. Francis Hsu termed this relationship 'kin-tract' because it partakes of characteristics of both kinship groups and modern contract-based associations. Through this system, masters transmit knowledge to disciples, often in nonverbal fashion, and the system has been credited with transmitting quality consciousness across generations.

## Key facts

- The iemoto relationship was called 'kin-tract' by Francis Hsu, indicating it combines kinship and contractual characteristics. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p415]
- Iemoto organizations transmit skills from generation to generation through master-disciple relationships, often nonverbally. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p418]
