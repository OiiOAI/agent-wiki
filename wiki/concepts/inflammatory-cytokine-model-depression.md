# Inflammatory Cytokine Model of Depression

## Summary

Major depression may be an extreme form of sickness behavior induced by inflammation. Depression is more common in those with inflammatory conditions like heart disease, arthritis, diabetes, and cancer. The volume of inflammatory markers in the blood and the risk for depression correlate—the higher the levels of these markers, the more severe the depression. Lab animals injected with bacterial LPS (lipopolysaccharide) exhibit symptoms of depression, supporting this model.

## Key facts

- Major depression may be an extreme form of sickness behavior, which is well known to farmers, zookeepers, and scientists. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
- Depression is more common in those with inflammatory conditions like heart disease, arthritis, diabetes, and cancer, and the volume of inflammatory markers correlates with depression severity. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
- The inflammatory cytokine model of depression has challenged the preexisting paradigm for treatment. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
