# The Entrepreneur, The Manager, and The Technician

## Summary

Gerber proposes that everyone who goes into business is actually three-people-in-one: The Entrepreneur (visionary, dreamer, living in the future), The Manager (pragmatic, craving order, living in the past), and The Technician (doer, living in the present, wanting to do everything personally). The problem is that while each wants to be the boss, none wants to have a boss. Most small business owners are 10% Entrepreneur, 20% Manager, and 70% Technician—badly unbalanced.

## Key facts

- The Entrepreneur is the visionary, the dreamer, living in the future, always looking for opportunities and change. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p29]
- The Manager is the pragmatic personality that brings planning, order, and predictability; he lives in the past and craves order over change. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p31]
- The Technician is the doer who loves the feel of things and getting things done, living in the present and mistrusting those who want more work than is necessary. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p32]
- Most small business owners are approximately 10% Entrepreneur, 20% Manager, and 70% Technician. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p35]
- When these three personalities are balanced, you have an incredibly competent individual; when unbalanced, the Technician typically seizes control and runs the business into the ground. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p35]
