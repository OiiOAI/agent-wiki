# Action Selection

## Summary

Action selection is the neural process by which organisms choose among competing possible actions based on sensory information, internal states, and learned outcomes. It involves assessing situations, determining needs, simulating and evaluating possible actions, and ultimately releasing one action while inhibiting others. This process is mediated by interactions between the cortex, basal ganglia, thalamus, and midbrain motor command centers.
