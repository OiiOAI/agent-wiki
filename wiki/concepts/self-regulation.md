# Self-Regulation

## Summary

Self-regulation refers to the ability to direct one's thoughts, emotions, and behaviors toward goal achievement. Oettingen's research presents mental contrasting as a powerful self-regulation tool that helps people engage more forcefully when goals are achievable and disengage when they are not. This dual function—increasing energy for feasible goals while promoting letting go of unattainable wishes—allows for more efficient allocation of mental and physical resources across life's challenges.

## Key facts

- Self-regulation enables people to allocate their energy efficiently between engaging with feasible wishes and disengaging from unattainable ones. [raw/books/psychology/Rethinking Positive Thinking - Gabriele Oettingen.pdf#p61]
- Mental contrasting functions as a self-regulation tool, helping people make wiser decisions about which wishes to pursue. [raw/books/psychology/Rethinking Positive Thinking - Gabriele Oettingen.pdf#p61]
- Research on vocational school students showed that those who mentally contrasted and had high expectations of success received better grades from their teachers in mathematics. [raw/books/psychology/Rethinking Positive Thinking - Gabriele Oettingen.pdf#p65]
