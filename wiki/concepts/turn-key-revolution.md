# The Turn-Key Revolution

## Summary

The Turn-Key Revolution is described as a revolutionary way of doing business that has transformed American small business. At its heart is the Business Format Franchise—a system that provides not just a brand name but an entire way of doing business. This revolution has created dramatically higher success rates for franchised businesses compared to independent businesses.

## Key facts

- The Turn-Key Revolution is a revolutionary development that has transformed American small business, comparable in impact to the Industrial Revolution. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p85]
- The Business Format Franchise not only lends its name to the smaller enterprise but also provides the franchisee with an entire system of doing business. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p89]
- Less than 5 percent of franchises are terminated annually, compared to 80 percent of independently owned businesses failing in the first five years. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p88]
- The true genius of Ray Kroc's McDonald's is the Business Format Franchise, which accounts for the franchise phenomenon's explosive growth over the past 40 years. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p88]
