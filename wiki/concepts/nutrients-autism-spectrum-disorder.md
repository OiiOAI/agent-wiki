# Nutrients for autism spectrum disorder

## Summary

Research on broad-spectrum multinutrient supplementation for autism spectrum disorder (ASD) has shown promising results including improvements in tantrums, hyperactivity, receptive language, overall functioning, social responsiveness, sensory profile, and nonverbal IQ. Self-harming behaviors were reduced by 50 percent in one study comparing nutrients to medication.

## Key facts

- A study led by Professor James Adams involving twenty children with ASD showed improvements in sleep and gastrointestinal problems, with trends suggesting some improved behavior, eye contact, and receptive language. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p125]
- A larger RCT with 141 children and adults with autism found positive effects of multinutrients over placebo in reducing tantrums and hyperactivity, as well as improving receptive language and overall functioning. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p125]
- In a study comparing multinutrients to conventional medications for ASD, multinutrients were superior to medication in reducing both irritability and self-harming behaviors, with self-harm reduced by 50 percent. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p125]
- There were far fewer side effects reported for those on multinutrients compared with those on medications (33 vs. 214). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p125]
