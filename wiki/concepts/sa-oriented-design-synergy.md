# SA-Oriented Design Synergy

## Summary

SA-oriented design is described as a holistic approach rather than a set of rules to create satisfactory designs. The design principles do not work in isolation—many support SA in multiple ways. At times, principles may seem at odds with each other, requiring designers to prioritize and select measures to evaluate the design. Understanding how principles support SA enables designers to make decisions that allow operators to achieve and maintain high SA, maximizing user-system performance.
