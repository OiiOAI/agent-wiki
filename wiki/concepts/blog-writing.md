# How to Write a Blog

## Summary

Blogs can be written to persuade (using VSN-C with slogan, symbol, salient idea, surprise, and story), to instruct (starting with an empowerment promise and concluding by delivering on it), or to tell a story. Effective blogs feature local color, dialogue, humor, and details. Positive reviews are encouraged with a recommended ratio of three positive reviews for every negative review. Pictures supply details that help sell ideas.

## Key facts

- Write a blog to persuade using the VSN-C framework plus Slogan, Symbol, Salient idea, Surprise, and Story. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p361]
- Write a blog to instruct by starting with an empowerment promise and concluding by delivering on your promise. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p361]
- Write a blog to tell a story to keep in touch with friends, customers, alumni, or people who share interests. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p361]
- Humans are storytelling animals who love stories that are funny or interesting, especially blogs about people and places. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p361]
- Include details because details sell, dialogue because it makes stories feel real, and pictures because pictures supply detail. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p368]
- Maintain a positive attitude by recommending three or more positive reviews for every negative review. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p365]
