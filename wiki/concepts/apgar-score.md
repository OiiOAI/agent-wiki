# Apgar Score

## Summary

A method for measuring the health of a newborn baby immediately after delivery, scored between 0 and 10 based on five criteria observed at 1 and 5 minutes after birth.

## Key facts

- The Apgar score ranges between 0 and 10, derived from five criteria: heart rate, respiratory effort, muscle tone, reflex irritability, and skin color. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L680]
- Each criterion receives a score between 0 and 2 at 1 and 5 minutes after birth, giving each child two Apgar scores. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L685]
- A score below 3 is considered critical and the baby may require medical attention; a score above 7 is considered normal. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L690]
- The Apgar score was devised by Virginia Apgar in 1952. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L695]
