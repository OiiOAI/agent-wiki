# Intersectionality Theory

## Summary

Intersectionality Theory addresses how multiple forms of oppression and identity categories (such as gender, race, class, and sexuality) intersect and create unique experiences of discrimination and privilege. Listed under structural oppression theories in the feminist theory chapter.

## Key facts

- Intersectionality Theory is categorized under structural oppression within modern feminist theory. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p24]
- Intersectionality theory holds that women experience oppression in varying configurations and degrees of intensity due to intersecting arrangements of social inequality including class, race, global location, sexual preference, age, and gender. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Intersectionality theory argues that it is intersection itself that produces a particular experience of oppression, and one cannot arrive at an adequate explanation by using an additive strategy of gender plus race plus class. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Patricia Hill Collins proposes the concept of 'the matrix of domination' to describe the intersecting hierarchical structures of oppression and privilege. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Intersectionality theory recognizes that the privilege exercised by some women and men turns on the oppression of other women and men. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Crenshawe showed that black women frequently experience discrimination as black women, but courts routinely refuse to recognize this unless it can be shown to be general 'sex discrimination' (read white women) or 'race discrimination' (read black men). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Lorde argues that 'the institutional rejection of difference is an absolute necessity in a profit economy which needs outsiders as surplus people,' and describes a 'mythical norm' in US society as 'white, thin, male, young, heterosexual, Christian, and financially secure.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
