# Structuration Theory

## Summary

Structuration Theory, developed by Anthony Giddens, addresses the relationship between agency and structure by arguing that structures are both the medium and outcome of human actions. It is presented as a major example of agency-structure integration in the chapter.

## Key facts

- Structuration Theory is presented as a major example of agency-structure integration. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p25]
- Giddens argues that 'every research investigation in the social sciences or history is involved in relating action to structure' and that 'there is no sense in which structure determines action or vice versa.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p423]
- The basic domain of study in structuration theory is 'social practices ordered across time and space.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p424]
- Giddens distinguishes between discursive consciousness (ability to describe actions in words) and practical consciousness (actions taken for granted without verbal expression). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p425]
- Giddens defines structure as 'the structuring properties [rules and resources]... which lend social practices systemic form.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- Structure is 'always both constraining and enabling.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- The concept of structuration involves that 'the structural properties of social systems are both medium and outcome of the practices they recursively organize.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p427]
