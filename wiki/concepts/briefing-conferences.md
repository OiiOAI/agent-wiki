# How to Run a Briefing Conference

## Summary

Briefing conferences introduce industrial participants to research developments. Success requires requiring a Business Messages slide from each speaker (a special Contributions slide showing business relevance), distributing copies of all slides to participants for their trip reports, and providing a summary of big ideas. The moderator should prepare the summary with the participants' trip report needs in mind.

## Key facts

- Briefing conferences introduce industrial participants to what is going on at the front lines of research. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p351]
- Require each talk to end with a slide titled Business Messages to tell participants there is relevance to business and to force speakers to think about business messages. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p352]
- Give participants copies of all slides because they need to prepare trip reports when they get home. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p353]
- Summarize the big ideas for participants, focusing on what you want them to remember for their trip reports. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p353]
- A Business Messages slide functions as a special kind of Contributions slide. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p352]
