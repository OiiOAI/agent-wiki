# Contrast Therapy

## Summary

Contrast therapy involves alternating between heat and cold exposure (such as sauna and cold plunge), creating good stress on the vascular system by constricting and expanding blood vessels. This pumping action delivers more oxygen and nutrients to muscles while also improving lymphatic circulation. The book recommends 15 minutes in a sauna followed by 3 minutes in a cold plunge, repeated several times.
