# Creatine and Brain Function

## Summary

Creatine acts as a high-energy buffer in the brain, helping rapidly recycle ATP. While ATP use holds stable during mental exertion, creatine levels drop in support of the brain's energetic needs, and higher levels of brain creatine are correlated with better memory performance. Vegetarians and vegans have lower blood creatine levels than omnivores and may benefit from supplementation. Carriers of the Alzheimer's risk gene ApoE4 have lower levels of brain creatine.
