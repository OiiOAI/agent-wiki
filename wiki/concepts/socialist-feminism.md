# Socialist Feminism

## Summary

Socialist feminism develops around three goals: critiquing the distinctive yet interrelated oppressions of patriarchy and capitalism from women's experience; developing methods for social analysis out of an expanded understanding of historical materialism; and incorporating the significance of ideas into a materialist analysis. It uses the concept of domination (a relationship where the dominant makes the subordinate an instrument of the dominant's will) to unify analyses of patriarchy and capitalism. Socialist feminism broadens Marxian 'material conditions' to include sexuality, procreation, childrearing, unpaid domestic tasks, emotional care, and knowledge production.

## Key facts

- Socialist feminism seeks to critique the distinctive yet interrelated oppressions of patriarchy and capitalism from a standpoint in women's experience. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p374]
- Engels (in The Origins of the Family, Private Property, and the State, 1884) argued that women's subordination results from social relations with a clear and traceable history, not from immutable biology. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p375]
- Engels traced the 'world historic defeat of the female sex' to the replacement of hunting and gathering by herding and farming economies, where men's physical advantages gave them systematic economic advantage over women. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p375]
- Socialist feminism uses 'domination' as a unifying concept: a relationship where the dominant makes the subordinate an instrument of the dominant's will, refusing to recognize the subordinate's independent subjectivity. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p377]
- Socialist feminism broadens Marxian 'material conditions' to include sexuality, procreation, childrearing, unpaid domestic tasks, emotional care, and the production of knowledge. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p377]
- Dorothy E. Smith's approach to socialist feminism focuses on the 'relations of ruling,' exploring how capitalist patriarchal domination is enacted through an interdependent system of control including the economy, the state, and the privileged professions. ## Inferences

- Inference: Socialist feminism identifies three emphases: materialist feminism (gender relations within global capitalism), relations of ruling (control through texts), and cultural materialism (state policies, ideologies, and mass media interacting with subjectivity).
