# Law of Exposure

## Summary

The Law of Exposure states that boundaries must be made visible to others and communicated in relationship. Secret boundaries lead to problems; boundaries affect relationships whether or not they are communicated. The authors emphasize that healing always takes place in the light.

## Key facts

- The Law of Exposure says that boundaries need to be made visible to others and communicated in relationship. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p105]
- Boundary problems arise from relational fears including guilt, not being liked, loss of love, loss of connection, loss of approval, receiving anger, and being known. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p105]
- The authors state: 'Boundaries exist, and they will affect us, whether or not we communicate them. If our boundaries are not communicated and exposed directly, they will be communicated indirectly or through manipulation.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p106]
- Ephesians 5:13-14 is cited: 'Everything exposed by the light becomes visible, for it is light that makes everything visible.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p106]
- The authors emphasize: 'Healing always takes place in the light.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p106]
- Psalm 51:6 is cited: 'Surely you desire truth in the inner parts; you teach me wisdom in the inmost place.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p106]
