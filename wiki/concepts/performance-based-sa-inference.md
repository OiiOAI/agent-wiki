# Performance-Based SA Inference

## Summary

Measuring situation awareness by inference from operator performance and actions, discussed in Chapters 9-10. Chapter 9 by Pritchett and Hansman demonstrates successful applications, while Chapter 10 by Wickens provides important caveats about the method.

## Key facts

- Chapter 9 by Amy Pritchett and R. John Hansman examines the use of expected performance-based responses to measure SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]
- Their work has been among the most successful in measuring SA by inference from operator actions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]
- Chapter 10 by Christopher Wickens shows that different performance measures can lead to entirely different inferences about SA. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]
- Wickens' research carries important lessons for those undertaking to infer SA from performance. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p11]

## Inferences

- Inference: Performance-based inference is useful but requires careful selection of performance measures to avoid misleading conclusions about SA levels.
