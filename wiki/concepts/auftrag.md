# Auftrag (Mission Command)

## Summary

Auftrag (mission/mission contract) is a device between superior and subordinate that fosters responsibility without stifling initiative. Once a subordinate accepts the contract, they have total freedom within its constraints as to how to proceed. The mission concept includes: Here's what I think we face, Here's what I think we should do and why, Here's what we should keep our eye on, Now talk to me. The mission should include an explicit or implied 'in order to' statement. Micromanaging or failing to live up to the contract destroys this mechanism.
