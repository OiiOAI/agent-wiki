# Lotka-Volterra Dynamics

## Summary

Lotka-Volterra dynamics are nonlinear dynamical systems that can generate sequential patterns, used in Active Inference for timing and temporal learning. Originally describing predator-prey population oscillations, generalized versions with multiple populations can generate sequences of peaks. They have been applied to eyeblink conditioning and handwriting generation.

## Key facts

- Generalized Lotka-Volterra dynamics follow f(x,v) = x ° (v + Ax) where x is a vector, ° denotes elementwise product, v contains intrinsic rates, and A encodes prey-predator relationships. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p158]
- Lotka-Volterra systems have been applied to eyeblink conditioning to simulate cerebellar function and timing of responses relative to conditioned stimuli. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p160]
- Sequential peaks in Lotka-Volterra dynamics can be associated with alternative attracting points, enabling generation of sequences like handwriting. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p166]
