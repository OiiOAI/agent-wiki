# Balancing and Centering

## Summary

Balancing and centering is a technique from Leonard Energy Training that involves distributing body weight evenly and concentrating awareness in the abdomen rather than the head or shoulders. The practice serves as a metaphor for psychological balance and can help individuals respond more effectively to crisis situations.

## Key facts

- To be balanced means the weight of the body is distributed evenly, right and left, forward and back, from head to toes. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L788-L790]
- To be centered means bodily awareness is concentrated in the center of the abdomen, an inch or two below the navel. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L791-L792]
- Psychological balance and centering depends to a great extent on being physically balanced and centered. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L794-L795]
- The body can be considered a metaphor for everything else—relationships, work, chores, and entire life can be centered and balanced. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Term Fulfillment - George Leonard.epub#L875-L877]

## Inferences

- Inference: The exercise of touching the physical center during crisis can significantly alter attitude and ability to deal with situations.
