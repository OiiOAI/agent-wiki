# The Four Laws of Behavior Change

## Summary

The Four Laws of Behavior Change is the core framework presented in the book for creating new habits. The laws are: (1) Make It Obvious—create clear cues for the desired behavior; (2) Make It Attractive—pair the desired behavior with something appealing; (3) Make It Easy—reduce friction that impedes the behavior; and (4) Make It Satisfying—create immediate rewards that reinforce the behavior. The framework applies inversely to breaking bad habits: make them invisible, unattractive, difficult, and unsatisfying.
