# Abreaction

## Summary

Abreaction is the process by which affectively-charged ideas are discharged through verbal or emotional expression, thereby reducing the excitatory charge. When abreaction occurs fully, recollections release only a minimal quantity of excitation compared to the unprocessed original experience. The concept underlies the therapeutic method of catharsis in hysteria.

## Key facts

- The abreaction of the love she had so long kept down had not been carried out very fully, which contributed to incomplete therapeutic resolution. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p142]
- If the original affect was discharged not in a normal but in an abnormal reflex, this latter is equally released by recollection; the excitation arising from the affective idea is converted into a somatic phenomenon. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p183-184]
- We may conclude from the observations that hysterical symptoms immediately and permanently disappeared when the memory of the event was brought to light and the accompanying affect was aroused and put into words. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p221]
- Freud described how, during hypnotic investigation, the moment at which the physician discovers the occasion when a symptom first appeared is also the moment at which the symptom vanishes through abreaction. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p929]
- Freud observed that the memory of traumatic events remained unusually vivid and retained its full affect, unlike normal memories which undergo wearing away and forgetting. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p929]
- Hysterical patients were understood to suffer from incompletely abreacted psychical traumas. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p931]
- If someone cannot get rid of the accretion of stimulus by abreacting it, the event may remain a psychical trauma. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p930]

## Inferences

- Inference: The therapeutic value of psychoanalysis derives from enabling abreaction of repressed affects that have not found normal discharge.
