# Pattern Generators

## Summary

Pattern generators are ensembles of neurons in the brain stem reticular formation that produce stereotyped, coordinated motor patterns for innate behaviors such as facial expression, breathing, eating, and vomiting. They can operate with minimal input from higher brain regions.

## Key facts

- Pattern generators in the brain stem reticular formation produce stereotyped innate responses including facial emotional expression, breathing, and eating. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1040]
- Facial pattern generator neurons project bilaterally to facial motor neurons, so spontaneous facial expressions are virtually always symmetric. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1041]
- Licking movements are organized near the hypoglossal nucleus; chewing near the trigeminal motor nucleus; sucking near the facial and ambiguus nuclei; and swallowing near the nucleus ambiguus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1041]
- Even a decerebrate rat can make appropriate choices of which foods to swallow and which to reject. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1041]
- The pre-Bötzinger complex in the rostral medulla generates respiratory rhythm and can produce rhythmic firing even in a 0.5 mm thick transverse brain slice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1042]
