# Micro-Curiosities

## Summary

Micro-curiosities are small puzzles, mysteries, or challenges within one's comfort zone that motivate exploration of a larger domain of interest. The concept suggests that big curiosity journeys are not reached by a single dive into the abyss but are composed of many smaller journeys. By breaking ambitious explorations into achievable bits, individuals stretch their boundaries, build confidence gradually, and progress toward completing larger goals.
