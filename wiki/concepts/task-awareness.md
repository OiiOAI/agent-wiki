# Task Awareness

## Summary

Task awareness describes the operator's awareness of what tasks have been done, need to be done, and in what priority. In automated or multioperator systems, task awareness includes knowledge of task queuing and task responsibility—who is responsible for each task. A key trade-off exists between design support for routine versus unexpected circumstances. During routine operations, checklists (paper or automated) provide excellent support for task awareness. During unexpected or unusual operating circumstances, checklists are less effective because departures from preplanned sequences and unexpected shifts in responsibility may be required. Research found that crews who adopted procedural responses to unexpected failures or adhered to standard priority schemes were less successful than those flexible in task management strategies.
