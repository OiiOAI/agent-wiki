# Background Feeling

## Summary

Background feelings are the continuous, often low-grade emotional feelings that define our mental state even when we are not experiencing overt emotions like fear or anger. They arise from background emotions and include sensations such as fatigue, energy, wellness, tension, and relaxation. Background feelings are intimately related to drives and motivations, and are closely tied to core consciousness as an index of momentary inner organism state.

## Key facts

- Background feelings arise from background emotions and include sensations such as fatigue, energy, wellness, sickness, tension, and relaxation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p286]
- Background feelings are so closely tied to core consciousness that they are not easily separable; they serve as a faithful index of momentary parameters of inner organism state. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p287]
- The core ingredients of background feelings include the operations of smooth musculature in blood vessels and organs, the chemical profile near muscle fibers, and chemical profiles signifying threat or optimal homeostasis. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p287]
