# People Strategy

## Summary

A People Strategy is about creating an environment where 'doing it' is more important to employees than not doing it. It involves communicating a compelling game or philosophy that gives employees a sense of purpose, community, and opportunity for growth. The strategy flows from the Primary Aim and Strategic Objective through organizational structure to daily operations.

## Key facts

- You cannot get your people to do what you want—you must create an environment in which 'doing it' is more important to your people than not doing it. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p197]
- A business can create a Game Worth Playing that becomes a place of community with purpose, order, and meaning. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p208]
- The hotel Boss's philosophy stated: 'The work we do is a reflection of who we are. If we're sloppy at it, it's because we're sloppy inside.'. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p199]
- The Boss's game was built on three principles: make the customer feel right whether they are or not, expect everyone to work toward being the best they can be, and view the business as a place where what we know is tested by what we don't know. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p201]
