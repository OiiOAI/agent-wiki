# The Challenge Formula

## Summary

No matter how difficult the situation, we always have three options: (1) Leave, (2) Stay and live by your values (do whatever you can to improve the situation, make room for the pain, and treat yourself kindly), or (3) Stay but do things that either make no difference or make it worse. This formula is used by the WHO in ACT programs for refugees.

## Key facts

- Option 1 (Leave) is not always possible, such as when in prison or a refugee camp. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p245]
- Option 2 means do whatever you can to improve the situation, make room for your pain, and treat yourself kindly. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p245]
- Option 3 comes naturally to all of us when we get hooked by difficult thoughts and feelings. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p245]
- The challenge formula is a central pillar of the WHO ACT program used in refugee camps around the world. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p245]
