# Curiosity Resilience

## Summary

Curiosity resilience is the ability to push through frustrations and setbacks on curiosity journeys. It involves reconnecting to purpose, changing one's narrative about setbacks, creating strong support networks, putting positive emotions to work, and cracking each setback like a detective by formulating plans, breaking problems into components, processing evidence, and shifting directions when hitting dead ends.

## Key facts

- The more one puts oneself in challenging situations, the more one develops skills and coping mechanisms to face challenges. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p148]
- Kazuhide Sekiyama states that one must have a strong core that allows persistence through lots of hardships. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p150]
- Hazel Forsyth treats the absence of evidence as negative evidence, which is almost as important as the evidence itself. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p147]
- Adrian Newey depersonalizes problems by focusing on making sure everyone treats failures as engineering problems with reasons that simply are not yet understood. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p154]
- Journaling helps organize and make sense of events, giving a way of putting setbacks in perspective. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p155-156]
- Not all setbacks are of equal magnitude; imagining oneself ten years forward discussing the current problem helps assess its true significance. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p156-157]
