# Confront Reality

## Summary

Behavior #8 in Covey's framework. Confronting reality means taking tough issues head-on, sharing bad news as well as good, naming 'elephants in the room,' addressing 'sacred cows' and 'undiscussables.' Based on principles of courage, responsibility, awareness, and respect.

## Key facts

- Admiral James Stockdale demonstrated the 'Stockdale Paradox': never confuse faith that you will prevail with the discipline to confront the most brutal facts of current reality. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p230]
- Cheryl Bachelder restored trust at Popeyes by confronting reality with facts—compiling and analyzing profit and loss statements from every restaurant. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p231]
- Mercer study shows only 39% of employees believe senior management does a good job confronting issues before they turn into major problems. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p234]
