# Climate Change Denial

## Summary

Climate change denial is presented as an example of a viral false belief that poses an existential threat. It cannot be directly experienced by individuals, requires ignoring scientific evidence, and spreads through claims that climate policies threaten personal freedoms or represent government overreach.

## Key facts

- There is overwhelming evidence that human activity is leading to large-scale changes in Earth's climate. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p170]
- Climate change cannot be directly observed: local weather has always been variable and extreme weather events have always occurred. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p183]
- Climate-change deniers claim that policies to mitigate climate change threaten personal freedoms, perhaps forming a global government or benefiting a political party. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p184]
- Evidence suggests climate-change deniers become believers if they personally experience extreme weather events or flooding due to rising seas. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p170]
- Hawkins argues that Mars being once more Earth-like and now a desert illustrates that we could make Earth uninhabitable. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p184]

## Inferences

- Inference: Hawkins presents climate change denial as one of the most dangerous viral false beliefs because it prevents action on what could make Earth uninhabitable.
