# Body Scan

## Summary

A body scan is a mindfulness practice involving slowly scanning the body (or part of it), tuning in to sensations, noticing them with curiosity, and allowing them to be there. It is particularly valuable for people who feel disconnected from their bodies or experience emotions mainly in their heads. Benefits include increased vitality and sense of 'coming back to life,' access to the full range of emotions (both painful and pleasant), greater control over actions, improved decision-making, access to intuition and gut feelings, feeling safer in one's body, increased emotional intelligence, and better relationships. Body scans can vary enormously in duration from thirty seconds to thirty minutes. The practice typically involves starting with shorter exercises of three to four minutes and building up over time, moving attention slowly from head to toe or toe to head, observing sensations without struggling with them.
