# Fingerspitzengefühl (Intuitive Knowledge)

## Summary

Fingerspitzengefühl (literally 'fingertip feel') is intuitive knowledge that allows faster OODA loop cycles by individuals. Research by Gary Klein demonstrated that firefighters and others who must act quickly in rapidly changing situations do best when intuition gives them an acceptable course of action immediately. Junior military officers used recognition-primed decisions about 40% of the time; senior battlefield commanders could rely on intuition in 95% of decisions. This level of skill is what makes the Toyota Production System work—people can sense the state of the system and make improvements intuitively without waiting for formal decision processes.

## Key facts

- Boyd insisted that we should 'emphasize implicit over explicit in order to gain a favorable mismatch in friction and time for superiority in shaping and adapting to circumstances.'. - Gary Klein's research showed senior battlefield commanders relied on recognition-primed intuition in upwards of 95% of decisions, compared to 40% for junior officers. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p89]
- Someone who understands the Toyota system can walk into a factory, close their eyes, and tell by listening if everything is working properly. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p90]
- Bruce Gudmundsson concluded that the key to decentralized execution lies in a 'self-educating officer corps' where practically all officers believed the organization's fate depended directly upon their tactical competence. ## Inferences

- Inference: Developing Fingerspitzengefühl requires practice under varied, challenging conditions with feedback from knowledgeable individuals, not just repetitive rote activities.
