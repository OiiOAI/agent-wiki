# Non-Neediness

## Summary

Non-neediness is the central concept of the book, defined as when a man places a higher priority on his own perception of himself than the perceptions of others. It is the foundation of attractiveness to women, with the less needy a man is, the more attractive he becomes. A non-needy man's actions and words are primarily motivated by embodying his own values and desires rather than seeking approval from others.

## Key facts

- A man's attractiveness is inversely proportional to how needy he is — the less needy he is, the more attractive he will be to women on average. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p23]
- Neediness is when a man places a higher priority on others' perceptions of him than his perception of himself. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p23]
- Non-neediness is when a man places a higher priority on his own perception of himself than the perceptions of others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p23]
- By investment, Manson means the degree to which you sacrifice or alter your own thoughts, feelings, and motivations for someone else. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p24]
- Women unconsciously detect neediness by sensing the intentions behind a man's behavior and words. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p25]
- Highly needy men will end up in relationships only with highly needy women, resulting in toxic dynamics where one creates drama and the other endlessly fixes it. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p26]
- Non-neediness doesn't mean you should only care about yourself — this is narcissism — but rather how you prioritize other people's perceptions versus your own. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p25]
- Women detect a man's level of investment relative to them within moments of speaking to him, and often even before speaking to him. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p27]
- Non-neediness means feeling the fear and not letting it define you; it's feeling the fear and deciding that something else is more important. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p178]
- The author states that the difference between his approach in 2005 versus later was that back then he was incredibly needy and highly invested in how women perceived him, which amplified anxiety. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p178]
- A man who is extremely needy will have intentions dominated by seeking validation and approval and will therefore be unattractive regardless of what he says. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p189]
- Non-neediness is defined as being more invested in your opinion of yourself than the opinions others have of you. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- Non-neediness is one component of having high self-esteem and the root of all attractive behavior. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]
- True Confidence is achieved through practicing vulnerability and in investing in oneself. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p268]

## Inferences

- Inference: From evolutionary psychology, non-neediness signals high status because throughout human history, high-status men didn't need to defer to others or beg for approval.
- Inference: Neediness is intuited rather than consciously calculated by women — it's instinctual, like a 'gag reflex' when a man displays it.
- Inference: Non-neediness is presented as trainable through self-investment and confronting fears, rather than being an innate trait.
