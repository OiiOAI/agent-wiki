# Sickness Behavior

## Summary

Sickness behavior refers to the behavioral changes (lethargy, depression, anxiety, reduced grooming, social withdrawal) that occur when animals (including humans) are inflamed. This is a motivational state—an adaptive strategy to reserve the body's energy for healing and isolate the individual from the healthy group. Humans become irritable, lose interest in food and socializing, and have trouble focusing and remembering recent events when sick.

## Key facts

- When animals are inflamed, they exhibit strange behavioral changes displaying symptoms of lethargy, depression, anxiety, and reduced grooming, and they retract from the herd. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p248]
- Sickness behavior is a motivational state—an adaptive strategy on the part of our biology to aid in survival. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
- Humans exhibit sickness behaviors including irritability, loss of interest in food and socializing, and trouble focusing and remembering recent events. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p249]
