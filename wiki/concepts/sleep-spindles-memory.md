# Sleep spindles and memory consolidation

## Summary

Sleep spindles are bursts of oscillatory brain activity during NREM sleep that have been linked to memory consolidation processes. Research indicates that sleep spindle activity is associated with the integration of new memories with existing knowledge.

## Key facts

- Sleep spindle activity is associated with the integration of new memories and existing knowledge, as demonstrated by Tamminen and colleagues. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p3]
- The degree of lexical integration over a night of sleep is associated with sleep spindles. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p3]
