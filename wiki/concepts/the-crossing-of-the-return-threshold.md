# The Crossing of the Return Threshold

## Summary

The final crisis of the hero's journey—crossing back from the mystic realm into the land of common day. The hero must re-enter with his boon the atmosphere where men who are fractions imagine themselves to be complete, confronting society with his ego-shattering, life-redeeming elixir.

## Key facts

- The two worlds, divine and human, can be pictured only as distinct yet are actually one. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p169]
- The realm of the gods is a forgotten dimension of the world we know. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p169]
- The hero's ultimate difficult task is to render back into light-world language the speech-defying pronouncements of the dark. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p170]
- The first problem of the returning hero is to accept as real, after an experience of soul-satisfying vision, the passing joys and sorrows of life. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p171]
