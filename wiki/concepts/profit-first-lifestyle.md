# Profit First Lifestyle

## Summary

The Profit First Lifestyle extends the business cash management system to personal finances, treating the individual as a 'conjoined twin' with their business. It involves setting up personal accounts for income, vault (emergency savings), recurring payments, day-to-day expenses, and debt destruction, while applying the same allocation principles used in business.

## Key facts

- Michalowicz states: 'If you own a business, your personal financial health is in lockstep with the financial health of your business. A better analogy is that your business is your conjoined twin.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p184]
- The five foundational personal accounts are: INCOME, VAULT (emergency savings), RECURRING PAYMENTS, DAY-TO-DAY, and DEBT DESTROYER. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p185]
- The VAULT account starts with one month's rent or mortgage payment and grows toward Suze Orman's recommended eight months of living expenses. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p185]
- For personal Profit First, the author recommends cutting up credit cards while keeping one emergency credit card with a reduced limit, given to a trusted friend to hold. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p186]
- Every quarter, as debt is paid down, reduce the credit limit by 50% of the amount paid down until the balance is zero and the limit is $5,000 for emergencies only. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p187]
- The 'death-to-debt party' involves using 99% of quarterly profit disbursements to pay down debt (smallest first) and 1% as a celebration reward. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p190]
- After core debt eradication, 45% of profit disbursements go toward long-term debt payoff and 55% for splurges. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p191]
- Parkinson's Law states that if income increases, expenses will expand to match, so lifestyle must be locked in for five years to build the wealth gap. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p192]
- The Wedge system allows gradual lifestyle upgrades by saving 50% of any income increase while living on the other 50%. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p193]
