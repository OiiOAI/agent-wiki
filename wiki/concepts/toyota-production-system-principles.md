# Toyota Production System Principles

## Summary

The Toyota Production System embodies three interconnected Japanese terms that form a continuously improving system: muri (unreasonableness, avoided by standardized work, flow, and time), mura (inconsistencies, avoided by just-in-time systems and pull), and muda (waste, eliminated by continuous improvement or kaizen). Allen Ward's study showed that these techniques did not transfer directly to non-manufacturing functions, demonstrating that Toyota's principles cannot simply be cookie-cutters into other domains.
