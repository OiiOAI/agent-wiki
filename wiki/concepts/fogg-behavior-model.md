# Fogg Behavior Model

## Summary

The Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP) is a framework that explains all human behavior through the convergence of three elements: Motivation, Ability, and Prompt. When all three elements converge at the same moment, behavior occurs. The model includes an Action Line that visually represents the relationship between motivation and ability—behaviors above the line happen, while those below do not. A key insight is that motivation and ability have a compensatory relationship: higher motivation can compensate for lower ability, and vice versa.

## Key facts

- For a behavior to occur, Motivation, Ability, and Prompt must converge at the same moment. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p31]
- The curved Action Line in the model shows that when a behavior is above the line, it happens; below the line, it does not. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p32]
- Motivation and ability have a compensatory relationship—when one is weak, the other must be strong to get above the Action Line. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p36]
- Prompts are described as essential—no prompt means no behavior occurs, regardless of motivation or ability levels. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p37]
- The Fogg Behavior Model states: Behavior happens when three things come together at the same moment—Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p315]
- The model is visualized in two dimensions: Motivation on a vertical axis (high to low) and Ability on a horizontal axis (easy to hard). [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p315]
- The Action Line is a curved line showing the relationship between motivation and ability—if someone is above the Action Line when prompted, they will do the behavior. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p316]
- If someone is below the Action Line when prompted, they won't do the behavior; to change this, either increase motivation, make the behavior easier, or both. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p316]
- The book provides a two-minute script for teaching the Fogg Behavior Model to others. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p315]

## Inferences

- Inference: The model applies universally to all human behavior, including both positive habits (like desk tidying) and negative habits (like scrolling in bed), because they share the same underlying components
