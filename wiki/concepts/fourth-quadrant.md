# The Fourth Quadrant

## Summary

The Fourth Quadrant is the region where tail events are not computable in fat-tailed domains, but where exposure to these events can still be assessed. Taleb's technical paper demonstrates that economic methods relying on standard deviation, regression, and correlation fail in fat-tailed environments because 80% of kurtosis in such data can come from a single observation.

## Key facts

- The Fourth Quadrant paper demonstrates that fat tails are both severe and intractable, and all methods with 'squares'—regression, standard deviation, correlation—don't work with socioeconomic variables. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p579]
- Tail events are not computable in fat-tailed domains, but we can assess our exposure to the problem. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p551]
- Technically, 80% of the Kurtosis in 10,000 pieces of data can come from one single observation, meaning all measures of fat tails are just sampling errors. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p579]
