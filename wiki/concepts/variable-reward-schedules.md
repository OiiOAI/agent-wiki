# Variable Reward Schedules

## Summary

A psychological mechanism derived from B.F. Skinner's research on operant conditioning, where unpredictable rewards (hearts, likes, retweets) create stronger behavioral patterns than predictable rewards. Social media platforms use this by making notification timing and approval uncertain, creating craving loops similar to slot machine mechanics.

## Key facts

- B.J. Fogg taught students at Stanford to use reinforcement principles from B.F. Skinner to shape user behavior in their apps. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p119]
- Tristan Harris explained that once conditioned to expect variable rewards, 'it's very hard to be with reality, the physical world, the built world — because it doesn't offer as frequent and as immediate rewards.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p144]

## Inferences

- Inference: The comparison to slot machines is apt: both systems exploit the psychological vulnerability to unpredictability to create compulsive use patterns.
