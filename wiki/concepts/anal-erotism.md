# Anal Erotism

## Summary

Anal erotism encompasses the drive impulses and psychical significance associated with the anal zone. Freud details how faeces acquire multiple unconscious meanings across development: as a 'gift' (first sacrifice for a loved one), as a 'baby' (both emerge through the anus), and as 'money' (a libidinal interest displacing the original excretory pleasure). The concepts of 'faeces', 'baby', and 'penis' form an unconscious unity as 'a little one' that can become separated from the body. In this patient, anal erotism expressed his feminine (homosexual) attitude toward his father.
