# Mind Mapping

## Summary

Mind mapping is a brainstorming technique popularized by Tony Buzan for capturing and organizing ideas in a graphic format. The core concept involves placing a central idea in the center with associated ideas radiating outward in a free-form fashion.

## Key facts

- Mind mapping was coined by Tony Buzan, a British researcher in brain functioning, to describe brainstorming ideas into a graphic format. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p113]
- In mind mapping, the core idea is presented in the center, with associated ideas growing out in a somewhat free-form fashion around it. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p113]
- Mind mapping can be done on Post-its stuck on a whiteboard, in word processing or outlining programs, or through dedicated mind-mapping software applications. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p113]
- Mind mapping is one of several graphics-oriented brainstorming techniques that also include clustering, patterning, webbing, and fish boning. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p113]
