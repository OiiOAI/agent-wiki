# Level 3 SA: Projection

## Summary

Level 3 Situation Awareness is the highest level, involving the ability to forecast future situation events and dynamics. This ability to project from current events and dynamics to anticipate future events and their implications allows for timely decision making. In almost every field studied (aircraft, ATC, power plant operations, maintenance, medicine), experienced operators rely heavily on future projections—it is the mark of a skilled expert. The dynamic nature of real-world situations means that as the situation is always changing, the person's SA must constantly change or be rendered outdated and inaccurate. Level 3 SA requires understanding of system dynamics and is essential for proactive rather than merely reactive performance.
