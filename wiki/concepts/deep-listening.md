# Deep Listening

## Summary

Deep listening (also called active listening) is a concentration practice involving full attention on what another person is saying without preparing responses or filling silences. The technique includes waiting one full second before responding, dedicating the entire time another person speaks to actually hearing and considering their words, and using concentration skills on the sounds of speech. The book argues this practice is rare in modern conversation where people engage in 'reactive' dialogue—waiting for pauses to deploy pre-prepared responses. Deep listening is presented as both a meditation practice (training concentration) and a relationship skill (creating profound connection and reducing existential isolation).
