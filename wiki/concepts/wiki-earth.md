# Wiki Earth

## Summary

Wiki Earth is a proposed concept for preserving human knowledge by archiving it in satellites orbiting the Sun. The archive would be designed to last tens of millions of years, allowing future intelligent species to discover and read humanity's accumulated knowledge after human extinction.

## Key facts

- Wiki Earth proposes archiving human knowledge in satellites orbiting the Sun to preserve it for future intelligent species. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p205]
- Wikipedia is suggested as a knowledge base because it is constantly updated, documents events up to societal failure, and covers a broad range of topics. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p206]
- The archive should not be located on Earth because Earth might be partially destroyed in a singular event. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p206]
- The satellite archive would allow sending automatic updates but its content could not be erased. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p206]
- Hawkins argues that if a previous intelligent species had placed such satellites, we would have discovered them by now. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p206]

## Inferences

- Inference: The proposal assumes that intelligent life capable of discovering the archive will eventually exist, whether on Earth or arriving from elsewhere.
