# Somatic Compliance

## Summary

The contribution of somatic factors that enables conversion in hysteria. Certain bodily tissues or organs provide a pathway for unconscious material to find physical expression.

## Key facts

- Somatic compliance refers to the somatic contribution that enables unconscious material to find expression in hysterical symptoms. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2329]
- Every hysterical symptom requires both psychical significance and somatic compliance. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2329]
- In Dora's case, her oral erotogenic zone (from childhood thumb-sucking) provided somatic compliance for expressing unconscious sexual material through throat symptoms.
