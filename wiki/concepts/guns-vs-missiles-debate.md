# Guns vs Missiles Debate

## Summary

The guns versus missiles debate was one of the most contentious arguments in the Air Force regarding aircraft armament. While missiles were considered the future of air combat, Vietnam War rules of engagement required visual identification before firing at distances greater than identification was possible. The Israeli Air Force's success with gun kills in the Six Day War demonstrated the limitations of missiles and validated those who advocated for fighter aircraft armed with guns.

## Key facts

- Air Force policy dictated pilots visually identify enemy aircraft before firing missiles, but minimum missile-launch range exceeded visual identification range. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
- Signs appeared in the Pentagon saying 'It takes a fighter with a gun to kill a MiG-21'. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
- Israeli Air Force achieved a 6:1 kill ratio in the Six Day War with every kill being a gun kill. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
- When asked how the IAF got sixty gun kills, Mordecai Hod reportedly said 'Why waste a missile on an Arab?'. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
