# Situation model

## Summary

The situation model construct, introduced by Van Dijk and Kintch (1983), is necessary to account for comprehending events whether experienced directly, read about, or heard about. Situation models have four key properties: (1) Reference - closing the gap between conceptual meaning and reference to elements in a possible world; (2) Coherence - representing relations between referenced things differently than in input data; (3) Situation Parameters - representing specific parameters like time and location; (4) Perspective - providing a stable reference for multiple perspectives. In aviation, situation models form the central organizing feature from which all decision making and actions result.

## Key facts

- Situation models are necessary to account for the phenomenon of comprehending events whether one experiences them directly, reads about them, or hears about them. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p320]
- A situation model is needed to close the gap between input data's conceptual meaning and its reference to elements such as properties, relations, and facts in a possible world. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p320]
- The situation model represents relations among referenced things that are different than the relations in the input data, richly elaborated by internal knowledge from episodic and semantic memory. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p320]
- Situation models represent specific parameters such as time and location that are difficult or impossible to account for in text representation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p321]
- A situation model provides a stable reference for taking into account multiple perspectives, enabling teammates to collaborate when they view events from different points of view. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p321]
- The situation model can be thought of as a parameterized mental model representing one's SA and understanding of a specific situation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p322]
