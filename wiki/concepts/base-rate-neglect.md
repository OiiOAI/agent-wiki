# Base-Rate Neglect

## Summary

Base-Rate Neglect is the disregard of fundamental statistical distribution levels when evaluating probabilities. When provided with specific descriptive information about an individual, people ignore the prior probability (base rate) of that characteristic in the population.

## Key facts

- Base-rate neglect is the disregard of fundamental distribution levels and is one of the most common errors in reasoning; virtually all journalists, economists and politicians fall for it regularly. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p72]
- Germany has 10,000 times more truck drivers than Frankfurt has literature professors, making it more likely that Mark is a truck driver than a professor, despite matching a stereotypic professor description. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p72]
- Warren Buffett explains his avoidance of biotech companies by noting that the most likely scenario is these firms will hover somewhere in the middle of success rates. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p73]
