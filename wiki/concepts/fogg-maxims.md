# Fogg Maxims

## Summary

The Fogg Maxims are two guiding principles that form the foundation of Behavior Design. They are: (1) Help people do what they already want to do, and (2) Help people feel successful. Fogg spent more than ten years developing these principles and states that the evidence is clear—if you don't do these two things, your product or service will not engage people over time. These maxims apply both to designing products and services and to designing change in one's own life.
