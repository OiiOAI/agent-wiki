# Dramaturgy

## Summary

Dramaturgy is Goffman's view of social life as a series of dramatic performances akin to those performed on a stage. The self is not an organic thing with a specific location but rather the product of dramatic interaction between actor and audience. Goffman's dramaturgical approach examines how performers present idealized self-images, manage impressions, and maintain stable performances despite the tension between spontaneous selves and socialized selves.

## Key facts

- Goffman perceived the self not as a possession of the actor but as the product of the dramatic interaction between actor and audience. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p267]
- The self is a dramatic effect arising from a scene that is presented. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p267]
- Goffman spoke of a front stage—the part of the performance that generally functions to define the situation for observers. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p267]
- The front includes setting (physical scene necessary for performance) and personal front (expressive equipment identified with performers). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p267]
- The personal front is subdivided into appearance (indicating social status) and manner (indicating expected role). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p267]
- Fronts tend to become institutionalized, leading to collective representations about what is to go on in a certain front. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p268]
- Fronts tend to be selected, not created, conveying a more structural image than most symbolic interactionists would accept. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p268]
- Actors try to present an idealized picture of themselves and hide things such as secret pleasures, errors, process, dirty work, and insults. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p268]
- A team is any set of individuals who cooperate in staging a single routine, and Goffman concluded that a team is a kind of secret society. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p269]
- There is a back stage where facts suppressed in the front or various kinds of informal actions may appear, usually adjacent to but cut off from the front stage. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p269]
