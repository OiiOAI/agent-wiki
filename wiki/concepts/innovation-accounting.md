# Innovation Accounting

## Summary

Innovation accounting is a disciplined, systematic approach for evaluating whether a startup is making progress and achieving validated learning. It replaces traditional accounting methods that are designed for predictable businesses and enables startups to prove objectively that they are learning how to grow a sustainable business.

## Key facts

- Innovation accounting turns leap-of-faith assumptions into a quantitative financial model, providing assumptions about what the business will look like at a successful point in the future. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p108]
- Standard accounting is not helpful in evaluating entrepreneurs because startups are too unpredictable for forecasts and milestones to be accurate. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p108]
- Innovation accounting works in three steps: (1) use an MVP to establish real baseline data, (2) tune the engine from baseline toward ideal, and (3) decide to pivot or persevere. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p110]
- If a company is making good progress toward the ideal, it's learning appropriately; if not, the management team must conclude its current product strategy is flawed. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p110]
- The sign of a successful pivot is that engine-tuning activities are more productive after the pivot than before. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p110]

## Inferences

- Inference: ovation accounting enables startups to objectively demonstrate progress even when traditional metrics show growth, by focusing on whether the drivers of the growth model are improving.
