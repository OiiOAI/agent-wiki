# Self-Selection

## Summary

The unconscious process of the assortment effect. Self-Selection is the idea that no matter what you do or who you are, you are going to be attractive to one particular demographic and unattractive to others.

## Key facts

- Self-Selection is defined as the unconscious process of the assortment effect. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
- Self-Selection is the idea that no matter what you do or who you are, you are going to be attractive to one particular demographic and unattractive to others. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
- Self-Selection reflects superficial preferences, whereas the assortment effect reflects beliefs and attitudes. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p270]
