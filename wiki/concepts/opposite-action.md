# Opposite Action

## Summary

Opposite Action is a DBT emotion regulation skill that involves acting opposite to an emotion's action urge. When emotions do not fit the facts of a situation or when acting on emotions is not effective, performing the opposite action can help change the emotional response.

## Key facts

- Opposite Action is used when emotions do not fit the facts or when acting on emotions is not effective. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p255]
- Every emotion has an action urge, and changing the emotion can be achieved by acting opposite to its action urge. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p255]
- For fear, opposite actions include approaching what you are afraid of, keeping eyes and ears open and focused, and changing posture to a confident stance. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p256]
- For anger, opposite actions include gently avoiding the person, taking a time out with deep breathing, and being kind rather than mean. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p257]
- For sadness, opposite actions include getting active, avoiding avoiding, building mastery, and increasing pleasant events. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p262]
- Opposite action is used when an emotion (or its intensity or duration) does not fit the facts—meaning the emotion is not justified by the situation. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p312]
- The process involves: identifying the emotion, checking if it fits the facts, listing action urges, determining opposite actions, doing opposite action all the way. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p312]
- Opposite action must be practiced all the way, including opposite body language, facial expression, posture, gestures, and thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p312]

## Inferences

- Inference: Opposite Action is based on the principle that behaviors can influence emotions—by changing our actions, we can change how we feel.
