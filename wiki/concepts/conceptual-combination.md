# Conceptual Combination

## Summary

Conceptual combination is the brain's ability to combine existing concepts to create novel ones. This power allows people to experience emotions even without a specific word for them, by assembling instances from related concepts. For example, one can experience chiplessness (disappointment, relief, guilt, and hunger about an empty chip bag) without ever having heard the word.

## Key facts

- Conceptual combination allows the brain to construct a potentially limitless number of novel concepts from existing ones. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p124]
- The Dutch concept gezellig (comfort, coziness, togetherness) can be experienced through combination of Close Friend, Love, Delight, Comfort, and Well-Being concepts. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p124]
- One can experience emotions without a word for them by combining existing emotion concepts. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p161]
- Conceptual combination plus words equals the power to create social reality. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p125]

## Inferences
