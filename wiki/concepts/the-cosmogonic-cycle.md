# The Cosmogonic Cycle

## Summary

The mythological image of the world's coming to manifestation and subsequent return into the nonmanifest condition. The cycle represents universal consciousness passing from the deep sleep zone of the unmanifest, through dream, to the full day of waking, then back through dream to the timeless dark. The cycle is surrounded and permeated by silence—the unknown.

## Key facts

- The cosmogonic cycle is normally represented as repeating itself, world without end. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p204]
- During each great round, lesser dissolutions are commonly included, as the cycle of sleep and waking revolves throughout a lifetime. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p204]
- The philosophical formula is that of the circulation of consciousness through three planes: waking experience, dream experience, and deep sleep. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p207]
- The Hindu sacred syllable AUM represents this mystery: A is waking consciousness, U dream, M deep sleep, the surrounding silence is the unknown. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p208]

## Inferences

- Inference: The cosmogonic cycle provides the metaphysical framework underlying all hero journeys: descent into darkness and return is modeled on the eternal rhythm of manifestation and dissolution
