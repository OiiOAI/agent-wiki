# Endocannabinoid System

## Summary

The endocannabinoid system consists of neurotransmitters anandamide and 2-arachidonoylglycerol (2-AG) that bind to cannabinoid receptors in the brain—the same receptors activated by THC in marijuana. These neurotransmitters are produced naturally by the body and brain during exercise. They travel through the bloodstream to activate receptors in the spinal cord (blocking pain) and throughout the reward system and prefrontal cortex (affecting dopamine). Anandamide is being used to treat pain syndromes like chronic fatigue and fibromyalgia.
