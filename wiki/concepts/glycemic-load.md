# Glycemic Load

## Summary

Glycemic load takes into account how much sugar a typical serving size of a given food will release into the blood, providing a more accurate measure of a food's blood sugar impact than glycemic index alone. Combined with insulin AUC (area under the curve), these metrics help assess the hormonal and metabolic impacts of carbohydrate ingestion.

## Key facts

- Glycemic load takes into account how much sugar a typical serving size of a given food will release into the blood. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p139]
- Insulin AUC (area under the curve) is the total amount of insulin a food or meal will stimulate. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p139]
- Whole-wheat bread has both a higher glycemic index and glycemic load than table sugar. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p137]
