# Concepts (Psychology)

## Summary

Concepts are mental representations of categories that allow the brain to efficiently organize knowledge. Unlike classical view assumptions of fixed definitions, modern understanding holds that concepts are constructed on-the-fly from populations of instances, organized by goals rather than perceptual similarity. The brain separates similarities from differences to maximize efficiency, compressing sensory input like video compression.

## Key facts

- A concept is a mental representation of a category, where a category is a collection of objects, events, or actions grouped as equivalent. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p105]
- Classical view of concepts (dictionary definitions with necessary and sufficient features) collapsed in the 1970s. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p106]
- Modern view holds that concepts are represented as populations of varied instances rather than prototypes. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p107]
- Goal-based concepts group together diverse instances sharing only a common purpose rather than perceptual similarity. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p109]
- The brain separates similarities from differences to represent information efficiently, like video compression. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p134]
- Concepts are constructed on-the-fly from instances based on the goal in a particular situation. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p110]

## Inferences

- Inference: Because concepts are goal-based and constructed dynamically, the same object or situation can be categorized differently depending on the context and purpose.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact neural mechanisms of how concepts are stored and retrieved remain areas of active research.
