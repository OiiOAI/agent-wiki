# Demonstrate Respect

## Summary

Behavior #2 of the Thirteen Behaviors, Demonstrate Respect has two critical dimensions: showing fundamental respect for people and demonstrating caring and concern. It is based on the principles of respect, fairness, kindness, love, and civility, with the overarching principle being the intrinsic worth of individuals. This is the Golden Rule in action, recognized by almost every culture and religion worldwide. The counterfeit is showing respect only for those who can help you but not for those who cannot.
