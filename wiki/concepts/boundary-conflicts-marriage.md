# Boundary Conflicts in Marriage

## Summary

The authors address how boundaries foster rather than destroy marriage intimacy. They discuss individual responsibilities for feelings, desires, and limits, and apply the Laws of Boundaries (Sowing and Reaping, Responsibility, Power, Evaluation, Exposure) to marital conflicts.

## Key facts

- Marriage mirrors Christ's relationship with the church: Christ has things only he can do, the church has things only it can do, and they have things they do together—similarly, spouses retain individual identities while becoming one. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p157-158]
- The authors state: 'Feelings are also a warning signal telling us that we need to do something... If you are angry, even if someone else has sinned against you, it is your responsibility to do something about it.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p160]
- The Law of Sowing and Reaping applied to marriage: spouses need to let out-of-control partners suffer natural consequences of their behavior. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p163-165]
- The authors provide contrast examples: BEFORE boundaries ('Stop yelling at me') vs AFTER boundaries ('You can continue to yell if you choose to. But I will choose not to be in your presence when you act that way'). [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p166]
- The authors emphasize: 'A boundary always deals with yourself, not the other person... You are setting boundaries to say what you will do or will not do.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p167]
- The authors address submission: both husbands and wives are supposed to practice submission (Ephesians 5:21), and true submission requires free choice and grace, not slavery to the law. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p169-170]
- The authors note: 'We have never seen a submission problem that did not have a controlling husband at its root.'. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p170]

## Inferences

- Inference: The authors position healthy boundaries as enabling rather than preventing true biblical submission.
