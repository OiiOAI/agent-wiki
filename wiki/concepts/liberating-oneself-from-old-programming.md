# Liberating Oneself from Old Programming

## Summary

NVC helps people liberate themselves from cultural conditioning that limits their humanity. This includes inherited language that served domination societies, training that blocks consciousness of needs, and judgmental internal dialogue. The transformation requires literacy of needs and the ability to get in touch with oneself.

## Key facts

- People have learned things from well-intentioned parents, teachers, and clergy that limit them as human beings, with destructive cultural learning so ingrained that consciousness of it is lost. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p262]
- The transformation from destructive learning requires literacy of needs and the ability to get in touch with oneself, which are difficult in modern culture. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p262]
- Cultural training actively blocks consciousness of needs, and the word 'needy' applied to a person suggests inadequacy or immaturity. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p263]
- When people express their needs, they are often labeled selfish, and the use of the personal pronoun 'I' is at times equated with selfishness or neediness. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p263]
- NVC heightens awareness of cultural conditioning by encouraging separation of observation and evaluation, acknowledging thoughts or needs shaping feelings, and expressing requests in clear action language. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p263]
