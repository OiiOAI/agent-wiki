# Organizational Well-being and Cynefin

## Summary

The Cynefin Framework provides a lens for re-imagining workplace well-being. Well-being is an emergent property of messy interactions between people, relationships, and organizing practices. The chapter proposes shifting well-being approaches from the Complicated domain (surveys, expert-driven interventions) to the Complex domain (story-based sense-making, co-created experiments).

## Key facts

- Well-being is defined as an emergent property of the messy and moveable interactions between people, their relationships, interactions and the organizing practices, procedures and structures in which people find themselves. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p282]
- Well-being is not a problem to be solved but an opportunity to discover and create an environment where people can flourish. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p282]
- Well-being practices are typically on the right side of the Cynefin Framework (Clear and Complicated domains), but the authors propose shifting to the left side (Complex domain) for a new approach. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p279]
- The SenseMaker Wellbeing Pulse applies narrative-based methods to focus specifically on well-being in organizations. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p289]
- Many stressed employees reside in the Complex, Confused, or Chaotic domains, while well-being fixes tend to come from the Clear and Complicated domains, making them merely temporary. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p283]
