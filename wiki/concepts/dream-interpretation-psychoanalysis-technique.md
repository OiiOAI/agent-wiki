# Dream Interpretation in Psychoanalysis

## Summary

Freud's guidelines for how analysts should handle dream interpretation within psychoanalytic treatment. Rather than pursuing complete interpretation of every dream, analysts should integrate dream work with the overall flow of the treatment, attending to the most recent productions and recognizing that dreams cannot be fully mastered if they are based on the entire pathogenic material of a case.

## Key facts

- Analysts should not sacrifice the therapeutic aim of maintaining awareness of the patient's current mental surface to interest in dream interpretation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3802]
- If fresh dreams occur before earlier ones are disposed of, the more recent productions should be attended to first. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3802]
- Analysts should guard against displaying very special interest in dreams, as this may direct the patient's resistance toward producing dreams. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3802]
- Complex dreams in severe neurosis are often based on the entire pathogenic material and cannot be completely solved until the analysis is nearly complete. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3803]
- Writing down dreams immediately upon waking is superfluous for therapeutic work because associations will not come to the preserved text. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3805]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud distinguished between the analyst's knowledge from dream interpretation and the patient's knowledge, emphasizing that therapeutic progress requires the patient to consciously understand the dream material, not merely the analyst.
