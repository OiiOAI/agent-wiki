# Happiness

## Summary

Happiness is a skill that can be learned, like fitness or nutrition. It is not about positive thoughts or negative thoughts but about the absence of desire, especially the absence of desire for external things. Happiness is being satisfied with what you have. The fewer desires one can have, the more one can accept the current state of things. Peace is happiness at rest, and happiness is peace in motion.

- Happiness is learned and is a skill, like fitness or nutrition. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p106]
- Happiness is a choice you make and a skill you develop. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p110]
- Happiness is the state when nothing is missing; when nothing is missing, your mind shuts down and stops running into the past or future. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p107]
