# Standard Deviation

## Summary

A statistical measure of dispersion indicating the average difference between values in a dataset and their mean. A large standard deviation indicates values are far from the mean.

## Key facts

- The standard deviation of a set of numbers is the mean difference between those numbers and their mean. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1555]
- A large standard deviation indicates that on average the elements of a population are a long way from the mean. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1560]
