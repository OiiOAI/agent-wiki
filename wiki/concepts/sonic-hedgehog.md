# Sonic Hedgehog

## Summary

Sonic hedgehog (Shh) is a secreted morphogen critical for patterning the ventral neural tube. Synthesized as a precursor, it undergoes autoproteolytic cleavage and cholesterol modification. Shh from the notochord and floor plate creates a ventral-to-dorsal concentration gradient that specifies distinct neuronal subtypes at different threshold concentrations. It acts through the Patched-Smoothened receptor complex, activating Gli transcription factors. Gli repressors in the absence of Shh become activators when Shh signaling is active, converting the extracellular gradient into a nuclear transcription factor gradient.

## Key facts

- Shh is synthesized as a precursor protein, autocatalytically cleaved to generate an active amino-terminal fragment modified by covalent cholesterol addition. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1225]
- Shh acts as a morphogen with two- to three-fold concentration differences specifying different ventral neuronal identities. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1225]
- Shh signaling is transduced through Patched (inhibits Smoothened in absence of Shh) and Smoothened (activates Gli transcription factors). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1225]
- Mutations in human Shh pathway genes cause holoprosencephaly, spina bifida, and limb deformities. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1227]
