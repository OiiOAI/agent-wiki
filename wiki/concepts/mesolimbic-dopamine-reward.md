# Mesolimbic Dopamine and Reward

## Summary

Mesolimbic dopamine pathways, projecting from the ventral tegmental area to the ventral striatum and frontal cortex, play a critical role in reward and motivation. Both schizophrenic delusions and the urge to self-stimulate the brain are linked to excessive dopamine activity in these pathways.

## Key facts

- Both schizophrenic delusions and the urge to self-stimulate are linked to excessive dopamine activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p198]
- Mesolimbic dopamine bursting diminishes when a reward is found, potentially accompanied by glutamate transmission. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p199]
- Destruction of mesolimbic dopamine systems diminishes psychostimulant seeking and self-administration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p199]
