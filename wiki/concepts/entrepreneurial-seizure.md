# Entrepreneurial Seizure

## Summary

An Entrepreneurial Seizure is the moment when a technician working for someone else suddenly decides to start their own business. This seizure is characterized by thoughts like 'Why am I working for this guy? I know as much about this business as he does. Any dummy can run a business. I'm working for one.' Gerber argues this seizure is driven by dissatisfaction rather than true entrepreneurial intent.

## Key facts

- An Entrepreneurial Seizure happens when a technician suddenly decides to start their own business, often triggered by seemingly minor events like a paycheck or a sideways glance from a boss. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p17]
- During an Entrepreneurial Seizure, the person becomes obsessed with independence, being their own boss, and doing their own thing. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p18]
- The Entrepreneurial Seizure starts the business, but it is the Technician who goes to work in the business. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p19]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Entrepreneurial Seizure creates an entrepreneurial dream that turns into a technician's nightmare when the founder realizes they must do all the work they previously avoided
