# Attack Into the Ambush

## Summary

Attack Into the Ambush is a military response where soldiers who find themselves ambushed charge toward the enemy rather than retreating or taking cover. When you attack into the ambush, you regain initiative and put the enemy on the defensive, disrupting their planning. However, recruits taught to attack must discover the lesson as commonsense rather than memorizing it as a program.

## Key facts

- When you attack into the ambush, you achieve two wins: you regain your initiative by pushing events instead of being pushed, and you put your enemy on the defensive, disrupting his planning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p141]
- Recruits taught the lesson as a program succeed in training then fail catastrophically in combat when environments shift or ambushes are irregular. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p141]
- To really learn to attack into an ambush, recruits must discover the lesson for themselves, acquiring it not as a program but as commonsense. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p141]
