# Cognitive Ease

## Summary

Cognitive ease refers to the subjective feeling of mental effortlessness when processing information. When experiencing cognitive ease, people feel comfortable, believe things are true and familiar, and trust their intuitions. Cognitive ease is influenced by repetition, clarity, good mood, and priming. It creates a sense of truth and liking that can be misleading when produced by factors unrelated to actual truth.

## Key facts

- Cognitive ease is experienced as a pleasant feeling of no strain, signaling that things are going smoothly. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p54]
- Cognitive ease is produced by repetition, clear display, primed concepts, and good mood. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p81]
- When cognitively at ease, people are more intuitive, creative, prone to cognitive errors, and believe statements more readily. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p88]
- Cognitive ease creates an illusion of truth: repeated statements are judged true even if meaningless. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p83]
- Studies show words displayed in clear fonts are judged more truthful than identical words in poor-quality print. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p86]

## Inferences

- Inference: Cognitive ease is both a cause and consequence of positive affect, creating a feedback loop.
