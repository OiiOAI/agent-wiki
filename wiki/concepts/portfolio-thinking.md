# Portfolio Thinking

## Summary

Portfolio thinking is the framework for managing four major kinds of work simultaneously: (1) research and development of new innovations, (2) growth and scale-up of successful products, (3) optimization and operational excellence of established products, and (4) managing legacy products and infrastructure through cost reduction and outsourcing. The challenge is that employees often follow products through phases, causing creative managers to get stuck optimizing rather than creating new innovations. The solution is to manage the four kinds of work differently, allowing cross-functional teams to develop around each area.

## Key facts

- The four major kinds of work companies must manage are: (1) research and development, (2) growth and scale-up, (3) optimization and operational excellence, and (4) managing legacy products and infrastructure. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p245]
- Employees often follow the products they develop as they move from phase to phase, causing strong creative managers to get stuck working on growth and optimization rather than creating new products. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p246]
- Entrepreneurship should be considered a viable career path for innovators inside large organizations, with managers having business cards that say 'Entrepreneur' and being held accountable via innovation accounting. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p247]
- When products move from phase to phase, they are handed off between teams, allowing employees to choose whether to move with the product or stay behind to begin work on something new. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p247]

## Inferences

- Inference: This framework addresses the common reason established companies struggle to find creative managers to foster innovation: every new innovation competes for resources with established projects, and talent is one of the scarcest resources.
