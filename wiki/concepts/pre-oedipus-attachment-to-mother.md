# pre-Oedipus attachment to mother

## Summary

The pre-Oedipus phase refers to the early attachment of little girls to their mother before the development of the Oedipus complex. Freud acknowledged that this phase was poorly understood and that the most important addition made in his later paper on 'Female Sexuality' (1931b) was the discovery of the strength and duration of this attachment.

## Key facts

- The most important addition made in the later paper on 'Female Sexuality' (1931b) was the discovery, based on fresh case material, of the strength and duration of the little girl's pre-Oedipus attachment to her mother.
