# Induction (Psychology)

## Summary

Induction is a psychological phenomenon where perceiving something positive automatically generates a contrasting negative image in our minds, and vice versa. This mechanism underlies much of human desire—taboos create fascination with the forbidden, and what we lack becomes what we crave.

## Key facts

- In vision, seeing a color like red intensifies perception of the opposite color (green) around it, creating a halo effect. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p178]
- The brain cannot help seeing opposites—every 'no' sparks a corresponding 'yes', and forbidden desires become more compelling. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p178]
- The Victorian outlawing of pornography created the first major pornographic industry, illustrating how prohibition stimulates the opposite desire. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p178]
