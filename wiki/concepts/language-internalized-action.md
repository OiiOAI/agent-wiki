# Language and Internalized Action

## Summary

Language may be based on internalized action, with the mirror neuron system allowing us to interpret the actions and intentions of others through body language. Broca's speech area in humans and area F5 in non-human primates are homologous brain regions, suggesting that speech evolved atop the mirror system for actions. Verbs express intentions and encode what is happening in motion, serving as the architectural centerpiece of sentences. The process that gives meaning to words relates them to motor actions and real-world experiences through 'semantic grounding.'

## Key facts

- The mirror neuron system may underlie neurophysiological mechanisms of language, linking gestural communication of limbs, ears, and face muscles to verbal communication. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p155]
- Broca's area and area F5 in non-human primates are homologous brain regions, suggesting Broca's area evolved atop the mirror system for actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p156]
- Action, symbolized by the verb, is the architectural centerpiece of a sentence; verbs express intentions and encode what is happening in motion. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p156]
- Semantic grounding is the process that gives meaning to verbs and relates them to motor actions and real-world experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p156]
- Motor cortex and somatosensory cortex are involved in linking action words to their direct meaning, while inferior frontal cortex, temporal lobe, and inferior parietal cortex encode more abstract features. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p157]

## Inferences

- Inference: Language may have evolved using the same neural syntax that assists in generating speech and music.
- Inference: The invention of language accelerated externalization of brain function, creating a collective species memory.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The specific neural mechanisms underlying semantic grounding of nouns and words in general are not known.
