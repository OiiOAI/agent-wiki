# Fantasy in Relationships

## Summary

Fantasy in relationships encompasses the role of erotic imagination, the sharing or non-sharing of fantasies with partners, and how fantasies function psychologically. Perel explores whether disclosure is necessary, how fantasies differ from real desires, and the ways fantasies can be integrated into couples' erotic lives.

## Key facts

- Disclosure is not a necessary part of working with fantasy. Not everyone would choose to live in an atmosphere of True Confessions. Some people keep their imaginings to themselves not out of shame but out of awareness that exposure to bright light will cause them to wither on the vine. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p151]
- Nat explains his pornography use: 'It is kind of a fetish for me. I don't think people always understand their fetishes. Why do some people like shoes? I have no clue. It has been a long-standing thing for me, right back to when I was a teenager, regardless of my actual sex life.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p151]
- For Nat, the women in his pornographic fantasies are appealing precisely because they are not real. It is the very absence of psychological complexity that fuels his arousal—if these women were real, the fantasy would not work. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p152]
- Nat distinguishes between what excites him and who he actually loves: 'I do find these women sexy. But do I want to spend the rest of my life with that person? No. Do I want to jeopardize my relationship with you to go fuck that person? No. I think I can recognize the difference between something I see as a turn-on versus somebody that I actually love.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p152]
- Inviting someone into the recesses of our erotic mind is risky. When the fantasy is poorly received it can be devastating. But when it is received in a way that makes us feel recognized and accepted, it can be richly affirming. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p154]
- Entering the erotic mindscape of another requires an effort of understanding and a considerable degree of emotional separateness. If our partner is aroused by something foreign to us, the temptation is to judge first and ask questions later. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p154]
- Fantasy maps our psychological and cultural preoccupations; exploring them can lead to greater self-awareness. When we cordon off our erotic interiors, we are left with sex that is truncated, devoid of vibrancy, and not particularly intimate. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p154]

## Inferences

- Inference: The distinction between fantasy and desire for actual fulfillment suggests that having erotic fantasies about something does not necessarily mean one wants to act on them or that the partner should feel threatened by them.
