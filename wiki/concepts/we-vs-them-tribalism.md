# We vs. Them (Tribalism)

## Summary

Tribalism and Us/Them thinking refer to the cognitive tendency to divide people into in-groups and out-groups, often leading to preferential treatment of the in-group and hostility toward out-groups. This tendency has deep evolutionary roots and is expressed across human cultures in contexts ranging from sports rivalries to ethnic conflicts.
