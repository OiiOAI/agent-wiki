# Frame Analysis

## Summary

In Frame Analysis (1974), Goffman moved toward the study of small-scale structures of social life. Frames are schemata of interpretation that enable individuals to locate, perceive, identify, and label occurrences within their life space. Frames are principles of organization that define experiences and are assumptions about what we are seeing in the social world. Goffman came to recognize the limitations of the theater metaphor, noting that ritual in everyday life had been concealed by dramaturgical analysis.
