# The Commitment Screen

## Summary

The Commitment Screen is a structured list of questions to assess readiness to pursue a dream or goal. It covers two aspects: what is needed for the job (essential, significant, and desirable requirements) and what challenges might be faced (hurdles, barriers, and pitfalls). The screen includes questions about whether it is really a dream, what plans have been made, strategies for dealing with downsides, and what will keep you going when facing problems. The million-pound question tests whether you would complete the task if given a million pounds—if yes, then any reason for not doing it is just an excuse.

## Key facts

- The Commitment Screen is composed of two aspects: first, working out what you will need to do the job (essential, significant, desirable); second, preparing solutions to overcome anything that might stop you from succeeding (hurdles, barriers, pitfalls). [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p169]
- The million-pound question asks: if you were given a million pounds to do the task before the end of the day, could you do it? If yes, then any reason you offer for not doing it is just an excuse for not being disciplined. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p173]
- Suggested strategies to stay committed include: be realistic with resources (money and time are not elastic); work effectively not just efficiently; prioritise and do not allow distraction; avoid negative people; listen to advice; and once you have all the information, make the decision and follow it through. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p172]
- When you find yourself wanting to give up your dream, ask: why do you want to give up, can you change anything, can you find a different approach, who have you talked it through with, what are the advantages and disadvantages of giving up, and what plans do you have for after stopping. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p172]
