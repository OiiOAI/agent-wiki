# Heart Chakra

## Summary

The heart chakra is described by Michael Singer as a center of spiritual energy that can be opened through energy transfer. When Amrit Desai placed his hand over Singer's forehead, energy flowed through Singer's body and burst open his heart, leaving a permanent warm flow of energy passing through his heart. This event occurred in the mid-1970s and, combined with earlier experiences of energy rising to the point between the eyebrows, created two permanent energy flows that Singer describes as never having subsided.

## Key facts

- Amrit Desai placed his right hand over Singer's forehead, and Singer felt a gentle flow of warm energy passing into his body. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p94]
- The energy built up within Singer and rose toward his heart until it filled and burst open. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p94]
- A warm flow of energy has continuously passed through Singer's heart since that event, more than thirty-five years without subsiding. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p95]

## Inferences

- Inference: Singer describes this heart opening as comparable to earlier meditation experiences that left a constant energy flow rising to the point between his eyebrows—both permanent energy channels opened through spiritual experiences.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The text does not provide specific dates for when these chakra events occurred, only references to being in the mid-1970s.
