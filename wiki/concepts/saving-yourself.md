# Saving Yourself

## Summary

Ravikant emphasizes personal responsibility for self-improvement, asserting that external experts—doctors, nutritionists, teachers, gurus, mentors, trainers—cannot transform you; you must take responsibility for your own health, wealth, and fulfillment.

## Key facts

- Doctors will not make you healthy. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p129]
- Nutritionists will not make you slim. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p129]
- Teachers will not make you smart. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p129]
- Ultimately, you have to take responsibility and save yourself. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p129]
