# Shaktipat

## Summary

A special blessing by a Siddha master in which powerful spiritual energy is awakened within a student. Singer experienced this firsthand when Baba Muktananda touched his third eye point, causing a dislodging from his body and a powerful energy surge up his spine.

## Key facts

- Shaktipat is described as a special blessing by a Siddha master in which a very powerful spiritual energy is awakened within. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p86]
- Baba touched the point between Singer's eyebrows during shaktipat, exactly where he always felt energy flow. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p85]
- The experience felt like ten thousand volts of electricity jumping from the base of Singer's spine to meet Baba's hand. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p85]
- Singer was dislodged from his body and experienced tornado-force winds trying to blow him out, struggling to hold on. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p85]
- When Baba rubbed his hand across Singer's back, the experience stopped and his heart, which had been fluttering, returned to normal beating. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p86]
