# Experiential Avoidance

## Summary

Experiential avoidance is the ongoing attempt to avoid or get rid of unwanted inner experiences such as thoughts, feelings, emotions, memories, and sensations. It is normal in low levels, but high levels lead to excessive use of struggle strategies with three main costs: wasted time and energy, frustration, and lowered quality of life. High experiential avoidance is a major factor in depression, anxiety, addiction, and many other psychological problems.
