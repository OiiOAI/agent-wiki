# Latent Dream Thoughts

## Summary

Latent dream thoughts are the unconscious mental processes that underlie and give rise to the manifest dream content. They represent the true psychological meaning of the dream, including repressed wishes, conflicts, and unconscious purposes. The dream as experienced (manifest content) is a distorted, disguised substitute for the latent thoughts, which are inaccessible to ordinary consciousness but can be reached through the technique of free association.

## Key facts

- Latent dream thoughts are the concealed material reached by pursuing the ideas that occur to the dreamer during interpretation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4896]
- The task of interpreting a dream is to discover this unconscious material behind the distorted manifest content. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4891]
- The latent material is described as 'inaccessible to the dreamer's consciousness' or 'unconscious'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4890]
