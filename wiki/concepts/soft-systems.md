# Soft Systems

## Summary

Soft Systems are defined as animate (living) things or ideas within a business. People are Soft Systems, as are concepts like the script for Hamlet. Selling systems are presented as Soft Systems, and Gerber argues that the 20% of salespeople who produce 80% of sales are doing so because they use a system while the 80% do not. Soft Systems also encompass all written or verbal communications with anyone who contacts the business.

## Key facts

- Soft Systems are either animate-living or ideas. People are Soft Systems; so is a script for Hamlet. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p241]
- A selling system is identified as a Soft System that can produce 100% to 500% increases in sales. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p244]
- The observation that '80 percent of our sales are produced by 20 percent of our people' is cited, with Gerber claiming the difference is that the 20% are using a system and the 80% are not. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p244]
- Gerber states that 'every written or verbal communication with anyone who comes into contact with your business is a Soft System.'. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p257]
