# Sexual Constitution

## Summary

Sexual constitution refers to the innate variety in the development of erotogenic zones and the indirect sources of sexual excitation. Different strengths of contributions from various sources, combined with varying development of individual sources, help differentiate sexual constitutions. Every individual possesses oral erotism, anal erotism, urethral erotism, etc.; the existence of mental complexes corresponding to these implies no judgement of abnormality or neurosis. Differences separating normal from abnormal lie only in the relative strength of individual components and their developmental use.
