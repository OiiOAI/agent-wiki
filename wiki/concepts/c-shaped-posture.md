# C-Shaped Posture

## Summary

A rounded-forward body position where shoulders slouch and upper back curves, creating a C silhouette. This posture disrupts the shoulder-neck-upper back functional system, causes positional inhibition of movement, and can lead to tightness, discomfort, pain, and loss of function.

## Key facts

- C-shaped posture disrupts the octopus-seashell relationship of the shoulder as the shoulder blade is pulled forward. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1353-L1356]
- C-shaped posture causes positional inhibition, prohibiting effective and forceful movement even if one is not weak. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1410-L1412]
- Constant C-shaped posture causes the body to 'hang on' structural and functional systems, compromising them like tugging on a sweater. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1417-L1421]
- For every inch the head projects forward from C posture, it adds a 10-pound load to the neck. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1383-L1384]

## Inferences

- Inference: Avoiding C-shaped posture and practicing mobilizations to counteract it can help maintain proper movement function throughout life.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact mechanism by which C posture causes muscle adaptation over time is not fully explained in the text.
