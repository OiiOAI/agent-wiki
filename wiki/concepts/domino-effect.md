# Domino Effect

## Summary

The Domino Effect describes how extraordinary success builds sequentially when one right action topples the next, creating geometric rather than linear progression. A single domino can topple another domino 50% larger, and this principle applies to life: doing the right thing leads to doing the next right thing, accumulating into extraordinary outcomes.

## Key facts

- In 1983 Lorne Whitehead discovered that a domino can topple another domino 50% larger. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p11]
- A domino fall follows a geometric progression: the 10th domino would be as tall as Peyton Manning, the 23rd would rival the Leaning Tower of Pisa. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p13]
- Extraordinary success is sequential, not simultaneous—what starts out linear becomes geometric. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p13]
- On November 13, 2009, Weijers Domino Productions set a world record with over 4.4 million dominoes releasing 94,000 joules of energy. [raw/books/productivity/The ONE Thing - Gary Keller.pdf#p11]

## Inferences

- Inference: The domino metaphor suggests that identifying and executing the 'first domino' (the ONE Thing) is the critical starting point for all subsequent success.
