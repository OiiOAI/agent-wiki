# Avoidant Attachment Style

## Summary

The avoidant attachment style affects approximately 25 percent of the population. People with this style equate intimacy with a loss of independence and constantly try to minimize closeness. They value self-sufficiency and use various deactivating strategies to suppress their attachment needs. Research shows they still have attachment needs but actively suppress them.

## Key facts

- Avoidant people equate intimacy with a loss of independence and constantly try to minimize closeness. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p16]
- Avoidant people value their independence greatly and prefer autonomy to intimate relationships. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p43]
- Avoidants tend not to open up to their partners and often complain that they are emotionally distant. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p43]
- Avoidants use deactivating strategies to suppress their attachment needs on a day-to-day basis. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p97]
- Avoidants are unlikely to date one another, lacking the emotional glue to stay together. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p80]
