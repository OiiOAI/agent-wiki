# Renshaw Cell

## Summary

Renshaw cells are spinal interneurons that provide recurrent inhibition to motor neurons. They are excited by collateral branches from motor neuron axons and in turn inhibit those same motor neurons, forming a negative feedback system that regulates motor neuron excitability and stabilizes firing rates. Renshaw cells also send collaterals to synergist motor neurons and Ia inhibitory interneurons that synapse on antagonist motor neurons.

## Key facts

- Renshaw cells are excited by collaterals from motor neurons and inhibit those same motor neurons, providing negative feedback to regulate excitability.
