# The Primacy Effect

## Summary

The Primacy Effect is a psychological principle stating that humans place additional significance on whatever they encounter first. In traditional accounting, placing Sales and Expenses first primes business owners to focus on revenue and spending while treating Profit as an afterthought. Profit First leverages this by placing profit allocation first, making it the primary focus.

## Key facts

- The Primacy Effect causes humans to weight earlier information more heavily than later information in any sequence. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p55-56]
- When profit comes first in the business formula, it becomes the focus and is never forgotten. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p56]
