# Causal Factors in Air Traffic Control Errors

## Summary

Causal factors in ATC operational errors are categorized in FAA Form 7210-3 and include: data posting errors, radar display issues (misidentification, inappropriate use of displayed data), communications errors (phraseology, transposition, misunderstanding, readback, acknowledgment), coordination failures, and position relief briefing deficiencies. Research distinguishes between factors associated with greater versus lesser severity outcomes.

## Key facts

- Factors associated with greater severity included misuse of conflict alert (52% moderate severity), interfacility coordination (53.8%), and readback errors (40.8%). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p100]
- Factors associated with lesser severity included misuse of displayed data excluding conflict alert (22% moderate severity) and controller awareness of developing error (21% moderate severity). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p100]
- Readback errors involving altitude information resulted in 41.1% moderate severity compared to 40.8% for all readback errors. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p100]
- Only horizontal separation, not vertical separation, was significantly affected by causal factors. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p107]
- OE causal factors were modified to include three factors directly assessing SA loss: failure to detect, comprehend, and project future status of displayed data. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p115]

## Inferences

- Inference: Causal factors resulting in greater loss of separation are those likely to reduce controller awareness, whereas errors caught early by the controller tend to be less severe.
