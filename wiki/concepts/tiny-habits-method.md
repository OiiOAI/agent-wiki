# Tiny Habits Method

## Summary

The Tiny Habits Method is Fogg's system for creating lasting behavior change by starting with extremely small behaviors—actions that take less than thirty seconds—and growing them naturally over time. The method is based on three universal elements: Anchor Moment (an existing routine or event that reminds you), New Tiny Behavior (a simplified version of the desired habit), and Instant Celebration (creating positive emotion immediately after the behavior). The core philosophy is that tiny is fast, tiny can start now, tiny is safe, tiny can grow big, and tiny doesn't rely on motivation or willpower.

## Key facts

- The three elements of a Tiny Habit are Anchor Moment, New Tiny Behavior, and Instant Celebration. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p19]
- The recipe format taught is: After I [Anchor], I will [Tiny Behavior], and I will immediately [Celebrate]. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p11]
- The method replaces willpower, accountability measures, and reward promises with a process based on how habits really work. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p7]
- With Tiny Habits, people change best by feeling good, not by feeling bad. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p7]
