# Teleology

## Summary

Teleology is the study of purpose rather than cause, and represents a fundamental shift from Freudian etiology in understanding human behavior. Adlerian psychology adopts the teleological stance, arguing that people act to achieve goals they themselves set, rather than being driven by past causes. This means behavior is forward-looking and purposeful, not determined by historical traumas or childhood experiences.

- Teleology studies the purpose of a phenomenon rather than its cause, in contrast to etiology. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p33]

- Freudian etiology leads to determinism, where the present and future are seen as determined by past occurrences. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p32]
