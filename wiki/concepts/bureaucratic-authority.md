# Bureaucratic Authority

## Summary

Bureaucratic authority, as conceptualized by Max Weber, is a form of legal-rational authority characterized by stable distribution of command power, strict delimitation by formal rules, and hierarchical organization. Weber emphasized that authority is distributed according to rules concerning the coercive means—physical, sacerdotal, or otherwise—that officials may employ.

## Key facts

- Weber defined bureaucratic authority as having power to command distributed in a stable way and strictly delimited by rules. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p428]
- Lindblom noted the pervasiveness of bureaucratic forms in modern life. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p428]
