# Pornography Addiction and Neuroplasticity

## Summary

Pornography addiction demonstrates how sexual tastes can be acquired through neuroplastic change. Internet pornography, delivered by high-speed connections, satisfies all prerequisites for plastic change: massive amounts of practice, focused attention, dopamine release during orgasm, and no embarrassment or punishment. Users develop tolerance, requiring increasingly extreme content, and may experience erectile dysfunction because the problem is in their brain maps, not their penises.

## Key facts

- Pornography delivered by high-speed Internet connections satisfies every prerequisite for neuroplastic change: rapid repetition, focused attention, dopamine release, and no embarrassment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p111]
- Hardcore pornography has evolved from explicit sexual intercourse between two aroused partners to increasingly sadomasochistic themes of forced sex, ejaculations on women's faces, and angry anal sex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p112]
- Men reported increasing difficulty in being turned on by their actual sexual partners even though they still considered them objectively attractive. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p113]
- Men increasingly required sexual fantasies to match porn scripts; their sexual fantasy lives were dominated by scenarios 'downloaded' into their brains. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p113]
- Pornographers must push the envelope with harder themes because their customers are building up a tolerance to the content. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p114]
- The misleading term 'erectile dysfunction' implies a problem in the penis, but the problem is in the brain's sexual maps. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p114]
- Sensitization leads to increased wanting of pornography, though not necessarily liking it, and is caused by accumulation of the protein ΔFosB. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p116]
- Pornography hyperactivates the appetitive system by offering an endless harem of sexual objects, and viewers develop new maps in their brains based on the images they see. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p117]
