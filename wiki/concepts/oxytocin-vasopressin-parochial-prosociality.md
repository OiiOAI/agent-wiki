# Oxytocin and Vasopressin: Prosociality and the Dark Side

## Summary

Oxytocin and vasopressin are chemically similar neuropeptides that facilitate mother-infant bonding, monogamous pair-bonding, decrease anxiety, enhance trust, and promote cooperation. However, these hormones have a crucial dark side: they increase prosociality only toward 'Us' (in-group members) while promoting ethnocentrism and negative behaviors toward 'Them' (out-groups). Oxytocin is not a universal 'love hormone' but rather a 'parochial' one that enhances social competence to help identify who counts as an in-group member.

## Key facts

- Oxytocin and vasopressin facilitate mother-infant bond formation and monogamous pair-bonding. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p114]
- Oxytocin decreases aggression in rodent females but increases aggression in defense of offspring. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p118]
- Oxytocin makes people more prosocial to teammates but more likely to betray players from other teams in high-stakes scenarios. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p119]
- Oxytocin exaggerated unconscious biases against Middle Easterners and Germans in Dutch subjects. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p120]
- Oxytocin made subjects less likely to sacrifice 'Dutch' names to save five people, but showed no such protection for 'German' or 'Middle Eastern' names. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p120]
- Oxytocin enhances charitability but only in people who are already charitable. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p118]
- Oxytocin and vasopressin also foster social interest and competence rather than generic prosociality, making people more accurate at gathering social information. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p116]

## Inferences

- Inference: Oxytocin evolved to enhance social competence to identify who is 'Us', which would have been adaptive in ancestral environments where distinguishing in-group from out-group had survival implications. The finding that believing one has received testosterone (regardless of actual administration) makes people behave worse suggests that cultural beliefs about hormones shape behavior.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise mechanisms by which oxytocin distinguishes in-group from out-group members remain under investigation
- Uncertain: Whether findings from intranasal administration translate to naturally occurring oxytocin signaling is uncertain
