# Schema

## Summary

Schema (plural: schemata) are prototypical states of mental models that provide processing efficiencies. They are retained in memory and represent typical states or patterns. People use pattern matching to link cues from the current situation to schemata to pick the best match. Schema allow rapid classification and understanding of information, functioning like shortcuts that provide comprehension and projection as a single step. They are formed by direct experience or vicariously through reading or hearing about similar cases.
