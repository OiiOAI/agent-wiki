# Flaking

## Summary

The phenomenon where women give phone numbers, express interest, but then fail to respond or show up for dates. The book frames flaking as primarily a symptom of insufficient attraction rather than a communication problem.

## Key facts

- The way to prevent flakes is to meet and attract women who are so interested in you that they would never consider flaking—treating the illness not the symptom. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p227]
- 99% of flake prevention strategies rarely convince a girl who was never attracted to you to suddenly become attracted to you. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p227]
- The author's 'Three strikes' policy is: try once more after a flake, and if there's still nothing after three attempts, move on. [raw/books/psychology/Models - Mark Manson.pdf#p229]
