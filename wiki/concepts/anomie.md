# Anomie

## Summary

Anomie is Durkheim's term for what happens to a society that no longer has a shared moral order. It means, literally, 'normlessness.' Haidt uses this concept to argue that societies that forgo the exoskeleton of religion should reflect carefully on what will happen to them over several generations. The concept connects to research showing that when societies lose their grip on individuals, allowing all to do as they please, the result is often a decrease in happiness and an increase in suicide.

## Key facts

- Anomie means, literally, 'normlessness'—what happens to a society that no longer has a shared moral order. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p291]
- Societies that forgo the exoskeleton of religion should reflect carefully on what will happen to them over several generations. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p291]
- When societies lose their grip on individuals, allowing all to do as they please, the result is often a decrease in happiness and an increase in suicide, as Durkheim showed more than a hundred years ago. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p291]
- Human beings feel an acute sense of unease—what Emile Durkheim labeled anomie—in the absence of norms and rules binding them to others. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p16]
- The modern workplace serves to moderate and overcome anomie by connecting people to a wider social world. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p16]
- Merton defined anomie as "an acute disjunction between the cultural norms and goals and the socially structured capacities of members of the group to act in accord with them". [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p147]
- Merton defined culture as "organized set of normative values governing behavior" and social structure as "organized set of social relationships". [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p147]
- When cultural goals are emphasized but structural means are blocked, anomie results, leading to deviant behavior using alternative means to achieve culturally valued goals. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p147]
