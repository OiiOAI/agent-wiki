# Cognitive Load Management

## Summary

Cognitive load management is a distinguishing focus of team design in the Team Topologies approach. As the complexity of a system increases, so do the cognitive demands on the organization building and evolving it. Managing cognitive load through teams with clear responsibilities and boundaries is essential to achieving duly scoped, bounded responsibilities.

## Key facts

- Managing cognitive load through teams with clear responsibilities and boundaries is a distinguishing focus of team design in the Team Topologies approach. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L425-L426]
- As the complexity of the system increases, generally the cognitive demands on the organization building and evolving it also increase. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L425]
- To achieve duly scoped, bounded responsibilities, natural and relatively independent system structure is sought to align teams to. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L426-L427]
