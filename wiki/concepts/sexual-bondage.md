# Sexual Bondage

## Summary

A psychoanalytic concept introduced by von Krafft-Ebing describing a person's unusually high dependence on a sexual partner, involving loss of independent will and willingness to sacrifice personal interests. Freud connected this to the overcoming of psychical resistances during defloration.

## Key facts

- Von Krafft-Ebing defined sexual bondage as a condition where a person acquires exceptional dependence and lack of self-reliance through a sexual relationship. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3628]
- Freud argued that the decisive factor in sexual bondage is the amount of psychical resistance overcome, concentrated in a single experience. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3629]
- Sexual bondage occurs more frequently and intensely in women than in men, resulting from overcoming psychical impotence through one particular person. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3629]
