# Mortality Awareness

## Summary

Mortality awareness is the conscious confrontation with one's own death and the deaths of others. The text argues that most people spend their lives avoiding thoughts of death, yet understanding the shortness of life fills one with purpose and urgency. Training oneself to confront and accept mortality helps manage inevitable setbacks, separations, and crises, while seeing mortality in everyone creates empathy and equalizes all people.

## Key facts

- Most people spend their lives avoiding the thought of death instead of continually keeping it in mind. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p682]
- Understanding the shortness of life fills us with a sense of purpose and urgency to realize our goals. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p682]
- Seeing mortality in everyone equalizes and connects us all, counteracting tendencies to feel superior or separated. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p682]
- By becoming deeply aware of our mortality, we intensify our experience of every aspect of life. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p682]
- Children become aware of mortality early, and this anxiety sits in adults in a powerfully latent form. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p577]
- Modern society has made death largely invisible, something that occurs only in hospitals, giving it an unreal aspect. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p576]

## Inferences
