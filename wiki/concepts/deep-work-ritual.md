# Deep Work Ritual

## Summary

A structured approach to scheduling deep work sessions, consisting of strict and idiosyncratic practices that minimize friction in transitioning to depth. A deep work ritual must address three key questions: where to work, how to work once started, and how to support the work.

## Key facts

- A deep work ritual should specify a location for deep work efforts, which can be as simple as an office with the door shut or a dedicated conference room. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- The ritual should give a specific time frame for each session to make it a discrete challenge rather than an open-ended slog. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- Rules should be established for how to work during deep sessions, such as banning Internet use or maintaining a productivity metric. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- The ritual should ensure the brain gets necessary support through food, coffee, light exercise, or environmental organization. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- Finding an effective ritual may require experimentation, as each person and project type requires a different fit. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]

## Inferences

- Inference: The deep work ritual concept is based on observations of successful thinkers like Robert Caro and Charles Darwin, who used strict rituals to enable repeated deep work.
