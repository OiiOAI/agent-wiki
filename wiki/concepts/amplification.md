# Amplification

## Summary

Amplification is the opposite of resonance and the mechanism by which ideology emerges from myth. When a single voice is sufficiently amplified, it becomes speaking that makes it impossible for any other voices to be heard. We do not listen to a loudspeaker for what is being said, but only because it is all that is being said. Ideology is the amplification of myth—the assumption that since the beginning and end of history are known, there is nothing more to say. The amplified voice seeks obedient action and an immediate end to other speech; it is designed to bring itself to an end as completely and swiftly as possible. What ideologists conceal is the choral nature of history, the symphony of very different, even opposed, voices that nonetheless make each other possible.
