# Swarm of Behaviors

## Summary

The Swarm of Behaviors is a visualization technique that breaks down a general habit into specific behaviors that contribute to it. By listing 10+ specific habits around a central cloud, people can see the complexity of what seemed like a simple habit and identify which specific tangles to address first.

## Key facts

- The Swarm of Behaviors technique involves writing a general habit in a cloud and listing specific habits that contribute to it in surrounding boxes. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p230]
- Focusing on abstract General Habits like 'stop eating junk food' leads to frustration; specific habits make progress manageable. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p229]
- After listing specific habits, people often feel bad about themselves—this is normal and temporary. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p230]
- The technique can be applied to stopping bad habits or to understanding complex desired behaviors. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p250]
- Fogg used the technique on himself and found 15+ specific habits contributing to his general habit of leaving things out of place. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p230]
- The Swarm of Behaviors is listed as both a Model and a Method in the Behavior Design framework. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p314]
- Fogg has templates online for the Swarm of Behaviors that can be used with friends and family. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p303]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Swarm of Behaviors approach suggests that looking at many small behaviors together can reveal patterns that wouldn't be apparent when focusing on individual habits in isolation.
