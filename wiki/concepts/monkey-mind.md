# Monkey Mind

## Summary

The 'monkey mind' is Ravikant's term for the restless, anxious, fear-driven aspect of human consciousness that constantly predicts, plans, and judges, pulling us away from present reality.

## Key facts

- Monkey mind activates around puberty with the onset of desire and long-range planning. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p156]
- When walking down the street, all people are talking to themselves in their head, judging everything, playing back movies of yesterday, and living in fantasy worlds of tomorrow. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p156]
- Ravikant wants the mind to be a servant and tool, not a master. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p156]
- A busy mind accelerates the passage of subjective time. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p156]
