# SNARE Proteins

## Summary

SNARE proteins are evolutionarily conserved molecular machines that catalyze fusion of synaptic vesicles with the plasma membrane. They consist of v-SNAREs (synaptobrevin/VAMP on vesicles) and t-SNAREs (syntaxin and SNAP-25 on the plasma membrane) that form a stable alpha-helical coiled-coil complex.

## Key facts

- SNAREs are universally involved in membrane fusion, from yeast to humans, mediating both constitutive trafficking and synaptic vesicle exocytosis. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p392]
- Each synaptic vesicle contains synaptobrevin (v-SNARE), while the presynaptic active zone contains syntaxin and SNAP-25 (t-SNAREs). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p392]
- The crystal structure of the SNARE complex reveals that four SNARE motifs bind tightly to form an alpha-helical coiled-coil complex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p392]
- After fusion, NSF and SNAP proteins use ATP hydrolysis to dissociate SNARE complexes, regenerating free SNAREs for efficient vesicle recycling. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p393]
- Munc18 (an SM protein) binds to syntaxin before SNARE complex assembly and is essential for all synaptic fusion; deletion prevents all synaptic fusion in neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p393]
- SNARE proteins are universally involved in membrane fusion from yeast to humans, mediating both constitutive trafficking and regulated exocytosis. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p330]
- Synaptobrevin (VAMP) is the vesicle SNARE (v-SNARE), while syntaxin and SNAP-25 are target membrane SNAREs (t-SNAREs). [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p330]
- The SNARE complex consists of a bundle of four alpha-helices, one each from synaptobrevin and syntaxin and two from SNAP-25. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p331]
- Botulinum and tetanus toxins are potent inhibitors of transmitter release because they cleave SNARE proteins. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p330]
