# Intuitive Psychology (Theory of Mind)

## Summary

Intuitive psychology, or theory of mind (TOM), is the human ability to understand that others have invisible mental states such as beliefs, desires, intentions, and goals that can cause behaviors. This domain may be uniquely human or more developed in humans than in other animals.

## Key facts

- Theory of mind allows humans to ascribe mental states to other humans and to some extent to animals, even though animals may not possess it to the same degree. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p269]
- People attribute desires and intentions even to geometric shapes moving in ways that suggest goal-directed behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p269]
- Autistic individuals suffer from 'mind-blindness' and lack intuitive psychology, making social interactions difficult. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p275]
- Humans may be the only animals that form concepts about imperceptible things and processes in the psychological realm. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p270]
