# Long-Term Potentiation and Depression

## Summary

Long-term potentiation (LTP) strengthens connections between neurons during learning, while long-term depression (LTD) weakens connections during unlearning. Both are plastic processes essential for brain adaptation. Unlearning existing memories is necessary to make room for new memories. Love requires massive unlearning as people must radically alter existing intentions and modify other attachments to integrate a new person into their lives.

## Key facts

- Long-term potentiation (LTP) is a chemical process that strengthens connections between neurons when we learn something new. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p125]
- Long-term depression (LTD) is the chemical process by which the brain unlearns associations and disconnects neurons, with nothing to do with depressed mood state. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p125]
- Unlearning and weakening connections between neurons is just as plastic a process, and just as important, as learning and strengthening them. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p125]
- Evidence suggests that unlearning existing memories is necessary to make room for new memories in our networks. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p125]
- Falling in love means entering a new developmental stage and demands massive unlearning as people must radically alter existing and often selfish intentions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p126]
- Millions of neural networks have to be obliterated and replaced when falling in love, which is why it feels, for so many people, like a loss of identity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p126]

## Inferences

- Inference: The process of grief involves unlearning the idea that a person exists and can be relied on, accomplished one memory at a time, which explains why grief is so difficult and takes time.
