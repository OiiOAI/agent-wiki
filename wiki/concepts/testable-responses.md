# Testable responses for situation awareness measurement

## Summary

Testable responses are isolated, experimentally controlled events designed to unambiguously ascertain subjects' situation awareness from their performance. These events require a discernible, identifiable action (or set of actions) from the operator that cannot be anticipated through any means other than good situation awareness. The technique removes ambiguity in inferring SA from performance by using situations that demand practiced, proceduralized responses where decision-making is minimal and SA becomes the dominant factor in subject performance.

## Key facts

- Testable responses are isolated, experimentally controlled events that cannot be anticipated through any means other than good situation awareness, and require a discernible, identifiable action from the operator. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p215]
- The technique of using events with testable responses tests subjects' situation awareness at occasional, discrete events. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p216]
- Situations with testable responses must have the ability to mandate a clear and unambiguous response. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p216]
- Four mechanisms for generating testable responses exist: hard-wired/programmed events, confederate agents, pseudo-agents, and automatic control with real-time feedback. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p218]
- Automatic control can enable more consistent robustness in situations and may cause situations to occur within tighter parameters than experimenters acting as confederates. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p221]
- The use of testable responses is limited to tasks and situations that require unambiguous, proceduralized responses. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p217]
- Testable responses are best for examining the sufficiency of an operator's situation awareness to off-nominal or emergency conditions. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p217]

## Inferences

- Inference: Testable responses are particularly suited for trained professionals like airline pilots where procedures mandate specific responses, as described in the flight simulator studies cited.
- Inference: The fidelity of testable situations requires that the controllable system dynamics follow commensurate levels of fidelity and that all information available to the subject corroborates the primary source.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The text notes that testable responses may be impractical in situations too complex, with cues too subtle to manipulate, or with too many operators to control reliably.
