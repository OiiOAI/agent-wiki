# Deprival Super-Reaction Syndrome

## Summary

A powerful psychological response where people react disproportionately to losing something they already have, making 'takeaways' extremely difficult to negotiate. This syndrome helps explain gambling behavior and consumer resistance to product changes.

## Key facts

- Deprival super-reaction syndrome explains why people go broke by refusing to stop pursuing a losing investment—they cannot bear to lose what is already committed. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p247]
- Changing Coca-Cola's flavor would trigger deprival super-reaction syndrome because consumers have deeply conditioned preferences for the original taste. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p298]
- The syndrome makes most gamblers irrational and is responsible for much poor decision-making in both gambling and business. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p298]
