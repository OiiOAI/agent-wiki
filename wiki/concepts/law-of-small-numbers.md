# Law of Small Numbers

## Summary

The law of small numbers causes us to draw unwarranted conclusions from small samples. Small retail branches show extreme theft rates in both directions simply due to sample size, not actual patterns. Even experienced scientists succumb to this bias.

## Key facts

- In 1,000 stores, the 100 branches with highest theft rates and the 100 with lowest theft rates are both primarily rural because small branches show more extreme results in both directions. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p139]
- Daniel Kahneman revealed that even experienced scientists succumb to the law of small numbers. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p140]
