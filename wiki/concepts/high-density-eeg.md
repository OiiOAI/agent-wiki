# High-density EEG

## Summary

High-density EEG (hd-EEG) recording technique using 256 channels that enables high spatial resolution imaging of brain electrical activity during sleep. The study used this method to perform source modeling and contrast neural activity between dreaming and no-experience states at the cortical level.

## Key facts

- Recordings were made using a 256-channel high-density EEG system (Electrical Geodesics, Inc.) with four electrodes at outer canthi for eye movement monitoring. [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p11]
- Source localization was performed using sLORETA (standardized low-resolution brain electromagnetic tomography) with a 4-shell head model based on the Montreal Neurological Institute atlas. [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p12]
- The source space was restricted to 2447 dipoles distributed over 7x7x7 mm cortical voxels. [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p12]
- Spectral power density was computed using Welch's modified periodogram method in 2s Hamming windows with 50% overlap. [raw/books/neuroscience/The neural correlates of consciousness and unconsciousness in sleep.pdf#p12]
