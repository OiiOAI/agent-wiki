# Incompletion Triggers

## Summary

Incompletion Triggers is a comprehensive checklist for conducting a mind sweep, covering both professional and personal life areas including projects, commitments, communications, finances, administration, and health.

## Key facts

- The Incompletion Triggers list is organized into professional and personal sections to help identify all open loops during the mind sweep. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p157]
- Professional triggers include projects started or needing to be started, commitments to others, communications, writing, meetings, financial, planning, and organizational development items. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p157-161]
- Personal triggers include projects, commitments to family, communications, events, administration, leisure, financial, health, transportation, clothing, errands, and community items. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p161-164]
