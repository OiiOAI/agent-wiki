# Half-Smiling and Willing Hands

## Summary

Half-smiling and willing hands are reality acceptance techniques that use body positioning to facilitate acceptance of reality. Half-smiling involves relaxing facial muscles and slightly lifting the corners of lips. Willing hands involves keeping hands open with palms up and fingers relaxed, communicating openness to acceptance to the brain through the body.

## Key facts

- Half-smiling: relax face from top of head down, let corners of lips go slightly up so you can feel them, adopt a serene facial expression. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p371]
- Willing hands (standing): drop arms from shoulders, keep straight or slightly bent, turn hands outward with thumbs out, palms up, fingers relaxed. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p371]
- Your face and hands communicate to your brain; the body connects to the mind. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p371]
- Practice opportunities include upon waking, during free moments, while listening to music, when irritated, in lying or sitting positions, and when contemplating someone you dislike. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p372]
