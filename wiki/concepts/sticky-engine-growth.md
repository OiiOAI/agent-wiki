# Sticky Engine of Growth

## Summary

The sticky engine of growth is a mechanism where sustainable growth comes from retaining existing customers over time. Products using this engine are designed to attract and retain customers for the long term, and growth occurs when the rate of new customer acquisition exceeds the churn rate. The speed of growth is determined by the compounding rate (natural growth rate minus churn rate).

## Key facts

- In the sticky engine of growth, the rule is: if the rate of new customer acquisition exceeds the churn rate, the product will grow. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p195]
- The speed of growth in the sticky engine is determined by the compounding rate, which is the natural growth rate minus the churn rate. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p195]
- A startup using the sticky engine with 61 percent retention and 39 percent new customer growth had a compounding growth rate of just 0.02 percent because churn offset new acquisition almost perfectly. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p196]

## Inferences

- Inference: Companies using the sticky engine should focus on improving customer retention rather than investing in sales and marketing when seeking growth, which is counterintuitive to standard business intuition.
