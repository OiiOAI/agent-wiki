# Beaufort Wind Scale

## Summary

An empirical scale for estimating wind speeds based on observable effects, originally devised by Admiral Sir Francis Beaufort based on wave effects at sea, later adapted for land use.

## Key facts

- The Beaufort scale was originally devised by Admiral Sir Francis Beaufort (1774-1857) based on the effect of wind on waves at sea. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L830]
- The internationally recognized scale ranges from 0 to 12, extended to 17 by the U.S. Weather Bureau in 1955. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L835]
