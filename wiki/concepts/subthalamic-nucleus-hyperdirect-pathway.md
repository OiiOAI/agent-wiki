# Subthalamic Nucleus and Hyperdirect Cortical Pathway

## Summary

The subthalamic nucleus (STN) is a key component of the basal ganglia circuitry that provides a hyperdirect cortical input pathway. Motor and premotor projections concentrate in the lateral, dorsal, and caudal parts, while prefrontal projections concentrate in the anterior, ventral, and medial regions, maintaining functional topography.

## Key facts

- The hyperdirect pathway defines a topographic anatomic connection composed of a rostral limbic component concentrated in the medial tip of the STN and adjacent lateral hypothalamus, a cognitive component in the medial half, and a motor component centered in the lateral half. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p14]
- The STN connection with the ventral pallidum, along with a hyperdirect pathway from the ventral medial prefrontal cortex, orbitofrontal cortex, and anterior cingulate cortex to the medial STN, highlights the role of the STN in the reward pathway. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p10]
- STN neurons at the interface of functional territories are likely to receive convergent inputs onto their proximal dendrites due to the orientation of dendrites along the long axis of the nucleus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p14]
- Dopamine transporter is relatively low throughout the ventral striatum including the core, consistent with dorsal tier dopamine neurons expressing relatively low mRNA for the dopamine transporter compared to the ventral tier. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p12]

## Inferences

- Inference: The convergence of inputs from different functional domains onto STN neurons suggests this nucleus may serve as an integration site for reward, cognitive, and motor information.
- Inference: The hyperdirect cortical pathway may provide a rapid route for prefrontal information to influence basal ganglia output, complementing the slower corticostriatal pathway.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise functional role of STN in reward-based decision-making compared to its established role in motor control requires further investigation.
