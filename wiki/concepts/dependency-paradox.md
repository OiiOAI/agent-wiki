# Dependency Paradox

## Summary

The dependency paradox is the psychological finding that the more effectively dependent people are on one another, the more independent and daring they become. When emotional needs are met early and consistently, people can turn their attention outward and pursue goals with confidence. The secure base concept illustrates this: knowing someone is available as support allows people to explore and take risks.

## Key facts

- The dependency paradox states that the more effectively dependent people are on one another, the more independent and daring they become. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p31]
- Most people are only as needy as their unmet needs; when emotional needs are met, they usually turn their attention outward. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p26]
- If you want to take the road to independence and happiness, find the right person to depend on and travel down it with that person. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p32]
- Once we choose a partner, powerful and often uncontrollable forces come into play regardless of how independent we are. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p31]
