# Value vs Salience in Decision-Making

## Summary

Value and salience are key variables for associative learning, decision-making, and attention that are related but distinguishable. Value is a signed currency that varies monotonically from negative to positive, increasing with magnitude and probability of reward but decreasing with magnitude and probability of punishment. Salience is an unsigned currency where the more negative or positive the predicted outcome, the more salient it is. Value signals arise in striatum, orbitofrontal cortex, ventromedial prefrontal cortex, and superior parietal areas, while magnitude-based salience signals arise in anterior cingulate cortex and temporoparietal junction, and probability-based salience signals in ventromedial prefrontal cortex.

## Key facts

- Value is defined as a signed currency that guides appropriate action selection in a magnitude-dependent fashion, increasing with reward and decreasing with punishment. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p109]
- Salience is defined as an unsigned currency where the absolute value of the predicted outcome determines its salience, irrespective of whether the outcome is a reward or punishment. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p109]
- Value signals arise in striatum, orbitofrontal cortex, ventromedial prefrontal cortex, and superior parietal areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p109]
- Magnitude-based salience signals arise in the anterior cingulate cortex and inferior parietal cortex extending into the temporoparietal junction. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p115]
- Probability-based salience signals have been found in the ventromedial prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p117]
- Striatal activity integrates punishment and reward value in response to stimuli predicting mixed outcomes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p111]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The computational equivalence of probability-based versus magnitude-based salience in the brain remains unclear.
- Uncertain: Whether different forms of salience subserve the same behavioral functions is not yet established.
