# Rule of Three

## Summary

The Rule of Three is a technique for ensuring genuine commitment by getting the counterpart to agree to the same thing three times in one conversation. This uncovers counterfeit 'yes' responses, liars, and incongruences between words and body language, because it is difficult to repeatedly fake conviction.

## Key facts

- The three kinds of 'yes' are Commitment, Confirmation, and Counterfeit. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p156]
- The first agreement can be a simple 'yes,' the second a 'that's right' through labeling or summarizing, and the third a calibrated 'how' or 'what' question about implementation. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p156]
- The Rule of Three uncovers falsehoods and the incongruences between words and body language. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p156]
- When someone says 'I'll try,' it often means they plan to fail rather than genuine commitment. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p149]
