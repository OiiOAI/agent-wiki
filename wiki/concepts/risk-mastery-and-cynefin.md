# Risk Mastery and Cynefin

## Summary

Risk mastery through the Cynefin Framework involves understanding that the mastery of risk extends across all five domains of the framework. The Cynefin Framework serves as a navigation tool for risk practitioners, helping them work with uncertainty, change, and surprise by applying appropriate approaches in each domain.

## Key facts

- Risk is defined as 'the effect of uncertainty on objectives'—a deviation from what is expected that gives rise to opportunity, threat, or both. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p294]
- The Cynefin Framework helps risk practitioners navigate both intentional and emergent change, functioning as a map of the decision field. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p295]
- Resilience is defined as 'the capacity to sustain development in the face of change, incremental and abrupt, expected and surprising'—drawing on socio-ecological systems thinking. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p297]
- In the Complex domain, risk is managed by taking a broad and diverse perspective, detecting weak signals of change, and responding quickly through safe-to-fail experiments. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p315]
- Root cause analysis can be unhelpful when dealing with complexity; more is learned by considering how agents at different levels of the organization act and respond to error feedback. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p303]
