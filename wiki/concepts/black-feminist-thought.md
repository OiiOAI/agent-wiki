# Black Feminist Thought

## Summary

Black Feminist Thought is a theoretical framework developed by Patricia Hill Collins that presents social theory as the understandings of Black women. It draws on a wide range of voices to articulate Black women's understanding of their oppression through intersections of race, gender, and class, and their historic struggle against that oppression. The framework uncovers the distinctive epistemology by which Black women assess truth and validity, and argues for a feminist standpoint epistemology. Collins's work 'Black Feminist Thought' (1990) received both the Jessie Bernard Award and the C. Wright Mills Award.
