# 13 Behaviors of Trust

## Summary

A comprehensive framework by Stephen Covey identifying 13 specific behaviors that build trust in organizational and personal relationships. These behaviors are grouped by their relationship to the Four Cores of Credibility (Integrity, Intent, Capabilities, Results). The behaviors span from Demonstrate Respect (#2) through Extend Trust (#13), with each building upon and reinforcing the others to create high-trust cultures.

## Key facts

- The 13 Behaviors are organized around the Four Cores of Credibility: Integrity, Intent, Capabilities, and Results. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p187]
- Only 29% of employees believe management cares about them developing their skills and only 42% believe management cares about them at all. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p187]
- Mercer study shows only 39% of employees believe senior management does a good job confronting issues before they turn into major problems. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p234]
