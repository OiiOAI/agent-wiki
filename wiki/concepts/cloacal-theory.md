# Cloacal Theory

## Summary

The cloacal theory is the child's belief that sexual intercourse takes place at the anus rather than through the vagina. Freud describes how when the patient was one and a half, his first understanding of the primal scene would have been based on this theory. When later learning about castration at age four awakened doubt about this theory, he behaved like children generally do—he rejected what was new (from motives connected with his fear of castration) and clung to what was old. He repudiated the new information about the vagina and clung to the old cloacal theory, which provided the material for his identification with women.

## Key facts

- When the child's Christmas dream conjured up the picture of parental intercourse, the first view to come up was the old one, according to which the part of the female body which received the male organ was the anus. ## Inferences
