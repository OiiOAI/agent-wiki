# Confusion Domain

## Summary

The Confusion domain in Cynefin represents a state of mismatch between the situation and the response. It can manifest as being swamped by events and making inappropriate responses, or as a highly aware 'viewing platform' state that allows clearer discernment.

## Key facts

- Cultivating Leadership distinguishes two states of Confusion: being swamped by events, or being on a viewing platform with puzzled curiosity allowing clearer sight. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p174]
- Chris Corrigan uses the word 'economically' as a prompt to illustrate Confusion, where the ambiguous adverb activates biases, assumptions, and societal meanings. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p166]
- In diabetes management, burnout and confusion require first offering acceptance and understanding before exploring possibilities. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p203]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Confusion domain is not merely a problem to escape but can be a productive state for gaining perspective.
