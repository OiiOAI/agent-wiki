# pH

## Summary

A measure of acidity or alkalinity of a solution based on hydrogen ion concentration. pH 7 indicates neutrality, lower values indicate acidity, higher values indicate alkalinity.

## Key facts

- pH stands for potentiality of hydrogen and equals -log10C where C is the concentration of hydrogen in moles per liter. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1165]
- A pH of 7 indicates a neutral solution; higher pH indicates greater alkalinity; lower pH indicates greater acidity. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L1-L1170]
