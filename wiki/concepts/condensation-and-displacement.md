# Condensation and Displacement

## Summary

Condensation (Verdichtung) and displacement (Traumverschiebung) are the two primary mechanisms of the dream-work that transform latent dream thoughts into manifest dream content. Condensation compresses multiple dream thoughts into single elements, creating composite structures that represent common elements. Displacement transfers psychical intensity from important to unimportant elements, making the dream's true significance unrecognizable. These same mechanisms operate in psychoneurotic symptom formation.

## Key facts

- Condensation in the dream-work creates composite structures, combining elements from different dream thoughts that share common properties. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1886]
- Dream displacement transfers psychical intensity from important elements to unimportant ones, producing the characteristic difference between manifest and latent dream content. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1888]
- The essential determining condition of displacement is a motive related to the censorship and the state of repression of certain dream thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1901]
- Condensation and displacement are invariable characteristics not only of dream formation but also of hysterical symptoms, phobias, obsessions and delusions. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1901]
- Each element in the content of a dream is overdetermined by material in the dream thoughts, traceable to multiple elements rather than a single source. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1889]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery that the same mechanisms operate in dreams and psychopathological formations suggests a fundamental continuity between normal and pathological mental processes.
