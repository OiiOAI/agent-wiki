# Enactive evolution

## Summary

Enactive evolution is Thompson's reconceptualization of natural selection and adaptation based on the autopoietic and developmental systems perspectives. It holds that evolution should not be described as organisms adapting to independent environments, but as the conservation of adaptation through autopoiesis and structural coupling. Natural selection is reconceived not as an external force but as the differential propagation of developmental systems — an emergent consequence of autonomous systems structurally coupling with their environments.

## Key facts

- According to enactive evolution, central to evolution is not the optimization of adaptation but the conservation of adaptation — as long as a living being maintains its autonomous integrity, it is adapted. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p200]
- Adaptation is an invariant background condition: structural variations arise through reproduction and development, and many produce individuals capable of continuing their lineage. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p200]
- Enactive evolution reconceptualizes natural selection as an emergent consequence of autopoiesis, not its cause: natural selection requires reproduction, but reproduction presupposes autopoietic unities. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p207]
- Natural selection results from the 'satisficing' of viable trajectories effected by autonomous networks in their structural coupling with environments, rather than being an external force impinging on networks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p203]

## Inferences

- Inference: Self-organization and natural selection are not opposed but two interwoven aspects of a single process of enactive evolution, reciprocally process and product with respect to each other.
- Inference: Neither simply continuous with the Darwinian tradition nor a radical departure, enactive evolution inscribes principles of autonomous self-organization in evolutionary narratives while maintaining continuity with Darwinian heritage.
