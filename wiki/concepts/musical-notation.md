# Musical notation and measurement

## Summary

Systems for measuring and describing musical sound, including pitch (frequency in Hz), note values (whole, half, quarter notes), tempo (beats per minute), and dynamic markings (piano to fortissimo).

## Key facts

- Standard concert pitch sets A above middle C at 440 Hz, established by ISO in 1955. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- An octave has the frequency ratio of 1:2 and comprises eight steps of a diatonic scale. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- In equal temperament, the octave is divided into 12 exactly equal semitone steps. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
- The decibel measures sound intensity on a logarithmic scale where each 10 dB step represents a tenfold increase in sound intensity. [raw/books/strategy/How_to_Measure_Anything.epub#L2620-L2620]
