# Soil microbiome and nutrient depletion

## Summary

The soil microbiome is essential for plant nutrient production. Without adequate minerals in the soil, plants cannot produce the vitamins humans need. Depletion of soil minerals jeopardizes the ability to get necessary nutrients even from a healthy diet. The overuse of herbicides like glyphosate has impacted how crops are grown and their nutritional value.

## Key facts

- Most people don't realize that without adequate minerals in the soil, plants can't produce the vitamins we need. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
- Depletion of soil minerals jeopardizes our ability to get the nutrients we need even if we eat a super-healthy diet. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
- The overuse of herbicides like glyphosate and other chemicals has had a severe impact on how crops are grown as well as their nutritional value. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p102]
- There are about four million acres of vegetables and fruit in the United States.
