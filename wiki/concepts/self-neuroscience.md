# SELF (Neuroscience)

## Summary

The SELF is a proposed ancient neural process for the generation of behavioral coherence and bodily awareness. It consists of an intrinsically reverberating neural network linked to basic body tone and gross axial movement generators, providing a coherent matrix in which sensory stimuli become hedonically valenced. The SELF is posited as the foundational substrate of primary-process affective consciousness, believed to reside in centromedial zones of the midbrain.

## Key facts

- The SELF consists of an intrinsically reverberating neural network linked to basic body tone and gross axial movement generators. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- The SELF is proposed to be situated near the core of the brain and richly connected to higher and lower brain areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- The SELF is hypothesized to be highly multimodal, allowing for rerepresentation at many levels of the neuroaxis during ontogenetic development. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p164]
- The SELF may be topographically like a body of quite primordial shape, possibly compared metaphorically to a stingray. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The SELF provides the first executive mechanism for behavioral coherence and bodily awareness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The SELF does not have thoughts or clearly defined perceptions, but it elaborates primitive feelings and serves as an anchor that stabilizes or binds many other brain processes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The mesencephalic roots of the SELF are more richly connected with frontal motor areas than with posterior sensory areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p166]
- Primary-process affective and intentional consciousness is more critically linked to motor than to sensory cortices. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p166]

## Inferences

- Inference: The SELF may be the neurosymbolic representation that resolves the infinite regress problem of consciousness, since all higher monitors are entranced by a central process that itself does not observe.
- Inference: The SELF may be functionally divided into somatic (motor) and visceral components that operate as an interactive unity.
- Inference: As organisms develop, reflections of the SELF may come to reside in many brain areas, providing higher circuits a concrete value focus.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The predominant anatomical source of the primal SELF in the brain remains uncertain; candidates include deep cerebellar nuclei and centromedial areas of the midbrain.
- Uncertain: The exact manner in which subjective experience emerges from neurodynamics of the SELF remains unspecified.
