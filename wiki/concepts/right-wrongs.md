# Right Wrongs

## Summary

Behavior #4 in Covey's framework. Righting wrongs means more than apologizing—it includes making restitution, making up, making whole, and taking action to correct mistakes plus a little more. Based on principles of humility, integrity, and restitution.

## Key facts

- Maple Leaf Foods CEO Michael McCain immediately took total responsibility when listeria was found in products, filmed a personal apology, and recalled all products from 191 product lines. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p199]
- Oprah Winfrey publicly apologized to viewers when she discovered James Frey had embellished his 'nonfiction' book. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p201]
- Doug Wead apologized for taping President Bush without permission, cancelled his book promotion, assigned future royalties to charity, and turned over the tapes to the president. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p202]
- Watergate was characterized as fundamentally a failure to recognize mistakes, take responsibility, and apologize accordingly. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p203]
