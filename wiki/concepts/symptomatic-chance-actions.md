# Symptomatic and Chance Actions

## Summary

Symptomatic actions (or chance actions) are motor manifestations to which consciousness attaches little value or which it overlooks entirely. These actions serve to express a wide variety of unconscious or withheld impulses, functioning as symbolic representations of repressed phantasies and wishes. Unlike more significant bungled actions, internal conflict becomes less important in symptomatic actions, allowing unconscious material broader expression. These phenomena occupy a position on a continuum with neurotic symptoms, representing 'formes frustes' of neurosis where the pathology manifests in minor rather than major life functions.
