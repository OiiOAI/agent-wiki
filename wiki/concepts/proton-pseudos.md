# Hysterical Proton Pseudos

## Summary

The proton pseudos (literally 'first false step') in hysteria refers to the pathogenic mechanism where a memory arouses an affect that the original experience did not generate. This occurs through 'deferred action' (Nachträglichkeit): the trauma of childhood (Scene II: assault by shopkeeper) was not pathogenic at the time, but becomes so when recalled after puberty (Scene I: shop assistants laughing), which provides new understanding of the memory. The retarded development of sexuality allows memories to acquire pathogenic significance that they lacked as fresh experiences.

## Key facts

- The proton pseudos in hysteria occurs when a memory arouses an affect which it did not arouse as an experience, because puberty has made possible a different understanding of what was remembered. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p356]
- Every adolescent must carry the germ of hysteria within him, as all have memory traces that can only be understood with the emergence of sexual feelings. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p357]
- The retardation of puberty makes possible posthumous primary processes, as the memory (not the perception) unexpectedly releases unpleasure. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p359]
- The case of Emma illustrates how the memory of childhood assault (Scene II) became pathogenic only when associated with the later scene of laughing shop assistants (Scene I). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p354]

## Inferences

- Inference: The concept of the proton pseudos represents Freud's early understanding of how trauma requires psychic processing to become pathogenic, anticipating later concepts like Nachträglichkeit
- Inference: The hypothesis was later superseded by the discovery of infantile sexuality, but the idea that a trauma could have deferred pathogenic effects remained valid

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud later revised this theory when he recognized unconscious drive impulses and infantile sexuality as pre-existing factors rather than products of deferred understanding
