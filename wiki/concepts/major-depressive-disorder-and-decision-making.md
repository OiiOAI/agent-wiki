# Major Depressive Disorder and Decision-Making

## Summary

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) is conceptualized as a disorder of decision-making, with indecisiveness being a diagnostic criterion. Research reveals shifts from reflective, model-based decision-making to more habitual strategies in MDD patients, along with reward hyposensitivity and maladaptive responses to punishment.

## Key facts

- Indecisiveness is a diagnostic criterion of MDD in DSM-5, reflected in difficulties in thinking, concentration, and decision-making. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p362]
- MDD has been associated with a shift from reflective and model-based decision-making to habitual and reflexive strategies utilizing minimal task representation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p363]
- MDD patients exhibit reward hyposensitivity, characterized by reduced emotional reactions to anticipated rewards and decreased likelihood to modulate behavior in the presence of reward information. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p363]
- Functional neuroimaging shows increased amygdala activation in response to negatively valenced cues in MDD, along with altered salience network (SN) activity including insula and dorsal anterior cingulate cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p364]
- The S allele of 5-HTTLPR polymorphism is associated with increased amygdala activation and with risk for depression in interaction with environmental adversity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p347]
- Val66Met BDNF polymorphism impairs activity-dependent BDNF signaling and has been associated with decreased hippocampal volume in Met carriers. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p347]

## Inferences

- Inference: The SN controls balance between the extrinsic mode network (EMN) and default mode network (DMN), with MDD associated with increased DMN dominance over EMN, related to biased SN engagement.
- Inference: Depressive mode may result in maladaptive responses under gain-maximization conditions but could be beneficial under loss-minimization conditions.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The genetic makeup of a patient moderates the relationship between stressors and clinical symptoms, but specific disease pathways remain to be identified.
