# Amateur Spirit

## Summary

The book's argument that amateurs—people who pursue work in the spirit of love rather than for fame or money—often have advantages over professionals. Amateurs are willing to try anything, experiment, follow whims, and are not afraid of making mistakes or looking ridiculous. The term 'amateur' comes from the French word meaning 'lover.'

## Key facts

- Kleon cites Charlie Chaplin: 'That's all any of us are: amateurs. We don't live long enough to be anything else.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p21]
- The word 'amateur' comes from the French word meaning 'lover,' someone who pursues work in the spirit of love regardless of potential for fame, money, or career. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p21]
- Amateurs have little to lose, are willing to try anything and share results, take chances, experiment, and follow whims. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p21]
- Kleon cites Zen monk Shunryu Suzuki: 'In the beginner's mind, there are many possibilities. In the expert's mind, there are few.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p22]
- Clay Shirky is quoted: 'The stupidest possible creative act is still a creative act. On the spectrum of creative work, the difference between the mediocre and the good is vast. The real gap is between doing nothing and doing something.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p22]
- Amateurs make a point of learning in the open so others can learn from their failures and successes. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p22]
- Thom Yorke is cited as saying his greatest strength is 'That I don't know what I'm doing,' and like Tom Waits, he picks up instruments he doesn't know to write with when songwriting gets too comfortable. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p24]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book argues that maintaining an amateur mindset throughout one's career leads to continued growth and creative risk-taking.
