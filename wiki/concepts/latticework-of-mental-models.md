# Latticework of Mental Models

## Summary

The latticework of mental models is a concept popularized by Charlie Munger representing an interconnected network of theoretical frameworks, concepts, and principles from various disciplines. Building such a latticework is superior to memorizing isolated facts because it enables better understanding, prediction, and application of knowledge across different situations.

## Key facts

- The more we know, the more information hooks we have to connect new information to, making it easier to form long-term memories. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p111]
- Building a latticework of theories and mental models to which information can stick creates a virtuous circle where learning facilitates learning. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p112]
- The slip-box builds the same connections in our heads while developing them in the slip-box, making facts easier to remember because they have a latticework to attach to. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p111]
