# Adult Neurogenesis

## Summary

Adult neurogenesis is the production of new neurons from resident stem cells in the adult brain. Contrary to long-held dogma, neurogenesis continues throughout life in specific brain regions including the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus and the subventricular zone (which supplies neurons to the olfactory bulb). These new neurons arise from astrocytic stem cells, extend processes, form synapses, and integrate into functional circuits. Adult neurogenesis can be modulated by environment, exercise, and disease states.

## Key facts

- New neurons are generated throughout life in the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb, arising from resident neural stem cells that can integrate into functional circuits. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1296]
- Neural stem cells in the adult brain are derived from radial glia and remain quiescent near the ventricular surface until activated to re-enter the cell cycle. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1296]
- Adult neurogenesis in the dentate gyrus can be decreased in animal models of depression and chronic stress, and increased by environmental enrichment or physical exercise. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1296]
- Neuroblasts generated in the subventricular zone migrate to the olfactory bulb via chain migration along astrocytes, differentiating into granule cells and periglomerular cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1298]
- Neurogenesis continues into adulthood in the dentate gyrus of the hippocampus and the olfactory bulb of rodents, nonhuman primates, and humans. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1347]
- Neurons generated in the adult subventricular zone migrate rostrally through the rostral migratory stream to populate the olfactory bulb. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1348]
- Neural stem cells, embryonic stem cells, and induced pluripotent stem (iPS) cells can give rise to neurons and oligodendrocytes for potential cell replacement therapies. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1350]
- Generation of new neurons can be stimulated by traumatic or ischemic injury in areas such as the cerebral cortex where neurogenesis normally fails to occur. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1351]
- Joseph Altman at MIT provided the first evidence for adult neurogenesis in 1965 using radioactive tagging of newly synthesized DNA. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p150]
- Altman and Kaplan's findings were rejected by senior neuroscientists including Pasko Rakic, who famously told Kaplan 'Those may look like neurons in New Mexico, but they don't in New Haven'. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p150]
- Fernando Nottebohm at Rockefeller University provided crucial evidence for adult neurogenesis in birds studying birdsong. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p150]
- Elizabeth Gould at Princeton and Fred Gage at Salk Institute provided convincing evidence of adult neurogenesis in mammals. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p150]
- Pasko Rakic eventually accepted adult neurogenesis after initially rejecting it. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p150]
- Hippocampal neurogenesis is enhanced by learning, exercise, estrogen, antidepressants, environmental enrichment, and brain injury. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p151]
- New hippocampal neurons integrate into preexisting circuits with the perky excitability of young neurons in the perinatal brain. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p151]
- New hippocampal neurons are essential for 'pattern separation'—learning that two things previously thought identical are actually different. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p151]
- Adult neurogenesis was discovered in rats by Altman and Das in 1965 using autoradiographic and histological methods. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Neurogenesis in adult canaries was demonstrated in vocal control nuclei by Goldman and Nottebohm in 1983. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Continuation of neurogenesis in the hippocampus of adult macaque monkeys was confirmed by Kempermann and Rakic in 1999. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Neurogenesis in adult human hippocampus was demonstrated by Eriksson et al. in 1998. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Sleep deprivation inhibits adult neurogenesis in the hippocampus by elevating glucocorticoids. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- New neurons in the adult brain are recruited into functional circuits. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Adult hippocampal neurogenesis affects learning and memory formation. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]
- Running enhances neurogenesis, learning, and long-term potentiation in mice. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p764]
- Hippocampal neurogenesis is required for the behavioral effects of antidepressants. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p764]
- Voluntary exercise alters the cytoarchitecture of the adult dentate gyrus by increasing cellular proliferation, dendritic complexity, and spine density. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p763]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery of adult neurogenesis has influenced research on brain repair by demonstrating that neurons can be generated and integrated into existing circuits, suggesting potential for therapeutic stimulation of endogenous precursors.
- Inference: The acceptance of adult neurogenesis after decades of rejection illustrates how scientific dogma can delay recognition of discoveries, particularly when established researchers cannot replicate findings with newer techniques
- Inference: The psychological importance of neuroplasticity is that it makes tangible the fact that brains change, supporting the idea that people can change
- Inference: The requirement of hippocampal neurogenesis for antidepressant effects suggests potential therapeutic targets for mood disorders

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The functional significance of adult neurogenesis in humans remains debated, and it is unclear whether the rate is sufficient for significant tissue repair following injury or disease.
- Uncertain: The exact reasons for the long delay in accepting adult neurogenesis remain debated—ranging from legitimate quality control concerns about early methods to the inability of established researchers to accept findings they couldn't replicate
