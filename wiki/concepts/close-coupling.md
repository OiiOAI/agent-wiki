# Close Coupling

## Summary

Close coupling is one of the subtlest sources of advantage, especially a new inventive coupling of activities not previously combined. When facing a tough competitor, the crux often lies in reaching a new market through coupling existing technology with something else the competitor won't immediately imitate. Examples include GE's turbofan engines combining engineering, materials, and construction; Steve Jobs's iPhone coupling hardware and software deeper than elsewhere; Amazon's coupling of online shopping with efficient warehousing and logistics; and the Wright brothers' coupling of aerodynamic intuition, lightweight airframe construction, and gasoline engine design. To create advantage, one must bring together skills and ideas with different knowledge or experience bases that have not yet been combined.
