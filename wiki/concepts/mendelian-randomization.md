# Mendelian Randomization

## Summary

Mendelian randomization uses genetic variants as instrumental variables to establish causal relationships. Genes are randomized at conception, providing a natural instrument free from confounding.

## Key facts

- Mendelian randomization exploits genes as instrumental variables because genes are randomized at conception, equivalent to random assignment. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L2355-L2370]
- A 2012 study by Kathiresan used Mendelian randomization to show that HDL ('good' cholesterol) has no observable benefit against heart attacks, while LDL has a large effect. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L2370-L2385]
- The study found decreasing LDL by 34 mg/dl would reduce heart attack risk by about 50 percent. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L2385-L2395]
- A caveat noted is that Mendelian randomization reflects lifetime LDL effects, while statins only lower LDL from the day of administration, potentially overestimating statin benefits. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L2395-L2410]

## Inferences

- Inference: Mendelian randomization provides a powerful bridge between observational associations and causal conclusions, addressing confounding without experimental intervention.
