# Internal Boundary Problems

## Summary

Internal Boundary Problems refer to areas where individuals cannot set limits on themselves, including overeating, money mismanagement, time management issues, failure to complete tasks, uncontrolled speech, sexual boundarylessness, and substance abuse. These problems stem from various root causes and cannot be solved through willpower alone; they require relationship with God and others.

## Key facts

- Overeating serves as a false boundary for some people, used either to avoid intimacy by gaining weight or to obtain false closeness through binge eating. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p218]
- Money problems include impulse spending, careless budgeting, living beyond means, credit problems, chronically borrowing from friends, and enabling others financially. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p218]
- Time out-of-control stems from omnipotence (unrealistic expectations), overresponsibility for others' feelings, lack of realistic anxiety about planning, and rationalization of lateness. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p219]
- Task completion problems arise from resistance to structure, fear of success, lack of follow-through, distractibility, inability to delay gratification, and inability to say no to other pressures. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p220]
- The tongue can be a source of curse when used to talk nonstop to hide from intimacy, dominate conversations, gossip, make sarcastic remarks, threaten, flatter falsely, or seduce. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p221]
- Sexual boundarylessness becomes a tyrant demanding and insatiable; individuals feel deeply isolated and shameful, preventing them from seeking help in relationship. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p223]
- Alcohol and substance dependencies create devastating effects including divorce, job loss, financial havoc, medical problems, and death—clearest examples of internal boundary problems. [raw/books/business/Boundaries - Henry Cloud & John Townsend.pdf#p224]
