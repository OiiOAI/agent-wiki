# Pareto Overlap

## Summary

The Pareto Overlap is an advanced Profit First technique that connects the 80/20 rule applied to clients with the 80/20 rule applied to products/services. It identifies which clients purchase the most profitable offerings versus which clients consistently buy low-margin products, enabling strategic decisions about client retention and service optimization.

## Key facts

- The Pareto Principle states that 20 percent of clients yield 80 percent of revenue, and 80 percent of profit is derived from 20 percent of products/services. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p161]
- The top quartile of clients generates 89 percent of total revenue while the lowest quartile accounts for only 1 percent of revenue. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p159]
- Each group of clients requires pretty much the same amount of effort (cost and time), meaning serving low-revenue clients costs as much as serving high-revenue clients. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p160]
- The bottom quartile of clients, generating only 1 percent of revenue, results in a profit loss of 50 percent. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p160]
- The profits generated from top clients are used, in part, to pay for losses accrued in serving bottom clients. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p160]

## Inferences

- Inference: Firing the bottom quartile of unprofitable clients would eliminate 50 percent of losses while only losing 1 percent of revenue.
