# Ronald Burt's Structural Theory of Action

## Summary

Ronald Burt's structural theory of action develops a perspective that 'circumvents the schism between atomistic and normative action,' bridging these two views intellectually. In this theory, actors find themselves in a social structure that defines their social similarities, which pattern their perceptions of advantages to be gained by alternative actions. Social structure also differentially constrains actors in their ability to take actions. Actions taken are a joint function of actors pursuing their interests to the limit of their ability, where both interests and ability are patterned by social structure. Importantly, actions taken under structural constraint can modify social structure itself, creating new constraints.

## Key facts

- Burt develops a perspective that 'circumvents the schism between atomistic and normative action,' one that 'is less a synthesis of the existing two perspectives on action than it is a third view intellectually bridging the two views.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p332]
- In Burt's theory, actors find themselves in a social structure that defines their social similarities, which in turn pattern their perceptions of the advantages to be had by taking each of several alternative actions. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p333]
- Burt's criterion for marginal evaluation is an actor's status/role-set as generated by the division of labor: 'An actor evaluates the utility of alternative actions partly in regard to his personal conditions and partly in regard to the conditions of others.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p332]
- Burt's premise states: 'Actions taken under social structural constraint can modify social structure itself, and these modifications have the potential to create new constraints to be faced by actors within the structure.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p333]

## Inferences

- Inference: Burt's structural theory offers an integrative alternative to both atomistic (rational choice) and normative (cultural) sociological approaches, allowing for structural constraint while preserving agency.
