# The World Navel

## Summary

The World Navel is the center of the symbolic circle of the universe—the Immovable Spot of the Buddha legend around which the world revolves. It is the point through which energies of eternity break into time. The miracle of the flow of life into the world may be represented as circulation of food substance, streaming of energy, or manifestation of grace. The hero as incarnation of God is himself the navel of the world.

## Key facts

- The World Navel is the center of the symbolic circle of the universe, the Immovable Spot around which the world may be said to revolve. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p36]
- Beneath this spot is the earth-supporting head of the cosmic serpent, the dragon, symbolical of the waters of the abyss, the divine life-creative energy and substance of the demiurge. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p36]
- The hero as the incarnation of God is himself the navel of the world, the umbilical point through which the energies of eternity break into time. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p36]
- The World Navel is ubiquitous; any blade of grass may assume, in myth, the figure of the savior and conduct the questing wanderer into the sanctum sanctorum of his own heart. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p38]
- The dome of heaven rests on the quarters of the earth; the hearth in the home, the altar in the temple, is the hub of the wheel of the earth, the womb of the Universal Mother. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p37]
