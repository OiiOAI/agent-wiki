# Psychical (or Mental) Treatment

## Summary

Psychical or mental treatment denotes treatment taking its start in the mind, using measures that operate immediately upon the human mind. The primary tool is words. The paper traces the history of mental treatment from antiquity through hypnotic methods to Freudian psychoanalysis, emphasizing that mental treatment influences somatic processes through affects, concentration of attention, expectation, and faith. The text predates Freud's fully developed psychoanalytic technique and discusses hypnotic and cathartic methods.
