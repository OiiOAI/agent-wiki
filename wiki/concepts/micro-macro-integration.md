# Micro-Macro Integration

## Summary

Micro-macro integration refers to the theoretical movement, largely in American sociology beginning in the 1980s, away from micro-macro extremism toward integrating micro and macro theories and levels of social analysis. While structural functionalism, conflict theory, and neo-Marxian theory represented macro-extreme positions, symbolic interactionism, ethnomethodology, exchange theory, and rational choice theory represented micro-extreme positions. The integration movement seeks to bridge these divides through various theoretical frameworks including Ritzer's integrated sociological paradigm, Alexander's multidimensional sociology, Collins's micro foundations approach, and Elias's figurational sociology.
