# Punishment Dreams

## Summary

Punishment dreams constitute a class of dreams that are hard to reconcile with the theory of wish fulfillment. Unlike wish-fulfillment dreams, these dreams contain distressing content and appear to side with self-criticism rather than unlawful wishes. Freud proposes that their foundation is formed by an exaggeratedly ambitious phantasy, but humiliating thoughts that 'pour cold water' on this phantasy find their way into the dream instead. Masochistic impulses may be responsible for this reversal. Freud suggests distinguishing these as 'punishment dreams' without implication for the broader theory.

## Inferences

- Inference: After the division of the personality into ego and superego, punishment dreams can be recognized as fulfillments of the wishes of the superego.
- Inference: Even in punishment dreams, a wish fulfillment is present—the underlying wish may be for youth, immortality, or the removal of an obstacle.
