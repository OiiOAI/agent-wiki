# Inscape

## Summary

Inscape is a concept developed by Gerard Manley Hopkins to represent the unique quality of a thing, person, place or event—the 'thisness' (haeccitas) of things following Duns Scotus. It captures what Hopkins called 'that taste of myself, of I and me above and in all things, which is more distinctive than the taste of ale or alum'. Inscape is sustained by 'instress', an energy that holds it together.

## Key facts

- Hopkins coined the term inscape to represent the unique quality of a thing, person, place or event. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p522]
- He coined instress to represent the energy that sustained inscape, something akin to authentic Dasein. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p522]
- Hopkins was a passionate observer of things as they are: 'moonlight hanging or dropping on treetops like a blue cobweb'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p522]
- He saw that the ground of beauty was sameness within difference, and difference within sameness. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p523]
