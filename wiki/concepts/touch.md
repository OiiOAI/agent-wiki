# Touch

## Summary

Touch is the characteristic paradoxical phenomenon of infinite play, referring to genuine encounter between persons as whole beings. Touch is reciprocal: one cannot touch another without being touched in response. It occurs from one's center, spontaneously and originally, in contrast to being moved by external forces. Touching heals rather than cures; it restores persons to their center where freedom is not compromised. Sexual touching is original to culture, not derivative of society, and represents a drama of origins.

- Touching is always reciprocal; you cannot touch me unless I touch you in response. [raw/books/philosophy/Finite and Infinite Games_ A Vision of Life as Play and - Carse, James.pdf#p100]
