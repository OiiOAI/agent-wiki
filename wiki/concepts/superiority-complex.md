# Superiority Complex

## Summary

A superiority complex develops when someone suffering from inferiority feelings lacks the courage to compensate through healthy striving, and also cannot tolerate the inferiority complex. The person then compensates by fabricating a sense of superiority through behaviors like boasting, giving authority to others, or even bragging about their own misfortunes. At its root lies intense feelings of inferiority.

- A superiority complex is a fabricated feeling of superiority, not genuine confidence. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p88]
- Adler stated: 'The one who boasts does so only out of a feeling of inferiority.'. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p91]
- People who boast about their misfortunes are trying to be 'special' through their experience of misfortune and place themselves above others. [raw/books/psychology/The courage to be disliked_ the Japanese phenomenon that - Ichiro Kishimi, and Fumitake Koga.pdf#p91]
