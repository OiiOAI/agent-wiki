# Dream Displacement

## Summary

Displacement is a mechanism identified in dream analysis whereby psychical emphasis shifts from significant to indifferent ideas. The original intense cathexis is transferred through intermediate associations, allowing the indifferent material to achieve sufficient strength to enter consciousness. This process explains why trivial experiences often appear in dream content.

## Key facts

- Freud describes displacement as 'a kind of psychical emphasis' wherein 'ideas which originally had only a weak charge of intensity take over the charge from ideas that were originally more intensely cathected.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1442]
- The botanical monograph dream demonstrates how an indifferent impression (seeing a book in a shop window) becomes the manifest content through links to significant material (the conversation about cocaine with Dr Königstein). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1440]
- Intermediate links like the names Flora (a patient) and Gärtner (a colleague) forge the connection between indifferent and significant impressions. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1441]
- Dream displacement results in the dream content being 'differently centred' from the dream thoughts—elements that are central to the dream thoughts may not appear at all, while trivial elements become prominent. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1532]
- Displacement occurs through a transference of psychical intensities from important to trivial elements, creating new values for elements that had low psychical value in the dream thoughts. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1560]
- Freud attributed dream displacement to the censorship imposed by one psychical agency over another—it is one of the chief methods by which dream distortion is achieved. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1561]
- Dreams feel free to represent any element by its wishful contrary, so that there is no way of deciding at first glance whether any element is present in the dream thoughts as a positive or negative. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1569]

## Inferences

- Inference: Displacement operates because the censorship cannot be openly challenged; instead, the significant material is smuggled past censorship under the guise of innocent associations.
- Inference: Displacement allows unconscious wishes to bypass the censorship by attaching their intensity to harmless or irrelevant material.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud acknowledges that 'displacement' may be a pathological-seeming process but argues it represents a primary rather than pathological psychical mechanism.
