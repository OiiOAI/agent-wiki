# Fixed-Schedule Productivity

## Summary

Fixed-schedule productivity is a strategy where you fix a firm goal of not working past a certain time, then work backward to find productivity strategies that allow you to satisfy this declaration. The approach shifts you into a scarcity mindset, making your default answer to new obligations "no" and forcing ruthless efficiency in organizing efforts that do pass these obstacles.

## Key facts

- Fixed-schedule productivity involves fixing a firm goal of not working past a certain time and working backward to find strategies that allow you to satisfy this declaration. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p139]
- Cal Newport has practiced fixed-schedule productivity for more than half a decade, not working at night and rarely on weekends. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p139]
- Between 2011 and 2014, Newport published around twenty peer-reviewed articles, won two competitive grants, and published books while avoiding grueling schedules. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p140]
- The scarcity mindset created by fixed-schedule productivity makes your default answer become no, the bar for gaining access to your time and attention rises, and you organize efforts with ruthless efficiency. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p142]
- Fixed-schedule productivity works because practitioners are asymmetric in culling—ruthlessly reducing shallow work while preserving deep work, freeing up time without diminishing new value generated. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p142]
- During 2013-2014, Newport more than doubled his average academic productivity, publishing nine peer-reviewed papers while writing this book and maintaining his prohibition on evening work. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p154]
