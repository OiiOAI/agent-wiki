# Essential Intent

## Summary

An essential intent is both inspirational and concrete, both meaningful and measurable. It is one decision that settles one thousand later decisions. An essential intent guides the greater sense of purpose and helps chart life's path by eliminating competing priorities.

## Key facts

- An essential intent is both inspirational and concrete, both meaningful and measurable. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p118]
- An essential intent is one decision that settles one thousand later decisions, like deciding to become a doctor instead of a lawyer. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p118]
- To craft an essential intent, ask: 'If we could be truly excellent at only one thing, what would it be?'. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p120]
