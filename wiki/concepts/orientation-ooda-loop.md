# Orientation (Boyd's OODA Loop)

## Summary

Orientation is the key phase in Boyd's OODA loop, functioning as the 'Schwerpunkt' or center of gravity. It is made up of genetic heritage, cultural tradition, experience, and unfolding circumstances, and shapes how we observe, decide, and act. Orientation is the genetic code of an organism or organization.

## Key facts

- Orientation shapes the way we interact with the environment; it determines how and what we observe, decide and act. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Orientation is made up of genetic heritage, cultural tradition, experience, and unfolding circumstances. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Boyd describes orientation as the 'genetic code' of an organism or organization and the 'Schwerpunkt' for any command concept. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- The present OODA loops shape the character of future orientation; orientation feeds forward and backward. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p286]
- Boyd insists on having a repertoire of relevant schemata combined with the capability to validate them before and during operations and devise new ones. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p287]

## Inferences

- Inference: Orientation is central to Boyd's theory because it is through orientation patterns that individuals and organizations make sense of the uncertain environment and generate appropriate responses.
