# Nachträglichkeit

## Summary

The concept that impressions from the pre-sexual period which produced no effect on the child attain traumatic power at a later date as memories, when the individual has acquired an understanding of sexual life. Freud applied this to explain why Katharina's early experiences of her uncle's sexual advances (which she did not understand at age fourteen) became traumatic only after the scene of discovery at age sixteen, when she suddenly understood their significance.

## Key facts

- Freud observed that in every analysis of hysteria based on sexual traumas, impressions from the pre-sexual period which produced no effect on the child attain traumatic power at a later date as memories, when the girl has acquired an understanding of sexual life. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p657]
- In Katharina's case, the experiences at fourteen (her uncle's advances) did not become traumatic until she was sixteen and discovered him in intercourse, when she suddenly connected the new impression with the old recollections, understood them, and at the same time fended them off. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p655]
- Freud doubted whether a splitting of consciousness due to ignorance is really different from one due to conscious rejection, and whether adolescents do not possess sexual knowledge far oftener than supposed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p657]
- Nachträglichkeit describes how childhood appears to adults as a happy time without wishes, an idealization that obscures the child's actual struggles. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3560]
- The concept explains how experiences from one period are only fully elaborated or traumatic when interpreted by later psychic material. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3560]
- In Leonardo's case, Freud argues that his illegitimate birth and mother's tenderness profoundly disturbed his development through the mechanism of Nachträglichkeit. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3563]
