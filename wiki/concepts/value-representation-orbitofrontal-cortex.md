# Value representation in orbitofrontal cortex

## Summary

Neurons in the orbitofrontal cortex (OFC) encode the subjective value subjects assign to different goods during economic choice. This representation is invariant to changes in menu (what other goods are available) and independent of sensory-motor contingencies of choice.

## Key facts

- Neurons in OFC encode the value subjects assign to the available goods during economic choice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p443]
- The representation of value in OFC is menu invariant - neuronal responses encoding the value of one particular good do not depend on what other goods are available at the same time. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p445]
- Neurons in OFC represent value independently of the visuo-motor contingencies of choice - they encode the value of the chosen juice per se, independently of the action used to indicate the choice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p444]
