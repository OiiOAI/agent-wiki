# Dialectics

## Summary

Dialectics in DBT refers to a philosophical approach that views reality as composed of opposing forces and seeks synthesis between opposites. A dialectical stance involves seeing truth as constantly in flux, looking for the whole picture, and moving toward synthesis rather than absolute truth. It is essential for walking a middle path and decreasing isolation, conflict, and polarities in relationships.

## Key facts

- A dialectical stance means seeing truth as constantly in flux rather than fixed, and moving toward synthesis rather than holding onto absolute truth. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p150]
- Dialectics helps decrease a sense of isolation, conflict, and polarities in relationships. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p150]
- Wise Mind is the middle path between opposites such as reasonable mind and emotion mind, doing mind and being mind, and self-denial and self-indulgence. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p74]
- Dialectics tells us that two things that seem like opposites can both be true simultaneously. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p152]
- Dialectics teaches that the universe is filled with opposing sides and that there is always more than one way to see a situation and solve a problem. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p174]
- Dialectics teaches that everything and every person is connected in some way. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p174]
- Dialectics teaches that change is the only constant and that reality itself changes with each moment. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p174]
- Dialectics teaches that change is transactional: what we do influences our environment and other people, and they influence us. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p174]
- Thinking and acting dialectically involves looking for both sides, letting go of extremes, balancing opposites, embracing confusion, and playing devil's advocate. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p175]
