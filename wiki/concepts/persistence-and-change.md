# Persistence and Change

## Summary

The central theme of the book, framed by the French proverb 'plus ça change, plus c'est la même chose' (the more something changes, the more it remains the same). The authors argue that persistence and change need to be considered together despite their apparently opposite nature. Western philosophy has tended to develop theories of persistence OR theories of change, but not theories of both. When observing persistent problems despite desire and effort to change them, two questions arise equally: 'How does this undesirable situation persist?' and 'What is required to change it?'
