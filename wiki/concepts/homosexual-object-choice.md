# Homosexual Object Choice

## Summary

Freud outlines the psychical processes connected with the origin of homosexuality, identifying several contributing factors: fixation on the mother, narcissistic object choice, the high value set upon the male organ and inability to tolerate its absence, and regard for the father. He introduces a new mechanism: homosexual object choice originating in rivalry overcome and aggressive impulses that have become repressed. This new mechanism contrasts with paranoia, where the loved person becomes the hated persecutor, whereas here hated rivals are transformed into love objects.

## Key facts

- Freud lists the established factors in homosexual object choice: fixation on the mother, narcissistic object choice, high value on the male organ, and fear of the father. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5804]
- A new mechanism is identified: in some cases, intense jealousy against older brothers led to hostile and aggressive impulses that could reach the pitch of death wishes, but under the influences of upbringing yielded to repression and underwent transformation so that rivals became the first homosexual love objects. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5804]
- This new mechanism represents a complete contrast to the development of persecutory paranoia, where the person who has before been loved becomes the hated persecutor, whereas here the hated rivals are transformed into love objects. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5805]
- In homosexuality with marked social interests, it appears that the detachment of social feeling from object choice has not been fully carried through. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5806]

## Inferences

- Inference: The social drive impulses developed in homosexuals may arise from the same process as social drives in general—both originate from jealous and hostile impulses that cannot achieve satisfaction, with affectionate and social feelings arising as reactive formations against repressed aggressive impulses.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud cannot say how large a part this new mechanism plays in the formation of extreme, manifest and exclusive type of homosexuality.
