# The Law of Gender Rigidity

## Summary

The Law of Gender Rigidity states that all people possess both masculine and feminine qualities—some genetic and some from the influence of the opposite-sex parent. However, to present a consistent identity in society, people tend to repress these qualities, overidentifying with expected masculine or feminine roles. This rigidity causes people to lose valuable dimensions of character, makes thinking and action rigid, and damages relationships with the opposite sex. The path to power lies in exploring the middle range between masculine and feminine, playing against expectations, and reconnecting with repressed qualities. The original human nature incorporated more qualities from both parents and opposite-sex traits that are biologically part of us.

## Key facts

- All people have masculine and feminine qualities—some genetic and some from the influence of the parent of the opposite sex. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p393]
- People tend to repress qualities and overidentify with expected gender roles to present a consistent identity. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p393]
- Boys lose their rich range of emotions and repress natural empathy in the struggle to develop masculine identity. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p408]
- Girls are expected to be nice, smiling, deferential, and sacrifice assertive sides, internalizing critical voices about their worth. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p412]
- Power lies in exploring the middle range between masculine and feminine and playing against people's expectations. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p408]
- Masculine thinking tends toward focusing on contrasts, categorizing, linear sequence, and emotional detachment. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p430]
- Feminine thinking focuses on the whole, organic process, hidden connections, and intuitive insights. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p430]

## Inferences

- Inference: Greene suggests that gender role rigidity is a universal human phenomenon across cultures and time periods, though specific expectations vary
