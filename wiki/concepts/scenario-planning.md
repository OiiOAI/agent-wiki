# Scenario Planning

## Summary

Scenario planning is a mental time-travel technique that involves identifying possible futures resulting from a decision and estimating their probabilities. Rather than assuming a single outcome, this approach acknowledges uncertainty by considering the full range of possible outcomes, their likelihoods, and the strategic responses each would require. The method is used by poker players, military planners, and businesses to improve decision quality.

## Key facts

- Scenario planning involves two steps: imagining the range of potential futures and making best-guess estimates of each future's probability. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1796-L1798]
- Nate Silver advocates scenario planning as a way to consider a broad range of possibilities for how the future might unfold to guide long-term planning. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1801-L1804]
- Expert poker players use scenario planning by considering each opponent's possible responses (fold, call, raise), their likelihood, and subsequent decisions, often planning several moves ahead. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1814-L1818]
- A nonprofit called After-School All-Stars improved grant prioritization by calculating expected value (grant amount × probability of winning), discovering that smaller grants with higher win rates were worth more than large grants with low probabilities. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1833-L1846]
- The D-Day Normandy invasion represents a monumental historical example of scenario planning, with extensive preparation for multiple contingencies. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2078-L2088]
- Nate Silver developed 14 different scenarios for possible Trump presidency outcomes using scenario planning methodology. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2078-L2088]
- Scenario planning involves reconnaissance—gathering information about possible futures—to inform present decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2078-L2088]
- The approach acknowledges uncertainty while enabling proactive preparation rather than passive prediction. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2078-L2088]

## Inferences

- Inference: Scenario planning protects against hindsight bias by memorializing all considered futures, making it harder to treat the outcome that occurred as inevitable.
- Inference: Scenario planning differs from simple forecasting by embracing multiple possible futures rather than seeking a single prediction.
