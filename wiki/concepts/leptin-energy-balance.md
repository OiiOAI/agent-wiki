# Leptin and Energy Balance

## Summary

Leptin is a hormone produced by adipose tissue that regulates energy balance and food intake by acting on hypothalamic receptors. The discovery of leptin emerged from studies of obese (ob/ob) mice, and it acts by inhibiting medial hypothalamic neuropeptide Y systems. Leptin resistance contributes to obesity in humans.

## Key facts

- Leptin is a plasma protein encoded by the obese gene that reduces body weight. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p200]
- The weight-reducing effect of leptin is mediated via inhibition of medial hypothalamic neuropeptide Y systems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p200]
- Ob/ob mice lacking functional leptin are obese, but treatment with recombinant leptin corrects sterility in females. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p208]
