# Quantal Transmitter Release

## Summary

Quantal release is the fundamental unit of neurotransmitter release at chemical synapses, where transmitter is released in discrete packets (quanta) that produce postsynaptic potentials of fixed amplitude. Each quantum represents the contents of a single synaptic vesicle, and the total postsynaptic response is the sum of the responses to multiple released quanta.

## Key facts

- Transmitter is released in discrete amounts called quanta, each quantum producing a postsynaptic potential of fixed size called the quantal synaptic potential. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p378]
- Spontaneous miniature end-plate potentials of approximately 0.5 mV represent responses to the spontaneous release of small packets of ACh from presynaptic nerve terminals in the absence of an action potential. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p378]
- A miniature end-plate potential of 0.5 mV represents the synchronized release of approximately 5,000 molecules of ACh. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p379]
- The amplitude of each end-plate potential is an integral multiple of the unit potential, indicating that transmitter is released in fixed packets. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p379]
- The mean synaptic response E = n · p · a, where n is the number of releasable quanta, p is the probability of release, and a is the response size to a quantum. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p381]
