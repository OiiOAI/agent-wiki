# The Art of Success

## Summary

The Art of Success represents Boyd's most concise formulation of what constitutes winning in conflict. It synthesizes the destructive and constructive themes into two complementary imperatives: appearing as an unsolvable cryptogram while operating in a directed way to isolate and collapse the adversary; and shaping events to magnify one's spirit and strength while drawing others toward one's philosophy.

## Key facts

- The Art of Success advises to appear as an unsolvable cryptogram while operating in a directed way to penetrate adversary vulnerabilities and weaknesses in order to isolate him from his allies, pull him apart, and collapse his will to resist. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p241]
- Simultaneously, one should shape or influence events so that one not only magnifies spirit and strength but also influences potential adversaries and the uncommitted so they are drawn toward one's philosophy and are empathetic toward success. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p241]
- Boyd's 'Appropriate Bits and Pieces' include: compress own time and stretch-out adversary time; generate unequal distributions for local superiority; diminish own friction and magnify adversary friction; operate inside adversary's OODA loops; penetrate adversary organisms; and amplify spirit while draining adversary resolve. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p242]
- Boyd concluded that the central theme lies in evolving and exploiting insight/initiative/adaptability/harmony together with a unifying vision via a grand ideal, as basis to shape events and operate inside adversary's OODA loops to penetrate his moral-mental-physical being. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p242]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Art of Success represents Boyd's distillation of 2500 years of military history and strategic thought into a conceptually rich but very abstract formula that combines physical, mental, and moral dimensions of conflict.
