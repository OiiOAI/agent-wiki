# Constructal Law

## Summary

Constructal law, stated by Adrian Bejan, states that 'For a finite-size system to persist in time (to live), it must evolve in such a way that it provides easier access to the imposed (global) currents that flow through it.' The Flow System applies constructal law by viewing flow as a by-product of interactions where organizational structure removes inhibiting constraints.
