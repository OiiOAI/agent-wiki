# Phoneme

## Summary

A phoneme is the smallest unit of sound in a language that distinguishes meaning. Each language has a distinctive set of phonemes and rules for combining them. Phonetic units are distinguished by formant frequencies—systematic variations in vocal tract resonances. Categorical perception allows infants and fluent speakers to readily distinguish phonemic boundaries but not acoustic variations within a category.
