# Indexical Reference

## Summary

Indexical reference is an intermediate form of reference where a sign is connected to its referent through physical or temporal correlation (contiguity). Unlike iconic reference which depends on similarity, indexical reference depends on the recognition that two things have been repeatedly experienced together. Examples include alarm calls correlating with predators, smoke indicating fire, or symptoms indicating illness. Indexical reference is built upon iconic reference and is necessary for the development of symbolic reference.

## Key facts

- Indexical reference requires at least three iconic relationships: the indicating stimulus must resemble other instances, instances must correlate in space/time and resemble each other, and past correlations must themselves be recognized as similar. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p82]
- Vervet monkey alarm calls provide indexical reference because the calls co-occurred with predators during evolution, establishing a correlation-based relationship. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p57]
- Indexical reference depends on iconic reference—recognition of similarity between instances is required before their correlation can be noted. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p81]
- Indexical relationships require temporal and spatial correlation; when the correlation breaks down, the association is extinguished. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p87]
- In Boy Who Cried Wolf, the indexical function fails due to lack of real-wolf correlation, even though symbolic reference remains. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p87]
- Individual indexical associations are essentially independent; extinguishing one has little effect on others. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p87]
- Stimulus generalization and learning-set transfer are sophisticated forms of indexical association, based on iconic projection. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p86]
