# Brain Unmasking

## Summary

Brain unmasking is one of the main ways the plastic brain reorganizes itself. When key neural pathways are blocked or damaged, the brain uses older 'secondary' pathways to go around the block. These pathways, initially masked or dormant, become exposed with use and are strengthened. Bach-y-Rita compared this to finding alternate routes when a main bridge is out—first taking longer secondary roads, then discovering shorter paths as those roads are used more.

## Key facts

- Bach-y-Rita compared brain reorganization to driving when a main bridge is out—first taking secondary roads, then finding shorter paths. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p20]
- Unmasking is generally thought to be one of the main ways the plastic brain reorganizes itself. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p20]
