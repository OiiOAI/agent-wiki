# Smooth-Sailing Fallacy

## Summary

The smooth-sailing fallacy is the assumption that a lack of recent tremors and storms means there is no risk. This fallacy was institutionalized by the financial industry's doctrine of measuring risk by analyzing past vibrations in prices. A system with a critical design flaw does not necessarily display the flaw until it collapses, and this collapse is not due to 'black swans' but to foreseeable design flaws.
