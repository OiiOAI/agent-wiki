# Activating Strategies

## Summary

Activating strategies are thoughts and feelings that compel someone to seek closeness with their partner when the attachment system is activated. For people with anxious attachment, these include difficulty concentrating, idealizing the partner, believing this is their only chance for love, and believing they should stay despite unhappiness.

## Key facts

- Activating strategies are any thoughts or feelings that compel you to get close, physically or emotionally, to your partner. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p70]
- Once a partner responds in a way that reestablishes security, the person can revert back to their calm, normal self. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p70]
- Activating strategies include putting the partner on a pedestal while underestimating one's own talents and abilities. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p71]
- Anxious people may believe 'it's my only chance for love' and that 'it takes years to meet someone new'. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p71]
- Activating strategies include believing an unhappy relationship should continue because 'all couples have problems' or 'he can change'. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p71]
