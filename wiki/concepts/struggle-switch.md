# The Struggle Switch

## Summary

The struggle switch is a metaphorical switch at the back of the mind that, when switched on, causes vigorous struggle against emotional pain. When the switch is on, unpleasant feelings are perceived as huge problems, leading to anxiety about anxiety, sadness about anxiety, or anger about sadness—creating vicious cycles that amplify emotional pain. When the switch is off, whatever emotion shows up is acknowledged and allowed without struggle, allowing emotions to freely flow through, come and stay and go in their own time. The struggle switch is described as an 'emotion amplifier'—with it on, emotions are stuck and hang around long, extra suffering is created, and time and energy are wasted. With it off, emotions can flow freely, time and energy are invested in meaningful activities, and unnecessary pain is not created. The TAME technique and making room are methods for turning the struggle switch off.
