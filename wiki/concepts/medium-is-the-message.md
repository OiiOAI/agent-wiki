# The Medium Is the Message

## Summary

Marshall McLuhan's insight that the medium through which information is communicated shapes human perception as much as the content itself. Each new communication technology — the printed book, television, social media — acts like 'goggles' with specific colors and lenses that change how people see the world.

## Key facts

- McLuhan argued that when a new technology arrives, it should not be understood as a neutral pipe pouring in information — it actively transforms perception. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p91]
- The 'message' of a medium is the set of assumptions and worldviews embedded in its format before any specific content is consumed. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p91]
