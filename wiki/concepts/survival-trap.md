# The Survival Trap

## Summary

The Survival Trap describes the reactive cycle where struggling businesses make desperate decisions focused on immediate cash needs rather than long-term vision. Entrepreneurs caught in this trap accept any sale, work with bad clients, and take on unprofitable projects, which moves them further from their original business goals while creating larger problems.

## Key facts

- The Survival Trap occurs when entrepreneurs focus on getting out of immediate crisis rather than advancing toward their vision. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p37-39]
- Reactive crisis decisions often move businesses in the opposite direction from their stated goals. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p38]
- The Survival Trap makes it impossible to become efficient because crisis-mode justifies accepting work at any cost. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p39]
- Bank balance accounting contributes to the Survival Trap by creating reactive spending based on available cash rather than strategic priorities. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p39-40]

## Inferences

- Inference: Profit First escapes the Survival Trap by requiring regular allocations regardless of cash position, forcing proactive rather than reactive financial management
