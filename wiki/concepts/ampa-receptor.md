# AMPA Receptor

## Summary

The AMPA receptor is an ionotropic glutamate receptor that mediates fast excitatory synaptic transmission in the CNS. Most AMPA receptors are impermeable to Ca2+ due to RNA editing of the GluA2 subunit. AMPA receptors are tetrameric proteins composed of GluA1-GluA4 subunits.

## Key facts

- AMPA receptors mediate the early, fast component of excitatory synaptic currents with rapid rising and decaying phases. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p266]
- Most AMPA receptors have low Ca2+ permeability because the GluA2 subunit undergoes RNA editing that replaces a glutamine with arginine in the pore. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p271]
- The GluA2 gene is edited by an enzyme that converts a glutamine codon to an arginine codon in the mRNA. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p271]
- AMPA receptors are tetrameric proteins composed of four subunits (GluA1-GluA4), often heteromers of two different subunit types. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p268]
- AMPA receptors interact with TARP (transmembrane AMPA receptor regulatory protein) auxiliary subunits that are essential for their trafficking and localization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p273]
- AMPA receptors conduct both Na+ and K+ with nearly equal permeability, giving a reversal potential near 0 mV. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p264]

## Inferences

- Inference: RNA editing of GluA2 represents a crucial regulatory mechanism that protects neurons from Ca2+ toxicity.
- Inference: The AMPA receptor/TARP complex forms the core of fast excitatory synaptic transmission in the brain.
