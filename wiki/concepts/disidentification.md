# Disidentification

## Summary

Disidentification is the process of separating one's sense of self from the mind and its contents. The book states that the single most vital step toward enlightenment is learning to disidentify from the mind. This does not mean losing the ability to think, but no longer believing that one IS the thinking mind. When disidentification occurs, the false self (ego) collapses like an inflatable toy when the plug is pulled. The result is access to a 'vast realm of intelligence beyond thought' and the arising of consciousness in its pure state. The practice of watching the thinker is the primary method for achieving disidentification.

## Key facts

- The single most vital step on the journey toward enlightenment is 'learn to disidentify from your mind'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p32]
- When consciousness withdraws from identification with the unhappy self, 'this withdrawal must have been so complete that this false, suffering self immediately collapsed'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p19]
- Once you disidentify from the mind, 'whether you are right or wrong makes no difference to your sense of self at all'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p52]
- Disidentification allows one to realize 'a vast realm of intelligence beyond thought, that thought is only a tiny aspect of that intelligence'. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p29]
- Disidentification with the pain-body is achieved by observing it with presence rather than being controlled by it. [raw/books/psychology/The Power of Now - Tolle, Eckhart.pdf#p47]
