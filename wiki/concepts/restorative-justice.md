# Restorative Justice

## Summary

Restorative justice is an approach to justice that focuses on the harm caused by crime rather than strict legal rules, emphasizing rehabilitation, reconciliation, and the repair of harm to victims, communities, and offenders. Truth and Reconciliation Commissions (TRCs) are one implementation of restorative justice principles.
