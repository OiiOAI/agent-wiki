# Terrible Simplifications

## Summary

The term (from French 'terribles simplificateurs,' coined after the events of May 1968) describes the stance of denying that problems exist while attacking those who point them out or try to deal with them. This compound of denial and attack depends on gross simplification of interaction complexities. The term describes exponents of this stance. Consequences include: (a) acknowledging or attempting to solve the problem is defined as madness or badness; (b) the problem requiring change becomes compounded by problems created through its mishandling.
