# Tax-Efficient Investing

## Summary

Munger's quantitative demonstration that minimizing tax drag through long holding periods dramatically improves after-tax returns. Paying taxes only at the end of a 30-year period versus annually can yield over 3.5 percentage points additional annual return.

## Key facts

- An investment compounding at 15% for 30 years with a single 35% tax at the end yields 13.3% after tax; paying 35% annually yields only 9.75%—a difference exceeding 3.5 percentage points. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p201]
- With a 10% annual return over 30 years, paying 35% tax at the end yields 8.3% after-tax; paying annually yields 6.5%—nearly 2 percentage points difference. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p201]
- Sitting on great companies for long stretches gives a huge edge from the way income taxes work, adding one to three percentage points per annum compounded. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p202]
- Munger's advice: anytime somebody offers you a tax shelter, don't buy it; anytime anybody offers you anything with a big commission and 200-page prospectus, don't buy it. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p201]
