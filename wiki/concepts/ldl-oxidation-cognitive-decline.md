# LDL Oxidation and Cognitive Decline

## Summary

Oxidized low-density lipoprotein accumulates in vascular dementia and is inversely associated with cognitive performance. Oxidation of LDL occurs in vivo through infection and inflammation, and the oxidized form is particularly harmful to vascular health and brain function. This connects cardiovascular risk factors to neurodegenerative processes.

## Key facts

- Oxidized LDL modification is increased in vascular dementia and inversely associated with cognitive performance. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p463]
- Infection and inflammation induce LDL oxidation in vivo. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p463]
- LPS-binding protein circulates in association with ApoB-containing lipoproteins and enhances endotoxin-LDL/VLDL interaction. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p464]
- Dietary fat intake changes can alter plasma levels of oxidized LDL. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p463]

## Inferences

- Inference: Reducing systemic inflammation and oxidative stress may help prevent LDL oxidation, potentially reducing vascular dementia risk.
