# Erotic Blueprints

## Summary

Erotic blueprints are unconscious templates for desire shaped by childhood experiences with primary caregivers. These blueprints determine what turns a person on and off, how much closeness they can tolerate, and whether they can experience pleasure within emotional intimacy. The psychology of desire often lies buried in childhood memories, where we learn about pleasure, trust, receiving, and protection. What caused us the most pain in childhood sometimes becomes the greatest source of excitement later on, as the erotic mind ingeniously transforms and redresses past traumas.
