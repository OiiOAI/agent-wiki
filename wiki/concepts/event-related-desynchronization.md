# Event-Related Desynchronization (ERD)

## Summary

Event-Related Desynchronization (ERD) represents changes in EEG activity related to stimulus processing, specifically reductions in alpha band energy following stimulus presentation. Reductions in alpha band amplitude are associated with information processing, selective attention, and motor response preparation. Increased task complexity is accompanied by increased ERDs. ERD could potentially be used to determine whether an operator detected an SA-relevant stimulus, though its utility in complex task situations has been questioned.

## Key facts

- ERDs represent changes in EEG alpha band energy following stimulus presentation. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p182]
- Reductions in alpha band amplitude are associated with information processing, selective attention, and preparation for motor responses. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p182]
- Increases in task complexity are accompanied by increased ERDs. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p182]
- ERD could potentially determine whether an operator detected an SA-relevant stimulus. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p182]

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The utility of ERDs in complex task situations has been questioned (Fournier, Wilson, and Swain, 1999).
