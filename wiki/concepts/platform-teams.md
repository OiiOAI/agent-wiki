# Platform Teams

## Summary

Platform teams are one of the four team patterns in Team Topologies. They provide internal services that serve as platforms for stream-aligned teams, supporting all phases of development, delivery, and operations for multiple stream teams. Platform teams reduce load for stream-aligned teams, where stream-aligned teams are internal customers of the platform capabilities.

## Key facts

- Platform teams reduce load for stream-aligned teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
- Stream-aligned teams are internal customers of platform teams' capabilities. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
- Platform teams support all phases of development, delivery, and operations for multiple stream teams. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L432]
