# TIP Skills

## Summary

TIP skills are distress tolerance techniques designed to rapidly reduce extreme emotional arousal by changing body chemistry. The acronym stands for Temperature (cold water), Intense exercise, Paced breathing, and Paired muscle relaxation. These skills are particularly useful when emotional intensity is so high that other skills cannot be accessed.

## Key facts

- TIP stands for Temperature (cold water), Intense exercise, Paced breathing, and Paired muscle relaxation. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p353]
- Cold water (holding breath, putting face in cold water or using cold pack on eyes and cheeks) triggers the dive response, slowing heart rate and redirecting blood flow to brain and heart. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p354]
- Paced breathing involves breathing deeply into the belly, slowing to 5-6 breaths per minute, and exhaling more slowly than inhaling (e.g., 5 seconds in, 7 seconds out). [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p353]
- Paired muscle relaxation involves tensing muscles while inhaling, then saying 'Relax' and releasing tension while exhaling. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p353]
- Medical caution: cold water decreases heart rate rapidly; intense exercise increases it. Consultation with healthcare provider recommended for heart conditions, certain medications, or eating disorders. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p353]
