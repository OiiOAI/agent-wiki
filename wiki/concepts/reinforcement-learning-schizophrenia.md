# Reinforcement Learning in Schizophrenia

## Summary

Reinforcement learning models propose that schizophrenia symptoms—including delusions and anhedonia—emerge from dysregulation of brain systems involved in reward and punishment learning, particularly dopaminergic and glutamatergic circuits. Abnormal prediction-error signaling in the mesolimbic and mesostriatal pathways may cause aberrant attribution of incentive salience to irrelevant stimuli.

## Key facts

- Schizophrenia has a lifetime prevalence estimated at 0.7–1%. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p250]
- Dopamine dysfunction in the striatum can lead to abnormal associative learning processes which may contribute to psychotic symptoms. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p251]
- Gray and Hemsley proposed that disruption of latent inhibition and Kamin blocking in schizophrenia causes patients to attribute causal efficacy to stimuli that normal individuals would ignore. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p251]
- NMDA receptor hypofunction (via ketamine or PCP) produces schizophrenia-like symptoms in healthy subjects and worsens symptoms in patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p253]
- fMRI studies in psychosis patients show attenuated ventral striatal activation during reward anticipation, even in drug-naïve first-episode patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p255]
- Patients with psychosis show disrupted prediction-error signals in the dopaminergic midbrain, striatal and limbic regions, and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p255]

## Inferences

- Inference: Schizophrenia patients show deficits in reinforcement learning even before antipsychotic medication, suggesting that reward-processing abnormalities are intrinsic to the illness rather than iatrogenic.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise mechanism by which dopamine dysregulation produces specific delusions rather than generalized cognitive impairment remains uncertain.
- Uncertain: The relationship between the severity of reinforcement learning deficits and specific symptom dimensions (positive vs. negative symptoms) is not fully established.
