# Process-Centric Email

## Summary

Process-centric email is a strategy where, when faced with an interrogative email, you pause and identify the project the message represents and the most efficient process for bringing it to a successful conclusion. Instead of quick responses that bounce responsibility back to the sender, you reply with a message that describes the process, points out the current step, and emphasizes the next step.

## Key facts

- Interrogative emails generate an instinct to dash off quick responses that temporarily clear the inbox, but this responsibility bounces back repeatedly, sapping time and attention. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p146]
- The process-centric approach requires asking: 'What is the project represented by this message, and what is the most efficient process for bringing this project to a successful conclusion?'. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p146]
- Process-centric responses reduce both the number of emails received and the amount of mental clutter generated. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p147]
- Process-centric messages 'close the loop' with respect to projects, allowing the mind to reclaim mental real estate the project once demanded. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p148]

## Inferences

- Inference: The extra two to three minutes spent crafting process-centric responses saves many more minutes reading and responding to unnecessary extra messages later.
