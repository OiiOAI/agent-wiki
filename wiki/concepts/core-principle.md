# The CORE Principle

## Summary

The CORE Principle addresses the preparation to undertake a task. Commitment means checking that you have a real possibility of achieving what you set out to do, using the Commitment Screen to assess essential, significant, and desirable requirements plus hurdles, barriers, and pitfalls. Ownership means having your plan so you have either formed it yourself, had major say in it, or believe the plan is ideal for you. Responsibility introduces accountability and discipline in carrying out the plan. Excellence means setting very high but achievable goals and aiming for personal excellence, defined as doing your best.

## Key facts

- Motivation is Chimp-driven, based on emotion, and can shift quickly; commitment comes from the Human and does not depend on feelings—it means following a plan even if you do not feel like it that day. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p169]
- The Commitment Screen assesses what is needed for the job by dividing requirements into Essential (critical for the venture to succeed), Significant (will definitely influence the final outcome), and Desirable (may or may not help but make things more comfortable). [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p169]
- Challenges are divided into Hurdles (things with no getting round them, requiring a coping strategy), Barriers (things that can be gotten around with good planning), and Pitfalls (areas that could easily be your downfall if not looking out for them). [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p170]
- Having ownership of your plans always excites the Human and Chimp because it offers all kinds of rewards; following someone else's plans, no matter how good, without full agreement diminishes chances of success. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p173]
- Being organised is the easy bit; most people can sort out a plan but are then unable to discipline themselves to carry it out—responsibility is all about managing the Chimp and its feelings, and getting down to business without excuses. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p174]
- Personal excellence is defined as doing your best—you may still be disappointed with the level you achieve but you can hold your head up and say it was your best, and the Chimp is very likely to live with this. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p175]
- The biggest factor for success is for you to function at your best, practically and emotionally. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p172]
