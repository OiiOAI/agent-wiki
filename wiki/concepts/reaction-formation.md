# Reaction-Formation

## Summary

Reaction-formation is a technique used by the ego to reinforce individual acts of repression, usually by strengthening impulses that stand in an opposite relation to those that are to be repressed.

## Key facts

- The ego creates reaction-formations to reinforce individual acts of repression, usually by strengthening impulses that stand in an opposite relation to those that are to be repressed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7031]
- Reaction-formations against repressed drive impulses play no less a role in character formation than do the two early identifications with the father and with the mother. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7031]
- Repression of an initial predilection for dirt is replaced by a strong tendency towards over-cleanliness or by an aesthetic attitude in general. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7031]

## Inferences

- Inference: Reaction-formations contribute significantly to personality development, creating character traits that are essentially opposite to underlying unconscious impulses.
