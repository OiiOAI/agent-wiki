# Emotion Schema

## Summary

Emotion schema are nonconscious bodies of knowledge about emotions that help conceptualize situations involving challenges and opportunities. LeDoux proposes that emotions are cognitively assembled by pattern-completion of emotion schema activated by survival circuit activity, self-schema, and other lower-order factors. This builds on Lisa Barrett's work on constructed emotion.

## Key facts

- Emotion schema are nonconscious knowledge structures that provide templates for interpreting situations and constructing emotional experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p344]
- Barrett described emotions as 'conceptual acts' and emotions schema as 'ready-made frames' for interpreting present, remembering past, anticipating future. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p344]
- LeDoux proposes that a fear schema — accumulated memories of threats, harm, danger, and fear — provides a template for top-down conceptualization when threats are present. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p345]
- Emotion schema develop over time through personal experience, becoming more refined and differentiated as language provides conceptual categories. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p346]
- Emotion and self-schema overlap — one's fear schema is personally unique, sculpted by individual experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p346]

## Inferences

- Inference: Emotion schema provide the personal meaning that distinguishes fear from anxiety, dread from terror — the same threat may activate different schema elements in different people.
