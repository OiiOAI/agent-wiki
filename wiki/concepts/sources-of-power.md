# Sources of Power

## Summary

Gary Klein's 1998 book 'Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions' documents how implicit communications and what the Germans call Fingerspitzengefühl ('fingertip feel' or intuition) actually work. This research validates that experience-based decision-making under time pressure relies on mental simulations rather than rational analysis, which directly supports Boyd's concepts about rapid OODA loops and pattern recognition.
