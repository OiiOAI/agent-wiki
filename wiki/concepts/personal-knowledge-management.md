# Personal Knowledge Management

## Summary

Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) is the individual counterpart to Knowledge Management, which studies how organizations make use of their knowledge. PKM emerged in the 1990s to help university students handle the increased volume of information from Internet-connected libraries. The field helps individuals harness the full potential of what they know, using technology to extend and enhance natural cognitive abilities.

## Key facts

- PKM emerged in the 1990s to help university students handle information from Internet-connected libraries. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p11]
- More than half the workforce today can be considered 'knowledge workers' for whom knowledge is their most valuable asset. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p25]
- The author positions PKM alongside personal computers and personal finance as fundamental innovations for modern life. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p10]
