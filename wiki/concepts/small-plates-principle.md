# Small Plates Principle

## Summary

The Small Plates Principle applies the behavioral science of dietary portion control to business finance. Just as using smaller dinner plates naturally leads to eating less food without conscious dieting effort, limiting available business cash to specific account allocations naturally leads to more efficient operations. The principle is one of four core behavioral strategies underlying Profit First.

## Key facts

- American plate sizes grew 23% between 1900 and 2012, from 9.6 inches to 11.8 inches. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p52]
- The four health principles that inform Profit First are: use small plates, serve sequentially, remove temptation, and enforce a rhythm. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p52-53]
- When using smaller plates, people automatically consume fewer calories without changing their behavior of finishing what is served. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p49]

## Inferences

- Inference: The parallel between portion control and cash management works because both leverage Parkinson's Law: constrained supply naturally produces constrained consumption
