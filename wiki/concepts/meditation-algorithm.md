# The Meditation Algorithm

## Summary

A six-step iterative process for mindfulness meditation derived from the three elements (CCA). Steps: Notice the focus object, Label it, Allow awareness to deeply contact it, Feel acceptance toward it, Continue focusing/contacting/accepting for about 5 seconds, and Repeat.

## Key facts

- The meditation algorithm consists of six steps: notice, label, contact deeply, accept, continue for ~5 seconds, repeat. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p59]
- Steps 1 and 5 are concentration steps - placing attention on the object and maintaining contact. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p59]
- Step 2 is the labeling step, where you mentally repeat a word to help maintain focus. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p60]
- Step 3 is the sensory clarity step, where awareness deeply contacts the focus object to notice fine details. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p60]
- Step 4 is the acceptance step, trying to let go of reactivity and not judge or try to change the experience. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p60]
- Taft estimates one iteration of the algorithm takes approximately five seconds. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p66]
