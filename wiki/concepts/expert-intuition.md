# Expert Intuition

## Summary

A collaboration between Kahneman and Gary Klein (who usually defended intuition) established conditions for when expert intuition can be trusted. Valid intuitions require a regular environment with predictable patterns and adequate opportunity for learning through prolonged practice with rapid and clear feedback. Intuition is unreliable in unpredictable environments or when experts have not had sufficient opportunity to learn the relevant patterns.

## Key facts

- Kahneman and Gary Klein collaborated over seven to eight years to identify conditions under which intuitive expert judgment could be trusted, producing the paper 'Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p303]
- Valid expert intuition requires two basic conditions: a sufficiently regular environment that can be learned, and adequate opportunity to learn the regularities through prolonged practice. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p310]
- Gary Klein's recognition-primed decision (RPD) model describes how experts use pattern recognition to quickly identify a promising course of action and mentally simulate it to check viability, without comparing alternatives. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p305]
- Kahneman and Klein agreed that subjective confidence is not a reliable indicator of the validity of intuition; people can be highly confident in invalid intuitions that answer the wrong question through substitution. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p309]

## Inferences

- Inference: Chess, firefighting, and anesthesiology provide environments regular enough for genuine expertise, while long-term financial forecasting and political prediction occur in environments of near-zero validity where intuition is essentially unreliable.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The boundary between predictable and unpredictable environments is not precisely defined, leaving some cases of intuition in ambiguous domains without clear guidance.
