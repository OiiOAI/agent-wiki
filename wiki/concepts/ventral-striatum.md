# Ventral Striatum

## Summary

The ventral striatum is a key component of the reward circuit, serving as the main input structure of the ventral basal ganglia. It receives inputs from the ventral medial prefrontal cortex, orbitofrontal cortex, dorsal anterior cingulate cortex, amygdala, and hippocampus, and is critically involved in reward prediction, reward anticipation, and the transition from reward-based learning to habit formation.

## Key facts

- The ventral striatum contains a subterritory called the shell, which plays a particularly important role in goal-directed behaviors, behavioral sensitization, and changes in affective states. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p12]
- Calbindin is the most consistent marker for the shell across species, distinguishing it from the rest of the ventral striatum. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p12]
- The ventral striatum receives dense projections from the amygdala and hippocampus that are not present in the dorsal striatum, providing limbic-related inputs essential for reward processing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p9]
- Efferent projections from the ventral striatum terminate in the ventral pallidum, substantia nigra pars compacta, and ventral tegmental area, forming the striatonigrostriatal pathway that allows the ventral striatum to influence the dorsal striatum. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p9]
- The ventral striatum is involved not only in appetitive rewarding behavior but also in negative motivational behavior, controlling approach/avoidance behavior and anxiety-related disorders. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p5]
- Signals related to reward expectancy have been abundantly found in striatum and many subregions of prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p163]
- The ventral striatum is commonly activated by both the concrete reward of money and the more abstract social reward of good reputation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p201]
- The ventral striatum is activated when ratings matched with group ratings during social conformity tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p204]
- A subset of striatal neurons were sensitive to reward inequity, coding either disadvantageous inequity or advantageous inequity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p183]

## Inferences

- Inference: Through the striatonigrostriatal pathway organized in an ascending spiral, the ventral striatum progressively influences dorsal striatal regions during learning and habit formation.
- Inference: The convergence of cortical inputs from reward-related and dorsal prefrontal regions in the rostral striatum may serve as hubs for integrating motivational, reward, and cognitive control information.
- Inference: Disruption of the ventral striatal pathway may induce not only psychiatric symptoms but also abnormal value-based decision-making.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise mechanisms by which the shell compartment distinctively processes goal-directed versus habit-based behaviors require further investigation.
