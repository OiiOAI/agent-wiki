# Muscle Traps

## Summary

Muscle traps are psychomotor gumption traps related to understanding which affects what happens to the machine. The most frustrating is inadequate tools; other traps include bad surroundings (poor lighting, extreme temperatures), and muscular insensitivity. 'Mechanic's feel' is the deep kinesthetic sense for the elasticity of materials that prevents damage. Understanding the elasticity ranges—finger-tight, snug, and tight—of different metals (steel, cast iron, brass, aluminum) prevents stripped threads or broken assemblies.

## Key facts

- Muscle traps are psychomotor traps related to understanding which directly affects what happens to the machine. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p276]
- The most frustrating muscle trap is inadequate tools; good tools don't wear out and secondhand tools are better than inferior new ones. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p276]
- Mechanic's feel is a deep inner kinesthetic feeling for the elasticity of materials that prevents damage to precision parts. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p276]
- With nuts and bolts, there are three states: finger-tight (contact), snug (easy surface elasticity), and tight (all elasticity taken up). [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p277]
