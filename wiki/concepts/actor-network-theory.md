# Actor-Network Theory

## Summary

Actor-network theory (ANT) applies semiotics to material entities, arguing that entities take their form as a result of relations with other entities. It accords significance to nonhuman actors and rejects micro-macro and agency-structure dichotomies. Key figures include Bruno Latour and John Law. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p546]
