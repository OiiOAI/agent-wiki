# Effort Justification

## Summary

Effort justification is the tendency to overvalue results we put energy into. John who had a hole punched in his chest for a parachute pin displays it decades later. The IKEA effect makes self-assembled furniture seem more valuable than expensive designer pieces.

## Key facts

- Effort justification is a special case of cognitive dissonance: to have a hole punched in your chest for a merit badge borders on the absurd, so the brain compensates by overvaluing the badge. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p137]
- The IKEA effect: furniture we assemble ourselves seems more valuable than any expensive designer piece. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p138]
- When instant cake mixes were made slightly more difficult (beating in an egg yourself), women's sense of achievement and appreciation for the convenience food increased. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p138]
