# Intuition

## Summary

Intuition, from Damasio's perspective, is the result of covert somatic marker operation. When negative outcomes come to mind, somatic markers may inhibit approach behaviors without the individual being consciously aware of the marker. This creates the experience of suddenly 'knowing' the right answer or having a gut feeling, without conscious deliberation.

## Key facts

- Intuition originates from a covert mechanism in which imagery related to a negative outcome inhibits regulatory neural circuits mediating approach behaviors, reducing chances of negative decisions without conscious deliberation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p208]
- Henri Poincaré described invention as discernment and choice, noting that sterile combinations never present themselves to the inventor's consciousness, suggesting unconscious preselection. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p209]
- Leo Szilard noted that insights in science leading to breakthroughs were not logically derived from preexisting knowledge, with creative processes operating at the level of the subconscious. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p209]
