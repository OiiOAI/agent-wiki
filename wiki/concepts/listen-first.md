# Listen First

## Summary

Behavior #11 in Covey's framework. Listening first means genuinely seeking to understand another person's thoughts, feelings, experience, and point of view before trying to diagnose, influence, or prescribe. Based on principles of understanding, respect, and mutual benefit.

## Key facts

- Peter Drucker elevated 'listen first, speak last' to a rule for effective executives. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p257]
- Covey's trust surveys show Listen First is the lowest rated (least practiced) of the 13 Behaviors. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p257]
- Face-to-face communication regarding attitudes and feelings is 7% what people say, 38% how they say it, and 55% body language. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p260]
