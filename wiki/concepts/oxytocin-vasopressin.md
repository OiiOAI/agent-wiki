# Oxytocin and Vasopressin

## Summary

Oxytocin and vasopressin are related neuropeptides that mediate distinct social-emotional processes. Oxytocin promotes female-typical calm nurturance and social acceptance, while vasopressin conveys male-typical sexual persistence and aggressive assertiveness.

## Key facts

- Oxytocin mediates female-typical calm nurturance, maternal acceptance, and readiness for social bonding, while vasopressin promotes male-typical persistence and aggressive assertiveness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p65]
- Oxytocin inhibits separation distress and mediates maternal behavior and social memory. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p65]
- The tripeptide PLG (proline-leucine-glycine), which can be derived from oxytocin breakdown, has antidepressant effects, suggesting fragment relationships to parent molecules. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p60]
