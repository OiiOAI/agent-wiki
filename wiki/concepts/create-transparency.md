# Create Transparency

## Summary

Behavior #3 in Covey's framework. Creating transparency means being open, real, genuine, and telling the truth in a way people can verify. It is based on principles of honesty, openness, integrity, and vulnerability, including the principle of light—when something is transparent, light flows through it.
