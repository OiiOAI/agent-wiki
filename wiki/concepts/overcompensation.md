# Overcompensation

## Summary

A response by antifragile systems that exceeds the immediate stimulus, producing more of something (strength, capacity, attention) than would seem necessary. Overcompensation is ubiquitous in nature and human systems, underlying phenomena from post-traumatic growth to innovation sparked by necessity. It represents the mechanism by which stressors benefit antifragile systems.

## Key facts

- Overcompensation is ubiquitous—systems that overcompensate are necessarily in overshooting mode, building extra capacity and strength in anticipation of worse outcomes and in response to information about hazards. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p69]
- Innovation and sophistication spark from initial situations of necessity, in ways that go far beyond the satisfaction of such necessity—the excess energy released from overreaction to setbacks is what innovates. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p64-65]
- Difficulty wakes up the genius, according to Ovid—overcompensation turns setbacks into sources of innovation and improvement. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p64]
- The best horses lose when competing with slower ones and win against better rivals; undercompensation from absence of a stressor degrades the best. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p67]
- Overcompensation is just a form of redundancy—an additional head for Hydra is no different from an extra kidney for humans or additional capacity to withstand stressors. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p68-69]

## Inferences

- Inference: Overcompensation provides a mechanism for understanding how antifragile systems convert harm into benefit, explaining the existence of phenomena ranging from exercise-induced strength gains to post-traumatic growth.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether overcompensation can be systematically induced or if it requires specific conditions to manifest.
