# Constraints in Crisis Management

## Summary

Constraints are fundamental to crisis management - without constraints there would be no evolution. They are key enablers of creativity. Draconian constraints set unquestionable boundaries early to gain time and increase downstream options. Constraints can be connecting or containing, resilient or robust, rigid or flexible, permeable or dark. Effective constraints have low granularity and fix either the why, what, or how, but not all three simultaneously.

## Key facts

- Without constraints there would be no evolution. They are a key enabler of creativity. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p12]
- Set unquestionable constraints early to gain time and increase downstream options: no exceptions, avoid constraints too rigid or lasting too long, keep communication open and frequent. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p12]
- Rigid and flexible boundaries break catastrophically when their design conditions are exceeded; permeable boundaries may clog. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p15]
- Dark constraints are like hidden meanings a term can assume for different people - we can see their effect but not know the cause. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p15]
- Enabling constraints have low granularity and fix either why or what or how, but not all three simultaneously. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p12]
