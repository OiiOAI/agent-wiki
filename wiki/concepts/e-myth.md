# The E-Myth (Entrepreneurial Myth)

## Summary

The E-Myth is the central thesis of Gerber's book—the myth that small businesses are started by entrepreneurs risking capital to make a profit. Gerber argues this is simply not true; most businesses are started by technicians who suffer an 'Entrepreneurial Seizure' and believe that knowing the technical work of a business means understanding the business itself.

## Key facts

- The E-Myth says small businesses are started by entrepreneurs risking capital to make a profit, which Gerber states is simply not true. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p9]
- The E-Myth is identified as the most important factor in the devastating rate of small business failure. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p9]
- Understanding and applying the E-Myth understanding can be the secret to any business's success. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p9]
