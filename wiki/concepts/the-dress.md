# The Dress

## Summary

The Dress was a viral internet phenomenon from February 2015 where a photograph of a dress appeared blue and black to some people but white and gold to others. The debate about its colour revealed individual differences in how visual systems 'discount the illuminant'—adjusting colour perception for ambient lighting conditions. This demonstrates that perceptual experiences are internal constructions shaped by personal biology and history.

## Key facts

- The Dress appeared blue and black to some people but white and gold to others when viewing the same image. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p89]
