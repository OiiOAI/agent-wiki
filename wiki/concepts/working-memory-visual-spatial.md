# Working Memory

## Summary

Working memory refers to the neural mechanism by which a limited amount of information can be held in mind for a short period. Patricia Goldman-Rakic's research demonstrated that sustained firing of prefrontal cortex neurons maintains visual spatial information during delay periods, serving as a neural substrate for working memory in monkeys performing delayed-response tasks.

## Key facts

- Patricia Goldman-Rakic and colleagues at Yale demonstrated that specific neurons in the prefrontal cortex maintain their firing, often for many seconds, after a target stimulus is switched off until the monkey makes its response. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p257]
- In these experiments, a particular neuron always responded when the target was at a particular place on the screen, and if the sustained activity was not maintained, the monkey was highly likely to make a mistake. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p257]
- Using the 2-deoxyglucose technique, increased activity was shown during such tasks in areas connected to the prefrontal cortex, including the hippocampal formation, posterior parietal cortex, and medial dorsal nucleus of the thalamus. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p257]
- The sustained firing of these neurons does not immediately suggest the existence of reverberatory circuits, leaving the exact neural mechanism unclear. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p258]

## Inferences

- Inference: These neurons in the prefrontal cortex appear to be part of a working memory system specifically for visual spatial location.
- Inference: The monkey may be mentally rehearsing the task during the delay period, maintaining the neural representation through sustained firing rather than through synaptic modification.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: What would happen neuronally if the monkey had to perform two distinct tasks simultaneously is not known.
- Uncertain: The exact neural mechanism that keeps these neurons firing during the delay period is not yet known.
