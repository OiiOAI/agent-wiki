# The Four Thieves of Productivity

## Summary

The Four Thieves of Productivity are obstacles that can undermine focus and success: (1) Inability to Say No—when saying yes to something, you are saying no to everything else; (2) Fear of Chaos—mess and disorder are inevitable when focusing on ONE Thing and must be accepted; (3) Poor Health Habits—personal energy mismanagement is a silent thief of productivity; (4) Environment Doesn't Support Goals—both the people around you and your physical surroundings must support your objectives.
