# Dream Characters

## Summary

Dream characters are the people and beings we encounter in our internally generated dream worlds. Some appear as one-dimensional extras while others have such a sense of realness that they elicit strong emotional responses. Research by Paul Tholey found that when lucid dreamers asked dream characters to perform tasks like writing, drawing, or solving math problems, characters were willing to try but struggled particularly with math—about two-thirds of their answers were wrong. Even when lucid dreamers consciously know they are dreaming and that characters are creations of their imagination, they cannot predict how characters will react, suggesting characters follow their own apparent ideas and intentions.

## Key facts

- Paul Thalley's study with nine proficient lucid dreamers found that dream characters were willing to try tasks like writing, drawing, and coming up with rhymes, but struggled with math—about two-thirds of character answers were wrong. - Even when lucid dreamers consciously decide to ask dream figures specific questions and know they are dreaming, they have little to no idea of how the dream character will react. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p259]
- When dream characters were asked to solve math problems in one study, one character started to cry, another ran away, and some acted as if the question was overly personal. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p259]
