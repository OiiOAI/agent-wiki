# Ventromedial Frontal Lobe and Decision-Making

## Summary

The ventromedial frontal lobe (VMF), encompassing the ventromedial prefrontal cortex and orbital surfaces, is critically involved in reward-guided decision-making. Lesion studies demonstrate that VMF damage disrupts value-based choice consistency, flexible learning from reward, and attentional priming by rewarded stimuli, without impairing basic value representation.

## Key facts

- VMF damage in humans disrupts less consistent preference judgments across stimulus categories, supporting a critical role for this region in value-based choice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p269]
- VMF damage disrupts reward priming of visual attention: patients with VMF lesions fail to show enhanced attentional capture by stimuli associated with high versus low reward. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p283]
- VMF damage spares simple value ratings and within-session consistency of value judgments, but produces more variance in assessment of low-value stimuli. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p269]
- The lateral OFC within the VMF was most strongly associated with impaired integration of multiple attributes (attractiveness and competence) in political voting decisions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p269]
- VMF damage in macaques produces deficits in stimulus-value learning but not action-value learning, demonstrating a double dissociation with the anterior cingulate cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p281]

## Inferences

- Inference: The deficit in reward priming of attention after VMF damage suggests that VMF contributes to guiding attention toward reward-predictive features of complex stimuli, a function that may be critical for associative learning in dynamic environments.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact boundary within VMF that is critical for value-based choice versus other functions is uncertain; different studies use different ROI definitions.
