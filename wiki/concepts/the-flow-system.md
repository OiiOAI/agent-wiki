# The Flow System

## Summary

The Flow System is a framework developed by John R. Turner and Nigel Thurlow that builds upon the Toyota Production System and Lean thinking to help leaders navigate complex, disrupted, and unpredictable environments. It operates through three interconnected helixes: complexity thinking, distributed leadership, and team science, all directed toward delivering customer value through organizational flow.

## Key facts

- The Flow System was co-created by John R. Turner and Nigel Thurlow, who also authored The Flow System: The Evolution of Agile and Lean Thinking in an Age of Complexity. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p384]
- The Flow System derives its foundation from the Toyota Production System, Lean thinking, and the Toyota Way. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p397]
- The Flow System is positioned on the left-hand side of the Cynefin Framework (Complex and Chaotic domains), while the Toyota Production System belongs to the right-hand side (Clear and Complicated domains). [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p398]
- The Triple Helix of Flow consists of three interconnected areas: complexity thinking, distributed leadership, and team science. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p396]
- Nigel Thurlow is the creator of the award-winning Scrum the Toyota Way training and served as the first ever Chief of Agile at Toyota. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p384]
- John R. Turner is an assistant professor at the University of North Texas and serves as Editor-in-Chief for the Performance Improvement Quarterly journal. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p384]
