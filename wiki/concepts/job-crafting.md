# Job Crafting

## Summary

Job crafting is the practice of looking creatively at work circumstances and finding ways to reconfigure one's situation to make it more engaging and fulfilling. It involves examining what activities engage you most, changing interactions with others, and changing how you see what you do. Employees who practice job crafting often end up more satisfied with their work lives, achieve higher levels of performance, and report greater personal resilience.

## Key facts

- Job crafting involves looking creatively at work circumstances and finding ways to reconfigure one's situation to make it more engaging and fulfilling. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p172]
- Employees who try job crafting often end up more satisfied with their work lives, achieve higher levels of performance in their organizations, and report greater personal resilience. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p172]
- The first step to job crafting is to pay attention to what activities engage you the most. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p172]
- Job crafting can involve changing the nature or extent of interactions with other people. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p173]
- To get buy-in for job-crafting ideas, one must focus on ways to achieve what you want and also create value for the organization. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p174]

## Inferences

- Inference: Job crafting represents a proactive approach to career development that allows employees to align their work with their values and interests, even within constrained job environments.
