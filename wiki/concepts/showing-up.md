# Showing Up

## Summary

Showing Up is the first movement in the four-part emotional agility framework. It means facing into thoughts, emotions, and behaviors willingly, with curiosity and kindness. The concept emphasizes that difficult emotions should not be avoided or suppressed but rather acknowledged and embraced as part of the human experience. Showing Up requires self-acceptance and the willingness to observe one's inner states without judgment, understanding that both helpful and unhelpful thoughts are part of who we are.

## Key facts

- Showing Up means facing into thoughts, emotions, and behaviors willingly, with curiosity and kindness. [raw/books/psychology/Emotional Agility - Susan David.pdf#p18]
