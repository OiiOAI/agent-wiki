# Determinism

## Summary

Determinism is the philosophical position that all events are caused by prior states according to physical laws. Mitchell distinguishes between hard determinism (which holds the future is fully predetermined) and soft determinism (which allows for randomness but locates all causation at the lowest physical levels). He argues against both positions, claiming they fail to account for the genuine agency that evolved in living systems.

## Key facts

- Hard determinism holds that current state plus laws of physics equals next state, implying the entire history of the universe was predetermined. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p13]
- Stephen Hawking stated that biological processes are as determined as the orbits of planets and that free will is an illusion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p13]
- Brian Greene asserts that free will is merely the sensation of making a choice while laws of physics determine the future. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p13]
- Neuroscientist Patrick Haggard states 'As a neuroscientist, you've got to be a determinist'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p15]
- Newton's laws of motion appeared to be completely deterministic, allowing prediction of system states from position and momentum of all components. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p146]
- Einstein's view of a static block universe meant the future is as determined as the past, with all space and time points essentially given at once. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p146]
- Laplace's Demon imagined an omniscient intellect knowing all forces and positions that could embrace all movements in a single formula with nothing uncertain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p147]
- The core theory in modern physics (Standard Model plus general relativity) claims to be causally comprehensive with no room for additional causal factors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p166]
