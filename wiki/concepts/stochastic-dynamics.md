# Stochastic Dynamics

## Summary

Stochastic dynamics in Active Inference involves stochastic differential equations augmented by random terms, enabling modeling of biological systems where fluctuations are generated by dynamical processes. Key concepts include nonequilibrium steady states and generalized coordinates of motion.

## Key facts

- A stochastic differential equation includes a random term distributed as normal with mean zero. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p245]
- Systems that maintain steady state must exhibit dynamics that on average minimize their surprise (self-evidencing dynamics). [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p246]
- Generalized coordinates of motion represent beliefs about position, velocity, acceleration, and subsequent temporal derivatives. [raw/books/neuroscience/Active Inference _ The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, - Thomas Parr; Giovanni Pezzulo; Karl J Friston; MIT Press.pdf#p241]
