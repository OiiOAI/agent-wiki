# Civilized Sexual Morality

## Summary

A term coined by Freud to describe the stage of civilization in which only legitimate reproduction is allowed as a sexual aim. Freud argued that this form of sexual morality, dominant in Western society, causes an increase in nervous illness by demanding sacrifices from individuals whose constitutions cannot comply, and produces harmful effects on marriage, gender relations, and cultural development.

## Key facts

- Freud distinguished three stages of civilization: one where sexual drive is freely exercised, a second where it is suppressed except for reproductive purposes, and a third where only legitimate reproduction is allowed—this third stage being 'civilized' sexual morality. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3065]
- Civilized sexual morality demands abstinence before marriage and lifelong abstinence for those who do not marry, a task which most people cannot fulfill without psychic damage. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3068]
- The neuroses are the negative of the perversions: perverse impulses, after being repressed, manifest themselves from the unconscious. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3072]
- Most people are constitutionally unfit to face the task of abstinence demanded by civilized sexual morality and fall ill of neurosis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3074]
- Sexual intercourse in marriage cannot compensate for the restrictions imposed before marriage, because contraceptives impair sexual enjoyment and the couple's spiritual sympathy is destroyed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3075]
- Civilized sexual morality produces frigidity in women, because education forbids them sexual knowledge, makes them ignorant of their role, and condemns any impulse of love that does not lead to marriage. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3077]
- A restriction of sexual activity in a community is accompanied by an increase of anxiety about life and fear of death, and diminished inclination to beget children. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3081]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud's critique of civilized sexual morality anticipates his later mature thesis, developed fully in Civilization and Its Discontents, that civilization is built on the suppression of drives.
