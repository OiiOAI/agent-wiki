# Status Games

## Summary

Status is a person's place in the social hierarchy and is an old zero-sum game. Wealth creation is an evolutionarily recent positive-sum game. Those attacking wealth creation are often just seeking status. The problem with status games is that to win, you have to put somebody else down, making you into an angry, combative person.
