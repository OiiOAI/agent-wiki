# Extend Trust

## Summary

Behavior #13 in Covey's framework. Extending trust shifts from 'trust' as a noun to 'trust' as a verb—helping you become a more trusting leader rather than just a more trusted person. It creates reciprocity; when you trust people, they tend to trust you in return. Based on principles of empowerment and reciprocity.
