# Smart Notes

## Summary

Smart notes are the core practice of the slip-box method. Literature notes capture the gist of what is read with references, while permanent notes capture one's own thoughts, written in dialogue with existing notes. The process involves asking what new information means for one's own thinking and explicitly connecting ideas.

## Key facts

- Literature notes collect the gist of something read with references to the source. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p89]
- Taking permanent notes is a form of self-testing: do they still make sense in writing? Can the thought be captured on paper?. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p89]
- When taking permanent notes, it is much more a form of thinking within the medium of writing than a protocol of preconceived ideas. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p89]
- The key question for permanent notes is: What does this mean for my own research and the questions in my slip-box?. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p91]
- Each note should fit onto the screen with no need for scrolling, containing only one idea. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p120]
