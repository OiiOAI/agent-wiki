# Law of the Vital Few

## Summary

The principle that in many settings, 80 percent of a given effect is due to just 20 percent of the possible causes, applied to identifying which activities most contribute to achieving life goals.

## Key facts

- The Law of the Vital Few states that in many settings, 80 percent of a given effect is due to just 20 percent of the possible causes. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p118]
- For each life goal, a small number of activities—the top two or three—provide the bulk of the benefit. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p118]
- All activities consume the same limited store of time and attention, making low-impact activities a zero-sum trade-off against high-impact activities. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p119]

## Inferences

- Inference: To abandon a network tool based on the craftsman approach is not to miss small benefits but to get more out of activities already known to yield large benefits.
