# Joint Attention

## Summary

Joint attention is described by Donald as a 'primary cultural guidance device' that allows children to follow cultural signals. It is a characteristically human form of structural coupling in which two or more individuals jointly attend to some shared thing and to one another's attention through the reciprocal coupling of their lived bodies. Joint attentional scenes occupy a middle ground between the perceptual world and the linguistic world, and they provide the basis for language acquisition, symbolic representation, and the ability to examine one's own thinking from the perspective of the other.
