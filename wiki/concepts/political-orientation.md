# Political Orientation (Left/Right Differences)

## Summary

Political orientation (liberalism vs. conservatism) shows remarkable consistency across diverse policy domains and reflects underlying cognitive and affective styles. Conservatives are more sensitive to disgust, threat, and ambiguity; liberals more readily engage in situational reasoning. Both show internal ideological consistency, suggesting political views are one manifestation of broader personality traits.

## Key facts

- Lower IQ predicts adherence to right-wing authoritarianism (RWA) after controlling for education and socioeconomic status—possibly because RWA provides simple answers ideal for poor abstract reasoning. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p460]
- Conservatives are more anxious about ambiguity, dislike novelty, are more comforted by structure and hierarchy, and more readily perceive circumstances as threatening. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p464]
- Priming people to think about their mortality makes them more conservative (terror management theory). [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p465]
- Despite being more anxious, conservatives report being happier than liberals—possibly due to system justification rationalizing inequality. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p465]
- Liberalism is associated with larger gray matter in the cingulate cortex (empathy); conservatism with an enlarged amygdala (threat perception). [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p467]
- Twin studies report ~50% heritability for political orientation; genome-wide studies have found associated genes, though effects are small. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p467]

## Inferences

- Inference: Political orientation appears to be one manifestation of broader personality traits—a person's views on dogcatcher elections, ice cream licking, and military interventions may all reflect the same underlying psychology.
- Inference: The link between disgust sensitivity and conservative social attitudes suggests that visceral/p bodily experiences can shape abstract political beliefs.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The causal direction between brain structure and political orientation is unclear—does a larger amygdala make someone conservative, or does being conservative enlarge the amygdala?
