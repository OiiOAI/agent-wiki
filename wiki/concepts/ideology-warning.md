# Ideology Warning

## Summary

Munger's warning that heavy ideology is one of the most extreme distorters of human cognition, capable of distorting even genius-level thinkers like Noam Chomsky. He advocates avoiding extreme ideology while maintaining commitment to accuracy, diligence, and objectivity.

## Key facts

- If ideology can screw up the head of Chomsky, imagine what it does to ordinary people—heavy ideology is one of the most extreme distorters of human cognition. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p225]
- Islamic fundamentalists gunning down Greek tourists shouting 'God's work!' demonstrates how ideology does strange things and distorts cognition terribly. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p225]
- Warren Buffett observed heavy ideologues as a child and decided ideology was dangerous, staying away from it throughout his life—enormously helping the accuracy of his cognition. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p226]
- One should be wary of heavy ideology while having heavy ideology in favor of accuracy, diligence, and objectivity—being totally sure with strong violent ideology turns you into a lousy thinker. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p227]
- Ideology pounds ideas in better than it convinces out, making it a very dangerous thing—getting heavy ideology young and expressing it locks the brain into an unfortunate pattern. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p226]

## Inferences

- Inference: Munger considers ideological avoidance essential for maintaining accurate cognition and worldly wisdom, applying this principle to his own thinking and identifying it as a key reason for Buffett's success.
