# Primacy and Recency Effects

## Summary

The primacy effect causes us to overweight first impressions while the recency effect causes us to overweight the most recent information. Alan (smart, hard-working, impulsive) and Ben (jealous, stubborn, impulsive, hard-working, smart) seem like different people despite identical descriptions.

## Key facts

- Alan (smart, hard-working, impulsive, critical, stubborn, jealous) and Ben (jealous, stubborn, critical, impulsive, hard-working, smart) seem like different people because the brain pays more attention to first adjectives. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p163]
- Nobel laureate Daniel Kahneman switched to grading exam questions in batches rather than student-by-student to cancel out the primacy effect. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p163]
- If you have an opinion, air it first to influence colleagues; if chairing a committee, ask opinions in random order so no one has unfair advantage. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p164]
