# PEACE & LOVE Protocol

## Summary

PEACE & LOVE is an alternative to the RICE protocol for treating musculoskeletal injuries, recommended in the British Journal of Sports Medicine. It emphasizes protecting and loading the injured area, avoiding anti-inflammatories, compression, education, optimism, vascularization, and exercise. Unlike RICE, PEACE & LOVE discourages icing and anti-inflammatory medications, which can delay healing.
