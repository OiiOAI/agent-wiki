# Kin-tract

## Summary

Kin-tract is a term coined by anthropologist Francis Hsu to describe organizations that partake of characteristics of both kinship groups and modern associations based on contract. It was specifically applied to Japanese iemoto organizations.

## Key facts

- Francis Hsu coined the term 'kin-tract' to describe how iemoto organizations combine kinship and contractual characteristics. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p415]
