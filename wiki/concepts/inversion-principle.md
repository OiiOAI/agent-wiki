# Inversion Principle

## Summary

The inversion principle, expressed as 'Invert, always invert' (originally from mathematician Jacobi), is a problem-solving approach that addresses difficult problems backward. Charlie Munger used this method in his famous Harvard School commencement speech by prescribing how to guarantee misery rather than happiness.

## Key facts

- Jacobi was known for his constant repetition: 'Invert, always invert.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p150]
- Munger states: 'Many hard problems are best solved when they are addressed backward.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p150]
- Einstein discovered special relativity by making a 180-degree turn and revising Newton's laws to fit Maxwell's. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p150]
