# Core Mindfulness Skills

## Summary

Core mindfulness skills in DBT consist of seven skills organized into three sets: Wise Mind; the 'what' skills (observe, describe, participate); and the 'how' skills (nonjudgmentally, one-mindfully, effectively). These skills teach how to observe and experience reality as it is, to be less judgmental, and to live in the moment with effectiveness.

## Key facts

- The 'what' skills are observe, describe, and participate—they describe what you do when practicing mindfulness. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p53]
- The 'how' skills are nonjudgmentally, one-mindfully, and effectively—they describe how you practice mindfulness. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p60]
- Observe means paying attention on purpose to the present moment without judging or clinging to what you see. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p53]
- One-mindfully means doing one thing at a time and staying completely present to the current moment. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p60]
- Effectively means focusing on what works and doing what is needed for the situation you are in, not the situation you wish you were in. [raw/books/psychology/DBT® Skills Training Handouts and Worksheets, Second Edition - Marsha M_ Linehan.pdf#p60]
