# John Kang

## Summary

Co-CEO of Medical Manager Corporation alongside Michael Singer. John Kang set up the meeting between Singer and Marty Wygod that led to the Synetic merger. He was one of three senior executives who went to trial and was ultimately vindicated when Judge Norton dismissed all charges.

## Key facts

- John Kang was co-CEO of Medical Manager Corporation alongside Michael Singer. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p175]
- John Kang set up the meeting between Singer and Synetic chairman Marty Wygod at a private airport in Midway, Texas in May 1999. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p174]
- John Kang and John Sessions were the two executives who went to trial after the other eight defendants had charges dropped. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p212]
- Judge Norton dismissed all charges against John Kang on May 27, 2010, ruling the government had not proven any conspiracy. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p213]
