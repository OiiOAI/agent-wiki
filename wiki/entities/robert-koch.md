# Robert Koch

## Summary

Robert Koch is an engineer, artist, researcher, risk and resilience practitioner, and senior manager in the energy sector. Listed by ESI Magazine in 2015 as one of the most influential figures in African power, he has coordinated national responses to major power system disaster incidents and applies complexity-informed approaches to risk and resilience.
