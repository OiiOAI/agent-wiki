# Aaron Franklin

## Summary

Pitmaster and owner of Franklin Barbecue in Austin, Texas, widely considered one of the best barbecue joints in America. He became famous despite (or because of) sharing his smoking techniques openly in videos and tutorials, demonstrating that teaching others doesn't create competition—it builds community and reputation.

## Key facts

- Aaron Franklin runs Franklin Barbecue in Austin, Texas, which Bon Appétit called 'the best BBQ in Texas, if not America.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p111]
- Franklin explains his smoking techniques in front of camera crews in a crowdfunded YouTube series called BBQ with Franklin, showing viewers every step of the barbecue process. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p111]
- Despite running a notoriously secretive competitive barbecue business, Franklin shares openly. His sons explained social media to him and got him set up on Twitter and other networks. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p43]
- Franklin told Kleon that his technique is 'actually very simple, but it takes years and years to master. There's an intuition that you only gain through the repetition of practice.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p113]
- Franklin: 'Teaching doesn't mean instant competition. Just because you know the master's technique doesn't mean you're going to be able to emulate it right away.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p113]
