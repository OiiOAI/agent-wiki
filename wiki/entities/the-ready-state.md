# The Ready State

## Summary

The Ready State (formerly MobilityWOD) is a company founded by Kelly and Juliet Starrett in 2010. Starting with daily YouTube videos focused on mobility, it evolved into a comprehensive movement and health company that has worked with military branches, professional sports teams (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL), Olympic athletes, university sports teams, and Fortune 500 companies.

## Key facts

- The Ready State was founded in 2010 by Kelly and Juliet Starrett as MobilityWOD. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L128]
- The company began by posting a daily video on YouTube focusing on mobility. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L128]
- The company has worked with all branches of the military, NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL players and coaches, Olympic athletes, university sports teams, and Fortune 500 companies. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L135-L137]
