# Eric Jorgenson

## Summary

Eric Jorgenson is the author of The Almanack of Naval Ravikant. He created this book as a public service to preserve and organize Naval's wisdom shared over the past decade through Twitter, blog posts, and podcasts. He describes how reading, listening, and applying Naval's principles of wealth and happiness has given him calm confidence and taught him to enjoy every moment of the journey.
