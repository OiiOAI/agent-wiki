# Andri

## Summary

Andri is a woman who chose to use nutrients to manage psychotic symptoms rather than medications against all medical advice. Her journey involved psychiatric services, hospitalizations, medications, and ECT for major depression with psychotic features. She found that hospital notes revealed low potassium, which she learned impacts brain functioning and could contribute to psychosis. She credits multinutrients along with yoga, meditation, and social connections for her recovery.

## Key facts

- Andri had extended involvement with psychiatric services, including hospitalizations, medications, and ECT, after being diagnosed with major depression with psychotic features. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p133]
