# W. Schultz

## Summary

Wolfram Schultz is a neuroscientist at the University of Cambridge who authored Chapter 2 on the electrophysiological correlates of reward processing in dopamine neurons. He is renowned for his work establishing the dopamine reward-prediction error signal.

## Key facts

- W. Schultz is affiliated with the University of Cambridge, Cambridge, United Kingdom. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p20]
- Schultz's research identified that dopamine neurons report reward-prediction errors through bidirectional responses: activation for positive errors and depression for negative errors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p21]
- He demonstrated that the dopamine prediction error signal reflects formal economic utility and fits into competitive decision models. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p21]
- His work bridges animal learning theory (prediction error) and economic decision theory (utility) through the dopamine utility prediction error signal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p21]
