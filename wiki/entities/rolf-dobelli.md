# Rolf Dobelli

## Summary

Rolf Dobelli is a Swiss novelist, entrepreneur, and author of 'The Art of Thinking Clearly.' He studied business, has written two literary novels, and became a student of social and cognitive psychology after being inspired by Nassim Nicholas Taleb's work. The book originated as a personal list of cognitive errors for investing decisions.

## Key facts

- Dobelli studied business and has written two literary novels before becoming interested in cognitive psychology. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p10]
- The list of cognitive errors was originally designed for personal use to avoid 'frivolous gambles with the wealth' accumulated from his literary career. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p11]
- Dobelli sees himself as a translator who interprets and synthesizes what he has read and learned, not as a social scientist conducting experiments. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p12]
- Dobelli holds an MBA and PhD in economic philosophy from the University of St. Gallen, Switzerland. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p224]
- He is co-founder of getAbstract, the world's leading provider of book summaries. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p224]
