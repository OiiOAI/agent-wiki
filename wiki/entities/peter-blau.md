# Peter Blau

## Summary

Peter Blau (1918–2002) was a Romanian-born American sociologist who made important contributions to exchange theory and structural theory. He emigrated to the US in 1939, received his Ph.D. from Columbia in 1952, and taught at the University of Chicago and later at the University of North Carolina. His book Exchange and Power in Social Life (1964) took Homans's primarily small-scale exchange theory and applied it to larger-scale issues. He also coauthored The American Occupational Structure (1967) with Otis Dudley Duncan.
