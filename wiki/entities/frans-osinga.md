# Frans Osinga

## Summary

Frans Osinga is a Dutch military strategist and former officer in the Royal Netherlands Air Force who earned his doctorate from Leiden University in 2005. His dissertation, Science, Strategy and War, provides the most comprehensive academic study of John Boyd's strategic theory. Osinga's work addresses the incomplete interpretations of Boyd's ideas and demonstrates Boyd's influence on Western military doctrine.
