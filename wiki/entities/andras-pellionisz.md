# Andras Pellionisz

## Summary

Andras Pellionisz was a collaborator with Llinás who worked on tensor analysis of cerebellar function and the mathematical modeling of sensorimotor transformations. Together they proposed that the brain predicts by implementing a Taylor Series-like function at the neuronal circuit level, with neurons sensitive to rate of change responding faster than the stimuli themselves.

## Key facts

- Pellionisz and Llinás proposed that the brain predicts by taking advantage of differences in electrical behavior among neurons, implementing a process analogous to Taylor Series Expansion through neurons that respond to the speed of change of a stimulus. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p45]
- Pellionisz and Llinás addressed how internal functional space operates and how continuity between perception and execution must exist, publishing papers spanning over a decade on cerebellar function and sensorimotor transformations.
