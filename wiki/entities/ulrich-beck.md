# Ulrich Beck

## Summary

Ulrich Beck is a German sociologist best known for his concept of the 'risk society' and theories of reflexive modernity and cosmopolitanism. His work examines how modernity has moved beyond classical industrial society to a new form characterized by manufactured risks.

## Key facts

- Beck's subtitle 'Towards a New Modernity' indicates he rejects the notion of postmodernity, viewing contemporary society as a new form of modernity. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p459]
- Beck argues that modernization is dissolving industrial society and another modernity is coming into being. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p460]
- The prior stage of classical modernity was associated with industrial society, while the emerging new modernity is associated with the risk society. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p459]
- In classical modernity the central issue was wealth and its distribution; in advanced modernity the central issue is risk and how it can be prevented, minimized, or channeled. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p460]
- Beck distinguishes between globalism (dominance of economics and capitalist world market) and globality (closed spaces becoming illusory due to transnational processes). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p478]
- Beck sees 'second modernity' as defined by the decline of the power of nations and national borders. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p479]

## Inferences

- Inference: Beck's risk society thesis represents a continuation and modernization of classical sociological concerns with industrial society, now focused on self-made risks of modernity.
