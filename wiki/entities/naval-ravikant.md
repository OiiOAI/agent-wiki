# Naval Ravikant

## Summary

Naval Ravikant is an Indian-American entrepreneur, angel investor, and philosopher based in Silicon Valley. He is the founder and chairman of AngelList and has invested in companies including Uber, Twitter, and hundreds of others. Born in Delhi, India in 1974, he moved to Queens, New York at age 9. He is known for sharing his philosophy on wealth creation and happiness through Twitter and podcasts, attracting millions of followers worldwide.
