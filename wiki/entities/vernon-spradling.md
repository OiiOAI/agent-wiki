# Vernon Spradling

## Summary

Vernon "Sprad" Spradling was a 22-year Air Force veteran with a master's degree in public administration who was Boyd's supervisor and closest friend at the Fighter Weapons School. He monitored instructor performance and became "Mr. Fighter Weapons School," the institutional memory of the school.

## Key facts

- Spradling was responsible for selecting and training FWS instructors and monitoring their performance, requiring them to demonstrate both knowledge and teaching skills. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p82]
- Spradling offered to let Boyd dictate the Aerial Attack Study and had his secretary transcribe it, then edited the document for him. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p108]
- Spradling received late-night phone calls from Boyd several nights a week, usually after midnight, where Boyd talked for hours about calculus equations to work through his thoughts. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p84]
- Spradling tolerated the late-night calls because Boyd was developing radical new tactics and was becoming a legend in the fighter-pilot community. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p84]
- Spradling said of all who passed through FWS in twenty-two years, the memory of John Boyd burns brighter than anyone else. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p83]
