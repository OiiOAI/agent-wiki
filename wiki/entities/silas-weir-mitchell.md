# Silas Weir Mitchell

## Summary

Silas Weir Mitchell (1829–1914) was an American physician who developed the 'rest cure' treatment for nervous diseases. Freud discussed this treatment extensively in his writings on hysteria, considering it of extraordinary value when combined with other approaches. The rest cure involved isolation, massage, and general faradization.

## Key facts

- In recent years the so-called 'rest cure' of Weir Mitchell has gained a high reputation as a method of treating hysteria in institutions, and deservedly so. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p98]
- Freud later recommended a combination of Weir Mitchell's rest cure with Breuer's cathartic treatment. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p98]
