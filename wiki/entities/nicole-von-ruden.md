# Nicole von Ruden

## Summary

Nicole von Ruden is a woman who was left partially paralyzed on her entire right side as a severe side effect of radiation treatment for a brain tumor. Born in 1967, she had been an active person who loved whitewater rafting, mountain biking, and running marathons. After six months of depression and being unable to care for herself, she underwent CI therapy at the Taub Clinic. She made remarkable recovery, returning to work full-time as a television producer and regaining the ability to drive, swim, and even ski.

## Key facts

- Nicole von Ruden was born in 1967 and worked as an elementary school teacher and producer for CNN and Entertainment Tonight. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p157]
- At age 33, she was diagnosed with a rare inoperable brain tumor (glioma) in the brain stem and given three to nine months to live. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p158]
- Radiation treatment saved her life but caused severe side effects—paralysis and loss of sensation on her whole right side. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p158]
- She gained weight from 125 to 190 pounds due to immobility and steroids, and became so depressed she stopped speaking for six months. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p159]
- After CI therapy, she could make coffee with her affected arm, open the refrigerator, turn off lights, and put shampoo on her head. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p161]
- She returned to full-time work as a producer for Entertainment Tonight, working 50+ hour weeks. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p161]
