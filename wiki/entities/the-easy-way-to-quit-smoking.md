# The Easy Way To Quit Smoking

## Summary

A book by Allen Carr that teaches smokers to quit by reframing their mindset about smoking, claiming that quitting is easy rather than difficult. The book's five-hour seminar achieves a 53.3% success rate, significantly higher than the 10-20% success rate of other quit smoking methods.

## Key facts

- The book has no statistics about health risks like lung cancer, heart attacks, or strokes, and uses no scare tactics. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p123]
- The book's core technique involves repeating 'Quitting is easy' throughout the text. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p123]
- Carr's five-hour seminar based on the book has a 53.3% success rate. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p123]
- Other quit smoking methods have approximately a 10-20% success rate. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p123]
- The book's success comes from getting smokers to believe that quitting smoking is easy, rather than difficult. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p123]
