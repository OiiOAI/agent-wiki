# Tim Bliss

## Summary

Tim Bliss was a postdoctoral student who, together with Terje Lømo, discovered long-term potentiation in the hippocampus in 1973. This discovery established a neural analog of learning based on neuronal activity per se rather than conventional behavioral paradigms.

## Key facts

- In 1973, Tim Bliss and Terje Lømo discovered that neuronal pathways leading to the hippocampus of rabbits could be strengthened by a brief burst of neural activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p293]
- Bliss and Lømo called this form of synaptic facilitation long-term potentiation, noting that it differed from their earlier work in that it was based on neuronal activity per se rather than behavioral paradigms.
