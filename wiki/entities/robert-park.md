# Robert Park

## Summary

Robert Park (1864-1944) was an American sociologist who became the dominant figure in the Chicago department of sociology. He studied with Simmel in Berlin and brought European ideas to American sociology. He emphasized the importance of urban problems and field research through personal observation, contributing to the Chicago school's focus on urban ecology.
