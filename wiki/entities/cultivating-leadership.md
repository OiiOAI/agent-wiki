# Cultivating Leadership

## Summary

Cultivating Leadership is a global leadership consultancy founded by Jennifer Garvey-Berger and Keith Johnston with over 50 colleagues from 14 countries. The firm evolved from using Cynefin as a tool to living it as a mindset, applying complexity principles to their own organizational development.

## Key facts

- Cultivating Leadership grew from 4 founders to over 50 people from 14 countries around the world. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p174]
- The firm developed 'advice calls' as their core experiment in complexity, evolving from a control mechanism to emergent learning and knowledge-sharing events. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p175]
- Keith Johnston created a rope-based physical Cynefin Framework in Adelaide in 2015, transforming how the firm taught the framework. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p173]
- The firm starts every meeting with a 'complexity check-in' using pattern recognition questions. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p177]

## Inferences

- Inference: The firm's journey from tool to mindset represents a model for how organizations can authentically integrate complexity thinking into their operations.
