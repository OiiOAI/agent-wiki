# Marshall McLuhan

## Summary

A Canadian professor who, in the 1960s, developed the theory that the arrival of new communication technologies transforms how humans perceive the world. His central insight was that 'the medium is the message' — meaning the way information reaches us shapes our consciousness as much as the content itself.

## Key facts

- McLuhan argued that every new medium acts like a set of 'goggles' with its own 'special colors and lenses' that change how we see the world. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p91]
- According to McLuhan, television teaches people that the world is fast, about surfaces and appearances, with everything happening all at once. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p91]
- McLuhan's insight was that how information gets to you is more important than the information itself. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p91]
