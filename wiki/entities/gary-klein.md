# Gary Klein

## Summary

A researcher who developed the critical incident technique for studying decision making in naturalistic settings. His work is discussed in Chapter 3 of this book, where he explores situation awareness and decision making through field research.

## Key facts

- Gary Klein discusses work using the critical incident technique to explore decision making by experts in naturalistic settings. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p10]
- Klein examines benefits of exploring SA and decision making through naturally occurring field research. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p10]
- He is chairman and chief scientist of Klein Associates. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- He has conducted naturalistic decision making research in fire fighting, aviation, command and control, market research, and software troubleshooting. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- He has developed significant new models of proficient decision making. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- He received his PhD in experimental psychology from the University of Pittsburgh in 1969. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- He has edited two books on naturalistic decision making. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- His research interests include individual and team decision making under conditions of stress, time pressure, and uncertainty. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p16]
- Gary Klein is the author of Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p2]
- Klein founded his research and development company Klein Associates in 1978. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p14]
- Klein's specialty was how to develop expertise, leading him to investigate decision making in the early 1980s from the perspective of developing expertise rather than calculating probabilities. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p14]
- Klein's decision to start a research company in 1978 was stimulated by Hubert Dreyfus's critique of artificial intelligence and information-processing accounts of cognition. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p12]
- Klein co-designed the ShadowBox training program with colleagues to foster effective decision making. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p16]
- Klein served on a team redesigning the White House Situation Room to support more effective decision making. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p16]
- Klein collaborated with Nobel prize winner Daniel Kahneman. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p16]
- Klein conducted his first research project on decision making almost two decades before this book, studying firefighters to see how they made high-stakes decisions in just a few seconds. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p12]
- Klein's 1998 book Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions (MIT Press) described the Recognition-Primed Decision model and attracted significant media attention including coverage in the Wall Street Journal, Fast Company, and O Magazine. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p15]
- Klein worked with the U.S. Marine Corps starting in 1995 to develop a decision-training program, which helped make the term intuitive decision making acceptable in military contexts. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p14]
- Klein's company has compiled a database of more than a thousand difficult and critical decisions using cognitive task analysis methods in over seventy different fields. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p14]
- Gary Klein introduced the premortem technique for prospective failure analysis. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2095-L2105]
- His Recognition-Primed Decision model describes how experts make effective decisions under time pressure. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2095-L2105]
- The premortem is published in Harvard Business Review (September 2007). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2095-L2105]
- Gary Klein developed the recognition-primed decision (RPD) model, showing that experts often recognize a promising action pattern directly and mentally simulate it for viability rather than comparing multiple alternatives. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p305]
- Klein's research on fireground commanders showed that they typically considered only one option, evaluating it through mental simulation to check for problems rather than comparing multiple choices. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p305]
- Klein proposed the 'premortem' procedure to counteract overconfidence and groupthink by asking team members to imagine the plan has failed and write a brief history of the disaster. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p317]
- Klein co-authored 'Conditions for Intuitive Expertise: A Failure to Disagree' with Kahneman, finding broad agreement that valid intuition requires a regular environment and adequate learning opportunity. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking, Fast and Slow (Cover Baru) - Daniel Kahneman.pdf#p303]
