# Karl Friston

## Summary

Karl Friston is a British neuroscientist who coined the term 'active inference'. His work on the free energy principle and predictive processing has been highly influential in connecting Bayesian brain theories with neural implementation.

## Key facts

- Karl Friston coined the term 'active inference' for minimizing prediction error through action. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p112]
- Active inference is a term Friston introduced to describe how actions can fulfill perceptual predictions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p112]
- Karl Friston is an eminent British neuroscientist who developed the 'free energy principle' and its application to neuroscience through active inference. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p189]
- The first thing that strikes you about the free energy principle is that it's a really big idea bringing together concepts from biology, physics, statistics, neuroscience, engineering, machine learning, and elsewhere. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p189]
- For Friston, the free energy principle explains all features of living systems, from a single bacterium to brain details to body plans of animals, reaching as far as the broad strokes of evolution. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p189]
- Friston describes the view from the FEP as organisms gathering and modelling sensory information so as to maximize the sensory evidence for their own existence. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p193]
