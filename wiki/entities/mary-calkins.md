# Mary Whiton Calkins

## Summary

Mary Whiton Calkins (1863-1930) was a pioneering female psychologist at Wellesley College who conducted one of the first statistical analyses of dream content in 1893. She became the first woman president of the American Psychological Association and developed standardized methods for studying dreams that embodied the future science of dreams.

## Key facts

- Calkins analyzed almost four hundred dream reports collected over two months using herself and a male subject. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p41]
- She found that nine out of ten dream reports showed evident connections between dream content and elements from waking life. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p41]
- Calkins proposed the 'continuity hypothesis' of dreaming, stating there is 'congruity and continuity' between waking and dreaming life. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p41]
- She developed a hierarchy of sensory representations in dreams that was later confirmed by modern studies. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p42]
