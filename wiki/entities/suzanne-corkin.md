# Suzanne Corkin

## Summary

Suzanne Corkin (1937–2016) was an American neuroscientist and professor at MIT who studied Henry Molaison for 46 years, from 1962 until his death in 2008. Born near Hartford, Connecticut, Corkin had a personal connection to Henry's story—her childhood friend's father was William Beecher Scoville, the surgeon who operated on Henry. She joined Brenda Milner's laboratory at the Montreal Neurological Institute as a graduate student and first met Henry in 1962. After moving to MIT in 1977, she continued studying Henry for the rest of his life, accumulating 55 years of behavioral data that she linked to post-mortem analysis of his brain.
