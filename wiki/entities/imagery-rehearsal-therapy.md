# Imagery Rehearsal Therapy

## Summary

Imagery rehearsal therapy (IRT) is a cognitive behavioral intervention that teaches nightmare sufferers to rescript their nightmares and rehearse the altered versions through visual imagery. It is the recommended non-pharmacological treatment for trauma-related nightmares, recurrent nightmares, and chronic idiopathic nightmares. Studies show IRT reduces nightmares in diverse populations including children, veterans, and trauma victims, with gains maintained over time.

## Key facts

- Best-practice guides for nightmare treatment consistently recommend IRT for trauma-related, recurrent, and chronic idiopathic nightmares. [raw/books/neuroscience/When Brains Dream - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p218]
- In IRT, nightmare sufferers rescript their nightmares in whatever way feels right and rehearse the new dream through visual imagery for several minutes daily. [raw/books/neuroscience/When Brains Dream - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p218]
- IRT reduces nightmares in children, veterans, trauma victims, and patients with mental illness, with improvements maintained over time. [raw/books/neuroscience/When Brains Dream - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p219]
