# Central Park Five

## Summary

The Central Park Five were five Black and Latino teenagers wrongly convicted of the brutal 1989 assault and rape of jogger Trisha Meili. They confessed to the crime as a result of police coercion. Their convictions were overturned in 2002 when another man confessed to the attack. Their story was documented in the 2012 PBS documentary 'The Central Park Five' and 2019 Netflix miniseries 'When They See Us.'

## Key facts

- The five men were convicted of committing the brutal 1989 assault and rape of Trisha Meili in Central Park after falsely confessing to the crime as a result of police coercion. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p182]
- The men were eventually exonerated in 2002 when another man confessed to the attack. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p182]
- The 2012 PBS documentary 'The Central Park Five' and the 2019 Netflix miniseries 'When They See Us' brought their story to a wide audience and sparked discussion of the dangers of false confessions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p182]
