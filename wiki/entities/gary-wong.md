# Gary Wong

## Summary

Gary Wong has over 45 years of experience starting with his career at BC Hydro, working in engineering, line operations, business consulting, and training roles. He later joined Ernst & Young Consulting (now Capgemini) as Senior Manager in Strategy & Transformation. He has operated his own independent consulting practice for 15 years focusing on complexity thinking and safety. He holds an engineering degree from UBC and an MBA from SFU.
