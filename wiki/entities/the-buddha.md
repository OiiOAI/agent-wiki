# The Buddha

## Summary

The Buddha represents the supreme hero who broke past even the zone of the creative gods and came back from the void announcing salvation from the cosmogonic round. His death narrative describes his final passage through the meditative trances in reverse order before passing into Nirvana.

## Key facts

- The Blessed One lay on his couch between twin sal-trees that had completely burst forth into bloom though it was not the flowering season; blossoms scattered themselves over his body, heavenly sandal-wood powder fell from the sky, and music sounded in the sky in worship. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p282-p283]
- Deities throughout ten worlds came to behold the Tathagata; an extent of twelve leagues about the city Kusinara, there was not a spot of ground large enough to stick the point of a hair into that was not pervaded by powerful deities. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p283]
- Deities free from passion bore the Buddha's passing patiently, saying 'Transitory are all things. How is it possible that whatever has been born, has come into being, and is organized and perishable, should not perish?'. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p284]
- The Buddha's last word was 'And now, O priests, I take my leave of you; all the constituents of being are transitory; work out your salvation with diligence.'. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p285]
- The Buddha entered the four trances in succession, then the realm of infinity of space, infinity of consciousness, nothingness, neither perception nor non-perception, and finally the cessation of perception and sensation—then rose and retraced all steps in reverse before passing into Nirvana. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p285-p286]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Buddha's death represents the complete dissolution of the individual into the universal, passing through all the stages of cosmogonic emanation in reverse before ultimate release from the cycle of rebirth.
