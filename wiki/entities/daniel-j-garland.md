# Daniel J. Garland

## Summary

Daniel J. Garland is an editor of the book 'Situation Awareness: Analysis and Measurement' and co-authored Chapter 14 on situation awareness, automaticity, and training. He is listed as an author alongside Wayne L. Shebilske and Barry P. Goettl.

- Daniel J. Garland is one of the editors of the book. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p7]
- He co-authored Chapter 14 on situation awareness, automaticity, and training. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p7]
- He is a senior research associate with SA Technologies. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p14]
- He received a PhD in experimental psychology from the University of Georgia in 1989. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p14]
- He is author or coauthor of more than 80 scientific articles and reports. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p14]
- He edited the 1999 Handbook of Aviation Human Factors (with J.A. Wise and V.D. Hopkin). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p14]
