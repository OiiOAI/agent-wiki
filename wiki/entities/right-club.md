# Right Club

## Summary

The Right Club was a British political organization founded by MP Archibald Ramsay in May 1939. Its stated purpose was to oppose communism, Freemasonry, and what Ramsay termed Jewish influence in British politics. The organization maintained a secret membership list and was shut down at the outbreak of World War II. Ramsay was detained and the organization was targeted partly due to his associations with Tyler Kent.

## Key facts

- The Right Club was founded by Captain Archibald Ramsay in May 1939. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p452]
- The club's stated aim was to oppose and expose organized Jewry and communist activities. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p458]
- The organization maintained strict secrecy regarding its membership list, which Ramsay kept in a 'Red Book'. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p436]
- The club was closed down at the outbreak of World War II in September 1939. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p453]
