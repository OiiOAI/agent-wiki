# Joseph LeDoux

## Summary

Joseph LeDoux is a neuroscientist specializing in the neurobiology of fear and anxiety, particularly the role of the amygdala. He is the Henry and Lucy Moses Professor of Science at New York University, where he directs the Emotional Brain Institute. A member of the National Academy of Sciences, he is also a musician who performs with the rock band The Amygdaloids.
