# Joseph LeDoux

## Summary

Joseph LeDoux is a neuroscientist specializing in the neurobiology of fear and anxiety, particularly the role of the amygdala. He is the Henry and Lucy Moses Professor of Science at New York University, where he directs the Emotional Brain Institute. A member of the National Academy of Sciences, he is also a musician who performs with the rock band The Amygdaloids.

## Key facts

- Joseph LeDoux is the Henry and Lucy Moses Professor of Science at New York University. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He is a member of the Center for Neural Science and Department of Psychology at NYU. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He directs the Emotional Brain Institute at NYU and at the Nathan Kline Institute. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He is a member of the National Academy of Sciences. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He is a member of the rock band The Amygdaloids. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He is the author of Synaptic Self and The Emotional Brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He lives in Brooklyn, New York. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- He joined NYU's faculty of Arts and Sciences in 1989. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p14]
- He has been continuously funded by the National Institute of Mental Health since 1986. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p14]
- In 1997 he was appointed director of the Emotional Brain Institute through collaboration between NYU and New York State. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p14]
- Joseph LeDoux and Michael Davis found that in rodents, both innate and learned fear recruit a neural circuit focused on the amygdala, and they delineated how information from conditioned and unconditioned stimuli reaches the amygdala. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p352]
- LeDoux showed that the amygdala receives information about the conditioned stimulus through both a direct pathway bypassing the cortex and an indirect pathway through the auditory cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p353]
- LeDoux was Gazzaniga's graduate student in the 1970s and studied confabulation in split-brain patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p264]
- LeDoux proposed the implicit/explicit distinction for fear: the amygdala controls implicit fear responses; conscious fear emerges from cortical cognitive circuits. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p333]
- LeDoux wrote 'The Emotional Brain' (1996) which contributed to the popular misconception of the amygdala as a 'fear center', which he later sought to correct. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p331]
- LeDoux proposes emotions are autonoetic conscious experiences assembled by higher-order prefrontal networks from emotion schema, survival circuit activity, and self-schema. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p344]
