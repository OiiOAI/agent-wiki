# Daily Essential Nutrients

## Summary

Daily Essential Nutrients (DEN) is a broad-spectrum multinutrient formula studied for ADHD, smoking cessation, emotional dysregulation, trauma symptoms, aggression, insomnia, stress, and anxiety. The full therapeutic dose is 4 capsules three times daily.

## Key facts

- DEN full daily dose for psychiatric symptoms is 4 capsules three times a day (12/day). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p260]
- Available as a powder for those who cannot swallow capsules. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p260]
- Optimal Balance product is designed for stress management at lower dose of 3 capsules twice daily. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p260]
- Available at www.hardynutritionals.com. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p260]
