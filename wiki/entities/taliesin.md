# Taliesin

## Summary

A Welsh bard who, having tasted three drops from the poison kettle of inspiration, was eaten by the hag Caridwen, reborn as an infant, and committed to the sea. Found by Elphin in a fishtrap, he spoke in verse and foretold honor and glory. Campbell presents Taliesin as exemplifying 'Freedom to Live'—the champion of things becoming who, having died to his personal ego in the hag's womb, arose established in the Self, capable of instructing the whole universe.

- Taliesin was originally little Gwion who tasted three drops from Caridwen's poison kettle of inspiration and was then eaten by the hag and reborn from her womb. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p186]
- The larger portion of Taliesin's song is devoted to the Imperishable which lives in him, only a brief stanza to the details of his personal biography. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p187]
