# Gabriel Jurj

Gabriel Jurj is an executive with significant experience leading Applications Development, Service Delivery, and Infrastructure Operations. He applied Cynefin and SenseMaker at Waters Corporation, achieving a dramatic improvement from 17% to 100% on-time delivery within approximately one year.
