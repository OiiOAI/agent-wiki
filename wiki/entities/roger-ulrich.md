# Roger Ulrich

## Summary

Roger Ulrich was a professor at the Centre for Healthcare Architecture at Chalmers University of Technology in Sweden. He conducted research demonstrating that hospital room windows overlooking gardens significantly improved patient recovery.

## Key facts

- Roger Ulrich conducted a study showing that people assigned to hospital rooms with windows overlooking gardens healed faster and needed fewer painkillers than people whose rooms faced a brick wall. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p186]
