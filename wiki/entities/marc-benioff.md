# Marc Benioff

## Summary

Marc Benioff is the founder and CEO of Salesforce.com who dreamed up the cloud-based CRM model while at Oracle. His company exemplifies the journey approach to strategy—facing sequential challenges including attracting developers, acquiring customers, surviving the dot-com crash, and expanding the platform. The book uses Salesforce.com to illustrate how strategy is an ongoing process of confronting challenges rather than a fixed destination.

## Key facts

- Benioff dreamed of building a cloud-based CRM after seeing Amazon.com's model—he envisioned tabs for Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities instead of products. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p59]
- He left Oracle in 1999 with $2 million seed capital from Oracle CEO Larry Ellison plus additional venture funding. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p59]
- Salesforce.com's initial challenge was attracting developers—he built publicity through parties and adopted a no-software symbol with red slash. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p59]
- The crux of early customer acquisition was that IT departments controlled purchasing—Benioff shifted to allow free sign-ups for five users. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p60]
- Salesforce.com was the first dot-com listed on the New York Stock Exchange and by 2021 was valued at $243 billion. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p61]
