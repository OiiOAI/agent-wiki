# Peter D. Kaufman

## Summary

Peter D. Kaufman is an editor and author who compiled and introduced Poor Charlie's Almanack. He is the son of Charles T. Munger and contributed the comprehensive introduction to the book that outlines Munger's intellectual approach, wit, and values.

## Key facts

- Peter D. Kaufman wrote the introduction to Poor Charlie's Almanack. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p4]
- Kaufman describes Munger as having encyclopedic knowledge that allows him to cite references from classical orators to pop culture icons. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p5]
- Kaufman notes that Munger uses humor, inversions, and paradox to provide counsel. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p5]
- Kaufman observes that Munger places a premium on life decisions over investment decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p6]
