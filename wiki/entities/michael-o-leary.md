# Michael O'Leary

## Summary

Michael O'Leary is the CEO of Ryanair who transformed the airline by adopting Southwest Airlines' low-cost model. After visiting Southwest, he implemented an extreme version of unbundled pricing where everything except air travel was charged separately. His leadership style is known for provocative statements while delivering consistent profitability.

## Key facts

- O'Leary visited Southwest Airlines in the United States and called the experience like the road to Damascus. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p62]
- He implemented unbundled pricing where baggage, priority boarding, seat selection, food, and drinks were all charged separately. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p63]
- Ryanair's cost of carrying most passengers equaled its fares—profit came entirely from ancillary fees. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p63]
- Ryanair grew to become the largest budget carrier in Europe carrying more international passengers than any other airline. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p64]
- In April 2020, O'Leary dismissed 3,000 employees as COVID-19 forced drastic reduction in air travel. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p64]
