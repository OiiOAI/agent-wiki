# Mark Manson

## Summary

Mark Manson is the author of Models: Attract Women Through Honesty, a former dating coach who became a bestselling author. Born in Texas, he was raised by a single mother and struggled with women in his youth. After a traumatic breakup in 2005, he spent three years obsessed with validation from women, reading pickup books and going out 4-5 nights a week. He began coaching other men and discovered that most dating problems were emotional rather than tactical. His book was self-published in 2011 and became one of the best-selling men's dating advice books. By 2013 he left dating advice to focus on general personal development writing, and his website reached over 20 million readers in 2015 alone.
