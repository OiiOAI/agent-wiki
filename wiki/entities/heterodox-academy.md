# Heterodox Academy

## Summary

Heterodox Academy is an organization founded in 2015 by Jon Haidt, Philip Tetlock, and others to fight drift toward homogeneity of thought in science and academia. It publishes research documenting political imbalance in social psychology, demonstrates how homogeneity reduces quality of science, and provides specific recommendations for encouraging diversity and dissenting opinions in academic institutions.

## Key facts

- Heterodox Academy was founded in 2015 by Jon Haidt, Philip Tetlock, José Duarte, Jarret Crawford, Lee Jussim, and Charlotta Stern. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1390-L1395]
- The organization published findings in Behavioral and Brain Sciences journal, documenting political imbalance in social psychology and how it reduces quality of science. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1395-L1400]
- Political values became 'embedded into research questions in ways that make some constructs unobservable and unmeasurable, thereby invalidating attempts at hypothesis testing.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1400-L1405]
- Researchers concentrated on topics that validated their shared narrative and avoided topics that contested that narrative, such as stereotype accuracy. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1405-L1410]
- Heterodox Academy recommendations include stated antidiscrimination policies against opposing viewpoints, developing ways to encourage people with contrary viewpoints to join, and surveying to gauge actual heterogeneity of opinion. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1445-L1450]
