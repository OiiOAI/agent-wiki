# Richard P. Rumelt

## Summary

Richard P. Rumelt is a strategy researcher, teacher, and consultant who authored the book Good Strategy/Bad Strategy (2011). He is recognized for his non-simplistic approach to strategy that focuses on structured, intelligent thinking about business rather than fast-food approaches to strategic planning.
