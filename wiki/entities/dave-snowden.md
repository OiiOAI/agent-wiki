# Dave Snowden

## Summary

Dave Snowden is known for developing the Cynefin framework for assessing domain complexity. Together with Mary Boone, he authored a 2007 Harvard Business Review paper titled 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making.'

## Key facts

- Dave Snowden co-authored the 2007 Harvard Business Review paper 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making' with Mary Boone. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L497-L498]
- Snowden developed the Cynefin framework, which is used for assessing domain complexity. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L497-L498]
- Dave Snowden first presented the early Cynefin Framework in Frankfurt in 1999 at a Knowledge Management Special Interest Group meeting of Lotus Consulting. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p184]
- Snowden worked at IBM where he and Larry Prusak were KM thought leaders, proposing an organic, humanistic approach to knowledge management. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p185]
- Dave Snowden developed the ASHEN model for knowledge building blocks: Artefacts, Skills, Heuristics, Experience, and Natural talent. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p185]
- In 2016, Dave Snowden took a Professorship at Bangor University to establish the Cynefin Centre. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p129]
- Snowden often uses the Apollo 13 film as a reference when talking about innovation, noting the famous quote 'failure is not an option' which contrasts with his approach of safe-to-fail probes. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p128]
- Dave Snowden is Welsh and is described as a 'hidden Living National Treasure' of Wales. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p249]
- Snowden's background includes theoretical physics, philosophy, and theology, enriched by street smarts earned as a 1970s radical. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p256]
- The Cynefin Framework was established within IBM around 2002 or 2003. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
- Dave Snowden co-founded Cognitive Edge with Cynthia Kurtz. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
- Dave Snowden's demeanor combines that of a mad scientist, priest, and philosopher, wrapped around the skills of a debate champion. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p256]
- Snowden's favorite place in the world is on top of Tryfan, a dinosaur-shaped mountain in alpine North Wales. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p250]
- Dave Snowden developed the Cynefin Framework, which is now used in U.S. Armed Forces officer training. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p249]
