# Hazel Forsyth

## Summary

Hazel Forsyth is a senior curator at the Museum of London specializing in Medieval and Post-Medieval Collections. She has written influential books including The Cheapside Hoard about Elizabethan jewelers and Butcher, Baker, Candlestick Maker about Londoners' survival after the Great Fire of 1666. She exemplifies resilience in research, persisting through dead ends and treating absence of evidence as valuable information.

## Key facts

- Hazel Forsyth is senior curator of the Museum of London's Medieval and Post-Medieval Collections. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p99]

- Her book Butcher, Baker, Candlestick Maker traces how citizens and traders rebuilt London after the Great Fire of 1666. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p99]
