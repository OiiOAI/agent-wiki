# Cochlear Nuclei

## Summary

The cochlear nuclei are brainstem nuclei that receive all input from the cochlear nerve and are organized tonotopically. They contain distinct neuronal types (bushy, stellate, octopus, and fusiform cells) that extract different acoustic features and convey information through parallel ascending pathways.

## Key facts

- The cochlear nuclei are organized tonotopically with low-frequency inputs terminating ventrally and high-frequency inputs terminating dorsally. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p702]
- Bushy cells receive large synaptic terminals called end bulbs of Held and convey precise timing information about sound onset. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p703]
- Stellate cells encode the spectrum of sounds and project to the ipsilateral dorsal cochlear nucleus, ventral nucleus of the lateral lemniscus, and inferior colliculus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p702]
- Octopus cells detect onsets of sounds and terminate in large excitatory calyceal endings on ventral nucleus of the lateral lemniscus neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p702]
- Fusiform cells in the dorsal cochlear nucleus integrate acoustic inputs with somatosensory information from the parallel fiber system to process spectral cues for sound localization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p704]

## Inferences

- Inference: The dorsal cochlear nucleus's integration of somatosensory and vestibular information through its molecular layer suggests a role in distinguishing self-generated sounds from external sounds.
- Inference: The morphological differences in dendritic architecture between cell types reflect their distinct integrative functions: bushy cells require large synaptic currents for precise timing, while octopus cells sum inputs from many fibers for broad temporal integration.
