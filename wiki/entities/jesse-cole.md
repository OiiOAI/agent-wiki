# Jesse Cole

## Summary

Jesse Cole is the owner of the Savannah Bananas baseball team, featured for his ability to wipe out $1.3 million in debt in less than two years. Michalowicz uses Cole as an example of what's possible when committing to debt elimination.

## Key facts

- Jesse Cole is the owner of the Savannah Bananas, featured in commercials after renovating a house with his wife. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p184]
- Michalowicz states: 'If he can wipe out a $1.3 million debt in less than two years, you can commit to burning through your debt.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p188]
