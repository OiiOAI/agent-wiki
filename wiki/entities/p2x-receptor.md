# P2X Receptor

## Summary

P2X receptors are ionotropic ATP-gated cation channels that mediate excitatory synaptic transmission at certain synapses. They are trimeric channels with only two transmembrane segments per subunit and are permeable to both monovalent cations and Ca2+, with a reversal potential near 0 mV.

## Key facts

- P2X receptors are ionotropic receptors for ATP that serve an excitatory function at selected synapses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p337]
- P2X receptor-channels are permeable to both monovalent cations and Ca2+, with a reversal potential near 0 mV. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p337]
