# Giacomo Rizzolatti

## Summary

Italian neuroscientist at the University of Parma who discovered mirror neurons in 1996 along with Leonardo Fogassi and Vittorio Gallese. His work on the mirror neuron system has had major implications for understanding action recognition, empathy, and the evolution of language.

## Key facts

- Rizzolatti and colleagues discovered mirror neurons in the premotor area (F5) of monkey brains in 1996. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p71]
- Rizzolatti and Michael Arbib proposed that the mirror system was fundamental for the development of speech and intentional communication. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p71]
