# Iain McGilchrist

## Summary

British psychiatrist, neuroscientist, and author of 'The Master and His Emissary.' Former Consultant Psychiatrist and Clinical Director at the Bethlem Royal & Maudsley Hospital, London. Has researched in neuroimaging at Johns Hopkins University Hospital, Baltimore. Previously taught English at Oxford University, where he was three times elected a Fellow of All Souls College.
