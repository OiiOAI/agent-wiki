# Robert Cialdini

## Summary

Distinguished psychology professor at Arizona State University who wrote 'Influence,' a popular book describing clever experiments in which man manipulates man to his detriment through intrinsic thinking flaws. He became a super-tenured 'Regents Professor' at a very young age through this work. Munger sent copies to all his children and gave Cialdini Berkshire stock.

## Key facts

- Cialdini's book 'Influence' sold hundreds of thousands of copies despite not claiming to improve sex life or make readers money. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p463]
- Munger sent copies of Cialdini's book to all his children and gave him a share of Berkshire Class A stock. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p463]
- Munger notes with irony that a huge number of Cialdini's books were bought by salesmen wanting to learn how to mislead customers more effectively. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p464]
- Cialdini's zoo experiment demonstrated that reciprocation of concessions tripled acceptance rates from 1/6 to 1/2. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p527]
