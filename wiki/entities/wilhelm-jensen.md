# Wilhelm Jensen

## Summary

German novelist and playwright (1837-1911) who wrote the novella 'Gradiva' (1903). Freud analyzed this work as demonstrating psychoanalytic principles about repression, delusion, and the return of the repressed. Jensen corresponded briefly with Freud after the publication of Freud's analysis.

## Key facts

- Wilhelm Jensen (1837-1911) was a North German playwright and novelist, respected but not regarded as of very great distinction. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2905]
- Jensen's 'Gradiva' was first published in 1903 and described by its author as a 'Pompeian phantasy'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2907]
- After Freud sent Jensen a copy of his psychoanalytic interpretation, a short correspondence followed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2905]
- Jensen's novel 'Gradiva' was published in 1903. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2944]
- Jensen denied any knowledge of psychoanalytic theories when asked by Freud's colleague. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2973]
- Jensen died in 1911. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2975]
