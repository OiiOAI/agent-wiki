# Greg Crabtree

## Summary

Greg Crabtree is the author of 'Simple Numbers, Straight Talk, Big Profits' whom Michalowicz cites for labor cost benchmarks. Crabtree recommends that Real Revenue must be 2.5x total labor cost for tech businesses and 4x for cheap-labor businesses.

## Key facts

- Greg Crabtree is the author of 'Simple Numbers, Straight Talk, Big Profits.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p177]
- According to Crabtree, for tech businesses, Real Revenue must be two and a half times total labor cost due to expensive, high-impact labor. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p177]
- For cheap-labor businesses (like fast food), Real Revenue must be four times total labor cost. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p177]
