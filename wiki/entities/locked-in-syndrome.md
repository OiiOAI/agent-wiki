# Locked-in Syndrome

## Summary

A condition in which patients are fully conscious and aware but completely paralyzed, unable to move or speak. Communication is possible through eye movements or brain-computer interfaces. The French Association of Locked-In Syndrome is mentioned as a support organization.

## Key facts

- Patients with locked-in syndrome are fully conscious but cannot move or speak due to brainstem damage. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p201-202]
- Communication is possible through eye movements, and brain-computer interfaces may enable more complex communication. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p215-216]
- The condition is distinct from vegetative and minimally conscious states in that consciousness is preserved. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p207-209]
