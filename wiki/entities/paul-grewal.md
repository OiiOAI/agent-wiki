# Paul Grewal

## Summary

Paul Grewal, MD is an internal medicine physician and speaker who co-authored 'Genius Foods' with Max Lugavere. He focuses on diet and lifestyle strategies for weight loss, metabolic health, and longevity. He has lost and kept off nearly one hundred pounds himself and helps others find sustainable paths to health.
