# Amanda Palmer

## Summary

Musician Amanda Palmer performed as a living statue in Harvard Square for six years, standing motionless on a box for 90 minutes at a time while wearing a wedding dress and face paint. She found that the challenge was mental rather than physical—she had to resist the urge to react to insults, grabbers, and even mosquitoes. She credits this training with strengthening her self-control, saying it gave her 'balls of steel' and taught her to 'stay focused' on one project at a time.
