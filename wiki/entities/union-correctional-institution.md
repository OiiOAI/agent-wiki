# Union Correctional Institution

## Summary

A maximum-security prison about forty miles north of Gainesville where Singer began leading Buddhist meditation groups starting in 1973. This came about when his neighbor Bob Tilchin asked him to visit inmate Jerry. The prison work became one of the most rewarding aspects of his life, lasting over thirty years.

## Key facts

- Union Correctional Institution is a maximum-security prison located about forty miles north of Gainesville. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p76]
- Singer's neighbor Bob Tilchin asked him to visit inmate Jerry as a favor, which led to the prison meditation work. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p76]
- Jerry organized a group of five to six inmates who wanted to meet for group meditation, which became the first Buddhist group in a North Florida prison. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p77]
- Singer felt a powerful increase in spiritual energy when entering the prisons and his meditations were deeper with inmates than alone at home. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p77]
- Baba Muktananda later visited UCI, and Siddha Yoga prison work worldwide traces back to this visit. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p88]
