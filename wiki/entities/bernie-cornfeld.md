# Bernie Cornfeld

## Summary

Bernie Cornfeld was a mutual fund salesman who in the 1960s started Investors Overseas Services (IOS), incorporated in Switzerland. He raised $2.5 billion and engaged in lavish personal consumption, ultimately falling into disgrace and becoming a cautionary example in investment management.

## Key facts

- Bernie Cornfeld, born in Turkey, came to America and became a mutual fund salesman in the 1950s. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p332]
- In the 1960s, he started IOS, incorporated in Switzerland, hiring thousands of salespeople who sold funds door-to-door in Europe, raising $2.5 billion while Cornfeld engaged in lavish personal consumption. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p332]
