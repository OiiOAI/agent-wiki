# Klein Associates

## Summary

Klein Associates is a research and development company founded by Gary Klein in 1978 to conduct studies of decision making. The company marshaled resources to edit, fact-check, and produce Sources of Power after Klein's first draft was completed during his sabbatical in Jerusalem in 1992.

## Key facts

- Klein founded Klein Associates in 1978 after leaving his job with the Air Force. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p14]
- Buzz Reed, the CEO of Klein Associates, marshaled the company's resources to edit, track down references, fact check, and produce Sources of Power. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p15]
- Klein Associates conducted the original 1984-1985 study of firefighter decision making that led to the RPD model. [raw/books/strategy/Sources of Power How People Make Decisions by Gary A. Klein .epub.pdf#p26]
