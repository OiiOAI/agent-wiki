# Angelman Syndrome

## Summary

Angelman syndrome is an inherited neurodevelopmental disorder caused by deletion of multiple genes on chromosome region 15q11-q13. The syndrome can include severe mental retardation, epilepsy, absence of speech, hyperactivity, and inappropriate laughter. It results from deletion of genes that are expressed only from the maternally derived chromosome. The most important gene appears to be UBE3A, encoding a ubiquitin-protein ligase expressed only from the maternally derived chromosome. Whether a child receives the abnormal chromosome from the father or mother determines disease expression.
