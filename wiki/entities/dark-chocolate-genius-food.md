# Dark Chocolate (Genius Food #4)

## Summary

Dark chocolate is among the richest natural food sources of magnesium and is high in flavanols, a type of polyphenol. Cocoa flavanols have been shown to reverse signs of cognitive aging, improve insulin sensitivity, vascular function, and blood flow to the brain. A study found that people who ate chocolate at least once a week had stronger cognitive performance on visual-spatial memory, working memory, and tests of abstract reasoning. For maximum benefit, choose chocolate with 80%+ cacao content that has not been processed with alkali (Dutch processing).

## Key facts

- Cacao beans are among the richest natural food sources of magnesium. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p150]
- Cocoa flavanols have been shown to reverse signs of cognitive aging and improve insulin sensitivity, vascular function, blood flow to the brain, and even athletic performance. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p150]
- Among nearly one thousand cognitively healthy people aged twenty-three to ninety-eight, those who ate chocolate at least once a week were found to have stronger cognitive performance on visual-spatial memory and working memory and tests of abstract reasoning. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p150]
- Dutch processing (cocoa processed with alkali) greatly degrades the phytonutrient content of cocoa. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p150]
- Look for chocolate with a cacao content above 80 percent for maximum benefit with minimal sugar. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p151]
