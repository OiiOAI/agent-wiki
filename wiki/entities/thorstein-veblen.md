# Thorstein Veblen

## Summary

Thorstein Veblen (1857-1929) was an American economist and social theorist best known for his book The Theory of the Leisure Class (1899). He analyzed the clash between business and industry and criticized the leisure class for conspicuous consumption. His work anticipated the current shift in social theory toward consumption.
