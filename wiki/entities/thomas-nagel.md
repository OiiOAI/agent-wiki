# Thomas Nagel

## Summary

Thomas Nagel is an American philosopher best known for his 1974 paper 'What is it like to be a bat?' which introduced the influential characterization of consciousness as involving subjective experience or 'what-it-is-like-ness'. His approach emphasizes phenomenology—the first-person, subjective properties of experience—over functional or behavioural criteria.

## Key facts

- Nagel argued that 'an organism has conscious mental states if and only if there is something that it is like to be that organism'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p21]
- Nagel's approach emphasizes phenomenology over functional properties of consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p21]
