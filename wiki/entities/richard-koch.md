# Richard Koch

## Summary

Richard Koch is a writer and consultant known for popularizing the 80/20 Principle, cited multiple times in Essentialism's Discern chapter.

## Key facts

- Richard Koch authored 'The 80/20 Principle: The Secret of Achieving More with Less' published by Nicholas Brealey in 1997. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p230]
- He also wrote 'The Power Laws' (2000), 'The 80/20 Revolution' (2002), and 'Living the 80/20 Way' (2004). [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p230]
- His work is cited as a basis for the book's approach to discerning the vital few from the trivial many. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p230]
