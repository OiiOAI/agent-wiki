# Juanita Uribe

## Summary

Juanita Uribe has dedicated her professional life to communicating complex things in accessible ways. She has worked with children and adults, designing and implementing public-private partnerships and programs in citizen, childhood, and youth education and sustainable development for governments, the private sector, and foundations in the U.S. and Latin America.
