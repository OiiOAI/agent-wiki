# Cheryl Amirault LeFave

## Summary

Cheryl Amirault LeFave was convicted along with her brother Gerald and mother Violet of molesting children at their Fells Acres daycare center. After prolonged legal battles including denied parole offers, new trials granted then overturned, she was finally freed in late October 1999 on time served, remaining a convicted felon. Her case highlighted issues of child suggestibility in legal proceedings.
