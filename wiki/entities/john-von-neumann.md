# John von Neumann

## Summary

John von Neumann was a mathematician who contributed to game theory and modeled it on poker. He was a key figure in the Manhattan Project, pioneered computer science, and developed the concept of mutually assured destruction. Duke identifies him as a hero whose work on decision-making under uncertainty remains influential.

## Key facts

- In his last ten years of life, von Neumann contributed to the Manhattan Project, pioneered hydrogen bomb physics, developed the first computers, and created the concept of mutually assured destruction (MAD). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L389-L395]
- Von Neumann collaborated with Oskar Morgenstern to publish Theory of Games and Economic Behavior in 1944, recognized as one of the most influential books of the twentieth century. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L391-L394]
- Von Neumann described chess as 'a well-defined form of computation' but said real games and real life 'consist of bluffing, of little tactics of deception,' which is what games are about in his theory. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L396-L401]
- Game theory has informed economics, psychology, sociology, political science, biomedical research, and business, with at least eleven economics Nobel laureates connected to it. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L400-L404]
- Duke notes that von Neumann was a poker player, and he modeled game theory on a stripped-down version of poker because of its elements of uncertainty, hidden information, and chance. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L360-L363]
- John von Neumann co-authored 'Theory of Games and Economic Behavior' with Oskar Morgenstern in 1944, establishing game theory as a field. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- Von Neumann worked at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, described as 'hallowed ground' in the book. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- Von Neumann shared physical characteristics with the character Dr. Strangelove and is cited as an influence on the character. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- Other influences on Dr. Strangelove include Wernher von Braun, Herman Kahn, Edward Teller, and Henry Kissinger. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- The influence of John von Neumann on game theory, and of game theory on modern economics, is unquestioned. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- At least eleven Nobel laureates in economics have been cited for work connected to or influenced by game theory. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
