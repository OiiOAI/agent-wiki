# Michael Phelps

## Summary

Michael Phelps is an American former competitive swimmer who won a record eight gold medals at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. His success was attributed to meticulously designed pre-race routines and mental visualization practices that made competition feel like 'another step in a pattern that started earlier that day.'

## Key facts

- Michael Phelps followed the same precise routine for every race: arriving two hours early, specific stretching patterns, never lying down before races, always sitting alone. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p186]
- Phelps practiced 'watching the videotape'—visualizing his perfect race in exquisite detail and slow motion every night and morning for years. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p187]
- Coach Bob Bowman designed both Phelps's physical routine and mental preparation, telling him 'When the race arrives, he's more than halfway through his plan'. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p187]
