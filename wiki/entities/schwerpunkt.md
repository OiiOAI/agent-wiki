# Schwerpunkt

## Summary

Schwerpunkt (literally 'hard point') is a German military concept meaning any device or concept that gives focus and direction to organizational efforts. It is the fourth attribute of the organizational climate for operational success. The distinguishing characteristic is that all other activities must support the focus, and people conducting these activities must understand what the main effort is. Conversely, subordinates are expected to use initiative to exploit opportunities that further the Schwerpunkt, even if it means setting aside previously issued orders. The concept often involves 'cheng maneuvers' (setting-up activities) followed by 'ch'i' (the decisive knockout punch). In business, the focus is often a concept rather than a unit, requiring mental as well as physical change.
