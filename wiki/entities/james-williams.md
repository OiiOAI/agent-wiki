# James Williams

## Summary

A former senior Google strategist who left the company to study human attention at Oxford University. He has become a leading critic of the attention economy and argues that digital detox solutions are insufficient.

## Key facts

- James Williams argued that a digital detox is 'not the solution, for the same reason that wearing a gas mask for two days a week outside isn't the answer to pollution.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p114]
- He stated that the solution is not primarily about personal abstinence but about 'the environmental changes that will really make the difference.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p114]
- Williams used a GPS metaphor: if a GPS took you slightly off course each time, you would stop using it, but social media 'takes us off track' and we continue using it anyway. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p153]
- At a gathering of tech designers, Williams asked 'How many of you want to live in the world you are designing?' — and no one raised their hand. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p134]
