# Brendan Dassey

## Summary

Brendan Dassey was a sixteen-year-old who confessed to assisting his uncle Steven Avery in the sexual assault and murder of Teresa Halbach in 2005. His case was featured in the Netflix documentary series 'Making a Murderer.' Dassey later recanted what he claimed was a coerced confession, but was convicted and sentenced to life in prison despite a judge's ruling that his confession was coerced.

## Key facts

- Brendan Dassey, age sixteen, confessed to assisting his uncle Steven Avery in the sexual assault and murder of photographer Teresa Halbach. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p183]
- Dassey later recanted what he claimed was a coerced confession, but was nonetheless convicted and sentenced to life in prison. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p183]
- A judge ruled that Dassey's confession was coerced and that he should be released, but this decision was overturned on appeal, and a clemency petition was denied in December 2019. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p183]
