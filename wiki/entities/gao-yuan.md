# Gao Yuan

## Summary

Gao Yuan (born Gao Jianhua, b. 1952) is the author of Born Red (1987), a nonfiction account of his experiences during the Cultural Revolution at Yizhen Middle School in communist China. As a young student, he initially dreamed of becoming a writer and was admitted to a prestigious key school. His story documents how he and fellow students became swept up in the Cultural Revolution, forming factions (East-Is-Red Corps and Red Rebels) that engaged in escalating violence. His friend Zongwei was tortured to death by classmates. The account illustrates how the Cultural Revolution failed because it attempted to uproot human nature, which merely reemerged in different forms.

## Key facts

- Gao Yuan was born Gao Jianhua in 1952 in communist China and attended Yizhen Middle School, a prestigious 'key school' where over 90% of students went to college. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p479]
- He wrote Born Red (1987), a nonfiction account of events at his school during the Cultural Revolution. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p492]
- His friend Zongwei, an artist, was captured and flailed with steel hooks until he died for refusing to admit crimes, with his death quickly covered up by the victorious faction. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p490]
- Gao Yuan joined the Red Rebels faction, participated in guerrilla activities against the East-Is-Red Corps, and was later tortured and forced to name fellow rebels. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p489-490]
- His father, a respected communist war veteran and government official, was also accused of being a revisionist and suffered through struggle meetings. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p486]
