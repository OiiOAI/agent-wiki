# Heinrich Obersteiner

## Summary

Heinrich Obersteiner (1847–1922) was a neuroanatomist and practicing neurologist who later became head of the Institute of Neurology in Vienna. Freud reviewed his monograph 'Der Hypnotismus mit besonderer Berücksichtigung seiner klinischen und forensischen Bedeutung' (1887). Obersteiner supported Freud's controversial views on cocaine and endorsed early conceptualizations of hysteria and therapeutic innovations.
