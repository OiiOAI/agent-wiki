# Robert DeWeese

## Summary

Robert DeWeese is an artist and former colleague of Phædrus at the Montana university. He lives with his wife Gennie in a house in a steep V canyon surrounded by pines in Montana. The house features an unfinished porch deck and an abstract iron sculpture. He taught art at the local college and is a friend who remained loyal despite Phædrus's breakdown. He attempted to assemble a barbecue rotisserie using factory instructions, which prompted the narrator's discussion of the difference between artistic (continuous) and technical (chunked) approaches to understanding.

## Key facts

- DeWeese lives 'in a V canyon in the mountain,' in 'a large grey house with an enormous abstract iron sculpture attached to one side,' with an unfinished porch deck and a horse grazing below. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p131]
- DeWeese is an artist who taught at the college and tried to persuade a sculptor to join the faculty. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p137]
- DeWeese brought over rotisserie assembly instructions he wanted 'totally damned' and the narrator notes that what really frustrated DeWeese was 'the lack of artistic continuity, something an engineer couldn't care less about.'.
