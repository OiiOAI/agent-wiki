# Paul Zientarski

## Summary

Physical education coordinator at Naperville Central High School, known as 'Mr. Z'. Former football coach who became a key implementer of the Naperville 'New PE' philosophy. He transformed PE at Central into a fitness-focused program with climbing walls, fitness centers, and social skills instruction including mandatory square-dancing classes.

## Key facts

- Zientarski is described as having 'the presence of Mike Ditka and Bill Parcells rolled into one formidable figure of authority.'. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p20]
- He recalls that approximately 65 percent of boys couldn't do one chin-up under the old PE system. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p29]
- He introduced mandatory square-dancing classes for freshmen designed to teach social skills through movement. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p29]
- Zientarski states: 'In our department, we create the brain cells. It's up to the other teachers to fill them.'. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p21]
- He states: 'My job is to make them know all of the things they need to know to keep themselves fit.'. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p24]
