# Endel Tulving

## Summary

Endel Tulving is a memory psychologist who proposed the crucial distinction between semantic and episodic memory, and between noetic and autonoetic consciousness. His work established that episodic memory depends on mental time travel — the ability to project oneself into personal past and future — which he linked to uniquely human autonoetic consciousness.

## Key facts

- Tulving proposed the distinction between semantic memory (facts) and episodic memory (personal experiences tied to specific what-where-when representations). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p291]
- Tulving proposed that consciousness of semantic memory requires noesis (awareness of facts), while episodic memory requires autonoesis (awareness of oneself as part of the experience). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p292]
- Tulving argued that mental time travel — recollecting past experiences and imagining future ones — is the key feature of autonoetic consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p293]
