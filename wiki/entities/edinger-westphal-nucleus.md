# Edinger-Westphal Nucleus

## Summary

The Edinger-Westphal nucleus contains preganglionic parasympathetic neurons that control pupillary constriction and lens accommodation through the ciliary ganglion. It lies at the midbrain level and receives input from the olivary pretectal nucleus for pupillary light reflexes.

## Key facts

- The Edinger-Westphal nucleus contains preganglionic neurons that control pupillary constriction and lens accommodation through the ciliary ganglion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1038]
- Light-responsive retinal ganglion cells project to the olivary pretectal nucleus, which projects to the Edinger-Westphal nucleus to mediate pupillary light reflexes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1040]
- Injury to the oculomotor nerve eliminates parasympathetic tone to that pupil, resulting in a fixed and dilated pupil. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1040]
