# David Rosenthal

## Summary

David Rosenthal proposed the higher-order theory of consciousness (HOT), which holds that conscious mental states arise when nonconscious lower-order states are represented by higher-order states. With Hakwan Lau, he developed the first formal neural account of HOT, implicating prefrontal cortex circuits. Rosenthal argues that consciousness requires language capacity for higher-order thoughts.

## Key facts

- Rosenthal's higher-order theory proposes that conscious awareness results when nonconscious first-order sensory information is re-represented by a higher-order state. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p276]
- Rosenthal argued that sincere verbal report is a reliable indicator of whether a psychological state is conscious. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p316]
- Rosenthal proposed that consciousness requires the capacity for higher-order thoughts about mental states, which requires language — creatures without language cannot be conscious in the way humans are. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p325]
