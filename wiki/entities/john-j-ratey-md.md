# John J. Ratey, MD

## Summary

An associate clinical professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School and author of 'Spark: The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the Brain,' known for his research on ADHD, aggression, and the connection between exercise and brain health.

## Key facts

- John J. Ratey is an associate clinical professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- He has a private practice in Cambridge, Massachusetts. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- He has published more than sixty papers in peer-review journals in the fields of psychiatry and psychopharmacology. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- In 1986 he founded the Boston Center for the Study of Autism. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- In 1988 he formed a new American Psychiatric Association study group on aggression related to his research on novel drug treatments for aggressive behavior. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- He coauthored 'Driven to Distraction' with Dr. Edward Hallowell in 1994. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- He coauthored 'Shadow Syndromes' with Catherine Johnson in 1997. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
- He authored 'A User's Guide to the Brain' in 2001. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p226]
