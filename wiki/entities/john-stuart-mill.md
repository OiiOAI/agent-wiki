# John Stuart Mill

## Summary

John Stuart Mill is a 19th-century philosopher and hero of thinking in bets whose work 'On Liberty' remains relevant to understanding the importance of diversity of opinion. His insight that combining people with different viewpoints in a group exposes them to diverse opinions, allows testing of alternative hypotheses, and moves toward accuracy forms the foundation of productive decision groups.

## Key facts

- According to Mill, 'The only way in which a human being can make some approach to knowing the whole of a subject, is by hearing what can be said about it by persons of every variety of opinion, and studying all modes in which it can be looked at by every character of mind.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1335-L1345]
- Mill wrote that 'No wise man ever acquired his wisdom in any mode but this; nor is it in the nature of human intellect to become wise in any other manner.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1335-L1345]
- Diversity and dissent are not only checks on fallibility, but the only means of testing the ultimate truth of an opinion. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1335-L1345]
