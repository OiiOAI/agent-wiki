# Jim Owens

## Summary

Jim Owens was a branch president at a Gainesville bank who personally championed Michael Singer's construction loan application when it was rejected by normal standards. He drove out to Singer's property, looked at the Temple and house, and hand-carried the application through the loan committee. Ten years later, the roles reversed when Singer encountered Owens working at a video store and made him a loan to help his business.

## Key facts

- Jim Owens was a branch president at a bank in downtown Gainesville who personally supported Singer's $20,000 construction loan application. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p105]
- He drove out to Singer's land and looked in the windows of the house and Temple building before personally presenting the application to the loan committee. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p105]
- Owens stated that a community bank should try to support community businesses. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p105]
- Ten years later, Singer encountered Owens working at a video store where he was needing a $20,000 working capital loan. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p106]
- Singer made Owens the loan, essentially repaying his act of kindness from a decade earlier. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p107]
