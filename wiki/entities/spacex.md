# SpaceX

## Summary

SpaceX was founded by Elon Musk in 2002 to reduce the cost of space access through reusability. The company's strategy focused on identifying the crux—reentry and reusability—and designing a solution using extra fuel to enable soft landings. By 2018, SpaceX achieved a 23x cost reduction compared to the space shuttle, revolutionizing the launch industry.

## Key facts

- SpaceX was founded in 2002 with a philosophy of complete redesigns done in a low-cost, spare manner. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p13]
- SpaceX would not adapt intercontinental ballistic missiles and would not be one of thousands of contractors. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p13]
- Falcon 9 became the first rocket to gain orbit and then turn around for a soft landing on its tail in 2015. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p14]
- By 2018, Falcon Heavy cut Falcon 9's cost per pound in half. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p14]
- SpaceX carried two NASA astronauts to the International Space Station on May 30, 2020. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p14]
