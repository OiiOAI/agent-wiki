# Jeffrey Alexander

## Summary

Jeffrey C. Alexander is an American sociologist known for developing 'multidimensional sociology' as a framework for integrating micro and macro levels of analysis. His model combines the problem of order (micro-macro continuum) with the problem of action (materialist-idealist continuum), resulting in four levels of social analysis. Alexander accords priority to the collective-normative level and argues that any individual or micro perspective must be rejected because it ends with 'randomness and complete unpredictability' rather than order.

## Key facts

- Jeffrey Alexander developed 'multidimensional sociology' as a 'new theoretical logic for sociology' affecting sociological thought at every level. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p378]
- His model combines the problem of order (individual-collective) with the problem of action (instrumental-normative), yielding four levels of social analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p379]
- Alexander argues that according privilege to the micro levels is 'a theoretical mistake.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p379]
- He argues that 'the general framework for social theory can be derived only from a collectivist perspective.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p379]
- Alexander's sympathies lie with the collective-normative level, and he believes 'the hope for combining collective order and individual voluntarism lies with the normative, rather than the rationalist tradition.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p379]
