# Alexander Luria

## Summary

Alexander Luria (1902-1977) was a pioneering Russian neuropsychologist who founded the field. He spent 30 years studying Solomon Shereshevsky, documenting his remarkable memory in 'The Mind of a Mnemonist.' He sent Brenda Milner a note describing her work on H.M. as awakening 'the sleeping beauty of the brain.' His work contributed to understanding how the brain enables complex mental functions.

## Key facts

- Alexander Luria studied Solomon Shereshevsky for 30 years and documented his remarkable memory in 'The Mind of a Mnemonist' (1968). [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p73]
- Luria sent Brenda Milner a note saying 'Memory was the sleeping beauty of the brain and now she is awake.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p41]
