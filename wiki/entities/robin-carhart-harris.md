# Robin Carhart-Harris

## Summary

Robin Carhart-Harris is a British neuroscientist at Imperial College London who pioneered research on psychedelic states and consciousness. His work has shown that psilocybin and LSD cause dramatic changes in brain connectivity patterns, breaking down the organized networks characteristic of normal waking consciousness. He collaborated with Seth to test whether psychedelic-induced content changes affect measures of conscious level.

## Key facts

- Carhart-Harris discovered that psychedelic states involve uncoupling of normally coordinated brain networks and integration of normally independent regions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p54]
- A 2016 collaboration found that psilocybin, LSD, and ketamine all increased algorithmic complexity measures of brain activity above waking rest baseline. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p55]
- Classic psychedelics (LSD, psilocybin, mescaline, DMT) work primarily by affecting the brain's serotonin system, binding to 5-HT2a receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p54]
