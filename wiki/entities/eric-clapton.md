# Eric Clapton

## Summary

Eric Clapton is a celebrated English rock guitarist and songwriter who struggled with alcoholism for decades before achieving sobriety through Alcoholics Anonymous. His story illustrates the power of bright lines, surrender to a higher power, and social support in overcoming addiction. At Hazelden treatment center, he experienced a transformative moment when he knelt and asked for God's help, after which his compulsion to drink vanished. He later demonstrated the power of 'bright lines' by telling an AA group that he never doubted he would remain sober, even on the day his son died—a statement that helped another woman find the will to save herself.
