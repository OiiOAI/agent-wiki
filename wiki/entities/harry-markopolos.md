# Harry Markopolos

## Summary

Harry Markopolos is an independent fraud investigator who spent nearly a decade trying to alert regulators about Bernie Madoff's Ponzi scheme, starting in 2000. He represents the 'Holy Fool' archetype—someone who does not default to truth and can therefore see deception that others miss.

## Key facts

- Markopolos first suspected Madoff in the late 1980s when he couldn't replicate Madoff's trading strategy despite having relationships with major investment banks. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p58]
- Markopolos testified to Congress in February 2009 that he had provided the SEC with sufficient evidence to shut down the Madoff Ponzi scheme as early as May 2000. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p56]
- Markopolos stated he never 'entertained the thought that it was truly fraudulent' despite having significant doubts about Madoff. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p55]
- Markopolos described himself as a 'quant' who prefers not to meet people personally, saying 'For me, math is truth.'. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p57]
- After his failures with the SEC, Markopolos began carrying a handgun and took extensive security precautions, believing his life was in danger. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p62]
