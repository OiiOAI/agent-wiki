# Aza Raskin

## Summary

A technology designer who invented 'infinite scroll' — the feature that automatically loads more content as users reach the bottom of a page. He later became deeply troubled by how his invention contributed to screen addiction and now advocates for fundamental changes to tech business models.

## Key facts

- Aza Raskin designed infinite scroll, which automatically loads more content when users reach the bottom of a page, removing natural stopping points. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p130]
- He calculated that infinite scroll causes an additional 200,000 human lifetimes worth of time to be spent scrolling every single day. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p131]
- Raskin said he felt 'sort of dirty' after realizing his invention was being used to drain people's time rather than serve their actual interests. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p131]
- He described surveillance capitalism using the metaphor of a 'voodoo doll' inside Facebook and Google's servers that is assembled from users' metadata and used to predict and manipulate their behavior. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p137]
- Raskin said: 'I think we're in the process of reverse-engineering ourselves. We discovered a way to open up the human skull, find the strings that control us, and start pulling on our own marionette strings.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p153]
