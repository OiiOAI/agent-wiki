# Guillain-Barré Syndrome

## Summary

Guillain-Barré syndrome is an acute inflammatory demyelinating neuropathy that typically follows respiratory infection or infectious diarrhea. It is attributed to autoimmune attack on peripheral nerves by circulating antibodies. Treatment includes infusions of gamma globulin and plasmapheresis to remove pathogenic antibodies.

- The syndrome is attributed to autoimmune attack on peripheral nerves by circulating antibodies. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1462]
