# Paul Broca

## Summary

French physician after whom Broca's area (the speech production center in the left inferior frontal gyrus) is named. McGilchrist notes that the naming was 'probably unjust' since Broca's observations were anticipated by Marc Dax approximately 25 years earlier.

## Key facts

- Broca's area in the left frontal lobe was named after him, though McGilchrist notes this naming was 'probably unjust' as his observations were anticipated by Marc Dax. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p41]
- Broca and Dax observed that those who suffered stroke or other damage to this part of the brain tended to lose their faculty of speech. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p41]
