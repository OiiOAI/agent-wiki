# Stewart Resnick

## Summary

Stewart Resnick is chairman of privately held Roll International Corporation, a serial entrepreneur who with his wife Lynda has built successful companies across diverse industries including alarm services, Teleflora, Franklin Mint, agricultural businesses (pistachios, almonds, pomegranates), POM Wonderful pomegranate juice, Fiji Water, and Suterra. He defines a business as interesting when he can see ways to increase its value, and his approach involves complex coordinated maneuvers over extended time periods.

## Key facts

- Stewart and Lynda Resnick created Teleflora's concept of flowers in keepsake containers and expanded it from one-tenth the size of FTD to twice its size. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p65]
- Resnick defines a business as interesting when he can see ways to increase its value—the typical nut farmer cannot control his own destiny but Roll's large holding allowed them to earn back costs of research and market development. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p65]
- The seven-to-ten year lag in agricultural competitive response gave Roll time to invest in planting, branding, processing, and merchandising, then build large-scale processing facilities with scale economies. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p65]
