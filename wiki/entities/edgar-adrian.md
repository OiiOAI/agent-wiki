# Edgar Adrian

## Summary

Edgar Adrian discovered the all-or-none properties of action potentials in sensory neurons with Yngve Zotterman in 1925, demonstrating that the frequency of firing varies with stimulus strength. This established the fundamental principle that stimulus intensity is encoded in neural firing frequency.

## Key facts

- In 1925 Adrian and Zotterman discovered that the frequency of action potentials varies with the strength of the stimulus and time of stimulation, with stronger stimuli evoking higher frequency firing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p513]
- Adrian and Zotterman found that transmission in nerve fibers occurred according to impulse frequency modulation (FM), anticipating FM radio technology by twenty years. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p513]
