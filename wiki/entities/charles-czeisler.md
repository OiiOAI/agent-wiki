# Charles Czeisler

## Summary

Charles Czeisler is a Harvard professor and researcher who became one of the world's leading experts on sleep. His groundbreaking research in 1981 demonstrated that sleep deprivation causes severe cognitive impairment, and he has advised organizations from the Boston Red Sox to the U.S. Secret Service on sleep science.

## Key facts

- In 1981, while researching hormone release timing, Czeisler discovered that sleep-deprived participants took 10 times longer to respond to prompts—4-6 seconds instead of 0.25 seconds. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p72]
- He discovered that after 19 hours awake, people become as cognitively impaired as legally drunk individuals. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p72]
- He found that even 1-2 hours of sleep loss per night for just 1-2 weeks produces equivalent impairment to total sleep deprivation. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p75]
- He discovered 'attentional blinks' and 'local sleep'—states where parts of the brain are awake while other parts are asleep, causing people to appear alert but be unable to see or think clearly. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p73]
- He argues that every time we turn on a light, we are 'inadvertently taking a drug that affects how we will sleep.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p82]
- He states that our economic system has 'become dependent on sleep-depriving people' and returning to healthy sleep would be 'an earthquake' for consumer capitalism. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p83]
- He runs the unit on sleep problems at a major Boston hospital and teaches at Harvard Medical School. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p72]
