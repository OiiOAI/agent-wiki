# Felicity Aston

## Summary

Felicity Aston is a British polar explorer, Antarctic scientist, author, speaker, and expedition leader. Born in Hildenborough, Kent, her curiosity for cold climates began despite growing up without snow or glaciers nearby. After university, she joined the British Antarctic Survey and spent 39 months in Antarctica. She has completed numerous expeditions including a 360-mile race across Arctic Canada (2005), Siberia (2007), and the Marathon des Sables Sahara ultramarathon (2009). In 2012, she became the first woman to ski alone across Antarctica, completing the 1,744-kilometer journey in 59 days and earning a Guinness World Record.
