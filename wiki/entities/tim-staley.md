# Tim Staley

## Summary

Tim Staley was a professional software developer and senior IT consultant who moved his family to High Springs, Florida, and was hired by Personalized Programming in 1994. A rocket scientist who had worked at Harris Corporation writing missile guidance systems code, he became the solution to both the software development problems and the eventual need to completely rewrite The Medical Manager into a new product called Intergy.
