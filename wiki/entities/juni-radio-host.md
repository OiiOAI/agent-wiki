# Juni (Radio Host)

## Summary

Juni is a radio talk show host who overcame a severe sugar addiction using Behavior Design methods. After her mother died from diabetes complications, Juni used ice cream and cake to suppress her grief, gaining 15 pounds and impairing her running and professional performance. She used the Behavior Change Masterplan systematically—creating positive habits first, then addressing specific sugar-eating behaviors one by one. She ultimately eliminated sugar entirely and completed her second marathon.

## Key facts

- Juni's sugar habit included double caramel macchiatos for lunch, Funyuns during commercial breaks, and ice cream for dinner. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p220]
- Juni joined Fogg's Behavior Design Boot Camp hoping for professional insights to share with her team, but applied the techniques to her own well-being. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p222]
- What ultimately worked for Juni was addressing her grief prompt at its root through journaling and reaching out to friends on social media. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p222]
- Juni celebrated going one day without sugar, then a couple of hours, understanding the importance of lowering expectations. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p222]
- She untangled her sugar habit knot starting with the easiest specific behavior (no dessert after dinner one day, then two days), working up to removing ice cream from her home freezer. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p224]
- After Juni changed her habits, her son (who is autistic) began singing for the first time, her husband gave up soda, and she completed her second marathon in Austin, Texas. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p248]
