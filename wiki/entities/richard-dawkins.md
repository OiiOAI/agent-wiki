# Richard Dawkins

## Summary

Richard Dawkins is a biologist who coined the term 'meme' in his 1976 book The Selfish Gene. His work on genes and memes profoundly influenced Hawkins' worldview, particularly the idea that evolution is about gene survival rather than species survival, and that cultural replication through memes parallels genetic replication.

## Key facts

- Richard Dawkins introduced the term 'meme' to represent cultural units that replicate and evolve like genes through culture. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p171]
- In The Selfish Gene, Dawkins argues that evolution is not about survival of species but about survival of individual genes. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p188]
- Dawkins' theory implies that complex animals exist solely to help genes replicate. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p188]
- Dawkins wrote the foreword to A Thousand Brains. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p238]
- Dawkins was born in Nairobi of British parents. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- He completed his doctorate under Niko Tinbergen, a Nobel Prize-winning ethologist, at Oxford. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- From 1967 to 1969 he was an Assistant Professor at the University of California at Berkeley. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- He later became University Lecturer and Reader in Zoology at New College, Oxford before becoming the first holder of the Simonyi Chair. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- He is an Emeritus Fellow of New College, Oxford. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- He is a Fellow of both the Royal Society and the Royal Society of Literature. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- His publications include The Extended Phenotype (1982), The Blind Watchmaker (1986), River Out of Eden (1995), Climbing Mount Improbable (1996), Unweaving the Rainbow (1998), The Ancestor's Tale (2004), The God Delusion (2006), and The Greatest Show on Earth (2009). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
- Awards include the 1987 Royal Society of Literature Award, the 1990 Michael Faraday Award, the 1997 International Cosmos Prize, and the 2009 Nierenberg Prize for Science in the Public Interest. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p2]
