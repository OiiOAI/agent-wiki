# Superior Colliculi

## Summary

The superior colliculi are part of the midbrain tectum that, along with the periaqueductal gray, constitute the neuroanatomical focus of the intrinsic motor SELF. They contain a basic motor mapping system that interacts with visual, auditory, vestibular, and somatosensory systems.

## Key facts

- The deeper layers of the colliculi constitute a basic motor mapping system of the body that interacts with visual, auditory, vestibular, and somatosensory systems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The superior colliculi flexibly harvest information about the location of visual stimuli while the underlying motor system generates appropriate orienting movements using a stable set of action coordinates. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The sensory maps of the tectum shift markedly in reference to stable motor coordinates when the motor map initiates specific actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The somatomotor eye-movement map bordering the PAG is intrinsically a more stable tectal circuit than the overlying sensory maps of the superior colliculi. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p165]
- The deep tectal zones and underlying PAG are more richly connected with frontal motor areas where plans and intentions are generated than with posterior sensory areas where perceptions are constructed. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p166]
- The superior colliculi receive visual information directly from the retina in their top layer and from visual cortices just a few layers deeper, plus auditory and massive somatosensory information. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p265]
- The integrative activity of the superior colliculi maps the temporal appearance and spatial position of an object as well as varied aspects of body state. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p265]
- In species with little cortical development, the superior colliculi might be the source of simple core consciousness accompanying attentive behaviors. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p266]

## Inferences

- Inference: The stability of the somatomotor system indicates it has primacy in the evolution of psychobehavioral coherence.
- Inference: Motor maps may be more central to the SELF than incoming somatosensory processes.
- Inference: humans, the superior colliculi probably contribute to but cannot independently support core consciousness—they require thalamic and cingulate structures to be functional.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The extent to which the superior colliculi directly support consciousness versus merely contribute to orienting responses that enable conscious perception remains uncertain.
