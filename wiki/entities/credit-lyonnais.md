# Crédit Lyonnais

## Summary

A major French state-owned bank that became a symbol of poor judgment and political interference in financial decision-making. The bank required repeated government bailouts, with Fukuyama noting that key loans were made for political motives rather than commercial viability.

## Key facts

- Crédit Lyonnais exemplified poor judgment on the part of banks and financial institutions in the public sector. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p399]
- A number of key loans at Crédit Lyonnais appear to have been made for political motives that would not exist for a private sector bank. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p400]
