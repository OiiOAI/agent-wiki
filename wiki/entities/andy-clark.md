# Andy Clark

## Summary

Andy Clark is a professor of cognitive philosophy with eclectic interests spanning philosophy, neuroscience, psychology, and artificial intelligence. He is the author of 'The Experience Machine: How Our Minds Predict and Shape Reality' (2023), which presents the theory of predictive processing.

## Key facts

- Andy Clark is a professor of cognitive philosophy with interests spanning philosophy, neuroscience, psychology, and artificial intelligence. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p7]
- He has been helping construct the theory of predictive processing for the past decade. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p8]
- Andy Clark co-authored the extended mind paper with David Chalmers in the early 1990s at Washington University in St. Louis. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p163]
- Clark has argued that human beings are natural-born cyborgs whose daily abilities are realized by complex webs of biological and nonbiological structures. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p148]
- Clark argues that predictive brains do not care whether key information is stored internally or outside in notebooks, apps, and GPS systems—what matters is that information is predictably available when needed. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p144]
- Andy Clark is Professor of Cognitive Philosophy at the University of Sussex, UK and Macquarie University, NSW, Australia. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
- He is the author of several books including Surfing Uncertainty: Prediction, Action, and the Embodied Mind and Being There: Putting Brain, Body, and World Together Again. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
- He is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh and of the British Academy. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
- He is an occasional academic consultant with Google U.K. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
- He was principal investigator on a European Research Council Advanced Grant titled 'Expecting Ourselves: Embodied Prediction and the Construction of Conscious Experience'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
- His current research interests include embodied and extended cognition, robotics, computational neuroscience, and computational psychiatry. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p323]
