# Charlotte Selver

## Summary

Charlotte Selver developed the practice of Sensory Awareness, an approach to allowing one's inner functioning and coming more into contact with one's true self. She had notable students including psychiatrists Erich Fromm, Fritz Perls, and Clara Thompson. Her work influenced understanding of how giving up ego involvement and self-pressure paradoxically allows people to change and succeed.
