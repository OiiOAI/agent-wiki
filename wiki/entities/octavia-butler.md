# Octavia Butler

## Summary

Octavia Estelle Butler (1947-2006) was an acclaimed science fiction writer who won multiple Hugo and Nebula awards and received a MacArthur 'Genius' Fellowship in 1995. Born in Pasadena, California, she overcame shyness and dyslexia to become one of the most influential voices in science fiction, pioneering the Afrofuturism genre. Her meticulous commonplace books containing 9,062 items in 386 boxes were donated to the Huntington Library after her death.
