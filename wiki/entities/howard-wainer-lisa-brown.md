# Howard Wainer and Lisa Brown

## Summary

Howard Wainer and Lisa Brown are statisticians who in 2006 revised Lord's paradox to make the research question well-defined by focusing on the effect of diet (rather than gender) on weight gain.

## Key facts

- Wainer and Brown changed the story so that the quantity of interest was the effect of diet on weight gain, with the two ellipses representing two dining halls with different diets. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L1910-L1920]
- In their revision, initial weight affects diet choice (arrow from W_I to D), making initial weight a confounder rather than a mediator. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L1930-L1940]
- Wainer and Brown concluded that the data alone cannot determine whom to believe, but Pearl argues a causal diagram can settle the issue. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L1920-L1930]
