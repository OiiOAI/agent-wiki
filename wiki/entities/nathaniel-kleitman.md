# Nathaniel Kleitman

## Summary

Nathaniel Kleitman was Aserinsky's graduate school advisor at the University of Chicago and co-discoverer of REM sleep. He continued to advance sleep research and published the seminal paper connecting REM sleep to dreaming with his student William Dement in 1957.

## Key facts

- Kleitman was Aserinsky's graduate advisor at the University of Chicago. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p58]
- Kleitman and Dement published the paper in 1957 showing the connection between REM sleep and dreaming. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p58]
- REM sleep recurs approximately every 90 minutes throughout the night. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p60]
