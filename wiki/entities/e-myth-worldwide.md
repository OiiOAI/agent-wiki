# E-Myth Worldwide

## Summary

E-Myth Worldwide is the company founded by Michael E. Gerber in 1977 to help small business owners apply the principles outlined in this book. The company has developed the Business Development Process and the E-Myth Mastery Program, which Gerber claims has assisted over 25,000 small business owners in transforming their businesses.

## Key facts

- E-Myth Worldwide was founded in 1977, approximately eight years before The E-Myth was published. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p2]
- Over 25,000 small business owners have been assisted through enrollment in the E-Myth Mastery Program. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p9]
- Gerber states that no organization has had more direct experience applying the lessons of the Turn-Key Revolution and the Business Development Process to small business development. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p10]
