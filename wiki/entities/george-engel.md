# George Engel

## Summary

George Engel was a physician who developed the biosocial model of illness, which expanded the purely biological understanding of disease to include psychological and social factors. His model recognizes that genetic, biochemical, personality, emotion, cognition, family, and culture all interact to cause illness, paving the way for understanding how the mind can affect the body.

## Key facts

- Engel developed the biosocial model of illness, which is now the prevalent view in medicine. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p101]
- The biosocial model recognizes that biological (genetic, biochemical), psychological (personality, emotion, cognition), and social factors (family, culture) interact to cause illness. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p101]
