# Stephen Guise

## Summary

Stephen Guise is the author of 'Mini Habits: Smaller Habits, Bigger Results' and the creator of the Mini Habits system. He struggled for ten years to make exercise a habit before discovering the power of extremely small steps on December 28, 2012. His system has helped readers worldwide develop consistent habits in fitness, writing, reading, and other areas through the One Push-up Challenge.

## Key facts

- Stephen Guise struggled for ten years to make exercise a habit before his breakthrough with the One Push-up Challenge. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p15]
- He started mini habits on December 28, 2012, after failing to motivate himself for a 30-minute workout and deciding to do just one push-up instead. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p15]
- From the One Push-up Challenge, he developed the ability to do 16 pull-ups in a row, improved his physique, and built daily reading and writing habits. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p13]
- He recommends pursuing a maximum of four mini habits at once and currently uses three: writing 50 words for anything, writing 50 words toward a book, and reading at least two pages daily. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p82]
- He uses a large desk calendar to track his mini habits, checking off every day he completes them and noting gym days. [raw/books/productivity/Mini Habits_ Smaller Habits, Bigger Results - Guise, Stephen.pdf#p102]
