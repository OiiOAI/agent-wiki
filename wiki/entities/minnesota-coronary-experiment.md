# Minnesota Coronary Experiment

## Summary

A landmark clinical trial conducted from 1968-1973 that tested the effects of a low-saturated-fat diet on cardiovascular risk. The study's data was largely forgotten until 2013 when it was recovered and re-analyzed, challenging the traditional diet-heart hypothesis by showing no cardiovascular benefit from replacing saturated fat with polyunsaturated fat.

## Key facts

- The Minnesota Coronary Survey tested the effect of lipid lowering by diet on cardiovascular risk from 1968-1973. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p462]
- Recovered data from the Minnesota Coronary Experiment led to re-evaluation of the traditional diet-heart hypothesis. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p462]
- The re-analysis was published in the BMJ in 2016. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p462]

## Inferences

- Inference: The recovery and re-analysis of this historical trial data provided important evidence questioning the universal recommendation to reduce saturated fat intake.
