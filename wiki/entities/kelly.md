# Kelly

## Summary

A 27-year-old rape survivor whose case is used throughout the book to illustrate survival signals and the gift of fear. She was counseled by the author after being raped at gunpoint by an intruder who had gained entry by pretending to help with groceries. Her story demonstrates how intuition saved her life when she followed a survival signal to escape.

## Key facts

- Kelly was raped at gunpoint by an intruder who entered her apartment building by waiting near the entrance, then offered to help carry her groceries. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p10]
- Kelly felt apprehensive from the moment she heard the stranger's voice but dismissed her intuition as having no good reason. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p11]
- The attacker closed her window before leaving the bedroom, which was her survival signal that he planned to kill her rather than leave as promised. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p11]
- She escaped by following him silently, pulling the sheet from the bed, and walking out her front door into a neighbor's unlocked apartment. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p11]
- The author learned from Kelly's case that listening to one small survival signal saved her life while failing to follow many others had put her at risk. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p12]
