# William Beecher Scoville

## Summary

William Beecher Scoville (1906–1984) was an American neurosurgeon who established the Department of Neurosurgery at Hartford Hospital in 1939. He performed the bilateral medial temporal-lobe resection on Henry Molaison in 1953. Scoville held a BA from Yale University and MD from the University of Pennsylvania, and trained at leading medical centers including New York Cornell Hospital, Bellevue Hospital, Mass General, and Lahey Clinic. He was known as an independent thinker who rode motorcycles and had a passion for old cars. Scoville later expressed deep regret about the outcome of Henry's operation, calling it 'a tragic mistake' in a 1974 lecture.
