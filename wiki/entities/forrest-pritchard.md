# Forrest Pritchard

## Summary

A sustainable farmer who runs Smith Meadows farm, demonstrating sophisticated tool selection through analyzing not just direct costs but opportunity costs and long-term effects.

## Key facts

- Pritchard sold his hay baler because while direct costs were a wash, buying hay instead provided better soil fertility through manure and avoided compacting soil with heavy machinery. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p112]
- He assesses tools by considering direct costs, opportunity costs (what else could be done with that time), and secondary value effects on long-term goals. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p112]

## Inferences

- Inference: Pritchard's example illustrates that skilled laborers apply sophisticated analysis to tool decisions, and knowledge workers should do the same with network tools.
