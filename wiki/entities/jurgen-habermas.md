# Jürgen Habermas

## Summary

Jürgen Habermas (1929-) is a German sociologist and philosopher who is the most prominent contemporary critical theorist. His work focuses on communicative action, discourse theory, and the relationship between knowledge and human interests.

## Key facts

- Habermas began as Theodor Adorno's research assistant in 1955. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Habermas argued that Marx failed to distinguish between work (purposive-rational action) and social interaction (communicative action), reducing the latter to the former. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p181]
- Habermas argued that communicative action, not purposive-rational action (work), is the most distinctive and most pervasive human phenomenon and the foundation of all sociocultural life. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p181]
- Habermas distinguished among three knowledge systems: analytic science (oriented to technical prediction and control), humanistic knowledge (oriented to understanding), and critical knowledge (oriented to human emancipation). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p180]
- Habermas developed the 'ideal speech situation' in which force or power does not determine which arguments win out; instead the better argument emerges victorious. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p184]
- Habermas adopted a consensus theory of truth rather than a copy or reality theory of truth. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p184]
- Habermas drew parallels between what psychoanalysts do at the individual level and what he thinks needs to be done at the societal level, seeing psychoanalysis as a theory of distorted communication. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p182]
- Habermas was born on June 18, 1929, in Düsseldorf, Germany, to a middle-class traditional family. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p466]
- Habermas received his doctorate from the University of Bonn in 1954 and worked as a journalist for two years. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p466]
- In 1956 Habermas arrived at the Institute for Social Research in Frankfurt and became associated with the Frankfurt School, working as a research assistant to Theodor Adorno. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p466]
- Habermas defines society as both a system and a lifeworld, differentiated in the course of social evolution. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p443]
- The rationalization of the life-world involves growth in the rationality of communicative action, increasingly freed from normative constraint and relying on everyday language. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p444]
- Delinguistified media (especially money and power) come to replace everyday language in coordinating action. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p444]
- Habermas sees modernity as an 'unfinished project,' implying far more remains to be done before considering postmodernity. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p465]
- The hallmark of modernity to Habermas is the colonization of the life-world by the system. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p469]
- Habermas focuses on communication rather than work, viewing it as a more general process than Marx's labor. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p468]

## Inferences

- Inference: Habermas's theory integrates Marxian, Weberian, and critical theory traditions into a synthetic framework.
- Inference: The colonization concept represents Habermas's critique of how system rationality (money, administrative power) encroaches upon communicative rationality in the life-world.
