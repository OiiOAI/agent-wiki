# Jill Price

## Summary

Jill Price (formerly known as AJ in research publications) was the first individual identified with highly superior autobiographical memory (HSAM) in 2006. She could recall her past experiences with extraordinary detail and accuracy, describing her memory as 'non-stop, uncontrollable and totally exhausting.'

## Key facts

- Price wrote to memory researcher James McGaugh describing her ability to recall her past since age eleven, noting she automatically traveled back to any date she saw. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p58]
- In testing, Price could correctly identify the day of the week for any date from 1974 onward and provide accurate autobiographical details. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p58]
- Price published her memoir 'The Woman Who Can't Forget' in 2008, revealing her identity and experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p58]
- Price described her recollections as 'non-stop, uncontrollable and totally exhausting' and noted she 'always needed order in her life.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p59]
