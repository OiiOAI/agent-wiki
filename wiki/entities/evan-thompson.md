# Evan Thompson

## Summary

Evan Thompson is a Canadian philosopher specializing in phenomenology, cognitive science, and philosophy of mind. He is a professor at the University of Toronto. He co-authored 'The Embodied Mind: Cognitive Science and Human Experience' (1991) with Francisco Varela and Eleanor Rosch. This book, 'Mind in Life,' was originally intended as a follow-up co-authored with Varela, but Thompson completed it alone after Varela's death in 2001. His work focuses on the enactive approach, neurophenomenology, and the integration of phenomenological philosophy with neuroscience.
