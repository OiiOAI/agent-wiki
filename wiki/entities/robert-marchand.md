# Robert Marchand

## Summary

Robert Marchand was a French centenarian cyclist who set age-group world records in cycling, demonstrating that VO2 max can be improved at extremely advanced ages through dedicated training.

## Key facts

- In 2012, at age 101, Robert Marchand set an age-group world record by cycling 24.25 kilometers in one hour. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p257]
- Following a strict program designed by top coaches and physiologists, he managed to boost his VO2 max from 31 to 35 ml/kg/min, putting him in the elite 2.5% of men in their eighties. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p257]
- Two years later, now 103, he returned and broke his own record, riding almost 27 kilometers in an hour. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p258]
