# Wagner Dodge

## Summary

Wagner Dodge was the supervisor of a smokejumper team during the Mann Gulch fire of 1949. He invented a survival tactic of setting a backfire but was unable to communicate it to his team in time, resulting in most crew members perishing while he survived.

## Key facts

- Dodge realized his team couldn't outrun the fire and invented a backfire tactic on the spot, setting fire to create burned ground he could take refuge in. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p222]
- He tried to get his men to join him but was unable to explain the logic of his tactic under time pressure. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p222]
- Dodge dove into the ashes and survived; the rest of the crew continued trying to outrace the flames and perished. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p222]
