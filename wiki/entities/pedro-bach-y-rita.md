# Pedr

## Summary

Pedro Bach-y-Rita was Paul Bach-y-Rita's father, a Catalan poet and scholar. In 1959, at age 65, he suffered a massive stroke that paralyzed his face and half his body and left him unable to speak. Doctors said he had no hope of recovery. His son George, a medical student, took him to Mexico and designed an intensive home rehabilitation program. Pedro recovered fully and returned to teaching at City College in New York, then San Francisco State, working until age 70. He died at 72 from a heart attack during mountain climbing. His autopsy revealed 97% of nerves from his cerebral cortex to his spine were destroyed—damage that should have caused permanent paralysis, demonstrating the brain had reorganized itself.
