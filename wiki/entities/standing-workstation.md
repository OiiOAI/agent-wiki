# Standing Workstation

## Summary

A Standing Workstation is an ergonomic setup that allows users to work while standing, with key guidelines covering ground surface, desk height, positioning aids, proper standing position, and gradual training. The authors emphasize that expensive equipment is not necessary.

## Key facts

- The five fundamentals for setting up a standing workstation are: Ground Control, Desk Height, Positioning Aids, Proper Standing Position, and gradual training. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4075-L4085]
- The desk should be at elbow height plus one inch when standing with forearms parallel to the floor. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4110-L4125]
- Positioning aids include a barstool for leaning or perching and a foot support to prop up one foot like on a bar rail. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4125-L4140]
- Users should start with 30 minutes per day and increase gradually to avoid pain, treating it like marathon training. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4155-L4170]
- Standing while working leads to more movement overall, with research showing 17% more calorie expenditure than seated students. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4130-L4145]

## Inferences

- Inference: Standing workstations serve as a gateway to increased movement rather than being stationary themselves, as people naturally fidget and shift positions when standing.
