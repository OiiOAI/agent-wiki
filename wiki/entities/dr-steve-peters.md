# Dr Steve Peters

## Summary

Dr Steve Peters is a consultant psychiatrist who developed the Chimp Model for mind management. He has worked in clinical psychiatry for over 20 years and has been associated with elite British sport since 2001, notably with British Cycling and later Sky ProCycling.

## Key facts

- Dr Peters holds degrees in mathematics and medicine, a Masters in medical education, and postgraduate qualifications in sports medicine, education and psychiatry. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p5]
- He has been Senior Lecturer at Sheffield University since 1994 and is Undergraduate Dean at the Medical School. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p5]
- Peters has been resident psychiatrist with the British Cycling team since 2001 and the Sky ProCycling Team. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p5]
- His techniques were credited with helping transform British Cycling performance, contributing to 14 medals from Beijing Olympics including eight golds. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p5]
- Sir Chris Hoy, Bradley Wiggins and Victoria Pendleton have publicly credited the Chimp Model with improving their performance. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p6]
- Peters has worked with 12 other Olympic Sports including Taekwondo and Canoeing, as well as England Rugby and Premier League Football. [raw/books/psychology/The Chimp Paradox_ How Our Impulses and Emotions Can - Peters, Dr Steve.pdf#p5]
