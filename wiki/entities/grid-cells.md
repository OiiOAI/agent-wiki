# Grid Cells

## Summary

Grid cells are neurons in the medial entorhinal cortex discovered by the Moser group that provide a metric for neural representation of space through hexagonal firing patterns. Unlike the square grids of typical city maps, grid cells tile the two-dimensional surface in a honeycomb fashion. Neighboring grid cells have similar patterns with slight spatial offsets, arranged in semi-independent modules or clusters along the dorsal-to-ventral axis where grid scales vary from small to large. The discovery earned Edvard Moser and May-Britt Moser the 2014 Nobel Prize.

## Key facts

- Grid cells in the medial entorhinal cortex tile the two-dimensional surface of the environment in a honeycomb fashion, discovered by the Moser group in 2005. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p130]
- Neighboring grid cells have similar patterns but with slight spatial offsets, arranged as semi-independent modules or clusters along the dorsal-to-ventral axis of medial entorhinal cortex where grid scales vary from small to large. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p130]
- When superimposed, firing patterns of many grid cells precisely tile the entire floor of the testing apparatus as a sequence of population vectors describing the animal's position with high precision. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p131]
- Damaging the anterodorsal nucleus of the thalamus, a main hub of the head direction system, leads to disappearance of grid dynamics. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p116]

## Inferences

- Inference: Grid cells may support periodic encoding in multiple cortical areas for abstract categorization and general knowledge organization beyond spatial navigation.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Despite numerous models and experiments, there is no agreed-upon explanation of how the grid pattern is supported by the circuits in which they are embedded.
