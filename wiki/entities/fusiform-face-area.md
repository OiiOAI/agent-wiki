# Fusiform Face Area

## Summary

The fusiform face area is a face-selective region located in the human temporal lobe, discovered by Nancy Kanwisher and colleagues using fMRI. It responds significantly more during the presentation of faces compared to other objects. This finding supported the hypothesis that prosopagnosia could result from focal lesions of face-selective neurons clustered in exclusive regions.
