# Radhika Nagpal

## Summary

Radhika Nagpal is the Fred Kavli Professor of Computer Science at Harvard University who practiced fixed-schedule productivity during her pre-tenure years. She set a 50-hour-per-week limit and worked backward to determine what rules and habits were needed to satisfy this constraint. She earned tenure on schedule and then reached full professor level after only three additional years.
