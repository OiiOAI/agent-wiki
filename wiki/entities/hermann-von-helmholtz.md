# Hermann von Helmholtz

## Summary

Hermann von Helmholtz was a German physicist and polymath of the nineteenth century who invented the ophthalmoscope used by opticians to examine the eye and formulated the law of conservation of energy. He was also interested in theories of perception and argued that we perceive the world only thanks to a kind of unconscious reasoning or inference.

## Key facts

- Helmholtz was the inventor of the ophthalmoscope used by opticians to examine the eye and formulated the law of conservation of energy. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p20]
- Helmholtz argued that we perceive the world only thanks to a kind of unconscious reasoning or inference in which the brain asks itself, 'Given everything I know, how must the world be for me to be receiving the pattern of signals currently present?'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p20]
- Helmholtz formulated the principle of conservation of energy and postulated the notion of free energy in thermodynamics. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
- He invented the ophthalmoscope to examine the retina. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
- He measured the conduction speed within nerves. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
- He derived a mathematical description of acoustic vibrations. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
- He established the modern theory of colors using three variables (hue, saturation, and brightness) to characterize them. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
- He lived from 1821 to 1894. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p173]
