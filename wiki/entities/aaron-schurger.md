# Aaron Schurger

## Summary

Aaron Schurger is a neuroscientist who in 2012 provided an important reinterpretation of Benjamin Libet's readiness potential experiments. Schurger showed that readiness potentials might not be signatures of brain initiation of action but artifacts of measurement, reflecting fluctuating brain activity that occasionally crosses a threshold.

## Key facts

- In 2012, Aaron Schurger provided an important reinterpretation showing readiness potentials might not be signatures of brain initiating action but artifacts of measurement. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p205]
- Schurger's key finding was that when people responded quickly to a beep prompt, EEG showed what looked like a readiness potential even though no voluntary action was being prepared. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p205]
- Schurger proposed that the readiness potential reflects fluctuating brain activity that occasionally passes a threshold, triggering voluntary action when it does. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p205]
