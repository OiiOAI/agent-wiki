# Ap Dijksterhuis

## Summary

A psychologist known for research on unconscious thought and its benefits for complex decision making. His work demonstrates that for complex decisions, unconscious thought produces better outcomes than conscious deliberation.

## Key facts

- Dijksterhuis showed that when a creative task is preceded by a period of distraction, the generated ideas are less obvious and more creative compared with those generated after deliberate thought. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p368]
- His studies demonstrate that for complex decisions with many attributes, unconscious thought leads to better decisions than conscious deliberation, with mental representations becoming clearer and better organized during unconscious processing. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p370]
