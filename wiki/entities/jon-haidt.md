# Jon Haidt

## Summary

Jon Haidt is a professor at NYU Stern School of Business and a leading expert in exploring group thought in politics. In 2011, he noted the lack of viewpoint diversity in social psychology and, along with Philip Tetlock and others, founded Heterodox Academy to fight drift toward homogeneity of thought in science and academia.

## Key facts

- Jon Haidt reported in 2011 that he could identify only one conservative social psychologist with any degree of field-wide recognition among 1,000 social psychologists. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1380-L1385]
- Haidt co-founded Heterodox Academy along with Philip Tetlock and four others to fight drift toward homogeneity of thought in science and academics. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1390-L1395]
- Surveys found that 85%–96% of members of sociological professional organizations self-identified as left of center, with the ratio of liberals to conservatives in social psychology growing from 4-to-1 in the 1990s to greater than 10-to-1. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1385-L1395]
