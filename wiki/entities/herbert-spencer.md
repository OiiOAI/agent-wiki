# Herbert Spencer

## Summary

Herbert Spencer (1820-1903) was a British sociologist and philosopher known for his evolutionary theory of society and Social Darwinism. He coined the phrase 'survival of the fittest' before Darwin. His major works include Social Statics and his evolutionary theory posited movement from militant to industrial societies. He had enormous influence on early American sociology through William Graham Sumner.
