# Fat Tony and Nero Tulip

## Summary

Two fictional characters representing different approaches to antifragility. Fat Tony (Tony DiBenedetto) is a New Jersey-based intuitive who 'smells' fragility, reads little, and makes large profits by betting against fragile systems. Nero Tulip is a statistically-trained intellectual who understands antifragility theoretically and together with Fat Tony predicted and profited from the 2008 crisis by betting against fragilistas.

## Key facts

- Fat Tony and Nero Tulip were brought together by conviction that a crisis was inevitable simply because there were suckers (fragilistas) building fragile systems. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p193]
- Fat Tony is a 'smeller of fragility' who claims to figure out people by seeing them walk into a restaurant—literally sniffing them like a dog. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p191]
- Nero Tulip's formal training was in statistics and probability, approached as a branch of philosophy, and he spent his life writing 'Probability and Metaprobability.'. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p192]
- Fat Tony made a bundle in the high eight to low nine figures from the 2008 crisis; Nero made less but was satisfied having won the argument. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p194]
- By betting against fragility, both Fat Tony and Nero Tulip were antifragile—they profited from the collapse of fragile systems. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p194]
