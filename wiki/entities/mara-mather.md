# Mara Mather

## Summary

Mara Mather is a psychologist who demonstrated that emotional arousal constrains what we pay attention to, making us better at perceiving salient information. Her research showed how emotional arousal influences the 'neural elections' that determine what we will perceive and remember.

## Key facts

- Mara Mather showed that emotional arousal ratchets up the stakes for attention, making us better at perceiving salient things that are important or stand out. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p96]
- Mather's research showed emotional arousal influences the outcomes of neural elections that determine what we will perceive, funneling resources to the strongest candidates. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p96]
