# Roger Sperry

## Summary

Pioneering neuroscientist who, with Joseph Bogen and colleagues, performed the first split-brain procedures in the 1950s and 1960s. Awarded the Nobel Prize for his work on the functional specialization of cerebral hemispheres.

## Key facts

- Sperry and Bogen and their colleagues in California performed the first split-brain procedures in the 1950s and 1960s, which were revolutionary for both patients and researchers. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p57]
- McGilchrist cites Sperry's work on split-brain subjects extensively in discussing hemisphere differences. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p75]
