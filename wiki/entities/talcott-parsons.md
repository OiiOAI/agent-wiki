# Talcott Parsons

## Summary

Talcott Parsons (1902-1979) was the dominant figure in American sociology for introducing European theorists to American audiences, for his own sociological theories, and for his many students who became major sociological theorists. Born in Colorado Springs, he studied at Amherst, then at the London School of Economics and Heidelberg, where he was influenced by Max Weber's work. He joined Harvard in 1927 and became the dominant figure in American sociology with the 1937 publication of "The Structure of Social Action" and later works like "The Social System" (1951). Parsons developed structural functionalism based on the AGIL scheme (Adaptation, Goal attainment, Integration, Latency), focusing on large-scale social structures and their maintenance of equilibrium. His work lay at the core of structural functionalism, which gained dominance until the 1960s. Though criticized for his static orientation, he eventually developed evolutionary theory. He is also known for his concept of generalized media of interchange (money, power, influence, value commitments).

- Parsons studied at Heidelberg where Max Weber had worked, and Weber's influence survived through meetings at Weber's widow's home. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p132]
- Parsons published "The Structure of Social Action" in 1937, which introduced Durkheim, Weber, and Pareto to American sociologists. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p98]
- Parsons devoted almost no attention to Marx or Simmel in "The Structure of Social Action," resulting in Marxian theory being largely excluded from legitimate sociology. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p98]
- Parsons developed the AGIL scheme: Adaptation (A), Goal attainment (G), Integration (I), and Latency/L-pattern maintenance (L). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p129]
- The behavioral organism handles adaptation, personality performs goal-attainment, the social system handles integration, and the cultural system performs latency. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p130]
- Parsons conceived of society as having four subsystems: economy (adaptation), polity (goal attainment), fiduciary system (latency), and societal community (integration). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p136]
- Parsons eventually developed evolutionary theory, describing social change through differentiation, adaptive upgrading, and generalization of values. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p140]
- Parsons introduced the concept of generalized media of interchange (money, political power, influence, value commitments) to bring dynamism to structural analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p141]
