# Victor Nee

## Summary

Victor Nee is an economist and sociologist whose research on Chinese rural development documented the free rider problem in collective farming. His work examined how peasant households focused on maximizing individual advantage over collective interests, and how the disparity between productivity in collective and private sectors illustrated fundamental problems with collective farming systems.

## Key facts

- Victor Nee's research on Chinese collective farming documented the free rider problem where households worried their extra effort might subsidize those working less. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p408]
