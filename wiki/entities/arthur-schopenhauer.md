# Arthur Schopenhauer

## Summary

Arthur Schopenhauer was a German philosopher known for his pessimistic view of human freedom. Mitchell quotes Schopenhauer's statement that 'A man can do what he will, but not will as he will' to capture the essence of the problem with free will—even if we make choices, those choices are not free from prior causes over which we had no control.
