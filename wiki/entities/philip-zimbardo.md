# Philip Zimbardo

## Summary

Philip Zimbardo is a Stanford psychologist best known for conducting the 1971 Stanford Prison Experiment, which examined the psychological effects of assigning college students to roles as prisoners or guards. His work has been influential in understanding situational influences on behavior and the psychology of evil.

## Key facts
