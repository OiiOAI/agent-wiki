# Protein Count

## Summary

A nutrition assessment for tracking daily protein intake. The book emphasizes protein because it is essential for building and maintaining muscle, connective tissue, and the lining of the small intestine, with needs increasing as people age.

## Key facts

- Protein plays a decisive role in building and maintaining muscle, which is essential for body functionality. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1524-L1526]
- Protein also sustains connective tissue and the lining of the small intestine. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1526-L1527]
- The body begins losing muscle mass in one's thirties at about 3 to 5 percent per decade, partly due to reduced ability to convert dietary protein to muscle. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1528-L1531]
