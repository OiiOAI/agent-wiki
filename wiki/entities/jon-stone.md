# Jon Stone

## Summary

Jon Stone is a University of Edinburgh neurologist and professor who treats functional neurological disorders. He documented the case of a teenager with functional blindness and used techniques including TMS (transcranial magnetic stimulation) and careful scene setting to help her recover her sight.

## Key facts

- Jon Stone is a University of Edinburgh neurologist and professor. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p54]
- Stone documented the case of a teenager who progressively became effectively blind, diagnosed with a functional neurological disorder, and helped her recover sight through TMS and explanation of the nature of functional disorders. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p54]
- Stone likes to emphasize to patients the difference between their voluntary leg movement (which is severely impaired in functional weakness) and their involuntary (automatic) movements (which are not). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p59]
