# Anjanette Harper

## Summary

Anjanette Harper is Michalowicz's writing partner who shared the story of Camp Widjiwagan, a winter survival camp in Minnesota. Her experience there—where campers achieved zero food waste through mutual accountability—inspired Michalowicz's emphasis on accountability buddies in Profit First implementation.

## Key facts

- Anjanette Harper is described as 'one of my best pals' and 'the best damn writer on this planet.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p197]
- At age 13, she attended Camp Widjiwagan near Ely, Minnesota, where campers were forced to reduce food waste per meal to near zero or face consequences including forced square-dancing. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p197]
- Through mutual accountability—passing leftovers, shouting at each other when plates were too full—they achieved zero waste by the final dinner. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p198]
