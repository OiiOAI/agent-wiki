# MOSAIC-20

## Summary

An artificial intuition system designed by de Becker's firm to assess the details of a woman's domestic violence situation as she reports it to police, flagging cases with the highest danger of homicide. It represents technology used to protect high government officials being applied to help battered women.

## Key facts

- MOSAIC-20 is described as an artificial intuition system that assesses the details of a woman's situation as she reports it to police, flagging those cases in which the danger of homicide is highest. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p128]
- The system was developed for use with the Los Angeles County District Attorney's Office, Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department, and Los Angeles Police Department—the nation's first use of such technology for domestic violence assessment. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p128]
- De Becker states the system 'brings to regular citizens the same technologies and strategies used to protect high government officials,' noting that 'battered women are at far greater risk of murder than most public figures.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p129]
- Part of the proceeds from the book go toward continued development of MOSAIC-20. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p128]

## Inferences

- Inference: The system was created in response to psychologist Lenore Walker's incorrect claim that 'there's no way to predict' spousal homicide, representing a systematic approach to what de Becker considers the most predictable serious crime in America.
