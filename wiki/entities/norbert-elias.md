# Norbert Elias

## Summary

Norbert Elias (1897-1990) was a German-British sociologist who developed figurational (or process) sociology as a framework for overcoming the micro-macro distinction. His major work 'The Civilizing Process' (1939) examines the historical transformation of behavior and psychological makeup in Western societies. Elias proposed the concept of figuration to describe the interdependent networks of people that shape both individual behavior and social structures. His work was largely ignored until late in his life, when he received increasing recognition, particularly in continental Europe.
