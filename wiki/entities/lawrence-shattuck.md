# Lawrence Shattuck

## Summary

A U.S. Army officer who conducted research on communicating intent for his Ph.D. dissertation at Ohio State University. He studied how military officers communicated and interpreted commander intent statements, revealing significant gaps in mutual understanding.

## Key facts

- Shattuck obtained brigade-level orders and had battalion commanders convert them into orders for company commanders, both verbally and in writing. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p248]
- When Shattuck presented unexpected events to company commanders and asked how they would respond, only 34 percent matched the battalion commander's expectations. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p249]
- When Shattuck told company commanders what battalion commanders expected, their typical response was 'How did he expect me to know that?'. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p249]
- Shattuck later became head of the United States Military Academy's Department of Behavioral Sciences and Leadership at West Point. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p248]
