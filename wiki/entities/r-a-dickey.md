# R.A. Dickey

## Summary

Major League Baseball knuckleball pitcher who exemplifies the concept of sharing freely within a community of peers. Knuckleball pitchers form a brotherhood and share tips openly, unlike other pitchers who guard their techniques as 'state secrets.'

## Key facts

- R.A. Dickey throws the knuckleball—a slow pitch released with minimal spin that moves unpredictably due to air currents against the seams. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p132]
- Dickey notes that there's 'no chance that an opposing pitcher, no matter how nice a guy, is going to invite me to watch how he grips and throws his split-fingered fastball or slider. Those are state secrets.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p133]
- With fellow knuckleballers, however, 'Knuckleballers don't keep secrets. It's as if we have a greater mission beyond our own fortunes. And that mission is to pass it on, to keep the pitch alive.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p133]
