# Jean Van de Velde

## Summary

Jean Van de Velde is a French golf professional who experienced a dramatic collapse at the 1999 British Open, blowing a three-shot lead on the final hole. Unlike Donnie Moore, Van de Velde did not become trapped by persistent memories, instead explaining his decisions and placing the experience in broader perspective. His contrasting case illustrates how appraisals and self-schemas influence vulnerability to persistence.
