# Claude Shannon

## Summary

Claude Elwood Shannon (1916–2001) was an American mathematician and electrical engineer who founded information theory. He graduated from the University of Pittsburgh at age 20, earned a master's degree at MIT, worked on cryptography at Bell Labs during World War II, and later dedicated his career to studying the encoding and optimal transmission of information. He introduced concepts such as Shannon entropy, which measures the amount of information in bits contained in a message.
