# Rodolfo Llinás

## Summary

Rodolfo R. Llinás is a Colombian-American neuroscientist and the author of this book. He is a single-cell physiologist specializing in neuronal integration and synaptic transmission. His work has focused on the intrinsic electrical properties of neurons, cerebellar function, and the nature of consciousness. He proposed the closed-system hypothesis of brain function and the concept that mindness represents the internalization of movement.

## Key facts

- Llinás writes from the privileged position of a single-cell physiologist interested in neuronal integration and synaptic transmission, situated between the realms of molecular and systemic brain function. [raw/books/neuroscience/I of the Vortex _ From Neurons to Self - Rodolfo Riascos Llinás.pdf#p3]
- Llinás proposed the closed-system hypothesis in 1974 and 1987, arguing that the brain operates primarily as a self-activating system whose organization is geared toward the generation of intrinsic images.
