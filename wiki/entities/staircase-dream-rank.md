# Staircase Dream (Otto Rank)

## Summary

A dream recorded and interpreted by Otto Rank involving rushing down a staircase in pursuit of a little girl, culminating in sexual act symbolism and emission. The dream demonstrates the staircase as a symbol of copulation based on shared rhythmical patterns.

## Key facts

- The dreamer rushed down the staircase extraordinarily fast, not putting his feet on separate steps but 'flying' down, reflecting childhood experiences of sliding down banisters. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p369]
- Rank interpreted stairs and going upstairs in dreams as almost invariably standing for copulation, with the rhythmical pattern of copulation reproduced in going upstairs. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p370]
- The dream culminated in an emission, showing the staircase symbolism represented copulation and confirming Freud's view about the erotic factor in movement dreams. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p371]
