# Mesencephalic Central Gray

## Summary

The mesencephalic central gray (periaqueductal gray) surrounds the cerebral aqueduct in the midbrain and contains basic neural components for many emotional processes including fear, anger, sexuality, pleasure, and pain.

## Key facts

- The mesencephalic central gray is situated just below the corpora quadrigemina and contains basic neural components for many emotional processes. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p47]
- This tissue contains circuits for the coherent emission of rage and defensive behaviors, fear, separation distress, exploratory urges, sexual urges, and experiences of pleasure and pain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
- The periaqueductal gray is one of the lowest integrative centers for emotional operating systems, essential for spontaneous engagement with the world. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p48]
