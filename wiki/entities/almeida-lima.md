# Almeida Lima

## Summary

Almeida Lima was a neurosurgeon who collaborated with Egas Moniz in developing cerebral angiography and prefrontal leucotomy. Damasio trained with Lima, who was described as both a skillful surgeon and a compassionate man. Lima had been using a modified leucotomy for the management of intractable pain and was convinced the procedure was justifiable in desperate cases. Damasio witnessed Lima perform these operations and observed patients post-surgery who showed remarkable relief from suffering while still able to perceive pain.

## Key facts

- Almeida Lima was a neurosurgeon who helped Egas Moniz develop cerebral angiography and prefrontal leucotomy and performed the first such leucotomy operation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p285]
- Lima was described as both a skillful surgeon and a compassionate man who had been using a modified leucotomy for the management of intractable pain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p285]
- Damasio witnessed Lima perform a prefrontal leucotomy on a patient crouching in profound suffering; two days after the operation the patient was relaxed and happily playing cards. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain.pdf#p285-286]
