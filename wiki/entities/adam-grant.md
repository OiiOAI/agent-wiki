# Adam Grant

## Summary

Adam Grant is a professor at the Wharton School of Business who became the youngest full professor at Wharton through exceptional productivity. He published 7 articles in major journals in 2012 alone, plus the bestselling book Give and Take in 2013. Grant employs a bimodal deep work strategy, batching his teaching into fall semesters and isolating himself for 3-4 day periods during research phases, using out-of-office auto-responders to enforce deep focus.
