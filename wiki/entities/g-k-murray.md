# G.K. Murray

## Summary

G.K. Murray is a neuroscientist at the University of Cambridge who, together with C. Tudor-Sfetea and P.C. Fletcher, authored Chapter 21 of this volume on reinforcement learning models of schizophrenia. Murray's research has focused on dopaminergic dysfunction and prediction-error signaling in psychosis, using fMRI and computational modeling approaches.

## Key facts

- Murray et al. (2008) demonstrated abnormal prediction-error signals in the dopaminergic midbrain and ventral striatum of psychosis patients using fMRI. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p255]
- Murray contributed to the finding that first-episode psychosis patients show deficits in reinforcement and reversal learning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p254]
