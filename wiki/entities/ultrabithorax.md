# Ultrabithorax

## Summary

Ultrabithorax (Ubx) is a homeotic gene mutation in fruit flies that produces an additional thorax segment with a duplicated set of wings. It was among the first Hox genes discovered.

## Key facts

- Ultrabithorax is a fruit-fly mutation that produces duplicated additional body segments. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
- The mutation specifically produces an additional thorax segment with a duplicated set of wings. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
- Ultrabithorax was among the first of the homeotic genes discovered through investigation of these segment-duplicating mutations. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
