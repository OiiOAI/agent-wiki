# McMartin Preschool Case

## Summary

The McMartin preschool case was a highly publicized 1980s daycare abuse case in Los Angeles where children made accusations involving bizarre rituals. Prosecutors failed to obtain a conviction and eventually gave up trying. Transcripts of interviews with children revealed social pressure techniques that influenced the accuracy of children's reports.

## Key facts

- The McMartin preschool case involved accusations of revolting, even horrific, acts including bloody torture, murder, being forced to eat dead babies, and trips on alien spaceships. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p173]
- Prosecutors failed to obtain a conviction in the McMartin case and finally gave up trying. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p174]
- Transcripts of McMartin interviews revealed interviewers used praise, rewards, expressions of disappointment, repeated questions, and invitations to pretend—techniques that dramatically increased false reports. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p178]
