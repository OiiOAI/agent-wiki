# 5-HT3 Receptor

## Summary

The 5-HT3 receptor is an ionotropic serotonin receptor that mediates a minority of fast excitatory synaptic actions in the brain. It is a pentameric receptor structurally similar to nicotinic ACh receptors, with four transmembrane segments per subunit, and is permeable to monovalent cations with a reversal potential near 0 mV.

## Key facts

- 5-HT3 receptors mediate a minority of fast excitatory synaptic actions in the brain, activated by the neurotransmitter serotonin (5-HT). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p337]
- 5-HT3 receptor-channels are structurally similar to nicotinic ACh receptors, pentameric with subunits containing four transmembrane segments. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p337]
- Like ACh receptor-channels, 5-HT3 receptor-channels are permeable to monovalent cations and have a reversal potential near 0 mV. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p337]
