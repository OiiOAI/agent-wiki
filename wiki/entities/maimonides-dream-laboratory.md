# Maimonides Dream Laboratory

## Summary

The Maimonides Dream Laboratory, founded in 1962 by Montague Ullman, was the first and only sleep laboratory dedicated to the experimental study of dream telepathy. Ullman was joined in 1964 by Stanley Krippner, and together they spent a decade studying whether mental images could be transmitted from senders to dreamers. Their studies included using simple line drawings, classic paintings, and famously, a 1973 experiment with Grateful Dead concert fans as senders for receivers sleeping 40 miles away.
