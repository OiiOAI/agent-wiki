# Alexis de Tocqueville

## Summary

French political thinker and historian Alexis de Tocqueville (1805–1859) is cited extensively in Fukuyama's 'Trust' regarding his observations on American associational life. His work 'Democracy in America' provides the epigraph for Chapter 6, describing Americans' propensity to form associations for every conceivable purpose—commercial, religious, moral, entertainment, and philanthropic.

## Key facts

- Tocqueville observed that Americans of all ages, conditions, and dispositions constantly form associations for commercial, religious, moral, and philanthropic purposes. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- According to Tocqueville, wherever one sees the government in France or a man of rank in England, in the United States one will find an association instead. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- Tocqueville noted Americans form associations to give entertainments, found seminaries, build inns, construct churches, diffuse books, send missionaries, and found hospitals, prisons, and schools. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p377]
- Alexis de Tocqueville was born on July 29, 1805, in Paris, into a prominent aristocratic family. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p40]
- He traveled to the United States in 1831 to study the American penitentiary system and ended up studying democracy, equality, and freedom. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p40]
- He published Democracy in America in 1835 (volume 1) and 1840 (volume 2). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p40]
- His central concern was the growth of centralization in government and the threat it poses to freedom. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p41]
- He claimed to 'invent' the concept of individualism, which he linked to equality and diminished concern for community. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p41]
- He died on April 16, 1859. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p41]
