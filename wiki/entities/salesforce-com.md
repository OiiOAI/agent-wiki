# Salesforce.com

## Summary

Salesforce.com was founded in 1999 by Marc Benioff as a pioneer of cloud-based customer relationship management software. The company faced sequential strategic challenges: attracting talent, acquiring customers, surviving the dot-com crash, and platform expansion. Its trajectory illustrates that strategy is a journey through challenges rather than a fixed plan.

## Key facts

- Traditional CRM systems cost $2.8 million for 200 users with $1.9 million in license fees plus support and customization costs. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p59]
- The cloud-based model eliminated local servers, installation charges, maintenance fees, and IT department requirements. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p59]
- During the dot-com crash, Benioff raised rates for monthly customers and pressed best customers for longer-term deals. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p60]
- AppExchange introduced in 2005 was called an iTunes for business, allowing third-party applications on the platform. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p60]
- By 2021, Salesforce.com had 60,000 employees and was ranked number two in Fortune's best companies to work for. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p61]
