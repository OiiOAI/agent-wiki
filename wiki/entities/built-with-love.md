# Built with Love

## Summary

Built with Love was a construction company started by Michael Singer in December 1976 after a sheriff's deputy asked him to build an addition onto his house. The company grew from small home improvement jobs for sheriff's department officers to building custom homes. All profits were donated to the Temple of the Universe to support land purchases and community expenses.

- The first construction job was a cost-plus basis project where Singer worked with Radha as his assistant. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p100]
