# Harry Jerison

## Summary

Harry Jerison was a prominent researcher whose work on brain evolution and intelligence introduced the concept of 'proper mass.' His widely cited book provided the clearest explanation of the relationship between brain size, encephalization, and intelligence, arguing that brain size should reflect optimal balance between evolutionary costs and benefits of neural processing.
