# Center for Nonviolent Communication

## Summary

CNVC is an international nonprofit peacemaking organization founded by Marshall B. Rosenberg in 1984 with a vision of a world where everyone's needs are met peacefully. The organization supports the spread of NVC worldwide through training, certification, projects, and resources.

## Key facts

- The Center for Nonviolent Communication was founded in 1984 by Dr. Marshall B. Rosenberg. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p302]
- CNVC's vision is a world where everyone's needs are met peacefully. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p302]
- NVC is now taught in more than sixty countries by hundreds of certified trainers. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p302]
