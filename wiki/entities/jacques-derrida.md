# Jacques Derrida

## Summary

Jacques Derrida is one of the acknowledged leaders of poststructuralism. His critique targets logocentrism—the search for a universal system of thought that reveals what is true, right, beautiful. Derrida is associated with deconstruction, which involves decomposing unities to uncover hidden differences. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p507]
