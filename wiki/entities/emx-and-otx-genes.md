# Emx and Otx genes

## Summary

Emx and Otx are homeobox genes expressed during embryonic brain development. They are part of the broader family of genes involved in patterning the anterior-posterior axis of the developing organism.

## Key facts

- Emx and Otx genes are expressed in the developing brain and are most prominent in the embryonic brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
- These genes are also expressed in other regions of the body and change their expression patterns over developmental time. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
- This diverse multifunctionality contradicts any claim that they function like simple blueprint genes for the brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p511]
