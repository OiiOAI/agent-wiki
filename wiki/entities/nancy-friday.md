# Nancy Friday

## Summary

Nancy Friday is the author of Men in Love, a work on male sexuality that Perel quotes on the nature of sexual fantasy. Friday describes fantasy as 'a map of desire, mastery, escape, and obscuration; the navigational path we invent to steer ourselves between the reefs and shoals of anxiety, guilt, and inhibition.' She characterizes it as 'a work of consciousness, but in reaction to unconscious pressures'—revealing the unconscious personality of the person who invented it.
