# Statue of Responsibility

## Summary

The Statue of Responsibility is a conceptual monument proposed by Viktor Frankl to complement the Statue of Liberty on the East Coast of the United States. He suggested it should be placed on the West Coast as a symbol emphasizing that freedom is only half of the truth—responsibleness is its positive counterpart. Freedom is in danger of degenerating into mere arbitrariness unless it is lived in terms of responsibleness. Various organizations have since advocated for the actual construction of such a statue.

## Key facts

- Frankl recommended that the Statue of Liberty on the East Coast be supplemented by a Statue of Responsibility on the West Coast. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p91]
- He argued that freedom is only part of the story and half of the truth; freedom is in danger of degenerating into mere arbitrariness unless it is lived in terms of responsibleness. [raw/books/psychology/Man's Search for Meaning_ Young Adult Edit - Viktor E. Frankl.pdf#p91]
