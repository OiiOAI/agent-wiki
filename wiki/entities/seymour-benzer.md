# Seymour Benzer

## Summary

Seymour Benzer (1921-2007) was an American geneticist who pioneered the genetic analysis of behavior in Drosophila. Around 1970, he and his colleagues used random mutagenesis and classical genetic analysis to identify mutations affecting circadian rhythms, courtship behavior, movement, visual perception, and memory. His work established that specific genes could influence complex behaviors, a revolutionary finding that helped establish the field of behavioral genetics.

## Key facts

- Seymour Benzer and his colleagues initiated the first large-scale studies of gene influence on behavior around 1970 using Drosophila. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p81]
- Benzer's group identified mutations in the period gene that affect all circadian behaviors in Drosophila. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p87]
