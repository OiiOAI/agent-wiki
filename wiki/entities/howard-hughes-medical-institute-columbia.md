# Howard Hughes Medical Institute at Columbia

## Summary

The Howard Hughes Medical Institute at Columbia University supported Kandel's research on the molecular biology of memory. Founded in 1983, the neural science initiative provided resources and intellectual flexibility for cutting-edge work, supporting researchers including Kandel, Richard Axel, Tom Jessell, and Steven Siegelbaum.

## Key facts

- The motto of HHMI is 'People, not projects,' believing science flourishes when outstanding researchers are provided resources and flexibility. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p263]
- Kandel was senior investigator of the neural science initiative starting in 1983. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p264]
- By 2004, the institute supported 350 investigators with an endowment over $11 billion. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p263]
