# Anna O. (Bertha Pappenheim)

## Summary

Anna O. was the pseudonym of Bertha Pappenheim, the first patient treated by Josef Breuer using what became known as the 'talking cure' or cathartic method. Her case, published by Breuer and later discussed by Freud, became foundational for psychoanalytic theory. The case demonstrated how hysterical symptoms arose from specific traumatic experiences and could be relieved by having the patient verbally recount these memories in hypnosis.

## Key facts

- Anna O. was treated by Breuer in 1880-1882 for hysteria with symptoms including visual disturbances, paralysis, speech loss, and contractures. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p576]
- She developed 108 separate instances of not hearing when someone came in, 27 instances of not understanding when several people talked, and 50 instances of not hearing when alone and directly addressed. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p574]
- Her first hysterical symptom appeared when she hallucinated a black snake approaching her sick father while she was nursing him, during which her right arm had gone to sleep. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p576]
- She could only think and pray in English after her first hallucination, though German was her native language. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p576]
- Her 'private theatre' habit of daydreaming prepared the psychological ground for the dissociation of her mental personality. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p578]
- She recovered completely after treatment and later became a social worker. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p577]
