# Presenilin-1

## Summary

Presenilin-1 (PS1) is a component of the γ-secretase multiprotein complex encoded by a gene on chromosome 14. Mutations in presenilin-1 are among the most common causes of autosomal dominant early-onset AD, typically leading to mutant γ-secretase with higher than normal activity or generating an increased ratio of Aβ42 to Aβ40.

## Key facts

- Presenilin-1 is a component of the γ-secretase complex, encoded by a gene on chromosome 14. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
- Presenilin-1 was one of the first three genes identified for autosomal dominant early-onset AD, along with APP and presenilin-2. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
- In most presenilin mutants, the mutant γ-secretase has higher than normal activity or generates an increased ratio of Aβ42 to Aβ40. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
