# NMDA Receptor

## Summary

The NMDA receptor is an ionotropic glutamate receptor that functions as a molecular coincidence detector, requiring both glutamate binding and membrane depolarization for activation due to voltage-dependent Mg2+ block, and conducts Ca2+ influx critical for synaptic plasticity.

## Key facts

- NMDA receptors are tetramers composed of two GluN1 subunits and two GluN2 subunits (encoded by five genes: one GluN1 and four GluN2A-D). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p279]
- At resting membrane potential, extracellular Mg2+ binds tightly to the NMDA receptor channel pore, blocking ionic current; depolarization expels Mg2+ by electrostatic repulsion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p283]
- NMDA receptor-channels are highly permeable to Ca2+ in addition to Na+ and K+, with Ca2+ permeability determined by asparagine residues in the M2 pore loop. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p281]
- The NMDA receptor acts as a molecular coincidence detector because it opens only when glutamate is present AND the cell is sufficiently depolarized to relieve Mg2+ block. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p284]
- NMDA receptor activation produces a late, slow component of the EPSC that decays more slowly than AMPA receptor current due to intrinsic kinetics of ligand gating. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p284]
- LTP induction requires Ca2+ influx through NMDA receptors, which activates calcium-dependent protein kinases including CaMKII and PKC. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p286]
- Excess NMDA receptor activation can cause glutamate excitotoxicity, contributing to neuronal damage in stroke, status epilepticus, and neurodegenerative diseases. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p285]

## Inferences

- Inference: The requirement for both presynaptic activity (glutamate release) and postsynaptic depolarization makes NMDA receptors ideally suited for detecting correlated activity between neurons, a fundamental requirement for Hebbian synaptic plasticity and memory formation.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The precise mechanisms by which postsynaptic Ca2+ signals trigger the release of retrograde messengers that enhance presynaptic transmitter release during LTP are not fully established.
