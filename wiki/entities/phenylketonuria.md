# Phenylketonuria

## Summary

Phenylketonuria (PKU) was the first gene discovered for a neurological disease in humans, described by Asbørn Følling in Norway in 1934. It affects one in 15,000 children and results from recessive mutations in the PKU gene coding for phenylalanine hydroxylase. Without treatment, it causes severe cognitive impairment. The phenotype results from interaction of the genotype with the environment (diet), and a low-protein diet can prevent intellectual disability.
