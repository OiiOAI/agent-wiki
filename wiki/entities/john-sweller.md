# John Sweller

## Summary

John Sweller is a psychologist who characterized cognitive load in 1988 as the total amount of mental effort being used in working memory. He defined three types of cognitive load: intrinsic, extraneous, and germane.

## Key facts

- John Sweller characterized cognitive load in 1988 as the total amount of mental effort being used in the working memory. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L922-L923]
- Sweller defines intrinsic cognitive load relating to aspects fundamental to the problem space, extraneous cognitive load relating to the environment, and germane cognitive load relating to learning and high performance. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L928-L934]
