# Dr. Daniel Gottlob Moritz Schreber

## Summary

Dr. Daniel Gottlob Moritz Schreber (1808–1861) was a German physician and the father of Daniel Paul Schreber. He was a prominent figure who founded therapeutic gymnastics in Germany and exerted lasting influence on physical education and health promotion, particularly in Saxony.

## Key facts

- He was born in 1808 and died in 1861 at age 53. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3762]
- He founded therapeutic gymnastics in Germany; his book 'Ärztliche Zimmergymnastik' (1855) went through nearly forty editions. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3763]
- He promoted physical culture and manual work to raise health standards, and coordinated home and school education. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3762]
- His memory is preserved by numerous Schreber Associations flourishing especially in Saxony. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3762]
- At his death in 1861, his son Daniel Paul Schreber was 19 years old. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3762]
