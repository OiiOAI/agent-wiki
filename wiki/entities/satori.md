# SATORI

## Summary

SATORI (Situation Assessment Through Recreation of Incidents) is a system developed by the FAA to recreate ATC incidents and integrate various data sources into a coherent picture for quality assurance investigations. It allows QA specialists to review synchronized communication and radar display data associated with incidents, reducing the time spent piecing together the dynamics of ATC situations.

## Key facts

- SATORI was developed to recreate ATC incidents and collect data to assist in determining the location and likelihood of breakdowns in the human-machine system. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p109]
- SATORI enables QA teams to spend more time identifying sources of problems and less time reconstructing the dynamics of ATC situations. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p109]
- SATORI provides the capability to collect normative information about ATC incidents beyond just descriptive views. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p110]
- QA personnel can use SATORI to observe the recreation of an incident and seek independent confirmation of controller situation awareness. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p116]

## Inferences

- Inference: The development of SATORI represents an improvement in the investigative process for operational irregularities by providing synchronized data integration rather than relying on fragmented sources.
