# Elliot Aronson

## Summary

Elliot Aronson is a social psychologist who developed the jigsaw classroom technique as a cooperative learning approach to reduce prejudice and improve intergroup relations in desegregated schools.

## Key facts

- Aronson developed the jigsaw classroom approach to learning in response to racial tensions following school desegregation. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p165]
- Aronson was consulted to address problems in Austin, Texas schools, where he observed the competitive dynamics that foster intergroup hostility. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p161]

## Inferences

- Inference: Aronson's work demonstrates that simply bringing different groups together without restructuring interactions can increase rather than decrease prejudice.
