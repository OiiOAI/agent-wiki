# Acetylcholinesterase

## Summary

Acetylcholinesterase is an enzyme anchored to collagen fibrils in the basement membrane of the neuromuscular junction that rapidly hydrolyzes acetylcholine, terminating synaptic signaling.

## Key facts

- Acetylcholinesterase is anchored to collagen fibrils within the basement membrane of the synaptic cleft. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p255]
- The enzyme hydrolyzes ACh into acetate and choline, limiting transmitter action to one hit because AChE is inhibited by high ACh concentrations, allowing most molecules to reach receptors first.
