# Petzl

## Summary

Petzl is a French company founded by caver Fernand Petzl that designs and manufactures high-performance climbing, caving, and safety equipment. The company's coherent strategy centers on extreme product quality and trust in life-critical situations, illustrated by its rapid development of the Exo personal evacuation system for FDNY firefighters in 2005.

## Key facts

- Fernand Petzl began producing pulleys and brakes for cavers around 1968, with products known for both high quality and safety. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p118]
- In 2005, Petzl created a quick building-escape system for FDNY firefighters when traditional rappel devices wouldn't work on thin firefighter rope. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p118]
- Petzl fixed a rope-tearing problem in only two days during firefighter training, making the system standard for FDNY. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p118]
- Petzl's coherence comes from its intense product focus, deep knowledge of principals, quality products, and carefully cultivated image of safety. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p119]
