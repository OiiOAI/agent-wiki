# Alan Greenspan

## Summary

Former Federal Reserve Chairman whom Taleb identifies as 'the top economic iatrogenist of all time.' Greenspan's attempts to iron out the boom-bust cycle caused risks to accumulate invisibly until the 2008 financial crisis. Despite being a self-described libertarian committed to leaving systems alone, Greenspan's interventions exemplify naive interventionism—the belief that volatility can and should be suppressed.

## Key facts

- Alan Greenspan is identified as 'the top economic iatrogenist of all time' whose attempt to iron out the boom-bust cycle caused risks to accumulate until they blew up the economy. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p158]
- Greenspan kept an eye on fluctuations like vacuum cleaner sales in Cleveland to 'get a precise idea about where the economy is going'—micromanaging based on noise rather than signal. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p168]
- Despite being a libertarian seemingly convinced of leaving systems alone, Greenspan applied naive interventionism—demonstrating that people can fool themselves endlessly. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p158]
