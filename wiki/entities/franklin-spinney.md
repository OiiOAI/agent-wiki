# Franklin Spinney

## Summary

Franklin 'Chuck' Spinney was a United States Air Force captain and mathematician who became one of John Boyd's Acolytes. He joined Boyd's office in 1973 and would become the most well-known of the Acolytes, staying in the battle long after others moved on. He was known for his brash personality and passion for fiscal responsibility in military procurement.

## Key facts

- Spinney met Boyd as a young lieutenant when he outmaneuvered Tom Christie to win a $500,000 grant. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p288]
- He received an MBA with emphasis on applied statistics and was assigned to deliver mail at the Pentagon. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p289]
- Boyd invited Spinney to lunch after his interview, treating him as a potential Acolyte. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p289]
- Spinney became the fourth Acolyte and would become the most well-known of them all. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p290]
