# Jigoro Kano

## Summary

Jigoro Kano (1860-1938) was the founder of judo and the world's highest-ranking judoist. According to Leonard's account, near death Kano asked to be buried in his white belt—the symbol of the beginner—realizing that at the moment of death, the ultimate transformation, we are all white belts. The story illustrates that life continually makes beginners of us, and the master carries the image of the newest student within.
