# Grass-Fed Beef (Genius Food #6)

## Summary

Grass-fed beef is a rich source of essential minerals like iron and zinc in highly bioavailable forms, omega-3 fats, vitamin B12, vitamin E, and creatine. Researchers believe access to these nutrients (along with cooked meat's caloric energy) catalyzed human brain evolution. A study by Dr. Felice Jacka found that women who didn't eat the recommended three to four servings of beef per week were twice as likely to suffer from depression or anxiety disorders. A Kenyan study found that children who consumed meat showed greater cognitive performance, physical activity, and leadership behaviors compared to those who didn't.

## Key facts

- Grass-fed beef is a rich source of essential minerals like iron and zinc in a form that the body can easily utilize, unlike iron from spinach or zinc from legumes. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p223]
- Grass-fed beef is also a great source of omega-3 fats, vitamin B12, vitamin E, and creatine. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p223]
- Women who didn't eat Australia's national recommendation of three to four servings of beef per week were twice as likely to be depressed or suffer from anxiety or bipolar disorder. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p224]
- In a Kenyan study, children who consumed ground beef showed greater cognitive performance in math and language subjects, more muscle mass, fewer health problems, and greater confidence and leadership behaviors. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p225]
- Look for humanely raised 100 percent grass-fed and grass-finished beef, ideally organic and from local farms. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p226]
