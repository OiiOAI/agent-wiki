# Cheryl A. Bolstad

## Summary

Cheryl A. Bolstad is a researcher at North Carolina State University who co-authored Chapter 13 on Situation Awareness and Aging. Her work focuses on the intersection of cognitive aging and SA, including empirical studies on driving performance across age groups.

## Key facts

- Cheryl A. Bolstad is affiliated with North Carolina State University. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p289]
- Bolstad conducted driving research comparing young (18-39), middle-aged (40-59), and older (60-84) drivers using subjective SA measures. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p310]
- Preparation of the chapter was facilitated by grant AG05552 from the National Institute on Aging. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p311]
