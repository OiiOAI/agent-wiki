# Phædrus

## Summary

Phædrus was the narrator's former identity—a brilliant, isolated philosopher who pursued the ghost of rationality to the point of insanity. His Stanford-Binet IQ was recorded at 170 (one in fifty thousand). He was systematic like a laser beam rather than a general illuminator. He pursued rational truth fanatically until it consumed him, eventually leading to his destruction through electroconvulsive therapy ordered by a court. The narrator now carries fragments of Phædrus's memories and seeks to understand and synthesize what he discovered.
