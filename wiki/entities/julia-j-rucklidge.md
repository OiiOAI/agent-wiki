# Julia J. Rucklidge

## Summary

Julia J. Rucklidge, PhD, is a clinical psychologist and professor at the University of Canterbury in Christchurch, New Zealand, where she leads the Mental Health and Nutrition Research lab. She began publishing micronutrient research in 2009 after being fascinated by Kaplan's preliminary data. Her research includes clinical trials of broad-spectrum multinutrients for ADHD, mood disorders, and stress, and she is co-author of The Better Brain. Her 2014 TEDx Talk on nutrition and mental health has received over 1.6 million views.

## Key facts

- Rucklidge earned her PhD in 1998 based on research examining psychosocial outcomes of women with ADHD. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p16]
- She began publishing micronutrient research in 2009, observing results consistent with Kaplan's findings. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p17]
- Her first clinical observation involved a teenager with OCD who showed near-complete symptom disappearance within one week of starting multinutrient supplementation, after CBT had produced minimal change over a year. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p17]
- Her TEDx Talk 'The Surprisingly Dramatic Role of Nutrition in Mental Health' (October 2014) has received over 1.6 million views on YouTube. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p21]
