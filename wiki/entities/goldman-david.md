# David Goldman

## Summary

David Goldman was an American biophysicist who developed the Goldman equation, an extension of the Nernst equation that calculates membrane potential when the membrane is permeable to multiple ion species.

## Key facts

- Goldman derived the equation Vm = (RT/F) ln[(PK[K+]o + PNa[Na+]o + PCl[Cl−]i) / (PK[K+]i + PNa[Na+]i + PCl[Cl−]o)] for multi-ion membrane potential.
