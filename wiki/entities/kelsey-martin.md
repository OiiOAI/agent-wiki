# Kelsey Martin

## Summary

Kelsey Martin is a cell biologist who earned a combined M.D.-Ph.D. at Yale and worked with Kandel at Columbia. She developed elegant cell culture systems to study how synaptic changes are localized to specific synapses during long-term memory formation.

## Key facts

- Martin developed a cell culture system with a single sensory neuron with a branched axon forming synapses with two separate motor neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p279]
- She found that five pulses of serotonin applied to one set of synapses produced long-lasting facilitation and growth of new synaptic terminals only at the stimulated synapses. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p279]
- When Martin blocked CREB action in the nucleus, it suppressed both facilitation and growth at the stimulated synapse. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p280]
- Martin discovered that two things must occur at a marked synapse: activation of protein kinase A and activation of local protein synthesis machinery. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p281]
