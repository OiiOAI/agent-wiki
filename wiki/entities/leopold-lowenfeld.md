# Leopold Löwenfeld

## Summary

Leopold Löwenfeld (1847–1923) was a Munich psychiatrist who criticized Freud's anxiety neurosis theory. Despite this controversy, he maintained friendly relations with Freud, attended early Psychoanalytical Congresses, and included chapters by Freud in his own books.

## Key facts

- Löwenfeld was a well-known psychiatrist practising in Munich and an acquaintance of Freud's who continued on friendly terms with him despite theoretical disagreements. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p107]
- Löwenfeld criticized Freud's anxiety neurosis theory, arguing that anxiety states could arise from psychical shock alone without sexual aetiology. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p126]
- He authored Pathologie und Therapie der Neurasthenie and published criticisms in the Neurologisches Zentralblatt. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p111]
