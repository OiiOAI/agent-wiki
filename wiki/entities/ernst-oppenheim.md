# Ernst Oppenheim

## Summary

Austrian classical scholar (1881–1944) who collaborated with Freud on 'Dreams in Folklore' (1911). Oppenheim collected folkloric material on dream symbolism from sources such as Anthropophyteia and Kryptadia, while Freud provided the psychoanalytic commentary and interpretation. A member of the Vienna Psychoanalytical Society (1910–1911), Oppenheim later joined Adler's group. He perished at the Theresienstadt concentration camp during World War II.

## Key facts

- Oppenheim collected folklore material on dream symbolism from Anthropophyteia and Kryptadia periodicals and submitted it to Freud for psychoanalytic commentary. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3896]
- Freud and Oppenheim co-authored 'Dreams in Folklore' (1911), documenting that folklore dream symbolism coincides with psychoanalytic interpretation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3899]
- Oppenheim died in 1944 at the Theresienstadt concentration camp; his wife preserved the manuscript until after her death. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3896]
