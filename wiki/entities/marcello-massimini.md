# Marcello Massimini

## Summary

Marcello Massimini is an Italian neuroscientist who pioneered the Perturbational Complexity Index (PCI) for measuring consciousness levels. He initially worked with Giulio Tononi at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and later established his own group at the University of Milan. His work has demonstrated that PCI can detect consciousness in brain-injured patients who cannot communicate behaviourally.

## Key facts

- Massimini pioneered the Perturbational Complexity Index (PCI) using TMS and EEG to measure consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p46]
- His 2013 landmark study showed PCI values correlated with consciousness impairment levels in brain-injured patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p47]
- Massimini documented a case where PCI detected consciousness in a patient who appeared vegetative but was only unresponsive in Italian, responding when addressed in Arabic. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p49]
