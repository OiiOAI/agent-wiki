# Batja Mesquita

## Summary

Batja Mesquita is a cultural psychologist who was born and raised in the Netherlands and immigrated to America for postdoctoral training. She discovered emotion acculturation, finding that people's emotion concepts not only vary from culture to culture but also transform when individuals are exposed to new cultures. Her personal experience illustrates this: when living in the Netherlands, she felt her emotions were natural, but after moving to the United States, she soon noticed her emotions were not a good fit for American culture. Americans struck her as unnaturally happy, constantly speaking in an upbeat tone, smiling tremendously, and answering positively when asked how they were doing.
