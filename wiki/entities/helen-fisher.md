# Helen Fisher

## Summary

Evolutionary anthropologist whose research on the biochemistry of love and romance Perel cites. Fisher has studied the neurochemical basis of romantic love, attraction, and attachment.

## Key facts

- Helen Fisher states that the hormonal cocktail of romance (dopamine, norepinephrine, and PEA) is known to last no more than a few years at best. Oxytocin, the cuddling hormone, outlasts them all. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p176]
- According to Fisher, the fruits of ripening love—companionship, deep respect, mutuality, and care—are considered by many to be a fair trade for erotic heat. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p176]
