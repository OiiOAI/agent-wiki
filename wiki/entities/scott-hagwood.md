# Scott Hagwood

## Summary

Scott Hagwood is a four-time USA Memory Championship winner and the first American memory athlete to be ranked as a grand master by the International Association of Memory. Originally an average student with mediocre grades and test anxiety, he developed his extraordinary memory skills after being diagnosed with thyroid cancer at age 36 and learning that radiation therapy would damage his memory. After discovering Tony Buzan's memory training methods, he won his first national championship a year later and defended the title three more times. His story demonstrates that exceptional memory performance comes from trained strategies rather than innate gifts.

## Key facts

- Scott Hagwood was diagnosed with thyroid cancer at 36 and underwent radiation therapy that would damage his memory. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p55]
