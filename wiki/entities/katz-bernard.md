# Bernard Katz

## Summary

Bernard Katz (1911-2003) was a German-born British physiologist who shared the 1970 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Ulf von Euler and Julius Axelrod for his work on the synthesis, storage, and release of neurotransmitters. He also contributed to understanding the ionic basis of synaptic transmission.

## Key facts

- Katz demonstrated that acetylcholine release at the neuromuscular junction occurs in discrete quanta, establishing the vesicle theory of neurotransmitter release.
