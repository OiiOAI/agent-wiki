# Ronald Moen

## Summary

Ronald Moen is a quality management consultant associated with Associates in Process Improvement-Detroit (API). He had unique access to Deming from 1981-1993, managed Deming's monthly visits to Pontiac Motor Division of General Motors from 1982-1986, and served as a Deming helper at 70 of his 4-day seminars. He co-authored 'Improving Quality Through Planned Experimentation' and authored the history of PDSA/PDCA.

## Key facts

- Moen managed Deming's monthly visits to Pontiac Motor Division of General Motors from 1982 through 1986. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p1]
- Moen was a Deming helper at 70 of his famous 4-day seminars from 1982 through 1993. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p1]
