# Thomas Parr

## Summary

Thomas Parr is a researcher in theoretical neuroscience and a co-author of 'Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior' (MIT Press, 2022), credited by Karl Friston in the preface as the primary author of the book. His year of birth is listed as 1993 in the Library of Congress cataloging data.
