# Kevin J. Mitchell

## Summary

Kevin J. Mitchell is a professor of genetics and the author of 'Free Agents: How Evolution Gave Us Free Will' (2023). His research and writing focus on how agency and free will could exist in a physical universe, arguing from an evolutionary perspective that traces the development of choice and autonomous control from the origin of life through human consciousness.
