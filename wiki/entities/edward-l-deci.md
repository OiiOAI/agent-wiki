# Edward L. Deci

## Summary

Edward L. Deci is a professor of psychology at the University of Rochester and director of its human motivation program. He is one of psychology's most influential researchers on human motivation, having conducted research on intrinsic motivation, autonomy, and self-determination for over twenty-five years, largely in collaboration with Richard Ryan. He is the author of 'Why We Do What We Do' and a leading figure in Self-Determination Theory.
