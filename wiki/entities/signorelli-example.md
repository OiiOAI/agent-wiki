# Signorelli Example

## Summary

Freud's famous self-observation from 1898 where he forgot the name 'Signorelli' (the painter of frescoes in Orvieto cathedral) and replaced it with 'Botticelli' and 'Boltraffio'. Analysis revealed the forgetting was caused by repressed thoughts about death and sexuality encountered just before.

## Key facts

- Freud forgot the name Signorelli while traveling from Ragusa to Herzegovina when discussing travel in Italy. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2003]
- The substitute names Botticelli and Boltraffio are connected to the repressed topic through associations with Bosnia, Herzegovina, Trafoi, and Herr (translation of Signor). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2004]
- The repressed material concerned a patient who committed suicide due to an incurable sexual disorder, which Freud had wanted to forget. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2004]
- The name Signorelli was divided: 'elli' recurred in Botticelli, while 'Signor' became 'Herr' and connected to the repressed topic. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2005]

## Inferences

- Inference: The forgetting demonstrated that repression operates through superficial verbal associations rather than logical connections.
