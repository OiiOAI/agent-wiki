# School of One

## Summary

School of One is an educational program that enables teachers to work in small batches by providing students with daily personalized 'playlists' of learning tasks tuned to each student's readiness and learning style. The program includes assessments built into each activity that feed data back to teachers, enabling rapid experimentation with curriculum changes. Time magazine included School of One in its 'most innovative ideas' list as the only educational organization to make the list.

## Key facts

- School of One provides students with daily 'playlists' of learning tasks that are tuned to each student's learning needs based on readiness and learning style. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p182]
- Assessments are built into each activity so data can be fed back to the teacher to choose appropriate tasks for the next playlist, and data can be aggregated across classes, schools, or districts. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p182]
- Teachers can see immediately the impact of curriculum changes on students who are at that point in the curriculum, and if judged good, can roll it out automatically for every student. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p182]
- Time magazine included School of One in its 'most innovative ideas' list and it was the only educational organization to make the list. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p183]
