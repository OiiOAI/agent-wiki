# Thieme Hennis

## Summary

Thieme Hennis is a Dutch researcher who completed his PhD in complex systems design at Delft University of Technology and became instrumental in developing programs for Border Sessions, a tech cultural festival. He launched AstroPlant, an open-source project creating small-scale artificial ecosystems for space exploration under ESA's MELiSSA program.

## Key facts

- Thieme Hennis completed his PhD in complex systems design at Delft University of Technology in 2016. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p105]
- He launched AstroPlant as an open-source, collaborative community project coordinated with ESA's MELiSSA program. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p105-106]
- AstroPlant kits include seeds provided by ESA and are distributed worldwide for users to cultivate plants under different environmental conditions. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p106]
- Hennis describes starting new projects by writing scenarios in spreadsheets and iterating to develop working models. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p126]
