# Gerald Reaven

## Summary

Gerald Reaven was a Stanford endocrinologist who in the 1960s observed that excess weight traveled with other markers of poor health. He identified that heart attack patients often had high fasting glucose, high triglycerides, elevated blood pressure, and abdominal obesity. In the 1980s he labeled this 'Syndrome X' and determined that insulin resistance was the X factor. He fought for decades for insulin resistance to be recognized as a primary cause of type 2 diabetes. He died in 2018 at age 89.
