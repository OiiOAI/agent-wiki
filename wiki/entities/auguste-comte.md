# Auguste Comte

## Summary

Auguste Comte (1798-1857) was a French philosopher who coined the term 'sociology' in 1839. He developed positivism as a philosophical system and believed sociology should be modeled after the physical sciences. His six-volume Cours de Philosophie Positive outlined his view that sociology was the ultimate science. He proposed the law of three stages of intellectual development: theological, metaphysical, and positivistic.
