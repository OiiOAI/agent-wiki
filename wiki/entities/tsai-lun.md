# Ts'ai Lun

## Summary

Ts'ai Lun was an official in the royal court of the Han dynasty in China who is credited with the invention of paper in the year 105 CE. The manufacture of paper spread to the Muslim world after the Battle of Talas in 751, when Arab forces captured Chinese prisoners who taught them papermaking. Paper manufacturing secrets remained unknown in Europe until the twelfth century.
