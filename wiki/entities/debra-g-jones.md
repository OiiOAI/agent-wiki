# Debra G. Jones

## Summary

Dr. Debra G. Jones is a principal research associate with SA Technologies in Marietta, Georgia. Her expertise focuses on the design of large-scale and complex systems to support situation awareness and dynamic decision-making, determining SA requirements in various domains, and developing measures of SA for complex systems. She received a PhD in industrial engineering from Texas Tech University.
