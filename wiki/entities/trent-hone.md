# Trent Hone

## Summary

Trent Hone is a Fellow with Excella in Arlington, VA, and an award-winning naval historian. His book 'Learning War: The Evolution of Fighting Doctrine in the U.S. Navy, 1898-1945' was the U.S. Naval Institute's Book of the Year for 2018.

## Key facts

- Trent Hone documented how Admiral Chester Nimitz fostered innovation during WWII using parallel experimentation to develop the Combat Information Center. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p229]
- Nimitz issued instructions on November 26, 1942, ordering each ship to establish a CIC as an enabling constraint. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p230]
- Three feedback loops provided information about CIC effectiveness: combat experience, informal collaborative networks, and Pacific Fleet action report analysis. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p231]
- The CIC approach used exaptive practice by taking existing plotting techniques and applying them in new integrated ways. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p232]
