# Michael Perry

## Summary

Michael Perry was a mentally ill man who murdered five people in Jennings County, Louisiana in July 1983, including his parents. He was obsessed with a famous client of Gavin de Becker & Associates, who had him on the highest hazard assessment category.

## Key facts

- On July 20, 1983, Michael Perry murdered five people in Jennings County, Louisiana, including his parents Chester and Grace Perry. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p174]
- Perry had shot out his parents' eyes with a shotgun and had also killed an infant nephew and two people in a neighboring house. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p175]
- At the murder scene, police found a list of names including Sandra Day O'Connor and de Becker's famous client, which he had narrowed down to ten people he intended to kill. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p175]
- Perry explained his obsession with the actress by saying she looked like his mother from 1961, the year she allegedly pushed him against a burning wall heater, leaving permanent scars. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p180]
- Perry was apprehended on July 31, 1983, in Washington D.C. by a police officer who stopped him for a minor dispute about a stolen radio. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p179]
- In Room 136 of the Annex Hotel near the Supreme Court, Perry had nine television sets all plugged in and tuned to static, with 'My Body' written on one in red marker. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p181]
- Perry was convicted of five murders and sentenced to death, but remained alive due to a Supreme Court ruling that he could not be forced to take medication just to be executed. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p182]
