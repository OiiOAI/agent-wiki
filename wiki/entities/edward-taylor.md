# Edward Taylor

## Summary

Edward Taylor was a Los Angeles man who stalked radio personality Jim Hicklin for over a year, escalating his pursuit after being visited by private detectives and police, ultimately murdering Hicklin and his wife aboard a cruise ship in 1973.

## Key facts

- Taylor began sending friendly, praising letters to Hicklin, but the intervention by private detectives who told him to stop transformed the situation into an adversarial relationship. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p93]
- Taylor felt 'emasculated' by the intrusion and began a campaign of litigation and complaints to the FAA questioning Hicklin's competency. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p94]
- After being arrested for misdemeanor libel, Taylor purchased a .38 caliber handgun. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p96]
- Taylor murdered Hicklin and his wife on April 2, 1973, as they boarded the Italia cruise ship, shooting them in front of friends who had come to see them off. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p96]
- Taylor continued writing letters from prison until the day he died, demonstrating that arrest did not stop his persistence. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p96]
