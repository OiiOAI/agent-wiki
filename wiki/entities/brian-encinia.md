# Brian Encinia

## Summary

Brian Encinia was a Texas State Trooper who stopped Sandra Bland on July 10, 2015, resulting in her arrest and subsequent death in custody. He was fired for violations of professional conduct guidelines.

## Key facts

- Encinia was a proactive police officer who wrote 1,557 tickets in under a year, routinely stopping drivers for minor violations as part of a Kansas City-style aggressive policing strategy. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p183-184]
- He believed Bland's agitated demeanor and body language indicated something suspicious, interpreting her distress as potential criminal behavior. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p186-187]
- He stopped Sandra Bland on FM 1098, which he characterized as a 'high-crime, high-drug area,' though crime data did not support this characterization. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p191-192]
- He was fired for violating Chapter 5, Section 05.17.00 of the Texas State Trooper General Manual, which requires courtesy, tact, patience, and discretion. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p181]

## Inferences

- Inference: Gladwell suggests Encinia was following his training precisely—using proactive policing techniques and interpreting demeanor through the transparency illusion—but was deployed in the wrong location according to coupling theory.
