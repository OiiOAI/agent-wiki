# Temple of the Universe

## Summary

Temple of the Universe is a spiritual center founded by Michael Singer in Gainesville, Florida, originally on his ten acres of land. Formed as a federally recognized nonprofit in March 1976, the temple grew from a small meditation group to a community serving 70-80 people weekly for over 35 years. The temple is notable for its universal approach, incorporating icons and items representing all religions. It led to the expansion of surrounding land holdings and was the birthplace of Personalized Programming.

## Key facts

- Temple of the Universe was formed as a federally recognized nonprofit organization in March 1976. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p97]
- Michael Singer signed over ownership of his ten acres along with the Temple building, Donna's cabin, and his house to the nonprofit organization. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p97]
- The Temple featured a unique butterfly roof design that was low in the middle and sloped up on both sides. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p91]
- The temple was completed in September 1975 and held its first Sunday service that month. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p92]
- For over 35 years, 70 to 80 people attended Sunday services weekly without advertising or signs directing the way. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p96]
- The Temple grew to encompass 170 acres of contiguous land through organic purchases over two decades. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p146]
