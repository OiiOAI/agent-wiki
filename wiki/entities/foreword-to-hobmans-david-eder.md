# Foreword to Hobman's David Eder

## Summary

A foreword written by Sigmund Freud in 1939 (published 1945) for J.B. Hobman's biography of David Eder. Freud describes Eder as distinguished by 'a rare combination of absolute love of truth and undaunted courage, together with tolerance and a great capacity for love,' noting he was the first, and for a time the only, doctor to practise psychoanalysis in England.

## Key facts

- Freud described M.D. Eder as 'one of those men distinguished by a rare combination of absolute love of truth and undaunted courage, together with tolerance and a great capacity for love.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7453]
- Freud stated Eder was 'the first, and for a time the only doctor to practise the new therapy in England.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7453]
- Freud noted that Eder was also engaged in constructive activity in the Zionist movement, though Freud had 'not then a full insight into the wealth of his personality.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7453]
- Montague David Eder (1865–1936) was an early British psychoanalyst and an ardent Zionist. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7453]
