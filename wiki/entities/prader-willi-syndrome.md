# Prader-Willi Syndrome

## Summary

Prader-Willi syndrome is a neurodevelopmental behavioral disorder caused by deletion of multiple genes on chromosome 15q11-q13, but the implicated genes are expressed only from the paternally derived chromosome. This represents the reciprocal pattern of inheritance compared to Angelman syndrome. Prader-Willi syndrome is associated with obesity and psychiatric symptoms including obsessive-compulsive behavior, bipolar disorder, and pervasive developmental disorders.
