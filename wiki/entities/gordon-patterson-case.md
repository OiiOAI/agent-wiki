# Gordon Patterson Case

## Summary

Gordon Patterson caught his wife Roberta 'in a state of semiundress' with her boyfriend John Northrup. He shot Northrup twice in the head, killing him. Patterson confessed but argued 'extreme emotional disturbance' reduced his culpability. The court agreed, finding him guilty of second-degree murder rather than first-degree, illustrating how the heat-of-passion defense treats emotional killing as less culpable than rational killing.
