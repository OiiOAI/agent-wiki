# Breakthrough Starshot

## Summary

Breakthrough Starshot is an initiative that envisions using high-powered space-based lasers to propel tiny spacecraft to Alpha Centauri, our nearest neighboring star. The primary goal is to take pictures of planets orbiting Alpha Centauri and beam them back to Earth.
