# Connexin 36

## Summary

Connexin 36 is the most common connexin protein forming gap junction channels in mammalian neurons, encoded by the GJA9 gene in humans.

## Key facts

- Connexin 36 is the predominant connexin in neurons of mammals, encoded by a gene distinct from other connexin family members. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p244]
- Gap junction channels formed by connexin 36 subunits have been crystallized and their structure determined at high resolution, revealing the voltage gate formed by the N-terminal helix. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p246]
