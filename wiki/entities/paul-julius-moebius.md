# Paul Julius Moebius

## Summary

Paul Julius August Moebius (1853–1907) was a German neurologist from Leipzig who specialized in neurology and functional nervous disorders. Freud reviewed his monograph on migraine, praising Moebius's critical thinking and his rejection of vasomotor theories.

## Key facts

- Moebius was a neurologist from Leipzig who studied theology and philosophy before turning to medicine. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p136]
- He wrote a monograph on migraine in Nothnagel's handbook, rejecting the vasomotor theory and arguing that parenchymal processes are primary. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p137]
- Freud described Moebius as having 'the best mind among the neurologists' who was 'not on the track of sexuality'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p136]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud's review indicates both admiration for Moebius's scientific approach and the beginning of Freud's own path toward emphasizing sexuality in pathology.
