# Ronald P. Dore

## Summary

Ronald P. Dore was a British sociologist who conducted pioneering comparative studies of British and Japanese industrial relations. His major works include 'British Factory, Japanese Factory' (1973), which examined differences in employment practices between the two countries.

## Key facts

- Ronald P. Dore authored British Factory, Japanese Factory (London: Allen and Unwin, 1973). [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p420]
- Dore noted that although some British trade unionists accept that industry health is important to them, more militant ones hope their industry will do poorly to expedite collapse of the capitalist system. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p420]
- Dore discussed how Japanese companies maintain flexibility despite the appearance of rigid lifetime employment commitments. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p419]
