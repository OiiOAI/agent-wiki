# Kausik Si

## Summary

Kausik Si is an Indian scientist who joined Kandel's laboratory in 1999. With a background in protein synthesis research from yeast, he discovered that CPEB in Aplysia neurons has prion-like properties, representing the first identification of a normal physiological function for a prion-like protein.

## Key facts

- Si discovered that CPEB in Aplysia has prion-like properties, with one end of the protein having characteristics of a prion. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p283]
- Si proposed the 'Sleeping Beauty' hypothesis that dormant messenger RNA molecules are activated only at appropriately marked synapses. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p283]
- The conversion of CPEB from inactive to active form is controlled by serotonin, the transmitter required for converting short- to long-term memory. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p285]
- This was the first discovery of a self-propagating form of a prion serving a normal physiological function, rather than causing disease. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p285]
