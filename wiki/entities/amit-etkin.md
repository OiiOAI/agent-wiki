# Amyt Etkin

## Summary

Amit Etkin was an M.D.-Ph.D. student who collaborated with Kandel and Joy Hirsch to study how conscious and unconscious perceptions of emotional stimuli are processed differently in the brain. This research extended Crick and Koch's paradigm into the emotional sphere, exploring how people respond consciously and unconsciously to fearful versus neutral facial expressions, using images cataloged by Peter Ekman.

## Key facts

- Amit Etkin and Kandel, in collaboration with Joy Hirsch, studied how conscious and unconscious perceptions of emotional stimuli are processed, extending Crick and Koch's cognitive paradigm into the emotional sphere. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p394]
- The study used pictures of people with neutral or fearful facial expressions provided by Peter Ekman, who cataloged more than 100,000 human expressions showing that seven facial expressions have universal meaning across cultures. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p394]
