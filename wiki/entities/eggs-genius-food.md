# Eggs (Genius Food #5)

## Summary

Eggs, especially the yolks, are among the most nutritious foods for the brain. An egg yolk is perfectly designed by nature to contain everything needed to grow a healthy, optimally performing brain. They contain nearly every vitamin and mineral required by the human body, including vitamin A, B12, E, selenium, zinc, choline (important for acetylcholine production), lutein, and zeaxanthin. Studies show that even high egg consumption does not increase risk for cardiovascular disease or Alzheimer's disease. The recommendation is to consume eggs with runny yolks to preserve vulnerable fats and cholesterol from oxidation.

## Key facts

- Egg yolks contain a little bit of nearly every vitamin and mineral required by the human body, including vitamin A, vitamin B12, vitamin E, selenium, and zinc. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
- Egg yolks provide an abundant source of choline, which is important for both healthy, flexible cell membranes and the learning and memory neurotransmitter acetylcholine. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
- Egg yolks contain lutein and zeaxanthin, two carotenoids shown to protect the brain and improve neural processing speed. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
- Eating just 1.3 egg yolks per day for 4.5 weeks increased blood levels of zeaxanthin by 114 to 142 percent and lutein by 28 to 50 percent. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
- Studies show that even a high degree of egg consumption does not increase risk for cardiovascular disease or Alzheimer's disease. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
- A study in men and women with metabolic syndrome found that with a reduced-carbohydrate diet, three whole eggs per day reduced insulin resistance, raised HDL, and increased the size of LDL particles. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p184]
