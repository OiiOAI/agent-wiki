# Lawrence W. Barsalou

## Summary

Lawrence W. Barsalou is a leading cognitive scientist who developed the theory that concepts are goal-based rather than defined by perceptual similarity, showing that instances are unified by common purposes rather than physical features.

## Key facts

- Barsalou proposed that concepts are not fixed definitions or prototypes but populations of instances unified by goals. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p110]
- According to Barsalou, your brain has many instances of a concept and imposes similarities between them based on your goal in a situation. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p110]
