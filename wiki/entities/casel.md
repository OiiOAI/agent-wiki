# CASEL

## Summary

CASEL (Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning) was formed in 1994 with the goal of establishing high-quality, evidence-based social and emotional learning as an essential part of preschool through high school education. It provides the widely-cited definition of SEL used in the field.

## Key facts

- CASEL defines a school that prioritizes SEL as one where 'children and adults understand and manage emotions, set and achieve positive goals, feel and show empathy for others, establish and maintain positive relationships, and make responsible decisions.'. - CASEL was formed in 1994 with the goal of establishing high-quality, evidence-based social and emotional learning as an essential part of preschool through high school education. - According to CASEL, SEL recognizes that three intertwined factors—cognition, behavior, and emotion—must all be considered to create emotionally healthy environments where learning can take place.
