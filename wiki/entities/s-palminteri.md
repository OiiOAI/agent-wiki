# S. Palminteri

## Summary

Stefano Palminteri is a researcher affiliated with University College London and the École Normale Supérieure who, together with M. Pessiglione, authored Chapter 23 on opponent brain systems for reward and punishment learning. His research focuses on the computational and neural mechanisms of reinforcement learning in humans, with particular emphasis on causal evidence from pharmacological and lesion studies.

## Key facts

- Palminteri and Pessiglione review evidence for four hypotheses regarding the neural implementation of punishment learning, drawing on drug and lesion studies in humans.
