# Nadine Burke Harris

## Summary

Dr. Nadine Burke Harris is a pediatrician and former Surgeon General of California who discovered that many children diagnosed with ADHD were actually experiencing hypervigilance due to trauma and toxic stress. Her research, rooted in the Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACE) Study, demonstrated that children who experienced four or more types of trauma were 32.6 times more likely to be diagnosed with attention or behavior problems than children with no trauma exposure. She pioneered a treatment approach that addresses root causes rather than simply prescribing stimulants.

## Key facts

- Children who experienced four or more types of trauma were 32.6 times more likely to be diagnosed with attention or behavior problems than children who had not experienced any trauma. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p190]
- She was appointed Surgeon General of California, the most senior medical position in the state. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p193]
- She grew up in Palo Alto, California, with a mother who had mental illness, which gave her personal insight into how fear and hypervigilance affect children. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p186-187]
- She practiced in Bayview, one of the last non-gentrified parts of San Francisco, a poor neighborhood with high violence. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p187]
