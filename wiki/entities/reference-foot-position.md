# Reference Foot Position

## Summary

Reference Foot Position is a neutral standing position used as a baseline for proper body alignment during walking and standing. In this position, feet are straight and underneath the hips, with 50% of weight on the balls of the feet and 50% on the heels. The ankles should be in the middle of the feet. This position provides optimal balance and helps train the brain for better locomotion.

## Key facts

- In Reference Foot Position, feet should be straight and underneath the hips. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1252-L1255]
- Weight distribution should be 50% on the balls of the feet and 50% on the heels. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1252-L1255]
- Ankles should be in the middle of the feet, not collapsing in, out, toward the front, or toward the back. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1255-L1258]
- Walking with feet pointing straight ahead allows access to natural mechanics and puts more power into the walk. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1260-L1263]
- The arch of the foot is a nonloading surface meant to be springy rather than bear weight. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1269-L1273]
