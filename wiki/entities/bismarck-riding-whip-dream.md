# Bismarck's Riding Whip Dream

## Summary

A dream recorded by Bismarck in 1863 during the Constitutional Conflict, where he rode on a narrow Alpine path, struck a rock with his whip which grew to endless length, opening a broad path with Prussian troops. The dream was analyzed by Hanns Sachs revealing phallic symbolism.
