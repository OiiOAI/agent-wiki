# Tom Shadyac

## Summary

Tom Shadyac is a film director who gave Jim Carrey his first big role in Ace Ventura: Pet Detective and went on to direct Liar Liar, Bruce Almighty, and other hits. By the early 2000s, his films had grossed over two billion dollars and he was worth over fifty million dollars. He then underwent a values-driven life transformation, selling his mansion, flying economy class, riding bikes for local transport, and becoming choosier about film projects. He redefined success as intrinsic ('It's love. It's kindness. It's community') rather than extrinsic (wealth and status). His story illustrates 'walking your why.'
