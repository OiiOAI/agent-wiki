# Stanislas Dehaene

## Summary

Stanislas Dehaene is a French cognitive neuroscientist and author of this book. He leads research on the cognitive neuroscience of reading and numerical cognition, and has developed the 'global neuronal workspace' theory of consciousness. He is a professor at the Collège de France.
