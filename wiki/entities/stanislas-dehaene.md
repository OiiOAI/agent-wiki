# Stanislas Dehaene

## Summary

Stanislas Dehaene is a French cognitive neuroscientist and author of this book. He leads research on the cognitive neuroscience of reading and numerical cognition, and has developed the 'global neuronal workspace' theory of consciousness. He is a professor at the Collège de France.

## Key facts

- Dehaene is a French cognitive neuroscientist working at the Collège de France. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p10]
- He previously authored 'Reading in the Brain' and 'The Number Sense'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p2]
- He proposes the 'global neuronal workspace' theory of consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p25]
- Stanislas Dehaene is affiliated with the Laboratoire de Sciences Cognitives et Psycholinguistique, CNRS/ENS/EHESS, Paris, France. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p15]
- Dehaene is a Series Editor for the Contemporary Topics in Cognitive Neuroscience Series published by Psychology Press. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p4]
- Dehaene and Sigman developed the global workspace theory, proposing that consciousness and executive processes require engagement of a brain-scale neural network. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p58-59]
- Dehaene developed a neuronal model of a global workspace in effortful cognitive tasks with Changeux and Kerszberg in 1998. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p65]
