# Richard W. Pew

## Summary

Richard W. Pew is a researcher at BBN Technologies who authored this chapter on the state of situation awareness measurement. He provides a formal definition of situation awareness and presents a taxonomy of measurement methods for assessing SA. Pew's work distinguishes between ideal, achievable ideal, and actual SA, and he critically examines various measurement techniques including SAGAT and SART. His collaborators on these ideas included Yvette J. Tenney, Marilyn Jager Adams, Stephen Deutsch, and William H. Rogers, with work supported by NASA Langley Research Center.
