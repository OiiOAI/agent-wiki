# Lia DiBello

## Summary

A consultant who helped save a financially strapped iron foundry from bankruptcy. With a Ph.D. examining how people use tools for conceptual tasks, she specializes in helping large organizations implement complex software schedulers with a hands-on approach of visiting work sites in person.

## Key facts

- DiBello's company, Workplace Technologies Research, Inc., has worked on more than twenty projects for introducing complex software schedulers with zero failures. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p230]
- She has worked extensively with the New York Transit System, investigating bus and subway maintenance firsthand. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p230]
- DiBello used a simulation game and follow-up review to let foundry workers understand why their mental models weren't working and made them eager to try the 'To be cast' report. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p241]
