# Antoine Bechara

## Summary

Antoine Bechara is a decision neuroscientist who holds the CRC Chair in Decision Neuroscience at McGill University and the University of Southern California. He is a prominent contributor to the neuroscience of decision making, known for developing the Iowa Gambling Task and for his research on the role of emotion in decision making.

## Key facts

- Antoine Bechara holds the CRC Chair in Decision Neuroscience at McGill University, Canada and the Department of Psychology at the University of Southern California, USA. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p15]
- Bechara developed the Iowa Gambling Task, a paradigm that successfully detects decision-making impairments in neurological patients that standard tests fail to reveal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p108]
- Bechara's research examines the neural basis of decision making and the role of emotion in advantageous choice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p106-107]
- He outlines specific conditions under which emotions can be useful and disruptive to decision making, and describes the multiple cortical systems comprising the emotional circuitry of the brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p20]
