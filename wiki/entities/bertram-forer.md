# Bertram Forer

## Summary

Bertram Forer was a psychologist who conducted a famous 1948 experiment demonstrating that people rate identical vague personality descriptions as highly accurate when told they were personalized.

## Key facts

- Bertram Forer crafted a personality description from astrology columns and gave it to students as a personalized assessment; they rated it 4.3 out of 5 for accuracy. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p145]
