# Henri Lefebvre

## Summary

Henri Lefebvre (1901-1991) was a French Marxist sociologist and philosopher who developed groundbreaking work on the production of space. His tripartite distinction among spatial practice, representations of space, and representational spaces has been highly influential in geography, urban studies, and social theory.

## Key facts

- Lefebvre argued for shifting the focus of Marxian theory from the means of production to the production of space. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Lefebvre distinguished spatial practice (production and reproduction of space), representations of space (space as conceived by elites like urban planners), and representational spaces (lived experiences). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Representations of space are dominant over both spatial practices and representational spaces, with elites considering this 'true space' while using it to achieve dominance. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p201]
- Lefebvre emphasized the role of the state, more than economic forces, in exercising power over abstract space, though that exercise of power is hidden. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
- Lefebvre argued that abstract space serves as a tool of power, with the ruling class using it to gain control over increasingly large spaces. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
- Space can take roles as: a force of production, a vast commodity, a politically instrumental entity, a basis for reproducing property relations, a form of superstructure, and a space of positive potential. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p202]
