# Esther Perel

## Summary

A couples therapist of Belgian origin who trained in Israel and completed her training in the United States. She speaks eight languages and specializes in cross-cultural psychology, working with refugee families, international families, and intercultural couples. Perel brings a multicultural perspective to her analysis of love, desire, and intimacy.

## Key facts

- Perel grew up in Belgium, studied in Israel, and completed her clinical training in the United States, giving her a multicultural perspective on relationships. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p12]
- Her parents were survivors of Nazi concentration camps who taught her an embodied understanding of eroticism as a quality of aliveness and a pathway to freedom, separate from the narrow modern definition of sex. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p13]
- Perel has practiced for twenty years as a couples therapist and sees a wide spectrum of clients including straight, gay, married, committed, single, and remarried individuals across cultures, races, and classes. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p12]
- She inverts traditional therapeutic priorities by examining how sexual dynamics illuminate relationship conflicts, rather than assuming relationship improvement leads to better sex. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p11]
