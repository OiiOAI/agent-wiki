# AgRP Neurons

## Summary

AgRP (agouti-related peptide) neurons are hunger-promoting neurons in the arcuate nucleus of the hypothalamus. They release three factors: AgRP (an inverse agonist of MC4R), NPY, and GABA. AgRP neurons are antagonistically opposed to POMC neurons in controlling energy balance. Activation of AgRP neurons rapidly induces hyperphagia even in calorically replete animals, while genetic ablation causes starvation. AgRP neurons also receive top-down feedforward information from environmental cues predicting food.

## Key facts

- AgRP neurons release AgRP (MC4R antagonist), NPY, and GABA, which together increase hunger by inhibiting PVH-MC4R satiety neurons. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1084]
- Optogenetic stimulation of AgRP neurons rapidly induces extreme hyperphagia even in sated mice, demonstrating their sufficiency to drive feeding. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1084]
- Genetic ablation of AgRP neurons causes starvation, demonstrating their necessity for feeding behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1084]
- AgRP neuron activity decreases when fasted mice are presented with food alone without ingestion, indicating top-down feedforward regulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1084]
- Activation of AgRP neurons is aversive, creating an unpleasant state that motivates feeding behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1085]

## Inferences

- Inference: The aversive quality of AgRP neuron activation provides a mechanism for why dieting is difficult—it generates an unpleasant hunger state.
