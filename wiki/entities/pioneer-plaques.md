# Pioneer Plaques

## Summary

The Pioneer plaques are metal plaques attached to the Pioneer 10 and 11 spacecraft launched in the early 1970s, designed by Carl Sagan. They show where the craft came from and include a picture of a man and woman, intended as messages to any extraterrestrial beings who might find them.

## Key facts

- The Pioneer planetary probes were launched in the early 1970s and have exited the solar system. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p199]
- Astronomer Carl Sagan advocated for including a plaque showing where the craft came from with a picture of a man and woman. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p199]
- At the rate they are traveling, it will be tens of thousands of years before the Pioneer probes could possibly reach another star. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p199]
- Hawkins describes the Pioneer plaques as the first 'messages in a bottle' and mostly symbolic because the chance they encounter anything is tiny. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p199]
