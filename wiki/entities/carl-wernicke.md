# Carl Wernicke

## Summary

Prussian neurologist who discovered that language comprehension is distinct from speech production and is located further back in the left hemisphere, in the posterior superior temporal gyrus (Wernicke's area).

## Key facts

- Carl Wernicke discovered through observation that the comprehension of language was distinct from speech and located further back in the left hemisphere, in the posterior superior temporal gyrus. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p41]
