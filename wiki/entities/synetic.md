# Synetic

## Summary

A New Jersey company that was building a healthcare Internet portal with one of the finest management teams in the industry. Synetic merged with Medical Manager Corporation in May 1999 in a $1.3 billion deal, with the combined company keeping the Medical Manager Corporation name.
