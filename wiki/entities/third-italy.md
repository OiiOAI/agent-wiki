# Third Italy

## Summary

The Third Italy (Terza Italia) is a concept developed by sociologist Arnoldo Bagnasco to describe the distinctive economic model of central and northeastern Italy. This region developed through networks of small and medium-sized enterprises specializing in flexible, specialized production, contrasting with both the industrial North (First Italy) and the agrarian South (Second Italy).

## Key facts

- The concept of Third Italy was articulated by Arnoldo Bagnasco in 'Tre Italie' (1977). [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p394]
- The Third Italy is characterized by small-scale industrialization and flexible production networks. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p394]
