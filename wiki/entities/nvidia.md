# Nvidia Corporation

## Summary

Nvidia was founded in 1993 by Jen-Hsun Huang, Curtis Priem, and Chris Malachowsky to become a designer of 3-D graphics chips. After an initial product failure (NV1), the company reformed its strategy to focus on 3-D graphics for desktop PCs using the SGI-based triangle rendering method. Nvidia implemented a six-month product release cycle through three overlapping development teams, investing heavily in emulation facilities to enable early driver development. By 2007, Nvidia dominated the high-performance 3-D graphics chip market and was named 'Company of the Year' by Forbes.
