# Patient SM

## Summary

Patient SM is a woman who suffered from Urbach-Wiethe disease, a rare genetic condition that results in bilateral calcification of the amygdala. She felt no fear and consequently was victimized numerous times—held up at knifepoint and gunpoint, nearly killed in a domestic violence incident, and received multiple death threats. Her impairment included a marked inability to detect looming threats and learn to steer clear of dangerous situations. Her dreams are short, simple, and manifestly wishful, reflecting her fearless imagination.
