# Dr. J (Mass Murderer of Steinhof)

## Summary

Dr. J was described by Frankl as the only man he would dare to call a satanic figure—a psychiatrist who held all the strings in the Nazi euthanasia program and was so fanatic in his job that he tried not to let one single psychotic individual escape the gas chamber. After the war, he escaped to South America via the help of comrades. However, he was later imprisoned in Moscow's Lubyanka prison, where he contracted cancer and died at age forty. Before dying, he showed himself to be the best comrade, giving consolation to everyone and living up to the highest moral standards—illustrating Frankl's point that no prediction of human behavior can be definitive.
