# Daniel Kilov

## Summary

An Australian memory champion who won back-to-back silver medals in the Australian Memory Championships, who transformed from a struggling student with ADD to graduating with first-class honors through memory training.

## Key facts

- Kilov can memorize a shuffled deck of cards, 100 random digits, or 115 abstract shapes in five minutes. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p103]
- He went from a struggling student with attention deficit disorder to graduating from university with first-class honors through intensive memory training. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p104]
- Research by Henry Roediger found that the biggest difference between memory athletes and others is attentional control—the ability to maintain focus on essential information. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p104]
