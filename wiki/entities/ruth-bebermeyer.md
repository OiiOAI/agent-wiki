# Ruth Bebermeyer

## Summary

Ruth Bebermeyer is a musician and poet who contributed lyrics to Nonviolent Communication materials. Her song 'Words Are Windows (or They're Walls)' opens Chapter 1 of Rosenberg's book, and her poem 'Given To' illustrates the concept of giving from the heart.

## Key facts

- Ruth Bebermeyer wrote the poem 'Words Are Windows (or They're Walls)' which appears at the beginning of Chapter 1 of Nonviolent Communication. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p24]
- Her song 'Given To' (1978) illustrates the concept of giving from the heart in NVC. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p29]
- She contrasted static and process language in a song illustrating the difference between evaluation and observation. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p52-54]
- She wrote the song 'I Never Feel More Given To' which is used in NVC workshops. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p29]
