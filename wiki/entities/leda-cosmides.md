# Leda Cosmides

## Summary

Leda Cosmides is an evolutionary psychologist who demonstrated that humans possess specialized cognitive machinery for detecting cheaters in social exchange situations, showing that cheater detection operates faster and more accurately than general logical reasoning.

## Key facts

- Cosmides used the Wason Test to demonstrate that 65-80% of people correctly identify cheaters in social exchange scenarios, compared to only 5-30% solving equivalent logic problems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p108]
- Cosmides found that cheater detection develops early, operates regardless of experience and familiarity, and detects intentional cheating but not unintentional violations. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p109]
- Cosmides proposed that the cheater detection ability is a component of universal human nature designed by natural selection to produce evolutionarily stable reciprocal altruism. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p109]
