# Gilgamesh

## Summary

Legendary king of the Sumerian city of Erech who sought the watercress of immortality, the plant 'Never Grow Old.' After a journey past lions, scorpion men, and the sea, he learned the secret of the plant from Utnapishtim but lost it to a serpent while sleeping.

## Key facts

- Gilgamesh passed safely the lions that guard the foothills and the scorpion men who watch the heaven-supporting mountains. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p143]
- Siduri-Sabitu advised Gilgamesh to accept mortal joys, saying 'When the gods created man, they put death upon mankind, and held life in their own hands'. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p144]
- Gilgamesh tied stones to his feet and plunged to the bottom of the cosmic sea to pluck the immortality plant, though it mutilated his hand. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p145]
- A serpent smelled the plant's perfume while Gilgamesh slept and carried it away, gaining the power to slough its skin and renew its youth. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p145]
