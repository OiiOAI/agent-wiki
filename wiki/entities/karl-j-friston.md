# Karl J. Friston

## Summary

Karl J. Friston is a British neuroscientist and theoretical neurobiologist at University College London, known as the originator of the free energy principle and Active Inference framework. He is a co-author of 'Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior' (MIT Press, 2022). Friston is a Wellcome Trust Principal Research Fellow and has developed key computational theories in neuroscience including the random effects analysis of functional neuroimaging data (Statistical Parametric Mapping), voxel-based morphometry, and dynamic causal modeling. His work on the free energy principle provides a unified account of action, perception, and learning in the brain.
