# Roger Nicoll

## Summary

Roger Nicoll at UC San Francisco independently discovered with Gary Lynch that calcium influx into postsynaptic cells acts as a second messenger triggering long-term potentiation. He further showed that calcium activates a kinase that strengthens synaptic connections by inserting additional AMPA receptors into the postsynaptic membrane.

## Key facts

- Roger Nicoll at UC San Francisco and Gary Lynch at UC Irvine independently discovered that calcium ion flow into the postsynaptic cell acts as a second messenger triggering long-term potentiation. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p294]
- Nicoll showed that calcium influx and activation of calcium/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase lead to strengthening of synaptic connections by causing additional AMPA receptors to be assembled and inserted into the postsynaptic membrane. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p295]
