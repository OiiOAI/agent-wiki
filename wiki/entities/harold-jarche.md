# Harold Jarche

## Summary

Harold Jarche is a workplace learning consultant and former Canadian Armed Forces officer. He applies the Cynefin Framework to organizational learning, knowledge management, and the integration of work and learning in complex environments.

## Key facts

- Harold Jarche first encountered Cynefin in late 2007, the same year as The Cluetrain Manifesto. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p239]
- Jarche developed principles of network management as an alternative to Taylor's scientific management for knowledge work. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p240]
- He recommends Glenda Eoyang's three types of accountability: outcome-based for stable systems, learning-based for active self-organizing systems, and sharing-based for random chaotic systems. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p242]
- Jarche advocates for personal knowledge mastery as a framework for sense-making in complex work environments. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p244]
