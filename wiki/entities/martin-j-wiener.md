# Martin J. Wiener

## Summary

British historian and author of 'English Culture and the Decline of the Industrial Spirit' (Cambridge University Press, 1981), a work cited by Fukuyama regarding contrasts in work attitudes between Britain and America.

## Key facts

- Martin J. Wiener wrote 'English Culture and the Decline of the Industrial Spirit' published by Cambridge University Press in 1981. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p435]
- Wiener's work examines the decline of industrial spirit in English culture. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p435]
- According to Wiener, British attitudes toward industrial work became increasingly negative over time. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p435]
