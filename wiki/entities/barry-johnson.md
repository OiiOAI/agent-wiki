# Barry Johnson

## Summary

Barry Johnson is an expert on leadership and author of Polarity Management: Identifying and Managing Unsolvable Problems. Esther Perel draws on his framework of polarity management to explain that desire in long-term relationships requires reconciling opposing forces like freedom and commitment—not solving a problem but managing a paradox. Johnson describes polarities as sets of interdependent opposites belonging to the same whole, where neither can be chosen over the other because the system needs both to survive.
