# Hip Extension Isometrics

## Summary

Hip Extension Isometrics are isometric exercises that place the body in exaggerated versions of natural positions to help the brain accept greater ranges of motion. They involve contracting and releasing muscles while in position. Three variations are provided: Kneeling Isometric, Standing Isometric, and Couch Isometric (Rear Foot Elevated Mini Lunge).

## Key facts

- Isometric exercises require contracting and releasing muscles as you go. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1021-L1023]
- Making a tight fist is an example of an isometric contraction. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1021-L1023]
- The purpose is to place the body in exaggerated natural positions to signal the brain that movement into those positions is safe. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1025-L1029]
- Kneeling Isometric: Kneel with right leg at 90 degrees, left knee behind, squeeze right glute and hold while breathing for one minute. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1033-L1043]
- Standing Isometric: Lunge position with left foot back, squeeze left glute and hold while breathing for 30 seconds. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1045-L1053]
- Couch Isometric (Rear Foot Elevated Mini Lunge): Step forward on couch arm with right foot, rest left shin on arm, bend right knee and squeeze glute for 30 seconds. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1055-L1062]
