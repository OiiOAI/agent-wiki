# Aardvark

## Summary

Aardvark was a virtual personal assistant product created by Max and Damon that allowed customers to ask questions via instant messaging, routing them to the customer's social network. It was the sixth prototype in a series of experiments, each taking two to four weeks, and was ultimately acquired by Google for $50 million.

## Key facts

- Aardvark was created by Max and Damon who spent six months building a series of functioning products to test different approaches to solving customer problems. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p98]
- Aardvark was the sixth prototype; each prototype took two to four weeks to build and used humans to replicate the backend. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p99]
- The product worked via instant messaging (IM), routing questions to a customer's friends and friends of friends within their social network. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p100]
- Aardvark was acquired by Google for a reported $50 million. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p100]
- The founders raised seed and Series A rounds before the system was automated, using humans to manage queries and classify conversations for nine months. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p99]
