# Fei Xu

## Summary

Fei Xu is a developmental psychologist who demonstrated that infants as young as ten months can learn statistical regularities and form goal-based concepts, showing infants estimate probabilities to maximize desired outcomes.

## Key facts

- Xu demonstrated that ten- to fourteen-month-old children can estimate probabilities based on observed patterns to maximize outcomes. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p114]
- Xu showed infants use sounds of a word to predict whether objects make the same noise, learning patterns transcending physical appearance. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p117]
- Infants can learn goal-based concepts given a word more easily than concepts defined by physical similarity without a word. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p117]
