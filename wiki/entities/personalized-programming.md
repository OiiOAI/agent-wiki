# Personalized Programming

## Summary

Personalized Programming was a software company founded by Michael Singer that grew from a one-person operation in the woods of Alachua, Florida to a major player in the medical software industry. Starting in 1979 when Singer bought his first Radio Shack TRS-80 computer, the company developed The Medical Manager and merged with other dealers in 1997 to form Medical Manager Corporation, a public company trading on NASDAQ under the symbol MMGR.

## Key facts

- Personalized Programming was incorporated in the State of Florida in 1980 after Singer's brother-in-law Harvey suggested it for liability purposes. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p121]
- The company began after the Radio Shack store manager referred customers to Singer who wanted custom programs written. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p118]
- Singer's first computer was a Radio Shack TRS-80 Model I with 16k of memory purchased for $600. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p116]
- By late 1979, the company was selling and supporting accounting software packages and related hardware. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p118]
- The company was generating over $100,000 a year by the early 1980s. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p121]
- In 1997, Personalized Programming merged with Systems Plus and several dealerships to form Medical Manager Corporation, which went public on February 2, 1997. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p170]
- Michael Singer became CEO and chairman of the board of Medical Manager Corporation. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p170]
- The company grew from 4,300 square feet to over 85,000 square feet of office space. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p155]
