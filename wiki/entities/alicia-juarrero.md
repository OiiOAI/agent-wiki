# Alicia Juarrero

## Summary

Alicia Juarrero is author of 'Dynamics in Action, Intentional Behavior as a Complex System' (MIT Press) and co-editor of 'Emergence, Self-Organization and Complexity.' Named 2002 U.S. Professor of the Year by the CASE/Carnegie Foundation, she is President and Co-Founder of VectorAnalytica, Inc. Her work on contextual description and complex dynamics influenced early Cynefin thinking.

## Key facts

- Alicia Juarrero authored 'Dynamics in Action, Intentional Behavior as a Complex System' published by MIT Press. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p253]
- She was named 2002 U.S. Professor of the Year by the CASE/Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p253]
- Juarrero's 1999 book was identified by the Cynefin website as a foundational text, leading to her invitation to early Cynefin meetings at IBM. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
