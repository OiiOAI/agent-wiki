# Wellbeing of Future Generations Act

## Summary

The Wellbeing of Future Generations Act is Welsh legislation passed in 2015 requiring public services to act sustainably and consider future generations' impact. It is the first law of its kind globally and is aligned with the Cynefin Framework.

## Key facts

- Wales is the first country to have a legal requirement on public services to act sustainably and consider the impact of future generations' decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p132]
- The Act requires Five Ways of Working: Collaboration, Long Term Thinking, Integration, Citizen Involvement, and Prevention. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p132]
- The Act's architect, former Welsh Government Minister Jane Davidson, was a faculty member at a Cynefin retreat. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p133]
- The Cynefin Framework and Wellbeing of Future Generations Act are described as perfect partners. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p133]
