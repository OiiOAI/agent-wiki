# FBI Crisis Negotiation Unit

## Summary

The FBI's Crisis Negotiation Unit (CNU) is the elite negotiation unit attached to the FBI's Hostage Rescue Team, considered the special forces of negotiations and a national counterterrorist response asset based at Quantico.

## Key facts

- The CNU is the equivalent of the special forces of negotiations and is attached to the FBI's Hostage Rescue Team (HRT). [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p87]
- Both CNU and HRT are national counterterrorist response assets described as 'the best of the best.'. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p87]
- The CNU is based at the FBI Academy in Quantico, Virginia, considered a center of knowledge for law enforcement negotiations. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p87]
- Chris Voss became the FBI's lead international kidnapping negotiator as a result of the Schilling case. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p89]
