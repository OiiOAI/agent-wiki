# Jean-Francois Lyotard

## Summary

Jean-Francois Lyotard is one of the most important postmodernists, particularly known for his rejection of grand narratives. He identified modern scientific knowledge with single grand synthesis or metadiscourse. His famous definition is that postmodern is 'increduity to metanarratives.'
