# Neuroglial Cell

## Summary

Glial cells are non-neuronal cells in the nervous system that support nerve cells. They outnumber neurons 2 to 10 times in the vertebrate CNS. Unlike neurons, glia do not have dendrites and axons and are not electrically excitable. They provide myelin insulation for axons, help establish the chemical milieu for neuronal operation, and couple nerve activity to vascular supply. Recent research shows they play important roles in guiding connectivity during development and stabilizing altered connections during learning.
