# DevOps Topologies

## Summary

DevOps Topologies refers to the earlier work by Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais that cataloged and analyzed organizational design patterns for IT organizations undergoing DevOps and SRE transformations. This work served as the foundation for the book 'Team Topologies'.

## Key facts

- DevOps Topologies was a crucial framework for understanding DevOps state and defining visions for aspirational DevOps operating models. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- DevOps Topologies helped organizations navigate pitfalls and organizational anti-patterns during transformation. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- The book 'Team Topologies' grew from the success of DevOps Topologies. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- DevOps Topologies was created by Matthew Skelton in 2013 in a blog post titled 'What Team Structure Is Right for DevOps to Flourish?'. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L446]
- DevOps Topologies presented a static view of team interrelationships that was useful for initial discussions but limited in scope. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L452-L453]
- DevOps Topologies patterns were expanded by Manuel Pais in 2015 with community contributions. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L450-L451]
- DevOps Topologies patterns have been referenced in talks, articles, and conversations worldwide. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L451-L452]
- DevOps Topologies evolved into Team Topologies as a dynamic and evolving approach to organizational design. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L455-L456]
- DevOps Topologies was originally created by Matthew Skelton in 2013 and later expanded by Manuel Pais. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- The catalog is an online collection of team design and interaction patterns and anti-patterns that work well for kick-starting conversations around team responsibilities, interfaces, and collaboration. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- Successful patterns are strongly dependent on contextual aspects like organization and product size, engineering maturity, and shared goals. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- The topologies were never meant to be static structures but rather a depiction of a moment in time influenced by multiple factors. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
- There is no 'right' topology, but several 'bad' topologies for any one organization. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1271-L2183]
