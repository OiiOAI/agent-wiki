# Fredric Jameson

## Summary

Fredric Jameson is a moderate postmodern social theorist associated with Marxian analysis of postmodernism. He argues that capitalism continues to be dominant in its 'late' phase and has spawned a new cultural logic—postmodernism. His major work 'Postmodernism, or The Cultural Logic of Late Capitalism' argues for continuity between modernity and postmodernity. He proposes cognitive mapping as a solution to postmodern disorientation. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p522]

## Key facts

- Jameson sees postmodernism as the cultural logic of late capitalism, arguing for continuity rather than radical break with modernity. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p522]
- He sees postmodern society characterized by superficiality, waning of emotion, loss of historicity, and new reproductive technologies. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p525]
- Jameson proposes cognitive mapping as the political form needed to help subjects position themselves in postmodern society. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p526]
