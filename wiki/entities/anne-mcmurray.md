# Anne McMurray

## Summary

Anne McMurray is an independent organizational development consultant specializing in leadership and team development, strategy formulation, and change implementation. A practitioner with Cognitive Edge since 2004, she has supported organizations including health, education, social services, and local government to use SenseMaker methodology.

## Key facts

- Anne McMurray has been a practitioner with Cognitive Edge since 2004. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p328]
- She initiated the 10000+ Voices project in the Northern Ireland health system, which led to changes in policy and practice and became a demonstrator project internationally. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p328]
- McMurray was introduced to Dave Snowden through David Gurteen's Knowledge Café meeting in March 2003 in London. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p328]
