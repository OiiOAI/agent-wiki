# Lisa Feldman Barrett

## Summary

Lisa Feldman Barrett is a psychologist and neuroscientist whose groundbreaking book 'How Emotions Are Made' introduced the concept of body budgeting—the idea that the brain tracks and anticipates the use and replenishment of key resources for maintaining bodily functioning.

## Key facts

- Lisa Feldman Barrett is a psychologist and neuroscientist who wrote the groundbreaking book 'How Emotions Are Made.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p96]
- Barrett captures the bedrock role of predictions in maintaining viable bodily state using the metaphor of body budgeting, tracking income and expenditure of resources like water, salt, and glucose. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p96]
- Barrett argues that the whole of our mental lives reflects the brain's busy and deeply anticipatory body-budgeting activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p98]
- Barrett proposes that every brain region central to the generation and experience of emotion is a body-budgeting region. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p98]
- Barrett describes depression as a disorder of allostasis involving inefficient energy regulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p105]
- Barrett coined the term affective realism to describe how inner bodily sensations may be taken as evidence for active threat in perception. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p128]

## Inferences

- Inference: Barrett's work bridges neuroscience, psychology, and clinical practice, providing a unifying framework for understanding how prediction errors and body-budgeting underlie both normal emotional experience and psychiatric conditions [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p105]
