# Francesco Pagnini

## Summary

Francesco Pagnini is a researcher who worked with Langer at Harvard and later replicated the counterclockwise study in Italy. He has conducted extensive research on mindfulness interventions for chronic illness including ALS, multiple sclerosis, and Parkinson's disease, developing attention-to-variability treatments.

## Key facts

- Pagnini replicated the counterclockwise study in Italy by having people live as their younger selves, again finding improved physical function. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p104]
- Pagnini and colleagues collected correlational data on ALS patients showing slower loss of function in those with higher mindfulness scores. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p149]
- He conducted research with autistic children showing they showed more engaged behaviors when interacting with mindful adults. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p163]
