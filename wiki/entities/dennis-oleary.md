# Dennis O'Leary

## Summary

Dennis O'Leary is a researcher at the University of California, San Diego, whose studies on cortical development provided dramatic evidence of nonspecificity and selection processes in brain development. His work on fetal cortex transplantation demonstrated that cortical connections do not follow the origin of transplanted tissue but connect based on target location.

## Key facts

- O'Leary's studies showed that when immature cortex is transplanted from one site to another before connections form, the connections follow the new location, not the tissue origin. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p219]
- O'Leary showed that experimentally produced changes in inputs modifying cortical maps also affect cortical output connection patterns. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p225]
- O'Leary noted that the fornix projection persists into adulthood in elephants and some humans but is normally eliminated in rats, possibly due to size differences affecting competitive milieu. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p226]
