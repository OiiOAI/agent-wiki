# Lou Simpson

## Summary

Lou Simpson is a highly intelligent and honorable investment manager who ran the equity investment portfolio for GEICO and later Berkshire Hathaway. Over 25 years, he achieved an average annual return 6.8 percentage points above the S&P 500. Charlie Munger describes him as having one of the greatest investment minds of our time and a shoo-in for the investment Hall of Fame.
