# John R. Boyd

## Summary

Colonel John R. Boyd (1927-1997) was a United States Air Force fighter pilot and military strategist who created the OODA Loop concept and developed the theory of maneuver conflict. His major work, Patterns of Conflict (1975-1986), synthesized principles from Blitzkrieg warfare, judo, Zen Buddhism, and thermodynamics to explain how smaller or less technologically advanced forces can defeat larger opponents by operating at faster decision cycles. His ideas were adopted as doctrine by the US Marine Corps and influenced military strategy from Vietnam through the Gulf Wars.

## Key facts

- Boyd was a retired US Air Force colonel who wondered about strategy starting in the mid-1970s. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p5]
- Created his major work Patterns of Conflict between about 1975 and 1986. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p19]
- Gave hundreds of briefings on Patterns of Conflict to officials in Washington and at military installations. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p19]
- His concepts were adopted as doctrine by the US Marine Corps. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p14]
- Died in 1997. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p7]
- Famous for the mantra 'People, ideas, and hardware—in that order!'. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p39]
- Worked with SAMS founder Colonel Huba Wass de Czege and lectured at the School of Advanced Military Studies. [raw/books/strategy/Certain to Win - The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to - Richards, Chet, Richards, Chester W.pdf#p57]
- Boyd was a fighter pilot who developed the Energy-Maneuverability theory for aerial combat before creating his strategic theory. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p223]
- Boyd's A Discourse on Winning and Losing spans 2500 years of military history and strategic thought. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p227]
- Boyd completed his final presentation, The Essence of Winning and Losing, on June 28, 1995, two years before his death. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p268]
- Boyd's work draws on Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems, Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle, and the Second Law of Thermodynamics to argue that one cannot determine the character of a system within itself. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p249]
