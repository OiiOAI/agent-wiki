# John R. Boyd

## Summary

Colonel John R. Boyd (1927-1997) was a United States Air Force fighter pilot and military strategist who created the OODA Loop concept and developed the theory of maneuver conflict. His major work, Patterns of Conflict (1975-1986), synthesized principles from Blitzkrieg warfare, judo, Zen Buddhism, and thermodynamics to explain how smaller or less technologically advanced forces can defeat larger opponents by operating at faster decision cycles. His ideas were adopted as doctrine by the US Marine Corps and influenced military strategy from Vietnam through the Gulf Wars.
