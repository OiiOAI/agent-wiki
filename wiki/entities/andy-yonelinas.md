# Andy Yonelinas

## Summary

Andy Yonelinas is a colleague of Charan Ranganath at UC Davis who proposed that episodic memory and familiarity are distinct memory systems. He reasoned that episodic memory provides access to tangible memories from specific places and times, while familiarity provides a sense of prior encounter without specific contextual details.

## Key facts

- Andy Yonelinas reasoned that episodic memory gives us access to tangible memories from specific places and times, leading us to feel confident we are reliving a genuine moment from the past. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p114]
- Yonelinas proposed that familiarity can be strong (like certainty of having seen something) or weak (like a hunch), but either way it doesn't provide anything specific to hold onto. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p114]
- Yonelinas and Ranganath conducted the 'beer bet' study that provided evidence for two different kinds of memory—episodic (hippocampus) and familiarity (perirhinal cortex). [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p115]
