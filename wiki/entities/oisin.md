# Oisin

## Summary

An Irish hero who descended consciously (awake) into Tir na n-Og (Land of Youth) with the daughter of its king, who had a pig's head due to a Druidic spell. He lived there many years as a king, but when he returned to Erin after 300 years (perceiving only 3), he lost his centering in the transcendent and collapsed into a blind old man when his foot touched the earth. Campbell uses Oisin to illustrate the greater dangers faced by those who have transmuted the values of timelessness.

## Key facts

- Oisin dwelled in Tir na n-Og for what he thought was three years but was actually three hundred years. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p172]
- Since his entire personality had been brought into accord with powers and forms of timelessness, all of him stood to be refuted by the impact of forms and powers of time. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p172]
- When Oisin reached down to lift a great horn, he slipped so that one foot touched the earth, and instantly the steed was gone and he lay on the ground a blind old man. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p173]
