# Olaf Blanke

## Summary

Olaf Blanke is a neuroscientist who conducted pioneering research on out-of-body experiences and full body illusions. His work demonstrated that the first-person perspective can be manipulated through virtual reality and electrical brain stimulation.

## Key facts

- Olaf Blanke led a 2007 study in Lausanne using virtual reality to generate out-of-body-like experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p153]
- Blanke first became fascinated by OBEs when a patient, stimulated in the angular gyrus, reported seeing herself 'lying in bed, from above'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p154]
- Blanke's work shows OBEs result from disruption in brain regions dealing with vestibular input and multisensory integration. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p154]
- Blanke discovered that electrical perturbation of the right temporoparietal junction causes out-of-body experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p63]
- Blanke created illusions of out-of-body experience in normal brains using synchronized but delocalized visual and touch signals. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p63]
- Dehaene uses Blanke as an example that 'even outlandish subjective phenomena can be traced back to their neural origins'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p63]
