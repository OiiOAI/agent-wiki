# Dagmar O'Connor

## Summary

Sex therapist whose work Perel cites on the limitations of requiring married sex to always express love. Author of 'How to Make Love to the Same Person for the Rest of Your Life and Still Love It.'

## Key facts

- Dagmar O'Connor argues that for married sex to be 'meaningful,' it must always be an expression of love—preferably of lifelong, abiding love—every time. She states this is an incredible burden that eliminates sex stimulated by other emotions and sensations, including playful sex, angry sex, quick 'mindless' sex, and 'naughty' sex.
