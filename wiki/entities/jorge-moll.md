# Jorge Moll

## Summary

A neuroscientist who has contributed to understanding the neural basis of planning and social cognition. His work examines how prefrontal cortex regions subserve planning functions and how moral and social factors influence decision making.

## Key facts

- Moll and Grafman proposed that plans are just one form of structured event complex represented within PFC networks, with neurons involved in storing event sequences and planning multiple future events. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p396]
- Research by Moll and colleagues demonstrates that ventromedial and frontopolar sectors of PFC are engaged by moral judgments and passive exposure to morally salient social events. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p396]
