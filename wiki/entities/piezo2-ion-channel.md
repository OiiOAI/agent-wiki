# Piezo2 Ion Channel

## Summary

Piezo proteins are transmembrane ion channels composed of approximately 2,500 amino acids with at least 26 transmembrane alpha-helices. The ion channel is a trimer formed from three identical Piezo protein subunits. Piezo2 is expressed in mechanosensory dorsal root ganglion and trigeminal neurons that mediate touch and proprioception, as well as in vagal afferents innervating smooth muscle of the lung. Piezo1 is found primarily in non-neural tissue such as epithelia in blood vessels, kidney, and bladder. Piezo proteins couple mechanical stimuli to channel gating through a propeller-like N-terminal structure.
