# Marla Sokolowski

## Summary

Marla Sokolowski is a Canadian geneticist who identified the first gene associated with behavioral variation among normal individuals. Her work on Drosophila foraging behavior revealed that the forager (for) gene, encoding a cGMP-dependent protein kinase, controls the rover/sitter behavioral polymorphism. This gene is also expressed differently in honeybees at different life stages, showing how the same gene can regulate behavior in different ways across species.

## Key facts

- Sokolowski identified the forager (for) gene as the major gene determining the difference between rover and sitter Drosophila larvae. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p89]
- The for gene encodes a protein kinase activated by cGMP (cyclic guanosine 3',5'-monophosphate). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p89]
- In honeybees, the for gene is expressed at high levels in foraging bees and low levels in stationary nurse bees. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p91]
