# Robert Stickgold

## Summary

Robert Stickgold (also referred to as Bob Stickgold) is a co-author of 'When Brains Dream' along with Antonio Zadra. He is a neuroscientist specializing in sleep and dream research, and runs a sleep research laboratory.

## Key facts

- Robert Stickgold is one of the two authors of the book 'When Brains Dream'. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p1]
- Robert Stickgold holds the copyright to the book published in 2021. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p6]
- Alan Alda mentions having slept in Robert Stickgold's laboratory while participating in dream research. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p3]
- Robert Stickgold conducted research showing that learning the computer game Tetris resulted in participants dreaming about it at sleep onset. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p102]
- With Margaret O'Connor, Stickgold studied Tetris dreams in amnesic patients with hippocampal damage, finding that three of five patients saw Tetris images despite having no memory of playing. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p102]
- Stickgold supervised Erin Wamsley's maze learning research demonstrating that dreaming about a task predicts subsequent performance improvement. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p103]
- In 1999, Stickgold published research on semantic priming showing that weak associations are preferentially activated during REM sleep. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p110]
- Stickgold co-developed the NEXTUP theory of dream function with Tony Starner. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p109]
