# Jennifer Aniston neuron

## Summary

A neuron in the human hippocampus discovered by Rodrigo Quian Quiroga's research team that responds selectively to multiple photographs of Jennifer Aniston and to no other images, including eighty photographs of other celebrities. This was one of the first demonstrations of a neuron encoding an abstract concept rather than specific visual features.

## Key facts

- The Jennifer Aniston neuron responded to seven different photographs of the actress and to no other pictures displayed—including eighty photographs of celebrities like Kobe Bryant, Julia Roberts, Oprah Winfrey, and Pamela Anderson, as well as photos of ordinary people, places, and animals. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p132]
- The photographs of Aniston were chosen because she was very familiar to the patient, and well-known things are represented by more neurons since they have more memories and associations related to them. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p132]

## Inferences

- Inference: The discovery of the Jennifer Aniston neuron provided the first neural evidence for abstract concept encoding in the hippocampus, suggesting concepts are the unit of memory storage rather than specific visual details.
