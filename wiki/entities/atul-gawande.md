# Atul Gawande

## Summary

Atul Gawande is a surgeon and author who has been at the forefront of developing ways to reduce medical errors. He pioneered the use of surgical checklists to ensure surgical teams follow standard procedures and do not mindlessly overlook small details that could be costly to patient health.

## Key facts

- Gawande pioneered surgical checklists to ensure surgical teams follow standard procedures and do not mindlessly overlook small details that could be costly to patient health. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p182]
- Gawande collected data on approximately 1,000 procedures in 8 hospitals and found that the use of checklists reduced errors by 50 percent. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p182]
- Atul Gawande is the author of the books 'Better' and 'Complications'. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p145]
- He is a MacArthur Fellow. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p145]

- He is a staff writer for The New Yorker. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p145]
