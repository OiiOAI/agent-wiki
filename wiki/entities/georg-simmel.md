# Georg Simmel

## Summary

Georg Simmel (1858-1918) was a German sociologist and philosopher known for his work on micro-level social phenomena. Unlike Marx and Weber who focused on large-scale issues, Simmel was best known for his analysis of individual action and interaction. He developed concepts such as forms of interaction and types of interactants. His work significantly influenced American sociology through the Chicago school and symbolic interactionism.
