# S.N. Haber

## Summary

Suzanne N. Haber is a neuroscientist at the University of Rochester School of Medicine who authored Chapter 1 on the anatomy and connectivity of the reward circuit in the book Decision Neuroscience: An Integrative Perspective.

## Key facts

- S.N. Haber is affiliated with the University of Rochester School of Medicine, Rochester, NY, United States. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p10]
- Haber's chapter describes the anatomy and connectivity of the reward circuit in nonhuman primates, including how cortical-basal ganglia loops are topographically organized. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p5]
- Haber has conducted extensive research on the striatonigrostriatal pathway showing it forms an ascending spiral from the shell to the dorsolateral striatum. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p13]
- Her research describes convergence zones within the striatum where inputs from different prefrontal regions interface with associative cortical connections. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p8]
