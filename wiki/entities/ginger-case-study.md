# Ginger

## Summary

Ginger was a woman diagnosed with schizophrenia in 1984 who spent most of her days in bed, terrified by hallucinations. After her husband gave her nutrients beginning in late December 1996, she improved substantially over two months and was able to significantly reduce her medications. By four months she was symptom-free. When she ran out of nutrients during a visit to family, she began to decline, and when restarted on nutrients she responded well again.

## Key facts

- Ginger was diagnosed with schizophrenia in 1984 and was taking several medications. For at least five years, Ginger had spent her days mostly in bed, terrified by the bugs she felt crawling on her skin and saw climbing up the walls.
