# Herbert H. Bell

## Summary

Herbert H. Bell is a researcher at the Air Force Research Laboratory Human Effectiveness Directorate who co-authored the chapter on using observer ratings to assess situation awareness in fighter pilots. He worked with Don R. Lyon on this research effort to measure SA in operational fighter squadrons and multiship air combat simulations.
