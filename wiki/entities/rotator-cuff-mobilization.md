# Rotator Cuff Mobilization

## Summary

A physical practice using a ball to mobilize the rotator cuff, involving lying faceup and applying pressure to the rotator cuff area while moving the forearm back and forth. The authors note it has a dramatic impact on shoulder rotation.

## Key facts

- The mobilization involves lying faceup with knees bent and placing a ball under where the right shoulder meets the upper arm. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1445-L1447]
- The individual extends the arm out to the side with elbow bent at 90 degrees, then alternately moves the forearm forward and back while keeping the elbow on the floor. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1448-L1451]
- The authors state the effect on shoulder rotation is stunning and suggest retaking the Shoulder Rotation Test after first performing this mobilization. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1443-L1444]
