# Sitting Inventory

## Summary

The Sitting Inventory is an assessment from Vital Sign 9 that tracks the number of hours spent sitting in a 24-hour period. The test helps individuals understand their sedentary behavior patterns and provides scoring categories from 6 hours or less to 13+ hours.

## Key facts

- The Sitting Inventory tracks time spent sitting in chairs, on stools, benches, beds, or sofas, excluding floor sitting/squatting and seated exercise. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3275-L3300]
- Scoring: 6 hours or less earns an A, 7-9 hours earns B+ to C+, 10-12 hours earns C-, and 13+ hours is classified as failing. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3325-L3340]
- Research shows women who sit more than 6 hours daily are 37% more likely and men 18% more likely to die earlier than those who sit less than 3 hours. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L3220-L3240]

## Inferences

- Inference: The assessment is designed to be retaken daily to track progress in reducing sedentary behavior.
