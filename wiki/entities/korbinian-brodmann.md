# Korbinian Brodmann

## Summary

Korbinian Brodmann (1868-1918) was a German anatomist who used cytoarchitectonic methods to classify the human cerebral cortex into 52 distinct functional areas based on distinctive nerve cell structures and arrangements of cell layers.

## Key facts

- Using cytoarchitectonic methods, Brodmann distinguished 52 anatomically and functionally distinct areas in the human cerebral cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p18]
- Several areas defined by Brodmann have been found to control specific brain functions, such as area 4 as motor cortex and areas 1, 2, and 3 constituting primary somatosensory cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p18]
- Brodmann's numbering scheme (Brodmann areas) is still widely used today and is continually updated with additional functional subdivisions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p18]
