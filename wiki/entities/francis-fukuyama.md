# Francis Fukuyama

## Summary

Francis Fukuyama is an American political scientist and economist, author of this book on trust and economic performance. He previously wrote 'The End of History and the Last Man' and is a senior fellow at the RAND Corporation. Fukuyama argues that economic life reflects, shapes, and underpins modern life, and that trust—understood as the expectation of honest, cooperative behavior based on shared norms—is the single most important cultural factor determining economic prosperity. His father, Yoshii Fukuyama, was a sociologist of religion.
