# Patrick Henry Winston

## Summary

Patrick Henry Winston (1943-2019) was an American computer scientist and professor at MIT who specialized in Artificial Intelligence. He was known for his annual How to Speak talk at MIT and developed the VSN-C communication framework and Winston's Star model for memorable presentations.

## Key facts

- Patrick Henry Winston was a Professor of Artificial Intelligence at MIT. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p33]
- He taught The Human Intelligence Enterprise, a class focused on reading original papers and discussing them. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p47]
- His annual How to Speak talk became an MIT tradition with several hundred attendees each year. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p46]
- He developed concept-learning programs and focused on story understanding as a key to human intelligence. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p43]
- He wrote a textbook on Artificial Intelligence in 1992 with full-sentence headings on almost every page. [raw/books/strategy/Make it Clear - Patrick Henry Winston.pdf#p18]
