# Liz Keogh

## Summary

Liz Keogh is a Lean and Agile consultant based in London, known for her work connecting the Cynefin Framework with software development practices. She is a core member of the behavior-driven development community and has written extensively on applying Cynefin to understand why the Agile movement has not achieved its intended impact.

## Key facts

- Liz Keogh is a Lean and Agile consultant based in London and a core member of the behavior-driven development community. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p265]
- Keogh argues that the Agile movement has not taken off as intended because organizations apply it in a token manner surrounded by traditional planning rigor. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p269]
- She maps software development across all five Cynefin domains: code is Clear/Complicated, development teams operate in Complex, deployment errors cause Chaos, and large enterprises remain Confused. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p270]
