# SATORI System

## Summary

The Systematic Air Traffic Operations Research Initiative (SATORI) system is used at NAS Air Route Traffic Control Centers (ARTCCs) to recreate error situations in a format similar to what was originally displayed to the controller. Following OE detection, investigators use SATORI to re-create the radar and radio communication information available during the error occurrence, allowing for more accurate determination of the factors involved in the incident.

## Key facts

- SATORI re-creates the error situation in a format similar to what was originally displayed to the controller. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p92]
- SATORI allows for a more accurate determination of the factors involved in an OE incident. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p92]
- SATORI is used at each of the NAS Air Route Traffic Control Centers (ARTCCs). [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p92]
