# Anna Freud

## Summary

Anna Freud (1895–1982) was Sigmund Freud's youngest daughter, who became a major figure in child psychoanalysis. Freud mentions her in the context of applications of psychoanalysis to education, noting she made this study her life's work and compensated for his neglect of the subject.
