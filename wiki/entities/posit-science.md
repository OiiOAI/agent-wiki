# Posit Science

## Summary

Posit Science is a company founded by Michael Merzenich dedicated to helping people preserve brain plasticity as they age and extend their mental lifespans. The company has 36 scientists working on five areas that decline with age: memory, visual processing, executive functions, fine motor control, and gross motor control.

## Key facts

- Posit Science was founded by Merzenich to address age-related cognitive decline. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p95]
- Adults aged 60-87 who trained on the auditory memory program for 40-50 hours functioned like people in the 40-60 year range on memory tests. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p99]
- PET scans showed program users didn't show the metabolic decline typically seen in aging brains and instead showed increased metabolic activity in right parietal lobes and other brain areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p99]
- Merzenich claims exercises can make an 80-year-old function operationally like someone 50-60 years old. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p100]
- The company is developing exercises for memory, visual processing, executive functions, fine motor control, and gross motor control. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p100]
