# James A. Russell

## Summary

James A. Russell is a psychologist who developed the affective circumplex model, a two-dimensional circular structure representing affect with valence and arousal as dimensions and distance from origin representing intensity.

## Key facts

- Russell developed the affective circumplex, a two-dimensional space with valence and arousal as dimensions and intensity as distance from the origin. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p92]
- Russell showed that affect can be described as a single point on the circumplex at any moment. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p92]
