# Mental Research Institute

## Summary

The Mental Research Institute (MRI) in Palo Alto, California, was founded by Dr. Don D. Jackson. It housed the Brief Therapy Center established in 1966 by Richard Fisch. The authors were research associates at MRI with several years of experience in human communication research and interactional (couple and family) psychotherapy developed by the Palo Alto Group under Gregory Bateson's theoretical and Don D. Jackson's clinical leadership.
