# Patient PN

## Summary

Patient PN suffered a cerebral hemorrhage in his forties that damaged his left frontal lobe, combined with recent personal setbacks including divorce, job difficulties, and bankruptcy. He developed amnesia for the nineteen years prior to his hemorrhage. PET scans showed decreased right frontal lobe activity when viewing photographs from the amnesic period, but increased precuneus activity suggesting early retrieval processes beginning before the frontal control system shut down.

## Key facts

- PN showed less activity in the right frontal lobe when looking at photographs taken during the nineteen-year amnesic gap compared to photographs from other periods. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p117]
- PN showed increased activity in the precuneus when looking at photographs from the amnesic gap, possibly indicating earliest stages of the retrieval process beginning to develop. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p117]
- The gap contained key adverse events in PN's life, suggesting negative emotion from beginning retrieval may have led to shutdown of the frontal control system. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p118]
