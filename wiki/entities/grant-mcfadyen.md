# Grant McFadyen

## Summary

A self-made Irish man who ran away from an uneducated farm background as a youth, became a brilliant and charming businessman in Omaha, and served as a role model for Charlie Munger's approach to life and business through his treatment of employees, customers, and problems.

## Key facts

- Grant McFadyen was Omaha's Pioneer Ford dealer, a self-made Irishman who ran away from an uneducated farm as a youth because his father beat him. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p273]
- Grant McFadyen was a brilliant man of enormous charm and integrity who treated his employees right, his customers right, and his problems right. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p273]
