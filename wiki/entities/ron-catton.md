# Ron Catton

## Summary

Ronald Catton was a Marine Corps pilot who fought Boyd to a dead heat in simulated aerial combat and became the first Marine to attend the FWS. He achieved the unprecedented feat of earning perfect scores in every academic course at the Fighter Weapons School despite arriving under a cloud of disciplinary problems.

## Key facts

- Catton drove a red Corvette from Cannon AFB to Nellis with goals of defeating Forty-Second Boyd and becoming an FWS instructor. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p109]
- Catton was arrested for drunk driving on his second or third day at Nellis and faced court-martial before Colonel Newman relented. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p110-111]
- Boyd told Catton that no one had ever gone through the FWS with a perfect academic record and challenged him to achieve this. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p111]
- Catton studied from 2:30 AM daily, achieved perfect scores in air combat maneuvering, air-to-ground, bombs and fuses, gun sight, and missiles and rockets courses. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p112-113]
- Catton scored 100 on the nuclear weapons course despite the instructor's question having an ambiguous aircraft specification, and he became the first fighter pilot to ace every academic course at the FWS. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p114-115]
- Catton graduated with a trophy for having the best academic record in his class and was subsequently called back as an FWS instructor. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p115]
- Catton later flew F-4s with the 555th Fighter Squadron (Triple Nickel) and taught Boyd's maneuvers to other pilots. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p98]
