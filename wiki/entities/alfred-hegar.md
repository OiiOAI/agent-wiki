# Alfred Hegar

## Summary

Alfred Hegar (1830–1914) was a German Professor of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at the University of Freiburg and a pioneer of surgical gynaecology. Freud reviewed his socio-medical study of the sexual drive, criticizing it as tendentious in its attempt to minimize the importance of sexuality.

## Key facts

- Hegar was Professor of Obstetrics and Gynaecology at the University of Freiburg and a pioneer of surgical gynaecology. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p141]
- His book Der Geschlechtstrieb was designed to rebut August Bebel's feminist treatise Die Frau und der Sozialismus. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p141]
- Freud criticized Hegar for portraying sexuality as 'relatively harmless' despite evidence of its powerful influence, noting Hegar failed to address the problem of separating conception from coitus. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p141]

## Inferences
