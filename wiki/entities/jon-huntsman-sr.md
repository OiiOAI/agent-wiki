# Jon Huntsman Sr.

## Summary

Founder of Huntsman Chemical and author of Winners Never Cheat. He is presented as an example of integrity in keeping commitments. Most famously, he honored a handshake deal to sell 40% of a company division for $54 million despite the value increasing to $250 million during the six months it took to finalize paperwork. His response to the buyer's offer to split the difference was 'You negotiate for your company, Emerson, and let me negotiate for mine.' He lived by the principle that 'My word was my bond.'
