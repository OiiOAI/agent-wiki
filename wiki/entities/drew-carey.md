# Drew Carey

## Summary

Comedian and actor Drew Carey struggled with information overload despite his successful career. After reading David Allen's book 'Getting Things Done,' he hired Allen personally for a year to organize his office and teach him the GTD system. Carey describes reaching 'zero' (empty inbox) as feeling 'euphoric' like coming out of meditating in the desert.

## Key facts

- Carey had piles of unpaid bills, unanswered letters, and unfinished tasks cluttering his office. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p74]
- He hired David Allen for a full year at significant cost to personally teach him GTD. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p75]
- When he reached 'zero' (empty inbox), he felt 'euphoric' like coming out of meditating in the desert. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p82]
- With his desk cleared, he can read books and do yoga without feeling guilty and focus on creative work. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p83]
