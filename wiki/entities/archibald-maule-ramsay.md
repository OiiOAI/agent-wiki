# Archibald Maule Ramsay

## Summary

Archibald Maule Ramsay (1894-1955) was a British Conservative Member of Parliament for Midlothian and Peeblesshire from 1931 to 1945. A decorated WWI veteran, he was detained without charge under Regulation 18B from May 1940 to September 1944 for his anti-communist activities and alleged fascist sympathies. He founded the Right Club in 1939 and authored The Nameless War during his imprisonment.

- Ramsay served as a British Army officer during World War I, being severely wounded in 1916. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p474]
- He was elected MP for Midlothian and Peeblesshire in 1931 and served until 1945. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p474]
- He founded the Right Club in May 1939 as an organization opposing communism and what he termed Jewish influence. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p452]
- He was arrested on 23 May 1940 and imprisoned in Brixton Prison under Regulation 18B. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p434]
- He was released on 26 September 1944 after nearly four years of detention without trial. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p434]
