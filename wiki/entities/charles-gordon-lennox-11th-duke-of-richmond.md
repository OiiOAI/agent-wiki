# Charles Gordon-Lennox

## Summary

Charles Gordon-Lennox, the 11th Duke of Richmond, transformed the struggling Goodwood Estate by launching the Goodwood Festival of Speed in 1993 and Goodwood Revival in 1998. His curiosity-driven approach to estate management demonstrates how seeking new opportunities and engaging with family history can revive struggling enterprises.

## Key facts

- Charles Gordon-Lennox, 11th Duke of Richmond, took over the 12,000-acre Goodwood Estate in the early 1990s when farming and horse racing income were insufficient. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p135]
- The first Goodwood Festival of Speed in 1993 attracted 25,000 people instead of the expected 3,000. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p135]
- Goodwood Revival launched in 1998 after restoring the 1950s racetrack to host historic vehicle events with period-appropriate dress. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p136]
- The Duke states his biggest curiosity is how to take the Goodwood brand internationally. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p136]
