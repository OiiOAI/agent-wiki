# Joshua Cohen

## Summary

Joshua Cohen was a gifted but learning-disabled student at the Ontario Institute for Studies in Education (OISE) who collaborated with Barbara Arrowsmith Young. He ran a small clinic for learning-disabled kids and was her first husband, co-founding the Arrowsmith School with her in 1980.
