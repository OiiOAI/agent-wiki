# Santa Fe Community College

## Summary

A community college in Gainesville, Florida that underwent major expansion in the early 1970s. Alan Robertson became its president, and he recruited Singer to both tutor him and teach courses there. The teaching position became another unexpected doorway in Singer's surrender journey.

## Key facts

- Santa Fe Community College was expanded in the early 1970s with Alan Robertson appointed as its new president. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p58]
- Singer taught entry-level social science courses there on a half-time basis starting in September. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p65]
- Singer taught universal thought exploring the possibility that one underlying truth exists, weaving together physics, biology, psychology, and religion. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p74]
- Class enrollment grew from twenty students to forty by semester's end, with overflow sitting in hallways. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p74]
