# Edmund Rolls

## Summary

Edmund Rolls is a behavioural neuroscientist quoted by Solms to illustrate why different needs cannot be reduced to a single common denominator. Rolls pointed out that if food reward were always much stronger than other rewards, animals would not survive because they would never drink water—each need must be satisfied in its own right.
