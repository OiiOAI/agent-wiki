# Adaptive Structuration Theory

## Summary

Adaptive Structuration Theory (AST) is a concept coined by Gerardine DeSanctis and Marshall Scott Poole in their Organization Science article. The theory emphasizes that the impact of technology is not predetermined, as it depends on how groups and organizations perceive and use it.
