# Hal Hershfield

## Summary

Hal Hershfield is a marketing professor whose research focuses on consumer behavior, intertemporal choice, and the psychology of future self-continuity. His work demonstrates that people who feel more connected to their future selves are more likely to save for retirement and make better long-term decisions. Studies using age-progression technology (showing people images of their aged selves) show increased retirement savings contributions, providing practical applications for reducing temporal discounting.

## Key facts

- Hal Hershfield's research shows that increased psychological connectedness to one's future self reduces temporal discounting. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L2180-L2195]
