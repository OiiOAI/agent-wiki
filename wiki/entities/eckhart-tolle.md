# Eckhart Tolle

## Summary

Eckhart Tolle (born 1948) is a German-born spiritual teacher and author of 'The Power of Now'. He experienced a profound spiritual awakening around his thirtieth year after a period of severe psychological distress. Following his awakening, he spent approximately two years in a state of intense joy, sitting on park benches in Europe. He later became a spiritual teacher, giving seminars and meditation classes in Europe and North America. His teaching synthesizes elements from various spiritual traditions including Christianity, Buddhism, Zen, and Sufism, with the core message being the transcendence of egoic mind-identification through presence in the Now.
