# Anne Mangen

## Summary

Anne Mangen is a professor of literacy at the University of Stavanger in Norway who has spent two decades researching how reading on screens differs from reading on paper. Her work demonstrates that screen reading creates a different—and less effective—mode of processing text.

## Key facts

- Mangen's research shows that reading from screens trains people to 'scan and skim,' and this behavior 'bleeds over' to affect how they read printed books. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p88]
- She conducted studies where one group received information in printed books and another on screens; people understood and remembered less from screens. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
- 54 studies confirm 'screen inferiority,' showing people consistently perform worse comprehending and retaining screen-based text. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
- Screen inferiority in elementary school children equals two-thirds of a year's growth in reading comprehension. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
- She worries we are losing 'our ability to read long texts anymore' and 'cognitive patience' for 'cognitively challenging texts.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p89]
