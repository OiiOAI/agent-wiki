# Judge Norton

## Summary

Chief U.S. District Judge of South Carolina who took over the federal criminal case against Medical Manager executives after Judge Blatt stepped down. He dismissed the case on May 27, 2010, and approved a motion for new trial on January 19, 2011, issuing a nineteen-page opinion criticizing the government's case as lacking evidentiary support.

## Key facts

- Judge Norton is Chief U.S. District Judge of South Carolina and took over the case when Judge Blatt stepped down in March 2009. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p210]
- Judge Norton dismissed the entire case against John Kang and John Sessions on May 27, 2010, almost three months after trial. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p213]
- Judge Norton issued a nineteen-page opinion stating the government had not proven any conspiracy among executives and found the government's chief witnesses, Bobby Davids and Caroline, to be noncredible. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p214]
- Judge Norton approved the defendants' motion for a new trial on January 19, 2011, ruling the jury verdict was not supported by the weight of the evidence. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p214]
- Judge Norton questioned why the government kept people under indictment for five years and then dropped charges before trial, noting defense costs exceeded $190 million. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p213]
