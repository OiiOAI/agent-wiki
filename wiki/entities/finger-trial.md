# FINGER Trial

## Summary

The FINGER trial (Finnish Geriatric Intervention Study to Prevent Cognitive Impairment and Disability) is the world's first large-scale, long-term randomized control trial measuring the impact of dietary and lifestyle choices on cognitive health, involving over 1,200 at-risk older adults.

## Key facts

- The FINGER trial involves 1,200+ at-risk older adults in a randomized control trial. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p28]
- Participants in the intervention group received nutritional counseling, exercise programs, and social support to reduce psychosocial risk factors. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p28]
- After two years, overall cognitive function improved 25%, executive function improved 83%, and processing speed improved 150% in the intervention group. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p28]

## Inferences

- Inference: The FINGER trial provides the best evidence to date that cognitive decline does not have to be an inevitable part of aging.
