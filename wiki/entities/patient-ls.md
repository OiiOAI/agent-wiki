# Patient LS

## Summary

Patient LS was a 41-year-old Italian man who in July 1988 sustained damage to parts of the frontal and temporal lobes in the left cerebral hemisphere from a riding accident. He developed virtual inability to produce proper names while having no difficulty with common names. Testing revealed he could name all 50 common objects shown but only 2 of 25 famous people. He could recognize faces as familiar, identify people by conceptual knowledge, match names and faces, and repeat names, but could not generate them himself. His case was one of the first described with problems restricted to retrieving proper names, now called 'proper-name anomia.'

## Key facts

- Patient LS could name all 50 common objects but only 2 of 25 famous people despite recognizing all faces as familiar. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p100]
- LS could retrieve conceptual knowledge about people and places he could not name, such as correctly identifying a face as that of the prime minister. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p101]
