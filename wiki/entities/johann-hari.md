# Johann Hari

## Summary

Johann Hari is a British writer and author of 'Stolen Focus: Why You Can't Pay Attention—and How to Think Deeply Again.' He has written two New York Times bestselling books translated into thirty-seven languages, praised by figures from Oprah Winfrey to Noam Chomsky.
