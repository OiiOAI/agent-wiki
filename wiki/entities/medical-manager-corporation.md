# Medical Manager Corporation

## Summary

A medical practice management software company founded by Michael Singer that grew to over 2,300 employees and $300 million in annual revenue. The company went public in 1997, merged with Synetic in 1999 for $1.3 billion, and merged with Healtheon/WebMD in 2000 for $3.5 billion. The company was raided by the FBI in 2003 and executives were indicted, though all charges were ultimately dismissed.

## Key facts

- Medical Manager Corporation grew to almost 2,300 employees generating more than $300 million a year in revenue at its peak. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p187]
- Medical Manager was the most widely installed practice management vendor in the country. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p187]
- Medical Manager merged with Synetic in May 1999 for $1.3 billion, with the combined company keeping the Medical Manager Corporation name. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p177]
- Medical Manager merged with Healtheon/WebMD on February 14, 2000 for $3.5 billion. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p179]
- Medical Manager was installed into the archives of the Smithsonian Institution in 2000 as part of the documentation of the Information Technology Revolution. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p185]
