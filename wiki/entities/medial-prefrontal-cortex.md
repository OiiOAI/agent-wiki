# Medial Prefrontal Cortex (MPFC)

## Summary

The medial prefrontal cortex (MPFC) is associated with control of social behavior and theory of mind. It encompasses several cytoarchitectonically distinct areas including areas 9, 10, 11, 14, 24, and 32. Different subregions support different computations: dorsal ACC monitors outcomes of others' decisions, ventromedial regions process valuation, and area 10 is involved in mentalizing. Its connectivity patterns with temporal cortex and posterior cingulate cortex are similar between humans and macaques.

## Key facts

- The MPFC is associated with the control of social behavior, and patients with damage to this region are described as having 'acquired sociopathy'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p191]
- The MPFC has become synonymous with 'theory of mind', which is the act of attributing thoughts and feelings to others. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p191]
- The pMFC is activated when participants' ratings conflicted with group ratings during social conformity tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p204]
- Macaques with anterior cingulate gyrus lesions (areas 24a,b and 32) lose interest in social stimuli. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p192]
