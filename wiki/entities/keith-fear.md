# Keith Fear

## Summary

Keith Fear is a hot air balloon business owner who operates in St. Louis, Missouri, Albuquerque and Taos, New Mexico, and Cottonwood, Arizona near Sedona. He represents an entrepreneur who initially dismissed Profit First for two years before implementing it and achieving dramatic profit improvements, with net profit increasing over 1,700% in early 2015.

## Key facts

- Keith's hot air balloon business surpassed $1 million in revenue but he still needed a separate full-time job to make ends meet. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p20]
- He initially read Profit First and did nothing for two years because he couldn't imagine it would work. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p20-21]
- After implementing Profit First, his net profit year-over-year from 2014 to 2015 was up 1,721 percent in early 2015. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p21]
- He ended 2015 with net profit up 335.3 percent overall and approximately 22 percent net profit percentage. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p21]
