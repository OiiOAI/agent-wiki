# J.-C. Dreher

## Summary

J.-C. Dreher (Jean-Claude Dreher) is affiliated with the Institute of Cognitive Science (CNRS), Lyon, France. He is the co-author of Chapter 17 on Social Dominance Representations in the Human Brain and has contributed extensively to understanding the neural basis of social decision-making, reward processing, and hormonal influences on social behavior.
