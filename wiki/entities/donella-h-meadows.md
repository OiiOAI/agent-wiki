# Donella H. Meadows

## Summary

Donella H. Meadows (1941–2001) was an American environmental scientist, writer, and systems thinker. She was a lead author of The Limits to Growth (1972) and its updates. A Pew Scholar in Conservation and the Environment, she taught at Dartmouth College and worked with the MIT System Dynamics group. She wrote Thinking in Systems to bring systems thinking concepts to a wider audience.
