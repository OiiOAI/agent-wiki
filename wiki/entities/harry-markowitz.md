# Harry Markowitz

## Summary

Harry Markowitz was an American economist who won the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences (1990) for his work on portfolio theory. He proposed that utility should be linked to changes in wealth rather than absolute wealth states—an insight that Kahneman and Tversky recognized as the correct foundation for Prospect Theory, even though Markowitz's own proposal had been ignored for a quarter century.

## Key facts

- Markowitz proposed linking utility to changes in wealth rather than states of wealth, an idea that Kahneman and Tversky immediately recognized as the right approach and built into Prospect Theory, even though Markowitz's proposal had existed for 25 years without attracting attention.
