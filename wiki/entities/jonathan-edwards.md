# Jonathan Edwards

## Summary

Puritan minister of New England who thundered vivid sermons threatening divine wrath, using imagery of arrows, floods, and flames to describe God's judgment. His sermons revealed the ogre aspect of the father archetype through imagery of damnation.

## Key facts

- Edwards depicted God's wrath as great Waters dammed that increase higher and higher until an Outlet is given. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p103]
- Edwards compared the sinner to a spider held over fire, with God's wrath burning like fire and the sinner as venomous as a serpent in God's eyes. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p104]
- Edwards taught that only the 'mighty power of the Spirit of God' upon souls produces the great Change of Heart and new creature status. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p105]
