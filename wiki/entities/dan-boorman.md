# Dan Boorman

## Summary

A veteran pilot who spent two decades developing checklists and flight deck controls for Boeing aircraft, serving as technical leader behind the 787 Dreamliner's flight deck design and as keeper of Boeing's flight philosophy.

## Key facts

- Dan Boorman is a veteran pilot who spent the last two decades developing checklists and flight deck controls for Boeing aircraft from the 747-400 forward. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p86]
- Boorman was a technical leader behind the flight deck design for the Boeing 787 Dreamliner, including its pilot controls, displays, and system of checklists. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p86]
- Boeing issues over one hundred checklists per year, either new or revised, through Boorman's flight operations group. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p88]
- Boorman helped translate the British Airways Flight 38 crash findings into a revised polar flight checklist within about two weeks, which was then distributed to every Boeing 777 owner worldwide. [raw/books/strategy/The checklist manifesto _ how to get things right - Atul Gawande.pdf#p98]
