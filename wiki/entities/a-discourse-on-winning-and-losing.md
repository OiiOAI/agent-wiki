# A Discourse on Winning and Losing

## Summary

A Discourse on Winning and Losing is John Boyd's comprehensive strategic theory, consisting of four briefings (Patterns of Conflict, Organic Design for Command and Control, The Strategic Game of ? and ?, and Revelation) plus the essay Destruction and Creation. Also known as 'The Green Book,' it was completed in 1987 with revisions continuing until 1995. The work synthesizes Boyd's insights from military history, science, and philosophy into a general theory of organizational survival in conflict.

## Key facts

- A Discourse consists of four briefings and an essay, first completed in 1987, also labeled 'The Green Book'. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p12]
- The essay Destruction and Creation (written 1976) forms the philosophical foundation for Boyd's proposition that uncertainty pervades everything. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p12]
- Patterns of Conflict is the historical heart of the work and longest presentation, containing 193 slides analyzing warfare and theories for victory. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p12]
- Organic Design for Command and Control 'surfaces the implicit arrangements that permit cooperation in complex, competitive, fast moving situations'. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p13]
- The Strategic Game of ? and ? emphasizes 'the mental twists and turns we undertake to surface appropriate schemes or designs for realizing our aims or purposes'. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p13]
- Boyd gave portions of his briefings about 1500 times, with sessions sometimes lasting 14-18 hours. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p19]
