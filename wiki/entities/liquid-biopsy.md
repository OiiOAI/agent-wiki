# Liquid Biopsy

## Summary

Liquid biopsy refers to blood tests that detect cancer by identifying cell-free tumor DNA circulating in the bloodstream. These tests are used both for detecting cancer recurrence in treated patients and for screening healthy individuals. Key technologies include tests developed by Max Diehn's team at Stanford and Grail's Galleri test, which analyzes methylation patterns to detect cancer and predict its tissue of origin.

## Key facts

- Liquid biopsies detect cancer by identifying cell-free tumor DNA shed into the bloodstream by growing cancer cells. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p179]
- In early-stage cancers, cell-free DNA from cancer may represent only 0.01 to 0.001 percent (one part in ten thousand to one hundred thousand) of total cell-free DNA. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p179]
- Max Diehn, an oncology professor at Stanford, pioneered liquid biopsy research starting in 2012 for detecting lung cancer recurrence after surgery. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p178]
- Grail's Galleri test analyzes methylation patterns of cell-free DNA and can determine both whether cancer is present and where in the body it likely originated. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p180]
- The Galleri test demonstrated specificity of approximately 99.5% in the CCGA validation study, meaning only 0.5% false positives. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p180]
- Detection rates vary by tumor subtype: stage I/II hormone receptor-negative breast cancer detection rate is about 75%, compared to 25% for hormone receptor-positive. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p181]
- Liquid biopsies have higher resolution than radiographic tests like MRI, detecting tumors even when they remain invisible to imaging. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p180]
- Cancers that shed more cell-free DNA tend to be more aggressive and deadly—precisely the cancers most important to detect early. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p181]

## Inferences

- Inference: The two functions of liquid biopsies are: (1) determining cancer's presence or absence, and (2) gaining insight into specific cancer biology and danger level.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The sensitivity of liquid biopsies like Galleri can be low depending on cancer stage, meaning a negative result does not necessarily rule out cancer.
