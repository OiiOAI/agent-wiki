# KNM-ER 1470

## Summary

KNM-ER 1470 is a fossil specimen formerly assigned to Homo habilis, though recent proposals suggest placing this individual in a separate species designated Homo rudolfensis due to its many other australopithecine-like features. It had a brain size of approximately 750 cc.
