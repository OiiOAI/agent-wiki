# Alfred Chandler

## Summary

American business historian known for his work on 'Scale and Scope: The Dynamics of Industrial Capitalism' (1990), who linked British economic decline to the family-oriented nature of British business enterprises.

## Key facts

- Alfred Chandler linked the British failure to exploit entrepreneurial opportunities in key industries of the second industrial revolution to the family-oriented nature of British business. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p436]
- His major work 'Scale and Scope: The Dynamics of Industrial Capitalism' was published by Belknap Press of Harvard University Press in 1990. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p436]

## Inferences

- Inference: Chandler's analysis suggests that trust relationships embedded in family structures may have constrained British firms from developing the professional management hierarchies seen in American and German competitors.
