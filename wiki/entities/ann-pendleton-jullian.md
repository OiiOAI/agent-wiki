# Ann Pendleton-Jullian

## Summary

Ann M. Pendleton-Jullian is an architect, writer, and educator. She is a professor and former Director at the Knowlton School of Architecture at Ohio State University, a Distinguished Visiting Professor at the Pardee RAND Graduate School of Public Policy, and was a Fellow at Stanford's Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences in 2018.
