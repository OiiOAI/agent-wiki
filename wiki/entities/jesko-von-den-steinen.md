# Jesko von den Steinen

## Summary

Jesko von den Steinen is a former principal solo artist with Cirque du Soleil and co-creator with Franco Dragone of 'The House of Dancing Water,' the world's largest water-based theatrical experience at the City of Dreams in Macau. He now helps organizations confront digital, strategic, and cultural change as lead facilitator and designer for 'The Difference,' a complex problem solution hub for experience consulting with PwC in Hong Kong.
