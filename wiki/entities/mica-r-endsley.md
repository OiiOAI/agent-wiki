# Mica R. Endsley

## Summary

Dr. Mica R. Endsley is the president of SA Technologies in Marietta, Georgia, and is a recognized world leader in the design, development, and evaluation of systems to support situation awareness and decision-making. She has worked on next-generation systems for aviation, air traffic control, medical, power, and military operations. Prior to forming SA Technologies, she was a visiting associate professor at MIT in the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics and an associate professor of industrial engineering at Texas Tech University. She received a PhD in industrial and systems engineering from the University of Southern California.
