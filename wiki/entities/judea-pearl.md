# Judea Pearl

## Summary

Judea Pearl is a computer scientist and philosopher, professor at UCLA, and one of the primary architects of the Causal Revolution. He wrote the technical book Causality (2000) and co-authored The Book of Why (2018) with Dana Mackenzie. His work on causal inference, Bayesian networks, and the do-operator has transformed how science approaches cause-and-effect questions.
