# Jason Fried

## Summary

Jason Fried is the co-founder and president of 37signals, a profitable software company known for creating Basecamp, Highrise, and other web-based business tools. Along with David Hansson, he co-authored the book Rework, which presents contrarian views on business practices. Fried advocates for small, focused companies and has been critical of traditional business approaches like meetings, long-term planning, and outside funding.
