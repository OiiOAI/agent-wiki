# Karl Marx

## Summary

Karl Marx (1818-1883) was a German philosopher, economist, and revolutionary socialist whose work laid the foundation for modern socialism and communism. Although not a sociologist and did not consider himself one, his work contains a sociological theory. His major works include The Communist Manifesto (with Engels), The German Ideology, and Capital. Marx developed dialectical materialism and a theory of alienation under capitalism.
