# Michael Polanyi

## Summary

Michael Polanyi was a British-Hungarian philosopher who made an observation in his 1966 book 'The Tacit Dimension' that became known as Polanyi's Paradox: 'We know more than we can say.' This concept describes tacit knowledge—tasks humans can perform easily but cannot fully explain—and has implications for understanding human creativity and the limits of artificial intelligence.

## Key facts

- Michael Polanyi made an observation in his 1966 book that became known as Polanyi's Paradox, summarized as 'We know more than we can say'. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p202]
- Polanyi's observation applies to many tasks we easily perform but cannot fully explain, such as driving a car or recognizing a face. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p202]
