# Helen Mayberg

## Summary

Helen S. Mayberg is a neurologist who developed groundbreaking work on depression treatment through electrical brain stimulation. Her technique instantly relieves the agony of depression (while the current is on) as the patient's brain shifts from all-consuming internal focus to the external world, allowing it to predict and process prediction error normally. Her work supports the view that depression is a brain disease and not just a shortage of happy thoughts.
