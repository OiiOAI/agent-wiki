# Ramachandran

## Summary

Vilayanur Ramachandran is a neuroscientist whose experiments on split-brain patients and denial phenomena are cited by McGilchrist. His work demonstrates that the left hemisphere is a 'conformist' indifferent to discrepancies, while the right is highly sensitive to perturbation. He found that patients with left-hemisphere damage almost never experience denial of paralysis, unlike those with right-hemisphere damage.

## Key facts

- Ramachandran found that patients with denial of paralysis almost never have left-hemisphere damage, demonstrating left-hemisphere willingness to accept absurd ideas and deny reality. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p330]
- Ramachandran found 'The left hemisphere is a conformist, largely indifferent to discrepancies, whereas the right hemisphere is the opposite: highly sensitive to perturbation'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p330]
- Ramachandran's experiments 'flatly contradict the theory that the brain consists of a number of autonomous modules acting as a bucket brigade'. [raw/books/neuroscience/THE MASTER AND HIS EMISSARY - Iain McGilchrist.pdf#p317]
