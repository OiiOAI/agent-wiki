# Walter Nernst

## Summary

Walther Nernst (1864-1941) was a German physical chemist who won the 1920 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. His work on the theory of solution led to the Nernst equation, which calculates the equilibrium potential for an ion species across a membrane based on its concentration gradient.

## Key facts

- Nernst derived the equation Ex = (RT/zF) ln([X]o/[X]i) for calculating equilibrium potentials from basic thermodynamic principles in 1888. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p241]
