# Otto Rank

## Summary

Otto Rank (1884-1939) was an Austrian psychoanalyst and close associate of Freud who contributed significant work on dream theory. His publications cited in this chunk include works on birth dreams, symbolism in arousal dreams, the stratification of symbols, and disguised Oedipus dreams. Rank showed that birth dreams use the same symbolism as dreams with urinary stimulus, and demonstrated connections between urethral erotism and sexual symbolism.

## Key facts

- Otto Rank showed from a series of dreams that birth dreams make use of the same symbolism as dreams with a urinary stimulus. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p360]
- Rank's paper on the stratification of symbols in arousal dreams discusses how erotic stimulus is represented as urinary stimulus which has made a first attempt at regressive satisfaction in infantile urethral erotism. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p361]
- Rank published an example of a disguised Oedipus dream and detailed analyses of eye symbolism in dreams. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p357]
- Rank recorded and analyzed a girl's dream involving wordplay on 'Ähren' (ears of corn) and 'Ehren' (honour) to represent 'Kuss in Ehren' (honourable kiss). [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p364]
