# Abu Sayyaf

## Summary

Abu Sayyaf is an Islamic militant group based in the southern Philippines, responsible for numerous kidnappings including Jeffrey Schilling and the Dos Palmas incident. Led by Abu Sabaya, the group was linked to Al-Qaeda after 9/11.

## Key facts

- Abu Sayyaf kidnapped Jeffrey Schilling, a 24-year-old American, demanding $10 million ransom. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p88]
- Abu Sabaya was the rebel leader who personally negotiated for Schilling's ransom, known for violent past including rape, murder, and beheadings. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p89]
- Abu Sabaya calculated the $10 million ransom based on $5 million US bounty for WTC bombers and $20 million paid for six European captives. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p89]
- Abu Sabaya was killed in June 2002 in a shoot-out with Philippine military units. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p93]
