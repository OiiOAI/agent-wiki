# Mariano Sigman

## Summary

Mariano Sigman is a cognitive neuroscientist at the Integrative Neuroscience Laboratory, Physics Department, University of Buenos Aires. He co-authored Chapter 1 of this volume with Stanislas Dehaene on the cognitive and neural architectures of simple decisions, examining why decision making takes time.

## Key facts

- Mariano Sigman leads the Integrative Neuroscience Laboratory at the Physics Department, University of Buenos Aires, Argentina. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p15]
- Sigman and Dehaene analyze the cognitive and neural architectures of simple decisions, presenting behavioral and neural evidence that three key puzzles (decision duration, variability, and slow processing) arise from brain architecture. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p19]
- Their work examines why it takes so much time to make a decision, why decision time is so variable, and why only one decision can be made at a time. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p28]
