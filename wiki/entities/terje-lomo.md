# Terje Lømo

## Summary

Terje Lømo was a postdoctoral student in Per Andersen's laboratory in Oslo who, together with Tim Bliss, discovered long-term potentiation in 1973. They found that brief bursts of neural activity could strengthen synaptic connections in hippocampal pathways for hours to days.

## Key facts

- In 1973, Terje Lømo and Tim Bliss, postdoctoral students in Per Andersen's laboratory in Oslo, discovered that neuronal pathways leading to the hippocampus could be strengthened by a brief burst of neural activity. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p293]
- Lømo and Bliss applied a very rapid train of electrical stimuli (100 impulses per second) and found synaptic connections were strengthened for several hours to one or more days, calling this long-term potentiation. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p293]
