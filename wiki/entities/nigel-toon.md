# Nigel Toon

## Summary

Nigel Toon is the cofounder and CEO of Graphcore, an AI computer maker that revolutionized the global chip-making industry and achieved unicorn status in 2018. He emphasizes making experts feel like they are the teacher, which encourages open information sharing. His approach to building Graphcore involved interviewing diverse scientists, engineers, and investors.
