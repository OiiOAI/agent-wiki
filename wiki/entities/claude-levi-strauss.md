# Claude Lévi-Strauss

## Summary

Claude Lévi-Strauss is considered the father of French structuralism and extended Saussure's work to anthropological issues, particularly myths and kinship systems. He reconceptualized social phenomena as systems of communication amenable to structural analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p505]

## Key facts

- Lévi-Strauss is called 'the father of structuralism.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p505]
- He applied structuralism to kinship systems, analyzing them like linguistic systems. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p505]
