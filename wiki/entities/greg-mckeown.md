# Greg McKeown

## Summary

Greg McKeown is the author of Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less. He is a public speaker who offers keynote presentations and workshops on Essentialist leadership and the disciplined pursuit of less but better. His work is informed by various management thinkers including Peter Drucker, Jim Collins, and Clayton Christensen.
