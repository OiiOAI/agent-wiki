# Kamar al-Zaman

## Summary

The Arabian Nights prince who, while sleeping in his tower, was visited by the Princess Budur, transported by Jinn. When he found her beside him, he took her seal-ring and slipped it on his own finger. Upon waking separated by the width of Asia, the talismanic ring betokened that the heart had been aware of the reality of the deep. Kamar al-Zaman had the best luck of all returning heroes—he retained his self-assurance through the convincing talisman of his unbelievable adventure.
