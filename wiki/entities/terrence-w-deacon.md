# Terrence W. Deacon

## Summary

Terrence W. Deacon is a biological anthropologist and neuroscientist who authored The Symbolic Species (1997). His work focuses on the evolution of the human brain and language, particularly examining the relationship between brain structure and symbolic cognition. Deacon received his Ph.D. from Harvard University and has conducted research on primate behavior and neuroanatomy.
