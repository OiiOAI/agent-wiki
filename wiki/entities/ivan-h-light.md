# Ivan H. Light

## Summary

Ivan H. Light was a sociologist known for his research on ethnic enterprise and entrepreneurship among Chinese, Japanese, and Black Americans in America. His seminal work 'Ethnic Enterprise in America' (1972) examined how immigrant and minority groups mobilize resources for business development.

## Key facts

- Ivan H. Light authored 'Ethnic Enterprise in America: Business and Welfare Among Chinese, Japanese, and Blacks' published by University of California Press in 1972. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p445]
- Light documented that ethnic enterprises include both proprietors and employees of ethnic businesses, not just business owners. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p445]
- Light studied rotating credit associations as a mechanism for mobilizing capital within ethnic communities. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p446]
- Light and Bonacich co-authored 'Immigrant Entrepreneurs: Koreans in Los Angeles, 1965-1982' examining Korean business formation. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p445]

## Inferences

- Inference: Light's research on ethnic enterprise suggests that social trust and networks within ethnic communities can substitute for formal financial institutions in providing startup capital
