# Johnny Carson

## Summary

Johnny Carson (1925-2005) was America's late-night king of comedy, hosting The Tonight Show from 1962 to 1992. He delivered a famous graduation speech offering prescriptions for guaranteed misery, which Charlie Munger adapted and expanded for his own Harvard School commencement address in 1986.

## Key facts

- Johnny Carson was born in Corning, Iowa and hosted NBC television's The Tonight Show for thirty years from 1962 to 1992. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p160]
- Carson told Harvard graduates he couldn't tell them how to be happy, but could tell them from personal experience how to guarantee misery. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p152]
- Carson's prescription for sure misery included: ingesting chemicals, envy, and resentment. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p152]
- Munger described Carson's speech as the only Harvard graduation speech he had heard that he wished was longer. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p151]
