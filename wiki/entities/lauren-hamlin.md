# Lauren Hamlin

## Summary

Lauren Hamlin was BJ Fogg's closest collaborator in transforming his research insights and hands-on experience into the polished prose of the book. Introduced to the project by Doug Abrams, she brought 'East Coast toughness' to Fogg's 'West Coast optimism.'

## Key facts

- Lauren Hamlin was introduced to the project by Doug Abrams and became Fogg's closest collaborator on the book. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p308]
- Hamlin brought 'East Coast toughness' to Fogg's 'West Coast optimism,' and together they created the book. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p308]
- Fogg acknowledges that Hamlin made personal sacrifices to get the ideas 'into your hands, and into your hearts and minds.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p308]
