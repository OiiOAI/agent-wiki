# August Aichhorn

## Summary

August Aichhorn (1878-1949) was an Austrian educator and psychoanalyst who worked for many years as director of municipal institutions for juvenile delinquents before studying psychoanalysis. His book 'Verwahrloste Jugend' (Wayward Youth, 1925) applied psychoanalytic principles to the understanding and education of delinquent youth. Freud praised his intuitive understanding of troubled children.

- Aichhorn worked for many years in an official capacity as a director of municipal institutions for delinquents before becoming acquainted with psychoanalysis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6171]
- Aichhorn founded the Budapest Component Society of the International Psychoanalytical Association in 1913. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6164]
