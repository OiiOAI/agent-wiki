# Judson Brewer

## Summary

Judson Brewer is a neuroscientist and meditation researcher who studies the Default Mode Network and the relationship between meditation and creativity. His work has found that effortless awareness is important for both reducing DMN activity and cultivating creativity.

## Key facts

- Judson Brewer studies the Default Mode Network extensively. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p207]
- Brewer found that the less effort expended in meditation, the more powerfully it switches off the DMN and induces flow states. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p207]
- Brewer wrote that 'effortlessness may be a key aspect of cultivating the fertile soil for creativity to grow.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p207]
