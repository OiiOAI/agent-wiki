# Peter Beck

## Summary

Peter Beck is a New Zealand engineer and the founder and CEO of Rocket Lab, a California-based aerospace manufacturer. Growing up in Invercargill, New Zealand, he developed a fascination with space as a boy watching stars with his father. As a teenager, he built rockets in his garden shed and at age 18 strapped a rocket engine to a custom bicycle, reaching about 100 mph. Instead of university, he took a toolmaking apprenticeship, later working at Industrial Research Ltd. In 2006, he founded Rocket Lab with the vision of enabling cost-effective space travel. Rocket Lab's Electron rocket costs approximately $5 million per launch compared to NASA's up to $1.6 billion, making space more accessible for small satellite deployment.
