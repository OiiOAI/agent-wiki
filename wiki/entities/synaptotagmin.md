# Synaptotagmin

## Summary

Synaptotagmin is a synaptic vesicle protein that serves as the calcium sensor for triggering synchronous neurotransmitter release. It binds multiple calcium ions cooperatively, and calcium binding triggers conformational changes that mediate rapid vesicle fusion with the plasma membrane through interactions with the SNARE complex.

## Key facts

- Synaptotagmin is the calcium sensor for transmitter release, requiring binding of approximately five Ca2+ ions to trigger vesicle fusion. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p376]
- The relationship between Ca2+ concentration and transmitter release is highly nonlinear, consistent with cooperative binding of multiple Ca2+ ions to synaptotagmin. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p376]
- Synaptotagmins are the major calcium sensors for fast, synchronous neurotransmitter release; synaptotagmin-1, -2, and -9 mediate fast fusion while synaptotagmin-7 mediates slower asynchronous release. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p394]
- The two C2 domains of synaptotagmin bind a total of five calcium ions, which is the minimal number required to trigger release of a quantum of transmitter. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p394]
- When synaptotagmin-1 is deleted in mice, flies, or worms, action potentials can no longer trigger fast synchronous release, but slower asynchronous release persists mediated by synaptotagmin-7. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p394]
- Mutations that reduce the calcium binding affinity of synaptotagmin-1 by approximately two-fold change the calcium required for transmitter release by the same amount. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p394]
