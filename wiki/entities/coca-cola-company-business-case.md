# Coca-Cola Company (Business Case)

## Summary

Munger's detailed business case analysis of how to build a $2 trillion company from a $2 million investment in 1884 Atlanta using the Coca-Cola name, applying principles of psychology (Pavlovian conditioning, operant conditioning, social proof) and basic academic concepts.

## Key facts

- The Glotz problem: Starting with $2 million in 1884 Atlanta, design a plan to create a $2 trillion company by 2034 (150 years) selling only non-alcoholic beverages, paying out dividends. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p289]
- The core strategy: Create and maintain conditioned reflexes—the Coca-Cola trade name acts as the stimulus; purchase and ingestion are the desired response. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p292]
- Operant conditioning requires maximizing rewards (food value, flavor, stimulus via sugar/caffeine, cooling effect) and preventing competing products from creating conflicting habits through ubiquity. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p293]
- Pavlovian conditioning requires associating the brand with things consumers like or admire—spending heavily on advertising to create powerful associative effects. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p294]
- Social proof means each increase in sales improves selling power—increased consumption triggers more consumption through imitation. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p295]
- Four things to avoid: aftertaste that stops consumption, losing half the trademark name, bad effects from envy, and any huge sudden change in flavor. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p297]
- The real Coca-Cola Company, despite losing half its trademark and granting poor perpetual bottling franchises, is on track to hit $2 trillion value by 2034—proving the strategy works even with execution errors. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p299]

## Inferences

- Inference: The case demonstrates Munger's method: start with basic academic concepts, identify the core mechanism (conditioned reflexes), then build strategy around psychological principles that create lollapalooza effects.
