# Marie Monfils

## Summary

Marie Monfils conducted research in LeDoux's laboratory that serendipitously discovered that inserting a short break between the first and second trial of extinction dramatically improved its effects. This finding led to the discovery that conducting extinction during the reconsolidation window opened by a single retrieval trial prevents spontaneous recovery and renewal. This procedural refinement has been replicated in both animals and humans.

## Key facts

- Monfils found that inserting a gap of 10 minutes to 4 hours between retrieval and extinction prevented spontaneous recovery and renewal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p424]
- The effective window for this enhanced extinction effect corresponded to the 4-6 hour reconsolidation window. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p424]
