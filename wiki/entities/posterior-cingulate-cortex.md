# Posterior cingulate cortex (CGp)

## Summary

A brain region strongly interconnected with areas involved in learning, motivation, and motor control. CGp neurons encode subjective value signals that correlate with risk preferences and may contribute to updating value representations for guiding future behavior.

## Key facts

- CGp neurons encode an ongoing estimate of the subjective value or salience of extra-personal events and actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p454]
- CGp neuronal activity correlates with subjective risk preferences rather than objective value. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p454]
- CGp is anatomically positioned to receive information about visual events, action, and attention, and is strongly interconnected with areas involved in learning and motivation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p452]
