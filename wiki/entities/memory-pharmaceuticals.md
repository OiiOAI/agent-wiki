# Memory Pharmaceuticals

## Summary

Memory Pharmaceuticals was founded in 1996 by Eric Kandel, Walter Gilbert, Jonathan Fleming, and Axel Unterbeck. The company focused on developing drugs to treat age-related memory loss and early Alzheimer's disease based on research showing that cyclic AMP-enhancing drugs like Rolipram could reverse memory deficits in aged mice.

## Key facts

- Memory Pharmaceuticals was founded in 1996 by Kandel, Walter Gilbert, Jonathan Fleming, and Axel Unterbeck to develop a 'little red pill' for age-related memory loss. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p335]
- The company was based on research showing that Rolipram and other drugs enhancing cyclic AMP signaling could reverse hippocampus-dependent memory deficits in older mice. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p338]
- Memory Pharmaceuticals developed four new families of drugs for age-related memory loss that are substantially better than off-the-shelf compounds; some improve memory in rats for months. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p341]
- The company went public in 2004 and is located in Montvale, New Jersey. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p341]
