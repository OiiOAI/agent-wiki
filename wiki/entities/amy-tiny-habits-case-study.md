# Amy (Tiny Habits case study)

## Summary

Amy is a freelance educational-media writer and single mother of three who used Tiny Habits to transform her life during a difficult divorce. Her key habit—writing her most important task on a Post-it after dropping off her daughter—broke her productivity logjam and eventually led to quadrupling her income and gaining custody of her children. She later created a Pearl Habit that transformed her hostile relationship with her ex-husband into a civil coparenting arrangement.

## Key facts

- Amy created a habit of writing down her most important task on a Post-it after dropping her daughter Rachel at kindergarten, using the car door shutting as her Anchor. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p120]
- Amy's habit recipe was: 'After I drop off Rachel, I will write one important thing on a Post-it and put it on my dashboard.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p120]
- Six months later, Amy was divorced, had quadrupled her income, had gained custody of her kids, and was sleeping peacefully. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p121]
- Amy created a Pearl Habit: 'After I feel insulted by my ex-husband, I will think of something nice to do for myself,' which eventually led to them cohosting their youngest daughter's graduation party. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p143]
- By using her ex-husband's negativity as a prompt for self-care, Amy found compassion for him, which helped him change his behavior over time. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p144]
