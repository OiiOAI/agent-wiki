# Neuregulin

## Summary

Neuregulin is a developmental signaling gene identified as a schizophrenia risk gene through genome-wide association studies examining statistical correlations between specific loci and schizophrenia in thousands of patients from different families. Neuregulin affects cell migration and synapse formation. Together with DISC1 studies, neuregulin research suggests that developmental defects in the brain can underlie schizophrenia.
