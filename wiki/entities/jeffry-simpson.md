# Jeffry Simpson

## Summary

Dr. Jeffry Simpson is a professor of psychology at the University of Minnesota who studies how adult attachment orientations are associated with relationship functioning and well-being, particularly when partners are distressed. He also researches empathic accuracy—the condition under which people tend to be accurate or inaccurate in gauging their partner's feelings.

## Key facts

- Jeffry Simpson is a professor of psychology at the University of Minnesota who studies how adult attachment orientations are associated with relationship functioning and well-being. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p101]
- Simpson researches empathic accuracy—the condition under which people tend to be accurate or inaccurate in gauging their partner's feelings. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p101]
- In a study with Steve Rholes, Simpson found that avoidant individuals were less accurate than anxious individuals at perceiving their partners' thoughts and feelings, and it was common for avoidants to misinterpret their partner's upset reaction as indifference. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p102]
- Simpson, along with Steven Rholes, Lorne Campbell and Carol Wilson, found that during the shift into parenthood, anxiously attached women were more likely to move toward security if they perceived their spouses as available, supportive, and accepting during pregnancy—all secure traits. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p119]
