# Healtheon/WebMD

## Summary

A healthcare Internet company that merged with Synetic and Medical Manager Corporation. Healtheon/WebMD acquired Envoy, the largest claims clearinghouse in the industry, for $2.5 billion in January 2000, ultimately leading to Medical Manager merging with the combined company. The company experienced massive valuation swings during the Internet bubble, with stock falling from over $100 to $3 by August 2001.

## Key facts

- Healtheon and WebMD had already merged together and were soliciting Medical Manager doctors on an ever-increasing basis before the Synetic merger. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p174]
- Healtheon/WebMD acquired Envoy, the largest claims clearinghouse in the industry, for $2.5 billion on January 25, 2000. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p178]
- Medical Manager merged with Healtheon/WebMD on February 14, 2000 in a $3.5 billion deal. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p179]
- WebMD stock once traded above $100 per share but fell to $3 by August 2001 during the Internet bubble burst. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p180]
