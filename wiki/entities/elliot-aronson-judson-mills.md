# Elliot Aronson and Judson Mills

## Summary

Elliot Aronson and Judson Mills were researchers who conducted a 1959 study demonstrating that persons who go through a great deal of trouble or pain to attain something tend to value it more highly. Their initiation ceremony study showed that severe embarrassment during group entry caused newcomers to overvalue the group.

## Key facts

- Aronson and Mills found that women who endured a severely embarrassing initiation to gain access to a sex discussion group convinced themselves the group was valuable, even though the group had been rehearsed to be 'worthless and uninteresting.'. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p86]
- Additional research showed that the more electric shock a woman received as part of the initiation ceremony, the more she later persuaded herself that her new group was interesting, intelligent, and desirable. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p86]
