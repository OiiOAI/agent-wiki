# MOSAIC

## Summary

A threat assessment system designed by Gavin de Becker for predicting violent behavior. It is used by government agencies and corporations to screen threats against high-profile targets including Supreme Court justices and U.S. Senators.

## Key facts

- MOSAIC was designed by Gavin de Becker and is used for screening threats to justices of the U.S. Supreme Court. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
- The system is used by U.S. Senators and other government officials for threat assessment. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p21]
- The author notes that his method of breaking down individual elements of violence as a child became the foundation for the most sophisticated artificial intuition systems predicting violence. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
