# Wolf Singer

## Summary

Wolf Singer is a German neuroscientist who, with Charles Gray and colleagues at Frankfurt, conducted pioneering experiments on oscillations in the visual cortex. Their work demonstrated that neurons in the cat's visual cortex exhibit synchronized oscillations in the 35-75 Hz (gamma) frequency range, suggesting a mechanism for binding visual features.

## Key facts

- Wolf Singer and Charles Gray and their colleagues at Frankfurt observed oscillations in the cat's visual cortex in the 35- to 75-Hertz range. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p259]
- Singer and colleagues found that neurons near one electrode, if they fire at all, tend to fire in synchrony with those near another electrode even when electrodes are 7 millimeters apart.
