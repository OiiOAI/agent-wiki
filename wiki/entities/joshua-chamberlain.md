# Joshua Lawrence Chamberlain

## Summary

A Union officer at Gettysburg during the Civil War, known for his successful bayonet charge at Little Round Top using a well-practiced wheeling maneuver despite his men being out of ammunition. His decision to use familiar procedure rather than improvise exemplifies expert intuition under pressure.

## Key facts

- Chamberlain's unit at Little Round Top was about to be overrun by Confederate troops with no ammunition remaining. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p221]
- He ordered his men to fix bayonets and charge downhill using a wheeling maneuver they had practiced on the parade grounds. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p221]
- He chose a well-learned maneuver and his troops executed it without having to think, and he trusted his intuition that the procedure would be successful. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p221]
- His quick thinking thwarted the Confederate army's advance and turned the tide of the battle. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p222]
