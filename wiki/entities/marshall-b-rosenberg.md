# Marshall B. Rosenberg

## Summary

Marshall B. Rosenberg (1934-2015) was a clinical psychologist who developed Nonviolent Communication (NVC). He founded the Center for Nonviolent Communication and authored multiple books on compassionate communication. His work has been applied worldwide in contexts ranging from intimate relationships to international conflict mediation.

## Key facts

- Rosenberg was a clinical psychologist who developed Nonviolent Communication over decades of work focused on what empowers people to stay connected to their compassionate nature under difficult circumstances. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p26]
- His preoccupation with compassionate communication began in childhood after experiencing the 1943 Detroit race riot and being beaten because of his Jewish name. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p26]
- He studied with Professor Carl Rogers, whose research on helping relationships influenced the development of NVC. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p23]
- He was influenced by Professor Michael Hakeem, who helped him see the limitations of pathology-based psychology. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p23]
- Rosenberg's work has been used in conflict resolution and mediation in Israel, the Palestinian Authority, Nigeria, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, and elsewhere. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p36]
- He passed away at age 80 in 2015. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p19]
- Rosenberg first used NVC in federally funded school integration projects during the 1960s. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p334]
- He founded the Center for Nonviolent Communication in 1984. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p334]
- Rosenberg worked with educators, managers, health care providers, lawyers, military officers, prisoners, police, government officials, and families in over 60 countries. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p334]
- He received the International Listening Association Listener of the Year Award in 2000. [raw/books/psychology/Nonviolent Communication_ A Language of Li - Marshall B. Rosenberg.pdf#p334]
