# Alfred Adler

## Summary

Alfred Adler (1870-1937) was an Austrian psychiatrist and founder of Individual Psychology, one of the three giants of nineteenth-century psychology alongside Sigmund Freud and Carl Jung. Originally a core member of the Vienna Psychoanalytic Society led by Freud, Adler split from the group due to disagreements and proposed his own psychological theories. His work emphasizes teleology over etiology, the pursuit of superiority, community feeling, and the importance of interpersonal relationships.
