# Henry E. Singleton

## Summary

Henry E. Singleton (1916–1999) was co-founder and CEO of Teledyne, Inc. for three decades. A Naval Academy graduate who transferred to MIT for electrical engineering degrees (Bachelors, Masters, PhD), he was also an accomplished chess player rated within 100 points of Grandmaster level. Singleton is widely regarded as having one of the best operating and capital deployment records in American business history, with Teledyne returning 20.4% compound annual returns from 1963 to 1990 versus 8.0% for the S&P 500.

- Teledyne returned 20.4% compound annual return to shareholders from 1963 to 1990, compared to 8.0% for the S&P 500. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie’s Almanack_ Charles T_ Munger, Peter D_ Kaufman, Ed Wexler, Warren E_pdf.pdf#p138]
