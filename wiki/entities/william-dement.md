# William Dement

## Summary

William Dement was a student of Nathaniel Kleitman who conducted the seminal studies showing that dreaming occurs primarily during REM sleep. His 1957 study with Kleitman found that coherent dream reports were obtained 80 percent of the time upon awakening from REM sleep, compared to only 7 percent from non-REM sleep.

## Key facts

- Dement recorded the sleep of nine adults for 61 nights, awakening them an average of six times per night and collecting 351 dream reports. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p58]
- After REM awakenings, participants provided coherent dream reports 80 percent of the time, compared to only 7 percent after non-REM awakenings. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p58]
