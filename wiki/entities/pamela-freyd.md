# Pamela Freyd

## Summary

Pamela Freyd was the director of the False Memory Syndrome Foundation, which she co-founded in 1992. She reported on the declining need for the organization's services over time, eventually leading to the foundation's dissolution in 2019.

## Key facts

- Pamela Freyd, as director of the False Memory Syndrome Foundation, reported in Winter 1999 that the foundation was receiving dramatically fewer calls and letters and could phase out that part of the organization. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p173]
- Freyd announced the dissolution of the False Memory Syndrome Foundation on December 31, 2019, stating 'the need for the FMS Foundation has diminished dramatically over the years.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p185]
