# Alan Robertson

## Summary

A successful Florida banker appointed as president of the newly expanded Santa Fe Community College. He needed a doctoral degree to hold the position, so the university paired him with Singer as a tutor. This unexpected relationship became one of the first major tests of Singer's surrender practice.

## Key facts

- Alan Robertson was a successful banker appointed president of Santa Fe Community College but lacked a doctoral degree required by Florida law. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p58]
- The university paired him with Singer, then a doctoral student, to help him earn his doctorate. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p58]
- Singer describes them as quite a pair: a three-piece-suit banker and a ponytailed yogi in jeans and sandals. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p60]
- Alan was a warm, open-minded person and they became good friends, sometimes meeting at Singer's land. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p60]
- Singer later taught at Santa Fe Community College on Alan Robertson's insistence. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p64]
