# Vivienne Read

## Summary

Vivienne Read is an Australian-based organizational consultant and one of the first independent practitioners trained in Cynefin and associated methods in 2003. She has developed expertise in complex facilitation and incorporated Cynefin tools and methods into her consulting practice.

## Key facts

- Vivienne Read was one of the first independent practitioners to be trained in Cynefin and associated methods in 2003. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p354]
- Read worked alongside Professor Bill Ford pioneering new approaches to workplace reform in Australia, including assignments at Lend Lease, Carlton United Breweries, Sydney Opera House, ICI, and Westpac. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p357]
- Read's work on complex facilitation has been applied extensively, and she describes the Cynefin Framework as an approach one 'settles into,' becoming more trusted over time. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p370]
