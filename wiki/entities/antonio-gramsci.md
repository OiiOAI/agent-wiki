# Antonio Gramsci

## Summary

Antonio Gramsci (1891-1937) was an Italian Marxist thinker who played a key role in the transition from economic determinism to more modern Marxian positions. His concept of hegemony became central to cultural studies and critical theory.

## Key facts

- Gramsci was critical of Marxists who are 'deterministic, fatalistic and mechanistic.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p171]
- Gramsci wrote 'The Revolution against Capital' celebrating 'the resurrection of political will against the economic determinism.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p171]
- Gramsci operated with a rather elitist conception in which ideas were generated by intellectuals and then extended to the masses and put into practice by them. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p171]
- Gramsci defined hegemony as cultural leadership exercised by the ruling class, contrasting it with coercion exercised by legislative or executive powers. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p172]
- Gramsci saw a key role for communist intellectuals and a communist party in gaining cultural leadership over the rest of society through revolution. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p172]
