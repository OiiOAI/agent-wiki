# Oshin Vartanian

## Summary

Oshin Vartanian obtained his PhD in experimental psychology at the University of Maine and is currently a Defence Scientist at DRDC Toronto. He holds an Adjunct Assistant Professor position at the University of Toronto Scarborough and is recipient of the Daniel E. Berlyne Award from APA Division 10.

## Key facts

- Oshin Vartanian obtained his PhD in experimental psychology at the University of Maine, followed by postdoctoral fellowships in cognitive neuroscience at York University and DRDC Toronto. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p3]
- He is currently a Defence Scientist at DRDC Toronto and holds an Adjunct Assistant Professor position in the Department of Psychology at the University of Toronto Scarborough. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p3]
- He is the recipient of the Daniel E. Berlyne Award from the American Psychological Association's Division 10 for outstanding research by a junior researcher. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p3]
- Vartanian co-authored Chapter 2 with Mandel on frames, brains, and content domains, and also authored Chapter 13 on decision junctures in the creative process. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p8-9]
- Vartanian, Martindale, and Matthews (2009) conducted studies showing that people with higher divergent thinking ability are faster at judging relatedness between concepts. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p414]
- Vartanian, Martindale, and Kwiatkowski (2007) conducted studies testing the Cognitive Disinhibition Hypothesis using reaction time tasks with unambiguous and ambiguous stimuli. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p422]
- Vartanian and Goel (2005) conducted neuroimaging studies on Match Problems, identifying neural correlates of hypothesis generation and set shifting. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p417]
- Vartanian, Goel et al. (2009) administered ill-structured travel planning tasks to normal controls and patients with right frontal lobe lesions, finding that planning difficulties were linked to stagnation in concretization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p423]
