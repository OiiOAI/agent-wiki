# Steven Pinker

## Summary

Steven Pinker is a psychologist at Harvard who authored the monumental 2011 book The Better Angels of Our Nature: Why Violence Has Declined. He argued that violence has declined over the last half millennium while acknowledging that warfare and barbarity are as old as the human species. His work synthesized evidence that prehistoric and traditional societies had higher rates of violence than modern Western societies.

## Key facts

- Pinker argued that violence and the worst horrors of inhumanity have been declining for the last half millennium. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p318]
- Pinker reported 10 to 30 percent of deaths as war-related in New Guinea tribes and 35 to 60 percent for Waorani and Jivaro tribes in the Amazon. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p322]
- Pinker estimated that twenty-seven nonstate societies surveyed averaged 524 violent deaths per 100,000 people per year, compared to 1 for modern Europe. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p322]
