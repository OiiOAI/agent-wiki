# Richard Ryan

## Summary

Richard M. Ryan is a psychologist who collaborated with Edward Deci for nearly twenty years on human motivation research. He moved to the University of Rochester in 1977 and contributed philosophical and psychoanalytic psychology perspectives that complemented Deci's training in mathematics and experimental psychology. Ryan conducted important studies on different interpersonal styles of administering rewards, the coexistence of limits and autonomy, and the role of relatedness in motivation. Together, Deci and Ryan developed Self-Determination Theory.
