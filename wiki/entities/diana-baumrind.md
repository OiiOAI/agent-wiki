# Diana Baumrind

## Summary

Berkeley psychologist who in the 1960s identified three key parenting styles (authoritative, authoritarian, permissive), work that has been replicated and extended to various cultures. Her typology was influenced by post-WWII scholarship on the roots of fascism.
