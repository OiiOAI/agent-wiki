# Andrew Huxley

## Summary

Andrew Huxley (1917-2012) was a British physiologist who, together with Alan Hodgkin, developed the Hodgkin-Huxley model explaining the ionic basis of the action potential. He shared the 1963 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Hodgkin and Bernard Katz.

## Key facts

- Huxley and Hodgkin used voltage-clamp data to derive equations describing gNa and gK as functions of voltage and time that could accurately predict action potential shape and conduction velocity.
