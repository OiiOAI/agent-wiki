# Clifford Nass

## Summary

A late Stanford communications professor known for his research on behavior in the digital age, particularly showing that constant attention switching online has lasting negative effects on the brain.

## Key facts

- Nass discovered that people who multitask all the time can't filter out irrelevancy, can't manage a working memory, and are chronically distracted—they become 'mental wrecks.'. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p94]
- Multitaskers initiate much larger parts of their brain that are irrelevant to the task at hand. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p94]
- Nass found that people who multitask constantly claim they can focus when needed, but they've developed habits of mind that make laser-focus impossible—'they're suckers for irrelevancy.'. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p94]
