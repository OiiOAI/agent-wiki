# Random Dot Motion Task

## Summary

The Random Dot Motion (RDM) task is a classic perceptual decision-making paradigm in which subjects view a patch of randomly moving dots with a fraction moving coherently in one direction. Subjects must indicate which of two targets reflects the coherent direction of motion by executing an eye movement to earn reward. This task has been fundamental for understanding the neural basis of decision making.

## Key facts

- In the RDM task, subjects are rewarded a fixed amount for correct answers and receive nothing for incorrect responses. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p428]
- Difficulty in the RDM task is varied by changing the percentage of dots that move coherently. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p428]
