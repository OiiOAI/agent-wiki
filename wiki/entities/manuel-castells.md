# Manuel Castells

## Summary

Manuel Castells is a Spanish sociologist known for his trilogy 'The Information Age: Economy, Society and Culture' examining the emergence of informational capitalism and the network society. His work analyzes how information technology has transformed the global economy and social life.

## Key facts

- Castells examines the emergence of a new society, culture, and economy in light of the revolution in informational technology beginning in the 1970s in the United States. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p470]
- Castells defines informationalism as a mode of development in which the main source of productivity is the qualitative capacity to optimize the combination and use of factors of production on the basis of knowledge and information. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p471]
- The informational/global economy is global because it has the capacity to work as a unit in real time on a planetary scale. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p471]
- Castells argues that the dominant functions and processes in the information age are increasingly organized around networks defined as sets of interconnected nodes. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p473]
- Castells discusses the emergence of the culture of 'real virtuality,' a system in which reality is entirely captured, fully immersed in a virtual image setting. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p473]
- A new spatial logic, the 'space of flows,' has emerged, with processes dominating over physical locations. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p473]
