# Quad-Thigh Mobilization

## Summary

Quad-Thigh Mobilization is a foam rolling exercise targeting the quadriceps muscles to restore suppleness to stiff tissue. The quadriceps are one of the largest muscle groups and tend to adapt to the shortened position required for sitting. The mobilization uses a foam roller (or substitutes like a wine bottle, rolling pin, or taped baseballs) applied to the top of the thigh from hip to knee.

## Key facts

- The quadriceps are one of the largest muscle groups in the body. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1001-L1003]
- The mobilization helps restore suppleness to tissue stiffened by sitting. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1003-L1005]
- The exercise requires a foam roller or substitutes like a wine bottle, rolling pin, or taped baseballs. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1005-L1008]
- The roller should be positioned under the top of the thigh, from hip to knee. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1008-L1010]
- The technique involves rolling from side to side while keeping movements slow, working from the outside to the inside of the leg. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1008-L1015]
- Pressure should be significant but allow for full breathing; if breath is being held, lighten the pressure. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1010-L1013]
- Recommended duration is two to three minutes per side initially, working up to four to five minutes. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1015-L1017]
