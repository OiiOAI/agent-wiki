# Tom Insel

## Summary

Tom Insel served as director of the National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH) and became a vocal critic of DSM diagnostic categories. He argued that disorders defined by clinical consensus fail to align with neuroscience and genetic findings, do not predict treatment response, and may not capture underlying mechanisms of dysfunction. Under his leadership, NIMH launched the Research Domain Criteria (RDoC) project in 2010 as an alternative approach to psychiatric research.

## Key facts

- Insel pointed out that diagnostic categories based on clinical consensus fail to align with findings from clinical neuroscience and genetics. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p347]
- Insel led NIMH in developing the RDoC approach to research on psychiatric disorders. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p349]
