# Locus Ceruleus

## Summary

The locus ceruleus is a noradrenergic nucleus (A6 cell group) in the pons that plays a key role in attention, arousal, and stress responses. Its neurons project widely throughout the brain and spinal cord.

## Key facts

- The locus ceruleus (A6) provides the major noradrenergic input to the dorsal horn of the spinal cord for pain modulation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1046]
- Locus ceruleus neurons exhibit phasic firing during focused attention and tonic firing during search for new behavioral goals. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1052]
- In the phasic mode, bursts of firing occur just before the monkey responds to stimuli to which it has been attentive, facilitating selective attention. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1052]
- Performance is optimal when baseline locus ceruleus activity is moderate; performance is poor at low levels (not alert) and at high levels (incompatible with focused attention). [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1052]
- The locus ceruleus contains noradrenergic neurons that project widely throughout the forebrain, brain stem, and spinal cord. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1091]
- Stimulation of noradrenergic neurons in the locus ceruleus causes increases in EEG arousal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1091]
- Neurons of the locus ceruleus play an important role in attention, exhibiting phasic mode activity correlating with selective attention and tonic mode activity that may facilitate behavioral flexibility. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1097]
- Performance on target detection tasks is optimal when baseline locus ceruleus activity is moderate and phasic activation follows presentation of the target. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1097]
- Noradrenergic neurons in the locus ceruleus fire maximally during waking, decrease firing progressively during non-REM sleep, and stop firing almost completely during REM sleep. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1092]

## Inferences

- Inference: The locus ceruleus's role in both attention and arousal suggests it acts as a switch between focused and flexible behavioral states.
