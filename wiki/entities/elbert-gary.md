# Elbert Gary

## Summary

Chairman of US Steel from 1901 to 1927, known for articulating the principle that ethical practices pay because they are good, not merely because they are profitable. His philosophy aligned with Munger's view that integrity and ethical conduct are ends in themselves.

## Key facts

- Elbert Gary stated: 'Ethical practices aren't good because they pay; they pay because they are good.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p429]
