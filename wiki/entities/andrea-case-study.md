# Andrea

## Summary

Andrea is a fifty-nine-year-old divorced architect from Vancouver who maintained a seven-year romantic relationship with Michael, a married man. Perel presents her case as representative of long-term mistresses who make pragmatic choices about their position, weighing trade-offs between freedom and commitment.

## Key facts

- Andrea describes her seven-year romance with married man Michael as the most emotionally and sexually honest relationship she has ever had, conducted largely through fifty messages per night. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p200]
- Andrea acknowledges she sometimes fantasizes about Michael ending his marriage but has decided not to pressure him, believing that if he truly loved her he would overcome obstacles on his own. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p201]
- Andrea rationalizes her position by noting: 'She gets his loyalty, family, financial support, daily companionship, holidays, shared friends. I get everything that was denied to me in my own marriage—a deep emotional, sexual, and intellectual connection, romance, mutual respect, trust, and joy.'. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p203]
