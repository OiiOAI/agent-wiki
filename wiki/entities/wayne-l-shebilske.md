# Wayne L. Shebilske

## Summary

Wayne L. Shebilske is a researcher at Wright State University who contributed Chapter 14 on Situation Awareness, Automaticity, and Training. He co-developed the interactive iterative model of skill acquisition with Goettl and Regian (1999), which challenges single cycle models of skill development. Shebilske also developed the Active Interlocked Modeling (AIM) dyad protocol for training complex skills.

## Key facts

- Wayne L. Shebilske is from Wright State University and co-authored Chapter 14 on Situation Awareness, Automaticity, and Training. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p314]
- Shebilske, Goettl, and Regian (1999) proposed the interactive iterative model which applies learning phases iteratively within components rather than to whole tasks. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p314]
- Shebilske, Regian, Arthur, and Jordan (1992) developed the Active Interlocked Modeling (AIM) dyad protocol for training complex skills. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p330]
