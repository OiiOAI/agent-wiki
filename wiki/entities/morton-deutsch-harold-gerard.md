# Morton Deutsch and Harold Gerard

## Summary

Morton Deutsch and Harold Gerard were prominent social psychologists who conducted a famous experiment demonstrating that public commitments are more lasting than private or unrecorded commitments. Their research showed that students who publicly recorded their positions most resolutely refused to change them when given contradictory evidence.

## Key facts

- Deutsch and Gerard arranged for some students to commit publicly (writing and signing), some privately (Magic Pad then erased), and some not at all to their initial line-estimation judgments. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p79]
- The experiment found that publicly committed students were most stubborn in maintaining their positions, while uncommitted students were most influenced by new contradictory information. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p80]
