# Barbara Duncan

## Summary

Barbara Duncan was a University of Florida graduate who had taken programming classes and became Personalized Programming's first full-time employee in 1980. She showed up at the Temple offering to work even without salary and became a foundation block of both the company and the Temple community for twenty years. Together with Singer, she developed The Medical Manager software and helped manage the growing team of programmers.
