# Urbach-Wiethe Syndrome

## Summary

Urbach-Wiethe syndrome is a rare disease that causes calcification and damage to the amygdala. Individuals with this condition can remember past events but do not show emotional responses to emotional content that would normally elicit reactions in healthy individuals.

## Key facts

- People with amygdala damage due to Urbach-Wiethe syndrome can remember past events, but unlike individuals with functioning amygdalae, they don't respond differently to gruesome photos than to mundane pictures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p97]
- Urbach-Wiethe syndrome demonstrates the division of labor between the hippocampus (which can form and retrieve factual memories) and the amygdala (which connects memories with emotional responses). [raw/books/neuroscience/Why We Remember_ Unlocking Memory's Power - Charan Ranganath.pdf#p97]
