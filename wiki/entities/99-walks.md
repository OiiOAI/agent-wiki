# 99 Walks

## Summary

99 Walks is a company founded by Joyce and Eric Shulman that encourages people to walk through challenges, classes, and an app that virtually connects walkers worldwide. The company conducted surveys showing that 73% of women walkers felt lonely, but those who walked regularly with friends were 2.5 times less likely to experience loneliness.
