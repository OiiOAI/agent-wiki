# Voyager Golden Records

## Summary

The Voyager Golden Records are gold-plated records containing sounds and images from Earth, attached to the Voyager spacecraft launched in the late 1970s. Like the Pioneer plaques, they serve as messages to potential extraterrestrial intelligence.
