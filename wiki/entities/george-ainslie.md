# George Ainslie

## Summary

George Ainslie is a renowned psychiatrist and behavioral economist who worked out the mathematics of hyperbolic discounting. His research explains why we can resist temptations when they're distant but succumb when they're immediately available—as we approach a short-term temptation, our tendency to discount the future follows a steep hyperbolic curve. He also coined the term 'bright lines' to describe clear, unambiguous rules that help people maintain self-control.

## Key facts

- George Ainslie worked out the mathematics of hyperbolic discounting, showing how we irrationally devalue future rewards when facing immediate temptation. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p170]
- Ainslie coined the term 'bright lines' from legal terminology to describe clear, simple, unambiguous rules for self-control. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p171]
