# Theodor Reik

## Summary

A prominent non-medical member of the Vienna Psychoanalytical Society who was charged in late spring 1926 with breach of an old Austrian law against quackery—making it illegal for a person without a medical degree to treat patients. Freud intervened immediately and energetically in Reik's defense, writing the present work in part as a defense of lay analysis. The proceedings were ultimately stopped after a preliminary investigation.

- Reik was officially prohibited from practising psychoanalysis by the Vienna Municipal Council on February 24, 1925. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p6393]
- The Public Prosecutor stopped the proceedings after a preliminary investigation, partly as a result of Freud's intervention but partly because the evidence was unsatisfactory.
