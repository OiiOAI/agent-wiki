# Richard Fisch

## Summary

A psychiatrist and research associate at the Mental Research Institute in Palo Alto. He proposed the establishment of the Brief Therapy Center in 1966 and directed its early investigations into the phenomena of human change. Fisch had a sense of fun and humor that shines through the text of 'Change.' He shared with his colleagues training and experience in hypnosis and contact with Milton Erickson's innovative techniques.
