# Interactive SA Trainer

## Summary

The Interactive SA Trainer (ISAT) is a computer-based training system that employs rapid experiential learning to support mental model and schema development. Developed by Strater et al., it exposes trainees to many situations in a compressed timeframe using multimedia techniques. ISAT uses a 'what, so what, now what' model mapping to the three levels of SA. Virtual instructors (avatars) guide trainees through scenarios, focusing on critical metacognitive skills, cue identification, and contingency planning. Training scenarios are developed from GDTA analysis of key decisions in the domain.

## Key facts

- ISAT employs rapid experiential learning to build mental models and schema by exposing trainees to many situations in a compressed timeframe. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p247]
- ISAT uses a 'what, so what, now what' description model that maps to the three levels of SA for trainee understanding. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p247]
- ISAT training focuses on recognizing critical cues, evaluating information relevance, and developing contingency plans for likely near-future events. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p248]
- Military cadets trained with ISAT performed better in field exercises and showed greater confidence in their SA than those who did not receive training. [raw/books/strategy/Designing for Situation Awareness _ An Approach to - Jones, Debra G_; Endsley, Mica R.pdf#p249]
