# RULER

## Summary

RULER is an evidence-based, systemic approach to social and emotional learning developed at the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence. The acronym RULER stands for Recognizing emotions, Understanding their causes and consequences, Labeling feelings with a nuanced vocabulary, Expressing emotions appropriately, and Regulating emotions effectively. The approach has been adopted by more than two thousand pre-K through high schools across the United States and in other countries.
