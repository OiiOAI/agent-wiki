# Myelin

## Summary

Myelin is a fatty insulating sheath that wraps around large axons to increase the speed of action potential conduction. It is produced by glial cells (oligodendrocytes in the CNS, Schwann cells in the PNS) and is interrupted at regular intervals by nodes of Ranvier, uninsulated spots where action potentials are regenerated.
