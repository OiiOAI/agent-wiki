# Scott Carmichael

## Summary

Scott Carmichael was a counterintelligence officer at the Defense Intelligence Agency who was assigned to investigate suspicions about Ana Montes. Despite conducting an interview with Montes, he dismissed colleague Reg Brown's concerns due to Montes's credible explanations and his own truth-default bias.

## Key facts

- Scott Carmichael was the DIA counterintelligence investigator who interviewed Ana Montes in response to Reg Brown's suspicions. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p39]
- Carmichael found Montes credible during the interview, noting she had logical explanations for all suspicious behavior. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p51]
- Carmichael later realized he missed a crucial clue when Montes reacted with silence rather than indignation when accused—behavior inconsistent with an innocent person. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p50]
- Carmichael was the one who finally identified Montes by searching the DIA computer system for travel to Guantanamo Bay matching the NSA's decoded information. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p53]
