# John Sutherland

## Summary

A friend and traveling companion of the narrator, married to Sylvia. He rides a BMW R60 motorcycle and is a professional drummer. He exemplifies the romantic/technophobic attitude, refusing to engage with motorcycle maintenance or mechanical work.

## Key facts

- John owns a BMW R60 motorcycle that he does not maintain himself, preferring to let a mechanic handle repairs. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p12]
- He is a professional drummer who works with musical groups around town. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p48]
- John once got stuck in a traffic jam because he had his choke on full even after the engine had been running for thirty minutes on a hot day. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p14]
