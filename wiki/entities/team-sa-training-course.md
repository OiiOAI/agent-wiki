# Team SA Training Course

## Summary

The Team SA Training Course is an 8-hour classroom-based training program developed by Endsley and Robertson to enhance situation awareness in aviation maintenance teams. Based on a three-year analysis of SA requirements in aircraft maintenance settings, the course addresses five key areas: mental model alignment between teams, verbalizing decision rationale, cross-team feedback, shift meeting effectiveness, and individual SA threats. Evaluation with 72 maintenance personnel showed 89% rated it as very or extremely useful, with significant positive changes in self-reported behaviors including communication and teamwork practices.

## Key facts

- The Team SA Training Course was developed based on analysis of SA requirements and problems in aviation maintenance teams across multiple groups including aircraft maintenance technicians, stores, maintenance control, and inspection. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p367]
- The course was designed for a mixed cross-section from different maintenance operations organizations because much of its benefit comes from interaction when trainees share different viewpoints. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p368]
- The course evaluation revealed significant changes (p < 0.05) on 7 of 33 behavioral items between pre-test and post-test measurements. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p370]
- Participants were 15% more likely to try to understand others' viewpoints when engaged in a disagreement with other departments. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p371]
- The one-month follow-up showed no significant changes on any test items, indicating participants carried out the behaviors they intended following training. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p372]

## Inferences

- Inference: The Team SA Training Course represents a replicable model for developing domain-specific team SA training programs in other high-risk industries

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Follow-up research is still needed to validate on-the-job behavior changes and impact on critical maintenance performance measures
