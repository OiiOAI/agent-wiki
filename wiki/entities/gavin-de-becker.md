# Gavin de Becker

## Summary

Security expert and author who founded Gavin de Becker & Associates, a firm that predicts violent behavior for government agencies, corporations, and individuals. He developed the MOSAIC threat assessment system and has worked with clients including the U.S. Marshals Service, CIA, and public figures.

## Key facts

- The author designed MOSAIC, the assessment system used for screening threats to justices of the U.S. Supreme Court, Senators, and other public figures. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
- Gavin de Becker received an award from the U.S. Attorney General and the Director of the FBI for designing MOSAIC. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
- His firm, Gavin de Becker & Associates, has worked with federal agencies including the U.S. Marshals Service, Federal Reserve Board, and CIA, as well as prosecutors, corporations, universities, and media figures. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p21]
- The author's childhood included exposure to violence, including witnessing his mother shoot his stepfather when he was ten years old. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p19]
- He worked as a bodyguard for Elizabeth Taylor and Richard Burton at age seventeen, and later for other presidents and celebrities. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
