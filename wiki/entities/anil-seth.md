# Anil Seth

## Summary

Anil Seth is a British neuroscientist and Professor of Consciousness Science at the University of Sussex, where he is Co-Director of the Sackler Centre for Consciousness Science. He is one of the world's leading consciousness researchers, known for developing the 'controlled hallucination' framework of perception and the 'real problem' approach to consciousness science. His work spans consciousness measurement, predictive brain theory, and disorders of consciousness.

## Key facts

- Seth is Co-Director of the Sackler Centre for Consciousness Science at the University of Sussex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p14]
- He worked for six years at the Neurosciences Institute in San Diego with Nobel Laureate Gerald Edelman before returning to Sussex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p14]
- Seth has developed 'causal density' as a measure of neural complexity related to consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p60]
- He co-authored research with Robin Carhart-Harris on psychedelic states and consciousness measures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p53]
- Anil Seth is Professor of Cognitive and Computational Neuroscience at the University of Sussex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p296]
- He is Co-Director of the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research's Program on Brain, Mind and Consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p296]
- He is a European Research Council Advanced Investigator. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p296]
- He has published over 160 academic papers and is recognized as a 'highly cited' researcher. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p296]
- In January 2019, Seth came face to face with a living human brain for the first time, observing a hemispherotomy. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p253]
- He was mentored by Gerald Edelman for more than six years as a postdoctoral researcher. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p260]
- He is a Wellcome Trust Engagement Fellow. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p296]
