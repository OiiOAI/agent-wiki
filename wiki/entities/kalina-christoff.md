# Kalina Christoff

## Summary

A researcher who has studied the neural basis of spontaneous thought and mind wandering. Her work demonstrates that spontaneous thought shares common attributes with goal-directed thought and is associated with default network and memory system activation.

## Key facts

- Christoff et al. (2004) proposed that spontaneous thought is a complex form of cognition worthy of empirical investigation, demonstrating that brain regions recruited in spontaneous mentation overlap with those in goal-directed thought. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p351]
- Research by Christoff and colleagues links mind wandering to the default network and temporal lobe memory regions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p355]
