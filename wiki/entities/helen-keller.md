# Helen Keller

## Summary

Helen Keller was born in Alabama in 1880 and was struck by illness at nineteen months old, leaving her deaf and blind. Despite these immense challenges, she learned to read and write and became a prolific author and powerful advocate for social change. Russ Harris quotes her statement that 'Life is a daring adventure, or nothing.'
