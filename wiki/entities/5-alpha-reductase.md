# 5α-Reductase

## Summary

5α-Reductase is the enzyme that converts testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT), a more potent androgen. Two isoforms exist: 5α-reductase II is expressed at high levels in the external genitalia during fetal development and is essential for virilization of male external genitalia.

## Key facts

- 5α-Reductase converts testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT), which has approximately three-fold higher affinity for the androgen receptor than testosterone. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1264]
- 5α-Reductase II is expressed at high levels in the precursor of external genitalia, where DHT induces secondary male characteristics including facial hair, body hair, and prostate growth. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1264]
- 5α-reductase II deficiency causes males (XY) to be born with feminized or ambiguous external genitalia but virilize dramatically at puberty when testosterone levels rise. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1264]
