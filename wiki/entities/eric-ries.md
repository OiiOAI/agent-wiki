# Eric Ries

## Summary

Eric Ries is the author of The Lean Startup and a pioneer of the Lean Startup methodology. He co-founded IMVU in 2004 and served as Chief Technology Officer, where he pioneered many of the practices described in his book. Ries has worked with hundreds of entrepreneurs, startups, and Fortune 500 companies to apply lean principles to innovation. He started the blog 'Startup Lessons Learned' and helped establish Lean Startup communities in over 100 cities worldwide.
