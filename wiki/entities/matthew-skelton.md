# Matthew Skelton

## Summary

Matthew Skelton is a co-author of the book 'Team Topologies' and a recognized expert in DevOps, team topology, and software delivery. He has worked with organizations to help them understand and implement effective team structures for DevOps and continuous delivery.

## Key facts

- Matthew Skelton is co-author of 'Team Topologies'. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- Matthew Skelton co-created the DevOps Topologies models that preceded the book. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- Matthew Skelton is co-author of the Team Topologies book. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L424]
- In 2013, Matthew created the original DevOps Topologies patterns in a blog post titled 'What Team Structure Is Right for DevOps to Flourish?'. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L446]
- Matthew spoke at QCon London 2015 on Conway's law and early DevOps Topology patterns. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L449]
- Matthew introduced DevOps and Continuous Delivery at a UK company in 2013, where he devised the original DevOps Topologies patterns. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L446]
- Matthew Skelton has been building, deploying, and operating commercial software systems since 1998. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He has worked for organizations including London Stock Exchange, GlaxoSmithKline, FT.com, LexisNexis, and the UK government. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He is Head of Consulting at Conflux (confluxdigital.net). [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He holds a BSc in computer science and cybernetics from the University of Reading, an MSc in neuroscience from the University of Oxford, and an MA in music from the Open University. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He is a chartered engineer (CEng) in the UK. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
