# DISC1

## Summary

DISC1 (Disrupted in Schizophrenia 1) is a schizophrenia risk gene identified through genetic mapping studies of a large Scottish family with high rates of schizophrenia. In mice, DISC1 affects neuronal development, neuronal signaling, and the incorporation of newborn neurons into neural circuits. A DISC1 mutation greatly increases schizophrenia risk but is mutated only in a small fraction of schizophrenic patients, suggesting that multiple genetic factors contribute to the disorder.
