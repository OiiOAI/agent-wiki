# Naperville School District 203

## Summary

A suburban Chicago school district of approximately 19,000 students that implemented a revolutionary physical education program focused on fitness rather than traditional sports. The program uses heart rate monitors, personal best tracking, and varied lifetime fitness activities. Students consistently rank among the top academic performers in Illinois and internationally, with dramatically lower obesity rates than national averages.

## Key facts

- Naperville District 203 includes fourteen elementary schools, five junior highs, and two high schools. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p16]
- Naperville Central High School's per-pupil operating expense in 2005 was $8,939 versus $15,403 at Evanston's New Trier High School, yet Central performed better on composite state tests. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p16]
- Naperville's composite ACT score for the graduating class of 2005 was 24.8, well above the state average of 20.1. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p16]
- On the 1999 TIMSS test, Naperville's eighth graders finished first in the world in science and sixth in math among 230,000 students from 38 countries. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p16]
- The district is 83 percent white, with only 2.6 percent in the low income range, compared with 40 percent for Illinois as a whole. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p17]
- An astonishing 97 percent of freshmen in 2001 and 2002 were at a healthy weight according to body mass index guidelines. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p21]
- In Zero Hour PE, students must stay between 80 and 90 percent of their maximum heart rate during exercise. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p14]
- Zero Hour literacy students showed a 17 percent improvement in reading and comprehension compared to 10.7 percent for other literacy students. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p15]
