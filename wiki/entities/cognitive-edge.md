# Cognitive Edge

## Summary

Cognitive Edge is a management consulting firm founded by Dave Snowden and Cynthia Kurtz, specializing in complexity thinking and sense-making methodologies. The company developed SenseMaker software and has trained practitioners worldwide in Cynefin-based approaches.

## Key facts

- Dave Snowden and Cynthia Kurtz were growing frustrated with a rigid corporate culture at IBM that emphasized standard operating procedures and best practices over innovative applications informed by complex systems science. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
- Cognitive Edge was founded to provide a home for Cynefin-related work and training. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
- The Cynefin Framework's evolution coincided with transformations in data entry technology enabled by price reductions in mobile devices and explosive cell phone penetration worldwide. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p255]
