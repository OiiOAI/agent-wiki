# Phædrus

## Summary

Phædrus is the narrator's past identity, a rhetoric teacher who pursued an investigation into the meaning of Quality. He sought to enter the University of Chicago's interdisciplinary program 'Analysis of Ideas and Study of Methods' to pursue his thesis on Quality. His combative approach led to conflict with the committee, particularly its Aristotelian chairman. Phædrus studied ancient Greek philosophy obsessively, seeking the origin of Quality, eventually discovering aretē in the Sophists. He became convinced that Plato's subordination of the Good (Quality) to Truth (Dialectic) was the root of Western civilization's split between Quality and Reason.
