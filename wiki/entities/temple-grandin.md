# Temple Grandin

## Summary

Temple Grandin is one of the most famous and outspoken individuals with autism, who writes clearly about her lack of prediction and overwhelming prediction error. She describes how sudden loud noises hurt her ears like a dentist's drill hitting a nerve. She struggled to form concepts as a child, categorizing dogs from cats by size until a Dachshund caused confusion because she couldn't understand why a small dog wasn't a cat. Her writings are cited as evidence for the speculation that autism may be a disorder of prediction.
