# Nick Littlehales

## Summary

Nick Littlehales is a sports sleep coach who developed the R90 Sleep Recovery Program over nearly two decades working with elite athletes. He began his career as international sales and marketing director at Slumberland, Europe's largest sleeping comfort group, before becoming Manchester United's sleep coach in the late 1990s at Sir Alex Ferguson invitation. He later worked with British Cycling, Team Sky, British Olympians and Paralympians, and numerous football clubs including Real Madrid.

- Littlehales was international sales and marketing director at Slumberland, Europe's largest sleeping comfort group, in the late 1990s. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p8]
- He was invited to work with Manchester United after writing to Sir Alex Ferguson, who had never considered sleep as a performance factor. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p8]
- He introduced probably the first training-ground sleep recovery room at Manchester United's Carrington facility. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p9]
- He worked with British Cycling and Team Sky as part of Sir Dave Brailsford's marginal-gains approach, designing portable sleep kits for riders. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p9]
- He has also worked with Real Madrid, Arsenal, Manchester City, Liverpool, Chelsea, and USA NBA and NFL teams. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p9]
- He was chairman of the UK Sleep Council, a consumer education organization, where he worked with Professor Chris Idzikowski. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p9]
