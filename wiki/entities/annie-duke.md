# Annie Duke

## Summary

Annie Duke is a former professional poker player and author who won more than $4 million in tournament earnings over a twenty-year career. She holds degrees in English and psychology from Columbia University and completed doctoral course work in cognitive psychology at the University of Pennsylvania. After retiring from poker in 2012, she became a consultant and speaker applying poker-derived decision strategies to business and professional contexts.
