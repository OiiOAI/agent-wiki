# Adrian Newey

## Summary

Adrian Newey is widely regarded as one of the world's greatest motorsport engineers, serving as chief technical officer of Red Bull Racing Formula One team. His designs have won over 150 Grand Prix and 10 Constructors' Championships with three different teams. He emphasizes depersonalizing failures as engineering problems rather than emotional setbacks.

## Key facts

- Adrian Newey is the chief technical officer of Red Bull Racing and the only engineer to have won 10 Constructors' Championships with three different Formula One teams. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p153]
- His car designs won the Indianapolis 500 for three consecutive years (1985-1987) in American IndyCar. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p153]
- Newey depersonalizes problems by focusing on treating failures as engineering problems where the reason simply is not yet understood. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p154]
- He plans to become more involved with long-distance sports car racing like Le Mans and with smaller, more efficient cars. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p168]
