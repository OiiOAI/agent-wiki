# Anxiety Disorders

## Summary

Anxiety disorders are the most common psychiatric disorders, affecting 28.5% of the US population over their lifetimes. They represent dysregulation of fear circuitry and include panic disorder, posttraumatic stress disorder, generalized anxiety disorder, social anxiety disorder, and specific phobias. They frequently co-occur with depression.

- Anxiety disorders represent significant dysregulation of fear circuitry. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1551]
- Panic attacks are characterized by discrete periods of intense foreboding, sense of doom, fear of losing control, and prominent bodily symptoms including heart palpitations, breathing difficulty, and sweating.
