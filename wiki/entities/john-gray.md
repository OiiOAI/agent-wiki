# John Gray

## Summary

John Gray is the author of the enormously popular book 'Men Are from Mars, Women Are from Venus.' His personal story of misunderstanding his wife Bonnie after childbirth illustrates how stress and effective communication can help invoke a secure working model in someone with avoidant tendencies, helping them realize their partner's well-being is their sacred duty.

## Key facts

- John Gray wrote the enormously popular book Men Are from Mars, Women Are from Venus. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p102]
- Gray's aha moment came when his wife Bonnie, after a painful delivery, spent a day in pain taking care of their newborn because he had returned to work, and he misinterpreted her distress as anger, becoming defensive. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p102]
- When Bonnie stopped him from leaving and said 'This is when I need you most,' Gray went over to her and silently held her—marking the first time he didn't leave her in that moment of need. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p102]
- This event helped invoke a secure working model in Gray, helping him realize that his wife's well-being is his responsibility and sacred duty, shifting him from someone looking out for his own needs to a more secure mind-set. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p103]
