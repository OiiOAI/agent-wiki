# Joe Girard

## Summary

Joe Girard was a Chevrolet salesman from Detroit who earned over $200,000 per year on the showroom floor, winning the title of "number one car salesman" for twelve consecutive years and earning recognition from Guinness World Records as the world's greatest car salesman.

## Key facts

- Joe Girard earned over $200,000 per year as a Chevrolet showroom salesman. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p155]
- He won the "number one car salesman" title for twelve consecutive years. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p155]
- His sales formula consisted of offering customers a fair price and a salesperson they liked. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p155]
- Girard sent personalized "I like you" holiday cards monthly to his 13,000 former customers. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p159]

## Inferences

- Inference: Girard's success demonstrates the practical application of the liking rule in real-world sales, showing that customer affection for a salesperson can be as important as the product itself.
