# Dieter Rams

## Summary

Dieter Rams was the lead designer at Braun for many years and is driven by the idea that almost everything is noise and few things are essential. His design criteria is captured in three German words: 'Weniger aber besser' (Less but better). As a young designer, he had the courage to eliminate nonessentials, such as when he designed a record player with only a clear plastic cover instead of the conventional solid wooden lid.

## Key facts

- Dieter Rams' design criteria is 'Weniger aber besser' meaning 'Less but better,' which the author states is a fitting definition of Essentialism. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p10]
- At age 24, Rams designed a record player with only a clear plastic cover, eliminating the conventional solid wooden lid or furniture-style housing, which was revolutionary and initially worried the company it might fail.
