# Doomsday Clock

## Summary

The Doomsday Clock is a symbol maintained by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists since 1947 representing how close humanity is to self-inflicted extinction. It was originally inspired by nuclear weapons and expanded in 2007 to include climate change.

## Key facts

- The Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists has maintained the Doomsday Clock since 1947 to highlight how close we are to making Earth uninhabitable. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p175]
- The Doomsday Clock was first inspired by the possibility of nuclear war destroying Earth. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p175]
- The Doomsday Clock was expanded in 2007 to include climate change as a second potential cause of self-inflicted extinction. [raw/books/neuroscience/A Thousand Brains _ A New Theory of Intelligence - Jeff Hawkins; Richard Dawkins; Basic Books.pdf#p175]
