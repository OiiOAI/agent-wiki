# Everest Riccioni

## Summary

Everest Riccioni was a full colonel, test pilot, and fighter pilot who became a key member of the Fighter Mafia. He was a volatile, emotional Italian-American who favored the idea of a lightweight, high thrust-to-weight fighter. He obtained the $149,000 study grant that funded early work on what became the YF-16 and YF-17 prototypes. He nicknamed their group the 'Fighter Mafia' and served as its self-appointed 'godfather.'

## Key facts

- Riccioni obtained $149,000 in research funding split between Northrop and General Dynamics for lightweight fighter design studies. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p244]
- He wrote a book called 'Tigers Airborne' criticizing Air Force tactics, which nearly ended his career. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p237]
- Riccioni suggested the group call themselves the 'Fighter Mafia' with him as the godfather. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p239]
- He was transferred to Korea after openly criticizing the F-15 to the vice chief of staff. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p249]
