# Intel Corporation

## Summary

Intel Corporation is a semiconductor company founded in 1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, with Andrew Grove joining early on. The company pioneered the microprocessor business after exiting the DRAM market in the mid-1980s under Japanese competitive pressure. Grove used Intel as the primary case study and practical laboratory for the management principles in High Output Management.

## Key facts

- Intel was founded in 1968 and had over 30,000 employees by the mid-1990s. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L270-L271]
- About 25 percent of Intel employees made products, 25 percent supervised and maintained machines, 25 percent worked in administration, and 25 percent designed and sold products. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L273-L276]
- Intel was forced to exit the DRAM business in the mid-1980s due to Japanese competition, refocusing on microprocessors. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L116-L122]
- Intel grew to become the largest semiconductor manufacturer in the world after prevailing over Japanese competitors. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L127-L129]
- Intel was known as the best-managed company in Silicon Valley with legendary operational precision. [raw/books/business/High_Output_Management.epub#L335-L337]
