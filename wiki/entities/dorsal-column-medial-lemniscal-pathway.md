# Dorsal Column-Medial Lemniscal Pathway

## Summary

The dorsal column-medial lemniscal pathway is the major afferent pathway for somatosensory information from the body. Sensory information enters the CNS through dorsal root ganglion cells, travels through the gracile and cuneate fasciculi in the spinal cord, synapses in the medulla, crosses to the contralateral side, ascends through the brainstem via the medial lemniscus to the thalamus, and ultimately reaches the somatosensory cortex. Fibers maintaining orderly body representation form neural maps at each relay.
