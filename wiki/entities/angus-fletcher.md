# Angus Fletcher

## Summary

Angus Fletcher is a professor who founded Project Narrative at Ohio State University, a think tank housed on the top floor of the university's former administrative headquarters. He developed the theory of Primal Intelligence over two decades of academic research. In March 2021, he was contacted by U.S. Army Special Operations to help with recruits who had high IQ scores but performed poorly at real-world decision-making. His collaboration with Special Operations lieutenant colonel Tom Gaines led to the development of Primal training, which was subsequently tested across military units, corporations, universities, and elementary schools.

## Key facts

- Project Narrative is housed on the top floor of Ohio State's former administrative headquarters, a rusted structure with a broken heater. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p7]
- Fletcher claims the Fletcher lab identified a primordial brainpower driving intuition, imagination, commonsense, and smart emotion that is neglected in modern schools and impossible for computer AI. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p8]
- Fletcher collaborated with Special Operations lieutenant colonel Tom Gaines to develop training methods for VUCA (volatility, uncertainty, complexity, and ambiguity) environments. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p14]
- Fletcher speaks with a fake American accent despite being born in England, which he adopted after high school to be his 'authentic self.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p32]
- As a child, Fletcher refused to sit on a school bus without seatbelts, standing in the aisle and requesting one until removed from the bus—an example of 'fighting his fight.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Primal Intelligence_ You Are Smarter Than - Angus Fletcher.pdf#p67]
