# SenseMaker

## Summary

SenseMaker is software developed by Cognitive Edge that enables the collection and sensemaking of narratives, stories, and fragmented information. It serves as an essential tool for working with the Cynefin Framework, particularly in the Complex domain, allowing organizations to engage with people's stories at the level they are.

## Key facts

- SenseMaker was developed by Cognitive Edge and allows collection and sensemaking of narratives, stories, and fragmented information. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p127]
- Friso Gosliga was part of the prototype development team for SenseMaker in 2004. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p182]
- SenseMaker is described as an essential tool for working with Cynefin, particularly in the Complex domain. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p127]
- The software enables engagement with people at the level that they are, without requiring them to jump through developmental hoops. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p156]
- SenseMaker is described as 'the world's first crowdsourcing method for human judgment, meaning, and feeling.'. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p262]
- SenseMaker provides both quantitative and qualitative data through narrative collection. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p289]
- The SenseMaker Wellbeing Pulse applies the software specifically to focus on well-being in organizations. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p289]
- The Cynefin Framework's development was enabled by mobile devices and cell phone penetration, which allowed for easy-to-use interview tools and disintermediation of interpretation. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p256]
- Research into pilot mental health using stories uncovered that pilots were creating false names and email addresses to access mental health support, fearing license revocation. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p284]
