# John H. Weakland

## Summary

A collaborator of Paul Watzlawick at the Mental Research Institute in Palo Alto. He was instrumental in developing the interactional approach to psychotherapy and co-authored 'Change.' His mentor was psychiatrist Milton Erickson. Weakland's trademark was wry humor, which influenced the book's tone. He previously co-authored 'Pragmatics of Human Communication' with Watzlawick. As research associate at the Mental Research Institute, he had years of experience in human communication research and interactional psychotherapy.
