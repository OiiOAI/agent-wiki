# Hiroshi Ishiguro

## Summary

Hiroshi Ishiguro is a Japanese roboticist who has spent decades building 'Geminoids'—robots designed to be as similar as possible to human beings. He is a professor at Osaka University and creates humanoid robots using 3D body scans and pneumatic actuators for facial expressions. His work explores human-robot interaction and the uncanny valley effect.

## Key facts

- Hiroshi Ishiguro has spent decades building 'Geminoids'—robots designed to be as similar as possible to human beings, with applications in telepresence. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p243]
- Each Geminoid is constructed from detailed 3D body scans and has pneumatic actuators able to generate a wide range of facial expressions and gestures. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p243]
