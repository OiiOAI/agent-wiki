# Anatol Rapoport

## Summary

Anatol Rapoport was a well-known psychologist and games theorist from Toronto who submitted the winning strategy in both of Axelrod's computer tournaments. His simple 'Tit for Tat' strategy—cooperating on the first move and then copying the opponent's previous move—proved to be the most successful.

## Key facts

- Anatol Rapoport submitted Tit for Tat, the winning strategy in Axelrod's first computer tournament. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p249]
- Rapoport submitted Tit for Tat again in the second tournament and won for the second time. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p252]
