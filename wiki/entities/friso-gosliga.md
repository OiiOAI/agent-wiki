# Friso Gosliga

## Summary

Friso Gosliga is an organizational psychologist from the Netherlands and one of the original IBM Cynefin team members, working with Dave Snowden since 2000. He was part of the prototype development team for SenseMaker in 2004 and has run SenseMaker projects worldwide.
