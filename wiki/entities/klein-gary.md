# Gary Klein

## Summary

Gary Klein is a researcher at Klein Associates Inc. and a leading figure in Naturalistic Decision Making. He is the author of the Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) model and the developer of the Critical Decision Method (CDM). His work emphasizes that situation awareness is the perception of affordances and that decision makers use expertise to recognize situations and know what to do rather than comparing options.

## Key facts

- Gary Klein is affiliated with Klein Associates Inc. and authored Chapter 3 on analysis of situation awareness from critical incident reports. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p68]
- Klein formulated the Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) model with Calderwood and Clinton-Cirocco in 1986. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p71]
- Klein co-developed the Critical Decision Method (CDM) with Calderwood and MacGregor in 1989. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p75]
- Klein's book 'Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions' was published in 1998 by MIT Press. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p88]
- Klein and Crandall (1995) described mental simulation as a process for generating predictions of the future and explanations of the past. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p71]
