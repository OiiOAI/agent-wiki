# SGW Designworks

## Summary

SGW Designworks is a company in Boise, Idaho that specializes in rapid production techniques for physical products. The case study describes how they designed and delivered a complex field x-ray system for a military customer in three and a half weeks, using 3D CAD software, CNC prototyping, and small-batch principles. They used this process to design and deliver eight products serving a wide range of functions in a twelve-month period.

## Key facts

- SGW Designworks designed a supporting structure for a field x-ray system with requirements for stability, durability, easy deployment, and collapsibility into a backpack. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p180]
- The first physical prototypes were machined from aluminum using CNC directly from the 3D model three days after project initiation, followed by client review with minor design modifications. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p180]
- SGW delivered the first production run of forty completed units three and a half weeks after initiating the development project. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p180]
- SGW used the same small-batch process to design and deliver eight products serving a wide range of functions in a twelve-month period, with half generating revenue today. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p181]
