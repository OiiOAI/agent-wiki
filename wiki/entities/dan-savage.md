# Dan Savage

## Summary

Dan Savage is a sex columnist and writer whom Perel cites on multiple topics related to monogamy and infidelity. He coined the term 'monogamish' to describe relationships that remain emotionally committed but allow flexibility, and argues for redefining fidelity beyond sexual exclusivity.

## Key facts

- Dan Savage argues it is reductionist to make sexual exclusivity the sole marker of devotion, illustrating this with the story of a five-times-married woman who accused him of not being committed despite twenty years of nonexclusive partnership with his husband. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p217]
- Savage coined the term 'monogamish' to signify remaining emotionally committed but making space for third parties through fantasy, flirtation, flings, threesomes, or other forms. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p218]
- Savage suggests monogamy should be an 'opt-in,' arguing that providing choice might prevent people from getting 'in trouble for adultery' when they fail monogamy's standardized test. [raw/books/psychology/The State of Affairs - Esther Perel.pdf#p219]
