# Oscar T. Brooks

## Summary

Oscar T. Brooks was the first black instructor to serve at the Fighter Weapons School. In 1957, Boyd insisted on including Brooks in Friday brunches at the Sahara Hotel, effectively forcing the desegregation of Las Vegas hotels and restaurants.

## Key facts

- Oscar T. Brooks was a first lieutenant and the first black instructor to come to the FWS in 1957. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p95]
- When Spradling expressed concern about taking Brooks to the Sahara Hotel, Boyd said they would go as a group and if they were kicked out they would have to kick out the whole U.S. Air Force. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p95]
- Boyd stated: "The Air Force is integrated. We don't have a problem. It's their goddamn problem.". [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p95]
- Boyd and his fighter pilots desegregated Las Vegas that Friday in 1957 and returned almost every Friday until Boyd was transferred in summer 1960. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p96]
