# The Mountain Climber

## Summary

The Mountain Climber (nicknamed 'el Alpinista') was a CIA officer who ran the U.S. spy network inside Cuba. He was Aspillaga's role model and was unaware for years that nearly all his agents had been turned into double agents by Cuban intelligence.

## Key facts

- The Mountain Climber was a CIA officer so skilled that Soviet and East German intelligence taught a course on his tradecraft to their agents. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p18]
- The Mountain Climber was described as 'incorruptible' and spoke Spanish like a Cuban. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p19]
- After learning from Aspillaga that his agents were doubles, the Mountain Climber said: 'It turns out that I wasn't the one pulling the wool over their eyes. That was a bit of a blow.'. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p20]
