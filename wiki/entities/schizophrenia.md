# Schizophrenia

## Summary

A psychiatric disorder discussed in relation to consciousness and unconscious processing. The book examines how schizophrenia may involve disrupted consciousness signatures, particularly in relation to hallucinations and meaning processing.

## Key facts

- Schizophrenia is associated with disrupted unconscious processing and abnormal gamma-band synchronization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p254-259]
- Hallucinations in schizophrenia may result from failure of internal monitoring systems that normally distinguish self-generated from external information. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p254-258]
- Masking experiments reveal abnormal processing in schizophrenia patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p254-255]
- Schizophrenia involves disrupted meaning processing at the unconscious level. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p255-257]
