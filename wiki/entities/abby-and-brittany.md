# Abby and Brittany

## Summary

Abby and Brittany are conjoined twins who share a joint chest with two hearts, two hands, and two legs, but have separate heads and spinal cords. Each brain and spinal cord innervates one hand and one leg. They can perform many movement patterns requiring bilateral hand and leg coordination (walking, running, swimming, ball games, driving) despite having separate somatosensory innervation. This demonstrates that the physical constraints of the skeletal system enable incorporation of body extensions into the body scheme through action-induced joint experience.
