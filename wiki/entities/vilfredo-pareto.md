# Vilfredo Pareto

## Summary

Vilfredo Pareto (1848-1923) was an Italian sociologist and economist known for his elite theory of social change and his scientific conception of sociology. He developed a theory of the circulation of elites and argued that society is a system in equilibrium. His work was influential in the 1930s through Talcott Parsons.

## Key facts

- Vilfredo Pareto was born in 1848 and died in 1923. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p75]
- He developed an elite theory of social change holding that society is inevitably dominated by a small elite. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p76]
- Pareto conceived of society as a system in equilibrium consisting of interdependent parts. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p76]
- His systemic conception of society was an important influence on Talcott Parsons's theory and structural functionalism. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p76]
