# Finland Education Model

## Summary

Finland is frequently judged to have the most successful schools in the world by international league tables. Finnish children don't start school until age seven and between ages 7-16 arrive at 9 a.m. and leave at 2 p.m. They receive almost no homework and take almost no tests until graduating from high school. By law, teachers must give children fifteen minutes of free play for every 45 minutes of instruction. Only 0.1 percent of Finnish children are diagnosed with attention problems, and Finns are among the most literate, numerate, and happy people in the world.
