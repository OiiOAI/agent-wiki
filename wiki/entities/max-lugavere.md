# Max Lugavere

## Summary

Journalist and author Max Lugavere wrote 'Genius Foods' after his mother developed cognitive decline in her late fifties. His investigation into his mother's condition, including visits to leading neurology departments, led him to research the connection between diet, lifestyle, and brain health.

## Key facts

- Lugavere's mother Kathy was diagnosed in 2011 at age 58 with memory problems later associated with Parkinson's and Alzheimer's disease. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p9-10]
- Lugavere has interviewed dozens of leading researchers and visited research labs at Harvard, Brown, and Sweden's Karolinska Institutet. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p14]
- Lugavere has coauthored a chapter on Alzheimer's prevention in a textbook for neuropsychologists. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p15]
- Max Lugavere is a filmmaker, TV personality, and health and science journalist. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
- He directed 'Bread Head,' the first documentary about dementia prevention through diet and lifestyle. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
- He has contributed to Medscape, Vice, Fast Company, and the Daily Beast. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
- He has been featured on NBC Nightly News, The Dr. O Show, and The Doctors, and in the Wall Street Journal. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
- He has lectured at the New York Academy of Sciences and Weill Cornell Medicine. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
- He gave a keynote at the Biohacker Summit in Stockholm, Sweden. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p545]
