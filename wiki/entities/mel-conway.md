# Mel Conway

## Summary

Mel Conway is the author of a classic paper on organizational design for software development that introduced what became known as Conway's Law. His work on the relationship between organizational communication structures and system designs is foundational to the Team Topologies approach.

## Key facts

- Mel Conway wrote a classic paper on organizational design for software development that introduced Conway's Law. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L416]
- Conway's Law states that organizations which design systems are constrained to produce designs which are copies of the communication structures of these organizations. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L417-L418]
- Conway identified a criterion for structuring design organizations: design effort should be organized according to the need for communication. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L420-L421]
- Mel Conway is credited with originating Conway's law, which states that system design mirrors organizational communication structures. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L508-L509]
- Mel Conway published 'How Do Committees Invent?' in Datamation in 1968 exploring the relationship between organizational structure and resulting system design. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L728-L730]
- Conway's law states that organizations which design systems are constrained to produce designs which are copies of the communication structures of these organizations. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L734-L735]
- Conway observed the strong correlation between an organization's real communication paths and the resulting software architecture, which he called the homomorphic force. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L737-L739]
