# Phil Ivey

## Summary

One of the world's best professional poker players, known for exceptional skill across all forms and formats of poker. Ivey exemplifies productive habits of mind, actively seeking out potential errors in his play even after winning, demonstrating a commitment to truthseeking and learning rather than self-serving bias.

## Key facts

- Phil Ivey built a reputation starting in his early twenties as a top player in cash games, tournaments, heads-up play, and mixed games. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1115-L1118]
- After winning a televised final table in 2004, he spent dinner deconstructing every potential playing error he might have made, seeking my brother's opinion on each decision. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1120-L1126]
- At celebratory dinners following his World Series of Poker victories, he discussed in intricate detail with other pros points in hands where he could have made better decisions. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1127-L1130]
- He is considered an exception to the self-serving bias that dominates most poker players. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1118-L1119]
