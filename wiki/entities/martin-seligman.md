# Martin Seligman

## Summary

Martin Seligman is identified as a founder of positive psychology and author of 'Authentic Happiness.' The book describes how he revised his views after realizing that chasing transient happiness could lead to harmful behaviors. He developed the PERMA model of flourishing: Positive emotions, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, and Accomplishments. The book notes that activities like raising children may not produce happiness but contribute massively to wellbeing and a 'life well-lived.' Meaning is described as the factor that 'puts the other parts into perspective and gives them context.'

## Key facts

- Seligman revised his definition of positive psychology goals after realizing 'doing what makes me happy' could mean following harmful whims. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p147]
- His PERMA model includes Positive emotions, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, and Accomplishments. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p147]
- Seligman considers Meaning the factor that gives other wellbeing components context and perspective. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p148]
