# Patient AI

## Summary

Patient AI is a research participant unable to move her eyes since birth due to fibrosis in her eye muscles. She compensates for lack of eye movements by making head saccades—saw-tooth movements to the right averaging 200 ms duration, with head jerking back to the left at line ends to continue reading. During picture viewing, her head movements scan important details similarly to how sighted individuals deploy eye saccades. This demonstrates that actively moving sensors (head, eyes) enhances visual sensing ability.

## Key facts

- Patient AI cannot move her eyes since birth due to fibrosis in her eye muscles but compensates by making head saccades averaging 200 ms duration to read text and scan pictures. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p89]
- During picture viewing, AI's head movements scan important details of the picture similarly to how sighted individuals deploy eye saccades to extract interesting features. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p89]
- AI's saccade strategy is the same as that of normal subjects despite being considerably slower overall with less head stability, demonstrating that active movement of sensors enhances visual sensing ability. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p70]
