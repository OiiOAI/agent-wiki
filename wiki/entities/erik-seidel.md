# Erik Seidel

## Summary

Erik Seidel is a professional poker player and World Series of Poker champion who became a legend at the game. He was part of Annie Duke's learning pod—a group of smart, analytical East Coast players who helped her develop truthseeking habits. Duke describes him as communicating the elements of a productive group charter in three sentences, refusing to listen to complaints about luck and insisting on focusing only on questions about strategy.

## Key facts

- Erik Seidel was part of Annie Duke's learning pod, a group of world-class professional poker players who helped her develop truthseeking habits. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1175-L1180]
- Erik Seidel told Duke: 'I don't want to hear it. I'm not trying to hurt your feelings, but if you have a question about a hand, you can ask me about strategy all day long. I just don't think there's much purpose in a poker story if the point is about something you had no control over, like bad luck.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1255-L1260]
- Seidel discouraged confirmatory or biased thought like 'I got unlucky' and encouraged Duke to find things she might have control over and how to improve decisions about those. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1260-L1265]
