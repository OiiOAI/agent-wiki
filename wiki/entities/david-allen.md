# David Allen

## Summary

David Allen is a productivity consultant and author known for developing the Getting Things Done (GTD) methodology. He has over 35 years of experience as a management consultant and executive coach, and serves as chairman of the David Allen Company. His work has been featured in major publications and he conducts workshops internationally.

## Key facts

- Described as one of the world's most influential thinkers on productivity. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Has more than thirty-five years of experience as a management consultant and executive coach. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Serves as chairman of the David Allen Company. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Has conducted workshops for individuals and organizations across the United States. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Has been a keynote speaker for organizations including New York Life, the World Bank, the Ford Foundation, L.L. Bean, and the U.S. Navy. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Has been featured in Fast Company, Fortune, the Los Angeles Times, the New York Times, and the Wall Street Journal. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- His book Getting Things Done has been published in more than thirty foreign countries. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Resides in Amsterdam, Netherlands. [raw/books/productivity/Getting Things Done_ The Art of Stress - Fre - Allen, David.pdf#p2]
- Allen developed GTD through trial and error while working with executives at Lockheed and other corporations. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p75]
- He describes his goal as achieving a 'mind like water'—responding appropriately to inputs then returning to calm. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p75]
- The GTD system emphasizes identifying the specific 'next action' for each task, not vague to-do items. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p77]
- His Two-Minute Rule states that if something takes less than two minutes, do it immediately rather than adding it to a list. [raw/books/psychology/Willpower _ Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength - Roy F_ Baumeister, John Tierney.pdf#p82]
