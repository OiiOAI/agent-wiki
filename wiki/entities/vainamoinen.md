# Väinämöinen

## Summary

The immortal bard of the Finnish Kalevala, born of Ilmatar (daughter of the air) who descended into the primeval sea. Campbell cites this myth to illustrate the Virgin Birth concept and the cosmic waters as womb. Väinämöinen remained in his mother's body for thirty summers and thirty winters, then emerged by his own act and underwent the ordeal of the elemental ocean for five more years before reaching shore.

## Key facts

- Väinämöinen was born of Ilmatar, the virgin daughter of the air, who descended from sky mansions into the primeval sea and floated there for seven centuries. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p233]
- Väinämöinen remained in his mother's body for thirty summers and thirty winters before opening the bony gateway with his finger and toes and falling headlong into the water. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p234]
- Before reaching shore Väinämöinen underwent five years of ordeal in the cosmic ocean, planting his knees on land but resting his arms on the sea, until he could finally view the moon and sun. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p235]
