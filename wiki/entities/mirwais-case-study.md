# Mirwais

## Summary

Mirwais was at the Al-Noor mosque during the Christchurch mosque shootings. A bullet scraped the side of his skull, with millimeters making the difference between life and death. He suffered from depressed mood, fitful sleep, flashbacks, anger, impatience, and fear. Within days of taking multinutrients, his sleep improved. After a month, he was sleeping well without sleeping pills, his mood was more stable, flashbacks became infrequent, and anger and patience thresholds were back to normal.

- Before treatment, Mirwais described waking suddenly, sweating, with flashbacks to the event, with a couple of hours of sleep being a good night. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p172]
