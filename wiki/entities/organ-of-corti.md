# Organ of Corti

## Summary

The organ of Corti is the receptor organ of the inner ear, containing approximately 16,000 hair cells arrayed along the basilar membrane. It includes a single row of approximately 3,500 inner hair cells and approximately 12,000 outer hair cells in three rows. The organ is covered by the tectorial membrane and is the site of mechanoelectrical transduction where sound vibrations are converted into electrical signals.
