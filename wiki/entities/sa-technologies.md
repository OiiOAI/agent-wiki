# SA Technologies

## Summary

SA Technologies is a company in Marietta, Georgia, founded by Dr. Mica R. Endsley. The company specializes in the design, development, and evaluation of systems to support situation awareness and decision-making for aviation, air traffic control, medical, power, and military operations.
