# Tiago Forte

## Summary

Tiago Forte is the author of 'Building a Second Brain' and founder of Forte Labs, which offers online courses teaching personal knowledge management. Based in San Francisco, he developed his Second Brain system after experiencing a chronic functional voice disorder that led him to document and organize all information related to his health treatment. His online course has produced thousands of graduates from over 100 countries. His work has been featured in Harvard Business Review, The Atlantic, Fast Company, and Inc.

## Key facts

- Tiago Forte experienced a chronic functional voice disorder during his twenties that caused severe pain and required strong medication with side effects including memory loss. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p14-15]
