# William Ross Ashby

## Summary

William Ross Ashby (1904-1972) was a British psychiatrist and pioneer of cybernetics. He developed the concept of essential variables and co-authored the Good Regulator Theorem with Roger Conant in 1970. His earlier Law of Requisite Variety states that a successful control system must be capable of entering at least as many states as the environment perturbing it.

## Key facts

- William Ross Ashby co-authored the 'Good Regulator Theorem' with Roger Conant in 1970. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p175]
- Ashby introduced the concept of essential variables—physiological quantities that must be kept within strict limits for an organism to remain alive. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p177]
- Ashby's earlier Law of Requisite Variety states that 'only variety can force down variety'—a successful control system must be capable of entering at least as many states as the environment. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p213]
