# Marc Brackett

## Summary

Marc Brackett is a professor at the Yale Child Study Center and the founding director of the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence. He spent his childhood struggling with bullying, abuse, and family dysfunction, experiences that shaped his lifelong commitment to understanding and teaching emotion skills. His uncle Marvin's simple question 'how are you feeling?' became the catalyst for his career. He developed the RULER approach to social and emotional learning, now used in thousands of schools worldwide.
