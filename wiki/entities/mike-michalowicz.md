# Mike Michalowicz

## Summary

Mike Michalowicz is the author of Profit First and several other business books including The Toilet Paper Entrepreneur and The Pumpkin Plan. He built and sold two companies before experiencing a financial collapse that left him with only $10,000. His daughter's piggy bank gift on Valentine's Day 2008 inspired the core concept of Profit First. He is a professional speaker and entrepreneur who created the Profit First system to address the universal small business struggle with profitability.

## Key facts

- Michalowicz received a $388,000 check from selling his second company, a computer forensic investigations business. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p25]
- He lost nearly his entire fortune within a couple of years through poor investments in multiple startups. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p27]
- By February 2008, he was down to his last $10,000 and facing $28,000 in unexpected tax bills. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p27-28]
- His nine-year-old daughter Adayla gave him her piggy bank on Valentine's Day 2008 with the words 'Daddy, we're going to make it'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p29]
- He first published Profit First in 2014 and claims his newest company Profit First Professionals is 1,000% more profitable than the best year of any prior company. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p21]

- Inference: Michalowicz's personal financial collapse and recovery directly inspired the creation of the Profit First system, making it a solution born from firsthand experience with entrepreneurial poverty
