# Center for Neurobiology and Behavior at Columbia

## Summary

The Center for Neurobiology and Behavior at Columbia University's College of Physicians and Surgeons was where Kandel conducted much of his later research on the molecular biology of memory. Supported by the Howard Hughes Medical Institute, the center brought together researchers including Richard Axel, Tom Jessell, and Steven Siegelbaum.

## Key facts

- Kandel moved to Columbia in 1974 to replace his mentor Harry Grundfest who was retiring. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p259]
- In 1983, the Howard Hughes Medical Institute started three initiatives including neural science, with Kandel as senior investigator. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p263]
- The HHMI group recruited Tom Jessell and Gary Struhl from Harvard and Steven Siegelbaum to the center. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p264]
- Richard Axel collaborated with Kandel on applying molecular biology to studying behavior. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p260]
