# Mary E. Boone

## Summary

Mary E. Boone is president of Boone Associates in Connecticut, a strategic communication and leadership consultant. She co-authored the Harvard Business Review cover article 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making' with Dave Snowden, receiving an Academy of Management award and an Emerald Citation of Excellence Award.

## Key facts

- Mary E. Boone met Dave Snowden in 2000 during the early development of the Cynefin Framework while working on her book Managing Interactively with IBM funding. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p137]
- Tom Stewart, then Editor of Harvard Business Review, matched Boone's pragmatic approach with Snowden's academic approach for an HBR article. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p137]
- The HBR article 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making' took five years to complete, from initial call in 2002 to publication in November 2007. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p139]
- The original manuscript was 6,167 words and the final edited version was 5,042 words. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p139]
- Boone first suggested changing the name of the 'simple' domain to 'clear' during the initial writing meeting, though this change didn't occur for 20 years. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p138]
