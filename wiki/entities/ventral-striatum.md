# Ventral Striatum

## Summary

The ventral striatum, comprising the nucleus accumbens and adjacent ventral putamen, is a major target of dopaminergic projections from the VTA and is critically involved in encoding reward-related signals including state values, prediction errors, and Pavlovian values. fMRI studies consistently show prediction-error correlates in this region during reward learning tasks.

## Key facts

- First fMRI studies searching for prediction errors in humans implicated the nucleus accumbens and orbitofrontal cortex, both major dopaminergic targets. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p329]
- O'Doherty et al. (2003) and McClure et al. (2003b) used temporal-difference model analysis to identify neural correlates of prediction errors in the nucleus accumbens (ventral striatum) and putamen (dorsal striatum). [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p329]
- Ventral striatum shows prediction-error correlates in both Pavlovian and instrumental learning tasks, while dorsal striatum shows them only in instrumental tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p329]
- Ventral striatum activity is correlated with SV under essentially all reward and choice conditions studied. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p496]
- Ventral striatum encodes rewards and punishments, cumulative reward magnitude, anticipation and expectation of reward, and behavioral preference rankings. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p496]
- Activity in ventral striatum reflects the sum of activity passing through synapses from inferior frontal sulcus, insula, amygdala, posterior cingulate, superior temporal sulcus, caudate, putamen, and dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p497]
- The ventral striatum is proposed to be the locus of reward prediction encoding used in learning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p497]

## Inferences

- Inference: The ventral striatum appears to serve as a final common representation of SV for use by the choice mechanism.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: A definitive study ruling out that ventral striatum encodes only reward-prediction errors and not SV per se has not yet been conducted.
- Uncertain: Baseline spike rate as the reference point for SV's reference-dependent encoding requires further investigation.
