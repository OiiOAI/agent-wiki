# Kevin Feige

## Summary

Kevin Feige is president of Marvel Studios who identified the crux of Marvel's challenge as making characters beyond Spider-Man and X-Men valuable. He devised the Marvel Cinematic Universe strategy, creating shared fictional universes that generated value through multiple films, television series, toys, games, and theme park attractions.

## Key facts

- After Spider-Man was licensed to Sony and X-Men to Fox for low fees, Feige identified making remaining characters valuable as the crux. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p43]
- Feige's solution was having all Marvel characters inhabit the same fictional universe, raising money to create an independent studio. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p43]
- The first film Iron Man was followed by twenty-eight more feature films plus eleven television series. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p43]
- Marvel was acquired by Disney in 2009 and Disney continues to develop the Marvel Cinematic Universe. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p43]
