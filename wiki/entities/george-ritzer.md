# George Ritzer

## Summary

George Ritzer is Distinguished University Professor at the University of Maryland and a leading figure in sociological theory, known particularly for his concept of McDonaldization and his metatheoretical work.

## Key facts

- George Ritzer is Distinguished University Professor at the University of Maryland. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p8]
