# Muchukunda

## Summary

Ancient Hindu warrior-king born from his father's side after a miraculous fertility potion mishap. After helping the gods defeat demons, he requested eternal sleep as his boon. He slept through eons in a mountain cave until awakened by Krishna, whose enemy he incinerated with one glance from his awakening eyes.

## Key facts

- Muchukunda was born from his father's left side after the father accidentally swallowed a fertility potion meant for his wife. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p151]
- After helping the gods win their battle against demons, Muchukunda requested sleep without end as his boon, with any person waking him to be burned by his eye-glance. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p152]
- Muchukunda slumbered through revolving eons of creation, world history, and dissolution while Krishna incarnate lured an enemy king into the cave. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p152]
- Upon awakening, Muchukunda recognized Krishna as an incarnation of God, bowed before his Savior, and then retreated to mountains to pursue final liberation. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p153]
