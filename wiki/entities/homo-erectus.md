# Homo erectus

## Summary

Homo erectus was a highly successful hominid species spanning 1.5 million years with a relatively stable stone tool technology across diverse habitats. They achieved brain sizes matching those of modern humans and almost certainly possessed symbolic communication. Their tool technology required little modification to succeed in different climates because what cuts flesh works regardless of habitat.

## Key facts

- Homo erectus can be traced through a fossil record spanning 1.5 million years with a relatively stable array of tool types and manufacture techniques. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p398]
- Homo erectus achieved a highly successful adaptation and was almost certainly supported by a complex symbolic culture organizing reproduction, work, and social negotiations. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p398]
- Their brains and their symbolic forms of communication were undoubtedly co-evolving together even if the tools they were using were not progressing at a comparable pace. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p399]
- The reduction of sexual dimorphism in Homo erectus supports the identification of this species with a shift from polygyny to mostly pair bonding. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p424]

## Inferences

- Inference: Reports of erectus' mental stagnation are likely exaggerated given the evidence of brain expansion, diverse habitats conquered, and use of fire in later stages.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Whether erectus' symbolic communication was vocal, gestural, or some combination.
