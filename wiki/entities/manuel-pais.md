# Manuel Pais

## Summary

Manuel Pais is a co-author of 'Team Topologies' and an expert in DevOps, continuous delivery, and team organization. Together with Matthew Skelton, he developed the framework for structuring teams to enable fast flow in technology organizations.

## Key facts

- Manuel Pais is co-author of 'Team Topologies'. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- Manuel Pais co-created the DevOps Topologies models that preceded the book. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L1-L113]
- Manuel Pais is co-author of the Team Topologies book. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L424]
- Manuel interviewed Matthew Skelton at QCon London 2015, resulting in the article 'How Different Team Topologies Influence DevOps Culture' published by InfoQ. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L449-L450]
- Later in 2015, Manuel helped expand the DevOps Topology patterns with contributions from the community. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L451]
- Manuel Pais is an independent DevOps and Continuous Delivery consultant. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He focuses on team design, practices, and flow. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He helps organizations define and adopt DevOps and Continuous Delivery from both technical and human perspectives via strategic assessments, practical workshops, and coaching. [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
- He co-authored 'Team Guide to Software Releasability' (2018). [raw/books/business/Team_Topologies.epub#L3713-L5220]
