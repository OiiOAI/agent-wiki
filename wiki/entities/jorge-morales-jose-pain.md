# Jorge Morales and José Pain

## Summary

Jorge Morales and José Pain are the founders of Specialized ECU Repair, which unofficially stands as the first business owners to implement Profit First after reading 'The Toilet Paper Entrepreneur.' Their business grew from a two-person operation to over $1 million in annual revenue through systematic application of Profit First principles, allowing them to take dream vacations and live the lifestyle they envisioned.

## Key facts

- Jorge and José started Specialized ECU Repair in 2007 with a dream of enjoying profit and working less. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p106]
- They paid for their trip to a Profit First meetup in Newark, New Jersey from their PROFIT account. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p106]
- By 2013, they surpassed their accountant's revenue projections and were on track to break $1 million in annual revenue within two years. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p107]
- By 2018, they had six employees, paid well above industry average, and had increased their profit percentage to 9 percent. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p107]
- Jorge said: 'Profit First is a way of life. For all of life.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p129]
- They took dream vacations to Bermuda, Europe, Central America, and Australia, gifted to their loved ones. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p126]
