# Howard Schultz

## Summary

Founder and chairman of Starbucks, presented as an example of demonstrating caring behavior that inspires trust. After three Starbucks employees were murdered during a robbery, Schultz chartered a plane to Washington D.C., spent a week there, worked with police, consoled victims' families, attended funerals, and announced future profits from that store would go to victims' rights organizations. His behavior demonstrated care and concern that inspired trust among all employees.

## Key facts

- Howard Schultz immediately chartered a plane to Washington D.C. after three employees were murdered. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p111]
- He devoted all future profits from that store to organizations working for victims' rights and violence prevention. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p111]
- By showing profound concern for the few, he taught an entire company, resulting in higher-quality, more caring employees. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p112]
