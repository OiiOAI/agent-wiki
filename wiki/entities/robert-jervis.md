# Robert Jervis

## Summary

Robert Jervis is a political scientist who promotes the non-linear systems perspective in analyzing international relations and political life. His work System Effects examines how complex interactions and interdependencies shape political and social outcomes. Jervis notes that international history is full of inter-connections and that very little in social and political life makes sense except in the light of systemic processes.

## Key facts

- Jervis promotes the non-linear systems perspective, noting that international history is full of inter-connections and complex interactions and that very little in social and political life makes sense except in the light of systemic processes. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p166]
- Jervis identifies four characteristics of non-linear complex systems: it is impossible to do merely one thing; results cannot be predicted from separate actions; strategies depend on the strategies of others; and behavior changes the environment. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p166]
- Jervis concludes his book with three suggestions for strategies that may overcome or take into account the problems of non-linear systems: constraining the opponent's options, understanding the non-linearity of the environment, and aiming for indirect effects with multiple strategies. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p168]
- Jervis provides the example of the Allied Combined Bomber Offensive during WWII, where industry diverted rail traffic after bombing, creating bottlenecks that subsequently paralyzed the system. [raw/books/strategy/ScienceStrategyWar_Osinga.pdf#p166]
