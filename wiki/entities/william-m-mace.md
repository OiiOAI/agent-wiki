# William M. Mace

## Summary

William M. Mace wrote the Introduction to the Classic Edition (2014) of Gibson's 1979 book. He was a colleague at Trinity College and a significant figure in the ecological psychology community. Mace's introduction provides detailed intellectual history of Gibson's work and its subsequent influence across multiple disciplines.

## Key facts

- Mace was at Trinity College and was one of Gibson's colleagues who read and criticized portions of the manuscript. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p13]
- The introduction to the classic edition was written in 2014, noting the book had been available for 35 years. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p18]
- Mace noted that Chapter 8 'The Theory of Affordances' went through multiple revisions including 'purple perils' and a 1977 conference paper. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Ecological Approach to Visual Perception_ Classic - James J_ Gibson.pdf#p19]
