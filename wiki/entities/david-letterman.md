# David Letterman

## Summary

David Letterman is a talk show host who serves as an example of truthseeking gone wrong. During a 2008 interview with Lauren Conrad, he perceptively suggested that Conrad might be the source of her own drama, violating the assumed social contract by challenging her to bet on her outcome fielding when she hadn't agreed to truthseeking. His experience illustrates that truthseeking requires mutual agreement to be effective.

- In October 2008, David Letterman asked Lauren Conrad, 'That raises the question, maybe you're the problem, do you think?' during an interview, suggesting she might be the source of her own drama. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1094-L1105]
- Letterman realized he had taken the conversation into much deeper, more serious territory than either of them could have anticipated, and tried to soften the blow by sharing his own similar experience. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1105-L1115]
