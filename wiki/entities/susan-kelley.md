# Susan Kelley (Pediatric Nurse)

## Summary

Susan Kelley was a pediatric nurse who conducted interviews with children from Fells Acres daycare center. Her interviewing techniques—including never beginning with open-ended questions, asking specific questions, repeating questions, and holding out promises and bribes—were later criticized by memory researchers as highly suggestive and likely to produce false reports.

## Key facts

- Susan Kelley conducted interviews with Fells Acres children, never beginning by asking open-ended questions such as 'What happened?' but proceeding directly to specific questions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p175]
- Kelley frequently repeated specific questions, seeming to refuse to take no for an answer, and held out promises and bribes in exchange for testimony. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p175]
- With repeated interviews when initial interviews failed to yield satisfactory answers, negative responses were eventually replaced by positive ones. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p176]
