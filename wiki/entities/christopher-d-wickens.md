# Christopher D. Wickens

## Summary

Christopher D. Wickens is a professor of experimental psychology at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, specializing in human attention, perception, and cognition as applied to operator performance in complex environments. He holds multiple awards and fellowships in human factors.

## Key facts

- He is a professor of experimental psychology, head of the Aviation Research Laboratory, and associate director of the Institute of Aviation at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- He received his PhD from the University of Michigan. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- His research involves application of principles of human attention, perception, and cognition to modeling operator performance in complex environments. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- He received the Jerome H. Ely Award in 1981 and the Paul M. Fitts Award in 1985 from the Human Factors Society. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- He chaired a National Research Council Panel to examine human factors of automation of the air traffic control system from 1995 to 1998. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- He is a Fellow of the Human Factors Society, elected to the Society of Experimental Psychologists, and Fellow of the American Psychological Association. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p19]
- Christopher D. Wickens is affiliated with the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, Institute of Aviation, Aviation Research Laboratory. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p225]
- Wickens conducted research examining optimal display formats for aviation control, navigation, and hazard awareness. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p225]
- Wickens identified four commonalities underlying SA preservation: dissociation between SA contents and responses, dissociation between rapidly evolving SA state and long-term memory, distinction between SA products and processes, and trade-offs between routine and global SA design. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p225]
- Wickens outlined four alternative solutions to the design trade-off: compromise display, dual display, sequential display, and automated system approach. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p231]
