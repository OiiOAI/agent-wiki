# Jack Morin

## Summary

Jack Morin is a sex therapist whose work Perel cites on the connection between childhood trauma and adult eroticism. Morin explains that the erotic imagination is ingenious in undoing, transforming, and redressing the traumas of the past. This means that experiences causing the most pain in childhood can become the greatest sources of pleasure later on. Morin also notes that fantasy rapists in women's erotic imaginings are notably nonviolent—violence is subverted by gentleness, allowing safe experience of 'healthy dominance and powerful surrender.'
