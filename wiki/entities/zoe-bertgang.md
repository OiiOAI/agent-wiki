# Zoe Bertgang

## Summary

A fictional character in Jensen's 'Gradiva' and the real woman behind Hanold's delusional love. She is portrayed as clever and clear-sighted, and her surname 'Bertgang' (meaning 'one who steps along brilliantly') becomes the key to understanding the delusion, as Hanold had unconsciously transformed it into 'Gradiva'. She undertakes a form of psychical treatment of Hanold's delusion by entering into and then dissolving his fantasy.

## Key facts

- Zoe Bertgang is Hanold's childhood friend who had been his playmate until archaeology took hold of him and he ceased to notice her. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2924]
- Zoe reveals that 'Bertgang' means the same as 'Gradiva' and describes someone 'who steps along brilliantly'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2929]
- She treated Hanold's delusion by first taking up the same ground as the delusional structure and then investigating it as completely as possible, without contradicting it.
