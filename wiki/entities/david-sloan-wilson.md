# David Sloan Wilson

## Summary

Evolutionary biologist at Binghamton University who has spent decades defending group selection. He merged Darwin's evolutionary theory with Durkheim's sociology in books including Darwin's Cathedral and This View of Evolution. Wilson argues that religions function as group-level adaptations, helping communities achieve cooperation impossible for individuals.

## Key facts

- Wilson spent thirty years proving that genetic group selection could occur under conditions present in earlier human societies. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p281]
- His great achievement was merging Darwin and Durkheim, showing how tribal morality could emerge by group selection. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p281]
- Wilson argues that 'religions exist primarily for people to achieve together what they cannot achieve on their own.'. [raw/books/philosophy/The Righteous Mind - Jonathan Haidt.pdf#p284]
