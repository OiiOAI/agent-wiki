# Michael Bader

## Summary

Michael Bader is a therapist and author of the book Arousal, which Perel cites extensively. He links the concept of selfishness to sexual ruthlessness—defined as the quality enabling a person to surrender to their own rhythms of pleasure without guilt or shame. His work emphasizes the importance of differentiation in sexual experience: the capacity to hold on to oneself in the presence of another. Bader's analysis of fantasy as a sanctuary of the erotic mind informs Perel's therapeutic approach to helping individuals access desire within intimate relationships.
