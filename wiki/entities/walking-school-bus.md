# Walking School Bus

## Summary

The Walking School Bus is a community initiative endorsed by the U.S. Department of Transportation that organizes groups of children to walk to school together under adult supervision. The Starretts implemented this program for their daughters' elementary school, growing participation from 10-15 children to 40-50 children over eight years.

## Key facts

- The Walking School Bus is endorsed by the U.S. Department of Transportation. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1218-L1221]
- The program involves adults supervising groups of children walking to school. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1218-L1221]
- The Starretts walked their daughters approximately 1.5 miles to school, accumulating about 5,000 steps by 8:30 a.m. round-trip. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1234-L1239]
- The program grew from 10-15 children initially to 40-50 children participating. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1234-L1239]
- The website for the Walking School Bus is walkingschoolbus.org. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1218-L1221]
