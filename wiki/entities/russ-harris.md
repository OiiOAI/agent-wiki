# Russ Harris

## Summary

Russ Harris is an Australian medical doctor, psychotherapist, and author specializing in Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). He is the author of The Happiness Trap book series, including The Confidence Gap and ACT with Love. Harris presents workshops and trainings on ACT worldwide to both health professionals and lay audiences.
