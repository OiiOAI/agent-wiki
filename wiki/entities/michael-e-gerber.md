# Michael E. Gerber

## Summary

Michael E. Gerber is the author of 'The E-Myth Revisited' and founder of E-Myth Worldwide (1977). With over 24 years of experience working with small business owners, Gerber developed the Business Development Process and the Entrepreneurial Perspective framework. His work emphasizes that most small businesses fail because their owners confuse technical expertise with business acumen.

## Key facts

- Gerber founded E-Myth Worldwide in 1977, eight years before The E-Myth was published. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p2]
- Gerber has spent 15 years before and 15 years since The E-Myth's publication developing and refining his point of view through E-Myth Worldwide. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p2]
- Gerber has traveled throughout the world speaking to hundreds of thousands of small business owners. [raw/books/business/The E - myth revisited _ why most small businesses don't work - Michael E_ Gerber.pdf#p2]
