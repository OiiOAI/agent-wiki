# Andrew Reece

## Summary

Andrew Reece was Langer's PhD student whose dissertation designed a study to identify predictive markers of mental illness in social media data from Twitter and Instagram, using color analysis, face detection, semantic analysis, and natural language processing.

## Key facts

- Andrew Reece's PhD thesis scanned 279,951 Twitter posts and 43,950 Instagram posts using color analysis, face detection, semantic analysis, and natural language processing to identify features that might help predict depression. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p183]
- Reece's model was able to differentiate between healthy and depressed individuals and was as good or better than general practitioners' ability to make successful category-based diagnoses. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p184]
- Using Twitter data, depression could be diagnosed several months prior to a clinician's diagnosis. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Body_ Thinking Our Way to Chro - Ellen J. Langer.pdf#p184]

## Inferences

- Inference: Early screening and detection of mental illness through social media analysis could drastically reduce suffering and potentially make hospital care unnecessary.
