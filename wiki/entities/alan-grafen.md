# Alan Grafen

## Summary

Alan Grafen is an evolutionary biologist who formalized Amotz Zahavi's handicap principle mathematically. His work showed that honest signaling can evolve as an evolutionarily stable strategy under plausible conditions, resolving earlier criticisms. The chunk also notes his work on kin selection, group selection, and digger wasp nesting strategies.

## Key facts

- Grafen was quoted as having 'the most annoying habit of always being right' when he translated Zahavi's verbal handicap principle into a mathematical model showing it works. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p363]
- Grafen's Strategic Choice Handicap interpretation assumes males have private information about their quality and choose advertising levels conditionally, while females evolve response rules; this yields an ESS with honest advertising. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p364]
- Grafen pointed out that workers may be indifferent between siblings and offspring at any population sex ratio because rare males have high reproductive value despite low relatedness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p372]
- Grafen's work on natural selection, kin selection, and group selection in Grafen (1984) is recommended as clear-thinking sorting out of the neo-group-selection problem. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Selfish Gene_ 40th Anniversary edition - Richard Dawkins.pdf#p352]
