# Donna Wagner

## Summary

A woman who helped organize Sunday meditations at Singer's property and eventually moved into Sandy Boone's cabin on his land. She became Singer's wife and the mother of their daughter, representing how life guided him from solitude into relationship despite his initial resistance.

## Key facts

- Donna Wagner was finishing her degree at the university when she began sitting in on Singer's Santa Fe classes. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p82]
- She had a deep understanding of what Singer was teaching and attended most classes and all Sunday services. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p82]
- She helped organize the Sunday group after Sandy left and eventually just stopped going home, moving into the cabin. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p82]
- Singer and Donna were destined to fall in love, get married, and have a beautiful daughter together. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p83]
