# Leonard Energy Training

## Summary

Leonard Energy Training (LET) is a discipline created by George Leonard, inspired by his practice of aikido. Introduced since 1973 to approximately 50,000 people, LET uses the body as both a metaphor for how one deals with daily life problems and as a learning facility for changing those patterns, whether physical, mental, or emotional. The training is especially useful for those on the journey of mastery.

## Key facts

- LET was created by George Leonard and inspired by his practice of aikido. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p101]
- LET has been introduced to around 50,000 people since 1973. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p101]
- LET uses the body as a metaphor for how you deal with daily life problems and as a learning facility for changing those patterns. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p101]
- Core LET exercises include balancing and centering, returning to center, and gaining energy from unexpected blows. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p101-#p106]
- The balancing and centering procedure involves focusing attention on the abdomen, specifically a point an inch or two below the navel. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p102]
- To be psychologically balanced and centered depends to a great extent on being physically balanced and centered. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p102]

## Inferences

- Inference: The body-as-metaphor approach suggests that improving physical balance and centering can transfer to mental and emotional equilibrium in daily life.
