# Naoki Higashida

## Summary

Naoki Higashida is a thirteen-year-old boy with autism who wrote 'The Reason I Jump.' He notes his efforts to categorize by explaining: 'First, I scan my memory to find an experience closest to what's happening now. When I've found a good close match, my next step is to try to recall what I said the last time. If I'm lucky, I hit upon a usable experience and all is well.' This illustrates how, lacking a properly functioning conceptual system, Higashida has to work hard to do what other brains do automatically.
