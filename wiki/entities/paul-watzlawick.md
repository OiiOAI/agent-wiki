# Paul Watzlawick

## Summary

A psychologist born in Villach, Austria in 1921, who became a central figure in bringing the interactional approach to psychotherapy. Originally trained as a Jungian, he shifted to communication-based perspectives. He was a research associate at the Mental Research Institute in Palo Alto and co-founded the Brief Therapy Center in 1966. Listed as the first author of 'Change,' he passed away in March 2007. His philosophical bent is evident throughout the book, though it was made more clinical by colleagues Weakland and Fisch.
