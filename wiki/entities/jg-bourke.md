# J.G. Bourke

## Summary

Captain John Gregory Bourke, a U.S. Army officer and ethnologist who wrote 'Scatalogic Rites of All Nations' (1891). Freud wrote a preface to the German translation (1913), praising Bourke's compilation of coprophilic practices across cultures.
