# Gaspar Jekely

## Summary

A researcher who proposed the hypothesis that neurons first arose to improve the efficiency of sensory-motor integration during the transition from sponges to Cnidaria, based on studies of cilia-driven swimming in sponge larvae.

## Key facts

- Jekely proposed that neurons first arose to improve the efficiency of sensory-motor integration during the transition from sponges to Cnidaria. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p136]
- According to Jekely's hypothesis, the solution to inefficient chemical communication between distant body parts was rapid electrical communication within extensions (axons), with slow chemical communication across the short synaptic cleft. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p137]
