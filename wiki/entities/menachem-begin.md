# Menachem Begin

## Summary

Menachem Begin was an Israeli politician who became Prime Minister of Israel and was a surprising architect of the 1978 Camp David Peace Accords. Earlier, he led the Irgun paramilitary group, which carried out bombings including the King David Hotel attack. He later redefined his group's activities as anti-terrorism rather than terrorism.

- Menachem Begin was one of the surprising architects of the Camp David Peace Accords in 1978 as Prime Minister of Israel. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p862]
- In the 1940s Begin headed the Irgun, which carried out bombings including an attack on the King David Hotel that killed British officials and scores of Arab and Jewish civilians.
