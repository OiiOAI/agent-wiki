# Michael W. Taft

## Summary

An American meditation teacher and author with over 30 years of experience in Buddhist and Hindu meditation traditions. He serves as a senior facilitator in Shinzen Young's Basic Mindfulness system and teaches meditation at major technology companies including Google.

## Key facts

- Taft began meditating in the late 1970s as a teenager to address anxiety attacks. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- In the 1990s, he worked as editorial director for Sounds True, a publisher specializing in spiritual and psychological teaching programs. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- He helped create Shinzen Young's program 'The Science of Enlightenment.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- Taft is a senior facilitator in the Basic Mindfulness system. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- He developed the Being Human project with Peter Baumann, working with researchers including Richie Davidson, Judson Brewer, and David Eagleman. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p10]
