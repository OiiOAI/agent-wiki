# Baubo

## Summary

Baubo is a figure from Greek mythology associated with the Eleusinian mysteries. According to the legend, when Demeter arrived in Eleusis grieving for her abducted daughter Persephone and refused food or drink, Baubo entertained her by suddenly lifting her dress and exposing her body, making her laugh. Terracotta representations found during excavations at Priene in Asia Minor show Baubo as a body without a head or chest, with a face drawn on the abdomen.
