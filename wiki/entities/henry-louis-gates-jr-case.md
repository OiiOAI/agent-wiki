# Henry Louis Gates Jr. Case

## Summary

Harvard professor Henry Louis Gates Jr. was arrested on July 16, 2009, after a neighbor called 911 reporting a man trying to force open a door. Gates was actually trying to enter his own home, which had become stuck. This case illustrates affective realism: the neighbor's concepts about crime and skin color led her to perceive criminal intent where none existed.

## Key facts

- Gates was trying to force open the front door of his own home, which had become stuck while he was traveling. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p257]
- A neighbor called 911 based on affective realism; she had an affective feeling based on her concepts about crime and skin color, making a mental inference that Gates intended to commit a crime. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p257]
- Police arrived and arrested Gates, who was simply trying to enter his own home. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p257]
