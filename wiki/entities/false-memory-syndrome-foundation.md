# False Memory Syndrome Foundation

## Summary

The False Memory Syndrome Foundation was established in 1992 by parents, mainly those in conflicts with adult daughters who had recovered memories of childhood sexual abuse during therapy. The organization challenged the validity of recovered memories and advocated for accused individuals. It was dissolved on December 31, 2019, when the need for the organization had 'diminished dramatically.'

## Key facts

- The False Memory Syndrome Foundation was formed in 1992 by alarmed middle-aged adults, mainly parents in conflicts with adult daughters who had recovered memories of childhood sexual abuse during psychotherapy. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p166]
- The foundation's director, Pamela Freyd, reported in Winter 1999 that the foundation 'now receives dramatically fewer calls and letters from people asking for assistance.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p173]
- The False Memory Syndrome Foundation was dissolved on December 31, 2019 because 'the need for the FMS Foundation has diminished dramatically over the years.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p185]
