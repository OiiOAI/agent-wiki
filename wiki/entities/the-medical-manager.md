# The Medical Manager

## Summary

The Medical Manager was a medical practice management software system written by Michael Singer starting in 1980. It became the leading medical billing and practice management software in the United States, eventually being installed in 25% of all independent physicians' offices. The software revolutionized medical industry computerization and was recognized by the Smithsonian Institution in 2000 for its accomplishments in computerizing the medical industry.

## Key facts

- Michael Singer began writing The Medical Manager in early 1980 after being asked by two prospective clients who wanted a medical billing system. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p122]
- The first version of the software was completed after two years of development and installed for the original two clients in early 1982. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p127]
- Systems Plus, a Silicon Valley software company, agreed to distribute The Medical Manager after the president flew to Florida to review the product. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p129]
- The Medical Manager was launched at COMDEX in Las Vegas in November 1982. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p130]
- By 1987, The Medical Manager was the first practice management system in the country able to submit claims electronically in all 50 states. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p134]
- In 2000, The Medical Manager was installed in the permanent archives of the Smithsonian Institution. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p134]
- The software was eventually installed in 25% of all independent physicians' offices across the United States. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p158]
