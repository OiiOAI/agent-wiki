# David R. Mandel

## Summary

David R. Mandel is an adjunct professor of psychology at the University of Toronto and a senior defence scientist at DRDC Toronto, where he heads the Thinking, Risk, and Intelligence Group. He is co-editor of this volume and author of Chapter 2 on framing and content effects on decision making.

## Key facts

- David R. Mandel is an adjunct professor of psychology at the University of Toronto and senior defence scientist at DRDC Toronto, where he heads the Thinking, Risk, and Intelligence Group. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p4]
- His research interests include thinking and reasoning, judgment and decision making, and the application of behavioral science to issues in defence and security. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p4]
- He is the lead editor of The Psychology of Counterfactual Thinking published by Routledge/Taylor & Francis. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p4]
- Mandel and Vartanian co-authored Chapter 2 on frames, brains, and content domains, examining neural and behavioral effects of descriptive context on preferential choice. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p8]
