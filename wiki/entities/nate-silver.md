# Nate Silver

## Summary

Nate Silver is a statistician and founder of FiveThirtyEight, a data journalism website focused on political polling and forecasting. In Thinking in Bets, Silver is discussed in the context of the 2016 presidential election, where FiveThirtyEight gave Hillary Clinton approximately 60%–70% probability of winning. When Donald Trump won despite being the underdog, Silver faced significant criticism despite his probabilistic forecast correctly reflecting the substantial uncertainty involved.

## Key facts

- Nate Silver founded FiveThirtyEight, a website that updated polling and forecasting data on elections in real time. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- FiveThirtyEight gave approximately 60%–70% probability for a Clinton victory in the 2016 presidential election. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- After Trump's victory, searching 'Nate Silver got it wrong election' yielded 465,000 Google results. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
- Silver's case illustrates the public confusion about interpreting probabilistic predictions when the underdog wins. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1909-L1939]
