# Federal Express

## Summary

Company whose early struggles with night shift performance illustrated the power of incentive structures. Federal Express tried moral suasion without success until changing from hourly pay to per-shift pay, letting employees go home when all planes were loaded. This simple incentive change solved the problem.

## Key facts

- Federal Express required all packages to be shifted rapidly among airplanes in one central airport each night for system integrity. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p473]
- Federal Express tried moral suasion and 'everything in the world' before discovering that paying per shift instead of hourly solved the night shift performance problem. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p473]
