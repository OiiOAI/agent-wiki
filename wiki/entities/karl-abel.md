# Karl Abel

## Summary

German philologist whose 1884 pamphlet on the antithetical meaning of primal words in ancient Egyptian influenced Freud's theories on dream-work. Abel demonstrated that Egyptian and other ancient languages contained words with opposite meanings, and that concepts could only be formed through opposition.

## Key facts

- Karl Abel published 'Der gegensinnige Urworte' in 1884, demonstrating that Egyptian words could mean both a thing and its opposite. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3588]
- Abel showed that concepts are formed through comparison with their contraries, explaining why the oldest roots contain antithetical meanings. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3590]
- Freud credited Abel's work with explaining the dream-work's tendency to disregard negation and represent contraries indistinguishably. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p3588]
