# GABA-A Receptor

## Summary

GABA-A receptors are ionotropic chloride channels activated by γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA), mediating fast inhibitory synaptic transmission throughout the central nervous system.

## Key facts

- GABA-A receptors are pentameric ligand-gated chloride channels, part of the cys-loop receptor family that includes nicotinic ACh and glycine receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p278]
- Activation of GABA-A receptors increases Cl- conductance, producing hyperpolarization or shunting inhibition depending on the Cl- equilibrium potential relative to resting potential. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p287]
- GABA is the major inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brain and spinal cord, acting on both ionotropic GABA-A and metabotropic GABA-B receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p277]
- GABA_A receptors are pentamers usually composed of two α-, two β-, and one γ- or δ-subunit. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p335]
- Two molecules of GABA bind in clefts formed between the two α- and β-subunits to open the channel. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p335]
- GABA binds to a cleft between the α- and β-subunits, whereas benzodiazepines bind to a cleft between the α- and γ-subunits. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p335]
- Benzodiazepines (and barbiturates) bind more strongly to the receptor when GABA is also bound, stabilizing the channel in the open state. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p335]
- There are six types of GABA_A α-subunits (α1–α6), three β-subunits (β1–β3), three γ-subunits (γ1–γ3), and one δ-subunit. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
- Zolpidem binds selectively to GABA_A receptors containing the α1-subunit. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
- The anxiolytic effect of benzodiazepines requires binding to the α2- and γ-subunits. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
- GABA_A receptors are targets for general anesthetics, benzodiazepines, barbiturates, and alcohol. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
- Nonsense mutations resulting in truncations of GABA_A receptor α- and γ-subunits have been implicated in congenital forms of epilepsy. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p336]
