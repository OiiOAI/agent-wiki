# Ko Hung

## Summary

Chinese philosopher who spent the latter years of a long life preparing pills of immortality from cinnabar and honey, claiming they would turn white hair black, restore decayed teeth, and eventually grant eternal life. He reportedly vanished into the realm of the immortals.

## Key facts

- Ko Hung prescribed three pounds of genuine cinnabar mixed with one pound of white honey, dried in sun and roasted into pills the size of hemp seeds. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p146]
- According to Ko Hung, taking the immortality medicine for a year would turn white hair black, restore decayed teeth, and make the body sleek and glistening. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p146]
- When a friend visited Ko Hung, he found only the old man's empty clothes; Ko Hung had passed into the realm of the immortals. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p146]
