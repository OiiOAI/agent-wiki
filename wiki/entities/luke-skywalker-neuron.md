# Luke Skywalker neuron

## Summary

A neuron in the entorhinal cortex that responded to multiple photographs of Luke Skywalker and to his name, both written and spoken. Notably, this same neuron also responded to Yoda, another Star Wars character closely related to Luke Skywalker, demonstrating that neurons encode associations between concepts.

## Key facts

- A neuron in the entorhinal cortex responded to several photographs of Luke Skywalker and to the name Luke Skywalker, both written on the computer screen and spoken by a synthesized voice.
