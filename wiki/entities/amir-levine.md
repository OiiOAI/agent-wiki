# Amir Levine

## Summary

Dr. Amir Levine is a psychiatrist and co-author of the book 'Attached: Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Secure?' He worked part-time in the Therapeutic Nursery at Columbia University where he used attachment-guided therapy to help mothers create more secure bonds with their children. His work focuses on translating adult attachment research into practical guidance for romantic relationships.
