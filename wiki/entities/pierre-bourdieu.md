# Pierre Bourdieu

## Summary

Pierre Bourdieu (1930-2002) was a French sociologist and anthropologist known for developing constructivist structuralism (or genetic structuralism) as a framework to overcome the opposition between objectivism and subjectivism. His major concepts include habitus (internalized cognitive structures), field (a network of objective positions), and various forms of capital (economic, cultural, social, symbolic). His influential works include 'Distinction' (1984), which examined cultural taste, and 'Homo Academicus' (1984). Bourdieu's theory emphasizes practice as the mediating concept between structure and agency, and he introduced the concept of symbolic violence to describe how domination is reproduced through cultural mechanisms.
