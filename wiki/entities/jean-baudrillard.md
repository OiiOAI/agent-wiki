# Jean Baudrillard

## Summary

Jean Baudrillard is one of the most radical and extreme postmodern social theorists. Trained as a sociologist, his work moved from Marxian critique to analysis of simulations, simulacra, and hyperreality. He argues that the postmodern world is characterized by the implosion of signs and reality, where representations become more real than reality itself. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p527]

## Key facts

- Baudrillard was trained as a sociologist but his work left the confines of that discipline. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p527]
- He developed the concept of simulation leading to simulacra—reproductions where the distinction between signs and reality has imploded. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p528]
