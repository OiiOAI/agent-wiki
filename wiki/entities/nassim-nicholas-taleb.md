# Nassim Nicholas Taleb

## Summary

Lebanese-American essayist, scholar, and former trader specializing in risk management and probability theory. Author of the Incerto corpus including Fooled by Randomness, The Black Swan, and Antifragile. Taleb spent two decades as a financial trader before becoming a scholar, developing the concepts of antifragility, skin in the game, and the Triad framework for understanding volatility and uncertainty.

## Key facts

- Taleb spent two decades as a transactional trader and businessman in what he calls 'the strange profession'—working with volatility before turning to scholarship. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p34]
- Taleb only writes about things he has done personally; the risks he recommends to others are risks he has taken himself, and he will be the first to be hurt if wrong. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p31-32]
- Taleb grew up in Beirut, Lebanon, and witnessed the civil war and multiple destructions and rebuildings of the city, experiences that influenced his thinking about fragility and antifragility. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p54]
- The Incerto corpus consists of a trilogy plus philosophical and technical addenda, with Antifragile positioned as the main volume and The Black Swan as its junior appendix. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p31]
- Taleb developed personal physical fitness using maximum lift training under Lenny 'Cake,' a trainer who moonlighted as a security person, as a practical experiment in overcompensation and antifragility. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p70-71]
- Taleb has devoted his life to problems of uncertainty, probability, and knowledge. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
- He has led three careers centered on uncertainty: businessman-trader, philosophical essayist, and academic researcher. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
- He is currently Distinguished Professor of Risk Engineering at New York University's Polytechnic Institute. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
- His main subject matter is decision making under opacity—how to navigate a world we don't understand. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
- His books Fooled by Randomness and The Black Swan have been published in thirty-three languages. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
- Taleb explicitly rejects prizes, honorary degrees, awards, and ceremonialism, believing they debase knowledge by turning it into a spectator sport. [raw/books/strategy/Antifragile_ Things That Gain from Disorde - Taleb, Nassim Nicholas.pdf#p665]
