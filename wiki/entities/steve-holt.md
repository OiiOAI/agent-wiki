# Steve Holt

## Summary

Steve Holt is an expert on improvement methods at a large aerospace company. He was certified by the TOCICO and served on its Board. In 2007 he learned about Cynefin and began exploring how Theory of Constraints and Cynefin could be used together.

## Key facts

- Steve Holt discovered Theory of Constraints in 1997 and was later certified by TOCICO, serving on its Board. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p210]
- Holt gave the first presentation on Cynefin to the TOCICO annual conference in 2010 including Dr. Eli Goldratt. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p210]
- In 2019, Holt and Dave Snowden presented a workshop on using Cynefin and TOC together. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p210]
- Holt argues that TOC and Cynefin are aligned rather than adversarial, with TOC focusing on problem-solving while Cynefin provides sensemaking. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p221]

## Inferences

- Inference: Holt's analysis suggests that while TOC and Cynefin developed independently, they are complementary frameworks rather than competing alternatives.
