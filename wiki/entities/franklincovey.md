# FranklinCovey

## Summary

FranklinCovey is a global consulting and training company formed through the merger of Covey Leadership Center (founded by Stephen R. Covey) and Franklin Quest in 1997. Stephen M.R. Covey orchestrated the merger and served as CEO of the combined entity. The company specializes in leadership development and performance improvement training based on principle-centered approaches.

## Key facts

- The merger between Franklin Quest and Covey Leadership Center created FranklinCovey, which grew to $160 million in shareholder value under Stephen M.R. Covey's leadership. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p21]
- Covey describes the merger as presenting 'enormous challenges' in blending the two organizational cultures. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p31]
- The merged company held over a dozen four-hour 'dump' sessions where leaders listened without responding to consultant concerns about merger issues. [raw/books/business/The SPEED of Trust _ The One Thing that Ch - Covey, Stephen M.R.pdf#p32]
