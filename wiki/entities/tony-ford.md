# Tony Ford

## Summary

Tony Ford is a director of engineering at IGN Entertainment who served as the Five Whys master in the company's adoption of Lean Startup methods. He came to IGN through the acquisition of TeamXbox, where he served as lead software developer after building websites about video games in the late 1990s. His experience demonstrates both the challenges of implementing Five Whys without a narrow focus and the success that comes with a more experienced master keeping sessions on track.

## Key facts

- Tony Ford is a director of engineering at IGN Entertainment who was appointed as the Five Whys master. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p224]
- Tony got his start building websites about video games in the late 1990s and later served as lead software developer at TeamXbox before it was acquired by IGN in 2003. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p224]
- Tony's early Five Whys sessions failed because he started without picking a narrow problem area to focus on, leading to early setbacks and frustration. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p225]
- Tony stated that 'Having a Five Whys master is critical. Five Whys is easy in theory but difficult in practice, so you need someone who knows it well to shape the sessions for those who don't.'. [raw/books/business/The Lean Startup_ How Today's Entrepreneur - Eric Ries.pdf#p225]
