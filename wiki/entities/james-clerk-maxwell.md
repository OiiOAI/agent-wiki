# James Clerk Maxwell

## Summary

Nineteenth-century physicist famous for electromagnetic theory who published the foundational paper 'On Governors' (1867) analyzing steam engine control mechanisms. Maxwell mathematically demonstrated that tight control of engine speed paradoxically causes instability—light control works but close control leads to overreaction and crashes. Taleb uses this as scientific evidence that suppressing volatility creates fragility.

## Key facts

- Maxwell's 1867 paper 'On Governors' mathematically modeled steam engine speed control and showed that tightly controlling speed leads to instability—light control works, close control causes machinery to break into pieces.
