# Floyd Allport

## Summary

Floyd Allport was a twentieth-century American psychologist who wrote extensively on Darwin's ideas. In 1924, he made a sweeping inference from Darwin's writing that significantly changed the original meaning, claiming that expressions begin as vestigial in newborns but quickly assume function. This modification was inaccurate but supported the classical view of human nature and was eagerly adopted by like-minded scientists. Barrett identifies Allport as the person who 'hacked' Darwin's ideas and created a distortion that still affects emotion research today.
