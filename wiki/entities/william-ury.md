# William Ury

## Summary

William Ury is a consultant, writer, and lecturer on negotiation and mediation. He was Director of the Negotiation Network at Harvard University and Associate Director of the Harvard Negotiation Project. He has served as a consultant and third party in disputes ranging from the Palestinian-Israeli conflict to U.S.-Soviet arms control.

## Key facts

- William Ury is Director of the Negotiation Network at Harvard University and Associate Director of the Harvard Negotiation Project. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p2]
- He has served as a consultant and third party in disputes ranging from the Palestinian-Israeli conflict to U.S.-Soviet arms control to labor-management conflicts. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p2]
- He was educated in Switzerland and has degrees from Yale in Linguistics and Harvard in anthropology. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p2]
