# V. S. Ramachandran

## Summary

Vilayanur Subramanian Ramachandran is an Indian-American neurologist known for his pioneering work in neuroplasticity and phantom limb research. He directs the Center for Brain and Cognition at the University of California, San Diego, and is renowned for using simple, nineteenth-century-style equipment rather than high-tech devices to investigate brain function.

## Key facts

- V. S. Ramachandran was born in Madras, India, and is of Hindu background. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p184]
- He holds an M.D. degree and a Ph.D. in psychology from Trinity College, Cambridge. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p184]
- He directs the Center for Brain and Cognition at the University of California, San Diego. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p184]
- Ramachandran uses simple nineteenth-century machines in his office rather than high-tech equipment. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p184]
- He invented the mirror box therapy to treat phantom limb pain. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p192]
- He believes individual case studies have everything to contribute to science, famously using a pig-speaking analogy. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p185]
- His early research investigated patients with mental illusions, Capgras syndrome, optical illusions, and eye blind spots. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p186]
- He suspects brain 'sprouting' of new connections occurs when body parts are lost, with surviving brain maps releasing nerve growth factors. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p189]
- He developed the mirror box to allow patients to 'unlearn' phantom paralysis and pain through visual feedback. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p193]
- He has successfully eliminated phantom limbs in some patients using the mirror box, calling it 'the successful amputation of a phantom limb.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p194]
