# Jay W. Forrester

## Summary

Jay W. Forrester was the founder of the MIT System Dynamics group, where Donella Meadows was trained. He is a pioneer in systems thinking and computer modeling of complex systems. His work on feedback loops, delays, and system structure provided the theoretical foundation for the field that Meadows would later teach and write about.

## Key facts

- Jay Forrester is the founder of the MIT System Dynamics group. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking in Systems - Donella H. Meadows.pdf#p11]
- Meadows describes Forrester's work: 'Systems of information-feedback control are fundamental to all life and human endeavor.'. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking in Systems - Donella H. Meadows.pdf#p42]
