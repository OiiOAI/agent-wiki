# David Heinemeier Hansson

## Summary

David Heinemeier Hansson, known as DHH, is the co-founder of 37signals and co-creator of Ruby on Rails. He is one of the principals of the company who worked on Basecamp while in Denmark, creating a seven-hour time difference with the rest of the team. Along with Jason Fried, he co-authored Rework, presenting an alternative approach to business management that rejects conventional wisdom about growth, planning, and corporate structure.
