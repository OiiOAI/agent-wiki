# Rodney Garmanian

## Summary

A man hired by MacGuard security firm without any background check, who later murdered Teak Dyer. The case illustrates the catastrophic consequences of failing to conduct basic pre-employment screening.

## Key facts

- MacGuard gave Garmanian the uniform he used to lure an 18-year-old girl named Teak Dyer into his car, the car to drive her away, the keys to the locked building where he took her, the handcuffs, billy club, and gun he used to murder her. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p108]
- Garmanian failed to fill out most of his job application, listed his military service as only three months, and listed 'fired' as his reason for leaving two former jobs. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p108]
- A former employer reported that Garmanian once tried to have sex with a girl on the second floor when the building was closed; another said he drew sexually solicitous drawings and put them in the ladies' room. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p108]
- The murder Garmanian ultimately committed occurred in the ladies' room on the second floor of a closed building—strikingly similar to one former employer's report about his prior behavior. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p108]

## Inferences

- Inference: This case demonstrates that information available from simple reference checks—even a 25-cent phone call—could have prevented a murder if employers had bothered to obtain it.
