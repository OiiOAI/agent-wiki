# Minimally Conscious State

## Summary

A disorder of consciousness characterized by inconsistent but reproducible behavioral signs of awareness, such as following commands, intelligible verbalization, or purposeful behavior. Distinguished from vegetative state by the presence of clear consciousness markers.

## Key facts

- Minimally conscious state patients show inconsistent but reproducible signs of awareness. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p206-209]
- EEG patterns in minimally conscious patients show more preserved long-distance information sharing than in vegetative state. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p225-227]
- The local-global test can differentiate minimally conscious from vegetative state patients. [raw/books/neuroscience/Consciousness and the Brain_ Deciphering H - Stanislas Dehaene.pdf#p220-221]
