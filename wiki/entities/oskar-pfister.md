# Oskar Pfister

## Summary

A Zürich pastor and educationalist who was one of the first non-medical practitioners of psychoanalysis. Freud wrote an introduction to Pfister's book 'The Psychoanalytic Method' (1913), advocating for psychoanalytic work by non-medical educators.

## Key facts

- Pfister was a Zürich pastor and educationalist who developed psychoanalytic work with young people. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4047]
- Freud wrote that the practice of psychoanalysis calls much less for medical training than for psychological instruction and a free human outlook. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4048]
- Freud advocated that educators and pastoral workers should use psychoanalysis as prophylaxis, detecting early signs of neurosis development. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p4048]
