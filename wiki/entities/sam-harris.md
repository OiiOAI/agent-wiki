# Sam Harris

## Summary

Sam Harris is a prominent author and philosopher who argues that free will is an illusion. Mitchell quotes Harris's argument that we do not choose our parents, time of birth, gender, experiences, genome, or brain development, and that our brain makes choices based on preferences hammered into it over a lifetime—raising the question of where freedom lies.

## Key facts

- Sam Harris is described as a prominent free will skeptic. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p7]
- Harris argues we did not choose our parents, time and place of birth, gender, life experiences, genome, or brain development. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p7]
- Harris asks 'Where is the freedom in this?' and notes we are free to do what we want but questions where our desires came from. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p7]
