# Benjy

## Summary

Benjy was a twelve-year-old boy initially diagnosed with autism and pervasive developmental disorder, whose most challenging symptoms were irritable temperament and explosive outbursts. A crossover trial showed on/off control of symptoms with multinutrients. Bonnie has kept in touch with Benjy for over twenty years; he now lives independently, remains close to his family, and has become an excellent cook.

## Key facts

- Benjy was initially diagnosed at age two with autism, re-diagnosed at age four with pervasive developmental disorder, but the symptoms that challenged him most were his irritable temperament and explosive outbursts. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p127]
- The crossover trial showed that three weeks after beginning the nutrients, there was noticeable improvement in his temper and mood; when nutrients were stopped, mood and temper problems returned to baseline; reintroduction of nutrients resulted in improvements again (ABAB design). [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p127]
- Benjy is now in his early thirties, lives independently in his own apartment, remains very close to his entire family, has become an excellent cook, eats an exceptionally healthy diet, rides his bike everywhere, and gets lots of other exercise. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p128]
