# EMPowerplus

## Summary

EMP (EMPowerplus, EMPowerplus Advanced) is a broad-spectrum multinutrient formula containing minerals and vitamins studied extensively for mental health treatment. Research shows benefits for ADHD, mood dysregulation, PMS, trauma symptoms, aggression, depression, stress, and anxiety.

## Key facts

- EMP contains minerals and vitamins in balanced combination for brain function. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p259]
- Research dose is at least 4 capsules twice a day, compared to bottle-stipulated 2 capsules twice a day. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p259]
- Over 50 peer-reviewed studies show mental health benefits. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p259]
