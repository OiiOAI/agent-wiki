# Victor Tausk

## Summary

Victor Tausk (1879–1919) was a Vienna nerve specialist and close associate of Freud who took his own life on July 3, 1919. Originally a lawyer and journalist, he undertook the study of medicine in his thirties to practice psychoanalysis. He made contributions to the psychoanalysis of psychoses, philosophy, and judgment, and was known for his courses of lectures introducing psychoanalysis to large audiences.

## Key facts

- Tausk was 'only in his forty-second year' when he committed suicide on July 3, 1919. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5533]
- He was originally a lawyer who 'abandoned his career and turned to journalism' before studying medicine to enable him to practice psychoanalysis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5532]
- During the war he 'threw himself wholeheartedly, and with complete disregard of the consequences, into exposing the numerous abuses' tolerated by doctors. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5533]
- He produced 'valuable researches into various psychoses (e.g. melancholia and schizophrenia)'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5534]
- His philosophical training 'compelled him to formulate, and seek as well to master, the whole profundity and comprehensive meaning of the very difficult problems involved' in psychoanalysis. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5534]
- Tausk left letters expressing 'his unreserved belief' in psychoanalysis and hope 'that it will find recognition at a not too distant date'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5535]
- He was a member of the Vienna Psychoanalytical Society since the autumn of 1909. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p5534]
