# W. Edwards Deming

## Summary

W. Edwards Deming (1900-1993) was an American statistician, professor, author, and consultant known for his work in quality management, particularly in Japan after World War II. He edited Shewhart's 1939 book, created the Deming Wheel in 1950 at JUSE, and evolved the PDSA Cycle from 1986 to 1993. He was critical of the PDCA terminology, preferring PDSA.

## Key facts

- Deming edited Shewhart's 1939 book 'Statistical Method from the Viewpoint of Quality Control'. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p3]
- Deming modified the Shewhart cycle at a JUSE-sponsored eight-day seminar in 1950, creating the Deming Wheel. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p3]
- Deming reintroduced the Shewhart cycle in 1986 in his book 'Out of the Crisis'. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p6]
- In 1993, Deming called the cycle the 'Shewhart Cycle for Learning and Improvement—the PDSA Cycle'. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p7]
- Deming explicitly rejected PDCA, stating in 1990 to 'be sure to call it PDSA, not the corruption PDCA'. [raw/books/strategy/PDSA_History._16th_Deming_Research_Seminar_Feb._2010.Moen.docx.pdf#p7]
