# Harvard Negotiation Project

## Summary

The Harvard Negotiation Project is an initiative at Harvard Law School that developed the principled negotiation method presented in Getting to Yes. The project has trained diplomats, corporate executives, and other professionals in negotiation techniques.

## Key facts

- The Harvard Negotiation Project was established at Harvard Law School. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p10]
- The project developed the method of principled negotiation presented in this book. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p10]
- The project is directed by Roger Fisher and William Ury, with Bruce Patton as Deputy Director. [raw/books/strategy/Getting_to_Yes_Fisher_Ury.pdf#p2]
