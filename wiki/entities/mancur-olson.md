# Mancur Olson

## Summary

Mancur Olson was a political scientist known for his work on collective action and public goods. His book 'The Logic of Collective Action' (1965) established the free rider problem as a central issue for rational choice theory, and his later work 'The Rise and Decline of Nations' (1982) examined how social rigidities accumulate over time in stable societies.

## Key facts

- Mancur Olson authored 'The Logic of Collective Action: Public Goods and the Theory of Collective Action' published by Harvard University Press in 1965. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p408]
- Olson's work identified the free rider problem as one of the central issues for the rational choice school. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p408]
- Olson authored 'The Rise and Decline of Nations: Economic Growth, Stagflation, and Social Rigidities' published by Yale University Press in 1982. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p408]
