# Chris Hadfield

## Summary

Canadian astronaut and former commander of the International Space Station who gained massive public attention during his 2013 mission by sharing daily content on social media. His sons helped him set up Twitter and other platforms, and he posted photos, videos, and updates showing the day-to-day reality of life in space, making him an example of successfully 'showing your work.'

## Key facts

- In 2013, Chris Hadfield became commander of the International Space Station and gained massive public attention by sharing daily content on social media. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p42]
- His son Evan said 'Dad wanted a way to help people connect with the real side of what an astronaut's life is. Not just the glamour and science, but also the day-to-day activities.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p43]
- During his five-month mission, Hadfield tweeted, answered questions, posted photos of Earth, recorded music, and filmed YouTube videos of himself brushing teeth, sleeping, and performing maintenance—millions of people followed.
