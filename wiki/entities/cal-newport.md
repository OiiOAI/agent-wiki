# Cal Newport

## Summary

Cal Newport is a computer science professor at Georgetown University and one of the leading figures in the world of digital minimalism. He authored the book 'Digital Minimalism: Choosing a Focused Life in a Noisy World,' which serves as the theoretical foundation for James W. Williams' practical guide. Newport defines digital minimalism as a philosophy that helps people question what digital communication tools and behaviors add the most value to their lives.
