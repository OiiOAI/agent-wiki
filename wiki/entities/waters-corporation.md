# Waters Corporation

## Summary

Waters Corporation is described as the world's leading specialty measurement company focused on improving human health and well-being through analytical technologies. Gabriel Jurj led the largest software development R&D center at Waters and applied Cynefin and SenseMaker to improve organizational agility.
