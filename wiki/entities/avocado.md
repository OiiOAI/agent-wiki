# Avocado

## Summary

Avocados are Genius Food #2, providing the highest total fat-protecting capacity of any fruit or vegetable. They are rich in vitamin E, lutein, zeaxanthin, and potassium, supporting brain vasculature, cognitive function, and gut health.

## Key facts

- Avocados have the highest total fat-protecting capacity of any fruit or vegetable. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p80]
- One medium avocado contains 12 grams of fiber, supporting gut bacteria that produce anti-inflammatory brain-sustaining compounds. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p81]
- Avocados contain twice the potassium content of a banana, supporting vascular health for the brain's estimated 400 miles of microvasculature. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p81]
- Avocados are rich in lutein and zeaxanthin, carotenoids that boost processing speed when consumed with fat. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p80]
