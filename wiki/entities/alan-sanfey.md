# Alan G. Sanfey

## Summary

A cognitive neuroscientist known for pioneering work on the neural basis of fairness and decision making in economic games. His fMRI studies of the Ultimatum Game established the role of the anterior insula in processing unfair offers and predicting rejection behavior.

## Key facts

- Sanfey et al. (2003) conducted the landmark fMRI study examining unfair behavior in the Ultimatum Game, finding that anterior insula activation increased with offer unfairness and predicted rejection decisions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p320]
- This study showed that when offers were accepted, activation in DLPFC was greater than in anterior insula, whereas rejected offers showed less DLPFC activation than in anterior insula. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroscience of Decision Making - Mandel, David R.,Vartanian, Oshin.pdf#p341]
