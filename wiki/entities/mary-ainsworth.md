# Mary Ainsworth

## Summary

Mary Ainsworth was a developmental psychologist who, alongside John Bowlby, developed attachment theory in the 1950s and 1960s. She conducted the groundbreaking strange situation test to observe infant attachment behaviors, discovering that children display distinct attachment styles based on their caregivers' responsiveness.

## Key facts

- Mary Ainsworth was one of the founders of attachment theory in the 1950s and 1960s. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p27]
- Ainsworth conducted the strange situation test to observe children's attachment behaviors. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p33]
- Ainsworth and Bowlby's studies made clear that the connection between infant and caretaker was as essential for survival as food and water. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p27]
- She found that having an attachment figure in the room allowed a child to go out into an unknown environment and explore with confidence. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p33]
