# Frau P.

## Summary

A 32-year-old woman whose chronic paranoia case was reported by Freud as evidence that paranoia is a 'psychosis of defence.' Her symptoms included delusions of being watched, visual hallucinations of naked women, and voices commenting on her actions. Analysis revealed her symptoms derived from repressed sexual experiences with her brother occurring between ages six and ten.

## Key facts

- Frau P. developed paranoia six months after the birth of her child, becoming uncommunicative, distrustful, and believing neighbours behaved differently towards her. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1080]
- She experienced visual hallucinations of women's abdomens with pubic hair, which coincided with physical sensations in her own abdomen and were later traced to childhood experiences. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1082]
- Analysis showed she had been fond of her brother who lived nearby, and her depression began when a quarrel between her husband and brother caused the brother to stop visiting. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1083]
- She repeatedly made appointments with her brother to confide something important but never told him anything, acting on the unconscious motive that he would understand her suffering as the only one who knew its cause. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1084]
- After reproducing the scenes of her sexual relationship with her brother, the hallucinatory sensations and images disappeared and did not return. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p1084]

## Inferences

- Inference: The fact that her physical abdominal sensation 'joined in the conversation' during analysis of the childhood scenes, appearing with sensations or staying away according to whether scenes occurred in light or dark, demonstrates the somatopsychic encoding of traumatic memories.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The later addition indicates her condition worsened and she required institutionalization, though she later recovered and functioned for 12-15 years before a final relapse.
