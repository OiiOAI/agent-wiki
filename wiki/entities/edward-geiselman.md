# Edward Geiselman

## Summary

Edward Geiselman is a cognitive psychologist who, with Ronald Fisher, developed the Cognitive Interview in the 1980s. The technique improves witness recall through four components: complete reporting, mental reinstatement of context, varying temporal order, and perspective-taking.

## Key facts

- Ronald Fisher and Edward Geiselman developed the Cognitive Interview in the 1980s based on findings and ideas established in controlled studies of memory. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p161]
