# Jennifer Lerner

## Summary

Jennifer Lerner is a leader in the science of group interaction who, along with Philip Tetlock, described confirmatory and exploratory thought. Her research demonstrates that accountability to accuracy-focused audiences improves decision quality and that our craving for approval is so strong we will work to get it even from strangers.

## Key facts

- Jennifer Lerner and Philip Tetlock described the two kinds of group reasoning styles: confirmatory thought and exploratory thought. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1215-L1220]
- Despite the prevalence of minimal accountability manipulations, participants still reliably respond as if audience approval matters, even from strangers. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1266-L1272]
