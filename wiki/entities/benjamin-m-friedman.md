# Benjamin M. Friedman

## Summary

Harvard economics professor who wrote 'The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth' (2005, Alfred A. Knopf), dealing with the interplay of economics and morals. While Munger is interested in the impact of morals on economic growth, Friedman focuses on the impact of economic growth on morals. Both recognize reciprocal effects creating 'virtuous' or 'vicious' circles.

## Key facts

- Friedman's book 'The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth' was published by Alfred A. Knopf in 2005. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p429]
- Friedman supplies a quotation from Rabbi Elazar Ben Azariah: 'Where there is no bread, there is no law; where there is no law, there is no bread.'. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p429]
