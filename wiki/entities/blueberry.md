# Blueberry

## Summary

Blueberries are Genius Food #3, among the highest antioxidant fruits due to their abundance of flavonoids called anthocyanins. These compounds cross the blood-brain barrier and accumulate in the hippocampus, enhancing memory function and potentially delaying cognitive aging.

## Key facts

- Blueberry anthocyanins cross the blood-brain barrier and enhance signaling in brain regions handling memory. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p117]
- Blueberry anthocyanins accumulate specifically in the brain's hippocampus. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p117]
- A six-year study of 16,010 older adults found blueberry and strawberry consumption linked to delays in cognitive aging by up to 2.5 years. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p118]
- Twelve weeks of blueberry supplementation improved memory function, mood, and reduced fasting blood sugar in older adults at risk for dementia. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p117]
