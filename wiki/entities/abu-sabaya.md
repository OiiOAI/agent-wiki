# Abu Sabaya

## Summary

Abu Sabaya was the charismatic leader of Abu Sayyaf who personally negotiated kidnappings including Jeffrey Schilling. Known for his media-loving personality and violent tactics, he wore sunglasses and recorded his crimes. He was killed by Philippine military in 2002.

## Key facts

- Abu Sabaya always wore sunglasses, a bandana, black T-shirt, and camo pants and loved media attention. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p89]
- Sabaya wanted $10 million in 'war damages' and held firm for months, refusing the offer-counteroffer bargaining system. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p90]
- After the 'that's right' breakthrough, Sabaya said 'I was going to hurt Jeffrey. I don't know what you did to keep me from doing that.'. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p93]
- Abu Sabaya was killed in June 2002 in a shoot-out with Philippine military units. [raw/books/strategy/Never Split the Difference_ Negotiating As - Voss, Chris.pdf#p93]
