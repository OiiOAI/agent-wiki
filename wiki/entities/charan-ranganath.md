# Charan Ranganath

## Summary

Charan Ranganath is a neuroscientist and professor at UC Davis who has spent over 25 years studying memory. He is also a clinical psychologist with experience working with PTSD patients at the Westside VA Hospital in Chicago. His research bridges neuroscience and clinical psychology, examining how memory shapes our lives. He is a prolific researcher whose work has been published in major scientific journals. Beyond academia, he is also a musician who has performed with bands including Plug-In Drug.
