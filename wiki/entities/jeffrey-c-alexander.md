# Jeffrey C. Alexander

## Summary

Jeffrey C. Alexander is a prominent American sociologist who developed neofunctionalism as an effort to revive and reconstruct structural functionalism while addressing its major criticisms. His autobiographical sketch in this text describes his intellectual journey from student protest movements in the late 1960s and early 1970s, through dissatisfaction with New Left Marxism, to his work on theoretical synthesis.

## Key facts

- Alexander participated in student protest movements as an undergraduate at Harvard College and as a graduate student at the University of California, Berkeley in the late 1960s and early 1970s. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Alexander became dissatisfied with New Left Marxism for political and empirical reasons, including the New Left's turn toward sectarianism and violence. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Alexander published 'Theoretical Logic in Sociology' (1982-1983), which examined classical and contemporary theory and concluded that Parsons's work should be seen as a masterly modern effort at synthesis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p153]
- Alexander defines neofunctionalism as 'a self-critical strand of functional theory that seeks to broaden functionalism's intellectual scope while retaining its theoretical core.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p151]
- Alexander outlined problems associated with structural functionalism that neofunctionalism needs to surmount, including 'anti-individualism,' 'antagonism to change,' 'conservatism,' 'idealism,' and an 'antiempirical bias.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Alexander argued that neofunctionalism operates with a descriptive model of society that sees society as composed of elements which form a pattern, with parts 'symbiotically connected.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- Alexander stated in the mid-1980s that 'neofunctionalism is a tendency rather than a developed theory.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p152]
- In 'Neofunctionalism and After' (1998), Alexander indicated he had outgrown a neofunctionalist orientation and moved toward 'the new theoretical movement.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p154]
