# Jean Banks Case

## Summary

Jean Banks, an African American woman, stabbed and killed her live-in partner James 'Brother' McDonald after years of severe beatings requiring medical attention. When McDonald attacked her with a glass cutter, she grabbed a knife to defend herself. She was convicted of second-degree murder, despite claiming self-defense, and fared worse than light-skinned Judy Norman in a similar situation.

## Key facts

- Banks stabbed and killed McDonald after he had beaten her for years, sometimes so severely she required medical attention. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p250]
- During an argument, McDonald pushed Banks to the ground and attempted to slice her with a glass cutter; she grabbed a knife to defend herself and stabbed him through the heart. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p250]
- She claimed self-defense but was convicted of second-degree murder, faring worse than light-skinned Judy Norman who received voluntary manslaughter. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p250]
- African American women defending themselves reinforce the 'angry black woman' stereotype and are more likely to be charged with domestic violence even when acting in self-defense. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p250]
