# David Lewis

## Summary

American philosopher at Princeton University who died in 2001. Lewis argued that Hume's counterfactual definition of causation aligns more closely with human intuition than the regularity definition. He famously stated: 'We think of a cause as something that makes a difference, and the difference it makes must be a difference from what would have happened without it.' Lewis's work revived interest in counterfactual approaches to causality in both philosophy and science.
