# Marty Wygod

## Summary

Chairman of Synetic and later chairman of the combined WebMD company. A self-made billionaire who bred and raced thoroughbred horses, grew up on Wall Street buying and selling companies, and built Medco which he sold to Merck for six billion dollars. Described by multiple articles as a 'genius' and highly respected in upper echelons of business.
