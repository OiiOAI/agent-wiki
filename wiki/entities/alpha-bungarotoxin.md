# α-Bungarotoxin

## Summary

α-Bungarotoxin is a 74-amino acid peptide from the venom of Bungarus multicinctus (banded krait) that binds tightly and specifically to nicotinic ACh receptors, used experimentally to visualize receptor distribution.

## Key facts

- α-Bungarotoxin binds tightly and specifically to ACh receptors at the neuromuscular junction, enabling visualization using radiolabeled toxin and quantitative autoradiography. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p257]
- Iodinated α-bungarotoxin (125I-αBTX) showed that ACh receptors are packed at the crests of secondary folds at a surface density exceeding 10,000/μm². [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p257]
