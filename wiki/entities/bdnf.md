# Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor

## Summary

A neurotrophic protein nicknamed 'Miracle-Gro for the brain' that serves as the master molecule of learning. BDNF is elevated during exercise and is necessary for long-term potentiation, synaptic plasticity, and neurogenesis. It builds and maintains brain cell circuitry, improves neuron function, encourages growth, and strengthens and protects neurons against cell death.

## Key facts

- BDNF is described as 'Miracle-Gro for the brain,' a protein that when sprinkled on neurons in a petri dish causes them to automatically sprout new branches. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p37]
- BDNF binds to receptors at the synapse, unleashing ion flow to increase voltage and immediately improve signal strength. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p37]
- BDNF activates genes inside neurons to produce more BDNF as well as serotonin and proteins that build up synapses. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p37]
- About a dozen papers on BDNF were published before 1990, when scientists discovered it exists in the brain and nourishes neurons. Today over 5,400 papers exist on BDNF. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p36]
- People with a gene variation that reduces BDNF are more likely to have learning deficiencies. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p41]
- The book describes going for a run as 'like taking a little bit of Prozac and a little bit of Ritalin' because exercise elevates the same neurotransmitters these drugs target. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p35]
- Exercise boosts BDNF at least as much as antidepressants in the hippocampus, with one study showing combining exercise with antidepressants spiked BDNF by 250%. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p109]
- Mice bred with genes producing 50% less BDNF don't respond to antidepressants, suggesting BDNF is a necessary ingredient for drug efficacy. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p109]
- Every one of thirty depressed patients in one study had lower than normal BDNF levels, and postmortem studies of people with depression who died by suicide showed significantly decreased BDNF in their brains. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p109]
- Low BDNF levels correlate with personality traits making people more vulnerable to depression, such as neuroticism and hostility, even in healthy people. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p109]
- BDNF turns on genes to produce more neurotransmitters and neurotrophins, puts brakes on self-destructive cellular activity, releases antioxidants, and provides proteins for axon and dendrite construction. [raw/books/health/Spark _ The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the - John J_ Ratey; Eric Hagerman.pdf#p110]

## Inferences

- Inference: BDNF may be the essential ingredient that allows the brain to wire in positive memories that create detours around fear circuits, explaining exercise's particular effectiveness for anxiety.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Researchers can only measure BDNF in the bloodstream, which offers at best a rough approximation of brain levels.
