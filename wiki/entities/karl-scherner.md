# Karl Scherner

## Summary

Karl Scherner (1825-1889) was a German researcher who published the first psychologically sophisticated discussion of dream symbols in his 1861 book 'Das Leben des Traumes' (The Life of Dreams). He proposed that the dream depicts objects not directly but through different images representing key attributes.

## Key facts

- Scherner wrote that during sleep there is a weakening of the ego so that 'the activity of the soul we call fantasy is free from all the rules of reason.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p36]
- Scherner suggested that a house could symbolize the human body, with specific parts representing specific body parts. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p36]
- Freud later admitted that Scherner was 'the true discoverer of symbolism in dreams,' though Freud received full credit for these ideas. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p37]
