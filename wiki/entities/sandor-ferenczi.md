# Sándor Ferenczi

## Summary

Sándor Ferenczi (1873–1933) was a Hungarian psychoanalyst, a close collaborator and friend of Freud. He made significant contributions to psychoanalytic technique and theory, including the concept of mutual analysis and the Versuch einer Genitaltheorie.

## Key facts

- Sándor Ferenczi was born July 16, 1873 and died May 22, 1933. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7087]
- Freud and Ferenczi traveled together to Worcester, Massachusetts in 1909, where Freud delivered the Five Lectures on Psychoanalysis, with Ferenczi helping prepare the content. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7087]
- At the Nuremberg Congress of 1910, Freud arranged for Ferenczi to propose the organization of analysts into an international association, a scheme they had designed together. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7087]
- Ferenczi's Versuch einer Genitaltheorie (1924) applied psychoanalytic insights to the biology of sexual processes and organic life in general. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7088]
- Freud prophesied that some time in the future there would really be a 'bioanalysis' as Ferenczi had foretold. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7089]
- Ferenczi succumbed to pernicious anaemia shortly before completing his sixtieth year. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7089]

## Inferences

- Inference: Ferenczi's later work on mutual analysis and the importance of love in treatment may have contributed to his estrangement from Freud and the psychoanalytic mainstream in his final years.
- Inference: The Versuch einer Genitaltheorie emphasized the conservative nature of drives, which seek to re-establish states abandoned through external interference.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud does not fully explain the reasons for Ferenczi's 'drifting away' from the psychoanalytic community, though he mentions Ferenczi's focus on therapeutic aims.
