# Picasso's Bull

## Summary

Picasso's Bull (Le Taureau) is a series of 11 lithographs created in 1945-46 that demonstrates the process of distillation in art. Starting with detailed drawings and progressively simplifying, Picasso deconstructs the bull's form step-by-step until ending with a single continuous stroke that captures the animal's essence.

## Key facts

- Picasso's Bull is a sequence of 11 lithographs created in 1945-46. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p122]
- The process of distillation begins with the fourth image, progressively simplifying the bull's form. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p123]
- The final drawings show the bull as nothing more than a single continuous stroke. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p123]
- Picasso couldn't have started with the single line drawing; he needed to go through each layer step-by-step. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p123]
