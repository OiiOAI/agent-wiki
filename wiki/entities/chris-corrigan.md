# Chris Corrigan

## Summary

Chris Corrigan is a facilitator and teacher of participatory leadership living on Bowen Island, BC, Canada. He developed embodied exercises for teaching the Cynefin Framework using physical activities that mobilize body wisdom before analytical discourse.

## Key facts

- Chris Corrigan first encountered the Cynefin Framework in 2008 through friends in the knowledge management world. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p160]
- Corrigan developed three embodied exercises illustrating Clear, Complicated, and Complex domains: organizing by height alone, by height plus birth date and shoe size, and the Sun/Moon eclipse game. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p162]
- His background includes traditional Irish dance music, improvisational theater studying Augusto Boal's Theater of the Oppressed, and martial arts training. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p161]
- Corrigan follows a teaching protocol: state challenge, let group work it out, debrief quickly with specific questions. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p161]
