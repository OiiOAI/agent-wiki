# Garth Fagan

## Summary

A contemporary dance choreographer whose signature routine 'Prelude: Discipline Is Freedom' illustrates the relationship between discipline, responsibility, and freedom through dancers who achieve exceptional performance through autonomous action rather than external control.

## Key facts

- Garth Fagan is a true genius in the world of contemporary dance whose troupe has a signature routine called 'Prelude: Discipline Is Freedom.'. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p199]
- The dancers exhibit overwhelming energy and force without chaos or anarchy; they display responsibility and reliance on each other, with every dancer in precisely the right place at precisely the right time. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p199]
- The behavior of the Fagan dancers illustrates that responsibility need not be felt as obligation but can instead be associated with freedom; the responsible behavior is not controlled but autonomous. [raw/books/psychology/Why we do what we do _ understanding self - motivation - Edward L_ Deci, Richard Flaste.pdf#p199]
