# Epic Dreaming

## Summary

Epic dreaming is a largely understudied form of disordered dreaming first described in 1995 by Schenck and Mahowald. It is characterized by nights filled with long, tedious dreams of repetitive physical activity such as endless housework or slogging through snow or mud. The disorder affects women more than men, and while sleep lab assessments are usually clinically normal, the relentless dreams are followed by feelings of fatigue or exhaustion upon awakening. Emotions within epic dreams are usually described as neutral or entirely absent.
