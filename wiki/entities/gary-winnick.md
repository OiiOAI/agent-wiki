# Gary Winnick

## Summary

Gary Winnick is the chairman and founder of Global Crossing. He assembled the deal that financed the AC-1 transatlantic cable project and made a huge profit on the initial venture, selling 'condos' (capacity) for more than $2 billion on a $750 million construction cost.

## Key facts

- Gary Winnick and his three partners at Pacific Capital Group put together the deal for Global Crossing. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p98]
- Winnick made a huge profit on AC-1, constructing for $750 million and selling the 'condos' for more than $2 billion. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p99]
- Winnick became fascinated with how his sophisticated telecom customers were willing to overpay for STM-1 capacity. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p99]
