# Paccar

## Summary

Paccar is a manufacturer of heavy trucks under the Kenworth and Peterbilt brands, achieving 16 percent average return on equity over 20 years compared to 12 percent for competitors in a low-growth mature industry. Its strategy centers on quality leadership with a focus on owner-drivers and fleet operators, maintaining premium pricing through design consistency and a flexible manufacturing approach.

## Key facts

- Paccar earned 16 percent average return on equity over 20 years compared to 12 percent for competitors in a low-growth mature truck industry. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p57]
- Paccar has not lost money since 1939 and its profits have been remarkably stable despite strong upswings and downswings in truck demand. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p57]
- Paccar's strategy focuses on owner-drivers who care about the truck as their home and office, and on the classic American style aura of its brands alongside functional quality. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p57]

## Inferences

- Inference: Paccar demonstrates classic focus strategy—its various elements (design for owner-drivers, experienced dealers, high-end positioning) are coordinated to maintain the high ground against competitors in a mature industry.
