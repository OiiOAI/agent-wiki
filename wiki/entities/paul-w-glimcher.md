# Paul W. Glimcher

## Summary

Paul W. Glimcher is a prominent neuroscientist and one of the founding figures of neuroeconomics. He co-authored the introductory chapter of the major neuroeconomics textbook and has contributed foundational work on the neural basis of decision making, particularly regarding utility computation and the application of economic theory to neuroscience.

## Key facts

- Glimcher and colleagues proposed that the neoclassical revealed preference framework might serve as a useful theoretical tool for neuroscience. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p6]
- In Platt and Glimcher (1999), they demonstrated that single neurons in posterior parietal cortex fire as a lawful function of both probability and magnitude of expected rewards. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p8]
- Glimcher published 'Decisions, Uncertainty and the Brain: The Science of Neuroeconomics' in 2003, arguing that economics could serve as the source for needed normative theory in neuroscience. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p10]
- Glimcher was elected President of the Society for Neuroeconomics at the Kiawah Island meeting in 2004. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p8]

## Inferences
