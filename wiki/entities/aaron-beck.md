# Aaron Beck

## Summary

Aaron T. Beck is a psychiatrist who founded cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT). He argued that dysfunctional cognitions (beliefs/schemas) underlie pathological emotional states and that cognitive change is a prerequisite for sustainable behavioral and emotional change. Beck viewed maladaptive thoughts as stored in schemas and proposed that identifying and replacing these with realistic thought patterns would result in healthier thinking, behaviors, and feelings.

## Key facts

- Beck founded cognitive-behavior therapy and argued that cognitive change is a prerequisite for sustainable emotional and behavioral change. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p370]
- According to Beck, cognitive therapists modify mental content (maladaptive thoughts or beliefs) whereas behavior therapists try to change overt behaviors themselves. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p371]
