# Ernst Weber

## Summary

Ernst Weber demonstrated in 1834 that the sensitivity of a sensory system to differences in intensity depends on the absolute strength of the stimuli, establishing Weber's law (∆S = K·S) that laid the foundation for psychophysics.

## Key facts

- In 1834 Weber demonstrated that sensitivity to differences in intensity depends on absolute stimulus strength: we easily perceive 1 kg differs from 2 kg but not 50 kg from 51 kg. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p502]
- Weber's law (∆S = K·S) established that the just noticeable difference is proportional to the reference stimulus magnitude, founding the field of psychophysics. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p502]
