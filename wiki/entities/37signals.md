# 37signals

## Summary

37signals is a Chicago-based software company founded by Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson. Started in 1999 as a three-person web-design consulting firm, the company became known for creating successful online business tools including Basecamp, Highrise, Backpack, and Campfire. The company also created and open-sourced Ruby on Rails. Known for its intentionally small size (sixteen people as of 2010), remote-first workforce spanning eight cities across two continents, and refusal to take outside funding, 37signals has been cited as proof that traditional business practices like meetings, budgets, and aggressive growth are not prerequisites for success.
