# Oprah Winfrey

## Summary

Oprah Winfrey is an American media executive and philanthropist whose struggles with weight loss exemplify the challenges of dieting even for people with extraordinary self-control in other domains. Despite having personal trainers, chefs, nutritionists, and constant public monitoring, she could not maintain long-term weight loss. Her magazine cover showing her triumph at 160 pounds was followed years later by an honest acknowledgment of weighing 200 pounds. Her experience illustrates the 'Oprah Paradox'—that excellent self-control in other life areas does not translate into consistent weight control.
