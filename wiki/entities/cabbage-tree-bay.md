# Cabbage Tree Bay Aquatic Reserve

## Summary

A small bay near Sydney, Australia, designated as a marine sanctuary in 2002 with complete protection of its wildlife. Within a few years it became teeming with fish and other animals, and the author encountered the cephalopods that prompted him to write this book.

## Key facts

- In 2002, one small bay near Sydney was designated a marine sanctuary, with complete protection of its wildlife. [raw/books/neuroscience/Other Minds_ The Octopus, the Sea, and the - Peter Godfrey - Smith.pdf#p157]
- The area had been fished for too long by too many people, and by the 2000s the waters had been just about emptied. [raw/books/neuroscience/Other Minds_ The Octopus, the Sea, and the - Peter Godfrey - Smith.pdf#p157]
