# Edwin Smith Surgical Papyrus

## Summary

The Edwin Smith Surgical Papyrus is a medical treatise transcribed in the Seventeenth Century B.C. that contains the earliest reference to the brain anywhere in human records. According to James Breasted's 1930 translation and publication, the word 'brain' occurs only 8 times in ancient Egyptian, 6 of which appear on the pages describing compound fractures of the skull.
