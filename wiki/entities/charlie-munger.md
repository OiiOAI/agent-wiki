# Charlie Munger

## Summary

Charlie Munger is Warren Buffett's business partner and vice chairman of Berkshire Hathaway who advocates building a broad toolbox of mental models. He stresses that the key to worldly wisdom is having many models from different disciplines and connecting experience to them, warning against remembering isolated facts without a theoretical framework.

## Key facts

- Munger states: If you just remember isolated facts and try and bang 'em back, you can't really know anything. Facts don't hang together on a latticework of theory. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p111]
- Munger advocates looking out for the most powerful concepts in every discipline and understanding them so thoroughly they become part of thinking. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p110]
- The quote An idea or a fact is not worth more merely because it is easily available to you is attributed to Munger. [raw/books/productivity/How to Take Smart Notes_ One Simple Technique to Boost - Sönke Ahrens.pdf#p123]
