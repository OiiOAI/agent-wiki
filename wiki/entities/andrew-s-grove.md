# Andrew S. Grove

## Summary

Andrew S. Grove was a Hungarian-born American business executive who emigrated to the United States in 1956, co-founded Intel, and served as its president (1979) and CEO (1987). He was named Time magazine's Man of the Year in 1997, stepped down as CEO in 1998, and retired as Intel chairman in 2004. He also taught at Stanford Graduate School of Business for 24 years.
