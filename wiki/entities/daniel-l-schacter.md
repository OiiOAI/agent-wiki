# Daniel L. Schacter

## Summary

Daniel L. Schacter is a psychologist and professor at Harvard University who authored The Seven Sins of Memory (2001, updated 2021). His research focuses on memory, including neuroimaging studies of memory and memory distortions.

## Key facts

- Schacter is William R. Kenan Jr. Professor of Psychology at Harvard University. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p9]
- He began The Seven Sins of Memory during a sabbatical supported by a John Simon Guggenheim Memorial Fellowship. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p9]
- Schacter has received funding from Human Frontiers Science Program, National Institute on Aging, National Institute of Mental Health, and National Institute on Neurological Disorders and Stroke. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p9]
- His research group has used fMRI to study brain activity during memory encoding and retrieval processes. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p42]
- Schacter is William R. Kenan Jr. Professor of Psychology at Harvard University. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p417]
- He has been elected to the National Academy of Sciences. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p417]
- His research focuses on the cognitive neuroscience of memory, including amnesia, false memories, and brain imaging of memory processes. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p417]
- He co-developed the 'truth machine' paradigm using neuroimaging to distinguish true from false memories. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p382]
