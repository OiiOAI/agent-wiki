# D-cycloserine

## Summary

D-cycloserine (DCS) is an NMDA receptor facilitating drug that has been tested to enhance extinction learning and exposure therapy. Originally shown by Michael Davis to enhance threat conditioning memory strength, DCS was then hypothesized to enhance extinction as well. Collaborating with Barbara Rothbaum and Kerry Ressler, initial clinical trials showed DCS improved exposure therapy outcomes, though follow-up studies had mixed results.

## Key facts

- DCS facilitates NMDA receptor function and was shown to strengthen memory created by conditioning. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p410]
- Initial clinical trials with DCS showed improvement in exposure therapy for anxiety disorders, though subsequent studies had mixed results. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p410]
