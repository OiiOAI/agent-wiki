# Jeanne de Belciel

## Summary

Jeanne de Belciel, also known as Jeanne des Anges, was a French nun who became the prioress of the Ursuline house in Loudun in 1627. She exemplifies the Theatrical Narcissist type, orchestrating a famous possession scandal involving priest Urbain Grandier and later claiming miraculous stigmata that she displayed across Europe.

## Key facts

- In 1627, at age 25, Jeanne became prioress of the Ursuline house in Loudun after impressing her predecessor with displays of piety and spiritual knowledge. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p81]
- Jeanne reported erotic and violent dreams about local priest Urbain Grandier, who had declined her invitation to direct the Ursuline house. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p81]
- Jeanne became the star of public exorcisms, displaying the most violent contortions and demonic oaths among all possessed nuns. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p81]
- Her demonic possessions led to Grandier's arrest and execution by burning at the stake on August 18, 1634. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p82]
- Father Jean-Joseph Surin exorcised Jeanne, who then claimed miraculous stigmata including names appearing in her palm and fragrant oil on her chemise. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p82]
- Jeanne toured Europe displaying her miracles, meeting Cardinal Richelieu, the King and Queen of France, and generating crowds of thousands. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p83]
- At her death in 1665, her head was decapitated and mummified, displayed in a silver-gilt box until lost during the French Revolution. [raw/books/psychology/The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene.pdf#p83]

## Inferences

- Inference: Greene interprets Jeanne's behavior as a manifestation of deep narcissism driven by an insatiable appetite for attention that began in childhood, leading her to fabricate dramatic spiritual experiences.
