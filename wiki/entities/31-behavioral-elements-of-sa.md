# 31 Behavioral Elements of SA

## Summary

The 31 behavioral elements of situation awareness were identified by Waag and Houck (1994) with assistance from instructor pilots and other subject-matter experts (SMEs). These elements were categorized into 8 categories of mission performance: General Traits, Flight Management, Tactical Game Plan, Communication, Tactical Employment-General, Information Interpretation, System Operation, and Tactical Employment-Beyond Visual Range/Within Visual Range.
