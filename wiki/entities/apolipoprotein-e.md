# Apolipoprotein E

## Summary

Apolipoprotein E (ApoE) is an apolipoprotein expressed at high levels in the brain, primarily by astrocytes and to some extent by microglia. The three human APOE alleles (APOE2, APOE3, APOE4) differ by at most two amino acids. APOE4 is the major genetic risk factor for late-onset AD, while APOE2 is protective. ApoE4 promotes Aβ aggregation by diminishing Aβ clearance and promoting fibrillization.

## Key facts

- ApoE is an apolipoprotein expressed at high levels in the brain, primarily by astrocytes and to some extent by microglia, as a component of high density-like lipoproteins. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
- Three alleles of the APOE gene (APOE2, APOE3, APOE4) differ from each other by at most two amino acids. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
- APOE4 is present in about 25% of the general population but in as many as 60% of those with AD; one copy increases AD risk by 3.7-fold, two copies by 12-fold. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
- APOE2 protects against AD, decreasing risk by approximately 40% relative to the most common APOE3/APOE3 genotype. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
- ApoE4 promotes Aβ aggregation by diminishing Aβ clearance and promoting fibrillization, following the gradient ApoE4 > ApoE3 > ApoE2. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
- APOE4 may also act through additional mechanisms including influencing tau, the innate immune system, cholesterol metabolism, or synaptic plasticity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1622]
