# Susan David

## Summary

Susan David is a psychologist, researcher, and executive coach who has studied emotions and their role in human behavior for more than two decades. She is the author of Emotional Agility (2016) and has published influential work in Harvard Business Review. Her interest in emotional resilience began during her childhood in apartheid-era South Africa, where she witnessed trauma and violence, and was deepened when her father died of cancer when she was sixteen. She currently resides in the United States and works with clients ranging from individuals struggling with personal challenges to United Nations ambassadors and corporate leaders.
