# John Bowlby

## Summary

John Bowlby (1907-1990) was a British psychologist and psychiatrist who founded attachment theory in the 1950s and 1960s alongside Mary Ainsworth. He proposed that throughout evolution, genetic selection favored people who became attached because it provided a survival advantage, and that the need for attachment is embedded in our genes from womb to death.

## Key facts

- John Bowlby was one of the founders of attachment theory in the 1950s and 1960s. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p27]
- Bowlby proposed that throughout evolution, genetic selection favored people who became attached because it provided a survival advantage. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p19]
- Bowlby always claimed that attachment is an integral part of human behavior throughout the entire lifespan. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p28]
- Bowlby observed that even infants who had all nutritional needs met but lacked an attachment figure failed to develop normally. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p27]
- Bowlby understood that our need for someone to share our lives with is part of our genetic makeup. [raw/books/psychology/Attached_ Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Sec - Amir Levine.pdf#p31]
