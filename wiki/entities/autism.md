# Autism

## Summary

Autism is a common, devastating developmental disorder characterized by deficiencies in language acquisition, difficulties in social interactions, and stereotyped interests. It affects about 1 in 200 children with a strong heritable component, and about three times as many boys are affected as girls. Symptoms typically emerge in the first 3 years of life and may include a regression phase where children lose previously acquired language skills. Autism is genetically complex, not a single disorder but rather a cluster of related disorders caused by various genetic changes. Genes implicated include neurexins, neuroligins, and FOXP2.
