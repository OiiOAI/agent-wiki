# Wayne Lo

## Summary

Wayne Lo was a student at Simon's Rock College who shot and killed 2 people and wounded 6 others in 1991 after receiving a package from a gun manufacturer, refusing to explain its contents to staff, and receiving an anonymous warning that officials ignored.

## Key facts

- Lo received a package from 'Classic Arms' that a receptionist found suspicious; she correctly notified resident directors. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- Dean Bernard Rodgers refused to open the package, saying it would be improper to interfere with delivery of a student's mail. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- When asked about the package, Lo refused to open it in staff's presence, but opened it when alone and claimed it contained empty pistol clips and gun parts. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- An anonymous caller warned staff that Lo had a gun and planned to kill people, but officials did not call police before the shooting. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p101]
- At his arraignment, Lo wore a sweatshirt with 'SICK OF IT ALL' across the chest. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p101]
