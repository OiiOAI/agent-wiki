# Mary Karr

## Summary

Mary Karr is an American poet and memoirist who struggled with alcoholism and found recovery through Alcoholics Anonymous. A lifelong agnostic, she eventually became baptized as a Catholic and underwent the Spiritual Exercises of St. Ignatius. Her story illustrates how an AA sponsor—who reframed the 'higher power' concept as simply being outnumbered, outearned, and outweighed by the group—helped her achieve two months of sobriety. She exemplifies how social support and reframing religious concepts can aid recovery even for those resistant to traditional spirituality.
