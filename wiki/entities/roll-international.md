# Roll International Corporation

## Summary

Roll International Corporation is a privately held company controlled by Stewart and Lynda Resnick, one of the 200 largest private companies in America. It holds the largest citrus growing operation in California and is the world's largest nut grower. Its divisions include POM Wonderful (pomegranate products), Fiji Water, Suterra (pheromone-based pest control), and agricultural operations including almonds and pistachios.

## Key facts

- Roll International is one of the 200 largest private companies in America and is the largest citrus grower in California and largest almond and pistachio grower in the world. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p65]
- Roll's Wonderful brand commands a price premium in nuts and the scale economies in processing make it hard for smaller farmers to build competing facilities. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p65]
- The company funded over $30 million in pomegranate health research and created a new product category as 100 percent pure refrigerated antioxidant juice. [raw/books/strategy/Good strategy, bad strategy _ the difference and why it - Richard P_ Rumelt.pdf#p67]
