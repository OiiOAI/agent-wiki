# SAGAT (Situation Awareness Global Assessment Technique)

## Summary

SAGAT is a direct measurement methodology for assessing situation awareness (SA) developed by Mica R. Endsley. It freezes simulations at randomly selected times, blanks system displays, and queries operators about their perceptions of the situation at that moment. As a global measure, SAGAT covers all three levels of SA: Level 1 (perception of data), Level 2 (comprehension of meaning), and Level 3 (projection of the near future). The technique has demonstrated high validity, sensitivity, and reliability across diverse domains including aviation, air traffic control, nuclear power operations, and driving.

## Key facts

- SAGAT freezes a simulation at randomly selected times and queries operators about their perceptions while displays are blanked. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p163]
- SAGAT is a global measure that includes queries about all operator SA requirements across Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 SA components. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p163]
- SAGAT is a direct measure of SA that taps into the operator's perceptions rather than inferring them from behaviors influenced by many other factors. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p163]
- SAGAT has demonstrated high reliability with test-retest scores of 0.98, 0.99, 0.99, and 0.92 for mean SAGAT scores across fighter pilots. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- SAGAT has predictive validity; fighter pilots who reported enemy aircraft existence via SAGAT were three times more likely to later kill that target. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- SAGAT freezes have been shown to have no significant impact on subject performance, even when the threat of a stop exists. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p178]
- SAGAT has been used successfully in domains including en route air traffic control, TRACON air traffic control, fighter pilots, bomber pilots, commercial transport pilots, aircraft mechanics, and nuclear power operations. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p167]

## Inferences

- Inference: The random freeze methodology minimizes attentional bias because operators cannot prepare for queries covering nearly every aspect of the situation.
- Inference: The rich diagnostic data from SAGAT enables designers to identify specific SA deficiencies and develop targeted design modifications rather than simply approving or disapproving concepts.
