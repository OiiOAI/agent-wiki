# T. Singer

## Summary

Tania Singer is a researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, Germany. She is co-author of Chapter 20 on The Neuroscience of Compassion and Empathy and has conducted pioneering research on empathy, compassion, social cognition, and their neural underpinnings.

## Key facts

- T. Singer is affiliated with the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, Germany. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p247]
- Singer and colleagues showed that empathy for pain engages a network centered around the anterior insula and anterior midcingulate cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p249]
