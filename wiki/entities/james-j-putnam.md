# James J. Putnam

## Summary

James Jackson Putnam (1846–1918) was an American neurologist and Professor of Neuropathology at Harvard University who became a major supporter of psychoanalysis in America. He was President of the American Psychoanalytic Association and was respected for his unimpeachable character and high ethical standards. He attended the Weimar Congress of 1911 and contributed significantly to establishing psychoanalysis in American psychiatry.
