# Shiva

## Summary

Hindu Lord of the Universe whose cosmic dance balances creation and destruction. His extended right hand holds the drum (beat of time/creation) while his extended left holds the flame (destruction), with a second right hand in the 'fear not' gesture. He swallowed the poison Kalakuta during the churning of the Milky Ocean, turning his throat blue.

## Key facts

- Shiva danced the dance of universal destruction while the extended right hand holds the drum (beat of time) and the left holds the flame of destruction. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p105]
- The 'fear not' gesture of Shiva's second right hand symbolizes that at the center all is still despite the dynamism of creation and destruction. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p105]
- Shiva swallowed the poison Kalakuta during the churning of the Milky Ocean and held it in his throat, which turned blue (Nilakantha). [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p139]
- Shiva grants the charm to revive the dead to the titans' high priest, contributing to the great battle for the elixir of immortality. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p139]
