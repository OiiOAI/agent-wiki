# Bobby Davids

## Summary

Vice President of acquisitions at Medical Manager Corporation who orchestrated a massive fraud scheme involving kickbacks from dealers and embezzlement totaling $5.4 million across 53 schemes over five years. He later convinced federal prosecutors he was a whistleblower and implicated company executives in fabricated accounting fraud.

## Key facts

- Bobby Davids was VP of acquisitions at Medical Manager Corporation and was discovered to have been taking kickbacks from dealers he was acquiring. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p190]
- Bobby Davids stole approximately $5.4 million through 53 kickback schemes over a five-year period. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p198]
- In March 2003, Bobby Davids walked into the U.S. Attorney's Office in Charleston and presented himself as a whistleblower, claiming executives were involved in accounting fraud. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p195]
- Bobby Davids served one year and a day in prison after pleading guilty to one count of mail fraud. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p198]
- Judge Norton found Bobby Davids and his accomplice Caroline to be noncredible witnesses who appeared to be parroting Bobby's words. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p214]
