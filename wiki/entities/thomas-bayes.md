# Thomas Bayes

## Summary

Thomas Bayes (1702–1761) was a Presbyterian minister, philosopher, and statistician who lived in Tunbridge Wells, England. He never published the theorem immortalizing his name; his 'Essay towards Solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances' was presented to the Royal Society two years after his death by Richard Price.
