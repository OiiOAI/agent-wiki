# Ichiro Kishimi

## Summary

Ichiro Kishimi (born 1956) is a Japanese philosopher and author who, together with Fumitake Koga, wrote 'The Courage to Be Disliked.' He specializes in Greek philosophy and combines it with Adlerian psychology in his work. He practices philosophy through dialogue and counseling.
