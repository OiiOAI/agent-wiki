# Brief Therapy Center

## Summary

The Brief Therapy Center was established in 1966 at the Mental Research Institute in Palo Alto by Richard Fisch. Under his direction, the center investigated the phenomena of human change, requiring the researchers to take a fresh look at their beliefs, learning, and practice. The center's work led to the development of the theory of change presented in 'Change: Principles of Problem Formulation and Problem Solving.' The first year of operation was made possible by grants from the Luke B. Hancock Foundation, T. B. Walker Foundation, and Robert C. Wheeler Foundation.

- The Brief Therapy Center developed a pragmatic approach to problem resolution based on what is being done here and now that perpetuates problems, rather than why questions about past causes.
