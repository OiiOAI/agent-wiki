# Havelock Ellis

## Summary

Havelock Ellis (1859–1939) was an English physician, psychologist, and social reformer who wrote extensively on human sexuality. The term 'narcissism' was in part due to Ellis, according to a footnote in Freud's paper on narcissism. Ellis's work on the psychology of sex contributed to the conceptual vocabulary that Freud would later adapt for psychoanalytic theory.
