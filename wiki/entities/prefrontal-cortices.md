# Prefrontal Cortices

## Summary

Brain regions in the frontal lobe that are critical for rational decision-making, somatic marker acquisition, emotional processing, and categorization of life experience. The ventromedial sector is particularly important for somatic markers in the bioregulatory and social domain, while the dorsolateral sector handles knowledge of the external world. The prefrontal cortices are ideally positioned to integrate signals from all sensory regions, bioregulatory systems, and the body proper.

## Key facts

- The prefrontal cortices receive signals from all sensory regions where images constituting thoughts are formed, including somatosensory cortices representing past and current body states. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p201]
- Prefrontal cortices receive signals from bioregulatory brain regions including brain stem neurotransmitter nuclei (dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin), basal forebrain acetylcholine systems, amygdala, anterior cingulate, and hypothalamus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p202]
- Different prefrontal sectors categorize different domains: the ventromedial sector handles bioregulatory and social domains, while dorsolateral regions handle external world knowledge including objects, people, language, and mathematics. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p203]
- The ventromedial prefrontal cortices send signals to autonomic nervous system effectors and can promote emotional chemical responses from the hypothalamus and brain stem. [raw/books/neuroscience/Descartes' error _ emotion, reason, and the human brain - Antonio R_ Damasio,pdf.pdf#p204]

## Inferences

- Inference: The prefrontal cortices likely evolved to solve the problem of creating order from parallel spatial displays, a problem arising from the brain's design limiting conscious mental output at any one time.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The exact boundaries between prefrontal sectors and their functional specializations remain subjects of ongoing research.
