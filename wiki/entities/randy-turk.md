# Randy Turk

## Summary

Senior partner at Baker Botts who served as Michael Singer's lead criminal defense attorney. Practiced white-collar criminal defense for over thirty years with a distinguished record including successful defense of Hughes Aircraft Company against a $400 million government claim and defense of Michael Deaver at Reagan's White House.
