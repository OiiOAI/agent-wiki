# Jean-Bernard Lévy

## Summary

Jean-Bernard Lévy was chairman and CEO of Matra Communications in 1996 when Richard Rumelt interviewed him. He was forty years old at the time, having risen rapidly through the French Grand École system. Lévy had served in government and France Telecom, and was general manager of Matra's satellite business. He went on to become CEO of Vivendi in 2002, controlling Universal Music Group, Canal+, Activision Blizzard, and other businesses, and became chairman of Vivendi's management board in 2005.
