# B. J. Fogg

## Summary

A behavioral scientist and professor at Stanford University who founded the Persuasive Technology Lab, where he taught courses on designing technology to change human behavior. He was dubbed 'the millionaire maker' as many of his students went on to create successful apps using his techniques.

## Key facts

- B.J. Fogg taught that computers could be 'more persistent than human beings, offer greater anonymity,' and 'go where humans cannot go or may not be welcome' in their ability to persuade. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p118]
- Fogg previously worked on a course dedicated to 'the psychology of mind control' before founding the Persuasive Technology Lab. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p118]
- He assigned students including Tristan Harris books explaining hundreds of psychological insights and tricks for manipulating human behavior, many based on B.F. Skinner's principles of reinforcement. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p119]
- Students in Fogg's class, including Mike Krieger and Kevin Systrom, were inspired to create Instagram using principles of immediate reinforcement (hearts and likes) from his teachings. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p119]
- Fogg is a behavior scientist at Stanford University where he founded the Behavior Design Lab. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p7]
- The cofounder of Instagram was Fogg's former student, and what he learned about human behavior was used to design that breakthrough app. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p7]
- Fogg created Tiny Habits through self-experimentation, losing twenty pounds and significantly changing his life in six months before teaching the methods to others starting in 2011. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p8]
- BJ Fogg founded the Behavior Design Lab at Stanford University. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p361]
- Fogg had an airplane dream in 2016 that he describes as a wake-up call to share his insights broadly through a book. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p301]
- Fogg ran a Stanford course dubbed the 'Facebook Class' by the New York Times in 2007, where students created apps that engaged over 24 million people within six months. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p304]
- Fogg created a follow-up Stanford course called Peace Technology to explore how Behavior Design could contribute to world peace. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p305]
- Fogg grew up in a religious tradition that emphasized 'Where much is given, much is expected.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p305]
- Fogg states 'It's not ethical to have the potential to do good and not use it for the benefit of humanity.'. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p306]
- Fogg's website is bjfogg.com and Tiny Habits resources are at TinyHabits.com. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p361]

## Inferences

- Inference: Fogg's lab represents the birthplace of many techniques now used to create smartphone addiction, with students applying Skinnerian reinforcement principles to app design.
