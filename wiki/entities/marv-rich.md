# Marv Rich

## Summary

A seasoned turnaround specialist brought in by Marty Wygod to serve as president of WebMD during the massive reorganization following the Internet bubble burst. Marv led the effort to cut costs and restructure divisions, eventually deciding to spare Medical Manager Practice Services Division from layoffs after impressive presentations by Singer's team.

## Key facts

- Marv Rich was brought in as president of WebMD to handle the massive reorganization after the company lost hundreds of millions of dollars annually during the Internet bubble burst. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p180]
- Marv Rich's reorganization team came to Alachua for two days of meetings to evaluate Medical Manager Practice Services Division for potential layoffs. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p182]
- Despite arriving with intentions to cut costs, Marv Rich decided to skip the 'bloodletting' and allow Medical Manager to continue operations after being impressed with their business plan and performance.
