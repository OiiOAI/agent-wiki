# Helen S. Mayberg

## Summary

Helen S. Mayberg is a pioneering neurologist who developed deep brain stimulation therapy for treatment-resistant depression, directly manipulating body-budgeting regions to change patients' affective feelings.

## Key facts

- Mayberg developed deep brain stimulation therapy for treatment-resistant depression. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p96]
- During surgery, electrodes are sunk into a key predictive area in the patient's interoceptive network. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p96]
- When electrodes are turned on, patients report immediate relief from their agony; when turned off, dread approaches in synchrony. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p96]
- This work may represent the first time direct stimulation of the human brain has consistently changed affective feelings. [raw/books/psychology/How Emotions Are Made_ The Secret Life of - Lisa Feldman Barrett.pdf#p96]
