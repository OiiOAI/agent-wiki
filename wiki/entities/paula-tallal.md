# Paula Tallal

## Summary

Paula Tallal is a neuroscientist at Rutgers University who analyzed why children have trouble learning to read. Her research showed that children with language disabilities have auditory processing problems with consonant-vowel combinations spoken quickly—the 'fast parts of speech.' She initially feared these children were 'broken' with no fix for their basic brain defect, but later collaborated with Merzenich to develop Fast ForWord.

## Key facts

- Tallal discovered that children with language disabilities have auditory processing problems with common consonant-vowel combinations that are spoken quickly—called 'the fast parts of speech.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p79]
- She originally feared that language-impaired children were 'broken' with no way to fix their basic brain defect before collaborating with Merzenich. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p80]
- In 1996 Tallal co-founded Scientific Learning Corporation with Merzenich, Bill Jenkins, and Steve Miller to develop Fast ForWord. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p80]
