# Intuitive Surgical

## Summary

Intuitive Surgical is the world leader in physician-guided robot surgery, developing the da Vinci surgical system. The company was founded in 1995 when venture capitalists bought Stanford Research Institute's surgical robotics intellectual property, bringing in Gary Guthart who had worked on the technology. SRI's original technology was funded by the Department of Defense for battlefield telemedicine. Intuitive specialized the technology around a surgeon working alongside the patient. The company developed prototypes adding wrist-action movements, 3D vision systems, interchangeable instruments, and improved imaging. FDA approval for general surgery came in 2000. Gary Guthart became CEO in 2010. In 2021, Intuitive faces challenges from Alphabet and Medtronic entering robotic surgery, and the lack of tactile feedback remains a limitation.

- The US FDA gave approval for general surgery in 2000 for the da Vinci system. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p195]
