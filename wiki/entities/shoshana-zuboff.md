# Shoshana Zuboff

## Summary

A Harvard professor who coined the term 'surveillance capitalism' to describe the business model where technology companies track users online to build detailed psychological profiles that are sold to advertisers and used to manipulate behavior.

## Key facts

- Shoshana Zuboff coined the term 'surveillance capitalism' to describe the system where tech companies scan, sort, and store user data to build profiles for behavioral targeting. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p138]
- Zuboff's work is described as making it possible to understand 'a lot of what is happening now' with targeted advertising and behavioral manipulation. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p138]
