# ASPM

## Summary

The abnormal spindle-like microcephaly-associated gene, a specific regulator of brain size. Like microcephalin, ASPM mutations cause primary microcephaly. ASPM evolved most rapidly after the divergence of humans and chimpanzees, and a variant arose approximately 5,800 years ago coinciding with the spread of agriculture and written language.

## Key facts

- ASPM evolved most rapidly after the divergence of humans and chimpanzees, implicating it as a cause of the rapidly exploding brain size of human ancestors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p23]
- An ASPM variant arose about 5,800 years ago, coincident with the spread of agriculture, cities, and the first record of written language. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p24]
