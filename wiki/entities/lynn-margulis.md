# Lynn Margulis

## Summary

Lynn Margulis was an evolutionary biologist who greatly expanded and championed the endosymbiosis hypothesis in the late 1960s. Her work, initially considered outlandish, is now supported by overwhelming evidence and forms the foundation for understanding how eukaryotic cells with mitochondria arose.
