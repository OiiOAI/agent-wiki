# Life Formulas I

## Summary

Life Formulas I is Ravikant's 2008 framework breaking down happiness, health, and wealth into component parts and equations. It represents his attempt to systematize life outcomes into learnable, improvable metrics.

## Key facts

- Happiness = Health + Wealth + Good Relationships. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p186]
- Health = Exercise + Diet + Sleep. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p186]
- Wealth = Income + Wealth × (Return on Investment). [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p186]
- Income = Accountability + Leverage + Specific Knowledge. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p186]
- Wealth × (Return on Investment) implies compound interest drives wealth. [raw/books/philosophy/The Almanack of Naval Ravikant_ A Guide to - Eric Jorgenson.pdf#p186]
