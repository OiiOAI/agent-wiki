# Halle Berry neuron

## Summary

A neuron in the hippocampus that responded selectively to multiple photographs of actress Halle Berry, including images where her face was almost completely obscured (such as dressed as Catwoman), and even to her name written on a screen. This provided proof that the neuron was responding to the concept rather than visual features.

## Key facts

- The neuron responded to photos of actress Halle Berry, including those of her dressed as Catwoman, a character she played in the movie of the same name, even when Berry's face was almost completely obscured. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p133]
- The neuron responded to Halle Berry's name written on the screen, proving it was reacting to the concept and not to particular visual features in the pictures. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p133]
- The neuron did not respond to photos of other people, places, or animals, or to any other written name. [raw/books/neuroscience/The forgetting machine _ memory, perception, and the - Rodrigo Quian Quiroga; hoopla digital.pdf#p133]
