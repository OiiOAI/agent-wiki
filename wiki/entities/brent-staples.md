# Brent Staples

## Summary

Brent Staples is a Black journalist who described how his presence activated racial stereotypes in white strangers, causing them to avoid or fear him on quiet streets at night. To counter this, he began whistling Vivaldi's Four Seasons to signal he was 'benign,' demonstrating how activated stereotypes bias perception and behavior.

## Key facts

- Brent Staples described how a white businesswoman, suddenly aware of his presence on a quiet street at night, walked away quickly and then began to run. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p201]
- To counter being viewed stereotypically, Staples started whistling Vivaldi's The Four Seasons, noting that 'the tension drained from people's bodies when they heard me.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p201]
