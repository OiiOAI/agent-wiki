# Montague Ullman

## Summary

Montague Ullman was a New York-trained psychiatrist and psychoanalyst who founded the Maimonides Dream Laboratory in Brooklyn in the 1960s. He developed the dream appreciation method, an approach to working with dreams in groups that emphasizes discovery and safety.

## Key facts

- Ullman's dream appreciation method has several stages: the dream presenter describes their dream, group members discuss it as if it were their own, the presenter responds, and the dream is read back in second person for interactive discussion. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p214]
- In the dream appreciation method, group members use the 'If it were my dream' technique, discussing feelings they would experience and sharing personal associations and projections. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p214]
- Studies support that Ullman's method, when used as prescribed, can result in dream-related benefits including gains in personal insights. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p215]
