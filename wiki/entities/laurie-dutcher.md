# Laurie Dutcher

## Summary

Laurie Dutcher is the CEO and owner of Secretly Spoiled who began using Profit First three years prior to her interview with Michalowicz. Within months of implementation, her personal finances stabilized, and she took her family on their first Disneyland vacation using her first quarterly profit disbursement.

## Key facts

- Laurie Dutcher is CEO, accountant, and owner of Secretly Spoiled, a company that started using Profit First three years before her interview. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p183]
- Before Profit First, she stated 'I was living check to check. I wasn't taking a salary.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p183]
- She took her family on their first Disney vacation using her first-ever quarterly profit disbursement, and subsequently took 'quite a few more' trips. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p183]
- She observed: 'my business actually started to grow faster when I started paying myself and focusing on the profit first.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p183]
