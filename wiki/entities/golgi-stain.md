# Golgi Stain

## Summary

The Golgi stain is a staining technique invented by Camillo Golgi in 1873 that stains individual neurons completely without spreading to adjacent neurons. It was crucial for establishing the neuron doctrine by showing neurons are discrete entities.

## Key facts

- The Golgi stain was invented by Camillo Golgi in 1873 for staining brain tissue in a novel fashion. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p704]
- Crucially, the stain did not spread from one neuron to the next, revealing individual neurons as discrete entities. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p704]
