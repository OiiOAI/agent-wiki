# John Hennigan

## Summary

John Hennigan, known as 'Johnny World,' is a legendary professional gambler who has earned four World Series of Poker bracelets, a World Poker Tour championship, and more than $6.5 million in prize money. He is noted for his exceptional skills and willingness to bet on anything. His famous wager—moving to Des Moines for a month under restrictive conditions for $30,000—lasted only two days before he paid $15,000 to get out, illustrating how poorly we predict our future happiness.
