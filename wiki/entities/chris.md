# Chris

## Summary

The eleven-year-old son of the narrator, traveling on the motorcycle with his father. He is curious but sometimes bored, prone to stomach pains that have been diagnosed as early symptoms of mental illness, and has an Indian friend named Tom White Bear who believes in ghosts.

- He suffers from stomach pains that have been examined multiple times, once suspected to be appendicitis, and diagnosed as early symptoms of mental illness. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p53]
