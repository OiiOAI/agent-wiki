# Amrit Desai

## Summary

Amrit Desai was an Indian spiritual teacher who lived in America for many years and had a large spiritual community. He visited Temple of the Universe and performed an energy transfer on Michael Singer that permanently opened his heart chakra. Desai encouraged people to come to the Temple for daily meditations and became an important part of Singer's life for years. He later experienced a falling-out with his followers in 1994 and stayed at the Temple for three years while going through this difficult period.

- Amrit Desai had lived in America for many years and had a large spiritual community up north before visiting Temple of the Universe. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p94]

- At the end of 1994, Amrit had a major falling-out with his followers and was invited to stay at the Temple for three years. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p162]
