# Marcus Raichle

## Summary

A prominent neuroscientist who won the Kavli Prize and made the breakthrough discovery of the default mode network in the 1980s. His research fundamentally changed the understanding of what happens in the brain during mind-wandering.

## Key facts

- Marcus Raichle discovered that the brain is not dormant during mind-wandering — activity shifts from one part of the brain to another but remains highly active. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p103]
- He named the region of the brain that becomes more active during mind-wandering 'the default mode network.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p103]
- Raichle used PET scan technology to observe the default mode network 'light up' on brain scans, calling the moment 'God, there she was. The whole thing. It was just stunning.'. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p103]
- He compared thinking to a symphony requiring both focused attention and mind-wandering — 'you need to stand back from that' raw material to digest what has been absorbed. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p107]
