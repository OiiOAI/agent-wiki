# Sante de Sanctis

## Summary

Sante de Sanctis (1862-1935) was an Italian scientist who published 'Dreams: Psychological and Clinical Studies of an Alienist' in 1899. He developed a multipronged approach to dream research combining questionnaires, systematic awakenings, repeated observations, and statistical analyses, and was among the first to use electrophysiological instruments in sleep research.

## Key facts

- De Sanctis studied dreams of children, the elderly, criminals, patients with epilepsy, and the mentally ill. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p42]
- He showed that dreaming was less common during deep sleep in the first half of the night and more vivid in lighter sleep at the end of the night. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p43]
- De Sanctis proposed that a dream must be viewed as a 'mathematical sum' of the dreamer's fundamental state, current state, and immediate experiences. [raw/books/neuroscience/When brains dream _ understanding the science and mystery of - Antonio Zadra.pdf#p44]
