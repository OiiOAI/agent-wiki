# IGN Entertainment

## Summary

IGN Entertainment is a division of News Corporation and is the online video games media company with the biggest audience of video game players in the world, with more than 45 million gamers frequenting its portfolio of media properties. Founded in the late 1990s and acquired by News Corporation in 2005, IGN employs several hundred people including almost a hundred engineers. The company provides a case study in implementing the Five Whys, including both initial failures and subsequent successes under Tony Ford's leadership.
