# Pittsburgh Compound B

## Summary

Pittsburgh compound B (PIB) is a fluorescent analog of thioflavin T that binds with high affinity to fibrillar Aβ. Labeled with short-lived isotopes of carbon or fluorine, it can be detected by PET imaging to visualize amyloid plaques in living brains, enabling early diagnosis of Alzheimer disease before symptom onset.
