# Otto Snell

## Summary

Otto Snell was a German physician who in 1892 demonstrated the mathematical relationship between brain and body size in mammals, establishing that these dimensions scale according to the power function 2/3. This work initiated the modern era of research on brain size scaling.

## Key facts

- In 1892, Otto Snell showed that mammalian brains and bodies scale according to the power function 2/3. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p165]
- Snell's finding provided compelling evidence that brain and body weights relate by a systematic function resembling surface-to-volume relationships. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p165]
