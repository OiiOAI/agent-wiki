# Antonio Damasio

## Summary

Antonio Damasio is a neuroscientist who developed the somatic marker hypothesis, proposing that feelings arise from the brain's representation of bodily responses to emotional stimuli. He distinguishes emotions (nonconscious action programs) from feelings (conscious experiences). Damasio emphasizes feedback from the entire body and has more recently focused on subcortical body-sensing areas in the brainstem as potentially primary for basic emotional feelings. His work with the insular cortex highlighted the role of body-mapping in consciousness.

## Key facts

- Damasio proposed that emotions are nonconscious action programs, while feelings are conscious experiences resulting from representing body responses in body-sensing brain areas. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p197]
- The somatic marker hypothesis emphasizes that signals from the body are 'read' by brain areas including somatosensory cortex, insular cortex, hypothalamus, and periaqueductal gray. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p198]
- Damasio's 'as if' loop proposes that body states can be mentally re-created without actual sensory feedback. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p201]
- Damasio's research identified that patients with ventromedial prefrontal damage maintain preserved intellect but exhibit defective emotional behavior and decision making. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p209]
- Damasio proposed that biological value is tied to homeostasis and survival, with the physiological state of tissues within a homeostatic range constituting the deepest origin of biological value. [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p212]
- Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis distinguishes between emotion (an action program) and feeling (the conscious, cognitive sequel to the action program). [raw/books/neuroscience/Neuroeconomics _ Decision Making and the Brain - Paul W_ Glimcher, Ernst Fehr, Colin Camerer, Antonio Rangel,.pdf#p211]
- Antonio Damasio and colleagues described a woman with damage to the anterior cingulate sulcus who appeared unresponsive with an 'empty' mind, leading Crick to conclude she had lost her Will. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p282]
- Damasio also worked on prosopagnosia and facial recognition, and on 'convergence zones' that appear to be located not only in many cortical areas but also in most brain regions associated with the cortex. [raw/books/neuroscience/The astonishing hypothesis _ the scientific search for the - Crick, Francis.pdf#p290]
- Damasio proposes the Somatic Marker Hypothesis—that emotions are primarily somatic (body-based) events. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p129]
- His research shows the prefrontal cortex connects reasoning areas with body-sensation areas. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p129]
- Somatic markers are body-state memories used by the brain to assist decision-making. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p129]

## Inferences

- Inference: Damasio's work provides neuroscientific backing for meditation practices that focus on body sensations as the primary locus of emotional experience.
