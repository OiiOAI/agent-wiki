# Paul Näcke

## Summary

Paul Näcke (1853–1912) was a German alienist and psychiatrist who described a perversion in which an adult treats his own body with all the caresses usually devoted to an outside sexual object. Freud borrowed the term 'narcissism' from this description by Näcke (1899), making psychoanalysis' concept of narcissism partly dependent on earlier psychiatric observation of this perversion.
