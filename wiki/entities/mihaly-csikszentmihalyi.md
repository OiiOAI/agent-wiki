# Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi

## Summary

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi is a psychologist cited in Essentialism for his research on flow states, creativity, and the conditions that lead to optimal human experience. His TED talk on flow and his book 'Creativity' are referenced.

## Key facts

- Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi gave a TED talk titled 'Flow, the Secret to Happiness' in February 2004. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p233]
- His book 'Creativity: Flow and the Psychology of Discovery and Invention' is cited on page 241. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p241]
- He conducted research on what is now known as the 'flow' state—the condition of optimal human experience. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p233]
- Csikszentmihalyi was born in 1934 in Fiume (Italy) and survived WWII by fleeing air raids and hiding in bomb shelters among corpses. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p56-57]
- At age 8, fleeing Nazi bombs in Italy, he hid in a butcher's shop and encountered hanging bodies—a formative trauma that led him to question adult wisdom about living well. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p56]
- At age 13, he quit school and worked as a translator in Rome, eventually saving enough to study with Carl Jung in Zurich, who inspired his career in psychology. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p57-58]
- His brother Moricz was taken to a Soviet gulag and later worked as a railway stoker, but late in life found flow studying crystals—spending 10 hours lost in examining a single stone. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p64-65]
- Csikszentmihalyi discovered that 85 percent of people recognize having experienced a flow state and describe such moments as highlights of their lives. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p61]
- He built a broad and robust body of scientific evidence establishing flow states as a real and deep form of human attention. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p63]
- He was 87 years old when interviewed by Hari, having spent over 50 years studying flow states. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p63]

## Inferences
