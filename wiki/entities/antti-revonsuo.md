# Antti Revonsuo

## Summary

Antti Revonsuo is a Finnish philosopher and cognitive neuroscientist who is a Fellow of the Academy of Finland at the University of Turku. He is known for his work on consciousness as a natural biological phenomenon and has published widely in cognitive neuroscience, neuropsychology, and consciousness studies. He is the European Editor of Consciousness and Cognition and serves on the board of Directors of the Association for the Scientific Study of Consciousness. He is the author of the Threat Simulation Theory of dreaming.
