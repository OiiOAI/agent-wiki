# Hugh Thompson Jr.

## Summary

Warrant Officer Hugh Thompson Jr. was the helicopter pilot who halted the My Lai Massacre in Vietnam on March 16, 1968. When he witnessed American soldiers attacking civilians, he landed his helicopter between the villagers and the soldiers, trained his machine guns on his compatriots, and ordered his crew to fire if they continued harming civilians.

## Key facts

- Hugh Thompson Jr. was a twenty-five-year-old Warrant Officer flying a helicopter over My Lai when he saw masses of dead civilians and realized Americans were attacking them rather than protecting them from enemy fire.
