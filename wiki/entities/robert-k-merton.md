# Robert K. Merton

## Summary

Robert K. Merton (1910-2003) was one of the most influential sociologists of the 20th century, credited with coining terms including 'role model,' 'self-fulfilling prophecy,' 'reference group,' 'unintended consequences,' and 'focus group.' He founded the science of sociology and was the first sociologist awarded the National Medal of Science. His 1942 paper on the normative structure of science, refined and published in book form in 1973, established the CUDOS framework for truth-seeking communities.

- Merton published his normative structure of science paper in 1942 and refined it over 31 years, publishing the final version in 1973. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1325-L1477]
