# Microcephalin

## Summary

A gene that is a specific regulator of brain size. Mutations in microcephalin cause primary microcephaly, an autosomal recessive neurodevelopmental disorder resulting in markedly reduced head and brain size comparable to early hominids. Research shows microcephalin has undergone accelerated evolution along the primate lineage.

## Key facts

- Defects in microcephalin lead to primary microcephaly, with brain size three standard deviations below normal, comparable in size to early hominids. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p23]
- Microcephalin showed evidence of accelerated evolution along the entire primate lineage. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p23]
- A genetic variant of microcephalin arose approximately 37,000 years ago, coinciding with the emergence of culturally modern humans. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p24]
