# Rick Barry

## Summary

Rick Barry is a twelve-time NBA All-Star and Basketball Hall of Fame member who holds an 89.3% career free-throw record. He shot 'granny style' (underhand), a technique that produces more consistent results due to fewer variables and better backspin. Michalowicz uses Barry as a metaphor for Profit First being the 'granny shot of accounting'—simple but effective.

## Key facts

- Rick Barry is a twelve-time NBA All-Star with a free-throw record of 89.3%, compared to the NBA average of 75%. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p209]
- He shot underhand 'granny style' because it simplifies arm movement (fewer variables) and puts more backspin on the ball. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p209]
- Michalowicz uses Barry as a metaphor: 'Welcome to the granny shot of accounting and money management: Profit First.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p210]
