# Lenore Skenazy

## Summary

Lenore Skenazy is an activist and journalist who became known as 'America's worst mom' after letting her nine-year-old son ride the subway alone in New York City in 2008. She founded the 'Free-Range Kids' movement to advocate for children's freedom and independence, arguing that modern parenting practices have become excessively risk-averse and harmful to children's development.

## Key facts

- She was labeled 'America's worst mom' and denounced on major news shows after writing an article about letting her nine-year-old son take the subway and bus alone. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p260]
- She founded the Free-Range Kids movement to advocate for children's independence and freedom. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p259]
- She argues that children are now three times more likely to be struck by lightning than to be killed by a stranger. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p260]
- She noted that early episodes of Sesame Street from the 1960s carry a warning that 'may not be suitable for our youngest viewers' because they show children walking alone and talking to strangers. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p261]
