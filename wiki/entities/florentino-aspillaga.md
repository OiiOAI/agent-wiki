# Florentino Aspillaga

## Summary

Florentino 'Tiny' Aspillaga was a high-ranking officer in Cuba's General Directorate of Intelligence who defected in June 1987. His revelations that nearly the entire CIA spy network inside Cuba had been turned into double agents represented one of the worst intelligence failures of the Cold War.

## Key facts

- Aspillaga was named intelligence officer of the year in the Cuban spy service in 1985 and received a handwritten letter of commendation from Fidel Castro. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p17]
- Aspillaga revealed to the CIA that nearly the entire U.S. roster of secret agents inside Cuba were double agents working for Havana. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p19]
- After Aspillaga's defection, Castro released an eleven-part documentary showing Cuban intelligence had filmed and recorded CIA operations in Cuba for at least ten years. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p20]
- Aspillaga escaped two assassination attempts by his former employers before disappearing under a new identity. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p18]
