# Flannery O'Connor

## Summary

Flannery O'Connor (1925–1964) was an American novelist and short story writer known for her Southern Gothic fiction that explored dark and grotesque themes. After her father died of lupus erythematosus in 1941, she was diagnosed with the same disease in 1950 and spent most of her remaining years at her family's farm Andalusia in Milledgeville, Georgia, where she wrote her most celebrated works, including the novel Wise Blood (1952). Her proximity to death shaped her writing and philosophy, teaching her to appreciate every moment and deepening her empathy for others.
