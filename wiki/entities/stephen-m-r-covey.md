# Stephen M.R. Covey

## Summary

Stephen M.R. Covey (1953-2022) was an American author, businessman, and keynote speaker, son of Stephen R. Covey (author of The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People). He served as CEO of Covey Leadership Center and later FranklinCovey, orchestrating the merger between Franklin Quest and Covey Leadership Center that created the latter company. He is best known for The Speed of Trust, which presents trust as a measurable economic driver and actionable competency.
