# PSD-95

## Summary

PSD-95 (postsynaptic density protein 95) is a scaffolding protein containing PDZ domains that organizes glutamate receptors and associated proteins at excitatory synapses.

## Key facts

- PSD-95 contains three PDZ domains at its amino terminus and SH3 and guanylate kinase domains at its carboxyl terminus. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p282]
- PDZ domains of PSD-95 bind to specific sequences at the carboxyl terminus of NMDA receptor GluN2 subunits and TARP subunits of AMPA receptors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p282]
