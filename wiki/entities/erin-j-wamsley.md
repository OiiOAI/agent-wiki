# Erin J. Wamsley

## Summary

Erin J. Wamsley, PhD, is a researcher at Harvard Medical School and Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center who studies the relationship between sleep, memory consolidation, and dream experience. She is the first author of the reviewed article.

## Key facts

- Erin J. Wamsley is a PhD affiliated with the Department of Psychiatry at Harvard Medical School/Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p1]
- Her research has shown that intensive learning experiences can dramatically influence dream content, with 30% of sleep onset reports containing task-related imagery after video game training. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p6]
- Her work has demonstrated that dreaming of a spatial learning task predicts enhanced sleep-dependent memory consolidation. [raw/books/neuroscience/wamsley__stickgold_sleep_medicine_clinics_2011.pdf#p9]
