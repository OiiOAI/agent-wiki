# Zenji Abe

## Summary

Zenji Abe was a Japanese fighter pilot who participated in the attack on Pearl Harbor in 1941. Later in life, he made multiple trips to Hawaii to apologize to survivors of the attack, catalyzing a process of reconciliation that included developing a close friendship with survivor Richard Fiske.

## Key facts

- Zenji Abe was a fighter pilot in the 1937 Japanese invasion of China and throughout the Pacific during World War II, including helping to lead the attack on Pearl Harbor. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p668]
- In 1990, Abe approached survivors of Pearl Harbor at a commemorative event and apologized for his role in the attack, eventually developing a close friendship with survivor Richard Fiske.
