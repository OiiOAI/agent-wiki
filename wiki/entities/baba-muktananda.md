# Baba Muktananda

## Summary

An Indian yoga master (1908-1982) who brought Siddha Yoga to the West through a world tour in the 1970s. He performed shaktipat, a spiritual energy transfer, on Michael Singer during a December retreat, profoundly transforming his practice. Singer had invited him to Gainesville after receiving what he interpreted as guidance from life.

## Key facts

- Baba Muktananda was a Siddha master from India who came to America in the 1970s on a world tour. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p81]
- Singer wrote a letter on Santa Fe Community College letterhead inviting Baba to stop in Gainesville on his route from Atlanta to Miami. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p81]
- Baba performed shaktipat on Singer at a December retreat outside Atlanta, touching the point between his eyebrows. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p85]
- During shaktipat, ten thousand volts of electricity jumped from Singer's spine to meet Baba's hand, dislodging him from his body. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p85]
- Baba visited Union Correctional Institution in Gainesville, marking the beginning of Siddha Yoga prison work worldwide. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p88]
- Baba founded an American organization with Ron Friedland as its first president. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p89]
