# Shinzen Young

## Summary

An American meditation teacher known for his science-oriented, ecumenical approach to teaching meditation. He created the Basic Mindfulness system, which combines elements from multiple meditation traditions into a comprehensive, secular framework.

## Key facts

- Shinzen Young is described as an American meditation teacher with a science-oriented and ecumenical teaching style. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- He is nominally a Buddhist but can discuss spiritual practices from many religions intelligently. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- Shinzen is described as a 'geek' himself, fascinated by dead languages and abstract mathematics. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- He created the Basic Mindfulness system, which Taft describes as 'by far the most comprehensive and industrial-strength meditation system.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- Shinzen Young is described as 'the teacher behind most of the methods in this book'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p115]
- Shinzen is characterized as 'a science nerd, a former math professor' who uses pseudo-math equations to describe meditation principles. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p115]
- His formula P × R = S (Pain times Resistance equals Suffering) is described as the most famous of his equations. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p115]
- Shinzen coined the term 'skill at feeling' for the ability to perceive subtle body sensations developed through meditation. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p134]
