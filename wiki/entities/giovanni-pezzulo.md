# Giovanni Pezzulo

## Summary

Giovanni Pezzulo is a researcher in cognitive sciences and artificial intelligence and a co-author of 'Active Inference: The Free Energy Principle in Mind, Brain, and Behavior' (MIT Press, 2022). He is affiliated with the Institute of Cognitive Sciences and Technologies at the National Research Council of Italy. His research focuses on Active Inference, cognitive modeling, and robotics. He was funded by the European Research Council (ThinkAhead, Grant No. 820213) and the EU's Horizon 2020 Framework Programme under the Human Brain Project SGA3.
