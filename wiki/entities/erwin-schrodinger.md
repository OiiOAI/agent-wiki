# Erwin Schrödinger

## Summary

Erwin Schrödinger was a physicist who delivered lectures at Trinity College Dublin in 1943 that were published as 'What Is Life?', a landmark book that influenced Watson and Crick's thinking about DNA. Schrödinger articulated the central idea that living things must work hard to keep themselves organized against the second law of thermodynamics.
