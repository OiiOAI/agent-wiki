# Nerve Growth Factor (NGF)

## Summary

Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) is a neurotrophic factor essential for neuronal survival, discovered by Rita Levi-Montalcini and Stanley Cohen. It was the first neurotrophic factor identified and confirmed the neurotrophic factor hypothesis—that target cells secrete limited amounts of trophic factors required for neuronal survival.

## Key facts

- NGF was purified by Levi-Montalcini and Cohen in the 1970s and shown to be made by target cells, supporting survival of sensory and sympathetic neurons in vitro. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1147]
- Neutralizing antibodies directed against NGF cause profound loss of sympathetic and sensory neurons in vivo. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1147]
- NGF binds to the TrkA receptor, a transmembrane tyrosine kinase, which triggers dimerization and phosphorylation events leading to neuronal survival signaling. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1148]
- NGF was discovered through Levi-Montalcini's observation that mouse tumor tissue promoted sensory neuron survival in chick embryos. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1253]
- NGF binds TrkA (tyrosine kinase) for survival signaling and p75 (TNF receptor family) which can promote cell death. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1254]
- Anti-NGF antibodies cause loss of sympathetic and sensory neurons, demonstrating NGF is essential for their survival in vivo. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1253]
