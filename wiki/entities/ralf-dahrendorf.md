# Ralf Dahrendorf

## Summary

Ralf Dahrendorf (1929-2009) was a German sociologist, political scientist, and statesman who developed a major conflict theory of society. His work represents a systematic inversion of structural functionalism, focusing on authority, positions, interests, and conflict rather than order and equilibrium.

## Key facts

- Dahrendorf argued that society has two faces (conflict and consensus) and that sociological theory therefore should be divided into two parts. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p156]
- To Dahrendorf, every society at every point is subject to processes of change, contrasting with functionalists who see society as static or in moving equilibrium. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p156]
- Dahrendorf's central thesis is that the differential distribution of authority 'invariably becomes the determining factor of systematic social conflicts.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p157]
- Authority does not reside in individuals but in positions, and those in positions of authority dominate because of the expectations of those who surround them. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p157]
- Dahrendorf distinguished quasi groups (aggregates of incumbents with identical role interests) from interest groups (structured groups that are real agents of group conflict). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p159]
- Dahrendorf recognized that society could not exist without both conflict and consensus, which are prerequisites for each other. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p156]
- Dahrendorf was not optimistic about developing a single sociological theory encompassing both consensus and conflict processes. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p156]
