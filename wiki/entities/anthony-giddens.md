# Anthony Giddens

## Summary

British sociologist whose work on intimacy and sexuality Perel cites. His book 'The Transformation of Intimacy' provides theoretical framework for understanding how modern relationships navigate sexuality and emotional connection.

## Key facts

- Anthony Giddens describes heterosexual pornography as concerning itself almost exclusively with 'low emotion, high intensity sex.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p153]
- Perel cites Giddens noting that male potency displayed in pornography serves as reassurance against male insecurities. The female characters, themselves invulnerable, neutralize male vulnerability because they are always fully responsive and fully satisfied. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p153]
- Giddens notes that while opponents of porn focus primarily on aggression and violence, porn also serves the purpose of neutralizing male vulnerability through the depiction of always-satisfied female characters. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p153]
- Giddens's structuration theory is one of the best-known efforts to integrate agency and structure in social theory. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p423]
- Giddens argues that we must begin with 'recurrent social practices' as the basic domain of social science study. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p424]
- In Giddens's terms, agency and structure are a duality; all social action involves structure, and all structure involves social action. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p424]
- Giddens defines structure as 'the structuring properties [rules and resources]... the properties which make it possible for discernibly similar social practices to exist across varying spans of time and space.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- Giddens contends that 'structure only exists in-and through the activities of human agents.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- Structure is 'always both constraining and enabling' according to Giddens. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- Giddens defines social systems as 'reproduced relations between actors or collectivities organized as regular social practices.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p426]
- Giddens emphasizes that 'the constitution of agents and structures are not two independently given sets of phenomena, a dualism, but represent a duality' where structural properties are both medium and outcome of practices. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p427]
- Anthony Giddens was born on January 18, 1938, and studied at the University of Hull, the London School of Economics, and the University of London. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p452]
- Giddens was appointed lecturer at the University of Leicester in 1961 and moved to Cambridge University in 1969. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p452]
- His major theoretical work culminated in 1984 with 'The Constitution of Society: Outline of the Theory of Structuration'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p452]
- In 1985 Giddens was appointed professor of sociology at the University of Cambridge. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p452]
- In 1997 Giddens became director of the highly prestigious London School of Economics. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p453]
- Giddens describes modernity as a 'juggernaut,' a runaway engine of enormous power that threatens to rush out of control. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p452]
- Giddens defines modernity in terms of four basic institutions: capitalism, industrialism, surveillance capacities, and military power. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p454]
- The three essential aspects of Giddens's structuration theory relevant to modernity's dynamism are distanciation, disembedding, and reflexivity. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p455]
- Giddens proposes 'utopian realism' as a response to the risks of modernity, seeking balance between utopian ideals and realities. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p456]

## Inferences

- Inference: Giddens's structuration theory represents a significant theoretical synthesis that bridges the traditionally opposed micro and macro levels of sociological analysis.
- Inference: The theory's emphasis on recursive practices and reflexivity reflects Giddens's engagement with both interpretive and structural approaches in sociology.
- Inference: Giddens's work represents a significant effort to bridge agency and structure through structuration theory, avoiding both micro and macro reductionism.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: The text notes Ian Craib's criticism that Giddens's focus on social practices may lack 'ontological depth' and that his theory may be too fragmented to provide adequate critical analysis.
