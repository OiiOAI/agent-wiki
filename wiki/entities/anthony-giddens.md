# Anthony Giddens

## Summary

British sociologist whose work on intimacy and sexuality Perel cites. His book 'The Transformation of Intimacy' provides theoretical framework for understanding how modern relationships navigate sexuality and emotional connection.

## Key facts

- Anthony Giddens describes heterosexual pornography as concerning itself almost exclusively with 'low emotion, high intensity sex.'. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p153]
- Perel cites Giddens noting that male potency displayed in pornography serves as reassurance against male insecurities. The female characters, themselves invulnerable, neutralize male vulnerability because they are always fully responsive and fully satisfied. [raw/books/psychology/Mating in Captivity_ Unlocking Erotic Intelligence - Esther Perel.pdf#p153]
- Giddens notes that while opponents of porn focus primarily on aggression and violence, porn also serves the purpose of neutralizing male vulnerability through the depiction of always-satisfied female characters.
