# Robert B. Cialdini

## Summary

Robert B. Cialdini is an American psychologist and professor emeritus at Arizona State University who wrote 'Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion.' He identifies himself as a lifelong 'patsy' for compliance tactics, which motivated his research into the psychology of compliance. He conducted participant observation among compliance professionals for nearly three years to understand how influence principles work in practice.

## Key facts

- Cialdini describes himself as an 'easy mark for the pitches of peddlers, fundraisers, and operators of one sort or another' who has frequently found himself with unwanted magazine subscriptions or tickets. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p5]
- He conducted nearly three years of participant observation, infiltrating sales, advertising, and fund-raising organizations under disguised identity. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p6]
- He is Professor of Psychology at Arizona State University. [raw/books/psychology/Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B. Cialdini.pdf#p8]
