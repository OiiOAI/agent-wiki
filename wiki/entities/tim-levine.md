# Tim Levine

## Summary

Tim Levine is a psychologist at the University of Alabama at Birmingham who developed Truth-Default Theory (TDT). Over his career, he conducted hundreds of experiments on deception detection and concluded that humans default to truth as a rational evolutionary strategy.

## Key facts

- Tim Levine developed Truth-Default Theory, arguing that humans assume strangers are truthful because efficient communication depends on trust. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p43]
- Levine's experiments showed average deception detection accuracy of 54% across police officers, judges, therapists, and CIA officers. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p45]
- Levine's graduate student Hee Sun Park made the key insight that 54% accuracy averages high accuracy on truths with low accuracy on lies. [raw/books/psychology/Talking to Strangers - Gladwell, Malcolm.pdf#p46]
