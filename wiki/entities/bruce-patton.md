# Bruce Patton

## Summary

Bruce Patton is Deputy Director of the Harvard Negotiation Project and the Thaddeus R. Beal Lecturer on Law at Harvard Law School. A lawyer, he teaches negotiation to diplomats and corporate executives worldwide and works as a negotiation consultant and mediator in international, corporate, labor-management, and family settings.
