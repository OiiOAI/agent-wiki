# George Leonard

## Summary

George Leonard (1913-2010) was an American author, educator, and aikido master who served as president of the Esalen Institute. A former WWII flight instructor who later became a pioneer in applying martial arts principles to personal development, he authored multiple books on human potential including Education and Ecstasy, The Ultimate Athlete, and The Way of Aikido.

## Key facts

- George Leonard is the author of numerous books on human possibility and social change including Education and Ecstasy, The Transformation, The Ultimate Athlete, The Silent Pulse, Adventures in Monogamy, and The Way of Aikido. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p2]
- He was president of the Esalen Institute and founder of Leonard Energy Training (LET), a practice inspired by aikido. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p2]
- He practiced aikido since 1970 and taught it regularly since 1976. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p10]
- As a WWII flight instructor, he was one of six top graduates kept back to teach while others went to combat. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p45]
- He co-founded Aikido of Tamalpais in October 1976 with Richard Strozzi Heckler and Wendy Palmer. [raw/books/productivity/Mastery - The Keys To Success And Long - Ter - George Leonard.pdf#p48]
