# Joseph Takahashi

## Summary

Joseph Takahashi is a Japanese-American geneticist who conducted large-scale genetic screens in mice in the 1990s to identify circadian rhythm genes. He discovered the clock gene by screening hundreds of mutant mice for alterations in circadian locomotion period. His work demonstrated that similar molecular mechanisms control circadian rhythms in mammals as in Drosophila.

## Key facts

- Joseph Takahashi conducted genetic screens in mice in the 1990s and discovered the clock gene, which when mutated causes extremely long circadian periods or complete loss of rhythmicity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p87]
- The mouse clock gene encodes a transcriptional regulator whose activity oscillates through the day, sharing functional similarity with Drosophila circadian genes.
