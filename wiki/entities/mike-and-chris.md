# Mike and Chris

## Summary

Mike is a successful strategist who used Behavior Design to transform his relationship with his 21-year-old son Chris, who was living at home, struggling with basic demands of adulthood, and had a contentious relationship with his father. Mike started by breaking down the coffee filter task into tiny steps—first just asking Chris to take the filter out, then rinse it, then eventually Chris cleaned it without being asked. This small success with the coffee filter led to Mike and his wife applying the same approach to other behaviors, ultimately transforming their family dynamics.
