# Lawrence Keeley

## Summary

Lawrence Keeley is an archaeologist at the University of Illinois who authored the influential 1996 book War Before Civilization: The Myth of the Peaceful Savage. He argued that archaeological evidence shows warfare is ancient and widespread, challenging the view that prehistoric societies were peaceful. His work documented mass graves, projectile wounds, and fortifications from prehistoric times.

## Key facts

- Keeley authored War Before Civilization in 1996, arguing warfare is broad and ancient based on archaeological evidence. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p317]
- Keeley documented mass graves with skeletons bearing multiple fractures, caved-in skulls, parrying fractures, and stone projectiles embedded in bone. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p318]
- Keeley estimated that 90 to 95 percent of tribal societies engage in warfare when not pacified by outside government forces. [raw/books/psychology/Behave_ The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst - Robert M Sapolsky; Michael Goldstrom; Overdrive Inc.pdf#p321]
