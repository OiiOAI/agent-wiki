# Ken Burns

## Summary

Ken Burns is the award-winning documentary filmmaker known for films like The Civil War, Baseball, and Jazz. He has described how only a tiny percentage of his raw footage—sometimes just 1 out of 40 or 50 hours—makes it into the final cut, illustrating the radical act of distillation required in documentary filmmaking.
