# Amelia

## Summary

Amelia was a participant in one of Forte's courses who made a 180-degree change in her relationship to the Internet after building her Second Brain. She had previously seen the Internet as sensationalistic and offensive and avoided engaging with it. Once she had a place to curate the best of the Internet while ignoring what did not serve her, she began to see it in a completely new light. She is a skilled leadership coach who runs a clinic teaching leaders how to manage their nervous systems to improve well-being and effectiveness.
