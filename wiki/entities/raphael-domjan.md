# Raphaël Domjan

## Summary

Raphaël Domjan is a Swiss eco-explorer who conceived and led the first solar-powered circumnavigation of the globe aboard the 115-foot catamaran PlanetSolar. The voyage took 584 days using only power harvested from the sun. He is now working on the first solar aircraft to fly to the stratosphere.

## Key facts

- Raphaël Domjan conceived PlanetSolar, the first solar-powered circumnavigation of the globe aboard a 115-foot catamaran covered with 38,000 solar cells. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p123-124]
- The PlanetSolar voyage took 584 days (approximately 19 months) at a cost of nearly $17 million. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p124]
- Domjan spent four years finding funding, a year on studies before building, and 19 months constructing the vessel. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p123]
- He describes longer journeys as more unpredictable than shorter trips, requiring pushing through despite uncertainty. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p137]
