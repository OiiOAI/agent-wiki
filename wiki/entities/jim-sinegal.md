# Jim Sinegal

## Summary

Jim Sinegal was the co-founder and longtime CEO of Costco Wholesale. Charlie Munger considers him one of the top five retailers of the past century, comparing him to Carnegie, Rockefeller, and James J. Hill. Sinegal works 80 hours per week and is described as a moral and practical leader with exceptional operational efficiency in warehouse operations.
