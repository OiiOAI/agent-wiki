# Lateral Hypothalamus

## Summary

The lateral hypothalamus (LH) is a brain region critical for the SEEKING system and feeding behavior. It contains circuits that promote appetitive arousal, exploration, and foraging. ESB of the LH produces self-stimulation and stimulus-bound behaviors including sniffing, feeding, drinking, and gnawing.

## Key facts

- The lateral hypothalamus contains SEEKING circuits that can promote diverse motivated behaviors when electrically stimulated. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p86]
- Sniffing evoked by LH stimulation is probably elaborated by both ascending and descending neural connections and is not controlled directly by DA systems. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p86]
- LH lesions produce severe feeding deficits, with animals becoming essentially parkinsonian with difficulty performing most behaviors. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p97]
- The LH-SS system is among the first brain areas to learn an appetitive task and among the first to express learning in well-trained animals. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p87]
- Tail pressure can evoke feeding, drinking, and gnawing similar to LH stimulation, and these behaviors are diminished by DA receptor blockade. [raw/books/neuroscience/Affective Neuroscience_ The Foundations of Human and Animal - Panksepp, Jaak.pdf#p89]

## Inferences

- Inference: The LH may contain a body-weight set-point mechanism that modulates the arousability of SEEKING impulses based on metabolic status.
