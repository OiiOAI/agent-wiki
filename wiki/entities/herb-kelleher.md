# Herb Kelleher

## Summary

Herb Kelleher was the leader of Southwest Airlines who exemplified Essentialist thinking in business. He made deliberate trade-offs including point-to-point flights only, no meals, open seating, and coach-only service to keep costs down. He famously said companies must look at every opportunity and say 'no' to things that don't contribute much to the end result.

## Key facts

- Herb Kelleher deliberately chose to offer only point-to-point flights, serve no meals, let people choose seats as they boarded, and offer only coach service—all as part of a strategy to keep costs down. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p49]
- Kelleher said: 'You have to look at every opportunity and say, "Well, no… I'm sorry. We're not going to do a thousand different things that really won't contribute much to the end result we are trying to achieve."'. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p50]
- Southwest Airlines generated the largest return on investment among S&P 500 companies from 1972 to 2002, demonstrating the success of Kelleher's Essentialist approach. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p49]
