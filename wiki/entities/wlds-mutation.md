# Wlds Mutation

## Summary

The Wlds (Wallerian degeneration slow) mutation is a spontaneously occurring mutant in mice that dramatically delays axonal degeneration after transection. The mutation results in fusion of two normal proteins: one involved in biosynthesis of NADH and one involved in ubiquitination. Distal stumps persist for about 10-fold longer than in normal mice, proving that Wallerian degeneration is actively regulated rather than passive.

## Key facts

- In Wlds mutant mice the distal stumps of peripheral nerves persist for several weeks after transection, about 10-fold longer than in normal mice. - Expression of the Wlds fusion protein significantly delays axonal loss and can extend the lifespan of certain mouse models of motor neuron disease.
