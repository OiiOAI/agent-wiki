# Ronald Fisher

## Summary

Ronald Fisher is a cognitive psychologist who, with Edward Geiselman, developed the Cognitive Interview in the 1980s. The interview technique is based on findings from controlled studies of memory and specifically avoids suggestive or leading questions to improve witness recall without increasing suggestibility.

## Key facts

- Ronald Fisher and Edward Geiselman developed the Cognitive Interview in the 1980s based on findings and ideas established in controlled studies of memory. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p161]
- The Cognitive Interview specifically avoids the use of suggestive or leading questions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p161]
