# Jennifer Thompson and Ronald Cotton Case

## Summary

A wrongful conviction case in which Jennifer Thompson incorrectly identified Ronald Cotton as her rapist. After Cotton served years in prison, DNA evidence exonerated him. Remarkably, Thompson and Cotton later became close collaborators advocating for eyewitness identification reform.

## Key facts

- Jennifer Thompson identified Ronald Cotton at an initial lineup saying 'That's the closest,' then changed her identification to 'I'm absolutely positive that's the man' during trial.
