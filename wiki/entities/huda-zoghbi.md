# Huda Zoghbi

## Summary

Huda Zoghbi is a Lebanese-American neurogeneticist who discovered that mutations in the methyl CpG binding protein 2 (MeCP2) gene cause Rett syndrome. Her work has been fundamental in understanding how this X-linked gene regulates transcription and how its dysfunction leads to progressive neurodevelopmental impairment.

## Key facts

- Huda Zoghbi and colleagues found that the major cause of Rett syndrome results from mutations in the MeCP2 gene. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p95]
- MeCP2 binds methylated DNA as part of a process that regulates mRNA transcription. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p95]
