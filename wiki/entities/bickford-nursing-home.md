# Bickford (Nursing Home)

## Summary

Bickford was a nursing home in Connecticut where Henry Molaison lived from December 1980 until his death in 2008. Staff members provided daily care and participated in research by documenting his behavior and cognitive status. Henry became a beloved fixture at the facility, engaging in activities like Bingo, crossword puzzles, and crafts, while staff carefully maintained his privacy and anonymity.

## Key facts

- Henry was admitted to Bickford in December 1980, two hours from MIT. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p268]
- The facility was decorated in pastel greens, flowered wallpaper, and oak furniture. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p268]
- Henry participated in activities including choral practice, Bingo, Bible study, film watching, poetry readings, arts and crafts, and bowling. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p269]
- A dog named Sadie arrived as a puppy and remained throughout Henry's stay, often sitting next to his wheelchair. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p270]
- In October 1982, staff considered moving Henry to a mental health facility due to behavioral episodes, but his behavior returned to normal. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p271]
- By 2005, Henry had developed dementia, showing global deterioration of cognitive capacities beyond his original amnesia. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p278]
- Henry died at Bickford on December 2, 2008. [raw/books/neuroscience/Permanent Present Tense _ The Unforgettable Life of the - Suzanne Corkin.pdf#p287]
