# Glenn F. Wilson

## Summary

Glenn F. Wilson is a researcher at the Air Force Research Laboratory who authored Chapter 8 on strategies for psychophysiological assessment of situation awareness. His work extensively covers the application of psychophysiological measures including EEG, heart rate, and eyeblink analysis to operator state assessment in aviation and air traffic control contexts.

## Key facts

- Glenn F. Wilson is affiliated with the Air Force Research Laboratory. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p176]
- Wilson co-authored the only known study using psychophysiological measures for SA with Vidulich, Stratton, and Crabtree in 1994. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p177]
- Wilson and Fisher (1991) demonstrated that physiological data recorded during flight could identify flight segment with accuracies better than 90%. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p183]
- Wilson and Fisher (1995) showed 86% average accuracy in determining task categories and difficulty levels using EEG data. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p183]
- Wilson, Monett, and Russell (1997) achieved at least 85% accuracy in mental workload classification during simulated air traffic control. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p183]
- Wilson, Swain, and Ullsperger (1998) studied P300 components elicited in response to warning stimuli and their influence of task difficulty. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p181]
