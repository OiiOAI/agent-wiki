# Francisco Varela

## Summary

Francisco Varela (1946-2001) was a Chilean biologist, cognitive scientist, and neuroscientist who was a key figure in enactive cognitive science and neurophenomenology. He co-authored 'The Embodied Mind' with Evan Thompson and Eleanor Rosch. Together with Humberto Maturana, he developed the theory of autopoiesis. He introduced neurophenomenology as a research program combining phenomenological philosophy with neuroscience. He died on May 28, 2001, after suffering from Hepatitis C.
