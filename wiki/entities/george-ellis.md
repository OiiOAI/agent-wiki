# George Ellis

## Summary

George Ellis is a contemporary philosopher and mathematician who argues that physical indeterminacy creates 'causal slack' in physical systems, opening the door for top-down causation. Ellis applies this framework to understanding how higher-level features like purposes and meanings can have causal power in the physical world without violating physical laws.

## Key facts

- George Ellis argues that physical indeterminacy creates causal slack in physical systems, opening the door for top-down causation. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p181]
- Ellis uses computer programs as an example of top-down causation, where abstract entities constrain physical electron flows without violating physical law. [raw/books/neuroscience/Free Agents _ How Evolution Gave Us Free Will - Kevin J_ Mitchell.pdf#p213]
