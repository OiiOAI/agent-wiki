# Mae Jemison

## Summary

Mae Jemison is an American physician and astronaut who became the first Black woman to go into space in 1992 aboard the space shuttle Endeavour. After leaving NASA in 1993, she founded businesses, a foundation, and an international science camp, exemplifying perpetual curiosity and transitioning from one curiosity project to the next.

## Key facts

- Mae Jemison became the first Black woman to go into space on September 12, 1992, aboard the space shuttle Endeavour. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p163]
- She graduated with an MD from Cornell University Medical College in 1981 and worked in various medical centers before joining NASA. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p163]
- After leaving NASA in 1993, she founded businesses, a foundation, and the Earth We Share international science camp. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p163]
- She exemplifies how satisfaction of curiosity leads to the beginning of new curiosity adventures. [raw/books/productivity/Purposeful Curiosity_ the Power of Asking - Constantine Andriopoulos.pdf#p163]
