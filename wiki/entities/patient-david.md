# Patient David

## Summary

Patient David is a profoundly amnesic patient studied by Damasio for over twenty years. He suffered herpes simplex encephalitis at age 46, causing damage to both temporal lobes including the hippocampus. He cannot learn new facts (memory lasting less than one minute) and cannot recall most old facts from his life. Despite this, he has perfectly intact core consciousness—wakefulness, attention, purposeful behavior, background emotions, and the sense of self in the here-and-now are all normal.

## Key facts

- David's memory for new facts is normal only within a window of less than one minute; after that, he cannot retain any new information. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p115]
- David cannot recall most unique facts from his past life, including what his wife, children, and relatives look like or recognize them in photographs. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p115]
- David has intact wakefulness, sustained attention over long periods, continuous background emotions, and purposeful behavior appropriate to context. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p117]
- David demonstrates that factual knowledge at a unique and specific level is not a prerequisite for core consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p120]
- David's extensive temporal lobe damage (including hippocampus, medial cortices, polar temporal region, and amygdala) did not compromise core consciousness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p120]
