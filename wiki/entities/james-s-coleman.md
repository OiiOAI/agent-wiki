# James S. Coleman

## Summary

James S. Coleman (1929–1995) was a highly influential American sociologist who pioneered rational choice theory in sociology. He earned his Ph.D. from Columbia University in 1955, where he was mentored by Robert Merton, Paul Lazarsfeld, and Seymour Martin Lipset. His major works include the landmark 'Coleman Report' on educational equality, mathematical sociology texts, and especially Foundations of Social Theory (1990). He founded the journal Rationality and Society in 1989 and served as president of the American Sociological Association in 1992.
