# David Grether

## Summary

David Grether is an American economist who, together with Charles Plott, published a landmark 1979 study in the American Economic Review attempting to replicate and explain away the preference reversal phenomenon discovered by Lichtenstein and Slovic. Their rigorous investigation tested 13 alternative explanations and found that only one survived: that the psychologists' findings were correct and that individual choices systematically depend on the context of decision-making—a direct challenge to the coherence axioms of rational choice theory.

## Key facts

- Grether and Plott tested 13 alternative theories to explain preference reversals, including the hypothesis that results were caused by the experiments being conducted by psychologists. After exhaustive testing, only the original psychological explanation survived, leading them to acknowledge that 'individual choice depends on the context in which it is made'—a fundamental violation of the coherence doctrine of rational choice theory.
