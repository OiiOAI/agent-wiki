# The Amygdaloids

## Summary

The Amygdaloids is a rock band founded and led by neuroscientist Joseph LeDoux. The band's name is derived from the amygdala, the brain structure central to LeDoux's research on fear. In 2009, they released an album titled Theory of My Mind featuring Rosanne Cash, and the song 'Anxious' from that era became the namesake of LeDoux's 2015 book.

## Key facts

- Joseph LeDoux is a member of the rock band The Amygdaloids. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p2]
- In 2009 The Amygdaloids released an album titled Theory of My Mind on the Knock Out Noise label. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p16]
- Rosanne Cash sang two songs with LeDoux on the Theory of My Mind album. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p16]
- The song 'Anxious' was recorded during that session but not included on the album, and inspired the title of LeDoux's 2015 book. [raw/books/neuroscience/Anxious - Joseph LeDoux.pdf#p16]
