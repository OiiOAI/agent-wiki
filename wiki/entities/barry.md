# Barry

## Summary

Barry was an 80-year-old entrepreneur who, after retiring, decided to rebuild his neglected body working with trainer Beth Lewis. Starting from near-zero physical capability and constant pain, he progressed to performing pull-ups and mastering complex movement patterns, exemplifying the transformative power of exercise at any starting point.

## Key facts

- Barry spent fifty years sitting in a chair building his business, leaving him in terrible physical condition and almost constant pain by retirement. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p296]
- He began with basic exercises including abdominal breathing, segmental Cat/Cow, and toe movements, progressing to balance drills, walking lunges, and push-ups. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p297]
- Barry eventually progressed to hopping off yoga blocks and landing in a squat, and mastered getting up off the ground using only one arm. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p297]
- His stated goal was to be able to do a pull-up, which he achieved. [raw/books/health/Outlive.pdf#p297]
