# Isaiah

## Summary

Isaiah was a nine-year-old boy with severe ADHD and aggression who was unable to brush his teeth or tie his shoelaces and had been expelled from multiple schools. After participating in Julia Rucklidge's nutrient study and being randomized to the multinutrient group, he showed substantial improvement within weeks. By age 17, he was an avid skateboarder, had caught up on seven years of schooling, and was academically on track.
