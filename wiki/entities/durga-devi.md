# Durga Devi

## Summary

Durga Devi is the daughter of Michael Singer and Donna, born in August 1977 at Temple of the Universe. She was born into what had become a spiritual community and received traditional gifts of blessings for health, prosperity, and spirituality from Amrit and Mataji. Her birth coincided with Built with Love building an addition onto Donna's cabin to accommodate both her and the new baby.
