# Southwest Airlines

## Summary

Southwest Airlines is cited throughout the book as the one consistently profitable major US airline that operates according to maneuver conflict principles, despite having no apparent access to Boyd's concepts. Founded by Herb Kelleher, Southwest uses time as its primary strategic device through short ground times, point-to-point routes, no assigned seating, and rapid turnaround. The airline's practices—such as not charging egregious change fees or charging business travelers ten times vacation fares—contrast sharply with competitors who lost $19 billion in 2001-2002. Many ex-service members work at the airlines, possibly explaining the organic application of military principles.
