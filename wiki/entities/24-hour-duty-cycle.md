# 24-Hour Duty Cycle

## Summary

The 24-Hour Duty Cycle is the authors' example daily schedule that integrates all ten Vital Signs practices, showing how to weave movement, nutrition, standing work, and sleep hygiene into a typical day from 6:00 AM wake-up to 10:00 PM lights out.

## Key facts

- The schedule begins at 6:00 AM with waking, water with sea salt and lemon, and preparing for exercise. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4295-L4315]
- Exercise is scheduled from 6:30-7:30 AM, followed by cool-down walking of 3,000 steps. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4315-L4330]
- Breakfast, lunch, and dinner each include one-third of the daily produce and protein quota. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4330-L4345]
- The schedule includes 8,000-10,000 total daily steps through multiple walking sessions. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4330-L4345]
- Technology is turned off at 8:30 PM, followed by hot tub/shower, soft-tissue mobilizations, and reading before 10:00 PM lights out. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4355-L4370]

## Inferences

- Inference: The schedule demonstrates the practical integration of Vital Signs practices into daily life without requiring separate dedicated workout time.
