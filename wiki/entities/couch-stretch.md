# Couch Stretch

## Summary

The Couch Stretch is a hip extension exercise performed by positioning the body with one knee on the floor or couch and the other leg bent behind, with the torso upright. It has three progressively challenging positions and can be done on the floor against a wall or on a couch. The exercise involves squeezing the glutes while breathing deeply for three minutes per side.

## Key facts

- The Couch Stretch is essentially the Couch Test performed for longer duration. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L959-L962]
- The exercise can be done on the floor against a wall or on a couch. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L959-L962]
- Deep breathing while in the position helps challenge fascia and connective tissue differently than shallow breathing. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L968-L971]
- If you can't breathe in a position, you don't own that position. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L973-L974]
- The exercise has three progressively challenging positions. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L976-L1000]
- Each position involves squeezing the glutes while inhaling to a slow count of five, then relaxing as exhaling to a slow count of five, for three minutes. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L979-L985]
- Modifications include doing the stretch in shorter intervals, moving the knee away from the wall, or placing hands on a chair for support. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L974-L978]
