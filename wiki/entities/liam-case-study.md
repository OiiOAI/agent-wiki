# Liam

## Summary

Liam was a six-year-old boy with severe emotional dysregulation who had meltdown after meltdown at home, though he was described as a lovely child at school. After taking liquid minerals, he had not had another long meltdown for over three years. When he learned to swallow pills and switched to a broad-spectrum multinutrient formula, his parents report that only when they forget to provide the minerals or when he is overtired does he still have brief, mild meltdowns.

## Key facts

- Liam was having meltdown after meltdown at home, though school reported he was a lovely, sweet child with no emotional challenges. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p145]
- After starting liquid minerals, Liam has not had another long meltdown since then—for over three years. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p145]
- Liam still occasionally began to work up to a tantrum for several days, but in each case he was able to calm himself down and self-regulate his emotions. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p145]
- If Liam's parents forget to provide the minerals on too many occasions, or if he is especially overtired, he still has fifteen-minute meltdowns, but they are mild and brief compared to the hour-long loss of emotional control in the pre-supplement days.
