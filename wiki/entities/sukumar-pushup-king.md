# Sukumar (Push-up King)

## Summary

Sukumar is a man from Chennai, India, who transformed from someone who thought 'I'm not an exercise guy' into a regular gym-goer who took group exercise classes, made friends in spin class, and eventually started his own business using Tiny Habits to enable transformation in large organizations.

## Key facts

- Before starting Tiny Habits, Sukumar identified as 'not an exercise guy,' not into eating healthy food, and someone who had trouble sleeping. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p211]
- Sukumar started with two push-ups after brushing his teeth, focusing on form first. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p205]
- He recognized that his form was better even on that last push-up after a week, which inspired him to do more. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p205]
- Sukumar became skilled at finding his 'comfort edge' and pushed himself just enough to make progress without feeling pain. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p205]
- When he didn't want to do a lot of push-ups, he did two and felt good about keeping the habit alive. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p205]
- His identity shifted from someone bad at exercise to someone who understood strength building and was capable of it. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p211]
- His success with exercise emboldened him to challenge being a poor eater; he cut portion sizes, switched from white to brown rice, and lost his sweet tooth as a ripple effect. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p212]
- Sukumar eventually left the company where he'd worked for nineteen years to start his own business using Tiny Habits for organizational transformation. [raw/books/productivity/Tiny Habits_ The Small Changes that Change Everything - BJ Fogg.pdf#p212]
