# Antonio R. Damasio

## Summary

Antonio R. Damasio is a neuroscientist and author of 'Descartes' Error' (1994). He is described by reviewers as 'one of the world's foremost neurologists' and 'one of modern neuroscience's major thinkers.' He holds a medical degree (M.D.) and has conducted research on the frontal lobes and the relationship between emotion and reason.
