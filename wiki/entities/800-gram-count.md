# 800-Gram Count

## Summary

A nutrition assessment test where individuals estimate and measure their daily intake of micronutrient-rich foods to see if they meet the recommended 800 grams per day. The test serves as a spot check on micronutrient intake, as people often misjudge their eating habits.

## Key facts

- The 800-Gram Count is a spot check assessment for micronutrient needs, requiring individuals to estimate their daily intake of micronutrient-rich foods. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1552-L1555]
- The target is 800 grams of micronutrient-rich foods daily. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1556-L1557]
- A case study showed a client who considered herself a healthy eater was consuming only 100 grams of micronutrient-rich foods, far below the target. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L1554-L1558]
