# L.K. Fellows

## Summary

L.K. Fellows is a neuroscientist at McGill University who, together with A.R. Vaidya, authored Chapter 22 on the neuropsychology of decision-making from a frontal lobe perspective. Fellows' research uses lesion studies in humans to define the necessary contributions of frontal subregions to value-based choice, reward learning, and attention.

## Key facts

- Fellows and Farah (2005) identified a double dissociation between ventromedial and dorsolateral frontal lobe damage in humans on value-based decision-making tasks. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p275]
- Fellows' group demonstrated that VMF damage in humans disrupts the ability to direct attention to reward-predictive visual features. [raw/books/neuroscience/Decision Neuroscience _ An Integrative Perspective - Jean - Claude Dreher; Léon Tremblay; Institut de sciences.pdf#p283]
