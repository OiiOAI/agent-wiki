# Michael Merzenich

## Summary

Michael Merzenich is a pioneering neuroscientist at the University of California, San Francisco, recognized as 'the world's leading researcher on brain plasticity.' He demonstrated that adult brains remain plastic throughout life, showed how brain maps change in response to experience, and developed practical applications including Fast ForWord for learning disabilities and Posit Science for age-related cognitive decline.

## Key facts

- Merzenich was called 'the world's leading researcher on brain plasticity' by neuroscientist Ian Robertson. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p54]
- He developed the most commonly used design for the cochlear implant, which allows congenitally deaf children to hear. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p55]
- Merzenich discovered that cutting peripheral nerves caused brain maps to reorganize, proving adult brain plasticity. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p63]
- He proposed that practicing a new skill under the right conditions can change hundreds of millions and possibly billions of connections between nerve cells in brain maps. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p56]
- He founded Scientific Learning Corporation with Paula Tallal, Bill Jenkins, and Steve Miller in 1996 to develop Fast ForWord. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p80]
- He founded Posit Science to help people preserve brain plasticity as they age. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p95]
- He was born in Lebanon, Oregon, of German stock and became a professor at UCSF in 1971 in the department of otolaryngology and physiology. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p55]
- Torsten Wiesel, who had opposed adult plasticity, eventually accepted it and acknowledged Merzenich's pioneering experiments. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain That Changes Itself - Norman Doidge.pdf#p71]
