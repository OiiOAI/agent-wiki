# Randall Collins

## Summary

Randall Collins is an American sociologist who developed a more integrative conflict theory that moves beyond macro-level analysis to include microsociological perspectives. His work bridges conflict theory with phenomenological and ethnomethodological traditions.

## Key facts

- Collins stated that his main contribution to conflict theory was 'to add a micro level to these macro-level theories,' showing that 'stratification and organization are grounded in the interactions of everyday life.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p161]
- Collins focused on social stratification because it touches so many features of life, including 'wealth, politics, careers, families, clubs, communities, lifestyles.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p162]
- Collins criticized Marxian theory as 'a monocausal explanation for a multicausal world.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p162]
- Collins believed that conflict theory must focus on real life rather than abstract formulations, viewing people as animals whose actions are maneuvers to obtain various advantages. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p163]
- Collins developed five principles of conflict analysis: focus on material arrangements, examination of exploitation in inequality, analysis of culture from the viewpoint of interests and power, and commitment to scientific study. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p163]
- Collins argued that 'coercion leads to strong efforts to avoid being coerced' and that 'Control by material rewards leads to compliance to the extent that rewards are directly linked to the desired behavior.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p164]
- Randall Collins was born in 1944; his father was working for military intelligence at the end of World War II and later joined the State Department as a foreign service officer. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p410]
- He went to Harvard, where he changed his major half a dozen times, studying literature, mathematics, philosophy, and finally social relations. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p410]
- He was at Berkeley in the summer of 1964, just in time to join the civil-rights movement. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p411]
- Collins developed 'radical microsociology' focused on 'interaction ritual chains,' bundles of individual chains of interactional experience. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- Collins argues that 'all macrophenomena' can be translated 'into combinations of micro events' and that social structures may be translated into patterns of repetitive micro interaction. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]
- He states that 'only people do anything; structures, organizations, classes, and societies never do anything. Any causal explanation must ultimately come down to the actions of real individuals.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p381]

## Inferences

- Inference: Collins's conflict theory shows more influence from Weber, Durkheim, and phenomenology than from Marxian theory.
- Inference: Collins's experience of social stratification during his childhood in different countries likely influenced his sociological focus on conflict and inequality.
