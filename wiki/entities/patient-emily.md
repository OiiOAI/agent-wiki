# Patient Emily

## Summary

Patient Emily has associative prosopagnosia due to bilateral damage at the junction of occipital and temporal lobes (fusiform gyrus). She cannot recognize familiar faces visually but can recognize familiar voices. Her core consciousness is perfectly intact, and she is conscious of what she does not know—she generates core consciousness for every stimulus regardless of her ability to identify it.

## Key facts

- Emily could not recognize her own face in a mirror but recognized her voice from an audio recording; she applied similar logic to all familiar persons. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p162]
- Emily's damage was in Brodmann's areas 19 and 37 in the fusiform gyrus at the occipitotemporal junction of both hemispheres. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p165]
- Emily generates core consciousness for stimuli she cannot identify, being conscious both of what she knows and of what she does not know. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p163]
- Emily could still recognize emotions expressed in faces even when she could not identify whose face it was. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Feeling of What Happens_ Body and Emot - Antonio R. Damasio.pdf#p163]
