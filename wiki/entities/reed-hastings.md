# Reed Hastings

## Summary

Reed Hastings is the CEO of Netflix who led the company's dramatic pivot from DVD rental to streaming. The book analyzes Netflix's strategic challenges in 2018 including content costs, competition from Disney and Apple, and the crux opportunity to leverage international advantage for content creation.

## Key facts

- Hastings moved Netflix's center to online streaming starting in 2010, leaving DVD rental behind. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p24]
- Netflix faced rising content costs after Starz demanded $300 million versus $30 million annually for content. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p25]
- Disney planned to remove its content from Netflix in 2019 and launch its own streaming platform with 75 years of cultural capital. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p26]
- Netflix's cash profit was strongly negative—adding new debt to finance growth—with costs per subscriber exceeding revenue. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p24]
- The crux analysis identifies Netflix's stronger international position as an asymmetry to leverage against domestic competitors. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p33]
