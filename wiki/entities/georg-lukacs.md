# Georg Lukacs

## Summary

Georg Lukacs (1885-1971) was a Hungarian Marxist philosopher and sociologist who played a key role in developing Hegelian Marxism. His work 'History and Class Consciousness' (1922) was seminal in developing the concepts of reification and class consciousness.

## Key facts

- Lukacs characterized the commodity as 'the central, structural problem of capitalist society.'. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p169]
- The crucial difference between fetishism of commodities and reification lies in extensiveness: fetishism is restricted to the economic institution, while reification is applied by Lukacs to all of society. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p170]
- Class consciousness refers to the belief systems shared by those who occupy the same class position, and is neither the sum nor the average of individual consciousnesses. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p170]
- Lukacs argued that the structural position of the proletariat within capitalism gives it the unique ability to achieve class consciousness. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p171]
- Lukacs distinguished between a 'class in itself' (structurally created entity) and a 'class for itself' (class conscious of its position and mission). [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p171]
- The early work of Marx, especially 'The Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844,' which was more heavily influenced by Hegelian subjectivism, was largely unknown to Marxian thinkers until its publication in 1932. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p169]
