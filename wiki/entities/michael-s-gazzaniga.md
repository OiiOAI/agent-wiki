# Michael S. Gazzaniga

## Summary

A prominent American neuroscientist known for pioneering work on split-brain patients and the cognitive neuroscience of consciousness. He has spent over 45 years studying the biological bases of human uniqueness and is a leading figure in the field of cognitive neuroscience.

## Key facts

- Gazzaniga has spent over 45 years studying split-brain patients to understand brain function. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p21]
- He developed the 'left brain interpreter' concept based on split-brain research showing how the left hemisphere generates explanations for actions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p21]
- Gazzaniga received his graduate education at Caltech in the J. Alfred Prufrock house, surrounded by physicists. [raw/books/neuroscience/Human _ the science behind what makes us unique - Michael S_ Gazzaniga.pdf#p7]
