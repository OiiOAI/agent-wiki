# Richard Farley

## Summary

Richard Farley was an employee at ESL (a TRW subsidiary) in Silicon Valley who shot and killed 7 coworkers and wounded 10 others in 1988 after being fired for refusing to stop pursuing coworker Laura Black, who had rejected his romantic advances.

## Key facts

- Farley asked Laura Black to go out with him, and when she declined, he refused to accept her rejections. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
- He sent Laura Black a key to her front door to demonstrate he had access to her home. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
- When supervisors told Farley he would be fired for his behavior, he responded 'Not just you' to the question of whether he would kill them. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
- Farley was fired and banned from ESL premises but returned with shotguns, rifles, and handguns, blasting through the access doors. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
- The shooting occurred the day before Farley and Black were to appear in court to make a temporary restraining order permanent. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
