# Maurice Merleau-Ponty

## Summary

Maurice Merleau-Ponty (1908-1961) was a French phenomenological philosopher whose work on the lived body (Leib) profoundly influenced embodied cognitive science. His book 'The Structure of Behavior' (1942) analyzed behavior as form that cannot be decomposed into parts. He developed the concept of the habit-body and emphasized that our body comprises the habitual and the present. His later work 'Phenomenology of Perception' established phenomenology of the body as central to philosophy. He is a key figure bridging phenomenology and contemporary embodied approaches to mind.

## Key facts

- Merleau-Ponty introduced the notion of the habit-body in 'Phenomenology of Perception' while discussing phantom limb experience. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p42]
- He wrote in 'The Structure of Behavior' that 'our body comprises as it were two distinct layers, that of the habit-body and that of the body at this moment.'. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p42]
- The habitual body acts as guarantee for the body at this moment by being a developmental being thick with its own history. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p42]
- Sedimented patterns span and interweave the lived body and its environment, forming a unitary circuit of lived-body-environment. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p42]
- The nervous system links sensory surfaces and effectors within the body, integrating the organism as a mobile unity. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p56]
- Merleau-Ponty recognized that no animal is a mere passive respondent; every animal meets the environment on its own sensorimotor terms. [raw/books/neuroscience/Mind in Life _ Biology, Phenomenology, and the Sciences of - Evan Thompson.pdf#p56]
