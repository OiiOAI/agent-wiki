# Tristan

## Summary

Tristan was a 27-year-old British military munitions specialist who suffered a traumatic brain injury (TBI) in 2005 when hit by a drunk driver. Despite years of rehabilitation, he was left with extremely dysregulated emotions and explosive anger. Eight years post-injury, he began multinutrient treatment and over twelve months showed remarkable improvement in mood stability, eventually approaching excellent control.

## Key facts

- Tristan sustained injuries to his brain as well as numerous fractures when hit by a drunk driver in 2005. He was in intensive care requiring ventilation for two and a half weeks and transferred to a rehabilitation center for eighteen months. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p150]
- A psychiatric report five and a half years post-trauma stated that Tristan's continued mood changes and aggressive outbursts would continue to be barriers to complete rehabilitation and that his situation was expected to be permanent. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p151]
- By the end of twelve months of multinutrient treatment, Tristan approached the maximum score of 30 on a mood instability measure, indicating excellent control of his mood. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p151]
