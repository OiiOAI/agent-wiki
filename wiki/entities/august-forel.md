# August Forel

## Summary

August Forel (1848–1931) was Professor of Psychiatry at Zürich and author of 'Der Hypnotismus, seine Bedeutung und seine Handhabung' (1889). He was a supporter of the Nancy school's suggestion theory and introduced Freud to Bernheim. Though eventually highly critical of psychoanalysis, Forel's work provided an important synthesis of hypnotic theory that Freud reviewed favorably in 1889.
