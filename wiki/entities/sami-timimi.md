# Dr. Sami Timimi

## Summary

Dr. Sami Timimi is a leading child psychiatrist in Britain and one of the most prominent critics of the current approach to ADHD. Based in Lincoln, he investigates children's environments and social contexts before considering medication, and believes that most attention problems stem from environmental factors rather than inherent biological disorders.

## Key facts

- He inherited from his predecessor twenty-seven children who were being prescribed stimulant drugs for ADHD. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p239]
- He believes ADHD is not a diagnosis but a description of certain behaviors that sometimes occur together. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p241]
- He treats children like Michael by identifying underlying problems (such as absent fathers) and addressing them rather than relying on medication. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p239-241]
- He helped Aden's parents move him to a less chaotic school after discovering his 'ADHD' was actually a response to his teacher's chaotic classroom management. [raw/books/productivity/Stolen Focus _ Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think.pdf#p241]
