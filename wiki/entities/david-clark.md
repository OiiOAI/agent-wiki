# David Clark

## Summary

David Clark, known as 'Creature,' was a leader of the Outlaws motorcycle gang who was serving multiple life sentences in Union Correctional Institution. He became a sincere spiritual seeker attending Singer's prison meditation classes, developing a deep practice of meditation and chanting. He organized meditation sessions for other inmates in his cell block and was visited by Amrit Desai in solitary confinement at the Rock. His spiritual transformation demonstrated to Singer the transformative power of surrender regardless of one's past.

## Key facts

- David Clark was a leader of the Outlaws motorcycle gang, similar to the Hells Angels, and was serving multiple life sentences. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p112]
- He first met Singer in 1975 when he walked into a meditation class at Union Correctional Institution and introduced himself as 'Creature.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p112]
- David began meditating deeply and organized meditation sessions for men in his cell block. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p112]
- He was placed in the high-security lockup cells of the Rock while awaiting trial for discovering bodies of rival gang members. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p112]
- Amrit Desai visited David in solitary confinement, and during that meeting David was overcome with tears of love. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p113]
- At trial, David received time concurrent with his existing sentence, effectively working out his past karma without additional time. [raw/books/psychology/The Surrender Experiment_ My Journey into Life's Perfection - Michael A_ Singer.pdf#p114]
