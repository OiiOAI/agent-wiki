# GEICO

## Summary

GEICO exemplifies the 'cancer surgery formula' for turnarounds: the company had a magnificent underlying business ruined by management folly, and Berkshire's intervention simply cut away the foolishness to restore the core enterprise.

## Key facts

- GEICO had a perfectly magnificent business in a mess but still working; misled by success, they got to thinking they knew everything and suffered huge losses. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p205]
- The fix: cut out all the folly and go back to the perfectly wonderful business that was lying there—this is a very simple model repeated over and over. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p205]
- GEICO was a wonderful business combined with a bunch of foolishness that could easily be cut out, with management temperamentally and intellectually designed to cut it out. [raw/books/strategy/Poor Charlie's Almanack_ The Wit and Wisdo - Charles T. Munger, Peter D. Kaufman, Ed We.pdf#p206]
