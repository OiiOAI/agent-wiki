# Harold Garfinkel

## Summary

Harold Garfinkel (1917–1987) is the founder of ethnomethodology. He was born in Newark, New Jersey, and studied at the University of North Carolina, Harvard, Princeton, and Ohio State before joining UCLA in 1954, where he remained until retirement in 1987. He coined the term ethnomethodology at the 1954 ASA meetings while working on a jury project. His major work is Studies in Ethnomethodology (1967). Notable students include Harvey Sacks, Emmanuel Schegloff, and Gail Jefferson who developed conversation analysis.
