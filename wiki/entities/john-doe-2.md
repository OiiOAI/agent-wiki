# John Doe 2

## Summary

John Doe 2 was the name given to an alleged second suspect in the 1995 Oklahoma City bombing who was never found. The description was based on eyewitness Tom Kessinger's recollection, but the features he described actually belonged to Private Todd Bunting, who had visited Elliott's Body Shop a day after Timothy McVeigh rented the van. Kessinger had misattributed Bunting's features to the earlier rental, exemplifying source memory failure and unconscious transference in eyewitness testimony.

## Key facts

- The FBI pursued John Doe 2 based on mechanic Tom Kessinger's recollection of seeing two men when McVeigh rented the van, including a shorter, stockier man with dark hair and a blue and white cap. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p128]
- Kessinger's memory actually came from an unrelated visit a day later by Army Sergeant Michael Hertig and Private Todd Bunting, who also rented a van. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p128]
- Kessinger correctly recalled Bunting's features but misattributed them to the wrong episode, illustrating unconscious transference in eyewitness testimony. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p128]
