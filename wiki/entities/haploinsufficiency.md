# Haploinsufficiency

## Summary

Haploinsufficiency is a phenomenon where having only one functional copy of a gene (heterozygosity) is insufficient to produce a normal phenotype. Recent progress in gene discovery has demonstrated that the human genome is more sensitive to haploinsufficiency than previously appreciated. The severity and manifestation of haploinsufficiency varies from individual to individual, a phenomenon known as variable, partial, or incomplete penetrance.
