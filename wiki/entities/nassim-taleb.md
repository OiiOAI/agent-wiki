# Nassim Taleb

## Summary

Nassim Taleb is a scholar who coined the term 'Black Swan' for improbable high-impact events and developed the concept of 'antifragile' systems. His rule: assume technology that has survived X years will last another X years.

## Key facts

- Nassim Taleb warns: 'Don't cross a river if it is (on average) four feet deep.'. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p127]
- Taleb coined the term 'Black Swan' and authored the book with the same title. [raw/books/strategy/The Art of Thinking Clearly _ Better thinking, Better - Rolf Dobelli; Nicky Griffin.pdf#p167]
