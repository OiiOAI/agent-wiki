# Patient WB

## Summary

Patient WB was a twelve-year-old boy with bilateral frontal lobe abscesses who periodically saw the world rotated 180 degrees. His drawings of the Rey Complex Figure showed systematic inversion, providing objective evidence of his inverted predictive model. This case illustrates that normal perception requires the brain to make corrective inferences that we are normally unaware of.

## Key facts

- Patient WB had bilateral abscesses in his frontal lobes caused by sinusitis and periodically saw the world rotated through 180 degrees. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p198]
- WB's drawings of the Rey Complex Figure showed systematic inversion, providing objective evidence of his predictive model's inversion. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Hidden Spring - Mark Solms.pdf#p202]
