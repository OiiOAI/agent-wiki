# Ernst Sellin

## Summary

German biblical scholar whose work Freud cites to support the theory that Moses was murdered by the Jews. Sellin discovered the tradition of Moses' violent end, which Freud considers essential evidence for his reconstruction of Jewish religious history.

## Key facts

- Ernst Sellin discovered that the Jews, headstrong and unruly towards their lawgiver, rose against Moses one day, killed him, and threw off the religion of the Aten. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7230]
- Freud cites Sellin's judgment that the true religion of Moses became necessarily the property of a small circle, not found in the official cult or beliefs of the people. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7222]
- Sellin's tradition about the end of Moses flatly contradicted the official account and was far nearer the truth, according to Freud. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7237]
