# Medea

## Summary

Daughter of King Aeëtes of Colchis who fell in love with Jason and used her magic to help him accomplish the impossible tasks set for winning the Golden Fleece. She then fled with him and killed her own brother Apsyrtus to delay her father's pursuit.

## Key facts

- Medea conceived an overpowering passion for Jason and compounded charms enabling him to master the fire-breathing bulls and survive the dragon's teeth army. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p159]
- Medea conducted Jason to the oak where the Golden Fleece hung and used herb juice to put the dragon guardian to sleep. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p159]
- To escape King Aeëtes's pursuit, Medea persuaded Jason to kill her younger brother Apsyrtus and scatter the dismembered body so her father would stop to gather the pieces. [raw/books/philosophy/The Hero With a Thousand Faces (Paladin Bo - Joseph Campbell.pdf#p159]
