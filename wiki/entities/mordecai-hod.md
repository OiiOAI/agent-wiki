# Mordecai Hod

## Summary

Mordecai Hod was the head of the Israeli Air Force (IAF) who came to the Pentagon in fall 1967 to purchase F-4 Phantoms. Under his leadership, the IAF achieved remarkable success in the Six Day War, shooting down 60 Arab jets while losing only 10. His visit galvanized the U.S. Air Force to reconsider its reliance on missiles and reinforced the arguments for including guns on fighters.

## Key facts

- Hod led the IAF to a 6-to-1 kill ratio in the Six Day War, with every kill being a gun kill. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
- When asked how the IAF achieved sixty gun kills, he said 'Why waste a missile on an Arab?'. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
- He came to the Pentagon wearing 'the aura of a man who was an icon in the fighter-pilot community'. [raw/books/strategy/Boyd - Robert Coram.pdf#p220]
