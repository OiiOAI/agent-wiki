# Bonnie J. Kaplan

## Summary

Bonnie J. Kaplan, PhD, is an American-Canadian experimental psychologist with postdoctoral training in neurophysiology. She was a professor in the Department of Pediatrics at the University of Calgary, where she studied neurodevelopmental disorders and the biological basis of learning and attention problems. After encountering the micronutrient research of Tony Stephan and David Hardy in 1996, she began investigating the effects of broad-spectrum multinutrients on mental health, publishing results from 2001 onward. She is co-author of The Better Brain.

## Key facts

- Kaplan earned academic degrees in America as an experimental psychologist, with postdoctoral training in neurophysiology. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p16]
- She began publishing micronutrient research in 2001, showing that people not only got well but stayed well with none of the common side effects of psychiatric medications. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p16]
- She co-authored the APrON (Alberta Pregnancy Outcomes and Nutrition) study, tracking 2,200 pregnant women from 2008 onward. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p63]
- She has no financial ties to any supplement company, maintaining no conflicts of interest in her research. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Better Brain - Bonnie J. Kaplan.pdf#p21]
