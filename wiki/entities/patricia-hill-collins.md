# Patricia Hill Collins

## Summary

Patricia Hill Collins is a pioneering Black feminist sociologist known for her work on the matrix of domination, Black feminist thought, and intersectionality theory. She developed the concept of 'the matrix of domination' to describe intersecting hierarchical structures of oppression and privilege. Her major works include Black Feminist Thought: Knowledge, Consciousness, and the Politics of Empowerment (1990) and Fighting Words: Black Women and the Search for Justice (1998). Her work has been foundational in developing intersectionality theory and challenging the universalizing assumptions of mainstream feminist theory.
