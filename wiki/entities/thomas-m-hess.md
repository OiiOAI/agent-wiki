# Thomas M. Hess

## Summary

Thomas M. Hess is a professor of psychology at North Carolina State University whose research focuses on understanding influences of aging on cognitive functioning. He is a Fellow in multiple professional societies and has published extensively on aging and cognition.

## Key facts

- He is a professor of psychology at North Carolina State University. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p15]
- His research interest is in understanding both positive and negative influences of aging on cognitive functioning. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p15]
- He has edited three books and published numerous research articles on aging. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p15]
- He is a Fellow in the American Psychological Association, American Psychological Society, and Gerontological Society of America. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p15]
- He is on the editorial boards of Journal of Gerontology: Psychological Sciences, Aging, Neuropsychology, and Cognition. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p15]
- Thomas M. Hess is affiliated with North Carolina State University. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p289]
- Hess has extensively studied how schemas influence memory processing in older adults, finding that schema activation and use are minimally affected by aging. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p300]
- Hess and colleagues demonstrated that age differences in recall are greater for schema-inconsistent than schema-consistent information. [raw/books/strategy/Situation awareness _ analysis and measurement - edited by Mica R_ Endsley, Daniel J_ Garland.pdf#p300]
