# 21-Day Built to Move Challenge

## Summary

The 21-Day Built to Move Challenge is a structured program that introduces assessments, everyday practices, and mobilizations gradually over 21 days. Days 1-10 focus on taking tests while beginning to layer in practices, with increasing complexity and standing time each day.

## Key facts

- The first 10 days involve taking all ten Vital Sign assessments while beginning to practice floor sitting, nose breathing, sit-stands, and daily walking. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4400-L4430]
- Each day includes specific mobilizations from the book, with the selection varying throughout the program. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4400-L4430]
- Standing time increases gradually from 30 minutes on Day 9 to 2 hours 30 minutes by Day 21. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4430-L4450]
- Sit-stands increase by one each day, starting from 2 on Day 2 and reaching 21 on Day 21. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4400-L4430]
- The challenge is designed as a gateway to help people incorporate new habits into their lives. [raw/books/health/Built_to_Move.epub#L4450-L4460]

## Inferences

- Inference: The progressive nature of the challenge allows for gradual adaptation, reducing the risk of injury or burnout from sudden lifestyle changes.
