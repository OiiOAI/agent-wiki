# James C. Abegglen

## Summary

James C. Abegglen was one of the first Western scholars to systematically describe the Japanese postwar lifetime employment system in his 1958 book 'The Japanese Factory: Aspects of Its Social Organization'. His work became a foundational text in comparative industrial relations, though later scholars challenged aspects of his interpretation.

## Key facts

- James C. Abegglen published The Japanese Factory: Aspects of Its Social Organization in 1958. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p419]
- Abegglen's interpretation has been challenged by later writers for ignoring the Japanese small-business sector, where lifetime employment is not the rule. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p419]
- Abegglen noted specific features of Japanese factory organization including employee training practices and supervision patterns. [raw/books/sociology/Trust - Francis Fukuyama.pdf#p419]

## Inferences

- Inference: Abegglen's early work established the vocabulary and framework through which Western scholars would discuss Japanese employment practices for decades.
