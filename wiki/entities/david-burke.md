# David Burke

## Summary

David Burke was a USAir employee who hijacked Flight 1771 in 1990 and crashed it into the ground, killing all 43 people aboard, after being fired from the airline for stealing $69 and blaming his supervisor Ray Thompson.

## Key facts

- Burke had a history including drug trafficking, shoplifting, auto theft, and violence toward his girlfriend, including beating her, cutting her car wires, and threatening her with a gun. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- He had obtained a restraining order from his girlfriend and left a death threat on his supervisor Ray Thompson's answering machine, blaming Thompson for his problems. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- Another USAir employee lent Burke a .44 magnum revolver, which he used to kill Thompson on the plane. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- USAir failed to revoke Burke's airport ID badge after firing him, allowing him to pass through security with his weapon. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
- Burke wrote on an airsickness bag: 'Hi Ray. I think it's sort of ironical that we end up like this. I asked for some leniency for my family, remember? And I got none. And you'll get none.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p100]
