# Judith Butler

## Summary

Judith Butler argues that gender and sexuality are both performances based on repetition. She contends that heterosexuality claims 'naturalness' through constant repetition. Her concept of 'gender performativity' suggests sexuality is a form of drag. She offers hope through 'repetitive disruption' to undermine heteronormative repetition.
