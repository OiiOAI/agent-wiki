# David Marr

## Summary

David Marr was a towering figure in neuroscience, computer vision, and AI whose work ranks among the most important contributions to cognitive science. His computational model of vision depicted visual processing as a mostly feedforward analyzer of incoming sensory information, which was the standard picture for many years even into the twenty-first century.

## Key facts

- Marr was a towering figure in neuroscience, computer vision, and AI whose work ranks among the most important contributions to cognitive science. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p17]
- In Marr's model, visual processing starts by detecting basic ingredients in incoming signals, and from there layered processing slowly builds toward more complex understanding through operations like edge or stripe detection. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p17]
- The Marr model was for many years the standard picture not just in artificial vision but in neuroscience too, even into the twenty-first century. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p17]
- Notably absent from Marr's model was the backward direction of influence running from deep within the brain down toward the eyes and other sensory organs. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Experience Machine_ How Our Minds Pred - Andy Clark.pdf#p18]
