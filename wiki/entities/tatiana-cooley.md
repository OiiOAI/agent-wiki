# Tatiana Cooley

## Summary

Tatiana Cooley was the National Memory Champion of the United States in 1999, demonstrating exceptional ability to memorize thousands of numbers and words using elaborative encoding techniques. Despite her championship memory, she was highly absent-minded in everyday life and relied on sticky notes.

## Key facts

- Cooley won the 1999 US Memory Championship by memorizing thousands of numbers, words, faces, names, poems, and card decks using visual imagery and association techniques. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p65]
- Despite her championship memory abilities, Cooley considered herself 'incredibly absent-minded' and depended on to-do lists and Post-it Notes. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p65]
- Her reliance on external memory aids despite mnemonic expertise illustrates the distinction between transience and absent-mindedness. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p66]
