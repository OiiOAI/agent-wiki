# The Claustrum

## Summary

The claustrum is a thin sheet of brain tissue located below the cerebral cortex. Little was known about it until Crick and Koch's final hypothesis proposed it as the neural structure mediating the unity of consciousness. The claustrum connects to and exchanges information with almost all sensory and motor regions of the cortex as well as the amygdala. Crick compared it to the conductor of an orchestra because its neuroanatomical connections could allow it to bind together and coordinate the various brain regions necessary for conscious awareness.
