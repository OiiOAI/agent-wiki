# Dean Falk

## Summary

Dean Falk is a paleoneurologist who has disputed Ralph Holloway's identification of the lunate sulcus in australopithecine brains. She argues that australopithecine brains retain the morphology characteristic of ape brains, contrary to Holloway's interpretation of humanlike reorganization.

## Key facts

- Dean Falk disputed Holloway's identification of the lunate sulcus feature in australopithecine endocasts. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p515]
- Falk argues that australopithecine brains retain the morphology characteristic of ape brains, contrary to Holloway's interpretation. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Symbolic Species - Terrence W. Deacon.pdf#p515]
