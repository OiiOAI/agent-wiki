# Presenilin-2

## Summary

Presenilin-2 (PS2) is a component of the γ-secretase multiprotein complex encoded by a gene on chromosome 1. Mutations in presenilin-2 cause autosomal dominant early-onset AD, typically by increasing Aβ42 production.

## Key facts

- Presenilin-2 is a component of the γ-secretase complex, encoded by a gene on chromosome 1. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
- Presenilin-2 was one of the first three genes identified for autosomal dominant early-onset AD, along with APP and presenilin-1. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
- Presenilin-2 mutations account for approximately 10 mutations in familial early-onset AD. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p1620]
