# Mayfair Club

## Summary

The Mayfair Club was a New York-based club where exceptional poker and backgammon players gathered. It served as the training ground for a remarkable group of poker professionals who went on to achieve significant success at the World Series of Poker, collectively earning multiple bracelets and millions in tournament earnings.

## Key facts

- The Mayfair Club was located in New York. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1975-L1978]
- The club was a gathering place for playing backgammon and poker. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1975-L1978]
- Notable members included Erik Seidel, Dan Harrington, Steve Zolotow, and Jason Lester. [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1975-L1978]
- Members of this group earned a combined total of seven World Series of Poker bracelets (excluding Erik Seidel's eight additional bracelets). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1975-L1978]
- Combined tournament earnings of this group were nearly $18 million (excluding Erik Seidel's $32 million). [raw/books/strategy/Thinking_in_Bets.epub#L1975-L1978]
