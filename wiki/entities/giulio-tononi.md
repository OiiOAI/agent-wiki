# Giulio Tononi

## Summary

Giulio Tononi is an Italian neuroscientist and the creator of Integrated Information Theory (IIT) of consciousness. He previously worked at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and now leads the Center for Sleep and Consciousness at the University of Madison. Tononi's theory proposes that consciousness is identical to integrated information (Φ), measured by cause-effect structure within a system.

## Key facts

- Tononi developed Integrated Information Theory (IIT), proposing that consciousness equals integrated information (Φ). [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p66]
- In 1998, Tononi and Edelman published a landmark paper proposing that consciousness is both informative and integrated. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p56]
- Tononi and Sporns developed 'neural complexity' as a measure of consciousness in the 1990s. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p60]
