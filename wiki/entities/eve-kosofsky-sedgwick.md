# Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick

## Summary

Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick is one of the early pioneers of queer theory. Her book 'Epistemology of the Closet' explores the concepts of 'the closet' and 'coming out' as politically charged processes that crystallize homosexual identity.
