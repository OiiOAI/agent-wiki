# Hakwan Lau

## Summary

Hakwan Lau, with David Rosenthal, developed the first formal neural account of higher-order consciousness, proposing that prefrontal cortex circuits (dorsal lateral, ventral lateral, frontal pole) constitute the core circuitry for conscious awareness. Lau has also addressed criticisms of HOT and demonstrated that prefrontal activity can carry fine-grain perceptual content.

## Key facts

- Lau and Rosenthal identified the dorsal lateral prefrontal cortex, ventral lateral prefrontal cortex, and frontal pole as key nodes in the higher-order consciousness network. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p279]
- Lau and Richard Brown proposed HOROR theory — that higher-order states alone may be sufficient for conscious experience even when first-order states are absent. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p277]
- Lau and Odegaard showed using sophisticated methods that detailed content can be decoded from prefrontal neural activity signals, addressing criticisms that prefrontal areas lack fine-grain representations. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Deep History of Ourselves_ 4 - Billion - Year Story of How - Joseph E LeDoux; Caio Da Silva Sorrentino.pdf#p281]
