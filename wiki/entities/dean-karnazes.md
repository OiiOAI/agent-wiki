# Dean Karnazes

## Summary

Dean Karnazes is a 44-year-old ultramarathoner known for completing 50 marathons in 50 consecutive days across 50 different states, as well as running 350 miles without stopping. He represents an example of exercise dependence, noting that 10-20% of the time he thinks of running as an addiction. He averages 70-90 miles per week and has maintained this routine for over 15 years, with the longest break being just 3 days when he had the flu. He describes craving the feeling of bliss or satiety after exercise, and when unable to exercise feels trapped and ready to implode.
