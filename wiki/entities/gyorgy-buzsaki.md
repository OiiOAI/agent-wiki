# György Buzsáki

## Summary

György Buzsáki is a Hungarian-American neuroscientist and the author of 'The Brain from Inside Out'. He is a Professor at New York University and Rutgers University. His research focuses on the mechanisms of brain circuits, memory, and cognition. He received the Cortical Discoverer Prize from the Cajal Club in 2001 and has been a leading figure in hippocampal and cortical circuitry research.

## Key facts

- Buzsáki's childhood curiosity about explanations never evaporated, leading him to become a scientist striving to understand the true meaning behind explanations. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p10]
- His main intellectual influences include his mentor Endre Grastyán and postdoctoral advisor Cornelius (Case) Vanderwolf, both of whom shared his frustration with explanatory words that don't truly explain. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p11]
