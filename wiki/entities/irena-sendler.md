# Irena Sendler

## Summary

Irena Sendler was a Polish woman who, as a child, was told by her doctor father that 'if you see someone drowning, you must jump in to save them.' During World War II, this value of helping led her to shelter and feed Jewish neighbors, create thousands of false papers to aid families in escaping the Warsaw Ghetto, and personally smuggle children out disguised as a social worker checking for typhus. She was arrested by the Gestapo and sentenced to death but escaped with help from a guard. Despite this, she continued her work and saved at least 2,500 Jewish children. Her story illustrates 'walking your values' even in extreme circumstances.
