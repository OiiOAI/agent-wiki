# Vartan Bhanji

## Summary

The Vartan Bhanji is an institutionalized gift exchange ritual practiced in parts of Pakistan and India. The custom formalizes reciprocal obligations through a carefully structured system where departing guests receive gifts of sweets, with the host explicitly noting which items are repayment and which are new gifts. This system ensures that outstanding obligations are never fully eliminated, creating an ongoing cycle of social indebtedness and reciprocal exchange.
