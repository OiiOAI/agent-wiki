# Paul Ingram

## Summary

Paul Ingram was a Washington State sheriff's deputy who in the mid-1990s confessed to sexual abuse of his daughters and participation in bizarre cult activities including satanic rituals, animal sacrifices, and baby murders. Under coercion, he produced full-blown 'memories' he came to believe he had previously repressed. No evidence was ever produced for any of the confessed activities.

## Key facts

- Paul Ingram confessed to the sexual abuse of his two daughters and participation in a bizarre cult that included satanic rituals, animal sacrifices, and the murder of babies. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p164]
- In response to coercion and bullying from police officers, Ingram reported full-blown 'memories' of these ghastly activities that he came to believe he had previously repressed. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p164]
- No hard evidence for any of the confessed activities was ever produced, and Ingram eventually retracted his confession, but he served time until his release in 2003. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Seven Sins of Memory - Daniel L. Schacter.pdf#p164]
