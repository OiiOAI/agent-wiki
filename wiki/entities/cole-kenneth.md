# Kenneth Cole

## Summary

Kenneth Cole (1900-1986) was an American biophysicist who developed the voltage-clamp technique and made pioneering measurements showing that membrane conductance increases dramatically during the action potential.

## Key facts

- Cole and Howard Curtis demonstrated in 1939 that membrane conductance increases dramatically during the action potential, providing evidence that action potentials result from increased membrane permeability. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p260]
- Cole developed the voltage-clamp apparatus that enabled Hodgkin and Huxley to make the quantitative measurements leading to the Hodgkin-Huxley model. [raw/books/neuroscience/Eric Kandel PRINCIPLES OF NEURAL SCIENCE Sixth Edition - Eric R Kandel; John Koester; Sarah Mack; Steven Siegelbaum.pdf#p260]
