# Bill Gates

## Summary

Bill Gates is cited as an example of an Essentialist who creates space to think. He takes 'Think Week' twice a year, secluding himself for a full week to read articles, books, study technology, and think about the bigger picture. This practice dates back to the 1980s and continued through Microsoft's height of expansion.

## Key facts

- Bill Gates takes Think Week twice a year during the busiest and most frenetic time in Microsoft's history, spending the week reading and thinking without daily distractions. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p67]
- During Think Week, Gates reads articles and books, studies technology, and thinks about the bigger picture; his record is 112 articles in one week. [raw/books/productivity/Essentialism_ The Disciplined Pursuit of L - Mckeown, Greg.pdf#p67]
