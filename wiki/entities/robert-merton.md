# Robert Merton

## Summary

Robert K. Merton (1910-2003) was a student of Talcott Parsons who authored some of the most important statements on structural functionalism and helped give it continuing usefulness. Unlike Parsons, Merton favored middle-range theories rather than grand overarching theories. He criticized extreme postulates of functional analysis and introduced concepts like manifest and latent functions (intended versus unintended consequences), dysfunctions (negative consequences), and nonfunctions (irrelevant consequences). His analysis of the relationship between culture, structure, and anomie explained how disjunctions between cultural goals and social-structural means lead to deviance. He also developed the concepts of role-set and status-set. Merton's work is notable for being more favorable toward Marxian theories than Parsons's work.

## Key facts

- Merton criticized three postulates of early functional analysis: functional unity of society, universal functionalism, and indispensability. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p142]
- Merton introduced the concept of "dysfunction" to describe negative consequences that social structures or institutions can have for other parts of the social system. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p143]
- Merton's concepts of manifest functions (intended consequences) and latent functions (unintended consequences) became important additions to functional analysis. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p146]
- Merton defined anomie as "an acute disjunction between the cultural norms and goals and the socially structured capacities of members of the group to act in accord with them". [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p147]
- Merton's anomie theory explains deviance as resulting from the disjunction between cultural values (like material success) and the social-structural means available to achieve them. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p147]
- Merton favored middle-range theories rather than grand overarching theories, unlike Parsons. [raw/books/sociology/现代社会学理论 - 第7版 - 美 - 乔治 - 瑞泽尔 - 后浪大学堂 - 2018.pdf#p142]
