# Richard Rumelt

## Summary

Richard Rumelt is a strategy scholar and consultant known for developing the concept of the crux in strategic thinking. He served as a professor at UCLA Anderson School of Management and is described as a global authority on strategy. His previous works include Good Strategy/Bad Strategy, and The Crux represents his latest contribution to strategic management scholarship and practice.

## Key facts

- Rumelt was a professor at UCLA Anderson School of Management. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p21]
- He worked with Shell's legendary head of strategy Pierre Wack, learning to see correlations among elements of a situation. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p35]
- He observed that skilled strategists have a nose for what is vital and an ability to concentrate energy on critical issues. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p36]
- Gary Hamel described Rumelt as the thinker with the biggest impact on his career. [raw/books/strategy/The Crux - Richard Rumelt.pdf#p3]
