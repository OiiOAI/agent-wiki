# Richard Emerson

## Summary

Richard Emerson (1925–1982) was an American sociologist who developed integrative exchange theory and the power-dependence approach. He earned his Ph.D. from the University of Minnesota in 1955 and joined the University of Washington faculty in 1965. His 1962 paper on power-dependence relations became a citation classic. He collaborated with Karen Cook for a decade on the empirical development of social exchange theory using computerized laboratory research.
