# Moses

## Summary

In Freud's controversial thesis in Moses and Monotheism, Moses was not Jewish but an Egyptian aristocrat who introduced Akhenaten's Aten monotheism to the Jewish people during the Exodus (c. 1350 BCE). According to Freud, Moses was murdered by his people for imposing this 'spiritualized' religion, and his followers (the Levites) later preserved his memory until a compromise with the Yahweh worshippers occurred at Kadesh.

## Key facts

- The name 'Moses' is derived from Egyptian vocabulary, meaning 'child,' as an abbreviation of names like 'Amen-mose' or 'Ptah-mose'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7183]
- Freud argues that the legend of Moses being rescued from the Nile is an inverted exposure legend—the humble first family (Jewish) was the real one, and the aristocratic second family (Egyptian royalty) was fabricated. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7189]
- Freud identifies circumcision as Egyptian in origin, arguing that Moses imposed this custom on the Jews as a mark of consecration making them 'a holy nation.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7200]
- According to Freud, Moses was murdered by the Jews in a revolt against his harsh monotheistic religion, as discovered by Ernst Sellin in 1922 from hints in the Prophets. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7209]
- The Levites were originally the followers and retinue of Moses who survived his death and preserved his memory. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p7211]

## Inferences

- Inference: Freud theorizes that the god Yahweh was originally a Midianite volcano god, and the later Jewish religion represents a synthesis between Moses's Aten religion and the Midianite Yahweh worship, with Yahweh absorbing Moses's achievements.
- Inference: The 'return of the repressed' in Jewish religious history mirrors the psychoanalytic concept—Moses's killed teaching survived unconsciously and eventually returned to shape the evolved religion.

## Uncertainties

- Uncertain: Freud acknowledges that his reconstruction lacks direct historical evidence and is based on psychoanalytic inference about tradition and distortion.
- Uncertain: Whether the historical Moses was a priest, prince, or provincial governor, or combinations of these roles.
