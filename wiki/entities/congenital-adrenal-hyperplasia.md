# Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia

## Summary

Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) is an autosomal recessive genetic deficiency in corticosteroid synthesis that results in overproduction of testosterone and related androgens. In female fetuses, excess androgens lead to virilization of external genitalia. Early exposure to androgens is correlated with subsequent changes in gender-related behaviors.

## Key facts

- CAH is an autosomal recessive deficiency occurring in approximately 1 in 10,000-15,000 live births that results in overproduction of testosterone and related androgens. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1361]
- In girls born with CAH, excess androgens lead to some masculinization of the external genitalia, called virilization. [raw/books/neuroscience/Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition (Principles of - Eric R_ Kandel; James H_ Schwartz; Thomas M_ Jessell; Steven.pdf#p1361]
- Girls with CAH tend to exhibit toy preferences and play typical of boys, and show increased incidence of homosexual and bisexual orientation.
