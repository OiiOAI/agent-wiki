# Joe Kable

## Summary

Joseph Kable is a psychology professor at the University of Pennsylvania and primary investigator at the Kable Lab, which studies decision-making, intertemporal choice, and the neural basis of valuation. Duke cites his research on the neural pathways involved in imagining the future and remembering the past, which informs the book's discussion of mental time travel. His collaborative work with Paul Glimcher on the neural correlates of subjective value during intertemporal choice is frequently referenced.
