# James Gibson

## Summary

James Gibson (1904-1979) was an American psychologist known for his ecological approach to perception. He argued that we often perceive the world in terms of 'affordances'—opportunities for action such as a door for opening or a ball for catching—rather than action-independent representations of 'the way things are.'
