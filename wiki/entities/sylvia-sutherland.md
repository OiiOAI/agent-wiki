# Sylvia Sutherland

## Summary

John's wife and travel companion. She shares her husband's technophobic attitude, finding motorcycles and mechanical things alienating. She is perceptive and emotionally sensitive, noticing the sadness in commuting drivers and identifying with the narrator's observations about modern life.

## Key facts

- Sylvia has a soft voice and becomes irritated when interrupted while talking, especially combined with background noise like a dripping faucet. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p15]
- She said technology is 'just a whole other thing,' likening it to garbage when in a less thoughtful mood. [raw/books/philosophy/Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance - Robert M. Pirsig.pdf#p13]
