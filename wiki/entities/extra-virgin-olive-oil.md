# Extra-Virgin Olive Oil

## Summary

Extra-virgin olive oil (EVOO) is a staple of the Mediterranean diet and Genius Food #1. It contains oleocanthal, a phenol with anti-inflammatory effects comparable to ibuprofen, and has been shown to help clear amyloid plaque and protect against cognitive decline.

## Key facts

- Oleocanthal in extra-virgin olive oil has anti-inflammatory effects comparable to taking a small dose of ibuprofen without side effects. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p37]
- Oleocanthal helps the brain clear amyloid plaque by increasing the activity of enzymes that degrade it. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p37]
- Consumption of up to a liter of EVOO per week has been shown to protect the brain against decline and improve cognitive function. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p37]
- EVOO blocks fatty acid synthase, an enzyme in fatty tissue that creates fat from excess dietary carbohydrates. [raw/books/health/Genius Foods - Max Lugavere.pdf#p38]
