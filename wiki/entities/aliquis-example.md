# Aliquis Example

## Summary

Freud's analysis of a young man who forgot the word 'aliquis' in a Virgil quotation ('Exoriare...aliquis nostris ex ossibus ultor'). The forgetting revealed unconscious thoughts about menstruation and the desire for descendants.

## Key facts

- The young man forgot 'aliquis' (an indefinite pronoun) in the Virgil quotation while discussing the social status of his generation. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2009]
- Free associations revealed thoughts about menstruation, relics, and liquefaction, connected to the phrase 'blood that starts to flow on a particular day'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2011]
- The young man's unconscious was expressing a contrary thought: 'Have you really so keen a wish for descendants? That is not so.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological - Sigmund Freud, James Strachey , Mark Solms.pdf#p2013]
