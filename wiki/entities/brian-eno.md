# Brian Eno

## Summary

English musician, record producer, and visual artist best known for pioneering ambient music. He coined the term 'scenius' as an alternative to the 'lone genius' myth of creativity, describing how great ideas emerge from collaborative scenes of creative individuals rather than isolated individuals.

## Key facts

- Brian Eno coined the term 'scenius' to describe creative environments where groups of artists, curators, thinkers, and tastemakers collectively nurture ideas. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p16]
- Eno's 'scenius' model describes a group of creative individuals making up an 'ecology of talent,' as opposed to the lone genius myth. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p16]
