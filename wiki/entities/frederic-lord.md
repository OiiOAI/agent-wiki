# Frederic Lord

## Summary

Frederic Lord was a statistician who in 1967 posed a paradox about comparing weight gains between groups with different initial weights, now known as Lord's paradox.

## Key facts

- Lord posed his paradox in 1967 regarding diet effects on student weight gain. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L1840-L1845]
- Lord concluded that both statisticians' conclusions were 'visibly correct,' and the question could not be answered rigorously from available data. [raw/books/strategy/The_Book_of_Why.epub#L1882-L1888]
