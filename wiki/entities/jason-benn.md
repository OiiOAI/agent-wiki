# Jason Benn

## Summary

Jason Benn is a former financial consultant who learned to code through an intensive deep work regimen after recognizing that his job could be automated by Excel macros. He studied 18 programming books, locked himself in a room with textbooks and notecards, and eventually excelled at Dev Bootcamp, graduating as the top student. He then landed a job at a San Francisco tech startup earning $100,000—more than double his previous $40,000 salary.
