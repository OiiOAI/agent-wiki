# Samuel Zelman

## Summary

Samuel Zelman was a surgeon in Topeka, Kansas who in the 1960s encountered a patient with a fatty liver despite being a non-drinker. The patient drank up to twenty bottles of Coca-Cola daily, taking in an extra 1,600 calories. Zelman recruited nineteen other obese but nonalcoholic subjects for a clinical study, finding that all bore signs of impaired liver function similar to alcoholics. His work contributed to identifying what would later be called NAFLD/NASH.
