# The Chimp Paradox: How Our Impulses and Emotions Can Determine Our Lives

## Summary

The Chimp Paradox by Dr. Steve Peters is a psychology book that explores how the mind works and how understanding its different components can help manage thoughts, emotions, and behavior. The book uses the metaphor of a 'Chimp' to represent the emotional, impulsive side of the brain, a 'Human' to represent rational thinking, and a 'Computer' to represent stored programs and habits. Part 1 of the book focuses on understanding and exploring the inner mind.
