# The Lean Startup

## Summary

The Lean Startup by Eric Ries provides a scientific approach to creating and managing startups. It introduces the Build-Measure-Learn feedback loop and emphasizes validated learning over traditional business planning. The book argues that entrepreneurship is a form of management requiring new disciplines suited to extreme uncertainty, and demonstrates how this approach can be applied across industries—from Silicon Valley startups to Fortune 500 companies and government agencies. The methodology draws from lean manufacturing principles pioneered at Toyota.
