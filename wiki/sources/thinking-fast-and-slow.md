# Thinking, Fast and Slow

## Summary

Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman presents a comprehensive theory of human judgment and decision-making, built on decades of research conducted primarily with collaborator Amos Tversky. The book introduces a dual-system framework distinguishing between fast, automatic System 1 and slow, deliberate System 2 thinking. It documents systematic biases in intuitive judgment (heuristics) and explains Prospect Theory, the behavioral economic model that earned Kahneman the 2002 Nobel Prize in Economics.
