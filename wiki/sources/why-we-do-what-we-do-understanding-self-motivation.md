# Why We Do What We Do: Understanding Self-Motivation

## Summary

This book by psychologist Edward L. Deci with Richard Flaste examines the dynamics of personal autonomy and its relationship to motivation, responsibility, and human flourishing. Based on over twenty-five years of empirical research (largely with collaborator Richard Ryan), the book argues that true motivation and responsible behavior must come from within the self rather than from external controls. Deci contends that controlling approaches—rewards, punishments, threats, surveillance, and competitive pressure—undermine intrinsic motivation and lead to alienation, while autonomy-supportive environments enhance both motivation and performance. The book introduces three basic psychological needs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness, arguing these are essential for healthy development and authentic living.
