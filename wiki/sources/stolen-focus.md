# Stolen Focus: Why You Can't Pay Attention - and How to Think

## Summary

Stolen Focus (2021) by Johann Hari investigates the crisis of declining attention spans in the modern world. Drawing on interviews with over 250 experts across 30,000 miles of travel, Hari identifies twelve deep causes of our attention crisis, including information overload, the destruction of flow states, sleep deprivation, and the collapse of sustained reading. The book argues this is a systemic problem being done to us all by powerful forces, not merely a personal failing.
