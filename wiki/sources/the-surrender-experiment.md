# The Surrender Experiment: My Journey into Life's Perfection

## Summary

A spiritual autobiography by Michael A. Singer recounting his journey from a graduate economics student in 1970 who discovered he could observe his own thoughts, through decades of meditation practice, to becoming CEO of a billion-dollar healthcare software company—all by practicing surrender to life's flow rather than resisting it. The book chronicles pivotal moments including a transformative meditation in Ocala National Forest, building a meditation house in Gainesville, teaching at Santa Fe Community College, and encountering Baba Muktananda.
