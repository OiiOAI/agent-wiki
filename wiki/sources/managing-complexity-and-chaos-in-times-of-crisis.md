# Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis

## Summary

A field guide published by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre and the Cynefin Centre, providing decision-makers with a four-stage approach (Assess, Adapt, Exapt, Transcend) to navigate crises using the Cynefin framework as a compass. The guide emphasizes sense-making, constraint management, distributed engagement, and radical repurposing of available assets to enhance organisational resilience in complex and chaotic environments.

## Key facts

- The book is a joint effort by the Joint Research Centre (JRC), the European Commission's science and knowledge service, and the Cynefin Centre. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p2]
- The authors are Dave Snowden (creator of the Cynefin framework) and Alessandro Rancati (architect and designer at JRC). [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p3]
- The book proposes a four-stage approach: assess the type of crisis and initiate a response, adapt to the new pace and build sensing networks, repurpose existing structures for radical innovation, and transcend the crisis by formalising lessons learned. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p3]
- Published in Luxembourg by the Publications Office of the European Union in 2021. [raw/books/strategy/Managing complexity (and chaos) in times of crisis Cynefin.pdf#p2]
