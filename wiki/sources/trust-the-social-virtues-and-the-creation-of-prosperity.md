# Trust: The Social Virtues and the Creation of Prosperity

## Summary

A book by Francis Fukuyama examining how social trust influences economic prosperity. Fukuyama argues that trust—the expectation of honest, cooperative behavior among individuals—functions as a crucial "social virtue" that enables large-scale economic cooperation and creates prosperity. The book was first published in 1995 and later released as a Free Press Paperback Edition in 1996.
