# Attached: Are you Anxious, Avoidant or Secure?

## Summary

Attached is a psychology book that translates decades of adult attachment research into practical guidance for romantic relationships. Written by psychiatrists Amir Levine and Rachel Heller, it explains how the attachment styles—Secure, Anxious, and Avoidant—determine behavior in intimate relationships and provides tools to help readers identify their own style and that of partners.
