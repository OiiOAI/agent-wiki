# Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions

## Summary

Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions is a seminal work in naturalistic decision making by Gary A. Klein, first published in 1998 with a 20th anniversary edition in 2017 by MIT Press. The book challenges traditional decision research by documenting how experienced decision makers—such as firefighters, military commanders, nurses, and chess masters—make effective decisions under time pressure, uncertainty, and high stakes using sources of power like intuition, mental simulation, metaphors, and storytelling rather than formal analytical methods.
