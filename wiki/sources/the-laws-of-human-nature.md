# The Laws of Human Nature

## Summary

A comprehensive psychology book published in 2018 that examines the fundamental forces driving human behavior, presenting 18 laws of human nature based on psychological research, evolutionary biology, and historical examples. The book aims to help readers understand both their own irrational tendencies and the manipulative behaviors of others, offering strategies for developing rationality, empathy, and self-mastery.
