# Affective Neuroscience: The Foundations of Human and Animal Emotions

## Summary

This book presents a comprehensive framework for understanding emotions through the lens of neuroscience, arguing that basic emotional systems are evolutionarily conserved across all mammals. Panksepp proposes that distinct brain circuits generate core emotional states (SEEKING, FEAR, RAGE, PANIC, LUST, CARE, PLAY) that can be studied through animal research and applied to human emotional experience.
