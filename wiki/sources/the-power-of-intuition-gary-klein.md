# The Power of Intuition

## Summary

The Power of Intuition is a 2003 book by Gary Klein that provides a pragmatic approach to developing and applying intuitive decision-making skills. Based on cognitive task analysis research across over seventy different fields, the book argues that intuition is a natural outgrowth of experience rather than a mystical gift, and presents methods for strengthening intuitive abilities through deliberate practice, decision-making exercises, and feedback.

## Key facts

- Klein defines intuition as the way we translate our experience into action, enabling us to recognize what is going on (making judgments) and how to react (making decisions). [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p13]
- The book presents the Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) model, which explains how people can make good decisions without generating and comparing a set of options. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p46]
- Research conducted by Klein and colleagues compiled a database of more than a thousand difficult and critical decisions in over seventy different areas, including firefighting, critical care nursing, and business. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p14]
- The book includes an intuition skills training program with three basic elements: identifying decision requirements, practicing difficult decisions in context, and reviewing decision-making experiences. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p59]
- The book provides a framework called 'Planning to Adapt' with a checklist including: modular vs integrated plans, potential for revising goals, planner-implementer connection, communication of changes, problem detection, and centralized authority. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p214]
- The book introduces the STICC framework for communicating intent: Situation, Task, Intent, Concerns, Calibration. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p253]
- Master coaching involves three dimensions: assessing and diagnosing, tailoring instruction, and setting the climate. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p272]
- The book argues that metrics can mislead through loss of history about data collection and loss of context for interpretation. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p287]
- Klein conducted more than 100 research studies examining how people make decisions under real-world conditions. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p352]
- The book presents intuition as how experience is turned into action, based on pattern recognition and mental models rather than mystical powers. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p343]
- The Recognition-Primed Decision model describes how experts recognize patterns and activate action scripts to make rapid decisions. [raw/books/strategy/The Power of Intuition - Gary Klein, Ph.D.pdf#p355]
