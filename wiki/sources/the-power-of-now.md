# The Power of Now: A Guide to Spiritual Enlightenment

## Summary

The Power of Now is a spiritual guide by Eckhart Tolle (born 1948), first published by Namaste Publishing in Vancouver in 1997. Originally released as 3,000 copies, it spread through word-of-mouth before reaching #1 on the New York Times bestseller list five years after publication. The book teaches that suffering arises from identification with the mind and the inability to live fully in the present moment. It offers practices for achieving 'presence'—a state of consciousness beyond thought—and describes the transformation from egoic mind-identification to enlightenment. The text draws on teachings from Jesus, Buddha, Zen masters, Sufism, and Meister Eckhart, synthesizing them into an accessible modern spiritual teaching.
