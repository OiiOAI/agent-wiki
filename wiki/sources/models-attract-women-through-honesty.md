# Models: Attract Women Through Honesty

## Summary

Models: Attract Women Through Honesty is a dating advice book written by Mark Manson, first self-published in July 2011 and subsequently revised in 2012 and 2015. The book challenges conventional 'pickup artist' advice and instead advocates for building genuine attractiveness through non-neediness, vulnerability, and honest self-expression. It became one of the best-selling men's dating advice books on Amazon, outselling titles such as Neil Strauss's The Game and Erik Von Markovik's The Mystery Method.
