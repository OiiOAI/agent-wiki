# Tiny Habits: The Small Changes that Change Everything

## Summary

Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg presents a comprehensive system for behavior change called Behavior Design, centered on the principle that lasting habits are best built by starting extraordinarily small. The book introduces the Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP), which states that behavior occurs when Motivation, Ability, and Prompt converge simultaneously. Fogg, a Stanford researcher who helped create Instagram's behavior-based design, tested these methods with over 40,000 people and argues that the failure to change habits is not a personal flaw but a design flaw in traditional approaches.
