# Algorithms to Live By

## Summary

'Algorithms to Live By' by Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths applies computer science algorithms to everyday human decision-making problems. This chunk covers Chapter 9 on game theory, exploring recursion, Nash equilibrium, the prisoner's dilemma, mechanism design, information cascades, and the concept of computational kindness. The book bridges computer science and human behavior, drawing on interviews with leading researchers including Tim Roughgarden, Christos Papadimitriou, and Noam Nisan.
