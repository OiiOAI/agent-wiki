# Principles of Neural Science, Sixth Edition

## Summary

Principles of Neural Science, Sixth Edition is a comprehensive neuroscience textbook edited by Eric R. Kandel, John D. Koester, Sarah H. Mack, and Steven A. Siegelbaum. The book provides an integrated overview of how genes, molecules, neurons, and neural circuits give rise to behavior, covering topics from cellular neurobiology to cognitive neuroscience and neurological disorders.
