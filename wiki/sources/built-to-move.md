# Built to Move: The Ten Essential Habits to Help You Move Freely and Live Fully

## Summary

Built to Move is a 2023 health book by Kelly Starrett and Juliet Starrett that presents a program of 10 "Vital Signs"—assessments and physical practices—to help readers improve mobility, reduce pain, and maintain functional movement throughout life. The book emphasizes that mobility (not exercise) is the foundation of health and provides practical tests and mobilizations for hip extension, breathing, floor-sitting, and other essential movement patterns.

## Inferences

- Inference: The book's central thesis is that modern sedentary lifestyles have created a population-wide mobility deficit that causes pain, injury, and decreased quality of life.
- Inference: The authors argue that mobility practices are distinct from exercise and should be the foundation upon which exercise is built.
- Inference: The book takes a practical, test-then-treat approach to mobility, where assessments inform specific physical practices.
- Inference: The inclusion of college student fall statistics suggests the book aims to counter the assumption that balance issues are only relevant to elderly populations.
