# Decision Neuroscience: An Integrative Perspective

## Summary

This comprehensive textbook addresses fundamental questions about how the brain makes perceptual, value-based, and complex decisions in nonsocial and social contexts. The book presents recent neuroimaging, electrophysiological, lesional, and neurocomputational studies, combined with hormonal and genetic studies, that have led to a clearer understanding of the neural mechanisms underlying decision-making processes.
