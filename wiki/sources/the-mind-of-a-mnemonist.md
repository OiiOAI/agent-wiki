# The Mind of a Mnemonist: A Little Book about a Vast Memory

## Summary

A clinical study by Soviet neuropsychologist A.R. Luria documenting his 30-year observation of a man known as 'S.' who possessed extraordinary memory capabilities. The book examines how an exceptional memory shaped the subject's entire personality, thinking patterns, and life. Luria uses this case as a 'syndrome' analysis, exploring how one psychic feature—memory—influenced all aspects of the subject's psychological makeup.

## Key facts

- The book documents nearly 30 years of systematic observation of a man designated 'S.' whose remarkable memory was among the keenest described in the literature. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p3]
- Luria's approach treats the case as a study of a syndrome, examining how an unusually developed feature produces changes in the entire structure of psychic life. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p5]
- The study began in the 1920s when S., then a newspaper reporter, was sent to Luria's psychology laboratory after demonstrating remarkable memory abilities to his editor. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p17]
- S. could reproduce any lengthy series of words presented to him, even though they had originally been presented a week, month, year, or many years earlier. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p21]
- The book was originally published in Russian in 1968 under the title 'Malen'kaya kniga o bol'shoy pamyati'. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p131]
- The English translation was by Lynn Solotaroff with an introduction by Jerome Bruner. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p131]
- Luria studied S. over many years, with research records cited from 1934 through 1938 in this section alone. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Mind of a Mnemonist_ A Little Book about a Vast Memory, - Aleksandr R_ Luria, Lynn Solotaroff, Jerome Bruner.pdf#p131]

## Inferences

- Inference: The book represents a pioneering approach in clinical psychology, treating memory pathology not as an isolated phenomenon but as part of a broader personality syndrome.
- Inference: This book represents one of the earliest detailed case studies of synesthesia and extraordinary memory, combining Luria's expertise in neuropsychology with his narrative style.
