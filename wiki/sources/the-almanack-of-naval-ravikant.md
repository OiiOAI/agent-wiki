# The Almanack of Naval Ravikant: A Guide to Wealth and Happiness

## Summary

The Almanack of Naval Ravikant is a book compiled by Eric Jorgenson that collects and organizes the wisdom shared by Naval Ravikant over the past decade through Twitter, blog posts, and podcasts. The book is presented entirely in Naval's own words and covers two main topics: wealth creation and happiness. It was created as a public service and is available for free download. Naval is not earning any money from this book.
