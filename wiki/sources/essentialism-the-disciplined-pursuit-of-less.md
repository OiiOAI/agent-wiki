# Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less

## Summary

Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less is a productivity book by Greg McKeown that explores the philosophy of selective, deliberate focus. The book advocates for distinguishing between the vital few and the trivial many, and investing only in activities that truly matter. The work was published in 2014 by Crown Business, an imprint of Crown Publishing Group.
