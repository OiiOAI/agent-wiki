# The Checklist Manifesto: How to Get Things Right

## Summary

A book by surgeon Atul Gawande exploring how checklists can help manage complexity and prevent avoidable failures in medicine, construction, finance, and other fields. The book argues that in the 21st century, the primary challenge is no longer ignorance but ineptitude—the failure to apply known knowledge correctly—and that simple checklists provide an effective solution.
