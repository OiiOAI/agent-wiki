# Building a Second Brain

## Summary

A book by Tiago Forte presenting a systematic method for organizing digital information and managing personal knowledge. The book introduces the CODE method (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express) and PARA system (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) as frameworks for building what Forte calls a "Second Brain" — an external system for storing, organizing, and deploying knowledge to support creative work and personal productivity.

## Key facts

- The book was first published in Great Britain in 2022 by Profile Books Ltd and in the United States by Atria Books. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p4]
- The book is structured in three parts: The Foundation, The Method (CODE), and The Shift (making things happen). [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p6]
- Forte developed his Second Brain system after personal health challenges, including a chronic functional voice disorder that led him to begin documenting and organizing all information related to his treatment. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p14-16]
- The book is dedicated to Lauren, described as Forte's partner, muse, and greatest inspiration. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p5]
- The book argues that it is never a person's toolset that constrains their potential, it is their mindset. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p195]
- Our attitude toward information profoundly shapes how we see and understand the world and our place in it. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p196]
- The book presents CODE as the four steps of personal knowledge management: Capture, Organize, Distill, and Express. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p205]
- Twelve practical steps are provided for getting started with a Second Brain, including choosing a notes app, setting up PARA, practicing Progressive Summarization, and joining the PKM community. [raw/books/productivity/Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte.pdf#p206-207]
