# The Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological Works of Sigmund Freud, Volume I

## Summary

Volume I of the Revised Standard Edition of The Complete Psychological Works of Sigmund Freud covers Freud's pre-psychoanalytic publications and unpublished drafts from 1886 to 1899. It opens with Freud's Report on his studies in Paris under Charcot and Berlin under other scholars, followed by his early translations of Charcot and Bernheim, case studies on hysteria and hypnotism, extracts from the Fliess correspondence including the famous 'Project for a Scientific Psychology' (1895), and draft materials documenting the development of Freud's psychological thought before the cathartic method was published with Breuer in 1893.
