# Mastery: The Keys to Success and Long-Term Success

## Summary

This book explores the concept of mastery as a lifelong journey rather than a destination, presenting mastery as available to anyone willing to commit to long-term dedicated practice. Leonard illustrates the mastery process through personal experience in aikido and interviews with elite athletes, identifying three common learner types (the Dabbler, the Obsessive, and the Hacker) who fail to stay on the path, and presenting five master keys: Instruction, Practice, Surrender, Intentionality, and The Edge.
