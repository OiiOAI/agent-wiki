# Permission to Feel: Unlocking the Power of Emotions to Help Your Kids, Your Family, and Yourself

## Summary

Permission to Feel by Marc Brackett explores the science of emotional intelligence and presents RULER, a five-skill framework for recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing, and regulating emotions. The book draws on three decades of research to argue that emotion skills are essential for success, well-being, and healthy relationships, and should be taught systematically in schools, workplaces, and families.
