# Rethinking Positive Thinking: Inside the New Science of Motivation

## Summary

Rethinking Positive Thinking (2014) by Gabriele Oettingen presents twenty years of research challenging the conventional wisdom that positive thinking and optimistic fantasies help people achieve their goals. The book introduces 'mental contrasting'—a technique that juxtaposes dreams with obstacles—and its practical application through the WOOP method (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan). Based on studies across Germany and the United States, Oettingen demonstrates that positive fantasies disconnected from reality actually reduce motivation and performance, while mental contrasting energizes action when goals are achievable.
