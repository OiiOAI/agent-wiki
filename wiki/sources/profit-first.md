# Profit First

## Summary

Profit First is a business book by Mike Michalowicz presenting a cash management system that reverses the traditional accounting formula (Sales - Expenses = Profit) to (Sales - Profit = Expenses). The system uses five foundational bank accounts to allocate percentages of revenue to profit, owner's compensation, taxes, and operating expenses on a regular schedule, working with human behavioral tendencies rather than against them.

## Key facts

- Profit First was first published in 2014 by Mike Michalowicz. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p16]
- The book contains eleven chapters, three appendices, a glossary, and index covering setup, assessment, and advanced techniques. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p12-13]
- The author claims approximately 30,000 companies have implemented Profit First since 2014. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p20]
- The book includes testimonials from entrepreneurs reporting significant profit increases and business growth after implementation. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p9-11]
- The author derived the Target Allocation Percentages (TAPs) from surveys of approximately one thousand of the most fiscally elite companies. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p93]
- The book was first released as 'The Toilet Paper Entrepreneur' which contained a short section on Profit First. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p106]
- Mike Michalowicz co-founded the Profit First Professionals network and ProfitCON conference. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p165]
- The revised and expanded edition incorporates lessons learned from thousands of businesses that implemented the system. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p107]
- The book covers chapters 9-11 and the Epilogue, Quick Setup Guide, Glossary, and Index from page 174 onward. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p174]
- The book's core principle is to shift from Sales - Expenses = Profit (GAAP mentality) to Sales - Profit = Expenses, treating profit as the first priority rather than an afterthought. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p174]
- Michalowicz defines financial freedom as 'doing what you choose to do whenever you choose to do it.'. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p184]
- The author identifies eight common mistakes entrepreneurs make when implementing Profit First, including going it alone, allocating too much to profit too soon, growth-first mentality, cutting wrong costs, plowing back profits, raiding the tax account, adding complexity, and skipping bank account setup. [raw/books/business/Profit First - Mike Michalowicz.pdf#p179]
