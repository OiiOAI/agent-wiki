# Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition

## Summary

Principles of Neural Science, Fifth Edition is a comprehensive neuroscience textbook published by McGraw-Hill in 2013. The book is organized into nine parts containing 67 chapters, covering topics from basic cell and molecular biology of neurons through synaptic transmission, neural basis of cognition, perception, movement, unconscious and conscious processing, development, and higher cognitive functions including language, learning, and memory.
