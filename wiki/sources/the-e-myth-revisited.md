# The E-Myth Revisited: Why Most Small Businesses Don't Work and What to Do About It

## Summary

Written by Michael E. Gerber, this business book challenges the common misconception that technical expertise alone is sufficient for business success. It introduces the concept of the E-Myth (Entrepreneurial Myth) and argues that most small businesses fail because their founders suffer from an 'Entrepreneurial Seizure'—starting businesses based on technical skills rather than entrepreneurial thinking. The book presents the Turn-Key Revolution and the Franchise Prototype as models for building sustainable small businesses.
