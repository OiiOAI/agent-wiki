# Anxious

## Summary

Anxious is a 2015 book by Joseph LeDoux that presents a new view of fear and anxiety, challenging the conventional understanding that the amygdala is the 'seat of fear' in the brain. The book argues that fear and anxiety are conscious feelings that arise from different brain circuits than those controlling defensive behaviors like freezing, and proposes a more rigorous conceptual framework for understanding what animal research does and does not reveal about human fear and anxiety.
