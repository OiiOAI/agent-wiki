# High Output Management

## Summary

High Output Management is a business management book by Andrew S. Grove, originally published in 1983 by Random House with subsequent Vintage Books editions in 1995 and 2015. The 2015 edition includes a foreword by Ben Horowitz. The book synthesizes Grove's management experience gained through collaboration with Intel managers, with special acknowledgment to Intel cofounder Gordon Moore. The work covers industrial management principles and is classified under Dewey Decimal classification 658.5.
