# Antifragile: Things That Gain from Disorder

## Summary

A 2012 book by Nassim Nicholas Taleb exploring the concept of 'antifragility'—the property of systems that not only withstand disorder and volatility but actually improve and grow stronger from them. The book is organized into Books and includes a Prologue and an Appendix on 'The Triad' mapping three properties of systems.
