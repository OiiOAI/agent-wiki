# Consciousness and the Brain: Deciphering How the Brain Codes Our Thoughts

## Summary

This book by Stanislas Dehaene presents a scientific framework for understanding consciousness, introducing the 'global neuronal workspace' theory and documenting experimental methods (binocular rivalry, attentional blink, masking) that allow consciousness to be studied in the laboratory. It argues that conscious access—the moment when information enters awareness—can be distinguished from vigilance and attention, and that a significant amount of sophisticated mental processing occurs unconsciously.

- Inference: The global workspace theory implies that nonbiological systems could, in principle, achieve consciousness if they implement similar workspace functions
