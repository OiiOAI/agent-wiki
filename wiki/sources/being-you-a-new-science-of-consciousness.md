# Being You: A New Science of Consciousness

## Summary

Being You presents Anil Seth's framework for a science of consciousness, arguing that conscious experiences are 'controlled hallucinations' arising from predictive brain processes. The book is organized into four parts—Level (consciousness quantity), Content (what we are conscious of), Self (the experience of being a self), and Other (consciousness in non-human animals and machines).

## Key facts

- The book is divided into four parts: Level, Content, Self, and Other. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p17]
- Seth argues that consciousness has more to do with being alive than with being intelligent. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p17]
- The author is Co-Director of the Sackler Centre for Consciousness Science at the University of Sussex. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p14]
- Seth proposes that experiences of being a self emerge from how the brain predicts and controls the internal state of the body. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p17]
- The book presents the 'real problem' approach to consciousness, which accepts that consciousness exists and asks how phenomenological properties relate to brain mechanisms. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p255]
- The book argues for a 'beast machine' theory—a twenty-first-century version of La Mettrie's 'l'homme machine'—where consciousness is tied to physiological reality through predictive processing and the free energy principle. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p256]
- The strategy of consciousness science mirrors how scientific understanding of life transcended vitalism by individuating properties of living systems and accounting for each in terms of underlying mechanisms. - The book investigates the possibility of organoid consciousness in collaboration with Tim Bayne and Marcello Massimini. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p251]
- The book introduces the 'controlled hallucination' theory: perceptual experience is an active construction shaped by brain-based predictions. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p95]
- Seth applies predictive processing and Bayesian inference to explain both perception of the external world and perception of self. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p100]
- The book argues that the self is another perception — a controlled hallucination of a very special kind. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p146]
- The 'beast machine' theory proposes that conscious experiences happen with and because of living bodies. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p168]
- The book addresses the hard problem of consciousness, arguing it can be dissolved by understanding perception as construction rather than direct access. [raw/books/neuroscience/Being You _ A New Science of Consciousness - Seth, Anil.pdf#p136]
