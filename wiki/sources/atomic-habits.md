# Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones

## Summary

Atomic Habits by James Clear is a productivity book that presents a practical framework for building good habits and breaking bad ones. The core framework consists of Four Laws of Behavior Change: Make It Obvious, Make It Attractive, Make It Easy, and Make It Satisfying. The book emphasizes that small, sustainable improvements compound over time, and that habits are most effective when aligned with identity change rather than outcome-based goals. The author draws on scientific research in psychology, neuroscience, and behavioral economics to explain why habits form and how to change them.
