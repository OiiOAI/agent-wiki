# The Symbolic Species: The Co-Evolution of Language and the Brain

## Summary

The Symbolic Species is a neuroscience book by Terrence W. Deacon that investigates the co-evolution of language and the human brain. Published in 1997, it argues that language is not merely a communication system but an outward expression of symbolic representation, and that the difficulty other species face in learning language stems from the symbolic threshold rather than mere complexity. The book examines the missing simple languages paradox—why no species besides humans exhibits even simple forms of symbolic communication—and traces this to differences in how reference works in language versus other forms of animal communication.
