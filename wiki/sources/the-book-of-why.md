# The Book of Why: The New Science of Cause and Effect

## Summary

The Book of Why is a 2018 popular science book by Judea Pearl and Dana Mackenzie that presents the theory of causal inference and its practical applications. It introduces readers to the 'Causal Revolution' in science, explaining how causal diagrams and symbolic languages like the do-operator enable scientists to answer questions about cause-and-effect relationships that traditional statistics could not address. The book covers topics including confounding, counterfactuals, mediation, and the implications of causal reasoning for artificial intelligence.
