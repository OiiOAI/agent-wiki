# The Mindful Body: Thinking Our Way to Chronic Health

## Summary

The Mindful Body is a psychology book by Ellen J. Langer exploring how mindfulness and mindset affect physical health and well-being. The book examines topics including the social construction of rules, risk perception, decision-making, and the mind-body connection. Published by Ballantine Books in 2023, it presents original research on how thoughts and perceptions can influence physical health outcomes.
