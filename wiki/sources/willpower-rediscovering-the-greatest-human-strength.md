# Willpower: Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength

## Summary

This book by psychologist Roy F. Baumeister and journalist John Tierney presents research on willpower and self-control, arguing that self-control is the key to success in life. The authors describe Baumeister's experiments demonstrating that willpower functions like a muscle that becomes fatigued with use, that it draws on glucose as its biological fuel, and that improving willpower is psychology's best hope for contributing to human welfare. The book covers ego depletion, decision fatigue, the history of productivity systems, and practical strategies for improving self-control.
