# Mastery: The Keys to Success and Long-Term Fulfillment

## Summary

This book explores the concept of mastery as a long-term journey of learning and self-development, presenting it as an alternative to the quick-fix, goal-oriented mentality prevalent in modern American society. George Leonard draws on his experience in aikido, interviews with elite athletes, and insights from psychology and neuroscience to map the path of mastery and identify its five essential keys: instruction, practice, surrender, intentionality, and the edge.
