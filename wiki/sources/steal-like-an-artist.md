# Steal Like an Artist: 10 Things Nobody Told You About Being Creative

## Summary

A practical guide to creativity by Austin Kleon, presenting ten principles for creative work. The book argues that all creative work builds on influence, that one should fake it till making it, write what one wants to read, use analog tools, embrace side projects, share work publicly, leverage the Internet for connection, be kind, maintain boring routines, and practice creative subtraction.

## Key facts

- The book presents ten principles about creativity applicable to anyone trying to inject creativity into their life and work. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p10]
- The book originated from a blog post that went viral, which Kleon expanded into a list format at a community college talk. [raw/books/productivity/Steal Like an Artist_ 10 Things Nobody Tol - Kleon, Austin [Kleon, Austin].pdf#p106]
- The ten chapters are: Steal Like an Artist; Don't Wait Until You Know Who You Are to Get Started; Write the Book You Want to Read; Use Your Hands; Side Projects and Hobbies Are Important; The Secret: Do Good Work and Share It with People; Geography Is No Longer Our Master; Be Nice; Be Boring; Creativity Is Subtraction.
