# Rework by Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson

## Summary

Rework is a business book published in 2010 by Crown Business that challenges conventional business wisdom. Written by the founders of 37signals, Jason Fried and David Heinemeier Hansson, the book presents contrarian advice on starting, running, and growing a business. Based on their real-world experience building a profitable software company of only sixteen people, the authors argue against traditional practices like business plans, meetings, budgets, growth for growth's sake, and outside funding. The book is organized into chapters covering topics from initial concepts to company culture, promoting ideas like embracing constraints, launching quickly, and building half a product.
