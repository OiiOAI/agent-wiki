# The Forgetting Machine: Memory, Perception, and the 'Jennifer Aniston Neuron'

## Summary

A neuroscience book by Rodrigo Quian Quiroga exploring how memory and perception work in the brain, arguing that the brain constructs meaning from minimal information rather than recording experiences verbatim. The book covers neural mechanisms of memory, the illusion of detailed visual perception, the importance of forgetting, mnemonic techniques, and the nature of concept representation in the brain.
