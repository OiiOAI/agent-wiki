# 般若波罗蜜多心经（简体注音本）

## Summary

This electronic book presents the Prajnaparamita Heart Sutra (般若波罗蜜多心经), a foundational Mahayana Buddhist text attributed to the Tang Dynasty monk and translator Xuanzang (玄奘, 602–664 CE). The text is based on the Qianlong Edition of the Buddhist Canon (《乾隆大藏经》), with pinyin romanization and sentence delimiters added by Renhui Caotang (仁慧草堂). The volume includes not only the core Heart Sutra text but also a comprehensive liturgy for recitation: preparatory purification rituals, opening chants (开经偈, 杨枝净水赞), various supporting mantras (真言) such as the Great Compassion Mantra (大悲咒, 84 verses), praise verses (观音菩萨赞, 赞观音文), dedication prayers (回向偈), and the Three Refuges (三皈依).

- The Heart Sutra contains the celebrated emptiness doctrine: '色不异空，空不异色，色即是空，空即是无受想行识，亦复如是.'. [raw/books/philosophy/般若波罗蜜多心经(简体注音本) - 唐三藏法师玄奘,仁慧草堂.pdf#p6]

## Inferences

- Inference: The inclusion of extensive preparatory rituals, mantras, and dedication prayers suggests this edition is designed for lay practitioners conducting home liturgy rather than monastic scholarly study.
- Inference: The Great Compassion Mantra (大悲咒) was likely appended because it frequently accompanies Heart Sutra recitation in Chinese Buddhist practice, particularly in Pure Land and Esoteric traditions.
