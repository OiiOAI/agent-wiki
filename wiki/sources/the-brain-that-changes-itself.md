# The Brain That Changes Itself

## Summary

A groundbreaking neuroscience book by Norman Doidge, M.D., documenting the revolutionary discovery that the human brain can change itself through thought and activity. The book presents stories of scientists, doctors, and patients who have achieved remarkable neuroplastic transformations without operations or medications, challenging the centuries-old belief that brain anatomy is fixed after childhood.
