# The Happiness Trap: How to Stop Struggling and Start Living

## Summary

The Happiness Trap is a self-help book by general practitioner and psychotherapist Russ Harris, first published in 2007 with this second edition in 2022. Based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), the book teaches readers how to escape a psychological trap where the pursuit of happiness paradoxically increases suffering. It presents practical skills for handling difficult thoughts and feelings, making values-guided choices, and building a meaningful life.

- ACT was developed in the United States in the mid-1980s by psychologist Steven C. Hayes and his colleagues Kelly Wilson and Kirk Strosahl. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p11]

- Russ Harris is a medical doctor and psychotherapist who practices Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. [raw/books/psychology/The Happiness Trap_ How to Stop Struggling - Russ Harris.pdf#p11]
