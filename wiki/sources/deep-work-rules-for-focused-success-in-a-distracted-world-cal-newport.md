# Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World

## Summary

In this book, Cal Newport argues that focused, distraction-free work—termed 'deep work'—is increasingly rare and increasingly valuable in the modern economy. He contends that the few who cultivate this skill and make it the core of their working life will thrive. The book is organized into two parts: first establishing the deep work hypothesis through arguments about value, rarity, and meaning, then providing practical rules for integrating deep work into one's schedule.

## Key facts

- Deep Work: Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p5]
- Shallow Work: Noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p7]
- The Deep Work Hypothesis states that the ability to perform deep work is becoming increasingly rare at exactly the same time it is becoming increasingly valuable in our economy. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p11]
- The book is written by Georgetown University computer science professor Cal Newport. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p1]
- The book presents four rules for cultivating deep work: Work Deeply, Embrace Boredom, Quit Social Media, and Drain the Shallows. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p72]
- Cal Newport has been a depth devotee for more than a decade, writing quality peer-reviewed papers while rarely working past five on weekdays. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p152]
- Newport's research productivity improved from two good papers per year as a graduate student to four good papers per year on average once he became a professor. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p153]
- Bill Gates worked with such intensity during the Altair BASIC project that he would often collapse into sleep on his keyboard, then sleep for an hour or two and pick up right where he left off. - Paul Allen described Gates's concentration during the Altair project as 'a prodigious feat of concentration.'. [raw/books/productivity/Deep Work_ Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World - Cal Newport.pdf#p151]
