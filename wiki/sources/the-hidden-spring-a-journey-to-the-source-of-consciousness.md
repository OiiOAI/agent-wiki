# The Hidden Spring: A Journey to the Source of Consciousness

## Summary

The Hidden Spring is a book by Mark Solms that proposes a radical solution to the hard problem of consciousness. Solms argues that consciousness is not generated by the cerebral cortex, as traditionally assumed, but by ancient brainstem structures that humans share with fishes — the 'hidden spring' of the title. The book integrates findings from affective neuroscience, neuropsychology, psychoanalysis, and computational neuroscience (particularly Karl Friston's free energy principle) to argue that feelings are the essence of consciousness and that consciousness is engineerable. Solms draws on his own research on dreaming, on the work of Jaak Panksepp, Antonio Damasio, and Bjorn Merker, and on his clinical experience with neurological patients to build his case.
