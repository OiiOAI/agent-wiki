# Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf

## Summary

A collaborative anthology celebrating 21 years of the Cynefin Framework, featuring contributions from practitioners worldwide applying complexity thinking to diverse fields including public services, healthcare, law enforcement, military, and organizational leadership. The book documents the evolution, applications, and practical implementations of Dave Snowden's sensemaking framework.

## Key facts

- The Cynefin Framework was created by Dave Snowden and has been influencing public service practice since before 2008. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p134]
- SenseMaker is software developed by Cognitive Edge that allows collection and sensemaking of narratives, stories, and fragmented information reflecting the reality of life. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p127]
- The Cynefin Centre was established at Bangor University in 2016 as part of the Psychology Department with links to the Wales Centre for Behaviour Change, Computing Department, and Design and Innovation Department. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p129]
- Wales is the first country to have a legal requirement on public services to act sustainably and consider the impact of future generations' decisions through the 2015 Wellbeing of Future Generations Act. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p132]
- The HBR article 'A Leader's Framework for Decision Making' by Snowden and Boone won an Academy of Management award in 2008 and an Emerald Citation in 2011. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p139]
- The book celebrates the 21st anniversary of the Cynefin Framework. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p249]
- The Cynefin Framework has aided problem definition and resolution in military conflicts, factional disputes, software development, food chain risk management, and network science. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p249]
- The name 'Cynefin' is Welsh, derived from 'Combrogi' meaning 'fellow countrymen' and relates to 'the place of your birth and upbringing, the environment in which you live and to which you are naturally acclimatised.'. [raw/books/strategy/Cynefin - Weaving Sense - Making into the Fa - Dave Snowden.pdf#p250]
