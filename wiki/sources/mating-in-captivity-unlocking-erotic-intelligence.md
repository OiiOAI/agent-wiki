# Mating in Captivity: Unlocking Erotic Intelligence

## Summary

A couples therapist explores the fundamental paradox of modern relationships: that the security and intimacy we seek in committed partnerships often undermine the mystery and excitement that fuel erotic desire. Drawing on clinical experience and cultural analysis, Perel examines why desire fades in long-term relationships and offers perspectives on reconciling eroticism with domestic life.
