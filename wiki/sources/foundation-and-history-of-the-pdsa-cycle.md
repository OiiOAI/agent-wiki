# Foundation and History of the PDSA Cycle

## Summary

This paper by Ronald Moen provides a comprehensive history of W. Edwards Deming's PDSA Cycle for Learning and Improvement, tracing its origins from the scientific method foundations of the 1600s through to Deming's 1993 version. It documents the evolution of the Shewhart Cycle (1939), the Deming Wheel (1950), the Japanese PDCA (1951 and 1985), and the subsequent evolution of Deming's PDSA from 1986 to 1993, concluding with Deming's documented reactions to the PDCA.
