# Good Strategy/Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters

## Summary

A 2011 book by Richard P. Rumelt that distinguishes between good and bad strategy in business, government, and organizational leadership. The book introduces key concepts including 'the kernel' of strategy and 'the proximate objective', and identifies common strategy failures such as fluff, blue-sky objectives, and failure to face problems. Published simultaneously in the UK by Profile Books and in the US by Crown Business.
