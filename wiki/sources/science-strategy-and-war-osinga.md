# Science, Strategy and War: The Strategic Theory of John Boyd

## Summary

This book is Frans Osinga's doctoral dissertation from Leiden University (defended January 11, 2005), examining the strategic theory of John Boyd. It provides a comprehensive interpretation of Boyd's A Discourse on Winning and Losing, arguing that his work is far more comprehensive than the popular 'rapid OODA looping' interpretation suggests. The study analyzes Boyd's formative factors including his military experience, study of military history, and engagement with scientific developments in thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, and chaos theory. Osinga positions Boyd as potentially the first post-modern strategist and demonstrates the significant influence of Boyd's ideas on U.S. and Western military doctrine.
