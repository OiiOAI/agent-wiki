# The Mindful Geek: Secular Meditation for Skeptics and Geeks

## Summary

A practical guide to mindfulness meditation written specifically for rational, science-oriented individuals who may be skeptical of religious or spiritual approaches. The book presents meditation as a 'technology for hacking the human wetware,' emphasizing evidence-based benefits while stripping away cultural and religious contexts.

## Key facts

- The book teaches Basic Mindfulness, a comprehensive meditation system created by Shinzen Young, adapted by Taft for tech-oriented practitioners. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- Taft has over 30 years of meditation experience in Buddhist and Hindu traditions. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p9]
- The book recommends 30 minutes of meditation daily, five days a week as a target practice. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p29]
- Taft teaches meditation at Google and other major corporations. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p11]
- The book was written for 'smart people' who want a secular, science-based approach to meditation. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p108]
- Shinzen Young is identified as the primary teacher behind most methods described in the book. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p115]
- The book presents meditation as a tool for changing one's relationship to pain, emotions, and life circumstances. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p115]
- The book presents meditation as a set of trainable skills: concentration, sensory clarity, and acceptance. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p213]
- Taft recommends practicing only one meditation technique per session to avoid 'psychological indigestion.'. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p213]
- The author suggests 30 minutes of daily meditation if possible, or at least 10 minutes. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p216]
- Taft plans to cover life-changing psychological topics in a follow-up book, recommending Sam Harris's 'Waking Up' as a preview. [raw/books/psychology/The Mindful Geek_ Secular Meditation for S - Michael Taft.pdf#p216]
