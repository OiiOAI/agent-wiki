# The Gift of Fear: Survival Signals That Protect Us from Violence

## Summary

A bestselling book by security expert Gavin de Becker exploring how intuition and survival signals can protect people from violence. The book argues that violence is predictable and that everyone possesses an innate ability to detect danger through subtle behavioral cues. Published in 1997 with multiple editions and translations, it covers topics including stranger danger, domestic violence, stalking, workplace violence, and assassination.

## Key facts

- The book argues that violence is predictable rather than random, and that people can learn to recognize pre-incident indicators before violence occurs. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p17]
- The author developed MOSAIC, a threat assessment system used for screening threats to justices of the U.S. Supreme Court and other high-profile targets. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p20]
- The book covers survival signals, threats, intimate violence, stalking, workplace violence, and public figure threats across 15 chapters plus appendices. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p5]
- The author asserts that intuition is the body's most complex cognitive process and serves as a guardian that warns of hazards. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p16]
- The book was a #1 National bestseller in the US and Canada and published in 25 editions including multiple translations. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p7]
- The book presents the JACA framework for predicting violence: Justification, Alternatives, Consequences, and Ability. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p67]
- It includes detailed case studies of workplace violence including the ESL shooting, USAir Flight 1771, and various stalking cases. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p98]
- The book distinguishes between threats (unconditional statements of intent to harm) and intimidations (conditional statements with if/else/until/unless). [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p77]
- The author introduces the 'rule of opposites' for predicting dangerousness by listing favorable behaviors and noting their opposites. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p61]
- The book was written by Gavin de Becker, a security professional who founded Gavin de Becker & Associates. [raw/books/psychology/The Gift of Fear - Gavin de Becker.pdf#p205]
