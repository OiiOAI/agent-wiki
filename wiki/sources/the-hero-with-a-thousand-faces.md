# The Hero With a Thousand Faces

## Summary

The Hero With a Thousand Faces is a 1948 work of comparative mythology by Joseph Campbell that examines the universal pattern of the hero's journey found across world mythologies and religions. Campbell argues that myths from diverse cultures share common structural elements and symbolic meanings, and he employs psychoanalysis as a framework for interpreting these shared symbols. The book aims to reveal the underlying truths that religious and mythological narratives communicate through symbolic language, promoting human mutual understanding across cultural boundaries.
