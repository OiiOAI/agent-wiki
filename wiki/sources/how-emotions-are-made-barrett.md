# How Emotions Are Made: The Secret Life of the Brain

## Summary

This book by Lisa Feldman Barrett challenges the classical view of emotion—which holds that emotions are universal, innate, and have distinct biological fingerprints—by proposing the 'theory of constructed emotion.' Barrett argues that emotions are not triggered reactions but are actively constructed by the brain using concepts, past experience, and interoceptive sensations. The book synthesizes research from psychology, neuroscience, and anthropology across chapters covering the failed search for emotion fingerprints, the mechanisms of emotion construction, the myth of universal emotions, and the role of interoception and prediction in creating emotional experience.
