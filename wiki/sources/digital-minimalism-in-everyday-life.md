# Digital Minimalism in Everyday Life

## Summary

This book provides a practical guide to implementing digital minimalism in daily life. It covers understanding technology addiction, performing digital decluttering, conducting digital detoxes, and practicing digital mindfulness. The author draws inspiration from Cal Newport's theoretical framework but focuses on actionable steps for the average person to reduce technology dependence and reclaim freedom.
