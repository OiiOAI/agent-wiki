# Situation Awareness Analysis and Measurement

## Summary

This book provides a comprehensive examination of situation awareness (SA), its theoretical foundations, analytical approaches, and measurement methodologies. Edited by Mica R. Endsley and Daniel J. Garland, the volume addresses situation awareness in the context of human factors, particularly in aviation and automated systems environments. The book includes bibliographical references and an index.
