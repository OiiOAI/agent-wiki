# Purposeful Curiosity: The Power of Asking

## Summary

A productivity book exploring purposeful curiosity as a framework for innovation, personal growth, and transformative exploration. The author, a professor at Bayes Business School, spent eight years researching curiosity through interviews with over sixty pioneers across diverse fields including space exploration, journalism, design, athletics, and entrepreneurship. The book presents a nine-chapter 'master class in curiosity' covering mindset, fear conquest, expertise development, team assembly, and resilience building.
