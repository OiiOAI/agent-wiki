# The Speed of Trust

## Summary

The Speed of Trust by Stephen M.R. Covey presents a comprehensive framework for understanding, establishing, and restoring trust in personal and professional contexts. The book introduces the concept that trust is a tangible, measurable asset that directly impacts organizational speed and cost, arguing that trust affects every relationship, decision, and outcome in life. Covey proposes the '5 Waves of Trust' model and the '4 Cores of Credibility' as actionable frameworks for building trust at individual, relational, organizational, market, and societal levels.
