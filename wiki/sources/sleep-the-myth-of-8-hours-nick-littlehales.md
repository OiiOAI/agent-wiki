# Sleep: The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of Naps

## Summary

This book by sports sleep coach Nick Littlehales presents the R90 Sleep Recovery Program, a method developed over nearly two decades working with elite athletes including Manchester United, British Cycling, and Team Sky. The approach challenges the conventional 'eight hours of sleep' wisdom, instead advocating for sleep management through 90-minute cycles, understanding circadian rhythms and chronotypes, and incorporating pre- and post-sleep routines as well as daytime recovery periods.

- The book introduces the R90 (Recovery in 90 Minutes) Sleep Recovery Program developed over nearly two decades with elite athletes. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p13]
- Littlehales worked with Manchester United starting in the late 1990s, including Sir Alex Ferguson and the 'Class of '92' players. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p8]
- He later worked with British Cycling, Team Sky, British Olympians and Paralympians, and various football clubs including Real Madrid. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p9]
- The average person in Britain gets a little over six and a half hours of sleep per night, with over a third getting by on only five to six hours. [raw/books/health/Sleep_ The Myth of 8 Hours, the Power of N - Nick Littlehales [Littlehales, Nick].pdf#p18]
