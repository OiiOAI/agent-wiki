# Behave: The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst

## Summary

A comprehensive exploration of the biology underlying human behavior, examining factors from one second before a behavior (neurobiology) to millennia before (evolution). The book synthesizes neuroscience, endocrinology, primatology, and psychology to understand aggression, violence, cooperation, empathy, and morality.
