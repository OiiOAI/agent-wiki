# When Brains Dream: Exploring the Science and Mystery of Sleep

## Summary

A comprehensive neuroscience book by Antonio Zadra and Robert Stickgold that explores the scientific study of dreams, examining when, how, and why we dream. The book presents a novel neuroscientific model for understanding dream function and is noted for its rigorous scientific approach to explaining the phenomenon of dreaming.
