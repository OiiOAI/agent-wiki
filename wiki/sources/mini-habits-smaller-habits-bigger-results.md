# Mini Habits: Smaller Habits, Bigger Results

## Summary

A productivity book by Stephen Guise presenting the Mini Habits strategy for building lasting healthy habits. The author, after a decade of experimenting with personal development, discovered in 2012 that tiny daily actions (mini habits) produce more consistent and lasting behavior change than traditional motivation-based approaches. The book is structured into seven parts covering habit formation theory, brain science, willpower vs. motivation, and practical application.
