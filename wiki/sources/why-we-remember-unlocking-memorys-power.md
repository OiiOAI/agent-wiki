# Why We Remember: Unlocking Memory's Power

## Summary

This book explores the science of human memory, challenging the common belief that forgetting is a flaw. Rather than asking why we forget, Ranganath argues we should ask why we remember. The book covers fundamental mechanisms of memory, including the roles of the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus, how memories are constructed rather than retrieved, the relationship between imagination and memory, and how emotional experiences shape our memories. Ranganath, a neuroscientist at UC Davis, draws on research with patients, memory athletes, and cutting-edge neuroscience to explain how the 'remembering self' constantly shapes our present and future through the choices we make.
