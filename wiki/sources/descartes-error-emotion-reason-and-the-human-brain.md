# Descartes' Error: Emotion, Reason, and the Human Brain

## Summary

A neuroscience book by Antonio R. Damasio, M.D., published in 1994 by G.P. Putnam's Sons (Avon Books division). The book challenges Cartesian dualism by arguing that emotion and reason are inseparable in human cognition. It presents evidence that severe impairment of emotions precludes rationality, and proposes new physiological mechanisms regarding the role of the brain's frontal lobes in decision-making. The work synthesizes contemporary knowledge of brain function for educated laypersons, medical professionals, and scientists.
