# In Search of Memory: The Emergence of a New Science of Mind

## Summary

An intellectual autobiography by Nobel laureate Eric R. Kandel that interweaves his personal history—shaped by his childhood in Vienna during the Nazi era—with the scientific history of how the biology of mind emerged as a new discipline over the five decades of his career. The book traces his journey from fascination with memory (initially through history and psychoanalysis) to the cellular and molecular biology of memory storage.

- Kandel received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 2000 for his contributions to the study of memory storage in the brain. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p16]
- The book is structured into six parts covering Kandel's personal history, the history of neural science, the biology of learning in simple systems, molecules and memory, complex memory, and prospects. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p11]

- Kandel recounts how Karl Popper's philosophy of science transformed Eccles from an advocate of electrical transmission to accepting chemical transmission. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p109]
- The book details Kandel's collaboration with Alden Spencer at NIMH, where they obtained the first intracellular recordings from hippocampal pyramidal cells. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p151]
- Kandel explains his reasoning for choosing Aplysia as an experimental animal: simple nervous system, large identifiable neurons, and simple behaviors controlled by small neural circuits. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p157]
- The book presents Kandel's neural analogs of learning approach, showing how Pavlovian learning protocols can be simulated at the cellular level in Aplysia. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p171]
- Kandel received the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for his work on signal transduction in the nervous system. [raw/books/neuroscience/In Search of Memory_ The Emergence of a Ne - Eric R. Kandel.pdf#p236]
