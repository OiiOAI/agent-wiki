# Spark: The Revolutionary New Science of Exercise and the Brain

## Summary

This book explores the neuroscience connecting physical exercise to brain function, demonstrating how aerobic activity transforms the brain at the cellular level to improve learning, mood, memory, and mental health. The author presents research on neurotransmitters, neurotrophic factors like BDNF, and presents Naperville, Illinois's revolutionary PE program as a case study showing how exercise can make students fitter and smarter.
