# Poor Charlie's Almanack: The Wit and Wisdom of Charles T. Munger

## Summary

Poor Charlie's Almanack is a compilation of speeches and talks by Charles T. Munger delivered over a twenty-year period, first published in 2005 with an expanded third edition in 2007. The book presents Munger's approach to worldly wisdom, decision making, and investing through eleven speeches covering topics ranging from elementary wisdom in investment management to the psychology of human misjudgment.
