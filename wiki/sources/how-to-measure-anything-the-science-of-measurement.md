# How to Measure Anything: The Science of Measurement

## Summary

A comprehensive reference book covering measurement units, scales, and instruments across multiple disciplines including astronomy, geology, medicine, meteorology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics. The book traces the history of measurement from ancient civilizations to the modern SI system, providing definitions and explanations for hundreds of measurement concepts used in science, commerce, and everyday life.
