# Boyd: The Fighter Pilot Who Changed the World

## Summary

This biography by Robert Coram tells the story of Colonel John Richard Boyd, the fighter pilot who became one of the most important and influential military thinkers of the twentieth century. The book chronicles Boyd's journey from Erie, Pennsylvania, through his service in three wars, to his development of revolutionary military theories that changed air combat tactics, aircraft design, and U.S. military doctrine.
