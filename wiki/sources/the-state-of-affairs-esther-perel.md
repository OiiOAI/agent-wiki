# The State of Affairs: Explorations in Infidelity and Commitment

## Summary

Esther Perel's 'The State of Affairs' (2017) explores infidelity from multiple angles, arguing that understanding affairs requires moving beyond moral condemnation to examine their meanings and motives. The book presents infidelity as a window into modern relationships, addressing questions of desire, jealousy, trust, and commitment. Perel's approach combines clinical experience with anthropological insight, examining how cultural shifts have transformed both marriage and the impact of infidelity.
