# The Experience Machine: How Our Minds Predict and Shape Reality

## Summary

This book by Andy Clark presents the theory of predictive processing—the idea that the human brain is fundamentally a prediction machine that constructs experiences from mixtures of expectation and sensory evidence. The work explores how predictions shape perception, action, bodily experience, emotion, and psychiatric conditions, arguing that reality as we experience it is built from our own predictions rather than passively received from the world.
