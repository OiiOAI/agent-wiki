# The Brain from Inside Out

## Summary

This book by neuroscientist György Buzsáki presents an 'inside-out' framework for understanding brain function, arguing that the brain's primary job is to generate actions and examine their consequences, rather than passively perceive and represent the outside world as maintained by the dominant 'outside-in' empiricist paradigm. The work critically examines inherited neuroscience vocabulary, causation logic, and the relationship between perception and action.

- The author refers to his recommended strategy as the 'inside-out' approach, contrasting it with the dominant 'outside-in' framework of mainstream neuroscience. [raw/books/neuroscience/The Brain from Inside Out - György Buzsáki MD, PhD.pdf#p14]
