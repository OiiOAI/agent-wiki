# Certain to Win: The Strategy of John Boyd, Applied to Business

## Summary

This book by Chet Richards applies Colonel John R. Boyd's philosophy of maneuver conflict to business strategy. It examines how the principles behind the German Blitzkrieg and the Toyota Production System share a common strategic foundation rooted in Sun Tzu's ancient wisdom. The core thesis is that time—specifically decision cycle speed—can offset advantages in size and technology, enabling smaller or less advantaged competitors to prevail. Richards introduces Boyd's OODA Loop (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act) as the fundamental mechanism of competitive advantage and describes the organizational climate attributes necessary for rapid decision cycles.
