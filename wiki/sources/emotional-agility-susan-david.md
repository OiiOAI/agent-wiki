# Emotional Agility: Get Unstuck, Embrace Change, and Thrive in Work and Life

## Summary

Emotional Agility is a 2016 book by psychologist Susan David that presents a framework for navigating life's challenges by developing flexibility with one's thoughts, feelings, and behaviors. The book introduces a four-movement process—Showing Up, Stepping Out, Walking Your Why, and Moving On—designed to help readers unhook from self-defeating patterns and live in alignment with their core values. David draws on decades of research in psychology and her own experiences growing up in apartheid-era South Africa to argue that emotional agility is key to well-being and success.
