# Change: Principles of Problem Formulation and Problem Resolution

## Summary

A foundational text in change theory and problem resolution, presenting principles for understanding how problems are formulated and how change can be effectively induced. The work emerges from the Mental Research Institute in Palo Alto and draws on the interactional view of human communication and psychotherapy.
