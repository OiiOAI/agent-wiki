# Free Agents: How Evolution Gave Us Free Will

## Summary

This book by Kevin J. Mitchell argues that free will is real and emerged through evolution, challenging both hard determinism and compatibilism. Mitchell contends that agency is the defining characteristic of life, tracing its evolution from the origin of life itself through the development of nervous systems to conscious cognitive control in humans. The author presents a conceptual framework that naturalizes agency by grounding concepts of purpose, meaning, and value in biological organization rather than invoking either physical determinism or dualism.
