# Modern Sociological Theory, 7th Edition

## Summary

Modern Sociological Theory (7th Edition) is a comprehensive textbook by George Ritzer offering an overview of contemporary sociological theories and theorists. The book is structured into four parts: an introduction to sociological theory, major modern sociological schools (covering structural functionalism, neo-Marxism, systems theory, and symbolic interactionism), micro-macro and agency-structure integration, and contemporary developments including globalization theory, queer theory, critical race theories, and practice theory. The book includes biographical sketches of major theorists from Ibn-Khaldun to contemporary thinkers.
