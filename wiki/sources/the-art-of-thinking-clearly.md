# The Art of Thinking Clearly

## Summary

A comprehensive guide to cognitive biases and systematic thinking errors by Rolf Dobelli. The book presents 99 short chapters, each focusing on a specific psychological fallacy, offering practical insights into why humans consistently make irrational decisions and how to recognize and avoid these mental pitfalls in daily life, business, and investing.
