# Show Your Work!: 10 Ways to Share Your Creativity and Get Discovered

## Summary

A practical guide for creatives on sharing their work online. Kleon argues that in the digital age, artists don't need to self-promote; instead, they should share their process openly to attract an audience. The book presents ten principles organized around the theme of showing your work: not needing to be a genius, focusing on process over product, sharing small things daily, opening your cabinet of curiosities, telling good stories, teaching what you know, avoiding becoming human spam, learning to take criticism, selling out (monetizing), and sticking around for the long haul.

## Key facts

- The book presents a philosophy of radical openness in sharing creative work online as an alternative to traditional self-promotion. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p9]
- Kleon states: 'You don't really find an audience for your work; they find you. But it's not enough to be good. In order to be found, you have to be findable.'. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p9]
- The book is structured around ten numbered chapters covering different aspects of sharing creative work. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p4]
- Kleon's previous book Steal Like an Artist is described as being about stealing influence from others, while Show Your Work is about how to influence others by letting them steal from you. [raw/books/productivity/Show Your Work!_ 10 Ways to Share Your Cre - Austin Kleon.pdf#p9]
