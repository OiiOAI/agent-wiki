# The Neuropsychology of Dreams: A Clinico-Anatomical Study

## Summary

This book by Mark Solms represents the first systematic application of the clinico-anatomical method to the study of dreaming. Conducted between 1985 and 1991, the research investigated dream changes in 361 neurological and neurosurgical patients, correlating subjective dream reports with clinical presentations and pathological-anatomical findings. The central argument challenges the prevailing assumption that REM sleep mechanisms explain dreaming, asserting that dreaming and REM sleep are distinct phenomena that can occur independently. The book aims to establish a nosology of dream disorders with neuropathological significance, placing disorders of dreaming on equivalent footing with aphasias, apraxias, and agnosias in neuropsychology.
